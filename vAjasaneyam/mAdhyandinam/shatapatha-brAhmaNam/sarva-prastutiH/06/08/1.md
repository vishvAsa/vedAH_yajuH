<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

The gods, while driving about on wheels, saw [^egg_543] this rite (sacrificial performance), for it was indeed in driving about on wheels that they saw this rite: hence it is to the cart that the formulas relate at the (performance with) sacrificial cakes [^egg_544], and to the cart in the building of the fire-altar [^egg_545].
