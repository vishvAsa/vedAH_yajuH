The gods, while driving about on wheels, saw 2 this rite (sacrificial performance), for it was indeed in driving about on wheels that they saw this rite: hence it is to the cart that the formulas relate at the (performance with) sacrificial cakes 3, and to the cart in the building of the fire-altar 4.
