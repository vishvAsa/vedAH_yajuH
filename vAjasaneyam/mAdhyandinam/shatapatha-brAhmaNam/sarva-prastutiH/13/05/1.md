Here the passage 'One Sāman, many metres,' according to the commentary, refers to the Vāravantīya tune as being employed, on this occasion, for the texts of the Yajñāyajñīya, the Sākamaśva (II, 55-57, here the Calc. ed., by mistake, calls the second tune figured for chanting, like the first, Sākamaśva, instead of Vāravantīya), the Saubhara (II, 230-2, where the Calc. ed., by mistake, omits the name Vāravantīya), and the Tairaśca (II, 233-5; curiously enough, the Tairaśca is not mentioned, in Lāṭy.
