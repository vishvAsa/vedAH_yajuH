<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Well, that year is the bull among the seasons, and the thirteenth (or intercalary) month is an excrescence of the year; and this Aśvamedha is the bull among sacrifices; and inasmuch as the bull has an excrescence (hump), one may add on a thirteenth cubit to the rope as an excrescence to this (Aśvamedha): even as the bull's hump is attached [^egg_688] (to his back), suchlike would this be.

[^egg_688]: 276:2 Lit., spread out.
</details>
