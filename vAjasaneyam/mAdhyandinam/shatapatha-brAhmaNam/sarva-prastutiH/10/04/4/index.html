  1. Whilst he was practising austerities, lights went upwards from those hair-pits 1 of his; and those lights are those stars: as many stars as there are, so many hair-pits there are; and as many hair-pits as there are, so many muhūrtas there are in a (sacrificial performance) of a thousand years.
