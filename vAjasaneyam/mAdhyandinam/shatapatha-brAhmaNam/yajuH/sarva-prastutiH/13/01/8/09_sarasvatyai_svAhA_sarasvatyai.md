</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

5. 'To Sarasvatī hail! To Sarasvatī, the pure, hail! To Sarasvatī, the great, hail!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

by means of her he sustains it (the Aśvamedha) “To Sarasvatī-Svāhā, to Sarasvatī, the pure -Svāhā; to Sarasvatī - the great - Svāhā.” Sarasvatī is indeed speech. He thus sustains it by means of speech. “To Pūṣa-Svāhā; to Pūṣa - the guardian on the road - Svāhā; to Pūṣa the watchful-Svāhā”.
</details>
