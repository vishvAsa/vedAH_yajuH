<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Hail!'

for such-like it was, it was indeed golden.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They are the ‘progressively increasing’. They are ‘progressively decreasing (at which) by the first (day) he milks three (teats) and then (on the second day) two and then (on the third day) one. They are the ‘progressively decreasing’. These which are ‘progressively increasing’ they are (the same as) the ‘progressively decreasing’.
</details>
