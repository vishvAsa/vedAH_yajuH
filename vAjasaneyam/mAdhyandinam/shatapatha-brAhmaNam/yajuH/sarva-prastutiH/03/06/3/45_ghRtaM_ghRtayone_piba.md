<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

घृतं᳓ घृतयोने पिब प्र᳓प्र यज्ञ᳓पतिं तिर᳓ स्वा᳓हे᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he should make the offering taking four ladle- full of ghee with the Ṛks praising Viṣṇu. "Stride thou widely O Viṣṇu, make wide room for our stay. Thou born of ghee, drink the ghee and prompt the lord of the sacrifice (Yajamāna) ever onwards—Svāhā".
</details>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Hail!'
</details>
