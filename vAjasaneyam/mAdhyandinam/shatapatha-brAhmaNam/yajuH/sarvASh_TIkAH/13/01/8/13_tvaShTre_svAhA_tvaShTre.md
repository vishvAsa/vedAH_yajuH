<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

7. 'To Tvashṭr̥ hail! To Tvashṭr̥, the seminal, hail! To Tvashṭr̥, the multiform hail!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“To Tvaṣṭā - Svāhā; to Tvaṣṭā, the seminal - Svāhā”, to Tvaṣṭā - the Pururūpa (of multifarious forms) - Svāhā.” Tvaṣṭā is indeed the maker of the couples of animals. By means of forms, he thus sus- tains it.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त्व᳘ष्ट्रे स्वाहा॥  
त्व᳘ष्ट्रे तुरीपा᳘य स्वा᳘हा त्व᳘ष्ट्रे पुरुरूपा᳘य स्वाहे᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

त्व᳘ष्ट्रे स्वाहा॥  
त्व᳘ष्ट्रे तुरीपा᳘य [^wbr_1] स्वा᳘हा त्व᳘ष्ट्रे पुरुरूपा᳘य स्वाहे᳘ति

[^wbr_1]: The संहिता (२२,२०.) reads तुरी᳘पाय.
</details>
