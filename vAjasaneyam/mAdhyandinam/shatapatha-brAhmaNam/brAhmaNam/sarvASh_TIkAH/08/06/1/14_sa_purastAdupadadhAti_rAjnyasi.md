<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

5. In front he lays down one, with (Vāj. S. XV, 10), 'Queen thou art, the Eastern region,' for a queen indeed the eastern region is;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He places one of them on the east (front) (saying), "You the eastern quarter is the queen”, for, the eastern quarter is indeed the queen.
</details>
