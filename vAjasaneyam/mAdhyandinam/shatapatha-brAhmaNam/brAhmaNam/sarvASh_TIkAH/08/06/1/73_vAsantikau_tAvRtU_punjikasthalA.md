<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Puñjikasthalā and Kratusthalā are his Apsarās (attendant nymphs).” According to Māhitthi these two are the quarter and the intermediate quarter.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>
