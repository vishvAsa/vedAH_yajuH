<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

19. Then on the left (north) side (he places one), with (Vāj. S. XV, 17), 'This one on the left, of ever flowing blessings;'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then (he places one of them) on the left side (northwards) (say- ing), “This one on the left, the samyadvasu (bountiful wealth).” Sacrifice is indeed on the left.
</details>
