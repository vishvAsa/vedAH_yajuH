<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

23. Now; then, as to the order of proceeding. Having laid down (a brick) in front, he lays down those on the right, behind, on the left, and in the middle.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This is the order in which it should proceed. First laying a brick in front, he lays down those on the right side, then behind, then on the left and then in the middle.
</details>
