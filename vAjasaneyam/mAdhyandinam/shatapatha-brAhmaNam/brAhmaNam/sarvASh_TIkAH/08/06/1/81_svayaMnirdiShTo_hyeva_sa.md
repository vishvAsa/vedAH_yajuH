<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Let him disregard this also, for indeed marked out of himself is he whom he who knows this hates.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Or he can ignore this (naming the enemy) because whom he hates is already indicated (by the earlier statement).
</details>
