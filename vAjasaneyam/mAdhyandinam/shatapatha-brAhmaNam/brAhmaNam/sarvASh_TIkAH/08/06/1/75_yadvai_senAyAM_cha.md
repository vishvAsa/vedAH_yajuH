<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'mordacious beasts the shaft, manslaughter the missile,'--inasmuch as they fight in army and battle, those mordacious beasts are the shaft; 'manslaughter the missile,'--inasmuch as they slay one another, manslaughter is the missile; --'to them be homage!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“The killer beast is the arrow and man-slaughter is the missile”.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

यद्वै से᳘नायां च स᳘मितौ च ऽर्तीय᳘न्ते दङ्क्ष्ण᳘वः पश᳘वो हेतिः पौ᳘रुषेयो व्वधः प्र᳘हेतिरि᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

यद्वै सेनायां च समितौ च ऽर्तीयन्ते- ते दङ्क्ष्णवः पशवः । हेतिः पौरुषेयो, वधः प्रहेतिरिति ।
</details>
