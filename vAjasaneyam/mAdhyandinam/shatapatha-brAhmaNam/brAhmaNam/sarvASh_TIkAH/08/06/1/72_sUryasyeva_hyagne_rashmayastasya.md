<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'and Rathagr̥tsa and Rathaujas [^egg_193], his commander and his chieftain,' the two spring-months are these two;

[^egg_193]: 105:2 That is, 'skilled in chariot (-fight),' and 'mighty in chariot (-fight).'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"His Rathagṛtsa and Rathaujas are the commander and his head-man" – the two months forming the spring season are these two.
</details>
