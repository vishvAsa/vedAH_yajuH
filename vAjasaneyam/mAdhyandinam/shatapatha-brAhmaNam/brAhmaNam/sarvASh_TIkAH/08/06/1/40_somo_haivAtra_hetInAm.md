<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'Soma is the repeller of shafts,' for Soma, indeed, is here the repeller of shafts; --'The Ekaviṁśa-stoma may uphold thee on earth!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Soma is the repeller of arrows" — for here (in the north) Soma repels the arrows. "May the Ekaviṁśa stoma sustain you on the earth" — for, it is by means of the twenty- one-fold stoma it is sustained on the earth.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

सो᳘मो हैवा᳘त्र हेतीनां᳘ प्रतिध᳘र्तैकविᳫँ᳭श᳘स्त्वा स्तो᳘मः पृथिव्या᳘ᳫँ᳘ श्रयत्वि᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

सो᳘मो हैवा᳘त्र हेतीना᳘म् प्रतिधॗर्तैकविंश᳘स्त्वा स्तो᳘मः पृथिव्यां᳘ श्रयत्वि᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

सोमो हैवात्र हेतीनां प्रतिधर्ता । **"एकविंशस्त्वा स्तोमः पृथिव्यां श्रयतु"**- इति ।
</details>
