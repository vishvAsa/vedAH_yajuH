<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'and the nymphs Puñjikasthalā and Kratusthalā [^egg_194],'--'quarter and intermediate quarter,' said Māhitthi; but army and battle these two are;

[^egg_194]: 105:3 The meaning of these names is rather obscure: the symbolical explanations 'army and battle' might seem to point to some such meanings as 'grounded on heaps' and 'grounded on intelligence (or plan).'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

But actually they are the army and the warfare.
</details>
