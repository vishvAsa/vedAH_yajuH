<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'The divine Vasus are thine overlords [^egg_181],' for the divine Vasus are indeed the overlords of that region; --'Agni is the repeller of shafts,' for Agni, indeed, is here the repeller of shafts; --'The Trivr̥t-Stoma may uphold thee on earth!'

[^egg_181]: 100:1 Or, perhaps, 'the Vasus are thy divine overlords;' but see paragraph 9.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Agni is the repeller of the arrows. For here (in the eastern quarter) Agni repels the arrows. “Let the Trivṛt stoma sustain you on earth”—because it is by means of the Trivṛt stoma, it is sustained on the earth.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>
