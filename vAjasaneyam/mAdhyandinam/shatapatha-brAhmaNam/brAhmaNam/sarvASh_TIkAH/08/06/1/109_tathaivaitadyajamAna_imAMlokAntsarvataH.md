<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

In like manner, this Yajamāna, having closed up all these worlds on all sides, enters the world of heaven from below. (First Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त᳘थै᳘वैतद्य᳘जमान ऽइमाल्ँ᳘लोकान्त्सर्व्व᳘तः समपिधा᳘याव᳘स्तात्स्वर्गं᳘ लोकं प्र᳘पद्यते॥
</details>

</details>
