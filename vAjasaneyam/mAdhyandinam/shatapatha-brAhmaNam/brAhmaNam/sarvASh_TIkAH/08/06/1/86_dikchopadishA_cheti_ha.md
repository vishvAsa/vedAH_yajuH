<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

but these two are heaven and earth; --'Goblins the shaft, demons the missile;' for goblins indeed are here the shaft (weapon), and demons the missile; --'to them be homage . . .!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

According to Māhitthi, these two are the quarter and the intermediate quarter.
</details>
