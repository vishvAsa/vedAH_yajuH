<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

10. Br̥hatīs are the ribs, Kakubhs the breast-bone. The Br̥hatīs he places between the Trishṭubhs and Kakubhs, whence these ribs are fastened on both sides, on the breast-bone and the costal cartilages [^egg_208].

[^egg_208]: 114:2 That is, on both sides of the chest; see XII, 2, 4, 11, with note.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Bṛhatīs are the ribs and Kakubhs are the bones of the breast. He places the Bṛhatīs between the Triṣṭubhs and Kakubhs, whence these bones got fashioned on either side by the breast-bone and the ribs.
</details>
