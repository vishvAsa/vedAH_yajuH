<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He places them on the forepart (of the altar), for the head (of the animal or bird) is in front.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They are three for the head is threefold. They are placed in the front side because this head is in the front.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त्रिवृद्धि शि᳘रः पूर्व्वार्ध ऽउ᳘पदधाति पुर᳘स्ता᳘द्धीदᳫँ᳭ शि᳘रः॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

त्रिवृद्धि शि᳘रः पूर्वार्ध उ᳘पदधाति पुर᳘स्ताॗद्धीदं शि᳘रः॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

त्रिवृद्धि शिरः । पूर्वार्ध उपदधाति- पुरस्ताद्धीदं शिरः ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

त्रिवृद्धि शि᳘रः पूर्वार्ध उ᳘पदधाति पुरस्ता᳘द्धीदꣳ शिरः ॥६॥
</details>
