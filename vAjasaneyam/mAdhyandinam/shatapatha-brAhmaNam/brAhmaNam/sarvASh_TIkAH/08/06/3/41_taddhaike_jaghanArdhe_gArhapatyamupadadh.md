<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

14. Now some lay down the Gārhapatya on the hind part, and the Punaściti on the front part (of the built altar), for these two are the Āhavanīya and the Gārhapatya, and these two fires are (placed) in this way [^egg_216].

[^egg_216]: 121:1 In the ordinary sacrifices the Gārhapatya hearth is placed behind (west of), and the Āhavanīya on the front (or east) end of, the Vedi.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Some persons place the Gārhapatya on the rear side and the Punaściti on the front part and thus the Āhavanīya and the Gārhapatya are (remain- ing) two altars. But this way it should not be done.
</details>
