<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Now, in laying down the Gārhapatya (hearth) upon the Āhavanīya, he surely does what is improper; but when he lays down the Punaściti he thereby brings this Agni (or altar) that has been built, and builds it up again thereon; and because he again

 (punas) builds up (ci) that (Agni) already built, therefore (this is called) Punaściti.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And again why he places the punaściti — by laying down the Gārhapatya upon the Āhavanīya (which is already on the Agni-altar), he is certainly causing a disorder. But when he lays down the punaściti, he brings this Agni-altar that has been built and again builds ( punaḥ-ci ) and thus builds up the Agni which is already built.
</details>
