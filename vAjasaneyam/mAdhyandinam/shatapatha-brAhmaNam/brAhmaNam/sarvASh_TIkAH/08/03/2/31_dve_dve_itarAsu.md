<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

9. There are here four seasonal (bricks) he lays down in the middlemost layer; and two in each of the other layers,--animals (cattle) are four-footed, and the middlemost layer is the air: he thus places animals in the air, and hence there are animals that have their abode in the air.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus he places the cattle in that aerial region. Hence there are animals in the aerial region and the animals have the aerial region as their above.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

द्वे᳘ द्वे ऽइ᳘तरासु चि᳘तिषु च᳘तुष्पादा वै᳘ पश᳘वो ऽन्त᳘रिक्षं मध्यमा चि᳘तिरन्त᳘रिक्षे त᳘त्पशू᳘न्दधाति त᳘स्मादन्त᳘रिक्षायतनाः पश᳘वः॥
</details>

</details>
