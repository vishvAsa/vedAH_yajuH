<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Now the middle Viśvajyotis is Vāyu [^egg_113], for Vāyu (the wind) is all the light in the air-world: it is Vāyu he thus places therein.

[^egg_113]: 47:1 The three Viśvajyotis bricks, placed in (the fourth easterly place from the centre of) the first, third and fifth layer respectively, are supposed to represent the regents of the three worlds--earth, air and sky--which these three layers represent, viz. Agni, Vāyu and Āditya (Sūrya). See VI, 3, 3, 16.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The central viśvajyoti (all-light) is indeed Vāyu; for, Vāyu is indeed the entire brilliance in the aerial region. So he places only Vāyu.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

व्वायुर्व्वै᳘ मध्यमा᳘ व्विश्व᳘ज्योतिर्व्वायु᳘र्ह्ये᳘वान्तरिक्षलोके व्वि᳘श्वं ज्यो᳘तिर्व्वायु᳘मे᳘वैतदु᳘पदधाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

वायुर्वै᳘ मध्यमा᳘ विश्व᳘ज्योतिर्वायुॗर्ह्येॗवान्तरिक्षलोके वि᳘श्वं ज्यो᳘तिर्वायु᳘मेॗवैतदु᳘पदधाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

वायुर्वै मध्यमा विश्वज्योतिः । वायुर्ह्येवान्तरिक्षलोके विश्वं ज्योतिः । वायुमेवैतदुपदधाति ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

वायुर्वै᳘ मध्यमा᳘ विश्व᳘ज्योतिर्वायुर्ह्ये᳘वान्त᳘रिक्षलोके वि᳘श्वं ज्यो᳘तिर्वायु᳘मे᳘वैतदु᳘पदधाति
</details>
