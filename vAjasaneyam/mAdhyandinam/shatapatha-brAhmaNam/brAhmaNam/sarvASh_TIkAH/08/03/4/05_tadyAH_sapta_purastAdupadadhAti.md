<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

When he places seven in front, he thereby restores to him those seven (organs of the) vital airs here in front.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The seven *vālakhilyas* which he places in the front are the same as these seven *prāṇas* which are in the front part (of an animal). Those he puts into him are placed in such a way that they are not separated from those ten *prāṇabhṛt* bricks. Thus he ensures that *prāṇas* are not separated from the head.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

तद्याः᳘ सप्त᳘ पुर᳘स्तादुपद᳘धाति य᳘ ऽए᳘वेमे᳘ सप्त᳘ पुर᳘स्तात्प्राणास्ता᳘नस्मिन्नेत᳘द्दधाति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

तद्याः᳘ सप्त᳘ पुर᳘स्तादुपद᳘धाति य᳘ एॗवेमे᳘ सप्त᳘ पुर᳘स्तात्प्राणास्ता᳘नस्मिन्नेत᳘द्दधाति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

तद्याः सप्त पुरस्तादुपदधाति- य ऽएवेमे सप्त पुरस्तात्प्राणास्तानस्मिन्नेतदधाति ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
