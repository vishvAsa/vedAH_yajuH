<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Subsequently (to the central brick he lays them down): subsequently to (or upon) the vital air he thus places food.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The svayamātṛṇṇā is indeed, prāṇa. So he places food not separated from the prāṇa. He lays them close to each other (on the svayamātṛṇṇā); the food and the prāṇa.

The svayamātṛṇṇā is indeed prāṇa. So he places food also as not to be separated from prāṇa. Just over (the central brick) he lays them down and thereby just over the prāṇa he places food.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>
