<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Subsequently [^egg_109] (to the central brick he lays them down): subsequently to the air he thus sets up the regions. In all (four) directions he places them: he thus places the regions (quarters) in all directions, whence the regions are in all (four) directions. [He places them] on all sides so as to face each other: he thereby makes the regions on all sides face each other, and hence the regions on all sides face each other [^egg_110].

[^egg_109]: 45:3 Uttara seems here and elsewhere to have a double meaning, viz. that of subsequent, and upper, or left, inasmuch as looking towards these bricks from the centre of the altar, they are placed to the left of the particular section of the anūkas.

[^egg_110]: 45:4 See p. 26, note 3.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Just facing the svayamatṛṇṇā, he lays them, so that the quarters are placed facing the aerial region,within the boundaries of the two retaśsicā bricks. These two worlds are the retaśsicā bricks. These two worlds are the retaśsicas. In them he lays down the quarters. He places them (diśyās) on all sides, by which he places the diśyās (quarters) on all sides. That is why the quarters are on all sides. He places them straight and by that he places the quarters so as to face each other.
</details>

</details>
