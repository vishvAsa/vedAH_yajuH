<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Separately he lays them down, separately he settles them, and separately he pronounces the Sūdadohas over them, for separate are the regions.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He lays them down separately and settles them separately. He chants separate Sūdadohasa verses over them; for those quarters are indeed separate. (First Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>
