<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

8. Now some lay down a thirtieth (Stomabhāgā), with, 'Beautifully arrayed, quicken thou the kshatra for the kshatra!' saying, 'Of thirty syllables is the Virāj (metre) and this layer is virāj (far-shining).' But let him not do so: they (who do so) exceed (this layer so as not to be) amounting to the twenty-one-fold, and to the Gāyatrī;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Some people lay down a thirtieth (stomabhāga) brick saying, “You the charming fortune! hasten the Kṣatra for the Kṣatra.” (They argue) "The Virāḍ metre is of thirty syllables and this citi is Virāḍ (for shining)." Let him not do so.
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

तद्धैके- **"वेषश्रीः क्षत्राय क्षत्रं जिन्व"**- इति त्रिंशत्तमीमुपदधति । त्रिंशदक्षरा विराट् । विराडेषा चितिरिति ।
</details>
