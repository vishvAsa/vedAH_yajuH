<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

11. And, again, those which he lays down in front are the vital airs. There are ten of them, for there are ten vital airs. He places them in the front part, for these vital airs are in the front part.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Those which he lays down in the front are the prāṇas. They are ten in number corresponding to the ten prāṇas. He places them on the front side because these prāṇas are in the front part of the body.
</details>
