<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

14. And those on the left (north) side are the months,--two spring-months, two summer-months, two months of the rainy season, two autumn-months, and two winter-months.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Those on the left side are the months — two months of the spring, the two summer months, the two rainy months, autumnal months two and the two winter months.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(चा᳘) अ᳘थ या᳘ ऽउत्तरतः[[!!]]॥  
(तो) मा᳘सास्ते व्वा᳘सन्तिकौ द्वौ ग्रै᳘ष्मौ द्वौ व्वा᳘र्षिकौ द्वौ᳘ शारदौ द्वौ है᳘मन्तिकौ द्वौ[[!!]]॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

अ᳘थ या᳘ उत्तरतः᳟॥  
मा᳘सास्ते वा᳘सन्तिकौ द्वौ ग्रै᳘ष्मौ द्वौ वा᳘र्षिकौ द्वौ᳘ शारदौ द्वौ है᳘मन्तिकौ द्वौ᳟॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

(४) अथ या उत्तरतः- मासास्ते । वासन्तिकौ द्वौ, ग्रैष्मौ द्वौ, वार्षिकौ द्वौ, शारदौ द्वौ, हैमन्तिकौ द्वौ ॥ १४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
