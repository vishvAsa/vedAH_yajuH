<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

12. And those on the right (south) side are the deities,--Agni, the Earth, Vāyu, the Air, Āditya, the Sky, Candra (the moon), the Stars, Food, and Water.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Those on the right side are the gods — Agni, Earth, Vāyu, Ether, Sun, Sky, Moon, Stars, Food and Waters.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(ऽ) अ᳘थ या᳘ दक्षिणतः᳘॥  
(ऽ) एतास्ता᳘ देव᳘ता ऽअग्नि᳘श्च पृथिवी᳘ च व्वायु᳘श्चान्त᳘रिक्षं चादित्य᳘श्च द्यौ᳘श्च चन्द्र᳘माश्च न᳘क्षत्राणि चा᳘न्नं चा᳘पश्च॥
</details>

</details>
