<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'the Mind metre,'--the 'mind' metre, doubtless, is Prajāpati; 'the Extent metre,'--the 'extent' metre, doubtless, is yonder sun.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“manaśchandaḥ" — for, Prajāpati is the mind metre (manaśchandaḥ). “vyacaśchandaḥ;" for, Āditya is indeed the extent metre (vyacaśchandaḥ).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

रजा᳘पतिर्वै म᳘नश्छ᳘न्दो व्य᳘चश्छ᳘न्द ऽइ᳘त्यसौ वा᳘ ऽआदित्यो व्य᳘चश्छ᳘न्दः॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

रजा᳘पतिर्वै म᳘नश्छ᳘न्दो व्य᳘चश्छ᳘न्द इ᳘त्यसौ वा᳘ आदित्यो व्य᳘चश्छ᳘न्दः॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
