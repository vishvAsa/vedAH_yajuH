<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

13. And those behind are the regions (quarters),--four regions, four intermediate regions, the upper region, and this (earth).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Those on the back side are the quarters and there are four quarters, four intermediate quarters, the upper direction and this earth.
</details>
