<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

4. Whilst meditating, they saw these Asapatnā ('foeless') bricks; they laid them down, and by means of them they made that world foeless, undisturbed; and because by means of them they made that world foeless, undisturbed, these (are called) Asapatnās. And in like manner does the Sacrificer, by laying them down, now make that world foeless, undisturbed.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

While contemplating, they discovered three *asapatnā* bricks (foeless bricks). They laid them down and by means of them they rendered that world free of enemies and without affliction.
</details>
