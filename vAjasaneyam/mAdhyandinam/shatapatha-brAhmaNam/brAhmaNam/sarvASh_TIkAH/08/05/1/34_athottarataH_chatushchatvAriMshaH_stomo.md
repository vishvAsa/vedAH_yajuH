<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

11. Then on the left (north) side, with, 'The forty-four-fold Stoma, lustre, wealth!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he places them on the left side saying, "The forty-fourfold ''stoma'', effulgence and wealth." Triṣṭubh has forty-four syllables (in all the four quarters together) and the thunderbolt is of Triṣṭubh.
</details>

<details><summary>हरिस्वामी</summary>

**अथोत्तरत** इति । 'अथोत्तरतः' त्रैष्टुभस्त्रिष्टुप्संमितो वा 'चतुश्चत्वारिंशत्' अस्ति अपहितवधेव वज्रः, अन्तरिक्षमपि च चतुश्चत्वारिंशमेकैका दिगित्येवं गृह्यमाणा ॥ ११ ॥
</details>
