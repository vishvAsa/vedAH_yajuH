<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

They spake, 'Think ye upon this, how we shall make this world

foeless, undisturbed!' They spake, 'Meditate ye!' whereby, indeed, they meant to say, 'Seek ye a layer! Seek ye how we shall make this world foeless, undisturbed!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They told to themselves : 'Find out the way to make this world free of rivals and without affliction.' They concluded, “Contemplate;" which means plan a citi. So they contemplated as to how to make it free of enemies and without affliction.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

ते ऽब्रुवन्नु᳘प त᳘ज्जानीत य᳘थेमं᳘ लोक᳘मसपत्न᳘मनुपबाधं᳘ कर᳘वामहा ऽइ᳘ति᳘ ते ऽब्रुवंश्चेत᳘यध्वमि᳘ति चि᳘तिमिच्छते᳘ति वाव त᳘दब्रुवंस्त᳘दिच्छत य᳘थेमं᳘ लोक᳘मसपत्न᳘मनुपबाधं᳘ कर᳘वामहा ऽइ᳘ति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

ते ऽब्रुवन्- उप तज्जानीत- यथेमं लोकमसपत्नमनुपबाधं करवामहा ऽइति । ते ऽब्रुवन्- चेतयध्वमिति । चितिमिच्छतेति वाव तदब्रुवन् । तदिच्छत- यथेमं लोकमसपत्नमनुपबाधं करवामहा ऽइति ॥ ३ ॥
</details>
