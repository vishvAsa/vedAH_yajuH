<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Hence one who has a mark on the right side is considered as of good for- tune and on the left side, in the case of woman; for, woman is always on the left side (of man).

These bricks are indications of auspicious fortunes and so he places them on all sides. He who has marks on every side is called very lucky.
</details>
