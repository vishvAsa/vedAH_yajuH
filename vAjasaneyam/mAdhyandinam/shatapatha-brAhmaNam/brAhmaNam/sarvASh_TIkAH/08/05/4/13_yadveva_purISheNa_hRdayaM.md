<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

6. And, again, as to why with loose soil;--the Stomabhāgās are the heart, and the loose soil is the pericardium: he thus encloses the heart in the pericardium.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Again why with loose soil (does he cover)? The stomabhāga is the heart and that loose soil is the purī (rampart or fortress); he thus encloses that heart by the fortress.
</details>

हृ᳘दयं वै स्तो᳘मभागाः पुरीतत्पु᳘रीषᳫँ᳭ हृ᳘दयं त᳘त्पुरीत᳘ता प्र᳘च्छादयति॥

</details>
