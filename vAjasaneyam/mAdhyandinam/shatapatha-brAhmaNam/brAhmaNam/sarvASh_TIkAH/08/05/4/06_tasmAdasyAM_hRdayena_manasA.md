<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He lays them down on every side: he thus places the heart, the mind everywhere; whence everywhere on this (earth) one thinks with the heart, with the mind.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He lays them on all sides; because he places that heart, the mind everywhere.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त᳘स्मादस्याᳫँ᳭ हृ᳘दयेन म᳘नसा चेतयते सर्व्व᳘त ऽउ᳘पदधाति सर्व्व᳘तस्तद्धृ᳘दयं म᳘नो दधाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

त᳘स्मादस्याᳫं हृ᳘दयेन म᳘नसा चेतयते सर्व᳘त उ᳘पदधाति सर्व᳘तस्तद्धृ᳘दयम् म᳘नो दधाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

तस्मादस्यां हृदयेन मनसा चेतयते । सर्वत उपदधाति । सर्वतस्तद्धृदयं मनो दधाति ।
</details>

</details>
