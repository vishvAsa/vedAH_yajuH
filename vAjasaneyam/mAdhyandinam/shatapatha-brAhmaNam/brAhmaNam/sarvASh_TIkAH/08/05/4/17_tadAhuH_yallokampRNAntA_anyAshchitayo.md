<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now it is asked : “All other layers get completed with lokamprṇā bricks. Here he does not place lokamprṇās (space filling bricks) as such. Now what takes the role of lokamprṇās here ?" Yonder sun is surely the lokamprṇā and this layer is he (the sun) and so this layer by itself is a lokamprṇā (space filling brick).
</details>
