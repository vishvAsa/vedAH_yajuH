<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

10. And, again, as to why he lays down the Vikarṇī. When, on that (former) occasion, they make the horse smell (the pile of bricks of) the (first) layer [^egg_248], then yonder sun strings these worlds to himself on a thread.

[^egg_248]: 141:1 See VII, 3, 2, 13.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And why he places the vikarṇī is this — when (at the beginning) they make the horse smell (the pile of bricks) on the first layer, then thereby yonder sun strings these worlds to himself in a thread.
</details>
