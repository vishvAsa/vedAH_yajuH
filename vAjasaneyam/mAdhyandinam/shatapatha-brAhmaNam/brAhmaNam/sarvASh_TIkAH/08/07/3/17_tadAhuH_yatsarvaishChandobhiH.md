<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

8. Here, now, they say, 'Whilst he lays down the bricks with all kinds of metres, and with (verses addressed to) all deities, he now scatters (the soil) with a single (verse) addressed to a single deity,--how is this one half of Agni?'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they argue, “Bricks are placed with all the metres and with all the gods. How come, this is placed with one (Anuṣṭubh) metre and with one god (Indra)? How is it that half of it, is of Agni?” Indra is, indeed equal to all the gods. Hence he scatters with a verse dedicated to Indra.
</details>
