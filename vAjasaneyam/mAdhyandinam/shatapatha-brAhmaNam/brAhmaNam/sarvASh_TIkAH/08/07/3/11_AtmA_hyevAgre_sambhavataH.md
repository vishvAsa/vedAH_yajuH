<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

then the right wing, then the tail, then the left wing: that is in the rightward (sunwise) way, for this is (the way) with the gods.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he covers the right wing and then the left one. From right to left is the course of gods.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(त्या) आत्मा᳘ ह्येवा᳘ग्रे सम्भ᳘वतः सम्भ᳘वत्य᳘थ द᳘क्षिणं पक्षम᳘थ पु᳘च्छमथो᳘त्तरं त᳘द्दक्षिणावृत्तद्धि᳘ देवत्रा[[!!]]॥
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
