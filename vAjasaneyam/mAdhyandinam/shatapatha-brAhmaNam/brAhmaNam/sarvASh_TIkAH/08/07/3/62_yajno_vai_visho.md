<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“At the birth of gods” — the year is the birth of the gods “viśaḥ” — the viśaḥ are indeed the sacrifice, because it is in the sacrifice that all beings are ranged.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

यज्ञो वै व्वि᳘शो यज्ञे हि स᳘र्व्वाणि भूता᳘नि व्विष्टा᳘नि त्रिष्वा᳘ रोचने᳘ दिव ऽइ᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

यज्ञो वै विशः । यज्ञे हि सर्वाणि भूतानि विष्टानि । **“त्रिष्वा रोचने दिवः"**- (वा. सं. १५ । ६०) इति ।
</details>
