<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--bhūs (earth), doubtless, is this world, bhuvas is the air-world, and svar (light) is yonder world: into these worlds he thereby lays sap, the means of subsistence.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Bhūḥ stands for this world (earth). Bhuvaḥ stands for the aerial region and Svaḥ stands for the yonder (heaven).
</details>
