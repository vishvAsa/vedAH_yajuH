<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and in like manner does this one now bestow upon him that highest, immortal form [^egg_256].

[^egg_256]: 146:1 That is, the Sacrificer bestows it on Agni; with probably, however, the ḍouble eṇṭeṇṭe, 'this Adhvaryu priest bestows it on the Sacrificer.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

In the same manner, this one (Yajamāna) bestows on the altar that supreme form of immortality.
</details>
