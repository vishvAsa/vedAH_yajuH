<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

18. The seventh layer is immortality,--that is the last (layer) he lays down, and thus bestows immortality as the highest thing of all this (universe): therefore immortality is the highest thing of all this (universe);
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The seventh layer is immortality itself. He builds it up last. He thus confers that immortality which is the highest thing in all this (universe).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(त्य) अमृ᳘तमेव᳘ सप्तमी चि᳘तिः॥ 
(स्ता᳘) ता᳘मुत्तमामु᳘पदधात्यमृ᳘तं त᳘दस्य स᳘र्व्वस्योत्तमं᳘ दधाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

अमृ᳘तमेव᳘ सप्तमी चि᳘तिः ता᳘मुत्तमामु᳘पदधात्यमृ᳘तं त᳘दस्य स᳘र्वस्योत्तमं᳘ दधाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

(७) अमृतमेव सप्तमी चितिः । तामुत्तमामुपदधाति । अमृतं तदस्य सर्वस्पोत्तमं दधाति ।
</details>

</details>
