<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

3. Whilst meditating, they saw these sāmans (hymn-tunes), and sang them; and by means of them they laid sap, the means of subsistence, into these worlds;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

While meditating, they discovered these Sāman hymns. They sang them.
</details>
