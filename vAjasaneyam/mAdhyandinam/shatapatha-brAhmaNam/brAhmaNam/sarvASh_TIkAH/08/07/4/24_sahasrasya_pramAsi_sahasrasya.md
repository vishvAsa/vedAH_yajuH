<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

11. [He scatters, with, Vāj. S. XV, 65], 'The fore-measure of a thousand thou art,--The counter-measure of a thousand thou art,--The up-measure of a thousand thou art,--The thousandfold thou art, --For a thousand thee!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(While scattering, he says) "You are the measure of a thousand; you are the counter-measure of a thousand, you are the measure of altitude of a thousand; you are a thousandfold and to you, thousand"—for, the thou- sand means "all".
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

सह᳘स्रस्य प्रति᳘मा ऽसि सह᳘स्रस्यो᳘न्मा ऽसि साह᳘स्रो ऽसि सह᳘स्राय त्वे᳘ति
</details>
