<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

With that (kind of brick) he fills up the whole body: he thereby kindles the whole body by means of the sun, whence this whole body (of ours) is warm.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

With that same kind of bricks, he fills up the whole body. Thereby he warms up the whole body by means of the sun and that is why, (our) whole body is warm.
</details>

</details>
