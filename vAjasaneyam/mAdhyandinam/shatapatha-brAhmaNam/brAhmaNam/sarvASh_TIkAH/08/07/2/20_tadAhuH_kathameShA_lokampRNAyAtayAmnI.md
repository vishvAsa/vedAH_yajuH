<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

7. Here now they say, 'How does that Lokampr̥ṇā become of unimpaired strength?' Well, the Lokampr̥ṇā is yonder sun, and he assuredly is of unimpaired strength. And the Lokampr̥ṇā also is speech, and of unimpaired strength assuredly is speech.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they ask, “How this lokam̥pṛṇā becomes ever-fresh (of unim- paired strength)." The lokam̥pṛṇā is yonder sun and he is indeed ever-fresh (or of unimpaired strength). Moreover, the lokam̥pṛṇā is also speech and speech is ever-fresh.
</details>
