<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for the naturally-perforated one is the breath, and the 'space-filler' is the sun: he thus kindles the breath by means of the sun, whence this breath (of ours) is warm.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Because the naturally perforated one is the prāṇa and the lokam̥pṛṇā is the sun. He thus kindles the prāṇa by means of the sun and that is why, one's out-breath- ing is warm.
</details>
