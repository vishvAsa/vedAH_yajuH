<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

9. Those (bricks) possessed of formulas he places on the body (of the altar) itself, not on the wings and tail: he thus puts food into the body;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Those yajuṣmatī bricks, he places on the body (of the altar) only and not on the wings or the tail.
</details>
