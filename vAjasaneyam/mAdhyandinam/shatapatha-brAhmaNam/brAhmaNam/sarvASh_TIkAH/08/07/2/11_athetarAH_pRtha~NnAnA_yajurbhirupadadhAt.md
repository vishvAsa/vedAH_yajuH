<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The other bricks, he lays down one by one with separate formulas; thereby he makes the peasantry less powerful than the ruling class, with difference in speech and with different thoughts.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

अथे᳘तराः पृ᳘थङ्ना᳘ना य᳘जुर्भिरु᳘पदधाति व्वि᳘शं त᳘त्क्षत्रा᳘दवीर्य᳘तरां करोति पृथग्वादि᳘नीं ना᳘नाचेतसम्॥
</details>

</details>
