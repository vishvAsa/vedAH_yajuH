<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

18. Now, then, of the location [^egg_245] of (special) bricks. Any (special) brick he knows, provided with a formula, let him place in the middle (third) layer;

[^egg_245]: 138:1 Āvapana has also the meaning of 'throwing in, insertion,' which is likewise understood here, whilst further on in this paragraph ('the air is the āvapanam of all beings') it can scarcely have this meaning (? something injected). Cf. IX, 4, 2, 27.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now about the placement of the bricks — any brick he knows as yajuṣmatī (having a special Yajus formula associated with it), let him place it in the middle layer.
</details>
