<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

19. Here, now, they say, 'Let him not lay down (such special bricks) lest he should do what is excessive.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Some say in this context, “Let him not place (such special bricks) lest he should do something superfluous.” But nevertheless, he may lay them down;
</details>
