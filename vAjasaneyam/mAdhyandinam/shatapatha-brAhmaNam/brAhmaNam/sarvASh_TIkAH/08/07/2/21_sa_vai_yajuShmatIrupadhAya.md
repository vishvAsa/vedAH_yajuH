<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

8. Having laid down those (bricks) possessed of (special) sacrificial formulas, he covers (the altar) with the Lokampr̥ṇā; for the bricks possessed of formulas mean food, and the Lokampr̥ṇā means the body: he thus encloses the food in the body, whence food enclosed in the body is the body itself.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He having laid down the yajuṣmatī bricks (bricks with specific Yajus formulas), covers it (altar) with the lokam̥pṛṇā. Because the yajuṣmatī bricks are food and the lokam̥pṛṇā is the body.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

लोकम्पृण᳘या प्र᳘च्छादयत्य᳘न्नं वै य᳘जुष्मत्य ऽइ᳘ष्टका ऽआत्मा᳘ लोकम्पृणा᳘ ऽन्नं त᳘दात्म᳘ना प᳘रिदधाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

स वै यजुष्मतीरुपधाय लोकम्पृणया प्रच्छादयति । अन्नं वै यजुष्मत्य इष्टकाः, आत्मा लोकम्पृणा । अन्नं तदात्मना परिदधाति ।
</details>

</details>
