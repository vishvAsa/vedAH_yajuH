<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

But let him rather not lay them down, for just that much the gods then did.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Or, let him not lay them down because the gods (once) did only this much. (Second Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>
