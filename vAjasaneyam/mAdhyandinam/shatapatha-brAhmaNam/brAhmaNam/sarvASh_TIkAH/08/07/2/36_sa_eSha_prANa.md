<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

14. The Lokampr̥ṇā, doubtless, is the same as the vital air; he therewith fills up the whole body (of the altar): he thus puts vital air into the whole body.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That which is lokampṛṇā is the same as prāṇa, by which he covers the entire body. Thereby he infuses prāṇa in the whole body. If it (lokampṛṇā) is not to reach any part (of the body) to that part, the prāṇa does not reach and that part, assuredly dries up or withers away.
</details>
