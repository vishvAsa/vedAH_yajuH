<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

14. Now, the Carakādhvaryus lay down here yet other 'stepping-stones';
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The priests of the Caraka school lay here yet other stepping stones (above the ṛtavyās).

The priests of the Caraka school adopt different bricks for stepping over; but it should not be done because it is redundant.
</details>
