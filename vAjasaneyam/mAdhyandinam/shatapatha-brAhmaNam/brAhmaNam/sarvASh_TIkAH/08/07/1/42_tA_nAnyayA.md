<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And the seasonal ones, indeed, are also the year: by the (different) layers he thus builds up the year. Let him not thereafter place over them any other brick with a sacrificial formula, lest he should place the peasantry above the nobility.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He shall not place any other yajuṣmatī bricks (bricks placed with the chanting of a Yajus formula) above the ṛtavyās, lest he should place the commoners above the ruling class.
</details>
