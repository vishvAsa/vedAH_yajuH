<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

So at that place, where he places the first two ṛtavyās, at that same place all others are to be laid down. The ṛtavyās bricks are indeed also these (three) worlds; by the different layers he is actually building up these three worlds one above the other.
</details>
