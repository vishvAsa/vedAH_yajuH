<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That is why he places these two here.

(First Brāhmaṇa Ends.)

When he places these two bricks, he restores that part of Prajāpati's body corresponding to the winter season.

(Second Brāhmaṇa Ends.)

Now by placing these two (ṛtavyās representing the śiśira season), he restores that very thing, which is of its own body. That is why, he places these two here.

Now placing these two (ṛtavyās bricks), he restores that very thing which is of its own body. That is why, he places these two here.
