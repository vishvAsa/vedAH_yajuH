<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

2. And, again, as to why he lays down seasonal (bricks),--the seasonal (ones) are the nobility and these other bricks are the peasantry: he thus places the nobility as the eater among the peasantry.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And why he lays down the ṛtavyās is this — ṛtavyās represent the mar- tial prowess and these other bricks are the common people. He thus places the martial prowess as the eater among the peasantry.
</details>
