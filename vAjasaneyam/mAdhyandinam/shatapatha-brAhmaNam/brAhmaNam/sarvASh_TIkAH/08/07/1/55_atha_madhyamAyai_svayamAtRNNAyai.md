<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

19. And the (second) Viśvajyotis he lays down subsequent to the second naturally-perforated one (in the third, or central) layer;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now he places (one) viśvajyoti brick above the middle one of the naturally perforated brick.
</details>

(या ऽउ) उप᳘रिष्टाद्विश्व᳘ज्योतिषमु᳘पदधाति
</details>
