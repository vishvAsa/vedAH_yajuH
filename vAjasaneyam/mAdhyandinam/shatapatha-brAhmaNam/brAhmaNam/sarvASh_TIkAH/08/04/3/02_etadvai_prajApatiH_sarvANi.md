<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

For Prajāpati, having freed all beings from evil,

from death, he now desired, 'May I produce creatures, may I procreate!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Because Prajāpati, after releasing all beings of their evil and death, desired “May I create beings; May I procreate.” He told prāṇas — “Together with you, I shall bring forth these creatures.” (They asked) "With what shall we utter praises?” (He replied) "With me and with yourselves.” They said, "Yes.” They therefore sang the praises both with prāṇas and with Prajāpati.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(त्ये) एतद्वै᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिः स᳘र्व्वाणि भूता᳘नि पाप्म᳘नो मृत्यो᳘र्मु᳘क्त्वा ऽकामयत प्रजाः᳘ सृजेय प्र᳘जायेये᳘ति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

एतद्वै᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिः स᳘र्वाणि भूता᳘नि पाप्म᳘नो मृत्यो᳘र्मुॗक्त्वाकामयत प्रजाः᳘ सृजेय प्र᳘जायेये᳘ति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

एतद्वै प्रजापतिः सर्वाणि भूतानि पाप्मनो मृत्योर्मुक्त्वा ऽकामयत- प्रजाः सृजेय, प्रजायेयेति ॥ १ ॥
</details>

</details>
