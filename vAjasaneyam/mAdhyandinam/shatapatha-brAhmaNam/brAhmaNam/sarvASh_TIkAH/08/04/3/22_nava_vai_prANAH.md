<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

7. 'With nine they sang praises,'--there are nine vital airs, seven in the head, and two downward

ones: it is with them they then sang praises;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“They created pitṛs” — pitṛs indeed were now created.
</details>
