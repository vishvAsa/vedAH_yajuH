<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'the Fathers were created,'--the Fathers indeed were now created; --'Aditi was the ruler!' --Aditi indeed was now the ruler.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Aditi was the queen” — here Aditi was indeed the queen.
</details>
