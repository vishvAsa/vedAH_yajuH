<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'So be it!' So they sang praises both with the vital airs and with Prajāpati; and whatsoever the gods do, that they do with praise, that--praise being sacrifice--they do with sacrifice.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Whatever gods did then, now they do with sacrifice.
</details>
