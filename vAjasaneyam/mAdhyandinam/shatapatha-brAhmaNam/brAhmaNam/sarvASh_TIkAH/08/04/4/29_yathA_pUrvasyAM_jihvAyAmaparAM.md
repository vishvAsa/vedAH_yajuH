<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

That (brick) wherein the head is indeed (includes) the jaws and the tongue.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That brick representing the head does indeed have the jaws and the tongue.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

य᳘था पू᳘र्व्वस्यां जिह्वा᳘याम᳘परां जिह्वा᳘मनूपदध्या᳘त्तादृक्तद्यत्रा᳘हैव शि᳘रस्त᳘देव ह᳘नू त᳘ज्जि᳘ह्वा॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

यथा पूर्वस्यां जिह्वायामपरां जिह्वामनुपदध्यात् । तादृक्तत् । यत्राहैव शिरः- तदेव हनू, तज्जिह्वा ॥ ९ ॥
</details>

</details>
