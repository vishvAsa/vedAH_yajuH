<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He places them on the range of the Retaḥsic (bricks), for--the Retaḥsic being the ribs, and the ribs being the middle (of the body)--this body is in the middle (of the limbs) [^egg_150].

[^egg_150]: 79:2 Atha yā madhya npadadhāti sa ātmā, tā retaḥsicor velayopadadhāti--pr̥shṭayo vai retaḥsicau, madhyam u pr̥shtayo--madhyato hy ayam ātmā.--Here the two clauses with 'vai' are inserted to substantiate the reason introduced by 'hi.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The two *retassics* are indeed the ribs and the ribs are in the middle and this trunk is also in the middle.
</details>
