<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--the birthplace, doubtless, is the peasantry: by means of the seventeenfold hymn-form he frees the peasantry for living beings from evil, from death.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“The janitra is freed, the Saptadaśa stoma” — the birth place (janitra) is indeed the viś (commonfolk) and by means of the seventeenfold hymn he frees the commonfolk among the living beings, from sin and death.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

व्विड्वै᳘ जनि᳘त्रं व्वि᳘शं प्रजा᳘नाᳫँ᳭ सप्तदशे᳘न स्तो᳘मेन पाप्म᳘नो मृत्यो᳘रस्पृणोत्॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

विड्वै᳘ जनि᳘त्रं वि᳘शम् प्रजा᳘नाᳫं सप्तदशे᳘न स्तो᳘मेन पाप्म᳘नो मृत्यो᳘रस्पृणोत्॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

विड्वै जनित्रम् । विशं प्रजानां सप्तदशेन स्तोमेन पाप्मनो मृत्योरस्पृणोत् ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

विड्वै᳘ जनि᳘त्रं वि᳘शां प्रजा᳘नाꣳ सप्तदशे᳘न स्तो᳘मेन पाप्म᳘नो मृत्त्यो᳘रस्पृणोत् ॥४॥
</details>
