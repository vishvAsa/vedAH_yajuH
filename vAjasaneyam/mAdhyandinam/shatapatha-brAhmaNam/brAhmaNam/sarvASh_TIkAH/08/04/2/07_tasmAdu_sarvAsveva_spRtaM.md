<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And in like manner does this Sacrificer, by bestowing a share on some, and lordship on others, now free all beings from evil, from death; and hence (the word) 'spr̥tam (freed)' recurs with all of them.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That is why, in each instance (of laying down these bricks) the word spṛta is repeated.
</details>

प्रसङ्गात् तन्नाम निर्वक्ति- **यद् अस्पृणोत् तस्मात् स्पृत** इति । स्परणात्स्पृत इत्युक्तं भवति । 'तथैवैतत्' इत्यादि स्पष्टम् । तस्मात् सर्वास्विष्टकासु स्पृतं स्पृतमित्यनुवर्तते ॥ २ ॥
</details>
