<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

4. And, again, as to why he lays down the Stomas. The gods, at that time, said to Prajāpati, 'We will lay thee down here! So be it!' He did not say, 'What will therefrom accrue unto me?'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Why he lays down the *stomas* is this — the gods at that time told Prajāpati, “Let us lay you down here.” He said, “Be it so.” He did not ask, “What will then be to my benefit.” Normally whenever Prajāpati wanted something from gods he used to bargain ‘what will be the benefit.’ This is like a father wishing to obtain something from his sons and their asking in turn ‘what will accrue to us by that’.
</details>
