<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And as to his calling it 'array,' the year is indeed arrayed over all beings: it is that form he now lays down.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Why he calls it sambharaṇa(pervading or filling) is because the year per- vades all these beings. It is that form he lays down now.
</details>
