<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And as to his calling it 'the foundation,' the year is indeed the foundation of all beings: it is that form he now lays down.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Why he calls him pratiṣṭhā is because the year is the foundation of all beings. It is that form he lays down now.
</details>
