<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

But indeed the seventeenfold aerial space is also the year for which there are twelve months and five seasons. Why he calls it space is because the year indeed is space and being so it remained on the left side. It is that form of it he now lays down.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

व्योमा हि᳘ संव्वत्सरः᳘ संव्वत्सरो᳘ ह भू᳘त्वोत्तरत᳘स्तस्थौ त᳘देव त᳘द्रूपमु᳘पदधाति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

व्योमा हि संवत्सरः । संवत्सरो ह भूत्वोत्तरततस्तस्थौ । तदेव तद्रूपमुपधाति ॥ ११ ॥
</details>

</details>
