<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

But the firmament, the thirty-six-fold one, doubtless, is the year: in it there are twenty-four half-moons, and twelve months. And as to why he calls it 'the firmament' (nākam), it is because there is no pain (na akam) for whosoever goes there. And the firmament indeed is the year, the heavenly world is the year: it is that form he now lays down.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It has twenty-four half months and twelve months. And as to why he calls it nāka (heaven) is because nāka, the year is the heavenly world. It is that form he lays down here.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

कथं स नाक इति तदुपपादयति- **न हि तत्र गताय कस्मैचनाकं भवति** इति । तत्र स्वर्गं प्राप्ताय कस्मैचन पुरुषाय अकं दुःखं न खलु भवतीत्यतो न विद्यते अकमत्रेति 'नाकः' । 'हि'- शब्दो "न वा अमुं लोकं जग्मुषे किंचनाकम्" इत्यादिप्रसिद्धिद्योतनार्थः । "षडुदङ्ङेति हि मासान्मासेभ्यः संवत्सरं संवत्सरादादित्यम्" इति च च्छन्दोगश्रुतेः । सुकृतिभिः प्राप्तव्यत्वात् 'संवत्सरो वाव नाकः' अत एव सः 'स्वर्गो लोकः' ॥ २४ ॥
</details>
