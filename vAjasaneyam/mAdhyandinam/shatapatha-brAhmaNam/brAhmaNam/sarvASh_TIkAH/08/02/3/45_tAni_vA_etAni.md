<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus there are four kinds of vigour and four metres, making up a total of eight. Gāyatrī has eight syllables and certainly the same Gāyatrī is in the form of Prajāpati, who by his vigour, overtook those cattle. They say that he overtook by his vigour the insipid cattle and that is why at each time (while referring to Prajāpati assuming the form of a metre) the word 'vaya' (vigour) is repeated. Those cattle which went away from him (Prajāpati) are these fifteen Vyāhṛtis. Cattle are indeed the thunderbolt and thunderbolt is fifteenfold.
</details>
