<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He places them on every side: he thus places wind on all sides, whence the wind is everywhere.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That is why, wind is all-pervasive and is felt on every side running in the same direction.
</details>
