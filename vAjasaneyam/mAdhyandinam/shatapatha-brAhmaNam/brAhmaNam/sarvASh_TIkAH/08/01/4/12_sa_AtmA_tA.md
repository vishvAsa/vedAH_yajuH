<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and those

which he places in the middle are that body of his. He places these in the region of the two retaḥsic (bricks), for the retaḥsic are the ribs, and the ribs are the middle, and that body is in the middle (of the limbs).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

These (in the middle), he places between the retaśsica (bricks), because the retaśsics are the side-bones indeed.
</details>
