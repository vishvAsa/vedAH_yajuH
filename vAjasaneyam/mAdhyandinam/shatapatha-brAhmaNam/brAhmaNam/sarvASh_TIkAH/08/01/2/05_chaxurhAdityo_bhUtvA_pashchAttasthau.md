<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

The Sun, indeed, having become the eye, remained behind: it is that form he now bestows (on Agni).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The sun indeed, having become the eye, got stationed behind. It is that form he bestows now (on the altar).
</details>
