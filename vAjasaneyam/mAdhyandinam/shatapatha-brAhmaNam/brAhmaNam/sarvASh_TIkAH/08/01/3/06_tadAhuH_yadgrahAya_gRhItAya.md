<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

3. As to this they say, 'As they chant and recite for the cup when drawn, wherefore, then, does he put in verses and hymn-tunes [^egg_56] before (the drawing of) the cups?'

[^egg_56]: 13:2 In laying down the different sets of Prāṇabhr̥t-bricks the priest is said (in VIII, 1, 1, 5; 8; 2, 2; 5; 8) symbolically to put into the sacrificial work (or into the altar, Agni) 'both verses or metres (as Gāyatrī, Trishṭubh, &c.) and hymn-tunes (as Gāyatra. Svāra, &c.).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They say, "As they chant and recite on drawing the soma cups, where is the need for him to introduce chants and hymns before the drawing of the soma cups (as he is said to be doing in the previous Brāhmaṇa)" One has to keep in view the completion of the sacrificial ritual. Now with the Pratipadā Ṛk (marking the opening) the graha is collected.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

अथ मन्त्रगतानुपूर्व्या ऋक्सामग्रहाणां प्रतीयमानमानन्तर्यमाक्षिप्य समाधत्ते- **तदाहुर्यद्ग्रहाय गृहीताये**त्यादिना । एवं ह्यनुष्ठानक्रमः- प्रथमं ग्रहग्रहणम्, पश्चात्स्तोत्रम्, तदनन्तरं शस्त्रमिति । नन्वत्र गायत्रादिसामानि वैपरीत्येनोपदधातीत्याक्षेपः ।
</details>
