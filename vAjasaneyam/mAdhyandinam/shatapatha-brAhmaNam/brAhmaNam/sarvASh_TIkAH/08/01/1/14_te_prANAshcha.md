<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--So both the final airs and Prajāpati created that food, these Prāṇabhr̥t (bricks).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Prajāpati said, “Alright let us together produce that food.” They said, "Be it so." Thus Prajāpati and those prāṇas together created this food which are the prāṇabhṛt bricks.
</details>
