<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

With sixteen verses (Vāj. S. XIX, 80-94) he offers, for animals are of sixteen parts: he thus bestows excellence (or prosperity) on him part by part.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He offers with six- teen Ṛk mantras, for the Paśus have sixteen parts and corresponding to those parts, he bestows prosperity unto him.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

</details>
