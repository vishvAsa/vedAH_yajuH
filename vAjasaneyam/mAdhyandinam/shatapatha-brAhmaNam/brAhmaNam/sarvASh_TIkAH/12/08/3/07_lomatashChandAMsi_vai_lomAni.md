<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

on the hairy side (of the skin), for the hair is the metres: it is on the metres (or sacred writ) he thus consecrates him.

[^egg_629]: 249:2 See part i, p. 23, note 2.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

On the hairy side (of the skin he seates him) because the hairs are the sacred verses (metres). Thus he consecrates him on the sacred verses (metres).
</details>

</details>

</details>
