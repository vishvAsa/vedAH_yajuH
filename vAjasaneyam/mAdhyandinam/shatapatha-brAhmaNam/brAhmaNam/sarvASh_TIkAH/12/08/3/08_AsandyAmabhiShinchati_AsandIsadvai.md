<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

4. On a throne-seat he consecrates him, for imperial dignity is seated (established) on a throne-seat: by means of imperial dignity he thus causes him to attain imperial dignity.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He consecrates him (seated) on a throne-seat, for throne-seat is the symbol of imperial authority. By means of imperial authority he makes him win imperial power.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(त्या) आसन्द्या᳘मभि᳘षिञ्चति॥  
(त्या) आसन्दीसद्वै सा᳘म्राज्यᳫँ᳭ सा᳘म्राज्येनै᳘वैनᳫँ᳭ सा᳘म्राज्यङ्गमयति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

आसन्द्या᳘मभि᳘षिञ्चति ॥  
आसन्दीसद्वै सा᳘म्राज्यᳫं सा᳘म्राज्येनैॗवैनᳫं सा᳘म्राज्यं गमयति ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

आसंद्यामभिषिंचति । आसंदीसद्वै साम्राज्यम् । साम्राज्येनैवैनं साम्राज्यं गमयति ॥ ४ ॥
</details>
