<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

27. As to this they say, 'Seeing that this Sāman is sung, wherein then does the recitation (uktha) of this Sāman consist, and what is its foundation; for unsuccessful is what is chanted unless it be followed by a recitation?'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they ask - “This Sāman is sung alright; but where is the Uktha of the Sāman and what is its basis? Because is it not useless if it is sung as Stotra and not accompanied by recitation (Anuśaṁsana)?”
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त᳘दाहुः॥ 
(र्य्य᳘) य᳘देतत्सा᳘म गीयते᳘ ऽथ᳘ क्वैत᳘स्य सा᳘म्न ऽउक्थङ्का᳘ प्प्रतिष्ठा᳘ व्व्यृद्धᳫँ᳭ हि तद्य᳘त्स्तुतम᳘ननुशस्तमि᳘ति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

त᳘दाहुः ॥ 
य᳘देतत्सा᳘म गीयते᳘ऽथॗ क्वैत᳘स्य सा᳘म्न उक्थं [^wbr_2] का᳘ प्रतिष्ठाॗ व्यृद्धᳫं हि तद्य᳘त्स्तुतम᳘ननुशस्तमि᳘ति ॥  

[^wbr_2]: उत्थं A.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
