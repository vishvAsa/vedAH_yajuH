<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

1. Tvashṭr̥, seeing his son slain, brought Soma suitable for witchery, and withheld from Indra. Indra, committing a desecration of the sacrifice, by main force drank off his (Tvashṭr̥'s) Soma juice.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Tvaṣṭā, when his son was killed, fetched Soma capable of witchery after making it inaccessable to Indra. Indra forcefully desecrated the sacrifice and drank the Soma juice.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त्व᳘ष्टा हत᳘पुत्रः॥  
(त्रो ऽभि) अभिचरणी᳘यम᳘पेन्द्रᳫँ᳭ सो᳘ममा᳘हरत्तस्ये᳘न्द्रो यज्ञवेशसं᳘ कृत्वा᳘ प्प्रास᳘हा सो᳘ममपिबत्
</details>

</details>
