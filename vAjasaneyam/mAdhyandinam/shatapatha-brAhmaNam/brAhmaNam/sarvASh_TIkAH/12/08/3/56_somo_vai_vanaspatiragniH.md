<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Vanaspati is indeed Soma and the Sviṣṭakṛt is Agni. So he (encloses him by means of Agni and Soma and consecrates him. That is why those who know this as well as those who now not, say "A Kṣatriya is the consecrator of a Kṣatriya."
</details>

</details>
