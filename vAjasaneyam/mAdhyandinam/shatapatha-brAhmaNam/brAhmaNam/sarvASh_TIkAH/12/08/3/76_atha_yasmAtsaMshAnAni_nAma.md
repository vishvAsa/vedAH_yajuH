<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

26. And as to why (these hymns) are called 'bracers [^egg_647];' it is because by means of these Sāmans the gods braced Indra up to energy, or vital power: in like manner do the officiating priests, by means of these Sāmans, brace the Sacrificer up to energy, or vital power.

[^egg_647]: 256:2 Literally, sharpeners or sharpenings (saṁśāna).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now why these (Sāmāns) are called Saṁśānāni (those that sharpen) is because with these Sāmāns the gods sharpened (prepared) Indra for the vitality and power. Hence with these Sāmāns, the Ṛtviks sharpen (prepare) the Yajamāna for vitality and power.
</details>
