<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Well, he descends again upon the black antelope skin, and, the black antelope skin being the sacrifice, he thus finally establishes himself on the sacrifice.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He gets down upon the black antelop's skin and the black antelop's skin is indeed sacrifice. So he ultimately gets settled in the sacrifice itself.
</details>
