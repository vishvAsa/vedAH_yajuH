<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Let him, however, not do so, but let him only consecrate him by means of those deities, for those deities, indeed, are all this (universe).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He should consecrate him only (with the formulas of) these deities (Aśvins, Sarasvatī an Indra). Because these deities are no doubt, all this (universe).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

न त᳘था कुर्य्यादेता᳘भिरे᳘वैनन्देव᳘ताभिरभि᳘षिञ्चेदेता᳘ ऽउ᳘ ह्येव᳘ देव᳘ता ऽइदᳫँ᳭ स᳘र्व्वम्॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

न त᳘था कुर्यादेता᳘भिरेॗवैनं देव᳘ताभिरभि᳘षिञ्चेदेता᳘ उॗ ह्येव᳘ देव᳘ता इदᳫं स᳘र्वम् ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

न तथा कुर्यात् । एताभिरेवैनं देवताभिरभिषिंचेत् । एता उ ह्येव देवता इदं सर्वम् ॥ १८ ॥
</details>
