<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He offers (saying) “I have heard of two paths for the Pitṛs” - because they say there are only two paths; viz., that of the gods and that of the Pitṛs.” By those (two paths) all these living beings pass” - for, it is through either of those paths that everything living, passes. “What is there between the father and the mother” - The father, no doubt is this yonder (sky) and this (terres- trial world) is the mother.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

स᳘ जुहोति॥  
द्वे᳘ सृती᳘ ऽअशृणवम्पितृणा᳘महमि᳘ति द्वे वाव᳘ सृती ऽइ᳘त्याहुर्द्देवा᳘नाञ्चैव᳘ पितॄणाञ्चे᳘ति ता᳘भ्यामिदम्वि᳘श्वमेजत्स᳘मेती᳘ति
</details>

</details>

</details>
