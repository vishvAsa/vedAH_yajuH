<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Both cups of milk and cups of Surā-liquor are filled: they thereby restore to him his energies, or vital powers. On the northern fire they offer (from) the cups of milk, and thereby provide him [^egg_591] with the bright liquor, with the Soma-drink.

[^egg_591]: 231:1 Or, cause him to prosper, render him successful by means of the liquor; MS. I. O. 311 reads 'samardhayanti.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

By collecting the Grahas of milk and the Grahas of Surā, he restores in him that energy and that vital power. He offers the Grahas of milk into the northern fire and thereby enriches him with that bright Soma beverage.
</details>
