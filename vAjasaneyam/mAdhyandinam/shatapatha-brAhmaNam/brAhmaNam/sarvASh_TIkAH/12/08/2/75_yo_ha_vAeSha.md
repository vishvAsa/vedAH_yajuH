<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

31. The thirteenth draught the Sacrificer takes, for, indeed, that thirteenth month is manifestly the year itself: it is this he secures by obtaining (that draught).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Having obtained that (drink) he owns it (the year).
</details>
