<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And, indeed, the Sautrāmaṇī is the same as the year, and by means of this he wins everything, and secures everything for himself.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This Sautrāmaṇī is that year itself. He wins everything by that and secures everything.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

स[[!!]] वा᳘ ऽएष᳘ सम्वत्सर᳘ ऽएव य᳘त्सौत्रामणी ते᳘न स᳘र्व्वञ्ज᳘यति स᳘र्व्वम᳘वरुन्द्धे॥
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
