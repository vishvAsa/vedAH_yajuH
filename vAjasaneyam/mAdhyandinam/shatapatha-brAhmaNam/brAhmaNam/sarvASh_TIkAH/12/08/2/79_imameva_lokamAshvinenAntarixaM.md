<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

By them, he se- cures these three worlds. This terrestrail world by (the Paśu for) Aśvins; that aerial region by (the Paśu for) Sarasvatī and that heavely world by (the Paśu for) Indra. Thus according to their forms and according to their deities, he conquers these world and owns them.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

ऽइम᳘मेव᳘ लोक᳘माश्विने᳘नान्त᳘रिक्षᳫँ᳭ सारस्वते᳘न दि᳘वमैन्द्रे᳘ण यथारूप᳘मेव᳘ यथादेवत᳘मिमां᳘ल्लोकाञ्ज᳘यति चा᳘व च रुन्द्धे॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
