<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

27. And if (his Soma) were to meet with any mishap when drawn into the cups, let him perform an oblation with, 'To the All-gods hail!' for the All-gods he then is: he repels evil, and the sacrifice inclines to him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When collected in the Grahas, if something happens to him, let him offer an oblation (saying) - "To the Viśvedevas-Svāhā", for he then becomes Viśvedevas and the sacrifice inclines to him.
</details>
