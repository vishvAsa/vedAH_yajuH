<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

9. And if (his Soma) were to meet with any mishap when forthcoming for the purchase, let him perform an oblation with, 'To Indra and the Maruts hail!' for Indra and the Maruts he (Soma) then is: he repels evil, and the sacrifice inclines to him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When the Soma has come forward for being bought and at that time some mishap befalls on him, let him make an offering (saying) “To Indra and the Maruts-Svāhā", for then he becomes Indra and the Maruts and the sacrifice inclines to him.
</details>
