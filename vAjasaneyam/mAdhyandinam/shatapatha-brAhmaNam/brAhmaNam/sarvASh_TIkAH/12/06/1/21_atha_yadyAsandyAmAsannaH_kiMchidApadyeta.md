<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

15. And if (his Soma) were to meet with any mishap when seated on the throne, let him perform an oblation with, 'To Varuṇa hail!' for Varuṇa he then is: he repels evil, and the sacrifice inclines to him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When seated on the seat he (Soma) meets with some mishap, let him offer an oblation (saying) "To Varuṇa-Svāhā”, for he then becomes Varuṇa and the sacrifice inclines to him.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>
