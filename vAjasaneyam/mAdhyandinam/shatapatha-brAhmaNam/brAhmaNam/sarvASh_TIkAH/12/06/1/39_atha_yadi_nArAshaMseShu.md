<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

33. And if (his Soma) were to meet with any mishap when contained in the Nārāśaṁsa (cups [^egg_559]), let him perform an oblation with, 'To the Nārāśaṁsa Fathers hail!' for the Nārāśaṁsa (man-praising) Fathers he then is: he repels evil, and the sacrifice inclines to him.

[^egg_559]: 211:1 See part ii, p. 154, note 1.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When kept in the Nārāśaṁsa cups, if something happens to him, let him offer an oblation (saying) - "To the Nārāśaṁsa Pitṛs-Svāhā", for he then becomes the Nārāśaṁsa Pitṛ and the sacrifice inclines to him.
</details>
