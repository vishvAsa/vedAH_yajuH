<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

14. And if (his Soma) were to meet with any mishap when he has reached (the hall), let him perform an oblation with, 'To Soma hail!' for Soma he then is: he repels evil, and the sacrifice inclines to him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When he (Soma) has arrived (at the hall) something happens to him, let him offer an oblation (saying) "To Soma-Svāhā", for then he be- comes Soma and the sacrifice inclines to him.
</details>
