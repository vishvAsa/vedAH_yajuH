<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

34. And if (his Soma) were to meet with any mishap when ready for the purificatory bath [^egg_560], let him perform an oblation with, 'To the Stream hail!' for a stream he then is: he repels evil, and the sacrifice inclines to him.

[^egg_560]: 211:2 The pressed-out Soma-husks are taken down to (and thrown into) the water where the Sacrificer is to bathe, see IV, 4, 5, 1 seqq.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When taken up for the Avabhṛta oblation, if something happens to him, let him offer an oblation (saying) "To Sindhu-Svāhā", for he then be- comes the Sindhu and the sacrifice inclines to him.
</details>
