<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

23. And if (his Soma) were to meet with any mishap whilst being purified (strained), let him perform an oblation with, 'To Vāyu hail!' for Vāyu he then is: he repels evil, and the sacrifice inclines to him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

While being purified(strained), (strained) something happens to him, let him offer an oblation (saying) “To Vāyu-Svāhā", for he then becomes Vāyu and the sacrifice inclines to him.
</details>
