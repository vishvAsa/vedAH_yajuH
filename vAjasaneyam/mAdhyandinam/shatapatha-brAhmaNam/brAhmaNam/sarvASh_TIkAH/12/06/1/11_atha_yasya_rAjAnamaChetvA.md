<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

5. And if any one's (people), having gone in quest of the King (Soma), do not come back bringing (Soma-plants), let him perform an oblation with, 'To the plant hail!' for the plant he then is: he repels evil, and the sacrifice inclines to him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

If someone's (emissaries) having gone to fetch King Soma, return without securing him, let him offer an oblation (saying) "To the plants-Svāhā", for them he becomes the plant; he thus reples evil and the sacrifice inclines to him.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(त्य᳘) अ᳘थ य᳘स्य रा᳘जानम᳘च्छेत्त्वा᳘॥

</details>
