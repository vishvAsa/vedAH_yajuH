<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

6. They also say, 'If any one's Agnihotra (milk) were to be spilled whilst he gets it milked, what rite and what expiation would there be in that case?'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They ask, "When the Agnihotra milk of some one spills while being milked, what is to be done; what is the expiation?" Having touched it (spilled milk) while chanting the formula meant as expiation for spilling and washed it with water, he can offer the remaining milk.
</details>
