<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for inasmuch as he offers it on those (hot coals) it is offered, and inasmuch as he extinguishes it along with them it is not offered.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This way it becomes both offered and not offered.
</details>

</details>
