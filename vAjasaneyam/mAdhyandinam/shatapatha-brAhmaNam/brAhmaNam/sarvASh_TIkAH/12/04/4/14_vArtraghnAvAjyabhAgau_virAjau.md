<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

The course of procedure thereof (is as follows):--he should recite those same seventeen kindling-verses; the two butter-portions relate to the slaying of Vr̥tra, the saṁyājyās are two Virāj verses; and the invitatory and offering formulas are as follows:--(R̥g-veda VIII, 75, 15; Vāj. S. XI, 71), 'From the far region cross thou over to the near: protect thou that wherein I am!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Its procedure is thus - "After chanting the seventeen Samidhenis, the two Ajyabhāgas of the killer Vṛtra the two Saṁyājas in Virāḍ metre, let him utter these Vājya and Anuvākya - "You cross over from the far region to the near.
</details>
