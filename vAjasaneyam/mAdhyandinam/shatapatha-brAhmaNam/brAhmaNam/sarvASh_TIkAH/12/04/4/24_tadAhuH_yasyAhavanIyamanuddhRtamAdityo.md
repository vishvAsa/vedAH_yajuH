<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

6. They also say, 'If the sun were to set on any one's Āhavanīya not yet having been taken out, what rite and what expiation would there be in that case?'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they say - "Supposing the Sun sets even when one's Āhavanīya is not taken out, what is the rite to be observed and what is the expiation?" Those rays of the Sun are Viśvedevas and they are going away from him and that (Agnihotra) becomes futile.
</details>
