<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He takes this ghee in the same way as before, takes a log of wood and pro- ceeds to the Āhavanīya. Placing that log of wood on the Āhavanīya, he offers the ghee with his right knee bent, (saying) “to the Viśvedevas Svāhā.” The import is the same as before. There is no affliction nor any harm when this expiation is performed.
</details>
