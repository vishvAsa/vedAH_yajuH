<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

7. They also say, 'If the sun were to rise over any one's Āhavanīya not having been taken out, what rite and what expiation would there be in that case?'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they ask – "If the Sun were to rise before the Āhavanīya if some one is not taken out, what is the rite and what is the expiation?" Those rays (of the Sun are Viśvedevas) who having stayed there, are leaving him.
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>
