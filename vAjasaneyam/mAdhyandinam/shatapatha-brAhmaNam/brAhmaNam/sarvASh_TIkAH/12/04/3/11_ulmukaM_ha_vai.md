<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--let him do it in this way:--having taken a coal from a firebrand, let him crumble it on the two churning-sticks, for (in this way) he obtains both that desire which is contained in the (fire) churned out of a firebrand, and that which is contained in (the fire churned out from) the churning-sticks. This, then, is the rite performed in that case.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He may, of course proceed with a burning stick or a piece of it and do thus - let him take a coal from that burning stick and crumble it on the Araṇis. This way he realises both his desires. viz., obtaining fire from churing the burning stick and fire from the Araṇi pieces. This is the rite there.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

उल्मुकᳫँ᳭ ह वै᳘वादा᳘य च᳘रेयुरु᳘ल्मुकस्य वा ऽवव्व्र᳘श्चमित्थ᳘मेव᳘ कुर्य्यादु᳘ल्मुकाद᳘ङ्गारमादा᳘य त᳘मर᳘ण्योरभिवि᳘मथ्नीयादु᳘प ह तङ्का᳘ममाप्नोति य᳘ ऽउल्मुकम᳘थ्य ऽउ᳘पो तं᳘ यो ऽर᳘ण्योरेत᳘देव त᳘त्र क᳘र्म्म॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

उल्मुकं ह वैवादाय चरेयुः । उल्मुकस्य वा वव्रश्चम् । इत्थमेव कुर्यात् । उल्मुकादंगारमादाय । तमरण्योरभिविमथ्नीयात् । उप ह तं काममाप्नोति । य उल्मुकमध्ये । उपो तं, यो ऽरण्योः । एतदेव तत्र कर्म ॥ ३ ॥
</details>
