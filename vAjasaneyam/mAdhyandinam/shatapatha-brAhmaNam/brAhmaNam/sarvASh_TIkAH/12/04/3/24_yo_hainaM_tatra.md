<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

But let him not do this, for if, in that case, any one were to say of him, 'Surely this one has obstructed the forward vital airs [^egg_525]: this Sacrificer will die,' then that would indeed be likely to come to pass.

[^egg_525]: 190:1 ? Or, has forced them forward.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

If (while doing so) some one is to remark - “This (Priest) has choked the vital airs of the Yajamāna and this Yajamāna will die” - it may so happen.
</details>
