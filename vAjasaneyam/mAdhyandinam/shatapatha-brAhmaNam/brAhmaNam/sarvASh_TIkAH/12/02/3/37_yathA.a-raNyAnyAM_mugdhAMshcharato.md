<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This is as if one who is gullible (unfamiliar with the forest) enters into it, without the knowledge, will suffer from hunger or thirst and will be at- tacked by cruel Rakṣasas, any one (Yajamāna) venturing to enter into this labyrinth of sacrificial jungle without knowledge will suffer from hunger or thirst and will be attacked by cruel Rakṣasas. (On the other hand) just as a knowledgeable person (entering the floods) moves on from one stream to another and from one spot of safety to another;
</details>
