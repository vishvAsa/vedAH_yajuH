<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

13. As to this they say, 'How many onward, and how many backward days are there?'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They ask – “How many are the forward moving days from the year and how many backward moving?” Those that are performed once each are the onward moving days;
</details>
