<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

3. This (third finger) is the second day,--this (upper joint) is its morning-service, this (middle joint) its midday-service, and this (lower joint) its evening-service: it is in place of the Trishṭubh, whence this (third finger) is larger than this (little finger).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This (ring-finger) is its second day. This upper joint is its morning Savana; this middle joint is its mid day Savana and this lower joint is its third (evening) Savana. It is in the place of Tṛṣṭhubh metre and hence it is longer than the little finger.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(ष्ठेद᳘) इद᳘मेव᳘ द्विती᳘यम᳘हः॥  
(स्त᳘) त᳘स्येद᳘मेव᳘ प्रातःसवन᳘मिदम्मा᳘ध्यन्दिनᳫँ᳭ स᳘वनमिद᳘न्तृतीयसवन᳘न्त्रिष्टु᳘भ ऽआय᳘तने त᳘स्मादिय᳘मस्यै व्व᳘र्षोयसी॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

इद᳘मेव᳘ द्विती᳘यम᳘हः ॥  
त᳘स्येद᳘मेव᳘ प्रातःसवन᳘मिदं मा᳘ध्यन्दिनᳫं स᳘वनमिदं᳘ तृतीयसवनं᳘ त्रिष्टु᳘भ आय᳘तने त᳘स्मादिय᳘मस्यै व᳘र्षोयसी ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

इदमेव द्वितीयमहः । तस्येदमेव प्रातःसवनम् । इदं माध्यन्दिनं सवनम् । इदं तृतीयसवनम् । त्रिष्टुभ आयतने । तस्मादियमस्यै वर्षीयसी ॥ ३ ॥
</details>
