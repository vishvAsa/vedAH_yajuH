<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and, verily, whosoever thus knows that year as established in the body, establishes himself by offspring and cattle in this, and by immortality in the other, world.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

स᳘ ऽएष᳘ सम्वत्स᳘रो ऽध्यात्मं प्र᳘तिष्ठितः स यो᳘ हैव᳘मेत᳘ᳫँ᳘ सम्वत्सर᳘मध्यात्मं प्र᳘तिष्ठितम्वे᳘द प्रतितिष्ठति[[!!]] प्रज᳘या पशु᳘भिरस्मिं᳘ल्लो᳘के ऽमृतत्वे᳘नामु᳘ष्मिन्॥
</details>

</details>
