<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

His father, looking at him, said, 'Knowest thou, long-lived one, the fording-footholds of the year?' --'I know them,' he replied, for, indeed, he said this as one knowing it.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

His father looking at him asked "O, son of long life! do you know the shallow fonds and foot-holds of the Saṁvatsara (year long sacri- fice)." "I know them" he replied, because he said thus as one who knew it. (First Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त᳘ᳫँ᳘ ह पि᳘तोपे᳘क्ष्योवाच व्वे᳘त्थ᳘ न्वायुष्मन्त्सम्वत्सर᳘स्य गाधप्रतिष्ठा ऽइ᳘ति व्वेदे᳘ति होवाचैत᳘द्ध त᳘द्विद्वा᳘नुवाच॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

[^wbr_2]: त᳘द्विनुवाच A.
