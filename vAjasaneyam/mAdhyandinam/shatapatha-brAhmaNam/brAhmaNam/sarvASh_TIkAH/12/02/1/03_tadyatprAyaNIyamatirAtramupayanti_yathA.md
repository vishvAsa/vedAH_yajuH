<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and

when they enter on the Prāyaṇīya Atirātra it is just as if they were entering the ocean by a flight of steps.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Prāyaṇīya Atirātra is the entry-point (path leading to the waters); because one gets into the water (to cross it) through the entry-point (flight of steps) and when they commence the Prāyaṇīya Atirātra, they are as if entering the ocean through the entry-point (by the steps).
</details>
