<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

for there are thirty-three gods and Prajāpati is the thirty-fourth. Since it gains all the gods, there is one Ukthya with Ṣoḍaśī (Stotra); for Ukthyā is food and Ṣoḍaśī is vitality. It is by means of this vitality and this food, the Gods at- tained all their desires and enjoyed all that they wished for.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त्र᳘यस्त्रिᳫँ᳭शद्वै᳘ देवाः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिश्चतुस्त्रिᳫँ᳭शः स᳘र्व्वासान्देव᳘तानामा᳘प्त्या ऽए᳘क ऽउ᳘क्थ्यः षोडशि᳘मान᳘न्नं वा᳘ ऽउ᳘क्थ्यो व्वी᳘र्यᳫँ᳭ षोड᳘शी॥
</details>

</details>
