<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Let him reply, 'Thereby, that there are six of the one, and six of the other.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(It is because) there are six days of one and six days of the other (i.e. since the number of days is equal, the dissimilarity in Stomas does not matter).
</details>
