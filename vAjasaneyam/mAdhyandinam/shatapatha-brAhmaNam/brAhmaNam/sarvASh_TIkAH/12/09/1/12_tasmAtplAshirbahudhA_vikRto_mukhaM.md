<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--and inasmuch as that (pan) is much perforated, therefore that organ is much divided,--the bowl (sata) is his mouth, the strainer

his tongue, the dish (capya) his anus, the tail (whisk) his bladder,
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Since that vessel happens to have many holes, his male organ is of many shapes. The bowl (Sata in which the remainings of the Grahas are poured) is his mouth; the purifying strainer (bunch of grass) is his tongue; the dish Cāpya his arms and the tail whisk is his bladder.
</details>

</details>
