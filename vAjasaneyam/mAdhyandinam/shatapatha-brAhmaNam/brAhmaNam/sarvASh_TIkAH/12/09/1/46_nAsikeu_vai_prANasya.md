<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

By means of the tongue one distinguishes the essence (taste) of food, and the nostrils, indeed, are the path of the breath; and in that, when the victim of Indra. is being (offered), he makes offering to these deities in common, thereby he puts those (parts of the body) together and takes them to himself.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He thus combines these (deities of the Prāṇa, tongue and nostrils) together and owns them unto himself.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

ना᳘सिके ऽउ वै᳘ प्प्राण᳘स्य प᳘न्थास्तद्यैदन्द्रे᳘ पशौ᳘ सत्य᳘थैता᳘ देव᳘ताः सह य᳘जत्येता᳘न्ये᳘वैत᳘त्सार्द्धङ्कृ᳘त्वा ऽऽत्म᳘न्द्धत्ते॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

ना᳘सिकेऽउ वै᳘ प्राण᳘स्य प᳘न्थास्तद्य᳘दैन्द्रे᳘ पशौ᳘ सत्य᳘थैता᳘ देव᳘ताः सह य᳘जत्येता᳘न्येॗवैत᳘त्सार्धं᳘ कृॗत्वात्म᳘न्धत्ते ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

नासिके उ वै प्राणस्य पंथाः । तद्यदैन्द्रै पशौ सति, अथैता देवताः सह यजति । एतान्येवैतत्सार्द्धं कृत्वा ऽऽत्मन् धत्ते ॥ १४ ॥
</details>
