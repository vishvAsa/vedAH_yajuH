<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and in that, when Savitr̥'s cake is being (offered), he makes offering to these deities in common, thereby he puts those (parts of the body) together and takes them to himself.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When he offers the Puroḍāśa to Savitā, he is offering to these deities in common. He thus combines these (deities of Prāṇa, Vyāna and male organ) together and owns them unto himself.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

तद्य᳘त्सावित्रे᳘ पुरोडा᳘शे सत्य᳘थैता᳘ देव᳘ताः सह य᳘जत्येता᳘न्ये᳘वैत᳘त्सार्द्ध᳘ङ्कृ᳘त्वा ऽऽत्म᳘न्द्धत्ते॥
</details>

</details>
