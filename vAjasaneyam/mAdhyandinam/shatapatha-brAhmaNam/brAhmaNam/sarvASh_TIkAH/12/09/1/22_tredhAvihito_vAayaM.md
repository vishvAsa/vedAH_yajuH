<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

By means of the *Paśu* for Aśvins, he gains that part of the body below the navel.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त्रेधाविहितो वा᳘ ऽअयम्पु᳘रुषस्या᳘त्मा ऽऽत्मा᳘नमे᳘वास्य तै᳘ स्पृणोति
</details>

</details>
