<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Whatever food a man eats in this world, that (food) in turn eats him in the world beyond.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

स य᳘द्ध वा᳘ ऽअस्मिं᳘ल्लोके पु᳘रुषो᳘ ऽन्नम᳘त्ति त᳘देनममु᳘ष्मिंल्लोके प्प्र᳘त्यत्ति
</details>

</details>
