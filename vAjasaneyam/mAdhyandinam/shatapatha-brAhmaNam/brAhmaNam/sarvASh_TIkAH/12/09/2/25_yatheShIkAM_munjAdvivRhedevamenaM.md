<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

he causes his garment to float away: even as one would pluck out a reed from its sheath, so he plucks him from out all evil. He bathes, and (thereby) drives the darkness (of sin) from himself.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Just as one would pluck out a reed from out of its sheeth, he draws him out of all sin. He then bathes and thereby drives away stuper (darkness).
</details>

</details>

</details>
