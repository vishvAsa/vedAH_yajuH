<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

1. Having performed the sacrifice they betake themselves to the purificatory bath; for after a Soma-sacrifice they do betake themselves to the purificatory bath, and the Sautrāmaṇī is the same as the Soma (sacrifice).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

After performing the sacrifice, they proceed for the *Avabhṛta* (final ablution). They go for *Avabhṛta* after completing *Soma Yāga* and this *Sautrāmaṇī* is a Soma sacrifice.
</details>

अवभृथमिष्ट्वा [^१_१४०] यन्ति । त्रिपशूनिष्ट्वा अवभृथयागं कर्तुं यन्तीत्यवभृथविधिः ॥ १ ॥
