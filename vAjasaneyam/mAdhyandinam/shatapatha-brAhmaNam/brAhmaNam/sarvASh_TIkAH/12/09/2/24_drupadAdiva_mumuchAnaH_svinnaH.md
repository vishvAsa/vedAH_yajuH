<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

7. With (Vāj. S. XX, 20), 'As one set free (is liberated) from the stake, as he who sweateth

 (is cleansed) from filth by bathing, as the ghee is purified by the strainer, so may the waters cleanse me from sin!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"As one is set free from a stake (imprisonment), as he who sheds away dirt by bathing, as the *ghee* is purified by the *Pavitra* (purifying bunch of grass), so the waters may cleanse me from sin." (Thus saying) he allows his garment to float away.
</details>

</details>
