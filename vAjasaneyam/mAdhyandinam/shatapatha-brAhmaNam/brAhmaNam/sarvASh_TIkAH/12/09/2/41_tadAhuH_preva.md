<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

12. As to this they say, 'Surely, he who goes down to the water for the purificatory bath falls away from this world!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

In this connection they say - "Surely he who enters into the Avabhṛta waters falls away from this world." (That is not so).
</details>
