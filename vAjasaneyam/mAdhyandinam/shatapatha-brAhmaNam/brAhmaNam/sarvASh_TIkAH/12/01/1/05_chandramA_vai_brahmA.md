<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Now the Brahman is the moon, and the moon is Soma, and plants belong to Soma [^egg_417]: he thus connects the plants with this (terrestrial) world.
</details>

the moon is Soma and the plants belong to Soma.

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>
