<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Now, the Hotr̥ [^egg_418] is Agni in respect of the deity, and speech in respect of the body; and rain is food: he thus connects both Agni (fire) and speech with food.

[^egg_418]: 136:1 Viz. as the offering-priest κατ᾽ ἐξοχήν, he who, by the recitation of his 'invitatory' and 'offering' verses, like Agni, draws the gods to the offering, and causes them to graciously accept it.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Hotā is Agni in his divine aspect. The speech is the physical aspect and rain is food. Thus he connects both Agni and speech with food.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(त्य) अग्निर्वै हो᳘ता ऽधिदेवतं व्वा᳘गध्यात्मम᳘न्नम्वृ᳘ष्टिरग्नि᳘ञ्च तद्वा᳘चञ्चा᳘न्नेन स᳘न्दधाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>
