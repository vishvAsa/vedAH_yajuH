<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'the fiery spirit and energy;' fiery spirit and energy, vital power, he thus bestows on him;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"The vigour and energy" - he thereby bestows on him the vigour and vital power.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
