<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and, when the night was clearing up, and the sun had not yet risen, Indra, thinking, 'It is neither by day nor by night,' therewith struck off the head of Namuci, the Asura.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When the night was ending and the Sun had not yet risen, Indra considering it as neither night, nor day, cut off Namuci's head.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

तेने᳘न्द्रो न᳘मुचेरासुर᳘स्य᳘ व्व्युष्टायाᳫँ᳭ रा᳘त्रावनुदित ऽआदित्ये न दि᳘वा न न᳘क्तमि᳘ति शि᳘र ऽउ᳘दवासयत्॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

</details>
