<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

4. Wherefore it has been said by the R̥shi (R̥g-veda S. VIII, 14, 13), 'With foam of water, Indra, didst thou sever the head of Namuci, when thou wert subduing all thine enemies.' Now, Namuci is evil: having thus, indeed, slain that evil, his hateful enemy, Indra wrested from him his energy, or vital power.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That is why the Ṛṣi said - “O Indra, you cut off Namuci's head with foam of water. Thus you conquerred all enemies.” Namuci is evil and by killing that hateful evil enemy, Indra captured his energy or vital power from him.
</details>
