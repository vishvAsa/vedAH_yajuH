<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

12. With (Vāj. S. XIX, 5), 'The Brahman

and Kshatra he purifieth,' he purifies the milk [^egg_586]: he thus produces the Kshatra from out of the Brahman, for from out of the priesthood the nobility is produced;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He purifies the milk saying - "The Brahman purifies the Kṣatra" - thereby he produces the Kṣatra out of the priesthood; for it is from Brah- man, the Kṣatra is produced.
</details>
