<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

They perceived that (means of) drinking separately (one of) the two liquids,--'King Soma, the drink of immortality, is pressed [^egg_582];' --and having thereby made that (Soma) palatable, they took it in (as food).

[^egg_582]: 223:1 Vāj. S. XIX, 72 seq. On the myth cp. Muir, O. S. T., vol. v, p. 94.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They found out a way to separate the two and make it (Soma) drinkable. “King Soma, the drink of immortality, is pressed” - and thereby made that (Soma) tasty and took it (as food).
</details>
