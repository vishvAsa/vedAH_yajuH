<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

There are (for this sacrifice) thirty-three Dakshiṇās (presents to priests), for thirty-three were the gods who healed him: whence they say, 'Dakshiṇās are healing medicine.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

There are thirty-three Dakṣiṇās for this, because thirty-three gods healed him (Indra). Therefore they say Dakṣiṇā is medicine (First Brāhmaṇa Ends)..
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त्र᳘यस्त्रिᳫँ᳭शद्धि त᳘न्देव᳘ता ऽअ᳘भिषज्यंस्त᳘स्मादाहुर्भेषजन्द᳘क्षिणा ऽइ᳘ति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

त्र᳘यस्त्रिᳫंशद्धि तं᳘ देव᳘ता अ᳘भिषज्यंस्त᳘स्मादाहुर्भेषजं द᳘क्षिणा इ᳘ति ॥ २ [७.१.] ॥ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

त्रयस्त्रिंशद्धि तं देवता अभिषज्यन् । तस्मादाहुः । भेषजं दक्षिणा इति ॥ १४ ॥
</details>
