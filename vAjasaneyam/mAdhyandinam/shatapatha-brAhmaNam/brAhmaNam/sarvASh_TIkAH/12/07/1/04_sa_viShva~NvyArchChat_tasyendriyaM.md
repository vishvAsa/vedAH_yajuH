<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He went asunder in every direction, and his energy, or vital power [^egg_566], flowed away from every limb.

[^egg_566]: 214:2 'Vīrya' (virile power) is constantly used to explain 'indriya.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Indra drank that by force and thus desecrated the sacrifice. He wandered about in all directions and his vital essence flowed out of all his limbs.
</details>
