<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

20. Varuṇa's (cake) is one on ten potsherds, for the Virāj consists of ten syllables, and Varuṇa is Virāj (the widely ruling), the lord of food: through Varuṇa he thus secures food for him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Varuṇa is the lord of food. Through Varuṇa, he thus secures food for him.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

द᳘शाक्षरा वै᳘ व्विराड᳘न्नं व्विराड्व᳘रुणो᳘ ऽन्नपतिर्व्वरुणेनै᳘वास्मा ऽअ᳘न्नम᳘वरुन्धे
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
