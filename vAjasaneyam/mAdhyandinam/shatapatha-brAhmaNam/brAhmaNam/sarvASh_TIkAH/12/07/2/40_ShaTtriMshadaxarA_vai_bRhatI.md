<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He thereby secures the ferocity and lordship of birds. Thus there are these thirty-six (items); and the Bṛhatī metre has thirty- six syllables and cattle belong to Bṛhatī. Thus by means of the Bṛhatī, he secures cattle for him.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

ष᳘ट्त्रिᳫँ᳭शदक्षरा वै᳘ बृहती बा᳘र्हताः पश᳘वो बृह᳘त्यै᳘वास्मै पशून᳘वरुन्धे॥
</details>
