<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

11. And if the Anvāhāryapacana were to do so, one may know that the food-eating fire has reached him first: that he will eat food, and that those behind him will eat food (be prosperous) in this world.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

If the Anvāhāryapacana (fire of the Dakṣiṇāgagni) were to reach him first, one can conclude that the fire which is the eater of food has reached him first and that he will be eating food(in the other world) and that those who come after him will eat plenty of food (prosper) in this world.
</details>
