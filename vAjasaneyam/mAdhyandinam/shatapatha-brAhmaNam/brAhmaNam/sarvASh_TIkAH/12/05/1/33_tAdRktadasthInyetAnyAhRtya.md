<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Having brought home the bones, let him throw them on a black antelope skin, and arrange them in accordance with man's form, and having covered them with wool and sprinkled with ghee, let him by burning unite him

with his fires: he thus causes him to be born from his own (maternal) womb.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Having brought home the bones, let him place them in a black antelop's skin and arrange them in the shape of a man. Then cover them with wool and sprinkle with ghee and burn them by uniting them with his fires. Thus he makes him born out of his own (mother's) womb.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

तादृक्तद᳘स्थीन्येता᳘न्याहृ᳘त्य कृष्णाजिने न्यु᳘प्य पुरुषविधि᳘म्विधायो᳘र्ण्णाभिः प्रच्छाद्या᳘ज्येनाभिघार्य्य[[!!]] त᳘मग्नि᳘भिः समु᳘पोषेत्त᳘देनᳫँ᳭ स्वाद्यो᳘नेः प्र᳘जनयती᳘ति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

[^wbr_3]: प्रजि᳘जनयिषे᳘त्ता॰ A.P.M.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
