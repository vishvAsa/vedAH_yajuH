<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

but let him not do this, for this would be as if he were to seek to cause the seed implanted in one womb to be born forth from another womb.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It is like, planting seed in one womb and wishing to get it born through another womb.
</details>
