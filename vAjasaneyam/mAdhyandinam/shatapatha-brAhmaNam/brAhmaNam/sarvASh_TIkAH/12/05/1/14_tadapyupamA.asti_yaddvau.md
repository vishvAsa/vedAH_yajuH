<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

5. Concerning this there also is a simile:--if two smashed cars were to (be made to) unite there would be at least one (fit) for driving.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

There is a comparison for this. If two damaged carts are brought to- gether, one of them (at least) will start rolling by being repaired (with the parts of the other).
</details>
