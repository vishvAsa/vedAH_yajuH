<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

4. Fitted out with the Abhijit, the Svarasāmans join the Vishuvat on both sides:--how do these enter man, and how do they become united with the vital airs?
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"How does the Svarasāman, fitted out with Abhijit, get united with Viṣuvat on both sides and how do these enter the Puruṣa and become one with the Prāṇas?”
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

अभिजि᳘ता स्व᳘रसामानः॥  
(नो ऽभि᳘) अभि᳘क्लृप्ता ऽउभय᳘तो व्विषुव᳘न्तमु᳘पयन्ति।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

अभिजि᳘ता स्व᳘रसामानः ॥  
अभि᳘क्लृप्ता उभय᳘तो विषुव᳘न्तमु᳘पयन्ति ॥
</details>
