<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

4. They also say, 'Seeing that the performers of a year's session become initiated for a year, how does their Full-moon oblation come to be uninterrupted?' Let him reply, 'By the ghee and the sacrificial cake.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they ask - “When those who sit in the Samvatsara sacrifice (of Gavām Ayanam) enter into year-long initiation, how is it that their Pūrṇamāsa offering continues uninterrupted?” The reply to it is “By means of ghee and Purodāśa (offerings).”
</details>
