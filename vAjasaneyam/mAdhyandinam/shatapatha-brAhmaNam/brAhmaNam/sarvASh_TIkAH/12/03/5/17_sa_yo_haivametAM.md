<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and, verily, whosoever thus knows this entering of the sacrificial rites into the year becomes a sharer in the heavenly world.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He who knows thus the merger of the sacrificial rituals in the Saṁvatsara, becomes one who shares the heavenly world.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>
