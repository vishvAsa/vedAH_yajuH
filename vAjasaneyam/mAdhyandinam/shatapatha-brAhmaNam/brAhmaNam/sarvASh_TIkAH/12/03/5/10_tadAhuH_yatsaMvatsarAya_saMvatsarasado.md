<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

5. They also say, 'Seeing that the performers of a year's session become initiated for a year, how does their New-moon oblation come to be uninterrupted?' Let him reply, 'By the sour curds and the cake.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they ask - “when those who sit in the Samvatsara sacrifice (of Gavām-Ayanam) enter into year long initiation, how is it that their Pitṛ-Yagña (sacrifice to the Pitṛs) continues uninterrupted?” The reply to it is “By the performance of Aupāsanas.”
</details>
