<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

9. They saw the (session of a) year to be an accelerated Soma-feast in lieu of the Tāpaścita [^egg_497], for there are those (same) Stomas, those Pr̥shṭḥas, and those metres.

[^egg_497]: 171:4 See part iv, p. 317, note 2.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They discovered the Saṁvatsara as a quick moving Soma-Sava to be a substitute for the Tāpaścita; since it has the same Stomas, the same Pṛṣṭhas and the same metres (as the Tāpaścita).
</details>
