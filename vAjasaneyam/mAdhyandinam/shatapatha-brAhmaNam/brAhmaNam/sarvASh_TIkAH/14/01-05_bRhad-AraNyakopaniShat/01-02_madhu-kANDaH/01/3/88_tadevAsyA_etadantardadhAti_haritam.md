<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

It is yellow, for yellow, as it were, is the sky.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It is yellow in colour; for, yellow is the colour of the sky.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त᳘दे᳘वास्या ऽएत᳘दन्त᳘र्द्दधाति ह᳘रितम्भवति ह᳘रिणीव हि द्यौः॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

तदेवास्या एतदन्तर्दधाति । हरितं भवति । हरिणीव हि द्यौः ॥ २९ ॥
</details>

</details>
