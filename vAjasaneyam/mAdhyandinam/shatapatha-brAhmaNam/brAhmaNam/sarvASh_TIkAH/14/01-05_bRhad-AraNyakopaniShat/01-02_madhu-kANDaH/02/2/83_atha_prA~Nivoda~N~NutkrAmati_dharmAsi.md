<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

29. He steps out towards the north-east with, 'A well-supporting support thou art,'--he who shines yonder is indeed a support, for he supports everything here, and by him everything here is supported;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He gets out towards the north-east direction saying, “You are a support who sustains well.” The one who burns yonder is indeed a support, for he sustains everything here and everything here is supported by him.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

**अथ प्राङिवोदङुत्क्रामति धर्म्मा ऽसि सुधर्म्मेती**ति 'अथ प्राङिवोदङ्' प्राङ्मुख इव भूत्वा उदङ्मुखः 'उत्क्रामति' [^१_८३] आहवनीयदक्षिणदेशात् वेदिमतिक्रम्य वेदेरुत्तरतो गच्छेत् "धर्मा ऽसि" इति मन्त्रेण ।

[^१_८३]: धर्म्मा ऽसीत्युत्क्रामत्युत्तरपूर्वार्द्धम् । का. श्रौ. सू. २६ । ९७ ।
</details>
