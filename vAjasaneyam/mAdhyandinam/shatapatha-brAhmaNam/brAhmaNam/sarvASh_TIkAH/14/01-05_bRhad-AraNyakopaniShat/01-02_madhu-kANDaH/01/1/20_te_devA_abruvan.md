<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

11. The gods spake, 'Verily, our great hero (mahān viraḥ) has fallen:' therefrom the Mahāvīra pot (was named).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now the gods said- "Our great hero lies fallen. In other words he has become Mahā Vīra, a sacrificial vessel." The sap that flowed from him, they wiped up with their hands. Since they wiped him up with their hands, it used to be called Samrāṭ (Sām-Mārj).
</details>
