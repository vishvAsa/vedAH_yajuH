<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Let him not spit whilst the sun shines, lest he should spit upon him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And when the sun shines, let him not discharge urine lest he discharges on sun himself, and the sun, while shining, is indeed great and should so act as not to injure him.
</details>
