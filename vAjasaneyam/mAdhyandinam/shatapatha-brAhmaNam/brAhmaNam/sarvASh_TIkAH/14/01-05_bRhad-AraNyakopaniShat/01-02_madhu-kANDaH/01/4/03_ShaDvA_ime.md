<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Six of them [^egg_1244] step up to it, for six in number are these vital airs in the head: it is these he thus lays into it.

[^egg_1244]: 469:2 Viz. the Sacrificer and the priests with the exception of the Prastotr̥.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It is these that he puts into it.
</details>

</details>
