<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

51. And, again, they say, 'Seeing that elsewhere there are fore-offerings and after-offerings, wherefore are there not any on this occasion?'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Again they ask - "Elsewhere they offer the fore-offering and after-offer- ing and why is it that they are not done here?" The Prayājas and the Anuyājas are indeed the vital airs;
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त᳘दाहुः॥  
(र्य्य᳘) य᳘त्प्रयाजानुयाजा᳘ ऽअन्य᳘त्र भ᳘वन्त्य᳘थ क᳘स्माद᳘त्र न᳘ भवन्ती᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

त᳘दाहुः॥  
य᳘त्प्रयाजानुयाजा᳘ अन्य᳘त्र भवन्त्य᳘थ क᳘स्माद᳘त्र न᳘ भवन्ती᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

तदाहुः- यत्प्रयाजानुयाजा अन्यत्र भवन्ति । अथ कस्मादत्र न भवन्तीति ।
</details>

</details>
