<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

he places first, and the deity last [^egg_1236]; for the call of 'hail!' is he who shines yonder, and the Pravargya also is that (sun): it is him he thus gratifies; and hence he places the call of 'hail!'

[^egg_1236]: 466:2 Literally, the call of 'hail!' (svāhā-kāra) he makes to be the nearer, and the deity the farther.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Because the Svāhākāra is he 329 who shines yonder and this Pravargya is also that one (Sun).
</details>
