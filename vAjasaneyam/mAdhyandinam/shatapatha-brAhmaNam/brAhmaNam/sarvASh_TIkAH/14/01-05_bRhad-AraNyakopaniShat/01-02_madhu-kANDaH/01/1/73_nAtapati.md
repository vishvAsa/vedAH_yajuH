<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Let him not cover himself (with a garment) whilst the sun shines, lest he should be concealed from that (sun).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Simi- larly he should not spit when sun shines lest he should spit on him.
</details>
