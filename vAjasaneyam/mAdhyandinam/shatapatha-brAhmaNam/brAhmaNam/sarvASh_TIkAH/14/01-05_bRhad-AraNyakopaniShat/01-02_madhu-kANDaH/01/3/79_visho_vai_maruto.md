for the Maruts are the (common) people: he thus surrounds the nobility by the people, whence the nobility here is surrounded on both sides by the people.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus, it is him he gratifies and hence he utters the Svāhākāra first and the deity last. "Be en- circled by Marut"- (meaning) the Maruts are the common people and he thus encircles the princely class by the people and that is why here the Kṣatra is surrounded on either sides by the people.
</details>
