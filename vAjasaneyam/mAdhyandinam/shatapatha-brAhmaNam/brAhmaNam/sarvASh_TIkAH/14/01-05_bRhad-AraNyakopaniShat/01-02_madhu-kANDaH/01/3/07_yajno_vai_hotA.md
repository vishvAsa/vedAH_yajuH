<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

he says, because the Hotr̥ is the sacrifice: he thus thereby says to him, 'Restore the head of the sacrifice!' and accordingly the Hotr̥ begins to recite--
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus, he tells him - "restore the head of the sacrifice." Accordingly the Hotā recites.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

यज्ञो वै हो᳘ता त᳘मेवै᳘त᳘दाह यज्ञ᳘स्य शि᳘रः प्र᳘तिधेही᳘ति प्र᳘तिपद्यते हो᳘ता॥
</details>

</details>
