<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--Makha, doubtless, is he who shines yonder, and the Pravargya also is that one: it is him he thus gratifies, and therefore he says, 'For Makha thee.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus he propitiates him and hence he says, "You for Makha (I sprinkle)."
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

एष᳘ ऽउ प्रव᳘र्ग्यस्त᳘देत᳘मे᳘वैत᳘त्प्रीणाति त᳘स्मादाह मखा᳘य त्वे᳘ति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

एष᳘ उ प्रव᳘र्ग्यस्त᳘देत᳘मेॗवैत᳘त्प्रीणाति त᳘स्मादाह मखा᳘य त्वे᳘ति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

एष उ प्रवर्ग्यः । तदेतमेवैतत्प्रीणाति । तस्मादाह- **"मखाय त्वा"**- इति ॥ ५ ॥
</details>
