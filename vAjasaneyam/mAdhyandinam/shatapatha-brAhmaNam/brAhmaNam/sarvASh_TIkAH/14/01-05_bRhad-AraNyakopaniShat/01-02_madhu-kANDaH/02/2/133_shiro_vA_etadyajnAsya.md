<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Well, this--to wit, the Praṇītā (water)--being the head of the sacrifice, and the Pravargya also being its head, (he does so) thinking, 'Lest I cause the head to be overtopped by a head.'
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>
