<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

26. He then lays pieces of (split) Vikaṅkata wood round (the Mahāvīra), two pointing to the east [^egg_1235], with (Vāj. S. XXXVII, 13), 'Hail! be thou encompassed by the Maruts!' --the call of 'hail!'

[^egg_1235]: 466:1 That is, along the north and the south sides of the pot, on the burning sheaths of reed grass; or rather on hot cinders heaped thereon. Katy. XXVI, 3, 9. They would partly serve the purpose of the ordinary (three) enclosing-sticks; and Āpast., indeed, calls them 'paridhi.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He places twigs of Vikankata wood around the Mahā Vīra. Two pieces he lays to the east saying "Svāhā, be encircled by the Maruts." Here he makes XVI.1.3.26] the Svāhākāra first and then names the deity.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

अथ त्रयोदशभिर्वैकंकतसमिद्भिः महावीरस्य परिश्रयणं विधित्सुः तासु द्वाभ्यां समिद्भ्यां समंत्रकाभ्यां प्रथमं परिश्रयणं कार्यमित्याह- **अथ वैकंकतौ० करोती**ति । 'अथ' अभिमर्शनानंतरं 'वैकंकतौ' विकंकतवृक्षसंबंधिनौ द्वौ 'शकलौ प्राञ्चौ' प्रागग्रौ महावीरस्य दक्षिणतः उत्तरतश्च 'परिश्रयति' निदधातीत्यर्थः । "स्वाहा मरुद्भिः" इति अस्य मंत्रः ।
</details>
