now an offering is a sacrifice, and (consequently) the Vikaṅkata is

the sacrifice: with the sacrifice he thus supplies and completes it.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

An offering is a sacrifice and hence the Vikankata is the sacrifice. Therefore with the sacrifice he thus equips and completes it.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>
