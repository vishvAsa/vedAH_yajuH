<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'with the divine Savitr̥,'--for with the divine Savitr̥ he has indeed united; --'with Sūrya he hath shed light,'--for with Sūrya he has indeed shed light.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The significance of this is the same "with the divine Savita; with Sūrya; he has spread light" for he has indeed united with divine Savita and spread the light along with Sūrya.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

सᳫँ᳭ हि दै᳘व्येन सवित्रा᳘ गत सᳫँ᳭ सू᳘र्य्येणारूरुचते᳘ति सᳫँ᳭ हि सू᳘र्य्येणा᳘रूरुचत॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

सᳫं हि दै᳘व्येन सवित्रा᳘गत सᳫं सू᳘र्येणारूरुचते᳘ति सᳫं हि सू᳘र्येणा᳘रूरुचत॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

सं हि दैव्येन सवित्रा गत । **"सं सूर्येणारूरुचत"**- (वा. सं. ३७ । १५) इति । सं हि सूर्येणारूरुचत ॥ ६ ॥
</details>
