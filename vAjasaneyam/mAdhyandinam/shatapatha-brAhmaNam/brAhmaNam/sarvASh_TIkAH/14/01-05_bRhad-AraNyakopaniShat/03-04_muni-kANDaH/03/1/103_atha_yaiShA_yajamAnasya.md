<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

34. And that cow which yielded the Sacrificer's

fast-milk he gives to the Hotr̥;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That cow which was milked to prepare the *Vrata* milk for Yajamāna, he offers to the Hotā.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(त्य᳘) अ᳘थ᳘ यैषा य᳘जमानस्य व्व्रतदु᳘घा॥  
ताᳫँ᳭ हो᳘त्रे ददाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>
