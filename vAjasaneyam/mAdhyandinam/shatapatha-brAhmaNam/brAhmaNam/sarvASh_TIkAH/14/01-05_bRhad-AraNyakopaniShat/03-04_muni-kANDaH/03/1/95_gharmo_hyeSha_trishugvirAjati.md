<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'in me the fitness, in me the intelligence,'--fitness and intelligence he thus secures to himself; --'the Gharma of triple fires shineth,'--for this Gharma of triple fires indeed shines;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"The *Gharma* of the triple fires shines" for this *Gharma* of triple fires is indeed shining.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

घर्म्मो᳘ ह्येष᳘ त्रिशु᳘ग्विरा᳘जति व्विरा᳘जा ज्यो᳘तिषा सहे᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

घर्मोॗ ह्येष᳘ त्रिशु᳘ग्विरा᳘जति विरा᳘जा ज्यो᳘तिषा सहे᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

घर्मो ह्येष त्रिशुग्विराजति । **"विराजा ज्योतिषा सह"**- इति ।
</details>
