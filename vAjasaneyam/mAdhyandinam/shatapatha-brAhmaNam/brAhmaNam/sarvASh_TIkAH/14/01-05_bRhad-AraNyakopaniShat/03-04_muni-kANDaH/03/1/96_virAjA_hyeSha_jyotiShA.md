<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'together with the shining light,'--for together with the shining light (the sun) it indeed is;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Together with the effulgent light" - meaning "Let it shine together with the Sun who is the light." "May it shine together with the fire, the Brahman, it indeed is "The seed of the milk has been brought" - ofcourse it is the seed of the milk that has been brought. "May we obtain the milk therefrom during the coming years." He thereby seeks a boon. They then clean themselves at the *cātvāla* pit and the significance of this is the same as before.
</details>
