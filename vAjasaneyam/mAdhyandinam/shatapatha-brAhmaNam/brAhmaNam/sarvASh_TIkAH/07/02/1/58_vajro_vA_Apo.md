<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

With 'Homage to (the goddess of) Prosperity who hath done this!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Waters are thunderbolt and it is with thunderbolt he demolishes the sinful nirṛti.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

</details>
