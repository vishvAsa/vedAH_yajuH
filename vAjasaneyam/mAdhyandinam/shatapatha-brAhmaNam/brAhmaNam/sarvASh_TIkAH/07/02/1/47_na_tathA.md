<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Let him not do so, but let him lay them down in the direction away from him: he thus drives evil, corruption, away from him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They have to be arranged down in the direction away from him. Then only the evil is driven away from him.
</details>
