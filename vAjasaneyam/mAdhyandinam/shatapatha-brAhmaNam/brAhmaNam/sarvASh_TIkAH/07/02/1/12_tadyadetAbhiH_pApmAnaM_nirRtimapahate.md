<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and because he removes by them evil, corruption (nirr̥ti), therefore these are Nirr̥ti's (bricks).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Therefore, what is sin and nirṛti, is destroyed by these bricks. As the sinful nirṛti is destroyed by them, they are called nairṛtyaḥ.
</details>
