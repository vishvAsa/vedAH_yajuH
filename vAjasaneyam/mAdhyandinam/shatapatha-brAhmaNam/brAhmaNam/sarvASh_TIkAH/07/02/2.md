<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

but that which (is put) into the wings and tail does not benefit either the body or the wings and tail.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

If he were to put food only into the wings and the tail, it would not benefit the body, nor even the wings and the tail.

If it is supplied to the wings and tail, it never nourishes the body or the wings and the tail.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त᳘त्पक्षपुच्छान्य᳘थ य᳘त्पक्षपुच्छे᳘षु᳘ नैव त᳘दात्मा᳘नम᳘वति न᳘ पक्षपुच्छा᳘नि॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

तत्पक्षपुछान्य᳘थ य᳘त्पक्षपुछे᳘षुॗ नैव त᳘दात्मा᳘नमवति न᳘ पक्षपुछा᳘नि॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

तत्पक्षपुच्छानि । अथ यत्पक्षपुच्छेषु- नैव तदात्मानमवति, न पक्षपुच्छानि ॥ ८ ॥
</details>
