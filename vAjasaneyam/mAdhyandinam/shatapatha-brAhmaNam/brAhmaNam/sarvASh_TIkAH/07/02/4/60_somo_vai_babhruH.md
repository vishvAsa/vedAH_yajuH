<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'of the brown ones will I ponder,'--the brown one, doubtless, is Soma, and the herbs are related to Soma, and the Purusha (Prajāpati) is related to herbs [^egg_643];

[^egg_643]: 340:1 Or, consists of herbs.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“I set my mind upon the brown” for, soma is brown and the herbs are related to Soma and Puruṣa is made of herbs. “The hundred powers ”— because he (Puruṣa) lives for hundred years and he has hundredfold merit, hundredfold energy and so he is of hundred powers. “And seven”— he thereby speaks of those seven prāṇas in the head.
</details>
