<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

7. And, again, why he pours out jarfuls of water; --at that time the gods, being about to put him (Agni-Prajāpati) together, in the first place put water into him;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Again, why he pours jarfuls of water is this — once the gods, at the point of fashioning the Agni-altar put water into him, initially.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

</details>

</details>
