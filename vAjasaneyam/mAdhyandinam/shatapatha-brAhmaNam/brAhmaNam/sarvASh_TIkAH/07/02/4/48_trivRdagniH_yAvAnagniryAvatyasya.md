<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He sows (each time) with three-three Ṛk mantras. Because Agni is threefold; as great as Agni is, as great as is his size, with so much he heals him.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>
