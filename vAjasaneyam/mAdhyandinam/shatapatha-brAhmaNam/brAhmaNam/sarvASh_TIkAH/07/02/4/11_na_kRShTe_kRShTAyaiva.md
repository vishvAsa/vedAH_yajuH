<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And were he only to pour it on the unploughed ground, and not on the ploughed, it would only rain for the unploughed land, and not for the ploughed.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

If he were to pour only on the unploughed area (of the altar-site) and not on the ploughed, there will be rain only on uncultivated land and not on the furrowed land.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

न᳘ कृष्टे᳘ ऽकृष्टायैव व्व᳘र्षेन्न᳘ कृष्टा᳘य कृष्टे चा᳘कृष्टे च नि᳘नयति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

न᳘ कृष्टे᳘ कृष्टायैव व᳘र्षेन्न᳘ कृष्टा᳘य कृष्टे चा᳘कृष्टे च नि᳘नयति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

नाकृष्टे, कृष्टायैव वर्षेत्- नाकृष्टाय । अथ यदकृष्ट ऽएव निनयेत्- न कृष्टे, अकृष्टायैव वर्षेत्- न कृष्टाय । कृष्टे चाकृष्टे च निनयति ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

न᳘ कृष्टे᳘ऽकृष्टा᳘यैव व᳘र्षेन्न᳘ कृष्टा᳘य कृष्टेचा᳘कृष्टे च नि᳘नयति
</details>
