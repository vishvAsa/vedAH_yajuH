<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--the song is speech, and yield means food; --'and let the ripe crop go anigh the sickle!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(He says) "With these words of praise of ours, let there be a good harvest"— word means speech and śruṣṭi means crop. (He says) “ Let the ripe crop be within the reach of the sickle.” When the crop gets ripe, people approach it with sickle (for harvesting).
</details>

</details>

</details>

</details>
