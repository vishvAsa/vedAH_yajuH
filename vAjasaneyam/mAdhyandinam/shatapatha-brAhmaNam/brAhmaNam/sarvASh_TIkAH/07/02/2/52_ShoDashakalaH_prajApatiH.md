<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

17. Both kinds (of furrows) amount to sixteen,--of sixteen parts Prajāpati consists, and Prajāpati is Agni: he thus puts into him food proportionate to his body.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Both of them together account for sixteen and Prajāpati has sixteen parts. Prajāpati is Agni and thus he puts into him food proportionate to his body.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

</details>
