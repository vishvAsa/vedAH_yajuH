<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--gloom doubtless means famine: thus, 'we have come to the end of this famine;' --'we have attained the light!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(He says) ”We have reached the light”– for he who attains the gods i.e. sacrifice, indeed reaches light.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

अशनाया वै तमः । अगन्मास्या ऽअशनायायै पारमित्येतत् । **"ज्योतिरापाम"**- इति ।
</details>

</details>
