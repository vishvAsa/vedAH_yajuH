<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And inasmuch as he puts on those (bricks) in this way, he thereby establishes those two (the sun and the fire) in each other, and (accordingly) those two are established in each other; for both of them he now makes out to be the twenty-first, and both

of them are then here [^egg_583], as the Āhavanīya and the Gārhapatya.

[^egg_583]: 309:1 That is to say, they will be here after the completion of the two altars,--the Gārhapatya fire being the Agni proper, and the Āhavanīya fire the sun.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus those two are established in each other by making each of them the twenty-first. Thus both of them are here as the Āhavanīya and the Gārhapatya.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

एतदेव विवृणोति- **तद्यदि**ति । 'तत्' तत्र 'यत्' यस्मात् 'एताः' परिश्रितः इष्टकाश्च 'एवं' प्रत्येकमेकविंशतिसंख्याकाः ‘उपदधाति' 'एतत्' एतेन कारणेन 'एतावेव' अग्न्यादित्यौ 'अन्यो ऽन्यस्मिन्' 'प्रतिष्ठापयति' । अत एवेदानीं 'तौ' 'एतौ' 'अन्यो ऽन्यस्मिन् प्रतिष्ठितौ' दृश्येते । आदित्यो रात्रावग्निं प्रविश्य प्रतितिष्ठति, अग्निश्चोद्यन्तमादित्यं प्रविश्य प्रतिष्ठितो भवति । एतच्चाग्निहोत्रब्राह्मणे प्रतिपादितम् (श. प. २ । ३ । १ । ३) ।
</details>
