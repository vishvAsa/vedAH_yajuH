<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

10. And again why he scatters sand,--sand is nothing else than the seed of Agni Vaiśvānara [^egg_560], and him, Agni Vaiśvānara, he is about to build up; but nothing is fashioned from out of the seedless: 'May he (Agni) be fashioned from out of this seed!'

[^egg_560]: 300:3 This notion is apparently based on the supposed etymological connection of 'śikatā,' sand, with the root 'sic.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Why he scatters the sand—sand is the seed of Agni Vaiśvānara whom he wants to build up here and nothing is produced without the seed.
</details>
