<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'into his belly, craving it,'--for the belly is the centre; --'thousandfold strength, like a swift racer,'--the thousandfold strength, doubtless, is the waters,--'thou, having gained, art exalted, O knower of beings!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Gārhapatya hearth is this world and the soma juice is the waters. Indra thus took up the waters in this world. "With avidity he took it into his belly". For the belly is the centre (where the food taken in gets deposited).
</details>
