<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

11. [He scatters it, with Vāj. S. XII, 46] 'Agni's ashes thou art! Agni's soil thou art!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

While scattering he says, “You are the ashes of Agni; you are the soil of Agni”.
</details>
