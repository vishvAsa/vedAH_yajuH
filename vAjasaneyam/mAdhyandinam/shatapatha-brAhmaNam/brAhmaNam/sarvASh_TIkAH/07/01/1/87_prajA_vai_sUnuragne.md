<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

he therewith invokes a blessing.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"May there be a son to us, the perpetuator of the race". He seeks an offspring and invokes Agni's blessings by saying "O Agni! let that be your boon to us".
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

प्रजा वै᳘ सूनुर᳘ग्ने सा᳘ ते सुमति᳘र्भूत्वस्मे ऽइ᳘त्याशि᳘षमा᳘शास्ते॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

प्रजा वै सूनुः । **"अग्ने सा ते सुमतिर्भूत्वस्मे"**- (वा. सं. १२ । ५१) इति । आशिषाशास्ते ॥ २७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
