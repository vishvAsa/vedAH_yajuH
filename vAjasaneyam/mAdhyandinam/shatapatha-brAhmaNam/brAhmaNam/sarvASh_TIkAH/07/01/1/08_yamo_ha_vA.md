<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--for Yama indeed rules over the settling on this earth, and it is he who grants to this (Sacrificer) a settlement on this earth.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It is Yama indeed who has swayed over settlements on this earth and it is he who grants this place on earth for this sacrificer.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

यमो᳘ ह वा᳘ ऽअस्या ऽअवसा᳘नस्येष्टे स᳘ ऽएवास्मा ऽअस्या᳘मवसा᳘नं[[!!]] ददाति॥
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>

</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

**यमो ह वा** इत्यादि । 'अस्याः' पृथिव्याः सम्बन्धिनः 'अवसानस्य' स्थानस्य 'यमः' खलु 'ईष्टे' अतः 'स एव' 'अस्मै' यजमानाय 'अस्याः' पृथिव्याः 'अवसानं' स्थानं 'ददाति' ॥ ३ ॥
</details>
