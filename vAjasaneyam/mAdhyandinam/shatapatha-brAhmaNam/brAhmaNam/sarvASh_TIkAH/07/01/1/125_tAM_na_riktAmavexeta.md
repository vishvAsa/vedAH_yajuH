<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

40. Let him not look at that (pan) while empty: 'I must not look at the empty one!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

'I must not look at the empty one' so he should think.
</details>

[^१_२२]: रिक्तां नावेक्षेतोखाम् । का० श्रौ० सू० १७ ।२० ।
</details>
