<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

31. These two are his (Agni's) arms,--separately he puts them on, separately he 'settles' them, separately he pronounces the Sūdadohas [^egg_575] verse on them; for separate are these two arms. There are two of them, for there are two arms. He puts them on in the forepart, for these arms are here in front.

[^egg_575]: 307:2 Vāj. S. XII, 55; R̥k S. VIII, 69, 3. 'At his birth the well-like milking, speckled ones mix the Soma (draught), the clans of the gods in the three spheres of the heavens.' This difficult verse has been differently translated by different translators. The Brāhmaṇa itself also gives a very different, doubtless quite fanciful, interpretation of it at VIII, 7, 3, 21.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

These are the two separate arms and always the arms are in pair.
</details>
