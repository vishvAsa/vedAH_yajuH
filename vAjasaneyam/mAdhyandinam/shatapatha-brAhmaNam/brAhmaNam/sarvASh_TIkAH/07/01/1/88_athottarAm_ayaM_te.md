<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

28. Then the northern one, with (Vāj. S. XII, 52; R̥k S. III, 29, 10), 'This is thy natural womb, whence born thou shonest forth,'--that is, 'this (householder's hearth) is thy primeval, perennial womb (birth-place), whence born thou wert enkindled;' --'knowing it, ascend, O Agni, and increase our substance!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he takes the northern one and chants "This is your womb from which you are born and shines forth".
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

अथ पाश्चात्त्ययोरुत्तरस्या इष्टकाया उपधानं विधत्ते- **अथोत्तरामि**ति । उपदधातीति शेषः । मन्त्रस्यायमर्थः,- हे 'अग्ने !' 'ऋत्वियः' ऋतौ भवः सनातनः अयं भूप्रदेशस्तव 'योनिः' उत्पत्तिकारणम् । 'यतः' यस्माद्योनेः 'जात' सन् 'अरोवथाः,' अदीप्यथाः, 'तं' योनिभूतम् देशं 'जानन्' 'आरोह' प्राप्नुहि । अथ अनन्तरम् 'नः' अस्माकं 'रयिं' धनं 'वर्धय' ॥
</details>
