<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

21. That same Anushṭubh, speech, is threefold.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This Anuṣṭubh in the form of speech is of three kinds. This Agni becomes the prāṇa and goes along with the speech. The fire which is on the Āhavanīya altar is the prāṇa breathing out and is the same as the yonder Sun.
</details>

</details>
