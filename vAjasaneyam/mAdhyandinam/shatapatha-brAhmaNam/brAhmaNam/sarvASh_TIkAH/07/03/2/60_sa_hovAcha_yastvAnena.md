<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

15. He (Prajāpati) said, 'Whoever shall seek thee in that form (of a white horse), shall find thee!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Prajāpati said, “You should be accessible to anyone who seeks you in this form (of a white horse).” That is why whoever seeks Agni in this form, to him Agni becomes accessible and after obtaining him, he (the Yajamāna) builds him up.
</details>

</details>
