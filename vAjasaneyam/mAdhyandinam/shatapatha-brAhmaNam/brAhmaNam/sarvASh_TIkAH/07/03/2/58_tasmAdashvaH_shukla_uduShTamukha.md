<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He eyed him, and he (Agni) scorched him: hence the white horse has, as it were, a scorched mouth [^egg_675], and indeed is apt to become weak-eyed.

[^egg_675]: 360:2 That is, according to Sāyaṇa, a reddish mouth.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Agni felt, ‘I have perhaps caused harm to Prajāpati’.
</details>
