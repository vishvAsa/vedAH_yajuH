<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

14. And, again, why he makes it smell; Agni went away from the gods; he entered the water.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And again why is he made to smell — Agni bolted from the gods and entered the waters. The gods asked Prajāpati to search for him saying, “To you who are his own father, he will reveal himself.” Assuming the form of a white horse, Prajāpati went in search of Agni.
</details>

</details>
