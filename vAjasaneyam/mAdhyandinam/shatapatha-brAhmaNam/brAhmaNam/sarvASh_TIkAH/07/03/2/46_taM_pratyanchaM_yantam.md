<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

12. Whilst it goes westward he makes it smell (kiss) that layer (of bricks); --that horse is yonder sun, and those bricks are the same as all these creatures (on earth): thus even as he makes (the horse) smell, so yon sun kisses these creatures [^egg_673].

[^egg_673]: 359:1 According to Sāyaṇa, it is by his rays (identified with the vital airs of living beings) that the sun kisses (or puts himself in contact with) the creatures (and animates them); so that every one feels that he is 'labdhātmaka,' or has obtained 'a self,' or life and being.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

तस्याश्वस्य प्रत्यङ्मुखनयनसमये चितेरवघ्रापणं विधत्ते [^१_११४]- **तं प्रत्यञ्चमि**ति । पुच्छस्य दक्षिणतो निहिताम् 'एताम्' 'चितिम्' 'प्रत्यञ्चम्' प्रत्यङ्मुखम् 'यन्तम्' गच्छन्तमश्वम् 'अवघ्रापयति' यथा सो ऽश्वश्चितिम् अभिजिघ्रति तथा तं प्रेरयेदित्यर्थः । "घ्रा गन्धोपादाने" (धा. पा. भ्वा. प. ९५१) इत्यस्माण्णिचि "अर्तिह्नी" (पा. सू. ७ । ३ । ३६) इत्यादिना पुक् ॥

[^१_११४]: प्रत्यंचमागतं चितिमवघ्राप्य । का. श्रौ. सू. १७ । ७० ।
</details>
