<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Having brought that (clod), he puts it on the body (of the altar) on the middle of the junction of the (left) wing (and the body): he thereby puts into him (Agni) what vital sap flowed away in the northerly direction; and also the northern region itself he bestows upon him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus, he restores to him that vital sap which flowed out towards the northern direction. Thereby he brings the north- ern direction itself under his sway.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

स य ऽउ᳘दीच्यां दिशि र᳘सो ऽत्य᳘क्षरत्त᳘मस्मिन्नेत᳘द्दधात्य᳘थो ऽउ᳘दीचीमे᳘वास्मिन्नेतद्दि᳘शं दधाति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
