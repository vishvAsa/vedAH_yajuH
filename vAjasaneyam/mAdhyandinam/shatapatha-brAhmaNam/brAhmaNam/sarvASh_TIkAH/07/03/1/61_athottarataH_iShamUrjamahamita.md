<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

23. Then from the north, with (Vāj. S. XII, 105), 'Sap and strength have I taken from here [^egg_653],'--that is, 'Sap and strength I take from here;'

[^egg_653]: 347:2 Mahīdhara takes 'ādam' here as the regular imperfect of 'ad,' I ate.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then from the northern side (he brings a logeṣṭakā saying) “I have taken (eaten) this sap and strength from hither.” It means ‘I have collected sap and strength from here.’
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

'आदम्'- इति क्रियापदस्यार्थमाह- **आदद इत्येतदि**ति ।
