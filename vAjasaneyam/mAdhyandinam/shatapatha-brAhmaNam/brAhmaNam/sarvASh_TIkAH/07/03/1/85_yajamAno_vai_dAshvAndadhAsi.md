<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

worship doubtless is the Sacrificer: thus, 'Upon the worship thou bestowest, O sage!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"O wise one ! You bestow upon the one who worships you"-here the one who worships is the Yajamāna and he means, 'you bestow (food) on the Yajamāna, wise as you are.
</details>
