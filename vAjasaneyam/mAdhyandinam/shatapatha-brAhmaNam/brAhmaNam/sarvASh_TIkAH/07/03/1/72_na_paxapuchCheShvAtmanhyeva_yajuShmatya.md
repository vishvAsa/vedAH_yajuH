<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for bricks having special prayers are placed on the body, not on the wings or tail.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

These bricks are Yajuṣmatyaḥ (having individual Yajus formula for each) which he places in the body itself and not in the wings or the tail because those with Yajus formula are meant only for the body proper and not for the wings or tail.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

न᳘ पक्षपुच्छे᳘ष्वात्म᳘न्ह्येव य᳘जुष्मत्य ऽइ᳘ष्टका ऽउपधीय᳘न्ते न᳘ पक्षपुच्छे᳘षु॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

न᳘ पक्षपुछे᳘ष्वात्मॗन्ह्येव य᳘जुष्मत्य इ᳘ष्टका उपधीय᳘न्ते न᳘ पक्षपुछे᳘षु॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

न पक्षपुच्छेषु । आत्मन्ह्येव यजुष्मत्य इष्टका उपधीयन्ते, न पक्षपुच्छेषु ॥ २५ ॥
</details>
