<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for the Gārhapatya is this (terrestrial) world, and saline soil means cattle: he thus bestows cattle on this world, whence there are cattle in this world.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Gārhapatya represents this world and salt stands for cattle. Bestowing cattle (salt) on this earth he endows the world with cattle.
</details>
