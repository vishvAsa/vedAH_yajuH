<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He places it on the mortar,--the mortar is the air, and everything that is above this

earth is air;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This way, he places the womb in that (altar). He places it (ukhā) upon the mortar, because mortar is the aerial region.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

उलूखलमुसलोपधानानन्तरमुखाया उपधानं विधत्ते- **अथे**ति- (का. श्रौ. सू. १७ । १०३) ।

इष्टकाचितिषु निधास्यमानस्याग्नेरुखायां कृत्वोत्पादनादुखायाः योनित्वेन तदुपधाने योनेरेवोपधानं भवतीत्याह- **योनिर्वा** इति ।

विहितमुपधानमुलूखले कर्तव्यमित्याह- **तामि**ति ।
