<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Lord of the waters; the bull among bricks” – for, indeed the tortoise is the lord of the waters and the bull (best) among the bricks. "Endowing cattle unto the Yajamāna (covering the cattle in the form of bricks) go thee to that abode to which those with meritorious deeds have gone”– (meaning) 'you go to that abode to which others (other tortoises earlier laid down in this manner on the altar) have gone’ i.e. you go there where your predecessors have gone by this performance.
</details>
