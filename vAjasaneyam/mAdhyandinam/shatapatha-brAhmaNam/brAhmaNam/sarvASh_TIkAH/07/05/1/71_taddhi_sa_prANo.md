<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Having become the great Prajāpati” – for, he indeed became great when those gods entered him.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

तद्धि स᳘ प्राणो᳘ ऽभवन्महा᳘न्भूत्वा᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिरि᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
