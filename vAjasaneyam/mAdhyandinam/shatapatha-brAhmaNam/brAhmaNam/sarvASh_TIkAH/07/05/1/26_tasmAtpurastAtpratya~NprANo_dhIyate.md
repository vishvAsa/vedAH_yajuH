<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He puts it down in front looking towards the back: he thus puts in the breath in front tending towards the back; whence the breath is taken in from the front backwards. [He puts it down so as to be] turned towards the (gold) man: he thus puts breath into the Sacrificer.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He places it to the right of the aṣāḍhā brick, for, the tortoise is prāṇa and prāṇa is the male partner of speech (the female).
</details>

</details>
