<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

This tortoise is that life-sap of these worlds which flowed away from them when plunged into the waters [^egg_720]: that (life-sap) he now bestows on (Agni).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This tortoise is that life- sap of these worlds which oozed out from them when they got drowned in the waters. That very sap he now lays down (on Agni-altar).
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

उक्तमेव कूर्मस्य रसत्वं प्रतिपादयति- **यो वै स** इति । 'एषाम्' पृथिव्यादीनां 'लोकानाम्' 'अप्सु' 'प्रविद्धानाम्' मग्नानां 'सः' प्रसिद्धो 'यो' 'रसः' 'पराङ्' अनावृत्तः 'अत्यक्षरत्' अस्रवत् । 'सः' रसः 'एषः' इदानीमस्माभिरुच्यमानः 'कूर्मः' अतः 'तमेव' रसम् एतेन कूर्मोपधानेनोपहितवान् भवति ।
</details>
