<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

The left (north) side of that (Agni or altar) is more raised,--that Agni is an animal, and hence the left side of the belly of a well-filled beast is more raised (than the right side).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This Agni-altar is paśu(animal). That is why, the left side of the well-fed animal's belly is bulging (raised than the right side). (Second Brāhmaṇa Ends.) (Chapter Four Ends.)
</details>
