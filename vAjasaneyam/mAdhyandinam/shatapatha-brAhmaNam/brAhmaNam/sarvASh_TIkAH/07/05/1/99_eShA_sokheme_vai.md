<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--that same Prajāpati who became disjointed doubtless is this same fire-pan, for the fire-pan is these worlds, and Prajāpati is these worlds. He places it on the mortar: he thereby establishes him (Prajāpati) in all that--breath, food, strength;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For, ukhā is these worlds and Prajāpati is these worlds. He places her (ukhā) on the mortar.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

एषा᳘ सो᳘खेमे वै᳘ लोका᳘ ऽउ᳘खेमे᳘ लोकाः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिस्ता᳘मुलू᳘खल ऽउ᳘पदधाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

एषाॗ सोॗखेमे वै᳘ लोका उॗखेमे᳘ लोकाः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिस्ता᳘मुलू᳘खल उ᳘पदधाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

एषा सोखा । इमे वै लोका उखा । इमे लोकाः प्रजापतिः । तामुलूखल ऽउपदधाति ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>
