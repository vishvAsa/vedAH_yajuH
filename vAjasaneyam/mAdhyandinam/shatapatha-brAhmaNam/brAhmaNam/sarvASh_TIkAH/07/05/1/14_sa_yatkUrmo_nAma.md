<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

5. And as to its being called 'kūrma' (tortoise); Prajāpati, having assumed that form, created living beings. Now what he created, he made;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And why it is named kūrma is this – Prajāpati, after having assumed that form, created living beings. When he created, he actually did(akarot) and inasmuch as he did (kṛ), he is called kūrma and it is same as Kāśyapa.
</details>

</details>
