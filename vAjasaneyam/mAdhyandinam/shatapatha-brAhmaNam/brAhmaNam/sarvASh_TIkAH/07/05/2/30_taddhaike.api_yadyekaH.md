<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

10. Now, even if there is only one victim [^egg_740], some people thrust five times seven (into that one head), thinking, 'Those five victims he puts down (symbolically), and there are seven vital airs in each victim: thus we put the vital airs into all of them.'

[^egg_740]: 403:1 Viz. a he-goat, as the animal sacrifice to either Prajāpati, or (Vāyu) Niyutvat; see pp. 178, 184.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Even if there is only one paśu, some people insert five times seven pieces into it, thinking that five paśus are (in effect) put in and each is to have seven pieces so that all of them may be endowed with prāṇa.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

**तद्धैक** इति । यदि 'एकः' अपि 'पशुर्भवति' तदापि 'पञ्चकृत्वः' एव 'सप्त-सप्त' सुवर्णशकलान् प्रत्यस्येदिति 'एके' शाखिन आहुः । तथा वदतां च तेषामयमाशयः- पञ्चानामपि पशूनां स्थाने एकस्योपधानादसावध्वर्युः 'पञ्च' खलु 'एतान्' 'पशूनुपदधाति' । तथा च 'एकैकस्मिन्पशौ' 'सप्तसप्त' 'प्राणाः' विद्यन्ते, तस्मात्तावतां हिरण्यशकलानां प्रत्यसनेनैतेषु 'सर्वेषु' अपि 'प्राणान् दधाति' इति ।
</details>
