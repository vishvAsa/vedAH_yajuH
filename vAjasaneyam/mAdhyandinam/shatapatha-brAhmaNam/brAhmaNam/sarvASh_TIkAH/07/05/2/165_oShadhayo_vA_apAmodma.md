<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

the swell of the waters is the plants, for wherever the waters keep swelling there plants grow: in the plants he settles this (brick).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The plants are the upsurge of waters, because wherever there is upsurge of waters, there the plants grow. So he places this brick in the plants.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(त्यो᳘) ओ᳘षधयो वा᳘ ऽअपामो᳘द्म य᳘त्र ह्या᳘प ऽउन्द᳘न्त्यस्ति᳘ष्ठन्ति तदो᳘षधयो जायन्त ऽओ᳘षधिषु ता᳘ᳫँ᳘ सादयति॥
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
