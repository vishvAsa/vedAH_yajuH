<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

the two-footed animal doubtless is the same as man: thus, 'do not harm that one!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

it is the man's, he first dispels by saying, "Do not hurt this two-footed animal".
</details>
