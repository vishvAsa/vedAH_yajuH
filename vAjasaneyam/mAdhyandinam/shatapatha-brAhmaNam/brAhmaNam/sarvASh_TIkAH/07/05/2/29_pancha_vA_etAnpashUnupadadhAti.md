<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for those five victims he puts on (the fire-pan), and there are seven vital

airs in each victim: he thus puts the vital airs into all of them.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And if there are five paśus let him insert seven pieces five times, because he is placing those five paśus (in the ukhā) and each of them has seven prāṇās. Thereby he puts vital airs in all of them.

Let him not do so.
</details>

</details>
