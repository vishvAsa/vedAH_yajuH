<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

35. Then that of the sheep, with (Vāj. S. XIII, 50), This woollen--,' that is, 'this woolly,'--'navel of Varuṇa,' for the sheep is sacred to Varuṇa;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he dispels the pain of the sheep, saying, "This woollen,” which means the sheep having wooly body.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(रि) इम᳘मूर्णायुमि᳘त्यूर्णाबलमि᳘त्येतद्व᳘रुणस्य ना᳘भिमि᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

अथा᳘वेः॥  
इम᳘मूर्णायुमि᳘त्यूर्णावलिमि᳘त्येतद्व᳘रुणस्य ना᳘भिमि᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

अथावेः । **“इममूर्णायुम्"**- इति । ऊर्णाबलमित्येतत् । **"वरुणस्य नाभिम्"**- इति ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
