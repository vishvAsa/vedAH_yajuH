<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“O Agni! kindly accept the pseudo-man as food"— a kimpuruṣa is a pseudo-man and thus he pleads 'kindly accept a pseudo-man as food.' "With that you build up your forms and get established” – meaning 'get your form built up with that food and get established'. The form is the body and he only says 'thus get yourself built up and be established.” “Let your burning heat reach the kimpuruṣa; let the burning heat reach him whom we hate”— thereby lays the burning heat into the pseudo-man and into the man whom he hates.
</details>
