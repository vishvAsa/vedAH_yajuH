<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

15. The horse and ram on the left (north) side: he thereby puts those two (kinds of) cattle in that region;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then places the horse and the ram on the north side and thereby puts those two cattle in that region.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(त्ता᳘ ऽश्वं) अ᳘श्वं चा᳘विं चोत्तरतः᳘॥  
(ऽ) एत᳘स्यां त᳘द्दि᳘श्येतौ᳘ पशू᳘ दधाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

अ᳘श्वं चा᳘विं चोत्तरत᳟॥  
एत᳘स्यां त᳘द्दिॗश्येतौ᳘ पशू दधाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

अश्वं चाविं चोत्तरतः। एतस्यां तद् दिश्येतौ पशू दधाति ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
