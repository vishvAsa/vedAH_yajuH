<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

57. 'In the home of the waters I settle thee!' the home of the waters is the air: in the air he settles this one.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"I place you in the region of the waters" – the region of waters is indeed the aerial region. So he places it in the aerial region.
</details>
