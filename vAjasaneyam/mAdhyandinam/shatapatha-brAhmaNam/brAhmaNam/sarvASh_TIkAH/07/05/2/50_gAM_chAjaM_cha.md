<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

16. The bull and he-goat on the right (south) side: he thereby puts those two (kinds of) cattle in that region;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He places the head of the bull and of the he-goat on the southern side.
</details>

</details>
