<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'(thou) the thousand-eyed, being built for pith;' --the thousand-eyed he (Agni) is on account of the chips of gold; 'for pith,' that is, 'for food.'--'Graciously accept thou, O Agni, the sham-man, the victim, as pith!' a sham-man is a kim-purusha (mock-man) [^egg_757]: thus, 'accept graciously the kim-purusha, O Agni!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The two-footed animal is indeed man and he only says 'do not hurt man'.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

द्विपाद्वा᳘ ऽएष᳘ पशुर्यत्पु᳘रुषस्तं मा᳘ हिᳫँ᳭सीरि᳘त्येत᳘त्सहस्राक्षो मे᳘धाय चीय᳘मान ऽइ᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

द्विपाद्वा᳘ एष᳘ पशुर्यत्पु᳘रुषस्तम् मा᳘ हिंसीरि᳘त्येत᳘त्सहस्राक्षो मे᳘धाय चीय᳘मान इ᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

द्विपाद्वा ऽएष पशुर्यत्पुरुषः । तं मा हिंसीरित्येतत् । **"सहस्राक्षो मेधाय चीयमानः"**- इति ।
</details>
