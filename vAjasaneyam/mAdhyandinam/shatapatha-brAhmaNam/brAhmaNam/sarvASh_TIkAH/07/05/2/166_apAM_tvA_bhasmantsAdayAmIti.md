<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

48. 'In the ashes of the waters I settle thee!' the ashes of the waters are the foam: in foam he settles this one.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"I place you in the ashes of waters”-(so saying, he places the third apasyā). The cloud is indeed the ash of waters. In the cloud, he places this brick.
</details>
