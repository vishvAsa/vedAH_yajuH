<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'the skin of animals, two-footed and four-footed,' for that (sheep) indeed is the skin of both kinds of animals [^egg_760], two-footed and four-footed; --'the first birth-place of Tvashṭr̥'s creatures,' for Tvashṭr̥ indeed fashioned this as the first form; --'harm not, O Agni, in the highest region!'

[^egg_760]: 411:1 Viz. inasmuch as its wool serves as a cover for man and beast.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

'Navel of Varuṇa'— the sheep is sacred to Varuṇa.
</details>
