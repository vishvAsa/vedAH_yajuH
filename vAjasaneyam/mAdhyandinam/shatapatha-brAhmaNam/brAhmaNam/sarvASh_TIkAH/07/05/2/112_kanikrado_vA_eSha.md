<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

the one-hoofed animal doubtless is the same as the horse: thus, do not harm that one!--'the racer neighing among the racers;' for neighing indeed he is, and a racer among racers; --'The wild fallow (beast) do I assign unto thee,' he thereby assigns to him the wild fallow (beast) [^egg_759]; --'building up therewith thy forms, get thee settled!'

[^egg_759]: 410:2 Thus Mahīdhara (gauravarṇaṁ mr̥gam). In the St. Petersburg dictionary 'gaura' is taken here in the sense of 'buffalo, bos gavæus.' The parallelism in the next two formulas might indeed seem to point to that meaning.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"The swift-runner, neighing among the runners" for, he is indeed neighing and is a runner among the runners. "The wild beast I do assign to you” – he thereby assigns to him the wild beast. "With that you build up your forms and get established,” which means 'with that, fashion your body and get settled.'
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

**कनिक्रदो वा एष** इति । 'एषः' अश्वः 'कनिक्रदः' अत्यर्थं क्रन्दिता, 'वाजिनेषु' वेगवत्सु 'वाजी' अत्यर्थं वेगयुक्तः । 'गौरम्' [^१_२०७] सिंहम् । स्पष्टार्थमन्यत् ॥ ३३ ॥

[^१_२०७]: महीधरस्तु 'गौरं' गौरवर्णं मृगमिति व्याचख्यौ ।
</details>
