<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

51. 'In the flood, the seat, I settle thee!' the flood is the breath: in the breath he settles this one.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"In the flood, the home, I place you " - the flood is prāṇa and in the prāṇa, he lays it down.
</details>
