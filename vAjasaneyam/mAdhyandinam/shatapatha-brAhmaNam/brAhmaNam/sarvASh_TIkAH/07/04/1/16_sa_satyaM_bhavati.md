<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and he (himself) becomes the truth; and truth turns unto him; and true becomes that wish of his for which he performs this rite.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That makes truth its mouth and he becomes truth and truth gets well-disposed to him. With what wish he does this act, it becomes true.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

स᳘ सत्यं᳘ भवति सत्य᳘मेनम᳘नुवर्त्तते स᳘त्यो ऽस्य स का᳘मो भवति य᳘त्काम ऽएत᳘त्कुरुते॥
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
