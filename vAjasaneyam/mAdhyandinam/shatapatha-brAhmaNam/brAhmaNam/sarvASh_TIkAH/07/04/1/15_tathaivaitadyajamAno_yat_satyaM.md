<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

5. And in like manner when the Sacrificer now, at the outset, sings the 'true hymn,' he thereby makes the truth his (Agni's) mouth;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

In the same manner, this Yajamāna sings the Satyam Sāman at the outset.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(नो) य᳘त्सत्यᳫँ᳭ सा᳘म पुर᳘स्ताद्गा᳘यति त᳘दस्य सत्यं मु᳘खं करोति
</details>

</details>

</details>
