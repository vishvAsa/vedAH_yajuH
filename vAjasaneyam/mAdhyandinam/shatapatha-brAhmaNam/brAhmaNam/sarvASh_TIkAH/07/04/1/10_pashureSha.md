<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Having taken Agni

into his own self whilst standing, he builds him up sitting; --Agni is an animal: hence the animal, having received the foetus standing, gives birth after lying down.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This altar is an animal. Therefore the animal gets impregnated while standing and gives birth (to the calf) without sitting (lying).
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

एतदुभयं स्तौति- **पशुरेष** इति । 'पशु-' रूपो हि 'एषः' 'अग्निः', 'तस्माद्' एव हि कारणाद्भवादिलक्षणः 'पशुस्तिष्ठन्' एव 'गर्भं' धत्ते 'अनूपविश्य विजायते' प्रसूते । अतो ऽग्निग्रहणं गर्भधारणभूतत्वात्तिष्ठतैव कार्यम्, चयनं तु प्रसवरूपत्वादुपविष्टेनेति तात्पर्यम् ॥ २ ॥
</details>
