<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and whatever forms, whatever stomas, whatever pr̥shṭḥas, whatever metres he will be applying to that fire-altar, that will be the perfection, that will be the growth of those two: let him therefore make arms to that (gold) man.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Because these sruks are only representing the arms. The two arms which are besides these, are the wings. Whatever forms, whatever stomas, pṛṣṭhas and metres happen to be associated with this Agni-altar, are only innovations, of these two; developments of these two. Therefore this goldman should be provided with this pair of arms. (Fourth Brāhmaṇa Ends.) (Chapter Three Ends.)
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

यान्त्स्तो᳘मान्या᳘नि पृष्ठा᳘नि या᳘नि च्छ᳘न्दाᳫँ᳭स्येत᳘योरेव सा सं᳘स्कृतिरेत᳘योर्व्वृ᳘द्धिस्त᳘स्मादु कुर्या᳘दे᳘वैत᳘स्य पु᳘रुषस्य बाहू[[!!]]॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

यान् स्तोमान्, यानि पृष्ठानि, यानि च्छन्दांसि- एतयोरेव सा संस्कृतिः, एतयोर्वृद्धिः । तस्मादु कुर्यादेव- एतस्य पुरुषस्य बाहू ॥ ४५ ॥
</details>
