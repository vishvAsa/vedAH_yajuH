<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

33. And, again, why he offers thereon.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Why he makes this offering – the gods having placed that body of theirs, became worried that the terrible Rakṣasas may attack this body (of theirs).
</details>
