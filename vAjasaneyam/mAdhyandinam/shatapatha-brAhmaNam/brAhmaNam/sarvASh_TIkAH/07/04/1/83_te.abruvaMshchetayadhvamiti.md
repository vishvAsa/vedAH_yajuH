<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

They said, 'Meditate ye (cetay)!' whereby, doubtless, they meant to say, 'Seek ye to build up (citim ish)! seek ye how we shall put vigour into this man!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They said, "Meditate”, whereby they meant 'intend to build up' and they intended as to how we may put vigour into this man.
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

एतद्वाक्यं श्रुतिः स्वयं व्याचष्टे- **चितिमिच्छते**ति । चितिरत्र ज्ञानविशेषः ।

किमर्थं चेतयध्वमिति प्रार्थ्यत इत्याशङ्क्य तस्य प्रयोजनमुक्तम्- **तदिच्छते**ति । 'यथा' खलु 'अस्मिन्' शुष्कफलकवन्नीरसे 'पुरुषे' 'वीर्यम्' स्थापयाम तमुपायमिच्छतेत्यर्थः ॥ २३ ॥
</details>
