<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

41. The kārshmarya one he lays down on the right side, with (Vāj. S. XIII, 13), 'By Agni's fiery spirit I settle thee!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He places the kārṣmarya sruk on the right side (saying), “I lay you down with the valour of Agni.” Thereby he restores that valour of Prajāpati which Agni snatched away from him and moved to the south.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

इत्थं कार्ष्मर्योदुम्बरयोरुत्पत्तिमग्नेरिन्द्रस्य च बाहुरूपतां च प्रतिपाद्य तदुपजीवनेनाग्नेयमन्त्रेण कार्ष्मर्यमय्या उपधानं विधत्ते- **स कार्ष्मर्यमयीमि**ति 'यत्तेजः' 'आदाय' अग्निः निरगात् तेनाग्नेः सम्बन्धिना प्रजापतिशरीरतेजसा त्वां सादयामीति मन्त्रार्थः ।
</details>
