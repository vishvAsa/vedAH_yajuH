<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

43. Indra and Agni indeed are those two arms of his (Prajāpati's): they join him with fiery spirit and vigour.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

These two; Indra and Agni are his (Prajāpati’s arms). They join him with valour and lustre.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

**तावस्यैतावि**त्यादि । 'तौ' तथाविधौ 'एतौ' 'इन्द्राग्नी' 'एव' 'अस्य' प्रजापतेः 'बाहू' अभूताम् । भूत्वा च 'तौ' 'तेजसा' अग्निसम्बन्धिना प्रकाशेनेन्द्रसम्बन्धिना 'वीर्येण' च 'सह प्रपद्येते' प्राप्नुतः ।
</details>
