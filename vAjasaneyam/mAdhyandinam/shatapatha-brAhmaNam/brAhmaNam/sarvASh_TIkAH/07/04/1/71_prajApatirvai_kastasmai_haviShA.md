<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Ka (Who?) is Prajāpati: thus, 'let us do homage to Him by offering!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Let us worship with offerings unto the god 'Ka'- Prajāpati foresooth is Ka and unto him we may pay homage with the offerings."
</details>
