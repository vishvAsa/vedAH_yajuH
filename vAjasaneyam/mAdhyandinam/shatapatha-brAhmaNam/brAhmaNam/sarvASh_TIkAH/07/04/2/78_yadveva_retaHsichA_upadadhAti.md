<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

24. And, again, why he lays down two seed-shedders; the seed-shedders are the testicles, for only he who has testicles sheds seed.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Again why he lays down the retassics is because the retassics are the testicles; for, only he with testicles sheds seed.
</details>
