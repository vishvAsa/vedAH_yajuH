<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'on the back of the waters, on the way of the ocean,' the back of the waters doubtless is this earth, and the way of the ocean is this earth; --'thee, the wide, the broad one!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(He says) “On the back of the waters and on the way of ocean” – the back of the waters is indeed the earth and that is the way of the ocean.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

अपा᳘ᳫँ᳘ हीयं᳘ पृष्ठ᳘ᳫँ᳘ समुद्र᳘स्य᳘ हीयमे᳘मा व्व्य᳘चस्वतीं प्र᳘थस्वतीमि᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

अपाॗᳫंॗ हीय᳘म् पृष्ठ᳘ᳫं᳘ समुद्र᳘स्यॗ हीयमे᳘म व्य᳘चस्वतीम् प्र᳘थस्वतीमि᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

अपां हीयं पृष्ठम् । समुद्रस्य हीयमेमा । **"व्यचस्वतीं प्रथस्वतीम्"**- इति ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

अपाꣳ हीयं᳘ पृष्ठ᳘ꣳ᳘ समुद्र᳘स्य᳘ हीयमे᳘मा व्य᳘चस्वतीं प्र᳘थस्वतीमि᳘ति
</details>
