<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

17. Here now they say, 'If (the dviyajus) is that same Sacrificer who is that gold man, which then is that (real) form of his?'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they raise the question -"If this dviyajus is the same as Yajamāna, what becomes of that goldman (who was also identified with the Yajamāna); which of these (two) is his real form?" Well, that (form of goldman) is his divine form and this (brick) is his human one (body).
</details>
