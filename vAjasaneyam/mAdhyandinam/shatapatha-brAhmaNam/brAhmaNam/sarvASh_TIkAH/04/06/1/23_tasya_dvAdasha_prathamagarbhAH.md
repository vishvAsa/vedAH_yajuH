<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

11. Twelve heifers pregnant with their first calf are the priests' fee for this (graha).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Twelve young cows, pregnant for the first time are the dakṣiṇā (for the priest for this graha).
</details>
