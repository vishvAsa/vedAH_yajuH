<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Because the Puroruk is indeed Manliness-lest he should bestow manliness on women—therefore he draws it without a Puroruk.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

व्वीर्यं᳘ वै᳘ पुरोरुङ्ने᳘त्स्त्रीषु᳘ व्वीर्यं᳘ द᳘धानी᳘ति त᳘स्मादपुरोरु᳘क्कं गृह्णाति॥
</details>
