<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

whereby he puts flavour (juice) into the whole sacrifice, imbues it with flavour.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus he infuses juice (rasa) in the whole sacrifice. The entire sacrifice, he endows with flavour.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

स᳘र्व्वस्मिन्ने᳘वैत᳘द्यज्ञे र᳘सं दधाति स᳘र्व्वं यज्ञᳫं᳭ र᳘सेन प्र᳘सजति॥
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

सर्वस्मिन्नेवैतद् यज्ञे रसं दधाति । सर्वं यज्ञं रसेन प्रसजति ॥ १३ ॥
</details>

</details>

</details>
