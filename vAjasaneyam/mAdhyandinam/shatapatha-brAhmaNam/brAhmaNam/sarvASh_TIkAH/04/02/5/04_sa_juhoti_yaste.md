<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

2. He offers with (Vāj. S. VII, 26; Rig-veda X, 17, 12), 'Whatever drop of thine leapeth

away, whatever stalk of thine,'--whatever particle (of Soma) is spilt, that is a drop, that indeed he means;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He offers (saying) "Whatever particle of thine spills; whatever stalk (of Soma plant gets scattered) "—whatever drop of Soma is spilt, that is a particle;
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

स᳘ जुहोति॥  
य᳘स्ते द्रप्स स्क᳘न्दति य᳘स्ते ऽअᳫँ᳭शुरि᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

य᳘स्ते द्रप्स स्क᳘न्दति य᳘स्ते अंशुरि᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

स जुहोति- **"यस्ते द्रप्स स्कन्दति यस्ते ऽअंशुः"**- (वा. सं. ७ । २६) इति ।
</details>
