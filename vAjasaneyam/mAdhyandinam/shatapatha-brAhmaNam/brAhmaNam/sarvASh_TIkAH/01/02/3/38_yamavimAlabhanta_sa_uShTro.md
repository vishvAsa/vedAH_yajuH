<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

The sheep which they had sacrificed, became a camel. The goat which they had sacrificed, became a śarabha [^egg_180].

[^egg_180]: 52:1 A fabulous kind of deer with eight legs, which was supposed to kill elephants and lions.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The goat which they had offered, that became a Sarabha, (a strange animal with eight legs, capable of killing even the lion).
</details>
