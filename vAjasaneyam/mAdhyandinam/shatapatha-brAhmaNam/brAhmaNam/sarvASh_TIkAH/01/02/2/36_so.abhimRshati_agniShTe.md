<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

12. He touches it over, with the text (Vāj. S. I, 22): 'May the fire not injure thy skin!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He touches it, saying, “May Agni not hurt your skin?” For he is going to heat it (thus) now, (he is) going to cook (it).
</details>
