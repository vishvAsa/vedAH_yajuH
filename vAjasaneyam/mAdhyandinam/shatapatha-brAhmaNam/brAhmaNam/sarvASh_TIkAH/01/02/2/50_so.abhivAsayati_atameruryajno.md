<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

17. He covers it over, with the text (Vāj. S. I, 23): 'May the sacrifice not be liable to languish, nor the sacrificer's race liable to languish!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He covers it (thinking), “May the sacrifice not be (prone to be) languied and may the progeny (offspring) of the sacrificer not be (prone to be) languied”.
</details>
