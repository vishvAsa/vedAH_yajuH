<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

2. They thereupon said: 'Well then, let us divide this world between us; and having divided it, let us subsist thereon!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They said, "Well, let us divide (share) this earth. Having divided it, let us subsist on it".
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

ते᳘ होचुः॥  
(र्ह᳘) ह᳘न्तेमां᳘ पृथिवीं᳘ विभ᳘जामहै तां᳘ व्विभज्यो᳘पजीवामे᳘ति
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
