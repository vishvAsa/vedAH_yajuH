<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

13. He then covers them over with (hot) coals, whilst muttering the text (Vāj. S. I, 18 f): 'May ye be heated with the heat of the Bhr̥gus and

 Aṅgiras [^egg_151]!'

[^egg_151]: 38:1 The old families of the Bhr̥gus and Aṅgiras are frequently mentioned together, and often also in conjunction with the Atharvans: it is indeed to these three families that the native authorities attribute the texts and ritual of the Atharva-veda, or fourth Veda, which is generally referred to in the later Vedic writings under the designation Atharvāṅgirasas. It is probable that the Bhr̥gu-Aṅgiras in the above formula of the Vājas. Saṁhitā are intended as equivalent to the latter term. Cf. Weber, Omina et Portenta, p. 346.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He places cinders on them (chanting), “Be heated with the heat of the Bhṛgus and Angiras”. This is really the brightest brilliance, what belongs to the Bhṛgus and Angiras. (He covers them with cinders), so that they might be well heated.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

उपहितेषु कपालेष्वङ्गाराध्यूहन विधत्ते [^१_४९]- **अथे**ति ।

मन्त्रे भृगूणामङ्गिरसामिति तपसो यद् विशेषणं तद् व्याचष्ठे- **एतद्वा** इति । यद् ‘भृग्वङ्गिरसाम्‘ महर्षीणां तपोविशेषजनितं तेजः, ‘एतत्‘ खल्वग्न्यादितेजोन्तरात् ‘तेजिष्ठम्‘ तेजस्वितमम्, अतिशयेन दीप्तम् । तेजस्विशब्दादिष्ठनि ”विन्मतोर्लुक्” (पा० सू० ५. ३. ६५) इति लुक् ।

[^१_४९]: ”भृगूणामित्यङ्गारैरभ्यूहति” -श्रौ० सू० २. १२१ ।
</details>
