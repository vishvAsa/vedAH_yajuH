<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And in this way alone a regular copulation can take place, since the woman lies on the left (or north) side of the man.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus indeed is a pair arranged; for, to the north (left) of the male does the female lie down near. Therefore he places (the water pot) to the north.
</details>
