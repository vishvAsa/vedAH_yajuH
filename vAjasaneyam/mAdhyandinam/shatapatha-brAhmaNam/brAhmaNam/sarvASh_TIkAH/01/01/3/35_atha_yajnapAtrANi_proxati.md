<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

12. He then sprinkles the sacrificial vessels [^egg_119],

with the text (Vāj. S. I, 13 g), 'Be ye pure for the divine work, for the sacrifice to the gods!'

[^egg_119]: 22:3 According to some authorities the vessels are placed together  on one heap, and are then consecrated together by one sprinkling. According to others, each vessel must be consecrated separately. Kāty. Śr. II, 3, 39.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he sprinkles the (sacrificial) vessels, (chanting), “Become pure for the ritual relating to the gods, for the sacrifice to the gods. They become pure, indeed, for work related to the gods, for the sacrifice to the gods”.
</details>
