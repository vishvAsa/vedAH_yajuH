<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

The fifteen-versed chant [^egg_267], doubtless, is the thunderbolt,

and the thunderbolt means strength; so that he thereby converts the sāmidhenīs into strength: hence, if he should hate any one, he may crush him with his great toes [^egg_268] at the time when those (verses) are recited.

[^egg_267]: 96:3 The pañcadaśa-stoma, or form of recitation in fifteen verses at the Soma-sacrifice, is sacred to Indra (Nirukta 7, 10), the wielder of the thunderbolt.

[^egg_268]: 97:1 Or, with his thumbs (aṅgushṭḥābhyām). The Kāṇva text has 'pādyābhyām aṅgushṭḥābhyām;' but Kāty. III, 1, 7 has 'aṅgushṭḥābhyāṁ pādyābhyāṁ vā,' which would seem to leave a choice between the thumbs and the great toes; the commentator, however, takes vā in a restrictive sense. The sacrificer is to press down the earth with his great toes (or thumbs) each time when a kindling verse is recited.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They become fifteen (1 × 3 + 9 + 3 × 1). ‘Fifteen’ is verily the thunderbolt. Virility is thunderbolt? He produces virility; thus, to the Sāmidhenīs (the kindling chants).
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

**पञ्चदशो वै वज्र** इति । पञ्चदशस्तोमस्येन्द्रस्य च सह प्रजापतिबाहुसकाशादुत्पत्तेरिन्द्रसम्बन्धाद् [^१_१२६] वज्रो ऽपि पञ्चदशस्तोमात्मकः ।

**सामिधेनीरभी**ति । "अभिरभागे" - (पा० सू० १. ४. । ९१) इति लक्षणे अभेः कर्मप्रवचनीयत्वात् "कर्मप्रवचनीययुक्ते" ( पा० सू० २. ३. ८) इति द्वितीया । शिष्टं सुगमम् ।

[^१_१२६]: ऐ. ब्रा. ५ । ५ । ७ । इत्यत्र द्रष्टव्यम् ।
</details>
