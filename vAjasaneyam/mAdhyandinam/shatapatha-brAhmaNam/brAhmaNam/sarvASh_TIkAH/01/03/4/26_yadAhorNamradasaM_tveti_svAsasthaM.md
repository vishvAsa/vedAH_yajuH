<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and by 'pleasant to sit upon for the gods' he means to say 'forming a good seat for the gods.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He verily says (means), "good (fine) for the gods". By (saying), "Who remains in a comfortable seat for the gods", he verily says (means) this, "Provider of comfortable seat for the gods".
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

यदाहो᳘र्णम्रदसं त्वे᳘ति स्वासस्थं᳘ देवे᳘भ्य ऽइ᳘ति स्वास᳘दं देवे᳘भ्य ऽइ᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

यदाहो᳘र्णम्रदसं त्वे᳘ति स्वासस्थं᳘ देवे᳘भ्य इ᳘ति स्वास᳘दं देवे᳘भ्य इ᳘त्येॗवैत᳘दाह॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

यदाहोर्णम्रदसं त्वेति । स्वासस्थं देवेभ्य इति- स्वासदं देवेभ्य इत्येवैतदाह ॥ ११ ॥
</details>

</details>
