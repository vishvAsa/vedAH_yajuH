<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

13. Then Agni is the last (to whom offering is made). Agni assuredly is the gāyatrī;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And then (he offers) to Agni, lastly Gāyatrī is indeed, Agni.
</details>

</details>

</details>
