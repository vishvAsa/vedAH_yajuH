<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

'For the Ādityas thee!' For these--to wit, the Vasus, Rudras, and Ādityas--are three (classes of) gods: 'for them (I anoint) thee,' he thereby says.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(He chants), “May the Vasus, Rudras and Ādityas reach you (sit on you) ”. These are verily three groups of gods viz. Vasus, Rudras and Ādityas. He verily says (means), “May these sit upon you”.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

एते वै᳘ त्रया᳘ देवा यद्व᳘सवो रुद्रा᳘ ऽआदित्या᳘ ऽएते᳘भ्य᳘स्त्वे᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह᳘॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

एते वै᳘ त्रया᳘ देवा यद्व᳘सवो रुद्रा᳘ आदित्या᳘ एते᳘भ्यस्त्वे᳘त्येॗवैत᳘दाह॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

एते वै त्रया देवाः- यद् वसवो रुद्रा आदित्याः । एतेभ्यस्त्वेत्येवैतदाह ॥ ८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

एते वै᳘ त्रया᳘ देवा यद्व᳘सवो रुद्रा᳘ आदित्या᳘ एते त्वा᳘सीदन्त्वि᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह
</details>
