<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

24. He seizes them for the Viśve Devāḥ (the All-gods). For, assuredly, when any sacrificial food is taken without being announced to any one deity, then all the gods think that they have a share in it.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(Viśvedevas). Whatever 'havis' is taken up without having been announced for (any particular) deity, all the gods, indeed, consider (themselves) as sharers in it.
</details>
