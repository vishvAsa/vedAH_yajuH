<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for whosoever has cattle, will sacrifice hereafter, as he has sacrificed heretofore.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus he prays indirectly for the blessing of cattle for this person (the sacrificer). He who has cattle performs sacrifice later (too), after having performed the sacrifice formerly.
</details>
