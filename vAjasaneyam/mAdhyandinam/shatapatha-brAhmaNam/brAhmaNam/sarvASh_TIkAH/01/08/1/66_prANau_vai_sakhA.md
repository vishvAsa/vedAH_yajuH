<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--the friend, the food, doubtless, means breath: hence he thereby calls hither the breath. 'Hither is called the Hek [^egg_526]!'

[^egg_526]: 223:3 Apparently, like hikkā (verb hikk), imitative of the internal sound of the hiccough. The Kāṇva MS. has harik instead; and the Black Yajus ho, which it identifies with the self (ātman).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For this (iḍā) crosses (overcomes) all evils. (He says), "Here has been called the friend, the food. Indeed the vital breath is the friend, the food". Thus he calls here the vital breath.
</details>
