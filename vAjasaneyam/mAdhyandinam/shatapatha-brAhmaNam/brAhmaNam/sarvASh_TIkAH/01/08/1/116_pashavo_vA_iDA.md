<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

All of them

eat (of the iḍā): 'May it be offered for me in all!' thus (he thinks). Five eat of it,--the iḍā indeed means cattle, and cattle are fivefold: hence five eat of it.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The cattle are indeed *iḍā*. The cattle are verily fivefold. Therefore, five eat (it).
</details>
