<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

For this reason they offer up Soma to all the gods; and hence they say that Soma is all the deities.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Therefore they offer soma to all the deities. Therefore they say “Soma is all the deities”.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>

</details>
