<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

For this reason they sacrifice in Agni (the fire) to all the gods; and for this reason they say that Agni is all the deities.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Therefore they make the offerings for all the gods into Agni. Therefore they say “Agni is all the deities”.
</details>

</details>

</details>
