<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for one Arvāvasu by name was the Hotr̥ of the gods [^egg_339], and on his seat he accordingly sits down.

[^egg_339]: 137:1 According to the Kaushīt. Br. VI, 10, Arvāvasu was the Brahman of the gods. Weber, Ind. Stud. II, 306.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Indeed, the Hotṛ of the gods was known as ‘Arvāvasu’. On this very seat does he (this Hotṛ) sit down thus.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

र्व्वाव᳘सुर्वै ना᳘म देवा᳘नाᳫँ᳭ हो᳘ता त᳘स्यै᳘वैतत्स᳘दने सीदति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

र्वाव᳘सुर्वै ना᳘म देवा᳘नाᳫं हो᳘ता त᳘स्यैॗवैतत्स᳘दने सीदति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>

</details>
