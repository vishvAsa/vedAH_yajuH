<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--Manu, indeed, worshipped with sacrifice in olden times, and doing as he did these descendants of his now sacrifice: therefore he says 'like as Manu.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Indeed formerly (at first) Manu performed a sacrifice. Immitating it, now these (his) progeny perform sacrifice. Therefore he says “Like Manu”.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

**मनुष्वदि**ति तृतीयमन्त्रभागगतस्य 'मनुष्वत्' पदस्य सामर्थ्यलब्धो ऽर्थः- **तदनुकृत्ये**त्यादि । अयमर्थः- यथा मनुः स्वकीये यज्ञे देवान् अनुक्रमेणेष्टवान्, एवम् 'इमाः प्रजाः' अपि 'यजन्ते' तद्वदग्निरपि होता भूत्वा यजत्वित्यर्थः ।
</details>
