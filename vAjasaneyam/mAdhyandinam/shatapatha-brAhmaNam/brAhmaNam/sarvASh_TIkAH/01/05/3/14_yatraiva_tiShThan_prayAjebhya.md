<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

he should not move from that same spot where he may be standing when he calls for the fore-offerings. Let

him rather offer the (five) oblations in that part (of the fire) where he thinks there is the fiercest blaze;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He should not indeed move away from that place standing where he is to make the call for the fore-offerings. He should pour the offerings only there (in the fire) where he considers it is most kindled (blazing).
</details>

</details>
