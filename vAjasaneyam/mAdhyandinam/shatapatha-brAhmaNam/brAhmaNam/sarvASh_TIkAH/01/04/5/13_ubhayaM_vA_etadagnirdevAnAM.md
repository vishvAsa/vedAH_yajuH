<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for Agni is both Hotr̥ and messenger to the gods: hence he thereby says, 'know thou [^egg_324] both (offices) which thou art (holding) for the gods!' --'May earth and heaven guard thee! Guard thou earth and heaven!'

[^egg_324]: 129:1 Veḥ, in the formula, our author refers to vid, 'to know,' instead of to vī, 'to strive after, undertake.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(He recites), “Oh Agni! Take up the function of the Hotṛ; take up the function of a messenger”. Verily Agni is to the gods, both a Hotṛ and a messenger.
</details>
