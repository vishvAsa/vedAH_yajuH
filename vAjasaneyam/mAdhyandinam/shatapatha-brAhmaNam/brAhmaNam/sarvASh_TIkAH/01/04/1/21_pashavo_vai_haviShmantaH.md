<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

'In havis rich' he further says, because those that are rich in havis (milk, butter) are the cattle; it is cattle, therefore, that he thereby obtains through the recitation.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Verily cattle are possessors of (abundant) havis (milk etc.). Thus this has been said only about cattle.
</details>
