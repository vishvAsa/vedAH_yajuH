<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--with samidhs (kindling-sticks), indeed, the Aṅgiras kindled him. 'O Aṅgiras!' he says, for Agni is indeed Aṅgiras [^egg_289].--'With butter we exhilarate!'

[^egg_289]: 108:1 Rig-veda I, 31, 1, he is called the first of the Aṅgiras.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For, they kindle this fire with samidhs (kindling sticks). (They say), "Oh Angiras!" Verily Agni is Angiras. (Recit- ing) "With ghee, we make you grow", he verily kindles this (Agni). By that (Ṛk) he verily puts virility into this (Agni/sacrificer).
</details>
