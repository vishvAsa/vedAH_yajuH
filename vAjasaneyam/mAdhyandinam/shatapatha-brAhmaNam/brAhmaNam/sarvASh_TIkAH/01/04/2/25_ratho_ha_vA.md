<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for being a cart (as it were) he conveys the sacrifice to the

gods: this is the reason why he says 'the carrier of sacrifices.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Having verily become a chariot (cart) does this (Agni) convey the sacrifice (oblation) to the gods. Therefore he says, “Carrier of the sacrifices!”
</details>
