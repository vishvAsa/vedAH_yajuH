<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and when he makes offerings in the fire, he thereby satisfies them in regard to that (debt): hence whatever they offer up in the fire, is called avadānam (sacrificial portion) [^egg_438].

[^egg_438]: 191:1 The word is really derived from ava-dā (do), 'to cut off.' The Taitt. Br. gives the same fanciful etymological explanation of the term as here.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Therefore, whatever they offer into the fire is known as 'avadāna' (cutting off a portion and offering it).
</details>
