<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He fell from the cart, and in falling, broke his arm.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He fell down from his chariot. Having fallen from the chariot he broke (dislocated) his arm.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>
