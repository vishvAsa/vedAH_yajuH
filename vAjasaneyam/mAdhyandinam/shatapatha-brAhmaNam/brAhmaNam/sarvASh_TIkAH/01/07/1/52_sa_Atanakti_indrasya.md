<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

19. He coagulates it, with the formula (Vāj. S. I, 4 d), 'With Soma I coagulate thee, the portion of Indra!' Just as on a former occasion [^egg_435], when taking sacrificial food for a deity, he announces it (to that deity), in like manner he now announces it to the deity, saying, 'Thee, the portion of Indra!'

[^egg_435]: 189:3 See I, 1, 2, 18.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He coagulates it, chanting “With soma, do I coagulate you who are Indra's share".
</details>
