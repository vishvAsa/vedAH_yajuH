<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Just as taking up that havis (sacrificial food) he announces it to that deity, in the very same manner he announces it thus to this deity when he says, "The share for Indra".

Just as then (formerly) taking the havis (sacrificial food) for a deity he announces it, in the very same manner he announces for this deity.

Just as he addressed that deity, holding up the havis for it, in the same manner, he addressed this deity. When she (that deity namely the Vapā) arrives and is proceeded with.
</details>
