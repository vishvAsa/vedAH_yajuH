--he thereby means to say, 'may the evil spirits, the Rakshas, not lord it over you!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

In this there is nothing that is obscure as it were. (He chants), "May the thief not have power over you, may not the ill-wisher!" (By this) he verily says (means), "May not any destructive beings have power over you".
</details>

</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>
