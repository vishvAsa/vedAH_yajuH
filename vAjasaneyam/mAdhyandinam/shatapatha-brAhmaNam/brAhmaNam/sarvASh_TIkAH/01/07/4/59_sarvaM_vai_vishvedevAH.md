<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'all the gods,' doubtless, means the All: hence he thereby restores (the sacrifice) by means of the All.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He puts together (mends) thus by means of 'everything' (or with everything)—whatever of the sacrifice that might have been destroyed.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>
