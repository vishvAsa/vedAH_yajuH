<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

18 It is regarding this that it is said by the R̥c (X, 12, 3), 'The All-gods have gone after this thy Yajus,'--for all beings, all the gods, indeed, become the Yajus here.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

With reference to this it is said by the Ṛk – “All gods (Viśvedevas) have followed this you Yajus” – for all beings, all the gods indeed become the Yajus here.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त᳘देत᳘दृ᳘चा᳘ ऽभ्युक्तम्॥  
(म्वि᳘) व्वि᳘श्वे देवा ऽअ᳘नु तत्ते य᳘जुर्गुरि᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

त᳘देत᳘दृॗचाॗभ्युक्तम्॥  
वि᳘श्वे देवा अ᳘नु तत्ते य᳘जुर्गुरि᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

तदेतदृचा ऽभ्युक्तम् - **"विश्वे देवा अनु तत्ते यजुर्गुः"** इति ।
</details>
