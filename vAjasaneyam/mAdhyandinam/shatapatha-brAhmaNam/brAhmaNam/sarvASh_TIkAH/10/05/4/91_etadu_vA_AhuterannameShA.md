<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--because the moon resides in (or with) the Nakshatra, therefore the oblation resides in the fire-wood: that [^egg_787], indeed, is the food of the oblation, and its support;

[^egg_787]: 390:1 Viz. the staying (of the moon) in, or with, the Nakshatras, whose name is then made use of for a fanciful etymology.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This is the food of the oblation and it is its support.
</details>
