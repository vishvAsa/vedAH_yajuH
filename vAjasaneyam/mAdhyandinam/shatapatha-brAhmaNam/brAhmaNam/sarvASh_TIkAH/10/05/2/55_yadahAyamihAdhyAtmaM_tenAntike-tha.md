<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Let him say, 'Both near and far away;' for inasmuch as he is here on earth in the body he is near, and inasmuch as he is that one in yonder world he also is far away.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

There is a verse regarding this (which can be interpreted in two ways as follows) - "This orb (of the sun which is shining is food and the Puruṣa who is in the centre of the orb is the eater and he is shining while concealed in that food." This is the interpretation from the deity point of view.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

यद᳘हाय᳘मि᳘हाध्यात्मं[^१_१४५] ते᳘नान्तिके᳘ ऽथ य᳘दसा᳘वमु᳘त्र ते᳘नो दूरे[[!!]]॥

[^१_१४५]: अत्र 'यत्, अहः, अयं,' इति पदच्छेदो राजहंससरस्वतीकृतव्याख्यानुरोधान्मन्तव्यः । 'अहायं' इति सन्धिस्तु छान्दसः ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

यदहायमिहाध्यात्मम्- तेनान्तिके । अथ यदसावमुत्र - तेनो दूरे ॥ १७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
