<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

23. Now, that man in yonder orb (of the sun), and this man in the right eye, are no other than Death [^egg_740]; and he becomes the body (self) of him who knows this: whenever he who knows departs this world he passes into that body, and becomes immortal, for Death is his own self.

[^egg_740]: 374:2 Sāyaṇa seems to construe this somewhat differently: sa esho gnir yajurātmakodhidaivaṁ maṇḍalamadhyavartī adhyātmaṁ dakshiṇākshivartī purusho mr̥tyurūpaḥ.
</details>
