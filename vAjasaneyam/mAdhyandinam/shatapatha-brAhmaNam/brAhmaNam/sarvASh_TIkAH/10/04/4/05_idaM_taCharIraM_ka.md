<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

But what is man that he could secure for himself a (life) of a thousand years [^egg_702]?

[^egg_702]: 362:1 Tasmai sahasrasaṁvatsarajīvanāya ko vā manushyaḥ śaknuyāt; manushyāvadhiḥ śatam ato jīvato manushyeṇa sahasrasaṁvatsarāḥ prāptum aśakyāt. Sāy.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

But who is the human being capable of keeping on (his body) for thousand years (since man’s life is only hundered years).
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

**क उ तस्मै मनुष्य** इत्यादिना । सहस्रोपासनायाः प्रसङ्गमाह 'यः सहस्रसंवत्सरमवरुन्धीत' जीवन्निति शेषः । तस्मै सहस्रसंवत्सरजीवनाय को वा मनुष्यः शक्नुयात् ? – मनुष्यायुषः परमावधिः शतम्, अतो जीवता मनुष्येण सहस्रसंवत्सराः प्राप्तुमशक्याः ।
</details>
