<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

By knowledge, assuredly, he who knows secures for himself (the benefits of a performance) of a thousand years.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It is by Vidyā that one who knows it is capable of holding on for thousand years.
</details>
