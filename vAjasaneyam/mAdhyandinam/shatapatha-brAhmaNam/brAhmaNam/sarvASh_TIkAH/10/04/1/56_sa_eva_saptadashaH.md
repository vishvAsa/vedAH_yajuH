<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That which is offered in between them is the seventeenfold Prajāpati. What is a unit in human terms is the akṣara or syllable in the case of gods.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

स᳘ ऽएव᳘ सप्तदशः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिर्यो[[!!]] वै᳘ कला᳘ मनुष्याणामक्ष᳘रं[[!!]] त᳘द्देवा᳘नाम्॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

[^wbr_1]: प्रजा᳘पतिर्येी वै᳘ A.

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
