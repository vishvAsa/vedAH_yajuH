<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He offers it to him proportionate to his body. For, food which is proportionate to the body, nourishes, it does not affect.

Having prepared it, he offers it to him proportionate to his body. For, food which is proportionate to the body satisfies and does not hurt.

Having prepared it, he offers what is proportionate to his body. Food which is proportionate to the body satisfies and does not harm the body.
</details>
