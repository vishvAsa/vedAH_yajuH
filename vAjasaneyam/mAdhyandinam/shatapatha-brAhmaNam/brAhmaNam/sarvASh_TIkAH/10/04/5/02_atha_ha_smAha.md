<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And either Śraumatya, or Hāliṅgava, said, 'Agni is no other than Vāyu: wherefore the Adhvaryu, when he performs the last work [^egg_709], passes into that (wind).'

[^egg_709]: 363:3 That is, the concluding rites of the sacrifice,--tasmād adhvaryuḥ yadā uttamaṁ yajñasamāptilakshaṇam karma karoti, tadaitam eva vāyum apyeti, tathā hi, samishṭayajurhome devā gātuvido gātuṁ vittvā gātum ita manasaspata iti mantrevāci, svāhā vāte dhāḥ svāhā, iti vāyau dhāraṇam uktam. Sāy. See IV, 4, 4, 53, where the sacrifice (though not the Adhvaryu priest) is consigned to the wind by means of the Samishṭayajus formulas.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Perhaps Śraumatya or Hālingava it was who said that Agni is, no doubt, Vāyu and that is why, when the Adhvaryu performs the final rite he (as sacrifice personified) enters into that (Vāyu).
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

अथ वायुरूपत्वेनाग्न्युपासनं सयुक्तिकं प्रदर्शयति- **अथ ह स्माहे**ति । वा-शब्दौ समुच्चयार्थौ । श्रौमत्य आचार्यः, हालिङ्गवो नामाचार्यश्च वायुरेवाग्निरित्याह । एवशब्दो ऽन्योपाध्युपासनानिरासार्थः । यतो वायुरेवाग्निः तस्मादध्वर्युर्यदा उत्तमं यज्ञसमाप्तिलक्षणं कर्म करोति तदैतमेव वायुमप्येति । तथाहि समिष्टयजुर्होमे "देवा गातुविदो गातुं वित्त्वा गातुमित । मनसस्पते" - (वा. सं. २ । २१) इति मन्त्रे "यज्ञं स्वाहा वाते धाः स्वाहा" इति वायौ धारणमुक्तम्, अतो वायुरेवाग्निरित्यर्थः ॥ १ ॥
</details>
