<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

5. He made himself three bodies,--in each of them there were three eighties of bricks I he did not succeed.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He split himself into three, each of the three with three sets of eighty bricks (i.e. two hundred and forty in each of the three parts to make seven hundred and twenty); but did not succeed.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(त्त्री᳘) त्री᳘नात्म᳘नो ऽकुरुत॥  
तिस्र᳘स्ति᳘स्रो ऽशीत᳘य ऽए᳘कैकस्ये᳘ष्टका ऽअभवन्त्स᳘ नैव᳘ व्व्याप्नोत्॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

त्री᳘नात्म᳘नोऽकुरुत॥  
तिस्र᳘स्तिॗस्रोऽशीत᳘य ए᳘कैकस्ये᳘ष्टका अभवन्त्सॗ नैवॗ व्याप्नोत्॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

त्रीनात्मनो ऽकुरुत । तिस्रस्तिस्रो ऽशीतय एकैकस्येष्टका अभवन् । स नैव व्याप्नोत् ॥ ५ ॥
</details>
