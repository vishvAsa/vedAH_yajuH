<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus in a year, he is completely fashioned.

In the course of a half-month, his first body is fashioned, in another (half-month) the next (body) and in yet another (half-month) his next (body) – thus in a year he is made up whole and complete.
</details>
