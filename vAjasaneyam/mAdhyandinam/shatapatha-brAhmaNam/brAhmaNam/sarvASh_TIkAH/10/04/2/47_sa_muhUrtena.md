<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

muhūrta by muhūrta he gained a fourscore (of syllables), and muhūrta by muhūrta a fourscore was completed [^egg_689].

[^egg_689]: 353:4 That is, within the year, for the year has 360 × 30 = 10,800 muhūrtas, which is just the amount of eighties of which the three Vedas were said to consist. I do not see how any division of the 'muhūrta' itself into eighty parts (as supposed by Professor Weber, Ind. Streifen, I, p. 92, note 1) can be implied here.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

By each *muhūrta*, he gained eighty (two Paṅktis together forming eighty syllables) and by every *muhūrta*, one set of eighty is completed.
</details>
