<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

11. He made himself ten bodies of seventy-two bricks each: he did not succeed. He did not develop elevenfold.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He made himself into ten parts with seventy-two bricks in each, but did not succeed. He did not split into eleven parts.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(द्द᳘) द᳘शात्म᳘नो ऽकुरुत॥  
द्वा᳘सप्ततीष्टकान्त्स᳘ नैव᳘ व्व्याप्नो᳘न्नैकादशधा᳘ व्व्यभवत्[[!!]]॥
</details>

</details>

</details>

</details>
