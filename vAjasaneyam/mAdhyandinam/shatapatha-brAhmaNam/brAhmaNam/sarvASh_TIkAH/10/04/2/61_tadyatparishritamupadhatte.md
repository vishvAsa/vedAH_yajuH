<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

30. And whenever he lays down an enclosing-stone, he lays down a night, and along with that fifteen muhūrtas, and along with the muhūrtas fifteen eighties (of syllables).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When he places a pariśrit (enclos- ing stone), he lays down a night and with that he places fifteen muhūrtas and in placing the fifteen muhūrtas, he lays down fifteen (times) eighty sets (of the syllables of the sacred lore).
</details>
