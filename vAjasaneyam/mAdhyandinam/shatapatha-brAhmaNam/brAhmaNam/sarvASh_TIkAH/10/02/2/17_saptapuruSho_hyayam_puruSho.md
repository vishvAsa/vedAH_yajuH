<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--to wit, the body of four, and the wings and tail of three, for of four the body of that Person consisted, and of three his wings and tail.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Because his body proper (trunk) is of seven man-measure and his wings and tail measure three; for, his trunk measures four man-measures and his wings and tail account for three man-measures.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

तस्य सप्तपुरुषात्मकत्वमाह- **स वा** इति । 'सः' सुपर्णरूपः प्रजापतिः सप्तपुरुषसम्मितो भवेत् । हि यस्माद् 'अयम्' 'पुरुषः' सप्तभिः पुरुषैर्निर्मितः ।

तानेव पुरुषानाह- **यच्चत्वार आत्मा त्रयः पक्षपुच्छानी**ति । मध्यभागः चतुःपुरुषप्रमाणः । दक्षिणः पक्ष एकपुरुषप्रमाणः । तथा पुच्छम् । तथोत्तरः पक्ष इत्यर्थः ॥ ५ ॥
</details>
