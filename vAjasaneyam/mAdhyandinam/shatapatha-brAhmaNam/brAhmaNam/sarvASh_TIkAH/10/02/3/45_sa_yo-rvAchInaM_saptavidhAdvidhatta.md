<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He who constructs one lower than a sevenfold one cuts this Father Prajāpati in twain: he will be the worse for sacrificing as one would be by doing injury to his better.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He who constructs less than sevenfold, mutilates father Prajāpati and by performing sacrifice thereon, he becomes a sinner; just as one would by hurting someone venerable. One who constructs in excess of one-hundred and onefold, he goes beyond all this (universe); for Prajāpati is all this (universe).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

स᳘ यो ऽर्व्वाची᳘नᳫँ᳭ सप्त᳘विधाद्विधत्त᳘ ऽएत᳘ᳫँ᳘ ह स᳘ पित᳘रं प्रजा᳘पतिं व्वि᳘च्छिनत्ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

सॗ योऽर्वाची᳘नᳫं सप्त᳘विधाद्विधत्त᳘ एत᳘ᳫं᳘ ह स᳘ पित᳘रम् प्रजा᳘पतिं वि᳘छिनत्ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

स यो ऽर्वाचीनं सप्तविधाद्विधत्ते । एतं ह स पितरं प्रजापतिं विच्छिनत्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

स᳘ योऽर्वाची᳘नꣳ सप्त᳘विधाद्विधत्त᳘ एत᳘ꣳ᳘ तदा᳘ पित᳘रं प्रजा᳘पतिं वि᳘च्छिनत्ति
</details>
