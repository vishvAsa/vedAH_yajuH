<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

5. As to this they say, 'How does this sevenfold (Person, the fire-altar,) correspond to this Vedi (measuring ninety steps)?'
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त᳘दाहुः॥  
कथ᳘मेष᳘ सप्त᳘व्विध ऽएत᳘या व्वे᳘द्या स᳘म्पद्यत ऽइ᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

तदाहुः- कथमेष सप्तविध एतया वेद्या सम्पद्यत इति ।
</details>

</details>
