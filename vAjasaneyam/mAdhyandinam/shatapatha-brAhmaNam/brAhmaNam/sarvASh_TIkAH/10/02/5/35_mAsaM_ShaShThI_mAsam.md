<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

14. One month the sixth (layer of bricks takes),

and one month the layer of earth,--so long desire (lasts) in the dewy season: he thus builds for himself a body so as to obtain all of whatever desire there is in the dewy season.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The sixth layer (takes) one month (for layer of bricks) and one month for layer of loose soil. That much of desire in the dewy season. He thus builds up his body with that much of desire which is in the dewy season.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

**मासं षष्ठी**ति । स्तोमभागानन्तरं तूष्णीं पुरीषनिवापोक्तेः तदन्ता पञ्चमी चितिरिति कृत्वा नाकसत्पञ्चचूडादीष्टकाभिः षष्ठी चितिः सम्पादितेत्यर्थः । तदन्ते मुख्या पुरीषचितिरस्ति । तथा चाष्टमे काण्डे समाम्नातम्- "स्तोमभागा उपधाय पुरीषं निवपति सा पञ्चमी चितिः" । "अथ यदत ऊर्ध्वमापुरीषात्सा षष्ठी" (श. प. । ८ । ५ । ४ । ७ । ८) इति ।
</details>
