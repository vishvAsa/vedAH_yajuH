<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He expands them behind by four finger-breadths and contracts these in front by four finger-breadths. Thus he spreads them out just as much as he draws them in.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

</details>
