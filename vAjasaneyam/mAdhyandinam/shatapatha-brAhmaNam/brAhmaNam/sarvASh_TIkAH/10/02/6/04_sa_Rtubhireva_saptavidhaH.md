<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

2. By the seasons it is sevenfold,--six seasons (of two months), and the year itself as the seventh part.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

With the seasons only, it becomes sevenfold, six seasons and the year itself the seventh part.
</details>

</details>
