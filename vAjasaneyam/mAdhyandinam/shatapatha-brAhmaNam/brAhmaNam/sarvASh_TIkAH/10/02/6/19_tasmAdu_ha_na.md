<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Hence one ought not to yield to his own desire and pass away before (he has attained) the full extent of life, for (such shortening of one's life) does not make for the heavenly world [^egg_622]; and these are indeed the worlds, to wit, the days and nights, the half-moons, moons, and seasons, and the year.

[^egg_622]: 323:2 Literally, 'conducive to the world,' or, perhaps, 'conducive to a place (in yonder world).' Sāyaṇa interprets it by--his death is 'alokyam,' that is, not procuring the world consisting of immortality. Some such meaning as '(such conduct) is not world-winning' seems to be implied by the words which follow.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

By that (ending before hun- dred years on one's own accord) fails to win for him that (heavenly) worlds. These are indeed the worlds consisting of days and nights, the half months, months, seasons and the year.
</details>
