<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

6. It is regarding this that it is said in the R̥c (I, 22, 7; Vāj. S. XXX, 4), 'The distributer of wealth, the bright boon, we invoke, Savitr̥, the beholder of men.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This is how it is said in the (relevant) Ṛk passage, “We invoke the distribution of variety of boons in the form of longevity of life; that Savitā, the witness of (the actions of) men.” That is the full span of life which is long.
</details>
