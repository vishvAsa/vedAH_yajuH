<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And in like manner does the Sacrificer now finally make a golden form for his body; and inasmuch as (he does so) finally, this is the final form of his body;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

In the same way, this Yajamāna makes his own body in golden form.
</details>
