<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He then scatters loose soil thereon,--this, doubtless, is his flesh, and it is a mortal (element), for flesh is mortal: he establishes it on that immortal (element), and thereby this part of him becomes immortal.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he scatters loose soil upon it which, doubtless, is his sinew, which is mortal.
</details>
