<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

whence it is in front (of the sacrificial ground) that offering is made to the gods, for by means of the (channel of the) out-breathing the gods eat food.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

By means of apāna (down-breath) human beings eat food.
</details>
