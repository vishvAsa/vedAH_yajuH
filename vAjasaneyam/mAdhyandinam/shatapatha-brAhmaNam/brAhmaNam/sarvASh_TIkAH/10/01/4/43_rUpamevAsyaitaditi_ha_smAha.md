<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

'his hair,' said Sāptarathavāhani.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Śāṇḍilya said, "This is his form (i.e. he assumes a golden form).” The Saptaratha Vāhaṇī said, “hairs" (meaning his hairs become golden).
</details>

</details>
