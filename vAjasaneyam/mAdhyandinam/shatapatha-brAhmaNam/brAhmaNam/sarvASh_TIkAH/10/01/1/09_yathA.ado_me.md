<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

That (vital fluid) they obtain in the course of a year [^egg_523].

[^egg_523]: 282:3 Viz. by means of the sacrificial session of sacrificing, chanting, and reciting, called 'Gavām ayanam' (procession of the cows), lasting for one year, on the last day but one of which the Mahāvrata, or Great Rite, is performed.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That Yajus marches in front as if saying, “That one is mine; I will find it out”. They find out that (essence) in the course of a year.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

य᳘थादो᳘ मेऽमुत्रै᳘कं तदा᳘हरिष्यामी᳘त्येवं त्॥
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

**यथा ऽदो मे ऽमुत्रे**ति । लोके यथा 'अमुत्र' विप्रकृष्टे प्रदेशे 'अदः' दृष्टं 'मे' मदीयम् 'एकम्' वस्तु 'आहरिष्यामि' अहमिति पूर्वं गच्छति- 'एवं' यजुर्मन्त्रः ऋक्सामाभ्यां प्रथमं रसानयनाय गच्छति । 'तं रसं संवत्सरे' संवत्सरसाध्यगवा मयनोपान्त्यदिवसनिष्पाद्ये महाव्रते 'आप्नुवन्ति' ॥ ४ ॥
</details>
