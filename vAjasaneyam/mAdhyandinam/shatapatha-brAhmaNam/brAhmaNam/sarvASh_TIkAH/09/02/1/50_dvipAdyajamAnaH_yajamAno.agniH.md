<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

With two verses he sprinkles, for, the Yajamāna is two-footed and Yajamāna is the Agni-altar; as great as Agni is, as great as is his size, with so much he thus sprinkles.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

द्विपाद्य᳘जमानो य᳘जमानो ऽग्निर्या᳘वानग्निर्या᳘वत्यस्य मा᳘त्रा ता᳘वतै᳘वैनमेतत्स᳘मुक्षति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

द्विपाद्यजमानः । यजमानो ऽग्निः । यावानग्निर्यावत्यस्य मात्रा- तावतैवैनमेतत्समुक्षति ॥ १६ ॥
</details>
