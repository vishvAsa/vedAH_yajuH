<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

when he is built up, he is born, and he is born for every (kind of) food;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That (Agni) is born when he is built up. It is born for (consuming) all food.

That (Agni) when being built up is born (as it were).

He develops for (consuming) every kind of food. This food which is sesamum is food in two ways; it is grāmya (culti- vated) as well as āraṇya (grown without ploughing).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

ऽएष᳘ ऽएतद्य᳘च्चीय᳘ते स᳘ ऽएष स᳘र्व्वस्मा ऽअ᳘न्नाय जायते
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
