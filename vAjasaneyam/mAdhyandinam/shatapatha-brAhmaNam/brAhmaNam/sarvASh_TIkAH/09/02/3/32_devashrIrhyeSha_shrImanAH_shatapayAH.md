<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'encircling, the gods drew nigh unto the sacrifice,'--for encircling him (Agni), the gods drew nigh to the sacrifice; --'the gods stood ready to perform the cult unto the gods,'--the cult, doubtless, is the sacrifice, thus, 'the gods stood ready to perform the sacrifice to the gods.'
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

देवश्री᳘र्ह्येष श्री᳘मनाः शत᳘पयाः परिगृ᳘ह्य देवा᳘ यज्ञ᳘मायन्नि᳘ति
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
