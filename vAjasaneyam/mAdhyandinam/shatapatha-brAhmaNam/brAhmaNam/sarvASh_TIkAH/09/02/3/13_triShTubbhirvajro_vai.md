<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

With trishṭubh [^egg_324] (verses he does so),--the Trishṭubh is the thunderbolt: by the thunderbolt he thus drives off the Asuras, the mischievous fiends, in the south.

[^egg_324]: 193:1 The trishṭubh verse consists of 4 × 11 syllables, hence the twelve verses of together 528 syllables. The gāyatrī verse, on the other hand, consists of 3 × 8 syllables; and twenty-two such verses would thus consist of altogether 528 syllables.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(They are) in Triṣṭubh metre and Triṣṭubh is the thunder-bolt. Therefore he strikes at the terrible Asura-Rākṣasas in the south by means of thunder-bolt.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

तासां छन्दः प्रशंसति- **त्रिष्टुब्भिरि**ति । त्रिष्टुभो वज्रत्वं वज्रिणेन्द्रेण सहोत्पत्तेः । सा चोत्पत्तिः- तैत्तिरीयके श्रूयते- "उरसो बाहुभ्यां पञ्चदशं निरमिमीत तमिन्द्रो देवता ऽन्वसृज्यत त्रिष्टुप् छन्दः" (तै. सं. ७ । १ । १ । ४) इति । अथवा वीर्यवत्त्वात् त्रिष्टुभो वज्रत्वम्, वीर्यवत उरःप्रदेशात्तन्निर्माणात् । अत एव श्रुतं "तस्मात्ते वीर्यावन्तो वीर्याद्ध्यसृज्यन्त" (तै. सं. ७ । १ । १ । ५) इति ।
</details>
