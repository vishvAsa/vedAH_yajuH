<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

9. 'Glorifying on the kindled fire,'--the glorifying one is the Sacrificer;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Assuming greatness in the kindled fire” – the Yajamāna is the one who assumes greatness. "The Uktha hymn has the wings" - for, the Uktha are his wings. "Praise-worthy" (meaning) deserving to be praised.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(त्स᳘) स᳘मिद्धे ऽअग्नाव᳘धि मामहान ऽइ᳘ति॥  
य᳘जमानो वै᳘ मामहा᳘न ऽउक्थ᳘पत्र ऽइ᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

**"समिद्धे अग्नावधि मामहानः"**- इति । यजमानो वै मामहानः । **“उक्थपत्रः"**- इति ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>
