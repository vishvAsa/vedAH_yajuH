<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

50. Moreover, they say, 'It is Prajāpati himself who takes this (Agni) as his dear son to his bosom;'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They say, “It is Prajāpati himself who takes on his lap this dear son of his.” He who knows this, takes on his lap his dear son.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

**अथो ऽआहुरि**त्यादिकस्यायमर्थः । किंच गार्हपत्यादनुष्टुभः आहरणेन चित्याग्निरूपः प्रजापतिरेव प्रणीताग्निरूपं 'प्रियं पुत्रमुरसि' धारितवन् भवति 'इति' ब्रह्मवादिन 'आहुः' अतो ऽपि तस्मादनुष्टुभमाहरन्तीत्यर्थः ।
</details>
