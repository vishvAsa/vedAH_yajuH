<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'filling the two worlds and the air,' for even in rising he fills these (three) worlds, 'he scans the all-reaching, the butter-reaching,'--he thereby means the offering-ladles and the offering-grounds; --'between the front and back lights,'--that is, between this world and that one; or that (fire-altar) which is here at this moment being built, and that which was there built at first.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(Meaning) Between this world and the yonder one or between that fire-altar which is being built here, that is, (Āhavanīya) and that which is already built here (Gārhapatya).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

</details>
