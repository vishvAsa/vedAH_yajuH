<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“The Savitā, the sun-rayed and golden-haired ever raises up the light in front” – this Agni-altar is doubtless, yonder sun and that sun-rayed and golden-haired Savitā in front ever raises that light.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(त्सू᳘) सू᳘र्यरश्मिर्ह᳘रिकेशः पुर᳘स्तात्॥  
(त्स) सविता ज्यो᳘तिरु᳘द्रयाँ२॥ अ᳘जस्रमि᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

सू᳘र्यरश्मिर्ह᳘रिकेशः पुर᳘स्तात्॥  
सविता ज्यो᳘तिरु᳘द्रियां अ᳘जस्रमि᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

**"सूर्यरश्मिर्हरिकेशः पुरस्तात्सविता ज्योतिरुदयां अजस्रम्"**- इति ।
</details>
