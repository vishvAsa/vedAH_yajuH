<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"May the goddesses pro- tect the sacrifice." (Meaning) “Let that which is divine, protect this sacri- fice." "Keeping off hunger and ill-will." (Meaning) "Not longing for feed or not afflicted by hunger." "Gratifying to the lord of the sacrifice a share in wealth and in affluence." (Meaning) "Bestowing on the lord of the sacrifice a share in wealth and in affluence." "May the sacrifice be established on wealth and plenty." (Meaning) "May the sacrifice be founded on wealth and affluence."
</details>
