<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

14. And some say, 'Let him make them his own whilst sitting, for sitting one milks the cow.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Some suggest "Let him make them his own" (milch cows) while sitting, for, one milks the cow sitting. But let him make them his own, standing.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

विहितं धेनुकरणम् आसीनेन संपाद्यमिति केषांचिच्छाखिनां सोपपत्तिकं मतं पूर्वपक्षयितुं दर्शयति- **आसीन** इति ।

स्वमतमाह- **तिष्ठंस्त्वेवे**ति । 'तिष्ठन् एव कुर्वीत' न पुनरासीन इत्येवकारेण पूर्वमुपन्यस्तस्य मतस्य निरासः ।
</details>
