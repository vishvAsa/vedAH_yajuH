<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

7. And, again, as to why he perambulates it,--he then (in sprinkling the altar) goes after him [^egg_291] (Agni-Rudra): thereupon he now recovers his own self unto life, and so does he by that self of his obtain all vital power.

[^egg_291]: 170:1 See IX, I, 1, 33.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And again why he goes round (the altar) – (while sprinkling) he has gone after him (Agni-Rudra).
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

प्रकारान्तरेणापि विपल्ययनं प्रशंसति- **यद्वेवे**ति । प्रदक्षिणप्रक्रमावसरे 'एनम्' अग्निमनुगतो भवति । विपल्ययनात्तत एव जीवनायात्मानं पृथक्करोति । तथा सति 'अनेन' इदानीं ध्रियमाणेन शरीरेण च 'सर्वमायुः' प्राप्नोति ॥ ७ ॥
</details>
