<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and in like manner does this (Sacrificer) thereby lay into him that highest form, immortality.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

In same manner, this (Yajamāna) bestows on him the highest form of immortality.
</details>
