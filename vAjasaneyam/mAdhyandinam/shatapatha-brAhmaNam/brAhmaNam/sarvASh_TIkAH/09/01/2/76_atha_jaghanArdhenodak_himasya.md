<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

26. Then northwards along the hind part (of the altar), with (Vāj. S. XVII, 5), 'With an outer vesture of cold we encompass thee, O Agni: be thou bright and propitious unto us!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then along the head part northwards (he drags them) (saying) “We encompass you with the outer placenta of ice; you may be purifying and propitious for us.” That part of water which is coldest is called the pla- centa of the ice.
</details>
