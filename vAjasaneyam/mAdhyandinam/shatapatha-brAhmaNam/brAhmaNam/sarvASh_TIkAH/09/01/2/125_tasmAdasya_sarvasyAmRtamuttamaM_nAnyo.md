<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Let none other but the Adhvaryu [^egg_307] sing; for these (hymns) are bricks, and he (Agni, the fire-altar) would be built up in the wrong way [^egg_308], were any other than the Adhvaryu to sing.

[^egg_307]: 181:2 According to Lāṭy. I, 5, 1 seq., it is the Prastotr̥ who sings these sāmans. A similar conflict of competence in this respect is referred to not only in regard to detached sāmans (cf. Kāty. IV, 9, 6-9), but even in regard to such solemn performances as the chanting of the Mahāvrata-sāman (cf. note on X, 1, 1, 5).

[^egg_308]: 181:3 Vi-cita, in this sense, appears to be a ἅπαξ λεγόμενον. Sāyaṇa seems to have read vijita (parābhūta, defeated) instead.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Let not anybody else than the Adhvaryu sing (these Sāmans).
</details>
