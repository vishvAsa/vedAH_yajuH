<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

They called them by the name 'the widely-shining,' and they turned unto them.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They conferred the name “Virāj” to them and they turned to them (gods). That is why, after placing ten-ten bricks, he addresses them with the mantras referring to lokampṛṇās. Thus he makes them “Virājas” (by name) because the Virāṭ metre has ten syllables. (He says) “Wish milking and never failing” – thus he makes them yield all wishes and never-failing.
</details>
