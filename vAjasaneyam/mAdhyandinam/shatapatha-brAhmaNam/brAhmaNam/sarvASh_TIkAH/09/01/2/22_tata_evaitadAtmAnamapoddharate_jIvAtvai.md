<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now having recovered his life, he comes back. With this form, he gains his full āyus (full span of life) (or all prāṇas).

Now (by going round) he regains his own self, back to life. That is how indeed, he with that body of his, gets the full span of life.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त᳘त ऽए᳘वैत᳘दात्मा᳘नमपो᳘द्धरते जीवा᳘त्वै त᳘थो हाने᳘नात्म᳘ना स᳘र्व्वमा᳘युरेति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

त᳘त एॗवैत᳘दात्मा᳘नमपो᳘द्धरते जीवा᳘त्वै त᳘थो हाने᳘नात्म᳘ना स᳘र्वमा᳘युरेति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

तत एवैतदात्मानमपोद्धरते । जीवात्वै । तथो हानेनात्मना सर्वमायुरेति ॥ ७ ॥
</details>
