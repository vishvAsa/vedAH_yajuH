<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

8. Thrice he perambulates it, for thrice he walks round it (whilst sprinkling): thus as many times as he walks round it, so many times does he perambulate it.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thrice he goes round it and thrice he walks round (while sprinkling).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त्रि᳘र्व्विप᳘ल्ययते॥  
त्रिर्हि कृ᳘त्वः पर्ये᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

त्रिर्हि कृ᳘त्वः पर्ये᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
