<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

10. For at that time, the gods, having appeased him by the Śatarudriya and the water, thereby drove out his pain, his evil; and in like manner does this (Sacrificer) now, after appeasing him by the Śatarudriya and the water, cast away his pain, his evil.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This is how the gods having appeased him (Rudra) by means of the Śatarudriya and the waters, therewith destroyed his pain and evil. In the same way, this one (Yajamāna) having appeased him by means of the Śatarudriya and the waters, destroyed his pain and evil.
</details>
