<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

33. And, again, as to why he descends. Then (in the preceding oblations) he goes after those (gods), gratifying (propitiating) them.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Why he descends is this — formerly (in the earlier) oblations, for appeasing the gods, he went after them.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

य᳘द्वेव᳘ प्रत्यवरो᳘हति॥  
(त्ये) एतद्वा᳘ ऽएनानेत᳘त्प्रीण᳘न्नन्व᳘वैति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

एतद्वा᳘ एनानेत᳘त्प्रीण᳘न्नन्व᳘वैति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

यद्वेव प्रत्यवरोहति । एतद्वा एनानेतत्प्रीणन्नन्ववैति ।
</details>
