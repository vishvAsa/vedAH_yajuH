<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--they thus are gracious unto him; --'whomsoever we hate, and whosoever hateth us, him we cast into their jaws!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“To them be my saluation” — it is to them he prostrates. “Be they kind to us” — they are indeed kind to him (Yajamāna).

“May they be merciful to me”—by this they are merciful to him.
</details>
