<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

29. And, again, as to why he says 'at a thousand leagues,'--a thousand leagues means this Agni (fire-altar), for neither this way nor that way is there any other thing greater than he; and it is when he makes offering in the fire that he unstrings their bows at a thousand leagues.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And again why he says, "at a thousand yojanas" — a thousand yojanas means this Agni-altar, for, there is nothing else which is beyond, or greater than Agni-altar. When he makes the offering on the Agni-altar, that itself unstrings their bows at a distance of thousand yojanas. (He further says) "Countless thousands, in this great ocean." Thus, wherever they are, there he causes the unstringing of these bows.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

अथाग्नेः सहस्रयोजनत्वादग्नौ होमेन सहस्रयोजन एव धनुषामवरोपणं भवतीत्याह- **यद्वेवे**ति ।

'अयमग्निः' 'सहस्रयोजनम्' इत्येतदेवोपपाद्यते- **न ह्येतस्मादती**ति । एतद्व्यतिरिक्तम् 'अन्यत्' किञ्चिदपि वस्तु 'परम्' अधिकं 'नास्ति' । तस्याग्नेः प्रजापत्यात्मकत्वेन सर्वाधिकत्वात् । अतश्चाग्नेः सहस्रयोजनत्वेन तत्र होमे सहस्रयोजन एव धनुषामवरोपणं सम्पादितवान् भवति ॥ २९ ॥
</details>
