<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

15. That god who became the hundred-headed (Rudra) is the chief (kshatra [^egg_266]), and those others who originated from the drops are the peasants (clansmen): those peasants in the first place assigned to that chief this as his special share, to wit, this first chapter of formulas [^egg_267], and gratified him thereby.

[^egg_266]: 159:1 Literally, the ruling power.

[^egg_267]: 159:2 The first anuvāka of kāṇḍa XVI of the Vāj. S. consists of sixteen verses; which of these the fourteen referred to in the next paragraph are is not clear to me.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That god who became hundred-headed is Kṣatra (martial power). These others are the Viś (commoners) who came out of the drops (of tears).
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

'स एष क्षत्त्रम्' इत्यादिकस्यायमर्थः । प्रजापते रोदनावसरे 'सः' मन्युरूपः 'यो देवः' 'शतशीर्षा' रुद्रो ऽभूत् । 'सः' एव 'देवः' 'क्षत्त्रम्', 'विप्रुड्भ्यः' 'ये' जातास्ते 'इतरे विशः' ततश्च 'एता विशः' 'तस्मै' 'क्षत्त्राय' 'य एष प्रथमो ऽनुवाकः' 'एतं पुरस्तादुद्धारं' भागमब्रुवन्, 'तेन' भागेन 'एनं' रुद्रं प्रीणितवन्तः । यथा लोके प्रजा राज्ञे भागं प्रदाय प्रीणयन्ति तद्वत् । अतो यजमानो ऽपि तथैव तस्य प्रथमानुवाकलक्षणं भागं कृत्वा 'तेनैनं' 'प्रीणाति' प्रथमानुवाकस्य साध्यो होमो रुद्रस्य प्रीतिहेतुः पुरस्ताद्भागः सम्पादितो भवतीत्यर्थः ।
</details>
