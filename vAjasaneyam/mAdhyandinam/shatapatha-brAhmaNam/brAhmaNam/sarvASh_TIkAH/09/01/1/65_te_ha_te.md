<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--more terrible and more unappeased, indeed, are those (Rudras) that have 'reverence' on both sides: on both sides he thereby appeases them by sacrifice, by reverence.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Such of those who are more terrible and more angry have *namaskāra* on both sides. On both sides they are appeased by this obeisance, i.e., the sacrifice.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

ते᳘ ह ते᳘ घोर᳘तरा ऽअ᳘शान्ततरा य᳘ ऽउभय᳘तोनमस्कारा ऽउभय᳘त ऽए᳘वैनानेत᳘द्यज्ञे᳘न नमस्कारे᳘ण शमयति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

ते᳘ ह ते᳘ घोर᳘तरा अ᳘शान्ततरा य᳘ उभय᳘तोनमस्कारा उभय᳘त एॗवैनानेत᳘द्यज्ञे᳘न नमस्कारे᳘ण शमयति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

ते ह ते घोरतरा अशान्ततराः- य उभयतोनमस्काराः । उभयत एवैनानेतद्यज्ञेन नमस्कारेण शमयति ॥ २० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
