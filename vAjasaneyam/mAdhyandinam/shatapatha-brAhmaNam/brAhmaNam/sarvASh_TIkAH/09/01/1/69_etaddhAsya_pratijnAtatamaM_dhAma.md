<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for this is his favourite resort, either as a dear son or the heart: hence whenever he should be in fear of that god (Rudra), let him offer with those mystic utterances, for he draws nigh unto the favourite resort of that god, and so that god does not injure him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For, that is the most favoured abode of his, either as a dear son or the heart. So whenever he apprehends harm from this god, let him make offerings with these mystic utterances. For, by that he approaches the favourite resort of that god and so that god does not harm him.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

एत᳘द्धास्य प्र᳘तिज्ञाततमं धा᳘म य᳘था प्रियो᳘ वा पुत्रो हृ᳘दयं वा त᳘स्माद्य᳘त्रैत᳘स्माद्देवाच्छ᳘ङ्केत त᳘देता᳘भिर्व्या᳘हृतिभिर्जुहुयादु᳘प है᳘वैत᳘स्य देव᳘स्य प्रियं धा᳘म गच्छति त᳘थो हैनमेष᳘ देवो न᳘ हिनस्ति॥
</details>

</details>
