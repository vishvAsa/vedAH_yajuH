<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Whose arrows are the wind” — because, wind is their arrows; since by means of wind they harm those whom they want to hurt.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

व्वा᳘तो ह ते᳘षामि᳘षवो व्वा᳘तेन ह ते᳘ हिᳫँ᳭सन्ति यं जि᳘हिᳫँ᳭सिषन्ति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

वा᳘तो ह ते᳘षामि᳘षवो वा᳘तेन ह ते᳘ हिंसन्ति यं जि᳘हिंसिषन्ति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

वातो ह तेषामिषवः । वातेन ह ते हिंसन्ति- यं जिहिंसिषन्ति ॥ ३६ ॥
</details>
