<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

20. 'Heaven-like flame, hail!'--the flame is

this Agni: he thus establishes this Agni in yonder sun.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Heaven-like light, svāhā" — the light is indeed this Agni-altar and thus he establishes this light in yonder sun.
</details>
