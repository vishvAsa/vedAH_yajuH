<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

16. And, again, as to why he offers one relating

to Varuṇa. When Prajāpati was dismembered, his strength departed from him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

As to why he offers the offering to Varuṇa — from Prajāpati who was disjointed, the strength came out. When the gods replenished him, they put back that strength into him by means of this Vāruṇa offering.
</details>
