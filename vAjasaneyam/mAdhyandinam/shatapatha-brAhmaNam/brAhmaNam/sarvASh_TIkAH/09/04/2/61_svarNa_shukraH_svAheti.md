<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

21. 'The Heaven-like shining one, hail!' the shining one, doubtless, is yonder sun: he places him again up there.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Heaven-like brilliance, svāhā" — brilliance, no doubt is yonder sun. He thus places him on the Agni-altar.
</details>
