<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

10. He yokes it with the oblations of air [^egg_397],--the oblations of air are the vital airs it is thus with the

vital airs he yokes it [^egg_398].

[^egg_397]: 236:1 That is, these oblations are, as it were, to represent the team of the chariot.

[^egg_398]: 237:1 Or, he supplies him (Agni).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He yokes it by means of the Vāta homas. The Vāta homas are indeed prāṇas and it is with the prāṇas he yokes it.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

वातहोमानामेव नियोजनसाधनत्वं प्रशंसति- **वातहोमैर्युनक्ती**ति । अन्तःस्थानां प्राणानां वातात्मकत्वाद् 'वातहोमाः प्राणाः;' एवं चैतेन होमेन रथं प्राणैरेव नियुनक्ति ।

'प्राण उदानो व्यानः' इति प्राणानां त्रित्वात्तदात्मकानामपि वातहोमानां त्रित्वमुपपद्यत इत्याह- **त्रिभिर्युनक्ती**ति ।
</details>
