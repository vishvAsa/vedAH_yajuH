<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

22. 'Heaven -like light, hail!'--the light is this Agni: he places him again here (on the fire-altar).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Heaven-like glow, svāhā" — glow is indeed this Agni and thus he places him here.
</details>
