<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Let him, however, (not) assign it to him (till) the time of the offering-gifts.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Let him not give it away to him. At the time he distributes dakṣiṇā, he gives it away.
</details>
