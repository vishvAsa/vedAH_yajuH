<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And inasmuch as these deities are consecrated (quickened) by this consecration ceremony and quicken him for this consecration, they are (called) the Divine Quickeners.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Only he for whom the (other) kings approve vesting of sovereignty, becomes the king and not the one for whom, not so approved. These deities are being thus conse- crated by this consecration, approve of his consecration and hence they are called Devasva.
</details>

न स य᳘स्मै न तद्य᳘देता᳘ देव᳘ताः सुता᳘ ऽएते᳘न सवे᳘न य᳘द्वैनमेता᳘ देव᳘ता ऽएत᳘स्मै सवा᳘य सुव᳘ते त᳘स्मादेता᳘ देव᳘स्वः[[!!]]॥
</details>

</details>
