<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then touches the Agni-altar with the chant of the preliminary consecration, “May Savitā hasten you for Prasavās....” “This man, O people, is your king. Soma is the king of the Brāhmaṇas....” Thus he excludes Brāhmaṇas from the supremacy of the king and render them inaccessible for being exploited (fed upon) by the king.
</details>
