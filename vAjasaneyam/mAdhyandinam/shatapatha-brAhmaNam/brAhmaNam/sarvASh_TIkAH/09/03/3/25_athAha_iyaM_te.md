<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

10. He then says, 'This is thy realm; a supporter and sustainer art thou for the friend: for sustenance, for rain, for the lordship of creatures (do I consecrate) thee;'--sustenance, doubtless, means food, and rain means food: by food he thus gratifies him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then says, “This is your estate; you are the supporter and sustainer for the friend; for sustenance, for rain and for supremacy over beings, I consecrate you.” Sustenance is no doubt food and rain means food.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

वाजाय स्वाहेत्यादिमन्त्रहोमानन्तरम् "इयं ते राट्" इति मन्त्रस्य प्रयोगं विधत्ते- **अथाहे**ति । मन्त्रे 'ऊर्जे त्वा वृष्ट्यै' इति प्रयुज्यते । उर्क् इति साक्षादन्नमुच्यते, वृष्टिरन्नहेतुत्वादन्नम् । ततश्च 'ऊर्जे' 'वृष्ट्यै' इत्यनेनान्नस्य प्रतीतेरन्नेनैवैनमग्निं प्रीणातीत्याह- **अन्नं वा** इति ॥ १० ॥
</details>
