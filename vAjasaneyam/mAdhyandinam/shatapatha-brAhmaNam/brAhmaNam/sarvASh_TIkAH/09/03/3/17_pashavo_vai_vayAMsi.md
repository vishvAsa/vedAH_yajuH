<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and by cattle, for his food, he thus consecrates him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It is the cattle, with food, he pleases. The cattle itself being food, he consecrates him with those cattle, food.
</details>

</details>

</details>
