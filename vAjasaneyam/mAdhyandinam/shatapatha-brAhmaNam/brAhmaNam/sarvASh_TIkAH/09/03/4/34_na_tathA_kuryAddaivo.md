<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

But let him not do so, for that (Āhavanīya, the fire-altar,) is his (the Sacrificer's)

divine body, and this (Sacrificer, or Sacrificer's real body) is his human one: they thus attach that divine body of his to this his mortal body, if they anoint him in this way.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It is not to be done so. That Āhavanīya is the divine self of the Yajamāna; and this Yajamāna's real self is his human body.
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

**देवो वा** इति । 'अस्य' यजमानस्य 'एषः' आहवनीयो देवसम्बन्धी 'आत्मा' । अग्निचयनेन यजमानस्याग्नित्वप्राप्तेः । अयमिति च यजमानो निर्दिश्यते । अयं पुनर्मानुष आत्मा । एवं चाहवनीयसमीपे ऽभिषिञ्चन्तस्ते ऋत्विजो ऽस्य यजमानस्य दैवमात्मानम् 'मर्त्येन' 'आत्मना' 'अनुप्रसजन्ति' मेलयन्ति द्वावप्यात्मानौ संकीर्णौ कुर्वन्तीत्यर्थः ॥ १२ ॥
