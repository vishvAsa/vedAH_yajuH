<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

7. The gods now spake, 'Through whom shall we receive these objects of desire?
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now the gods ask, “Through whom shall we receive these objects of desire?” They themselves answered, “By our own self”, for, the sacrifice is the body of the gods and it is also the body of the Yajamāna.
</details>

</details>
