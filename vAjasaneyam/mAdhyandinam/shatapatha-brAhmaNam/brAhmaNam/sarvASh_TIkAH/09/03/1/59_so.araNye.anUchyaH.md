<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It is of seven potsherds; for, there are seven streams flowing westward.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

सो ऽरण्ये ऽनूच्यः । स सप्तकपालो भवति । सप्त हि ताः- याः प्रतीच्यः स्रवन्ति ।
</details>
