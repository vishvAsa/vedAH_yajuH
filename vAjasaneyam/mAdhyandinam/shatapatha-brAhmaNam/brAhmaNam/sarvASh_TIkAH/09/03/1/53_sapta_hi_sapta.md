<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He offers it close to the preceding one: he thus establishes the seven R̥shis in the seasons.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That he offers close to the earlier oblation (to seasons). He thus estab- lished the seven ṛṣis in the season.
</details>

</details>

</details>
