<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

19. And the first (cake) which he offers on the left (north) side, is the seasons; it is one of seven potsherds, for there are seven seasons.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The first Māruta puroḍāśa which he offers in the left side, represents the seasons, that is, in seven potsherds, since seasons are seven.
</details>

(त्यृ) ऋत᳘वः स स᳘ सप्त᳘कपालो भवति सप्त᳘ ह्यृतवः[[!!]]॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

अथ यं प्रथममुत्तरतो जुहोति- ऋतवः सः । स सप्तकपालो भवति । सप्त हि ऋतवः ॥ १९ ॥
</details>
