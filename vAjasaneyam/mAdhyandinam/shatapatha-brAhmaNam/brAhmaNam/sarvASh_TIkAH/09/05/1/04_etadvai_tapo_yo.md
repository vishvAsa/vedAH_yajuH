<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Having become initiated, they were living on fast-milk, for penance it is when, after becoming initiated, one lives on fast-milk. They heard the sound of it.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When one lives on milk alone after initiation, it amounts to his doing penance. They heard the sound of nectar.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

एतद्वै त᳘पो यो᳘ दीक्षित्वा प᳘योव्रतो᳘ ऽसत्त᳘स्य घो᳘षमा᳘शुश्रुवुः॥
</details>

</details>

</details>
