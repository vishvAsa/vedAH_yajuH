<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

On that occasion he offers the oblation continuously so as not to stop the seed, the sacrifice there being seed; --on this occasion (he offers) with the dipping-spoon at the Svāhā-call, for manifest is the seed when it is born.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

In that Sāvitra, he offers continuously so that there is no break in the flow of the seed, because it is the seed that constitutes the offering and it should have continuity. On this occassion (of Vaiśvakarman) he offers by means of the ladle at the pronouncement of svāhā. Because when it is born, the seed becomes distinct.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

रे᳘तो वै त᳘त्र यज्ञो रे᳘तसो᳘ ऽविच्छेदाय स्रुवे᳘णेह᳘ स्वाहाका᳘रं नि᳘रुक्तᳫँ᳭ हि रे᳘तो जातं भ᳘वति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

रे᳘तो वै त᳘त्र यज्ञो रे᳘तसो᳘ ऽविछेदाय स्रुवे᳘णेह᳘ स्वाहाका᳘रम् नि᳘रुक्तᳫं हि रे᳘तो जात भ᳘वति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

रेतो वै तत्र यज्ञः । रेतसो ऽविच्छेदाय । स्रुवेणेह स्वाहाकारम् । निरुक्तं हि रेतो जातं भवति ॥ ४४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

रेतो वै त᳘त्र यज्ञो रे᳘तसो᳘ऽविच्छेदाय स्रुवे᳘णेह᳘ स्वाहाकारं नि᳘रुक्तꣳहि रे᳘तो जातं᳘ भवति ॥ ३५॥
</details>
