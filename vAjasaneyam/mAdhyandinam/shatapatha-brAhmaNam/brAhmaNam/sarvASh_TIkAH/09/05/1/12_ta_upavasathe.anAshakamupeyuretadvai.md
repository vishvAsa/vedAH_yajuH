<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

On the day of preparation they underwent entire abstention from food; for the whole (practice of) penance it is when one abstains from food: let him therefore eat nothing on the day of preparation.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He abstains from food on the preparatory day. This is the full penance, since it involves complete abstenance from eating. So one should not eat on the previous day.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त᳘ ऽउपवसथे᳘ ऽनाशकमु᳘पेयुरेतद्वै स᳘र्व्वं त᳘पो यद᳘नाशकस्त᳘स्मादुपवसथे᳘ नाश्नीयात्॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

त᳘ उपवसथे᳘ ऽनाशकमु᳘पेयुरेतद्वै स᳘र्वं त᳘पो यद᳘नाशकस्त᳘स्मादुपवसथेॗ नाश्नीयात्॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

त उपवसथे ऽनाशकमुपेयुः । एतद्वै सर्वं तपो- यदनाशकः । तस्मादुपवसथे नाश्नीयात् ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
