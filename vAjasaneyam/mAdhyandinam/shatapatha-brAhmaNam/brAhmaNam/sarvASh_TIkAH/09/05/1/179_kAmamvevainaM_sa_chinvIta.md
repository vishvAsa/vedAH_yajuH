<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

66. And, indeed, let him by all means build who

intends to press Soma for a year, for he, manifestly, supports him by food (in the shape of) those libations.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Let him also build him, who intends to do Soma-pressing for one year because he directly sustains him by food in the form of those oblations."
</details>
