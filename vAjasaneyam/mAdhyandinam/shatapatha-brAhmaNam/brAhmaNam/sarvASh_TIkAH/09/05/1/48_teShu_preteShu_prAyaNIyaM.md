<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

20. When they had gone away, they (the gods) prepared the Opening-offering. But the Asuras

became aware of this also.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When they had gone away, the gods prepared the Prāyaṇīya (the opening offering). Immediately, the Asuras understood it and arrived there just when the Śamyōruktha (the first formula of the Prāyaṇīya) was chanted and that is why, that sacrifice stops with the chanting of Śamyōruktha.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

तेषु प्रगतेषु ‘प्रायणीयं' हविर्निरवपन् । उत्तरत्र 'तदु हासुरा' इत्यादिकं पूर्ववदेव ।

**शंयोरुक्तमासे**ति । शंयोर्देवस्य तदीयं मन्त्रवाक्यमुक्तमभूत् । शंयुवाकान्तं कर्म कृतमित्यर्थः ॥ २० ॥
</details>
