<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

10. And, on the morrow, having laid hold of it, and pressed it, he offers (of it) in the fire, and thereby bestows immortality on Agni. He then drinks (of it), and thereby bestows immortality on his own self, and becomes immortal;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Early morning, he takes hold of it and after pressing, offers it into the fire. Thus he offers nectar into the Agni-altar. Then he consumes it and thereby bestows nectar unto himself.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(त्त᳘) त᳘त्प्रात᳘रभिप᳘द्य॥  
(द्या) अभिषु᳘त्याग्नौ᳘ जुहोति त᳘दग्ना᳘वमृ᳘तं दधात्य᳘थ भक्षयति त᳘दात्म᳘न्नमृ᳘तं धत्ते᳘ सो ऽमृ᳘तो भवति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
