<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

63. 'A six-month (Agni) is the last [^egg_502] he may build,' they say, 'for six-month embryos are the last [^egg_502] that live when born.'

[^egg_502]: 273:1 That is to say, he must have been carried about for at least six months; and embryos less than six months old cannot live.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

At least six months should have been completed after the cayana (to build an altar) for, they say that six months old embryos are the last that live when born.
</details>
