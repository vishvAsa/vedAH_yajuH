<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

The gods asked him, 'Sage, seeing that they declare the building of the fire-altar not to be conducive to heaven, why then hast thou built one?'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The gods asked him, “O Sage, they say that building of Agni-altar is not a means to heaven and so why have you built it?”
</details>
