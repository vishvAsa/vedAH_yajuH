<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

For now the gods, having warded off Vr̥tra, evil, performed this rite freed from evil; and in like manner does the Sacrificer, having warded off Vr̥tra, evil, now perform this rite freed from evil.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Similarly, this Yajamāna, after killing Vṛtra, the evil, becomes free from sin and performs this rite.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

</details>
