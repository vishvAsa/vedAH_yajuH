<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

The rains are all the seasons, for the rains are indeed all the seasons: hence, in counting over years, people say, 'In such and such a year (or rain, varsha) we did it; in such and such a year (or rain) we did it.' The rains, then, are one of the forms of manifestation (rūpa) of all seasons [^egg_711]; and when people say, 'To-day it is as if in summer,' then that is in the rainy season; and when they say, 'To-day it is as if in spring,' then that, too, is in the rainy season.

[^egg_711]: 315:2 This speculation is based on the identity of the words for year (varsha; also 'rain') and the rains, or rainy season (varshāḥ).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Indeed the rainy season (itself) is all the seasons. Verily is the rainy season all the seasons. Hence, they consider (count) the years only with the rainy season, saying "We did (this) for a year (in that year) ", "We did this that year," for the rainy season is all the seasons. And therefore the rainy season itself is the form of all the seasons.
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

वर्षा वै सर्व ऋतवः । वर्षा हि वै सर्व ऋतवः- अथादो वर्षमकुर्म, अदो वर्षमकुर्मेति संवत्सरान् संपश्यन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

तस्य पुनराधेयस्य त्रैवर्णिकसाधारण्येन ऋतुविशेषं विधत्ते [^१_४७]- **स वा** इति ।

ऋत्वन्तरं परित्यज्य वर्षर्तुविधाने [^२_४७] कारणमाह- **वर्षा वा** इति ।

एतदुपपादयति- **वर्षा हि वा** इत्यादिना ।

यस्मादेवं वर्षर्तुः सर्वात्मकः, तस्मात् समुदायात्मकं संवत्सरं वर्षशब्देन लोको व्यवहरतीति दर्शयति- **अथे**ति ।

[^१_४७]: "वर्षासु मध्यंदिने वा" का० श्रौ० सू० ४. २६४ ।

[^२_४७]: 'वर्षर्त्तावेव विधाने' इति क्व. ।
</details>
