<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Glorious therefore he becomes, and sovereignty he obtains, whosoever, knowing this, establishes a sacrificial fire of his own.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

People do come to see the glorious. He who, knowing this is so, sets up (the fires), indeed, becomes glorious and attains sovereignty.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

य᳘शो ह भवति राज्यं᳘ गच्छति य᳘ ऽएवं᳘ व्विद्वा᳘नाध᳘त्ते॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>

</details>
