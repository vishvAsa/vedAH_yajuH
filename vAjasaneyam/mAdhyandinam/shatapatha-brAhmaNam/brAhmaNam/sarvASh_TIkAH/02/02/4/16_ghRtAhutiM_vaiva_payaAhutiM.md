<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

There he obtained (knew/considered) a ghee-offering or a milk-offering. Indeed both are milk.

Then he obtained (found) a ghee-offering or a milk-offering. Both indeed are milk.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

घृताहुतिं᳘ वैव᳘ पय आहुतिं᳘ वोभ᳘यᳫँ᳭ ह᳘त्वेव तत्प᳘य ऽएव᳘॥
</details>

</details>

</details>
