<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Now there is splendour (for the fire): for he thereby makes it to be possessed of divine seed, bestows splendour on it; and sets up a fire completely endowed with seed. That is why he brings gold.

[^egg_637]: 277:2 In the version of this myth given Taitt. Br. I, 1, 3, 8, the waters courted by Agni are called Varuṇa's wives.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He (the sacrificer) sets up the entire fire with its seed. Therefore does he procure gold.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

स᳘रेतसमेव᳘ कृत्स्न᳘मग्निमा᳘धत्ते त᳘स्माद्धि᳘रण्यᳫँ᳭ स᳘म्भरति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

स᳘रेतसमेव᳘ कृत्स्न᳘मग्निमा᳘धत्ते त᳘स्माद्धि᳘रण्यᳫं स᳘म्भरति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

सरेतसमेव कृत्स्नमग्निमाधत्ते । तस्माद्धिरण्यं सम्भरति ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

स᳘रेतसं कृत्स्न᳘मग्निमा᳘धत्ते त᳘स्माद्धि᳘रण्यꣳ सं᳘भरति ॥३॥
</details>
