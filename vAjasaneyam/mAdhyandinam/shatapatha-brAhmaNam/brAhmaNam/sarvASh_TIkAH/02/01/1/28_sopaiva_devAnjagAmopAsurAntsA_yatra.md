<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for the wind was tossing it hither and thither: now it came near the gods, now it came near the Asuras. When it came near the gods,--
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The wind carried it along. It (the earth) then came near the Devas and then came near the Asuras.
</details>

</details>
