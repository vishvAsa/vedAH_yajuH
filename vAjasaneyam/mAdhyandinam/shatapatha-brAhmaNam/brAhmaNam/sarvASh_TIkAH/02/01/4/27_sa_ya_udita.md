<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

By churning the Āhavanīya after sunrise he will obtain that (combination of blessings).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He churns before sunrise and then takes it to the east. (So the two fires are only nocturnal). Verily he who churns the Ahavanīya after the Sun has risen, will obtain that (benefit of setting up the fires).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>
