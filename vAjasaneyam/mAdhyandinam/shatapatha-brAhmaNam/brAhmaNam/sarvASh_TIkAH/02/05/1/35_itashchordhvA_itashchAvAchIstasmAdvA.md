<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

This is why there are these five oblations [^egg_865].

[^egg_865]: 387:1 While the five preceding oblations are common to all the seasonal offerings (Kāty. V, I, 15), the succeeding ones are peculiar to the Vaiśvadeva.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Similarly does this (sacrificer) produce (beget) creatures from the two fold pair (union)–those that are upward and those that are downward. Therefore are these five offerings (made).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

ऽइत᳘श्चोर्ध्वा ऽइतश्चा᳘वाचीस्त᳘स्माद्वा᳘ ऽएता᳘नि प᳘ञ्च हवी᳘ᳫं᳘षि भवन्ति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

इत᳘श्चोर्ध्वा᳘ इतश्चा᳘वाचीस्त᳘स्माद्वा᳘ एता᳘नि प᳘ञ्च हवीं᳘षि भवन्ति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

इतश्चोर्ध्वा इतश्चावाचीः । तस्माद्वा एतानि पञ्च हवींषि भवन्ति ॥ ११ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

इत᳘श्चोर्ध्वा᳘ इतश्चा᳘वाचीस्त᳘स्मादेतानि प᳘ञ्च हवी᳘ꣳ᳘षि भवन्ति ॥९॥
</details>
