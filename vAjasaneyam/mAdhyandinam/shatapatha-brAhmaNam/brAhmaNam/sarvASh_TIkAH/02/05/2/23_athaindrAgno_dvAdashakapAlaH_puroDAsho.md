<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

8. Then follows a cake on twelve potsherds for Indra and Agni.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then there is (the offering of) this purodāta, sanctified for Indra and Agni (together), on twelve potsherds. Verily the exhaled breath and the inhaled breath are Indra and Agni.
</details>
