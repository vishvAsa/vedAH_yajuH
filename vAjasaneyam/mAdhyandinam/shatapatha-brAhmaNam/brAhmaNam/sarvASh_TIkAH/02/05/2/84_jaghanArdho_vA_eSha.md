<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

'Go home,' she says, because that wife doubtless is the hind part of the sacrifice, and he has just now made her take her seat to the east of the sacrifice.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He has made her sit at the east. Indeed, the house is the resting place.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

अस्तं प्रेतेत्यस्याभिप्रायमाह- **जघनार्द्धो वा** इति । तामेतत् प्राचीमिति यज्ञस्यापरभागे ऽवस्थितां 'ताम्' 'एतत्' एतर्हि 'प्राचीं' प्राग् गच्छन्तीम् पूर्वभागसम्बन्धिनीं 'यज्ञं' 'प्रासीषदत्' प्रापीपत् प्रतिप्रस्थाता । सदेर्ण्यन्तात् लुङि चङि रूपम् ।
</details>
