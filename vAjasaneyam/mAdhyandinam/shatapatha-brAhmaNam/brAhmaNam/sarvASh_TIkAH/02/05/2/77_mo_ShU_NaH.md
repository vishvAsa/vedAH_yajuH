<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

28. 'Let there not, O Indra, be (fight) for us here in battles with the gods, since there is a share for thee in the sacrifice, O fiery one!--for thee, the mighty showerer of gifts, whose Maruts the song of the offerer stream-like celebrates.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Oh Indra! Let there be no fight for us in battles with gods. Indeed there is a sacrificial share (of oblations) for you, Oh strong one! whose glory of the bounteous one the word (of the sacrificer) adores abundantly".
</details>
