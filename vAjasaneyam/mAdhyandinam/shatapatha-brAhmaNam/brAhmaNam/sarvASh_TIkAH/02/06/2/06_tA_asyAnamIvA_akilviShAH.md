<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

This is why he performs these offerings.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They, his children, are born without disease and without blemish. Therefore indeed does he sacrifice with this.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

ता᳘ ऽअस्यानमीवा᳘ ऽअकिल्विषाः᳘ प्रजाः प्र᳘जायन्ते त᳘स्माद्वा᳘ ऽएष᳘ ऽएतै᳘र्यजते॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>

</details>
