<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Moreover he thereby offers to those (fathers) the share which the gods assigned to them; and thus he gratifies those whom the gods recalled to life, and leads his own fathers up to a better world; and whatever injury or loss he suffers through his own unrighteous conduct (or wrong sacrificial performance) [^egg_940] that is thereby made good to him: that is why he performs this sacrifice (to the fathers).

[^egg_940]: 420:3 Instead of 'ácaraṇena,' the Kāṇva MS. has 'caraṇéna (!).' Cf. Sāyaṇa's interpretation 'anucaraṇena anugamanena ca.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Also he pleases indeed, those whom the gods brought (back to life) and he also leads his own Pitrs to a higher (better) world.
</details>
