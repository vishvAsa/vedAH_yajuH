<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

After crossing over and calling for the 'Śraushaṭ,' he says (to the Hotr̥), 'Pronounce the offering-prayer to the gods!' He performs two after-offerings, omitting the one to the Barhis; for the Barhis means offspring: hence he performs two after-offerings, omitting the one to the Barhis, lest he should consign his offspring to the Fathers.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Having taken the two spoons, having crossed to the western side and having called attention, the Adhvaryu says, “Worship (pronounce the offering prayer for) the gods”. He offers two after-offerings, omitting the Barhis. Indeed progeny is Barhis. (Thinking). “If not, I might be putting the progeny admidst the manes”. Therefore he performs (only) the two after-offerings avoiding the Barhis (offering).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

स्रु᳘चावादा᳘य प्रत्यङ्ङ᳘तिक्रामत्यतिक्र᳘म्याश्रा᳘व्याह देवा᳘न्यजे᳘ति सो᳘ ऽपबर्हिषौ द्वा᳘वनुयाजौ᳘ यजति प्रजा वै᳘ बर्हिर्ने᳘त्प्रजाः᳘ पितृ᳘षु द᳘धानी᳘ति त᳘स्माद᳘पबर्हिषौ द्वा᳘वनुयाजौ᳘ यजति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

स्रुचावादाय प्रत्यङ्ङतिक्रामति । अतिक्रम्य आश्राव्याह- 'देवान् यज' इति । सो ऽपबर्हिषौ द्वावनुयाजौ यजति । प्रजा वै बर्हिः- नेत् प्रजाः पितृषु दधानीति । तस्मादपबर्हिषौ द्वावनुयाजौ यजति ॥ ४४ ॥
</details>

</details>
