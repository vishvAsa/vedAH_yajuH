<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He sacrificed with that sacrifice; and what race, what prosperity of Prajāpati was then produced through his performing that sacrifice, that same race he procreates, that same prosperity he obtains, whosoever, knowing this, performs that sacrifice: let him therefore perform that sacrifice.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

On his sacrificing with this sacrifice, there came to be this which is the progeny of Prajāpati and what prosperity (there is).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

स᳘ एते᳘न यज्ञे᳘नेष्ट्वा᳘ येयं᳘ प्रजा᳘पतेः प्रजा᳘तिर्या श्री᳘रेत᳘द्बभूव
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

स᳘ एते᳘न यज्ञे᳘नेष्ट्वाॗ येय᳘म् प्रजा᳘पतेः प्रजा᳘तिर्या श्री᳘रेत᳘द्बभूव
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

स एतेन यज्ञेनेष्ट्वा- येयं प्रजापतेः प्रजातिः, या श्रीः एतद्बभूव ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
