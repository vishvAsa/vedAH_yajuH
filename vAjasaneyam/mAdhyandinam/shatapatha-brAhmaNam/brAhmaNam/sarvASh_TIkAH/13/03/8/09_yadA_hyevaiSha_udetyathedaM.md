<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--the Sun, doubtless, is the eye of creatures, for when he rises everything here moves: by means of the eye (of the world) he thus bestows the eye upon it.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Sun is indeed the eye of the living beings. When he rises, all these beings start moving.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

यदा᳘ ह्ये᳘वैष᳘ ऽउदेत्य᳘थैतᳫँ᳭ स᳘र्व्वञ्चरति च᳘क्षुषै᳘वास्मिंस्तच्च᳘क्षुर्द्दधाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

यदाॗ ह्येॗवैष᳘ उदेत्य᳘थेदᳫं स᳘र्वं चरति च᳘क्षुषैॗवास्मिंस्तच्च᳘क्षुर्दधाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

यदा ह्येवैष उदेति । अथैतं सर्वं चरति । चक्षुषैवास्मिंस्तच्चक्षुर्दधाति ।
</details>

</details>
