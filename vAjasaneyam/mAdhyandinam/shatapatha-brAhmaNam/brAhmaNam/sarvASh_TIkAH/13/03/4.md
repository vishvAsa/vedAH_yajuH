<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He thus hides the cattle from Rudra. That is why Rudra does not capture the cattle in the area where this offering of the Aśvamedha is made.

Hence where this offering of Aśvamedha is made, their Rudra does not capture the cattle.
</details>
