<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for the one-hoofed (animals) are cattle, and the Svishṭakr̥t is Rudra: he thus shields the cattle from Rudra, whence Rudra does not prowl after the cattle where this oblation is offered at the Aśvamedha.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The single-hoofed animals are indeed cattle. Rudra is the Sviṣṭakṛt. He thus hides the cattle from Rudra.
</details>
