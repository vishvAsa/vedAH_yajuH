<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He offers no other as a final oblation [^egg_875]: were he to offer another as a final oblation, he would lose

his support.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

If he offers less or more number of oblations, he will cause damage to the parts of the cattle. So he offers only sixteen, for the cattle have sixteen Kalās. That is the measure of the cattle and thereby he enriches cattle with their measure.
</details>

</details>
