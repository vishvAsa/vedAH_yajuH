<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

4. Concerning this they say, 'Is the Aśvastomīya to be offered first, or the Dvipadā?'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they ask- "Is the Aśvastomiya to be offered first on the Dvipadās?" Aśvastomiyas are cattle and Dvipadā are men.
</details>
