<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Sixteen he offers, for cattle consist of sixteen parts: that is the measure of cattle, and he thus supplies cattle with their (right) measure.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That is the measure of the cattle. Thereby he enriches the Paśus with their measure.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
