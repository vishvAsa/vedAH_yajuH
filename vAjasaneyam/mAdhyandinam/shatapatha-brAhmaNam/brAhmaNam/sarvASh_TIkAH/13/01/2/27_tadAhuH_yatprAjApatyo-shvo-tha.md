<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

9. Concerning this they say, 'Seeing that the horse is sacred to Prajāpati, wherefore (does he say), "I sprinkle thee" for other deities also?'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they ask –“If the horse is of Prajāpati, how come, it is sprinkled for other gods?” Because all the gods are associated with the Āśvamedha and, by saying “I sprinkle you so as to be acceptable to all the gods”, he involves all the gods in this. Hence in the Āśvamedha, all the gods are associated.
</details>
