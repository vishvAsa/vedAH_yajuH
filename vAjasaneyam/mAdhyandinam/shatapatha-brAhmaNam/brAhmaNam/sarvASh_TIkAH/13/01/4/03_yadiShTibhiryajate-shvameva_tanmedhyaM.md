<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And when he performs ishṭis, the Sacrificer thereby searches for the horse (aśva) meet for sacrifice [^egg_711] (medhya).

[^egg_711]: 284:2 Or, for the horse full of life-sap; or, simply, the sacrificial horse.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus when he (the Yajamāna) performs Iṣṭis, he is thereby searching for the horse that is fit for sacrifice.
</details>
