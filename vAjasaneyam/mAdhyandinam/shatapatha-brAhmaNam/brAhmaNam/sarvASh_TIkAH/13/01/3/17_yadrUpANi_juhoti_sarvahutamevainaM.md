<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

When he offers (the oblations relating to) the Forms [^egg_704] (rūpa), he offers that (horse) as one that is wholly offered, so as to make good any spilling; for unspilled is what is spilled of the offered (material).

[^egg_704]: 282:1 These forty-nine oblations performed after the letting loose of the horse, are called Prakramas (i.e. steps, or movements); cf. XIII, 4, 3, 4; Kāty. XX, 3, 3.--Harisvāmin remarks, aśvarūpāṇāṁ hiṅkārādīnāṁ nishkramaṇātmikā (!) rūpākhyā āhutaya ucyante, tā evātra prakramā iti vakshyante.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When he offers the Rūpa offerings (oblations related to the forms), he offers that horse as one that is offered as a whole (without any part of its spilled); thus what is spilled is made unspilled.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

य᳘द्रूपा᳘णि जुहो᳘ति सर्व्वहु᳘तमे᳘वैनञ्जुहोत्य᳘स्कन्दाया᳘स्कन्नᳫँ᳭ हि तद्य᳘द्धुत᳘स्य स्क᳘न्दति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

य᳘द्रूपा᳘णि जुहो᳘ति सर्वहु᳘तमेॗवैनं जुहोत्य᳘स्कन्दाया᳘स्कन्न᳘ᳫं᳘ हि तद्य᳘द्धुत᳘स्य स्क᳘न्दति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

यद्रूपाणि जुहोति । सर्वहुतमेवैनं जुहोत्यस्कन्दाय । अस्कन्नं हि तत् । यद्धुतस्य स्कन्दति ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

य᳘द्रूपा᳘णि जुहो᳘ति सर्वहुतमे᳘वैनं जुहोत्य᳘स्कन्दाया᳘स्कन्नꣳ हि तद्य᳘द्धुत᳘स्य स्क᳘न्दति
</details>
