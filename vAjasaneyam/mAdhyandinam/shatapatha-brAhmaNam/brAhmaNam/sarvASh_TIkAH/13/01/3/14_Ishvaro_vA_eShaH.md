<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

4. But, verily, he who offers the oblations straight away, would be liable to fall (pass) right away [^egg_701]: he turns back again [^egg_702], and establishes himself in this (terrestrial) world.

[^egg_702]: 281:3 That is, by commencing the ten oblations again from the beginning.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He who offers straight away in a forward direction, may depart straight away. That is why he comes backward (in reverse) so that he may get estab- lished in this world.

Verily he who offers the oblations straight-away, he is liable to get harmed.
</details>
