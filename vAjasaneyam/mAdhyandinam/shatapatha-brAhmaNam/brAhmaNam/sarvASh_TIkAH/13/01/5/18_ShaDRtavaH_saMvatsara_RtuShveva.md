<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Three stanzas the one sings, and three stanzas the other, they amount to six,--six seasons make up a year: he thus establishes (the Sacrificer) in the seasons, in the year.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he gets established in the seasons in the year.
</details>
