<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

When poured forth, it weighed down the r̥c (hymn-verse) and the sāman (hymn-tune).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When it flowed out, it suppressed the Ṛks and the Sāmans. The Vaiśvadeva (offerings) sustained it (Aśvamedha).
</details>

</details>
