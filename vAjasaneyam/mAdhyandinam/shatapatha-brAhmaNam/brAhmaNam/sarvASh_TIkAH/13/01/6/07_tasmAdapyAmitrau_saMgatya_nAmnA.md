<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

whence even if two enemies [^egg_722], on meeting together, address one another by name, they get on amicably together.

[^egg_722]: 288:1 Harisvāmin, perhaps rightly, takes 'āmitrau' in the sense of 'amitrayoḥ putrau,' 'the sons of two enemies.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That is how even two enemies, while meeting each other, address one another by name; they de- velop mutual liking.
</details>
