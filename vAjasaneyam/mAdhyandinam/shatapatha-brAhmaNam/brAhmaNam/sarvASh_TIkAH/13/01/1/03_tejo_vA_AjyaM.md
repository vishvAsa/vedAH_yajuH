<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for ghee is (a type of) fiery spirit, and the horse is sacred to Prajāpati [^egg_679]: he thus endows Prajāpati with fiery spirit. Impure, and unfit for sacrifice, indeed, is that (animal), to wit, the horse.

[^egg_677]: 274:2 For further particulars regarding this opening ceremony of the sacrifice see XIII, 4, 1, 1 seqq.

[^egg_679]: 274:4 Or, the horse is of the nature of Prajāpati.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For, ghee is brilliance (vitality) and the horse be- longs to Prajāpati and thus he adds vitality to Prajāpati. Thus the horse which is (normally) impure (for sacrifice), becomes purified.
</details>
