<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Seven of them he offers; for seven were those deities that departed (from Prajāpati); it is by means of them that he (the priest) gains the Dīkshā for him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

By means of those deities he secures Dīkṣā for him (the Yajamāna).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

सप्त वै ता᳘ देव᳘ता अ᳘पाक्रामंस्ता᳘भिरे᳘वास्मै दीक्षाम᳘वरुन्द्धे॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

सप्त वै ता᳘ देव᳘ता अ᳘पाक्रामंस्ता᳘भिरेॗवास्मै दीक्षाम᳘वरुन्द्धे॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

सप्त वै ता देवता अपाक्रामन् । ताभिरेवास्मै दीक्षामवरुन्धे ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

सप्त वै ता᳘ देव᳘ता अ᳘पक्रामंस्ता᳘भिरे᳘वास्मै दीक्षाम᳘वरुन्धे ॥ १ ॥
</details>
