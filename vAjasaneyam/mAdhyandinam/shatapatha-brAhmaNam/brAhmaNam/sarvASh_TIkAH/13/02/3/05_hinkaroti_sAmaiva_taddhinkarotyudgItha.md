<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

It makes 'Hiṅ [^egg_774],' and thereby makes the. Sāman itself to be 'hiṅ': this is the Udgītha.

[^egg_774]: 306:1 On the mystic significance of this ejaculation (here compared with the neighing of the horse) in the sacrifice, and especially in the Sāman, see I, 4, 1, 1 seqq.; II, 2, 4, 12.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It produces the Hiṁkāra (Sound 'Him') and by that Hiṁkāra it actually sings the Sāman. It is the Udgītha Sāman. They pen up the mares which make a shrill noice (on seeing the horse).
</details>

</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
