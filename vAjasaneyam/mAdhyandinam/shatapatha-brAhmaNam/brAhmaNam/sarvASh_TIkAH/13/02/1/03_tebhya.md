<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He contrived these Anna-homas (food-oblations) for them: thus when he performs the Annahomas, it is the gods he thereby gratifies.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

So, when he offers the Anna-homas, he is only pleasing the gods.
</details>

</details>

</details>
