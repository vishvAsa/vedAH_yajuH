<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

he offers the two last oblations; for the dawn is the night, and heaven (the realm of light) is the day: it is day and night he thus gratifies.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus he gratifies the day and the night.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

रा᳘त्रिर्व्वै᳘ व्व्युष्टिर᳘हः स्व᳘र्ग्गो ऽहोरात्रे᳘ ऽएव त᳘त्प्रीणाति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

रा᳘त्रिर्वैॗ व्युष्टिर᳘हः स्वॗर्गोऽहोरात्रे᳘ एव त᳘त्प्रीणाति॥
</details>

</details>
