<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He does not go beyond a hundred and one: were he to go beyond a hundred and one, he would deprive the Sacrificer of his vital power.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

If he exceeds hundred and one, he will shorten the life of the Yajamāna. So he offers only hundred oblations; for man has hundred years’ life-span and his own self is the hundred and first. So he gets established in his life-span, in his own self.
</details>
