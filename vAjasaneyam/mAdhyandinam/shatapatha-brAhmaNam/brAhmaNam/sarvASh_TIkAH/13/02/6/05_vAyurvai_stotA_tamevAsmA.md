<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--the singer of praise, doubtless, is Vāyu (the wind) it is him he thereby places for him (the Sacrificer) on the other side, and so it does not go beyond that.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It is him (Vāyu) that he thereby places on the other side and thus the horse does not step beyond that (region of the Vāyu).
</details>

</details>

</details>
