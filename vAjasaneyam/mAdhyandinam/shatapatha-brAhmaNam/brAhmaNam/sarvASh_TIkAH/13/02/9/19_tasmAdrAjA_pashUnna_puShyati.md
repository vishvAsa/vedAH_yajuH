<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'it thinks not of the fat cattle,'--whence the king does not rear cattle; --'when the Śūdra woman is the Arya's mistress, he seeks not riches that he may thrive [^egg_830],'--hence he does not anoint the son of a Vaiśya woman.

[^egg_830]: 326:1 Mahīdhara interprets,--then he (her husband), the Śūdra, does not wish for wealth, but is unhappy.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“When the Śūdra lady is the Ārya’s con- cubine, he does not seek riches” – that is why he does not coronate the son of a Vaiśya.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त᳘स्माद्रा᳘जा पशून्न᳘ पुष्यति शूद्रा यद᳘र्य्यजारा न पो᳘षाय धनायती᳘ति त᳘स्माद्वैशीपु᳘त्रन्नाभि᳘षिञ्चति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

त᳘स्माद्रा᳘जा पशून्न᳘ पुष्यति शूद्रा यद᳘र्यजारा न पो᳘षाय न᳘ धनायती᳘ति त᳘स्माद्वैशीपुॗत्रं नाभिषिञ्चति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

तस्मात् राजा पशून्न पुष्यति । **"शूद्रा यदर्यजारा न पोषाय धनायति"**- (वा. सं. २३ । ३०) इति तस्माद्वैशीपुत्रं नाभिषिञ्चति ॥ ८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
