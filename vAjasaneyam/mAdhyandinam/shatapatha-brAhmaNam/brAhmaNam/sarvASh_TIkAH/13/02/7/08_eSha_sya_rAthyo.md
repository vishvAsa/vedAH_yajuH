<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

5. 'This male, fit for the chariot,'--he supplies the chariot with a horse, whence the horse draws nothing else than a chariot.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“This male is fit for the chariot” - thus he provides the chariot with a horse.
</details>
