<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

9. 'Trimmed up is the steed with the rein,'--with the rein one indeed completes the horse, whence the horse, when curbed by the rein, looks most beautiful.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"The horse has been got ready with the reins" - he thus readies the horse with reins. So the horse when controlled by the reins, looks greatly attractive.
</details>
