<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He followed it up by means of the fifteenfold (sets of victims [^egg_785]), and found it; and having found it, he took possession of it by means of the fifteenfold ones; for, indeed, they--to wit, the fifteenfold (sets)--are a symbol of the half-months, and when he seizes the fifteenfold ones, it is the

half-months the Sacrificer thereby takes possession of.

[^egg_785]: 308:4 See above, XIII, 2, 2, 11.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It became five fold (Paṅkti metre) and entered the year and those five parts became the half-months. He followed it up by means of the fifteen-fold sets of (Paśus) and gained it. Having gained it, he retained it by means of the fifteen-fold ones, because those fifteen-fold sets symbolise the half-months. By seizing the fifteen-fold sets of animals, the Yajamāna is gaining the half- months.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

सो ऽस्मात्सृष्टः प᳘राङैत्स᳘ पङ्क्ति᳘र्भूत्वा[[!!]] सम्वत्सरं प्रा᳘विश᳘त्ते ऽर्द्धमासा᳘ अभवंस्तं᳘ पञ्चदशि᳘भिरनु प्रायुङ्क्त[[!!]] त᳘माप्नोत्त᳘माप्त्वा᳘ पञ्चदशि᳘भिर᳘वारुन्द्धार्द्धमासा᳘नाम्वा᳘ ऽएषा᳘ प्प्रतिमा य᳘त्पञ्चदशि᳘नो य᳘त्पञ्चदशि᳘न ऽआल᳘भते ऽर्द्धमासा᳘नेव तैर्य्य᳘जमानो᳘ ऽवरुन्धे॥

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
