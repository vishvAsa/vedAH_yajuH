<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--the Jagatī consists of forty-eight syllables, and cattle are of Jāgata (movable) nature: by means of the Jagatī he (the priest) secures cattle for him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He ties forty-eight to the middle Yūpa, for the Jagatī metre consists of forty- eight syllables and the Paśus are Jāgata (of moving nature). By means of the Jagatī, he thus secures cattle for him (Yajamāna).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(ते ऽष्टा᳘) अष्टा᳘चत्वारिᳫँ᳭शदक्षरा ज᳘गती जा᳘गताः पश᳘वो ज᳘गत्यै᳘वास्मै पशून᳘वरुन्द्धे॥
</details>
