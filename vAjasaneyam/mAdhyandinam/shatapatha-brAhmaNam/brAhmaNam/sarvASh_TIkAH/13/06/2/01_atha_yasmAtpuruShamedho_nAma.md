<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

1. And as to why it is called Purushamedha:--The stronghold (pur) doubtless is these worlds, and the Purusha (spirit) is he that blows here (the wind), he bides (śī) in this stronghold (pur): hence he is the Purusha.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now, why it is called Puruṣamedha - these worlds are doubtless, the for- tress and this one who blows (Vāyu) is the Puruṣa. He is lying (Śī) in this fort (Pur); hence he is the Puruṣa.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘स्मात्पुरुषमेधो ना᳘म॥  
(मे) इमे वै᳘ लोकाः पू᳘रय᳘मेव पु᳘रुषो᳘ यो ऽयम्प᳘वते᳘ सो ऽस्या᳘म्पुरि᳘ शेते
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘स्मात्पुरुषमेधो ना᳘म॥  
इमे वै᳘ लोकाः पू᳘रय᳘मेव पु᳘रुषोॗ योऽयम् प᳘वतेॗ सोऽस्या᳘म् पुरि᳘ शेते
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

अथ यस्मात् पुरुषमेधो नाम । इमे वै लोकाः पूः । अयमेव पुरुषः । यो ऽयं पवते । सो ऽस्यां पुरि शेते ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
