<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--for the nobility he seizes a Rājanya, for the Rājanya is the nobility: he thus joins nobility to nobility; --for the Maruts (he seizes) a Vaiśya, for the Maruts are the clans (peasants): he thus joins peasantry to peasantry;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

for, Rājanya indeed is of the Kṣatra class. Thus by means of a Kṣatra he enriches the Kṣatra class. He binds a Vaiśya (merchant) for the Maruts, because Maruts are the Viś.
</details>

</details>
