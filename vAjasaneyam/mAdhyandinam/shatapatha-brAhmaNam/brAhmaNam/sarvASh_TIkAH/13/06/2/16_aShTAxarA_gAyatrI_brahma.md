<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--the Gāyatrī consists of eight syllables, and the Gāyatrī is the Brahman (n.): he thus makes the Brahman to be the ultimate thing of this universe, whence they say that the Brahman is the ultimate thing of this universe.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Lastly he ties (to the Yūpa) eight Paśus and the Gāyatrī has eight syllables. Gāyatrī is the Brahman. He thus makes the Brahman the greatest (ultimate) principle of this universe.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(ते ऽष्टा᳘) अष्टा᳘क्षरा गायत्री ब्र᳘ह्म गायत्री तद्ब्र᳘ह्मै᳘वैत᳘दस्य स᳘र्व्वस्योत्तमं᳘ करोति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

अष्टा᳘क्षरा गायत्री ब्र᳘ह्म गायत्री तद्ब्र᳘ह्मैॗवैत᳘दस्य स᳘र्वस्योत्तमं करोति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

अष्टाक्षरा गायत्री । ब्रह्म गायत्री । तत् ब्रह्मैवैतदस्य सर्वस्योत्तमं करोति ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

अऽष्टाक्षरा गायत्री ब्रह्म गायत्री तद्ब्र᳘ह्मै᳘वैत᳘दस्य स᳘र्वस्योत्तमं᳘ दधाति
</details>
