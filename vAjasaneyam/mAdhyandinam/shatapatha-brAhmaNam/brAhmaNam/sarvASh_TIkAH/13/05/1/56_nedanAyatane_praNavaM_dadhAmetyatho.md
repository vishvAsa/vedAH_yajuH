<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Let him not proceed thus, but let him insert the hymn as a whole.--'The swift racer hath gone forward to the slaying . . .'--The racer hath gone forward to the highest place . . .'--
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This they do with the idea of placing the Praṇava (OM) in an unusual place or to make a singular usage have the sense of plural.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

नेद᳘नायतने प्रणवन्द᳘धामेत्य᳘थो ने᳘देकवचने᳘न बहुवचन᳘म्व्यवा᳘यामे᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

नेद᳘नायतने प्रणवं द᳘धामेत्य᳘थो ने᳘देकवचने᳘न बहुवचन᳘म् व्यवा᳘यामे᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

नेदनायतने प्रणवं दधामेति । अथो नेत् एकवचनेन बहुवचनं व्यवायामेति ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

नेद᳘नायतने प्रणवं द᳘धामेत्य᳘थो ने᳘दकवचनेन बहु वचनं᳘ व्यवा᳘यामे᳘ति
</details>
