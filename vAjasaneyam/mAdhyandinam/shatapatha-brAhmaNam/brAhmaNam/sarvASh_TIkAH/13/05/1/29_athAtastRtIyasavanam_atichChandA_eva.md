<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

11. Then as to the evening-service. The atīchandas verse (Vāj. S. IV, 25), 'Unto that god Savitr̥ within the two bowls (do I sing praises [^egg_989]),' is the opening verse [^egg_990] of the Vaiśvadeva(-śastra [^egg_991]): the mystic import thereof is the same as of the former (atichandas verse).

[^egg_989]: 381:2 For the complete verse see III, 3, 2, 1 2.

[^egg_990]: 381:3 This verse is again recited thrice, and thus takes the place of the ordinary opening triplet.

[^egg_991]: 381:4 For this Śastra, recited after the Ārbhava-Pavamāna-stotra, see part ii, p. 361.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then about the evening savana—the starting hymn of the Vaiśvadeva is in Atichandas - "Abhi Tyam Devam Savitāramopyoh...." (to that God Savita en- closed within the two cups.....etc.)."
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(म) अथा᳘तस्तृतीयसवनम्॥  
(म᳘) अ᳘तिच्छन्दा ऽएव᳘ प्रतिप᳘द्वैश्वदेव᳘स्याभि त्य᳘न्देव᳘ᳫँ᳘ सविता᳘रमो᳘ण्योरि᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

अथा᳘तस्तृतीयसवनम्॥  
अ᳘तिछन्दा एव᳘ प्रतिप᳘द्वैश्वदेव᳘स्याभित्यं᳘ देव᳘ᳫं᳘ सविता᳘रमोॗण्योरि᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

अथातस्तृतीयसवनम् । अतिच्छन्दा एव प्रतिपत् वैश्वदेवस्य । **"अभि त्यं देव‍ँ सवितारमोण्योः"**- (वा. सं. ४ । २५) इति ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
