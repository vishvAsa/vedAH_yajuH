<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

7. Those same first two days, and an Aptoryāma [^egg_1036] Atirātra,--it was therewith that Kraivya, the Pāñcāla king, once performed sacrifice,

[^egg_1036]: 397:4 See part iii, introd. p. xx.

 Pāñcāla overlord of the Krivis seized a horse, meet for sacrifice, with offering-gifts of a hundred thousand (head of cattle).'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

An Aptoryama-Atirātra, they got performed by Kraivya the Pāñcāla king.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(नी ऽअ) अप्तोर्य्या᳘मो ऽतिरात्रस्ते᳘न हैते᳘न क्रै᳘व्य ऽईजे पा᳘ञ्चालो रा᳘जा क्रि᳘वय ऽइ᳘ति ह वै᳘ पुरा᳘ पञ्चालाना᳘चक्षते
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

अप्तोर्याॗमोऽतिरात्रस्ते᳘न हैते᳘न क्रै᳘व्यईजे पा᳘ञ्चालो रा᳘जा क्रि᳘वय इ᳘ति ह वै᳘ पुरा᳘ पञ्चालाना᳘चक्षते
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

अप्तोर्यामो ऽतिरात्रः । तेन हैतेन क्रैव्य ईजे पांचालो राजा । क्रिवय इति वै पुरा पंचालानाचक्षते ।
</details>
