<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And (he gives them) four gold plates weighing a hundred (grains): the meaning of this has been explained [^egg_889].

[^egg_889]: 348:3 XII, 7, 2, 13.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thousand means 'all' and Aśvamedha is all and it is for gaining all and for owning everything (he gives).
</details>
