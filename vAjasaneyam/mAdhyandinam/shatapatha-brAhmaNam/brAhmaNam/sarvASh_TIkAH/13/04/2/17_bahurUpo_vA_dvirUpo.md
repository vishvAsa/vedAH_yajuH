<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Which ever of these (varieties) multi-coloured, two- coloured are three-coloured, it should have the mark of the cart on the fore- head for being fit for immolation and it should ofcourse be very speedy.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>
