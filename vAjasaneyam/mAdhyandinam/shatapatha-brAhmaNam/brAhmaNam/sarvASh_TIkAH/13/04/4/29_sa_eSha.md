<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

The two (sets of) pītudāru (stakes) stand inside, and the bilva ones outside, for the eyes are inside, and the ears outside: he thus puts them in their own place.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Everything inside the fruit of that tree is eatable.
</details>
