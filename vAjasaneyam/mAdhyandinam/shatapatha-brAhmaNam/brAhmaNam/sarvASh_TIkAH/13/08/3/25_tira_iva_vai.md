<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and in secret, as it were, is what is unmarked: he thus secretes what is in secret.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That which is unmarked is concealed as it were and the Pitṛs are concealed. So he provides secrecy thereby.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

तिर᳘ ऽइव वै᳘ पित᳘रस्तिर᳘ ऽइव तद्य᳘दलक्षण᳘न्तिर᳘ एव त᳘त्तिरः᳘ करोति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

तिर᳘-इव वै पित᳘रस्तिर᳘-इव तद्य᳘दलक्षणं᳘ तिर᳘ एव त᳘त्तिरः᳘ करोति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

तिर इव वै पितरः । तिर इव तत् । यदलक्षणम् । तिर एव तत् तिरः करोति ॥ ८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
