<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Before sunrise (he does so), for, in secret, as it were, are the Fathers, and in secret, as it were, is the night; --in secret, indeed, he does this, (but) so that (the sun) should rise over him doing it: on both day and night he thus establishes him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He does it before the sunrise, for the Pitṛs are concealed as it were and night provides concealment and so he does it secretly.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

पु᳘रा ऽदित्य᳘स्योदया᳘त्तिर᳘ ऽइव वै᳘ पित᳘रस्तिर᳘ ऽइव रा᳘त्रिस्तिर᳘ ऽएव त᳘त्करोति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

पुॗरादित्य᳘स्योदया᳘त्तिर᳘-इव वै᳘ पित᳘रस्तिर᳘-इव रात्रिस्तिर᳘ एव त᳘त्करोति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

पुरा ऽऽदित्यस्योदयात् । तिर इव वै पितरः । तिर इव रात्रिः । तिर एव तत् करोति ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
