<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

While that (mound) is being made, they hold a bundle (of reed grass) to the left (north, uttarataḥ) of it,--that is offspring: they thus hold the (deceased's) offspring upwards (uttarataḥ).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

By making it upto the bottom of the knee, he does not leave room for another.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(त्त) तथा᳘ ऽपरस्मा ऽअवकाशन्न᳘ करोति त᳘स्य क्रिय᳘माणस्य तेजनी᳘मुत्तरतो᳘ धारयन्ति[[!!]]
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

तथा᳘परस्मा अवकाशं न᳘ करोति त᳘स्य क्रिय᳘माणस्य तेजनी᳘मुत्तरतो᳘ धार᳘यन्ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

तथा ऽपरस्मा अवकाशं न करोति । तस्य क्रियमाणस्य तेजनीमुत्तरतो धारयंति ।
</details>

</details>
