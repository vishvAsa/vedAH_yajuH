<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Those (altar bricks) he lays down with a formula, silently these: he thus keeps the divine separate from what belongs to the Fathers.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They are just like those bricks in the fire-altar. In the fire- altar, he placed them with (Yajus) formulas, whereas here he places them silently.
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>
