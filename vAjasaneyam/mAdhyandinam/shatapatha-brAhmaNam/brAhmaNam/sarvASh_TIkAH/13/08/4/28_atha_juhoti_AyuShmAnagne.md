<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

9. He then offers, with (Vāj. S. XXXV, 17), 'Long-lived be thou, O Agni, growing by offering, ghee-mouthed, ghee-born: drinking the sweet, pleasant cow's ghee, guard thou these, as a father does his son, hail!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then offers (saying) "O Agni, live long growing by offering, ghee- mouthed, ghee-born. Drinking the sweet, pleasing cow's ghee, you protect these, as a father does his son - Svāhā." He says thus so that he (Agni) may protect these.
</details>
