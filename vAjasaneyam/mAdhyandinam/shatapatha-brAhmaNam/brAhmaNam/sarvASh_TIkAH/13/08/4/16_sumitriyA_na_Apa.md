<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

5. They bathe at any place where there is water. With (Vāj. S. XXXV, 12), 'May the waters and plants be friendly unto us!' he takes water with his joined hands, --for water is a thunderbolt: with the thunderbolt he thus makes friendship,
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"May the waters and plants be friendly to us" - so saying he collects water with his two palms held close to each other.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
