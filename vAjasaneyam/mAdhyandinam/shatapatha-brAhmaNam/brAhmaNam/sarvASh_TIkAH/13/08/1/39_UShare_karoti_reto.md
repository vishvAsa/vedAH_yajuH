<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

14. He makes it on salt (barren) soil, for salt means seed;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He constructs it on barren soil, for, barren soil is vitality. Thus he par- takes in the productivity of that vitality, so that he gets progeny.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(त्यू) ऊषरे᳘ करोति॥  
रे᳘तो वा ऽऊ᳘षाः प्प्रज᳘ननन्त᳘देनम्प्रज᳘नन ऽआ᳘भजति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

ऊषरे᳘ करोति॥  
रे᳘तो वा ऊ᳘षाः प्रज᳘ननं त᳘देनम् प्रज᳘नन आ᳘भजति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

ऊषरे करोति । रेतो वा ऊषाः प्रजननम् । तदेनं प्रजनन आभजति ।
</details>
