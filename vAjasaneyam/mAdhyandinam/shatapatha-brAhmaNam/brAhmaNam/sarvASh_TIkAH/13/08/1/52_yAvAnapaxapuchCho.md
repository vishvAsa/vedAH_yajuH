<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

'Let him make it as large as the fire-altar without wings and tail,' say some, 'for like that of the fire-altar is this his (the Sacrificer's) body.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Let him make it as long as the first citi without its wings and tail" - so say some; because this body of his is as big as the fire-altar.
</details>

</details>

</details>
