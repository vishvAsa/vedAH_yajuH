<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--or in (the month of) Māgha, thinking, 'Lest (mā) sin (agha) be in us;' --or in summer (nidāgha), thinking, 'May thereby be removed (nidhā) our sin (agha)!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

or (he makes) in the month of Māgha, think- ing, "Let me have not (Mā) (Sin) (Agha), or in summer (Nidāgha) thinking let our sin (Agha) be removed (Nidha)."
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

माघे᳘ वा मा᳘ नो ऽघ᳘म्भूदि᳘ति निदाघे᳘ वा नि᳘ नो ऽघं᳘ धीयाता ऽइ᳘ति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

माघे᳘ वा मा᳘ नोऽघ᳘म् भूदि᳘ति निदाघे᳘ वा नि᳘ नोऽघं᳘ धीयाता इ᳘ति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

माघे वा । मा नो ऽघं भूदिति । निदाघे वा । नि नो ऽघं धीयाता इति ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

माघे᳘ वा मा नोऽघं᳘ भूदि᳘ति निदाघे वा नि᳘ नोघं᳘ धीयाता इ᳘ति ॥ ४ ॥
</details>
