<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

4. Let him make it in autumn, for the autumn is the Svadhā, and the Svadhā is the food of the Fathers: he thus places him along with food, the Svadhā;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(It should be made) in the autumn season, because (Śarad) autumn is Svadhā and Svadhā is the food of the Pitṛa. Thus he unites him (the departed one) along with his food, Svadhā;
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

शर᳘दि कुर्य्यात्॥  
(त्स्व) स्वधा वै᳘ शर᳘त्स्व᳘धो वै᳘ पितॄणाम᳘न्नं त᳘देनम᳘न्ने स्वधा᳘यान्दधाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

शर᳘दि कुर्यात्॥  
स्वधा वै᳘ शर᳘त्स्वॗधो वै᳘ पितॄणाम᳘न्नं त᳘देनम᳘न्ने स्वधा᳘यां दधाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

शरदि कुर्यात् । स्वधा वै शरत् । स्वधा उ वै पितॄणामन्नम् । तदेनमन्ने स्वधायां दधाति ।
</details>

</details>
