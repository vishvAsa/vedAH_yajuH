<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

18. One must not make it (too) large lest he should make the sin (of the deceased) large.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It (the monument) should not be very large, lest he should magnify the sin (of the deceased).

Let him not make it (the mound so made by keeping the loose soil so brought) too large; lest he magnifies the (deceased's) sin.
</details>
