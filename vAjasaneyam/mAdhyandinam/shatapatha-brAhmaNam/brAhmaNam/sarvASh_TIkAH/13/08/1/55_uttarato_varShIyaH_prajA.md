<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--and broader on the left (north [^egg_1136] or higher, uttara) side, for the later (uttara)

are offspring: he thus makes the offspring more excellent.

[^egg_1136]: 428:4 In reality, the north means here the side facing the north-east. The side of the tumulus is to form a quadrilateral, of which the two sides intersecting each other at the north corner, are to be longer than the two which intersect at the south corner; viz. each of the  former is to measure one man's length plus 9¼ aṅgulas (thumb's breadths), and each of the latter one man's length minus 9¼ aṅgulas. See comm. on Vāj. S. XXXV, 1.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus he makes his off-spring more excellent.
</details>
