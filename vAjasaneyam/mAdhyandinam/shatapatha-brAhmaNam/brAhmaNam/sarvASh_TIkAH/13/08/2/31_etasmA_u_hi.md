<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

To the right (south) side [^egg_1146] he removes this (plough and team), to the left (side) arty other: he thus keeps the divine separate from what' belongs to the Fathers.

[^egg_1146]: 432:2 Or rather to the south-west side, whilst at the Agnicayana (VII, 2, 2, 21) it was done towards the north-east.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He unyokes them towards the southern direction. On other occa- sion they are let off to the north. Thus he differentiates between the acts of gods and those Pitṛs. (Second Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>
