<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He throws out this (branch) to the right (south), the other [^egg_1142] to the left (north) side: he thus keeps the divine separate from what belongs to the Fathers.

[^egg_1142]: 431:1 Viz. that used in sweeping the Gārhapatya, VII, 7, 1, 5.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

So, for the sake of space for this deceased one, he begs of him (Yama). One of the sticks from the Palāśa bunch, he throws to the south and another to the north. Thus he differentiates between the godly act and the act concerning Pitṛs.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

यमो᳘ ह वा᳘ ऽअस्या᳘मवसा᳘नस्येष्टे त᳘मे᳘वास्मा ऽअस्या᳘मवसा᳘नं याचति ता᳘न्दक्षिणो᳘दस्यत्युदगि᳘तरान्दै᳘वञ्चैव[[!!]] तत्पि᳘त्र्यञ्च व्व्या᳘करोति॥
</details>

</details>

</details>
