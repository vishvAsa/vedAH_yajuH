<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

With a formula he sets up those, silently these: he thus keeps separate what refers to the gods and what refers to the Fathers. With (an) undefined (number of stones he encloses it), for undefined is yonder world.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He thus differentiates what is in respect of the Pitṛs from what is in respect of the gods. He does not keep a count of the stones (used for enclosing) because the yonder world is unlimited.
</details>
