<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

or lest that Agni should injure those cattle, for that (lump of clay) is Agni, and these (animals) are cattle.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For cattle are thunderbolts and this Agni is the seed; lest he should harm that seed by thunderbolt (he does not touch them) or (perhaps) this (lump of clay) being Agni and they animals, lest Agni should not harm these cattle (and so he does not touch them with it).
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

उपस्पर्शनाभावं प्रशंसति- **वज्रो वै पशव** इति । यतो वज्ररूपाः पशवः, मृत्पिण्डश्च रेतोरूपः, अतो वज्रेण रेतः विच्छिन्नं न करोमीति बुद्ध्या न स्पृशेत् पशून् । अथवा 'अयं' मृत्पिण्डो हि 'अग्निः,' अग्निः पशून् मा हिंसीदिति बुद्ध्या न स्पृशेत् । 'हिनसानि'- इति लोटि रूपम्, 'हिनसत्'- इति पञ्चमलकारे रूपम् ॥ ६ ॥
</details>
