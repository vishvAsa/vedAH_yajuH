<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--that is, 'do not injure anything!' He thereby lays vigour into the he-goat.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Don’t harm the sky and the earth, nor the air, nor the plants”. It means don’t scorch any of these. Thereby he imparts vigour into the he-goat.
</details>
