<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He does not touch them, lest he should injure that seed by the thunderbolt, for cattle are a thunderbolt, and this (clay) is seed;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He does not touch them (with it).
</details>
