<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For indeed Vāyu joins together or heals whatever wound or damage is caused.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

यद्वा᳘ ऽअस्यै᳘ क्षतं यद्वि᳘लिष्टं व्वायु᳘ना वै तत्स᳘न्धीयते
</details>

</details>

</details>
