<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

then thus, then thus. This is moving (from left) to right, for so (it goes) to the gods: with this and this one a means of healing is prepared; with this and this one he heals.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus starting with east, then thus and then thus from the south and thus moving (from the left) to the right, for in that direction it goes to the gods. (All these done) with this (ring finger). Thus healing is done with this (finger).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

अथेत्यथे᳘ति त᳘द्दक्षिणावृत्तद्धि᳘ देव᳘त्रा ऽन᳘या ऽन᳘या वै᳘ भेषजं᳘ क्रियते ऽन᳘यै᳘वैनामेत᳘द्भिषज्यति॥
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
