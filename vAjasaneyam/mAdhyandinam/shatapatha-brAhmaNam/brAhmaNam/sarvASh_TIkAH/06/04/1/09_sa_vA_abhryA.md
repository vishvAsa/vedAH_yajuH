<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

5. Now while digging with the spade, he says with speech 'I dig,' 'we dig,' for the spade is speech. It is for his undertaking that this bamboo (spade) is made;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

While he digs with the spade, he by word (orally) says "Let me dig—let us dig". For, the speech is spade. Only symbolically (for practical purpose) it is made of bamboo.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

स वा ऽअ᳘भ्र्या ख᳘नन्॥  
(न्वा) व्वाचा᳘ खनामि खनाम ऽइ᳘त्याह व्वाग्वा ऽअ᳘भ्रिरारम्भा᳘यै᳘वेयं᳘ व्वैणवी᳘ क्रियते
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

प्रागुक्तं खननसाधनं विदधत् तस्य वाग्रूपतामाह- **स वा अभ्र्या खनन्नि**ति ।

यदि वाग्भिस्तर्हि वाचैव खननं युक्तम्, किमभ्रिकरणेनेति, तत्राह- **आरम्भाये**ति । 'आरम्भाय' आलम्भनायैव इयं वेणुमयी अभ्रिः । क्रियते (श. प. ६ । ३ । १ । ३२ । भा.) ॥ ५ ॥
</details>
