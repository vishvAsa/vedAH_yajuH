<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

3. With two (formulas) he digs,--two-footed is the Sacrificer, and the Sacrificer is Agni: as great as Agni is, as great as is his measure, with so much he thus digs him up. And twofold also is that form of his, (consisting as it does of) clay and water.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

With two (chants) he digs—the Yajamāna is biped and Yajamāna is Agni, as great as Agni is; as great as is his size, with so much he thus digs him out. And two- fold is that form of his (Agni-altar) because it has clay and water.
</details>
