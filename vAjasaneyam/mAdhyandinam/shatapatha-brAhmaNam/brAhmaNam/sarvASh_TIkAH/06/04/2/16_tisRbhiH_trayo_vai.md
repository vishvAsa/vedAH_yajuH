<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

With three (verses); --there are three vital airs, the out-breathing, the in-breathing, and the through-breathing: these he thus lays into him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

These (chants) together have nine feet, for these are nine vital airs; seven in the head and two downwards, these (nine) he lays into him.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

त᳘द्दधाति ता᳘सां न᳘व पदा᳘नि न᳘व वै᳘ प्राणाः᳘ सप्त᳘तिसृ᳘भिस्त्र᳘यो ति᳘
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

तिसृभिः । त्रयो वै प्राणाः- प्राण उदानो व्यानः । तानेवास्मिन्नेतद्दधाति ।
</details>
