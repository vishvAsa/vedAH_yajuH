--thereby he implores a blessing upon the Sacrificer.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Thou gladdening the gods, make offerings to them”—it means, becoming a god, you worship, the gods with oblations. “O Agni! bestow plenty of wealth upon the Yajamāna”. Thereby he implores for blessings upon the sacrifice.
</details>

</details>

</details>
