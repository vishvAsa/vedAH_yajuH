<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And what comes to be like the year, comes to be like the Br̥hatī;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And what is made up in a year becomes made up of the Bṛhatī metre—because Bṛhatī is the year.
</details>
