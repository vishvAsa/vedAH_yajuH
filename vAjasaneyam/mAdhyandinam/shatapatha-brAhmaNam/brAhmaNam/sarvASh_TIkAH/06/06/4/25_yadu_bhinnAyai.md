<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now regarding the expiation in case of the ukhā getting broken, about which it was said that it will be explained later.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

य᳘दु भिन्ना᳘यै प्रा᳘यश्चित्तिमाहो᳘त्तरस्मिंस्त᳘दन्वाख्या᳘न ऽइ᳘ति॥
</details>

</details>
