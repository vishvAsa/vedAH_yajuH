<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

4. And when they give him (the Sacrificer) the fast-milk, he puts on a kindling-stick, after dipping it into the fast-milk.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When they offer him (the Yajamāna) the vrata milk (milk to be taken while fasting), he dips a samidh in that (milk) and places it (on the fire).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(स्ते᳘ ऽथ) अ᳘थ य᳘दा ऽस्मै व्रतं᳘ प्रय᳘च्छन्ति॥  
(न्त्य᳘) अ᳘थ व्व्रते न्य᳘ज्य समि᳘धमा᳘दधाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

अ᳘थ यॗदास्मै व्रत᳘म् प्रय᳘छन्ति॥  
अ᳘थ व्रते न्य᳘ज्य समि᳘धमा᳘दधाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

अथ यदा ऽस्मै व्रतं प्रयच्छन्ति- अथ व्रते न्यज्य समिधमादधाति ।
</details>

</details>

</details>
