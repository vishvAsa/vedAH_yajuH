<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

That vikaṅkata, then, is that first offering; it is that he now offers on this (fire), and he therewith gratifies him (Agni).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

From there the Vikaṅkata tree grew. So the first offering happens to be of Vikaṅkata. That he now offers into this (ukhā fire).
</details>

</details>
