<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

8. When the fire heats it, then the flame mounts up to it;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When the fire heats it (the ukhā), then the flame mounts upto it.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

तां᳘ य᳘दा ऽग्निः᳘ सन्त᳘पति॥  
(त्य᳘) अ᳘थैनामर्चिरा᳘रोहति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

तां᳘ यॗदाग्निः᳘ संत᳘पति॥  
अ᳘थैनामर्चिरा᳘रोहति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

तां यदा ऽग्निः सन्तपति- अथैनामर्चिरारोहति ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
