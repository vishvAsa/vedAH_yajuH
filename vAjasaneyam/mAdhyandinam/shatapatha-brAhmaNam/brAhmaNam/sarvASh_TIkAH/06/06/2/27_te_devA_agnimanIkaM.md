<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

The gods, having placed Agni in front, went up to the Asuras. The Asuras cut off the point of that flame held forward.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Those gods, having placed Agni in front, confronted the Asuras. The Asuras cut off the edges of his (Agni’s) flames that had come to their grip. That (cut edges of the flames) settled down on this earth. It became the Kramuka.
</details>
