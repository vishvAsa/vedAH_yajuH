<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

So only when the sacrifice is completed and the Audgrabhaṇas are offered, the ukhā should be mounted (on the fire).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

</details>
