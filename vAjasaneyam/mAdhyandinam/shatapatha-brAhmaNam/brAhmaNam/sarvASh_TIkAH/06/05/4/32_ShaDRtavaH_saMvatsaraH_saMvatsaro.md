<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

9. Now he digs with one (formula), he sets down (the fire-pan) with one, he kindles with one, he heats with one, he bakes (pac) with two, whence twice in the year food is ripened (pac); these amount to six,--six seasons are a year, and Agni is the year: as great as Agni is, as great as is his measure, so great does this become.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Six seasons (make) a year and Agni is the year. As great as Agni is, as great as is his size, so great does this become.

These come to six; six seasons (make) a year and Agni is the year; as great as Agni is, as great as is his size, so great does he become.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

अस्तु संवत्सरो ऽग्निः प्रजापतिना धृतः, तथा च किमायातमित्यत आह- **यावानग्निर्यावत्यस्य मात्रे**ति । यावानिति स्वरूपस्येयत्ता, यावतीत्यवयवस्य ।

**तावत्तद् भवती**ति । नैमित्तिकचित्या सह चितीनामप्यवयवानां षट्त्वात् सा मन्त्रगता सङ्ख्या ऋतुसङ्ख्याद्वारेणाग्नेर्मात्रया समा भवति । चित्यात्मकस्याग्नेः स्वरूपस्यैकत्वात् ऋतुषट्सङ्ख्यावतो ऽवयविनः संवत्सरस्याप्येकत्वाच्च सा मन्त्रगता सङ्ख्या संवत्सरद्वारेण अग्निर्यावान् तावती भवतीत्यर्थः ॥ ९॥
</details>
