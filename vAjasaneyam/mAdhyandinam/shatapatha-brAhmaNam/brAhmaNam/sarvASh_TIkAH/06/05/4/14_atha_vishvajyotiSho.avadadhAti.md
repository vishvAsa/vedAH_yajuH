<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He then lays down silently the all-light' (bricks). Having then placed fuel thereon he kindles it.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then silently places the Viśvajyoti bricks. Having then placed fuel thereon, he sets fire to it.
</details>

</details>
