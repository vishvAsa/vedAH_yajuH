<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--for Aditi, indeed, having fashioned it, offered it to the gods, her sons, to bake it; and in like manner does this one now, after fashioning it, offer it to the gods to bake it.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

In the like manner, this one (Yajamāna) having made it, gives to the Gods for baking.
</details>
