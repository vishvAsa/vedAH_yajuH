earthen womb; --'Aditi offered it unto her sons, thinking, They shall bake it!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"(She), gave it to her sons to bake"—after making the *ukhā* (she, Aditi) gave it to the gods, her sons for baking.
</details>
