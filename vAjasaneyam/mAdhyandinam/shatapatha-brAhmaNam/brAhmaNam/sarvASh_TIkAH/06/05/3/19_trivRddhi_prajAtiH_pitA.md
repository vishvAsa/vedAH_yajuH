<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

They are marked with three lines, for generation is threefold, father, mother, and son; or, the embryo, and the inner and outer membrane.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He draws three lines on them, because threefold is the generation viz., father, mother and the son. Hence (there are) the foetus, the membrane covering inner and the outer placenta.
</details>
