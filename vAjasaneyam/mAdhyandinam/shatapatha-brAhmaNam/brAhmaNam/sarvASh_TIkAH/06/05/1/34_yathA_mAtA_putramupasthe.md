<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'may she bear Agni in her womb, even as a mother (bears) her son in her lap!' --that is, 'as a mother would bear her son in her lap, so may she (Aditi) bear Agni in her womb!'
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>
