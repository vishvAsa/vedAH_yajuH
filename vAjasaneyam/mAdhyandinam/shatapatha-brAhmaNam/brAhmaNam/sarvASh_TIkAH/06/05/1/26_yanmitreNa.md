<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'by the wise, the clay suitable for the work;'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It is mixed by Vasus (devas); In as much as it was mixed by Rudras (in the form of the chant Rudraḥ saṁsṛjya) it is mixed by Rudras. “The clay fit for this work (of making the ukhā) is mixed by the wise”—for they (the Vasus and Rudras) are indeed wise and this clay is fit for the job.
</details>
