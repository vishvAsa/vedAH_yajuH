<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

9. Now when they said, 'Meditate ye (cetayadhvam)!' they doubtless meant to say, 'Seek ye a layer (citim icchata)!' and inasmuch as meditating (cetay) they saw them, therefore they are 'layers' (citayaḥ).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now, when they said, “Meditate (cetayadhvam)” they doubtless, meant to say, ‘Seek a layer (in the fire-altar—citimicchata)’ and in as much as they saw there (the bricks) while meditating, they are called citīs (layers).
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

चितिशब्दं निर्वक्ति- **ते यदब्रुवन्नि**ति । यस्मात् तेषु चोक्ताः प्रजापत्यादयः सर्वे ऽपि 'चेतयध्वमिति' उक्त्वा चितिं दृष्टवन्तः, 'तस्मात्' चितिसंसर्गात् चितिशब्दो निरुक्तः । "चिती सञ्ज्ञाने" (धा. पा. भ्वा. प. ३९) क्तिनि धातुतकारलोपे कृते रूपमिति मन्तव्यम् । यद्वा "चिञ् चयने"- (धा. पा. स्वा. उ. ५) इत्यस्य क्तिनि रूपम् ॥ ९ ॥
</details>
