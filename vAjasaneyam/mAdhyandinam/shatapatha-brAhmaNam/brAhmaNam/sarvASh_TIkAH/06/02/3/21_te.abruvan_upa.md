<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

8. They said, 'We will step nigh!' --'Wherewith?' --'With what is over and above these worlds!' --'So be it!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They said, “We will come near”—“Where with?”—they said “With what is over and above these worlds”—“Do so”.
</details>
