<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He searched for it; and what (part) of those (bodies) cast into the water had settled therein, that water he gathered; and what (had settled) in this earth, that clay (he gathered) [^egg_334]. And having gathered both that clay

and water, he made a brick: hence a brick consists of these two, clay and water.

[^egg_334]: 163:5 It seemed desirable here to leave the construction of the original text unchanged.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He collected those waters in which those cut-off (parts of the bodies) had settled. (Those parts) which were (settled) in this earth. That clay (he gathered). And having gathered, both that clay and water, he made a brick. Hence brick consists of these two, clay and water.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

तद्य᳘देषामप्सु प्र᳘विद्धानां प्रत्य᳘तिष्ठत्ता᳘ अपः स᳘मभरद᳘थ य᳘दस्यां तां मृ᳘दं त᳘दुभ᳘यᳫँ᳭ सम्भृ᳘त्य मृ᳘दं चापश्चे᳘ष्टकामकरोत्त᳘स्मादेत᳘दुभ᳘यमि᳘ष्टका भवति मृच्चा᳘पश्च॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

तद्य᳘देषामप्सु प्र᳘विद्धानाम् प्रत्यतिष्ठत्ता᳘ अपः स᳘मभरद᳘थ य᳘दस्यां ताम् मृ᳘दं त᳘दुभ᳘यᳫं सम्भृ᳘त्य मृ᳘दं चापश्चे᳘ष्टकामकरोक्ष᳘स्मादेत᳘दुभ᳘यमि᳘ष्टका भवति मृच्चा᳘पश्च॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

तद् यदेषामप्सु प्रविद्धानां प्रत्यतिष्ठत्- ता अपः समभरत् । अथ यदस्यां- तां मृदम् तदुभयं सम्भृत्य मृदं चापश्च- इष्टकामकरोत् । तस्मादेतदुभयमिष्टका भवति- मृच्चापश्च ॥ ८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
