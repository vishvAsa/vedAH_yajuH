<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

11. As to those glories, they are these same heads of the victims; and those (headless) trunks are these five layers (of the fire-altar): thus when he builds up the layers after putting on the heads of the victims, he thereby unites those trunks with those heads.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Those which are the glories are (same as) these heads of the paśus and those trunks (of the paśus) are those five layers of the fire-altar. That he puts on those heads of the paśus and builds up the altars, (virtually) amounts to uniting those trunks with those heads.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
