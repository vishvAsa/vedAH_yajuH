<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

2. He saw those five animals,--the Purusha (man), the horse, the bull, the ram, and the he-goat. Inasmuch as he saw (paś) them, they are (called) cattle (paśu).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He saw those, five paśus—the man, the horse, the bull, the ram and the he- goat. In as much as he saw (apaśyat) them, they are paśus (cattle).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(त्पु᳘) पु᳘रुषम᳘श्वं गाम᳘विमजं यद᳘पश्यत्त᳘स्मादेते᳘ पश᳘वः॥
</details>

पुरुषम᳘श्वं गाम᳘विमजं यद᳘पश्यत्त᳘स्मादेते᳘ पश᳘वः॥
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>
