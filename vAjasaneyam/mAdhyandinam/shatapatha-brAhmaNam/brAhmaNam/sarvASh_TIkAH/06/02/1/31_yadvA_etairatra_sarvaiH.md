for had Prajāpati then offered up all of them, he would certainly have reached the end of the fire (altar): hence were he (the Sacrificer) now to offer up all those (victims) he would certainly reach the end of the fire (altar).'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Here, then, some say, "It is in this context that one should offer up all those (five) paśus. For had Prajāpati offered sacrifice with all of them, he would have very well reached the completion of the fire (altar)".
</details>
