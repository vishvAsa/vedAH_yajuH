<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

7. He considered, 'Those glories (signs of excellence [^egg_330]) upon which I have set my mind are contained in the heads: well then, I will only put on the heads [^egg_331].'

[^egg_330]: 163:1 See VI, 1, 1, 4.

[^egg_331]: 163:2 That is, on the fire-altar, or (which is the same thing) on himself, Prajāpati, the sacrifice. The heads of the five victims are placed in (a dish introduced into) the bottom layer of the altar so as to impart stability to it. See VII, 5, 2, 1 seq.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He considered "Which glories I had contemplated are contained in the heads (of the paśus). Well, then, I will only take up the heads".
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

पशुशीर्षोपधानं विधास्यते, तदर्थं प्रजापतिकर्तृकं तदुपधानं दर्शयति- **स ऐक्षत या वै श्रीरि**ति । द्वितीया बहुवचनान्तम् । अत्र श्रीशब्देन अग्निरूपाणि विवक्षितानि । तानि 'अभ्यध्यासिषम्' ध्यातवानस्मि । 'ताः' इमाः श्रियः 'शीर्षसु' चक्षुराद्याश्रयेषु वर्त्तन्ते । 'हन्त'- इति । हर्षे ।

अतः **शीर्षाण्येवे**त्यादि निगदसिद्धम् [^१_३३] ।

[^१_३३]: कंठेषु तृणमंतर्द्धाय शिराँस्यादत्ते । का० श्रौ० सू० । १६ । १८
</details>
