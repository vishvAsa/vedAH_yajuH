<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

37. Now some, having in that way [^egg_344] obtained those heads, put them on (the fire-altar), thinking, 'Either way [^egg_345] are they animals.' But they (who do this) become mortal carcases, for unpropitiated are

those (heads) of theirs. In this way, indeed, they did put them on for Ashāḍḥi Sauśromateya [^egg_346];

[^egg_344]: 170:1 That is, according to Sāyaṇa, somehow or other, in some worldly manner, as by buying or begging them, without performing the animal sacrifice.

[^egg_345]: 170:2 That is to say, whether they are consecrated or unconsecrated, in either case they are 'paśavaḥ' or animal (victims). Sāy.

[^egg_346]: 171:1 The son of Ashāḍḥa and Suśromatā, according to Sāyaṇa.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It is (on the assumption) that they are paśus (fit for offering) on two grounds. (That they have been obtained and slaughtered). But those (who do thus) become decaying carcasses, for those heads (they offer) are unsanctified.
</details>
