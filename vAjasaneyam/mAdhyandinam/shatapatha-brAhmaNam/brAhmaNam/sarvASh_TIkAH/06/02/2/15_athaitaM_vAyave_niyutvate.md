<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

6. He then slaughters for Vāyu Niyutvat (the wind, driving a team of horses) that white, bearded (he-goat). When Prajāpati had produced living beings, he looked about him, and from exceeding delight his seed fell: it became that white, hornless, bearded he-goat (aja, 'unborn'); for seed is life-sap, and as far as there is life-sap, so far extends the self. And when he slaughters that one, then indeed he reaches the end of Agni (the fire-altar).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then, offers in sacrifice for Vāyu, the Niyutvat (the wind-god driven by a team of horses), that white hornless one (he-goat). When Prajāpati after creating the beings, looked around and experienced excess joy, due to which semen fell.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

वायव्य पशुपक्षं विधत्ते- **अथैतं वायव** इति । 'नियुत्वते' नियुत्वद्गुणकाय वायवे 'शुक्रः' वर्णतः 'तूपरः' विषाणरहितः, आलब्धव्यः । "प्रजापतिः प्रजाः सृष्ट्वा ऽनुव्यैक्षत" इत्यादिकं "तूपरो हि वायुः" इत्यन्तं ब्राह्मणं पशोः शुक्लत्वतूपरत्वयोर्नित्यत्वाद् वायुदेवत्यस्यैव स्तावकम् ।
</details>
