<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

It belongs to Vāyu, because Vāyu is the out-breathing; and to Niyutvat, because the teams are the in-breathing: he thus puts the out-breathing and in-breathing into him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It belongs to Vāyu because Vāyu is the prāṇa (in-going breath) and to Niyutvat since the team of horses (niyut) is udāna (outgoing breath). He thus puts (both) the prāṇa and the udāna into him.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>
