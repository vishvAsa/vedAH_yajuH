<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Verily, then, he who, knowing this, drives him about, makes up that whole and entire Prajāpati: let him therefore by all means drive him (Agni) about.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Let him therefore, by all means, drive him about (in the cart).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

स᳘ ह वा᳘ ऽएतᳫँ᳭ स᳘र्व्वं कृत्स्नं᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिᳫँ᳭ सं᳘स्करोति य᳘ ऽएवं᳘ विद्वा᳘न्वनीवाह्य᳘ते त᳘स्मादु व्वनीवाह्ये᳘तैव[[!!]]॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

स᳘ ह वा᳘ एतᳫं स᳘र्वं कृत्स्न᳘म् प्रजा᳘पतिᳫं स᳘ᳫं᳘स्करोति य᳘ एवं᳘ विद्वा᳘न्वनीवाह्य᳘ते त᳘स्मादु वनीवाह्ये᳘तैव᳟॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>

</details>
