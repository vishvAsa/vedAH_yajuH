<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--with whomsoever, mounted (on a chariot), the axle creaks, this is his own voice: hence when the axle creaks while Agni is mounted, this is the voice of Agni himself. It was Agni indeed whom the gods thereby praised and magnified; and in like manner does this (Sacrificer) thereby praise and magnify him: 'Agni roared like the thundering sky,'--the meaning of this has been explained [^egg_546].

[^egg_546]: 291:1 See above, VI, 7, 3, 2.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Hence, when the axle creaks then the Agni is mounted, this is the voice of Agni himself. And it is only Agni whom the gods praised and glorified. In the like manner does this one (Yajamāna) praise him and glorify him (by saying) "Agni roared like the thundering sky". The meaning of this has been told.
</details>
