3. Here now some say, 'It is by himself that he (Agni) is driven about; for by the Vishṇu-strides he drives forward, and by the Vātsapra he unyokes.' Let him not think this to be so;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Here now some say—'Going about driving Agni is achieved by his driving forward by Viṣṇu strides and by his unyoking by the Vātsapra'. It is not to be so construed.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

इत्थं वनीवाहनं कर्त्तव्यमिति विधाय, तस्य प्रकारान्तरेण प्राप्तिमाशङ्कते- **तद्धैक आहुः स्वयं वा एष** इति । अग्नेर्भृशं वहनमेवापेक्षितम् । तत् विष्णुक्रमणेनैव सवात्सप्रेण कृतमित्यभिप्रायः ।
</details>
