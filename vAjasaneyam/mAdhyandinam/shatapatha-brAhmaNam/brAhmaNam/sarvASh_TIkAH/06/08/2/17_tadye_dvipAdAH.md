<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--but at two separate times he throws them down: he thus throws them down by means of the two-footed animals.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He thus throws in two separate instalments symbolising the two- footed animals.
</details>

</details>
