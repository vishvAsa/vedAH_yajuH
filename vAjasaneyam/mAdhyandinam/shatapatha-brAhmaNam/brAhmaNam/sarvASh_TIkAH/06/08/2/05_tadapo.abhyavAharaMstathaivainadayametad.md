<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

They then took it down to (and threw it into) the water; and in like manner does this (Sacrificer) now take it down to the water.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

In the same manner, this Yajamāna throws the ashes into the water (and by taking a pinch of it recovers what is left of Agni's nature).
</details>

</details>
