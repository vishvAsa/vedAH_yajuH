<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

It thus is made equal to strength, and strength is indeed capable of sustaining him (Agni): by means of strength the gods did bear him, and by means of strength does he now bear him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Strength, it is, that deserves to carry him (Agni), because it is by means of strength that gods carried him. So this one (Yajamāna) carries him by means of strength.
</details>
