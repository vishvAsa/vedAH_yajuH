<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and inasmuch as they so dug out (ut-khan), it (the pan representing the worlds) is called 'utkhā,'--'utkhā' being what they mysteriously (esoterically) call 'ukhā,' for the gods love the mysterious.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Since they dug out, it became (*utkha*), and therefore it is called *ukhā* in a mystic style. For, gods like to be concealed (in mystic appellations).
</details>
