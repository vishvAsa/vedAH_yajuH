<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--with the hair (inside), for the hair are the metres, and the metres are indeed able to sustain him: by the metres the gods carried him, and by the metres he now carries him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(He carries him) with the hairs (inside), for the hairs are the metres and the metres deserve to hold him.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

लोमतश्छ᳘न्दाᳫँ᳭सि वै लो᳘मानि च्छ᳘न्दाᳫँ᳭सि वा᳘ ऽएतं य᳘न्तुमर्हन्ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

लोमतश्छ᳘न्दांसि वै लो᳘मानि छ᳘न्दांसि वा᳘ एतं य᳘न्तुमर्हन्ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
