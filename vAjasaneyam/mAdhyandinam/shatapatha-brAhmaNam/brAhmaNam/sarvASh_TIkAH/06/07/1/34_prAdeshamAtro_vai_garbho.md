<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for Vishṇu, as an embryo, was a span high: he thus makes the womb equal in size to the embryo.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This (seat) is a womb and the womb is made proportionate to the size of the embryo.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

प्रादेशमात्रो वै ग᳘र्भो व्वि᳘ष्णुर्यो᳘निरेषा ग᳘र्भसम्मितां तद्यो᳘निं करोति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
