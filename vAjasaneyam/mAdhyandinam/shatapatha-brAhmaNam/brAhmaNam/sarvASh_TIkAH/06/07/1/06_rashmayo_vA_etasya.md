<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He wears it with the knobs outside, for the knobs are his (the sun's) rays, and his rays arc outside.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It (the gold chain) has twenty-one knobs and he (the Sun) is the twenty-first. He wears it (in such a way) so that the knobs (project) outwards. The rays are the knobs of the Sun and the rays indeed, are (projecting) outwards.
</details>
