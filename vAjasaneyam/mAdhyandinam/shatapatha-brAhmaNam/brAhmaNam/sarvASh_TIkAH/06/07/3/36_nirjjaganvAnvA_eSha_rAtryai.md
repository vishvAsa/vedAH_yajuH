<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

10. [Vāj. S. XII, 13; R̥k S. X, 1, 1] 'The great hath stood up erect before the Dawns,'--for before the dawn the great one (Agni) indeed stands up erect; --'emerged from the gloom he hath come with light,'--for emerged from the gloom, the night, he indeed comes with light, with the day; --'well-shapen with white light,'--for he, Agni, is indeed well-shapen with white light;' --'when born, he hath filled all homesteads;' --all homesteads, doubtless, means these worlds, and these he indeed fills, when born.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(He does so saying) "The great one hath stood up erect before the dawns" for before the dawn, the great one (Agni) indeed stands up erect. "Emerged from the gloom he hath come with light"—for emerged from the gloom, the dark night, he indeed comes with light, with the day.
</details>
