<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--by saying 'Gracious--Gracious,' he appeases him, so that he may not injure any one, and thus he, being appeased, does not injure these worlds.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus he appeases him (Agni) so that he will be harmless. That is why he becomes appeased and does not injure these worlds.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

शम᳘यत्ये᳘वैनमेतद᳘हिᳫँ᳭सायै त᳘थो हैष᳘ ऽइमाल्ँ᳘लोका᳘ञ्छान्तो न᳘ हिनस्ति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

शम᳘यत्येॗवैनमेतद᳘हिंसायै त᳘थो हैष᳘ इमां᳘लोका᳘ञ्छान्तोन᳘ हिनस्ति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

शमयत्येवैनमेतद्- अहिंसायै । तथो हैष इमांल्लोकाञ्छान्तो न हिनस्ति ॥ १५ ॥
</details>

</details>
