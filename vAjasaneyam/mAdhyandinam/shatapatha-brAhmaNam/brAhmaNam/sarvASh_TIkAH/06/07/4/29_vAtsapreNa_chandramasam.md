<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

by the Vishṇu-strides Prajāpati created the regions, and by the Vātsapra the moon; by the Vishṇu-strides Prajāpati created that which has been, and by the Vātsapra that which shall be; by the Vishṇu-strides Prajāpati created possession (wealth), and by the Vātsapra hope;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

by Viṣṇu strides, Prajāpati created the regions and by Vātsapra, the moon; by the Viṣṇu strides, Prajāpati created that which has been and by the Vātsapra that which shall be;
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

व्वात्सप्रे᳘ण चन्द्र᳘मसं व्विष्णुक्रमैर्व्वै᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिर्भूतम᳘सृजत व्वात्सप्रे᳘ण भविष्य᳘त्
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

वा न᳘ विमुच्य᳘ते प्र त᳘द्दह्यतेत्
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

वात्सप्रेण चन्द्रमसम् (४) । विष्णुक्रमैर्वै प्रजापतिर्भूतमसृजत । वात्सप्रेण भविष्यत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

वात्सप्रेण च᳘न्द्रमसं विष्णुक्रमैर्वै᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिर्भूत᳘मसृजत वात्सप्रे᳘ण भविष्य᳘त्
</details>
