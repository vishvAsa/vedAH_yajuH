<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Well, on that (first) occasion, when he is being initiated, he, at the outset, combines both (performances) in the afternoon; for the afternoon is the same thing as the night.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Well, on that eve of his being initiated, he at the outset combines both (the performances) in the afternoon; for the afternoon is (as good as) night.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

एतद्वा᳘ ऽएने ऽअदो दी᳘क्षमाणः पुर᳘स्तादपराह्ण᳘ ऽउभे स᳘मस्यति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

</details>

</details>
