<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--he looks along the (four) quarters; he does not stretch forward his foot, thinking, 'Lest I lose these worlds!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

thinking, "Lest I devastate these worlds".
</details>
