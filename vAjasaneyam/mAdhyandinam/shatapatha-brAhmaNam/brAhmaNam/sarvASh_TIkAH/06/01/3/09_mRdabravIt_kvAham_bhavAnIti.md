<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

4. The clay (f.) said, 'What is to become of me?'--'Thou shalt be heated!' he said. It was heated,

and produced sand; for this clay becomes indeed heated when they plough it; and if only they plough very fine then it becomes, as it were, sandy. So much, then, as to that 'What is to become of me? what is to become of me [^egg_320]?'

[^egg_320]: 158:1 He means to say that he will leave this to be supplied in the enumeration of the subsequent creations.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The clay asked "What is to become of me". He said, "Get yourself heated". She got heated and she produced sand. Indeed the clay gets heated when they plough it; and if only they plough it fine, then it becomes (produced) like sand. Upto this (point) the question "What shall become of me". "What shall become of me". (Hereafter Prajāpati directly creates without heating of the raw materials).
</details>
