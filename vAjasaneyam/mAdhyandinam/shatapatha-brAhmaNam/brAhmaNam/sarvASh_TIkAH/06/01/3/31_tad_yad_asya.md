<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And because he gave him that name, the lightning became suchlike, for Aśani is the lightning: hence they say of him whom the lightning strikes, 'Aśani has smitten him.' He said, 'Surely, I am mightier than that: give me yet a name!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Lightening indeed, is the thunderbolt. That is why when some one is struck by lightening, they say that the thunderbolt killed him. The boy said, "With this (naming) I have become greater. So give me a name (again)".
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

तद्य᳘दस्य तन्नामा᳘करोद्विद्युत्त᳘द्रूप᳘मभवद्विद्युद्वा᳘ ऽअश᳘निस्त᳘स्माद्यं᳘ विद्युद्ध᳘न्त्यश᳘निरबधीदि᳘त्याहुः᳘ सो ऽब्रवीज्ज्या᳘यान्वा ऽअ᳘तो ऽस्मि धे᳘ह्येव᳘ मे नामे᳘ति॥
</details>
