<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Hence one should give a name to the boy that is born, for thereby one frees him from evil; --even a second, even a third (name), for thereby one frees him from evil time after time.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This shows that one should give a name to the son that is born; for thereby the evil (in the new born) is removed. He may be named for the second time or even for the third time and (each naming) destroys the evil in him.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त᳘स्मात्पुत्र᳘स्य जात᳘स्य ना᳘म कुर्यात्पाप्मा᳘नमे᳘वास्य तद᳘पहन्त्य᳘पि द्विती᳘यम᳘पि तृती᳘यमभिपूर्व्व᳘मे᳘वास्य त᳘त्पाप्मा᳘नम᳘पहन्ति॥
</details>

</details>
