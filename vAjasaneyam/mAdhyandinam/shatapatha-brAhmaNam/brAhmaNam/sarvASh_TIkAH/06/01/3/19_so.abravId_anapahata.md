<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He said, 'Nay, but I am not freed from (guarded against) evil; I have no name given me: give me a name!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He said "I am not rid of evil, nor I have any name given unto me. Give me a name".
</details>
