<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

It was heated, and produced clay; for indeed the foam is heated, when it floats on the water, covering it; and when one beats upon it, it indeed becomes clay.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For indeed, foam is heated when it gets tossed about on the waters by the wind and by getting beaten about, it, indeed, becomes clay.
</details>
