<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

9. Prajāpati said to him, 'My boy, why criest thou, when thou art born out of labour and trouble?'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Prajāpati said to him, "O Son! Why do you cry when you are born of toil and austerity".
</details>
