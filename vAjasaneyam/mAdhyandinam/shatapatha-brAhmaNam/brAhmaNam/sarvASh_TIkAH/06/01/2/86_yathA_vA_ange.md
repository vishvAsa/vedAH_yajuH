<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Let him not do so, for those bricks doubtless are his (Agni's) limbs, his joints; and it would be just as if he were to put a head on each limb, on each joint. But indeed, the fire which is deposited on the pile, that is the head of all those (bricks).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

These bricks are the limbs of Agni; the joints of Agni. This would (chanting the Yajus while touching the brick) amount to make a head at each limb and at each joint. Because, that Agni which is installed on the altar is indeed the head of all these (limbs).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

य᳘था वा ऽअ᳘ङ्गे ऽङ्गे प᳘र्व्वन्पर्व्वञ्छि᳘रः कुर्या᳘त्तादृक्तद्यो वाव᳘ चि᳘ते ऽग्नि᳘र्निधीय᳘ते त᳘दे᳘वैता᳘साᳫँ᳭ स᳘र्वासाᳫँ᳭ शि᳘रः॥
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

इष्टका इति यत् एतानि 'अस्य' अग्नेः 'अङ्गानि' 'परूंषि' पर्वाणि; तथा सति प्रतीष्टकं यजुरुपस्पर्शनेन शिरःकरणे प्रतिपर्व शिरः कृतवान् भवति, तच्चानिष्टम् अतः स्वाभिमतं शिरो दर्शयति- **यो वावे**ति । बिलप्रदेशे 'अग्निः' आहवनीयो निहितो भवति, 'तदेव‘ 'एतासाम्' इष्टकानां 'शिरः' इति उच्यते ॥ ३१ ॥
</details>
