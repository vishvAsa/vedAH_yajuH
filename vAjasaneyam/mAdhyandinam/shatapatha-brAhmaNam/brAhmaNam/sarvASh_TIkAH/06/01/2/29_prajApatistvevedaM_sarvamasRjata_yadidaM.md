<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

From the upper vital airs he created the gods, and from the lower vital airs the mortal creatures.' In whatever way he created thereafter, so he created; but indeed it was Prajāpati who created everything here, whatsoever exists.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Prajāpati himself, therefore, created all these and whatsoever that came to exist.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

प्रजा᳘पति᳘स्त्वे᳘वेदᳫँ᳭ स᳘र्व्वमसृजत य᳘दिदं किं᳘च॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

प्रजा᳘पतिॗस्त्वेॗवेदᳫं स᳘र्वमसृजत य᳘दिदं किं᳘ च॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

प्रजापतिस्त्वेवेदं सर्वमसृजत- यदिदं किंच ॥ ११ ॥
</details>
