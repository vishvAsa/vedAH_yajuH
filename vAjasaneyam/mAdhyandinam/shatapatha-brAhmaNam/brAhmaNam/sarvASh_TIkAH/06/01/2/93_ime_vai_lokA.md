<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'the cow forsooth means these worlds, for whatever walks (gam) that walks in these worlds [^egg_317]; and that

fire also is these worlds: therefore he may say, 'A cow.'

[^egg_317]: 156:1 It is not quite clear whether the author indulges in etymological trifling (go--gū). The Bombay MS. of Sāyaṇa reads,--imāṁstallokān gacchatīti kavana(?gavana)karmasādhanaṁ gośabdaṁ darśayati.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

These worlds are indeed cow, for, whatever walks (gam) that moves, towards these worlds. This fire (altar) that is being built up also encompasses these worlds. Therefore, he may say "A cow".
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

**इमे वै लोका गौरि**ति । 'यद्धि किञ्च‘- इत्यादिना गोत्वोपपादनम् । सर्वैर्गम्यमात्वेन इमे लोका गौः । कर्मार्थे गमेर्डोः प्रत्ययः (पा. उ. सू. २२५) । यतः चीयमानो ऽग्निः सर्वलोकात्मकः, स चैकः तस्माद् गौरेकः पशुरिति प्रतिवदेत् ॥ ३५ ॥
</details>
