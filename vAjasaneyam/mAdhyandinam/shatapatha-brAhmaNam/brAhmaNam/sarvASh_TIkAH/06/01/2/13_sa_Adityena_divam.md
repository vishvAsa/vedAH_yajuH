<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

By means of the Sun he entered into union with the Sky: thence an egg arose.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He caused a pair by uniting with the sky in his form as the Sun. Thence, came into being an egg.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

</details>
