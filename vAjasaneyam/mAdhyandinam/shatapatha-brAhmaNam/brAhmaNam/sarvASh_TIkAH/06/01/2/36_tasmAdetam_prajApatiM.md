<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

so (saying) Agni restored him: therefore, while being Prajāpati, they call him Agni; and verily, whosoever knows this, after him they call his father, grandfather, son, and grandson.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Prajāpati said, "They shall call me along with you. For, which one of the sons succeeds, by him they refer to his father and to his grand-father (and also refer to him) as the son (of so and so) and the grand son (of so and so). So let me be called after you; hence revive me". Agni said "So it be" and revived him. Therefore (since thus revived by Agni) being Prajāpati, he when revived, is called Agni.

They said "Yes". Hence, while being Prajāpati, they yet call him Agni.
</details>
