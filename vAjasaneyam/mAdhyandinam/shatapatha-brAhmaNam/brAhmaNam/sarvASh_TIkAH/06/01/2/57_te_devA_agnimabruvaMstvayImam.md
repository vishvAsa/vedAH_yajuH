<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

The gods said to Agni, 'In thee we will heal this our father Prajāpati.'--

 'Then I will enter into him, when whole,' he said.--'So be it!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The gods told Agni—"In thee (with your help) we will cure this Prajāpati, our father". He (Agni) replied—"Then, I shall enter into him as a whole".
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

ते देवा अग्निमब्रुवन्- त्वयीमं पितरं प्रजापतिं भिषज्यामेति । स वा ऽअहमेतस्मिन्त्सर्वस्मिन्नेव विज्ञानीति । तथेति ।
</details>
