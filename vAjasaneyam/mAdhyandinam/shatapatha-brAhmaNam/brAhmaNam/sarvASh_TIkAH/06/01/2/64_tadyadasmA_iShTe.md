<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

As happiness or cure was due to the offering (iṣṭa), therefore also, they are iṣṭakas (bricks).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

तद्य᳘दस्मा ऽइष्टे कम᳘भवत्त᳘स्माद्वेवे᳘ष्टकाः॥
</details>

</details>
