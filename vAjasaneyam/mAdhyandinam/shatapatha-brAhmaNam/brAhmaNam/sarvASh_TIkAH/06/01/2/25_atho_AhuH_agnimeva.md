<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

10. And so they say, 'After Agni having been created, the Vasus were created: he placed them on this (earth);
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Hence they say Agni was created and then followed the creation of Vasus, whom he placed on the earth.
</details>
