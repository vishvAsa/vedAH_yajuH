<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He touched it, saying, 'Bear thou seed!' From it the moon was created, for he (the moon) is seed.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He caressed it saying "Bear thou seed". From that was created the Moon. Moon is indeed the seed.
</details>
