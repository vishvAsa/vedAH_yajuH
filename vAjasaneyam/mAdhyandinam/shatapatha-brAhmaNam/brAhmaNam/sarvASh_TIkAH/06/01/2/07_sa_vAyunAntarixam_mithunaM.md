<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

By means of Vāyu he entered into union with the Air: thence an egg arose.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He caused a pair by uniting with the atmosphere in his form as vāyu. Thence, an egg appeared.
</details>
