<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He touched it, saying, 'Bear thou glory!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He caressed it saying "Bear thou glory". From that, yonder Sun was produced. He is indeed glory. The tears that flowed became the multi-coloured pebbles (aśma).
</details>
