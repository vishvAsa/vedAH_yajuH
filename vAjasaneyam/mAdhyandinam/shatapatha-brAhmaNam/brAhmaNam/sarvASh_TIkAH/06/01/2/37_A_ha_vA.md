<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Whosoever knows this, after him they call his father and grandfather (him) as son and grandfather.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

ऽआ᳘ ह वा᳘ ऽएनेन पित᳘रं पितामहं᳘ पुत्रम् पौ᳘त्रं चक्षते य᳘ ऽएवं व्वे᳘द॥
</details>
