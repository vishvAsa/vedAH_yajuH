<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

7. Let the largest be the one forming the southern flank;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The largest be the one forming the southern flank; then shorter and shorter till in the northern flank, the shortest (is errected).
</details>
