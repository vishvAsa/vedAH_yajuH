<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for Savitr̥ is the impeller (prasavitr̥) of the gods, and that stake is in reality the sacrificer himself; and sweet drink is everything here; he thus puts it in connection with all that, and Savitr̥, the impeller, impels it for him: therefore he says, 'The divine Savitr̥ anoint thee with sweet drink!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This Savitṛ (the prompter impells him (to do so). Therefore he says, "Divine Savitṛ may anoint thee with sweet drink".
</details>
