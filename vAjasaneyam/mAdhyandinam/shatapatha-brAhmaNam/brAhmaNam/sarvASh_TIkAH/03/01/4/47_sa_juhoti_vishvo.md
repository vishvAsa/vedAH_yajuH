<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

18. He offers, with the text (Vāj. S. IV, 8; Rig-veda V, 50, I), 'May every mortal espouse the friendship of the divine guide! every one prayeth for wealth: let him choose glory, that he may prosper, hail!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That he pours (offers) (chanting), “May every mortal (man) choose the friendship of the divine leader. Every one wishes for wealth. Let him choose splendour for thriving! Svāhā!”.
</details>
