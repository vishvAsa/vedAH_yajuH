<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Indeed this one (cow-speech) is the dominating one and this one is indeed sacrifice-worthy. (He says) "You are Aditi, having heads on both sides".
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

क्षत्रि᳘या᳘ ह्येषा᳘ यज्ञि᳘या᳘ ह्येषा᳘ ऽदितिरस्युभयतःशीर्ष्णी᳘ति
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

क्षत्रिया ह्येषा, यज्ञिया ह्येषा । **"अदितिरस्युभयतःशीर्ष्णी"**- (वा. सं. ४ । १९) इति ।
</details>
