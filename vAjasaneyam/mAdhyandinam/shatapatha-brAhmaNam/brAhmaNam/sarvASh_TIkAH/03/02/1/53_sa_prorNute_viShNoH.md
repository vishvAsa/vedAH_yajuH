17. He covers himself, with the text, 'Thou art Vishṇu's refuge, the refuge of the sacrificer.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He covers (him), (chanting) "You are the refuge of Viṣṇu, the refuge of the sacrificer".
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

</details>
