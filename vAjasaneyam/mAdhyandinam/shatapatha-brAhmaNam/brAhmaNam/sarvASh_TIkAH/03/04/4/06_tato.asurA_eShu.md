<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

The Asuras then built themselves castles in these worlds,--an iron one in this world, a silver one in the air, and a golden one in the sky.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then the Asuras built cities in these worlds, one of iron in this (earth), one of silver in the mid (aerial) region and one of gold in the sky.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

ततो᳘ ऽसुरा एषु᳘ लोके᳘षु पु᳘रश्चक्रिरे ऽयस्म᳘यीमेॗवास्मिं᳘लोके᳘ रजता᳘मन्तरिक्षे ह᳘रिणीं दिवि᳟॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

ततो ऽसुरा एषु लोकेषु पुरश्चक्रिरे- अयस्मयीमेवास्मिंल्लोके । रजतामन्तरिक्षे । हरिणीं दिवि ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
