<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And so does this one now become strong by means of cattle; the cattle turn unto him, and he makes the cattle subject to himself.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And so does this one (Yajamāna) now becomes strong by ''paśus'', ''paśus'' return to him and he makes ''paśus'' subject to himself.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

</details>

</details>
