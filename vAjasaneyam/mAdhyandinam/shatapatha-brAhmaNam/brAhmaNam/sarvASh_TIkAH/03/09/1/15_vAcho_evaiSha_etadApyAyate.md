<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And so does this one now become strong by speech, and speech turns unto him, and he makes speech subject to himself.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And so does this one (Yajamāna) becomes strong by speech. Speech returns to him and he makes speech subject to himself.
</details>
