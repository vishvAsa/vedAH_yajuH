<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And so does this one now become strong by means of the Brahman; the priesthood turns unto him, he makes the priesthood subject to himself.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And so does this one (Yajamāna) now becomes strong by means of the Brāhmaṇa; the priest-hood returns to him and he makes the Brāhmaṇa community subject to himself.
</details>
