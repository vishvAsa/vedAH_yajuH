<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And so does this one now become strong by abundance; abundance

turns unto him, and he makes abundance subject to himself.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And so does this one (Yajamāna) now becomes strong by abundance; abundance returns to him and he makes abundance subject to himself.
</details>
