<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

'May the divine Savitr̥ hear my prayer, Hail!' whereby he means to say, 'May the divine Savitr̥ hear this (prayer) of mine, may he vouchsafe it to me;' for Savitr̥ is the impeller of the gods; impelled by him he goes for that sap of the sacrifice.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“May the divine hear this offering prayer of mine”. For, Savitṛ, is the impeller of the gods and impelled by Savitṛ he goes for the sap of the sacrifice.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

सविता वै᳘ देवा᳘नां प्रसविता त᳘त्सवितृ᳘प्रसूत ए᳘वैत᳘द्यज्ञ᳘स्य र᳘सम᳘च्छैति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

सविता वै᳘ देवा᳘नाम् प्रसविता त᳘त्सवितृ᳘प्रसूत एॗवैत᳘द्यज्ञ᳘स्य र᳘सम᳘छैति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

सविता वै देवानां प्रसविता । । तत्- सवितृप्रसूत एवैतद् यज्ञस्य रसमच्छैति ॥ १४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

सविता वै᳘ देवा᳘नां प्रसविता सवितृ᳘प्रसूत ए᳘वैतं᳘ यज्ञ᳘स्य र᳘सम᳘च्छैति ॥१२॥
</details>
