<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

whereby, for the second time, as it were, the hair of the beard and the arm-pits and other parts of the body [^egg_195] grow on them; whereby it is on the head that one first

becomes grey, and then, again, in the last stage of life, one becomes grey all over.

[^egg_195]: 52:2 The word 'durbīriṇāni' is of doubtful meaning, the etymology proposed by Sāyaṇa having little claim to being seriously considered. In the St. Petersb. Dict. the meaning 'bristly' is assigned to it, as applied to the hair of the beard.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

**लुप्तोपममे**तत् । दुष्टानि बीरणानीव । पुनरेव यौवनावस्थायां 'अग्रे' प्रथमं 'शिर्षणि' शिरस्येव 'पलितो भवति' केशेषु शौक्ल्यं भवति । अनंतरं 'उत्तमे' चरमे वयसि । सर्वदेहः 'पलितो भवति' श्वेतरोमांकित इत्यर्थः ॥ ६ ॥
</details>
