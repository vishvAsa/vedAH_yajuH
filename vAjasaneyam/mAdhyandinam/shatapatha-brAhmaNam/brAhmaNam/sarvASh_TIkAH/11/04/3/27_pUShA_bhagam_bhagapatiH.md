<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

15. 'May Pūshan, wealth, the lord of wealth, bestow wealth upon me at this sacrifice, svāhā!' Pūshan, taking the oblation, departed and restored her wealth to her.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Pūṣā, the wealthy and lord of wealth, may in this sacrifice endow prosperity unto me – svāhā” – Pūṣā received the oblation and departed; later gave wealth unto her.
</details>
