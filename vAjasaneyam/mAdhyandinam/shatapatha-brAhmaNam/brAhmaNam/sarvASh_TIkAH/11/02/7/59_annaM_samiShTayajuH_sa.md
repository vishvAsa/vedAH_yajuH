<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

31. The Samishṭayajus is food; and, verily, whosoever knows the Samishṭayajus to be food secures for himself food; and whatever is to be gained by food all that he now gains.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The samiṣṭayajus is the food — whosoever knows the samiṣṭayajus as food, gains food. Whatever is obtainable by food, he attains all that.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(दा᳘) अ᳘न्नᳫँ᳭ समिष्टयजुः[[!!]]॥  
स यो᳘ ह वा ऽअ᳘न्नᳫँ᳭ समिष्टयजुरि᳘ति व्वेदा᳘व हा᳘न्नᳫँ᳭ रुंधे᳘ ऽथो यत्किञ्चा᳘न्नेन ज᳘य्यᳫँ᳭ स᳘र्व्वᳫँ᳭ हैव त᳘ज्जयति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

अन्नं समिष्टयजुः । स यो ह वा अन्नं समिष्टयजुरिति वेद । अव हान्नं रुन्धे । अथो यत्किंचान्नेन जय्यम्, सर्वं हैव तज्जयति ॥ ३१ ॥
</details>
