<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

The Āgnīdhra is the Spring,

whence forest-fires take place in spring, for that is a form of Agni. The Adhvaryu is the Summer, for summer is, as it were, scorched; and the Adhvaryu comes forth (from the sacrificial ground) like something scorched [^egg_173].

[^egg_173]: 45:1 Viz. from his constant attendance on the sacrificial fires.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That is a form of Agni. The Adhvaryu is the summer; because the summer is scorched as it were. The Adhvaryu emerges (from the sacrificial site) as if scorched.
</details>

</details>
