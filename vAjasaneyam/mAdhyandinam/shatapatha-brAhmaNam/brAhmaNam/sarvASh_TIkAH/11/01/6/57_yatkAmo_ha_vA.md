<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and, verily, for whatever wish one performs that sacrifice, that wish of his is fulfilled.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

With what desire, verily, one performs this sacrifice, that desire is fulfilled for him.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

य᳘त्कामो ह वा᳘ ऽएते᳘न यज्ञे᳘न य᳘जते᳘ सो ऽस्मै का᳘मः स᳘मृध्यते॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

य᳘त्कामो ह वा᳘ एते᳘न यज्ञे᳘न य᳘जतेॗ सोऽस्मै का᳘मः स᳘मृध्यते॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

यत्कामो ह वा एतेन यज्ञेन यजते- सो ऽस्मै कामः समृध्यते ॥ २० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
