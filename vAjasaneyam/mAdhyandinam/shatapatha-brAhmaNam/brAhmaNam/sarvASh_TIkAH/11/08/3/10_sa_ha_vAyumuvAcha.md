<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He said to man, 'I will perform sacrifice with thee: I will lay hands upon thee.'--'Nay,' said he, 'speak unto the cattle!'
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
