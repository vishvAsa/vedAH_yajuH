<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They con- tended with regard to this very sacrifice (which is) Prajāpati (thinking), "This should be ours. May this be ours!" Then the Asuras with arrogance (thought) "Wherein shall we offer?" They went on offering into their own mouths. Offering that way, they were vanquished.

It was for this very sacrifice they clamoured with Prajāpati (saying). "This one shall be ours; this one shall be ours".
</details>
