<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

11. Or after three days, thinking, 'There are three seasons in the year, and children are born when fashioned for a year: we thus lay speech into this one as soon as he has been born.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Or on the third day; for, there are three seasons for a year and children are born after a pregnancy of one year. So he thinks, "Let us put speech into him as soon as he is born."
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
