<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

8. Or after twenty-four days, thinking, 'There are twenty-four half-months in the year, and children are born when fashioned for a year: we thus lay speech into this one as soon as he has been born.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Or on the twenty-fourth day (after initiation), on the ground – "There are twenty-four fortnights for a year and children are born after one year's pregnancy and let us impart speech to him soon after he is born."
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

पक्षांतरमाह- **अथ चतुर्विंशत्यह** इति । चतुर्विंशतिसंख्याकानामह्नां समाहारश्चतुर्विंशत्यहः कालः । चतुर्विंशतिदिवसपरिमिते काले गते सतीत्यर्थः । **चतुर्विंशतिर्वा** इति- दिवसगतार्द्धमाससंख्यया संवत्सरसंपत्तिः । गतमन्यत् ॥ ८ ॥
</details>
