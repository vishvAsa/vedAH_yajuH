<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

10. Or after six days, thinking, 'There are six seasons in the year, and children are born when fashioned for a year: we thus lay speech into this one as soon as he has been born.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Or on the sixth day; for, there are six seasons for a year and chil- dren are born after one year's pregnancy. "Let us impart speech into him soon after he is born"– so he thinks.
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
