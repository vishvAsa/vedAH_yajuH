<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

But if he heals it in any other way than this, it would be just as if one tried to put together something that is broken with something else that is broken, or as if one were to apply some poison as lotion to a broken part [^egg_336]. Let him therefore appoint only one who knows this (to officiate as) his Brahman, and not one who does not know this.

[^egg_336]: 104:4 ? Or, as if one were to put some fluid into some broken (vessel; or, on some broken part),--yathā śīrṇena bhagnena anyac chīrṇaṁ bhagnaṁ vastu saṁdhitset saṁdhātum icchet; yathā vā śīrṇe garaṁ bhaktāvayave garam abhinidadhyāt praḥdattipeta (? prakshipet), Sāy.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This is how he rectifies the broken sacrifices. If he were to heal it in some other way, it would be like putting together two different parts or would be like applying some poison in the place of an ointment to a broken limb. Therefore the Brahmā should be one who knows thus (procedure of treatment) and not one who does not know.
</details>

</details>

</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

तत्र दृष्टांतमाह- **यथा शीर्णेने**ति । 'यथा' 'शीर्णेन' भग्नेन अन्यत् 'शीर्णं' भग्नं वस्तु 'संधित्सेत्' संधातुमिच्छेत् । 'यथा वा शीर्णे' रुग्णे ऽवयवे 'गरं' विषं 'अभिनिदध्यात्' प्रक्षिपेत् । एवमेव तत्तद्व्याहृतिहोमरूपं प्रायश्चित्तमित्यर्थः ।

उक्तप्रायश्चित्तपरिज्ञानवानेव ब्रह्मा कार्य इति विधत्ते- **तस्मादेवंविदमेवे**ति । 'एवंविदं' उक्तब्राह्मणार्थपरिज्ञानवंतम् । स्पष्टमन्यत् ॥ ६ ॥
</details>
