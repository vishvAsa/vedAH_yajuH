<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

But if it should not be known (where the mistake has occurred), ye should make offering in the Āhavanīya after uttering

rapidly all (the three sacred words [^egg_333]): thus one heals the R̥g-veda by the R̥g-veda [^egg_334], the Yajur-veda by the Yajur-veda, and the Sāma-veda by the Sāma-veda; --even as one would put together joint with joint [^egg_335], so does he put together (the broken part of the sacrifice) whoever heals it by means of these (three sacred words).

[^egg_333]: 104:1 According to Sāyaṇa, offering would be used with the formula 'Bhūr bhuvaḥ svaḥ, svāhā!'

[^egg_334]: 104:2 Viz. by the word 'bhūḥ,' representing that Veda.

[^egg_335]: 104:3 Yathā khalu loke bhagnaṁ hastapādādiparva tatsannihitenānyena parvaṇā purushāya saṁdadhyāt saṁśleshayet, evam evānena vyāhr̥tijñānena tat tad avedoktaṁ prabhr̥shṭam aṅgaṁ punaḥ sahitaṁ bhavati, Sāy.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

If it is due to Yajus, four ladle-full of ghee should be offered in the Āgnīdhrīya altar or Anvāhārya Vacana chanting "Bhūvah." If it fails due to Sāmaveda, four ladle-full of ghee should be offered in the Āhavanīya altar, chanting "Svah." If you cannot identify as to which of the three Vedas is the cause for failure, you should offer at the Āhavanīya chanting all the three (Bhūḥ, Bhūvaḥ and Svaḥ). In this way the Ṛgveda is healed by the Ṛk, the Yajurveda by the Yajus and the Sāmaveda by the Sāman even as one would join together the broken parts of the limbs (with their corresponding parts).
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

**स यथा पर्वणे**त्यादि । 'यथा' खलु लोके भग्नं हस्तपादादि 'पर्व' यत्सन्निहितेनान्येन 'पर्वणा' पुरुषाय 'संदध्यात्' संश्लेषयेत् । एवं अनेन तत्तद्व्याहृतिज्ञानेन तत्तद्वेदोक्तप्रभ्रष्टमंगं पुनः संहितं भवतीत्यर्थः ।

एतदेव व्यतिरेकेण द्रढयति- **अथ यो हे**ति । 'यः' खलु 'अतः' अस्मात् उक्तव्याहृतिहोमरूपात् भेषजात् 'अन्येन' कर्मणा 'भिषज्यति' ।
</details>
