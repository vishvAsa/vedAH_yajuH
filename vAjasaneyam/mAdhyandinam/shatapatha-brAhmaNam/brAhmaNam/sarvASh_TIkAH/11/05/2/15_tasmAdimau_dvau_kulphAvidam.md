<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and the oblation to Indra and Agni is the knuckles: this (oblation) belongs to two deities whence there are

these two knuckles. The (oblation) to Mahendra is this (shank); that to Viśvakarman this (thigh): this (oblation) is indistinct, whence this (thigh) also is indistinct.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This offering is indistinct and hence this (thigh) is also indistinct.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त᳘स्मादिमौ द्वौ᳘ कुल्फा᳘विदं᳘ माहेन्द्र᳘मिदं᳘ व्वैश्वकर्म्मणं तद्वा ऽअ᳘निरुक्तं भवति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

त᳘स्मादिमौ द्वौ᳘ कुल्फा᳘विद᳘म् माहेन्द्र᳘मिदं᳘ वैश्वकर्मणं तद्वा अ᳘निरुक्तम् भवति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

तस्मादिमी द्वौ कुल्फौ । इदं माहेन्द्रम् । इदं वैश्वकर्मणम् । तद्वा अनिरुक्तं भवति ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
