<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

he asked.--'Having, without delay, churned out fire, and taken out an offering-fire in whatever direction the wind might be blowing, I would perform an offering to Vāyu (the wind): I would then know that my Agnihotra would be successful, belonging as it would to all deities; for all beings, indeed, pass over into the wind, and from out of the wind they are again produced [^egg_279].

[^egg_279]: 84:1 At the time of dissolution (layakāle) they pass into the wind; and at the time of creation (sr̥shṭikāle) they are again created, Sāy.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"I would quickly churn out fire (afresh), take out the Āhavanīya in the direction where the wind blows and make an offering to Vāyu." I would thereby conclude that an Agnihotra is a success, since it would belong to all the dieties. Because all beings get absorbed in Vāyu and are again produced out of Vāyu. This would be the atonement and I should not be comitting that wrong (since I know the vidyā).
</details>
