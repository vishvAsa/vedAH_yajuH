<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He said, 'If, at that very time, the Anvāhāryapacana fire were to go out, dost thou know what danger there is in that case for him who offers?' --'I know it,' he replied; 'before long all the cattle would die in the case of him who would

not know this; but by dint of knowledge I myself have prevailed.'--'What is that knowledge, and what the atonement?'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He said – "If at that very time, the Dakṣiṇāgni is to get extinguished, what danger awaits the one who makes the offering?" Uddālaka said – "I know; very soon all his cattle will die, if he does not happen to know (the vidyā); I have got over it by my knowledge." "What is that knowledge and what is the expiation ?" asked Śauceya.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

**यत्र त** इत्यादि । 'यत्र' यस्मिन् 'एतस्मिन्नेव' प्रागुक्ते होमकाले 'अन्वाहार्यपचनः' दक्षिणाग्निरनुगच्छेत् ।

**वेत्थे**त्यादि पूर्ववत् ।

भयस्वरूपमाह- **पुरा ऽचिरादस्ये**ति । 'सर्वे' गवाद्याः पशवः । व्याख्यातचरमन्यत् ।
</details>
