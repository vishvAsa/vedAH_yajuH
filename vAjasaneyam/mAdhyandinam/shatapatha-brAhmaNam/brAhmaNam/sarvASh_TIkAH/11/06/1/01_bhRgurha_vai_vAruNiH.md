<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

1. Now, Bhr̥gu, the son of Varuṇa, deemed himself superior to his father Varuṇa in knowledge [^egg_347].

[^egg_347]: 108:3 On this legend, see Prof. Weber, Indische Streifen, I, p. 24 seqq., where the scenes here depicted are taken to be reflections of the popular belief of the time as to the punishments awaiting the guilty in a future existence.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Bhrgu, the son of Varuṇa, considered himself superior to his father in knowledge.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

भृ᳘गुर्ह वै व्वा᳘रुणिः॥  
(र्व्व᳘) व्व᳘रुणम्पित᳘रम्विद्यया᳘ ऽतिमेने
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

भृ᳘गुर्ह वै वा᳘रुणिः॥  
व᳘रुणम् पित᳘रं विद्यया᳘तिमेने
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

भृगुर्ह वै वारुणिर्वरुणं पितरं विद्यया ऽतिमेने ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
