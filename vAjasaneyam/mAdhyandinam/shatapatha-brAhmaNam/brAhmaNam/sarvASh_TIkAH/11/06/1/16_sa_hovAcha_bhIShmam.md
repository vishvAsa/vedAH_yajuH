<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

There men were cut- ting men, chopping off limb by limb and saying, "This to me; this you." He (Bhrgu) exclaimed, "What a horrible thing! men here have chopped off men limb by limb and sharing (among them)." They said, "This is how, indeed these men dealt with us in the yonder world and so we now return that (treatment) to them." He asked, "Is there not any atonement for this?" "Yes, there is," they said, "Your father knows it."
</details>
