<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--for Pūshan is cattle, and with cattle Varuṇa on that occasion stole along; and in like manner does this one now steal along with cattle.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Pūṣan is the cattle. It is with the cattle that Varuṇa chased it.
</details>
