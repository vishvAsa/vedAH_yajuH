<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

6. He prepares those (saṁsr̥p) oblations,--a cake on twelve, or eight, potsherds for Savitr̥; for Savitr̥ is the impeller of the gods: impelled by Savitr̥, Varuṇa on that occasion stole along; and in like manner does this one now steal along impelled by Savitr̥.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Therefore this one (Yajamāna) steals forth behind it only on being impelled by Savitr.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

सविता वै᳘ देवा᳘नां प्रसविता᳘ सवितृ᳘प्रसूत ऽएव तद्व᳘रुणो ऽनुस᳘मसर्पत्
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

सविता वै᳘ देवा᳘नाम् प्रसविता᳘ सवितृ᳘प्रसूत एव तद्व᳘रुणो ऽनुस᳘मसर्पत्
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

सविता वै देवानां प्रसविता, सवितृप्रसूत एव तद् वरुणो ऽनुसमसर्पत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

सविता वै᳘ देवा᳘नां प्रसविता᳘ सवितृ᳘प्रसूतो वा᳘ एनं तद्व᳘रुणोऽनुस᳘मसर्पत्
</details>
