<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--for Br̥haspati means holiness, and with holiness Varuṇa on that occasion stole along;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Bṛhaspati is Brahman. It is with the Brahman that Varuṇa chased it. Therefore this one (Yajamāna) steals forth behind it with Brahman.
</details>

</details>

</details>
