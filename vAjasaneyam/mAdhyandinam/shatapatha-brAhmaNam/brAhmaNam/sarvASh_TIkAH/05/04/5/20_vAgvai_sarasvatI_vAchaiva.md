<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--for Sarasvatī is speech, and it was with speech that Varuṇa on that occasion stole along;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Sarasvatī is indeed speech. It is with speech that Varuņa chased it.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

व्वाग्वै स᳘रस्वती व्वा᳘चैव तद्व᳘रुणो ऽनुस᳘मसर्पत्
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

वाग्वै स᳘रस्वती वाॗचैव तद्व᳘रुणो ऽनुस᳘मसर्पत्
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

वाग्वै सरस्वती । वाचैव तद्वरुणो ऽनुसमसर्पत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
