<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

The chariot is indeed a thunderbolt; and the Sacrificer is Indra; --he is Indra for a twofold reason, namely because he is a

 Kshatriya, and because he is a Sacrificer: therefore he says, 'Indra's thunderbolt thou art.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This person (Yajamāna) is Indra on two counts; he is a Kṣatriya and he is a Yajamāna. So he says "Thou art Indra's thunderbolt".
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

द्वये᳘न वा᳘ ऽएष ऽइ᳘न्द्रो भवति य᳘च्च क्षत्रि᳘यो य᳘दु च य᳘जमानस्त᳘स्मादाहे᳘न्द्रस्य व्व᳘ज्रो ऽसी᳘ति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

द्वयेन वा ऽएष इन्द्रो भवति- यच्च क्षत्रियः, यदु च यजमानः । तस्मादाह- इन्द्रस्य वज्रो ऽसीति ॥ ४ ॥
</details>
