<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'with that strength be thou strong and wealth-winning for us, O courser, and victorious at the gathering!' --wealth means food: he thus means to say, 'And be thou a food-winner for us at this our

sacrifice, at the gathering of the gods win thou this sacrifice, Prajāpati!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(He says) "Become winner of food (for us) and cause us to cross over the battle of the sacrifice". Vāja means food and (he says) "Become winner of food for us and enable us to cross over this divine battle (i.e. sacrifice)". Thereby he says 'Win thou this sacrifice, namely Prajāpati, for us' when he says "Become winner of food and enable us to cross over this battle of sacrifice".
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

**"तेन नो वाजिन् बलवान् बलेन वाजजिच्च भव, समने च पारयिष्णुः"**- (वा० सं० ९ । ९) इति । अन्नं वै वाजः, अन्नज्जिच्च न एधि । अस्मिंश्च नो यज्ञे देवसमन ऽइमं यज्ञं प्रजापतिमुज्जय- इत्येवैतदाह ॥ १० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

पूर्वार्द्धस्य तात्पर्यमाह- **तेन न** इति । यज्ञप्रजापत्योस्तादात्म्यं युक्तम् ।

‘समने पारयिष्णुः’-इत्यस्याभिप्रायमाह- **अस्मिंश्चनो यज्ञ** इति । 'देवसमने' समनमिति सङ्ग्रामनाम (निघ० २ । १७ । १६) । देवसमाज लक्षणे ऽस्मिन् यज्ञे । गतार्थमन्यत् ॥ १० ॥
</details>
