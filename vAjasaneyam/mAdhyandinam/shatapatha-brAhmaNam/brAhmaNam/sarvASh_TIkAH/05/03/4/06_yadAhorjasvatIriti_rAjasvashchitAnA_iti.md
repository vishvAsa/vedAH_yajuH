<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'wherewith they anointed Mitra and Varuṇa,' for therewith they did anoint (sprinkle) Mitra and Varuṇa; --'wherewith they guided
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Sapful and 'knowers of how to make kings"— by 'sapful' he means 'full of essence' and by 'knowers of how to make kings' he means to say "which are recognised as making the kings" (He says) "Wherewith they anointed Mitra and Varuṇa"—for, with those (waters), they did anoint Mitra and Varuṇa.
</details>
