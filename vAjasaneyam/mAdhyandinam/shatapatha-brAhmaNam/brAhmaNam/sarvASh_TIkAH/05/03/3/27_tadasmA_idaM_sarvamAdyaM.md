<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He thereby causes everything here to be food for him (the king);
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That makes all these (others) the food (enjoyable subjects) and only the Brahmin class (priesthood) is kept apart (not subordinate to him).
</details>
