<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And he, the Sūta, assuredly is one of his (the king's) jewels: it is for him that he is thereby consecrated; and him he makes his own faithful (follower).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He, the messenger is indeed one of his jewels. Hence he is consecrated by him.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

एतद्वा᳘ ऽअस्यै᳘कᳫँ᳭ र᳘त्नं य᳘त्सूतस्त᳘स्मा ऽए᳘वैते᳘न सूय᳘ते
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

एतद्वा᳘ अस्यैकं᳘ र᳘त्नं य᳘त्सूतस्त᳘स्मा एॗवैते᳘न सूय᳘ते
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

एतद्वा ऽअस्यैकं रत्नं- यत् सूतः । तस्मा ऽएवैतेन सूयते ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
