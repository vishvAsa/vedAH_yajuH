<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

11. He ascends, with, 'We have become Prajāpati's children;' for he who offers the Vājapeya indeed becomes Prajāpati's child:
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He ascends (thinking) "We have become Prajāpati's children". He who performs Vājapeya does indeed become Prajāpati's child.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

स᳘ रोहति॥  
प्रजा᳘पतेः प्रजा᳘ ऽअभूमे᳘ति प्रजा᳘पते᳘र्ह्येष᳘ प्रजा भ᳘वति यो᳘ व्वाजपे᳘येन य᳘जते॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

स᳘ रोहति॥  
प्रजा᳘पतेः प्रजा᳘ अभूमे᳘ति प्रजा᳘पतेॗर्ह्येष᳘ प्रजा भ᳘वति यो᳘ वाजपे᳘येन य᳘जते॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

स रोहति- **"प्रजापतेः प्रजा अमूम"**- इति । प्रजापतेर्ह्येष प्रजा भवति- यो वाजपेयेन यजते ॥ ११ ॥
</details>
