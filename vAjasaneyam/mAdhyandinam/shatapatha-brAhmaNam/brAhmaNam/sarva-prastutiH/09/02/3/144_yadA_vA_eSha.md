<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

यदा᳓ वा᳓ एष᳓ समिध्य᳓ते᳓ ऽथैत᳓स्मिन्हवीं᳓षिप्र᳓जुह्वति ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'on thee, when kindled, offerings are poured forth;' --for when he (the fire) is kindled, they make offerings on him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"We worship you with sacrifice at the place where- from you were born" -(meaning) “this(earth) is his own birth-place; where I worship by sacrifice." "On you, the offerings are poured forth when kindled"-for, when he blazes forth, then the oblations are offered into it.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

यदा वा᳘ ऽएष᳘ समिध्यते᳘ ऽथैत᳘स्मिन्हवी᳘ᳫँ᳘षि प्र᳘जुह्वति॥
</details>

</details>
