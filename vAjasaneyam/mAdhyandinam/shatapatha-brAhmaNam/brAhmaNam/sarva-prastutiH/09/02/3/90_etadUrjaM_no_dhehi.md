<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

एत᳓दू᳓र्जं नो धेहि द्विप᳓दे च᳓तुष्पद इ᳓त्याशि᳓षमा᳓शास्ते॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'Grant thou food to our two-footed and four-footed one!' he thereby invokes a blessing.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Give us energy, for the two- footed as well as the four-footed”—thereby he seeks a blessing.
</details>

</details>
