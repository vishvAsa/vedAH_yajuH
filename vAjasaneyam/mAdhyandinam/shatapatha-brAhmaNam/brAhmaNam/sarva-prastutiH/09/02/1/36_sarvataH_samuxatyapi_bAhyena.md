<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

सर्व᳓तः स᳓मुक्षत्य᳓पि बा᳓ह्येन परिश्रि᳓तः सर्व᳓त एवा᳙स्मिन्नेत᳓द्रूप᳓मुत्तमं᳓ दधाति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

everywhere he thus bestows on him the highest form; --by means of sacrificial grass-stalks (he sprinkles), for they are pure and meet for sacrifice; by means of their tops (he sprinkles), for the top (is sacred) to the gods.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He sprinkles all over even out- side the enclosing stones. (He sprinkles) with *darbha* grass, for *darbhas* are pure and fit for sacrifice.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

सर्व्व᳘तः स᳘मुक्षत्य᳘पि बा᳘ह्येन परिश्रि᳘तः सर्व्व᳘त ऽए᳘वास्मिन्नेत᳘द्रूप᳘मुत्तमं᳘ दधाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

सर्व᳘तः स᳘मुक्षत्य᳘पि बा᳘ह्येन परिष्रि᳘तः सर्व᳘त एॗवास्मिन्नेत᳘द्रूप᳘मुत्तमं᳘ दधाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

सर्वतः समुक्षति । अपि बाह्येन परिश्रितः । सर्वत एवास्मिन्नेतद्रूपमुत्तमं दधाति ।
</details>
