<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓थैवै᳙त᳓द्य᳓जमानो वैश्वानरे᳓ण पाप्मा᳓नं दग्ध्वा᳓पहतपाप्मैत᳓त्क᳓र्म कुरुते॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Sacrificer now, by Vaiśvānara, burn out evil, and perform this rite freed from evil.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Similarly, this Yajamāna, now after having burnt out all evil and becoming sinless, performs this rite.
</details>

</details>
