<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓थैवै᳙त᳓द्य᳓जमानो मृ᳓धः पाप्मा᳓नं हत्वा᳓पहतपाप्मैत᳓त्क᳓र्म कुरुते॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

For now the gods, having warded off the spurners, evil, performed this rite freed from evil; and in like manner does the Sacrificer now, having warded off the spurners, evil, now perform this rite freed from evil.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

In the same way, this Yajamāna now, hav- ing killed those evil ones who scorn him, gets freed of sin and performs this rite (of Upasthāna).
</details>

</details>
