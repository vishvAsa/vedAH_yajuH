<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

वा᳓र्त्रहत्याय श᳓वसे ।  
सह᳓दानुं पुरुहूत क्षिय᳓न्तमि᳓ति वा᳓र्त्रघ्नीभ्यां प्रथमा᳓भ्यामु᳓पतिष्ठते ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

4. [He approaches reverently the fire-altar [^egg_509], with, Vāj. S. XVIII, 68-74], 'For mighty strength that smiteth Vr̥tra, and for victory in battle, we call thee hither, O Indra!'

[^egg_509]: 275:3 According to Kāty. Śr. XVII, 7, 1, this ceremony should take place on the completion of each layer, after it has been covered with loose earth; cf. paragraph 11.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"O Indra, we call you here for the mighty prowess that killed Vṛtra; for victory in battle" and "O Indra! often-invoked! smash the handless Kuṇāvu hiding here with the Danus ......" thus with the first two mantras related to the killer of Vṛtra, he worshipfully stands by the Agni-altar.
</details>
