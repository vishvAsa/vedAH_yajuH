</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

The gods, espying the Asuras, snatched up the sacrifice, and began doing something else [^egg_453]. They (the Asuras) went away again, thinking, 'It is something else they are doing.'

[^egg_453]: 258:4 Prof. Delbrück, Altind. Syntax, p. 429, makes this last clause part of the Asuras’ speech or  thoughts,--'one thing they have undertaken to do, and another they are doing.' This can hardly be right.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The gods, noticing the Asuras, stopped the sacrifice and got engaged in some other work. Thinking that they, (gods) are doing something else the Asuras returned.
</details>
