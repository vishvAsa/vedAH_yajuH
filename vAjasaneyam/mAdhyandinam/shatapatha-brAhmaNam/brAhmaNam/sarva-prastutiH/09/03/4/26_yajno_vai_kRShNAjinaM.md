<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

यज्ञो᳓ वै᳓ कृष्णाजिनं᳓ यज्ञ᳓ एवै᳙नमेत᳓दभि᳓षिञ्चति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--for the black antelope skin is (a symbol of) the sacrifice: it is thus at the sacrifice that he anoints him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus he consecrates him by the sacrifice. He (anoints him) on the hairy side (of the skin); for, the hairs are the metres and thus he anoints him on the metres. He anoints him on the left side of the fire-altar; the significance of this will be told in due course.
</details>

</details>

</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

इत्थं सक्रमं पार्थहोममभिधायेदानीं यजमानस्य कृष्णाजिने ऽभिषेकं विधत्ते- **अथे**ति ।

तत् कृष्णाजिनं यज्ञात्मना प्रशंसति- **यज्ञो वा** इति । कृष्णमृगरूपेण यज्ञस्य पलायनात् कृष्णाजिनस्य यज्ञत्वम् । तथा पलायनं तैत्तिरीयके श्रूयते- "यज्ञो देवेभ्यो निलायत कृष्णो रूपं कृत्वा"- (तै. सं. ६ । २ । ४ । १) इति ।
</details>
