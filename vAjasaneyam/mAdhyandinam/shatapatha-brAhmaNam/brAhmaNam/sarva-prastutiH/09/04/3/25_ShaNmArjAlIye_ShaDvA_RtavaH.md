<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ष᳓ण्मार्जाली᳓ये ।  
ष᳓ड्वा᳓ ऋत᳓वः पित᳓रस्तं᳓ हैत᳓मृत᳓वः पित᳓रो दक्षिणतः᳓ प᳓र्यूहिरे ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

8. Six (he places) on the Mārjālīya,--these are the six seasons, the Fathers; for the seasons, the

 Fathers [^egg_413], indeed, heaped up (a rampart) round that (fire) from the south.

[^egg_413]: 244:1 Sāyaṇa refers to a passage in the Taittirīyaka, according to which a dying man is changed to whatever season he dies in, whence the six seasons are the representatives of all the deceased ancestors. Since the Fathers reside in the southern region it is from that quarter that, by laying down the bricks, they are supposed to raise a rampart for the Mārjālīya fire.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He places six bricks in the Mārjālīya hearth, for, there are six sea- sons, the pitṛs. The seasons, namely the pitṛs indeed put up a wall round that fire from the south.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

एवं- **षण्मार्जालीय** इति ।

अथ कथं तत्र षण्णामिष्टकानामुपधानमिति तत्राह- **षड्वा ऋतव** इति । यस्मिन्नृतौ यो म्रियेत स जनः स एवर्तुर्भवति । अयमर्थस्तैत्तिरीयके श्रूयते- "यदि वसन्ता प्रमीयते वसन्तो भवति"- (तै. ब्रा.) इत्यादिना । यतश्च ऋत्वात्मकाः पितरस्तं मार्जालीयं धिष्ण्यं दक्षिणभागे व्यवस्थापितवन्तः अत इदानीं स एष मार्जालीय एषां देवानां दक्षिणभागे ऽवतिष्ठते । एवमृत्वात्मकपितृसम्बन्धात्तत्रोपधेयानामिष्टकानामृतुसंख्यानुविधानमुपपन्नम् ।
</details>
