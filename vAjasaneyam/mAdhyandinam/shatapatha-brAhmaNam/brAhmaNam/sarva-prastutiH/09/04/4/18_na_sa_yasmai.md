<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

न᳓ स᳓ य᳓स्मै न᳓ त᳓द्य᳓दग्नौ᳓ जुहो᳓ति त᳓दग्नि᳓मभि᳓षिञ्चति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Now when he offers in the fire he thereby consecrates (anoints) Agni, and that divine body of his, being consecrated by Soma, becomes consecrated by the nectar of immortality.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When he offers (the pressed Soma) in the fire, he actu- ally anoints Agni and that divine body of Agni, being consecrated by Soma is virtually becoming consecrated by the nectar.
</details>
