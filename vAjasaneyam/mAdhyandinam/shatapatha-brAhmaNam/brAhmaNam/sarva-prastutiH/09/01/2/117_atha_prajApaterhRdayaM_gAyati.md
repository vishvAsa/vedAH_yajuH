<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

40. He then sings the heart of Prajāpati [^egg_304];

[^egg_304]: 180:1 The Prajāpati-hr̥daya, or Prajāpater hr̥dayam, as figured for chanting, is given, Sāma-v. Calc. ed. vol. ii, p. 499. It consists of the words, imāḥ prajāḥ prajāpate(r) hr̥dayam prajārūpam ajījane, with inserted stobhas and modulations. It is followed by a simpler form, which is perhaps the one used on the present occasion.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then sings (that Sāman) which is known as the heart of Prajāpati. The heart is yonder sun. He is oily; for, the heart is oily (smooth).
</details>
