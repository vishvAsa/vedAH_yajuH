<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

बहिर्वेदी᳙यं᳓ वै᳓ वे᳓दिरस्यै᳓ त᳓द्बहिर्धा᳓ शु᳓चं दधाति॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--outside the sacrificial ground (vedi); for the Vedi is this (earth): he thus puts pain outside this earth.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Outside this vedi (sacrifical site). Vedi is indeed this earth and he throws away the pain out- side this earth. Facing the east and standing by the side of the right thigh of the vedi, he throws it southwards (saying), "Let your pain reach him whom we hate." Thereby his pain reaches him whom he hates. One can also say, "Let the pain reach so and so" naming the one whom he hates so that enemy will have no sway any more on him.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

बहिर्व्वे᳘दीयं वै व्वे᳘दिरस्यै त᳘द्बहिर्धा शु᳘चं दधाति॥ (अर्धप्रपाठकः ५५)
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

बहिर्वेॗदीयं वै वे᳘दिरस्यै त᳘द्बहिर्धा शु᳘चं दधाति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

बहिर्वेदि । इयं वै वेदिः । अस्यै तद्बहिर्धा शुचं दधाति ॥ ११ ॥
</details>
