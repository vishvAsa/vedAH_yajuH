<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

They spake, 'Think ye upon this, how we may make this body of ours boneless and immortal!' They spake, 'Meditate (cit) ye!'
</details>
