<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

यज्ञो᳓ वै᳓ न᳓मो यज्ञे᳓नैवै᳙नमेत᳓न्नमस्कारे᳓ण नमस्यति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

'Reverence! reverence!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(He says), “Namo namaḥ.” Namaḥ is the sacrifice; by means of this sacrifice he does prostra- tion, he pays homage to him. So one should not say, “Namaste” (reverence to you) to anyone unworthy of sacrifice.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

</details>
