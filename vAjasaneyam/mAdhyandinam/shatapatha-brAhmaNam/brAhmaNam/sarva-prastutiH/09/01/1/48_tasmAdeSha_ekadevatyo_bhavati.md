<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓स्मादेष᳓ एकदेवत्यो᳙ भवति रौ᳓द्र एतं᳓ ह्ये᳙ते᳓न प्रीणा᳓ति॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Hence this (section) is addressed to a single deity, to Rudra; for it is him he thereby gratifies.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Therefore this (first anuvāka) is Ekadevatya (meant for a single deity) exclusively for Rudra. He satisfied him (Rudra) by means of this.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>
