<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

4. He contracts [^egg_565] (the right wing) inside on both

sides [^egg_566] by just four finger-breadths, and expands [^egg_567] it outside on both sides [^egg_566] by four finger-breadths: he thus expands it by just as much as he contracts it;

[^egg_565]: 300:5 Or, he draws in, draws together (upasamūhati).

[^egg_566]: 301:1 That is, on both sides of that part of the wing which joins the body of the altar he draws in by four finger-breadths the two long sides of the wing, thus changing the parallelogram into a trapezium, without altering the superficial area of the wing. On the plan of the altar given in part ii, p. 419, the effect of this manipulation on the wings and tail is indicated by pointed lines.--Sāyaṇa remarks,--ubhayataḥ pakshasya pārśvadvaye, antarataḥ cityāgner madhyadeśe caturaṅgulam upasamūhati saṁkarshati praveśayatīty arthaḥ; bāhyataḥ agnimadhyād bāhyadeśe caturaṅgulaṁ vyudūhati, ante vivardhayati.

[^egg_567]: 301:2 Or, he draws out, or draws asunder (vyudūhati).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He contracts inside on both sides (of the wing when it is connected to the body) by four finger- breadths and expands it outside on both sides by four finger-breadths. He thus expands it as much as he contracts it.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

पक्षपुच्छकरणे कञ्चिद्विशेषः कात्यायनेन सूत्रित- "पक्षपुच्छाप्ययेषु चतुरंगुलं चतुरंगुलं संकर्षति विकर्षत्यन्ते" इति (का. श्रौ. सू. १६ । ८ । २०) ।

अप्ययाः सन्धयः, तदिदं विधत्ते- **स चतुरंगुलमेवोभयत** इति । 'उभयतः' पक्षस्य पार्श्वद्वये 'अन्तरतः' चित्याग्नेर्मध्यदेशे 'चतुरंगुलम्' 'उपसमूहति' संकर्षति प्रवेशयतीत्यर्थः। पक्षस्य पार्श्वद्वये 'बाह्यतः' अग्निमध्यात् बाह्यप्रदेशे चतुरङ्गुलं 'व्युदूहति' अन्ते विवर्धयति । यावदेवोपसमूहतीत्यादेरयमर्थः- न्यूनातिरेकपरिहारेण समूहनसमानं व्युदूहनकरणं सप्तपुरुषपरिमितचित्याग्नेराधिक्यन्यूनतापरिहाराय इति ।
</details>
