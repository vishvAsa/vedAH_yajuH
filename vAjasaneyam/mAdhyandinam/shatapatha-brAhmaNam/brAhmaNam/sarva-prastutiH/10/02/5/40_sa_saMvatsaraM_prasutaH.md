<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Soma should be pressed for a year. The year is all, and one hundred and onefold is all; by means of all he thus gains everything.
</details>
