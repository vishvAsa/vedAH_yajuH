<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

6. He measures it (the altar) by the man with upstretched arms [^egg_580];

[^egg_580]: 305:3 That is to say, wherever he speaks of man's lengths, the height to which a man reaches with his upstretched arms is understood; the particular man who supplies this (relative) standard of measure being the Sacrificer.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He is measured by the length of a man with upstretched arms. For, the sacrifice is a man and by him (as unit) everything here is measured.
</details>
