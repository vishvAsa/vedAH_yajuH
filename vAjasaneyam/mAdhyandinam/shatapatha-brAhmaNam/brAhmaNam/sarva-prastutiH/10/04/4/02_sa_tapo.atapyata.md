<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स᳓ त᳓पो ऽतप्यत — सह᳓स्रं संवत्सरा᳓न् ।  
पाप्मा᳓नं विजि᳓हासन् ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He practised austerities for a thousand years, striving to leave evil behind him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He observed tapas (austerities) for one thousand years, hoping to get rid of that evil.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

स त᳘पो ऽतप्यत सह᳘स्रᳫँ᳭ संव्वत्सरा᳘न्पाप्मा᳘नं व्विजि᳘हासन्॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

स त᳘पोऽतप्यत सह᳘स्रᳫं संवत्सरा᳘न्पाप्मा᳘नं विजि᳘हासन्॥
</details>

</details>
