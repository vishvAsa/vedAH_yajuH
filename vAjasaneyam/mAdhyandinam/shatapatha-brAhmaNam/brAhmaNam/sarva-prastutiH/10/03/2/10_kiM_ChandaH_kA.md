<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

किं᳓ छ᳓न्दः ।  
का᳓ देव᳓ताष्ठीव᳓न्तावि᳓ति पङ्क्ति᳓श्छ᳓न्दो मरु᳓तो देव᳓ताष्ठीव᳓न्तौ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

10. 'What metre and what deity are the knees?' The metre Paṅkti and that deity, the Maruts, are the knees.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Which metre and which deity are his knees?" The metre Paṅkti and that deity, the Maruts are the knees.
</details>
