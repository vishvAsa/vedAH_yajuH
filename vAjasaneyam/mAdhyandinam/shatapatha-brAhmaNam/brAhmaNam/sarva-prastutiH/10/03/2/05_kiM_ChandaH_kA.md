<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

किं᳓ छ᳓न्दः का᳓ देव᳓ता म᳓ध्यमि᳓ति ।  
त्रिष्टु᳓प्छ᳓न्द इ᳓न्द्रो देव᳓ता म᳓ध्यम् ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

5. 'What metre and what deity are its waist?' The metre Trishṭubh and the deity Indra are its waist.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Which metre and which deity are its middle part?” The Triṣṭubh metre and Indra, the deity form the middle part.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

का᳘ देव᳘ता म᳘ध्यमि᳘ति त्रिष्टुप्छ᳘न्द ऽइ᳘न्द्रो देव᳘ता म᳘ध्यम्॥
</details>
