<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

किं᳓ छ᳓न्दः ।  
का᳓ देव᳓तोरू᳓ इ᳓त्यनुष्टु᳓प्छ᳓न्दो वि᳓श्वे देवा᳓ देव᳓तोरू᳓॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

9. 'What metre and what deity are the thighs?' The metre Anushṭubh and that deity, the Viśvedevāḥ, are the thighs.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Which metre and which deity for the two thighs?" The metre Anuṣṭubh and the deities Viśvedevās are (for) the two thighs.
</details>
