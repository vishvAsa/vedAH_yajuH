<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

किं᳓ छ᳓न्दः का᳓ देव᳓ता यो᳙ ऽय᳓म᳓वाङ् प्राण᳓ इ᳓ति ।  
यज्ञायज्ञि᳓यं छ᳓न्दो वैश्वानरो᳓ देव᳓ता ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

8. 'What metre and what deity are that downward vital air?' The metre Yajñāyajñiya and the deity Vaiśvānara.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Which metre and which deity for this downward breath?" The metre is Yajña Yajñīya and the deity is Vaiśvānara.
</details>

</details>

</details>
