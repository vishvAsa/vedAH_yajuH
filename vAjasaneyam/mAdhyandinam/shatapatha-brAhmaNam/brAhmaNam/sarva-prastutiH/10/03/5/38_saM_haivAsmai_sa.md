<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

सं᳓ हैवा᳙स्मै᳓ स᳓ का᳓म ऋध्यते य᳓त्कामो भ᳓वति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and whatever the wish he entertains, it will be fulfilled to him; for, assuredly, he who knows this attains this contentment, this successful issue, this joy, this soul.
</details>

</details>

</details>

</details>
