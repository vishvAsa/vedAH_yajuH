<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

शि᳓र एवा᳙ग्निः᳓ ।  
प्राणो᳓ महाव्रत᳓मात्मा᳓ मह᳓दुक्थं᳓ त᳓स्मादेता᳓नि स᳓र्वाणि सहो᳓पेयात् ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

5. The Fire-altar, indeed, is the head, the Great Rite the breath, and the Great Litany the body:

one ought therefore to undertake all these together, for head, breath, and body belong together;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The *Agni*-altar is the head and the *Mahāvrata* is the vital air and *Mahad* *Uktha* is the body. All these are to be gained simultaneously because it is along with the head that the vital air and body exist. As to why the *Agni*- altar is built first is because, whoever is born, his head is born first. There- fore when all these are produced simultaneously, the *Mahad Uktha* is con- sidered the highest (*Ātman*), for the *Mahad Uktha* is the self (*Ātman*).
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

अन्यथैतत्त्रयं स्तौति- **शिर एवाग्निरि**ति । अग्निचयनमहाव्रतसामबृहदुक्थानि शिरःप्राणात्मरूपाण्यनुसन्धेयानि, यस्मादग्निः 'प्रथमः चीयते' तस्मात् 'जायमानस्य' पुरुषस्य शिर एव प्रथमं योनेः सकाशादुत्पद्यते । तस्माद्यत्र यज्ञे 'एतानि' 'सर्वाणि' त्रीणि 'सह' 'क्रियन्ते' तत्र 'महदुक्थम्' 'आख्यायतेतमाम्' सम्पूर्णमुच्यते । बृहतीसहस्रात्मकत्वात्सम्पूर्णोक्तिः ।
