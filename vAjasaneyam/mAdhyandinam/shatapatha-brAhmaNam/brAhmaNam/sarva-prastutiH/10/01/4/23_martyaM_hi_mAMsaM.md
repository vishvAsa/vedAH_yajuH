<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

म᳓र्त्यं हि᳓ मांसं᳓ त᳓देत᳓स्मिन्नमृ᳓ते प्र᳓तिष्ठापयति ते᳓नास्यै᳓त᳓दमृ᳓तम् भवति॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He scatters loose soil over it which of course is his flesh and it is mortal; because flesh is perishable. He establishes it (flesh) on that immortal part (udāna) and thereby his that part (flesh) becomes immortal.
</details>

</details>
