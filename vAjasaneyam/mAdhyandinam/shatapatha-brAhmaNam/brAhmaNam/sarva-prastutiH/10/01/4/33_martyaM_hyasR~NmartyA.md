<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

म᳓र्त्यं ह्य᳓सृङ्म᳓र्त्या त्व᳓क्त᳓देत᳓स्मिन्नमृ᳓ते प्र᳓तिष्ठापयति ते᳓नास्यै᳓त᳓दमृ᳓तम् भवति॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He scatters loose soil thereon and this, doubtless, is his blood and his skin and they are mortal, because blood and skin are perishable. Thus he estab- lishes them on this immortal element (of speech) and thereby the skin and blood become immortal.
</details>

</details>
