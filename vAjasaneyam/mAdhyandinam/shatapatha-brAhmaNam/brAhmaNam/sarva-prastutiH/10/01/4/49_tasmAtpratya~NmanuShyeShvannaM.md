<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓स्मात्प्रत्य᳓ङ्मनुष्ये᳙ष्व᳓न्नं धीयतेऽपाने᳓न हि᳓ मनुष्या᳙ अ᳓न्नमद᳓न्ति॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

By means of the down-breathing men eat food, whence food

is introduced into men (from the front) towards the back, for by their down-breathing men eat food.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That is why, the food is introduced into the mouth. From the front towards the back through the route of the downward breath.
</details>

</details>

</details>
