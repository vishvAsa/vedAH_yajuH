<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓देषां सत्ये᳓न श्र᳓मेण त᳓पसा श्रद्ध᳓या यज्ञे᳓ना᳓हुतिभिर᳓वरुद्धं भवति ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The purpose of performing all these is attainable even by truth, by toil, by austerities, by devotion, by sacrifice and by offerings. (Third Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>

</details>

</details>
