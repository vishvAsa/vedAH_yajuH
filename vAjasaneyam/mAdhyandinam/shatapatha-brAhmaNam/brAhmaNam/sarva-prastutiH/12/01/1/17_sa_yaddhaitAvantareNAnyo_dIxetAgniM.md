<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स᳓ य᳓द्धैता᳓व᳓न्तरेणान्यो᳓ दी᳓क्षेताग्निं᳓ च त᳓द्वा᳓चं चा᳓न्नेन ना᳓ना कुर्यात् ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for, assuredly, were any one else to be initiated between those two, he would separate fire and speech from food, and (people) would be liable to starve: let therefore no other person be initiated between those two.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

If some one else were to be initiated in between them, it will amount to separating Agni and speech (mouth) from food and there will be starvation (among the people).
</details>
