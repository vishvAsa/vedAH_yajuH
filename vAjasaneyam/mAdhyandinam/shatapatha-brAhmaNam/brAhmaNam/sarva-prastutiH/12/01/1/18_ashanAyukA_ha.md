<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अशना᳓युका ह स्युस्त᳓स्मादेता᳓व᳓न्तरेणान्यो᳓ न᳓ दीक्षेतैतां᳓श्चतु᳓रोऽध्वर्यु᳓र्दीक्षयति ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Hence no one else should be initiated between these two. These four (priests) are initiated by the Adhvaryu.
</details>
