<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

5. The Pratiprasthātr̥ then initiates the Adhvaryu. Now, the Adhvaryu is the mind [^egg_419], and the Hotr̥ is speech: he thus connects mind and speech with one another.

[^egg_419]: 136:2 The Adhvaryu is the head of the sacrifice (IV, 1, 5, 16); and, as the mind, he marches in front. See also III, 2, 4, 11. 'Mind goes before Speech (prompting her), "Speak thus! say not this!"'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then the Pratiprasthātā initiates the Adhvaryu.
</details>
