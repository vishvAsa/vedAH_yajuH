<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓स्मादेता᳓व᳓न्तरेणान्यो᳓ न᳓ दीक्षेत ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Therefore no other person should be initiated between those two; for, assuredly, were any one else to be initiated between those two, he would separate (tear up) the plants from this (terrestrial) world, and they would be liable to dry up: let therefore no other person be initiated between those two.

[^egg_417]: 135:3 Soma is the king of plants, whence these are called 'soma-rājñī,' II, 3, 4; 4; V, 4, 2, 3; R̥g-veda S. X, 97, 8.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Therefore no one else should be initiated in between these two.

Therefore no one else should be initiated between these two (i.e., Udgātā and Hotā).

Therefore no one else should be initi- ated between them.
</details>
