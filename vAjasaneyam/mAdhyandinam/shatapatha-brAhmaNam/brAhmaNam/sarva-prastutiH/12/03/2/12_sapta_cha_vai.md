<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

सप्त᳓ च वै᳓ शता᳓नि विंशति᳓श्च ।  
संवत्सर᳓स्याहोरात्रा᳓णि सप्त᳓ च शता᳓नि विंशति᳓श्च पु᳓रुषस्या᳓स्थीनि च मज्जा᳓नश्चा᳓त्र त᳓त्सम᳓म् ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

4. And there are seven hundred and twenty days and nights in the year, and seven hundred and twenty bones and parts of marrow in man, and these (two) now are one and the same.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

There are seven hundred and twenty days and nights (together) for the year and there are seven hundred and twenty bones and marrow por- tions (together) in the Puruṣa. Thus they are the same.
</details>
