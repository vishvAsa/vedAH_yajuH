<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

शुक्रो᳓ वा᳓एष᳓ म᳓धुमानि᳓न्दुर्य᳓त्सो᳓मः सो᳓मं रा᳓जानमिह᳓ भक्षयामी᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'that clear, sweet draught,'--for clear and sweet indeed is that draught, Soma; --'King Soma I now drink,'--it is thus king Soma that comes to be drunk by him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“That clear sweet draught” for the Soma draught is indeed clear and sweet. “I am now drinking the King Soma” - it is thus the king Soma who is drunk by him.
</details>
