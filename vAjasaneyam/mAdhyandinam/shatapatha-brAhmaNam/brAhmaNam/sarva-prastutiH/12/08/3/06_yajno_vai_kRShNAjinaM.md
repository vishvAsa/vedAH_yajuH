<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for the black antelope skin is the sacrifice [^egg_629]: it is at the sacrifice he thus consecrates him;
</details>

Thus it is at the sacrifice, he consecrates him.
</details>
