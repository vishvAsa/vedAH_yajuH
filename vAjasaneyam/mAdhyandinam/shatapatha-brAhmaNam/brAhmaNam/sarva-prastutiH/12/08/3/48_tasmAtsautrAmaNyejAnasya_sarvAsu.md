<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓स्मात्सौत्रामण्ये᳙जान᳓स्य स᳓र्वासु दिक्ष्व᳙न्ना᳓द्यम᳓वरुद्धं भवत्य᳓थो य᳓ एव᳓मेत᳓द्वे᳓द ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

On. every side (he sprinkles him) whilst moving round: he thus bestows food on him from all quarters, whence food is secured from all quarters by him who has performed the Sautrāmaṇī, or even by him who thus knows this.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He pours moving all around him and thereby he supplies him with food from all quarters. So for one who performs Sautrāmaṇī, food is assured from all directions. So too for the one who knows thus.
</details>
