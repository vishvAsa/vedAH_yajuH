<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

19. He consecrates him prior to the Svishṭakr̥t (offering), for the Svishṭakr̥t is the Kshatra: he thus consecrates him by means of the Kshatra (or, by a Kshatriya).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Before the Sviṣṭakṛt offerings, he consecrates him.
</details>
