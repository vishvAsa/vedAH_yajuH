<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

पितृदेवत्य᳙मेवै᳙नत्त᳓त्करोति नो᳓दिङ्गयति नो᳓पमृष्टे न᳓ प्रा᳓श्नाति नो᳓दुक्षति पितृदेवत्य᳙मेवै᳙नत्त᳓त्करोति ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus he renders it fit for the *Pitṛs*. Neither he shakes the *sruks*, nor wipes it nor does he eat (the milk left over in it), nor he throws it out. Thereby he makes it an act meant for *Pitṛs*.
</details>
