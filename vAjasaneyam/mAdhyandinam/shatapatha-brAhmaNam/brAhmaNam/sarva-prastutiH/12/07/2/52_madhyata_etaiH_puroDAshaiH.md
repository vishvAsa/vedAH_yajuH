<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

मध्यत᳓ एतैः᳓ पुरोडा᳓शैः प्र᳓चरति म᳓ध्यं वा᳓एते᳓षां यो᳓निः स्वा᳓देवै᳙नान्यो᳓नेः प्र᳓जनयति ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

In the middle (of the sacrifice) they proceed with (the offering of) these cakes, for the centre means their (mother's) womb: he thus causes them to be produced from their own (mother's) womb.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

In the middle (of the sacrifice) they proceed with these Puroḍāśas. The middle means womb, and thereby he causes them to be produced by their own womb.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

मध्यत एतैः पुरोडाशैः प्रचरति । मध्यं वा एतेषां योनिः । स्वादेवैनान् योनेः प्रजनयति ॥ २० ॥
</details>
