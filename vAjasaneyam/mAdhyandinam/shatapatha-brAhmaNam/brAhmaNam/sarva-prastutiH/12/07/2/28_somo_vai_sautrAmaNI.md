<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

सो᳓मो वै᳓ सौत्रामणी᳓ यज्ञमुख᳓एव᳓ त᳓त्सोमरूपं᳓ करोति यज्ञ᳓स्य स᳓मृद्ध्यै ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Let him purchase from a seller of Soma, for the Sautrāmaṇī is Soma. By so doing he puts a form of Soma into the very mouth (at the outset) of the sacrifice in order to enrich the sacrifice.
</details>

</details>
