<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

तिस्रो᳓ हि᳓ रा᳓त्रीः सो᳓मः क्रीतो᳓ व᳓सति सोमरूप᳓मेवै᳙नां करोति ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He distils it with a view to (its being like) the Soma-pressing. For three nights it remains standing, for the Soma remains standing for three nights after it has been bought: he thus makes it a form of Soma.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus he makes it (assume) the form of Soma juice.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>
