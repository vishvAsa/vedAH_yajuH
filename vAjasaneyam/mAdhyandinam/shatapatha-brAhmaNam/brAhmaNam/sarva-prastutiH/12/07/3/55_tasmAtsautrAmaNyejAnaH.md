<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓स्मात्सौत्रामण्ये᳙जानः᳓ स᳓र्वमा᳓युरेत्य᳓थो य᳓ एव᳓मेत᳓द्वे᳓द ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and, indeed, he also lays vital power (or life) into him: whence he who has performed the Sautrāmaṇī, and even he who thus knows this, attains the full (measure of) life.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

There- fore the one who performs the Sautrāmaṇī lives his full-span of life; so too the one who knows thus.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त᳘स्मात्सौत्राम᳘ण्येजानः स᳘र्व्वमा᳘युरेत्य᳘थो य᳘ ऽएव᳘मेतद्वे᳘द॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

त᳘स्मात्सौत्रामॗण्येजानः स᳘र्वमा᳘युरेत्य᳘थो य᳘ एव᳘मेतद्वे᳘द ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

तस्मात् सौत्रामण्येजानः सर्वमायुरेति । अथो य एवमेतद्वेद ॥ १६ ॥
</details>
