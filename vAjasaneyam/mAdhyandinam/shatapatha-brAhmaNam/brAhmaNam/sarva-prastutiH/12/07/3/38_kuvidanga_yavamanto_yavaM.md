<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

कुवि᳓दङ्ग᳓ य᳓वमन्तो य᳓वं चिदि᳓ति ।  
पयोग्रहा᳓न्गृह्णाति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(He draws the Grahas saying) - “Just as owners of barley (crops) cut their barley”- thus (saying) he (Adhvaryu) fills three Grahas of milk.
</details>
