<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ते᳙ ऽब्रुवन् ।  
य᳓ एषो᳙ ऽजः᳓, स᳓ वां भाग᳓ इ᳓ति ।  
त᳓थे᳓ति ।  
त᳓स्मादाश्विनो᳓ धूम्रो᳓ भवति ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

They spake, 'That he-goat there shall be your guerdon.' They said, 'So be it!' and hence the smoke-coloured (he-goat) is sacred to the two Aśvins.
</details>

</details>
