<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

1. Verily, from this sacrifice the man [^egg_655] is born

and whatever food a man consumes in this world, that (food), in return, consumes him in yonder world.

[^egg_655]: 259:5 That is, the Purusha, Agni-Prajāpati; and the Sacrificer.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It is certainly from this sacrifice, man is born.
</details>
