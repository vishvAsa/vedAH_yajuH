<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

एत᳓द्ध स्म वै᳓ त᳓द्विद्वा᳓नाह श्वेत᳓केतुरारुणेयः᳓ ।  
संवत्सरा᳓य न्वा᳓ अहं᳓ दीक्षिष्य इ᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

9. Now, concerning this, Śvetaketu Āruṇeya,

knowing this, once said, 'I am now going to get myself initiated for one year.'
</details>
