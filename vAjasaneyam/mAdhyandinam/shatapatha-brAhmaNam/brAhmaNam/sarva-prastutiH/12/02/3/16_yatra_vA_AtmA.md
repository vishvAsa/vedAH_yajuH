<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓त्र वा᳓ आत्मा᳓ त᳓द᳓ङ्गानि य᳓त्रो अ᳓ङ्गानि त᳓दात्मा᳓ न᳓ वा᳓ आत्मा᳓ङ्गान्यतिरिच्य᳓ते ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and where the body is there are (or, that includes) also the limbs, and where the limbs are there is also the body; and neither is the body in excess of the limbs, nor are the limbs in excess of the body: and thus, indeed, that (day) belongs both to those (months) that go before and to those that follow.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

and the months are the limbs and where the body is, there the limbs are and where the limbs are, there the body is. The limbs do not stand exclusive or exceeding the body.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

य᳘त्र वा᳘ ऽआत्मा तद᳘ङ्गानि य᳘त्रो ऽअ᳘ङ्गानि त᳘दात्मा न वा᳘ ऽआत्मा᳘ ऽङ्गान्यतिरिच्य᳘ते᳘
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

य᳘त्र वा᳘ऽआत्मा तद᳘ङ्गानि य᳘त्रोऽअ᳘ङ्गानि त᳘दात्मा न वा᳘ऽआत्मा᳘ङ्गान्यतिरिच्य᳘तेॗ
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

यत्र वा आत्मा तदंगानि । यत्रो अंगानि तदात्मा । न वा आत्मा अंगान्यतिरिच्यते ।
</details>
