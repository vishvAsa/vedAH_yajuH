<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अ᳓थ य᳓दि भक्ष्य᳓माणः ।  
किं᳓चिदाप᳓द्येत भक्षा᳓य स्वा᳓हे᳓ति जुहुयाद्भक्षो᳓ हि᳓ स᳓ त᳓र्हि भ᳓वत्य᳓प पाप्मा᳓नं हतउ᳓पैनं यज्ञो᳓ नमति ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

32. And if (his Soma) were to meet with any mishap whilst being consumed, let him perform an oblation with, 'To Bhaksha hail!' for Bhaksha

 (drink) he then is: he repels evil, and the sacrifice inclines to him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

While being drunk, if something happens to him, let him offer an oblation (saying) "To Bhakṣa-Svāhā", for he then becomes Bhakṣa (drink) and the sacrifice inclines to him.
</details>
