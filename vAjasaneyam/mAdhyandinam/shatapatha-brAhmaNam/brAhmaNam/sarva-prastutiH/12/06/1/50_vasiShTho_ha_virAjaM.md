<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Now as to the meaning of these (formulas). Vasishṭḥa knew the Virāj [^egg_561]: Indra coveted it.

[^egg_561]: 212:1 That is, the 'far-shining;' or 'far-ruling' (metre).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now about the meaning of these mantras. Vasiṣṭha knew the Virāḍ and Indra coveted it.
</details>
