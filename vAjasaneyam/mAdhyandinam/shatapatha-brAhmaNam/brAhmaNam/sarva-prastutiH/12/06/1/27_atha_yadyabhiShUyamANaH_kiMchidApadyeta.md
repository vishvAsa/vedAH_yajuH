<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अ᳓थ य᳓द्यभिषूय᳓माणः ।  
किं᳓चिदाप᳓द्येत यमा᳓य स्वा᳓हे᳓ति जुहुयाद्यमो᳓ हि᳓ स᳓ त᳓र्हि भ᳓वत्य᳓प पाप्मा᳓नं हतउ᳓पैनं यज्ञो᳓ नमति ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

21. And if (his Soma) were to meet with any mishap whilst being pressed, let him perform an oblation with, 'To Yama hail!' for Yama he then is: he repels evil, and the sacrifice inclines to him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

While being pressed something happens to him, let him offer an oblation (saying) "To Yama-Svāhā", for then he becomes Yama and the sac- rifice inclines to him.
</details>
