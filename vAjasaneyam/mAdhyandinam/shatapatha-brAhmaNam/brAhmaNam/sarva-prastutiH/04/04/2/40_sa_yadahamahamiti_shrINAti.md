<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स᳓ य᳓दह᳓मह᳓मि᳓ति श्रीणा᳓ति पुं᳓स्वेवै᳙त᳓द्वीर्यं᳙ दधाति॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

In that he mixes with 'I--I,' thereby he bestows manhood on men.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus he mixes with (repeating) ‘aham’, ‘aham’ (‘I’, ‘I’), and he bestows manliness on men.
</details>
