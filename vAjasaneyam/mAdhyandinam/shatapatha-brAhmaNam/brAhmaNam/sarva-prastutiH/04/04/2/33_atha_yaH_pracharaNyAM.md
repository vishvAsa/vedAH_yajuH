<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अ᳓थ यः᳓ प्रचरण्यां᳓ संस्रवः᳓ प᳓रिशिष्टो भ᳓वति ।  
ते᳓नैनं श्रीणाति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

13. He (the Adhvaryu) then mixes it with the residue (of ghee) which is left in the pracaraṇī spoon.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

What (ghee) is left over, smeared on the pracaraṇī (spoon), he (Adhvaryu) mixes it.
</details>

ते᳘नैनᳫँ᳭ श्रीणाति
</details>

</details>
