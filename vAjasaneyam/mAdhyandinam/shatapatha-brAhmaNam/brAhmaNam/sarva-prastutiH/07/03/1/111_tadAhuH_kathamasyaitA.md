<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

38. As to this they say, 'How does it come to be put on for him by means of the day and the night?'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now the question arises as to what effect is brought about on it by the day and the night.
</details>
