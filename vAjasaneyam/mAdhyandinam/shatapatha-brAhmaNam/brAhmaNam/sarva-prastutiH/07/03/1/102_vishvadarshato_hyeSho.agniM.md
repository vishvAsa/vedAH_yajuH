<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

विश्व᳓दर्शतो ह्ये᳙षो᳙ ऽग्निं᳓ सुम्ना᳓य दधिरे पुरो᳓ ज᳓ना इ᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'the all-remarkable,' for he (Agni) is indeed remarkable to all; --'(thee), Agni, men have placed foremost for happiness,' happiness doubtless is the sacrifice, and for the sacrifice they indeed place him foremost;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Whom people place in the forefront of sumna (well being)" —sumna is sacrifice and it is for sacrifice that they place Agni at the helm.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

विश्व᳘दर्शतोॗ ह्येॗषो ऽग्नि᳘ᳫं᳘ सुम्ना᳘य दधिरे पुरो ज᳘ना इ᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

विश्वदर्शतो ह्येषः । **“अग्निं सुम्नाय दधिरे पुरो जनाः**- इति ।
</details>
