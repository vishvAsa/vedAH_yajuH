<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

32. 'Ruling, O Agni, spread thou by beings'--the beings are men: thus, 'Shining, O Agni, spread thyself by men!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(He says) "O Agni! grow luminously along with creatures, men of high birth (the Ṛtviks)." Here 'jantu' means men and he means 'shining forth.”
</details>
