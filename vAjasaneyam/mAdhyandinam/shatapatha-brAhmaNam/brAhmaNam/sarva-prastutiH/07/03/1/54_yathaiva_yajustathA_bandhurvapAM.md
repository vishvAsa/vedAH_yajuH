<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓थैव᳓ य᳓जुस्त᳓था ब᳓न्धुर्वपां᳓ ते अग्नि᳓रिषितो᳓ अरोहदि᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

as the text, so the meaning; --'Agni, sent forth, hath mounted thy skin;' whatsoever is on this (earth) that is her skin; and that (skin) Agni mounts, when sent forth, when blazing forth.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

As is the text, so is the meaning. (He says) “Agni who has been commissioned has got over your skin”.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

</details>
