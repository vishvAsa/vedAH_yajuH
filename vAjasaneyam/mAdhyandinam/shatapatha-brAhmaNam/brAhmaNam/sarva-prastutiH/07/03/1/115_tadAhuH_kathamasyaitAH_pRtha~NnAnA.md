कथ᳓मस्यै᳓ताः᳓ पृ᳓थङ्ना᳓ना य᳓जुर्भिरु᳓पहिता भवन्ती᳓ति ।

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

40. As to this they say, 'How is that (sand) of his put on separately with different prayers?'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they ask – “How is that sand of his put on separately with different Yajus mantras?” Mind is Yajus and this mind (thought) is ca- pable of pervading all sand.
</details>
