<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓दाहुः ।  
कथ᳓मस्यै᳓ता᳓ अहोरात्रैः᳓ स᳓म्पन्ना अ᳓न्यूना अ᳓नतिरिक्ता उ᳓पहिता भवन्ती᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

39. As to this they say, 'How does that (sand), put on by the days and nights, become complete (or perfect) for him, neither deficient, nor superabundant?'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they may ask, “How does this sand put on by days and nights become equally efficacious for him without being in excess or deficient?” The answer to this is that days and nights are endless (innu- merable) and sand is also infinite.
</details>
