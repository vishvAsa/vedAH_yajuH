<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स᳓र्वाभ्य एवै᳙त᳓द्दिग्भ्यो᳓ र᳓क्षांसि नाष्ट्रा᳓ अपह᳓त्या᳓थैनमु᳓दञ्चम् प्रा᳓ञ्चम् प्र᳓सृजति त᳓स्योक्तो᳓ ब᳓न्धुः॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

then southward, whereby they ward off evil from the south; then westward, whereby they ward off evil from the western region; then northward, whereby they ward off evil from the northern region. Having thus warded off the Rakshas, the fiends, from all the regions, he sets it (the horse) free towards northeast: the significance of this has been explained.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then they take it to the south and ward off evils in that region; then westward to clear the western region of all evil and then to the north to dispel evil from the north. Having thus cleared all regions of the evil Rakṣasas, he sets it (horse) free towards northeast. The purpose of this has been stated.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

स᳘र्व्वाभ्य ऽए᳘वैत᳘द्दिग्भ्यो र᳘क्षाᳫँ᳭सि नाष्ट्रा᳘ ऽअपहत्या᳘थैनमु᳘दञ्चं प्रा᳘ञ्चं प्र᳘सृजति त᳘स्योक्तो ब᳘न्धुः॥ (शतम् ४१००)॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

स᳘र्वाभ्य एॗवैत᳘द्दिग्भ्यो र᳘क्षांसि नाष्ट्रा᳘ अपहत्या᳘थैनमु᳘दञ्चम् प्रा᳘ञ्चम् प्र᳘सृजति त᳘स्योक्तो ब᳘न्धुः॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

सर्वाभ्य एवैतद्दिग्भ्यो रक्षांसि नाष्ट्रा अपहत्य- अथैनमुदञ्चं प्राञ्चं प्रसृजति । तस्योक्तो बन्धुः ॥ ११ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

स᳘र्वाभ्य ए᳘वैत᳘द्दिग्भ्यो र᳘क्षाꣳसि नाष्ट्रा᳘ अपहत्या᳘थैनमु᳘दञ्चं प्रा᳘ञ्चं प्र᳘सृजति त᳘स्योक्तो ब᳘न्धुः ॥९॥
</details>
