<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

सर्व᳓तः प᳓रिणयति सर्व᳓त एवा᳙स्मा एतं᳓ व᳓ज्रमभिगोप्ता᳓रं करोति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Thrice he leads it about: he thus makes that thunderbolt his (Agni's) threefold protector.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He takes him all around and thereby causes this thunderbolt to protect all around. By taking him round three times he makes the thunderbolt threefold protector.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

सर्व्व᳘तः प᳘रिणयति सर्व्व᳘त ऽए᳘वास्मा ऽएतं व्व᳘ज्रमभिगोप्ता᳘रं करोति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

सर्व᳘तः प᳘रिणयति सर्व᳘त एॗवास्मा एतं व᳘ज्रमभिगोप्ता᳘रं करोति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

सर्वतः परिणयति । सर्वत एवास्मा ऽएतं वज्रमभिगोप्तारं करोति ।
</details>
