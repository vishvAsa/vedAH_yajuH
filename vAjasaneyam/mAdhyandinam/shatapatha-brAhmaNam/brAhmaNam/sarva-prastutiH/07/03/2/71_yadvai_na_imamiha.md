<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓द्वै᳓ न इम᳓मिह᳓ र᳓क्षांसि नाष्ट्रा᳓ न᳓ हन्यु᳓रि᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Because at that time the gods feared that the evil Rakṣasas might smite that altar. Hence they converted this sun into a protective thunderbolt.
</details>

</details>
