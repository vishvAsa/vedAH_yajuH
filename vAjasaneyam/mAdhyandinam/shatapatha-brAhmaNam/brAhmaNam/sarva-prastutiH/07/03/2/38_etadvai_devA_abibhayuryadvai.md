<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

एत᳓द्वै᳓ देवा᳓ अबिभयुर्य᳓द्वै᳓ न इह᳓ र᳓क्षांसि नाष्ट्रा᳓ न᳓ हन्यु᳓रि᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

For at that time the gods were afraid lest the Rakshas, the fiends, should smite them here.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Once upon a time, the gods were scared that the evil Rakṣasas might attack them here.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

</details>

</details>
