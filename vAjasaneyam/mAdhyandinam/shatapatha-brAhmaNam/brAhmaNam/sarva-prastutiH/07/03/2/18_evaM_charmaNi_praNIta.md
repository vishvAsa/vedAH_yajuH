<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

एवं᳓ च᳓र्मणि प्र᳓णीत एव᳓म᳓श्वप्रणीत उ᳓द्यछन्त्येतां᳓ चि᳓तिम्॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

They lift up this (first) layer [^egg_670].

[^egg_670]: 357:1 The Adhvaryu's attendants take up the ox-skin with the bricks for the first layer lying on it.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This way this layer also becomes one led forward on the skin and one led forward by the horse.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

</details>
