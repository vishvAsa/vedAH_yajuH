<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

These naturally perforated bricks are the creatures. When he is made to smell them, yonder sun strings these creatures to himself as on a thread; and as to that thread, the significance (will be explained) later on.
</details>
