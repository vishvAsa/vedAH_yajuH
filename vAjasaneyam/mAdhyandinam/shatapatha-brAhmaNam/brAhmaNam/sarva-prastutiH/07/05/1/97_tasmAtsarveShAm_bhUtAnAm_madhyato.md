<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓स्मात्स᳓र्वेषाम् भूता᳓नाम् मध्यतो᳓ यो᳓निर᳓पि व᳓नस्प᳓तीनाम्॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thereby for all beings, the womb is in the middle, even for the trees.
</details>
