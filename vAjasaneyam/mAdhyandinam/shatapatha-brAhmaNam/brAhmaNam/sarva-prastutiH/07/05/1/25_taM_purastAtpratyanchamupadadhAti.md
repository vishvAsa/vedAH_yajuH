<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

तं᳓ पुर᳓स्तात्प्रत्य᳓ञ्चमु᳓पदधाति ।  
पुर᳓स्तात्त᳓त्प्रत्य᳓ञ्चं प्राणं᳓ दधाति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He places it in front, with face towards the back(west) because prāṇa is taken in from the front and goes backwards. He places it (tortoise) by the side of the goldman and thereby infuses prāṇa into the Yajamāna.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

</details>
