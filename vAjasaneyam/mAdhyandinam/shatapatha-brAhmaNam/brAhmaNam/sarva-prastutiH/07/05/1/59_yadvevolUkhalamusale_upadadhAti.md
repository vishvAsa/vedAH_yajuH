<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓द्वेवो᳙लूखलमुसले᳓ उपद᳓धाति ।  
प्रजा᳓पतेर्वि᳓स्रस्तात्प्राणो᳓ मध्यत᳓ उ᳓दचिक्रमिषत् ।  
त᳓म᳓न्नेनागृह्णात् ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

16. And, again, why he places a mortar and pestle thereon. From Prajāpati, when relaxed, the breath wanted to go out from within.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Again why he places the mortar and pestle—when Prajāpati got exhausted, the vital air wanted to go out of him. He stopped it(from going out) by means of food. Hence prāṇa(vital air) is retained by food and he who eats food, breathes. When the vital air was kept back, the food was about to go out of him (Prajāpati).
</details>
