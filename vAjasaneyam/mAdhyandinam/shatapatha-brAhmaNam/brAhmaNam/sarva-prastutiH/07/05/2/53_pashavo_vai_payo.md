<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

पश᳓वो वै᳓ प᳓यो य᳓जमानं त᳓त्पशु᳓षु प्र᳓तिष्ठापयत्यादित्यं᳓ ग᳓र्भम् प᳓यसा स᳓मङ्ग्धी᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Milk means cattle and thus he establishes the Yajamāna among the cattle (saying), "Unite embryo in the form of sun with milk, with embryo." The man is indeed the sun. (He means) you anoint him with milk.
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

</details>
