<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

3. And, again, why he puts the heads of the victims therein; --what (animal) perfections (śrī) [^egg_736] there were, they are these victims’ heads; and what rumps there were, they are those five layers (of the altar).

[^egg_736]: 401:1 See VI, 1, 1, 4; 2, 1, 7.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And why he places the heads of *paśus* – because these heads are those excellences (*śriyah*).
</details>
