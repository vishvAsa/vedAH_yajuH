<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

गायत्रे᳓ण त्वा छ᳓न्दसा सादयामि ।  
त्रै᳓ष्टुभेन त्वा छ᳓न्दसा सादयामि जा᳓गतेन त्वा छ᳓न्दसा सादयाम्या᳓नुष्टुभेन त्वा छ᳓न्दसा सादयामि पा᳓ङ्क्तेन त्वा छ᳓न्दसा सादयामी᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

61. 'By the Gāyatrī metre I settle thee!--

 By the Trishṭubh metre I settle thee!--By the Jagatī metre I settle thee!--By the Anushṭubh metre I settle thee!--By the Paṅkti metre I settle thee!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"I settle you with Gāyatrī metre; I settle you with Triṣṭubh metre; I lay you down with Jagatī metre; I place you with Anuṣṭubh metre; I settle you with Paṅktī metre"− thus from whatever of the metres, waters flowed out (of Prajāpati) those he restores to him and he puts back those metres into him.
</details>
