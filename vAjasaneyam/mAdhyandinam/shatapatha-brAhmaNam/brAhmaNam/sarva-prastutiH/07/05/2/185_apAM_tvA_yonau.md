<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अपां᳓ त्वा यो᳓नौ सादयामी᳓ति ।  
समुद्रो᳓ वा᳓ अपां᳓ यो᳓निः समुद्रे᳓ तां᳓ सादयति॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

58. 'In the womb of the waters I settle thee!' the womb of the waters is the sea: in the sea he settles this one.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"I lay you down in the womb of the waters" – the womb of waters is of course the ocean and thus he places it in the ocean.
</details>
