<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अपां᳓ त्वा पु᳓रीषे सादयामी᳓ति ।  
सि᳓कता वा᳓ अपा᳓म् पु᳓रीषं सि᳓कतासु तां᳓ सादयति॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

59. 'In the sediment of the waters I settle thee!' the sediment (purīsha) of the waters is sand: in the sand he settles this one.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"I place you in the sediment of waters" – the sediment of waters is indeed the sands and in the sands, he places it.
</details>
