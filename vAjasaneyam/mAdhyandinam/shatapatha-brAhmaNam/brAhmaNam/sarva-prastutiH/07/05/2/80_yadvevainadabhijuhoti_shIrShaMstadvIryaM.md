<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓द्वेवै᳙नदभिजुहो᳓ति ।  
शीर्षं᳓स्त᳓द्वीर्यं᳙ दधात्या᳓ज्येन जुहोति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

24. And, again, why he offers thereon:--he thereby lays vigour into the head.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Inasmuch as he makes offerings on him (man), he infuses vigour into this head.
</details>

</details>

</details>
