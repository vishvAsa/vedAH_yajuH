<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त्रिष्टु᳓ब्भ्यां त्रै᳓ष्टुभो ह्ये᳙ष᳓ सादयित्वा᳓ सू᳓ददोहसा᳓धिवदति त᳓स्योक्तो᳓ ब᳓न्धुः॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Having 'settled' him, he pronounces the Sūdadohas on him: the significance of this has been explained.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Having placed him, he recites the Sūdadohasa; the significance of which has already been said.
</details>
