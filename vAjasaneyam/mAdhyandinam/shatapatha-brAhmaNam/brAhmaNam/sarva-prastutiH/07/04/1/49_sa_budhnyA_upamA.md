<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'from the summit [^egg_682] he, the longing, overspread the shining,' the summit doubtless is the middle, the shining ones are these worlds, and the longing one is yonder sun,--he is the longing one inasmuch as he longed to be born; and in rising he overspreads [^egg_683] these (worlds) from the summit, from the middle; --'he (overspread) the nighest extents of the deep,' his nighest extents of the deep doubtless are the regions, for he (the sun) does extend nigh to them; --'the womb of the existent and of the non-existent did he overspread!'

[^egg_682]: 366:1 'Sīmataḥ' would rather seem to mean 'from the boundary line,' but the author here takes 'sīman' in the sense of (sīmanta) 'hair-line, parting of the hair, crown of the head (Scheitel).'

[^egg_683]: 366:2 In the Sanskrit participial (or gerundial) construction, the relation between the primary and secondary notions is usually the reverse of ours,--thus 'he rises in overspreading.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He is the eager one inasmuch as he is eager to appear and, by rising, he overspreads these (worlds) from the top or the middle (of the sky). “The quarters are his limits”, for, the regions are nearest to him.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

तृतीयपादमनूद्य व्याचष्टे- **स बुध्न्या** इति । बुध्नो मूलं तत्र भवाः "बुध्न्याः' पादाः ते च 'अस्य' सूर्यस्य दिगात्मका रश्मिप्रसरणहेतुत्वात् । 'ताः' बुध्न्यशब्दाभिधेया दिशः । उप समीपे स्वगतिभेदेन निर्मिमीत इति 'उपमाः' सूर्यः तथा ताः 'दिशो' ऽनुलक्ष्य 'वितिष्ठते' विविधं पूर्वपश्चिमादिदिग्भेदहेतुत्वेनावतिष्ठत इति 'विष्ठाः' । तिष्ठतेर्माङश्च "आतो मनिन्"- (पा. सू. ३ । २ । ७४ ।) इति विच्, एवम्भूतः सूर्यः 'सतः' लोकत्रयात्मकस्य भावरूपस्य 'असतः' अभावात्मकस्य च 'योनिम्' उत्पत्तिस्थानं 'विवः' विवृणोति ।
</details>
