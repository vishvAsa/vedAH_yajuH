<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓देवा᳙स्य त᳓दग्नि᳓स्ते᳓ज आदा᳓य दक्षिणा᳓कर्षत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--that fiery spirit of his (Prajāpati's) which Agni then took and carried off to the south, he now puts back into him.--'Agni, the head, the summit of the sky, he, the lord of the earth, animates the seeds of the waters,' for Agni indeed is this (spoon).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(He says,) “Agni is the crest of the sky”.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

य᳘दे᳘वास्य त᳘दग्निस्ते᳘ज ऽआदा᳘य दक्षिणा᳘ ऽकर्षत्
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

य᳘देॗवास्य त᳘दग्निस्ते᳘ज आदा᳘य दक्षिणा᳘कर्षत्
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
