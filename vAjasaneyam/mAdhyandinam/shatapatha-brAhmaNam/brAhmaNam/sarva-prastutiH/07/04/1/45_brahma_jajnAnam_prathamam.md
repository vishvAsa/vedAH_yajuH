<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ब्र᳓ह्म जज्ञान᳓म् प्रथम᳓म् पुर᳓स्तादि᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

14. [He puts it down, with Vāj. S. XIII, 3]

 'The Brahman first born in front;' the Brahman doubtless is yonder sun, and he is born day by day in front (in the east);
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(He places it (gold) saying) “Brahman first appeared in front.” Brahman doubtless is yonder sun and he appears day by day in front (i.e. the east).
</details>
