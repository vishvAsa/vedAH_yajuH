<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓द्वेव᳓ पुष्करपर्ण᳓ उपद᳓धाति ।  
प्रतिष्ठा᳓ वै᳓ पुष्करपर्ण᳓म् ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

12. And, again, why he puts it on the lotus-leaf;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Why he places on the lotus-leaf is because lotus-leaf is a foundation.
</details>
