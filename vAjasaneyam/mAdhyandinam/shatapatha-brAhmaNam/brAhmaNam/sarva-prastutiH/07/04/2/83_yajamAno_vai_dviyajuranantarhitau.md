<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓जमानो वै᳓ द्वि᳓यजुर᳓नन्तर्हितौ त᳓द्य᳓जमानादाण्डौ᳓ दधाति॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The dviyajus brick is the Yajamāna and thus he endows the Yajamāna with testicles.
</details>
