<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

पश᳓वो वै᳓ दूर्वेष्टका᳓ ।  
य᳓जमानं त᳓त्पशु᳓षु प्र᳓तिष्ठापयति ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He lays it down close to the dūrvā-brick the dūrvā-brick being cattle, he thus establishes the Sacrificer in (the possession of) cattle.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The dūrveṣṭakā is cattle and thus he establishes the Yajamāna near (possessed of) cattle.
</details>

</details>
