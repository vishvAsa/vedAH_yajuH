<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

प्राणो᳓ वै᳓ सू᳓ददोहाः प्राणे᳓नैवै᳙नमेत᳓त्सं᳓तनोति सं᳓दधाति॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for the Sūdadohas is the vital air: by means of the vital air he thus makes him (Agni) continuous, joins him together.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He chants the sūdadoha mantras and they are the vital airs. Thus he places them and arranges them with his own vital airs.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

प्राणो वै सू᳘ददोहाः प्राणे᳘नै᳘वैनमेतत्सं᳘तनोति सं᳘दधाति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

प्राणो वै सू᳘ददोहाः प्राणे᳘णैॗवैनमेतत्सं᳘तनोति सं᳘दधाति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

प्राणो वै सूददोहाः । प्राणेनैवैनमेतत्सन्तनोति, सन्दधाति ॥ २६ ॥
</details>
