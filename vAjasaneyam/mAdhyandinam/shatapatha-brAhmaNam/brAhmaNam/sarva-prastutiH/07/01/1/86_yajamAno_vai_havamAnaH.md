य᳓जमानो वै᳓ ह᳓वमानः᳓ स्या᳓न्नः सूनु᳓स्त᳓नयो विजावे᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--he that calls doubtless is the Sacrificer; 'May there be to us a son, the perpetuator of the race,'--a son means offspring; --'let that, O Agni, be thy good-will unto us!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He who invokes is the Yajamāna here and for him the blessings are invoked.
</details>
