<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अ᳓प्रदाहाय य᳓द्धि᳓ युक्तं᳓ न᳓ विमुच्य᳓ते प्र᳓ त᳓द्दह्यते ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for if that which is yoked is not unloosed it is chafed.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

for, when the yoked animal is not unyoked, it is chafed.
</details>
