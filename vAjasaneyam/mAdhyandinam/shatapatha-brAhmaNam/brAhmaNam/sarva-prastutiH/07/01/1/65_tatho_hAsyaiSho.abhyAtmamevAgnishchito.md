<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓थो हास्यै᳓षो᳙ ऽभ्यात्म᳓मेवा᳙ग्नि᳓श्चितो᳓ भवति॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and in this manner that Agni of his comes to be built up towards (or for the Sacrificer) himself.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

</details>
