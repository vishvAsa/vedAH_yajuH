<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

परिमण्डला᳓ हि᳓ यो᳓निर᳓थो अयं᳓ वै᳓ लोको᳓ गा᳓र्हपत्यः परिमण्डल᳓ उ वा᳓ अयं᳓ लोकः᳓॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

It is circular, for the womb is circular; and moreover the Gārhapatya is this (terrestrial) world, and this world doubtless is circular.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It is circular, since the womb is circular. Moreover, Gārhapatya is this terrestrial world which doubtless is circular.
</details>
