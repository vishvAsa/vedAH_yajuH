<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓दु वा᳓ आत्म᳓न्न᳓न्नं धीय᳓ते त᳓दात्मा᳓नम᳓वति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And, indeed, the food which is put into the body benefits the body as well as the wings and tail;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He thereby puts food into the body. The food supplied to the body, nourishes the body as well as the wings and the tail.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

अथात्मन्यन्नाधानं स्तौति- **यदु वा** इति । 'आत्मन्' आत्मनि यदेव 'अन्नम्' 'धीयते' स्थाप्यते, 'तत्' एवान्नं पक्षपुच्छसहितम् 'आत्मानं' रक्षति ।

पक्षपुच्छेष्वन्नाधानं त्वकिञ्चित्करमित्याह- **अथ यदि**त्यादिना ॥ ८ ॥
</details>
