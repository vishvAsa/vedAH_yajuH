<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓द्भू᳓यो हिन᳓स्ति त᳓द्य᳓त्क᳓नीयो न᳓ त᳓दवति॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That which is in excess, harms the body and that which is inadequate does not protect the body.

It does not harm. If it is in excess, it affects; if less, it does not nourish.
</details>
