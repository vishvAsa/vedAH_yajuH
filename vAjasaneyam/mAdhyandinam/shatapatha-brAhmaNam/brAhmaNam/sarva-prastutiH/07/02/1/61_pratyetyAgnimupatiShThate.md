<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

18. Having returned, he stands worshipping by the fire; for when he goes into that (south-western) direction whilst Agni is only half built up, he does what is improper: he now makes amends to him to prevent his doing any harm.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

After returning to the fire, he stands worshipfully; for, when he goes outside the vedi when the Agni is only half built, he is committing an offence. He now makes amends for that, so that Agni does not injure him. He stands in reverence chanting a verse and thus makes amends to that Agni.
</details>
