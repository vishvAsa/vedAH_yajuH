<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

10. 'Homage be unto thee full well, O sharp-edged Nirr̥ti!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“I bow to you; nirṛti the sharp-edged” and hence he bows to it.
</details>
