<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

न᳓ सादयति प्रतिष्ठा᳓ वै᳓ सा᳓दनं ने᳓त्पाप्मा᳓नम् प्रतिष्ठाप᳓यानी᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He does not 'settle' them,--settlement being a firm footing--lest he give a firm footing to evil.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Nor he arranges them, since arranging means establishing and he would not like to establish sin.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

न सादयति । प्रतिष्ठा वै सादनम् । नेत्पाप्मानं प्रतिष्ठापयानीति ।
</details>
