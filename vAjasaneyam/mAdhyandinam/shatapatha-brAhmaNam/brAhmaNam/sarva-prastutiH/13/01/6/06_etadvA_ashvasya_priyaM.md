<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

एत᳓द्वा᳓ अ᳓श्वस्य प्रियं᳓ नामधे᳓यम् प्रिये᳓णैवै᳙नं ना᳓म्नाभि᳓वदति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“You are Vāyu (of moving nature) by name; you are Śiśu (praise-worthy or suckling) by name.” This is the favourite name of the horse and so he addresses it. by its favourite name.
</details>

</details>

</details>

</details>
