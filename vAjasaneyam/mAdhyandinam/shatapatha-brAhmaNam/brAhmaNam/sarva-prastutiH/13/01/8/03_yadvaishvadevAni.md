<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓द्वैश्वदेवा᳓नि जुहो᳓त्यश्वमेध᳓स्यैवो᳓द्यत्यै ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

The Vaiśvadeva (offerings) upheld that (Aśvamedha): thus, when he offers the Vaiśvadevas, it is for the upholding of the Aśvamedha.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus when he makes the Vaiśvadeva offerings, it is to sustain the Aśvamedha.
</details>
