<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

एता᳓वन्तो वै᳓ स᳓र्वे देवा᳓स्ते᳓भ्य एवै᳙नं जुहोति प᳓राचीर्जुहोति प᳓राङिव वै᳓ स्वर्गो᳓ लोकः᳓ स्वर्ग᳓स्य लोक᳓स्याभि᳓जित्यै ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He offers them straight away [^egg_700] for the obtainment of the heavenly world, for straight away, as it were, is the heavenly world.

[^egg_700]: 281:1 According to Kāty. XX, 2, 3-5, he offers either a thousand oblations, or as many as he can offer till the dripping of the water from the horse has ceased. For every ten oblations he uses the formulas here given, after which he begins again from the beginning. The 'straight on' apparently means that he is neither to break the order of the deities, nor to offer more than one oblation at a time to the same deity.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This much are all the gods and to them he thus offers. He offers to them thus outwards (in a forward direction); for the heavenly world is, as it were, beyond, and hence (so) for gaining the heavenly world.
</details>
