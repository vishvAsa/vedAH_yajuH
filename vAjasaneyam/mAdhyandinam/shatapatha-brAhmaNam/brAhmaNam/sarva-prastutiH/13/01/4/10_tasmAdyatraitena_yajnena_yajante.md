<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓स्माद्य᳓त्रैते᳓न यज्ञे᳓न य᳓जन्ते कॢप्तः᳓ प्रजा᳓नां योगक्षेमो᳓ भवति ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And when he performs offerings in the morning, he seeks that (horse); whence it is in daytime that one goes to seek for what is lost. And again when he offers the Dhr̥tis in the evening, and the (Savitr̥) ishṭis in the morning, it is security of possession the Sacrificer thereby brings about, whence security of possession is brought about for the subjects where this sacrifice is performed.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Hence welfare and security is gained for the subjects when this sacrifice is performed. (Fourth Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>
