<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓दा᳓ज्यमुच्छिष्य᳓ते ते᳓न रशना᳓मभ्य᳓ज्या᳓दत्ते ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

What ghee is left over (after preparing the Brahmaudana), with that he smears the rope (made of grass to put around the horse's neck) and takes it.
</details>
