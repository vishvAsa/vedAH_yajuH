<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स᳓ य᳓च्चरु᳓र्भ᳓वति च᳓क्षुषा ह्य᳙य᳓मात्मा᳓ च᳓रति॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And as to why it is a pap (caru), it is because by means of the eye this self (body or mind) moves (car).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

By means of eye, he endows it with eye in the fire of that caru. It is by means of the eye, this self (body) moves.
</details>
