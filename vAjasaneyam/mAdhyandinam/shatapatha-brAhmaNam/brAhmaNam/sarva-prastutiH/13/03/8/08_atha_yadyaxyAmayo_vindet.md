<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अ᳓थ य᳓द्यक्ष्यामयो᳓ विन्दे᳓त् ।  
सौर्यं᳓ चरु᳓मनुनि᳓र्वपेत्सू᳓र्यो वै᳓ प्रजा᳓नां च᳓क्षुः ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

4. And if an eye-disease were to befal it, let him in addition prepare a pap to Sūrya;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

If the horse suffers from some eye-ailment, a caru to Sūrya should be prepared in addition.
</details>
