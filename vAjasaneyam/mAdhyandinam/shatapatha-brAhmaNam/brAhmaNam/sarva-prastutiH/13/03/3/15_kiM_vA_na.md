<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

किं᳓ वा न᳓ य᳓त्सं᳓कृत्यच्छावाकसाम᳓ भ᳓वत्य᳓श्वस्यैव᳓ सर्वत्वा᳓य स᳓र्वस्तोमोऽतिरात्र᳓ उत्तम᳓म᳓हर्भवति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

When the Saṁkr̥ti is the Achāvāka's Sāman, it is for (bringing about) the completeness of the horse (sacrifice).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Making the Saṅkṛti Sāman as the Sāman of the Acchāvāka, he ensures completeness of the horse sacrifice.
</details>
