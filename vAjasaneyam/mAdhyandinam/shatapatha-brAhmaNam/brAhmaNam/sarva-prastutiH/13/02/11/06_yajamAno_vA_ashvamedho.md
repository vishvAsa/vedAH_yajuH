<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓जमानो वा᳓ अश्वमेधो᳓ रा᳓जा महिमा᳓ राज्ये᳓नैवै᳙नमुभय᳓तः प᳓रिगृह्णाति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--the Aśvamedha, doubtless, is the Sacrificer, and that Mahiman (cup) is the king: it is with royal dignity he thus encompasses him on both sides. Some gods have the svāhā-call ('hail') in front, and the other gods have the svāhā-call behind [^egg_834]: it is them he thus gratifies. With 'Hail

to the gods!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Aśvamedha is indeed, the Yajamāna and that Mahima Graha is the king. Thus he envelopes him on ei- ther side with royal power. Some gods have (for their offerings) the Svāhākāra in the start and some others have the Svāhākāra behind.
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

</details>
