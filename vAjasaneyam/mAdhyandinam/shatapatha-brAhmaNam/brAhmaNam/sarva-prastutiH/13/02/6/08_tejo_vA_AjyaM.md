<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ते᳓जो वा᳓ आ᳓ज्यं ते᳓जो गायत्री᳓ ते᳓जसी एवा᳙स्मिन्त्समी᳓ची दधाति॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

the queen consort anoints (the forepart of the unharnessed horse); --ghee is fiery mettle, and the Gāyatrī also is fiery mettle: two kinds of fiery mettle he thus bestows together on him (the Sacrificer).
</details>

</details>

</details>
