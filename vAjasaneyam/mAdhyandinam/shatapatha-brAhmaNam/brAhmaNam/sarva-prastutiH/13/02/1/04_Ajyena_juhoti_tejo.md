<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

आ᳓ज्येन जुहोति ।  
ते᳓जो वा᳓ आ᳓ज्यं ते᳓जसैवा᳙स्मिं᳓स्त᳓त्ते᳓जो दधात्या᳓ज्येन जुहोति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

2. With ghee he makes offering, for ghee is fiery mettle: by means of fiery mettle he thus lays fiery mettle into him (the horse and Sacrificer).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He offering with ghee, for Ājya is effulgence. Thus by means of effulgence, he endows effulgence unto him.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(त्या᳘) आ᳘ज्येन जुहोति॥  
ते᳘जो वा ऽआ᳘ज्यन्ते᳘जसै᳘वास्मिंस्तत्ते᳘जो दधात्या᳘ज्येन जुहोति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

आ᳘ज्येन जुहोति॥  
ते᳘जो वा आ᳘ज्यं ते᳘जसैॗवास्मिंस्तत्ते᳘जो दधात्या᳘ज्येन जुहोति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

आज्येन जुहोति । तेजो वा आज्यम् । तेजसैवास्मिंस्तत्तेजो दधाति । आज्येन जुहोति ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

</details>
