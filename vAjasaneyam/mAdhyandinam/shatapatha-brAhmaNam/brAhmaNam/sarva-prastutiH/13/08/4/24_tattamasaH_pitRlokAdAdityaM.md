<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓त्त᳓मसः पितृलोका᳓दादित्यं᳓ ज्यो᳓तिरभ्या᳓यन्ति ते᳓भ्य आ᳓गतेभ्य आञ्जनाभ्यञ्जने᳓ प्र᳓यछन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

When they have arrived, ointments for the eyes and the feet are given them: such, indeed, are human means of embellishment, and therewith they keep off death from themselves.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

On their return, they are given collurium for the eyes and ointment for the feet.
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
