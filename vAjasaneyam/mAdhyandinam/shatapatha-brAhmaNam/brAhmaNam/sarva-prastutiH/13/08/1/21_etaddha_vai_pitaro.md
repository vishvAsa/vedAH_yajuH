<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

एत᳓द्ध वै᳓ पित᳓रो मनुष्यलोक᳓ आ᳓भक्ता भवन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He makes it on ground inclining towards the north, for the north is the region of men: he thus gives him (the deceased) a share in the world of men; and in that respect, indeed, the Fathers share in the world of men that they have offspring; and his (the deceased man's) offspring will, indeed, be more prosperous.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He thus make him (the deceased) share the human world.
</details>
