<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

आ᳓श्वमेधिकम् मध्यम᳓म् पञ्चमम᳓हर्भवति ।  
त᳓स्मिन्न᳓श्वम् मे᳓ध्यमा᳓लभतेऽश्वमेध᳓स्यैवा᳓प्त्यै॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

7. The fifth day is a central Āśvamedhika [^egg_1104] one: at this (sacrifice) he seizes a horse meet for sacrifice, for the sake of his gaining the sacrificial essence of the horse.

[^egg_1104]: 419:1 That is, an Ukthya sacrifice, cf. p. 259, note 2; XIII, 5, 1, 5 seqq.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The fifth day is a central Aśvamedhika (Ukthya) sacrifice. At this, a horse fit for sacrifice is immolated, to gain the (fruit of) Aśvamedha.
</details>
