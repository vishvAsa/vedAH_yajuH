<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓पसे शूद्र᳓म् त᳓पो वै᳓ शूद्र᳓स्त᳓प एव᳓ त᳓त्त᳓पसा स᳓मर्धयति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--

for (religious) toil (he seizes) a Śūdra, for the Śūdra is toil: he thus joins toil to toil; --according to their particular form he thus supplies these divinities with victims, and, thus supplied, they supply him with all his objects of desire.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus he enriches the merchant class by means of a Viś. He binds a Śūdra for the Tapas (religious austerity); for, the Śūdra is toil.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त᳘पसे शूद्रन्त᳘पो वै᳘ शूद्रस्त᳘प ऽएव तत्त᳘पसा स᳘मर्द्धयति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

**"तपसे शूद्रम्"** (वा. सं. ३० । ४) तपो वै शूद्रः । तप एव तत्तपसा समर्धयति ।
</details>
