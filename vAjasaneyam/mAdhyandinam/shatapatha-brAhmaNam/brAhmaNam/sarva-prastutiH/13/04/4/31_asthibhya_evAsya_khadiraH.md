<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

9. From his (Prajāpati's) bones the khadira, was produced, whence that (tree) is hard and of great strength [^egg_973], for hard, as it were, is bone: with that quality he thus endows it.

[^egg_973]: 374:3 The wood of Acacia Catechu is described as dark red, and extremely hard and durable, and hence not liable to be attacked by white ants, and not touched by Teredo navalis; being much used for pestles, seed-crushers, cotton-rollers, wheel-wright's work, ploughs, bows, spear and sword-handles.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Khadira tree emerged from his (Prajāpati's) bones. Hence it is hard and very strong, since bones are very hard and strong.
</details>
