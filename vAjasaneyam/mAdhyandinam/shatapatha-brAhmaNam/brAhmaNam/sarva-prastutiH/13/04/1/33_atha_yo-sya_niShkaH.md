<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अ᳓थ यो᳙ऽस्य निष्कः᳓ प्र᳓तिमुक्तो भ᳓वति ।  
त᳓मध्वर्य᳓वे ददाति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

11. The gold ornament which is attached to his (neck) he then gives to the Adhvaryu: in giving it to the Adhvaryu he secures to himself immortal life, for gold means immortal life.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then the gold pendant which is on his neck is re- moved and he gives it to the Adhvaryu.
</details>
