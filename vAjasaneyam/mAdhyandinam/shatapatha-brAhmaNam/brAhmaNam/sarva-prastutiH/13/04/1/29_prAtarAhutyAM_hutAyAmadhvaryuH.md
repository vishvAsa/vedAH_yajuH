<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

10. When the morning offering has been performed, the Adhvaryu performs a full-offering [^egg_895] with a view to his (the Sacrificer's) gaining and securing everything, for the full means everything, and the Aśvamedha is everything.

[^egg_895]: 349:5 For particulars regarding the 'pūrṇāhuti,' or oblation of a spoonful of ghee, see part i, p. 302, note 2.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

After the morning oblation is offered, the Adhvaryu offers the *Pūrņāhuti*, in order that the Yajamāna may achieve everything and he may own everything.
</details>
