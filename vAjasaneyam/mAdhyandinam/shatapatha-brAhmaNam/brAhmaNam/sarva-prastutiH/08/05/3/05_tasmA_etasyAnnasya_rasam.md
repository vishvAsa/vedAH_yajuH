<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓स्मा एत᳓स्या᳓न्नस्य र᳓सम् प्रा᳓यच्छत्ते᳓नैनं सह᳓ प्रा᳓पद्यत॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

so he gave him the essence of that food, and entered him therewith.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He gave him the essence of that food and with that entered him.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

</details>
