<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अ᳓न्नं पु᳓रीषवती ।  
स᳓ यो᳙ ऽस्यो᳓प᳓रिष्टात्पाप्मा᳓ ऽऽसीद᳓न्नेन त᳓म᳓पाहत ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

16. The soil-bedded one [^egg_172] means food: whatever evil there was above him, that he (Prajāpati) drove away by means of food;

[^egg_172]: 87:1 That is, the fifth Asapatnā, which has a bed or layer of loose soil (purīsha) spread under it.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The one covered by soil is food.
</details>
