<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓थैवै᳙त᳓द्य᳓जमानो यो᳙ ऽस्यो᳓प᳓रिष्टात्पाप्मा᳓ भ᳓वत्य᳓न्नेनैव᳓ त᳓म᳓पहते॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and in like manner does the Sacrificer now, by means of food, drive away whatever evil there is above him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

By means of this, he drove away whatever evil was there at the upper region; similarly, this Yajamāna now drives away by means of food what sin is there in the upper region.
</details>

</details>

</details>
