<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓स्मात्स᳓र्वेषाम᳓ङ्गानां वाचै᳙वा᳓न्नस्य र᳓सं वि᳓जानाति॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

whence it is through (the channel of) speech that one distinguishes the essence of food for all the limbs.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That is why one is able to know the essence of food for all parts of body through (the mouth, the channel of) speech.
</details>

</details>

</details>
