<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓स्मादस्यां᳓ सर्व᳓तो हृ᳓दयेन म᳓नसा चेतयते᳓ ऽथो पु᳓ण्या हैता᳓ लक्ष्म्य᳙स्ता᳓ एत᳓त्सर्व᳓तो धत्ते ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And, moreover, these (bricks) are lucky signs: he places them on all sides; whence they say of him who has a (lucky) sign (lakshman) on every (or any) side that he has good luck (puṇyalakshmīka).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That is why, on this earth, everywhere, one thinks with the heart, with the mind.
</details>
