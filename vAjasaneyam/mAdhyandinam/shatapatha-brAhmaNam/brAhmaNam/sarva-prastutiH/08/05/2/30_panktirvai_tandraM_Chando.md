<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

पङ्क्ति᳓र्वै᳓ तन्द्रं᳓ छ᳓न्दो ऽङ्काङ्कं᳓ छ᳓न्द इ᳓त्या᳓पो वा᳓ अङ्काङ्कं᳓ छ᳓न्दः॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'the Slow metre,'--the 'slow' metre, doubtless, is the Paṅkti; --'the Aṅkāṅka metre,'--the 'aṅkāṅka [^egg_175]' metre, doubtless, is water.

[^egg_175]: 89:1 Another word of doubtful meaning (? winding-winding).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“tandram chandah” is the slow metre and “paṅkti” is the slow metre. “aṅkāṅkam chandah” — for, the waters are indeed the watery metre.
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

पङ्क्तिर्वै᳘ तन्द्रं छ᳘न्दो ऽङ्काङ्कं छ᳘न्द इत्या᳘पो वा᳘ अङ्काङ्कं छ᳘न्दः॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
