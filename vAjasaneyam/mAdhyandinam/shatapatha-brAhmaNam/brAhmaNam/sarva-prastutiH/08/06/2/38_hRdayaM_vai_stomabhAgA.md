<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

हृ᳓दयं वै᳓ स्तो᳓मभागा हृ᳓दयमु वा᳓ उत्तम᳓म᳓थोद᳓रम᳓थ यो᳓निः॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Outside the Stomabhāgās (he places them), for the Stomabhāgās are the heart, and the heart is highest, then (comes) the belly, then the womb.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

These are placed outside the stomabhāgas, for the stomabhāgas are the heart. Because the head is highest, then come the belly and the womb.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

हृ᳘दयं वै स्तो᳘मभागा हृ᳘दयमु वा᳘ ऽउत्तमम᳘थोद᳘रम᳘थ यो᳘निः॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

हृ᳘दयं वै स्तो᳘मभागा हृ᳘दयमु वा᳘ उत्तम᳘थोद᳘रम᳘थ यो᳘निः॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

हृदयं वै स्तोमभागाः । हृदयमु वा ऽउत्तमम् । अथोदरम्, अथ योनिः ॥ १५ ॥
</details>
