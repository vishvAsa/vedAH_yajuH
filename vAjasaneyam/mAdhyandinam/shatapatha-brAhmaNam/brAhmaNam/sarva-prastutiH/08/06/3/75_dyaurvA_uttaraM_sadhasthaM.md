<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

द्यौ᳓र्वा᳓ उ᳓त्तरं सध᳓स्थं वि᳓श्वे देवा᳓ य᳓जमानश्च सीदते᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--as the text, so the meaning; --'Upon this, the higher seat,'--the higher seat, doubtless, is the sky;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Upon this, the highest seat”—the sky is the highest seat.
</details>

</details>

</details>
