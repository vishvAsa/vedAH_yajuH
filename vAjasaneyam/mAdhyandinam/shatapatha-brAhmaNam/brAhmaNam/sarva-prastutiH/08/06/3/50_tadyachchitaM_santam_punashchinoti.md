<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓द्य᳓च्चितं᳓ स᳓न्तम् पु᳓नश्चिनो᳓ति त᳓स्माद्वेव᳓ पुनश्चितिः᳓॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and inasmuch as he again (punas) builds up (ci) that (Agni) already built, therefore also it is (called) Punaściti.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He now builds him up again and in so far as he again (*punah*) builds up (*ca*), it is called *punāściti*.
</details>
