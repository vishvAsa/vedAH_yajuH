<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then (he places one of them) in the middle (saying), “This one above, the bestower of plenty, downwards (arvāg vasu)" — the one above is of course, the cloud (rain-god) and when he refers to him as "one above" he means the rain-god who is above.
</details>
