<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

एकविंशे᳓न हैषा᳓ स्तो᳓मेन पृथिव्यां᳓ श्रिता᳓ नि᳓ष्केवल्यमुक्थ᳓म᳓व्यथायै स्तभ्नात्वि᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for by the twenty-one-fold hymn this one is indeed upheld on earth; --'The Nishkevalya-śastra may support thee for steadiness’ sake!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"May the Niṣkaivalya śastra sup- port you from being shaken" — because it is by means of the Niṣkaivalya śastra it is steadied on the earth without being shaken.
</details>

</details>

</details>
