<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

आदित्या᳓ हैत᳓स्यै दिशो᳓ देवा᳓ अ᳓धिपतयो व᳓रुणो हेतीना᳓म् प्रतिधर्ते᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Ādityas are the divinities who are your overlords”—for, Ādityas, the gods are the masters of this quarter.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

आदित्या हैतस्यै दिशो देवा अधिपतयः । **"वरुणां हेतीनां प्रतिधर्ता"**- इति ।
</details>
