<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

व᳓रुणो हैवा᳓त्र हेतीना᳓म् प्रतिधर्ता᳓ सप्तदश᳓स्त्वा स्तो᳓मः पृथिव्यां᳓ श्रयत्वि᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'The divine Ādityas are thine overlords,' for the divine Ādityas are indeed the overlords of that region; --'Varuṇa is the repeller of shafts,' for Varuṇa, indeed, is here the repeller of shafts; --'The Saptadaśa-stoma may uphold thee on

earth!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Varuṇa, the repeller of arrows. “May the Saptadaśa stoma sustain you on the earth”—for it is by means of the Saptadaśa stoma, it is sustained on the earth.
</details>

</details>
