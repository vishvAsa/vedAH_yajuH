<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

पञ्चदशे᳓न हैषा᳓ स्तो᳓मेन पृथिव्यां᳓ श्रिता᳓ प्र᳓उगमुक्थ᳓म᳓व्यथायै स्तभ्नात्वि᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for by the fifteenfold hymn it is indeed upheld on earth; --'The Praüga-śastra may support thee for steadiness’ sake!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“May the Prauga śastra, support you from being shaken”—for it is by means of the Prauga śastra, it is steadied on the earth without being shaken.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

पञ्चदशेन हैषा स्तोमेन पृथिव्यां श्रिता । **"प्रउगमुक्थमव्यथायै स्तभ्नातु"**- इति ।
</details>
