<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓स्मात्सर्व᳓तो वायुः᳓ सर्व᳓तः समी᳓चीः सर्व᳓तस्त᳓त्सम्य᳓ञ्चं वायुं᳓ दधाति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

[He places them so as] on every side to run in the same direction [^egg_87]: he thus makes the wind everywhere (to blow) in the same direction, whence, having become united, it blows from all quarters in the same direction.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It is also because of that, the winds from everywhere get united and blow from all quarters in the same direction.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

**सर्वतः समीचीरि**ति । प्रागादिसर्वदिक्षु तिर्यक्त्वाभावेनार्जवेन यथा वर्त्तन्ते तथोपदध्यादित्यर्थः । 'तत्' तेन तथोपधानेन 'वायुम्' अपि 'सम्यञ्चम्' अकुटिलं स्थापितवान् भवति । 'तस्मात् सर्वतः' इत्यादिः स्पष्टः ।
</details>
