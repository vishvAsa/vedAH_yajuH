<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓स्मात्सर्व᳓तो दि᳓शः सर्व᳓तः समी᳓चीः सर्व᳓तस्त᳓त्समी᳓चीर्दि᳓शो दधाति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He thereby places the quarters within these two worlds. He lays them in every direction and thus places the quarters in all the four directions. That is why the quarters are in all the directions. They are to face each other and thereby make the quarters face each other and hence the quarters are facing each other.
</details>
