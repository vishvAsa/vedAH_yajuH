<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓स्मादन᳓योः प्रजाः᳓ सर्व᳓त उ᳓पदधाति सर्व᳓तस्त᳓त्प्रजा᳓ दधाति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He places (bricks) on every side: he thus places creatures everywhere, whence there are creatures everywhere.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That is why we have creatures everywhere.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त᳘स्मादन᳘योः प्रजाः᳘ सर्व्व᳘त ऽउ᳘पदधाति सर्व्व᳘तस्त᳘त्प्रजा᳘ दधाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
