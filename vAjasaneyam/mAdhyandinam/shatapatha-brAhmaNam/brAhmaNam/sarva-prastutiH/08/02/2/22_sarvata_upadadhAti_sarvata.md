<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

सर्व᳓त उ᳓पदधाति सर्व᳓त एवा᳙स्मिन्नेताः᳓ प्रजाः᳓ प्र᳓पादयति॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He places them on all sides: on all sides he thus causes the creatures to enter him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thereby, he places the creatures everywhere.

He lays them on all sides and then makes these creatures enter him from all sides. (Third Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

सर्व्व᳘त ऽउ᳘पदधाति सर्व्व᳘त ऽए᳘वास्मिन्नेताः᳘ प्रजाः प्र᳘पादयति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

सर्व᳘त उ᳘पदधाति सर्व᳘त एॗवास्मिन्नेताः᳘ प्रजाः प्र᳘पादयति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

सर्वत उपदधाति । सर्वत एवास्मिन्नेताः प्रजाः प्रपादयति ॥ ६ ॥
</details>
