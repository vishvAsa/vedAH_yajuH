<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

विश्व᳓कर्मा ह्ये᳙तां᳓ तृती᳓यां चि᳓तिम᳓पश्यदन्त᳓रिक्षस्य पृष्ठे᳓ ज्यो᳓तिष्मतीमि᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for Viśvakarman saw this third layer [^egg_115]; --'on the back of the air, thee the brilliant one!'

[^egg_115]: 47:3 See VIII, 3, 1, 4 with note.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

for, it was Viśvakarmā, who discovered this third layer.
</details>
