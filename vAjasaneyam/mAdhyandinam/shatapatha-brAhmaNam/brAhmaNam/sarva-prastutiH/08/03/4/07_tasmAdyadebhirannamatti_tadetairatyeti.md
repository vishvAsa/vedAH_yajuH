<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓स्माद्य᳓देभि᳓र᳓न्नम᳓त्ति — त᳓देतै᳓र᳓त्येति ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and hence by means of (the channels of) these breathings he passes over the food which he eats with those (other) breathings.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

By those seven which he places in the back, he provides counter-breathings to those (frontal) breathings. Hence when he eats food, that food is over- run by these counter-breathings.
</details>
