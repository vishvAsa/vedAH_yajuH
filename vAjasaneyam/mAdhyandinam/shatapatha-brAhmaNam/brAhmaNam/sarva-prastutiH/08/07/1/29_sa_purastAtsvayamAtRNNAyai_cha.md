<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स᳓ पुर᳓स्तात्स्वयमातृण्णा᳓यै च विश्व᳓ज्योतिषश्चर्तव्ये᳙ उ᳓पदधाति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

9. He lays down the two seasonal ones prior to the naturally-perforated one and to the Viśvajyotis;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He places the ṛtavyās bricks in front of the naturally perforated one and the viśvajyoti brick.
</details>

</details>
