<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स᳓र्वं वै᳓ सह᳓स्रं स᳓र्वमसि स᳓र्वस्मै त्वे᳓त्येत᳓त्॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--a thousand, doubtless, means everything: thus, 'Everything thou art,--thee for everything!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(It therefore means) "You are all and unto you who are all."
</details>

</details>
