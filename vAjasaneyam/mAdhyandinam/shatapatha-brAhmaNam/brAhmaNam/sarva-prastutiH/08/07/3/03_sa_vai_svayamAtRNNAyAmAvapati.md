<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

2. He also strews it on (the place where lies) the naturally-perforated (brick), for the naturally-perforated one means vital air, and the loose soil

means food: he thus puts food into (the channels of) the vital air.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He strews (loose soil) over the naturally perforated brick; for, the naturally perforated one is prāṇa and loose soil is food.
</details>
