<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

5. 'The man-viewers’ share thou art, the creator's lordship!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"You are the share of the Nṛcakṣas and the overlordship of Dhātā” — the Nṛcakṣas are indeed gods (literally meaning those who keep a watch on humans).
</details>
