<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

संवत्सरो᳓ हि᳓ स᳓र्वाणि भूता᳓नि प्रतिर᳓ति त᳓देव᳓ त᳓द्रूप᳓मु᳓पदधाति॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And as to his calling it 'speed,' the year indeed speeds all beings: it is that form he now lays down.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That is called pratūrti because it is the year that develops (brings to maturity) all these beings. It is that form he now lays down.
</details>
