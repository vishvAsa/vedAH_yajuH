<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓स्मादेता᳓ अ᳓त्रो᳓पदधाति ।  
स्तो᳓मानु᳓पदधाति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He lays down the Stomas: the stomas being the vital airs, and Vāyu (the wind) also being the vital airs, it is Vāyu he thus lays down.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Therefore he places them in this layer.
</details>

</details>
