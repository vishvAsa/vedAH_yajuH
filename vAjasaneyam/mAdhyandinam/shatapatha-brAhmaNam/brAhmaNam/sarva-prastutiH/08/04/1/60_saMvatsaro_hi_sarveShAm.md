<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And as to why he calls it 'vigour,' the year is indeed the most vigorous of all existing things: it is that form he now lays down.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

As to why he calls it varcas (brilliance) is because the year indeed is the most brilliant of all that exist here. It is that form he now lays down.
</details>
