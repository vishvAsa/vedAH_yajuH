<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

प्राणा᳓ वै᳓ स्तो᳓माः प्राणा᳓ उ वा᳓ ऋ᳓षय ऋ᳓षीनेवै᳙त᳓दु᳓पदधाति॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He lays down the Stomas:--the stomas being the vital airs, and the R̥shis also being the vital airs, it is the R̥shis he thus lays down.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He lays down the *stomas* which are *prāṇas* and are also *ṛṣis*. Therefore he (actually) lays down the *ṛṣis*.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

प्राणा वै स्तो᳘माः प्राणा᳘ ऽउ वा ऽऋ᳘षय ऽऋ᳘षीने᳘वैतदु᳘पदधाति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

प्राणा वै स्तो᳘माः प्राणा᳘ उ वा ऋ᳘षय ऋ᳘षीनेॗवैतदु᳘पदधाति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

प्राणा वै स्तोमाः । प्राणा उ वा ऋषयः । ऋषीनेवैतदुपदधाति ॥ ५ ॥
</details>
