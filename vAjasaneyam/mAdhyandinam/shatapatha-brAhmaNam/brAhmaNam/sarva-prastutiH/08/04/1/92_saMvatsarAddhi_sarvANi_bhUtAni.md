<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

संवत्सरा᳓द्धि᳓ स᳓र्वाणि भूता᳓नि विव᳓र्तन्ते त᳓देव᳓ त᳓द्रूप᳓मु᳓पदधाति॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

And as to his calling it 'the revolving sphere,' from the year all creatures indeed are evolved: it is that form he now lays down.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Why he calls it re- volving force is, because of the year, all these beings revolve (have a cyclic existence). It is that form he lays down now.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

संव्वत्सराद्धि स᳘र्व्वाणि भूता᳘नि व्विव᳘र्तन्ते त᳘देव त᳘द्रूपमु᳘पदधाति॥
</details>
