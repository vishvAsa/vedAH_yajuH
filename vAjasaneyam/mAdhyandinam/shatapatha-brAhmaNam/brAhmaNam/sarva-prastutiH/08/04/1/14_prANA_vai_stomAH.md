<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

प्राणा᳓ वै᳓ स्तो᳓माः प्राणा᳓ उ वै᳓ प्रजा᳓पतिः प्रजा᳓पतिमेवै᳙त᳓दु᳓पदधाति॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for in this way Prajāpati and the gods used of old to converse together. He lays down the Stomas: the stomas being the vital airs, and Prajāpati also being the vital airs, it is Prajāpati he thus lays down.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(The father always ready to grant the wishes of his sons without expecting anything in return and the sons fulfilling the wishes of their father only on *quid pro quo* basis.) He lays down the *stomas* which are *prāṇas* and *prāṇas* are the same as Prajāpati and so (actually) he lays down Prajāpati.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

प्राणा वै स्तो᳘माः प्राणा᳘ ऽउ वै᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिः प्रजा᳘पतिमे᳘वैतदु᳘पदधाति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

प्राणा वै स्तो᳘माः प्राणा᳘ उ वै᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिः प्रजा᳘पतिमेॗवैतदु᳘पदधाति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

प्राणा वै स्तोमाः । प्राणा उ वै प्रजापतिः । प्रजापतिमेवैतदुपदधाति ॥ ४ ॥
</details>
