<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓थैवै᳙त᳓द्य᳓जमान एते᳓न सप्तदशे᳓न संवत्सरे᳓ण प्रजा᳓पतिना प्रजनयित्रै᳙ताः᳓ प्रजाः᳓ प्र᳓जनयति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and having created them, he makes them enter his own self [^egg_145].

[^egg_145]: 76:1 That is, he makes them pass into his own power, makes them his own.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

In the same manner, this Yajamāna with the seventeenfold year, the Prajāpati, the progenitor, creates these beings and having created them he makes them enter his own self.
</details>
