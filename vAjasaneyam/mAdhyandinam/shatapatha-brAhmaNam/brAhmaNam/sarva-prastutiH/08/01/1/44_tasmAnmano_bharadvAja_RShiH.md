<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓स्मान्म᳓नो भर᳓द्वाज ऋ᳓षिः प्रजा᳓पतिगृहीतया त्व᳓ये᳓ति प्रजा᳓पतिसृष्टया त्व᳓ये᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

therefore the R̥shi Bharadvāja is the mind.--'By thee, taken by Prajāpati,'--that is, 'by thee, created by Prajāpati;' --'I take the mind for my descendants!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"By you taken hold of by Prajāpati" – (it means) 'by you who are created by Prajāpati.” “I take the mind for my descendants" — (so saying) he introduces the mind from the right side.
</details>
