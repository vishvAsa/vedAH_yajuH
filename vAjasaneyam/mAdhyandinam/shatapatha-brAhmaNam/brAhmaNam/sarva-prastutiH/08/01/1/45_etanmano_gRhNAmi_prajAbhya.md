<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

एत᳓न्म᳓नो गृह्णामि प्रजा᳓भ्य इ᳓ति म᳓नो दक्षिणतः᳓ प्रा᳓पादयत ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--therewith he introduced the mind from the right side.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He lays down (these ten bricks) separately. Thereby he introduces into it what separate desires there are in the mind.
</details>
