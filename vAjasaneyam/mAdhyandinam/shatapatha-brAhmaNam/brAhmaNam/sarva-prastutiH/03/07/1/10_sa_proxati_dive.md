<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स᳓ प्रो᳓क्षति ।  
दिवे᳓ त्वान्त᳓रिक्षाय त्वा पृथिव्यै᳓ त्वे᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

5. He sprinkles (the top, middle, and bottom parts) with, 'For the sky--thee! for the air--thee! for the earth--thee!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He sprinkles (saying) "For the sky-thee! For the air-thee! For the earth- thee!" The stake being a thunderbolt (he does so) for the protection of these worlds. 'I sprinkle thee for the protection of these worlds' is what he means to say.
</details>
