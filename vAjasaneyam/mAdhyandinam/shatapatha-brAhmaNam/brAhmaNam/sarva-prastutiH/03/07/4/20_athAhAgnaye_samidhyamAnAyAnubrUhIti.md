<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

7. Thereupon he says (to the Hotr̥), 'Recite to the fire being kindled!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now he says, "Make the (invitation) call for Agni who is being kindled".

He thereupon tells (the Hotṛ) “Recite to the fire that is being kindled”.
</details>
