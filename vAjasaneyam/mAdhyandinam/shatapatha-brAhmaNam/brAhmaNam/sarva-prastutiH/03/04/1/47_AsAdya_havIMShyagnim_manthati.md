<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

19. When he has placed the sacrificial dishes (on the altar) [^egg_239], he churns the fire.

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Having placed the sacrificial dishes (on the alter), he churns the fire. This thing viz. guest-offering is indeed the head of the sacrifice.
