<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

2. It is as Vāc [^egg_158] that he steps into them;

[^egg_158]: 59:1 Literally, 'by a form (rūpeṇa) of Vāc (speech),' viz. the text which is pronounced.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Indeed he goes out with the form of speech herself.
</details>
