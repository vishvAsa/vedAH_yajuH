<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ते᳓ होचुः ।  
स्वै᳓दायन त्व᳓या वीरे᳓णेम᳓म् प्रतिसं᳓यतामहा इ᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

3. They said, 'Svaidāyana, with thee as our champion we will fight this fellow.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They said "O Svaidāyana, we shall defeat this (enemy) with you as our leader." He (Svaidāyana) said, "You all remain here in peace; I will just go and know (about) him." He then proceeded to him (Uddālaka).
</details>
