<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

15. And inasmuch as the fore-offerings have ghee for their offering-material, a boy's seed is not productive, but is like water, for ghee is like water;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The prayājās (fore-offerings) have ghee as their offering material and that is why a boys semen is not productive;
</details>
