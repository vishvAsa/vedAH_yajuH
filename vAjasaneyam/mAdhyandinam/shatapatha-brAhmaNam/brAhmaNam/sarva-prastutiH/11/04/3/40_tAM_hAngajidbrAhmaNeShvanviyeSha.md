<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

तां᳓ हाङ्गजि᳓द्ब्राह्मणे᳓ष्व᳓न्वियेष ता᳓मु ह या᳓ज्ञवल्क्ये विवेद ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He enquired about her (asked about the identity of that iṣṭi) to brahmins who knew (all) the Veda portion. He came to know that Yājñavalkya knew her.
</details>
