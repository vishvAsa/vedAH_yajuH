<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स᳓ वायु᳓ना ब्र᳓ह्मणो द्वा᳓रेण प्रतिप᳓द्य ब्र᳓ह्मणः सा᳓युज्यं सलोक᳓तां जयति ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and, by entering through the wind-door of the Brahman, he wins his union with, and participation in the world of, the Brahman.
</details>

</details>
