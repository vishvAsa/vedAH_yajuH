<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अ᳓थो अ᳓प्याहुः ।  
सौर्य᳓ एवै᳙ष᳓ पशुः᳓ स्यादि᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

2. And they also say, 'To Sūrya (the sun) that victim should belong;'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Again they say, "That Paśu should belong to Sūrya"; that is why when the sun sets, the cattle are tied up;
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(त्य᳘) अ᳘थो ऽअ᳘प्याहुः॥  
सौर्य्य᳘ ऽए᳘वैष᳘ पशुः᳘ स्यादि᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

अ᳘थो अ᳘प्याहुः॥  
सौर्य᳘ एॗवैष᳘ पशुः᳘ स्यादि᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

अथो अप्याहुः । सौर्य एवैष पशुः स्यादिति ।
</details>
