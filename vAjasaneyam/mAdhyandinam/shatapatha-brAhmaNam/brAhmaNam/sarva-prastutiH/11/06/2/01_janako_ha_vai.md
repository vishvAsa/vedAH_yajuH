<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

जनको᳓ ह वै᳓ वै᳓देहो ।  
ब्राह्मणै᳓र्धाव᳓यद्भिः समा᳓जगाम ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

1. Now, Janaka of Videha once met some Brāhmaṇas who were travelling about [^egg_352], to wit, Śvetaketu Āruṇeya, Somaśushma Sātyayajñi, and Yājñavalkya.

[^egg_352]: 112:1 Or, driving about (and officiating at sacrifices); see XI, 4, 1, 1. For a translation of this story see Max Müller, History of Ancient Sanskrit Literature, p. 421 seqq.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Janaka (the king) of Videha once came across some priests going about (officiating at sacrifices);
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

तथा अग्निहोत्रहोमे पूर्वोक्तयोराहुत्योरूर्ध्वगत्यादिविषयां विद्यां वक्तुं आख्यायिका प्रस्तूयते- **जनको ह वै वैदेह** इत्यादिना । 'धावयद्भिः' देशांतरादागतैः ब्राह्मणैः 'समाजगाम' संगतो बभूव ।
