</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

1. Janaka of Videha performed a sacrifice accompanied with numerous gifts to the priests. Setting apart a thousand cows, he said, 'He who is the most learned in sacred writ amongst you, O Brāhmaṇas, shall drive away these (cows) [^egg_355]!'

[^egg_355]: 115:1 One might also construe,--These are yours, O Brāhmaṇas: he who is the most learned in sacred writ shall drive (them) away. Cf. Delbrück, Altind. Syntax, pp. 251, 363.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Janaka of Videha, once performed a sacrifice with plenty of dakṣiṇā.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

अथ अग्निहोत्रदर्शपूर्णमासादियागदेवतानां तत्त्वतः प्राणशब्दाभिधेयपरमात्मरूपतां विजिगीषुकथामुखेन विवक्षुः आख्यायिकां प्रस्तौति- **जनको ह वैदेह** इति । विदेहदेशानां राजा 'वैदेहः' ।

**बहुदक्षिणेने**ति । सहस्रसर्ववेदसदक्षिणेनेत्यर्थः । 'यज्ञेन ईजे' इष्टवान् । यजेर्लिटि यजादित्वात् संप्रसारणम् ।
</details>
