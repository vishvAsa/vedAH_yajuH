<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

किं᳓ विदितं᳓ का᳓ प्रा᳓यश्चित्तिरि᳓त्युदानः᳓ प्राण᳓म᳓प्यगादि᳓त्याहवनी᳓य आ᳓हुतिं जुहुयाम् ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

he asked.--'The upward breathing has entered the breath of the mouth--this (is the knowledge); and I would make the offering on the Āhavanīya--this would be the atonement, and I should not be committing that sin.'--'This much, then, reverend sir, we two (know) in common,' said (Śauceya).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"It means that the udāna (upward breath) has entered the prāṇa and I would make offering in the Āhavanīya fire, this is the attonement. Thereby I don't comit any wrong"- said Uddālaka.
</details>
