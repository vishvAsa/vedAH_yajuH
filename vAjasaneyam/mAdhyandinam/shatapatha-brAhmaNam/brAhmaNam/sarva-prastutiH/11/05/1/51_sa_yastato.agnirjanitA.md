<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स᳓ य᳓स्त᳓तो ऽग्नि᳓र्जनिता᳓, स᳓ एव᳓ स᳓ भविते᳓ति ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and taking each time three logs from this Aśvattha tree, anoint them with ghee, and put them on the fire with

verses containing the words "log" and "ghee": the fire which shall result therefrom will be that very fire (which is required).'
</details>

</details>
