<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अस्म᳓त्सखा त्वं᳓ देव सोम वि᳓श्वेषां देवा᳓नाम् प्रिय᳓म् पा᳓थो᳓ऽपीही᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

On that former occasion he possessed himself of the (three) Soma services;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

With them so refreshed, he performs that (part of the) sacrifice which is yet remaining.
</details>

</details>

</details>
