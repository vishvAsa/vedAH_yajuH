<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ऋत᳓व ऋत्वि᳓जः ।

</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

2. The officiating priests are the seasons; and, verily, sacrifice is offered at the end of the seasons of him whoso knows that the officiating priests are the seasons;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The officiating priests are the seasons. One who, knowing that the officiating priests are the seasons and offers sacrifice at the end of the sea- sons, by him, all that is done in the seasons, becomes attained, secured, and won for him.
</details>
