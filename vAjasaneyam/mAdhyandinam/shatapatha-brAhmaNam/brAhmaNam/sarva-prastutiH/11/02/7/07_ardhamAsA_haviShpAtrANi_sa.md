<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अर्धमासा᳓ हविष्पात्रा᳓णि ।  
स᳓ यो᳓ ह वा᳓ अर्धमासा᳓ हविष्पात्रा᳓णी᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

4. The oblation-vessels are the half-months; and, verily, sacrifice is offered at the end of the half-months of him whoso knows that the oblation-vessels are the half-months;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The oblation vessels are the fortnights. One who, knowing that the oblation vessels are the fortnights, offers sacrifice at the end of the fort- nights, by him, all that is done in the half-months becomes attained, se- cured and won for him.
</details>
