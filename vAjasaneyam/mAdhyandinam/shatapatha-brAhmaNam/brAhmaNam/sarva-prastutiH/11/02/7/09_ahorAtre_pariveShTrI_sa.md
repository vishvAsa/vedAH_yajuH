<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अहोरात्रे᳓ परिवेष्ट्री᳓ ।  
स᳓ यो᳓ ह वा᳓ अहोरात्रे᳓ परिवेष्ट्री᳓ इ᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

5. The two attendants [^egg_162] are the day and night;

[^egg_162]: 39:1 Sāyaṇa seems to take the two attendants (pariveshṭrī, preparers or servers-up of food) to mean the pair of fire-tongs (dhr̥shṭī):--ye pariveshaṇa-sādhane dhr̥shṭī tayor ahorātrabuddhiṁ vidhatte.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The pariveṣṭrīs (the pair of fire-tongs) are the day and night; one who, having known that the pariveṣṭrīs are the day and night;
</details>
