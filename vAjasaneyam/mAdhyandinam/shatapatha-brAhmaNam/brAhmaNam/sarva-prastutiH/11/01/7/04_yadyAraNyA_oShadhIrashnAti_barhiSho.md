<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓द्यारण्या᳓ ओ᳓षधीरश्ना᳓ति बर्हि᳓षो मे᳓धमश्नाति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

and if he eats forest plants he eats the essence of the barhis [^egg_116];

[^egg_116]: 21:2 The layer of sacrificial grass spread on the Vedi, serving as a seat for the deities to whom offering is made.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

If he eats what is widely grown, he eats the essence of the barhis grass. If he eats what is procured from trees, he eats the essence of the sacrificial samidhās. If he consumes milk, it will amount to, consuming the essence of the Sānnāya and the ghee.
</details>
