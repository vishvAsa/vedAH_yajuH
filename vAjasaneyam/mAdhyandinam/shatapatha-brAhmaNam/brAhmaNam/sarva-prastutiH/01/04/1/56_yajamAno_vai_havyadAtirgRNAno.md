<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓जमानो वै᳓ हव्य᳓दातिर्गृणानो᳓ य᳓जमानाये᳓त्येवै᳙त᳓दाह नि᳓ हो᳓ता सत्सि बर्हि᳓षी᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

'The giver of oblations [^egg_288],' of course, is the

sacrificer: hence 'invoked for the sacrificer' is what he thereby means to say.--'As Hotr̥ on the barhis sit!'

[^egg_288]: 107:3 Havyadāti, the correct meaning of the word is 'the giving of oblations.'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The sacrificer indeed is the giver of oblations. Indeed he verily says (means) this. "Speaking for (in favour of) the sacrificer".
</details>
