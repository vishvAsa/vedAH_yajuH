<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

देव᳓मेवै᳙त᳓त्सविता᳓रमाहान्धे᳓ त᳓मसि बधाने᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'Tie him down, O divine Savitr̥, to the furthest end of the earth!' he says (whilst throwing on the heap of rubbish the soil dug up); he thus

says to the divine Savitr̥: 'Tie him down to blind darkness!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When he says, “at the farthest (point of the) earth”, he verily says to the divine Savitṛ, “Bind (him) in blinding darkness”, (he says), “With a hundred nooses”.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

देव᳘मे᳘वैत᳘त्सविता᳘रमाहान्धेत᳘मसि बधाने᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

देव᳘मेॗवैत᳘त्सविता᳘रमाहान्धे त᳘मसि बधाने᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

देवमेवैतत्सवितारमाह । अन्धे तमसि बधानेति ।
</details>
