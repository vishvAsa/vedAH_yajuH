<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स᳓ नि᳓नयति — त्रिता᳓य त्वा द्विता᳓य त्वैकता᳓य त्वे᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He pours it out with the formulas, 'For Trita thee!' 'For Dvita thee!' 'For Ekata thee!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He pours out, saying, "For Trita (I pour) you" "For Dvita (I pour) you" "For Ekata (I pour) you".
</details>
