<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

28. He looks down on it, with the text (Vāj. S. I, 31), 'Lustrous art thou! resplendent art thou! immortal (or, ambrosia) art thou!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He looks down (into the ghee) (chanting), "You are lustrous, you are bright (pure), you are immortal". This chant is indeed true.
</details>
