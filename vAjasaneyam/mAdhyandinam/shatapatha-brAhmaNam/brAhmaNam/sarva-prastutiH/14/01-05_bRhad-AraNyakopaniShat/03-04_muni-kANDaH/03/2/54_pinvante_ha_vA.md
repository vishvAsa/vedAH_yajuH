<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

पि᳓न्वन्ते ह वा᳓ अस्मै वर्षा᳓ य᳓ एव᳓मेत᳓द्वे᳓द॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus, all these three are depended upon by all gods and all beings; and verily the rains pour down in abundance to the one who knows this.
</details>
