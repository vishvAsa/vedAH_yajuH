<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

14. Now sand has been strewed [^egg_1225] on the north side of it: below that he (previously) throws (a plate of) white gold [^egg_1226], with, 'Protect it from contact with the earth!'

[^egg_1225]: 462:2 North of the Gārhapatya and the Āhavanīya in the śālā two mounds (khara) are formed, covered with (or consisting of) sand. The one north of the latter fire is here alluded to.

[^egg_1226]: 462:3 That is, a silver plate weighing a hundred grains.
</details>
