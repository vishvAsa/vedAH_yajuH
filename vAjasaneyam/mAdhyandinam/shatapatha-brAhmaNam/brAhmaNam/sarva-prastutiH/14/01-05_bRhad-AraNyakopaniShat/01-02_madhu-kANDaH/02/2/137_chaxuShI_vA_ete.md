<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

च᳓क्षुषी वा᳓ एते᳓ यज्ञ᳓स्य य᳓दा᳓ज्यभागौ च᳓क्षुषी रौहिणौ᳓ ने᳓च्च᳓क्षुषा च᳓क्षुरभ्यारोह᳓याणी᳓ति॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Well, those two--to wit, the butter-portions--being the eyes of the sacrifice, and so also the two Rauhiṇa (cakes)--(it is so) lest he should overtop eye by eye.
</details>
