<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓दुभय᳓त आदीप्ता᳓ मौञ्जाः᳓ प्रलवा᳓ भवन्ति ता᳓नुपा᳓स्य ते᳓षु प्र᳓वृणक्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

When the sacrifice had its head cut off, its life-sap flowed away, and therefrom these plants grew up: with that life-sap he thus supplies and completes it.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When the head of the sacrifice was cut off, its essence flowed out and from that these plants grew.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त᳘दुभय᳘त ऽआदीप्ता᳘ मौञ्जाः᳘ प्रलवा᳘ भवन्ति ता᳘नुपा᳘स्य ते᳘षु प्र᳘वृणक्ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

त᳘दुभय᳘त आदीप्ता᳘ [^wbr_6] मौञ्जाः᳘ प्रलवा᳘ भवन्ति ता᳘नुपा᳘स्य ते᳘षु प्र᳘वृणक्ति

[^wbr_6]: The accent of आदीप्त᳘ is remarkable as in contradiction to the general rule. Are we perhaps to read उभयतआदीप्ता᳘ or उभय᳘तआदीप्ता as a compound?
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

तदुभयत आदीप्ता मौंजाः प्रलवा भवन्ति । तानुपास्य तेषु प्रवृणक्ति ।
</details>
