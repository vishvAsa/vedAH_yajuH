<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓स्मादाह सुक्षित्यै᳓ त्वे᳓ति तूष्णी᳓म् पि᳓न्वने तूष्णीं᳓ रौहिणकपाले᳓॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Silently (he takes out) the two milking-bowls, and silently the two Rauhiṇa-plates.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It is him, he thus gratifies. So he says "You for a good abode." He pulls out (from the kiln) the two milking bowls and the two Rauhiṇa plates silently. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

त᳘स्मादाह सुक्षित्यै त्वे᳘ति तूष्णीम्पि᳘न्वने तूष्णी᳘ᳫँ᳘ रौहिणकपाले॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

त᳘स्मादाह सुक्षित्यै त्वे᳘ति तूष्णीम् पि᳘न्वने तूष्णीं᳘ रौहिणकपाले॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

तस्मादाह- **"सुक्षित्यै त्वा"**- इति । तूष्णीं पिन्वने । तूष्णीं रौहिणकपाले ॥ २४ ॥
</details>
