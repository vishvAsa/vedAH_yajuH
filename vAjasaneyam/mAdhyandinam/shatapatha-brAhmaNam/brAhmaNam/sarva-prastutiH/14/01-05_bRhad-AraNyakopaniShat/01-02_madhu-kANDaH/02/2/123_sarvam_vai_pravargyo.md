<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स᳓र्वम् वै᳓ प्रव᳓र्ग्यो ने᳓त्स᳓र्वस्मा इव स᳓र्वं कर᳓वाणी᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

46. Let him not perform the Pravargya for any and every one, lest he should do everything for every one, for the Pravargya is everything;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Let him not perform the Pravargya indiscriminately for anybody; for that would amount to doing everything for every body and the Pravargya is everything.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

(त्स᳘) स᳘र्व्वम्वै᳘ प्प्रव᳘र्ग्यो नेत्स᳘र्व्वस्मा ऽइव स᳘र्व्वङ्कर᳘वाणी᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

स᳘र्वम् वै᳘ प्रव᳘र्ग्यो नेत्स᳘र्वस्मा इव स᳘र्वं कर᳘वाणी᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

सर्वं वै प्रवर्ग्यः । नेत्सर्वस्मा इव सर्वं करवाणीति ।
</details>
