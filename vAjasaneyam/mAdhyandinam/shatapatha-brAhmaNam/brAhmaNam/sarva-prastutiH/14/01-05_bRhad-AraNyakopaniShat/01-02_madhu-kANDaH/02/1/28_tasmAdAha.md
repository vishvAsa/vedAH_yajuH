<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓स्मादाह स्वा᳓हे᳓न्द्रवत्स्वा᳓हे᳓न्द्रवदि᳓ति त्रिष्कृ᳓त्व आह ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Thrice he says it, for threefold is the sacrifice.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Svāhākāra he utters first and then the deity for whom it is meant.
</details>
