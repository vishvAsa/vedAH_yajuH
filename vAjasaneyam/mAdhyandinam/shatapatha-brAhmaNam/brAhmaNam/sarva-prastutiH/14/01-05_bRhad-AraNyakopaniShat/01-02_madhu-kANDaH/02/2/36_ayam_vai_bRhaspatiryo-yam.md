<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अय᳓म् वै᳓ बृ᳓हस्प᳓तिर्यो᳙ऽय᳓म् प᳓वते त᳓स्मा एवै᳙नं जुहोति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--Br̥haspati indeed is he who blows here: it is to him he thus offers

it, and therefore he says, 'To Br̥haspati--thee;'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This one who blows is indeed Bṛhaspati and to him it is offered so he says, "To you Bṛhaspati." By saying accompanied by Viśvedevas, he includes all the gods in this.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

अयम् वै बृ᳘हस्प᳘ति᳘र्योऽयम् प᳘वते त᳘स्मा ऽए᳘वैनञ्जुहोति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

अयम् वै बृ᳘हस्प᳘तिॗर्योऽयम् प᳘वते त᳘स्मा एॗवैनं जुहोति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

अयं वै बृहस्पतिः । यो ऽयं पवते । तस्मा एवैनं जुहोति ।
</details>
