</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

30. Thereupon he says, 'Pronounce the invitatory prayer to Agni Kavyavāhana!' that being for (Agni as) the Svishṭakr̥t ('maker of good offering').
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he says, "Make the invitation prayer for Agni Kavyavdhana". That is what takes the place of Śviṣṭakṛt.
</details>
