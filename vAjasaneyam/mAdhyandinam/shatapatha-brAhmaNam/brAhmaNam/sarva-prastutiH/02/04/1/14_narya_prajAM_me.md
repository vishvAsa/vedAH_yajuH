<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

न᳓र्य प्रजां᳓ मे पाही᳓ति ।  
प्रजा᳓या हैष᳓ ईष्टे ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He worships Gārhapatya (chanting), “Oh one benign to men! please protect my progeny”. For this (Agni) has power over progeny.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>
