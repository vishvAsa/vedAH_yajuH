<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स᳓ य᳓त्त᳓तो रा᳓त्र्याश्ना᳓ति हुतोच्छिष्ट᳓मेव᳓ त᳓न्निर᳓वत्तबल्यश्नाति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

he eats verily the left over from what has been offered and portions of which have been distributed (to beings).
</details>
