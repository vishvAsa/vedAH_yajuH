<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓दभ्य᳙मृशत्पु᳓ष्यत्वि᳓ति पु᳓ष्यतु भू᳓यो ऽस्त्वि᳓त्येव᳓ त᳓दब्रवीत्॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

He touched it: 'May it grow! May it grow and multiply!' he said.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He caressed it saying "May you grow! May you further multiply". And the embryo which was inside was created as vāyu (the wind).
</details>
