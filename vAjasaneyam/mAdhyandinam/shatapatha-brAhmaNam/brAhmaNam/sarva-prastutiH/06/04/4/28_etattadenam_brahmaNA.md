<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

एत᳓त्त᳓देनम् ब्र᳓ह्मणा य᳓जुषैत᳓स्माछौ᳓द्राद्व᳓र्णादपा᳓दत्ते॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

By means of the brahman, the yajus (formula), he thus removes him (Agni) from the Śūdra caste.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

By using the word vītaye (in the sense of 'for feasting') which occurs in the Brahmanic Yajus chant (Agnī āyāhi vītaye), he removes him (Agni) from the Śūdra caste (of the ass).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>
