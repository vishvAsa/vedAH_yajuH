<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

रश्म᳓यो वा᳓ एत᳓स्याञ्ज᳓यो वाघ᳓तस्ता᳓नेत᳓दाह परोबाहु᳓ प्र᳓गृह्णाति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--as the text, so its meaning; --'upright, as a bestower [^egg_449] of strength,'--for standing upright he (the sun) indeed bestows [^egg_449] strength, food; --'when we utter our call with the shining offerers'--the shining offerers [^egg_450], doubtless, are his (the sun's) rays: it is these he means. He lifts it up beyond the reach of his arms, for beyond the reach of his arms is that (sun) from here.

[^egg_449]: 223:2 Or, a winner--wins.

[^egg_450]: 223:3 Añjayo vāghataḥ. See p. 217, note 2.
</details>
