<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

7. He then ties it (the lump) up: he thereby keeps the seed within the womb;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he ties it up (the lump). He thereby secures the seed in the womb, so that the seed kept safely in the womb does not escape.
</details>
