<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

असौ᳓ वा᳓ आदित्य᳓ एषो᳙ ऽग्नि᳓रमुं᳓ त᳓दादित्य᳓मित᳓ ऊर्ध्व᳓म् प्रा᳓ञ्चं दधाति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for this Agni is yonder sun: he thus places yonder sun upwards from here in the east, and hence yonder sun is placed upwards from here in the east. [He does so, with, Vāj. S. XI, 42; R̥k S. I, 36, 13] 'Upright for our protection, stand thou like the god Savitr̥!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

for this Agni is yonder Sun.

With this Vikṛti chant, he should hold him up from here eastwards. This Agni is yonder Sun.
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

असौ वा᳘ आदित्य᳘ एॗषो ऽग्नि᳘रमुं त᳘दादित्य᳘मित᳘ ऊर्ध्वम् प्रा᳘ञ्चं दधाति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

असौ वा ऽआदित्यः । एषो ऽग्निः । अमुं तदादित्यमित ऊर्द्ध्वं प्राञ्चं दधाति ।
</details>
