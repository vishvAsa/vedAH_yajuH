<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓स्योक्तो᳓ ब᳓न्धुर᳓ह्न एवै᳙ता᳓म᳓रिष्टिं स्वस्ति᳓मा᳓शास्ते ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--and thereby regales him with food for the day--with, 'Day by day bearing unremittingly;' --the meaning of this has been told: he prays for that same security and well-being for the day; and whatsoever he puts on thereafter by day, that he puts on as a libation offered to him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

By this, he seeks the same security and well-being for the day.
</details>
