<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓जमानो वै᳓ दाता᳓ प्र᳓ य᳓जमानं तारिष इ᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--the giver, doubtless, is the Sacrificer: thus, 'Onward lead thou the Sacrificer!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Encourage the one who gives us more and more”—(here) the Yajamāna is the giver and it means prompt (the Yajamāna to give more and more).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>
