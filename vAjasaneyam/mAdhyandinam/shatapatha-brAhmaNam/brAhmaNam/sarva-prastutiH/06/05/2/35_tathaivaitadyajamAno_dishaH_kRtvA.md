<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓थैवै᳙त᳓द्य᳓जमानो दि᳓शः कृत्वा᳓ ता᳓स्वेता᳓माशि᳓षमा᳓शास्ते॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

For the All-gods, the friends of all men, having fashioned the quarters, invoked this blessing on them; and in like manner the Sacrificer, having fashioned the quarters, now invokes this blessing on them.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

</details>

</details>
