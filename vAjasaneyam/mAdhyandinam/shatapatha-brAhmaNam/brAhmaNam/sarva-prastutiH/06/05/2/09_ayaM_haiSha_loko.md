<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अयं᳓ हैष᳓ लोको᳓ निधि᳓स्त᳓मेत᳓द्व᳓सवो गायत्रे᳓ण छ᳓न्दसाकुर्वंस्त᳓थैवै᳙नमय᳓मेत᳓द्गायत्रे᳓ण छ᳓न्दसा करोत्यङ्गिरस्व᳓दि᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--for the bottom part is this (terrestrial) world, and this the Vasus fashioned by means of the Gāyatrī metre; and in like manner does this one now fashion it by means of the Gāyatrī metre; --'Aṅgiras-like,' he says, for Aṅgiras is the breath.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This bottom part is this (terrestrial) world and this, the Vasus made by means of the Gāyatri metre and in the like manner, this one, (Yajamāna) now makes it (the bottom of the ukhā) by means of the Gāyatri Metre.
</details>
