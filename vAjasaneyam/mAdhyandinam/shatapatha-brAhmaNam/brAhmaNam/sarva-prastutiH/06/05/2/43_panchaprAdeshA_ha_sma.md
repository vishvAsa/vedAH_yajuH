</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

But, indeed, an arrow formerly used to be five spans long [^egg_467].

[^egg_467]: 236:1 Yasmin kāle dhanurvedānusāreṇa dharmataḥ kshatriyā yudhyante tasmin kāle pañcaprādeśeshur āsīt, adhunā tv iyam aniyataparimāṇā vartante, Sāy.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

But once the arrow, indeed used to measure five spans. (First Brāhmaṇa Ends)
</details>
