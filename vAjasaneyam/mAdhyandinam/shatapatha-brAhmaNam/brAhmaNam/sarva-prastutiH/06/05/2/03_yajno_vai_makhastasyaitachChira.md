<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

यज्ञो᳓ वै᳓ मख᳓स्त᳓स्यैत᳓च्छि᳓र आहवनी᳓यो वै᳓ यज्ञ᳓स्य शि᳓र आहवनी᳓यमु वा᳓ एतं᳓ चेष्य᳓न्भवति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--Makha, doubtless, is the sacrifice, and this is its head; for the Āhavanīya fire is the head of the sacrifice, and that Āhavanīya (fire-altar) he is now about to build: hence he says, 'Makha's head thou art!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(This lump of clay to be made into a seat of Agni in that Āhavanīya is as much the head of the sacrifice as the Āhavanīya altar itself).
</details>
