<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

पुर᳓स्ताद्धी᳙य᳓मात्म᳓नो वा᳓क्ता᳓मेत᳓स्या एव᳓ मृदः᳓ करोति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

The 'invincible' (brick) is speech: that she (the wife) makes first, for this speech is foremost in the body.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

She is made first; for, this speech is indeed the first in this self.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

पुर᳘स्ता᳘द्धीय᳘मात्म᳘नो व्वाक्ता᳘मेत᳘स्या ऽएव᳘ मृदः᳘ करोति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

पुर᳘स्ताॗद्धीय᳘मात्म᳘नो वाक्ता᳘मेत᳘स्या एव᳘ मृदः᳘ करोति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

पुरस्ताद्धीयमात्मनो वाक् । तामेतस्या एव मृदः करोति ।
</details>
