<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अ᳓थो यो᳓षा वा᳓ उखा᳓ यो᳓षायां त᳓त्प᳓यो दधाति त᳓स्माद्यो᳓षायां प᳓यः॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Moreover, the fire-pan (ukhā, f.) is a female: he thus lays milk into the female, whence there is milk in the female.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He thus puts life into the head. Moreover, *ukhā* is a female, he thus pours milk into the female. That is why there is milk in a female.
</details>

</details>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>
