<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ज᳓नयो हैता᳓म᳓ग्रे᳓ ऽछिन्नपत्रा देवी᳓र्विश्व᳓देव्यावतीः पृथिव्याः᳓ सध᳓स्थे अङ्गिरस्व᳓त्पेचुस्ता᳓भिरेवै᳙नामेत᳓त्पचति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for

of old the divine women, with unclipped wings, dear to all the gods, did bake it, like Aṅgiras, in the lap of the earth;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

At that time, they the Jānis with unclipped wings, dear to all Gods were those who cooked him in the lap of earth, just as Aṅgiras (did).
</details>

</details>
