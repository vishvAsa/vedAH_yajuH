<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ता᳓ ह ता᳓ ओ᳓षधय एवौ᳓षधयो वै᳓ देवा᳓नाम् प᳓त्न्य ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for of old the divine wives of the gods, dear to all the gods, indeed, like Aṅgiras, placed that (fire-pan) into the lap of the earth, and by (the help of) them he now places it.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

But surely these are the plants; for, surely the wives of gods are the plants; for it is by the plants that everything here is sustained.
</details>
