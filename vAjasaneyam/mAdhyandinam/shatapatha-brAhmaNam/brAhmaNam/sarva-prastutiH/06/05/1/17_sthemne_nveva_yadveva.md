<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स्थेम्ने᳓ न्वे᳙व᳓ य᳓द्वेव᳓ ते᳓नैता᳓वती वा᳓ इय᳓म᳓ग्रे ऽसृज्यत ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That is for firmness. Why with these (powders) because at the beginning, the earth was created with these and therefore he makes it (the earthen fire-pans) with these same.
</details>

</details>

</details>
