<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स᳓ य᳓दि काम᳓येतोपा᳓धिरोहेत्पार्श्वतो᳓ वा व्रजेत्॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

If he choose, he himself may mount up beside him (Agni), or he may walk by the side (of the chariot).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

If he so wishes, he himself may climb on the cart beside it (the fire) or he can move by the side (of the cart).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

स य᳘दि काम᳘येतोपा᳘धिरोहेत्पार्श्वतो᳘ वा व्व्रजेत्॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

स य᳘दि काम᳘येतोपा᳘धिरोहेत्पार्श्वतो᳘ वा व्रजेत्॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

स यदि कामयेत- उपाधिरोहेत्, पार्श्वतो वा व्रजेत् ॥ ७ ॥
</details>
