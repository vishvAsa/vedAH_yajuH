<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स᳓र्वैर्वा᳓ एष᳓ व᳓योभिरमृ᳓तो ऽभवद्य᳓देनं द्यौ᳓र᳓जनयदि᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Because the Dyaus (sky) did give birth to him”—for, it is indeed the Dyaus that bore him; “that which has good seed” because she (Dyaus) is indeed, (possessed) of good seed, whose seed is this (Agni).
</details>

</details>

</details>
