<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

7. He fashions him here (in the pan or womb) into (a bird) with wings and tail;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He shapes him here (in the wombi.e. ukhā) as one with wings and tail;
</details>
