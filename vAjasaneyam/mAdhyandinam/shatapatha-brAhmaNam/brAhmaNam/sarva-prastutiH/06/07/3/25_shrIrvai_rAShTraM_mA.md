<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

श्री᳓र्वै᳓ राष्ट्रं᳓ मा᳓ त्व᳓च्छ्री᳓र᳓धिभ्रशदि᳓त्येत᳓त्॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--the people are food: thus, 'may all food long for thee!' --'may thy rule not fall away from thee!' --rule means glory: thus, 'may thy glory not fall away from thee!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus ‘May all food long for thee!’ — ‘May thy rule not fall away from thee!’ — rule means glory. Thus ‘May thy glory not fall away from thee!’
</details>
