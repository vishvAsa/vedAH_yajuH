<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓ एते᳓नात्म᳓ना पर्ज᳓न्यो रूप᳓मभवंस्त᳓थैवै᳙त᳓द्य᳓जमान एते᳓नात्म᳓ना पर्ज᳓न्यो रूप᳓म् भवति॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

By that body (of his [^egg_528]) they became like Parjanya, and in like manner does the Sacrificer by that body (of his) become like Parjanya.

[^egg_528]: 277:1 Viz. by the Agni who is now being held up, and of whom Parjanya is said to be another form, at VI, 1, 3, 15. It is probably the smoke rising from the fire-pan that suggests the idea of the Jupiter pluvius sending forth his flashes of light from the dark cloud.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They with this body (of Agni) assumed the form of cloud. In the like manner, this Yajamāna with this body (of ukhyāgni) takes the form of the cloud.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

त᳘ एते᳘नात्म᳘ना पर्ज᳘न्यो रूप᳘मभवंस्त᳘थैॗवैतद्य᳘जमान एते᳘नात्म᳘ना पर्ज᳘न्यो रूप᳘म् भवति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

</details>
