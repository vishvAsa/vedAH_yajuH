<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

एत᳓द्वै᳓ देवा᳓ अब्रुवंश्चेत᳓यध्वमि᳓ति चि᳓तिमिछ᳓ते᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--for the gods then said, 'Meditate ye (cetay),' whereby, doubtless, they meant to say, 'Seek ye a layer (citi)!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now the gods said—"(Let us) meditate", whereby, they said "We seek a layer (in the fire-altar)".
</details>

</details>
