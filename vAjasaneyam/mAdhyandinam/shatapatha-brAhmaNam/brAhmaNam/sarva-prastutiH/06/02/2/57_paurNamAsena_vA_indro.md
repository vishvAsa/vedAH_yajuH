<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

पौर्णमासे᳓न वा᳓ इ᳓न्द्रो वृत्र᳓म् पाप्मा᳓नं हत्वा᳓पहतपाप्मैत᳓त्क᳓र्मा᳓रभत ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

For Indra, having driven away Vr̥tra, evil, by means of the full-moon offering, thus freed from evil entered upon this sacrificial performance;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Because, Indra slain the evil Vṛtra by the full moon offering and having cleansed (himself of) all evil, started this sacrificial ritual (of Paśvālambha).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

पौर्णमासे᳘न वा ऽइ᳘न्द्रो व्वृत्रं᳘ पाप्मा᳘नᳫँ᳭ हत्वा᳘ ऽपहतपाप्मैतत्कर्मा᳘रभत
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

पौर्णमासे᳘न वा इ᳘न्द्रो वृत्र᳘म् पाप्मा᳘नᳫं हत्वा᳘पहतपाप्मैतत्कर्मा᳘रभत
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

पौर्णमासेन वा ऽइन्द्रो वृत्रं पाप्मानं हत्वा ऽपहतपाप्मैतत्कर्मारभत ।
</details>
