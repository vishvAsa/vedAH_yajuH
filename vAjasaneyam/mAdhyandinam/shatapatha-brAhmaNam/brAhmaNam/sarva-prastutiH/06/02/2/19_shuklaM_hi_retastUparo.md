<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

शुक्लं᳓ हि᳓ रे᳓तस्तूपरो᳓ भवति तूपरं᳓ हि᳓ रे᳓तो वाय᳓वे भवति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

It is a white one, because seed is white.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That (goat) is white because the semen is white. It is hornless because the semen is hornless. It is meant for Vāyu since Vāyu is prāṇa (in-breathing).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

शुक्लᳫँ᳭ हि रे᳘तस्तूपरो᳘ भवति तूपरᳫँ᳭ हि रे᳘तो व्वाय᳘वे भवति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

शुक्लᳫं हि रे᳘तस्तूपरो᳘ भवति तूपरᳫं हि रे᳘तो वाय᳘वे भवति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - विस्वरम्</summary>

शुक्लं हि रेतः । तूपरो भवति । तूपरं हि रेतः । वायवे भवति ।
</details>
