<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

शुक्लो᳓ हि᳓ वायु᳓स्तूपरो᳓ भवति तूपरो᳓ हि᳓ वायुः᳓॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

It is white, because Vāyu (the wind) is white; and it is hornless, because Vāyu is hornless.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He is white because Vāyu is white. He is hornless because Vāyu is hornless.
</details>
