<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

Animals are food: he thus lays down in front whatever food there is; and seeing that, Agni turns unto him.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Since they constitute the food, he lays down all of them and seeing that, Agni turns unto it. They are five, for there are those five Agnis corresponding to the five layers (of the altar). (Accordingly) he thus lays down five homes for them.
</details>
