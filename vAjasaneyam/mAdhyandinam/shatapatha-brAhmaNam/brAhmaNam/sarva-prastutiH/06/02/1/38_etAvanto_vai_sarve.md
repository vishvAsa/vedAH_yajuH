<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

एता᳓वन्तो वै᳓ स᳓र्वे पश᳓वो᳓ ऽन्नम् पश᳓वस्त᳓द्या᳓वद᳓न्नं त᳓देत᳓त्पुर᳓स्तान्नि᳓दधाति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for such are all the animals (used for sacrifice).
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They together cover all animals (used for sacrifice).
</details>
