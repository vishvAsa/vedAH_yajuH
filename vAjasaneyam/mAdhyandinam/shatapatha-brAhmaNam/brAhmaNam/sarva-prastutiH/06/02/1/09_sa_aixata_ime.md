<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

इमे᳓ वा᳓ अग्नि᳓रिमा᳓नेवा᳙त्मा᳓नमभिसं᳓स्करवै ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

5. He considered, 'They are Agni: I will fit them unto mine own self [^egg_329].

[^egg_329]: 162:1 Or, I will make them part of mine own self.--Similarly St. Petersburg dictionary, 'I will change them into myself.' But differently Professor Delbrück, Altind. Synt., p. 239, 'I will make myself to be these, change myself into these.' This is on account of the middle form of the verb, which, however, is quite justified also in the former interpretation. Cf. VI, 8, 2, 1, where there is no question of changing the whole sacrifice into a heap of ashes, but of taking over the ashes, or some of it, to form part of the sacrifice.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He saw that these (animals) are of course Agni and I will refine myself with these.
</details>

<details><summary>सायणः</summary>

**स ऐक्षते**ति । 'सः' प्रजापतिः 'ऐक्षत' स्वमतं विचारितवान् ।

तदेव दर्शयति- **इमे वा अग्निरि**ति । 'अग्निः' 'इमे वै' पुरुषादिपञ्चपशव एव, ततः पृथक् दृश्यन्त इत्यर्थः । 'इमानेव' अग्न्यात्मकानेतान् पशूनेव आत्मानमभिलक्ष्य 'संस्करवै' पशुभिः स्वरूपैः स्वमात्मानं संस्करिष्य इति पर्यालोचितवान् ।
</details>
