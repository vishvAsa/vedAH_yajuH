<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓द्य᳓दिष्ट्वा᳓ पशु᳓ना᳓पश्यत् ।  
त᳓स्मादि᳓ष्टकास्त᳓स्मादिष्ट्वै᳙व᳓ पशु᳓ने᳓ष्टकाः कुर्याद᳓निष्टका ह ता᳓ भवन्ति याः᳓ पुरा᳓ पशोः᳓ कुर्व᳓न्त्य᳓थो ह त᳓दन्य᳓देव᳓॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

10. And inasmuch as he saw them after offering (ishṭvā) the animal, therefore they are bricks (ishṭakā). Hence one must make the bricks only after performing an animal sacrifice; for those which are made before (or, without) an animal sacrifice are 'anishṭakā [^egg_336].' And, moreover, there is this other (consideration).

[^egg_336]: 164:2 A play on the word which may mean either 'non-bricks,' or 'being without oblation (ishṭa).'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Those (bricks) that are made before (without) the animal sacrifice are aniṣṭakas (non-bricks or non-favourables). So doing like that is incorrect (making bricks without doing animal sacrifice is taboo).
</details>
