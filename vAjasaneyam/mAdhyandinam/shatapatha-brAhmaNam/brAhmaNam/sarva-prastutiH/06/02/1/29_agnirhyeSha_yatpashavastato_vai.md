<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अग्नि᳓र्ह्ये᳙ष᳓ य᳓त्पश᳓वस्त᳓तो वै᳓ प्रजा᳓पतिरग्नि᳓रभवत्॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

for inasmuch as Agni (was) the same as cattle, therefore Prajāpati (the lord of creatures or generation) became Agni.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Since Agni itself is paśu, Prajāpati (the Lord of creatures) became Agni.
</details>
