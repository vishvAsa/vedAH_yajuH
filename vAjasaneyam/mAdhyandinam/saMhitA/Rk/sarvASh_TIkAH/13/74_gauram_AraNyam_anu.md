<details><summary>Griffith</summary>

I dedicate to thee the forest Gaura: building thy bodies up with him be settled.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

let thy burning pain reach him whom we hate!
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (मैत्रायणीयम्)</summary>

The wild ram I assign unto thee;  
with him piling [the altar], sit down in thy body!
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

I point out to you the wild ram; building with it, sit down in your body!
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - माध्यन्दिनम्</summary>

गौ॒रमा॑र॒ण्यमनु॑ ते दिशामि॒ तेन॑ चिन्वा॒नस्त॒न्वो᳙ निषी॑द।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

गौर᳓म् आरण्य᳓म् अ᳓नु ते दिशामि  
ते᳓न चिन्वान᳓स् तन्वं᳙ नि᳓षीद
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काठकम्</summary>

मेष᳓मारण्य᳓म᳓नु ते दिशामि ते᳓न चिन्वान᳓स्तन्वो᳙ नि᳓षीद
</details>

<details><summary>पदपाठः - दयानन्दादि</summary>

गौ॒रम्। आ॒र॒ण्यम्। अनु॑। ते॒। दि॒शा॒मि॒। तेन॑। चि॒न्वा॒नः। त॒न्वः᳖। नि। सी॒द॒।
</details>

ते तव शुग्गौरं गच्छतु॥ ४८॥
