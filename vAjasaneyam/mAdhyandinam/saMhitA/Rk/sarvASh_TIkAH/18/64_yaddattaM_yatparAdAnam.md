<details><summary>Griffith</summary>

Our gifts, our charitable grants, our pious works, our fees to priests, May the Omnific's Agni set all this among the Gods in heaven.
</details>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

49. 'What gift, what bounty, what fulfilment, what offering-presents there are of ours,--Agni Vaiśvakarmaṇa shall deposit them in heaven with the gods!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Whatever is given as gift, what riches he has distributed, what acts of charity he has done and what dakṣiṇās he has given — all these, O Agni Viśvakarman, you entrust them to the gods in the heaven" — meaning "what- ever we will be giving now and henceforth, those of ours let Agni Viśvakarman deposit in the heavenly world."
</details>
