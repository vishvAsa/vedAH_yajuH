<details><summary>Griffith</summary>

Here I cast out the charm of magic power buried for me by equal or unequal.
</details>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

11. 'Here do I cast out the charm which my equal, which my unequal has buried for me!'
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - माध्यन्दिनम्</summary>

इ॒दम॒हं तं ब॑ल॒गमुत्कि॑रामि॒ यं मे॑ समा॒नो यमस॑मानो निच॒खान॑
</details>

य᳘म्मे समानो यम᳘समानो निचखाने᳘ति
</details>

</details>
