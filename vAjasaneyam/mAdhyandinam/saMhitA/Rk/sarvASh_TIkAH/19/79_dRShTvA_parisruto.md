<details><summary>Griffith</summary>

Seeing the farming liquor's sap, Prajâpati with the bright drank nut the bright the milk, the Soma juice. By Law, etc.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Having seen the sap of the Parisrut, with the bright Prajāpati drank apart the bright: milk, the Soma;  
by order, the truth, the faculty, the drink drawn apart, the bright [essence] of the juice:  
the faculty of Indra,  
this milk, the immortal honey.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - माध्यन्दिनम्</summary>

दृष्ट्वा प॑रि॒स्रुतो॒ रसँ॑ शु॒क्रेण॑ शु॒क्रं व्य॑पिब॒त्  
पयः॒ सोमं॑ प्र॒जाप॑तिः।  
ऋ॒तेन॑ स॒त्यमि॑न्द्रि॒यं वि॒पानँ॑ शु॒क्रमन्ध॑स॒ऽइन्द्र॑स्येन्द्रि॒यमि॒दं पयो॒ऽमृतं॒ मधु॑ ॥७९ ॥
