</details>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

6. 'May the divine protectresses, dear to all the gods, heat thee, O fire-pan, Aṅgiras-like, in the lap of the earth!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"O Ukhā! May the divine Varūtris, dear to all the Gods, heat thee in the lap of the earth, just as Aṅgiras (did) "—the Goddesses Varūtris were those who at that time, baked him in the bosom of the earth, just as Aṅgiras (did).
</details>

<details><summary>महीधर-टीका</summary>

ऋगबृहती।

विश्वदेवयुता वरूत्रयो देव्योहोरात्राभिमानिन्यो देवताः पृथिव्या उपरि अङ्गिरस इव हे उखे, त्वां श्रपयन्तु पच्यमानतां संपादयन्तु।

‘अहोरात्राणि वै वरूत्रयोऽहोरात्रैर्हीदᳪं᳭ सर्वं वृतम्’ (६। ५। ४। ६) इति

श्रुतेर्वरूत्रिशब्देनाहोरात्राणि।
</details>
