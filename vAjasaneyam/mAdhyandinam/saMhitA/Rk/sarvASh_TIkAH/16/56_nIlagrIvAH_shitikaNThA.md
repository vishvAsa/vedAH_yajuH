<details><summary>Griffith</summary>

Rudras are dwelling in the sky, whose necks are blue, whose throats are white: Of these do we unbend the bows a thousand leagues away from us.
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The Rudras who abide in the sky,  
Of black necks and white throats--
{Their bows we unstring  
At a thousand leagues.}
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

The Rudras, blue-necked, white-throated, who have resorted to heaven,  
their bows we unstring a thousand leagues away.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

Those Rudras who are blue-necked, white-throated, having resorted to heaven;  
at a thousand leagues away we unstring their bows.
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>उवट-टीका</summary>

नील-ग्रीवाः। द्यु-स्थाना उच्यन्ते। नील-ग्रीवाः। कृष्ण-वचनो नील-शब्दः।  
शिति-कण्ठाः शिति-शब्दः श्वेत-वचनः।  
दिवं द्यु-लोकं रुद्रा [[उपश्रिताः|उपाश्रिताः]] अधिष्ठिताः अध्याश्रिताः ये तेषाम् इत्य् उक्तम्॥ ५६॥
</details>

ये रुद्रा दिवं द्यु-लोकम् उपश्रिताः स्वर्ग-स्थास् तेषाम् इति पूर्ववत्।  
कीदृशाः।

विष-ग्रासात् कियान् कण्ठ-भागः कृष्णः कियान् श्वेत इत्य् अर्थः॥ ५६॥
</details>
