<details><summary>Griffith</summary>

Sarvas haunt realms beneath the earth—their necks are blue, their throats are white: Of these, etc.
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The Śarvas of black necks, and white throats,  
Who wander below on the earth--
{Their bows we unstring  
At a thousand leagues.}
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

The Śarvas, blue-necked, white-throated, who move upon the earth below,  
their bows we unstring a thousand leagues away.
</details>

at a thousand leagues away we unstring their bows.

नील-ग्रीवाः शिति-कण्ठाः शर्वाः रुद्रा अधः क्षमा-चराः अधः पृथिव्यां संचरन्ति ये तेषाम् इति कृत-व्याख्यानम्॥ ५७॥
