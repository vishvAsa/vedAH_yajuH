<details><summary>Griffith</summary>

Rudra, with that auspicious form of thine which healeth every day, Auspicious, healer of disease, be kind to us that we may live.
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

That auspicious form of thine, O Rudra,  
Auspicious and ever healing,  
Auspicious and healing (form of) Rudra,  
With that show mercy on us for life.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

That body of yours, O Rudra, which is auspicious, auspicious, having all-healing medicines,  
auspicious, having medicines for affliction, with that make us live happily!
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

which is auspicious, auspicious and always healing,  
auspicious and healing of suffering—with that be gracious to us, that we may live.
</details>

अतिशयार्थं पुनर्-वचनम्।  
विश्वाहा भेषजी सर्वदा भिषक्त्वेन वर्तते।

अपशकुन-हन्त्रीत्य् अर्थः।  
तया तन्वा नः अस्मान् मृड सुखय।  
जीवसे जीवनाय॥ ४९॥
</details>
