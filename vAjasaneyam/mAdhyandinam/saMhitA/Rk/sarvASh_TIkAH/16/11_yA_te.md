<details><summary>Griffith</summary>

Thy weapon, O Most Bountiful, the bow that resteth in thy hand,— With that, deprived of power to harm, protect thou us on every side.
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

O most bountiful one, the missile  
That is in thy hand, thy bow,  
With it on all sides do thou guard us,  
Free from sickness.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

What missile is thine, O most bounteous one, [and] thy bow has become auspicious,  
with that [and with] thy free-from-illness [body], protect us on all sides!
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

The weapon of yours, O most bounteous one, which has become a bow in your hand,  
with that, free from sickness, protect us completely on every side.
</details>

<details><summary>उवट-टीका</summary>

</details>
