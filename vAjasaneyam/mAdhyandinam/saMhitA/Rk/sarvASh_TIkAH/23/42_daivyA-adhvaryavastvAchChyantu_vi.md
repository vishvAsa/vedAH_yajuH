<details><summary>Griffith</summary>

Let the divine Adhvaryus flay thy body and dissect thy frame, And let the sacrificing lines prepare thy members joint by joint,
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

May the divine Adhvaryus cut thee, And divide thee; May the piercers piercing Joint thy limbs.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Let the divine Adhvaryus cut you up and carve you;  
joint by joint let them make stitchings, stitching your limbs.
</details>

<details><summary>उवट-टीका</summary>

दैव्या अध्वर्यवः।  
ये च दैव्या दिव्या अध्वर्यवः अश्वि-प्रभृतयः ते च त्वा त्वाम् आच्छ्यन्तु वि च शासतु चकारो भिन्न-क्रमः।  
विशासतु च।  
किंच गात्राणि पर्वशः ते तव सिमाः मर्यादाः कृण्वन्तु कुर्वन्तु।  
शम्यन्तीः मर्यादा-दर्शनेन शमनं कुर्वाणाः॥ ४२॥
</details>
