<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Griffith</summary>

pelican, cormorant, fish, these belong to the Lord of Rivers;
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

the plava-bird, the cormorant, the fish: those belong to the lord of streams;
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

प्ल॒वो म॒द्गुर्मत्स्य॒स्ते न॑दीप॒तये॑
</details>

<details><summary>पदपाठः - दयानन्दादि</summary>

प्ल॒वः। म॒द्गुः। मत्स्यः॑। ते। न॒दी॒प॒तय॒ऽइति॑ नदीऽप॒तये॑।
</details>

</details>
