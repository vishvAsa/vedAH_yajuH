<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

उपयाम᳓-गृहीतो ऽस्य् अग्न᳓ये त्वा व᳓र्चस एष᳓ ते यो᳓निर् अग्न᳓ये त्वा व᳓र्चसे।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Griffith</summary>

Taken upon a base art thou. For Agni thee, for splendour This is thy home. For Agni thee, for splendour.
</details>

</details>
