<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

उपयाम᳓-गृहीतो ऽसी᳓न्द्राय त्वौ᳓जस एष᳓ ते यो᳓निर् इ᳓न्द्राय त्वौ᳓जसे।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Griffith</summary>

Taken upon a base art thou. For Indra thee, for mighty strength. This is thy home. For Indra thee, for might.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

</details>

</details>
