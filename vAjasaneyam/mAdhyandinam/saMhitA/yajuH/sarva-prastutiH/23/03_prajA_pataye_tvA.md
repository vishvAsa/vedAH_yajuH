<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

प्रजा᳓-पतये त्वा जु᳓ष्टं गृह्णामि
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Griffith</summary>

I take thee welcome to Prajâpati.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

For Prajāpati, agreeable [to him], I take thee!
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (मैत्रायणीयम्)</summary>

I take you dear to Prajāpati.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

प्र॒जाप॑तये त्वा॒ जुष्टं॑ गृह्णामि
</details>

<details><summary>पदपाठः - दयानन्दादि</summary>

प्र॒जाप॑तय॒ इति॑ प्र॒जाऽप॑तये। त्वा॒। जुष्ट॑म्। गृ॒ह्णा॒मि॒।
</details>

<details><summary>उवट-टीका</summary>

प्रजा-पतये त्वा जुष्टम् अभिरुचितं गृह्णामि।
</details>

<details><summary>महीधर-टीका</summary>

उपयाम० प्रजापतये जुष्टं रुचितं त्वां हे ग्रह, अहं गृह्णामि।
</details>
</details>
