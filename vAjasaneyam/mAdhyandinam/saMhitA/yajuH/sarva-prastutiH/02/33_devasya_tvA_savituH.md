<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

देव᳓स्य त्वा सवितुः᳓ प्रसवे᳙ ऽश्वि᳓नोर् बाहु᳓भ्यां पूष्णो᳓ ह᳓स्ताभ्याम्।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Griffith</summary>

By impulse of God Savitar I receive thee with arms of Asvins, with the hands of Pûshan.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

May there be joy to the giver,  
joy to me, the receiver!"  
He recovered half of his virile power.  
Half of his virile power he recovers who,
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (मैत्रायणीयम्)</summary>

'On the impulse of the divine Savitṛ, with the arms of the Aśvins, with the hands of Pūṣan, I dig Agni,
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

At the impulse of the divine Savitṛ, with the arms of the Aśvins, with the hands of Pūṣan, I offer you acceptable to Agni.

At the impulse of divine Savitṛ, with the arms of the Aśvins, with the hands of Pūṣan, I take you;  
you are a woman.

At the impulse of the god Savitṛ, with the arms of the Aśvins, with the hands of Pūṣan, I receive you.

At the impulsion of god Savitṛ, with the arms of the Aśvins, with the hands of Pūṣan, I take you.

At the impulsion of god Savitṛ, with the arms of the Aśvins, with the hands of Pūṣan,

At the impulse of divine Savitṛ, with the arms of the Aśvins, with the hands of Pūṣan,

At the impulse of the divine Savitṛ, with the arms of the Aśvins, with the hands of Pūṣan,
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

दे॒वस्य॑ त्वा सवि॒तुः प्र॑स॒वे᳙ऽश्विनो॑र्बा॒हुभ्यां॑ पू॒ष्णो हस्ता॑भ्याम्।
</details>

<details><summary>पदपाठः - दयानन्दादि</summary>

दे॒वस्य॑। त्वा॒। स॒वि॒तुः। प्र॒स॒व इति॑ प्रऽस॒वे। अ॒श्विनोः॑। बा॒हुभ्या॒मिति॑ बा॒हुऽभ्या॑म्। पू॒ष्णः। हस्ता॑भ्याम्।
</details>

<details><summary>महीधर-टीका</summary>

देवस्य त्वा।  
इतः-प्रभृति ‘ॐ प्रतिष्ठ’ (ख॰ १३) इत्य्-अन्तं ब्रह्मत्वम्।  
तस्याङ्गिरसो बृहस्पतिर् ऋषिः।
</details>
