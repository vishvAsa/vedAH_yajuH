<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Griffith</summary>

Speed, the Ashtâdasa. Ardour, the Navadasa. Triumphant Onset, Savimsa.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

the forward-hasting is the eighteenfold;  
heat is the nineteenfold;  
the attacking is the twentyfold;
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>पदपाठः - दयानन्दादि</summary>
