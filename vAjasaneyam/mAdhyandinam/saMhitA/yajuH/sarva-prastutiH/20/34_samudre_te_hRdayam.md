<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

समुद्रे᳓ ते हृ᳓दयम् अप्स्व् अ᳙न्तः᳓ सं᳓ त्वा विशन्त्व् ओ᳓षधीर् उता᳓पः।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Griffith</summary>

Thy heart is in the flood, etc. Repeated from VIII. 25.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

In the ocean is your heart, within the waters; may plants and waters enter you!
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

Your heart is in the ocean, within the waters; may the plants, sown with water, enter you!
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स॒मु॒द्रे ते॒ हृद॑यम॒प्स्व᳙न्तः सं त्वा॑ विश॒न्त्वोष॑धीरु॒तापः॑।
</details>

<details><summary>पदपाठः - दयानन्दादि</summary>

स॒मु॒द्रे। ते॒। हृद॑यम्। अ॒प्स्वित्य॒प्सु। अ॒न्तरित्य॒न्तः। सम्। त्वा॒। वि॒श॒न्तु॒। ओष॑धीः। उ॒त। आपः॑।
</details>

<details><summary>उवट-टीका</summary>

</details>
