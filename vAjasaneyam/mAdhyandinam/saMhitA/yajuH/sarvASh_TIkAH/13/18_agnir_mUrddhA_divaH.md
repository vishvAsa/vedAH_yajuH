<details><summary>Griffith</summary>

Agni is head and height of heaven, the Master of the earth is he:
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And then, (he prays), "This Agni (is) the head, the shoulder of the sky, the lord of the earth. He pleases (excites) the seeds of waters".
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - माध्यन्दिनम्</summary>

अ॒ग्निर्मू॒र्द्धा दि॒वः क॒कुत्पतिः॑ पृथि॒व्याऽ अ॒यम्।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

अ᳘थाग्नि᳘र्मूर्धा᳘ दिवः᳘ ककुत्प᳘तिः पृथिव्या᳘ अयम् ।
</details>

<details><summary>पदपाठः - दयानन्दादि</summary>

</details>
