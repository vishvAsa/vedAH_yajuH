<details><summary>Griffith</summary>

Gods who have Soma as their guide, high-seated, worshipful, Hail to them!
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“To those gods seated above, who have Soma as their eyes and who are rich in food—Svāhā” (so saying) at the middle portion (he offers).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - माध्यन्दिनम्</summary>

ये दे॒वाः सोम॑नेत्राऽउपरि॒सदो॒ दुव॑स्वन्त॒स्तेभ्यः॒ स्वाहा॑ ॥३६॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काण्व-ब्राह्मणम्</summary>

ये᳘ देवास्सो᳘मनेत्रा उपरिस᳘दो दुव᳘स्यन्तस्ते᳘भ्यस्स्वाहे᳘ति ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>पदपाठः - दयानन्दादि</summary>

ये। दे॒वाः। सोम॑नेत्रा॒ इति सोम॑ऽनेत्राः। उ॒प॒रि॒सद॒ इत्यु॑परि॒ऽसदः॑। दुव॑स्वन्तः। तेभ्यः॑। स्वाहा॑। ३६।
</details>
