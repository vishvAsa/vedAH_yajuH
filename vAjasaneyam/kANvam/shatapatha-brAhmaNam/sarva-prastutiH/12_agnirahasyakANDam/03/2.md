## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दाहुः किं छ᳘न्दः का᳘ देव᳘ताग्नेः शि᳘र इ᳘ति गायत्री छ᳘न्दोऽग्नि᳘र्देव᳘ता शि᳘र इति किं छ᳘न्दः का᳘ देवता ग्रीवा इ᳘त्युष्णिक्छ᳘न्दः सविता᳘ देव᳘ता ग्रीवाः ॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they ask, “Which metre and which deity constitute the head of the fire-altar”. The metre Gāyatrī and the deity Agni are the head. “Which metre and which deity form (his) neck”— the Uṣṇik metre and Savitā, the deity form the neck.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

किं छ᳘न्दः का᳘ देव᳘तानूकमि᳘ति बृहती छ᳘न्दो बृ᳘हस्प᳘तिर्देवता᳘नूकम् ॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Which metre and which deity are its spine?” The metre Bṛhatī and Bṛhaspati, the deity form the spine.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

किं छ᳘न्दः का᳘ देव᳘ता पक्षावि᳘ति बृहद्रथन्तरे छ᳘न्दो द्या᳘वापृथिवी᳘ देव᳘ते पक्षौ ॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Which metre and which deity are its two wings?” The Bṛhad and Rathantara are the metres and the heaven and earth are the deities of the two wings.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

किं छ᳘न्दः का᳘ देव᳘ता म᳘ध्यमि᳘ति त्रिष्टुप् छ᳘न्द इ᳘न्द्रो देव᳘ता म᳘ध्यम् ॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Which metre and which deity are its middle part?” The Triṣṭubh metre and Indra, the deity form the middle part.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

किं छ᳘न्दः का᳘ देव᳘ता श्रो᳘णी इ᳘ति ज᳘गती छ᳘न्द आदित्यो᳘ दे᳘वता श्रोणी ॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Which metre and which deity are its hips?" Jagatī metre and sun is the deity for the hips.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

किं छ᳘न्दः का᳘ देव᳘ता यस्मादिदं᳘ प्राणाद्रे᳘तः सिच्य᳘त इत्य᳘तिच्छ᳘न्दाश्छ᳘न्दः प्रजा᳘पतिर्देव᳘ता ॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Which metre and which deity are for its *prāṇa* from where the seed flows?" The metre is Atichandas and the deity, Prajāpati.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

किं छ᳘न्दः का᳘ देव᳘ता योऽयम᳘वाङ् प्राण इ᳘ति यज्ञायज्ञि᳘यं छ᳘न्दो वैश्वानरो᳘ देव᳘ता ॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Which metre and which deity for this downward breath?" The metre is Yajña Yajñīya and the deity is Vaiśvānara.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

किं छ᳘न्दः का᳘ देव᳘तोरू इ᳘त्यनुष्टुप्छ᳘न्दो वि᳘श्वेदेवा᳘ देव᳘तोरू ॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Which metre and which deity for the two thighs?" The metre Anuṣṭubh and the deities Viśvedevās are (for) the two thighs.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

किं छ᳘न्दः का᳘ देव᳘ताष्ठीव᳘न्तावि᳘ति पङ्क्तिश्छ᳘न्दो मरु᳘तो देव᳘ताष्ठीव᳘न्तौ ॥९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Which metre and which deity are his knees?" The metre Paṅkti and that deity, the Maruts are the knees.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

किं छ᳘न्दः का᳘ देव᳘ता प्रतिष्ठे इ᳘ति द्वि᳘पदा छ᳘न्दो वि᳘ष्णुर्देव᳘ता प्रतिष्ठे ॥१०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Which metre and which deity for his feet?" The metre Dvipadā and the deity Viṣṇu are the feet.
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

किं छ᳘न्दः का᳘ देव᳘ता प्राणा इ᳘ति वि᳘च्छन्दाश्छ᳘न्दो वायुर्देव᳘ता प्राणाः ॥११॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Which metre and which deity are the *prāṇas*?" The metre is Vicchandas and the deity Vāyu are the *prāṇas*.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

किं छ᳘न्दः का᳘ देव᳘तो ना᳘तिरिक्तानी᳘ति न्यूनाक्षरा छ᳘न्द आ᳘पो देव᳘तो नातिरिक्ता᳘नि सै᳘षात्मवि᳘द्यै᳘वैतन्म᳘यो है᳘वैता᳘ देवता᳘ एत᳘मात्मा᳘नमभिसं᳘भवति न हो᳘त्राण्या᳘लोक्य᳘ताया आशी᳘रस्ति ॥१२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Which metre and which deity constitute the deficient and exces- sive parts?" The metre with less number of syllables and that deity, the wa- ters are the deficient and excessive parts. This then is the knowledge of the body (of the altar) and the deity that enters into it is also such like. In this (building up of the altar) there is nothing beyond what is said, to enable one to attain divine world. (Second Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>
