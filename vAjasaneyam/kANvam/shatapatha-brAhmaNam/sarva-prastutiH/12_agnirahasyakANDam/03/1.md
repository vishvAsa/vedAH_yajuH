## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

प्राणो᳘ गायत्री च᳘क्षुरुष्णिग्वागनुष्टुप्म᳘नो बृहती श्रो᳘त्रं पङ्क्तिर्य ए᳘वायं᳘ प्रज᳘ननः प्राण᳘ एष त्रिष्टुब᳘थ᳘योऽयम᳘वाङ् प्रा᳘ण ए᳘ष ज᳘गती ता᳘नि वा᳘ एता᳘नि सप्त छ᳘न्दाꣳसि चतुरुत्तरा᳘ण्यग्नौ क्रियन्ते ॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Gāyatrī is the prāṇa (of Prajāpati, the altar); the Uṣṇik is the eye; the Anuṣṭubh the speech; the Bṛhatī is the mind and Paṅkti is the ear. The Triṣṭubh is what is here the generative prāṇa and the Jagatī is what consti- tutes the downward breath. These are the seven metres, each having four syllables more than the previous one, that are produced in the Agni-altar.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

प्राणो᳘ गायत्री᳘ति तद्य᳘ एव᳘ प्राण᳘स्य महिमा य᳘द्वी᳘र्यं त᳘देत᳘त्सह᳘स्रं प्राण᳘स्यैवैत᳘द्वी᳘र्यं यद्ध्यस्य चिन्व᳘तः प्राण᳘ उत्क्रा᳘मेत्त᳘त ए᳘वै᳘षोऽग्निर्न᳘ चीयेतैते᳘नैवास्य रूपे᳘ण सह᳘स्रमेष᳘ गायत्रीः स᳘ञ्चितो भवति ॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Gāyatrī is the prāṇa — that greatness and that vigour is in this thousand (bricks). This vigour indeed, belongs to prāṇa. If, while building up, his prāṇa is to pass away, this altar itself cannot be built up. By this its form that (altar) becomes built (so as to contain) a thousand Gāyatrīs (This accounts for one thousand chandasyā bricks).
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

च᳘क्षुरुष्णिगि᳘ति तद्य᳘ एव च᳘क्षुषो महिमा य᳘द्वी᳘र्यं त᳘देत᳘त्सह᳘स्रं च᳘क्षुष ए᳘वैत᳘द्वी᳘र्यं य᳘द्ध्यस्य चिन्वतश्च᳘क्षुरुत्क्रा᳘मेत्त᳘त ए᳘वै᳘षोऽग्निर्न᳘ चीयेतैते᳘नै᳘वास्य रूपे᳘ण सह᳘स्रमेष᳘ उष्णि᳘हः स᳘ञ्चितो भवति ॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Uṣṇik is the eye — thus that greatness and that vigour which is in the eye, that is in this thousand (bricks). This vigour indeed belongs to the eye. If, while building up, his eye-sight is to pass away, this altar itself cannot be built up. By this its form that (altar) becomes built (so as to contain) thousand Uṣṇiks (This accounts for one thousand chandasyā bricks).
</details>

## ०७

य᳘ ए᳘वायं᳘ प्रज᳘ननः प्राण᳘ एष᳘ त्रिष्टुबि᳘ति तद्य᳘ ए᳘वैत᳘स्य प्राण᳘स्य महिमा यद्वी᳘र्यं त᳘देतत्सह᳘स्रमेत᳘स्यै᳘वैत᳘त्प्राण᳘स्य वी᳘र्यं य᳘द्ध्यस्य चिन्वत᳘ एष᳘ प्राण᳘ आलु᳘भ्येत्त᳘त ए᳘वै᳘षोऽग्निर्न᳘ चीयेतैते᳘नै᳘वास्य रूपे᳘ण सह᳘स्रमेष᳘ त्रिष्टुभः स᳘ञ्चितो भवति ॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Triṣṭubh is the prāṇa which is generative — thus that greatness and that vigour which is in that prāṇa, that is in this thousand (bricks). This vigour indeed, belongs to the prāṇa. If, while building up, his prāṇa passes away, this altar itself cannot be built up. By this, its form, that (altar) be- comes built (so as to contain) thousand Triṣṭubhs (This accounts for one thousand chandasyā bricks).
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ᳘ योऽयम᳘वाङ् प्राण᳘ एष ज᳘गती᳘ति तद्य᳘ ए᳘वैत᳘स्य प्राण᳘स्य महिमा य᳘द्वी᳘र्यं त᳘देत᳘त्सहस्रमेतस्यै᳘वैत᳘त्प्राण᳘स्य वी᳘र्यं य᳘द्ध्यस्य चिन्वत᳘ एष᳘ प्राण᳘ आलु᳘भ्येत्त᳘त ए᳘वै᳘षोऽग्निर्न᳘ चीयेतैते᳘नै᳘वास्य रूपे᳘ण सह᳘स्रमेष᳘ जगतीः स᳘ञ्चितो भवति ता᳘नि वा᳘ एता᳘नि सप्त छ᳘न्दाꣳसि चतुरुत्तराण्य᳘न्योन्य᳘स्मिन्प्र᳘तिष्ठितानि स᳘प्तमे पु᳘रुषे प्राणा᳘ अ᳘न्योन्य᳘स्मिन्प्र᳘तिष्ठितास्तद्या᳘वन्तमेवंविच्छ᳘न्दसां गण᳘मन्वा᳘ह छ᳘न्दसश्छ᳘न्दसो है᳘वास्य सो᳘ऽनूक्तो भवति स्तुतो᳘ वा शस्तो वोपहितो वा ॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Jagatī is the downward breathing — thus that greatness and that vigour which is in the downward breathing is in this thousand (bricks). This vigour indeed, belongs to that breathing. If, while building up, his downward breathing fails, this (altar) itself cannot be built up. By this its form, that (altar) becomes built (so as to contain) thousand Jagatīs (This accounts for one thousand chandasyā bricks). These are the seven metres, each successive one, increasing by four syllables from the previous one and they are established into one another. One who, knowing thus, recites as many number of metres, by so much his (altar) becomes built, metre by metre, and all the metres he would have well-uttered, or stotras chanted or śastras recited or bricks laid down upon. (First Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>
