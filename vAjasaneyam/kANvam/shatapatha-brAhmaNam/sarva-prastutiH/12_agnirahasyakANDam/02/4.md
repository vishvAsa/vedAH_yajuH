## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

संवत्सरो वै᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिरग्नि᳘रु स᳘र्वे का᳘माः सोऽय᳘ꣳ᳘ संवत्सर᳘ꣳ᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिरकामयताग्निꣳ स᳘र्वान्कामानात्मा᳘नमभिस᳘ञ्चिन्वीये᳘ति स᳘ एकशत᳘धात्मा᳘नं᳘ व्यधत्त स᳘ एकशत᳘धात्मा᳘नं विधा᳘याग्निꣳ स᳘र्वान्का᳘मानात्मा᳘नमभिस᳘मचिनुत स स᳘र्वे का᳘मा अभवत्त᳘स्मान्न क᳘श्चन᳘ बहिर्धा का᳘मोऽभवत्त᳘स्मादाहुः संवत्सरः स᳘र्वे का᳘मा इ᳘ति न᳘ ह संवत्सरात्क᳘श्चन᳘ बहिर्धा का᳘मोऽस्ति त᳘थै᳘वैतद्य᳘जमान एकशत᳘धात्मा᳘नं विधा᳘याग्निꣳ स᳘र्वान् का᳘मानात्मा᳘नमभिस᳘ञ्चिनुते स स᳘र्वे का᳘मा भवति त᳘स्मान्न क᳘श्चन बहिर्धा का᳘मो भवति ॥ १॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Prajāpati is, indeed the year and Agni is all objects of desire. This year i.e. Prajāpati wished, “May I build up a body for myself containing Agni, i.e. all objects of desire.” He made a body of hundred and onefold. By this making a body of hundred and onefold, he collected within his body, Agni; i.e. all objects of desire. He himself became all objects of desire. Not a single object of desire was beyond him. That is why, they say the year is all objects of desire. There is not a single object of desire outside the year. In like manner, this Yajamāna, making himself hundred and onefold, collects Agni, i.e. all objects of desire within him and he himself becomes all objects of desire. Therefore not a single object of desire is beyond him.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स यः स᳘ संवत्स᳘रोऽसौ स᳘ आदित्यः᳘ स᳘ एष ए᳘कशतविधस्त᳘स्य रश्म᳘यः शतं᳘ विधा᳘ एष᳘ ए᳘वैकशततमो य᳘ एष त᳘पत्यस्मिन्त्स᳘र्वस्मिन् प्र᳘तिष्ठितस्तथै᳘वैतद्यजमान एक᳘शतधात्मा᳘नं विधा᳘यास्मिन्त्स᳘र्वस्मिन् प्र᳘तितिष्ठति ॥ २॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That which is the year is yonder sun. He is the hundred and onefold; his rays are hundredfold and he himself who burns yonder is the hundred and first and he is established in everything here (universe). Similarly this Yajamāna, making himself hundred and onefold, gets himself established in all these (the universe).
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ वा ए᳘कशतविधः सप्त᳘विधमभिसं᳘पद्यत एकशतधा वा᳘ असा᳘वादित्यो वि᳘हितः सप्त᳘सु देवलोके᳘षु प्र᳘तिष्ठितः सप्त वै देवलोकाश्च᳘तस्रो दि᳘शस्त्र᳘य इमे लोका᳘ एते वै᳘ सप्त᳘ देवलोकास्ते᳘ष्वेषु प्र᳘तिष्ठितस्त᳘थै᳘वैतद्य᳘जमान एकशत᳘धात्मा᳘नं विधा᳘य सप्त᳘सु देवलोके᳘षु प्र᳘तितिष्ठति ॥ ३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

In other words, the hundred and onefold becomes sevenfold. Yon- der sun made of hundred and one parts is established in the seven divine worlds. The seven divine worlds are the four quarters and three, these worlds — thus they are seven divine worlds. In these he is established. In like man- ner, this Yajamāna having made himself hundred and onefold, gets estab- lished in the seven divine worlds.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

य᳘द्वेवै᳘कशतविधः सप्त᳘विधमभिसंप᳘द्यत एकशतधा वा᳘ असा᳘वादित्यो᳘ विहितः सप्त᳘स्वृतु᳘षु सप्त᳘सु स्तो᳘मेषु सप्त᳘सु पृष्ठे᳘षु सप्त᳘सु छ᳘न्दस्सु सप्त᳘सु प्राणे᳘षु सप्त᳘सु दिक्षु प्रतिष्ठितस्त᳘थै᳘वैतद्य᳘जमान एकशत᳘धात्मा᳘नं विधा᳘यैतस्मिन्त्स᳘र्वस्मिन्प्र᳘तितिष्ठति ॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And again how he of hundred and onefold becomes sevenfold (is thus) – yonder sun made of hundred and one parts is established in the seven seasons; in seven stomās, in seven pṛṣṭhas, in seven metres, in seven vital airs and in seven quarters. In the same way, this Yajamāna having made himself hundred and onefold gets himself established in all these.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

य᳘द्वेवै᳘कशतविधः सप्त᳘विधमभिसंप᳘द्यत एकशतधा वा᳘ असा᳘वादित्यो वि᳘हितः सप्ता᳘क्षरे ब्र᳘ह्मन् प्र᳘तिष्ठितः सप्ता᳘क्षरं वै ब्र᳘ह्मार्गित्ये᳘कमक्ष᳘रं य᳘जुरि᳘ति द्वे सामे᳘ति द्वे अ᳘थ यद᳘तोऽन्यद्ब्र᳘ह्मैव त᳘द्द्व्यक्षरं वै ब्र᳘ह्म त᳘देतत्स᳘र्वꣳ सप्ता᳘क्षरं ब्र᳘ह्म त᳘स्मिन्नेष प्र᳘तिष्ठितस्तथै᳘वैतद्य᳘जमान एकशत᳘धात्मा᳘नं विधा᳘य सप्ता᳘क्षरे ब्र᳘ह्मन्प्र᳘तितिष्ठति त᳘स्मादु सप्त᳘भिः-सप्तभिः प᳘रिश्रयन्ति त᳘स्मादे᳘कशतविधः सप्तविधमभिसं᳘पद्यते᳘ अथ वै᳘ सप्त᳘विध ए᳘कशतविधमभिसं᳘पद्यते ॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And again how he of hundred and one parts becomes sevenfold – yonder sun is indeed, made of hundred and one parts and he is estab- lished in the seven syllabled Brahman (Veda). Ṛk is one syllable, Yaju is of two syllables, Sāma is of two syllables and what other Veda there is, it is just Brahman of two syllables. All these (together) form the seven-syllabled Veda and this one (sun) is established in it (Veda or Brahman). Similarly, this Yajamāna, after making himself hundred and onefold, gets established in the seven-syllabled Brahman. That is why, they lay down (bricks) all round in sevens each time. Thus the hundred and onefold becomes sevenfold and the sevenfold one becomes hundred and onefold.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

सप्त᳘विधो वा अ᳘ग्रे प्रजा᳘पतिरसृज्यत स᳘ एत᳘मेकशत᳘धात्मा᳘नं वि᳘हितमपश्यत्प्राणभृ᳘त्सु पञ्चाशदि᳘ष्टकाः पञ्चाशद्य᳘जूꣳषि त᳘च्छतꣳ सा᳘दनं च सू᳘ददोहाश्चै᳘कशत᳘तमे तत्समान᳘ꣳ᳘ सादयित्वा हि सू᳘ददोहसाधिव᳘दति स᳘ एतेनै᳘कशतविधेनात्म᳘नेमां जि᳘तिम᳘जयदिमां᳘ व्यष्टिं᳘ व्याश्नुत त᳘थै᳘वैतद्य᳘जमान एतेनै᳘कशतविधेनात्म᳘नेमां जि᳘तिं ज᳘यतीमां᳘ व्यष्टिं᳘ व्यश्नुत एव᳘मु सप्त᳘विध ए᳘कशतविधमभिसं᳘पद्यते स य᳘ एवै᳘कशतविधः स᳘ सप्तविधो यः᳘ सप्त᳘विधः स ए᳘कशतविध इ᳘ति नु विधा᳘नम् ॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Initially, Prajāpati was created sevenfold. He saw this body made hun- dred and onefold. Fifty bricks in the prāṇabhṛt (variety), fifty in the yajuṣmatī (type) – that makes hundred. The settling and sūdadohasa together consti- tute the hundred and first, because they (settling and sūdadohasa) are one and the same; because it is after settling, the sūdadohasa is recited. He with this hundred and onefold of his body gained that victory and attained this success. Similarly that Yajamāna with his body of hundred and one parts gains this victory and attains this success. This is how the sevenfold be- comes hundred and onefold. He who is hundred and onefold is the seven- fold and he who is sevenfold is hundred and onefold. So much to the forms (of the altar).
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अथा᳘तश्चयनस्यैवान्त᳘रोपस᳘दौ चिनोत्येतद्वै᳘ देवा᳘ अबिभयुर्यद्वै᳘ न इम᳘मिह र᳘क्षाꣳसि नाष्ट्रा न᳘ हन्युरि᳘ति त᳘ एताः पु᳘रोऽपश्यन्नुपस᳘द इमा᳘नेव᳘ लोका᳘निमे वै᳘ लोकाः᳘ पु᳘रस्ताः प्रा᳘पद्यन्त ताः᳘ प्रपद्या᳘भयेऽनाष्ट्र एत᳘मात्मा᳘नꣳ स᳘मस्कुर्वत त᳘थै᳘वैतद्य᳘जमान एताः पु᳘रः प्रपद्या᳘भयेऽनाष्ट्र᳘ एत᳘मात्मा᳘नꣳ स᳘ꣳ᳘स्कुरुते ॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now what is the cayana (building up) – he builds up between the (performance of) the two Upasads. This is because here the gods got scared lest here this should not be smitten by the terrible (Rākṣasas). They saw these Upasads (fort cities), namely these worlds. For these worlds are in- deed fort-cities. They entered them and after entering them, they fash- ioned that body at a place free from fear and danger. Similarly, this Yajamāna having entered these fort cities, completed this body at a place free from fear and danger.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

यद्वे᳘वान्त᳘रोपस᳘दौ चिनोत्येतद्वै᳘ देवा᳘ अबिभयुर्यद्वै᳘ न इम᳘मिह र᳘क्षाꣳसि नाष्ट्रा न᳘ हन्युरि᳘ति त᳘ एतान्व᳘ज्रानपश्यन्नुपस᳘दो व᳘ज्रो᳘ वा᳘ उपस᳘दस्तान्प्रा᳘पद्यन्त ता᳘न्प्रपद्या᳘भयेऽनाष्ट्र एत᳘मात्मा᳘नꣳ स᳘मस्कुर्वत त᳘थै᳘वैतद्य᳘जमान एतान्व᳘ज्रान्प्रपद्या᳘भयेऽनाष्ट्र᳘ एत᳘मात्मा᳘नꣳ स᳘ꣳ᳘स्कुरुते ॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And as to why he builds up between the two Upasads is this — when the gods got scared lest these terrible Rākṣasas may not smite it here, they saw these thunderbolts (in the form of) Upasads. The Upasads are indeed thunderbolts. They entered these and after entering them they fashioned this body at a place rid of fear and danger. So, also this Yajamāna having entered these thunderbolts, fashions this body at a place free from fear and danger.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

एत᳘दु ह यज्ञे त᳘पो य᳘दुपस᳘दस्त᳘पो वा᳘ उपस᳘दस्तद्यत्त᳘पसि चीय᳘ते त᳘स्मात्तापिश्चितस्तद्वै या᳘वदे᳘वोपस᳘द्भिश्च᳘रन्ति ता᳘वत्प्रव᳘र्ग्येण संवत्सर᳘मे᳘वोपस᳘द्भिश्चर᳘न्ति संवत्सरं᳘ प्रव᳘र्ग्येण ॥९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

These Upasads are the austerities (heat) in sacrifice. Upasad is in- deed tapas (involving fasting and austerity). Since it is built (ca) in tapas (austerity) it is called Tāpaścitās. As long as they perform the Upasads, so long they perform Pravargya. If it is for one year they observe Upasad, they perform Pravargya also for one year.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अहोरात्रा᳘णि वा᳘ उपस᳘द आदित्यः᳘ प्रव᳘र्ग्योऽमुं त᳘दादित्य᳘महोरात्रे᳘षु प्र᳘तिष्ठापयति त᳘स्मादे᳘षोऽहोरात्रे᳘षु प्र᳘तिष्ठितः ॥१०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Upasads are indeed days and nights and the sun is Pravargya. He thus establishes that sun in the days and nights. That is why, he is estab- lished in days and nights.
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘दि च᳘तुर्विꣳशतिश्च᳘तुर्विꣳशतिर्वा᳘ अर्धमासा अर्धमासा᳘ उपस᳘द आदित्यः᳘ प्रव᳘र्ग्योऽमुं त᳘दादित्य᳘मर्धमासे᳘षु प्र᳘तिष्ठापयति त᳘स्मादे᳘षोऽर्धमासे᳘षु प्रतिष्ठितः ॥११॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And if (the Upasads are for twenty-four days), it is because there are twenty-four half months and the Upasads are half months and the sun is Pravargya. He establishes that sun in the half months. Thus he becomes established in half months.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘दि द्वा᳘दश द्वा᳘दश वै मा᳘सा मा᳘सा उपस᳘द आदित्यः᳘ प्रव᳘र्ग्योऽमुं त᳘दादित्यं मा᳘सेषु प्र᳘तिष्ठापयति त᳘स्मादेष मा᳘सेषु प्र᳘तिष्ठितः ॥१२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And if (the Upasads are) for twelve days, it is because there are twelve months and Upasads are the months and the sun is the Pravargya. He establishes that sun in the months. Thus he gets established in the months.
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘दि षट् षड्वा᳘ ऋत᳘व ऋतव उपस᳘द आदित्यः᳘ प्रव᳘र्ग्योऽमुं त᳘दादित्यमृतु᳘षु प्र᳘तिष्ठापयति त᳘स्मादेव᳘ ऋत᳘षु प्र᳘तिष्ठितः ॥१३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And if (the Upasads are) for six days, it is because six are the seasons. And Upasads are seasons and the sun is the Pravargya. He establishes that sun in the seasons. Thus he gets established in the seasons.
</details>


## १४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘दि तिस्रस्त्र᳘यो वा᳘ इमे᳘ लोका᳘ इमे᳘ लोका᳘ उपस᳘द आदित्यः᳘ प्रव᳘र्ग्योऽमुं त᳘दादित्य᳘मेषु᳘ लोके᳘षु प्र᳘तिष्ठापयति त᳘स्मादेष᳘ एषु᳘ लोके᳘षु प्र᳘तिष्ठितः ॥१४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And if (the Upasads are) for three days, it is because three are these worlds and these worlds are Upasads. The sun is the Pravargya. He estab- lishes that sun in these worlds. Thus he gets established in these worlds.
</details>


## १५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अथा᳘तश्चितिपुरीषा᳘णामेव᳘ मीमा᳘ꣳसा मासं प्रथमा चि᳘तिर्मा᳘सं पु᳘रीषमेता᳘वान्वा᳘सन्तिक᳘ ऋतौ कामस्तद्या᳘वान्वा᳘सन्तिक ऋतौ कामस्तं तत्स᳘र्वमात्मा᳘नमभिस᳘ञ्चिनुते ॥१५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now the inquiry about the layers of loose soil – one month for (the building up) of the first layer (of bricks) and one month for the layer of loose soil. That much of desire in the spring season. He thus builds up his body with that much of desire which is in the spring season.
</details>


## १६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

मा᳘सं द्विती᳘या मासं पु᳘रीषमेता᳘वान्ग्रै᳘ष्म ऋतौ का᳘मस्तद्या᳘वान्ग्रैष्म ऋतौ का᳘मस्तं तत्स᳘र्वमात्मा᳘नमभिस᳘ञ्चिनुते ॥१६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

One month for (the building up) of the second layer (of bricks) and one month for the layer of loose soil. That much of desire in the sum- mer season. He thus builds up his body with that much of desire which is in the summer season.
</details>


## १७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

मा᳘सं तृती᳘या मा᳘सं पु᳘रीषमेता᳘वान्वा᳘र्षिक ऋतौ का᳘मस्तद्या᳘वान्वा᳘र्षिक ऋतौ का᳘मस्तं तत्स᳘र्वमात्मा᳘नमभिस᳘ञ्चिनुते ॥१७॥

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

</details>


## १८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

मा᳘सं चतुर्थी मा᳘सं पु᳘रीषमेता᳘वाञ्छारद᳘ ऋतौ का᳘मस्तद्या᳘वाञ्छारद᳘ ऋतौ का᳘मस्तं तत्स᳘र्वमात्मा᳘नमभिस᳘ञ्चिनुते ॥१८॥

</details>


## १९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ पञ्चम्यै चि᳘तेरसपत्ना᳘ विरा᳘जश्च प्रथमाहमु᳘पदधाति स्तो᳘मभागा ए᳘कैकामन्वहं ताः᳘ सकृ᳘त्साद᳘यति सकृत्सू᳘ददोहसा᳘धिवदति तूष्णीं मा᳘सꣳ स्तोमभागाः पुरीष᳘मभिहरन्त्येता᳘वान्है᳘मन्तिक ऋतौ᳘ का᳘मस्तद्या᳘वान् है᳘मन्तिक ऋतौ का᳘मस्तं तत्स᳘र्वमात्मा᳘नमभिस᳘ञ्चिनुते ॥१९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

About the fifth layer (of bricks) he lays down the aspatnā brick and the virāj brick with first day and each day one stomabhāga brick. He settles them once and once recites the sūdadohasa. For a month they silently apply loose soil for the stomabhāga. This much is the desire in the winter season. He thus builds up his body with that much of desire which is in the winter season.
</details>


## २०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

मा᳘सꣳ षष्ठी मा᳘सं पु᳘रीषमेता᳘वाञ्छैशि᳘र ऋतौ का᳘मस्तद्यावाञ्छैशिर᳘ ऋतौ का᳘मस्तं तत्स᳘र्वमात्मा᳘नमभिस᳘ञ्चिनुत एता᳘वान्वै᳘ द्वादश᳘सु मा᳘सेषु का᳘मष᳘ट्स्वृतु᳘षु तद्या᳘वान्द्वादश᳘सु मासेषु का᳘मः ष᳘ट्स्वृतु᳘षु तं तत्स᳘र्वमात्मा᳘नमभिस᳘ञ्चिनुते ॥२०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The sixth layer (takes) one month (for layer of bricks) and one month for layer of loose soil. That much of desire in the dewy season. He thus builds up his body with that much of desire which is in the dewy season. This much is the desire (plan to build) in the course of twelve months or in six seasons. With that much of desire in the twelve months or in six seasons with all that, he builds up the body.
</details>


## २१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ त्रीण्यहान्युपा᳘तियन्ति यद᳘हः शतरुद्रि᳘यं जुहो᳘ति यद᳘हरुपव᳘सथो यद᳘हः प्र᳘सुतस्तद्यत्तेष्व᳘हः सूपस᳘दा च᳘रन्ति ता᳘नि त᳘स्य मा᳘सस्याहोरात्राण्य᳘थ य᳘त्प्र᳘वर्ग्येण त᳘दु त᳘स्मिन्नृता᳘वादित्यं प्र᳘तिष्ठापयत्येता᳘वान्वै᳘ त्रयोदश᳘सु मा᳘सेषु का᳘मः सप्त᳘स्वृतु᳘षु तद्या᳘वाꣳस्त्रयोदश᳘सु मा᳘सेषु का᳘मः सप्त᳘स्वृतु᳘षु तं तत्स᳘र्वमात्मा᳘नमभिस᳘ञ्चिनुते ॥२१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He exceeds (the year) by three days; they are the ones on which he makes the Śatarudrīya offering; the one when he makes preparation and the one on which the Soma is pressed. When he performs Upasads on those (three) days, they become the days and nights of that (thirteenth interca- lary) month and when he performs Pravargya (on those days) he estab- lishes yonder sun also in that (seventh) season. This much is the desire in the thirteen months and in the seven seasons. With that much of desire in the thirteen months and seven seasons – with all that he builds up his body:
</details>


## २२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ संवत्सरं प्र᳘सुतः स्यात्स᳘र्वं वै᳘ संवत्सरः स᳘र्वमे᳘कशतविधः स᳘र्वेणैव तत्स᳘र्वमाप्नोति य᳘दि संवत्सर न᳘ शक्नुया᳘द्विश्वजि᳘ता स᳘र्वपृष्ठेनातिरात्रे᳘ण यजेत त᳘स्मिन्त्सर्ववेदसं᳘ दद्यात्स᳘र्वं वै विश्वजित्सर्वं वै विश्वजित्स᳘र्वपृष्ठोऽतिरात्रः स᳘र्वꣳ स᳘र्ववे᳘दस᳘ꣳ स᳘र्वमे᳘कशतविधः स᳘र्वेणैव तत्स᳘र्वमाप्नोति ॥२२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Soma should be pressed for a year. The year is all, and one hundred and onefold is all; by means of all he thus gains everything. If he is unable to press Soma for a year, he should perform the Viśvajit Atirātra with all the pṛṣṭhas (Sarvapṛṣṭha Viśvajit Atirātra). In that he should give away all his property (as dakṣiṇā) because Viśvajit is “all” (i.e. conquering the whole universe) and Atirātra with all the pṛṣṭhas means everything. “All property” means everything and the hundred and onefold (altar) is everything. By means of all he gains everything. (Fourth Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>
