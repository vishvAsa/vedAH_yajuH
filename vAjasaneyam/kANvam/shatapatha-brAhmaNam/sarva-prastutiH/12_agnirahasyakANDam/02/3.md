<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now, there are here eleven steps including the two hand's length distance between the border of the vedi and the Gārhapatya. The Triṣṭubh meter has eleven syllables and Triṣṭubh is a thunderbolt. Triṣṭubh is strength. Thus by the thunderbolt and strength, the Yajamāna, at the out- set of the sacrifice, drives away the terrible Rākṣasas. This is the womb of the vedi, because it is from that womb, the gods produced the vedi.
</details>
