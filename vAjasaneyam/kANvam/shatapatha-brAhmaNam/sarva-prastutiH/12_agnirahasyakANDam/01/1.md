<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That part of it which is mortal, it is below and passes below the navel and having got split into two parts, enters this earth as urine and faeces. Now that which enters this earth, comes to the fire-altar built here (in the form of bricks) and that which enters the air, goes to that fire which is placed on the built-altar. This is also (a process of) entering therein. (First Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>
