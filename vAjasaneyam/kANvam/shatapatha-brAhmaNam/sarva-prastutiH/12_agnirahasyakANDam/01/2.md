<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

By the three eighties, the eighties of the Mahad Uktha are obtained; for the Mahad Uktha is counted by triplets of eighty. Out of the balance of forty-five, twenty- five represent this twenty-fivefold body and when the body is there, it in- cludes the wings and the tail. The balance twenty are the insertions (āvapana).
