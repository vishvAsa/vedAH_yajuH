<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (1/25 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Meditate upon Brahman (the cause of creation, sustenance and de- struction of this universe) as truth (eternal). Man is indeed the product of his will. How great his will with which he departs from this world, with that much will, he on passing away, appears in yonder world.
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Meditate upon the Ātman (self) which is made up of intelligence, endowed with aerial body, with an effulgent form, with an etheriel nature, with its shape changing at its will, swift as thought, with an unfailing resolve, firm in its purpose, hav- ing all odours, all tastes with its authority over all regions, pervading this whole universe, speechless and unattached. Even as a grain of rice, a grain of barley or a grain of millet or the smallest granule of millet, so is this golden Puruṣa in the heart; like an effulgence without smoke.
</details>
