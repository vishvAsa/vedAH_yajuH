Śātyāyani is of the view that Agni-altar is the year itself; the spring is his head, the summer is his right wing, the rainy season, his left wing; the autumn season is his middle body (trunk) and the dewy season and winter are his tail that supports. Agni (fire) is his speech, Vāyu is his prāṇa, Āditya is his eye; moon is his mind, the quarters are his ear, waters are his mate, tapas is his foundation; the months are his joints, the half-months are the veins; the days and nights are his golden and silver feathers.
