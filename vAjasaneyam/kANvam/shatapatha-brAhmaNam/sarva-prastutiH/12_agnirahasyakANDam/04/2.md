## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

संवत्सरो वै᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिरग्निः सो᳘मो रा᳘जा चन्द्र᳘माः स᳘ह स्वय᳘मे᳘वात्मा᳘नं प्रो᳘चे यज्ञ᳘वचसे राजस्तंबायना᳘य यावन्ति वाव᳘ मे ज्यो᳘तीꣳषि ता᳘वत्यो म इ᳘ष्टका इ᳘ति त᳘स्य वा᳘ एत᳘स्य संवत्सर᳘स्य प्रजा᳘पतेः सप्त च᳘ शता᳘नि विꣳशति᳘श्चाहोरात्रा᳘णि ज्यो᳘तीꣳषि ता इ᳘ष्टकाः षष्टि᳘श्च त्री᳘णि च शता᳘नि परि᳘श्रितः षष्टि᳘श्च त्री᳘णि च शता᳘नि य᳘जुष्मत्यः सोऽयꣳ संवत्सरः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिः स᳘र्वाणि भूता᳘नि ससृजे यच्च प्राणि यच्चाप्राणमुभयान्देवमनुष्यान्त्स सर्वाणि भूतानि सृष्ट्वा रिरिचान᳘ इव मेने स᳘ मृत्योर्बिभया᳘ञ्चकार ॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Prajāpati, the year is indeed Agni-altar and king Soma is the moon and he explained about himself to Yajñavacas Rājastambāyana thus, "As many units of effulgence I have, so many bricks I have." For, this Prajāpati, the year, has seven hundred and twenty days, (correspondingly) he has seven hundred and twenty bricks. There are three hundred and sixty pariśrit bricks and three hundred and sixty yajuṣmatī bricks. This Prajāpati, the year, produced all this creation; those that breathe and those that do not breathe –both gods and men. After producing all this creation, he felt as if emptied out and was afraid of death.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ हेक्षां᳘ चक्रे क᳘थं न्वह᳘मिमा᳘नि स᳘र्वाणि भूता᳘नि पु᳘नरात्म᳘न्नवपेय पु᳘नरात्मन् द᳘धीय कथं᳘ न्वह᳘मे᳘वैषाꣳ स᳘र्वेषां भूता᳘नां पु᳘नरा᳘त्मा स्यामि᳘ति ॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He contemplated, "How do I absorb all this creation into myself; how to put them back into me and how do I again become the body of all this creation?"
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ द्वे᳘धात्मा᳘नं᳘ व्यौ᳘हत्षष्टि᳘श्च त्री᳘णि च शता᳘न्यन्यतरस्ये᳘ष्टका अभव᳘न्नेवमन्यतर᳘स्य स न᳘ व्याप्नोत् ॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He split his body into two halves, with three hundred bricks in one half and the same (number) in the other. But he did not succeed in achiev- ing his objective.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त्री᳘नात्म᳘नोऽकुरुत तिस्र᳘स्ति᳘स्रोऽशीत᳘य ए᳘कैकस्ये᳘ष्टका अभवन्त्स᳘ नैव᳘ व्याप्नो᳘च्चतु᳘र आत्म᳘नोऽकुरुताशीति᳘शतेष्टकान्त्स᳘ नैव व्याप्नोत् ॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He split himself into three, each of the three with three sets of eighty bricks (i.e. two hundred and forty in each of the three parts to make seven hundred and twenty); but did not succeed. He made himself into four parts with one hundred and eighty bricks in each part; again he did not succeed.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

प᳘ञ्चात्म᳘नोऽकुरुत चतुश्च᳘त्वारि᳘ꣳ᳘शꣳ शतमे᳘कैकस्ये᳘ष्टका अभवन्त्स᳘ नैव᳘ व्याप्नोत् ॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He split himself into five parts with one hundred and forty-four bricks in each, but still did not succeed.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ष᳘ळात्म᳘नोऽकुरुत विꣳशति᳘शतेष्टकान्त्स᳘ नैव᳘ व्याप्नोन्न᳘ सप्तधा᳘ व्यभवत् ॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He made himself into six parts and each of them had one hundred and twenty bricks each; and he did not succeed. Nevertheless he did not split himself into seven.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अष्टा᳘वात्म᳘नोऽकुरुत नव᳘तीष्टकान्त्स᳘ नैव᳘ व्याप्नोत् ॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He made himself into eight parts and each with ninety bricks; but did not succeed.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

न᳘वात्म᳘नोऽकुरुताशीती᳘ष्टकान्त्स᳘ नैव᳘ व्याप्नोत् ॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He split into nine and had eighty bricks for each part; but still he did not succeed.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

दशात्म᳘नोऽकुरुत द्वा᳘सप्ततीष्टकान्तस᳘ नैव᳘ व्याप्नो᳘न्नैकादशधा᳘ व्यभवत् ॥९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He made himself into ten parts with seventy-two bricks in each, but did not succeed. He did not split into eleven parts.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

द्वा᳘दशात्म᳘नोऽकुरुत ष᳘ष्टीष्टकान्तस᳘ नैव᳘ व्याप्नो᳘न्न᳘ त्रयोदशधा᳘ व्यभवन्न᳘ चतुर्दशधा ॥१०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He split into twelve parts with sixty bricks in each; but did not succeed. He did not try thirteen fold and fourteen fold.
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

प᳘ञ्चदशात्म᳘नोऽकुरुताष्टाचत्वारिꣳ श᳘दिष्टकान्तस᳘ नैव᳘ व्याप्नोत् ॥११॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He made himself into fifteen parts with forty-eight bricks in each; but did not succeed.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

षोळशात्म᳘नोऽकुरुत पञ्चचत्वारिꣳ श᳘दिष्टकान्तस᳘ नैव᳘ व्याप्नोन्न सप्तदशधा᳘ व्यभवत् ॥१२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He split into sixteen parts with forty-five bricks in each; but did not succeed. He did not make himself into seventeen fold.
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अष्टा᳘दशात्म᳘नोऽकुरुत चत्वारिꣳश᳘दिष्टकान्तस᳘ नैव᳘ व्याप्नो᳘न्नैकात्रविꣳशतिधा᳘ व्यभवत् ॥१३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He split into eighteen parts with forty bricks in each; but did not succeed. He did not make himself nineteen fold.
</details>


## १४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

विꣳशति᳘मात्म᳘नोऽकुरुत ष᳘ळ्त्रिꣳश᳘दिष्टकान्तस᳘ नैव᳘ व्याप्नो᳘न्नैकविꣳशतिधा᳘ व्यभवन्न᳘ द्वाविꣳशतिधा᳘ न त्रयोविꣳशतिधा ॥१४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He split into twenty parts with thirty-six bricks in each; but did not succeed. He did not try twenty-one fold, nor twenty-two fold nor twenty-three fold.
</details>


## १५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

च᳘तुर्विꣳशति᳘मात्म᳘नोऽकुरुत त्रिꣳश᳘दिष्टकान्त्सो᳘ऽत्रातिष्ठत पञ्चदशे᳘ व्यूहे तद्यत्पञ्चदशे᳘ व्यूहे᳘ऽतिष्ठत त᳘स्मात्प᳘ञ्चदशापूर्य᳘माणस्य रूपा᳘णि प᳘ञ्चदशाप᳘क्षीयमाणस्य ॥१५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He split himself into twenty-four divisions with thirty bricks in each. There he stopped at the fifteenth splitting. Because he stopped splitting at that fifteenth time, there are fifteen forms (units) of the waxing and fifteen forms (units) of the waning (moon).
</details>


## १६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ यच्च᳘तुर्वि᳘ꣳ᳘शतिमात्म᳘नोऽकुरुत त᳘स्माच्च᳘तुर्विꣳशत्यर्धमासः᳘ संवत्सरः स᳘ एतैश्च᳘तुर्वि᳘ꣳश᳘त्या त्रिꣳशदिष्टकैरात्म᳘भिर्न व्यभवत्स प᳘ञ्चदशा᳘न्हो रूपा᳘ण्यपश्यदात्म᳘नस्त᳘न्वो मुहूर्ताँल्लोकम्पृणाः प᳘ञ्चदशैव रा᳘त्रेस्तद्य᳘न्मुहुस्रा᳘यन्ते त᳘स्मान्मुहूर्ता अ᳘थ य᳘त्क्षुद्राः स᳘न्त इमाँल्लोका᳘नापूरय᳘न्ति त᳘स्माल्लोकम्पृणाः᳘ ॥१६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now, because he split himself into twenty-four parts, the year has twenty-four half months. With these twenty-four parts each with thirty bricks for their bodies, he had not developed (his full stature). He saw the fifteen parts of the day, as his own bodies, namely, the muhūrtas (which are) lokampr̥ṇā bricks. Similarly, fifteen (muhūrtas) of the night. Because they readily (or from time to time muhūrtas come to) save (found handy to fill up or trāyante), they are muhūrtas. Though they are small but (still) fill up these worlds (space) they are lokampr̥ṇās.
</details>


## १७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

एष वा᳘ इदꣳ स᳘र्वं पचत्यहोरात्रै᳘रर्धमासैर्मा᳘सैरृतु᳘भिः संवत्सरे᳘ण त᳘दमु᳘ना पक्व᳘मयं᳘ पचति पक्व᳘स्य पक्तेति ह᳘ स्माह भारद्वाजोऽग्नि᳘ममु᳘ना हि᳘` पक्व᳘मयं पचती᳘ति ॥१७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That yonder one (sun) bakes everything here, by means of days and nights, by half-months and months, by seasons and by the year. This Agni (fire) is (again) baking what is baked by that one (sun). That is why, Bhāradvāja called him (Agni) as the baker of the baked; because he bakes what is already baked by him (sun).
</details>


## १८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ता᳘नि संवत्सरे द᳘श च सह᳘स्राण्यष्टौ᳘ च शता᳘नि स᳘मपद्यन्त सो᳘ऽत्रातिष्ठत दश᳘सु च सह᳘स्रेष्वष्टासु᳘ च शतेषु ॥१८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

These (muhūrtas) total to ten thousand and eight hundred. He (Prajāpati) stopped at this (point of) ten thousand eight hundred.
</details>


## १९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ स᳘र्वाणि भूता᳘नि प᳘र्यैक्षत्स᳘ त्रय्या᳘मेव विद्या᳘याꣳ स᳘र्वाणि भूता᳘न्यपश्य᳘द᳘त्र हि स᳘र्वेषां छ᳘न्दसामात्मा स᳘र्वेषाꣳ स्तो᳘मानाꣳ स᳘र्वेषां प्राणा᳘नाꣳ स᳘र्वेषां देवा᳘नामेतद्वा᳘ अ᳘स्त्येत᳘द्ध्यमृ᳘तं य᳘द्ध्यमृ᳘तं तद्ध्य᳘स्त्येत᳘दु तद्यन्म᳘र्त्यम् ॥१९॥
</details>


## २०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ ऐक्षत प्रजा᳘पतिस्त्रय्यां वाव᳘ विद्या᳘याꣳ स᳘र्वाणि भूता᳘नि ह᳘न्त त्रयी᳘मेव᳘ विद्या᳘मात्मा᳘नमभिस᳘ꣳ᳘स्करवा इ᳘ति ॥२०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He realised, "Indeed all the existing things are in the *Trayī* (triple Vedic lore) and so I will fashion for myself a body so that it includes the entire threefold lore."
</details>


## २१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ ऋचो᳘ व्यौहद्द्वा᳘दश बृहतीसहस्रा᳘ण्येता᳘वत्यो ह᳘र्चो याः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिसृष्टास्ता᳘स्त्रिꣳशत्तमे᳘ व्यूहे᳘ पङ्क्ति᳘ष्वतिष्ठन्त ता य᳘त्त्रिꣳशत्तमे व्यूहे᳘ऽतिष्ठन्त त᳘स्मात्त्रिꣳशन्मा᳘सस्य रा᳘त्रयो᳘ऽथ य᳘त्पङ्क्ति᳘षु त᳘स्मात्पा᳘ङ्क्तः प्रजा᳘पतिस्ता अष्टाशत᳘ꣳ शता᳘नि पङ्क्त᳘योऽभवन् ॥२१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He arranged the *Ṛks* into twelve thousand Bṛhatīs; this much are the *Ṛks* created by Prajāpati. They in the thirtieth arrangement ended up in Paṅkti metres. Since they ended up in the thirtieth alignment, there are thirty nights for a month. Since they (ended up) in Paṅktis, he (Prajāpati) is Pāṅkta (fivefold). Thus ten thousand and eight hundred Paṅktis came into being.
</details>


## २२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अथे᳘तरौ वे᳘दौ व्यौहद्द्वा᳘दशैव᳘ बृहतीसहस्रा᳘ण्यष्टौ य᳘जुषां चत्वा᳘रि सा᳘म्नामेता᳘वद्धैत᳘योर्वे᳘दयोर्य᳘त्प्रजा᳘पतिसृष्टं तौ᳘ त्रिꣳशत्तमे᳘ व्यूहे᳘ पङ्क्ति᳘ष्वतिष्ठे᳘तां तौ य᳘त्त्रिꣳशत्तमे᳘ व्यूहे᳘ऽतिष्ठेतां त᳘स्मात्त्रिꣳशन्मा᳘सस्य रा᳘त्रयो᳘ऽथ यत्पङ्क्ति᳘षु त᳘स्मा᳘त्पा᳘ङ्क्तः प्रजा᳘पतिस्ता अष्टाशतमेव शता᳘नि पङ्क्तयो᳘ऽभवन् ॥२२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he arranged the other two Vedas into twelve thousand Bṛhatīs — eight thousand of the *Yajus* and four thousand of the *Sāman*. Because that much was what was created by Prajāpati in these two Vedas. At the thirtieth aligriment it ended up in Paṅkti metre. Because they ended up at the thirtieth arrangement, there are thirty nights for a month; and because in Paṅktis (they ended up) he Prajāpati is Pāṅkta (fivefold). Thus ten thou- sand and eight hundred Paṅktis came into being.
</details>


## २३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते स᳘र्वे त्र᳘यो वेदा द᳘श च सह᳘स्राण्यष्टौ᳘ च शता᳘न्यशीतीना᳘मभवन्त्स᳘ मुहूर्ते᳘न मुहूर्तेनैवाशीतिमाप्नोत् मुहूर्तेन मुहूर्तेनाशीतिः स᳘मपद्यत ॥२३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

All the three Vedas totalled to ten thousand eight hundred eighties of syllables (i.e. 10,800 x 80 = 8,64,000 syllables). By each *muhūrta*, he gained eighty (two Paṅktis together forming eighty syllables) and by every *muhūrta*, one set of eighty is completed.
</details>


## २४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ एषु᳘ त्रिषु लोके᳘षूखा᳘यां यो᳘नौ रे᳘तो भूत᳘मात्मा᳘नमसिञ्चच्छन्दोम᳘यꣳ स्तोमम᳘यं प्राणम᳘यं देवताम᳘यं तस्यार्धमासे᳘ प्रथम᳘ आत्मा स᳘मस्क्रियत द᳘वीयसि प᳘रो द᳘वीयसि प᳘रः संवत्सर᳘ एव स᳘र्वः कृत्स्नः स᳘मस्क्रियत तद्य᳘त्परिश्रि᳘तमुपाधत्त तद्रा᳘त्रिमु᳘पाधत्त तद᳘नु पञ्चदश मुहूर्तान्मुहूर्तान᳘नु पञ्चदशाशीतीर᳘थ यद्य᳘जुष्मतीमुपा᳘धत्त तद᳘हरु᳘पाधत्त तद᳘नु प᳘ञ्चदशमेतां᳘ त्रयीं विद्या᳘मात्मन्ना᳘वपतात्म᳘न्नकुरुत सो᳘ऽत्रैव स᳘र्वेषां भूता᳘नामा᳘त्माभवच्छन्दोम᳘यस्तोमम᳘यः प्राणम᳘यो देवताम᳘यः स एतन्म᳘य एव भू᳘त्वोर्ध्व उ᳘दक्रामत्स यः स᳘ उद᳘क्रामदेष स᳘ चन्द्र᳘मास्त᳘स्यैषा प्रतिष्ठा य᳘ एष᳘ त᳘पत्येत᳘स्मादेवाध्यचीयतैत᳘स्मिन्न᳘ध्यचीयतात्म᳘न ए᳘वैनं तं नि᳘रमिमीतात्म᳘नः प्रा᳘जनयत् ॥२४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He (Prajāpati) passed his own self in the form of metres, *stomas*, *prāna* and deities, as seed into the womb, namely the *ukhā* which is in the form of these three worlds. In the course of a half-month, the first body (of his) was fashioned; in another (half-month), the next body, in a further one, the next. Thus in a year, he is completely fashioned. When he placed the enclosing stone, he laid down a night. With that he placed fifteen *muhūrtas* and in placing fifteen *muhūrtas*, he placed fifteen (times) eighty sets (of the syllables of the sacred lore). When he laid down a brick with a *Yajus* for- mula (yajuṣmatī brick) he placed a day. With that he laid down fifteen muhūrtas and in placing fifteen muhūrtas, he laid down fifteen (times) eighty sets (of the syllables of the sacred lore). In this way he puts this Trayī into his own self and made it his own. By means of this he came to contain (within his body) all existing things; including the metres, stomas, prāṇas and deities. Having become one with all these, he ascended. He who thus ascended is that moon. He who burns yonder (sun) is his foundation; be- cause he is built up over him. On him he was built up out of his own self. Thus he made him out of his own self.

When he (the Yajamāna) intending to build up an altar gets initi- ated, just as Prajāpati poured his own self made into seed into the ukhā, the womb, he (Yajamāna) pours into the ukhā, his own self, made of metres, stomas, prāṇas and deities, as seed into the womb, i.e. the ukhā.
