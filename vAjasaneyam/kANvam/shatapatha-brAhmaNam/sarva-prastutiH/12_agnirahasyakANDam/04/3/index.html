विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (1/39 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)

एष᳓ वै᳓ मृत्यु᳓र्य᳓त्संवत्सर᳓ एष᳓ हि᳓ म᳓र्त्यानामहोरात्रा᳓भ्यामा᳓युः क्षिणोत्य᳓थ म्रियन्ते त᳓स्मादेष᳓ एव᳓ मृत्युः᳓ स᳓ यो᳓ [[हैतं|हैतं᳓]] मृत्युं᳓ संवत्सरं᳓ वे᳓द न᳓ हास्यै᳓ष᳓ पुरा᳓ जर᳓सोऽहोरात्रा᳓भ्यामा᳓युः क्षिणो᳓ति स᳓र्वं है᳓वा᳓युरेति ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
C R Swaminathan

This year is no doubt, death; because it liquidates the lives of mor- tals by means of days and nights and they die (eventually). So this (year) itself is Mṛtyu. He who knows this year as death, his life, the day and night do not destroy before old age, and he attains his full span of life.

Now, there are some who want to get this total (of 10,800 lokampṛṇās) in the Āhavanīya itself. Their logic is that those fire-altars are different and why should we have those (bricks of the Gārhapatya and Dhiṣṇyās) taken into account here (in Āhavanīya)? One should not do so.
