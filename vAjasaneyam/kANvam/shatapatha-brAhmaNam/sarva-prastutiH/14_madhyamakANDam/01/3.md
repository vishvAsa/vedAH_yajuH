## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

यद्वै दी᳘क्षन्तेऽग्नावि᳘ष्णू एव᳘ देव᳘ते य᳘जन्तेऽग्नावि᳘ष्णू देव᳘ते भवन्त्यग्नावि᳘ष्ण्वोः सा᳘युज्यꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयन्ति ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now when they do the initiation, they are (actually) offering sacrifice to the twin deities Agnā-Viṣṇū. They themselves become the Agnā-Viṣṇū deities and attain identity with them and share the world of Agnā-Viṣṇū.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘त्प्रायणी᳘येन य᳘जन्ते᳘ऽदितिमेव᳘ देव᳘तां यजन्तेऽदितिर्देव᳘ता भवन्त्य᳘दितेः सा᳘युज्यꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयन्ति ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When they offer Prāyaṇīya (observe the opening sacrifice of Atirātra) to the deity of Aditi, they become the Aditi deity themselves and get identi- fied with Aditi and share the world of Aditi.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘त्क्रये᳘ण चरन्ति सो᳘ममेव᳘ देव᳘तां य᳘जन्ते सो᳘मो देव᳘ता भवन्ति सो᳘मस्य सा᳘युज्यꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयन्ति ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When they proceed with the purchasing of Soma plant (Soma Krayana) they worship the deity, Soma; become Soma deity themselves and get iden- tified with that deity and share the world of Soma.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘दातिथ्ये᳘न य᳘जन्ते वि᳘ष्णुमेव᳘ देव᳘तां य᳘जन्ते वि᳘ष्णुर्देव᳘ता भवन्ति वि᳘ष्णोः सा᳘युज्यꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयन्ति ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When they perform the Ātitthya (offering to guests), they indeed wor- ship the deity of Viṣṇu; become Viṣṇu themselves and get identified with that diety and share the world of Viṣṇu.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘त्प्रव᳘र्ग्येण य᳘जन्त आदित्य᳘मेव᳘ देव᳘तां य᳘जन्त आदित्यो᳘ देव᳘ता भवन्त्यादित्य᳘स्य सा᳘युज्यꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयन्ति ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When they offer sacrifice with Pravargya, they offer the deity of Āditya; become Āditya deity themselves; get identified with that deity and share the world of Āditya.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘दुपस᳘द उपय᳘न्त्येता᳘ एव᳘ देव᳘ता य᳘जन्ते या᳘ एता᳘ उपस᳘त्स्वेता᳘ देव᳘ता भवन्त्येता᳘सां देव᳘तानाꣳ सा᳘युज्यꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयन्ति ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When they proceed with the Upasada, they indeed offer sacrifice to those deities (who receive the oblations) of the Upasada; become Upasada deities themselves and get identified with those deities and share the world of those Upasada deities.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘दग्नीषोमी᳘येण पशु᳘ना य᳘जन्तेऽग्नीषो᳘मावेव᳘ देव᳘ते य᳘जन्तेऽग्नीषो᳘मौ देव᳘ते भवन्त्यग्नीषो᳘मयोः सा᳘युज्यꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयन्ति ॥ ७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When they perform sacrifice with the Paśu for Agni-Soma, they in- deed offer to the deities of Agni-Soma, become Agni-Soma deities them- selves, get identified with those deities and share the world of Agni-Soma.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘त्प्रायणी᳘यमतिरात्र᳘मुपय᳘न्त्यहोरात्रे᳘ एव᳘ देव᳘ते य᳘जन्तेऽहोरात्रे᳘ देव᳘ते भवन्त्यहोरात्र᳘योः सा᳘युज्यꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयन्ति ॥ ८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When they perform the Prāyaṇīya Atirātra (the opening ritual of Gavām Ayanam), they worship the deities of day and night; become day and night deities themselves and get identified with those deities and share the world of those deities of day and night.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘च्चतुर्विꣳशम᳘हरुपय᳘न्ति संवत्सर᳘मेव देव᳘तां य᳘जन्ते संवत्सरो᳘ देव᳘ता भवन्ति संवत्सर᳘स्य सा᳘युज्यꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयन्ति ॥ ९ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When they proceed with the Caturvimśa day, they worship the deity of the year itself and become that deity of the year themselves, get identified with that deity and share the world of the deity of the year.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘दभिप्लव᳘ꣳ᳘ षडह᳘मुपय᳘न्त्यर्द्धमासां᳘श्च मा᳘सांश्च देव᳘ता य᳘जन्तेऽर्द्धमासा᳘श्च मा᳘साश्च देव᳘ता भवन्त्यर्द्धमासा᳘नां च मा᳘सानां च सा᳘युज्यꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयन्ति ॥ १० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When they perform the Abhiplava and Ṣaḍaha, they worship the dei- ties of half-months and months and become the deities of half-months and months themselves; get identified with those deities and share the worlds of the deities of half-months and months.
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ यत्पृ᳘ष्ठ्यꣳ षडह᳘मुपय᳘न्त्यृतूनेव᳘ देव᳘ता य᳘जन्त ऋत᳘वो देव᳘ता भवन्त्यृतूनाꣳ सा᳘युज्यꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयन्ति ॥ ११ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When they proceed with the Pṛṣṭhya-Ṣaḍaha, they worship the deities of the seasons themselves and become the deities of the seasons; get iden- tified with those deities and share the world of the deities of the seasons.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘दभिजित᳘मुपय᳘न्त्यग्नि᳘मेव᳘ देव᳘तां य᳘जन्तेऽग्नि᳘र्देव᳘ता भवन्त्यग्नेः सा᳘युज्यꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयन्ति ॥ १२ ॥

## १५

## १६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘द्गो आयु᳘षी उपय᳘न्ति मित्राव᳘रुणावेव᳘ देव᳘ते य᳘जन्ते मित्राव᳘रुणौ देव᳘ते भवन्ति मित्राव᳘रुणयोः सा᳘युज्यꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयन्ति ॥ १६ ॥

अ᳘थ यद्दा᳘शरात्रिकं पृ᳘ष्ठ्यꣳ षडह᳘मुपय᳘न्ति दि᳘श एव᳘ देव᳘ता य᳘जन्ते दि᳘शो देव᳘ता भवन्ति दिशाꣳ सा᳘युज्यꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयन्ति ॥ १८ ॥

## २०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘द्दशमम᳘हरुपय᳘न्ति संवत्सर᳘मेव᳘ देव᳘तां य᳘जन्ते संवत्सरो᳘ देव᳘ता भवन्ति संवत्सर᳘स्य सा᳘युज्यꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयन्ति ॥ २० ॥

</details>


## २२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘दुदयनी᳘यमतिरात्र᳘मुपय᳘न्ति संवत्सर᳘मेव त᳘दाप्त्वा᳘ स्वर्गे᳘ लोके प्र᳘तितिष्ठन्ति ता᳘न्य᳘दि पृच्छे᳘युः का᳘मद्य᳘ देव᳘तां य᳘जध्वे का᳘ देव᳘ता स्थ क᳘स्यां देव᳘तायां वसथेत्य᳘त ए᳘वैकतमां᳘ ब्रूयुर्य᳘स्यै तु ने᳘दिष्ठꣳ स्युरेते᳘ ह वै᳘ सति स᳘द एते हि᳘ सती᳘षु देव᳘तासु सी᳘दन्तो य᳘न्ति सत्रस᳘दो हैवे᳘तरे स यो᳘ हैवं᳘ विदु᳘षां दीक्षिता᳘नां पा᳘पकꣳ सत्रे᳘ कीर्त᳘येदेता᳘भ्यस्त्वा देव᳘ताभ्य आ᳘वृश्चाम इ᳘त्येनं ब्रूयुः स पा᳘पीयान्भवति श्रे᳘याꣳस आत्म᳘ना ॥ २२ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When they proceed with the Udayanīya (the concluding Atirātra) then indeed, having gained the year, they get settled themselves in the world of heaven. If some one is to ask "to which deity you are offering today (in the Udayanīya); what deity you are becoming today and which deity's world you are sharing?" Each one of them should name that deity to whom they feel nearest in the Satra. Actually, they are well-seated because they are seated among good deities. Others (who do not know the above enumerated co- relation between these ritualistic items and the corresponding deities) are merely participating in the Satra (they don't gain identity with or share the worlds of those deities). If any one, doing the Satra, were to offend any of the initiated priests who know his relationship to the deities, he should be told "We will cut you off from the deities." He will become a sinner and these (priests) will be superior to him.
</details>


## २३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ एष᳘ संवत्सरस्त्रि᳘महाव्रतश्चतुर्विꣳशे᳘ महाव्रतं᳘ विषुव᳘ति महाव्रतं᳘ महाव्रत᳘ एव᳘ महाव्रतं त᳘ꣳ᳘ ह स्मैतं पू᳘र्व उ᳘पयन्ति त्रि᳘महाव्रतन्ते ते᳘ तेजस्वि᳘न आसुः सत्यवादि᳘नः स᳘ꣳ᳘शितव्रता अ᳘थ य᳘ उ हैनम᳘प्येत᳘र्हि त᳘थोपेयुर्य᳘थामपात्र᳘मुदक आ᳘सिक्ते विम्म्रि᳘त्ये᳘देव᳘ꣳ᳘ हैव ते वि᳘म्म्रित्येयुरुपर्यु᳘पयन्ति त᳘देषाꣳ सत्ये᳘न श्र᳘मेण त᳘पसा श्रद्ध᳘या यज्ञेना᳘हुतिभिर᳘वरुद्धं भवति ॥ २३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

There are three Mahāvratas in a year; namely the Caturvimśa Mahāvrata, the Visuvat Mahāvrata and the Mahāvrata itself. Formerly they used to conduct the Satra with (all) these three Mahāvratas and they be- came glorious, truth-speaking and faithful to their vow. Now-a-days if any one sitting in Satra were to perform (all of them), they will get dilapidated like an unbaked clay pot when filled with water. Because by doing those (all) they are over-doing. The purpose of performing all these is attainable even by truth, by toil, by austerities, by devotion, by sacrifice and by offerings. (Third Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>
