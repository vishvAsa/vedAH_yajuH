## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

विश्व᳘रूपं वै᳘ त्वाष्ट्रमि᳘न्द्रोऽहंस्तं त्व᳘ष्टा हत᳘पुत्रो᳘ऽभ्यचर᳘त्सोऽभिचरणी᳘यम᳘पेन्द्रꣳ सो᳘ममा᳘हरत्तस्ये᳘न्द्रो यज्ञवेशसं᳘ कृत्वा᳘ प्रास᳘हा सो᳘ममपिबत्स वि᳘ष्व᳘ङ्‌व्यार्च्छत्तस्येन्द्रियं᳘ वी᳘र्यम᳘ङ्गादङ्गादस्रवत् ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Indra killed Tvaṣṭā's son, Viśvarūpa. Tvaṣṭā, who lost his son performed Abhicāra (exocised) on him (Indra) and fetched Soma juice meant to de- stroy Indra by Abhicāra. Indra drank that by force and thus desecrated the sacrifice. He wandered about in all directions and his vital essence flowed out of all his limbs.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तस्या᳘क्षिभ्यामेव ते᳘जोऽस्रव᳘त्सोऽजः᳘ पशु᳘रभवद्‌धूम्रोऽथ यत्पक्ष्मभ्यस्ते᳘ गोधू᳘मा यद᳘श्रुभ्यस्तत्कुवलम् ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The essence that oozed out of his eyes became the grey-coloured he- goat and that which oozed out of his eye-lashes became wheat and that from out of his tears became the Kuvala fruit.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ना᳘सिकाभ्यामे᳘वास्य वी᳘र्यमस्रवत्सो᳘ऽविः पशु᳘रभवन्मेषो᳘ऽथ य᳘च्छ्लेष्मणस्ता᳘ उपवा᳘का य᳘त्स्नीहा तद्ब᳘दरम् ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That essence from out of his nostrils became that animal, the ram; and that which came out of the phlegm became Upavāka grain and his moisture became the Badara fruit.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

मु᳘खादे᳘वास्य ब᳘लमस्रवत्स गौः᳘ पशु᳘रभवदृषभो᳘ऽथ ये फे᳘नास्ते य᳘वा यत्स्ने᳘हस्त᳘त्कर्क्कन्धु ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The essence that came out of his mouth became the animal, the bull and the foam therein became barley grains. The moisture thereof (in the mouth) became the Karkandhu fruit.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

श्रो᳘त्रादे᳘वास्य य᳘शोऽस्रवत्तदे᳘कशफमभवद᳘श्वोऽश्वतरो᳘ गर्दभः ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The foam that oozed out of his ear became the one-hoofed animals- the horse, the mule and ass.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स्त᳘नाभ्यामे᳘वास्य शुक्र᳘मस्रवत्तत्प᳘योऽभवत्पशूनां ज्यो᳘तिरु᳘रसा ए᳘वास्य हृ᳘दयात्त्वि᳘षिरस्रवत्स᳘ श्ये᳘नोऽपाष्ठि᳘हाभवद्व᳘यसाꣳरा᳘जा ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The essence that flowed out of his breasts, became milk, the glory of the animals. From his heart and chest, what glow flowed out, became the eagle, the king of birds and the eater of the birds.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ना᳘भ्या ए᳘वास्य शू᳘षोऽस्रवत्तत्सी᳘समभवन्ना᳘यो न हि᳘रण्यꣳ रे᳘तस ए᳘वास्य रूप᳘मस्रवत्त᳘त्सुवर्णꣳ हि᳘रण्यमभवच्छिश्नादे᳘वास्य र᳘सोऽस्रवत्सा᳘ परिस्रु᳘दभवत्स्फिगी᳘भ्यामे᳘वास्य भा᳘मोऽस्रवत्सा सु᳘राभवद᳘न्नस्य र᳘सो मू᳘त्रादे᳘वास्यौ᳘जोऽस्रवत्स वृकोऽभवदारण्या᳘नां पशूनां᳘ जूतिरू᳘वध्यादे᳘वास्य मन्यु᳘रस्रवत्स व्या᳘घ्रोऽभवदारण्या᳘नां पशूनाꣳ रा᳘जा लो᳘हितादे᳘वास्य स᳘होऽस्रवत्स सि᳘ꣳ᳘होऽभवदारण्याना᳘मीशः ॥ ७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

His life-breath flowed out of his naval and became lead, which is other than iron and silver. From his seed, flowed out his form, which became gold. From his genitals his essence flowed and became Parisrut (hot unproc- essed liquor). His glow flowed out of his hips and became the Surā (pro- cessed liquor) which is the essence of food. His vigour came out of his urine and became the wolf, (possessed of) the dashing speed of the wild beasts. His wrath flowed out of his intestines and became the tiger; the king of the 1 wild beasts. His might flowed from his blood and became the lion, the ruler of wild-life.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

लो᳘मभ्य ए᳘वास्य चित्त᳘मस्रवत्ते᳘ श्यामा᳘का अभवंस्त्वच᳘ ए᳘वास्यापचितिरस्रव᳘त्सोऽश्वत्थो व᳘नस्प᳘तिरभवन्याꣳसे᳘भ्य ए᳘वास्यो᳘र्ग्गस्रवत्स᳘ उदुम्ब᳘रोऽभवद᳘स्थिभ्य ए᳘वास्य स्व᳘धास्रवत्स᳘न्यग्रो᳘धोऽभवन्मज्ज᳘भ्य ए᳘वास्य भक्षः᳘ सोमपी᳘थोऽस्रवत् ॥ ८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

His thought flowed out of his hair and became millet; from his skin his honour came out and became Udumbara tree. From his bones, flowed out Svadhā (his sweet drink) and became the Nyagrodha tree. From his marrows his beverage, the Soma flowed out.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते व्रीह᳘योऽभवन्नेव᳘मस्येन्द्रिया᳘णि वी᳘र्याणि व्युदक्रामन्न᳘थ ह वै त᳘र्हि न᳘मुचिनै᳘वासुरे᳘ण सह᳘ चचार स᳘ ऐक्षत न᳘मुचिरपुनर्वा᳘ अय᳘मभूद्ध᳘न्तास्येन्द्रियं वी᳘र्यꣳ सोमपीथ᳘मन्ना᳘द्यꣳ ह᳘राणी᳘ति त᳘स्यै त᳘यैव सुरयेन्द्रियं᳘ वी᳘र्यꣳ सोमपीथ᳘मन्ना᳘द्यमहरत्स᳘ ह᳘ न्यर्णः शिश्ये तं᳘ देवा᳘ उपसं᳘जग्मिरे श्रेष्ठो वै᳘ नोऽय᳘मभूत्त᳘मिमं᳘ पा᳘प्माविदद्धन्तेमं᳘ भिषज्यामे᳘ति ॥ ९ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It (Soma) became rice; thus his energies and vital powers flowed out of him. Then Indra was moving about in the company of the Asura called Namuci. Then Namuci thought - “He (Indra) is finally undone. I will grab his energy and vital power; his Soma drink and his food." By grabing his Surā - liquor, he took his energy, vital power, Soma drink and his food. He (Indra) lay there completely emptied. The gods flocked around him and said - "He (Indra) was the greatest among us and he has been struck down by evil. Let us treat him."
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तेऽश्वि᳘नाव᳘ब्रुवन्युवं वै᳘ ब्राह्मणौ भिष᳘जौ स्थो युव᳘मिमं᳘ भिषज्यतमि᳘ति ता᳘वब्रूताम᳘स्तु नौ भाग इ᳘ति᳘ तेऽब्रुवन्य᳘ ए᳘षोऽजः स᳘ वां भाग इ᳘ति तथे᳘ति त᳘स्मादाश्विनो᳘ धूम्रो᳘ भवति ॥ १० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They told Aśvins - "You are the brāhmin physicians you treat him (Indra).” They (Aśvins) said, “Let us have a share." They replied "That he- goat shall be your share." They said, "Yes." Hence the smoke coloured he- goat is sacred to the Aśvins.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाब्रुवन्नेता᳘वद्वा᳘ अस्मि᳘न्नेत᳘र्हि या᳘वदय᳘मृष᳘भो᳘ऽस्यै᳘वाय᳘मस्त्वि᳘ति तथे᳘ति त᳘स्मादैन्द्र᳘ ऋषभो᳘ भवति ता᳘वश्वि᳘नौ च स᳘रस्वती चेन्द्रियं᳘ वी᳘र्यं न᳘मुचेराहृ᳘त्य त᳘दस्मिन्पुनरदघुस्तं᳘ पाप्म᳘नोऽत्रायन्त सु᳘त्रातं बतैनं पाप्म᳘नोऽत्रास्मही᳘ति तद्वाव᳘ सौत्राम᳘ण्यभवत्त᳘त्सौत्रामण्यै᳘ सौत्रामणीत्वं त्रा᳘यते मृत्यो᳘रात्मा᳘नमुप पाप्मा᳘नꣳहते य᳘ एवमेत᳘त्सौत्रामण्यै᳘ सोत्रामणीत्वं वे᳘द त्र᳘यस्त्रिꣳशद्द᳘क्षिण भवन्ति त्र᳘यस्त्रिꣳशद्धि तं᳘ देव᳘ता अ᳘भिषज्यंस्त᳘स्मादाहुर्भेषज्यं द᳘क्षिणा इ᳘ति ॥ १२ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They (Aśvins) said - "Even now there is as much in him (Indra) as that bull. So let it (bull) be his. They (gods) said "Yes.” Therefore the bull belongs to Indra. Those Aśvins and Sarasvatī together took out that es- sence and vital power from out of that Namuci and replaced them in him (Indra). Thus saved him from affliction, (rejuvenated him). They said, "We have saved him from afflication and he is well-saved (sutrāta)." This (pro- cess of saving) came to be the Sutrāmaṇī. This is the reason for this (sacri- fice) to have the name, Sautrāmaṇī – that it saves the self from death and removes the affliction, of whosoever thus knows that saving potential of Sautrāmaṇī. There are thirty-three Dakṣiṇās for this, because thirty-three gods healed him (Indra). Therefore they say Dakṣiṇā is medicine (First Brāhmaṇa Ends)..
</details>
