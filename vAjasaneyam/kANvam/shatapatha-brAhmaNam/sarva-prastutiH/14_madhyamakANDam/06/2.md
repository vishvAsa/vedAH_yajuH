In this connection they say - "Surely he who enters into the Avabhṛta waters falls away from this world." (That is not so). He after emerging from the bath, offers a cup of curd to Mitrā-Varuṇa; Mitra is the terrestrial world and Varuṇa is that above and the cup of curd is what is here between those two worlds. In as much as he sacrifices with curd to Mitra and Varuṇa, he ultimately gets established in the Prāṇa and food. (Second Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
