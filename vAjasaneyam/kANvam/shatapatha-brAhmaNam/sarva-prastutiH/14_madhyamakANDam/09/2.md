<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (4/32 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)</summary>

अ᳓थ ह स्माह ना᳓को [[मौद्गल्यो|मौ᳓द्गल्यो]] मरिष्य᳓न्तं चे᳓द्य᳓जमानं म᳓न्येत य᳓त्रैवा᳙स्मा᳓ आ᳓श᳓मनं जोषितं᳓ स्या᳓त्त᳓दर᳓ण्योरग्नी᳓ [[समारो᳓ह्यान्निर्म᳓थ्य|समारो᳓ह्या᳓न्निर्म᳓थ्य]] जु᳓ह्वद्वसेत्स᳓ यदा᳙ऽस्मा᳓ल्लोका᳓द्य᳓जमानः [[प्रे᳓यात्|प्रेया᳓त्]] ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Nāka Maudgalya once said- "If one feels the Yajamāna is to die soon, let him transfer his (Yajamāna's) fires on two Aranis and go to the place which he (the Yajamāna) has desired for his immolation and having churned out the fire (from out of those Aranis) let him stay there making offerings." When the Yajamāna (ultimately) departs from this world.
