विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (4/53 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)

देवा᳓ ह वै᳓ सह᳓स्रसंवत्सराय दिदीक्षिरे [[तेषां|ते᳓षां]] प᳓ञ्च राता᳓नि संवत्सरा᳓णां पर्य᳓वेतान्यासुर᳓थेदं᳓ स᳓र्वमेव᳓ शश्राम ये᳓ स्तो᳓मा या᳓नि पृष्ठा᳓नि या᳓नि छ᳓न्दांसि त᳓तो देवा᳓ एत᳓द्यज्ञ᳓स्या᳓यातयामापश्यंस्ते᳓ना᳓यातयाम्ना या᳓ वे᳓दे व्य᳙ष्टिरा᳓सीत्तां᳓ [[व्याश्नुवता᳓यातयामा|व्या᳙श्नुवता᳓यातयामा]] वा᳓ अस्य᳓ वे᳓दा अ᳓यातयाम्नया हास्य त्रय्या᳓ विद्य᳓या᳓र्त्विज्यं [[कृतं|कृतं᳓]] भवति [[य|य᳓]] एव᳓मेत᳓द्वे᳓द ॥

The gods once performed the initiation for a sacrificial session of a thousand years. When five hundred years had passed, everything (about that sacrifice) namely, the Stoma, the Pṛṣṭhas and the texts withered away. Then the gods discovered the undecayed aspect of that sacrifice and by means of that undecayed part, they gained the success that was in the Vedas. For him who knows this secret, the Vedas are inexhaustible and with the inexhaustible threefold Vedas, the functions of the Ṛtviks (officiating priests) are being carried out.

Now, the inexhaustible aspect of the sacrifice is this “O Śrāvaya, Astu Śrauṣaṭ, Yaja; Ye Yajamahe and Vauṣat.” In these five utterances (associated with each offering in the sacrifices) together, there are seventeen syllables; O Śrāvaya consists of four; Astu Śrauṣaṭ has four, Yaja has two, Ye Yajāmahe has five syllables and the Vaṣaṭkāra (the utterance Vauṣaṭ) has two syllables. This is the seventeen-fold Prajāpati as established in the divine as well as physical aspects. Whoever knows thus, the seventeenfold Prajāpati, estab- lished in the divine and physical aspects, gets himself established with prog- eny and cattle wealth in this world and immortality in the world beyond.

He spends twelve months in initiation; twelve in Upasada and twelve in Sutya. Thus thirty-six (months) correspond to Bṛhatī metre with thirty-six syllables. Indeed it was with Bṛhatī, the gods tried to reach heaven and ob- tained the heavenly world by means of the Bṛhatī. In like manner, this one (Yajamāna) strives to reach the heavenly world and achieves the heavenly world by means of Bṛhatī. He who knows this, secures for himself whatever desire there is in Bṛhatī.

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (3/47 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)

त᳓द्वा᳓ [[एतत्त्रयं᳓|एत᳓त्त्रयं᳓]] सह᳓ क्रिय᳓तेऽग्नि᳓रर्क्क्यं᳙ मह᳓दुक्थ्यं᳓ स᳓ य᳓त्संवत्सरं᳓ दीक्षा᳓भिरे᳓ति संवत्सर᳓मुपस᳓द्भिस्ते᳓नास्याग्न्यर्क्का᳓वाप्तौ᳓ भवतो᳓ऽथ [[यत्संवत्सरं᳓|य᳓त्संवत्सरं᳓]] सुत्या᳓भिरेति᳓ ते᳓नो एवा᳙स्य मह᳓दुक्थ्य᳓माप्तं᳓ भवति स᳓ वा᳓ एष᳓ एव᳓ सह᳓स्रसंवत्सरस्य प्रतिमा᳓ [[यत्तापश्चित᳓|य᳓त्तापश्चित᳓]] एष᳓ प्रजा᳓नां प्र᳓जात्यै य᳓त्तापश्चितः᳓ ॥

In this, the three are simultaneously performed - the fire-altar, the Arkya and the Mahad Uktham. By passing a year with the rites of initiation and a year by performing the Upasada, Agnī (altar) and Arka are gained by him. In so far as he spends one year with the performance of Sutyas, by that he secures Mahad Ukthyam. Thus this Tāpaścita is the substitute for the thou- sand-year long sacrificial session. Thus Tāpaścita is for procreation of the beings (Third Brāhmaṇa Ends).
