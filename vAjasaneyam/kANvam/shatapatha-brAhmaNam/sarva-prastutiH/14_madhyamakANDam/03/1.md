## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

यद्बालाक इदं᳘ त्रिवृदे᳘ति स᳘र्वम᳘न्योन्य᳘मभिसंप᳘द्यमानम् । कथ᳘ꣳ᳘स्विद्यज्ञः पु᳘रुषः प्रजा᳘पतिर᳘न्योन्यं ना᳘तिरिच्यन्त एते ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“O Bālāki; whereas this threefold Universe (comprising of the Adhyātma, Adhiyagña and Adhidaiva) keeps on passing into one another, how, is it that these - the sacrifice, the Puruṣa and Prajāpati - do not exceed one another?”
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

य᳘दूर्ध्वा स्तो᳘मा अनुय᳘न्ति यज्ञ᳘मभ्याव᳘र्तꣳसा᳘मभिः क᳘ल्पमानाः । कथ᳘ꣳ᳘ स्वित्ते पु᳘रुषमा᳘विशन्ति कथं᳘ प्राणैः᳘ सयु᳘जो भवन्ति ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Whereas the upward Stomās follow the sacrifice, fitting themselves to the Sāmans simultaneously recited, how is it they enter the Puruṣa and get united with the Prāṇas?”
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

प्राय᳘णीयोऽतिरात्रश्चतुर्विꣳशम᳘हश्चत्वा᳘रोऽभिप्लवाः पृष्ठ्य इ᳘त्येते । कथ᳘ꣳ᳘ स्विते पु᳘रुषमा᳘विशन्ति कथं᳘ प्राणैः᳘ सयु᳘जो भवन्ति ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"How do the Prāyaṇiya Atirātra, the Caturviṁśa day, the four Abhiplavas and Pṛṣṭhys enter the Puruṣa and get united with the Prāṇas?”
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अभिजि᳘ता स्व᳘रसामानोऽभि᳘कॢप्ता उभय᳘तो विषुवन्तमु᳘पयन्ति । कथ᳘ꣳ᳘ स्वित्ते पु᳘रुषमा᳘विशन्ति कथं᳘ प्राणैः᳘ सयु᳘जो भवन्ति ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"How does the Svarasāman, fitted out with Abhijit, get united with Viṣuvat on both sides and how do these enter the Puruṣa and become one with the Prāṇas?”
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त्रिवृ᳘त्प्रायाः सप्तदशा᳘भिकॢप्तास्त्रयस्त्रिꣳशा᳘न्ताश्चतुरुत्तरे᳘ण । कथ᳘ꣳ᳘ स्विंत्ते पु᳘रुषमाविशन्ति कथं᳘ प्राणैः᳘ सयु᳘जो भवन्तीति ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Getting out with the Trivṛt, fitted out with the (Pañcadaśa and) Saptadaśa and ending with the Trayastriṁśa; with (the series of Stomas) in- creasing succesively by four (syllables) – how do these enter the Puruṣa and become one with the Prāṇas?”
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

शि᳘रस्त्रिवृ᳘त्पञ्चद᳘शोऽस्य ग्रीवा उ᳘र आहुः सप्तदशा᳘भिकॢप्तम् । एकविꣳश᳘मुद᳘रं कल्पयन्ति पार्श्वे प᳘र्शूस्त्रिणवे᳘ना᳘भिकॢप्ते ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Trivṛt is his (Puruṣa's) head; the Pañcadaśa his neck; the chest, as they say, corresponds to Saptadaśa Stoma; the belly they corelate to the Ekaviṁśa, the two side-ribs are made of the Triṇava Stoma.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अभिप्लवा᳘ उभय᳘तोऽस्य बाहू । पृष्ठं पृ᳘ष्ठ्य इ᳘ति धी᳘रा वदन्ति । अ᳘नूकमस्य चतुरुत्तरे᳘ण संवत्सरे᳘ ब्राह्मणाः᳘ कल्पयन्ति । क᳘र्णावस्याभिजि᳘द्विश्वजिच्चा᳘ख्या बाहुः स्व᳘रसामाभिकॢप्ते । न᳘स्यं प्राणं विषुव᳘न्तमाहु᳘र्गो आयुषी प्राणा᳘वेताव᳘वाञ्चौ । अ᳘ङ्गान्यस्य दशरात्र᳘माहुर्मु᳘खं महाव्रत᳘ꣳ संवत्सरे᳘ ब्राह्मणाः᳘ कल्पयन्ति । स᳘र्वस्तोमꣳ स᳘र्वसामानमेत᳘ꣳ᳘ संवत्सर᳘मध्यात्मं प्र᳘विष्टम् । समं धी᳘र आत्म᳘ना कल्पयित्वा ब्रध्न᳘स्यास्ते विष्टपे᳘ऽजातशोकः ॥ ७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Abhiplavas on both sides (of the Viṣuvat, the body) are his arms; the Pṛṣṭhya is the back - so say the wise. His spine, the Brahmins make up in the nearby means of the series of Stomas increasing successively by four syllables. The Abhijit and Viśvajit are his ears and (three parts of) his eyes, they say, correspond to the three sets of Svarasāmans. The Viśvajit, they say is the Prāṇa of his nostril. The Goṣṭoma and Āyuṣṭoma are his two downword Prāṇas. The Daśarātra they identify as his limbs and the Mahāvrata, the priests so arranged as to be the mouth of the year. Thus the Ātman or spirit has entered the body consisting of all Stomas and all Sāmans. The sage (wise one) thus makes the sacrifice equal to his own body and with no affliction (nearing him) reaches that high pedestal of the reddy Sun (First Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>
