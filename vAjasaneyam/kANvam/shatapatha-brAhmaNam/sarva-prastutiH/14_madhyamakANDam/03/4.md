<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

By the morning Savana, the Vasus have gone, by the mid day Savana, the Rudras have gone and by the third Savana the Ādityas. Now I have only the sacrificial ground (all other things they have taken away) and I am seated on the sacrificial site.
