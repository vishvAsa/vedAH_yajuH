<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (1/11 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)</summary>

In this, the three are simultaneously performed - the fire-altar, the Arkya and the Mahad Uktham. By passing a year with the rites of initiation and a year by performing the Upasada, Agnī (altar) and Arka are gained by him. In so far as he spends one year with the performance of Sutyas, by that he secures Mahad Ukthyam. Thus this Tāpaścita is the substitute for the thou- sand-year long sacrificial session.
