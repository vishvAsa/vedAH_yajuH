## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

देवा᳘ ह वै᳘ सह᳘स्रसंवत्सराय दिदीक्षिरे तेषां प᳘ञ्च राता᳘नि संवत्सरा᳘णां पर्य᳘वेतान्यासुर᳘थेदꣳ स᳘र्वमेव᳘ शश्राम ये स्तो᳘मा या᳘नि पृष्ठा᳘नि या᳘नि छ᳘न्दाꣳसि त᳘तो देवा᳘ एत᳘द्यज्ञस्या᳘यातयामापश्यंस्तेना᳘यातयाभ्ना या वे᳘दे᳘ व्यष्टिरा᳘सीत्तां व्याश्नुवता᳘यातयामा वा᳘ अस्य वे᳘दा अ᳘यातयाम्नया हास्य त्रय्या᳘ विद्यया᳘र्त्विज्यं कृतं भवति य एव᳘मेतद्वे᳘द ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The gods once performed the initiation for a sacrificial session of a thousand years. When five hundred years had passed, everything (about that sacrifice) namely, the Stoma, the Pṛṣṭhas and the texts withered away. Then the gods discovered the undecayed aspect of that sacrifice and by means of that undecayed part, they gained the success that was in the Vedas. For him who knows this secret, the Vedas are inexhaustible and with the inexhaustible threefold Vedas, the functions of the Ṛtviks (officiating priests) are being carried out.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘देत᳘द्यज्ञस्या᳘यातया᳘मौश्रावया᳘स्तु श्रौ᳘षड्यज ये य᳘जामहे वौ᳘षडि᳘ति ता᳘सां वा᳘ एता᳘सां पञ्चानां व्या᳘हृतीनाꣳ सप्त᳘दशाक्ष᳘रा᳘ण्योश्रावये᳘ति च᳘तुरक्षरम᳘स्तुश्रौ᳘षडि᳘त्ति च᳘तुरक्षरं यजे᳘ति᳘ द्व्यक्षरं ये य᳘जामह इ᳘ति प᳘ञ्चाक्षरं᳘ द्व्यक्षरो वषट्कारः स᳘ एष᳘ सप्तदशः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिरधिदेवतं᳘ चाध्यात्मं᳘ च प्र᳘तिष्ठितः स यो᳘ हैव᳘मेत᳘ꣳ᳘ सप्तदशं᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिमधिदेव᳘तं चाध्यात्मं᳘ च प्रतिष्ठितं वे᳘द प्र᳘तितिष्ठति प्रज᳘या पशु᳘भिरस्मिँ᳘ल्लो᳘केऽमृतत्वे᳘नामु᳘ष्मिन् ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now, the inexhaustible aspect of the sacrifice is this "O Śrāvaya, Astu Śrauṣaṭ, Yaja; Ye Yajamahe and Vauṣat." In these five utterances (associated with each offering in the sacrifices) together, there are seventeen syllables; O Śrāvaya consists of four; Astu Śrauṣaṭ has four, Yaja has two, Ye Yajāmahe has five syllables and the Vaṣaṭkāra (the utterance Vauṣaṭ) has two syllables. This is the seventeen-fold Prajāpati as established in the divine as well as physical aspects. Whoever knows thus, the seventeenfold Prajāpati, estab- lished in the divine and physical aspects, gets himself established with prog- eny and cattle wealth in this world and immortality in the world beyond.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते ह देवा᳘ ऊचुरू᳘प तं᳘ यज्ञक्रतुं᳘ जानीत यः᳘ सह᳘स्रसंवत्सरस्य प्रतिमा को हि त᳘स्मै मनुष्यो यः᳘ सह᳘स्रसंवत्सरेण समाप्नुयादि᳘ति ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The gods then said, “Let us find out that sacrificial rite which shall be a substitute (shortened version) of the one to be performed in a thousand years; for which man is there who can achieve over a period of one thou- sand years?" (there cannot be a human being who may live for thousand years to perform this).
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते᳘ विश्वजि᳘तमेव स᳘र्वपृष्ठं पृ᳘ष्ठ्यस्य षडह᳘स्याञ्जः सवत्वमश्यंस्ते हि स्तो᳘मा भ᳘वन्ति ता᳘नि पृष्ठा᳘नि ता᳘नि छ᳘न्दाꣳसि ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They saw this Viśvajit with Sarvapṛṣṭha (all the Pṛṣṭhas), a quick-moving Soma-Sava (Soma session) as a good substitute for the Pṛṣṭha-Ṣaḍaha, for in that there are the same Stomas, same Pṛṣṭhas and same metres (as the Pṛṣṭha- Ṣaḍaha).
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

पृष्ठ्यमेव᳘ षडहं᳘ द्वादशाह᳘स्याञ्जः सव᳘मपश्यंस्ते हि स्तो᳘मा भवन्ति तानि पृष्ठा᳘नि ता᳘नि छ᳘न्दाꣳ᳘सि ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They discovered that the Pṛṣṭha-Ṣaḍaha as a quick-moving Soma-Sava to be a good substitute for the Dvādaśāha; since it has the same Stomas, the same Pṛṣṭhyas and the same metres as (the Dvādaśāha).
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

द्वादशाह᳘मेव᳘ संवत्सर᳘स्याञ्जः सव᳘मपश्यंस्ते हि स्तो᳘मा भ᳘वन्ति ता᳘नि पृष्ठा᳘नि ता᳘नि छ᳘न्दाꣳसि ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They discovered that the Dvādaśāha as a quick-moving Soma-Sava to be substitute for the Saṁvatsara (year long session), since it has the same Stomas, the same Pṛṣṭhas and same metres (as the Saṁvatsara).
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

संवत्सर᳘मेव᳘ तापश्चित᳘स्याञ्जः सव᳘मपश्यंस्ते हि स्तो᳘मा भ᳘वन्ति ता᳘नि पृष्ठा᳘नि ता᳘नि छ᳘न्दाꣳसि ॥ ७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They discovered the Saṁvatsara as a quick moving Soma-Sava to be a substitute for the Tāpaścita; since it has the same Stomas, the same Pṛṣṭhas and the same metres (as the Tāpaścita).
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तापश्चित᳘मेव᳘ सह᳘स्रसंवत्सर᳘स्याञ्जः सव᳘मपश्यंस्ते हि स्तो᳘मा भ᳘वन्ति ता᳘नि पृष्ठा᳘नि ता᳘नि छ᳘न्दाꣳसि ॥ ८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They discovered the Tāpaścita itself a quick-moving Soma-Sava to be a substitute for the thousand-year long session; since it has the same Stomas, the same Pṛṣṭhas and the same metres.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वै᳘ संवत्सरं᳘ दीक्षा᳘भिरे᳘ति संवत्सर᳘मुपस᳘द्भिः संवत्सर᳘ꣳ᳘ सुत्या᳘भिः सय᳘त्संवत्सरं᳘ दीक्षा᳘भिरे᳘ति पूर्वार्धमेव ते᳘न सह᳘स्रसंवत्सरस्या᳘वरुन्द्धेऽथ य᳘त्संवत्सर᳘मुपस᳘द्भिर्म᳘ध्यमेव ते᳘न सह᳘स्रसंवत्सरस्या᳘वरुन्द्धेऽथ य᳘त्संवत्सर᳘ꣳ᳘ सुत्या᳘भिरुत्तमार्ध᳘मेव ते᳘न सहस्रसंवत्सरस्यावरुन्द्धे ॥ ९ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He performes the rites of initiation for one year; the Upasada for one year and the Sutyas for one year; By passing one year in initiation, he se- cures for himself the first part of the thousand years' session. By passing one year by performing the Upasadas, he secures for himself the middle part of that thousand years' session. By passing one year by Sutyas, he gains for himself the final part of the thousand years' session.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वै द्वा᳘दशमा᳘सान्दीक्षा᳘भिरे᳘ति द्वा᳘दशोपसद्भिर्द्वा᳘दश सुत्याभिस्तत्ष᳘ट्त्रिꣳशदक्षरा वै᳘ बृहती᳘ बृहत्या वै᳘ देवाः᳘ स्वर्गे᳘ लोके᳘ऽयतन्त बृहत्या᳘ स्वर्गं᳘ लोक᳘माप्नुवंस्त᳘थो ए᳘वैष᳘ एत᳘द्बृह᳘त्यैव᳘ स्वर्गे᳘ लोके य᳘तते बृहत्या᳘ स्वर्गं᳘ लोक᳘माप्नोत्य᳘थ यो᳘ बृहत्यां का᳘मस्त᳘मे᳘वैते᳘नैवंविद᳘वरुन्द्धे ॥ १० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He spends twelve months in initiation; twelve in Upasada and twelve in Sutya. Thus thirty-six (months) correspond to Bṛhatī metre with thirty-six syllables. Indeed it was with Bṛhatī, the gods tried to reach heaven and ob- tained the heavenly world by means of the Bṛhatī. In like manner, this one (Yajamāna) strives to reach the heavenly world and achieves the heavenly world by means of Bṛhatī. He who knows this, secures for himself whatever desire there is in Bṛhatī.
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तद्वा᳘ एतत्त्रय᳘ꣳ᳘ सह᳘ क्रिय᳘तेऽग्नि᳘र᳘र्क्क्यं मह᳘दुक्थ्यꣳ स य᳘त्संवत्सरं᳘ दीक्षा᳘भिरे᳘ति संवत्सर᳘मुपस᳘द्भिस्ते᳘नास्याग्न्यर्क्का᳘वाप्तौ᳘ भवतो᳘ऽथ यत्संवत्सर᳘ꣳ᳘ सुत्या᳘भिरेति ते᳘नो ए᳘वास्य मह᳘दुक्थ्य᳘माप्तं᳘ भवति स वा᳘ एष᳘ एव᳘ सह᳘स्रसंवत्सरस्य प्रतिमा यत्तापश्चित᳘ एष᳘ प्रजा᳘नां प्र᳘जात्यै य᳘त्तापश्चितः ॥ ११ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

In this, the three are simultaneously performed - the fire-altar, the Arkya and the Mahad Uktham. By passing a year with the rites of initiation and a year by performing the Upasada, Agnī (altar) and Arka are gained by him. In so far as he spends one year with the performance of Sutyas, by that he secures Mahad Ukthyam. Thus this Tāpaścita is the substitute for the thou- sand-year long sacrificial session. Thus Tāpaścita is for procreation of the beings (Third Brāhmaṇa Ends).
</details>
