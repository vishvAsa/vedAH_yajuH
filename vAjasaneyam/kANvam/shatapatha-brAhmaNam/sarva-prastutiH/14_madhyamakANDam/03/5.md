## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

सावित्र᳘ꣳ᳘ ह स्मैतं पू᳘र्वे पशुमालभन्तेऽथैत᳘र्हि प्राजापत्यं यो᳘ ह्ये᳘व᳘ सविता स᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिरि᳘ति व᳘दन्तस्त᳘स्मात्सन्नुयाग्रींस्ते᳘न यजेरन् गृह᳘पतेरे᳘वाग्नि᳘षु य᳘येदं जा᳘घन्या प᳘त्नीः संयाज᳘यन्ति तस्यां नो᳘ऽप्यसदि᳘ति ते त᳘तो यदा निका᳘मं दी᳘क्षन्ते ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

In olden days they were seizing the Paśu meant for Savitā (in the Gavām Ayanam). Now they seize the Paśu meant for Prajāpati, saying “he who is Savitā is same as Prajāpati.” Therefore after mixing together the sacrificial fires, they should perform this (Gavām-Ayanam) sacrifice on the Gṛhapati’s own fires, thinking “May we also have a share in this tail where- with the offerings are made to the wives (of the gods).” Thereafter they enter initiation whenever they choose.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दु वा᳘ आहुर्न्ना᳘नाधिष्ण्या एव᳘ स्युर्य᳘दिदीक्षित᳘स्योपत᳘पेत्पार्श्व᳘तोऽग्निहोत्रं जु᳘ह्वद्वसेत्स य᳘द्यगदो भ᳘वति सꣳसृ᳘ज्यैनं पुनरु᳘पह्वयन्ते य᳘द्यु म्रिय᳘ते स्वै᳘रेव त᳘मग्नि᳘मिर्द्दहन्त्य᳘शवाग्निभिरि᳘तरे य᳘जमाना आसत इ᳘ति तद᳘हैवा᳘हिताग्नेः क᳘र्म समानधिष्ण्या᳘स्त्वेव᳘ भवन्ति त᳘स्य त᳘देव ब्रा᳘ह्मणं य᳘त्पुरश्च᳘रणे ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Here they raise a doubt - “Let them have separate fire-places.” If one of the initiated priests becomes ill, he can separately perform Agnihotra. Later, if he becomes all right, the fires can be again mixed and he may be called upon to join (the Satra). If one of them were to die, he can be connected with his own (Agnihotra) fire. There is no need for a separate Pretāgni (fire for cremation) for him. Another Yajamāna will be continuing to attend to the sacrifice (and it will go on uninterrupted). Such is the performance in the case of one who keeps up his sacrificial fires; but indeed, they have their fire-places in common. The logical explanation for this is the same as for Puraścaraṇa.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दाहुर्य᳘त्संवत्सरा᳘य संवत्सरस᳘दो दी᳘क्षन्ते कथ᳘मेषामग्निहोत्र᳘मनन्तरितं भवती᳘ति व्रतेने᳘ति ब्रूयात् ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they ask - “When those who sit in the Samvatsara sacrifice (of Gavām Ayanam) enter into year-long initiation, how is it that their Agnihotra continues uninterrupted?” The reply to it is “Through the Vrata (fast-milk).”
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दाहुर्य᳘त्संवत्सरा᳘य संवत्सरस᳘दो दी᳘क्षन्ते कथ᳘मेषां पौर्णमास᳘ꣳ᳘ हविर᳘नन्तरितं भवतीत्या᳘ज्येन च पुरोळा᳘शेन चे᳘ति ब्रूयात् ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they ask - “When those who sit in the Samvatsara sacrifice (of Gavām Ayanam) enter into year-long initiation, how is it that their Pūrṇamāsa offering continues uninterrupted?” The reply to it is “By means of ghee and Purodāśa (offerings).”
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दाहुर्य᳘त्संवत्सरा᳘य संवत्सरस᳘दो दीक्षन्ते कथ᳘मेषां पितृयज्ञो᳘ऽनन्तरितो भवती᳘त्यौपासनैरि᳘ति ब्रूयात् ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they ask - “when those who sit in the Samvatsara sacrifice (of Gavām-Ayanam) enter into year long initiation, how is it that their Pitṛ-Yagña (sacrifice to the Pitṛs) continues uninterrupted?” The reply to it is “By the performance of Aupāsanas.”
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दाहुर्य᳘त्संवत्सरा᳘य संवत्सरस᳘दो दी᳘क्षन्ते कथ᳘मेषामामावास्य᳘ꣳ᳘ हविर᳘नन्तरितं भवती᳘ति दध्ना᳘ च पुरोळा᳘शेन चे᳘ति ब्रूयात् ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they ask - “When those who sit in the Samvatsara sacrifice (of Gavām Ayanam) enter into year long initiation, how is it that their Amāvasya offering continues uninterrrupted? The reply to it is “By curd and Purodāśa offerings.”
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दाहुर्य᳘त्संवत्सरा᳘य संवत्सरस᳘दो दी᳘क्षन्ते कथ᳘मेषामाग्रयणेष्टिर᳘नन्तरिता भवती᳘ति सौम्ये᳘न चरुणे᳘ति ब्रूयात् ॥ ७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दाहुर्य᳘त्संवत्सरा᳘य संवत्सरस᳘दो दी᳘क्षन्ते कथ᳘मेषां चातुर्मास्यान्य᳘नन्तरितानि भवती᳘ति पय᳘स्यये᳘ति ब्रूयात् ॥ ८ ॥

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they ask - “When those who sit in the Samvatsara sacrifice (of Gavām-Ayanam) enter into year long initiation, how is it that their Cāturmāsya sacrifice continue uninterrupted.” The reply to it is “By the Payasya (offerings).
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दाहुर्य᳘त्संवत्सरा᳘य संवत्सरस᳘दो दी᳘क्षन्ते कथ᳘मेषां पशुबन्धो᳘ऽनन्तरितो भवती᳘ति पशु᳘ना च पुरोळा᳘शेन चे᳘ति ब्रूयात् ॥ ९ ॥

</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दाहुर्य᳘त्संवत्सरा᳘य संवत्सरस᳘दो दी᳘क्षन्ते कथ᳘मेषां सोमो᳘ऽनन्तरितो भवती᳘ति स᳘वनैरि᳘ति ब्रूयात् ॥ १० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they ask “When those who sit in the Samvatsara sacrifice (of Gavām-Ayanam) enter into year long initiation, how is it that their Soma Yāga continues uninterruped”? The reply to it is “By means of the Savanas.”
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते वा᳘ एव᳘मेते᳘ यज्ञक्रत᳘वः संवत्सरम᳘पियन्ति स यो᳘ हैव᳘मेतां᳘ यज्ञक्रतूना᳘ꣳ᳘ संवत्सरे᳘ऽपीतिं वेदा᳘प्यस्य स्वर्गे᳘ लो᳘के᳘ भवति ॥ ११ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus the sacrificial rituals get merged in the Samvatsara (year long Gavām. Ayanam). He who knows thus the merger of the sacrificial rituals in the Saṁvatsara, becomes one who shares the heavenly world.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

संवत्सर᳘स्य सम᳘ता वेदितव्यै᳘कं ए᳘कं पुर᳘स्ताद्विषुव᳘तोऽतिरात्र᳘मुपयन्त्ये᳘कमुप᳘रिष्टात्त्र᳘यः पञ्चाशतं पुर᳘स्ताद्विषुव᳘तोऽग्निष्टोमा᳘नुपय᳘न्ति त्र᳘यः पञ्चशतमुप᳘रिष्टाद्विꣳशति᳘शतं पुर᳘स्ताद्विषुव᳘त उ᳘क्थ्यान्य᳘हान्युपय᳘न्ति विꣳशति᳘शतमुप᳘रिष्टादि᳘ति नु य᳘ उ᳘क्थ्यान्त्स्व᳘रसाम्न उपयन्ति ॥ १२ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The sequencial balance in the Saṁvatsara Satra is to be understood. They perform two Atirātras, one before and one after the Viṣuvat, fifty-three Agniṣṭomas before and the same number after the Viṣuvat. They also ob- serve hundred and twenty Ukthya days before Viṣuvat and another hun- dred and twenty after. This is the position in the case of those who observe the Svarasāman as Ukthyas.
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ᳘ येऽग्निष्टोमान्ष᳘ट्पञ्चाशतं पुर᳘स्ताद्विषुव᳘तोऽग्निष्टोमा᳘नुपय᳘न्ति ष᳘ट्पञ्चाशतमुप᳘रिष्टात्सप्तदश᳘ꣳ᳘ शतं᳘ पुर᳘स्ताद्विषुव᳘त उ᳘क्थ्यान्य᳘हान्युपय᳘न्ति सप्तदश᳘मु᳘प᳘रिष्टात्षट्पुर᳘स्ताद्विषुव᳘तः षोळशि᳘न उपय᳘न्ति ष᳘डुप᳘रिष्टात्त्रिꣳश᳘तं पुर᳘स्ताद्विषुव᳘तः षडहा᳘न्युपय᳘न्ति त्रिꣳश᳘तमुप᳘रिष्टादेषा हास्य सम᳘ता समे᳘न ह वा᳘ अस्या᳘ व्यृद्धेना᳘न्यूनेना᳘नतिरिक्तेना᳘यनेनेतं᳘ भवति य᳘ एव᳘मेतद्वे᳘द ॥ १३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Those who observe the Svarasāmans as Agniṣṭomas, perform fifty-six Agniṣṭomas before and fifty-six after the Viṣuvat, hundred and seventeen Ukthya days before and hundred and seventeen after; six Ṣoḍaśins before and six after; thirty Ṣaḍahas before and thirty after. This is the sequencial balance of that Saṁvatsara. He who knows thus performs the sacrificial ses- sion which is balanced and fruitful. It is neither defective nor repetitive. (Fifth Brāhmaṇa Ends.) (Chapter Three Ends.)
</details>
