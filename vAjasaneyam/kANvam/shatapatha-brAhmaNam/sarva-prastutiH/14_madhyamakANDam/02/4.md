## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

पु᳘रुषो वै᳘ संवत्सरस्त᳘स्य प्राण᳘ एव᳘ प्रायणी᳘योऽतिरात्रः᳘ प्राणे᳘न हि᳘ प्रय᳘न्ति वा᳘गेवारम्भणी᳘यम᳘हर्वाचा᳘ ह्यार᳘भन्ते य᳘द्यदारभन्ते ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The year indeed is Puruṣa. His Prāṇa is the Prāyaṇīya Atirātra; for it is with Prāṇa (men) move forward. The Ārambhaṇīya (the first day of the Caturviṁśa) is the speech; for it is by means of speech (men) start whatever they undertake.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अय᳘मेव द᳘क्षिणो ह᳘स्तोऽभिप्लवः᳘ षडहस्त᳘स्येद᳘मेव᳘ प्रातःसवन᳘मिदं मा᳘ध्यंदिनꣳस᳘वनमिदं᳘ तृतीयसवनं᳘ गायत्र्या᳘ आय᳘तने त᳘स्मादिय᳘मासाꣳ हृ᳘सिष्ठा ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Abhiplava Ṣaḍaha is this right hand (of the Puruṣa). Its first day is this (little finger). Its upper joint is its morning Savana; this middle joint is mid day Savana and this lower joint is its third (evening) Savana. It is in the place of Gāyatrī and hence this (little finger is the shortest among the fin- gers).
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

इद᳘मेव᳘ द्विती᳘यम᳘हस्त᳘स्येद᳘मेव᳘ प्रातःसवन᳘मिदं मा᳘ध्यंदिनꣳस᳘वनमिदं᳘ तृतीयसवनं᳘ त्रिष्टुभ आय᳘तने त᳘स्मादिय᳘मस्यै व᳘र्षीयसी ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This (ring-finger) is its second day. This upper joint is its morning Savana; this middle joint is its mid day Savana and this lower joint is its third (evening) Savana. It is in the place of Tṛṣṭhubh metre and hence it is longer than the little finger.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

इद᳘मेव᳘ तृती᳘यम᳘हस्त᳘स्येद᳘मेव᳘ प्रातःसवन᳘मिदं मा᳘ध्यंदिनꣳस᳘वनमिदं᳘ तृतीयसवनं᳘ ज᳘गत्या आय᳘तने त᳘स्मादिय᳘मासां व᳘र्षिष्ठा ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This (middle-finger) is its third day. This upper joint is its morning Savana; this middle joint is its mid day Savana and this lower joint is its third (evening) Savana. It is in the place of Jagatī metre and hence it is longer than the little finger.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

इद᳘मेव᳘ चतुर्थम᳘हस्त᳘स्येद᳘मेव᳘ प्रातःसवन᳘मिदं मा᳘ध्यंदिनꣳस᳘वनमिदं᳘ तृतीयसवनं᳘ विरा᳘ज आय᳘तने᳘ऽन्नं वै᳘ विराट् त᳘स्मादिय᳘मासा᳘मन्नादि᳘तमा ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This (fore-finger) is its fourth day. This upper joint is its morning Savana; this middle joint is its mid day Savana and this lower joint is its third (evening) Savana. It is in the place of Virāḍ metre. Virāḍ is indeed food and hence this (fore-finger) eats the maximum food among these fingers.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

इद᳘मेव᳘ पञ्चम᳘महस्त᳘स्येद᳘मेव᳘ प्रातःसवन᳘मिदं मा᳘ध्यंदिनꣳस᳘वनमिदं᳘ तृतीयसवनं᳘ पङ्क्तेरायतने पृथु᳘रिव वै᳘ पङ्क्तिस्त᳘स्माद᳘यमासां प्र᳘थिष्ठः ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This (thumb) is the fifth day. This upper joint is its morning Savana; this middle joint is its mid day Savana and this lower joint is its third (evening) Savana. It is in the place of Paṅkti metre. Paṅkti is big (with five feet). Hence this (thumb) is the thickest of these (fingers).
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

इद᳘मेव᳘ षष्ठम᳘हस्त᳘स्येद᳘मेव᳘ प्रातःसवन᳘मिदं मा᳘ध्यंदिनꣳस᳘वनमिदं᳘ तृतीयसवनम᳘तिच्छन्दस आय᳘तने त᳘स्मादय᳘मासां व᳘र्षिष्ठो गायत्र᳘मेतद᳘हर्भवति त᳘स्मादिदं फ᳘लकꣳह्र᳘सिष्ठꣳ स᳘ इ᳘तोऽभिप्लवः᳘ षडहः स᳘ इतः स᳘ इतः स᳘ इत᳘ आत्मा पृ᳘ष्ठ्य एत᳘द्ध स्म वै त᳘द्विद्वा᳘नाह पै᳘ङ्ग्यः प्ल᳘वन्त इव वा᳘ अभिप्लवास्ति᳘ष्ठतीव पृ᳘ष्ठ्य इ᳘ति प्ल᳘वत इव᳘ ह्ययमङ्गैस्ति᳘ष्ठतीवात्मनेति ॥ ७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This (right arm) is the sixth day. This (fore-arm) is its morning Savana; this (upper arm) is its mid day Savana and this (shoulder) is its third Savana. It is in the place of the Aticchandas (excess metre). Hence it is larger than those (fingers). That day is of Gāyatrī and hence this shoulder-blade is the shortest. This Abhiplave-Ṣaḍaha extends to these two hands and two legs and the Pṛṣṭhya is the body (trunk). This is what Paiṅgya who knew it told – "The Abhiplavas are floating about as it were and the Pṛṣṭhya is steady as it were." That is, he (the Puruṣa) leaps about as it were, with his limbs and is steady as it were with his body.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

शि᳘र ए᳘वास्य त्रिवृत्त᳘स्मात्त्रि᳘विधं भवति त्वग᳘स्थि मस्ति᳘ष्को ग्रीवाः᳘ पञ्चदशश्च᳘तुर्दश वा᳘ एता᳘सां करू᳘कराणि वी᳘र्यं पञ्चदशं त᳘स्मादेता᳘भिर᳘ण्वीभिः सती᳘भिर्गुरुं᳘ भार᳘ꣳ᳘ हरति त᳘स्माद्ग्री᳘वाः᳘ पञ्चदश उ᳘रः सप्तद᳘शोऽष्टा᳘वन्ये᳘ जत्र᳘वोऽष्टा᳘वन्य उ᳘रः सप्तदशं त᳘स्मादुरः सप्तदश उद᳘रमेकविꣳशो᳘ विꣳशतिर्वा᳘ अन्त᳘रूद᳘रे कु᳘न्तापान्युद᳘रमेकविꣳशं त᳘स्मादुद᳘रमेकविꣳशः पार्श्वे᳘ त्रिणवस्त्र᳘योदशान्याः प᳘र्शवस्त्र᳘योदशान्याः पार्श्वे᳘ त्रिणवे त᳘स्मात्पार्श्वे᳘ त्रिणवो᳘ऽनूकं त्रयस्त्रिꣳशो द्वा᳘त्रिꣳशद्वा᳘ एत᳘स्य करू᳘करान्य᳘नूकं त्रयस्त्रिꣳशं त᳘स्माद᳘नूकं त्रयस्त्रि᳘ꣳ᳘शः ॥ ८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The head (of the Puruṣa) is the Tṛvṛt Stoma. Hence it is threefold; skin, bone and brain. The neck is the Pañcadaśa Stoma; for there are fourteen joints (cervical vertebrae and dorsal vertebrae together) and the vital force is the fifteenth. Hence by means of that (neck), though it is small, man bears a heavy burden. Therefore the Pañcadaśa Stoma is the neck. The chest is the Saptadaśa Stoma; for there are eight jatru (rib-links) on one side and eight on the other and the chest itself is the seventeenth. Therefore the Saptadaśa Stoma is the chest( of the Puruṣa). The belly is the Ekaviṁśa Stoma; for there are twenty-Kuntāpas (spike-like projections of vertebrae in the stom- ach) and the belly (itself) is the twenty-first. Therefore the belly is Ekaviṁśa Stoma. The two sides (of the Puruṣa) are the Trinava Stoma – there are thir- teen ribs on the one side and thirteen on the other side. The two sides together constitute the twenty-seventh (Tṛṇava). Therefore the two sides are the Tṛṇava Stoma. The spine (of the Puruṣa) is the Trayastriṁśa Stoma. There are thirty-two Karūkaras (lumber-vertebrae) and the spinal append- ages together and the spine itself the thirty-third. Thus the Trayastriṁśa Stoma is the spine.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अय᳘मेव द᳘क्षिणः क᳘र्णोऽभिजिद्य᳘दिदभक्ष्णः᳘ शुक्लꣳ स᳘ प्रथमः स्व᳘रसामा य᳘त्कृष्णꣳ स᳘ द्वितीयो यन्म᳘ण्डलꣳ स᳘ तृती᳘यो ना᳘सिके विषुवान्य᳘दिद᳘मक्ष्णो म᳘ण्डलꣳ स᳘ प्रथ᳘मोऽर्वा᳘क्सामा य᳘त्कृष्णꣳ स᳘ द्विती᳘यो यच्छुक्लꣳ स᳘ तृतीयः ॥ ९ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This right ear (of the Puruṣa) is the Abhijit. That which is the white portion of the eye, is the first (set of) Svarasāman. That which is the black (portion of the eye is the second set of) Svarasāman. That which is the pupil (of the eye) that is the third Svarasāman. The nose (of the Puruṣa) is the Viṣuvat. This pupil of the eye is the first reversed Svarasāman the black in the eye is the second reversed Svarasāman and the white in the eye is the third reversed Svarasāman.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अय᳘मेवो᳘त्तरः क᳘र्णो विश्वजि᳘दुक्तौ᳘ पृष्ठ्याभिप्लवौ याव᳘वाञ्चौ प्राणौ ते᳘ गोआयु᳘षी अ᳘ङ्गानि दशरात्रो मु᳘खं महाव्रत᳘मुदान᳘ ए᳘वोदयनी᳘योऽतिरात्र᳘ उदाने᳘न᳘ ह्यु᳘द्य᳘न्ति स᳘ एष᳘ संवत्स᳘रोऽध्यात्मं प्र᳘तिष्ठितः स यो᳘ हैव᳘मेत᳘ꣳ᳘ संवत्सर᳘मध्यात्मं प्र᳘तिष्ठितं वे᳘द प्रतितिष्ठति प्रज᳘या पशु᳘भिरस्मिँ᳘ल्लो᳘केऽमृतत्वे᳘नामु᳘ष्मिन् ॥ १० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This left ear (of the Puruṣa) is the Viśvajit. The Pṛṣṭhya and Abhiplava have already been dealt with. The two downward Prāṇas (of the Puruṣa) are the Goṣṭoma and Āyuṣṭoma; the limbs (of the Puruṣa are the Daśarātra and the mouth is the Mahāvrata. The Udāna (up-breathing of the Puruṣa) is the Udayanīya Atirātra; for it is by means of the Udāna, men go upwards. Thus it is that year (year-long sacrifice) established in the human body. Whosoever knows thus the physical aspect of this year (sacrifice) gets himself estab- lished with offspring and cattle in this world and (gets established) in im- mortality in the world beyond. (Fourth Brāhmaṇa Ends.) (Second Chapter Ends.)
</details>
