## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दाहुः क᳘स्मादुभय᳘तो ज्योतिषोऽभिप्लवा भ᳘वन्त्यन्य᳘तोज्योतिः पृ᳘ष्ठ्य इ᳘तीमे वै᳘ लोका᳘ अभिप्लवा᳘ उभय᳘तोज्योतिषो वा᳘ इमे᳘ लोका᳘ अग्नि᳘नेत᳘ आदित्ये᳘नामुत ऋत᳘वः पृष्ठ्योऽन्य᳘तोज्योतिषो वा᳘ ऋत᳘व एष᳘ एषां ज्यो᳘तिर्य एष त᳘पति देवचक्रे वा᳘ एते᳘ पृष्ठ्य प्रतिष्ठिते य᳘जमानस्य पाप्मा᳘नं तृꣳहती प᳘रिप्लवते स यो᳘ हैवं᳘ विदु᳘षां दीक्षिता᳘नां पा᳘पकꣳ सत्रे᳘ कीर्त᳘यत्येते᳘ हास्य देवचक्रे शिरश्छिन्तो दशरात्र उद्धिः पृष्ठ्याभिप्लवौ᳘ चक्रे त᳘दाहुर्य᳘त्समे᳘ एव᳘ चक्रे भ᳘वतो᳘ऽथैते वि᳘षमा स्तो᳘माः कथ᳘मस्यैते᳘ समा स्तो᳘मा उ᳘पेता भवन्ती᳘ति य᳘देव षडन्यान्य᳘हानि ष᳘डन्या᳘नि तेने᳘ति ब्रूयात् ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they ask - "How come, the Abhiplavas have light (jyoti) on both sides and the Pṛṣṭhya has only on one side?" The Abhiplavas are these worlds and these worlds have light on both sides; Agni on this side and the Sun on the other side. The Pṛṣṭhya being the seasons, have light on only one side. The one who burns yonder (i.e. the Sun) is their light. Verily these two divine wheels established in the Pṛṣṭhyas revolve, crushing the sins of the Yajamāna. If anybody, knowing this, were to announce the sin of (talk ill of) the initiated priests, their heads will be chopped off by those divine wheels. The Daśarātra is the seat (of the chariot) and the Pṛṣṭhya and Abhiplava are the two wheels. On this the question arises - "The wheels (usually) are of the same dimensions; but how come, these two wheels are uneven?" Their Stomas being dissimilar, how do they come to be performed equally so as to coincide? (It is because) there are six days of one and six days of the other (i.e. since the number of days is equal, the dissimilarity in Stomas does not matter).
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

पृष्ठ्याभिप्लवौ त᳘न्त्रे कुर्वीते᳘ति ह स्माह पै᳘ङ्ग्यस्त᳘योः स्तोत्रा᳘णि च शस्त्रा᳘णि च स᳘ञ्चारयेदि᳘ति स य᳘त्सञ्चार᳘यति त᳘स्मादिमे᳘ प्राणा ना᳘ना स᳘न्त ए᳘कोतयः समान᳘भूति᳘मनु स᳘ञ्चरन्त्य᳘थ यन्न᳘ सञ्चार᳘येत्प्रमायुको य᳘जमानः स्या᳘देष᳘ ह वै᳘ प्रमा᳘युको᳘ योऽन्धो᳘ वा बधिरो᳘ वा न᳘वाग्निष्टोमा᳘ मासि सं᳘पद्यन्ते न᳘व वै᳘ प्राणाः᳘ प्राणा᳘ने᳘वैष्वेत᳘द्दधाति त᳘था स᳘र्वमा᳘युर्यन्ति त᳘थो ह न᳘ पुरा᳘युषोऽस्मा᳘ल्लोकात्प्र᳘यन्ति ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Let him make the Pṛṣṭhya and Abhiplavas woven in two wraps (so that they together become one). Let their Stotras and Śastras run together. By making them run together, these passages of the Prāṇas, though different, run together with one aim and merge into a common pattern (tapestry). If they are not made to run together, the Yajamāna is likely to have a mishap. One who loses the sight or hearing indeed suffers a mishap. There are nine Agniṣṭomas in a month; for there are indeed nine Prāṇas. Thereby he places only Prāṇas into them. Thus they reach the full span of life and they do not depart from this world before reaching the full span of life.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ए᳘कविꣳशतिरु᳘क्थ्याः द्वा᳘दश वै मा᳘साः संवत्सर᳘स्य प᳘ञ्चर्त᳘वस्त्रयो लोकास्त᳘द्विꣳशति᳘रेष᳘ ए᳘वैकविꣳशो य᳘ एष त᳘पत्येता᳘ मभिसम्प᳘दꣳस᳘ एत᳘या सम्प᳘दा मासि᳘ मासि᳘ स्वर्गं᳘ लोकꣳ रो᳘हति मासशः᳘ स्वर्गं᳘ लोकꣳ स᳘मश्नुत एकविꣳश᳘ञ्च स्तो᳘मं बृहतीं᳘ च छ᳘न्दश्च᳘तुस्त्रिꣳशदग्निष्टोमा᳘ मासि स᳘म्पद्यन्ते त्र᳘यस्त्रिꣳशद्वै᳘ देवाः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिश्चतुस्त्रिꣳशः स᳘र्वासां देव᳘तानामा᳘प्त्या ए᳘क उ᳘क्थ्यः षोडशि᳘मान᳘न्नं वा उक्थ्यो वी᳘र्यꣳ षोड᳘शी एते᳘न वै᳘ देवाः᳘ वी᳘र्येणा᳘न्नेन स᳘र्वान्का᳘मानाप्नुवन्त्स᳘र्वान्का᳘मानाश्नुवत त᳘थो ए᳘वैष᳘ एते᳘न वी᳘र्येणान्नेन स᳘र्वान्का᳘मानाप्नोति स᳘र्वान्का᳘मानश्नुते त᳘स्मात्पृष्ठ्याभिप्लवा उ᳘पै᳘वेयात्संवत्सरा᳘यदीक्षित᳘ एत᳘स्मै का᳘माय ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

There are twenty-one Ukthyās. A year has twelve months; there are five seasons and three worlds which together account for twenty and this one who burns(the Sun) is the twenty-first and this is the fulfilment. By this fulfilment, he month by month, ascends to the heaven and in monthly instalments he gains the heavenly world; the twenty-one Stomas and the Bṛhatī metre. Thirty-four Agniṣṭomas get completed month by month; for there are thirty-three gods and Prajāpati is the thirty-fourth. Since it gains all the gods, there is one Ukthya with Ṣoḍaśī (Stotra); for Ukthyā is food and Ṣoḍaśī is vitality. It is by means of this vitality and this food, the Gods at- tained all their desires and enjoyed all that they wished for. In like manner, this one (Yajamāna) achieves all his desires and enjoys all that he wishes for, by means of this vitality and this food. Therefore, one initiated for a year long Satra should proceed with the Pṛṣṭhya and Abhiplava together for attainment of that desire. ~
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थादित्या᳘श्च ह वा अ᳘ङ्गिरसश्चोभ᳘ये प्राजापत्या᳘ अस्पर्द्धन्त वयं पू᳘र्वे स्वर्गं᳘ लोक᳘मेष्या᳘मो वयं पू᳘र्व इ᳘ति त᳘ आदित्या᳘श्चतुर्भिस्स्तो᳘मैश्चतुर्भिः पृष्ठै᳘र्ल्लघु᳘भिः सा᳘मभिः स्वर्गं᳘ लोक᳘मभ्य᳘प्लवन्त य᳘दभ्य᳘प्लवन्त त᳘स्मादभिप्लवा᳘ अन्व᳘ञ्च इवा᳘ङ्गिरसः स᳘र्वैः स्तो᳘मैः स᳘र्वैः पृष्ठै᳘र्गुरु᳘भिः सा᳘मभिः स्वर्गं लोक᳘मस्पृशन्यद᳘स्पृशँस्तस्मात्पृष्ठ्योऽभिप्लवः षडहः ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now that Ādityas and the Angiras, both offspring of Prajāpati com- peted with each other saying – “We shall be the first to reach the heaven – we shall be the first.” The Ādityas crossed over to the heavenly world by means of four Stomas, four Pṛṣṭhas and simple Sāmans. Since they crossed over (Abhi-Aplavanta) with them, they are Abhiplavas. The Angiras followed them by means of all the Stomas, all the Pṛṣṭhas and complicated Sāmans, just reached the heavenly world. Since they just reached or touched (aspṛśan) it (six-days period) is called Pṛṣṭhya.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

षड्ढ्य᳘हानि भ᳘वन्त्यभिप्लवः᳘ पञ्चाहः प᳘ञ्च ह्य᳘हानि भ᳘वन्ति य᳘द्ध्येव᳘ प्रथमम᳘हस्त᳘दुत्तम᳘मभिप्लव᳘श्चतुरहश्चत्वा᳘रो हि स्तो᳘मा भवन्ति त्रिवृ᳘त्पञ्चदशः᳘ सप्तदश᳘ एकविꣳश इ᳘त्यभिप्लव᳘स्त्र्यह᳘स्त्र्यावृद्धि ज्यो᳘तिर्गौरा᳘युरभिप्लवो᳘ द्व्यहो द्वे᳘ ह्ये᳘व सा᳘मनी भ᳘वतो बृहद्रथन्तरे᳘ ए᳘वाभिप्लव᳘ एकाह᳘ एकाहस्यो हि स्तो᳘मैस्ताय᳘ते चतुर्णा᳘मुक्थ्याना द्वा᳘दश स्तोत्रा᳘णि द्वादश शस्त्राण्यति᳘यन्ति स᳘ सप्त᳘मोऽग्निष्टोम᳘ एव᳘मु स᳘प्ताग्निष्टोमाः सं᳘पद्यन्ते ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It is the Abhiplava of Ṣaḍaha since it consists of six days, or it is the Abhiplava of Pañcāha since it consists of five days, for the last day is the same as the first day. Or, it is the Abhiplava of Caturaha (four days) since there are only four Stomas (used in it); namely the Tṛvṛt Stoma, the Pañcadaśa Stoma, the Saptadaśa Stoma and the Ekaviṁśa Stoma (using three Stoma verses; fif- teen Stomās, seventeen or twenty-one Stoma verses respectively). Or it is the Abhiplava of Tryaha (three days Abhiplava) since it involves three turns, namely Jyoti, Go and Āyuṣ. Or it is the Abhiplava of Dvyaṅha (two day’s Abhiplava) because it involves only two Sāmans viz., Bṛhat and Rathantara or it is the Abhiplava of Ekāha (one day’s Abhiplava) since it is performed with the Sto- mas of one day’s (Soma sacrifice). Twelve Stotras and twelve Śastras of the four Ukthyās are in excess, which amout to a seventh Agniṣṭoma and thus there are seven Agniṣṭomas.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

प्रो᳘तिर्ह कौशाम्बेयः कौसुरिबिन्दिरुद्दालक आरुणौ ब्रह्मच᳘र्यमुवास त᳘ꣳ᳘ हाचा᳘र्यः पप्रच्छ कुमार क᳘ति ते पिता᳘ संवत्सरस्या᳘हान्यमन्येति ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Protr descendent of Kauśāmba (or hailing from Kauśāmbi) son of Kosuribindi lived with Uddālaka Āruṇi, as a student. The preceptor asked (the pupil) – "Son, how many days, did your father consider, that there are in the year ?”
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

दशे᳘ति होवाच द᳘श वा इ᳘ति होवाच द᳘शाक्षरा विरा᳘ड्वैराजो᳘ यज्ञः क᳘ति᳘ त्वेवे᳘ति ॥ ७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He (Protr) said, "Ten; ten indeed becasue the Virāḍ metre has ten syllables and the sacrifice is of the nature of Virād." (Again he asked) “How many (days) are there exactly?"
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

नवे᳘ति होवाच न᳘व वा इ᳘ति होवाच न᳘व वै᳘ प्राणाः प्राणै᳘रु यज्ञ᳘स्तायते क᳘ति᳘ त्वेवे᳘ति ॥ ८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He said, "Nine; definitely it is; for there are nine Prāṇas and it is in- deed by the Prāṇas, the sacrifice expands." (He again asked) "How many?"
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अष्टा᳘वि᳘ति होवाचाष्टौ वा इ᳘ति होवाचाष्टा᳘क्षरा गायत्री᳘ गायत्रो᳘ यज्ञः क᳘ति᳘ त्वेवे᳘ति ॥ ९ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He said, "Eight; definitely it is eight, because the Gāyatrī has eight syllables and the sacrifice is of Gāyatrī." (Again he asked) "How many?"
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

सप्ते᳘ति होवाच स᳘प्त वा इ᳘ति होवाच सप्त छ᳘न्दाꣳसि चतु᳘रुत्तरा᳘णि छ᳘न्दोभिरु यज्ञ᳘स्तायते क᳘ति᳘ त्वेवे᳘ति ॥ १० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He said, "Seven; seven indeed (he said), for there are seven metres, each having four syllables more than the previous one and the sacrifice gets spread by means of metres." (Again he asked) "How many?"
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

षडि᳘ति होवाच षड्वा इ᳘ति होवाच ष᳘डृत᳘वः संवत्स᳘रः संवत्सरो᳘ यज्ञः᳘ समान᳘मेतदहर्य᳘त्प्रायणीयोदयनीयौ क᳘ति᳘ त्वेवे᳘ति ॥ ११ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He said, "Six"; indeed it is six (he said); for there are six seasons for the year and the sacrifice is the year. The starting and concluding Atirātra are one and the same" (Again he asked) "How many?"
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

पञ्चे᳘ति होवाच प᳘ञ्च वा इ᳘ति होवाच पा᳘ङ्क्तो यज्ञः पा᳘ङ्क्तः पशुः प᳘ञ्चर्त᳘वः संवत्सर᳘स्य संवत्सरो᳘ यज्ञः᳘ समान᳘मेतद᳘हर्यच्चतुर्विꣳशमहाव्रते क᳘ति᳘ त्वेवे᳘ति ॥ १२ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He said, “Five”; "Of course five" he said. For, the sacrifice is fivefold and the Paśus are fivefold, the seasons for the year are five and the sacrifice is the year. The Caturviṁśa and the Mahāvrata are one and the same. (Again he asked) "How many?"
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

चत्वारी᳘ति होवाच चत्वा᳘रि वा इ᳘ति होवाच च᳘तुष्पादाः पश᳘वः पश᳘वो यज्ञः᳘ समान᳘मेतद᳘हर्य᳘न्पृष्ठ्याभिप्लवौ क᳘ति᳘ त्वेवे᳘ति ॥ १३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He said, "Four." "Indeed it is four" - he said; for the *Paśus* are quadripeds and the sacrifice is the *Paśus*. The *Pṛṣṭya* and *Abhiplava* are one and the same. (Again he asked) "How many?"
</details>


## १४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त्रीणी᳘ति होवाच त्री᳘णि वा इ᳘ति होवाच त्री᳘णि छ᳘न्दाꣳसि त्र᳘यो लोकास्त्रि᳘सवनो यज्ञः᳘ समान᳘मेतद᳘हर्य᳘दभिजिद्विश्वजितौ क᳘ति᳘ त्वेवे᳘ति ॥ १४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He said, "Three"; "Surely it is three", he said; for, there are three metres, three worlds, and the (Soma) sacrifice consists of three *Savanas* (sessions). The *Abhijit* and *Viśvajit* are one and the same. (Again he asked) "How many?"
</details>


## १५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

द्वे इ᳘ति होवाच द्वे वा इ᳘ति होवाच द्विपाद्वै पुरुषः पु᳘रुषो यज्ञः᳘ समान᳘मेतद᳘हर्यत्स्व᳘रसामानः क᳘ति᳘ त्वेवे᳘ति ॥ १५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He said, "Two." "Two indeed", he said, for, man is a biped and the sacrifice is *Puruṣa* and the *Svarasāmans* constitute one and the same day. (Again he asked) "How many?"
</details>


## १६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

एकमि᳘ति होवाचा᳘हरेवे᳘ति त᳘देतद᳘हरहरि᳘ति स᳘र्वꣳ संवत्सर᳘ꣳ᳘ सैषा᳘ संवत्सर᳘स्योपनिषत्स यो᳘ हैव᳘मेता᳘ꣳ᳘संवत्सर᳘स्योपनिष᳘दं वेदा᳘हास्माच्छ्रेयाञ्जायते सा᳘त्मा भवति संवत्सरो᳘ भवति संवत्सरो भूत्वा᳘ देवान᳘प्येति ॥ १६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He said, "One"; "Indeed a day", he said. for, the year is made of day after day. This is the mystic import of the year. Whosoever knows this mystic significance of the year becomes more prosperous and becomes possessed of a (new) body and gains the form of the year (itself). Becoming the year, he joins the gods. ( Second Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>
