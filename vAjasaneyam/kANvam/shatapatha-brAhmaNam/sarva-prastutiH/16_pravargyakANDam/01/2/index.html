He then takes up the Ādara plants saying “you are the might of Indra” - for when Indra absorbed (got united) with Viṣṇu, the essence of Viṣṇu who was so absorbed, flowed out and it lay there stinking. He said “Surely after bursting open (Ā-dar) this essence has uttered praises”; from there the Ādara plants grew. Because he lay there strinking (Pūy) they are also called Pūtikā and hence when they are placed on the fire as an offering, they blaze; for the same reason they also give out a sweet smell. This is because they originated from the essence of the sacrifice. In so far as Indra absorbed him with his might, he says “You are the might of Indra; I now complete the head of the sacrifice with you at a place of divine worship on the earth for the sacrifice; for the head of the sacrifice.” It has the same meaning.
