For whomsoever they take from the cart (the sacrificial food material) at its yoke they unyoke for him (saying), "Wherefrom we may yoke, there may we unyoke (release) ". Therefore this person (man) unites (yokes) and then releases (unyokes) and again unites (yokes). Having covered that way towards which he yokes (the draft-animal), finally he releases (unyokes) (it).
