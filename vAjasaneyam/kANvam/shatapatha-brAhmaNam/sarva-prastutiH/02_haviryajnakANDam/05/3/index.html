सर्वाष् टीकाः
C R Swaminathan

Whatever oblation they offer (pour into the fire), (they do so) only after having taken it up with ghee (smeared) on both sides, which (oblation) is therefore combined with the sacrificer. He who knows this to be so, does not depart from the sacrifice even though he leaves something undone (or, even though he does something that should not be done). (Third Brāhmaṇa Ends)
