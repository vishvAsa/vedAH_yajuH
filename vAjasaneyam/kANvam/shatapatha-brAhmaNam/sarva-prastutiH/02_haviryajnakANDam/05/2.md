## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वै᳘ समि᳘धो यजति प्राणा वै᳘ समि᳘धः प्राणैर्हायं पु᳘रुषः स᳘मिद्धस्त᳘स्माद्यादु- पतापिन᳘मभिमृ᳘श्येक्षस्वे᳘ति स या᳘वदुष्णः स्या᳘दै᳘वास्मिँꣳस्ता᳘वच्छ्ँसेतै प्राणै᳘र्हैव ता᳘वत्स᳘मिद्धः प्राणा᳘ने᳘वास्मिँस्तत्स᳘मिन्धे प्राणा᳘ने᳘वास्मिँꣳस्त᳘द्दधाति त᳘स्मात्समि᳘धो यजति॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He verily worships (pronounces the offering prayer for) *samidhs* (kindling sticks). Indeed the *samidhs* are the vital breath, for by the vital breaths is this person kindled (animated). Hence one (Adhvaryu) should say to him (the sacrificer) who is burning (with fear), after having touched him, "Behold!" (or "Touch yourself and then see!") When he is warm, then he may hope, for he is indeed kindled by the very vital breaths. Thus he kindles the very vital breaths (energies) in this (sacrificer). Thus he places the very vital energies in this one (sacrificer). There- fore he worships the *samidhs*.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ त᳘नून᳘पातं यजति रे᳘तो वै त᳘नून᳘पाद्यदा हि᳘ स्त्रिया᳘श्च पुंꣳस᳘श्च संतप्यते᳘ ऽथ रे᳘तः सिच्यते रे᳘त ए᳘वैत᳘त्सिञ्चति॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he worships *tanūnapāt*. Indeed *tanūnapāt* is the seed. When there is heat of the male and the female, then is the seed poured. He verily thus pours the seed.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अथे᳘ळो यजति यदा वै रे᳘तः सिक्तं᳘ प्रजा᳘यते᳘ ऽथेळि᳘तमिवा᳘न्नमिच्छ᳘मानं चरत्येत᳘दे᳘वैतद्रे᳘तः सिक्तं तत्प्र᳘जनयति॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he worships '*iḍs*'. When the seed that has been poured is born (as a living being), then it moves about, as though praised (prompted), wishing for food. Thus,indeed, the seed that has been poured produces (offspring).
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ बर्हि᳘र्यजति भूमा वै᳘ बर्हि᳘र्भूमा᳘नमे᳘वैतद᳘न्विमाः᳘ प्रजाः प्र᳘जनयति ता᳘ इमाः᳘ प्रजा᳘ भूमा᳘नम᳘नु प्र᳘जायन्ते॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he worships *barhis*. Verily *barhis* is 'abundance'. Thus he produces these offspring (creatures) verily after 'abundance'. These creatures arise after (follow- ing) abundance.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ स्वा᳘हा स्वाहेति यजति हेमन्तो वै᳘ स्वाहाकारः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिर्हेमन्तो᳘ हीमाः᳘ प्रजाः स᳘हसेव स्वं व᳘शमुपन᳘यते त᳘स्माद्धे᳘मनि म्ला᳘यन्त्यो᳘षधयः प्र᳘मुच्यन्ते व᳘नस्प᳘तीनां पलाशा᳘नि प्रतितरां᳘ पश᳘वो भवन्त्यधस्तरां व᳘याꣳसि पतन्ति वि᳘पतितलोमेव पापवर्णः पु᳘रुषो भवति स᳘हसेव᳘ हीमाः᳘ प्रजाः स्वं व᳘शमुपन᳘यते हेमन्तः स्वी᳘ ह वै तम᳘र्धं कुरुते श्रि᳘ये ऽन्ना᳘द्याय य᳘शसे य᳘स्मिन्न᳘र्धे भ᳘वति य᳘ एवं᳘ विद्वांन्त्स्वा᳘हा स्वाहे᳘ति य᳘जति॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he worships (chanting), "*Svāhā! Svāhā!*". Indeed the snowy season (winter) is the sound '*Svāhā*', (it is) Prajāpati. The snowy season (winter) brings these creatures under its control, as it were, by force. Therefore, in winter the plants wither, the leaves of trees fall off, the beasts retreat (shrink) further (and further) and the birds fly lower (and lower) and man, as with hair fallen off, becomes of evil (dark) complexion. By force (suddenly), as it were, does the snowy season bring these creatures within its control. He who, knowing thus, worships (chanting), “*Svāhā! Svāhā!*” makes that region wherein he is, his own for (conducive to) wealth, (good) food and glory.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अथ᳘ देवा᳘श्च ह वा अ᳘सुराश्चोभ᳘ये प्राजापत्या᳘ अस्पर्धन्त ते न᳘ व्यजयन्त दण्डैर्ध᳘नुर्भिस्ते᳘ होचुर᳘विजयमाना ह᳘न्त वा᳘च्येव ब्र᳘ह्मणि विजि᳘गीषामहा इ᳘ति स यो᳘ नो वा᳘चं व्या᳘हृतां मिथुने᳘न᳘ नानुनिष्क्रा᳘मात्स᳘ नः स᳘र्वं प᳘राजयातै स᳘र्वांभि᳘तरे जयानि᳘ति ते᳘ ह देवा इ᳘न्द्रमूचुस्तुर्व्या᳘हरे᳘ति॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The gods and the Asuras, both offspring of Prajāpati, contended. They did not (could not) conquer by means of staves and bows. Now attaining victory, they said, "Ah! May we desire to conquer verily in (by) speech, in (by) Brahman (Veda/ *mantra*)". "He who may not follow one pronounced speech with a pair may he be worsted and lose everything to us and may the others win everything. They, the gods, said to Indra, "Speak".
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ होवाचै᳘को ममेत्या᳘थास्मा᳘कमेकेत्य᳘सुरास्तत्त᳘न्मिधुन᳘मविन्दन्मिथुनꣳ ह्ये᳘कश्चै᳘का च॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He, indeed, said, "One (male) of me". Then the Asuras (said), "One (female) of ours". Thus they found a pair in it, for one (female) and one (male) certainly make a pair.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

द्वौ ममे᳘ति होवाचेन्द्रो᳘ ऽथास्मा᳘कं द्वे इत्य᳘सुरास्तत्त᳘न्मिथुन᳘मविन्दन्मिथुनꣳ हि द्वौ᳘ च द्वे᳘ च॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Indra said, "Two (males) of me". Then the Asuras (said), "Two (females) of ours". Thus they obtained a pair, for two males and two females are a pair indeed.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त्र᳘यो ममे᳘ति होवाचेन्द्रो᳘ऽथास्मा᳘कं तिस्र इत्य᳘सुरास्तत्त᳘न्मिथुन᳘मविन्दन्मिथुनꣳ हि त्र᳘यश्च तिस्र᳘श्च॥९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Indra said, "Three (males) for me". "Then three (females) for us" (said) the Asuras. Thus they got a pair, for three (males) and three (females) are a pair indeed.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

चत्वा᳘रो ममे᳘ति होवाचेन्द्रो᳘ऽथास्मा᳘कं च᳘तस्र इत्य᳘सुरास्तत्त᳘न्मिथुन᳘मविन्दन्मिथुनꣳ हि चत्वा᳘रश्च च᳘तस्रश्च॥१०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Indra said, "Four (males) for me". "Then four (females) for us" (said) the Asuras. Thus they found a pair, for four (males) and four (females) are indeed a pair.
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

प᳘ञ्च ममे᳘ति होवाचे᳘न्द्रस्त इ᳘तरे न᳘ मिथुन᳘मविन्दन्नह्या᳘त ऊर्ध्वं᳘ वाचो᳘ मिथुनम᳘स्ति ते प᳘ञ्च पञ्चै᳘त्येव स᳘र्वं प᳘राजयन्त स᳘र्वं देवा अ᳘जयन्त्स᳘र्वस्मादेनान्नि᳘रभजꣳस्त᳘स्मात्प्रथमे᳘ प्रयाज᳘ इष्टे᳘ ब्रूयात्॥११॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Five for me" said Indra verily. The others did not find a pair. For beyond this there is no pair for speech (male and female in numbers). Saying "pañca, "pañca" (five, five) (in number), they were defeated and they lost everything. The gods won everything of everything they (the gods) deprived them (of share). Therefore, when the first fore-offering has been performed, he should say.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ए᳘को ममेत्ये᳘कामुष्ये᳘ति यं᳘ द्विष्याद्य᳘द्यु नै᳘को ममै᳘का त᳘स्य᳘ योऽस्मान्द्वे᳘ष्टि यं᳘ च वयं᳘ द्विष्म इ᳘ति॥१२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"One (male) for me" and "One (female) for that person" (referring to by name) him whom he may hate. If there is none (whom he hates) (he should say), "One (male) for me, One (female) for him who hates us and whom we hate".
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ द्विती᳘ये द्वौ म᳘म द्वे त᳘स्य᳘ योऽस्मान्द्वे᳘ष्टि᳘ यं᳘ च वयं᳘ द्विष्म इ᳘ति॥१३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then, when the second (fore-offering) (has been made) (he should say), "Two (males) for me, two (females) for him who hates us and whom we hate".
</details>


## १४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ तृतीये त्र᳘यो म᳘म तिस्रस्त᳘स्य᳘ योऽस्मान्द्वे᳘ष्टि यं᳘ च वयं᳘ द्विष्म इ᳘ति॥१४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then, when the third (fore-offering) (has been made) (he should say), "Three (males) for me and three (females) for him who hates us and whom we hate".
</details>


## १५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ चतु᳘र्थे चत्वा᳘रो म᳘म च᳘तस्रस्त᳘स्य᳘ योऽस्मान्द्वे᳘ष्टि यं᳘ च वयं द्विष्म इ᳘ति॥१५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then, when the fourth (fore-offering) (has been made) (he should say), "Four (males) for me, four (females) for him who hates us and whom we hate".
</details>


## १६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ पञ्चमे प᳘ञ्च म᳘म न तस्यै᳘का चन᳘ योऽस्मान्द्वे᳘ष्टि यं᳘ च वयं᳘ द्विष्म इ᳘त्येव᳘ꣳ᳘ ह वाव᳘ द्विषतः᳘ सप᳘त्नस्य स᳘र्वं यज्ञꣳ सं᳘वृङ्क्त एवं᳘ द्विष᳘न्तꣳ सप᳘त्नꣳ स᳘र्वस्माद्यज्ञान्नि᳘र्भजति बहिर्धा᳘ करोति य᳘ एवं᳘ विद्वा᳘नेतज्ज᳘पति॥१६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then, when the fifth (fore-offering) (has been made), (he should say), "Five for me, not even one (female) for him who hates us and whom we hate". He who, knowing thus, meditatively repeats thus-he verily destroys the entire sacrifice indeed of his hateful rival. Thus he deprives his hateful rival of share from the entire sacrifice and keeps him out.
</details>


## १७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स य᳘द्देवा अ᳘ब्रुवन्ने᳘कोऽस्मा᳘कमि᳘ति म᳘नो हैव त᳘दूचु᳘रथ यद᳘सुरा अ᳘ब्रुवन्ने᳘कास्मा᳘कमि᳘ति वा᳘चं त᳘दूचुस्त᳘ एषां म᳘नसा वा᳘चमयुवतेष्टे हि त᳘स्य पु᳘रुषो य᳘द᳘व्याहरन्ना᳘सीत न त᳘स्येष्टे यदम᳘ना आ᳘सीत॥१७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When the gods said, "One for us" they verily then said (meant) 'the mind'. And when the Asuras said, "One (female) for us", they said (meant) 'speech', by that. They united the speech with their (gods) mind. When one may remain without speaking, a person, verily has power over him and when one remains absent-minded a person has no power over him.
</details>


## १८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘द्देवा अब्रुवन्द्वा᳘वस्मा᳘कमिति प्राणोदानौ त᳘दूचुर᳘थ यद᳘सुरा अ᳘ब्रुवन्द्वे᳘ अस्मा᳘कमि᳘त्यृक्साम᳘नी त᳘दूचुः॥१८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now, when the gods said, "Two (males) for us by that they said (meant) prāṇa (outgoing breath and udāna (the upward breath) and when the Asuras said two (females) for us, they said (meant) by that 'Ṛk and Sāman.'
</details>


## १९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘द्देवा अ᳘ब्रुवꣳस्त्र᳘योऽस्मा᳘कमि᳘तीमाꣳस्त ल्लोकानूचुरथ यदसुरा अब्रुवं- स्तिस्रोऽस्माकमिति त्रयीं तद्विद्यामूचुः॥१९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And when the gods said “Three (males) for us" they said (meant) these three worlds and when the Asuras said “Three (females) for us" they, by that, said (meant) the three-fold knowledge.
</details>


## २०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘द्देवा अ᳘ब्रुवꣳश्चत्वा᳘रोऽस्मा᳘कमि᳘ति पशूँस्त᳘दूचुर᳘थ यद᳘सुरा अ᳘ब्रुवꣳश्च᳘तस्रोऽस्मा᳘कमि᳘ति दि᳘शस्त᳘दूचुस्त᳘ एषां पशु᳘भिर्दिशोऽयुवत तस्माद्यां का᳘ञ्च दि᳘शं पशु᳘माने᳘ति जिता᳘मेवा᳘न्वेति॥२०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And when the gods said, "Four (males) for us", by that they said (meant) the cattle (beasts) and when the Asuras said "Four (females) for us", by that they said (meant) the (four) directions. They united the quarters with their (gods) cattle (animals). Therefore whatever quarter a man possessed of cattle goes to, he reaches it which has been verily conquered (by him).
</details>


## २१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘द्देवा अ᳘ब्रुवन्प᳘ञ्चास्मा᳘कमि᳘त्यृतूꣳस्त᳘दूचुस्त इ᳘तरे न᳘ मिथुन᳘मविन्दन्नह्यत ऊर्ध्वं᳘ वाचो᳘ मिथुनम᳘स्ति᳘ सैषा᳘ द्वेधा वि᳘भक्ता वाक्त᳘तोऽन्यतर᳘या देवा अ᳘जयन्नन्य᳘तरया᳘सुराः प᳘राजयन्त स य᳘या देवा अ᳘जयꣳस्त᳘या य᳘जमानो जयत्य᳘थ यया᳘सुराः᳘ प᳘राजयन्त त᳘यौस्य द्विषन्भ्रातृव्यः स᳘र्वं परा᳘जयते॥२१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now, when the gods said, "Five for us" they said (meant) the seasons. The others (Asuras) did not find a pair, for beyond that (number four) there is no pair (masculine and feminine) for speech (word indicating number). This expression is divided into two. The gods conquered by means of one of the two and the Asuras were vanquished by means of one (the other) of the two. The sacrificer wins by means of that (speech) by which the gods won. His hateful enemy (gets defeated and) loses everything by that by which the Asuras lost (were defeated). (Second Brāhmaṇa Ends)
</details>
