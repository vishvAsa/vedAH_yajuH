## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वै प्रो᳘क्षणीरादा᳘येध्म᳘मेवा᳘ग्रे प्रो᳘क्षति स᳘ समान᳘ एव प्रो᳘क्षणस्य ब᳘न्धुर्म᳘ध्यमे᳘वैत᳘त्करोति यत्किं᳘ च प्रोक्ष᳘ति॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Verily having taken the sprinkling waters, he sprinkles only the firewood first. The explanation (import) of sprinkling is verily the same. Whatever he sprinkles (with the sanctified water), he thus indeed makes sacrificially worthy (pure).
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स प्रो᳘क्षति कृ᳘ष्णो ऽस्याखरे᳘ष्ठो ऽग्न᳘ये त्वा जु᳘ष्टं प्रो᳘क्षामी᳘ति त᳘देन मग्न᳘येमे᳘ध्यं करोति॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He sprinkles, (chanting), “You are the black (antelope) that remains in its hair. I sprinkle you who are pleasing to Agni”. Thus he makes it (the firewood) fit for sacrifice (pure) for Agni.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ वे᳘दिं प्रो᳘क्षति वे᳘दिरसि बर्हि᳘षे त्वा जुष्टं प्रो᳘क्षामी᳘ति त᳘देनां बर्हि᳘षे मे᳘ध्यं करोति॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he sprinkles the altar (chanting), “You are the altar. I sprinkle you who are pleasing to the sacrificial grass seat”. Thus he makes it (the altar) sacrificially fit for the sacred grass seat.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थास्मै बर्हिः प्र᳘यच्छेति त᳘त्पुर᳘स्ताद् ग्रन्थ्यासा᳘द्य प्रो᳘क्षति बर्हि᳘रसि स्रुग्भ्य᳘स्त्वा जु᳘ष्टं प्रो᳘क्षामी᳘ति त᳘देनत्स्रुग्भ्यो मे᳘ध्यं करोति॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then they hand over the sacred grass to him. Having placed it (on the altar) with its knot in front (to the east), he sprinkles it (chanting), “You are the sacred grass (barhis). I sprinkle you who are pleasing to the sacrificial spoons”. Thus he makes this (sacred grass) sacrificially fit for the spoons.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ याः प᳘रिशिष्टाः प्रो᳘क्षण्यो भ᳘वन्ति ता᳘भिर्बर्हि᳘षो मू᳘लान्युपनि᳘नयत्य᳘दित्यै व्यु᳘न्दनमसी᳘तीयं वा अ᳘दितिरो᳘षधयो वै᳘ बर्हि᳘रस्या᳘ ए᳘वैत᳘त्पृथिव्या ओ᳘षधीनां मू᳘लान्यु᳘पोनत्ति त᳘स्माद्विमा᳘ आर्द्र᳘मूला ओ᳘षधयस्त᳘स्माद्यद्य᳘प्युत्तरार्धः शुष्को᳘ ऽथार्द्र᳘मूला ए᳘वास्या᳘ ह्येत᳘त्पृथिव्या ओ᳘षधीनां मू᳘लान्युपोनत्ति॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then, what sprinkling waters remain left over, with them he pours on the roots (bottom portions) of the sacred grass (chanting), “You are the moistening thing for Aditi”. This (earth) is indeed Aditi. The sacred grass is the plants. Thus he moistens verily the roots of the plants of this earth. Therefore, these plants are with moist roots. Hence, even though the upper portion is dry, even then they are verily with moist roots. He, therefore, moistens the roots of the plants of this earth.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ विस्र᳘ꣳ᳘स्य पुर᳘स्तात्प्रस्तरं᳘ गृह्णाति वि᳘ष्णो स्तु᳘पो ऽसी᳘ति यज्ञो वै वि᳘ष्णुरय᳘मु वै᳘ स्तुपो यच्छि᳘खा त᳘मे᳘वैत᳘द्यज्ञे᳘ दधाति स य᳘त्पु᳘रस्ताद्गृह्णा᳘ति त᳘स्मादयं᳘ पुर᳘स्तात्स्तूपः॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then, having untied (the knot), he takes at first (from the front portion) the ‘prastara’ (sacred grass cluster to be spread out), (chanting), “You are the crest of Viṣṇu”. Indeed, the sacrifice is-Viṣṇu. This crest (cluster on top) is what is his tuft. He (the sacrificer) verily thus places that in the sacrifice. As he takes it (the prastara) from the front, hence is this crest (top-knot) in front.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ संन᳘हनं विस्र᳘ꣳ᳘स्य प्र᳘कल्पयति त᳘था हास्य प्र᳘क्लृप्तꣳ स्त्री वि᳘जायते तद्द᳘क्षिणस्याःꣳ श्रो᳘णौ स्तृणाति नीवि᳘र्ह वा᳘ अस्यैषा᳘ दक्षिणत᳘ उ वा᳘ इयं᳘ नीविस्त᳘स्माद्द᳘क्षिणस्याꣳ श्रो᳘णौ स्तृणाति त᳘द्बर्हि᳘षाभि᳘च्छादयत्यभि᳘च्छन्नेव᳘ हीयं᳘ नीविः॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then, having loosened the bond, he arranges it (thereby is a female (child) definitely born to him). Thereby his woman brings forth a well-formed (off- spring). He spreads it over the right hip (portion of the altar). Indeed this is the waist-knot (girdle-knot at the navel) of this (sacrificer). This waist-knot is verily to the right. Therefore he spreads it over the right hip (portion of the altar). He covers it up with sacrificial grass. For, this waist-knot is, as it were, concealed.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स य᳘त्स्तृणात्ययं वाव स्तुपः᳘ प्रस्तरो᳘ऽथ यान्यतो᳘ऽवाञ्चि लो᳘मान्येता᳘न्ये᳘वास्य बर्हि᳘रेता᳘न्ये᳘वैत᳘द्यज्ञे᳘ दधाति यो᳘षा वा᳘ एषा यद्वे᳘दिस्तां᳘ दक्षिणतो᳘ देवा᳘श्च पर्या᳘सते य᳘ उ चेमे᳘ मनुष्यदेवा᳘ ब्राह्मणाः᳘ शुश्रुवा᳘ꣳ᳘सोऽनूचाना᳘ एते᳘ष्वे᳘वैनामेत᳘त्पर्या᳘सीनेष्व᳘नग्नां करोत्यनग्ना᳘ताया उ एव॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The reason why he spreads the sacred grass on the altar is (this). This prastara is the crest (tied up tuft). And the sacred grass (barhis) is verily these very hair that slope down from it. Thus he verily puts these in the sacrifice. Indeed, the altar (vedi) is female. To the right (south) of it do the gods, as well as these human gods, the Brahmins, who have studied the Vedas and who teach them, sit down. When these have verily sat down around it thus, he (the sacrificer) makes her non-naked (clad) indeed for only (securing) absence of nudity.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘द्वे᳘वैनाꣳस्तृणा᳘त्येता᳘वती ह वै᳘ पृथिवी या᳘वती वे᳘दिरो᳘षधयो वै᳘ बर्हिरो᳘षधीरे᳘वैत᳘दस्यां᳘ पृथिव्यां᳘ दधाति ता᳘ इमा ओ᳘षधयोऽस्यां᳘ पृथिव्यां प्र᳘तिष्ठितास्त᳘स्मादेनां बहुला᳘ꣳ᳘स्तृणीयाद्य᳘त्र᳘ ह्यस्या बहुल᳘तमा ओ᳘षधयस्त᳘दुपजीवनी᳘यतमं त᳘दु त᳘दाह᳘र्तर्य᳘धि त्रिवृ᳘त्स्तृणाति त्रिवृद्धि᳘ यज्ञो᳘ ऽथो प्रव᳘र्ह स्तृणन्ति बर्हि᳘रानुषगि᳘ति ह्यृ᳘विषाभ्य᳘नूक्तम᳘स्ति यद्वै᳘ प्रब᳘र्हं त᳘दानुष᳘गधरमूलꣳ स्तृणात्य᳘धरमूला इव᳘ हीमा ओ᳘षधयः प्र᳘तिष्ठिताः॥९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now, (the reason) why he spreads this (altar) over (with barhis) is; as much as is the altar so much is the earth, indeed. Verily the sacred grass (blades) are plants. Thus he puts the very plants in this earth. These plants are firmly established in this earth. Therefore one should spread over (cover) this (altar) thickly (with sacred grass). Where, of this (earth) the plants are most dense (abundant) that is most fit to subsist upon. But that (instruction) is only in the case of him who gathers them (the sacred grass blades). He spreads threefold, for verily three fold is the sacrifice. Then they verily spread (the holy grass) plucking up (lifting up) (the tops). It has been said by the Ṛṣi (seer) too, “They spread the barhis continuously (each touching (following) the one before)". What is prabarha' (plucking up) is that he spreads (the holy grass) continuously with the roots (placed) under. Indeed these plants are firmly established, as it were, with roots below.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ स्तृणात्यू᳘र्णाम्रदसं त्वा स्तृणामि स्वासस्थां᳘ देवे᳘भ्य इ᳘ति साधुं᳘ देवे᳘भ्य इ᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह यदाहो᳘र्णाम्रदसमि᳘ति स्वासस्थां᳘ देवे᳘भ्य इ᳘ति स्वास᳘दां देवे᳘भ्य इ᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह॥१०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He spreads (it) chanting "I spread you who are soft as wool and who are on a comfortable seat for the gods". In that he says, "Soft as wool". He verily says (means), "good (fine) for the gods". By (saying), "Who remains in a comfortable seat for the gods", he verily says (means) this, "Provider of comfortable seat for the gods".
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाग्निं᳘ कल्पयति शि᳘रो वा᳘ एत᳘द्यज्ञ᳘स्य य᳘दाहवनी᳘यः पूर्वार्धो᳘ ह्येष᳘ यज्ञ᳘स्य पूर्वार्धो हि शि᳘रो यज्ञ᳘स्यै᳘वैतच्छि᳘रः कल्पयति स᳘ उप᳘र्युपरि प्रस्तरं᳘ धार᳘यन्कल्पयत्ययं वाव᳘ स्तुपः᳘ प्रस्तर᳘ एत᳘मे᳘वैतच्छि᳘रसि प्र᳘तिदधाति त᳘स्मादयꣳ शि᳘रसि स्तुपः॥११॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now he arranges the fire. Indeed the Āhavaniya is the head of the sacrifice. Verily this is the fore-part of the sacrifice. Indeed the head is the fore-part (of the body). Thus he makes (arranges) the head verily of the sacrifice. He arranges it, holding the prastara (bunch of sacred grass) just over and above it. Thus prastara is the crest indeed. Thus he verily places this the top-knot on the head.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ परिधीन्प᳘रिदधाति य᳘त्र ह वा अ᳘ग्र देवा᳘ अग्नि᳘ꣳ᳘ हौत्रा᳘य प्रा᳘वृणतेदं᳘ नो हो᳘तैधीदं᳘ नो हव्यं᳘ वहे᳘ति॥१२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he places the enclosing sticks around, where verily the gods at first chose Agni for the function of Hotṛ, (saying), "Here be our Hotṛ. Convey this our oblation".
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ होवाच᳘ नैतदु᳘त्सहे य᳘द्धो हो᳘ता स्यां य᳘द्धो हव्यं व᳘हेयमि᳘ति त्रीन्पू᳘र्वान्प्रा᳘वृण्वं ते प्रा᳘धन्विषुस्तान्मेऽप्य᳘वकल्पयते᳘ति त᳘था वस्तदु᳘त्साक्ष्ये य᳘द्धो हो᳘ता स्यां य᳘द्वो हव्यं व᳘हेयमि᳘ति त᳘थेति ता᳘नस्मा अ᳘वाकल्पयꣳस्त᳘ एते᳘ परिध᳘यः॥१३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He (Agni) said verily, "I am not capable in that I should be your Hotṛ and in that I should convey your oblation. You chose three prior (conveyers). They passed away. Make them too (available) for me. Thereby I shall be capable for you in that I should be your Hotṛ and that I should convey your oblation". (They said), "So (be it)" and they procured them for him (Agni). They (those three prior Agnis) are these enclosing sticks.
</details>


## १४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ होवाच वषट्कारो वै तान्व᳘ज्रः प्रा᳘वृणगि᳘ति स᳘ वषट्काराद्व᳘ज्राद्विभेमि य᳘न्मा न᳘ प्रवृञ्ज्या᳘देतै᳘र्मा प᳘रिधत्ते᳘ति त᳘था मा व᳘ज्रो वषट्कारो न प्र᳘वृक्ष्यती᳘ति तथे᳘ति त᳘मेतैः प᳘र्यदधुस्त᳘त एनं व᳘ज्रो वषट्कारो न प्रा᳘वृणक्त᳘दग्र᳘य ए᳘वैतद्व᳘र्म नह्यति॥१४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He said, indeed, because the thunderbolt viz. Vaṣaṭkāra hurt them. I am afraid of Vaṣaṭkāra. Enclose me with these so that this thunderbolt, Vaṣaṭkāra, will not hurt me". (Saying), "So (be it)", they enclosed it with these. Therefore Vaṣaṭkāra, the thunderbolt did not hurt it. Thus does he (the sacrificer) bind (fix) this armour for Agni himself.
</details>


## १५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘ उ हैत᳘ ऊचुरिदं चे᳘न्नो यज्ञे᳘ युङ्क्तेत्य᳘स्त्वेव᳘ नो यज्ञे᳘ भाग᳘ एव᳘ नो यज्ञे᳘ भजते᳘ति तथे᳘ति होचुर्य᳘द्वर्हिष्परिधि स्क᳘न्दात्त᳘द्ध इ᳘ति य᳘द्व उप᳘र्युपरि होष्य᳘न्ति त᳘द्वो ऽविष्यती᳘ति त᳘द्धैनानवति य᳘देनानुप᳘र्युपरि जु᳘ह्वति त᳘द्धैनानवति य᳘दाहवनी᳘ये जु᳘ह्वति त᳘दु हैषां य᳘द्धविष्यरिधि स्क᳘न्दति त᳘स्मात्स्क᳘न्दन्ना᳘ग इव कुर्वीत ये वै ते प्रा᳘धन्वन्निमां वाव ते᳘ पृथिवीं प्रा᳘विशन्यद्वै किं च स्क᳘न्दत्य᳘स्यामेव तत्प्र᳘तितिष्ठति॥१५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Those (three prior Agnis) said, "If you thus employ us in the sacrifice, let there be a share verily for us in the sacrifice. Make us partake in the sacrifice". "So (be it)", they (the gods) said, "What may spill outside the enclosure, that is indeed yours, what they will offer (pour) upon you, that will satisfy you". What they offer (pour) upon these (enclosing sticks) that verily satisfies these. What they offer in the Āhavanīya and what spills outside the enclosure, that is theirs. Therefore, spilling, it (the oblation) may not be producing a sin as it were. Those whatever spills, that indeed remains stably in this very (earth).
</details>


## १६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दभि᳘मृशति भू᳘पतये स्वा᳘हा भु᳘वनपतये स्वा᳘हा भूता᳘नां प᳘तये स्वाहे᳘त्येता᳘नि वै ते᳘षामग्नीनां ना᳘मानि भू᳘पतिर्भु᳘वनपतिर्भूता᳘नां प᳘तिरि᳘ति त᳘दस्य य᳘था हुतꣳ स्वा᳘हाकृतꣳ स्या᳘देव᳘मेते᳘ष्वग्नि᳘षु हुतं᳘ भवति॥१६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That (what has been spilt) he touches, (chanting), “To the Lord of the earth—svāhā”. “To the Lord of the world—svāhā”. “To the Lord of beings—svāhā”. These viz. Bhūpati (Lord of the earth), Bhuvanapati (Lord of the world), Bhūtānāmpati (the Lord of the beings) are indeed the names of those fires. Thus, just as his offering (to the gods) would be made with svāhā, similarly this (spilt oblation) becomes (duly) offered into these (three prior) Agnis.
</details>


## १७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तान्है᳘क इध्म᳘स्यैव प᳘रिदधति त᳘दु त᳘था न᳘ कुर्याद᳘नवक्लृप्ता हास्यैते᳘ परिध᳘यो भवन्त्यभ्याधा᳘नाय᳘ ह्ये᳘वेध्मो य᳘स्यै᳘वैता᳘नन्या᳘नाह᳘रन्ति परिध᳘य इ᳘ति त᳘स्यै᳘वैते᳘ ऽवक्लृप्तास्त᳘स्मादन्या᳘नेवा᳘हरेयुः परिध᳘य इ᳘ति॥१७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Some place verily those (sticks) of the firewood around (as enclosing sticks). One should not do it so. For indeed, unsuitable are these of this (firewood) an enclosing material (for this) (Agni). For, the firewood is indeed for only placing over (the fire). Only these (sticks) of that (material) of which they bring others (sticks other than firewood) (saying), “(These are) enclosing things”—“(these (sticks) of that (material) alone are suitable (proper). Therefore, they should verily bring only others as enclosing materials”.
</details>


## १८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते पा᳘लाशाः स्युर्ब्रह्म᳘ वै᳘ पलाशो ब्र᳘ह्म वा᳘ अग्नि᳘रग्न᳘ग्यो हि त᳘स्मात्पा᳘लाशा अ᳘थो वै᳘कङ्कता बैल्वाः᳘ खादिरा औ᳘दुम्बराः कार्ष्मर्यम᳘या एते हि᳘ यज्ञि᳘या वृक्षास्त᳘ आर्द्राः᳘ स्युस्त᳘द्वैषां जीवं ते᳘न स᳘तेजस᳘स्ते᳘न वी᳘र्यवन्तस्त᳘स्मादार्द्राः᳘ स्युः॥१८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They (the enclosing sticks) should be of the Palāśa tree. Verily Palāśa is Brahman; Brahman indeed is Agni. Therefore the fires (the three prior fires serving as enclosure) one of Palāśa. Or (they may be, in the non-availability of Palāśa), of Vikaṅkata (tree), of Bilva (tree), of Khādira (tree), of Udumbara (tree), of Kārṣmarya (tree) (in that order). These are indeed trees fit for sacrifice. They should be green (fresh), for that is their life. By that they are bright, they are virile. Therefore they should be fresh (moist).
</details>


## १९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ मध्यमं᳘ परिधिम᳘ग्रे प᳘रिदधाति गन्धर्व᳘स्त्वा विश्वा᳘वसुः प᳘रिदधातु वि᳘श्वस्या᳘रिष्ट्यै य᳘जमानस्य परिधि᳘रस्याग्नि᳘रिळ᳘ ईळित इति॥१९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

At first he puts the middle enclosing stick (chanting), “May the Gandharva Viśvāvasu put (hold) you around for the security of the universe (all); you are the enclosure (fence) for the sacrificer. (You are) Agni, the invoked, worthy for invocation”.
</details>


## २०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ दक्षिणत इ᳘न्द्रस्य बाहु᳘रसि द᳘क्षिणो वि᳘श्वस्या᳘रिष्ट्यै य᳘जमानस्य परिधि᳘रस्याग्नि᳘रिळ᳘ ईळित इति॥२०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then at the south he puts the enclosing stick (chanting), “You are the dexterous (right) arm of Indra, for the security of the universe (all). You are the fence of the sacrificer. (You are) Agni the invoked, worthy of invocation”.
</details>


## २१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थोत्तरतो᳘ मित्राव᳘रुणौ त्वोत्तरतः प᳘रिधत्तां ध्रुवे᳘ण ध᳘र्मणा वि᳘श्वस्या᳘रिष्ट्यै य᳘जमानस्य परिधि᳘रस्याग्नि᳘रिळ᳘ ईळित इ᳘त्यग्ने᳘यो हि त᳘स्मादाहाग्नि᳘रिळ᳘ ईळित इति॥२१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then, (he places the enclosing stick) at the north, (chanting), “May Mitra and Varuṇa (twin deities) hold you up (place you) at the north with firm Dharman (Law) for the sake of the security of the universe (all); you are the fence of the sacrificer. You are Agni, the one worthy of praise, the one who is invoked (praised)”. They (the enclosing sticks) are verily the fires. Therefore does he say, “Agni the praise-worthy, the praised”.
</details>


## २२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ समि᳘धमभ्या᳘दधाति स᳘मिन्ध ए᳘वैनं त᳘या वी᳘र्यमे᳘वास्मिन्दधाति स᳘ मध्यमं᳘ परिधिम᳘ग्रे उ᳘पस्पृशति त᳘देतान्त्स᳘मिन्धे य᳘दग्ना᳘वभ्याद᳘धाति त᳘त्प्रत्य᳘क्षमग्निꣳ स᳘मिन्धे स᳘ एत᳘या गाय᳘त्र्याभ्या᳘दधाति वीति᳘होत्रं त्वा कवे द्युम᳘न्तꣳ स᳘मिधीमहि । अ᳘ग्ने बृह᳘न्तमध्वर इ᳘ति गायत्री᳘मे᳘वैत᳘या स᳘मिन्धे सा᳘ गायत्री स᳘मिद्धान्या᳘नि छ᳘न्दाꣳसि स᳘मिन्धे छ᳘न्दाꣳसि स᳘मिद्धानि देवे᳘भ्यो यज्ञं᳘ वहन्ति॥२२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he puts a samidh (kindling stick over the fire); he verily kindles this (fire). That way he indeed puts virility into it. He first touches the middle enclosing stick. Thus he kindles (symbolically) these (fires viz. the enclosing sticks). (The reason) why he puts the kindling stick into Agni is that he directly (visibly) kindles the fire. He puts (the samidh) with this Gāyatrī (chant), “May we kindle you the big one who invite (the gods) for the feast and who are possessed of brilliance, in the Adhvara (sacrifice) Oh Agni”. Thus he, with this, verily enkindles Gāyatrī. That Gāyatrī, having been enkindled, enkindles other metres. The enkindled metres convey the sacrifice to the gods.
</details>


## २३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ यां᳘ द्विती᳘यामभ्याद᳘धाति वसन्त᳘मेव त᳘या स᳘मिन्धे स᳘ वसन्तः स᳘मिद्धो ऽन्या᳘नृतून्त्स᳘मिन्ध ऋत᳘वः स᳘मिद्धाः प्रजा᳘श्च प्र᳘जनयन्त्यो᳘षधीश्च पचन्ति᳘ सो ऽभ्या᳘दधाति समि᳘दसी᳘ति समिद्धि᳘ वस᳘न्तो ऽभ्याधा᳘य जपति सू᳘र्यस्त्वा पुर᳘स्तात्पातु क᳘स्याश्चिदभि᳘शस्त्या इ᳘ति परिध᳘य इ᳘मे ऽभि᳘तो गुप्त᳘ये प᳘रिहिता भवन्ति सू᳘र्यमे᳘वास्यैत᳘त्पुर᳘स्तादभि गोप्ता᳘रं करोति ने᳘त्पुर᳘स्ताद्यज्ञं᳘ नाष्ट्रा र᳘क्षाꣳस्यवच᳘रानि᳘ति सू᳘र्यो हि᳘ नाष्ट्राणाꣳ र᳘क्षसाम᳘पहन्ता॥२३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then, the second kindling stick which he puts, with that he verily kindles spring. The spring (season), having been kindled, kindles the other seasons. The kindled seasons produce the creatures (progeny) and ripen the (produce of the) plants. He places (it) (chanting), “You are the enkindler”. Indeed spring is the enkindler. Having placed (it), he mutters, “May the sun protect you in the east from any curse”. These enclosing sticks are placed around (on three sides) for protec- tion. He thus makes the very sun the protector in the east, lest the destructive Rakṣasas should defile the sacrifice from the east. Verily the sun is the repeller of the destructive Rakṣasas.
</details>


## २४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ या᳘ममूं तृती᳘यामनुयाजे᳘ष्वभ्याद᳘धाति ब्राह्मण᳘मेव त᳘या स᳘मिन्धे स᳘ ब्राह्मणः स᳘मिद्धो देवे᳘भ्यो यज्ञं᳘ वहत्य᳘थ स्तीर्णा वे᳘दिमुपा᳘वर्तते त᳘स्या द्वे तृ᳘णे आदायो᳘दीची नि᳘दधाति सवितु᳘र्बाहू᳘ स्थ इ᳘त्ययं वाव᳘ स्तुपः᳘ प्रस्तरो᳘ ऽथेमे᳘ ए᳘वैते भ्रु᳘वौ स य᳘त्तिर᳘श्ची निद᳘धाति त᳘स्मादिमे᳘ तिर᳘श्च्यौ भ्रु᳘वौ क्षत्र᳘मु वै᳘ प्रस्तरो वि᳘शो बर्हिः᳘ क्षत्र᳘स्य च वा᳘ एते᳘ विश᳘श्च वि᳘धृती त᳘स्माद्वि᳘धृती ना᳘म॥२४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then, the third one which he places in the after-offerings, with that he verily kindles the Brāhmaṇa (the Brahmin priest). Having been kindled, that Brāhmin conveys the sacrifice to the gods. Then he (the Adhvaryu) returns to the altar which is strewn (with the holy grass). Having taken up two blades (of holy grass) from that (altar), he places them (with their tips pointing) to the north (chanting), “You are the two arms of Savitṛ”.This prastara (cluster of holy grass) is indeed the top-knot (tuft) (of the sacrifice) and these two (blades of grass) are verily the two eye-brows. (The reason) why he places them obliquely is that these two brows are slanting. The prastara is indeed Kṣatra (ruler) and the barhis is viś (common subjects). These two (blades of grass) are those who hold up the Kṣatra and viś separately. Hence they are known as Vidhṛti (the two that hold separately).
</details>


## २५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘त्प्रस्तर᳘ꣳ᳘ स्तृणात्यू᳘र्णाम्रदसं त्वा स्तृणामि स्वासस्थं᳘ देवे᳘भ्य इ᳘ति साधुं᳘ देवे᳘भ्य इ᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह यदाहो᳘र्णाम्रदसमि᳘ति स्वासस्थं᳘ देवे᳘भ्य इ᳘ति स्वास᳘दं देवे᳘भ्य इ᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाहा᳘ त्वा व᳘सवो रुद्रा᳘ आदित्याः᳘ सदन्त्वि᳘त्येते वै᳘ त्रया᳘ देवा यद्व᳘सवो रुद्रा᳘ आदित्या᳘ एते त्वा᳘सीदन्त्वि᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह त᳘देव᳘ सव्यः᳘ पाणिर᳘धि नि᳘हितो भवति॥२५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he spreads the prastara (over them) (chanting), “You, soft as wool, the one remaining in a fine seat for the gods, I spread”. (By) what he says “Soft like wool”, he verily says (means) this, “Good for the gods”. (By) “Remaining in fine seat for the gods”. He verily says (means) this, “One who provides a comfortable seat for the gods”. (He chants), “May the Vasus, Rudras and Ādityas reach you (sit on you) ”. These are verily three groups of gods viz. Vasus, Rudras and Ādityas. He verily says (means), “May these sit upon you”. Only then (after chanting this) the left palm is placed upon (it).
</details>


## २६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ जुहूं प्र᳘तिगृह्णाति ने᳘दिह᳘ पुरा᳘ नाष्ट्रा र᳘क्षाꣳस्यावि᳘शानि᳘ति ब्राह्मणो हि᳘ नाष्ट्रा᳘णाꣳ र᳘क्षसामपहन्ता॥२६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he takes up the juhū (thinking), “If not, the destructive Rakṣasas may enter here even before (at the outset) ”. The Brāhmin is indeed the repeller of the destructive Rakṣasas.
</details>


## २७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स प्र᳘तिगृह्णाति घृता᳘च्यसि जुहूर्नामे᳘ति घृता᳘ची हि᳘ जुहूर्हि ना᳘म᳘ सेदं᳘ प्रिये᳘ण धा᳘म्ना प्रिये स᳘दसि सीदेत्य᳘थोपभृ᳘तं घृता᳘च्यस्युपभृन्नामे᳘ति घृता᳘ची᳘ ह्युपभृद्धि ना᳘म᳘ सेदं᳘ प्रिये᳘ण धा᳘म्ना प्रिये᳘ स᳘दसि सीदेत्य᳘थ ध्रुवां᳘ घृता᳘च्यसि ध्रुवा नामे᳘ति घृता᳘ची हि᳘ ध्रुवा हि ना᳘म᳘ सेदं᳘ प्रिये᳘ण धा᳘म्ना प्रिये᳘ स᳘दसि सीदे᳘ति प्रिये᳘ण धा᳘म्ना प्रिये स᳘दसि सीदे᳘त्ये᳘वान्या᳘नि हवीꣳष्या᳘सादयति स य᳘दुप᳘रि जुहू᳘मासाद᳘यत्यध इ᳘तराः स्रु᳘चः क्षत्रं वै᳘ जुहूर्वि᳘श इ᳘तराः स्रु᳘चः क्षत्र᳘मे᳘वैत᳘द्विश उ᳘त्तरं करोति त᳘स्मात्क्षत्रि᳘यमुपर्या᳘सीनमध᳘स्ताद्वि᳘श इमाः᳘ प्रजा उ᳘पासते॥२७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He takes up, (chanting), “Known as juhū, you are fond of ghee, indeed”. Verily it is fond of ghee and indeed it is known as juhū. (He chants), “Such a one, you sit down here on your favourite seat, with a resort that is to your liking”. And then (he takes up) the upabhṛt (chanting), "Known as upabhṛt, you are fond of ghee, indeed". Verily it is fond of ghee and indeed it is known as upabhṛt. (He chants), "Such a one, you sit down here on your favourite seat, with a favourite resort". And then (he takes up) the dhruvā (chanting), "Known as dhruvā, you are fond of ghee indeed". Verily it is fond of ghee and indeed it is known as dhruvā. (He chants), "Such a one, you sit down here on your favourite seat, With your favourite resort". He places the other items of sacrificial food, chanting merely, "With your favourite abode, sit down on your favourite seat". (The reason) why he places the juhū above and the other spoons below is that the juhū is Kṣatra (ruler) and the other spoons are viś (subjects). Thus he makes the Kṣatra (ruler) above (superior to) the subjects. Therefore, these subjects, the viś (remaining lower down) below, wait upon the Kṣatriya who is seated (higher up) above.
</details>


## २८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ सं᳘मृशति ध्रुवा᳘ असदन्नि᳘ति ध्रुवा ह्य᳘सदन्नृत᳘स्य यो᳘नावि᳘ति यज्ञो वा᳘ ऋत᳘स्य यो᳘निर्यज्ञे ह्य᳘सदꣳस्ता᳘ विष्णो पाहि पाहि᳘ यज्ञं᳘ पाहि᳘ यज्ञ᳘पतिमि᳘ति तद्य᳘जमानमाह पाहि मां᳘ यज्ञ᳘न्यमि᳘ति त᳘दात्मा᳘नं᳘ नान्त᳘रेति यज्ञो वै वि᳘ष्णुस्त᳘स्मा ए᳘वैत᳘द्गुप्त᳘ये प᳘रिददाति सु᳘गुप्तमसदि᳘ति॥२८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he touches (chanting), "Firm they have sat". Firm they have sat on the lap of truth". Indeed the sacrifice is the lap (source) of truth. They have sat verily in the sacrifice. (He chants), "Oh Viṣṇu! protect them; protect the sacrifice, protect the lord of the sacrifice". That speaks of the sacrificer as, "Protect me the leader of the sacrifice". Thus he does not separate himself (from the sacrifice). Verily Viṣṇu is the sacrifice. To him (Viṣṇu) indeed does he thus hand over (these things) for protection (thinking), "May it be well protected". (Second Brāhmaṇa Ends)
</details>
