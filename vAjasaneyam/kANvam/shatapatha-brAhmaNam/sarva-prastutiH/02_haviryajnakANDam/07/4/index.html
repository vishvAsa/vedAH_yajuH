विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

ते᳓ वा᳓ एते᳓ उ᳓ल्मुके उ᳓दूहन्त्यनुयाजे᳓भ्यः स᳓ य᳓दे᳓ते उ᳓ल्मुके उ᳓दू᳓हन्ति यात᳓यामेव᳓ वा᳓ एत᳓दग्नि᳓र्भवति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः

They take out two burning sticks for the sake of after-offerings. Why they take out these two burning sticks is that (they think), “This fire becomes, as it were, spent out in time; for it had conveyed the sacrifice (offerings) to the gods. And why he makes the after-offerings is (this): Cattle indeed are the metres of the gods.
