सङ्क्षेपः

In the Kāṇva Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa of the Śukla Yajurveda there are 104 adhyāyas. Its promoter was the great sage Kaṇva. In it the subject matter is generally the same as in the Mādhyandina Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa, but a difference is seen in the mantras and in the order of the kāṇḍas. For example, the subject described in the second kāṇḍa of the Mādhyandina Śatapatha is the subject of the first adhyāya of the Kāṇva Śatapatha. Its division is as follows: there are 17 kāṇḍas, 104 adhyāyas, 435 brāhmaṇas and 6,806 kaṇḍikās. Its structure is systematic and extensive. In its subject matter too there are the ordinances of the principal sacrifices, the ritual performances, and philosophical exposition.
