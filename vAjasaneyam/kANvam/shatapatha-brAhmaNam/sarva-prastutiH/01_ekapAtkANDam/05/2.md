## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

वरुणप्रघासै᳘र्ह वै᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिः प्रजा᳘ वरुणपाशात्प्रामु᳘ञ्चता᳘ अस्यानमीवा᳘ अकिल्बिषाः᳘ प्रजाः प्रा᳘जायन्ता᳘थैतैः᳘ साकमेधै᳘रेत᳘र्ह वै᳘ देवा᳘ वृत्रं᳘ जघ्नुरेतै᳘नैव᳘ व्यजयन्त᳘ येय᳘मेषां वि᳘जितिस्त᳘थो वा᳘ एष᳘ एतैः᳘ पाप्मा᳘नं द्विष᳘न्तं भ्रा᳘तृव्यꣳ हन्ति त᳘थो एव वि᳘जयते स वै᳘ द्व्यह᳘मन्वहं᳘ यजते॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Prajāpati released the creatures from Varuṇa's noose indeed by means of 'Varuṇapraghāsas'. Those creatures of his were born without disease and without blemish. Now with these Sākamedhas —indeed by means of the Sākamedhas did the gods kill Vṛtra. Verily with these only they attained this victory which victory is theirs. In that manner, indeed, does this (sacrificer) destroy his evil hateful enemy by means of these (Sākamedhas) and verily similarly conquers (becomes glorious). He indeed performs (this sacrifice) every day, for two days.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ पूर्वेद्यु᳘रग्नये᳘ ऽनीकवतेऽष्टा᳘कपालं पुरोल्ला᳘शं नि᳘र्वपत्यग्नि᳘ꣳह वा अ᳘नीकं कृत्वा᳘ देवा᳘ उपप्रे᳘युः प्रात᳘र्वृत्र᳘ꣳ᳘ हनिष्य᳘न्तः स ते᳘जो ऽग्नि᳘र्माव्यथत त᳘थो वा᳘ एष᳘ पाप्मा᳘नं द्विष᳘न्तं भ्रा᳘तृव्यꣳ हनिष्य᳘न्नग्नि᳘मेवा᳘नीकं कृ᳘त्वोपप्रै᳘ति त᳘स्मादग्नये᳘ ऽनीकवते ऽष्टा᳘कपालं पुरोल्ला᳘शं नि᳘र्वपति॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

On the previous day (the first day) he offers an oblation (Puroḍāśa) on eight potsherds to Agni-Anikavat. For, the gods who would kill Vṛtra on the morning (of the morrow) went forward (towards Vṛtra), making Agni a sharp point (of weapon) (of making Agni the leader). That 'effulgence', Agni did not suffer (did not feel any pain). So also, this (sacrificer) too moves forward making Agni himself as a point (of weapon) (or a leader), in order to kill the evil hateful enemy. Therefore he offers a puroḍāśa on eight potsherds to Agni-Anikavat.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ मरु᳘द्भ्यः सान्तपने᳘भ्यो मध्यं᳘न्दिने चरुं नि᳘र्वपति मरु᳘तो ह वै᳘ सांन्तपना᳘ मध्यं᳘न्दिने वृत्रꣳ सं᳘न्तेपुः स सं᳘न्तप्तो᳘ ऽन्तरेवै प्रा᳘णान्प᳘रिदीर्णः शिश्ये त᳘थो वा᳘ एत᳘स्य पाप्मा᳘नं द्विष᳘न्तं भ्रा᳘तृव्यं मरु᳘त एव᳘ सांन्तपना᳘ मध्यं᳘न्दिने सं᳘न्तपन्ति त᳘स्मान्मरु᳘द्भ्यः सांन्तपने᳘भ्यो मध्यं᳘न्दिने चरुं नि᳘र्वपति॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then at mid-day he offers a caru (boiled rice) to the Maruts—Sāntapanas (Maruts the scorchers). For indeed the Maruts, being scorchers, scorched Vṛtra. Having been scorched, merely breathing in and breathing out, breathing heavily he lay, torn all over. In the same manner do the scorching Maruts scorch, at midday, the wicked hateful enemy of this sacrificer. Therefore does he offer caru, at midday to the Maruts-Sāntapanas.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ मरु᳘द्भ्यो गृहमेधि᳘भ्यो ऽपराह्ने᳘ पर्णशाख᳘या वत्सा᳘नपाकृ᳘त्य पवि᳘त्रवति संदो᳘ह्यैतं᳘ च᳘रु श्रपयति स᳘र्वो हि स᳘ चरुर्य᳘त्र᳘ क्व च तण्डुला᳘न्नाव᳘पन्ति मे᳘धꣳ ह वा᳘ एतं᳘ देवा᳘ आत्म᳘नि दधिरे प्रात᳘र्वृत्र᳘ꣳ᳘ हनिष्य᳘न्तस्त᳘थो वा᳘ एष᳘ पाप्मा᳘नं द्विष᳘न्तं भ्रातृ᳘व्यँ हनिष्य᳘न्नात्म᳘नि मे᳘धं धत्ते स य᳘त्क्षीरौदनो भ᳘वति मे᳘धो वै प᳘यो मे᳘धस्तण्डुला उभ᳘यमे᳘वैतन्मे᳘धमात्म᳘नि धत्ते॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then (he offers a caru) to Marut-Gṛhamedhins (Maruts the Householders) in the afternoon. Having removed the calves with a leafy turf and having drawn the milk into a vessel that has a purifying (device) (tuft of sacrificial grass), he heats this caru over a fire. All that is indeed a caru wherein they put rice grains (for cooking). About to kill Vṛtra on the (following) morning, the gods took (consumed) this nourishment within themselves. In that manner, indeed, this sacrificer (too) who is about to destroy his wicked hateful enemy, takes into himself nourishment in that he has milkfood. Indeed milk is nourishment; nourishment are grains of rice. He (the sacrificer) takes into himself verily this twofold nourishment.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘स्य द्व᳘य्यावृ᳘त्सैव वे᳘दिः स्तीर्णा᳘ भवति परिधी᳘ꣳ᳘श्चैव श᳘कलाꣳश्चोपनि᳘दधति स्रुवं᳘ चो एव स्रु᳘चं च सं᳘म्मार्ष्ट्य᳘थ द्वे पिशी᳘ले वा पा᳘त्र्यौ वा निर्णि᳘ज्य त᳘योरैतद्द्वे᳘धौदनमु᳘द्धरति त᳘योः सर्पिरासे᳘चनं कृत्वा᳘ सर्पि᳘रासि᳘च्य ता᳘वादा᳘योदा᳘द्रवति स ता᳘मेव वेदि᳘ꣳ᳘ स्तीर्णा᳘मभिमृ᳘श्य परिधी᳘न्परिधा᳘य या᳘वतः काम᳘यते श᳘कलाꣳस्ता᳘वतो ऽभ्या᳘दधात्या᳘सादयत्येता᳘वोदनौ सी᳘दति हो᳘ता होतृष᳘दने स्रुवं᳘ च स्रु᳘चं चाभिप᳘द्यमान आह॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For it there is a twofold method (two methods) of performance. The same altar is strewn with a covering (of sacrificial grass). They place the enclosing sticks and pieces (of wood) near it. He verily wipes clean the dipping spoon and the offering spoon. Having washed two small plates or two dishes, he takes this cooked rice in two parts in them. Having made a depression in those two (dishes) for pouring ghee and having poured ghee and having taken them (the two vessels) up, he rushes to that very altar. Having touched that altar covered (with sacrificial grass) and having placed the enclosing sticks around (the fire), he puts (into the fire) as many sticks as he likes. He places the (vessels of) two rice foods down. The Hotr sits on the Hotr's seat. Taking up the dipping spoon and the offering spoon (The Adhvaryu) says.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अग्नये᳘ऽनुब्रूही᳘त्याग्नेयमा᳘ज्यभाग᳘ꣳ सोऽस्य द᳘क्षिणस्यौदन᳘स्य चतुरा᳘ज्यस्या᳘वद्यत्यतिक्र᳘म्याश्राच्या᳘हाग्निं᳘ यजे᳘ति व᳘षट्कृते जुहोति॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Make the (invitation) call for Agni", with reference to Agni's share of the ghee. He cuts up (and takes) four times; of the ghee of the southern rice-food; having stepped beyond and having called attention, he says, "Worship (pronounce the offering prayer for) Agni". When Vaṣat has been uttered, he pours the libation (into the fire).
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

सो᳘माया᳘नुब्रूही᳘त्याह सौम्यमा᳘ज्यभाग᳘ꣳ᳘ सो ऽस्यो᳘त्तरस्यौदन᳘स्य चतुरा᳘ज्यस्या᳘वद्यत्यतिक्र᳘म्याश्रा᳘व्याह सो᳘मं यजे᳘ति व᳘षट्कृते जुहोति॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He says, "Make the call (of invitation) for Soma", about Soma's share of the ghee. He cuts up (and takes) four times, of the ghee of the northern rice-food. Having stepped beyond and having called attention he says, "Worship Soma". When Vaṣat has been pronounced, he makes the offering.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाह मरु᳘द्भ्यो गृहमेधिभ्यो᳘ ऽनुब्रूही᳘ति᳘ सोऽस्य द᳘क्षिणस्यौदन᳘स्य त᳘त एवा᳘ज्यमु᳘पस्तृणीते त᳘तो द्विर᳘वद्यत्यभि᳘घारयति प्र᳘त्यनक्त्यवदा᳘ने अतिक्र᳘म्याश्रा᳘व्याह मरु᳘तो गृहमेधि᳘नो यजे᳘ति व᳘षट्कृते जुहोति॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he says, "Make the call (of invitation) for Marut-Grhamedhins". He spreads (covers) the southern rice-food with the ghee from itself, makes two cuttings from it, sprinkles it with ghee but does not smear it over the two cut up (portions). Having stepped across and having called attention, he says, "Worship the Marut-Grhamedhins". He makes the offering after Vaṣat has been pronounced.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाग्न᳘ये स्विष्टकृते᳘ ऽनुब्रूही᳘ति᳘ सो ऽस्यो᳘त्तरस्यौदन᳘स्य त᳘त एवा᳘ज्यमु᳘पस्तृणीते त᳘तो द्विर᳘वद्यत्यभि᳘घारयति न प्र᳘त्यनक्त्यवदा᳘ने अतिक्र᳘म्याश्रा᳘व्याहाग्नि᳘ꣳ स्विष्टकृ᳘तं यजे᳘ति व᳘षट्कृते जुहोती᳘ल्लामे᳘वावद्य᳘ति न᳘ प्राशित्र᳘मेतन्न्वे᳘कमय᳘नम्॥९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he says, "Make the (invitation) call for Agni-Sviṣṭakrī". He spreads (covers) the northern rice-food with the ghee from itself, makes two cuttings from it, sprinkles it with ghee (but) does not smear the two cut up (portions); having stepped beyond and having called attention, he says, "Worship Agni-Sviṣṭakrī". When Vaṣat has been pronounced, he makes the offering. He cuts up only Idā and not Prāsitra. (the fore-portion that belongs to Brahmaw). This, indeed, is one way (of performance).
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ द्विती᳘य᳘ꣳ सैव वे᳘दि स्तीर्णा᳘ भवति परिधी᳘ꣳश्चैव श᳘कलाꣳश्चोपनि᳘दधति स्रु᳘वं चो एव स्रु᳘चं च सं᳘म्मार्ष्टि त᳘थो एवैतं᳘ पवि᳘त्रवति संन्दो᳘ह्य चरु᳘ꣳ᳘ श्रपयत्यम्यर्ध आ᳘ज्यꣳ स्थाल्याम᳘धिश्रयति तं᳘ यदा᳘ शृतं म᳘न्यते᳘ ऽथैनमभि᳘घार्योद्वासयति त᳘मुद्वा᳘स्यानक्ति तꣳ सो᳘खमे᳘वादा᳘योदा᳘द्रवति स ता᳘मेव वे᳘दिꣳ स्तीर्णा᳘मभिमृ᳘श्य परिधी᳘न्परिधा᳘य या᳘वतः काम᳘यते श᳘कलाꣳस्ता᳘वतो ऽभ्या᳘दधात्या᳘सादयत्येतꣳ सो᳘खमे᳘वाभ्यर्ध आ᳘ज्यमा᳘सादयति सी᳘दति हो᳘ता होतृष᳘दने स्रुवं᳘ च स्रु᳘चं चाभिप᳘द्यमान आह॥१०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now the second (mode of performance):— That very altar becomes covered (with sacrificial grass). They place the enclosing sticks and pieces (of wood) nearby. He wipes clean the dipping spoon and also the offering spoon. In the same way (as before) having malked in (a vessel) that has a purifier (bunch of sacred grass), he cooks the corn. Nearby he heats up the ghee in a pot. When he thinks it has been cooked, then he removes it after having sprinkled (ghee over) it. Having removed it, he smears it (with ghee). Having taken it up along with the salt earth (smeared on the outer underside of the pot), he hastens (towards) the altar. Having touched that very altar that has been covered (with holy grass) and having placed the enclosing sticks around (the altar), he places (in the fire) as many pieces (of wood) as he likes. He places down this food with the vessel, with the (smeared) earth (on the outside bottom of the vessel). He places the (pot of) ghee down nearby. The Hotr sits on the Hotr's seat. (The Adhvaryu), taking up the dipping spoon and offering spoon, says.
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अग्नये᳘ ऽनुब्रूही᳘त्याग्नेयमा᳘ज्यभाग᳘ꣳ सो ऽस्याः᳘ स्थाल्या᳘श्चतुरा᳘ज्यस्या᳘वद्यत्यतिक्र᳘म्याश्रा᳘व्याहाग्निं᳘ यजे᳘ति व᳘षट्कृते जुहोति॥११॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Make the (invitation) call for Agni", with reference to Agni's share of ghee. He cuts up (and takes four times from the ghee in the pot. Having stepped beyond and having called attention, he says, "Worship Agni". When Vaṣat has been pronounced, he makes the offering.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

सो᳘माया᳘नुब्रूही᳘त्याह सौम्यमा᳘ज्यभाग᳘ꣳ᳘ सो ऽस्याः᳘ स्थाल्या᳘श्चतुरा᳘ज्यस्या᳘वद्यत्यतिक्र᳘म्याश्रा᳘व्याह सो᳘मं यजे᳘ति व᳘षट्कृते जुहोति॥१२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He says, "Make the call (of invitation) for Soma", regarding Soma's share of the ghee. He cuts up (and takes) four times, of the ghee from the pot. Having stepped beyond and having made the call for attention he says, "Worship Soma". He makes the offering after Vaṣat has been pronounced.
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाह मरु᳘द्भ्यो गृहमेधिभ्यो᳘ ऽनुब्रूहीत्यु᳘पस्तृणीत आ᳘ज्यम᳘थास्य᳘ चरोर्द्विर᳘वद्यत्यभि᳘घारयति प्र᳘त्यनक्त्यवदा᳘ने अतिक्र᳘म्याश्रा᳘व्याह मरु᳘तो गृहमेधि᳘नो यजे᳘ति व᳘षट्कृते जुहोति॥१३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he says, "Make the call (of invitation) for Marut-Grhamedhins". He spreads ghee (in the offering spoon). Then he cuts up (and takes) two times of the caru. He sprinkles ghee (over it) and smears the two cut up portions (with ghee) (reanoints that part from where the cuttings have been made). Having stepped beyond and having called attention, he says, "worship the Marut-Grhamedhins". After Vaṣat has been uttered, he makes the offering.
</details>


## १४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाहाग्न᳘ये स्विष्टकृते᳘ ऽनुब्रूहीत्यु᳘पस्तृणीत आ᳘ज्यम᳘थास्य᳘ चरोः᳘ सकृद᳘वद्यति द्वि᳘रभि᳘घारयति न प्र᳘त्यनक्त्यवदा᳘नमतिक्र᳘म्याश्रा᳘व्याहाग्नि᳘ꣳ स्विष्टकृ᳘तं यजे᳘ति व᳘षट्कृते जुहोती᳘ळामे᳘वावद्य᳘ति न᳘ प्राशित्रम्॥१४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he says, "Make the call (of invitation) for Agni-Sviṣṭakṛt". He spreads ghee (in the offering spoon) and cuts up once from the caru; twice he sprinkles ghee (over it) (but) does not smear (does not anoint the part where from the cutting has been made) the cutting. Having stepped beyond and having called attention he says, "Worship Agni-Sviṣṭakṛt". When Vaṣat has been pronounced he makes the offering. He cuts up only the Iḍā and not the Prāśāra.
</details>


## १५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते या᳘वतो गृ᳘ह्या हवि᳘ष्यशाः स्युस्ते᳘ ऽस्यौदन᳘स्याश्नीयुर᳘थो ऋत्विजो᳘ ऽथो अन्ये᳘ ब्राह्मणा य᳘दि बहु स्याद᳘थेमाम᳘निरशितामेव᳘ कुम्भी᳘मपिधा᳘य नि᳘दधति प्रातः᳘ पूर्णदर्वा᳘य सम᳘वर्त्तन्ति वत्साꣳस्त᳘त्पश᳘व आत्म᳘नि मे᳘धं दधते॥१५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

As many members of the household as are (entitled) eaters of (the remainder of) the sacrificial food, may partake of this food; then the sacrificial priests and then other Brāhmins if there is much (food). Without its being made empty, they put the pot away after having covered it (with a lid). In the morning for the 'Pūrṇadarva' (full-spoon) ritual, they gather the calves together. Thereby the cattle acquire nourishment in themselves (in their body).
</details>


## १६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

यवा᳘न्वैताꣳ रा᳘त्रिमग्निहोत्रं᳘ जुहोति निवा᳘न्याया वत्सं᳘ यमयन्ति प्रातः᳘ पितृयज्ञाया᳘थ प्रात᳘र्हुते वा᳘हुते वाग्निहोत्रे᳘ यतर᳘था काम᳘येतास्या अ᳘निरशितायाः कुम्भ्याः᳘ पूर्णदर्वमु᳘पहन्ति पूर्णा᳘ दर्वि प᳘रापत सु᳘पूर्णा पु᳘नरा᳘पत । व᳘स्नेव वि᳘क्रीणावहा इ᳘षमू᳘र्जꣳ शतक्रतवि᳘ति य᳘था पुरोऽनुवा᳘क्यैव᳘मे᳘वैत᳘यै᳘वैनमेत᳘स्मै भागा᳘य ह्वयति॥१६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This night he performs Agnihotra with rice grael. In the morning they tie up the calf of a 'nivāmya' (cow suckling a calf other than its own), for (performing) the offering to the fathers (manes). Then in the morning, whether Agnihotra has been performed or not, (i.e. before or after the performance of Agnihotra), he cuts up a spoonful from this unemptied pot, whichever (portion) he pleases (saying), "Oh Spoon! Go away full. Come back well filled. Oh Indra (of a hundred sacrifices) let us, (you and me) exchange (sell to each other) sustenance and strength as for price. This is verily just as is the puroniudhya (prayer of invitation). With this (call), indeed, he (the sacrificer) invites him (Indra) for this share.
</details>


## १७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थर्षभमा᳘ह्वंयति स य᳘दि रुयात्स᳘ वषट्कार᳘ एतद्वा इ᳘न्द्रस्य रूपं य᳘दृषभ᳘स्य स्वे᳘नै᳘वैनमेत᳘द्रूपे᳘णैत᳘स्मै भागायैते᳘न भागे᳘न वृत्र᳘स्य वधा᳘य ह्वयति स य᳘दि रुयाद᳘थ विद्यादा᳘ म इ᳘न्द्रो याष᳘गन्त्से᳘न्द्रो मे यज्ञ इ᳘ति य᳘द्यु न᳘ रूयाद᳘पि ब्रह्मणा᳘ एव᳘ दक्षिणत आम᳘ब्रूयाज्जुहुधी᳘ति᳘ सो ए᳘वैन्द्री वाक्॥१७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then they call up a bull. If he should bellow, that (bellowing) is the Vaṣaṭkāra (permissive utterance for offering). What is the form of the bull, this verily is the form of Indra. (Thus) he (the sacrificer) calls this (Indra) with his own form for (accepting) this share and for killing Vṛtra with this share. If (the bull) should bellow, he (the sacrificer) should understand, "Indra has come to my sacrifice. My sacrifice has Indra (is with Indra)" If (the bull) may not bellow, even then a Brāhmin (priest) seated to the south may say, "Perform the Homa". That verily is the expression (voice) of Indra.
</details>


## १८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ जुहोति देहि᳘ मे द᳘दामि ते नि᳘ मे धेहि नि᳘ ते दधे । निहा᳘रं नि᳘हरामि ते निहा᳘रं नि᳘हरामि मे स्वाहेति॥१८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He makes the offering (chanting), "Give to me, I give to you. Bestow on me, I bestow on you. I offer to you a present. You give me a present— Srāhā".
</details>


## १९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ मरु᳘द्भ्यः क्रीळि᳘भ्यः सप्त᳘कपालं पुरोळा᳘शं नि᳘र्वपति मरु᳘तो ह वै क्रीळि᳘न इ᳘न्द्रं वृत्रं᳘ꣳ᳘ हनिष्य᳘न्तमा᳘गतमभि᳘तो मह᳘यन्तः प᳘रिचिक्रीळुस्त᳘थौ वा एतं᳘ पाप्मा᳘नं द्विष᳘न्तं भ्रा᳘तृव्यंꣳ हनिष्य᳘न्तं मरु᳘त एव᳘ क्रीळि᳘नो ऽभि᳘तो महयन्तः प᳘रिक्रीळन्ति त᳘स्मान्मरु᳘द्भ्यः क्रीळि᳘भ्यः सप्त᳘कपालं पुरोळा᳘शं नि᳘र्वपति स य᳘त्सप्त᳘कपालो भ᳘वति सप्त᳘ सप्त हि᳘ मारुतो᳘ गणः॥१९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he makes an offering of puroḍāśa on seven potsherds to 'Marut-Krīlins' (Maruts—the sporters). Indeed Maruts are sporters. Praising him all around, indeed the Maruts sported around Indra who had come, who was going, to kill Vṛtra. In that same manner, the sportive Maruts praising him, sport around this (sacrificer) who is going to kill his evil hateful enemy. Therefore does he offer a puroḍāśa on seven potsherds to the sportive Maruts. (The reason) why it is on seven potsherds is that the host of Maruts (is in) groups of seven each.
</details>
