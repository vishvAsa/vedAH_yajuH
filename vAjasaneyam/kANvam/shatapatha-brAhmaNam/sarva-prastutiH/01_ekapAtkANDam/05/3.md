## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

महाहवि᳘षा हवै᳘ देवा᳘ वृत्रं᳘ जघ्नुस्ते᳘नैव᳘ व्यजयन्त᳘ येय᳘मेषां वि᳘जितिस्त᳘थो वा᳘ एष एते᳘न पाप्मा᳘नं द्विष᳘न्तं भ्रा᳘तृव्यं हन्ति त᳘थो एव वि᳘जयते॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The gods killed Vṛtra verily by means of the Mahāhavis (the great oblation). By that indeed, did they attain this victory which is theirs. In that manner does this (sacrificer) too kill his wicked hateful enemy by means of this (mahāhavis) and similarly does he attain victory.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘स्यावृदु᳘पकिरत्युत्तरवेदिं᳘ गृह्णा᳘ति पृषदाज्यं म᳘न्थन्त्यग्निं न᳘व प्रयाजा न᳘वानुयाजास्त्री᳘णि समिष्टयजू ष्य᳘थैता᳘न्येव प᳘ञ्च हवीँ᳘ꣳषि भवन्ति॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Its method (of performance) –He spreads the northern altar (with sacred grass). (He binds up the northern altar). He takes up (uses) clotted ghee. He churns the fire. There are (in this sacrifice) nine fore-offerings, nine after-offerings and three 'Samiṣṭa-yajus'. Then there are these (following) five oblations.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स य᳘दाग्नेयो भ᳘वत्यग्नि᳘ना ह वा᳘ एनं ते᳘जसा जघ्नुः स ते᳘जो ऽग्नि᳘र्नाव्यथत त᳘स्मादाग्ने᳘यो ऽष्टा᳘कपालो भवति॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That (the oblation) is one dedicated to Agni (because) they (the gods) killed him (Vṛtra) with Agni, with sharpness. That sharp Agni did not suffer (did not feel pain). Therefore there is (an oblation) dedicated to Agni, on eight potsherds.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

सौम्य᳘श्चरुः सो᳘मराजानो ह वा᳘ एनं जघ्नुस्त᳘स्मात्सौम्य᳘श्चरु᳘र्भवति॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(There is) a caru (offering) dedicated to Soma. (The gods), verily having Soma as their king, killed him (Vṛtra). Therefore there is a caru (offering) dedicated to Soma.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ सावित्रो द्वाद᳘शकपालो वा भ᳘वत्यष्टा᳘कपालो वा सविता वै᳘ देवा᳘नां प्रसविता᳘ सवितृ᳘प्रसूता ह वा᳘ एनं जघ्नुस्त᳘स्मात्सावित्रो द्वा᳘दशकपालो वा भ᳘वत्यष्टा᳘कपालो वा॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then there is (an offering) dedicated to Savitṛ on twelve potsherds or on eight potsherds. Indeed Savitṛ is the producer (inspirer) of the gods. Produced (inspired) by Savitṛ, indeed did they kill this (Vṛtra). Therefore there is (an offering) dedicated to Savitṛ, of twelve potsherds or of eight potsherds.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

सारस्वत᳘श्चरुर्वाग्वै स᳘रस्वती सा हैनम᳘नुममादाभि᳘प्रघर्ष्य प्र᳘हर जही᳘ति त᳘स्मात्सारस्वत᳘श्चरुः᳘॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(Then there is offered) a caru dedicated to Sarasvatī. Indeed speech is Sarasvatī. She verily cheered him (Indra) up (saying), "Attack, strike, kill". Hence (the offer of) a caru dedicated to Sarasvatī.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

पौष्ण᳘श्चरु᳘रियं वै᳘ पृथिवी᳘ पू᳘षैषा᳘ है᳘वैनं प्रतिप्र᳘ददौ वधा᳘यैत᳘या है᳘वैनꣳ संप्र᳘त्तं जघ्नुस्त᳘स्मा त्पौष्ण᳘श्चरुः᳘॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(Then there is an offering of) a caru dedicated to Pūṣan. Verily this earth is Pūṣan (the nourisher). Indeed she surrendered him for killing. They (gods) killed this (Vṛtra) who was given up by this (earth). Therefore (is offered) a caru dedicated to Pūṣan.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ऐन्द्राग्नो द्वा᳘दशकपाल एते᳘न ह वा᳘ एनं जघ्नुर्ब्र᳘ह्माग्निः᳘ क्षत्रमि᳘न्द्रो ब्र᳘ह्म चै᳘वैत᳘त्क्षत्रं᳘ च सꣳर᳘भ्य ते᳘ सयु᳘जौ कृत्वा ता᳘भ्याꣳ है᳘वैनं जघ्नुर्ब्र᳘ह्म चै᳘वैत᳘त्क्षत्रं᳘ च सयु᳘जौ करोति त᳘स्माद्ब्र᳘ह्म च क्षत्रं᳘ च सयु᳘जौ॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(The oblation) dedicated to Indra and Agni (together) is on twelve potsherds. For indeed by means of that they killed this (Vṛtra); Agni is Brāhmin (the priest). Indra is Kṣatra (ruler). Having excited Brahman and Kṣatra and having made them close together, through those two, they (gods) indeed killed this (Vṛtra). Thus does he (the sacrificer) make Brahman and Kṣatra (be close) together. Hence are Brahman and Kṣatra together.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

माहेन्द्र᳘श्चरुरि᳘न्द्रो वा᳘ एष᳘ पुरा᳘ वृत्र᳘स्य वधादित्य᳘थ वृत्रं᳘ जघ्निवान्य᳘था महाराजो᳘ विजिग्यान᳘ एवं᳘ महेन्द्र इ᳘ति त᳘स्मान्माहेन्द्र᳘श्चरु᳘र्महा᳘न्तमु चै᳘वैनमेत᳘त्करोति वृत्र᳘स्य वधा᳘य॥९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

There (is offered) a caru dedicated to Mahendra (Indra the Great). This (god) was verily Indra before the killing of Vṛtra. Then he killed Vṛtra. Just as (a king) (after) conquering (becomes) a great king (Mahārāja), so also (Indra became) Mahendra too. Therefore is a caru dedicated to Mahendra. Thereby he (the sacrificer) makes this (Indra) great (strong) for killing Vṛtra.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

वैश्वकर्मण ए᳘ककपालो वि᳘श्वं वा᳘ एत᳘द्देवा᳘नां क᳘र्म कृत᳘मासीत्स᳘र्व जित᳘ꣳ᳘ साकमेधै᳘रीजाना᳘नां त᳘थो वा᳘ एत᳘स्य वि᳘श्वं क᳘र्म कृतं᳘ भवति स᳘र्वं जित᳘ꣳ᳘ साकमेधै᳘रीजान᳘स्य त᳘ एते᳘नेष्ट्वा᳘ देवा᳘ येयं᳘ देवा᳘नां जि᳘तिर्या श्रीस्त᳘द्वभूवुरेव᳘ꣳ᳘ ह वा᳘व जयत्येवꣳ श्रि᳘यं गच्छति य᳘ एनेनैवं᳘ विद्वान्य᳘जते॥१०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(Then, there is an oblation offered) dedicated to Viśvakarman, on a single potsherd. This action of gods who performed the Śāhamedha sacrifices was made complete (comprehensive), by which all was conquered. So also all work becomes done and everything (becomes conquered) for one who performs the Sākamedha sacrifice. He who knowing this performs a sacrifice thus with this (Sākamedha), verily conquers and attains splendour, which conquest and what splendour those gods attained after having performed with this (Sākamedha) (sacrifice).
</details>
