Prajāpati released the creatures from Varuṇa’s noose indeed by means of ‘Varuṇapraghāsas’. Those creatures of his were born without disease and without blemish. Now with these Sākamedhas —indeed by means of the Sākamedhas did the gods kill Vṛtra. Verily with these only they attained this victory which victory is theirs. In that manner, indeed, does this (sacrificer) destroy his evil hateful enemy by means of these (Sākamedhas) and verily similarly conquers (becomes glorious). He indeed performs (this sacrifice) every day, for two days.

On the previous day (the first day) he offers an oblation (Puroḍāśa) on eight potsherds to Agni-Anikavat. For, the gods who would kill Vṛtra on the morning (of the morrow) went forward (towards Vṛtra), making Agni a sharp point (of weapon) (of making Agni the leader). Therefore he offers a puroḍāśa on eight potsherds to Agni-Anikavat.

Then they call up a bull. If he should bellow, that (bellowing) is the Vaṣaṭkāra (permissive utterance for offering). What is the form of the bull, this verily is the form of Indra. (Thus) he (the sacrificer) calls this (Indra) with his own form for (accepting) this share and for killing Vṛtra with this share.
