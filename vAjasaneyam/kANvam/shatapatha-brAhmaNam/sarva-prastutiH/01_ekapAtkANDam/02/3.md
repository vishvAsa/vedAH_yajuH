## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

व᳘रुणो हैनन्द्राज्य᳘काम आ᳘दधे स᳘ राज्य᳘मगच्छत्त᳘स्माद्य᳘श्चैतद्वे᳘द य᳘श्च न व᳘रुणो राजे᳘त्येवा᳘चक्षते सो᳘मो य᳘शस्कामः स य᳘शो ऽभवत्त᳘स्माद्ये᳘ च सो᳘मे ल᳘भन्ते ये च न ल᳘भन्त उभ᳘य ए᳘वैनदा᳘गच्छन्ति य᳘श ए᳘वैतद्द्र᳘ष्टुमा᳘गच्छन्ति य᳘शो ह भव᳘ति राज्यं᳘ गच्छति य᳘ एनदेवं᳘ विद्वा᳘नाध᳘त्ते॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Varuṇa indeed, established this (fire) being desirous of sovereignty. He attained sovereignty. Therefore, he who knows this and he who does not, all say, "Varuṇa is the King". Soma was desirous of glory. (He established this fire). He became glorious. Therefore those who acquire (in the matter of) Soma and those who don't, both of them acquire this (glory) verily. People do come to see the glorious. He who, knowing this is so, sets up (the fires), indeed, becomes glorious and attains sovereignty.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अग्ना᳘ उ ह वै᳘ देवाः स᳘र्वाणि रूपा᳘णि संनि᳘दधिरे या᳘नि च ग्राम्या᳘णि या᳘नि चारण्या᳘नि विजयं᳘ वोपपरैष्य᳘न्तः कामचार᳘स्य वा का᳘मायायं᳘ नो गो᳘पिष्ठो गोपायत्वि᳘ति वा ता᳘न्ह्याग्निर्नि᳘चकमे ता᳘न्संगृ᳘ह्यर्तून्प्र᳘विवेश पु᳘नरे᳘म इ᳘ति देवा ए᳘दग्निं᳘ तिरो᳘भूतं ते᳘षाꣳ हेय᳘सैवास कि᳘मिह᳘ का᳘र्यं᳘ केह᳘ प्रज्ञे᳘ति॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Gods' deposited, once, all forms --(things and forms), those that were rural and those that were of the forest (domestic and wild), either because they (the gods) were going out to achieve victory (in battle against the enemies) or with a desire for free activity or with the thought, "This (Agni) is the best protector and may he protect us". Agni, indeed, coveted them much. Gathering them (the forms) he entered the seasons. The gods thought, "Let us go back" and came to (the place of) Agni who had disappeared. What is to be done here? What knowledge is there about this?
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘तो हैतत्त्व᳘ष्टा पुनराधे᳘यं ददर्श तदा᳘दधे योऽग्नेः᳘ प्रियं धामो᳘पजगाम त᳘स्मा एता᳘न्यग्नि᳘रुभ᳘यानि रूपा᳘णि प्रतिनिः᳘ससर्ज यानि च ग्राम्या᳘णि या᳘नि चारण्या᳘नि त᳘स्माद्वा᳘ आहुस्त्वष्ट्रू᳘णि रूपाणी᳘ति त्वष्टुर्ह वै स᳘र्वꣳ रूपमु᳘प तु᳘ है᳘वान्याः᳘ प्रजा᳘ यावच्छो᳘ यावच्छस्तिष्ठन्ते॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then, indeed, did Tvaṣṭā see (intuitively discovered) this, 'Re-installation' (re-installed fire). He established it and he reached Agni's favourite abode. Agni gave out to him both these forms, the domestic and the wild. Therefore they say, verily, "The forms are of Tvaṣṭā". For all form belongs to Tvaṣṭṛ. All other creatures (too) as much as they experience it.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘स्मै कं पुनराधे᳘यमा᳘दधीत त᳘था है᳘वाग्नेः᳘ प्रियं धामो᳘पगच्छति त᳘स्मा एता᳘न्यग्नि᳘रुभ᳘यानि रू᳘पाणि प्रतिनिः᳘सृजति या᳘नि च ग्राम्या᳘णि या᳘नि चारण्या᳘नि त᳘स्मिन्नेता᳘न्युभ᳘यानि रूपा᳘णि दृश्यन्ते परम᳘ता वै सा᳘ स्पृह᳘यन्ति हास्मै त᳘था पुष्यति लो᳘क्यमु वा अ᳘प्याग्ने᳘योऽयं᳘ यज्ञो ज्यो᳘तिरग्निः᳘ पाप्म᳘नो दग्धा᳘ सोऽस्य पाप्मा᳘नं दहति स ज्यो᳘तिरे᳘वेह᳘ श्रिया य᳘शसा भवति ज्यो᳘तिरमु᳘त्र पुण्यलोकत्वा॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

So one should set up a re-installed fire for him (Tvaṣṭṛ)? Thus, indeed, one reaches the favourite abode of Agni. To him, Agni re-delivers both these forms that are domestic and that are wild. In it (the fire) are seen both these forms. That is indeed the supreme state. They (people) like him. So does he flourish. (His position) is also noticeable, indeed. This sacrifice belongs to Agni. Agni is the light (flame), the burner of evil. He destroys the evil of this person (sacrificer). He (the sacrificer) becomes the very light with splendour and glory here (in this world) and the light in the yonder world (heaven), the state of a world of merit (the auspicious world).
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वै᳘ वर्षा᳘स्वेवा᳘दधीत वर्षा वै स᳘र्व ऋत᳘वो वर्षा हि वै स᳘र्व ऋतवो᳘ऽथादो᳘ वर्ष᳘मकुर्मादो᳘ वर्ष᳘मकुर्मे᳘ति वर्षा᳘भिरेव᳘ संवत्सरान्त्सं᳘पश्यन्ति वर्षा हि वै स᳘र्व ऋतवो᳘ऽथ वर्षा᳘ एव स᳘र्वेषामृतूना᳘ꣳ᳘ रूपम्॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He may set up (the fire - a second time) only in the rainy season. Indeed the rainy season (itself) is all the seasons. Verily is the rainy season all the seasons. Hence, they consider (count) the years only with the rainy season, saying "We did (this) for a year (in that year) ", "We did this that year," for the rainy season is all the seasons. And therefore the rainy season itself is the form of all the seasons.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

भ᳘वति वै त᳘द्वर्षा᳘सु य᳘त्राहु᳘र्ग्रीष्म᳘ ए᳘वाद्ये᳘ति भ᳘वति तद्य᳘त्राहुः शि᳘शिर ए᳘वाद्ये᳘ति वर्षादि᳘दु वर्षैष्वथै᳘ तत्परो᳘क्षमिव रूपं य᳘देव᳘ पुर᳘स्ताद्वा᳘ति तद्वसन्त᳘स्य रूपं य᳘त्स्तन᳘यति त᳘द्ग्रीष्म᳘स्य यद्व᳘र्षति त᳘द्वर्षा᳘णां य᳘द्विद्यो᳘तते त᳘च्छर᳘दो य᳘द्वृ᳘ष्टोद्गृह्णा᳘ति त᳘द्धेमन्त᳘स्य वर्षाः स᳘र्व ऋत᳘व ऋतून्प्रा᳘विशदृतु᳘भ्य ए᳘वैनमेतन्नि᳘र्मिमीते॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Indeed, it happens in the rainy season when they say, "Today is verily summer!" It happens in the rainy season when they say, "Today is verily winter!" From the rain (it goes) into the years, indeed. And besides, this is, as it were, (the year's) invisible form. What blows in the east, that itself is the form of spring; what thunders is of summer, what rains is of the rainy season, what flashes (lightning) is of autumn and what, having rained sucks it up is of the season of snow. The rainy season is all the seasons. He (Agni) entered the seasons. From the seasons themselves does he produce this (fire).
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

आदित्यो वाव स᳘र्व ऋत᳘वः स य᳘दै᳘वोदेत्य᳘थ वसन्तो᳘ यदा᳘ संगवो᳘ ऽथ ग्रीष्मो᳘ यदा᳘ मध्यं᳘ न्दिनो᳘ऽथ वर्षा᳘ यदा᳘ न्य᳘ह्नोऽपराह्णो᳘ऽथ शर᳘द्य᳘दास्तमेत्य᳘थ हेमन्तः॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Indeed, the Sun is all the seasons. When it rises, even then is it spring; when it is the time for being gathered together (for milking) it is summer; when it is mid-day it is the rainy season; when the day declines, i.e. when it is afternoon, it is autumn. When he sets, then it is the season of snow (winter).
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स य᳘त्र मध्यं᳘न्दिनः स्यात्त᳘द्धैवा᳘दधीत त᳘र्हि वा᳘ ए᳘षोऽस्य᳘ लोक᳘स्य ने᳘दिष्ठो भवति ने᳘दिष्ठादे᳘वैनमेतन्म᳘ध्यान्नि᳘र्मिमीते छा᳘यौ वा᳘ अयं᳘ पाप्मा᳘ मनु᳘ष्यम᳘नुषक्तश्चरति सो᳘ऽत्र क᳘निष्ठो भवत्यधस्पद᳘मिव ह्रासी᳘ति क᳘निष्ठमे᳘वैतत्स᳘न्तं पाप्मा᳘नम᳘वबाधते॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When it is mid-day, then only should one establish (the fire). Then, indeed, does this (Sun) remain closest to this world (earth). He produces this (fire) from the nearest centre of this (world). This evil, indeed, moves about clinging to a man as his shadow. That (evil) becomes smallest here (at mid-day). (Thinking thus), "You are as if under (my) foot", he afflicts (and drives away) that evil even when it remains smallest.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वै᳘ कुशैरु᳘द्धरति दा᳘रुभिर्वा अ᳘ग्न उ᳘द्धरति दा᳘रुभिर᳘ग्ने दा᳘रुभिर᳘परं जामि᳘ कुर्यात्सम᳘दं कुर्यादा᳘पो वै᳘ वर्षा आ᳘पः कुशा᳘ वर्षा᳘ ऋतून्प्रा᳘विशदद्भि᳘रे᳘वैनमेत᳘दद्भ्यो नि᳘र्मिमीते᳘॥९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Verily he takes it (fire) up by means of sacrificial grass. Or he takes it out with (pieces of) wood, the first time. (Should he take the fire) first with (pieces of) wood and the second time (too) with (pieces of ) wood, he would be committing repetition and he would be doing it with arrogance. Waters are the rainy season. Waters are the (blades of ) sacred grass and the rainy season. He (Agni) entered the seasons. Therefore he produces this (fire) with waters themselves from waters.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

सोऽर्कपलाशा᳘खाभ्यां व्रीहिम᳘यमपूपं᳘ कृत्वा य᳘त्र गा᳘र्हपत्यमाधास्यन्भ᳘वति तन्नि᳘दधाति तद्गा᳘र्हपत्यमभ्य᳘दधाति॥१०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Having prepared a cake of rice with two leaves of the arha plant, he places it at the spot where he is going to establish the Gārhapatya. And then he lays down the Gārhapatya there.
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अर्कपलाशा᳘खाभ्यामेव᳘ यवम᳘यमपूपं᳘ कृत्वा य᳘त्राहवनी᳘यमाधास्यन्भ᳘वति तन्नि᳘दधाति त᳘दाहवनी᳘यमभ्या᳘दधाति पू᳘र्वाभ्यामे᳘वैनमेत᳘दग्नि᳘भ्यामन्त᳘र्दधाति ॥११॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Having prepared a cake of barley with two leaves of the arha plant, he puts it at the place where he is going to establish the Āhavanīya. And then he lays down the Āhavanīya there. He covers this verily with the first two fires.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दु ना᳘द्रियेत रा᳘त्रिभिर्वाव᳘ सो ऽन्त᳘र्हितो᳘ ऽथैत᳘माग्नेयं प᳘ञ्चकपालं पुरोल्ला᳘शं नि᳘र्वपति त᳘स्य प᳘ञ्चपदाः पङ्क्त᳘यो याज्यानुवा᳘क्या भवन्ति प᳘ञ्चर्त᳘व ऋतूनप्रा᳘विशदृतु᳘भ्य ए᳘वैनमेतन्नि᳘र्मिमीते॥१२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

One need not favour that (practice), for it becomes covered indeed by the nights. Then he offers this oblation (purodāśa) cooked in five potsherds and dedicated to Agni. For it, the offering mantras and (connected) subsequent utterances (Yājyās and anuvākyās) are in Panikti (metre) with five pādas (feet); for, the seasons are five. He (Agni) entered the seasons. He (the sacrificer) thus produces this (fire) from the very seasons.
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

आग्नेयः स᳘र्वो भवति त᳘था᳘ ह्यग्नेः प्रियं धा᳘म त्व᳘ष्टोपा᳘गच्छदुपा᳘ꣳ᳘शुनेन चरन्ति यद्वै᳘ ज्ञाति᳘भ्यो वा स᳘खिभ्यो वा नि᳘ष्केवल्यं करिष्यन्भ᳘वति तिर᳘ इव वै ते᳘न बोभुवत्तिर᳘ इव खिद्वा᳘ एतद्य᳘दुपाꣳशु᳘ वैश्वदेवोऽन्यो᳘ यज्ञो᳘ऽथैष नि᳘ष्केवल्य ए᳘वाग्नेय᳘ उत्तम᳘मनुयाज᳘मुच्चै᳘र्यजति कृत᳘कर्मैव हि त᳘र्हि भ᳘वति स᳘र्वो हि᳘ कृत᳘मनुबु᳘द्ध्यते॥१३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The whole (sacrifice) belongs to Agni. That way, indeed, Tvaṣṭṛ reached the favourite abode of Agni. They perform it with this (formula), in a low voice, what one is going to do purely for his relatives or friends, he is verily secretive with it. This (which is uttered in) –a low voice is indeed like a secret. The other sacrifice belongs to all the gods. But this that belongs to Agni is special. The last ‘subsequent offering’ he (the sacrificer) offers in a loud voice. Then, indeed, he becomes, as it were, one who has done his work. Every one understands that it has been done (completed).
</details>


## १४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स य᳘त्राध्वर्यु᳘रतिक्र᳘म्याश्राव्याह समि᳘धो यजे᳘त्याग्नेय᳘मेव त᳘द्रूपं᳘ परो᳘क्षमिव᳘ त्वग्नी᳘न्यजे᳘ति हैव तत्र᳘ ब्रूयात्॥१४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When the Adhvaryu, having stepped beyond and having called (drawn attention) says, “Worship (offer) (pronounce the offering prayer for) ‘Samidhs’, it is, indeed, the form of Agni and it appears to be indirect. There one should say (directly) only “Worship the Agnis”.
</details>


## १५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ यजत्य᳘ग्न आ᳘ज्यस्य व्यन्तु वौ᳘षळि᳘त्यग्निमा᳘ज्यस्य वेतु वौ᳘षळि᳘त्यग्निना᳘ज्यस्य व्यन्तु वौ᳘षळि᳘त्यग्निरा᳘ज्यस्य वेतु वौ᳘षळिति॥१५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he recites (in worship) “Oh! Agni! May they (Samidhs) well reach (accept) the ghee”. “Vauṣat”. May he accept the fire of the ghee”. “vauṣat”. May they accept the ghee through Agni”. “Vauṣat”. or “May Agni accept the ghee”. “Vauṣat”.
</details>


## १६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ स्वा᳘हाग्निंꣳ स्वा᳘हाग्निं प᳘वमानमि᳘ति य᳘द्यग्न᳘ये प᳘वमानाय ध्रिये᳘रन्त्स्वा᳘हाग्निमि᳘न्दुमन्तमि᳘ति य᳘द्यग्न᳘य इ᳘न्दुमते ध्रिये᳘रन्त्स्वा᳘हाग्नी᳘नाज्यपा᳘न्जुषाणा᳘ अग्न᳘य आज्यपा आ᳘ज्यस्य व्यन्तु वौषळि᳘त्येव᳘मेता᳘न्प्रयाजान्त्स᳘र्वानाग्नेया᳘न्करोति॥१६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he says, “Svāhāgnim”. “Svāhāgnim Pavamānam”. If they decide on (offering it to) Agni Pavamāna. If they decide on (offering it to) Agni Indumat – (he says) “Svāhāgnim indumantam”. He (the sacrificer) makes all these principal sacrificial ceremonies dedicated to Agni, (uttering) “Svāhāgnim-ajyupām” (Svāhā; the fires that consume ghee) “May the Agnis, drinkers of ghee, coming together (being pleased), accept the ghee”. “Vauṣat”.
</details>


## १७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाहाग्नये᳘ ऽनुब्रूहीत्याग्नेयमा᳘ज्यभागंꣳ सो᳘ऽन्वाहाग्निꣳ स्तो᳘मेन बोधय समिधानो अ᳘मर्त्यम् । हव्या᳘ देवे᳘षु नो दधदि᳘ति स्व᳘पितीव खिद्वा᳘ एतद्यदु᳘द्वासितो भ᳘वति बोध᳘यत्ये᳘वैनमेत᳘त्समु᳘द्दीपयति जुषाणो᳘ अग्निरा᳘ज्यस्य वेत्वि᳘ति यजति॥१७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he says, “Pronounce the inviting prayer for Agni”, with regard to Agni’s portion of ghee. He (the Hoṭṛ) recites (following this instruction), “Wake up Agni” with praise, enkindling him the immortal, so that he takes our offerings to the gods”. Indeed, having been removed, he (Agni) appears to sleep. He wakes him thus and makes him blaze up. He makes the offering, saying “May Agni be pleased and may he accept the ghee”.
</details>


## १८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाहाग्न᳘ये प᳘वमानाया᳘नुब्रूही᳘ति य᳘द्यग्न᳘ये प᳘वमानाय धिये᳘रन्त्सो᳘ ऽन्वाहा᳘ग्न आ᳘यूꣳषि पवस आ᳘सुवो᳘र्ज्जमि᳘षं च नः । आरे᳘ बाधस्व दुच्छु᳘नामि᳘ति तथा᳘हाग्नेयो भ᳘वति सो᳘मो वै प᳘वमानो᳘ नो सौम्यादा᳘ज्यभागाद्यन्ति जुषाणो᳘ अग्निः प᳘वमान आ᳘ज्यस्य वेत्वि᳘ति यजति॥१८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he says, if they decide on (offering to ) Agni-Pavamāna, "Make the call for Agni-Pavamāna". Then he (Hotā) says, "Oh Agni! you breath (blow) life. Please produce for us food and strength. Drive far away misfortune". Thus indeed, it becomes of Agni. Soma indeed is the Pavamāna (the purifier). (The Soma elements) do not go away from the ghee portion belonging to Soma. "May Agni-Pavamāna being pleased, accept the ghee!" So he recites (on making the offering).
</details>


## १९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाहाग्न᳘य इ᳘न्दुमते᳘ ऽनुब्रूही᳘ति य᳘द्यग्न᳘य इ᳘न्दुमते धिये᳘रन्त्सो᳘ ऽन्वाहे᳘हूपू षु ब्र᳘वाणि ते᳘ ऽग्न इत्ये᳘तरा गि᳘रः । एभि᳘र्वर्द्धास इ᳘न्दुभिरि᳘ति तथा᳘हाग्नेयो भ᳘वति सो᳘मो वा इ᳘न्दु᳘र्नौ सौम्यादा᳘ज्य- भागाद्यन्ति जुषाणो᳘ अग्निरि᳘न्दुमाना᳘ज्यस्य वेत्वि᳘ति यजति॥१९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

If they decide on (offering to) 'Agni-Indumat' he says, "Make the call for 'Agni-Indumat'". He (Hotā) says, following this, "Come here, Oh Agni! I shall gladly say these and other words (of praise). May you grow with these drops." Then, indeed does it become of (dedicated to) Agni. Soma, indeed, is Indu (the drop). They (the Soma elements) do not go away from the portion of ghee belonging to Soma. He recites, in worship, "May Agni-Indumat (possessor of drops), being pleased, accept the ghee".
</details>


## २०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाहाग्नये᳘ ऽनुब्रूही᳘त्यग्निं᳘ यजे᳘त्यग्न᳘ये स्विष्टकृते᳘ ऽनुब्रूहीग्नि᳘ꣳ᳘ स्विष्टकृ᳘तं यजे᳘ति॥२०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he says, "Make the call for Agni". "Worship (recite the offering prayers to) Agni". "Make the call for Agni Sviṣṭakṛt. (who makes the offerings well offered)". "Recite the offering prayers to Agni Sviṣṭakṛt".
</details>


## २१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स य᳘त्राध्वर्यु᳘रातिक्रा᳘म्याश्राव्या᳘ह देवा᳘न्यजे᳘त्यग्नीद्यजे᳘ति हैव त᳘त्र ब्रूयात्॥२१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Where the Adhvaryu (priest) over-stepping (these) and calling attention says "Worship (recite the prayers) for the gods," there he should verily say (recite the prayers for) "worship Agnis".
</details>


## २२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ यजत्यग्ने᳘र्वसुव᳘ने वसुधेय᳘स्य वेतु वौ᳘षळि᳘त्यग्ना᳘ उ वसुव᳘ने वसुधे᳘यस्य वेतु वौ᳘षळि᳘ति देवो᳘ अग्निः᳘ स्विष्टकृदि᳘ति स्वय᳘मग्नेय᳘स्तृती᳘यो ऽनुयाजः॥२२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He recites "May it (Barhis) accept (the offering) for Agni re-acquisition of abundant wealth": "Vauṣat". "The divine Agni Sviṣṭakṛt". This third subsequent offering by itself belongs to Agni.
</details>


## २३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ता वा᳘ एताः षड्वि᳘भक्तयश्च᳘तस्रः प्रयाजे᳘षु द्वे᳘ अनुयाजे᳘षु षड्वा᳘ ऋत᳘व ऋतून्प्रा᳘विशदृतु᳘भ्य ए᳘वैनमेतन्नि᳘र्म्मिमीते ता᳘साꣳ षण्णां वि᳘भक्तीनां द्वा᳘दश वा त्र᳘योदश वाक्ष᳘राणि द्वा᳘दश वा वै त्र᳘योदश वा संवत्सर᳘स्य मा᳘साः संवत्सर᳘ ऋतून्प्रा᳘विशदृतु᳘भ्य ए᳘वैनमेतन्नि᳘र्म्मिमीते न द्वे᳘ चन᳘ सह᳘ करोति जामि हि᳘ कुर्य्याद्यद्द्वै᳘ चित्सह᳘ कुर्य्या᳘द्विष्वग्यन्त्वि᳘ति प्रयाजा᳘नाꣳ रूपं᳘ वसुवने᳘ वसुधे᳘यस्य वेत्वि᳘त्यनुयाजा᳘नाꣳ हि᳘रण्यं द᳘क्षिणाग्ने᳘यो᳘ऽयं᳘ य᳘ज्ञो ऽग्नेर्हि रे᳘तो हि᳘रण्यम- नड्वा᳘ननड्वान्हि व᳘हसाग्ने᳘यो᳘ ऽन्यवदर्धैमिव᳘ ह्यनळु᳘हो व᳘हो भ᳘वति देवा᳘नाꣳ हव्यवा᳘हनो ऽग्निर्वा᳘हत्येष᳘ मनु᳘ष्येभ्यः॥२३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

These are indeed the six case-forms. Four (are used) in primary offerings and two in after-offerings. Verily six are the seasons. He (Agni) entered the seasons. He, thus, produces him (Agni) only from the seasons. Of the six case-forms, there are twelve or thirteen syllables. Indeed twelve or thirteen are the months of the year. The year entered the seasons. He (Agni) entered the year, the seasons. He verily produces him (Agni) from the seasons. He does not put two together. Should he perform two together, he would be doing a fault. The form of (usage of) primary offerings is "May it accept". "May they accept". The form of (expression) after-offerings is "May it accept for abundant acquisition of riches" Gold is the sacrificial fee. This sacrifice verily belongs to Agni. Indeed gold is the seed of Agni. Or it (the sacrificial fee) is an ox. Verily the ox is related to Agni, by its shoulder. The shoulder of the ox looks as if it is burnt by fire. Agni is the carrier of the oblations to the gods. This ox carries (burdens) for the sake of men.
</details>
