## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

घ्न᳘न्ति ह वा᳘ एत᳘द्यज्ञं य᳘देनं तन्व᳘ते यद्रा᳘जानमभिषुण्व᳘न्ति तंत्तं᳘ घ्नन्त्य᳘थ य᳘त्पशु᳘ꣳ᳘संज्ञप᳘यन्ति य᳘द्विशा᳘सति तत्तं᳘ घ्नन्त्यूलूखलमुसले᳘न दृषडुपले᳘न हविर्यज्ञं᳘ घ्नन्ति स᳘ एव᳘ꣳ᳘हतो᳘ यज्ञो न᳘ ददक्षे तं᳘ देवा द᳘क्षिणाभिर्दक्षयां᳘ चक्रुस्त᳘स्माद्द᳘क्षिणा ना᳘म य᳘दाभिर᳘दक्षयꣳस्त᳘स्मादु द᳘क्षिणा दद्यात्स यद्द᳘क्षिणा द᳘दाति य᳘देव᳘ हत᳘स्य यज्ञ᳘स्य व्य᳘थते त᳘दे᳘वास्यै तद्द᳘क्षिणाभिर्दक्षयत्य᳘थ स᳘मृद्ध एव᳘ यज्ञः॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

In that they perform (elaborately) the sacrifice, they kill it. In that they press (ritually prepare the juice) the King (the Soma plant), they kill it. In that they quieten and immolate the (sacrificial) animal, they kill it. With mortar and pestle and with a pair of mill-stones they kill the haviryajña (the sacrificial food offerings). The sacrifice having been killed thus became incapable of producing the desired result. The gods made it capable with fees (ritual gifts to priests). As they invigorated (the sacrifice) with these, these (fees) came to be known as Dakṣiṇā (invigorating means). Therefore, indeed, must one give the fees. Whatever (part) of this destroyed sacrifice flags, that very thing of the sacrifice does one invigorate by means of the sacrificial fees that one gives. Then the sacrifice becomes verily rich (prospers).
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ताः ष᳘ड्दद्यात्षड्वा᳘ ऋत᳘वः संवत्सर᳘स्य संवत्सरो᳘ यज्ञः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिः स या᳘वानेव᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिर्या᳘वत्यस्य मा᳘त्रा ता᳘वतीभिरे᳘वैनमेतद्द᳘क्षयति॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

One should give six of them (fees/cows). Indeed six are the seasons of the year; the year is the sacrifice, (it is) Prajāpati. As much as is the sacrifice, as much as is its measure, by so much (many) fees only does he (the sacrificer) thereby invigorates it.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थो द्वा᳘दश दद्याद्द्वा᳘दश वै मा᳘साः संवत्सर᳘स्य संवत्सरो᳘ यज्ञः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिः स या᳘वानेव᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिर्या᳘वत्यस्य मा᳘त्रा ता᳘वतीभिरे᳘वैनमेत᳘द्दक्षयति॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Or, one may give twelve; indeed twelve are the months of the year. The year is the sacrifice, Prajāpati. As much as is the sacrifice, as much as is its measure, by so much only does he thereby invigorate it.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थो च᳘तुर्विꣳशतिं दद्याच्च᳘तुर्विꣳशतिर्वा᳘ अर्धमासाः᳘ संवत्सर᳘स्य संवत्सरो᳘ यज्ञः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिः स या᳘वानेव᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिर्या᳘वत्यस्य मा᳘त्रा ता᳘वतीभिरे᳘वैनमेत᳘द्दक्षयत्येता आ᳘दिष्टाः का᳘मं यथाश्रद्धं भू᳘यसीर्दद्यात्॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Or, one may give twenty-four. Indeed twenty-four are the half-months of the year. The year is the sacrifice, Prajāpati. As much as is the sacrifice, as much as is its measure, by so much only does he thereby invigorate it. These have been indicated (advised). One may give more according to his faith.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

द्वया᳘ उ वाव᳘ देवा᳘ देवा अ᳘है᳘वान्य᳘देवा अ᳘थैमे᳘ मनुष्यदेवा य᳘ इमे᳘ ब्राह्मणाः᳘ सुश्रुवा᳘ꣳ᳘सोनूचानास्ते᳘षां द्वेधा विभक्तो᳘ यज्ञ आ᳘हुतय एव᳘ देवा᳘नां द᳘क्षिणा मनुष्यदेवा᳘नां ब्राह्मणा᳘नाꣳ शुश्रु᳘वाꣳसोनूचानानाꣳ स आ᳘हुतिभिरेव᳘ देवा᳘न्प्रीणा᳘ति द᳘क्षिणाभिर्मनुष्यदेवा᳘न्ब्राह्मणा᳘ञ्छुश्रु᳘षो ऽनूचानाꣳस्त᳘ एनमुभ᳘ये देवाः᳘ प्रीताः᳘ सुधा᳘यां दधति य᳘थो वै रे᳘तो यो᳘नौ दध्या᳘देव᳘मेत᳘दृत्वि᳘जः स्वर्गे᳘ लोके य᳘जमानं दधति ते᳘भ्य एत᳘द्ददाति ये᳘ मेद᳘ꣳ᳘ संप्रा᳘पिपन्निती᳘र्ति नु द᳘क्षिणानाम्॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Verily the gods are of two kinds. The Gods indeed are of a different (one) kind of gods. Then these Brāhmins who have studied the Vedas and teach (them) are the human gods. Their sacrifice (also) is divided into two kinds: the very oblations (form the sacrifice) for the gods, for the human gods viz., the Brāhmins who have studied the Vedas and teach them, it is the sacrificial fee. He (the sacrificer) propitiates the gods only by the oblations and (propitiates) the human gods. the Brāhmins who have studied the Vedas and teach them, with sacrificial fees. Those two (kinds of) gods, being pleased, place this (sacrificer) in nectar (a state of bliss). As one would deposit semen into the genitals (of a woman), in the same manner, these priests place the sacrificer in heaven. The sacrificer presents this (fee) to them (priests), thinking, "These are those who have made me reach this (heaven)". This is indeed, concerning sacrificial fees.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ देवा᳘श्च ह वा अ᳘सुराश्चोभ᳘ये प्राजापत्या᳘ अस्पर्धन्त ते᳘ होभ᳘येऽनात्मा᳘न आसुर्मर्त्या᳘ ह्यासु᳘र्नात्मा हि स यो म᳘र्त्यस्ते᳘षु हाग्नि᳘रे᳘वामृ᳘तमास त᳘मुभ᳘य उ᳘पाजीवꣳस्ते᳘षाꣳ ह स्म यं घ्न᳘न्ति त᳘द्ध स्मैव स᳘ भवति त᳘तो ह देवास्तनीयाꣳस इव प᳘रिशिशिषिरे ते हा᳘र्चन्तः श्रा᳘म्यन्तः कथ᳘ꣳ᳘ सप᳘त्नान᳘सुरानभि᳘भवेमे᳘ति ते᳘ हैत᳘दे᳘वाग्न्याधे᳘यं ददृशुः॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Devas and Asuras, both of them sprung from Prajāpati, contended (with each other). Both of them were without Ātman (eternal soul). Indeed they were mortals. Verily he who is mortal has no (abiding) soul. Among them only Agni was immortal. Both of them (Devas and Asuras) lived by resorting to him (Agni). Whom (soever) among them they killed; he remained so (slain). Then the gods remained (were left over) thinner (in number or strength). They worshipped and tired themselves (in austerities), thinking, "How can we overcome our rivals the Asuras?" They indeed discovered this very 'Agnyādheya' (installation of Agni).
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते᳘ होचुर्ह᳘न्तेद᳘ममृ᳘तमन्त᳘रात्म᳘न्याधाये᳘त्यमृ᳘ता भू᳘त्वा स्तर्या स्त᳘र्यान्त्स᳘प᳘त्नान्म᳘र्त्यान᳘सुरानभि᳘ भविष्याम इ᳘ति॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They said, "Ah! installing this immortal thing (Agni) within ourselves and thereby becoming immortal and invincible, we shall overcome the Asuras, our mortal conquerable rivals.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते होचुरम᳘येषु वै᳘ नो ऽय᳘मग्नि᳘रिति हन्ता᳘सुरेभ्यः प्रतिप्रब्र᳘वामे᳘ति ते हा᳘सुराने᳘त्योचुरा वै वय᳘माग्निं᳘ धास्यामहे᳘ ऽथैनं यूयं किं᳘ करिष्यथे᳘ति ते हा᳘सुरा असूय᳘न्त इवोचुर᳘थैनं वयं᳘ न्येव᳘ धास्यामह इत्य᳘न्न तृ᳘णानि दहा᳘त्र दा᳘रूणि दहा᳘त्र माꣳसं᳘ पचा᳘त्रौदनं᳘ पचे᳘ति᳘ सो ऽय᳘मग्निर्यै᳘नेदं᳘ मनु᳘ष्या भुञ्ज᳘ते य᳘दिद᳘मिमाः᳘ प्रजा᳘ उपजी᳘वन्ति॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They said, "Verily this Agni is in both of us. Well, let us speak to the Asuras in a contrary way." They went to the Asuras and said, "Verily we are going to set up Agni (ceremoniously). What are you going to do with him (Agni)?" The Asuras spoke, as it were, maliciously "Then we shall surely lay it down," saying, 'Burn the (blades of) grass here; 'burn the logs of wood here;' 'cook meat here' and 'cook rice here'. This (what the Asuras laid down) was the fire with which men eat and resorting to which these creatures live.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थैनं देवा᳘ अन्त᳘रात्म᳘न्याधा᳘यामृ᳘ता भू᳘त्वास्तर्या स्तर्यान्त्सप᳘त्नान्म᳘र्त्यान᳘सुरान᳘भ्यभवꣳस्त᳘स्मै कमग्नी आ᳘दधीत᳘ नामृतत्व᳘स्या᳘शास्त्यर्यो᳘ हैव᳘ भवति न᳘ हैनँ तु᳘स्तूर्षमाणश्चन᳘ स्तृणुते त᳘स्माद्यद᳘हिताग्निश्वा᳘नाहिताग्निश्श्च स्प᳘र्धेते आ᳘हिताग्निरे᳘वाभि᳘भवत्यस्तर्यो हि स भ᳘वति॥९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then, the gods, installing this (Agni) within themselves and becoming immortal and invincible, overcame their rivals, the conquerable mortal Asuras. For that (which) there are two fires, shall one set up? There is no hope of (attaining) immortality. (But) he (the sacrificer) indeed becomes unconquerable. One who wishes to conquer him does not conquer him at all. Therefore, when one who has set up his fires and one who has not set up his fires contend (with each other), only the one who has set up his fires overcomes (the other). For he, indeed, becomes invincible.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तं य᳘त्रादो म᳘न्थन्ति त᳘देनं जात᳘मभिप्र᳘णिति प्राणो वा᳘ अग्नि᳘र्जात᳘मे᳘वैनं त᳘ज्जनयत्यथो᳘दनिति त᳘देनमन्त᳘रात्मन्या᳘धत्ते ऽथे᳘तरमु᳘ज्ज्वलयन्तीह᳘ होष्यामीह᳘ यक्ष्य इह᳘ साधु᳘ करिष्यामी᳘ति॥१०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then, where they churn that (produce that fire by churning), then he (the sacrificer) breathes upon this (fire) that has been born. Indeed Agni is breath. It produces (enlivens) this one that has been born. Then he inhales the breath up. By that he installs this (fire) within himself. Then he makes the other one blaze up, thinking, "Here shall I put the oblation". "Here shall I perform the sacrifice" and "Here shall I do it properly (I shall do the right thing here)".
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तद्वै᳘केषामा᳘ग इव भवत्य᳘न्वगन्नि᳘ति त᳘दु ना᳘द्रियेत न वा᳘ अस्यैषो᳘ ऽनुगच्छति᳘ योऽस्यान्त᳘रात्मन्या᳘हितो भ᳘वत्यनौ᳘ व्ययासीद्रथो᳘ व्ययासीत्॥११॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

To some, this appears to be a fault, that it (the fire) might go out. One need not mind it. The fire that has been installed in his own self, that does not go out. "The cart passed; the chariot passed (in between me and the fire)."
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अन्त᳘रागादि᳘ति हैकेषामा᳘ग इव भवति त᳘दु᳘ नो एवा᳘द्रियेत न वा᳘ अस्यैतम᳘नो विं᳘याति न र᳘थो वि᳘याति ना᳘न्तरेणैति क᳘श्चन᳘ यो ऽस्यान्त᳘रात्मन्या᳘हितो भ᳘वति॥१२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"It passed in between" --such, indeed, is a sense of fault (wrong) for some. That too one need not mind at all. The cart does not pass through this (inner fire) of this (sacrificer), nor does a chariot pass nor does any one come in between the fire that has been established within himself.
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते वा᳘ एते᳘ प्राणा᳘ ए᳘वाग्न᳘यः प्राणोदाना᳘वे᳘वाहवनी᳘यश्च गा᳘र्हपत्यश्च व्या᳘नो ऽन्वाहार्यप᳘चनस्त᳘स्य वा᳘ एत᳘स्याग्न्याधे᳘यस्य सत्य᳘मे᳘वोपचारः॥१३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

These breaths themselves are the fires. The Prāṇa (exhaled breath the vital breath) and Udāna (the inhaled/up-drawn) breaths verily are the Āhavanīya and Gārhapatya (fires). The Vyāna (pervasive breathing is the Anvāhāryapacana (the fire for cooking subsequent offerings). For this consecrated fire, worship consists, verily in (speaking the) truth.
</details>


## १४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स य᳘था स᳘मिद्धमग्निं᳘ घृते᳘नाभिषिञ्चेत्त᳘स्य वै भू᳘यो भूयस्ते᳘जो भवति स श्वः᳘श्वः श्रे᳘यान्भवत्येवं᳘ꣳ हैव᳘ कीर्त्या य᳘शसा श्रिया श्वः᳘श्वः श्रे᳘यान्भवति᳘ योऽ ग्नी᳘ आधा᳘य सत्यं व᳘दति॥१४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Just as one may sprinkle the kindled fire with ghee and its lustre becomes more and more and it (the fire) becomes greater day by day, in a very similar manner does a person who, having installed the fire, speaks the truth; becomes greater day by day with renown, glory and splendour (wealth).
</details>


## १५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘था स᳘मिद्धमग्नि᳘मुदके᳘नाभिषिञ्चेत्त᳘स्य वै क᳘नीयः कनीयस्ते᳘जो भवति स श्वः᳘श्वः पा᳘पीयान्भवत्येवं᳘ꣳ᳘ हैव᳘ कीर्त्या य᳘शसा श्रिया श्वः᳘ श्वः पा᳘पीयान्भवति᳘ योऽग्नी᳘ आधा᳘य मृषा व᳘दत्य᳘क्षणꣳ हौपवेशिं ज्ञत᳘य ऊचुः स्थ᳘विरो वा᳘ अस्यग्नी आ᳘धत्स्वे᳘ति॥१५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now, Just as (if) one sprinkles fire with water, its lustre becomes less and less and day by day it becomes weaker (more pitiable), verily like this, he who having installed the fire speaks untruth becomes weaker (more pitiable) by (in the matter of) renown, glory and splendour. The relatives told Aruṇa Aupaveśi, thus, "You are, indeed, old; set up the two fires".
</details>


## १६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ होवाच त᳘न्मैत᳘द्ब्रथ वाचंयम᳘ ए᳘वैधीति मा ब्रूथो᳘ह्यग्नी᳘ आधा᳘य मृषा व᳘दे᳘न्नो वाचा व᳘दतो᳘ ऽमृषोद्य᳘मस्ति त᳘स्मादु सत्य᳘मेव᳘दिषेत॥१६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He said, "Do not tell me this. Do not say, "Be one with a vow of silence (one with complete and habitual restraint on speech)" for one should, indeed, not speak untruth after having installed the two fires. For one who speaks, there is no non-speaking of untruth. Therefore, indeed, one must wish (strive) to speak only the truth.
</details>
