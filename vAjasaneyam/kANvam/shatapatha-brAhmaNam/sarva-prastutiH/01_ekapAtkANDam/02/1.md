## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

उद्धृ᳘त्याहवनी᳘यं पूर्णाहुतिं᳘ जुहोत्यन्नादं वा᳘ एतं᳘ जनयत आत्म᳘नो य᳘दग्निं त᳘स्मा एतद᳘न्नम᳘पिदधाति य᳘था वत्सा᳘य वा जाता᳘य त᳘रुणाय वा कुमारा᳘य स्त᳘नमपिदध्या᳘देव᳘मस्मां एतद᳘न्नम᳘पिदधाति ते᳘न शान्तः᳘ प्रीत उत्तराणि हवीꣳष्यु᳘परमते श्रप्य᳘माणानि श᳘श्वदध्वर्युं᳘ वा य᳘जमानं वा प्र᳘दहेद्य᳘दस्मिन्नेतामा᳘हुतिं न᳘ जुहुयुस्तौ᳘ ह्यास्य ने᳘दिष्ठमिव च᳘रतः॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Having taken out the Āhavanīya fire, he (the sacrificer) offers the full oblations, 'Pūrṇāhuti'. Indeed, he (thereby) makes this Agni become a consumer of food for his own sake. To him (Agni) he offers this food. Just as one (the cow or the mother) would offer the breast to the (just) born calf or to the young child (son), in the same manner one offers this food to this (fire). Appeased and pleased by that, he (Agni) waits quietly for the subsequent oblations that are cooked. If they (the sacrificers) do not offer this oblation, in this (Agni), it (the fire) will soon burn up the Yajamāna or the Adhvaryu (for) those two move, as it were, nearest to it (the fire).
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तां वै᳘ पूर्णां᳘ जुहोति स᳘र्वं वै᳘ पूर्णꣳ स᳘र्वेणै᳘वैनं त᳘च्छमयति स्वाहाकारे᳘ण जुहोत्य᳘निरुक्तो वै᳘ स्वाहाकारः स᳘र्वं वाँ अ᳘निरुक्तꣳ स᳘र्वेणै᳘वैनं त᳘च्छमयति या᳘मु वा᳘ अदः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिः प्रथमामा᳘हुतिम᳘जुहोत्स्वाहाकारे᳘ण वै ता᳘मजुहो᳘त्सो स्विद्धा᳘ एषा᳘ निदा᳘नेन त᳘स्मात्स्वाहाकारे᳘ण जुहोति त᳘स्यां व᳘रं ददाति स᳘र्वं वै व᳘रः स᳘र्वेणै᳘वैनं त᳘च्छमयति॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That (offering) he offers in full. Indeed, full is (means) 'all'. That (offering) appeases this (fire) with 'all'. He makes the offering uttering the sound 'svāhā'. The expression 'svāhā' is undefined (general). 'All' also is undefined (general). That, then, appeases this (fire) verily with 'all' (every thing). The first offering which Prajāpati offered, he indeed offered it with the utterance of the sound 'svāhā'. That is indeed this, primarily. Hence he (the sacrificer) makes the offering, muttering 'svāhā'. At (when) this (offering has been made), he (the sacrificer) presents a boon (gift) (to the priest). Everything is indeed a gift (a worthwhile thing). That (offering of gift) appeases this (fire) with every thing (all).
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दाहुरेता᳘मेवाहुतिꣳ हुत्वेत्यथो᳘त्तराणि हवी᳘ꣳ᳘षि ना᳘द्रियेते᳘त्येत᳘यैव तं का᳘ममाप्नोति य᳘स्मै का᳘मायो᳘त्तराणि हवी᳘ꣳषि निर्व᳘पते᳘ति॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Here they (some) say, "Having made this offering (Pūrṇāhuti) one need not care for the subsequent offerings. He surely achieves even by this that wish for which he may make the subsequent offerings.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स य᳘दग्न᳘ये प᳘वमानाय निर्व᳘पति प्राणं वा᳘ अस्मिꣳस्त᳘द्दधात्य᳘न्नं वै᳘ प्राणो᳘ऽन्नमु वा᳘ एषा᳘हुतिस्त᳘देत᳘यै᳘वास्मिꣳस्त᳘द्दधाति॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The fact that one makes an offering to Agni Pavamāna (the Blower) means that it puts life-breath, indeed, into this (sacrificer). Verily food is life breath. This offering is indeed food. Thus it puts into this (sacrificer) that (vital breath) by means of this (offering).
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘दग्न᳘ये पावका᳘य निर्व᳘पत्य᳘न्नं वा᳘ अस्मिꣳस्त᳘द्दधाति प्रत्य᳘क्षं वा᳘ एषा᳘हुतिर᳘न्नं त᳘दे त᳘यै᳘वास्मिꣳस्तद्दधाति॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then, the fact that one makes an offering to Agni Pāvaku (purifier) means that it indeed puts food into this (Agni). This offering (viz.,) food is directly visible. Therefore one puts that (food) into this (fire) by this very offering.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘दग्न᳘ये शु᳘चये निर्व᳘पति वी᳘र्यं वा᳘ अस्मिꣳस्त᳘द्दधात्येत᳘द्ध वा अस्य वी᳘र्यꣳ शु᳘चि य᳘ ए᳘षो ऽर्चि᳘रुज्ज्व᳘लति त᳘देत᳘स्यामेव᳘ हुता᳘यामुच्चैस्तरा᳘मिवोज्ज्वलति वी᳘र्यवत्तर इव भवति त᳘देत᳘यै᳘वास्मिँꣳस्त᳘द्दधाति त᳘स्मान्ना᳘द्रियेते᳘त्याहुस्त᳘दु नि᳘र्वपेदेव᳘ परो᳘क्षमिव वा᳘ एतत्त᳘दस्त᳘दिदमि᳘तीव या᳘वद्वा अ᳘जातो ग᳘र्भो मातु᳘र्यैव ता᳘वत्प्राणम᳘नु प्रा᳘णिति य᳘था वा॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That one makes an offering for Agni Śuci (the Brilliant) means that it puts virility into this (fire). That this flame blazes up; it blazes up higher only when an offering is put into this (flame). It (the fire) becomes, as it were, more virile. Therefore, one puts it (virility) into this (fire) only by means of this (offering). Therefore, they (some) say, "One need not care for it (the subsequent oblations)". One must certainly make that (subsequent offerings). This (fact) is, as it were, invisible (incomprehensible) that "this is that yonder thing and that is this". Just as, as long as the child (embryo) is not born, it breathes (lives) in consonance with the vital breath of the mother.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स य᳘दग्न᳘ये प᳘वमानाय निर्व᳘पति प्राणो वै प᳘वमानो जात᳘ ए᳘वास्मिꣳस्त᳘त्प्राणं᳘ दधाति॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That one makes the offering for Agni Pavamāna (the Blower) means that Pavamāna is the breath and he (the sacrificer) puts that into him (the fire) even as it (the fire) is born.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘दग्न᳘ये पावका᳘य निर्व᳘पत्य᳘न्नं वै᳘ पावकं᳘ जात᳘ ए᳘वास्मिꣳस्तद᳘न्नं दधाति॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he makes the offering for Agni Pāvaka (Purifier) for, food indeed is the purifier. As soon as this (fire) is born, he (the sacrificer) puts the food into this (fire).
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘दाग्नये शु᳘चये निर्व᳘पति वी᳘र्यं वा᳘ अस्मिꣳस्त᳘द्दधाति य᳘दो वा अ᳘न्नेन व᳘र्धते᳘ ऽथ वी᳘र्यं करोति त᳘देनमेतद᳘न्नेनैव᳘ वर्धयित्वा᳘थास्मिन्नेत᳘द्वी᳘र्यंꣳ शु᳘चि दधाति त᳘स्मान्नि᳘र्वपेदेव तद्वा᳘ एत᳘त्समान᳘मेव स᳘द्विप᳘र्यस्तमिव य᳘त्र ह वा᳘ अग्नि᳘र्देवे᳘भ्यो मनु᳘ष्यानुपावव᳘र्त त᳘द्धेक्षां᳘चक्रे᳘ मैव स᳘र्वेणैवात्म᳘ना देवे᳘भ्यो मनु᳘ष्यानुपा᳘वृत्सी᳘ति स तिस्रस्तनू᳘रपनि᳘दध एषु᳘ लोके᳘षु त᳘स्य यत्प᳘वमानꣳ रूपमा᳘सास्यां त᳘त्पृथिव्यां नि᳘दधे य᳘त्पावक᳘मन्त᳘रिक्षे तद्यच्छु᳘चि दिवि तद᳘थ मनु᳘ष्यानुपा᳘ववर्त त᳘दु ह᳘र्षयः प्र᳘तिबुबुधिरे ये त᳘र्हि मनु᳘ष्याणामृ᳘षय आसु᳘स्सर्वे वै᳘ नो ऽग्नि᳘रुपा᳘ववृती᳘ति त᳘स्मादेता᳘नि हवी᳘ꣳ᳘षि नि᳘रवपन्॥९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he makes the offering for Agni Śuci (the Brilliant) and it puts virility into this (fire). For when he (the fire) grows with food, then, he builds up strength. Thus making this (fire) grow by means of food, thereby he (the sacrificer) puts this bright strength into this (fire). Therefore, one must make that offering. This being alike, it (the practice of not making the subsequent offerings after Pārṇāhuti) appears to be reversed (wrong), in a matter where Agni approached men from the gods and thought, "Let me not go to the men from the gods with my full self (body)". He placed (his) three bodies in these (three) worlds; what was his Pavamāna (blowing) form he placed on this earth; what was Pāvaka (the purifier) (that he placed) in the mid region and that which was Śuci (bright) (he placed) in the sky. And then he came to the men. Those who were then sages among men realized that (and thought), "Agni has come to us not in his entire form". (Therefore) They made these offerings for him (Agni).
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स य᳘दग्न᳘ये प᳘वमानाय निर्व᳘पति᳘ यै᳘वास्यास्यां᳘ पृथिव्यां᳘ तनूस्ता᳘मेव ते᳘नाप्नोत्य᳘थ य᳘दग्न᳘ये पावका᳘य निर्व᳘पति᳘ यै᳘वास्यान्त᳘रिक्षे तनूः᳘ पावका ता᳘मेव ते᳘नाप्नोत्य᳘थ य᳘दग्न᳘ये शु᳘चये निर्व᳘पति᳘ यै᳘वास्य दिवि᳘ तनूः शु᳘चिस्ता᳘मेव ते᳘नाप्नोति तꣳ स᳘र्वं कृत्स्न᳘मग्निम᳘नपनिहितमा᳘धत्ते के᳘वलबर्हिः प्रथम᳘ꣳ᳘ हविर्भ᳘वति समान᳘बर्हींषि उ᳘त्तरे इमे वै᳘ लोका᳘ एता᳘नि हवीं᳘ꣳषि᳘ सो ऽय᳘मद्धातमा᳘मिव लोको᳘ बहुल᳘तमं इव लेला᳘यमिवेद᳘मन्त᳘रिक्षं लेला᳘येव द्यौ᳘रुभे᳘ चिदेनं प्रत्युद्गामि᳘नी भवतामि᳘ति सभार᳘तायै॥१०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

What he (the sacrificer) offers to Agni Pavamāna, by that he gets (reaches) that very body (of Agni) which is in this earth. And then what he offers for Agni Pāvaka, by that he gets at that very body (of Agni) which is in the mid region. And then what he offers for Agni Śuci, by that he verily gets at that very body (of Agni) which is in the sky. (Thus) He (the sacrificer) sets up the entire Agni who is not placed away (who is present in his fulness). The first oblation has an only (separate) seat of sacred grass and the subsequent two (oblations) have a common barhis (seat of sacred grass). These are the worlds, these are the oblations. This world (earth) is, as it were, fine and dense; the mid region appears to be oscillating and oscillating seems the sky. For (imparting) equal weightiness, so that those two (worlds) may counterpoise (may come towards) this (earth), (the last two oblations are offered with a common barhis).
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अष्टा᳘कपाला आग्नेयाः᳘ पुरोळा᳘शा भवन्त्यष्टा᳘क्षरा गायत्री᳘ गायत्र᳘मग्नेरछ᳘न्दः स्वे᳘नै᳘वैनं तच्छ᳘न्दसा᳘धत्ते ता᳘नि च᳘तुर्विंꣳशतिः सं᳘म्पद्यन्ते च᳘तुर्विंꣳशत्यक्षरा वै᳘ गायत्री᳘ गायत्र᳘मग्नेरछ᳘न्दः स्वेनैवैनं तच्छ᳘न्दसा᳘धत्ते गाय᳘त्र्यो याज्यानुवा᳘क्या भवन्ति गायत्र᳘मग्नेरछ᳘न्दः स्वे᳘नै᳘वैनं तच्छ᳘न्दसा᳘धत्ते᳘ऽथैष᳘ ए᳘वादित्य᳘श्चरु᳘रुप᳘रिष्टाद्भवति प्र᳘च्यावयत इव ह वा᳘ ए᳘षो ऽस्मा᳘न्मनुष्यलोका᳘द्देवलोक᳘म᳘भ्यात्मा᳘नं य᳘ एता᳘नि हवीं᳘ꣳषि निर्व᳘पति ऊर्ध्व᳘ इव हि᳘ समारो᳘हन्ने᳘तीय᳘मु वा अ᳘दितिरियं᳘ प्रतिष्ठा त᳘दस्या᳘मेव᳘ प्रतिष्ठा᳘यामन्ततः प्र᳘तितिष्ठति॥११॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The sacrificial cakes (intended) for Agni are cooked on eight potsherds. Gāyatrī (metre) has eight syllables (in a pāda or foot). Gāyatra is the metre of Agni. He (the sacrificer), then, establishes this (Agni) with its own metre. They (the potsherds) amount to twenty-four. Indeed Gāyatrī has twenty-four syllables (on the whole). Gāyatra is the metre of Agni. He then, sets up (the fire) with its own metre. The Yājyānuvākyas (the offering formulas and the connected expressions) are in the Gāyatrī metre. Gāyatra is the metre of Agni and he then sets up (the fire) with his own metre. Then subsequently, occurs this (offering of) cooked food dedicated to Aditi. He who prepares (and offers) these (preceding oblations) seems to make himself slip away from this world of men towards the world of the Gods. Indeed he moves, as it were, ascending above. This (earth) is Aditi. This is (the firm) resting place. Therefore, ultimately, he (the sacrificer) rests firmly on this (firm) resting place.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘स्य विरा᳘जौ संया᳘ज्ये स्यातां विरा᳘डीयमित्य᳘थो त्रिष्टु᳘भौ त्रिष्टु᳘ब्भ्यौमित्य᳘थो ज᳘गत्यौ ज᳘गती᳘ हीयमि᳘ति विरा᳘जौ᳘ त्वेव᳘ स्थितं त᳘स्य धेनुर्द᳘क्षिणा धेनु᳘रिव वा᳘ इयं᳘ मनु᳘ष्येभ्यः स᳘र्वान्का᳘मान्दुग्धे स᳘र्वान्वै का᳘मान्धेनु᳘र्दुग्धे मा᳘तेव वा᳘ इयं᳘ मनु᳘ष्याणां च पशूनां᳘ च मा᳘तेव धेनुस्त᳘स्मादस्य धेनुर्द᳘क्षिणैतन्त्वे᳘कमयनम्॥१२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Its two Samyājyas (inviting and offering formulas) are in Virāṭ (metre), for this (earth) is indeed vast (shining); and (or) two triṣṭubh verses for this (earth) is triṣṭubh indeed (three-propped) and (or) two Jagatī verses; indeed this (earth) is Jagatī (constantly moving). But anyway it remains that they are only two Virāṭ verses. For that (the offering to Aditi) the fee is a cow; for this (earth) like a cow, produces (fulfils) all desires for men. Indeed the cow fulfils all the desires (of men). This (earth) is indeed like a mother to men and animals, like the cow which is like a mother. Therefore the fee for this (offering) is a cow. This is one way (of performance).
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ द्विती᳘यमग्न᳘य ए᳘वाष्टा᳘कपालं पुरोळा᳘शं नि᳘र्वपति परो᳘क्षमिव वा᳘ एत᳘दग्न᳘ये प᳘वमानायाग्न᳘ये पावका᳘याग्न᳘ये शु᳘चय इत्या᳘थास्यैवं᳘ प्रत्य᳘क्षम᳘ञ्जसाग्नी आ᳘हितौ भवतस्त᳘स्य मूर्धन्व᳘त्यौ याज्यानुवा᳘क्ये भवतो᳘ ऽथैष᳘ ए᳘वादित्य᳘श्चरुरु᳘परिष्टात्त᳘स्यैषैव᳘ बन्धु᳘स्तैषा द᳘क्षिणा॥१३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now then (is) a second (way), one offers the cake cooked on eight potsherds only for Agni. This (offering) is, as it were, indirectly (invisibly) for Agni Pavamāna, Agni Pāvaka and Agni Śuci. Then the two Agnis of this (sacrificer) become visibly and truly established. There are two Yājyānuvākyas (one offering formula and one connected expression) containing the word 'Mūrdhan', for that (offering). Then (is offered) verily this cooked rice offering for Aditi, subsequently. This is indeed the relationship to it. This (cow) is the fee.
</details>
