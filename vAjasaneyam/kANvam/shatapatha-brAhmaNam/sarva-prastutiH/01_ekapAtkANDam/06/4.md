<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

In whatever season the performer of Cāturmāsya sacrifices goes to the yonder world that season hands him over to another season, that other one to yet another one (and so on). Thus the seasons, handing him over (from one to another), make him reach the supreme (highest) status and the supreme (highest) world. Therefore, they say, "They do not find the performer of the Cāturmāsya sacrifices; for he verily conquers the very highest (supreme) world, the highest (supreme) conquest"
