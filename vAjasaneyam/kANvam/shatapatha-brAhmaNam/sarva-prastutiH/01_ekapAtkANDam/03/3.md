## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

प्रजा᳘पतिꣳ ह वै᳘ भूतान्यु᳘पसेदुः प्रजा वै᳘ भूता᳘नि विदे᳘हि नो य᳘था जी᳘वामे᳘ति तं᳘ देवा᳘ यज्ञोपवीति᳘नो द᳘क्षिणं जान्वाच्यो᳘पसेदुस्ता᳘नहोवाच यज्ञो वो᳘ ऽन्नमित्यू᳘र्ग्वो ऽमृ᳘तं वः सू᳘र्यो वो ज्यो᳘तिरिति॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The living beings approached Prajāpau. The beings are indeed creatures. (They said), "Please ordain to us as to how we may live". Then the gods approached him, wearing the sacrificial thread (in the normal way) and bending the right knee. (He) told them, "The sacrifice is verily your food, immortality (nectar) your strength and the 'Sun' your light".
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ हैनं पित᳘रः प्राचीनाववीति᳘नः सव्यं जान्वाच्यो᳘पसेदुस्ता᳘नहोवाच मासि᳘ मासि वो᳘ ऽन्नमि᳘ति स्वधा᳘ वो मनोजवो᳘ वश्चन्द्र᳘मा वो ज्यो᳘तिरिति॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thereafter the manes approached him, with the sacred thread on the right shoulder and under the left arm and bending the left knee. He told them, "Your food (will be) (once) every month, Sradhā (will be your carrier) with the speed of your mind and the moon your light."
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ हैनंꣳ मनु᳘ष्याः प्रा᳘वृता ए᳘वोपस्थं कृत्वो᳘पसेदुस्ता᳘नहोवाच सायं᳘ प्रातर्वो᳘ ऽन्नमि᳘ति प्रजा᳘ वो मृत्यु᳘र्वो ऽग्नि᳘र्वो ज्यो᳘तिरिति॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then the human beings approached him covered (with dress) and bending their bodies. He told them, "Your food (will be) in the evening and in the morning, progeny your death and fire your light."
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ हैनं पश᳘व उपसेदुस्ते᳘भ्यो ह स्व᳘ एष᳘ च᳘कार य᳘दि यूयं᳘ काले यद्य᳘काले ल᳘भाध्वै त᳘र्ह्ये᳘वाश्नाये᳘ति त᳘स्मात्पश᳘वो य᳘दि काले यद्य᳘काले ल᳘भन्ते त᳘र्ह्ये᳘वाश्नन्ति स्व᳘ एषो᳘ ह्येभ्यः कृतः॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then the beasts approached him. To them he granted their wish (saying) "Whenever you get (any food) in time or untimely, then verily may you eat". Therefore whenever they get (any food) in proper time or out of time, then indeed do they eat. For, it was their wish that had been granted.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ हैनꣳ श᳘श्वदप्य᳘सुरा उ᳘पसेदुस्ते᳘भ्यो ह त᳘मश्च मायां᳘ च प्र᳘ददाव᳘स्ति᳘ ह्ये᳘वासुरमाये᳘तीव प᳘राभूता᳘स्त्वेव ताः᳘ प्रजास्ता᳘ इमाः᳘ प्रजास्तथो᳘पजीवन्ति य᳘थाभ्यः प्रजा᳘पतिर्व्य᳘दधात्॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And then the Asuras, too, approached him again and again. To them he gave darkness and illusion. There is certainly what is called Āsuramāyā (demonic illusion). Those creatures have really perished. These creatures so subsist as Prajāpati has ordained for them.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तन्न᳘ देवा᳘ अतिक्रा᳘मन्ति न᳘ पित᳘रो न᳘ पश᳘वो मनु᳘ष्या एवा᳘तिक्रामन्ति त᳘स्मान्मनु᳘ष्याणां यो मे᳘द्यत्य᳘शुमे मेद्यति वि हि हू᳘र्च्छति नह्य᳘यनाय चन भ᳘वत्यैनृतꣳ हि᳘ कृत्वा मे᳘द्यति त᳘स्मादु सायंप्रातरा᳘श्येव᳘ स्यात्स य᳘ एवं᳘ विद्वा᳘न्त्सायंप्रातराशी भ᳘वति स᳘र्वꣳ हैवा᳘युरेति य᳘दु ह किं᳘ च वाचा व᳘दति त᳘था हैव त᳘द्भवति देवसत्यꣳ हि गोपाय᳘ति॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That (the decree of Prajāpati) the gods do not transgress, nor do the manes, nor the animals. Only men violate (it). Therefore whoever, among men, grows fat, he grows fat in unrighteousness; he indeed cheats. He cannot move about. For he grows fat, doing falsehood. Therefore one should eat only in the evening and morning. He who knowing this eats (only) in the evening and morning, attains full (span of) life; whatever he speaks, it verily becomes so (true). For he keeps the truth of gods.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘द्वैतत्ते᳘जो ना᳘म ब्रा᳘ह्मणं᳘ यो ऽस्य᳘ शक्नो᳘ति व्रतं च᳘रितुꣳ स मासि᳘ मासि पितृ᳘भ्यो द᳘दद्यद᳘हरेष᳘ पुर᳘स्ताच्चन्द्र᳘मा नोदे᳘ति तद᳘हर्ददात्येष वै सो᳘मो रा᳘जो देवा᳘नाम᳘न्नं य᳘च्चन्द्र᳘माः स᳘ एताꣳ रा᳘त्रिं क्षीयते त᳘त्क्षीणे᳘ देवा᳘नाम᳘न्ने पितृ᳘भ्यो ददाति त᳘थैभ्यो᳘ ऽसमदं करोति᳘॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This is indeed Brahmanic lustre. He who can keep his (Prajāpati's) rule, offers (food) to the manes every month. He gives it on that day when the moon does not rise in the east (i.e. on the New Moon day). This moon is indeed King Soma, the food of the gods. This night (of the New Moon day) he dechines. When that food of gods is exhausted, he (the sacrificer) offers (food) to the manes. Thus he brings about absence of discord between them (gods and manes).
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

सो ऽपराह्णे᳘ ददाति पूर्वाह्णो वै᳘ देवा᳘नां मध्यं᳘न्दिनो मनु᳘ष्याणामपराह्णः᳘ पितॄणां त᳘स्मादपराह्णे᳘ ददाति॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He gives (food to the manes) in the afternoon. For the forenoon is of the gods and mid-day of men and the afternoon of the manes. Therefore does he give in the afternoon.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ ख᳘लु प्राचीनावीती᳘ भूत्वा᳘ जघ᳘नेन गा᳘र्हपत्यं दक्षिणा᳘सीनः सव्यं जान्वा᳘च्य तूष्णी᳘मे᳘वैतं᳘ चरुं᳘ गृह्णाति स त᳘त ए᳘वोपोत्थायो᳘त्तरेणान्वाहार्यप᳘चनं दक्षिणा ति᳘ष्ठन्न᳘वहन्ति स᳘ सकृ᳘देव᳘ फली᳘करोति सकृ᳘द्ध्येव प᳘राञ्चः पित᳘रः स य᳘देतां दि᳘शꣳ स᳘चन्त एषा हि᳘ पितॄ᳘णां दि᳘क् श्रपयि᳘त्वैतं चरुम᳘धिश्रित आ᳘ज्यं प्रत्या᳘नयत्यग्नौ वै᳘ देवे᳘भ्यो जु᳘ह्वत्युद्धृ᳘त्य मनु᳘ष्येभ्य उ᳘पनि᳘दधत्य᳘थैवं᳘ पितॄणां त᳘स्मादधिश्रित आ᳘ज्यं प्रत्या᳘नयति॥९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then, wearing the sacred thread over his right shoulder and under his left arm, being seated behind the Gārhapatya and facing south, bending his left knee, he silently takes out the caru (vessel with paddy for preparing the offering). Then getting up and standing to the north of Daksinagmi over which subsequent offerings are cooked and facing south he pounds it (the paddy for caru). Only once does he winnow it (to remove the chaff). For, once only (once for all) the fathers have passed away. That he takes to the southern quarter (is because) this is the quarter of the manes. Putting the caru on fire, he adds gher when it is cooked. Verily they offer to the gods into Agni; for men they take it off (the fire) and place it down. And for the manes (the patrs) (it is) this same way (as for men). Therefore he adds gher after cooking it (while it is still on the fire).
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

उद्वा᳘स्याग्नौ द्वे आ᳘हुती जुहोति देवान्वा᳘ एष उपा᳘वर्तते य आ᳘हिताग्निर्दर्शपूर्णमासा᳘भ्यां य᳘जते᳘ ऽथेदं᳘ पितृ᳘भ्यो दास्य᳘न्भवति स या᳘नेव᳘ देवा᳘नुपा᳘वृत्तो भ᳘वति ते᳘भ्य ए᳘वैतन्नि᳘ह्नुते स᳘ देवैर᳘तिसृष्टः पितृ᳘भ्यो ददाति स वा᳘ अग्न᳘ये चैव सो᳘माय च जुहोति स य᳘दग्न᳘ये जुहो᳘ति सर्व᳘त्र हो᳘वाग्नि᳘रन्वा᳘भक्तो᳘ऽथ यत्सो᳘माय पितृदेव᳘त्यो हि सो᳘मः॥१०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

After removing it (from the fire) he offers two offerings in the fire. For this Āhitāgani (one who has set up his fires) who makes the New Moon and the Full moon offerings returns to the gods. Then he has to offer this to the manes. He keeps this away from those gods to whom he has returned. Being permitted by the gods, he gives (the offering) to the manes. He offers to Agni and to Soma. He offers to Agni for Agni has a share in everything (offering). And then (He offers) to Soma for Soma is the deity of the manes.
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ जुहोत्यग्न᳘ये कव्य᳘वाहनाय स्वा᳘हा सो᳘माय पितृम᳘ते स्वाहे᳘त्यव्याघा᳘य मे᳘क्षणं जघ᳘नेनान्वाहार्यप᳘चनꣳ सकृ᳘देव स्फ्येनो᳘ल्लिखति त᳘द्वेदिभाजन᳘ꣳ᳘ सकृदु᳘ल्लिखति सकृ᳘द्ध्येव प᳘राञ्चः पितरः॥११॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He offers, chanting, "To Agni the conveyer of oblation to the departed ancestors, Svāhā". "To Soma associated with the pitrs (manes), Svāhā". Placing the lid of the vessel down, he makes a furrow with the wooden sword behind the fire (west of the fire) only once. That is a representation of the altar. He makes a furrow once. For the fathers have passed away once only.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अथो᳘ल्मुकं दक्षिणतो नि᳘दधाति य᳘द्ध वा अ᳘निधायो᳘ल्मुकं पितृ᳘भ्यो द᳘दत्यसुररक्षसा᳘नि हैषां तद्वि᳘मथ्नते स यदु᳘ल्मुकं निद᳘धात्यसुररक्षसानामेवा᳘पहतये॥१२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he places a fire-brand at the south. For when they offer (food) to the manes without placing the fire brand, Asuras and Rākṣasas indeed disturb that (food) of these (manes). That he places the firebrand is for driving away the Asuras and Rākṣasas.
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स नि᳘दधाति ये᳘ रूपा᳘णि प्रतिमुञ्च᳘माना अ᳘सुराः स᳘न्तः स्वध᳘या च᳘रन्ति परापु᳘रो निपु᳘रो ये भ᳘रन्त्यग्निष्टाँ᳘ल्लोकात्प्र᳘णुदात्यस्मादिति॥१३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He places (the fire brand) (chanting), "The Asuras who, assuming (many) forms move about at their will and who bear huge bodies or small bodies—may Agni drive them away from this world".
</details>


## १४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अथा᳘वनेजयत्य᳘साव᳘वनेनिक्ष्वे᳘ति य᳘जमानस्य पित᳘रम᳘साव᳘वनेनिक्ष्वे᳘ति य᳘जमानस्य पितामहम᳘साव᳘वनेनिक्ष्वे᳘ति य᳘जमानस्य प्र᳘पितामहं य᳘थाशिष्य᳘तो ऽवनेज᳘येदेव᳘मेतद्यथो᳘पमूले दिता᳘नि सकृदाच्छिन्ना᳘नि भवन्त्य᳘ग्रमिव वै᳘ देवा᳘नां म᳘ध्यमिव मनु᳘ष्याणां मू᳘लमिव पितॄणां त᳘स्मादु᳘पमूले दिता᳘नि भवन्ति सकृदाच्छिन्ना᳘नि भवन्ति सकृ᳘द्ध्येव प᳘राञ्चः पित᳘रः॥१४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he causes the (deceased) father of the sacrificer to wash his hands saying, "(Uttering the name of the father)(in the vocative)—wash (your hands)", the (deceased) grandfather of the sacrificer, "(Uttering the name of the grandfather in the vocative) wash (your hands)", and the (deceased) great grandfather of the sacrificer "(Uttering his name in the vocative) wash (your hands)". This is just as one would make those who are about to eat, wash (their hands) . They (the stalks of sacred grass) are cut near the root, cut once (only) (at one stroke). Indeed the top (top portion of the sacred grass) belongs to the gods, the middle to the men and the bottom to the manes. Therefore they are cut near the root. They are cut (only) once; for, the fathers have passed away (only) once.
</details>


## १५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ता᳘नि दक्षिणा᳘ स्तीर्त्वा ते᳘षु ददाति स इ᳘ति ददाती᳘ति वै᳘ देवे᳘भ्यो जु᳘ह्वती᳘ति मनु᳘ष्येभ्य उ᳘द्धरन्त्य᳘थैवं᳘ पितॄणां त᳘स्मादि᳘ति ददाति॥१५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Spreading them (their tops pointing) southward, he gives (food to the pitrs) on them. He gives thus because they offer (oblation) to the gods (in fire). For men, they take it out (and serve) and for pitrs (they give) thus (on sacred grass). Therefore he gives thus.
</details>


## १६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ ददात्य᳘सावेत᳘त्त इ᳘ति य᳘जमानस्य पित्रे ये᳘ च त्वा᳘मन्व᳘ञ्च इ᳘ति वा है᳘क आहुर्या᳘ꣳश्च त्व᳘मन्वञ्चसी᳘ति वा त᳘दु त᳘था न᳘ ब्रूयात्स्वय᳘ꣳ वै ते᳘षांꣳ सह ये᳘षांꣳ सहा᳘सावेत᳘त्त इ᳘त्येव य᳘जमानस्य पि᳘त्रे ऽसावेत᳘त्त इ᳘ति य᳘जमानस्य पितामहाया᳘सावेत᳘त्त इ᳘ति य᳘जमानस्य प्र᳘पितामहायेत: प᳘राङ्ददाति सकृ᳘च्छ्येव प᳘राञ्च: पितर:॥१६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He presents (the food) to the father of the sacrificer, saying, "(Name of the father in vocative), this is for you". Some say (in addition), "And those who come following you", and "Those after whom you come". One should not say it so. For, he himself is with them with whom he is. So he should present (the food) to the sacrificer's father, saying only "(name in the vocative), This is for you ", and to the sacrificer's grandfather saying, "(name in the vocative), This is for you ", and to the sacrificer's great grandfather saying, "(name in the vocative), This is for you ". Thus he presents (the food in a sequence) (moving) away from the present. For the fathers have passed away only once.
</details>


## १७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाहा᳘त्र पितरो मादयध्वं यथाभागमा᳘वृषायध्वमित्य᳘त्र पितरो यथाभाग᳘मश्रीते᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाहा᳘थ प᳘राङ् पर्या᳘वर्तते तिर᳘ इव वै᳘ पित᳘रस्तिर᳘ इवैत᳘द्भवति॥१७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he says, "Oh! fathers! regale yourselves here. Act like bulls, according to your share". In that he says (means), Oh! fathers, eat each one his share". Then he turns away (his face in the opposite direction, away from the food offered). For, the fathers are hidden (from view). This (offering) becomes, as it were, concealed (when he turns his face away).
</details>


## १८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दाहुरा त᳘मितोरासीत ता᳘वान्ह्य᳘सुरि᳘ति त᳘दु मुहुर्त᳘मे᳘वासि᳘त्त्वोपपर्या᳘वर्तेता᳘थाहा᳘मीमदन्त पित᳘रो यथाभागमा᳘वृषायिषतेत्या᳘शिष: पित᳘रो यथाभागमि᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह॥१८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They (some) say with regard to that, "He should remain (so turning away, holding his breath) till fainting. For, thus far is his vital strength". But then, he may turn round, after remaining (thus) for a very short time only. Then he says, "The fathers have regaled themselves; they have acted like bulls according to their share". He says (means) verily this, "The fathers have eaten according to their share".
</details>


## १९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थावनेजयत्य᳘साव᳘वनेनिक्ष्वे᳘ति य᳘जमानस्य पित᳘रम᳘साव᳘वनेनिक्ष्वे᳘ति य᳘जमानस्य पिताम᳘हमसाव᳘वनेनिक्ष्वे᳘ति य᳘जमानस्य प्र᳘पितामहं य᳘थो जक्षु᳘षो ऽवनेज᳘येदेव᳘मेतत्॥१९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

## २०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ नीवि᳘मुद्वृह्य न᳘मस्करोति पितृदेव᳘त्या वै᳘ नीवि᳘र्यज्ञ᳘ उ वै न᳘मो यज्ञि᳘याने᳘वैत᳘त्पितॄ᳘न्करोति षट्कृ᳘त्वो न᳘मस्करोति षड्वा᳘ ऋत᳘व ऋत᳘वो वै᳘ पित᳘र ऋतु᳘ष्वे᳘वैत᳘द्यज्ञं प्र᳘तिष्ठापयति गृहा᳘न्न: पितरो दत्ते᳘त्याहै᳘षो हैत᳘स्याशी: क᳘र्मणो गृहा᳘णामु ह वै᳘ पित᳘र ईशते त᳘स्मादाह गृहा᳘न्न: पितरो दत्तेत्य᳘थोखाम᳘वजिघ्रति स᳘ यजमानभाग: पुन: पि᳘ण्डान्सम᳘वदधत्यग्नौ᳘ सकृदाच्छिन्ना᳘न्यभ्या᳘दधाति पु᳘नरु᳘ल्मुकम᳘प्यर्जति॥२०॥

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then, loosening the tuck (of his garment) at the navel, he prostrates (in obeisance). For, the tuck at the navel is sacred to the patri. Indeed, obeisance is sacrifice. (By this) he makes these fathers worthy of worship (sacrifice). He performs obeisance six times. The seasons are six. Indeed the manes are the seasons. He establishes this sacrifice in the very seasons. He says, "Oh fathers! give us homes". This is indeed the blessing (he seeks) of this (ritual). The fathers, verily, have power over the houses. Therefore, he says, "Oh fathers! give us houses". Then he smells at the pot. That is the share of the sacrificer. They again put the rice-balls together.
