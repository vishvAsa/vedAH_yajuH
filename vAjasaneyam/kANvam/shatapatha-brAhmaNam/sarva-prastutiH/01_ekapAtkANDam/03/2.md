That (feeding of the four priests) verily is like the offering (made in Agni). He should give (as fee) in that (ritual) as much as he can afford. Some offer in Agnihotra (milk from new cow). One should not do so at all. For, thereby he makes it conflicting (between two deities of the two offerings). Indeed, different is Āgrayaṇa (the offering of first produce) and different is Agnihotra.
