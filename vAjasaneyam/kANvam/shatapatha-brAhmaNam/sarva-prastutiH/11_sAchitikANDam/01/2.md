<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He now lays down the chandasyā bricks. Now here this Agni-altar has become complete. He (the altar) now wished for Śrī (distinction). Because before this it was incomplete and was not qualified to own distinction or Śrī That is why it is said (in particular cases) that so and so person is not de- serving to have the distinction to assume the leadership of a country or village community. Gods gave these chandasyās bricks to confer Śrī (distinc- tion) to the altar. Chandasyās are same as cattle and chandasyās are food. Cattle and food constitute Śrī.
