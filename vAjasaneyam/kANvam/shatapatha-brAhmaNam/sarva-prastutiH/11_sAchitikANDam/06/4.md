<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (2/16 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)</summary>

अ᳓थ प्रातः᳓ प्रातरनुवाक᳓मुपाकरिष्य᳓न्नग्निं᳓ युनक्ति [[युक्तेन|युक्ते᳓न]] [[समश्नवा|स᳓मश्नवा]] इ᳓ति ते᳓न युक्ते᳓न स᳓र्वान् का᳓मान्त्स᳓मश्नुते ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Early morning, next day, getting ready for the morning prayer, he connects the Agni-altar thinking, "by getting this connected, I shall achieve all my desires." Accordingly, he obtains all the desires. First he connects it to the whole performance so all that is done after that becomes done on that connected fire-altar.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (1/12 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)</summary>

स᳓ [[मध्य᳓मं᳓|मध्यमं᳓]] परिधि᳓मुपस्पृ᳓श्यैत᳓द्य᳓जुर्जपत्यग्निं᳓ युनज्मि श᳓वसा घृते᳓ने᳓ति ।

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He touches the middle enclosing stick and chants this Yajus formula — "I yoke the fire with might, with ghee." Might means power; thus he means to say "the fire I yoke, with power and ghee." "The heavenly bird, huge with its vigour" — for, that Agni-altar is indeed a divine bird and great in vigour, in smoke.
</details>
