## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ वातहोमा᳘ञ्जुहोतीमे वै᳘ लोका᳘ ए᳘षोऽग्निर्वायु᳘र्वातहोमा᳘ एषु त᳘ल्लोके᳘षु वायुं᳘ दधाति तस्मादयमेषु लोकेषु वायुः ॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then offers the Vāta homas. This Agni-altar is these worlds and Vāta homas means Vāyu. So he puts wind in these worlds and hence there is wind in these worlds.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

बा᳘ह्येनाग्निमा᳘हरत्याप्तो वा᳘ अस्य स᳘ वायुर्य᳘ एषु᳘ लोकेष्व᳘थ य᳘ इमाँ᳘ल्लोकान्प᳘रेण वायुस्त᳘मस्मिन्नेत᳘द्दधाति बहिर्वे᳘देरियं वै वे᳘दिराप्तो वा᳘ अस्य स᳘ वायुर्योऽस्याम᳘थ य᳘ इमां परेण वायुस्त᳘मस्मिन्नेत᳘द्दधात्यञ्जलि᳘ना न᳘ ह्येतस्ये᳘ती᳘वाभि᳘पत्तिर᳘स्ति स्वाहाकारे᳘ण जुहो᳘ति᳘ ह्य᳘धोऽधो धु᳘रमसौ᳘ वा᳘ आदित्य᳘ एष र᳘थोऽर्वाची᳘नं त᳘दादित्या᳘द्वायुं᳘ दधाति त᳘स्मादे᳘षोऽर्वाची᳘नमेवा᳘तः पवते ॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

From outside the vedi, he brings Vāta (air). That wind which is in these worlds is already there in the altar and so he brings the air which is outside these worlds. He brings it by his two palms held together, for, it is only this way, it (wind) can be fetched. He offers it with a svāhākāra, for, he offers it just beneath the pole of the chariot. This chariot is yonder sun and thus he places the wind on this side of the sun. Hence wind blows on this side of the sun.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

समु᳘द्रोऽसि न᳘भस्वानि᳘त्यसौ वै᳘ लोकः᳘ समु᳘द्रो न᳘भस्वानार्द्र᳘दानुरि᳘त्येष᳘ ह्यार्द्रं दधाति त᳘द्योऽमु᳘ष्मिँल्लोके᳘ वायुस्त᳘मस्मिन्नेत᳘द्दधाति शंभू᳘र्मयोभू᳘रभि᳘ मा वाहि᳘ स्वाहे᳘ति शिवः᳘ स्यो᳘नोऽभि मा वाही᳘त्येत᳘त् ॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He says, "You are the windy ocean" — for, the windy ocean is no doubt, the yonder world (the sky). "You are the one who gives moisture" – for, it is he (the wind) that gives moisture. So, that wind which is in the yonder world, that he puts into it. "Blow kindly and in plenty upon me - svāhā” – which means "blow gently and in plenty."
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

मारु᳘तोऽसि मरु᳘तां गण इ᳘त्यन्तरिक्षलोको वै मारुतो᳘ मरुतां गणस्त᳘द्योऽन्तरिक्षलोके᳘ वायुस्त᳘स्मिन्नेतद्दधाति शंभू᳘र्मयोभू᳘रभि᳘ मा वाहि स्वाहे᳘ति शिवः᳘ स्यो᳘नोऽभि᳘ मा वाही᳘त्येत᳘त् ॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"You are the Maruta, the troupe of Maruts" – the Maruta and the troupe of Maruts is indeed the aerial region. Thereby he places that wind into it which is in the aerial region. “Blow kindly and in plenty upon me - svāhā” – which means "blow gently and in plenty."
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अवस्यू᳘रसि दु᳘वस्वानि᳘त्ययं वै᳘ लो᳘कोऽवस्युर्दु᳘वस्वाꣳस्त᳘द्योऽस्मिं᳘ल्लोके᳘ वायुस्त᳘मस्मिन्नेत᳘द्दधाति शंभू᳘र्मयोभू᳘रभि᳘ मा वाहि स्वाहे᳘ति शिवः स्यो᳘नोऽभि᳘ मा वाही᳘त्येत᳘त् ॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"You are the worshipable one and affording protection" - this ter- restrial world is the worshipable one and one that affords protection. Thereby he puts into it that wind which is in this world. "Blow kindly and in plenty upon me - svāhā" which means "blow gently and in plenty."
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त्रिभि᳘र्जुहोति त्र᳘य इमे᳘ लोका अ᳘थो त्रिवृ᳘दग्निर्या᳘वानग्निर्या᳘वत्यस्य मा᳘त्रा ता᳘वत्तै᳘व त᳘देषु᳘ लोके᳘षु वायुं᳘ दधाति ॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He makes offerings with three mantras, for, these worlds are three; and Agni is threefold. As great as Agni is, as great as is his size, with so much he thus puts air into these worlds.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

य᳘द्वेव᳘ वातहोमा᳘ञ्जुहो᳘त्येत᳘मे᳘वैतद्र᳘थं युनक्त्येतद्वै᳘ देवा᳘ एतꣳ र᳘थꣳ स᳘र्वेभ्यः का᳘मेभ्योऽयुञ्जत युक्ते᳘न स᳘मश्नवामहा इ᳘ति ते᳘न युक्ते᳘न स᳘र्वान्कामान्त्स᳘माश्नुवत त᳘थै᳘वैतद्य᳘जमान एतꣳ रथꣳ स᳘र्वेभ्यः का᳘मेभ्यो युङ्क्ते᳘ युक्ते᳘न स᳘मश्नवा इ᳘ति ते᳘न युक्ते᳘न स᳘र्वान्का᳘मान्त्समश्नुते ॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

As to why he makes the Vāta homas is—thereby he yokes that chariot of his. It was by them that the gods yoked that chariot for obtaining of all their desires, thinking “let us achieve all our desired objects by yoking it.” Similarly, this Yajamāna yokes that chariot for obtaining all his desires, think- ing “with it yoked, let me obtain.” By yoking it, he achieves all his desired objects.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

वातहोमैर्यु᳘नक्ति प्राणा वै᳘ वातहोमाः᳘ प्राणै᳘रे᳘वैनमेत᳘द्युनक्ति त्रिभि᳘र्युनक्ति त्र᳘यो वै᳘ प्राणाः᳘ प्राण᳘ उदानो᳘ व्यानस्तै᳘रैवैनमेत᳘द्युनक्ति ह᳘स्ताभ्याꣳ ह᳘स्ताभ्याꣳ हि यो᳘ग्यं युञ्जन्त्य᳘धोऽधो धु᳘रम᳘धोऽधो हि धु᳘रं यो᳘ग्यं युञ्ज᳘न्ति विपरिक्रा᳘मं विपरिक्रा᳘मꣳ हि यो᳘ग्यं युञ्ज᳘न्ति ॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He yokes it by means of the Vāta homas. The Vāta homas are indeed prāṇas and it is with the prāṇas he yokes it. He yokes with three oblations, because there are of course, three prāṇas, namely prāṇa, udāna and vyāna· and with them he yokes. He offers with his hands, for, it is with the hands, the horses are yoked (to a chariot). Below the pole (of the chariot) he offers, because the horse is yoked below the shaft. Moving round he offers, because the yoking (of the horse) is done by going round (the pole).
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ दक्षिणायुग्य᳘मेवा᳘ग्रे युनक्त्य᳘थ सव्यायुग्य᳘म᳘थ दक्षिणाप्रष्टि᳘मेवं᳘ देव᳘त्रेतर᳘था मानुषे तं नाभि᳘युञ्ज्यान्ने᳘द्युक्तमभियुन᳘जानी᳘ति वा᳘हनं तु᳘ दद्याद्युक्ते᳘न भुनजा इ᳘ति त᳘मुप᳘र्येव᳘ हर᳘न्त्याध्वर्यौ᳘रावसथा᳘दुप᳘रि᳘ ह्येष त᳘मध्वर्य᳘वे दधाति स हि ते᳘न करो᳘ति तं तु द᳘क्षिणानां काले᳘ऽनुदिशेत᳘ ॥९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He first yokes the right-side main horse (offers the first Vāta homa on the right side), then the left-side main horse (offers the second Vāta homa on the left side) and then the right-side horse (the third Vāta homa on the right side). This is the order in the case of the divine beings. The human practice is the other way. Let him not yoke that chariot again (with actual horses). By doing so he will be yoking the chariot already yoked (since the Vāta homas themselves are the horses). Let him give away the chariot think- ing “I will have the benefit of the yoking (since it is yoked).” They take it as far as the Adhvaryu’s residence holding it high, because above is that (Agni). He entrusts it to the Adhvaryu, for, it is he who handles it. Let him not give it away to him. At the time he distributes dakṣiṇā, he gives it away.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अथ रुड्मतीर्जुहोत्य᳘त्रैष स᳘र्वोऽग्निः स᳘ꣳ᳘स्कृत स᳘ एषो᳘ऽत्र रु᳘चमैच्छत्त᳘स्मिन्देवा᳘ एता᳘भी रु᳘ड्मतीभी रु᳘चमदधुस्त᳘थैवास्मिन्नय᳘मेत᳘द्दधाति ॥१०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he offers the Ruṇmati oblations. Here the whole Agni-altar was fashioned. He now desired to be shining (ruc) and the gods, by means of these shining oblations endowed him with brilliance. In the same way, this Yajamāna now bestows brilliance to him.
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

य᳘द्वेव रु᳘ड्मतीर्जुहो᳘ति प्रजा᳘पतेर्विस्रस्ताद्भुगु᳘दक्रामत्तं य᳘त्र देवाः᳘ सम᳘स्कुर्वꣳ स्त᳘दस्मिन्नेता᳘भी रुड्मतीभी रु᳘चमदधुस्त᳘थै᳘वास्मिन्नय᳘मेत᳘द्दधाति ॥११॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Why he offers the Ruṇmati oblations—from out of Prajāpati who got disjointed, brilliance came out. When the gods restored him, they by means of these Ruṇmati offerings restored his brilliance. In the like man- ner, this Yajamāna endows brilliance unto him.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

या᳘स्ते अग्ने सू᳘र्ये रु᳘चो या᳘ वो देवाः सू᳘र्ये रु᳘चो रु᳘चं नो देहि ब्राह्मणेष्वि᳘ति रु᳘चꣳ रु᳘चमि᳘त्य᳘मृतत्वं वै रु᳘ग्मृतत्वमे᳘वास्मिन्नेत᳘द्दधाति तिस्र आ᳘हुतीर्जुहोति त्रिवृ᳘दग्निर्या᳘वानग्निर्या᳘वत्यस्य मा᳘त्रा ता᳘वतै᳘वास्मिन्नेतद्रु᳘चं दधाति ॥१२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“O Agni, what brilliance of yours is in the sun.” “O gods, what bril- liance of yours is — are in the sun — give that brilliance in our Brahmans (priesthood).” In this mantra thus he repeats the word “rucam” each time. Brilliance is immortality and thereby he bestows immortality unto him. He makes three offerings, for, Agni is threefold. As great as Agni is, as great as is his size, that much brilliance he bestows unto him.
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ वारुणीं᳘ जुहोत्य᳘त्रैष स᳘र्वोऽग्निः स᳘ꣳ᳘स्कृतः स᳘ एषो᳘ऽत्र व᳘रुणो दे᳘वता त᳘स्मा एत᳘द्धवि᳘र्जुहोति त᳘देनꣳ हवि᳘षा देव᳘तां करोति य᳘स्यै वै᳘ देव᳘तायै हवि᳘र्गृह्य᳘ते सा᳘ देव᳘ता न सा य᳘स्यै न᳘ गृह्य᳘ते वारु᳘ण्यार्चा स्वे᳘नै᳘वैनमेत᳘दात्म᳘ना स्व᳘या देव᳘तया प्रीणाति ॥१३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then makes the offering to Varuṇa. The whole Agni that has been fashioned now is the deity Varuṇa. So he makes this offering of havis. With this oblation he makes him (Agni) a deity. That for which havis is prepared, that alone is a deity and not the one for whom it is not prepared. He offers it with Ṛk addressed to Varuṇa. Thus he gratifies him (Agni) by his own self and by his own deity.
</details>


## १४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

य᳘द्वेव᳘ वारुणीं᳘ जुहो᳘ति प्रजा᳘पतेर्विस्रस्ता᳘द्वी᳘र्यमु᳘दक्रामत्तं य᳘त्र देवा᳘ सम᳘स्कुर्वꣳस्त᳘दस्मिन्नेत᳘या वी᳘र्यमदधुस्त᳘थै᳘वास्मिन्नय᳘मेत᳘द्दधाति वारु᳘ण्यार्चा क्षत्रं वै᳘ व᳘रुणो वी᳘र्यं वै᳘ क्षत्रं वी᳘र्येणैवा᳘स्मिन्नेत᳘द्वीर्यं दधाति ॥१४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

As to why he offers the offering to Varuṇa — from Prajāpati who was disjointed, the strength came out. When the gods replenished him, they put back that strength into him by means of this Vāruṇa offering. Similarly, this Yajamāna puts that strength into this (Agni). He offers with a Ṛk ad- dressed to Varuṇa; for, Varuṇa is Kṣatra (nobility) and strength means Kṣatra. Thus he bestows strength by means of strength.
</details>


## १५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘त्त्वा यामि ब्र᳘ह्मणा व᳘न्दमान इ᳘ति त᳘त्त्वा याचे ब्र᳘ह्मणा व᳘न्दमान इ᳘त्येतत्तदा᳘शास्ते यज᳘मानो हवि᳘र्भिरि᳘ति त᳘दयमा᳘शास्ते य᳘जमानो हवि᳘र्भिरि᳘त्येतदु᳘हेलमानो वरुणेह बोधीत्य᳘क्रुध्यन्नो वरुणेह᳘ बोधी᳘त्येतदुरुशꣳस मा न आ᳘युः प्र᳘मोषीरित्यात्म᳘नः परिदां᳘ वदते ॥१५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He says, “I appeal to you, worshipping you with Vedic prayer” mean- ing, “I beseech you by praying with this Vedic prayer.” “This Yajamāna ap- peals to you with offering of havis”, meaning “this Yajamāna is requesting you with offerings.” “O Varuṇa listen here, without getting annoyed.” Mean- ing — “without getting angry, listen to this our request.” “O ruler of vast (territory), do not take our life from us” – thus he announces his surren- der.
</details>


## १६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थार्काश्वमेध᳘योः स᳘न्ततीर्जुहोत्ययं वा᳘ अग्नि᳘र᳘र्कोऽसा᳘वादि᳘त्योऽश्वमेधस्तौ᳘ सृष्टौ ना᳘नै᳘वास्तां तौ᳘ देवा᳘ एता᳘भिरा᳘हुतिभिः स᳘मतन्वन्त्स᳘मदधुस्त᳘थैवै᳘नावय᳘मेत᳘देता᳘भिरा᳘हुतिभिः स᳘न्तनोति सं᳘दधाति ॥१६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then offers the oblations called Santatis of Arka and Aśvamedha (those that join together the fire and the Aśvamedha). This Agni-altar is indeed the light and Aśvamedha is of course, yonder sun. When produced, they were separate. The gods knit the two together by these offerings and united them. In the same manner, this Yajamāna knits those two together with these offerings and unites them.
</details>


## १७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स्वर्ण᳘ घर्मः स्वाहे᳘त्यसौ᳘ वा᳘ आदित्यो᳘ घ᳘र्मोऽमुं त᳘दादित्य᳘मस्मि᳘न्नग्नौ घर्मस्त᳘दमुमादित्य᳘मस्मि᳘न्नग्नौ प्र᳘तिष्ठापयति ॥१७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Heaven-like heat, svāhā” – the heat or gharma is yonder sun; thus he establishes yonder sun in the Agni-altar.
</details>


## १८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स्वर्णर्कः स्वाहे᳘त्ययमग्नि᳘रर्कस्त᳘दिम᳘मग्निममु᳘ष्मिन्नादित्ये प्र᳘तिष्ठापयति ॥१८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Heaven-like light, svāhā" — the light is indeed this Agni-altar and thus he establishes this light in yonder sun.
</details>


## १९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स्वर्णशुक्रः स्वाहे᳘त्यसौ वा᳘ आदित्यः᳘ शुक्रस्तं पु᳘नर᳘मुत्र दधाति ॥१९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Heaven-like brilliance, svāhā" — brilliance, no doubt is yonder sun. He thus places him on the Agni-altar.
</details>


## २०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स्वर्णज्यो᳘तिः स्वाहे᳘त्यय᳘मग्निर्ज्योतिस्तं पु᳘नरिह᳘ दधाति ॥२०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Heaven-like glow, svāhā" — glow is indeed this Agni and thus he places him here.
</details>


## २१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स्वर्णसू᳘र्यः स्वाहेत्यसौ वा᳘ आदित्यः सू᳘र्यस्त᳘दमु᳘मादित्य᳘मस्य स᳘र्वस्योत्तमं᳘ दधाति त᳘स्मादे᳘षोऽस्य स᳘र्वस्योत्तमः प᳘ञ्चैता आ᳘हुतीर्जुहोति प᳘ञ्चचितिकोऽग्निः प᳘ञ्चर्त᳘वः संवत्सरः᳘ संवत्स᳘रोऽग्निर्या᳘वानग्निर्या᳘वत्यस्य मा᳘त्रा ता᳘वतै᳘वैनावेतत्सं᳘तनोति सं᳘दधाति य᳘द्वेवा᳘ह स्वर्ण᳘घर्मः स्वा᳘हा स्वर्णर्कः स्वाहे᳘त्य᳘स्यै᳘वैता᳘न्यग्नेर्नामा᳘नि ता᳘न्येत᳘त्प्रीणाति ता᳘नि हवि᳘षा देव᳘तां करोति य᳘स्यै वै᳘ देव᳘तायै हवि᳘र्गृह्य᳘ते सा᳘ देव᳘ता न सा य᳘स्यै न गृह्यते᳘ऽथो एता᳘ने᳘वैत᳘दग्नी᳘नस्मि᳘न्नग्नौ᳘ नामग्रा᳘हं दधाति प᳘ञ्चैता आ᳘हुतीर्जुहोति प᳘ञ्चचितिकोऽग्निः प᳘ञ्चर्तवः संवत्स᳘रः संवत्स᳘रोऽग्निर्या᳘वानग्निर्या᳘वत्यस्य मा᳘त्रा ता᳘वतै᳘वेनमेतद᳘न्नेन प्रीणाति ॥२१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Heaven-like Sūrya, svāhā" — Sūrya is doubtless, yonder sun. He thus establishes yonder sun, as the ultimate, because he is the highest of all. Thus he offers these five oblations; for, Agni-altar is of five layers, the year consists of five seasons and the year is Agni-altar. As great as Agni is, as great as is his size, with so much he thus knits together (the sun and Aśvamedha) and unites them. When he said, "Heaven-like heat" and "Heaven-like light", he refers to the names of this Agni-altar. They please him and they are made into deities by the havis. That for which the havis is prepared that alone is the deity and not the one for which it is not prepared. Thus he establishes these Agni-deities themselves into this fire - altar by calling them by their names. Why he offers five of these oblations is be- cause the Agni-altar is of five layers. The year is of five seasons and year is Agni-altar. As great as Agni is, as great as is his size, by so much food, he thus pleases him.
</details>


## २२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अथात आ᳘हुतीनामे᳘वाव᳘पनस्य यां कां᳘ च ब्रा᳘ह्मणवतीमा᳘हुतिं विद्यात्ता᳘मेत᳘स्मि᳘न् काले᳘ जुहुयात्का᳘मेभ्यो वा᳘ एतꣳ र᳘थं युङ्क्ते तद्यां कां चात्रा᳘हुतिं जुहो᳘त्याप्तां ता᳘ꣳ᳘ सतीं᳘ जुहोति ॥२२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now about the insertion (positioning) of the (other) offerings. If he knows of any oblations suggested by (indicated by) a Brāhmaṇa text, that he can offer at this time. For, he yokes this chariot for obtaining his wishes and whatever oblation he now offers, it is (as good as) a fulfilled wish.
</details>


## २३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दाहुर्न᳘ जुहुयान्ने᳘दतिरेच᳘यानी᳘ति स वै᳘ जुहुया᳘देव का᳘मेभ्यो वा᳘ एता आ᳘हुतयो हूयन्ते न वै का᳘मानामतिरिक्तमस्ति ॥२३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Some say, these (other) oblations should not be offered, since they will be in excess. Let him offer them. Because it is for (fulfilment of) special desires, these oblations are offered and there is no question of excess in the case of desires. (Second Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>
