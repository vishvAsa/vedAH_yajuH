<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

These Dhiṣṇyās are fires and thus he builds up fire-altars. They are like the Viś (followers) to the main Agni-altar which is like the Kṣatra (the royal head). Thus he sets up the Kṣatra and the Viś. He first builds up him (Kṣatra, the Agni-altar) and then these Viś (Dhiṣṇyās, the followers). That (Agni-altar) is only one; thus he makes the royal authority abide in one person. The others (Dhiṣṇyās) are numerous; thus there are many subjects. That (Agni-altar) is made of five layers and the others are single layered.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He now encloses each of the Dhiṣṇyās with the pariśrits (enclosing sticks). Pariśrits are indeed waters and it is with waters he encloses them. He merely places them all around. The waters which flow out through a chan- nel are the Kṣatra and these stagnating waters are the Viś (people). In the case of the Agni-altar, he encloses it with pariśrit stones dug in (fixed in the ground); thereby surrounds Kṣatra and adds Kṣatra (royal power) to Kṣatra (royalty). By just keeping these pariśrits around the Dhiṣṇyās, he surrounds the Viś with Viś and thus adds the people to the people.
