<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (1/37 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)</summary>

संवत्सरो᳓ वै᳓ यज्ञः᳓ प्रजा᳓पतिस्त᳓स्यैत᳓द्द्वा᳓रं य᳓दमावास्या᳙ चन्द्र᳓मा [[ए᳓व᳓|एव᳓]] द्वारपिधा᳓नः स᳓ यो᳙ऽमावास्या᳙यामग्नी᳓ आधत्ते᳓ य᳓था वि᳓वृतायां द्वारि᳓ द्वारा᳓ पु᳓रं प्रप᳓द्येत स᳓ त᳓त एव᳓ स्वर्गं᳓ लोक᳓मिया᳓देवं᳓ त᳓द्यो᳙ऽमावास्या᳙यामाधत्ते᳓ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Surely Prajāpati is the year, that is the sacrifice. The new-moon night is its opening and the moon itself is the bolt of the door. When one kindles the two fires on amāvāsyā, it is just as one would reach a city through the gateway when the doors are open. He who kindles (the fires) on amāvāsyā, reaches the heaven.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>
