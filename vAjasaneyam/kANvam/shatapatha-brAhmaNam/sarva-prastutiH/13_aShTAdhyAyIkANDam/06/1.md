## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

भृ᳘गुर्ह वै वा᳘रुणिर्व᳘रुणं पित᳘रं विद्यया᳘तिमेने त᳘द्ध व᳘रुणो विदा᳘ञ्चकारा᳘ति वै मा विद्य᳘या मन्यत इ᳘ति ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Bhrgu, the son of Varuṇa, considered himself superior to his father in knowledge. Varuṇa came to know that and thought, "He considers himself superior to me in knowledge."
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ होवाच प्राङ् पुत्रक व्र᳘जतात्त᳘त्र यत्प᳘श्येस्त᳘दृष्ट्वा᳘ दक्षिणा᳘ व्रजतात्त᳘त्र यत्प᳘श्येस्तदृष्ट्वा᳘ प्रत्य᳘ग्व्रजतात्त᳘त्र यत्प᳘श्येस्त᳘ट्दृष्ट्वो᳘दग्व्रजतात्त᳘त्र यत्प᳘श्य्येस्त᳘ट्दृष्ट्वैत᳘योः पू᳘र्वयोरु᳘ त्तरम᳘न्ववान्तरदेशं᳘ व्रजतात्त᳘त्र यत्प᳘श्येस्त᳘न्म आ᳘चक्षीथा इ᳘ति ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He told (Bhrgu), "My son, first go eastwards and having observed there what you might see, you proceed southwards; having seen there what you might see, you go westwards; having observed what you might come across there, you go northwards and having seen what you might see there, proceed to the intermediate direction to the north (north-east) and tell me what you shall see there.”
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ ह त᳘त एव प्रा᳘ङ्प्रवव्राजै᳘दु पु᳘रुषैः पु᳘रुषान्प᳘र्वाण्येषां पर्वशः᳘ संव्र᳘श्चं पर्वशो᳘ विभ᳘जमानानीदं त᳘वेदं ममे᳘ति स᳘ होवाच भीष्मं᳘ बत भोः पुरु᳘षान्न्वा᳘ एतत्पु᳘रुषाः प᳘र्वाण्येषां पर्वशः᳘ संव्र᳘श्चं पर्वशो᳘ व्यभक्षते᳘ति ते᳘ होचुरित्थं वा᳘ इ᳘मेऽस्मा᳘नमु᳘ष्मिँल्लो᳘केऽसचन्त ता᳘न्वय᳘मिद᳘मिह प्र᳘तिसचामहा इ᳘ति स᳘ होवाचा᳘स्तीह प्रा᳘यश्चिती३ रि᳘त्यस्ती᳘ति काऽति पिता᳘ ते वेदेति ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He accordingly went towards the eastern direction and there men were cutting men, chopping off one by one their limbs and saying (among themselves), "This to me, this to you." He (Bhrgu) exclaimed, "What a hor- rible thing! men here have chopped off men limb by limb and are sharing (among them)." They (the men who were cutting) said, "This is how, in- deed, these men dealt with us in the yonder world and so we now return that (treatment) to them." He (Bhrgu) asked, "Is there not any atonement for this?" "Yes, there is." They said, "Your father knows it."
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ ह त᳘त एव᳘ दक्षिणा प्र᳘वव्राजै᳘दु पु᳘रुषै पु᳘रुषान्प᳘र्वाण्येषां पर्वशः᳘ संक᳘र्तं पर्वशो᳘ विभ᳘जमानानीदं त᳘वेदं ममे᳘ति स᳘ होवाच भीष्मं᳘ बत भोः पुरु᳘षान्नवा᳘ एतत्पु᳘रुषाः प᳘र्वाण्येषां पर्वशः᳘ संक᳘र्तं पर्वशो᳘ व्यभक्षते᳘ति ते᳘ होचुरित्थं वा᳘ इमेऽस्मा᳘नमु᳘ष्मिँल्लोकेऽसचन्त ता᳘न्वय᳘मिद᳘मिह प्र᳘तिसचामहा इ᳘ति स होवाचा᳘स्तीह प्रा᳘यश्चित्ती३ रि᳘त्यस्ती᳘ति काऽति पि᳘तैव᳘ ते वेदेति ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then proceeded to the southern direction. There men were cut- ting men, chopping off limb by limb and saying, "This to me; this you." He (Bhrgu) exclaimed, "What a horrible thing! men here have chopped off men limb by limb and sharing (among them)." They said, "This is how, indeed these men dealt with us in the yonder world and so we now return that (treatment) to them." He asked, "Is there not any atonement for this?" "Yes, there is," they said, "Your father knows it."
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ ह त᳘त एव᳘ प्रत्यङ्प्र᳘वव्राजैदु पु᳘रुषैः पु᳘रुषाꣳस्तूष्णीमा᳘सीनाꣳ स्तूष्णीमा᳘सीनैरद्य᳘मानान्त्स᳘ होवाच भीष्मं᳘ बत भोः᳘ पु᳘रुषान्न्वा᳘ एतत्पु᳘रुषास्तूष्णीमा᳘सीनाꣳस्तूष्णीमा᳘सीना अदन्ती᳘ति ते᳘ होचुरित्थं वा᳘ इ᳘मेऽस्मानमु᳘ष्मिँल्लोकेऽसचन्त᳘ तान्वय᳘मिदमिह प्र᳘तिसचामहा इ᳘ति स᳘ होवाचा᳘स्तीह प्रा᳘यश्चिती३ रि᳘त्यस्ती᳘ति का᳘ऽति पि᳘तैव᳘ ते वेदे᳘ति ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then went towards the western direction. There men sitting quiet were being eaten by men, sitting still. He exclaimed, "What a horrible thing. Men sitting quiet are eating men sitting quiet." They replied, "This is how, indeed, these men dealt with us in the yonder world and so we now return that (treatment) to them." He asked, "Is there not any atonement for it?" "Yes, there is,” they said, "Your father knows it."
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ ह त᳘त एवो᳘दङ्प्र᳘वव्राजै᳘दु पु᳘रुषैः पुरुषानाक्रन्द᳘यत आक्रन्द᳘यद्भि᳘रद्य᳘मानान्त्स᳘ होवाच भीष्मं᳘ बत᳘ भोः पु᳘रुषान्न्वा᳘ एतत्पु᳘रुषा आक्रन्द᳘यत आक्रन्दयन्तोऽदन्ती᳘ति ते᳘ होचुरित्थं वा᳘ इ᳘मेऽस्मानमु᳘ष्मिँल्लो᳘केऽसचन्त ता᳘न्वय᳘मिद᳘मिह प्र᳘तिसचामहा इ᳘ति स᳘ होवाचा᳘स्तीह प्रा᳘यश्चित्ती३ रित्यस्तीति काऽति पि᳘तैव᳘ ते वेदे᳘ति ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He thereafter proceeded northwards. There men, shouting aloud were being eaten by men crying aloud. He exclaimed, “What a horrible thing! Here men shouting aloud are eating men who are crying aloud.” They replied, “This is how, indeed, these men dealt with us in the yonder world and so we now return that (treatment) to them.” He asked, “Is there not any atonement for it?” “Yes, there is,” they said, “your father knows it.”
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ ह त᳘त ए᳘वैत᳘योः पू᳘र्वयोरु᳘त्तरम᳘न्ववान्तरदेशं प्र᳘वव्राजै᳘दु स्त्रि᳘यौ कल्याणीं चा᳘तिकल्याणीं च ते अ᳘न्तरेण पु᳘रुषः कृष्णः᳘ पिङ्गाक्षो᳘ दण्ड᳘पाणिस्तस्थौ त᳘ꣳ᳘ हैनं दृष्ट्वा भी᳘र्विवेद स हे᳘त्य सं᳘विवेश त᳘ꣳ᳘ ह पि᳘तोवाचा᳘धीष्व स्वाध्यायं क᳘स्मान्नु᳘ स्वाध्यायं ना᳘धीष इ᳘ति स᳘ होवाच किम᳘ध्येष्ये न कि᳘ञ्च᳘नास्ती᳘ति तद्ध व᳘रुणो विदां चकारा᳘द्राग्वा इ᳘ति ॥ ७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then proceeded to the intermediate direction to the north (north- east). There were two women, one handsome and the other more handsome. A man stood between them dark in complexion, yellow-eyed, with a stick in his hand. On seeing him, he (Bhrgu) got scared and re- turned home and sat (quiet). On seeing him (idle), his father said, “Do your daily study (of scriptures). Why you are not studying your lessons?” He replied (in despair), “What shall I study ! there is nothing to study.” Then Varuṇa knew that he has indeed seen it (seen what he wanted to see, hence the despondency).
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ होवाच यान्वै तत्प्रा᳘च्यां दिश्य᳘द्राक्षीः पु᳘रुषैः पु᳘रुषान् प᳘र्वाण्येषां पर्वशः᳘ संव्र᳘श्चं पर्वशो᳘ विभ᳘जमानानिदं त᳘वेदं ममे᳘ति व᳘नस्प᳘तयो वै ते᳘ अभूवन्त्स यद्व᳘नस्प᳘तीनाꣳ समि᳘धमाद᳘धाति ते᳘न व᳘नस्प᳘तीनवरुन्धे᳘ तेन व᳘नस्प᳘तीनां लोकं᳘ जयति ॥ ८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He (Varuṇa) said, Those men whom you saw in the eastern region being cut by men chopping off limb by limb and saying “This to you, this me” by way of sharing, were the trees. When one puts the samidhās (col- lected) from the trees on (the fire) he thereby subjugates the trees and conquers the kingdom of trees.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ या᳘नेतद्द᳘क्षिणायां दिश्य᳘द्राक्षीः पु᳘रुषैः पु᳘रुषान्प᳘र्वाण्येषां पर्वशः᳘ संक᳘र्तं पर्वशो᳘ विभ᳘जमानानिदं त᳘वेदं ममे᳘ति पश᳘वो वै ते᳘ अभूवन्त्स यत्प᳘यसा जुहो᳘ति ते᳘न पशून᳘वरुन्धे ते᳘न पशूनां᳘ लोकं᳘ जयति ॥ ९ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Those men whom you saw in the southern region, being cut by chop- ping off limb by limb and saying “This to me, this to you” by way of sharing, were the cattle. When one makes offerings (in the fire) with milk, he subju- gates the cattle and conquers the world of cattle.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ या᳘नेत᳘त्प्रती᳘च्यां दिश्य᳘द्राक्षीः पु᳘रुषैः पु᳘रुषाꣳस्तूष्णीमा᳘सीनाꣳस्तूष्णी मा᳘सीनैरद्य᳘मानानो᳘षधयो वै ता᳘ अभूवन्त्स यत्तृ᳘णेनावद्योत᳘यति तेनौ᳘षधीर᳘वरुन्धे तेनौ᳘षधीनां लोकं᳘ जयति ॥ १० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Those men whom you saw in the west, sitting quiet were being eaten by men sitting still, were the herbs. When one lights up (shows a burning darbha over) the Agnihotra milk with a (lighted) grass, he subju- gates the herbs and conquers the world of herbs.”
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ या᳘नेत᳘दु᳘दीच्यां दिश्य᳘द्राक्षीः पु᳘रुषैः पु᳘रुषानाक्रन्द᳘यत आक्रन्द᳘यद्भिरद्य᳘मानाना᳘पो वै ता᳘ अभूवन्त्स य᳘दपः᳘ प्रत्यान᳘यति ते᳘नापो᳘ऽवरुन्धे ते᳘नापां᳘ लोकं᳘ जयति ॥ ११ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Those men whom you saw in the northern direction where men who were shouting were being eaten by men who were shouting, were the waters. When he brings back the waters, he subjugates the waters and con- quers the world of the waters.”
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ ये᳘ एते स्त्रि᳘याव᳘द्राक्षीः कल्याणीं चा᳘ति कल्याणीं च सा या᳘ कल्याणी सा᳘ श्रद्धा स यत्पू᳘र्वमा᳘हुतिं जुहोति ते᳘न श्रद्धाम᳘वरुन्धे ते᳘न श्रद्धां जयत्य᳘थ या᳘तिकल्याणी सा᳘ऽश्रद्धा स यर्दु᳘त्तरामा᳘हुतिं जुहो᳘ति तेना᳘श्रद्धाम᳘वरुन्धे तेना᳘श्रद्धां जयत्य᳘थ य᳘ एने सो᳘ऽन्तरेण पु᳘रुषः कृष्णः᳘ पिङ्गाक्षो᳘ दण्ड᳘पाणिर᳘स्तस्थात्क्रो᳘धो वै᳘ सोऽभूत्स य᳘त्स्रु᳘च्यप᳘ आनी᳘य निन᳘यति ते᳘न क्रोधम᳘वरुन्धे ते᳘न क्रो᳘धं जयति स यो᳘ हैवं᳘ विद्वा᳘नग्निहोत्रं᳘ जुहो᳘ति ते᳘न स᳘र्वं ज᳘यति स᳘र्वम᳘वरुन्धे ॥ १२ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Now about the two women whom you saw, the one handsome and the other more handsome – the handsome one is faith. When one makes the first offering (of the Agnihotra), he subjugates faith and conquers faith. The more handsome one is unbelief. When one offers, the second obla- tion (of Agnihotra) he subjugates unbelief and conquers unbelief (i.e. he loses faith in doing unholy acts). Now the man whom you saw between the two ladies, dark in complexion and yellow-eyed, with a stick in hand, he was anger; when having poured water in the spout of the sruk and pours it (into the fire), he subjugates anger and conquers anger. Surely, one who know- ing this, performs Agnihotra, he conquers everything and subjugates everything.” (First Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>
