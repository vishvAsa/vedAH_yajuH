## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तद्य᳘था ह वा᳘ इद᳘ꣳ᳘ रथचक्रं᳘ वा कौलालचक्रं वा᳘ प्रतिष्ठितं क्र᳘न्देदेव᳘ꣳ᳘ है᳘वेमे᳘ लोका अ᳘ध्रुवा अ᳘प्रतिष्ठिता आसुः ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Even as a chariot wheel or a potter’s wheel will break if not steadied (by an axle), these worlds were, indeed unfixed and unsteadied.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ ह प्रजा᳘पतिरीक्षांचक्रे कथं᳘ न्विमे᳘ लोका᳘ ध्रुवाः᳘ प्रतिष्ठिताः स्युरि᳘ति स᳘ एभि᳘श्चैव प᳘र्वतैर्नदी᳘भिश्चेमा᳘मदृꣳहद्व᳘योभिश्च म᳘रीचिभिश्चान्त᳘रिक्षं जीमू᳘तैश्च न᳘क्षत्रैश्च दि᳘वꣳ स म᳘ह इ᳘ति व्याहरत्पश᳘वो वै म᳘हस्त᳘स्माद्य᳘स्यैते᳘ बह᳘वो भ᳘वन्ति भू᳘यिष्ठमस्य कु᳘ले महीयन्ते बह᳘वो ह वा᳘ अस्यैते᳘ भवन्ति भू᳘यिष्ठꣳ हास्य कु᳘ले महीयन्ते त᳘स्माद्य᳘द्येनमाय᳘तनाद्बा᳘धेरन्वा प्रा वा याप᳘येयुरग्निहोत्र᳘ꣳ᳘ हुत्वा म᳘ह इत्यु᳘पतिष्ठेत प्र᳘ति प्रज᳘या पशु᳘भिस्तिष्ठति᳘ नाय᳘तनाच्च्यवते ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Prajāpati then contemplated, “How can these worlds be fixed and steadied ?” By means of the mountains and rivers, he stabilised this earth; by means of the birds and sun’s rays, the aerial region and by means of the clouds and stars, the sky. He (having stabilised them) exclaimed, “mahaḥ.” “maha” means cattle. That is why he who has plenty of these (cattle), his household is held in great esteem. This one (Yajamāna) indeed, comes to possess plenty of them and hence his household is held in great esteem. If he (the Yajamāna) is being driven out of his house or made to vacate, let him after performing Agnihotra, stand in veneration saying “mahaḥ.” He thereby gets steadied with offspring and cattle and is not thrown out of his house. (First Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>
