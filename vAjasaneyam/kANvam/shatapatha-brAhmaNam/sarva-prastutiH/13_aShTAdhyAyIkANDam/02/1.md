## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

संवत्सरो᳘ यज्ञः स यो᳘ ह वै᳘ संवत्सरो᳘ यज्ञ इ᳘ति वेदा᳘न्ते है᳘वास्य संवत्सर᳘स्येष्टं᳘ भवत्य᳘थो यत्कि᳘ञ्च संवत्सरे᳘ क्रिय᳘ते स᳘र्वꣳ है᳘वास्य त᳘दाप्तम᳘वरुद्धमभि᳘जितं भवति ॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Verily the year is sacrifice. One who, knowing that the year is sacri- fice, offers sacrifice at the end of the year, by him all that is done in the year becomes attained, secured and won for him.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ऋत᳘व ऋत्वि᳘जः स यो᳘ ह वा᳘ ऋत᳘व ऋत्वि᳘ज इ᳘ति वेदा᳘न्ते है᳘वास्यर्तूना᳘मिष्टं᳘ भवत्य᳘थो यत्कि᳘ञ्चर्तुषु क्रिय᳘ते स᳘र्वꣳ हैवास्य त᳘दाप्तम᳘वरुद्धमभि᳘जितं भवति ॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The officiating priests are the seasons. One who, knowing that the officiating priests are the seasons and offers sacrifice at the end of the sea- sons, by him, all that is done in the seasons, becomes attained, secured, and won for him.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

मा᳘सा हवी᳘ꣳ᳘षि स यो᳘ ह वै मा᳘सा हवीꣳषी᳘ति वेदा᳘न्ते है᳘वास्य मा᳘सानामिष्टं᳘ भवत्य᳘थो यत्कि᳘ञ्च मा᳘सेषु क्रिय᳘ते स᳘र्वꣳ है᳘वास्य त᳘दाप्तम᳘वरुद्धमभि᳘जितं भवति ॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The oblations are the months, one who, knowing that the oblations are the months and offers sacrifice at the end of the months, by him all that is done in the month becomes attained, secured and won for him.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अर्धमासा᳘ हविष्पात्रा᳘णि स यो᳘ ह वा᳘ अर्धमासा᳘ हविष्पात्राणी᳘ति वेदा᳘न्ते है᳘वास्यार्धमासा᳘नामिष्टं᳘ भवत्य᳘थो यत्कि᳘ञ्चार्धमासे᳘षु क्रिय᳘ते स᳘र्वꣳ है᳘वास्य त᳘दाप्तम᳘वरुद्धमभि᳘जितं भवति ॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The oblation vessels are the fortnights. One who, knowing that the oblation vessels are the fortnights, offers sacrifice at the end of the fort- nights, by him, all that is done in the half-months becomes attained, se- cured and won for him.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अहोरात्रे᳘ परिवेष्ट्री स यो᳘ ह वा᳘ अहोरात्रे᳘ परिवेष्ट्री इ᳘ति वेदा᳘न्ते है᳘वास्याहोरात्र᳘योरिष्टं᳘ भवत्य᳘थो यत्कि᳘ञ्चाहोरात्र᳘योः क्रिय᳘ते स᳘र्वꣳ है᳘वास्य त᳘दाप्तम᳘वरुद्धमभि᳘जितं भवति ॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The pariveṣṭrīs (the pair of fire-tongs) are the day and night; one who, having known that the pariveṣṭrīs are the day and night; and offers sacrifice at the end of the day and night, by him all that is done in the day and night becomes attained, secured and won for him.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

इय᳘मेव᳘ प्रथमा᳘ सामिधे᳘न्यग्नि᳘र्द्विती᳘या वायु᳘स्तृती᳘यान्तरिक्षं᳘ चतुर्थी द्यौः पञ्च᳘म्यादित्यः षष्ठी᳘ चन्द्रमाः सप्तमी म᳘नोऽष्टमी वा᳘ङ्नवमी तपो दशमी ब्र᳘ह्मैकाद᳘श्येता᳘ हि वा᳘ इदꣳ स᳘र्वꣳ समिन्ध᳘त ए᳘ता᳘भिरिदꣳ स᳘र्वꣳ स᳘मिद्धं त᳘स्मात्सा᳘मिधे᳘न्यो ना᳘म ॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The first sāmidhenī (kindling formula) is this earth; the second one is the fire; the third, the Vāyu; the fourth, the aerial region; the fifth one, the sky; the sixth, the sun; the seventh is the moon; the eighth is the mind; ninth the speech; the tenth is intense tapas and the eleventh is the Brahman. For, it is these that kindle, all this (universe) and by them all this is kindled. That is why these are called sāmidhenīs or kindling formulae.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वै त्रिः᳘ प्रथमम᳘न्वाह स य᳘त्प्रथम᳘मन्वा᳘ह प्रा᳘चीं ते᳘न दि᳘शं जय᳘ति य᳘द्द्विती᳘यं द᳘क्षिणां ते᳘न दि᳘शं जयति यत्तृती᳘य᳘मूर्ध्वा᳘मेव ते᳘न दि᳘शं जयति ॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He recites the first (sāmidhenī) three times, By reciting it first time, he conquers the eastern region; by the second time he gains the southern region and by the third recitation he gains the upper region.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त्रि᳘र्वे᳘वोत्तमाम᳘न्वाह स यत्प्रथम᳘मन्वा᳘ह प्रती᳘चीं ते᳘न दि᳘शं जयति य᳘द्द्विती᳘यमु᳘दीचीं ते᳘न दि᳘शं जयति य᳘त्तृती᳘यमिमा᳘मेव ते᳘न प्रतिष्ठां᳘ जयत्येव᳘मु हाभिरिमाँल्लोकाञ्ज᳘यत्येता दि᳘शः ॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He recites the last (sāmidheni) three times. By reciting it first time, he conquers the western region; by the second time, he wins the northern region and by the third recitation he gains this (earth) itself as a resting place. Thus by these sāmidhenis, he gains these worlds and these regions.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ऋत᳘मेव पू᳘र्व आघारः सत्यमु᳘त्तरो᳘ऽव ह वा᳘ ऋतसत्ये᳘ रुन्धे᳘ऽथो यत्कि᳘ञ्चर्तसत्या᳘भ्यां ज᳘य्यꣳ स᳘र्वꣳ हैव त᳘ज्जयति ॥९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The first āghāra (libation of ghee) is ṛta, the Eternal Law; the second one is satya or Truth and by these (ghee libations), he attains both ṛta and satya and he now gains all that is attainable by ṛta and satya.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त्वि᳘षिरेव᳘ प्रथ᳘मः प्र᳘याजो᳘ऽपचितिर्द्वितीयो य᳘शस्तृती᳘यो ब्रह्मवर्चसं᳘ चतु᳘र्थोऽन्ना᳘द्यं पञ्चमः स᳘ प्रथमं᳘ प्रया᳘जमनुमन्त्रयेत त्विषिमान्भूयासमित्य᳘पचिति᳘मान्भूयासमिति द्विती᳘यं यशस्वी भूयासमि᳘ति तृती᳘यं ब्रह्मवर्चसी᳘ भूयासमि᳘ति चतुर्थ᳘मन्नादो भूयासमि᳘ति पञ्चमं त्वि᳘षिमान्ह वा अ᳘पचितिमान्यशस्वी᳘ ब्रह्मवर्च᳘स्यन्नादो᳘ भवति य᳘ एवमे᳘तद्वेदैत᳘द्ध स्म वैत᳘द्विद्वा᳘नाह श्वेत᳘केतुरारुणेयः क᳘ꣳ᳘स्विदे᳘वापरी᳘षुमहानाग᳘मिवाभिस᳘ꣳसारं दिदृक्षिता᳘रो य᳘ एव᳘मेत᳘ प्रयाजा᳘नां य᳘शो वेदिते᳘ति ॥१०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The first prayāja (fore-offering) is effulgence; the second one is honour; the third is fame; the fourth is brahminical lustre and the fifth is food etc. The first prayāja is to be followed by his uttering, "Let me become effulgent"; the second to be followed by uttering, "Let me be honoured"; the third "let me be famous", the fourth "let me gain brahminic lustre" and the fifth to be followed by uttering "let me enjoy prosperity." Verily whoso- ever knows this becomes effulgent, honoured, famous, possessed of brahminical lustre and prosperous. Now knowing this, Śvetaketu Āruṇeya, once said, "People from all directions will one day converge on that one who knows that the greatness of the prayājas (fore-offerings) as if they are eager to see a divine cobra."
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

भूत᳘मेव पू᳘र्व आ᳘ज्यभागो भविष्यदु᳘त्तरो᳘ऽव ह वै᳘ भूतं᳘ च भविष्य᳘च्च रुन्धे᳘ऽथो यत्कि᳘ञ्च भूते᳘न च भविष्यता᳘ च ज᳘य्यꣳ स᳘र्वꣳ हैव त᳘ज्जयति ॥११॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The first ājyabhāga is no doubt the past, the second, the future and whatever is attainable by past and future, all that he now gains.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ब्र᳘ह्माग्नेयः᳘ पुरोळा᳘शः स यो᳘ ह वै ब्र᳘ह्माग्नेयः᳘ पुरोळा᳘श इ᳘ति वेदा᳘व ह ब्र᳘ह्म रुन्धे᳘ऽथो यत्कि᳘ञ्च ब्र᳘ह्मणाꣳ ज᳘य्यꣳ स᳘र्वꣳ हैव त᳘ज्जयति ॥१२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The purodāśa offered to Agni is the Brahman (priesthood) and whosoever knows Agni's purodāśa as Brahman, secures for himself the priest- hood and whatever is attainable by priesthood he gains all that.
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

क्षत्र᳘मुपाꣳशुयाजः स यो᳘ ह वै क्षत्र᳘मुपाꣳशुयाज इ᳘ति वेदा᳘व ह क्षत्र᳘ꣳ᳘ रुन्धे᳘ऽथो यत्कि᳘ञ्च क्षत्रे᳘ण ज᳘य्यं स᳘र्वꣳ हैव त᳘ज्जयति तद्य᳘दुपाꣳशुयाजं᳘ कुर्वन्त्ये᳘केनै᳘के त᳘स्मादुच्चै᳘श्चोपाꣳशु᳘ च क्षत्राया᳘चक्षते ॥१३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The upāmśuyāja (offering made in low voice) is kṣatra (martial valour). Whoever knows the upāṁśuyāja is kṣatra secures for himself the martial valour and whatever is attainable by martial valour, he gains all that. Some per- form it as upāṁśuyāja (in low voice) and other make the offering in loud voice and that also is for kṣatra so they say.
</details>


## १४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

विळु᳘त्तरः पुरोळा᳘शः स यो᳘ ह वै᳘ विळु᳘त्तरः पुरोळा᳘श इ᳘ति वेदा᳘व ह वि᳘शꣳ रुन्धे᳘ऽथो यत्कि᳘ञ्च क्षत्रे᳘ण ज᳘य्यं स᳘र्वꣳ हैव त᳘ज्जयति तद्यदाग्नेय᳘श्च पुरोळा᳘श उपाꣳशुयाज᳘श्च पू᳘र्वौ भ᳘वतस्त᳘स्मादुभे ब्र᳘ह्म च क्षत्रं᳘ च विशि प्र᳘तिष्ठिते ॥१४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The second purodāśa is the viś (common folk). Whosoever knows the second purodāśa as viś, secures for himself (the mastery over) the viś, whatever is attainable by the commonfolk, he gains all that. In so far as the purodāśa for Agni and the upāmśuyāga come first, therefore the priesthood (Brahma) and martial class (kṣatra) are established on the viś (common folk).
</details>


## १५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

राष्ट्र᳘ꣳ᳘ सान्नाय्यꣳ स यो᳘ ह वै᳘ राष्ट्र᳘ꣳ᳘ सान्नाय्यमि᳘ति वेदा᳘व ह राष्ट्र᳘ꣳ᳘ रुन्धेऽथो यत्कि᳘ञ्च राष्ट्र᳘ꣳ᳘ राष्ट्रे᳘ण ज᳘य्यꣳ स᳘र्वꣳ हैव᳘ तज्जयति तद्य᳘त्सन्न᳘यन्त्ये᳘केनै᳘क तस्माद्राष्ट्रꣳ सञ्चैति वि᳘ चा ॥१५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The sānnāya (a preparation of fresh milk and curd) is rāṣṭra (na- tionhood). Whosoever knows the sānnāya as nationhood, secures himself the (control of) rāṣṭra and whatever is attainable by nationhood, he gains all that. Some mix (milk and some curd) together and others do not. There- fore the rāṣṭra is both in unitary form and in a federal form.
</details>


## १६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘पः स्विष्टकृत्स यो᳘ ह वै त᳘पः स्विष्टकृदि᳘ति वेदाव ह त᳘पो रुन्धे᳘ऽथो यत्कि᳘ञ्च त᳘पसा ज᳘य्यꣳ स᳘र्वꣳ हैव त᳘ज्जयति ॥१६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The sviṣṭakṛt (the well-offered offering) is tapas (austerity). Whoso- ever knows the sviṣṭakṛt as tapas, secures for himself the tapas and whatever is attainable by tapas, he gains all that.
</details>


## १७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

लोकः᳘ प्राशित्रꣳ स यो᳘ ह वै᳘ लोकः᳘ प्राशित्रमि᳘ति वेदा᳘व ह लोक᳘ꣳ᳘ रुन्धे᳘ऽथो यत्किञ्च लोकं जय्यꣳ स᳘र्वꣳ हैव त᳘ज्जयति᳘ नो ह ल᳘वेन लोका᳘द्व्यथते ल᳘वेन ह वा᳘ अमु᳘ष्मिꣳल्लोके᳘ लोका᳘द्व्यथन्ते᳘ऽथ य᳘ एवं वे᳘द स᳘ ह बहु᳘ चन पापं᳘ कृत्वा᳘ लोका᳘द्व्यथते ॥१७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The prāśitra (the fore-portion on avadāna of that name) is the loka (heavenly abode). Whosoever knows prāśitra as the loka, secures himself the abode in heaven and whatever is attainable by the loka, all that he gains. Indeed he does not fall even a little from his abode. Even for a little, people fall from their heavenly abode. But this one (who knows this that prāśitra is loka) will not fall even if he had committed great sin. (First Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>
