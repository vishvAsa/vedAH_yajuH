<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He (Prajāpati) said If (it fails) due to Ṛk, take four ladle full of ghee and offer it in the Gārhapatya, chanting "Bhūḥ”. If it is due to Yajus, four ladle-full of ghee should be offered in the Āgnīdhrīya altar or Anvāhārya Vacana chanting "Bhūvah." If it fails due to Sāmaveda, four ladle-full of ghee should be offered in the Āhavanīya altar, chanting "Svah." If you cannot identify as to which of the three Vedas is the cause for failure, you should offer at the Āhavanīya chanting all the three (Bhūḥ, Bhūvaḥ and Svaḥ). In this way the Ṛgveda is healed by the Ṛk, the Yajurveda by the Yajus and the Sāmaveda by the Sāman even as one would join together the broken parts of the limbs (with their corresponding parts). This is how he rectifies the broken sacrifices. If he were to heal it in some other way, it would be like putting together two different parts or would be like applying some poison in the place of an ointment to a broken limb. Therefore the Brahmā should be one who knows thus (procedure of treatment) and not one who does not know.
