## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

प्रजा᳘पतिर्वा᳘ इदम᳘ग्र आसीदे᳘क एव᳘ सोऽकामयत स्यां प्र᳘जायेये᳘ति᳘ सोऽश्राम्यत्स त᳘पोऽतप्यत त᳘स्माच्छ्रान्ता᳘त्तेपानात् ॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

In the beginning Prajāpati was alone here and he desired, "Let me survive and proliferate.” He underwent austerities and got heated in penance. From him thus toiled, and got heated –
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त्र᳘यो लोका᳘ असृज्यन्त पृथि᳘व्यन्त᳘रिक्षं द्यौः स᳘ इमाꣳस्त्रीं᳘ल्लोकान᳘भि᳘तताप ते᳘भ्यस्तप्ते᳘भ्यः ॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

– the three worlds got created; the earth, the aerial region and the sky. He heated the three worlds and from those thus heated –
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त्री᳘णि ज्यो᳘तीꣳष्यजायन्ताग्नि᳘र्योऽयं प᳘वते सू᳘र्यः स᳘ इमा᳘नि त्री᳘णि ज्यो᳘तीꣳष्यभि᳘तताप ते᳘भ्यस्तप्ते᳘भ्यः ॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

– three luminous objects were born, viz. the fire, the one who blows (Vāyu) and the sun. He heated these three luminous bodies and from those thus heated –
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त्र᳘यो वे᳘दा अजायन्ताग्ने᳘रृग्वेदो᳘ वायो᳘र्यजुर्वेदः सू᳘र्यात्सामवेदः स᳘ इमाꣳस्त्रीन्वे᳘दानभि᳘तताप ते᳘भ्यस्तप्ते᳘भ्यः ॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

– the three Vedas appeared. The Ṛgveda from Agni; the Yajurveda from Vāyu and the Sāmaveda from the Sun. He heated these three Vedas and from those thus heated –
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त्री᳘णि शुक्रा᳘ण्यजायन्त भूरि᳘त्यृग्वेदाद् भु᳘व इ᳘ति यजुर्वेदा᳘त्स्वरि᳘ति सामवेदात्त᳘दृग्वेदे᳘नैव᳘ हौत्रम᳘कुर्वत यजुर्वेदेना᳘ध्वर्यवꣳ सामवेदे᳘नोद्गीथं य᳘देव᳘ त्रय्यै᳘ विद्या᳘यै शुक्रं ते᳘न ब्रह्मत्वमथो᳘च्चक्राम ॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

– three glowing flames (essence) were born; Bhūḥ from Ṛgveda; Bhuvaḥ from Yajurveda; and Svaḥ from Sāmaveda. The hautra (the duties of the Hotā priest) were derived from the Ṛgveda; the ādhvaryava (the duties of the Adhvaryu) were drawn from the Yajurveda and the udgīta (the duties of the Udgātā priest) were derived from the Sāmaveda and what pure es- sence (which remained) of all the three was available, from that he derived the duties of Brahmā.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते᳘ देवाः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिमब्रुवन् य᳘दि न ऋक्तो᳘ वा यजि᳘ष्टो वा सामतो᳘ वा यज्ञो ह्व᳘लेत्के नैनं भिषज्येमे᳘ति ॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Those gods asked Prajāpati, "If our sacrifice is to fail, due to the Ṛk or the Yajus or the Sāman, with what shall we heal it?”
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ होवाच य᳘दृक्तो भूरि᳘ति चतुर्गृहीतमा᳘ज्यं गृहीत्वा गा᳘र्हपत्ये जुहवथ य᳘दि य᳘जिष्टो भु᳘व इति चतुर्गृहीतमा᳘ज्यं गृही᳘त्वाग्नीध्री᳘ये जुहवथान्वाहार्यप᳘चने वा हविर्यज्ञे य᳘दि सामतः᳘ स्वरि᳘ति चतुर्गृहीतमा᳘ज्यं गृही᳘त्वाहवनी᳘ये जुहवथ यद्यु अ᳘विज्ञातमस᳘त्सर्वाण्यनुद्रु᳘त्याहवनी᳘ये जुहवथ त᳘दृग्वेदे᳘नै᳘वर्ग्वेदं᳘ भिषज्य᳘ति यजुर्वेदे᳘न यजुर्वेद᳘ꣳ᳘ सामवेदे᳘न सामवेदꣳ स य᳘था प᳘र्वणा प᳘र्व सन्दध्यादे᳘ꣳ᳘ हैव स स᳘न्दधाति य᳘ एता᳘भिर्भिषज्यत्य᳘थ यो हा᳘तोऽन्ये᳘न भिषज्य᳘ति य᳘था शीर्णेन शीर्ण᳘ꣳ᳘ सन्धि᳘त्सेद्य᳘था वा शीर्णे᳘ गर᳘मभिनिदध्या᳘देवं तत्त᳘स्मादेवंवि᳘दमेव᳘ ब्रह्मा᳘णं कुर्वीत ना᳘नेवंविदम् ॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He (Prajāpati) said If (it fails) due to Ṛk, take four ladle full of ghee and offer it in the Gārhapatya, chanting "Bhūḥ”. If it is due to Yajus, four ladle-full of ghee should be offered in the Āgnīdhrīya altar or Anvāhārya Vacana chanting "Bhūvah." If it fails due to Sāmaveda, four ladle-full of ghee should be offered in the Āhavanīya altar, chanting "Svah." If you cannot identify as to which of the three Vedas is the cause for failure, you should offer at the Āhavanīya chanting all the three (Bhūḥ, Bhūvaḥ and Svaḥ). In this way the Ṛgveda is healed by the Ṛk, the Yajurveda by the Yajus and the Sāmaveda by the Sāman even as one would join together the broken parts of the limbs (with their corresponding parts). This is how he rectifies the broken sacrifices. If he were to heal it in some other way, it would be like putting together two different parts or would be like applying some poison in the place of an ointment to a broken limb. Therefore the Brahmā should be one who knows thus (procedure of treatment) and not one who does not know.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तदाहुर्य᳘दृचा᳘ हौत्रं᳘ क्रिय᳘ते य᳘जुषा᳘ध्वर्यवꣳ सा᳘म्नोद्गीथो᳘ऽथ के᳘न ब्रह्मत्वमि᳘त्यन᳘या त्रय्या᳘ विद्ययेति ह ब्रूयात् ॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

If somebody should ask, "When the hautra is done with Ṛk, the ādhvarya is done with Yajus and udgītha is done with Sāman, with what should the deities of Brahmā be made of?". One should say with all the three vidyās. (Eighth Brāhmaṇa Ends.) (Chapter Five Ends.)
</details>
