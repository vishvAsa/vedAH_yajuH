<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That (havis) to Sarasvatī is this (ring) finger and that one to Pūṣā is this (little finger). This joint above the hand (wrist) is the oblation to the Maruts and this (elbow) is the oblation to the Viśvadevas. This joint between the shoulder and the arm is the havis to heaven and earth. Since the offering to heaven and earth is indistinct, this (joint) is also indistinct.
</details>
