That (havis) to Sarasvatī is this (ring) finger and that one to Pūṣā is this (little finger). Since the offering to heaven and earth is indistinct, this (joint) is also indistinct.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
C R Swaminathan

The right leg of his is of Varuṇapraghāsa. Its five oblations which it has in common (with the other Cāturmāsya sacrifices) are these five toes and the havis to Indra-Agni is the knuckles.
