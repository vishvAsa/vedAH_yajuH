## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अथातो हवि᳘षः स᳘मृद्धिः ष᳘ड्ढ वै ब्र᳘ह्मणो द्वा᳘रोऽग्नि᳘र्वायुरा᳘पश्चन्द्र᳘मा विद्यु᳘दादित्यः᳘ ॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now (we discuss) about the appropriateness of oblations — Indeed there are six doors to the world of Brahman. They are Agni, Vāyu, waters, moon, lightening and sun.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स य उ᳘पदग्धेन हवि᳘षा यजतेऽग्नि᳘ना ह स ब्र᳘ह्मणो द्वा᳘रेण प्र᳘तिपद्यते᳘ सोऽग्नि᳘ना ब्र᳘ह्मणो द्वा᳘रेण प्रतिप᳘द्य ब्र᳘ह्मणः सा᳘युज्यꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयति ॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He who makes offering with burnt oblation, enters the Brahman world through the door of fire and having entered through the door of Agni, he becomes one with Brahman and wins a place in the same world.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ यो वि᳘पतितेन हवि᳘षा य᳘जते वायु᳘ना स ह ब्र᳘ह्मणो द्वा᳘रेण प्र᳘तिपद्यते स᳘ वायु᳘ना ब्र᳘ह्मणो द्वा᳘रेण प्रतिप᳘द्य ब्र᳘ह्मणः सा᳘युज्यꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयति ॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He who makes offering with the oblation that has fallen on the ground, enters through the door of Vāyu and having entered through the door of Vāyu, becomes one with Brahman and shares the world of Brahman.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ यो᳘ऽशृतेन हवि᳘षा य᳘जतेऽद्भि᳘र्ह स ब्र᳘ह्मणो द्वा᳘रेण प्र᳘तिपद्यते᳘ सोऽद्भि᳘र्ब्रह्मणो द्वा᳘रेण प्रतिप᳘द्य ब्र᳘ह्मणः सा᳘युज्यꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयति ॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He who offers oblation with uncooked havis, enters the Brahman world through the door of water and having entered through the door of water, becomes one with Brahman and shares the world of Brahman.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य उ᳘परक्तेन हवि᳘षा यजते चन्द्र᳘मसा ह स ब्र᳘ह्मणो द्वा᳘रेण प्र᳘तिपद्यते स᳘ चन्द्र᳘मसा ब्र᳘ह्मणो द्वारेण प्रति᳘पद्य ब्रह्मणः सा᳘युज्यꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयति ॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He who offers brownish oblations, enters the Brahman world through the door of the moon and having entered through the door of the moon, becomes one with Brahman and shares the world of Brahman.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ यो लो᳘हितेन हविषा य᳘जते विद्यु᳘ता ह स ब्र᳘ह्मणो द्वा᳘रेण प्र᳘तिपद्यते स᳘ विद्यु᳘ता ब्र᳘ह्मणो द्वा᳘रेण प्रतिप᳘द्य ब्र᳘ह्मणः सा᳘युज्यꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयति ॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He who offers oblations with havis darkly browned (or reddened), enters the Brahman world through the door of lightening and having en- tered through the door of lightening becomes one with Brahman and shares the world of Brahman.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ यः सु᳘शृतेन हवि᳘षा य᳘जत आदित्ये᳘न ह स ब्र᳘ह्यणो द्वा᳘रेण प्र᳘तिपद्यते स᳘ आदित्ये᳘न ब्र᳘ह्मणो द्वा᳘रेण प्रतिप᳘द्य ब्र᳘ह्मणः सा᳘युज्यꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयति सैषा᳘ हवि᳘षः स᳘मृद्धिः स यो᳘ हैव᳘मेता᳘ꣳ᳘ हविषः स᳘मृद्धिं वे᳘द सर्व᳘समृद्धेन है᳘वास्य हवि᳘षेष्टं᳘ भवति ॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He who makes offering with well-cooked oblations, he enters the Brahman world through the sun and having entered through the door of the sun becomes one with Brahman and shares the world of Brahman. This much about the appropriateness of oblations. He who knows thus, the appropriateness of havis, when he makes sacrificial offering, it becomes offered by wholly appropriate havis oblations. (Fourth Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>
