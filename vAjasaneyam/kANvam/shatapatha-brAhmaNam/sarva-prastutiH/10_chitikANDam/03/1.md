## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तृती᳘यां चि᳘तिमु᳘पदधात्येतद्वै᳘ देवा᳘ द्विती᳘यां चि᳘तिं चित्वा᳘ समा᳘रोहन्य᳘दूर्ध्वं᳘ पृथिव्या᳘ अर्वाची᳘नमन्त᳘रिक्षादेव त᳘त्सꣳस्कृ᳘त्य समा᳘रोहन् ॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He builds up the third layer. This is the layer which gods ascended after building the second one and it is above the earth and below the aerial region. That is what they fashioned and ascended.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते᳘ऽब्रुवꣳश्चेत᳘यध्वमि᳘ति चि᳘तिमिच्छते᳘ति वाव त᳘दब्रुवन्नित᳘ ऊर्ध्वमिच्छते᳘ति ते चेत᳘यमाना अन्त᳘रिक्षमेव᳘ बृहतीं᳘ तृती᳘यां चि᳘तिमपग्र्यꣳस्ते᳘भ्य एष᳘ लो᳘कोऽछन्दयत् ॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They said (to themselves), “Contemplate”, by which they meant ‘plan a layer upwards from here.’ By contemplating, they discovered the aerial region itself as the large third layer. That world did please them.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘ इन्द्राग्नी अब्रुवन्युवं᳘ न इमां᳘ तृती᳘यां चि᳘तिमु᳘पधामि᳘ति किं᳘ नौ त᳘तो भविष्यती᳘ति युव᳘मेव᳘ नः श्रे᳘ष्ठौ भविष्यथ इ᳘ति तथे᳘ति ते᳘भ्य एता᳘मिन्द्रा᳘ग्नी᳘ तृती᳘यां चि᳘तिमु᳘पाधत्तां त᳘स्मादाहुरि᳘न्द्राग्नी᳘ एव देवा᳘नाꣳ श्रे᳘ष्ठावि᳘ति ॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They said to Indra and Agni “You both lay down this layer for us”. They asked, “What benefit will it cause to us?” (Gods said) “You two shall be the foremost among us.” “Be it so,” (they agreed). Those two, Indra and Agni laid down that third layer for them and that is why, Indra and Agni are the foremost among gods.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वा᳘ इन्द्राग्निभ्यामुपद᳘धाति विश्व᳘कर्मणा सादयतीन्द्राग्नी᳘ च वै᳘ विश्व᳘कर्मा चैतां᳘ तृती᳘यां चि᳘तिम᳘पश्यꣳस्त᳘स्मादिन्द्राग्नि᳘भ्यामुपद᳘धाति विश्व᳘कर्मणा सादयति ॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He (Yajamāna) similarly lays it down with the help of Indra and Agni and settles it with (the help of) Viśvakarmā. Because this third layer was discovered by Indra, Agni and Viśvakarmā, he lays it down with the help of Indra and Agni and settles it with the help of Viśvakarmā.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

य᳘द्वे᳘वेन्द्राग्निभ्यामुपद᳘धाति विश्व᳘कर्मणा साद᳘यति प्रजा᳘पतिं वि᳘स्रस्तं देव᳘ता आदा᳘य व्यु᳘दक्रामꣳस्त᳘स्येन्द्राग्नी᳘ च विश्व᳘कर्मा च म᳘ध्यमादा᳘योत्क्रम्यातिष्ठꣳस्ता᳘नब्रवीदुपमे᳘तत्प्र᳘ति म एत᳘द्ध᳘त्त येन मे यूय᳘मुद᳘क्रमिष्टे᳘ति किं᳘ नस्त᳘तो भविष्यती᳘ति युष्मद्दे᳘वत्यमेव᳘ म एत᳘दात्म᳘नो भविष्यती᳘ति तथे᳘ति त᳘दस्मिन्नेत᳘दिन्द्राग्नी᳘ च विश्व᳘कर्मा च प्र᳘त्यदधुस्त᳘द्यैषा᳘ मध्यमा᳘ स्वयमातृ᳘ण्णैत᳘दस्य त᳘दात्म᳘नस्तद्य᳘देताम᳘त्रोपद᳘धाति य᳘दे᳘वास्यै᳘षात्म᳘नस्त᳘दस्मिन्नेतत्प्र᳘तिदधाति त᳘स्मादेतामत्रो᳘पदधाति ॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And again why he lays it down by means of Indra and Agni and settles it by means of Viśvakarmā is this — when Prajāpati got exhausted, the gods took him (in parts) and dispersed in different directions. Indra, Agni and Viśvakarmā, collected his middle part and kept going away. He (Prajāpati) told them — “Come to me and return to me that (part of mine) with which you are going away.” They asked, “What will accrue to us by that?” “That (part of) my body shall have you as its presiding deities.” They said, “Yes” and restored that part unto him. So this middle part of the body is the naturally perforated brick in the centre. When he now lays down that (svayamātṛṇṇā) brick, he (the Yajamāna) is restoring that part of the body of his. That is why he now lays down that (brick).
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

इ᳘न्द्राग्नी अ᳘व्यथमानामि᳘ष्टकां दृꣳहतं युवमि᳘ति य᳘थैव य᳘जुस्त᳘था ब᳘न्धुः पृष्ठे᳘न द्यावापृथिवी᳘ अन्तरिक्षं च वि᳘बाधस इ᳘ति पृष्ठे᳘न ह्येषा द्या᳘वापृथिवी᳘ अन्तरिक्षं च वि᳘बाधते ॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“O Indra and Agni ! You make that brick firm so that it does not tilt” — as the text, so the meaning. “You pressurise the earth, the sky and the aerial region with your back” — because this svayamātṛṇṇā brick indeed, pressurises the earth, the sky and the aerial region.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

विश्व᳘कर्मा त्वा सादयत्वि᳘ति विश्व᳘कर्मा᳘ ह्येतां᳘ तृती᳘यां चि᳘तिमपश्यदन्त᳘रिक्षस्य च पृष्ठे व्य᳘चस्वतीं प्र᳘थस्वतीमि᳘त्यन्त᳘रिक्षस्य᳘ ह्येत᳘त्पृष्ठं व्य᳘चस्वत्प्र᳘थस्वदन्त᳘रिक्षं येछान्तरिक्षं दृꣳहान्त᳘रिक्षं मा᳘ हिꣳसीरि᳘त्यात्मा᳘नं यच्छात्मानं यच्छान्तरिक्षं दृꣳहात्मा᳘नं मा᳘ हिꣳसीरित्येतद्वि᳘श्वस्मै प्राणा᳘यापाना᳘य व्याना᳘योदाना᳘ये᳘ति प्राणो वै᳘ स्वयमातृ᳘ण्णा स᳘र्वस्मा उ वा᳘ एत᳘स्मै प्राणः᳘ प्रतिष्ठा᳘यै चरि᳘त्राये᳘तीमे वै᳘ लोकाः᳘ स्वयमातृ᳘ण्णा इम उ लोकाः᳘ प्रतिष्ठा᳘ चरि᳘त्रं वायु᳘ष्ठाभि᳘पात्वि᳘ति वायु᳘ष्टाभि᳘गोपायत्वि᳘त्येत᳘न्मह्या᳘ स्वस्त्ये᳘ति महत्या᳘ स्वस्त्ये᳘त्येत᳘च्छर्दि᳘षा श᳘न्तमेने᳘ति यच्छर्दिः श᳘न्तमं तेने᳘त्येत᳘त्सादयित्वा सू᳘ददोहसा᳘धिवदति त᳘स्योक्तो ब᳘न्धुर᳘थ सा᳘म गायति त᳘स्योप᳘रि ब᳘न्धुः ॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“May Viśvakarmā settle you” — because it is indeed Viśvakarmā who discovered this layer. “You the large and big on the back of the aerial region” — for, this (brick) is indeed the large and big back of the aerial region. “You support this aerial region, make it firm and do not injure the aerial region.” “For all the prāṇa, apāna, vyāna and udāna” — for, the svayamātṛṇṇā is indeed prāṇa (vital air) and the vital air is for everything here — for settling and for moving. The naturally perforated brick is these worlds and these worlds are indeed, the settling place and the moving space. “May Vāyu protect you” that is, let Vāyu safeguard you. “With great prosperity” meaning ‘with great well-being’. “With most peaceful protection” meaning ‘with such protection which is most peaceful.’ Having settled it he chants the Sūdadohasa upon it. Its significance has been told. Then he sings a Sāman, the purport of it will be explained later.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ दि᳘श्या उ᳘पदधाति दि᳘शो वै दि᳘श्या दि᳘श ए᳘वैतदु᳘पदधाति तद्या᳘भिरदो᳘ वायु᳘र्दिग्भिर᳘नन्तर्हिता᳘भिरुपैता एतास्ता᳘ ए᳘वैतदु᳘पदधाति ता᳘ उ ए᳘वामूः पुर᳘स्ताद्दर्भस्तबं᳘ च लोगेष्टकाश्चो᳘पदधात्यसौ वा᳘ आदित्य᳘ एता᳘ अमुं त᳘दादित्यं᳘ दि᳘क्ष्वध्यू᳘हति दिक्षु᳘ चिनोति ता यत्त᳘त्रैव स्यु᳘र्बहिर्धा त᳘त्स्युर्बहि᳘र्धो वा᳘ एतद्यो᳘नेरग्निकर्म य᳘त्पुरा᳘ पुष्करपर्णात्ता य᳘दि᳘हाहृ᳘त्योपद᳘धाति त᳘देना यो᳘नौ पुष्करपर्णे प्र᳘तिष्ठापयति त᳘थो हैता अ᳘बहिर्धा भवन्ति ता अ᳘नन्तर्हिताः स्वयमातृण्णा᳘ या उ᳘पदधात्यन्त᳘रिक्षं वै᳘ मध्यमा᳘स्वयमातृण्णा᳘नन्तर्हितास्त᳘दन्त᳘रिक्षाद्दि᳘शो दधात्यु᳘त्तरा उत्तरास्त᳘दन्त᳘रिक्षाद्दि᳘शो दधाति रे᳘तस्सिचोर्वे᳘लयेमे वै᳘ रेतस्सि᳘चावन᳘योस्तद्दि᳘शो दधाति त᳘स्मादन᳘योर्दिशः सर्व᳘त उ᳘पदधाति सर्व᳘तस्तद्दि᳘शो दधाति त᳘स्मात्सर्वतो᳘ दि᳘शः सर्व᳘तः समी᳘चीः सर्व᳘तस्त᳘त्समी᳘चीर्दि᳘शो दधाति त᳘स्मात्सर्व᳘तः समी᳘च्यो दि᳘शः ॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then places the diśyā bricks (regional bricks). Regional bricks are the regions themselves and so he places only regions. These are those same regions not separated (form the aerial region) where they stepped in then, along with Vāyu. It is them he now places. Before this, he places the brick of grass-bundle (darbhastamba brick) and the unbaked brick (logeṣṭakā). These diśyā bricks are the yonder sun and thus he places the yonder sun over the quarters and builds him up in the regions. If he were to place the diśyā simultaneoulsy as the darbhastamba bricks and the logeṣṭakās, they will be outside (the altar) and that means outside the womb. Only what sacrificial act is done before the laying of the lotus-leaf, falls outside the womb. Since he brings and lays these bricks (diśyā) now, he establishes them in the womb, the lotus-leaf. That is how they do not become excluded. They are not separated from the svayamātṛṇṇā. The central naturally perforated brick is the aerial region. So he lays the quarters in close proximity to the aerial region. Just facing the svayamatṛṇṇā, he lays them, so that the quarters are placed facing the aerial region,within the boundaries of the two retaśsicā bricks. These two worlds are the retaśsicā bricks. These two worlds are the retaśsicas. In them he lays down the quarters. He places them (diśyās) on all sides, by which he places the diśyās (quarters) on all sides. That is why the quarters are on all sides. He places them straight and by that he places the quarters so as to face each other. That is why the quarters are facing each other.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

य᳘द्वेव दि᳘श्या उपद᳘धाति छ᳘न्दाꣳसि वै दि᳘शो गायत्री वै प्रा᳘ची दिक्त्रिष्टुब् दक्षिणा ज᳘गती᳘ प्रतीच्यनुष्टु᳘बुदीची पङ्क्ति᳘रूर्ध्वा पश᳘वो वै छ᳘न्दाꣳस्यन्त᳘रिक्षं मध्यमा चि᳘तिरन्त᳘रिक्षे त᳘त्पशून् दधाति त᳘स्मादन्त᳘रिक्षायतनाः पश᳘वः ॥९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now why he lays down the diśyā bricks ? The quarters are the metres. The eastern direction is the Gāyatrī metre; the south is Triṣṭubh; the west is the Jagatī; the north is the Anuṣṭubh and the upper region is the Paṅkti. The metres are paśus and the middle layer is the aerial region. Thus he places the paśus (cattle) in the aerial region. Hence there are cattle with their abode in the aerial region.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

य᳘द्वेव दि᳘श्या उपद᳘धाति छ᳘न्दाꣳसि वै दि᳘शोः पश᳘वो वै छ᳘न्दाꣳस्य᳘न्नं पश᳘वो म᳘ध्यं मध्यमा चि᳘तिर्मध्यतस्तद᳘न्नं दधाति ता अन᳘न्तर्हिताः स्वयमातृण्णा᳘या उ᳘पदधाति प्राणो वै᳘ स्वयमातृण्णा᳘नन्तर्हितं त᳘त्प्राणाद᳘न्नं दधात्यु᳘त्तरा उ᳘त्त᳘रं त᳘त्प्राणाद᳘न्नं दधाति रे᳘तस्सि᳘चोर्वे᳘लया पृष्ट᳘यो वै᳘ रेतस्सि᳘चौ म᳘ध्य᳘मु पृष्ट᳘यो मध्यत᳘ ए᳘वास्मिन्नेतद᳘न्नं दधाति सर्व᳘त उ᳘पदधाति सर्व᳘त ए᳘वास्मि᳘न्नेतद᳘न्नं दधाति ॥१०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now again why he places the diśyā bricks ? The directions are the metres and metres are these cattle. The cattle are food. The middle layer is in the middle (of the body). He thus supplies food in the middle and they are placed so as they are not separated from the naturally perforated brick. The svayamātṛṇṇā is indeed, prāṇa. So he places food not separated from the prāṇa. He lays them close to each other (on the svayamātṛṇṇā); the food and the prāṇa. He places them within the boundaries of the retaśsics, for, the retaśsics are ribs and the ribs are in the middle. So he supplies food in the middle. He places them on all sides and thus provides food from all sides.
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

रा᳘ज्ञ्यसि प्रा᳘ची दिग्विरा᳘लसि द᳘क्षिणा दि᳘क्सम्रा᳘लसि प्रती᳘ची दि᳘क्स्वरा᳘ळस्यु᳘दीची दिग᳘धिपत्न्यसि बृहती दिगि᳘ति ना᳘मान्यासा᳘मेता᳘नि नामग्रा᳘हमे᳘वैना एतदु᳘पदधाति ता ना᳘नोप᳘दधा᳘ति ना᳘ना सादयति ना᳘ना सू᳘ददोहसा᳘धिवदति ता नाना हि दि᳘शः ॥११॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“You the eastern quarter ! You are the queen; you are the ruler, extensive; O southern quarter ! You are the overlord; O western quarter! you are the self-ruler; O northern quarter ! You are the sovereign; O the great (upper) region !” These are their names. He thus lays them down even while addressing them by name. He lays them down separately and settles them separately. He chants separate Sūdadohasa verses over them; for those quarters are indeed separate. (First Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>
