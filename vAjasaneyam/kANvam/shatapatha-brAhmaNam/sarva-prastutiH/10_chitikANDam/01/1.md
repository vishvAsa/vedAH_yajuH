<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He lays them down in tens, for, there are ten prāṇas. Tens may also mean many times; but here they mean only ten (in one lot). Five times (the lots of) ten are placed. He lays them (near the heads of the paśus), so that these (prāṇas) are not separated from the paśus. He places them all around so that on every side they are endowed with vital airs.
