ता᳓ है᳓के पु᳓रुषमुपा᳓र्प्यो᳓पदधत्येष᳓ वै᳓ [[प्राण᳓स्त᳓मेता|प्राण᳓स्त᳓मेता᳓]] बिभ्रति य᳓त्प्राणं᳓ बि᳓भ्रति त᳓स्मात्प्राणभृ᳓त इ᳓ति न᳓ त᳓था कुर्यात् ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः

Some people lay down (these prāṇabhṛt bricks) so as they are in contact with the human brick (goldman) (saying) that he (puruṣa) is the vital air and him these bricks sustain (bibhrati) and hence it is they are prāṇabhṛts. It shall not be done so.

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

परिश्रि᳓त्स्वेवो᳓पा᳓र्प्यो᳓पदध्याद᳓थ या᳓ म᳓ध्य उपद᳓धाति ता᳓भिरस्यै᳓ष᳓ आत्मा᳓ पूर्ण᳓स्ता᳓ उ एवै᳙त᳓स्माद᳓नन्तर्हिताः ॥

Now they ask, “When brick by brick the Agni-altar is fashioned, how is it that this Agni of his becomes completely fashioned and trim?” Now the Yajus formula chanted (for each brick) is the marrow, the brick is the bone, the Sūdadoha recitation is the skin, the formula used for purīṣa (filling of the gaps with earth) forms the hair and the purīṣa (the mud used to fill the gaps) is the food.
