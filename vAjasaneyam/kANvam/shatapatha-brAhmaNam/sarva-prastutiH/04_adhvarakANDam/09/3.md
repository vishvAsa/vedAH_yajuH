<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(He takes down Soma) before the pressing stones (lying) with their heads pointing to each other. Soma is Kṣatra (nobility) and the stones are viś (people). Thereby he raises the nobility over the viś (clans). Why (the stones) face each other (and the Soma is between)— The viś (clan) in the presence of Kṣatra is uncontentious. Therefore, in front of the stones (facing each other).
</details>
