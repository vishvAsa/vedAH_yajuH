ते वा᳘ ए᳘तेऽधिष᳘वणे पर्यु᳘पविशन्त्य᳘थास्याꣳ हि᳘रण्यं बध्नीते स य᳘दस्याꣳ हि᳘रण्यं बध्नीते᳘ द्वयं वा᳘ इदं न᳘ तृती᳘यमस्ति सत्यं᳘ चैवा᳘नृतं च त᳘तः सत्यं᳘ देवा᳘ अ᳘नृतं मनु᳘ष्या अग्ने᳘रु वा᳘ एतद्रे᳘तो यद्धि᳘रण्यꣳ सत्ये᳘नाꣳशू᳘नुपस्पृशानी᳘ति सत्ये᳘न सो᳘मं प᳘राहणानी᳘ति त᳘स्मादस्याꣳ हि᳘रण्यं बध्नीते ॥१॥

C R Swaminathan

Thereupon they sit down around the two Adhiṣavaṇas (pressing boards where soma is extracted). He (Adhvaryu) then ties a piece of gold in that (ring finger). Why he ties a gold piece on that finger two fold verily, is this, there is no third, namely truth and the untruth. Gods, forsooth, are the truth and men are untruth. This gold is Agni’s seed. “With the truth, I will touch the stalks (of the Soma plant); With the truth, I will take hold of Soma”— (thus thinking) he ties a piece of gold on that (ring-finger).

He then takes a pressing stone. That one (stone) which he takes first is the one with which Upāmśu-grahas is pressed out. Those (pressing stones) are of rock and these here are (also) of rock. विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (1/84 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)

त᳓मा᳓दत्ते देव᳓स्य त्वा सवितुः᳓ प्रसवे᳙ऽश्वि᳓नोर्बाहु᳓भ्यां पूष्णो᳓ ह᳓स्ताभ्याम् ।
आ᳓ददे रा᳓वासी᳓ति सविता᳓ वै᳓ देवा᳓नां प्रसविता᳓ त᳓स्मादाह देव᳓स्य त्वा सवितुः᳓ प्रसव᳓ [[इ᳓त्यश्वि᳓नोर्बाहु᳓भ्यामि᳓त्यश्विना|इ᳓त्यश्वि᳓नोर्बाहु᳓भ्यामि᳓त्यश्वि᳓ना]] उ वै᳓ देवा᳓नामध्वर्यू᳓ त᳓योरेवै᳙त᳓द्बाहु᳓भ्यामा᳓दत्ते न᳓ स्वा᳓भ्यां पूष्णो᳓ ह᳓स्ताभ्यामि᳓ति पूषा᳓ वै᳓ देवा᳓नां भागदुघो᳓ऽशनं पाणि᳓भ्यामुपनिधाता᳓ त᳓स्यैवै᳙त᳓द्ध᳓स्ताभ्यामा᳓दत्ते न᳓ स्वा᳓भ्यां त᳓न्न᳓ वा᳓ एत᳓स्य मनुष्यो᳙ भर्ता᳓ व᳓ज्रो ह्ये᳙ष᳓ त᳓देनं देव᳓ताभिरेवो᳓द्यच्छति देव᳓ताभिर्बिभर्ति त᳓थैनमेष᳓ व᳓ज्रो न᳓ हिनस्ति ॥

By saying ‘With the Vasus, with the Rudras’, he assigns a share to the Vasus and Rudras along with Indra. “Thee for Indra with the Ādityas”, whereby he assigns a share to the Ādityas along with Indra. “Thee for Indra, the slayer of the foes”, a foe is an enemy. While being beaten, he becoming a mortal, uttered this statement ‘Thou alone will be my generator and none other than thee’ to Indra. From Nigrābhya waters, they collect the several Grahas (cups of libations of soma). As to why they collect the grahas from the Nigrābhya waters is this. It was the waters which slew Vṛtra and by virtue of that prowess, they flow and nothing withholds it from flowing. The Vasatīvaris are collected from the flowing (waters). From the Vasatīvaris, (they collect) Nigrābhyas, from the Nigrābhyas (they collect) the Grahas which (in turn) have the vitality (brought into them) from the Hotṛ’s cup. The Hotṛ the Ṛk is a woman. From woman all these creatures are born. So they are collected from the woman, the Ṛk, in whom vitality is brought from the Hotṛ’s cup. And therefrom these (Grahas) are produced. So it is from Nigrābhyas, the Grahas are collected. (Fourth Brāhmaṇa Ends) (Chapter Nine Ends) ADHVARA KĀṆḌA ENDS
