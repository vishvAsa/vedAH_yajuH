सर्वाष् टीकाः
C R Swaminathan

When they (paśus) are taken northwards, the one for Agni is to be conducted first and then the others in the same order. When they strangle, they have to strangle the one for Agni first as the southern most; then the others in the same order. Later when they make those chief oblations, they perform in the order in which they are cooked. (First Brāhmaṇa Ends)
