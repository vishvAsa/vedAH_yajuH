Now there are three elevens at the paśu-sacrifice eleven fore-offerings (prayājās); eleven after-offerings (anu-yājās) and eleven by-offerings (upayājās). This much is the paśu-sacrifice. Now they argue "What then is done at the sacrifice since the vital air is friendly to all the limbs".
