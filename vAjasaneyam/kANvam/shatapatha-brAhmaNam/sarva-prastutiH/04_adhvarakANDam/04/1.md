<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (1/40 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)</summary>

स᳓ पू᳓र्वोऽती᳓त्य गृह्णीयाच्छ्रे᳓यान्वा᳓ एष᳓ एत᳓स्य गृहा᳓नभ्या᳓गच्छति य᳓त्सो᳓मः क्रीतो᳓ य᳓त्र वै᳓ श्रे᳓यानभ्याग᳓च्छति य᳓दि वै᳓ त᳓त्र तं᳓ [[नापचा᳓यन्ति|ना᳓पचा᳓यन्ति]] क्रु᳓ध्यति वै᳓ स᳓ त᳓त्र त᳓था हा᳓पचितो भवति त᳓स्मात्पू᳓र्वोऽती᳓त्य गृह्णीयात् ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He should take up (material for the guest worship) after having passed beyond (Soma) towards the east. Indeed a superior person viz. Soma who has been purchased comes to this persons (sacrificers) house. Where (when) a superior person visits, if they do not respect him there (then) he surely becomes angry there (then). Thereby does he become honoured. Therefore (the sacrificer) should take up, after going beyond (Soma) to the east.
</details>
