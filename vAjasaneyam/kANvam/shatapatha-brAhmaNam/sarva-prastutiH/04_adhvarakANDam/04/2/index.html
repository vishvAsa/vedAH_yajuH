सर्वाष् टीकाः
C R Swaminathan

Let us come to an agreement". “Let us not fall into the control of the enemies. Let us stand up for (i.e., be under) the excellence of one (of us)”. All those gods stood by Indra’s excellence (greatness). Therefore they say, “Indra is all the deities”. i.e., The gods have Indra as the best (chief). For it was by his excellence that they stood. Therefore, kinsmen should not separate (in discord). For, the hopeful enemy who is far away verily follows these. He who knowing thus comes to an agreement, does what is displeasing to the enemies and he does not fall into the power of the enemies.
