स᳓ य᳓द्वैसर्जिना᳓नि जुहो᳓ति या᳓वानेव᳓ यज्ञो᳓ या᳓वत्यस्य मा᳓त्रा ता᳓वन्तं यज्ञं᳓ संभृ᳓त्या᳓थेदं᳓ यथायतनं᳓ यथाप्रतिष्ठं᳓ वि᳓सृजति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः

Those who eat with the Yajamāna (i.e. Yajamānā’s kins) should be there where he starts (the Vaisarjinī offerings) and (while the offerings are made) they (relatives) should say “You set them free when we are witnessing”. he needs to start these offerings only if he wishes. Even if he wishes and (the relatives) do not approve, even then, he by himself can set these free and that too without their witnessing them.
