## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वा᳘ एतां यू᳘पैकादशिनीं मिनोति स य᳘देतां यूपैकादशिनीं मिनो᳘त्येता᳘वती ह वै᳘ पृथिवी या᳘वती वे᳘दिर्व᳘ज्रो वै यू᳘पाः स᳘ एतैर्व᳘ज्रैरिमां᳘ पृथिवी᳘ꣳ᳘ स्पृणुतेऽस्याः सप᳘त्नान्नि᳘र्भजति बहिर्धा᳘ करोति त᳘स्माद्वा᳘ एतां यू᳘पैकादशिनीं मिनोत्युपशयो᳘ द्वादशो वि᳘तष्टो भवति तं दक्षिणे वेद्यन्ते नि᳘दधाति ॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He instals eleven yūpas. Why he instals eleven yūpas is because the earth is indeed that extent as the altar. The yūpas are the thunderbolts. By means of these thunderbolts he takes possession of the earth. He strikes at her (earth's) enemies and drives them out. That is why he instals these eleven yūpas. The twelfth, not fully hewn, is kept (as a stand by). He keeps it down on the right side end of the altar.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

देवा᳘ ह वै᳘ यज्ञं᳘ तन्वाना᳘ असुररक्षसा᳘नां दक्षिणत᳘ आसङ्गा᳘द्बिभयां᳘ चक्रुर्य᳘थो वा इ᳘षुरा᳘यता᳘वसृष्टा त᳘या वै᳘ स्तृणुते᳘ वा न᳘ वा स्तृणुते यथो᳘द्यद्यत्प्र᳘हृतं ते᳘न वै᳘ स्तृणुते᳘ वा न᳘ वा स्तृणुत एवं वा᳘ एते यू᳘पा य उ᳘छ्रिता अ᳘थैष व᳘ज्र एव᳘ दक्षिणत उ᳘द्यतो यथे᳘षुरा᳘यता᳘नवसृष्टा यथो᳘द्यतम᳘प्रहृतमेवमे᳘व व᳘ज्रो दक्षिणत उ᳘द्यतो नाष्ट्राणाꣳर᳘क्षसाम᳘पहतये ॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now, the gods, while performing this sacrifice, were afraid of an attack of Asura-Rakṣasas from the southern direction. Those raised (yūpas) then were like an arrow discharged after being drawn-therewith one smites (the target) or does not smite or it is like a club wielded and hurled-therewith one strikes (the target) or does not strike. That is (the nature) of these errected yūpas. This thunderbolt (the twelfth yūpa lying down unerected) in the south is (like) an arrow drawn (on the string) but not discharged (yet) or (like) a club wielded but not thrown (at the target). This is (the nature of) the thunderbolt held up at the southern side for dispelling the terrible Rakṣasas.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘मभि᳘मृशत्येष᳘ ते पृथिव्यां᳘ लोक᳘ आरण्य᳘स्ते पशुरि᳘ति पशु᳘श्च वै यू᳘पश्च त᳘मस्मा एत᳘मारण्यं᳘ पशुम᳘नुदिशति ते᳘नैष᳘ पशुमा᳘न्भवति ॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He touches it (the twelfth yūpa) (saying) "This is thy place on earth; thine is the beast of the forest". There are the sacrificial animals and the yūpas and to this one he thereby assigns an animal of the forest and by that it (yūpa) becomes possessor of an animal.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तान्है᳘क उपवसथ᳘ एव स᳘र्वानु᳘च्छ्रयन्ति तत्तत्क᳘र्मण एव᳘ प्रकुब्न᳘तायै क्षिप्रं᳘ यज्ञꣳ स᳘ꣳ᳘स्थापयामे᳘त्येवा᳘थो उपवसथे वै यू᳘पस्योच्छ्र᳘यणमि᳘ति व᳘दन्तस्त᳘दु त᳘था न᳘ कुर्यान्न वै᳘ पुरा᳘ परिव्य᳘यणाद्यू᳘पम᳘न्वर्जन्त्य᳘परिवीता उ वा᳘ एत᳘ एताꣳ रा᳘त्रिं वसन्ति सा᳘ न्वेव᳘ परिचक्षा᳘थो पश᳘वे वै यू᳘पमु᳘च्छ्रयन्ति न वा᳘ एते᳘षु तद᳘हः पशुमालिप्स᳘मानो भवति᳘ ये᳘ऽन्येऽग्निष्ठाद्भ᳘वन्ति त᳘स्मादग्निष्ठ᳘मेव तद᳘हरु᳘च्छ्रयेत्तꣳ हि᳘ परिव्य᳘यति त᳘स्मिन्पशुमा᳘लभते त᳘स्मादग्निष्ठ᳘मेव तद᳘हरु᳘च्छ्रयेत् ॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Some instal all those yūpas on the previous day itself to ensure speedy completion of those rituals saying "Let us quickly setup the sacrifice. We shall install the yūpas in the previous day itself". But one should not do so. Because, before girdling them (with grass rope) the Adhvaryu does not leave his hold on them. (If they are errected on the previous day) they will remain ungirt during the night. This they would disapprove. The yūpas are errected for the sacrificial animals and the animals are not slaughtered that day except in the case of the one facing the fire. Therefore only the one facing the fire should he instal that day (previous day). That alone is girded and on that the animal is slaughtered. So only the one (yūpa) facing the fire should be errected that day.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तद्धै᳘के य᳘ ए᳘वाग्निष्ठादु᳘त्तरस्तम᳘ग्र उ᳘च्छ्रयन्त्य᳘थ द᳘क्षिणमथो᳘त्तरं य᳘था दक्षिणा᳘र्ध्यमुत्तम᳘मुच्छ्र᳘येदेवं त᳘था ह्यु᳘दीची भ᳘वती᳘ति त᳘दु त᳘था न᳘ कुर्याद्य᳘ एवाग्निष्ठाद्दक्षिणस्तमेवा᳘ग्र उच्छ्रयेदथो᳘त्तरम᳘थ द᳘क्षिणं य᳘थोत्तरा᳘र्ध्युमुत्तम᳘मुच्छ्र᳘येदेवं त᳘था ह्यु᳘दीची भ᳘वती᳘ति स यो व᳘र्षिष्ठः स᳘ दक्षिणा᳘र्ध्यः स्याद᳘थ हृ᳘सीयान᳘थ हृ᳘सीयान्य᳘थोत्तरा᳘र्ध्यो ह्र᳘सिष्ठ एवं त᳘था ह्यु᳘दीची भ᳘वतीति ॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Some instal the yūpa which is immediately to the north of the one facing the fire. Then the one in the south, then a northern one—last of all the one on the southern flank; thus it (the row of yūpas) inclines to the north. That should not be done so. That which is south of the one opposite to fire, he should instal first, then the north one, then the southern one—last of all the one on the northern flank; and thus indeed, his work attains completion towards the north. The largest be the one forming the southern flank; then shorter and shorter till in the northern flank, the shortest (is errected). Thus (the row) inclines to the north.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थैतं᳘ पत्नीयूपं᳘ त्रयोदशं प᳘त्नीभ्य उ᳘च्छ्रयति सर्वत्वा᳘य वा᳘ एतमु᳘च्छ्रयति कृत्स्न᳘तायै त्र᳘योदश वै मा᳘साः संवत्सर᳘स्य त᳘स्मादेतं᳘ त्रयोदशमु᳘छ्रयति ॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thereupon they set up the Patnī-yūpa (wife's yūpa) the thirteenth for the wives. It is erected for the sake of completeness (or) perfection. The year has thirteen months and hence the thirteenth is installed.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘त्त्वाष्ट्रं᳘ पशुमा᳘लभते त्व᳘ष्टा वै रे᳘तः सिक्तं वि᳘करोति त᳘देतद्रे᳘तः सिक्तं तत्त्वष्टा वि᳘करोति स᳘ मुष्करो᳘ भवति मुस्करो हि᳘ प्रजनयिता᳘ प्रज᳘ननꣳ हि त्व᳘ष्टा त᳘स्मान्मुष्करो᳘ भवति तं प᳘र्यग्निकृतमेवो᳘त्सृजति न स᳘ꣳस्थापयति य᳘द्धैनꣳसꣳस्थाप᳘येत्प᳘रिगृह्णीयात्प्रज᳘ननं त᳘त्प्रजा᳘ ए᳘वैतदु᳘त्सृजति ता᳘ इमा उ᳘त्सृष्टाः प्रजा अ᳘नुपस्थिताः प्र᳘जायन्ते त᳘स्मात्प᳘र्यग्निकृतमेवो᳘त्सृजति न स᳘ꣳ᳘स्थापयति ॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then they tie the animal (paśu) for Tvaṣṭā for Tvaṣṭā who fashions the semen that is impregnated and hence Tvaṣṭā fashions the semen now implanted. It (the animal of Tvaṣṭā) happens to be one with testicles, for, the one with testicles can procreate and Tvaṣṭā is (the cause of) procreation. Hence he is one with testicles. He (the animal) is let off after fire has been carried around it. It is not slain. Were he to slaughter it, his progeny will get stopped. Thus (letting it free) he sets free his progeny (impedes not his offspring). Thus let free, his progeny will be (born) unimpaired. Therefore after taking fire around it, it is let off; it is not slain. (Second Brāhmaṇa Ends)
</details>
