## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वा अ᳘भ्रिमेवा᳘दत्ते देव᳘स्य त्वा सवितुः प्रस᳘वेऽश्वि᳘नोर्बाहु᳘भ्यां पूष्णो ह᳘स्ताभ्याम् । आ᳘ददे ना᳘र्यसी᳘त्युक्तो य᳘जुषो ब᳘न्धुर᳘थ यदा᳘ह ना᳘र्यसी᳘ति यो᳘षा ह्य᳘भ्रिर्योषा हि ना᳘री त᳘स्मादाह ना᳘र्यसी᳘ति ॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He takes the spade itself (saying) "I take thee prompted by the divine Savitṛ, with the arms of the Asurās; with the hands of Pūṣan". "I take thee thou art a woman". The purport of this chant has already been told. Why he says 'Thou art a woman' is because the spade is a female and so he says 'Thou art a woman'.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘या यू᳘पावटं प᳘रिलिखत्यर्ध᳘मन्तर्वे᳘द्यर्धं᳘ बहिर्वे᳘दीद᳘महꣳर᳘क्षसो ग्रीवा अ᳘पिकृन्तामी᳘ति व᳘ज्रो वा अ᳘भ्रिव्र᳘ज्रेणै᳘वैत᳘न्नाष्ट्रा᳘णाꣳर᳘क्षसां ग्रीवा अ᳘पिकृन्तति तमु᳘परसंमितं यू᳘पावटं खनति ॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

With her (the spade) he draws (the lines) for the hole for the yūpa (to be installed) so that half (of the hole) is within the altar and half outside the altar (saying) "Herewith I cut off the neck of the Rākṣasas". For, the spade is a thunderbolt and with the thunderbolt he cuts off the necks of the Rākṣasas. he digs the pit equal to (hold) the bottom part of the stake.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ प्रा᳘ञ्चमुत्करमु᳘त्किरति तत्प्रा᳘ञ्चं यू᳘पं नि᳘दधात्य᳘थ बर्ही᳘ꣳ᳘षि यावन्मात्रा᳘णीवा᳘थ यूपशकलं᳘ चषा᳘लम᳘थ य᳘वमतीः प्रो᳘क्षणीः कुरुते᳘ सोऽसा᳘वेव ब᳘न्धुर्य᳘वमतीनाम᳘थ प्रो᳘क्षति स᳘ समान᳘ एव प्रो᳘क्षणस्य ब᳘न्धुर्मे᳘ध्यमे᳘वैत᳘त्करोति यत्कि᳘ञ्च प्रोक्ष᳘ति ॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He throws up a heap of earth (dug out) towards the east of it (pit). Thereon he puts sacrificial grass of the same size and there upon he places the head piece of the yūpa (Caṣāla). Then he takes the sprinkling water mixed with barley corns; the significance of this is the same as before. Then he sprinkles the water mixed with barley corns. The significance of this sprinkling is the same as before. Whatever he sprinkles, he only purifies it (so as to be fit for the sacrifice).
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स प्रो᳘क्षति दिवे᳘ त्वान्त᳘रिक्षाय त्वा पृथिव्यै त्वे᳘ति व᳘ज्रो वा᳘ एष यद्यू᳘प एषां᳘ लोका᳘नामभि᳘जितय एषां᳘ त्वा लोका᳘नामभि᳘जितये प्रो᳘क्षामी᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह ॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He sprinkles (saying) "For the sky-thee! For the air-thee! For the earth- thee!" The stake being a thunderbolt (he does so) for the protection of these worlds. 'I sprinkle thee for the protection of these worlds' is what he means to say.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ याः प᳘रिशिष्टाः प्रो᳘क्षण्यो भ᳘वन्ति ता यूपावटे᳘ऽवनयति शु᳘न्धन्तां लोकाः᳘ पितृष᳘दना इ᳘ति᳘ सोऽसा᳘वेव ब᳘न्धुरथो᳘दगग्राणि च प्रा᳘गग्राणि च बर्हीꣳष्य᳘वस्तृणाति पितृष᳘दनमसी᳘त्यसा᳘वेव ब᳘न्धुः ॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The sprinkling water that remains, he then pours into the yūpa-hole (saying) "Be the worlds pure where in the Pitṛs reside". It means the same as before. Thereupon he strews barhis grass, both northward-pointing and eastward- pointing (saying) "Thou art the seat of the Pitṛs". It means the same as before.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ यूपशकलं प्रा᳘स्यत्येत᳘द्ध वै ते᳘जो व᳘नस्प᳘तीनां यद्वा᳘ह्याः श᳘कलास्त᳘स्मादेतेष्व᳘पच्छिन्नेषु म्लायन्ति तत्ते᳘ज ए᳘वास्मिन्नेत᳘द्दधाति स᳘तेजसमेनमि᳘ति ते᳘जस्वन्तं मिनवानी᳘ति स यदेतदे᳘वैतत्प्रा᳘स्यत्येतद्धि य᳘जुष्कृतं मे᳘ध्यं भ᳘वति त᳘स्मादेत᳘दे᳘वैतत्प्रा᳘स्यति ॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then throws in the first chip of yūpa (removed), when the tree was cut). Now that chip of the outer (bark) doubtless is the vigour of trees; hence when they (chips of the outer bark) are cut off, they (the trees) dry up, for, it is their vigour. Hence (by throwing it there) he endows it with that very vigour, thinking 'I shall (plant it) with its vigour; let it be endowed with vigour'. Why this (particular) chip is thrown is because it has been produced with (the chanting) of a formula; it is sacrificially pure. So that very same (chip) is thrown.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स प्रा᳘स्यत्यग्रेणी᳘रसि स्वावेश᳘ उन्नेतॄणामित्येत᳘द्ध्यस्माद᳘ग्रेऽपच्छिनत्ति त᳘स्मादाहाग्रेणी᳘रसी᳘ति स्वावेश᳘ उन्नेतॄणा᳘मेत᳘स्य वित्ताद᳘धि त्वा स्थास्यतीत्य᳘धि᳘ ह्येनत्ति᳘ष्ठति ॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He throws it (saying) "Thou art a leader, easily accessible to the Unnetṛ priests (who draw the Soma). Because it was cut out first, he says "Thou art a leader'. "Thou art easily accessible to the Unnetṛ priests; be thou mindful of this (yūpa); it will stand upon thee", for the yūpa is to stand upon it.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाज्याहुतिं᳘ जुहोति व᳘ज्रो वा आ᳘ज्यं ने᳘दितो᳘ नाष्ट्रा र᳘क्षाꣳस्युपोत्ति᳘ष्ठानि᳘ति तद्व᳘ज्रेणा᳘ज्येना᳘वबाधते ॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he offers the ghee oblation; for, ghee is a thunderbolt. 'Lest the evil spirits should rise from below', he repels them with the thunderbolt i.e. ghee.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ पुर᳘स्तात्परी᳘त्याह यू᳘पायाज्य᳘मानाया᳘नुब्रूही᳘ति यू᳘पमञ्ज्मो᳘ऽनुब्रूही᳘ति वान्वा᳘ह हो᳘तानक्त्यध्वर्यु᳘र्देव᳘स्त्वा सविता म᳘ध्वानक्त्वि᳘ति सविता वै᳘ देवा᳘नां प्रसविता स᳘र्वमु वा᳘ इदं म᳘धु य᳘जमानो वै यू᳘पोऽने᳘नै᳘वैतत्स᳘र्वेण य᳘जमानꣳ स᳘ꣳ᳘स्पर्शयति त᳘दस्मै सविता᳘ प्रसविता प्र᳘सौति त᳘स्मादाह देव᳘स्त्वा सविता म᳘ध्वानक्त्विति ॥९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He moves to the front of it (yūpa pit) and says "Bring ghee for the yūpa and recite". Or (he may say) "We shall anoint the yūpa; you recite for it". The Hotṛ recites and let the Adhvaryu anoint (saying) "The divine Savitṛ may anoint thee with sweet drink". For, Savitṛ is the prompter of the gods and all these here are sweet drinks. The sacrificer himself is the yūpa. With all these he anoints the Yajamāna. This Savitṛ (the prompter impells him (to do so). Therefore he says, "Divine Savitṛ may anoint thee with sweet drink".
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थोभय᳘तश्चषा᳘लमक्त्वा प्र᳘तिगूहति सुपिप्पला᳘भ्यस्त्वौ᳘षधीभ्य इ᳘ति पि᳘प्पलमे᳘वास्मिन्नेत᳘द्दधाति स य᳘देतत्सो᳘पेनितमिव भ᳘वति त᳘स्मात्पि᳘प्पलं च वृक्षं चा᳘न्तरेणो᳘पेनितमिव ॥१०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Having then anointed the top ring on both sides, he covers (the yūpa) with it (saying) "To the full-berried plants thee" for that (top ring) is as its berry. By this he indeed connects the berry (with the trunk). Because the connecting part between· (the trunk and the fruit) is pressed as it were, it looks as if the berry fruit and the tree are contracted in the middle.
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाग्निष्ठा᳘मनक्ति य᳘जमानो वा᳘ अग्निष्ठा र᳘स आ᳘ज्यं य᳘जमानमे᳘वैतद्र᳘सेनानक्ति स᳘ परिव्ययणवेला᳘याꣳ समन्तं प᳘रिमृशति समन्त᳘मे᳘वास्मिन्नेतद्र᳘सं दधाति तमो᳘परादनक्ति ॥११॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then anoints the corner (of the stake) facing the fire, for, the corner facing the fire is the sacrificer and the ghee is sap. Thereby he anoints the Yajamāna with sap. He then grasps the girdling part all round and applies the sap from top to bottom.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाहोच्छ्रीय᳘माणाया᳘नुब्रूही᳘त्यन्वा᳘ह होतो᳘च्छ्रयत्यध्वर्युर्दि᳘वम᳘ग्रेणास्पृक्ष᳘ आन्त᳘रिक्षं म᳘ध्येनाप्राः पृथिवीमु᳘परेणादृꣳहीरि᳘ति व᳘ज्रो वा᳘ एष यद्यू᳘पस्त᳘देनेनेमाँ᳘ल्लोका᳘न्त्सृणुत एभ्यो᳘ लोके᳘भ्यो द्विष᳘न्तं भ्रा᳘तृव्यं नि᳘र्भजति बहिर्धा᳘ करोति त᳘स्मादेवमु᳘च्छ्रयति ॥१२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then says (to the Hotṛ) "Recite to the yūpa which is being set up". The Hotṛ says and the Adhvaryu raises it (the yūpa saying) "With thy crest, thou hast touched the sky, with thy middle, thou hast filled the aerial region, with thy foot thou hast steadied the earth" - This yūpa is none other than the thunderbolt. So (by raising it) he conquers all these words. He drives away from these worlds the hated enemies. He puts them out (of these worlds). That is why he then raises it.
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाभ्य᳘वहरति या᳘ ते धा᳘मान्युश्म᳘सि ग᳘मध्यै य᳘त्र गा᳘वो भू᳘रिशृङ्गा अया᳘सः । अत्रा᳘हैत᳘दुरुगाय᳘स्य वि᳘ष्णोः परमं᳘ पदम᳘वभाति भूरी᳘त्येत᳘या त्रिष्टु᳘भा व᳘ज्रो वै᳘ त्रिष्टुब्व᳘ज्रो वै यू᳘पस्त᳘स्मात्त्रिष्टु᳘भा मिनोति स य᳘द्वैष्णव्या मिनो᳘ति वैष्णवो हि यू᳘पः ॥१३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then plants it (yūpa into the hole) (saying) "To what resorts of thine we long to go, where are the swift-footed, many-horned, kine; there, foresooth, was imprinted wide-striding highest mighty step of Viṣṇu". With this chant in Triṣṭubh (he plants) - Triṣṭubh is verily a thunderbolt and the yūpa is a thunderbolt. Therefore he plants it with a Triṣṭubh chant. He plants it with a chant belonging to Viṣṇu because the yūpa is of Viṣṇu.
</details>


## १४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ संप्र᳘त्यग्नि᳘मग्निष्ठां᳘ करोति त᳘था य᳘जमान आय᳘तनान्न᳘ ह्वलति य᳘द्धाग्निष्ठा᳘मग्ने᳘र्ह्वल᳘येयुर्ह्व᳘लेद्ध य᳘जमान आय᳘तनात्त᳘स्मात्संप्र᳘त्यग्निमग्निष्ठां᳘ करोति ॥१४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He now turns the Agniṣṭha corner (of the yūpa) towards the fire so that the Yajamāna does not stir from his seat. If the Agniṣṭha corner turns away from the fire, it (would amount to) the Yajamāna moving away from his seat. Therefore he now turns the Agniṣṭha corner to face the fire.
</details>


## १५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ प᳘र्यूहति ब्रह्मव᳘नि त्वा क्षत्रव᳘नि रायस्पोषव᳘नि प᳘र्यूहामीत्य᳘थ प᳘र्यृषति ब्र᳘ह्म दृꣳह क्षत्रं᳘ दृꣳहा᳘युर्दृꣳह प्रजां᳘ दृꣳहे᳘ति समंभूमि᳘ पर्यृ᳘षणं भवत्युदपात्र᳘मुपनि᳘नयत्यसा᳘वेव ब᳘न्धुः ॥१५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then re-inforces (the bottom of the yūpa) with earth (saying) "Well disposed to Brahmins, well-disposed to the warrior class, winner of prosperity and wealth, I steady you". He then presses (the earth) around it (saying) "Uphold thou the priesthood; uphold the warrior class, uphold our life; uphole our progeny". Then pressing to level it with the ground, he pours water thereon. Its significance has been told.
</details>


## १६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ परिगृ᳘ह्य जपति वि᳘ष्णोः क᳘र्माणि पश्यत य᳘तो व्रतानि पस्पशे । इ᳘न्द्रस्य यु᳘ज्यः सखे᳘ति व᳘ज्रं वा᳘ एतत्प्रा᳘हार्षीद्यद्यू᳘पमुद शिश्रियदिमां वि᳘ष्णोर्वि᳘जितिं पश्यतेमां जि᳘तिमि᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाहे᳘न्द्रस्य यु᳘ज्यः सखेती᳘न्द्रो यज्ञ᳘स्य देव᳘ता वैष्णवो वै यू᳘पस्त᳘थास्यैष से᳘न्द्रो भवति त᳘स्मादाहे᳘न्द्रस्य यु᳘ज्यः सखेति ॥१६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He (Yajamāna) touches it (yūpa) and chants - "See you the deeds of Viṣṇu, where by he beheld the sacred ordinances. Indra's allied friend" - Indra is the god of the sacrifice and the yūpa is of Viṣṇu. Thus here he (Viṣṇu) is united with Indra. So he says Indra's allied friend.
</details>


## १७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ चषा᳘लमु᳘दीक्षते तद्वि᳘ष्णोः परमं᳘ पदꣳ स᳘दा पश्यन्ति सू᳘रयः । दि᳘वीव च᳘क्षुरा᳘ततमि᳘ति व᳘ज्रं वा᳘ एतत्पा᳘हार्षीद्यद्यू᳘पमुद᳘शिश्रियदिमां वि᳘ष्णोर्वि᳘जितिं पश्यतेमां जि᳘तिमि᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह ॥१७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then looks up at the top-ring (saying) "The wise ever behold that highest step of Viṣṇu, fixed like an eye in the heaven”. For he who has set up the yūpa has hurled the thunderbolt. (When he says) "see yee Viṣṇu's conquest”, he means this achievement (of planting the yūpa).
</details>


## १८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ प᳘रिव्ययत्य᳘नग्नतायै वा᳘ एनं प᳘रिव्ययति त᳘स्मान्नीविदघ्ने प᳘रिव्ययत्य᳘त्रैव᳘ हीदं वासो᳘ऽथो अन्ना᳘द्यमे᳘वास्मिन्नेत᳘द्दधात्य᳘त्रैव वा᳘ इदम᳘न्नं प्रतितिष्ठति त᳘स्यान्नी᳘विदघ्ने प᳘रिव्ययति त्रिवृ᳘ता प᳘रिव्ययति त्रिवृद्ध᳘य᳘न्नं पश᳘वो ह्य᳘न्नं पिता᳘ माता यज्जा᳘यते त᳘त्तृती᳘यं त᳘स्मात्त्रिवृ᳘ता प᳘रिव्ययति ॥१८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then girds (the yūpa with a rope made of kuśagrass). He girds it to cover its nakedness. He winds it at the level of the navel for it is there that the garment is (tied). He thereby puts food into him; for it is there that food settles. Therefore he girds it at the level of the navel. He winds it thrice for threefold is food and food means cattle and (there is) the father, the mother and what is born (of them) is the third. So he winds it three times.
</details>


## १९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स परि᳘व्ययति परिवी᳘रसि प᳘रि त्वा दै᳘वीर्वि᳘शो व्ययन्तां प᳘रीमं य᳘जमानꣳ रा᳘यो मनु᳘ष्याणामि᳘त्याशि᳘षमे᳘वैतदा᳘शास्ते यदा᳘ह प᳘रीमं यजमानꣳ रा᳘यो मनु᳘ष्याणामिति ॥१९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He girds it (saying) “Thou art enfolded; may the heavenly hosts enfold thee! May riches enfold this Yajamāna among men”. By this he seeks a blessing when he says 'May riches enfold this Yajamāna among men'.
</details>


## २०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ दिवः᳘ सूनु᳘रसी᳘ति यूपशकलम᳘वगूहति प्रजा᳘ है᳘वास्यैषा त᳘स्माद्यू᳘पैकादशिन्यां यथायथम᳘वगूहेत्त᳘था हास्या᳘नुव्रता अ᳘मुग्धाः प्रजाः प्र᳘जायन्ते᳘ऽथ य᳘स्य यू᳘पैकादशिन्यां मोह᳘यन्त्य᳘ननुव्रता हास्य मुग्धाः᳘ प्रजाः प्र᳘जायन्ते᳘ऽथ यत्स्व᳘रुर्ना᳘म स्व᳘ꣳ᳘ ह्यस्यैतद᳘रुरेत᳘स्मा᳘द्ध्येतत्स᳘र्वस्मादपतक्ष्णो᳘ति त᳘स्मात्स्व᳘रुर्ना᳘म स्वर्ग᳘स्य वा᳘ एत᳘ल्लोक᳘स्य संक्र᳘मणं य᳘द्यूपशकल᳘मिदꣳ हि᳘ रशना᳘ यूपशकलं᳘ चषा᳘लं त᳘त ऊर्ध्वः᳘ स्वर्गं᳘ लोकꣳ स᳘मश्नुते ॥२०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thereupon he conceals a chip of the yūpa (under the rope) (saying) "Thou art the son of the sky”. For it is doubtless the offspring of that (yūpa); hence if there be the full number of eleven yūpas, let him conceal in each its own chip (got while rough-heving the stake to make it eight cornered). So that his children are born in order and not foolish. If some one gets confused in (inserting these chips) in eleven different yūpas, he will get disorderly and foolish issues. And now why it is called svaru (very sore) - that (chip) is cut off from that (yūpa) and thus is its own (soru) sore (arms or wound). It is from that (yūpa tree) this is chopped off. Therefore it is called svaru. Moreover, that chip of the yūpa is a bridge to the heavenly world. This girdle (kuśa rope); (this) chip and the top-ring and further up he reaches the heavenly world.
</details>


## २१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तद्यन्नि᳘खातं ते᳘न पितृलोकं᳘ जयत्य᳘थय᳘दूर्ध्वं᳘ निख᳘ननादा᳘ रशना᳘यास्ते᳘न मनुष्यलोकम᳘थ य᳘दूर्ध्व᳘ꣳ᳘ रशना᳘या आ᳘ चषा᳘लात्ते᳘न देवलोकम᳘थ य᳘दूर्ध्वं᳘ चषा᳘लाद्द्व्यङ्गुलं᳘ वा त्र्यङ्गुलं वा᳘तिरिक्तं ते᳘न साध्या इ᳘ति देवास्ते᳘षाꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयति स᳘लोको ह साध्यै᳘र्देवै᳘र्भवति य᳘ एव᳘मेतद्वे᳘द ॥२१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

With that part of it which is dug in, he gains the world of the Pitṛs; and with what is above the pit upto the girdle, he gains the world of men and what is above the girdle and upto the top-ring, he gains the world of the gods. What space covered by two or three fingers breadths above the top-ring, that is of the gods called Sādhyas. He shares their world. He who knows this so, gains the same world with the Sādhya gods.
</details>


## २२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तं वा᳘ एतं᳘ पुर᳘स्तान्मिनोति व᳘ज्रो वै यू᳘पो व᳘ज्रो वै᳘ दण्डः᳘ पूर्वार्धं वै᳘ दण्डस्या᳘भिप᳘द्य प्र᳘हरति त᳘स्मात्पुर᳘स्तान्मिनोति पुर᳘स्ताद्वै᳘ मनोजवः᳘ पुर᳘स्तात्प्रज्ञा प्रा᳘ची देवयज्या त᳘स्मात्पुर᳘स्तान्मिनोति ॥२२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He instals that (yūpa) on the fore-part (of the altar). For, the yūpa is a thunderbolt, as the club is a thunderbolt. Of course in hunting the club one holds it in the fore-part. So he instals it in the fore-part. Momentum is in the fore-part. Wisdom is in the fore-part, the worship spot of the gods is in the east. Therefore he instals it on the fore-part.
</details>


## २३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

देवा᳘ उ ह वै᳘ यज्ञे᳘नेमां जि᳘तिं जित्वा᳘ येय᳘मेषां जि᳘तिस्ते᳘ होचुर्ह᳘न्तेदं त᳘था कर᳘वामहा इ᳘ति य᳘था न इद᳘मनभ्यारुह्यं᳘ मनु᳘ष्यैर᳘सदि᳘ति ते᳘ यज्ञ᳘स्य र᳘सं निर्धा᳘य य᳘था मधुकृ᳘तो निर्ध᳘येयुरेवं᳘ विदु᳘ह्य यज्ञं यूपेन योपयित्वा᳘ तिरो᳘ बभूवुस्तद्य᳘देनेना᳘योपयꣳ स्त᳘स्माद्यू᳘पो ना᳘म᳘ सोऽष्टा᳘श्रिर्भवत्यष्टा᳘क्षरा वै᳘ गायत्री᳘ पूर्वार्धो वै᳘ यज्ञ᳘स्य गायत्री᳘ पूर्वार्धो वा᳘ एष वे᳘देस्त᳘स्मादष्टा᳘श्रिर्भवति ॥२३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Verily, by means of the sacrifice the gods gained that supreme authority which they now wield. They said "Let us make this (world of ours) such that it cannot be reached by men". They consumed the sap of the sacrifice, as bees would suck out honey and having drained the sacrifice and scattered it by means of the yūpa, they disappeared. And because they scattered with this (yūpa), it is called Yūpa. It has eight corners. For the Gāyatrī metre has eight syllables and Gāyatrī is the fore-part of the sacrifice. This yūpa is (at) the fore-part of the altar. Therefore it is eight- cornered.
</details>


## २४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘ꣳ᳘ ह स्मैतं᳘ देवा य᳘त्रैवादः᳘ परिधी᳘ननुप्रहरन्ति त᳘द्ध स्मैवानुप्र᳘हरन्ति त᳘स्माद᳘प्येतर्ह्ये᳘तां वे᳘लामनुप्र᳘हरन्ति त᳘थो वै र᳘क्षाꣳस्यनू᳘दपिपत ॥२४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now the gods once throw those paridhis (enclosing sticks into the fire). Since they then threw, some (people) now throw that boundary. There upon the Rakṣasas (got entry inside) and sipped (the soma) after (the gods).
</details>


## २५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते᳘ होचुर्यूपशकल᳘मेव᳘ जुहवामे᳘ति तद᳘है᳘वै᳘षोऽनुप्र᳘हृतो भविष्यति त᳘त्स्वगा᳘कृत इ᳘ति त᳘थो र᳘क्षाꣳसि᳘ नानू᳘त्पास्यन्तेऽयं वाव व᳘ज्र उ᳘द्यत इ᳘ति ते᳘ यूपशकल᳘मे᳘वाजुहुवुस्तद᳘है᳘वै᳘षोऽनुप्र᳘हृत आ᳘सीत्त᳘त्स्वगा᳘कृतस्त᳘थो र᳘क्षाꣳसि᳘ नानू᳘दपिपतायं वाव व᳘ज्र उ᳘द्यत इ᳘ति त᳘स्माद्यूपशकल᳘मेव᳘ जुहुयात्तद᳘है᳘वै᳘षोऽनुप्र᳘हृतो भवति त᳘त्स्वगा᳘कृतस्त᳘थो र᳘क्षाꣳसि᳘ नानू᳘त्पिपतेऽयं वाव व᳘ज्र उ᳘द्यत इ᳘ति᳘ सोऽनुयाजा᳘निष्ट्वा᳘ जुहोति दि᳘वं ते धूमो᳘ गच्छत्वन्त᳘रिक्षं ज्यो᳘तिः पृथिवीं भ᳘स्मना᳘पृण स्वाहे᳘ति ॥२५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The gods told (Adhvaryu) "Offer thou only a chip of the yūpa" (Even by this) the paridhis would be deemed to have been thrown and the sacrifice will gain momentum. Then the Rakṣasas will not hereafter sip (the soma) taking that (chip) for a raised thunderbolt. Then, they (gods) offered only a chip of the yūpa. That itself (amounted to) throwing (the paridhis) and the sacrifice gained momentum and the Rakṣasas did not sip (soma) taking that (chip) for a raised thunderbolt. Therefore, only a chip of the yūpa should be thrown. That itself will (serve the purpose of) throwing (the paridhis). He after performing the Anuyājas (after- offerings) offers it (the chip) (saying) "May thy smoke rise up to the sky, thy light to the heavens! Fill the earth with ashes, Svāhā". (First Brāhmaṇa Ends)
</details>
