विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

अग्नि᳓र्वै᳓ स᳓र्वा देव᳓ता अग्नौ᳓ हि᳓ स᳓र्वाभ्यो देव᳓ताभ्यो जु᳓ह्वत्यग्नि᳓रु वा᳓ अवरार्धो᳓ यज्ञ᳓स्य वि᳓ष्णुः परार्ध᳓स्त᳓त्स᳓र्वाश्च देव᳓ता इ᳓ति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
C R Swaminathan

The reason why this oblation is connected with the consecration and is dedicated to Agni and Viṣṇu is that Agni is verily all the gods. Indeed they offer for all the gods into the fire. Agni is the lower part of the sacrifice and Viṣṇu the upper part. In that are all the deities. Therefore, the consecration takes place after encompassing the whole sacrifice. Therefore is it (the puroḍāśa) dedicated to Agni and Viṣṇu.
