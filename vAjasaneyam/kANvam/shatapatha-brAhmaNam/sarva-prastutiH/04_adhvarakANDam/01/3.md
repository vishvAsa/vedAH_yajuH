<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He offers a *puroḍāśa* (sacrificial cake) on eleven potsherds, dedicated to Agni and Viṣṇu (together), connected with the *dīkṣā* (consecration). The reason why this oblation is connected with the consecration and is dedicated to Agni and Viṣṇu is that Agni is verily all the gods. Indeed they offer for all the gods into the fire. Agni is the lower part of the sacrifice and Viṣṇu the upper part. In that are all the deities. Therefore, the consecration takes place after encompassing the whole sacrifice. Therefore is it (the *puroḍāśa*) dedicated to Agni and Viṣṇu.
</details>
