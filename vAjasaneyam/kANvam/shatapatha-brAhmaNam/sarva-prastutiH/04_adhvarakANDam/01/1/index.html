सर्वाष् टीकाः

for the east is the quarter of the gods. And it may incline towards the north; for the north is the quarter of men. It should be high (raised up) at the south. This (south) is indeed the quarter of the manes. If it should slope down towards the south, the sacrificer would soon reach the yonder world. Thereby (by making it incline towards the east or north) the sacrificer lives long.
