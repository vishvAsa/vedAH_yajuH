## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वै᳘ य᳘दैत᳘दाहुः᳘ समु᳘पहूताः स्म इत्य᳘थ पुरोळाशवृकल᳘मादा᳘याध्वर्युरुपो᳘त्तिष्ठत्य᳘थ य᳘त्रैतदा᳘सीनोऽच्छावा᳘कोऽन्वा᳘ह तदु᳘पसन्नो भवति त᳘स्मै पुरोळाशवृकल᳘मादा᳘याहा᳘च्छावाक व᳘दस्व य᳘त्ते वा᳘द्यमि᳘ति स य᳘दसौ पुरोळाशवृकल᳘माद᳘धात्य᳘हीयत वा᳘ अच्छावाकः ॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then, when they say, "We are invited together", the Adhvaryu stands up with a piece of the puroḍāśa and approaches the Acchāvāka priest who is seated and reciting and into his hand he places the puroḍāśa piece and says "O Acchāvāka, say, what thou has to say". Since he gives the puroḍāśa piece to him, Acchāvāka is eliminated (for Soma).
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘मिन्द्राग्नी᳘ अनु स᳘मतनुतां प्रजा᳘नां प्रजातये त᳘स्मादेष᳘ ऐन्द्रा᳘ग्नोऽच्छावाकः स᳘ एते᳘न चार्षेये᳘णय᳘देत᳘दन्वा᳘हैते᳘न च हवि᳘षा स᳘मश्नुते य᳘दस्मा एत᳘त्पुरोळाशवृकल᳘माद᳘धाति त᳘स्माद्वा᳘ अस्मै पुरोळाशवृकलमा᳘दधाति ॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Indra and Agni preserved him for the production of creatures; whence the Acchāvāka priest belongs to Indra and Agni. It is by means of that sacrificial havis, the piece of puroḍāśa now put into his hand and by means of that (mantra) of the Ṛṣis which he now recites, it is thereby they (Indra and Agni) preserve him (Acchāvāka).
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाच्छावाके᳘ सन्न᳘ ऋतुग्रहै᳘श्चरन्ति स य᳘दच्छावाके᳘ सन्न᳘ ऋतुग्रहैश्च᳘रन्ति मिथुनं वा᳘ अच्छावाक᳘ ऐन्द्राग्नो᳘ ह्यच्छावाको द्वौ᳘ हीन्द्राग्नी᳘ द्वन्द्वꣳहि᳘ मिथुनं᳘ प्रज᳘ननं त᳘न्मिथुना᳘दे᳘वैत᳘त्प्रज᳘ननादृतू᳘न्त्संवत्सरं प्र᳘जनयति त᳘स्मादच्छावाके᳘ सन्न᳘ ऋतुग्रहै᳘श्चरन्ति ॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When the Acchāvāka is seated, the Adhvaryu moves with the Soma grahas (cups) meant for the seasons. Now, as to why he moves with the Ṛtu grahaswhen the Acchāvāka is seated—the Acchāvāka represents a sexual union because he is the combination of Indra and Agni; Indra and Agni are two and two constitute a productive pair and only from that same productive pair, he produces the seasons and the year.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘द्वेवर्तुग्रहैश्च᳘रन्त्यृतू᳘नेवैत᳘त्संवत्सरं प्र᳘जनयति स᳘र्वं वा᳘ ऋत᳘वः संवत्सरः स᳘र्वमे᳘वैतत्प्र᳘जनयति ॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And again why he proceeds with the Ṛtu grahas (cups of libations for the seasons) is because he produces the seasons and the year. The seasons and the year constitute everything. So he produces everything.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तान्द्वा᳘दश गृह्णीयाद्द्वा᳘दश मा᳘साः संवत्सर᳘स्य त᳘स्माद्द्वा᳘दश गृह्णीयाद᳘थो त्र᳘योदश गृह्णीयादि᳘त्याहुर᳘स्ति त्रयोदशो मा᳘स इ᳘ति द्वा᳘दश᳘ त्वेव᳘ गृह्णात्ये᳘षैव स्थि᳘तिरेषा᳘ सम्पत् ॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Let him draw twelve of them (Ṛtu grahas) for, there are twelve months for a year. So he should draw twelve (cups). But he may also draw thirteen for, they say there is a thirteenth month. Let him nevertheless draw only twelve; for, that is the practise and that is completeness.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

द्रोणकलशा᳘द्गृह्णाति प्रजा᳘पतिर्वा एष पा᳘त्राणां य᳘द्द्रोणकलश᳘स्तत्प्रजा᳘पतेरे᳘वैत᳘दृतू᳘न्त्संवत्सरं प्र᳘जनयति त᳘स्माद्द्रोणकलशा᳘द्गृह्णाति ॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He draws them from the droṇakalaśa (soma trough) for, the droṇa kalaśa is the Prajāpati among the vessels. So he is producing these seasons and year from Prajāpati. Therefore he draws from the droṇakalaśa.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

उभय᳘तोमुखाभ्यां पा᳘त्राभ्यां गृह्णाति कु᳘तस्त᳘योर᳘न्तो ये᳘ उभय᳘तोमुखे त᳘स्मादय᳘मनन्तः᳘ संवत्सरः प᳘रिप्लवते तान्न᳘ सादयति त᳘स्माद्वयम᳘सन्नः संवत्सरः प᳘रिप्लवते᳘ ॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He draws with double-mouthed cups, for where is the end of those two (cups) which have mouths on either side? That is why this year revolves endlessly. He deposits them (after drawing the soma). Whence this year is ceaselessly rotating.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

नानुवा᳘क्याम᳘न्वाह ह्व᳘यति वा᳘ अनुवा᳘क्यया᳘गतो᳘ ह्ये᳘वाय᳘मृतुर्य᳘दि दि᳘वा यदि न᳘क्तं नानुय᳘जति नेदृतूनपवृण᳘जा इ᳘ति सह᳘ प्रथमौ᳘ स᳘होत्तमौ᳘ गृह्णीतः स᳘र्वमे᳘वैत᳘त्संवत्सरे᳘ण प᳘रिगृह्णीतस्त᳘दिदꣳस᳘र्वꣳसंवत्सरे᳘ण प᳘रिगृहीतं निष्क्रा᳘मत्ये᳘वान्यतरः प्रान्यतरः᳘ पद्यते त᳘स्मादिमे᳘ऽन्व᳘ञ्चो मा᳘सा यन्ति य᳘द्धोभौ᳘ सह᳘ निक्रा᳘मेतां पृ᳘थग्घि मा᳘सा ईयुस्त᳘स्मान्निष्क्रा᳘मत्ये᳘वान्यतरः᳘ प्रान्यतरः᳘ पद्यते ॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He recites no invitatory chant. One invites with an Anuvākya (invitatory chant), that which is yet to come. Here this season has already arrived, either by day or by night. Lest he should twin away the seasons, they (the Adhvaryu and Pratiprasthātā) simultaneously draw the two first and two last libations. Thus everything here is encompassed by means of the year. One (Adhvaryu) walks out (of the Havirdhāna) and the other (Pratiprasthātā) follows his steps; whence these months pass one after the other. But were both of them to walk out together, these months would surely go separated (not in succession). Therefore the one walks out (first) and the other follows his steps.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वा᳘ ऋतु᳘ना षट् प्र᳘चरति त᳘द्देवा अ᳘हर᳘सृजन्ता᳘थर्तुभिश्चतुस्तद्रा᳘त्रिमसृजन्त तद्य᳘देता᳘वदेवा᳘भविष्यद्रा᳘त्रिरे᳘वाभविष्यन्न᳘ व्यवत्स्यद᳘थ पु᳘नरृतु᳘ना द्विस्त᳘त्पुर᳘स्ताद᳘हः प᳘र्यदधुस्त᳘स्माद᳘द्येदम᳘हर᳘थ रा᳘त्रिर्भविष्यत्य᳘थ श्वो᳘ऽहर्भविता ॥९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"With the season" (so saying) they perform six times; thereby the gods created the day. "With the seasons" (so saying) they perform four times; thereby they created the night. Were it to be confined to this much (four times saying 'with the seasons'), there would be only night; it would never pass away. Again (saying) "With the season" they perform twice, thereby the gods subsequently generated the day (again) and hence now we have the day here, then it will be night (later) and (again) the day is to come tomorrow.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘द्वे᳘वर्तु᳘ना ते᳘न मनु᳘ष्यान᳘सृजन्त य᳘दृतु᳘भिस्ते᳘न पशून्त्स यत्त᳘दुभय᳘तो ये᳘न मनु᳘ष्यान᳘सृजन्त त᳘स्माद्विम᳘ उभय᳘तः प᳘रिगृहीताः पश᳘वो मनु᳘ष्याणां व᳘शमु᳘पेताः ॥१०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Ṛtunā—with the season"—(so saying) the gods created men; "With the seasons"—(so saying) the beasts. Since they created men on either sides (of the beasts), these beasts are bracketed on both sides and are submissive to (controlled by) men.
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वा᳘ ऋतु᳘ना चरि᳘त्वेतर᳘था विप᳘र्यस्यत्यृतु᳘भिश्चरि᳘त्वेतर᳘था विप᳘र्यस्यत्यन्यतर᳘त ए᳘वैतद᳘हः सृज᳘तेऽन्यतर᳘तो रा᳘त्रिमन्यतर᳘तो मनु᳘ष्यानन्यतर᳘तः पशूꣳस्त᳘स्माद्विपर्या᳘सं गृह्णाति ॥११॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Having performed with "With the season" (or having performed six times thus), they both turn round their vessels. They turn round their vessels after performing with "With the season" from one side they create day and from the other side, night; from one side, man and from the other side, beasts. That is why they turn round and draw the *grahas*.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ वा अ᳘तो गृह्णा᳘त्ये᳘वोपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसि म᳘धवे त्वे᳘त्यध्वर्यु᳘रुपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसि मा᳘धवाय त्वे᳘ति प्रतिप्रस्थाता तौ वा᳘सन्तिकावृतू᳘ वसन्ते ह्यो᳘षधयो जा᳘यन्ते व᳘नस्प᳘तयः पच्य᳘न्ते ते᳘न तौ म᳘धुश्च मा᳘धवश्च ॥१२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Or they draw only thus—"Thou art taken with a support" (so saying) the Adhvaryu (draws saying) "Thou for *Madhu* (months)", "Thou art taken with a support" (So saying) the Pratiprasthātā (draws saying) "Thou for *Mādhava* (month)". They (*Madhu* and *Mādhava*) are the spring season. It is indeed in the spring, plants sprout and trees ripen. Therefore these two are *Madhu* and *Mādhava* (the sweet one and the one after that).
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

उपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसि शुक्रा᳘य त्वे᳘त्यध्वर्युरुपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसि शु᳘चये त्वे᳘ति प्रतिप्रस्थाता तौ ग्रै᳘ष्मावृतू यत्त᳘योर्ब᳘लत्त᳘पति ते᳘न तौ᳘ शुक्र᳘श्च शु᳘चिश्च ॥१३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Thou art taken with a support"—(so saying) the Adhvaryu (draws saying) "Thee for *Śukra* (month)". "Thou art taken with a support". The Pratiprasthātā (draws) (saying) "Thee for *Śuci* (month)". They two form summer season. Because during those (months) it burns severest. Therefore they are *Śukra* and *Śuci* (they afflict).
</details>


## १४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

उपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसि न᳘भसे त्वे᳘त्ये᳘वाध्वर्यु᳘रुपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसि नभ᳘स्याय त्वे᳘ति प्रतिप्रस्थाता ता᳘वेव वा᳘र्षिकावमु᳘तो हि᳘ दिवो᳘ऽधि व᳘र्षति ते᳘न तौ न᳘भश्च नभ᳘स्यश्च ॥१४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Thou art taken with a support"—(so saying) the Adhvaryu (draws saying) "Thee for *Nabhas* (month)". "Thou art taken with a support". The Pratiprasthātā (draws) (saying) "Thee for *Nabhasya* (month)". They are the two rainy (months). In that (season), it rains from yonder sky. Hence they are *Nabhas* and *Nabhasya* (they cause rain from *nabhas* or sky).
</details>


## १५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

उपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसीषे त्वे᳘त्यध्वर्यु᳘रुपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽस्यूर्जे त्वे᳘ति प्रतिप्रस्थाता तौ᳘ शारदा᳘वृतू᳘ शर᳘दि᳘ ह्यूर्ग्र᳘स ओ᳘षधयः पच्य᳘न्ते ते᳘न ता᳘विष᳘श्चोर्ज᳘श्च ॥१५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Thou art taken with a support"—(so saying) the Adhvaryu (draws saying) "Thee for the month of *Iṣ* (sap)"; "Thou art taken with a support". The Pratiprasthātā (draws saying) "Thee for the month of *Ūrj* (food)". These two are the autumn season. Because in autumn, food grains grow (big *ūrj*) and juicy plants (*iṣ*) ripen. Therefore these two are *Iṣa* and *Ūrja*. (They cause production of food and nutri- tion).
</details>


## १६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

उपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसि स᳘हसे त्वे᳘त्यध्वर्यु᳘रुपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसि सह᳘स्याय त्वे᳘ति प्रतिप्रस्थाता तौ है᳘मन्तिकावृतू᳘ हेमन्तो᳘ हीमाः᳘ प्रजाः स᳘हसेव स्वं व᳘शमुपन᳘यते ते᳘न तौ स᳘हश्च सह᳘स्यश्च ॥१६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Thou art taken with a support—(so saying) the Adhvaryu (draws saying) "Thee for *Sahasya* (month)". "Thou art taken with a support". The Pratiprasthātā (draws saying)—"Thee for *Sahasya* (month)". These two form the winter season: because the winter by force (*sahas*) brings these creatures into his power; therefore these two are *Sahas* and *Sahasya*.
</details>


## १७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

उपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसि त᳘पसे त्वे᳘त्यध्वर्यु᳘रुपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसि तप᳘स्याय त्वे᳘ति प्रतिप्रस्थाता तौ᳘ शिशिरा᳘वृतू यत्त᳘योर्ब᳘लवच्छ्या᳘ यति ते᳘न तौ त᳘पश्च तप᳘स्यश्च ॥१७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Thou art taken with a support" (so saying)—the Adhvaryu (draws saying) "Thee for *Tapas* (month)". "Thou art taken with a support". The Pratiprasthātā (draws saying) "Thee for *Tapasya* (month)". These two are the months of the decay season; because during them it freezes most severely; therefore these two are *Tapas* and *Tapasya* (they cause shrinking).
</details>


## १८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

उपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽस्यꣳहसस्पत᳘ये त्वेति य᳘दि त्रयोदशं᳘ गृह्णात्य᳘थ प्रतिप्रस्थाता᳘ वाध्वर्योः पा᳘त्रे सꣳस्रव᳘मवन᳘यति प्रतिप्रस्थातुर्वाध्वर्युर्भक्ष᳘मध्वर्युर्हरत्य᳘थ त᳘थैवा᳘भक्षितेन प्रतिप्रस्था᳘तैत᳘मैन्द्राग्नं ग्र᳘हं गृह्णाति स यद᳘भक्षितेनैत᳘मैन्द्राग्नं ग्र᳘हं गृह्णाति न वा᳘ ऋतुग्रहा᳘मनुव᳘षट्करोति स᳘ एते᳘नैन्द्राग्ने᳘नानुव᳘षट्कृतो भवन्ति त᳘स्माद᳘भक्षितेन गृह्णाति ॥१८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Thou art taken with a support thee to the Aṃhasaspati"— (Thus saying)— if he draws the thirteenth (cup) then the Pratiprasthātā pours his residue into the Adhvaryu's vessel or the Adhvaryu pours into the Pratiprasthātās (vessel). He (the Adhvaryu) takes it (to the Sadas) for the purpose of drinking. Thereupon, the Pratiprasthātā draws the *Aindrāgṇa graha* with the vessel not used for drinking. As to why he draws the *Aindrāgṇa graha* with the vessel unused for drinking is that no second Vaṣaṭkāra is pronounced in the case of Ṛtu graha. He, by this *Aindrāgṇa graha* make them consecrated by the second Vaṣaṭkāra, so he draws with the vessel unused for drinking.
</details>


## १९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘द्वेवा᳘भक्षितेन गृह्णा᳘ति स᳘र्वं वा᳘ एतत्प्रा᳘जीजनद्य᳘दृतुग्रहान᳘ग्रहीत्तत्स᳘र्वं प्रजन᳘य्य प्राणोदान᳘योः प्र᳘तिष्ठापयतीमे हि द्या᳘वापृथि᳘व्यौ प्राणोदाना᳘वन᳘यो᳘र्हीदꣳस᳘र्वं प्र᳘तिष्ठितं त᳘स्माद᳘भक्षितेन गृह्णाति ॥१९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And again why he draws with the vessel unused for drinking—By drawing the *Ṛtu grahas* (cups to the season) he has generated this All; and having generated this All, he establishes it on the *prāṇa* and *udāna*. These sky and earth are the *prāṇa* and *udāna* and within these two, this All is established.
</details>


## २०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘द्वेवा᳘भक्षितेन गृह्णाति स᳘र्वं वा᳘ एतत्प्रा᳘जीजनद्य᳘दृतुग्रहान᳘ग्रहीत्तत्स᳘र्वं प्रजन᳘य्य त᳘स्मिन्त्स᳘र्वस्मिन्प्राणोदातौ᳘ दधाति ता᳘विमा᳘वस्मिन्त्स᳘र्वस्मिन्प्राणोदानौ᳘ हितौ त᳘स्माद᳘भक्षितेन गृह्णाति ॥२०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And again why he draws with the vessel unused for drinking—By drawing the *Ṛtu grahas*, he has generated this All and having generated this All, he lays the *prāṇa* and *udāna* into this All, hence these two, *prāṇa* and *udāna* are laid into (as beneficiaries) this All. Therefore he draws with the vessel unused for drinking.
</details>


## २१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ गृह्णाती᳘न्द्राग्नी आ᳘गतꣳसुतं᳘ गीर्भिर्न᳘भो व᳘रेण्यम् । अस्य᳘ पातं धिये᳘षिता । उपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसीन्द्राग्नि᳘भ्यां त्वैष᳘ ते यो᳘निरिन्द्राग्नि᳘भ्यां त्वे᳘ति सादयति ॥२१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He draws (saying) "O Indra and Agni, come Ye hither to the Soma, rendered desirable by our songs like the Sun. Requested by our thoughts (requested to the extend we can wish) take part of this Soma. Thou art taken with a support, thee to Indra and Agni" (so saying). "This is thy womb; thee to Indra and Agni" (so saying) he deposits.
</details>


## २२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थैतं᳘ वैश्वदेवं ग्र᳘हं गृह्णाति स य᳘देतं᳘ वैश्वदेवं ग्र᳘हं गृह्णा᳘ति स᳘र्वं वा᳘ एतत्प्रा᳘जीजनद्य᳘दृतुग्रहान᳘ग्रहीत्तद्य᳘देता᳘वदेवा᳘भविष्यद्या᳘वत्यो हैवा᳘ग्रे प्रजाः᳘ सृष्टास्ता᳘वत्य ए᳘वाभविष्यन्न प्रा᳘जनिष्यन्त स᳘र्वमु वै वि᳘श्वेदेवास्तत्स᳘र्वमनु᳘व्यर्जति त᳘स्माद्विमाः᳘ प्रजाः᳘ पुनरभ्याका᳘रं प्र᳘जायन्ते ॥२२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he draws the *Vaiśvadeva graha* (the *soma* cup for *Viśvadevas*). As to why he draws the *Vaiśvadeva graha* — By drawing the *Ṛtu grahas*, he generted this All. If it had been that much (stopped with that) whatever creatures were thereby generated at the beginning, that much alone would have been there and they would not have procreated. *Viśvedevas* are all and that all he reproduces and hence these creatures are born in the respective forms.
</details>


## २३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

शुक्रपात्रे᳘ण गृह्णात्येष वाव᳘ शुक्रो य᳘ एष त᳘पत्य᳘थ ये᳘ रश्म᳘यस्ते वि᳘श्वेदेवास्त᳘स्माच्छुक्रपात्रे᳘ण गृह्णाति ॥२३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He draws it with the *Śukra*cup, for the *Śukra* (bright) is the yonder one who burns (sun). What happens to be his rays are *Viśvedevas*. Therefore he draws with the *Śukra* cup.
</details>


## २४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ गृह्णात्यो᳘मासश्चर्षणीधृतो वि᳘श्वे देवास आ᳘गत । दाश्वा᳘ꣳ᳘सो दाशु᳘षः सुतम् । उपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसि वि᳘श्वेभ्यस्त्वा देवे᳘भ्य एष᳘ ते यो᳘निर्वि᳘श्वेभ्यस्त्वा देवे᳘भ्य इ᳘ति सादयति ॥२४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He draws it (saying) “Ye protectors and supporters of men, O Viśvedevas, come hither, Ye givers (off boons) towards the Soma of this benevolent (Yajamāna). Thou art taken with a support; there to the Viśvedevas”. (Then saying) “This is thy womb, thee to the Viśvedevas”, he deposits it. (Second Brāhmaṇa Ends)
</details>
