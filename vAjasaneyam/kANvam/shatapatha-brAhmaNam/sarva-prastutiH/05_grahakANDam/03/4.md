## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

इहा᳘ ३ इहा ३ इत्यभि᳘षुणोती᳘न्द्रमेव तदा᳘च्यावयति बृह᳘द्बृहदिती᳘न्द्रमेव तदा᳘च्यावयति स᳘ शुक्रा᳘मन्थि᳘नौ प्रथमौ᳘ गृह्णाति शुक्र᳘व᳘द्ध्येतत्स᳘वनम् ॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He presses out (The soma juice) (saying) 'iha! iha' (hither, hither). Whereby he draws nigh Indra himself. (By saying) 'bṛhat! bṛhat' (great, great) he draws nigh Indra himself. The Śukra and Manthi grahas he first draws, because this savana is indeed, with pure soma (Śukra).
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाग्रयणं᳘ गृह्णाति स᳘र्वेषु᳘ ह्येषु स᳘वनेषु गृह्य᳘ते त᳘स्मादाग्रयणं᳘ गृह्णात्य᳘थ मरुत्वती᳘यं गृह्णात्य᳘थो᳘क्थ्यं गृह्णात्युक्था᳘नि᳘ ह्यत्रा᳘पि भ᳘वन्ति तद्वा᳘ आहुरु᳘क्थ्यमेव पू᳘र्वं गृह्णीयादित्य᳘थ मरुत्वती᳘यमि᳘ति मरुत्वती᳘य᳘ त्वेव पू᳘र्वं गृह्णात्य᳘थो᳘क्थ्यम् ॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he draws the Āgrayaṇa graha because it is drawn in all the savanas. So he draws the Āgrayaṇa. Then he draws the Marutvatīya graha. Then draws the Ukthya, for him also there are ukthyas (songs of praise). Here some say that Ukthya should be drawn before Marutvatīya. But Marutvatīya is drawn first and then Ukthya.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तान्वा᳘ एतान्प᳘ञ्च ग्र᳘हान्गृह्णाति स य᳘देतान्प᳘ञ्च ग्र᳘हान् गृह्णा᳘त्येष वै व᳘ज्रो य᳘देष मा᳘ध्यन्दिनः प᳘वमानस्त᳘स्मात्प᳘ञ्चदशः प᳘ञ्चसामा भवति पञ्चदशो हि व᳘ज्रस्त᳘मेतैः᳘ पञ्च᳘भिर्ग्र᳘हैः प᳘ञ्च हीमा᳘ अङ्गु᳘लयोऽङ्गु᳘लिभिर्हि᳘ प्रह᳘रती᳘न्द्रो वृत्रा᳘य व᳘ज्रं प्रा᳘हरत्ते᳘न वृत्र᳘महꣳस्ते᳘न᳘ व्यजयन्त ते वि᳘जिते᳘ऽभयेऽनाष्ट्रे द᳘क्षिणा अनयन्त त᳘स्माद᳘प्येत᳘र्हि य᳘दै᳘वैते᳘न मा᳘ध्यन्दिनेन प᳘वमानेन स्तुवते᳘ऽथ द᳘क्षिणा नयन्ते वि᳘जित ए᳘वैतद᳘भयेऽनाष्ट्रे द᳘क्षिणा नयन्ते त᳘थो वा᳘ एष᳘ एतैः᳘ पञ्च᳘भिर्ग्र᳘हैरेतं व᳘ज्रं द्विषते भ्रा᳘तृव्याय प्र᳘हरति ते᳘न द्विष᳘न्तं भ्रा᳘तृव्यꣳहन्ति ते᳘न वि᳘जयते त᳘स्माद्वा᳘ एतान् प᳘ञ्च ग्र᳘हान्गृह्णाति ॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

There are the five grahas he draws; that he draws these five grahas is because this midday Pavamāna (chant) is a thunderbolt. Therefore it is a fifteen-fold (stoma). For thunderbolt is fifteen-fold. He (Pavamāna) is so by means of these five grahas, for, five are the fingers and with the fingers Indra hurled the thunderbolt at Vṛtra. He hurled and by that killed Vṛtra. With that he conquered. Having won freedom from fear and safety they led forth the sacrifice to happy completion. Therefore here too, when he praises with the midday Pavamāna chants, they lead (the sacrifice) to happy completion. Thus conquered, it is taken to completion free from fear and quite safe. That is why he with these five grahas, hurls this thunder- bolt (of Pavamāna), at his hateful enemy. By that he kills the hateful enemy. Thus he conquers. That is why he draws these five grahas.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘न्मरुत्वती᳘यान् गृह्णा᳘त्येतद्वा इ᳘न्द्रस्य नि᳘ष्केवल्यꣳ स᳘वनं यन्मा᳘ध्यन्दिनꣳ स᳘वनं ते᳘न वृत्र᳘मजिघाꣳसत्ते᳘न᳘ व्यजिगीषत क्षत्र᳘मु वा इ᳘न्द्रो वि᳘शो मरु᳘तो विशा वै᳘ क्षत्रि᳘यो ब᳘लीयान्भवति ॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now, as to why he draws the Marutvatīya grahas—This midday savana is indeed, the special savana of Indra. By that he strove to attack Vṛtra. By that he strove to vanquish. Indra is the kṣatras (modility) and the Maruts are the viś (people). It is with the people, kṣatra becomes strong.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

सा᳘ हाश्वत्थे ति᳘ष्ठत इ᳘न्द्रो मरु᳘त उपमन्त्रयां᳘चक्रे त᳘स्मादा᳘श्वत्थे ऋतुपात्रे᳘स्यातामि᳘त्याहुः कार्ष्मर्यम᳘ये᳘ त्वेव᳘ भवत उ᳘प मा᳘वर्तध्वं युष्मा᳘भिर्ब᳘लेन वृत्र᳘ꣳ᳘ हनानी᳘ति ॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He, Indra, called on those Maruts who were situated, on an Aśvattha tree (to join him in vanquishing Vṛtra), therefore those two Ṛtu cups (so they say) may be of Aśvattha wood. But they are actually of Kārṣmarya wood. (He said) "Do you join me so that with you as my army, I may kill Vṛtra".
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते᳘ होचुः कि᳘मस्मा᳘कं त᳘तः स्यादि᳘ति ते᳘भ्य एता ग्र᳘हावगृह्णादेतौ᳘ मरुत्वती᳘यौ ॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They (Maruts) asked "What will happen to us then". He (Indra) drew these two grahas. They are the two Marutvatīya cups.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते᳘ होचुरपनिधा᳘यैनमो᳘ज उपा᳘वर्तामहा इ᳘ति तथे᳘ति त᳘मपनिधायौ᳘ज उपा᳘ववृतुस्त᳘दु वा इ᳘न्द्रो ऽस्पृणुत तत्प्र᳘त्यबुध्यतापनिधा᳘य वै मौ᳘ज उपा᳘वर्त्स्यथे᳘ति ॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They (Maruts) said "Having put aside this one (cup) for our vigour, we will join thee". "Be it so" (said Indra). Having thus put aside one cup for their vigour, they joined (Indra). But Indra coveted that (cup put aside) realising 'these Maruts have joined after putting aside their vigour'.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ होवाच᳘ मैत᳘दपनि᳘धिध्वमि᳘ति स᳘ हैवमौ᳘जसोपा᳘वर्तध्वमि᳘ति ते᳘ होचुस्त᳘स्मै वै᳘ नोऽन्यं᳘ भागं᳘ कल्पये᳘ति त᳘स्मा एत᳘मन्यं᳘ भाग᳘मकल्पयदुपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसि मरु᳘तामो᳘जसे त्वे᳘ति त᳘ꣳ᳘ सहौ᳘जसोपा᳘वर्तन्त ते᳘न ब᳘लेन वृत्र᳘महस्ते᳘न᳘ व्यजिगीषत क्षत्र᳘मु वा इ᳘न्द्रो वि᳘शो मरु᳘तो विशा वै᳘ क्षत्रि᳘यो ब᳘लीयान् भवति क्षत्र᳘ ए᳘वैतद्ब᳘लं दधाति ॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He (Indra) said "Do not put aside that (vigour of yours); join me along with your vigour". They (Maruts) said "For that, you assign another share for us". Hence he assigned this another share (saying) "Thou art taken with a support, for the vigour of the Maruts; to thee (Maruts) with vigour". They joined him along with their vigour. With that strength he struck Vṛtra.; with that he conquered. Indra is indeed, the kṣatra and Maruts are the viś (people). Kṣatriya becomes strong by the people. Now (the Yajamāna) endows kṣatra with that strength.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तान्वा इ᳘न्द्रायैव᳘ मरुत्वते गृह्णाति न᳘ मरु᳘द्भ्यो य᳘द्ध मरु᳘द्भ्य इ᳘ति गृह्णीया᳘त्प्रत्युद्यामि᳘नीꣳ ह क्षत्रि᳘याय वि᳘शं कुर्यात्तदि᳘न्द्रमे᳘वैतद᳘नु मरु᳘त आ᳘भजत्यप्रत्युद्यामि᳘नीमे᳘वैत᳘त्क्षत्रि᳘याय वि᳘शं करोत्य᳘नुवर्त्मानमपक्रमा᳘दु है᳘वैषां बिभयां᳘चकार य᳘दिमेमं᳘ नापक्रा᳘मेयुरि᳘ति ता᳘नेत द᳘नपक्रमिण ए᳘वाकुरुत त᳘स्मादि᳘न्द्रायैव᳘ मरु᳘त्वते गृह्णा᳘ति न᳘ मरुद्भ्यः ॥९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They (grahas) are drawn for Indra, the Marutvat (Indra accompained by Maruts). If they were drawn exclusively for Maruts, (it would amount to) the commoners rising up as equals to Ksatriya. Here the Maruts only subserve Indra (by drawing for Indra, the Marutvat). So there is no rising up as equals on the parts of the commoners with Ksatriya (nobility). (Actually) Indra feared that these (Maruts) might desert him. (So he thought) "How these (Maruts) will not go out of my control". And made them non-deserters (loyal) so (the graha) is drawn only for Indra associated with Maruts and not to the Maruts (exclusively).
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ऋतुपा᳘त्राभ्यां गृह्णात्यृत᳘वो वै᳘ संवत्सरो᳘ यज्ञस्ता᳘नदः᳘ प्रत्य᳘क्षं प्रातःसवने᳘ऽव᳘कल्पयति य᳘दृतुग्रहा᳘न् गृह्णात्य᳘थैनानेत᳘त्परो᳘क्षं मा᳘ध्यन्दिने स᳘वने᳘ ऽपक्लृप्यन्ते य᳘दृतुपात्रा᳘भ्यां मरुत्वती᳘यान्गृह्णा᳘ति वि᳘शो वै मरुतो᳘ऽन्नमु वै वि᳘श ऋत᳘वो वा᳘ इदꣳस᳘र्वम᳘न्नं पचन्ति त᳘स्मादृतुपात्रा᳘भ्यां गृह्णाति ॥१०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He draws them with the two vessels of the seasons (the Ṛtu grahas). The seasons are the year, the sacrifice. They (the seasons) are directly attended to in the morning savana. In so far as he (now) draws in the Ṛtu grahas during the midday savana he attends to them (seasons) indirectly (covertly). So he draws the two Marutvatīya grahas in the two vessels of the seasons. The Maruts are the viś (commoners) and food means commoners and the seasons, indeed, cook all this food. So he draws with the Ṛtu grahas..
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ गृह्णाती᳘न्द्रमरुत्व इह᳘ पाहि सो᳘मं मरु᳘त्वन्तं वृषभं᳘ वावृधानं᳘ मरु᳘तामो᳘जसे त्वेति ॥११॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He draws (saying) "O Indra, leader of the Maruts, drink thou the soma here; to thee accompanied by Maruts; the mightest bull; for the vigour of the Maruts".
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थैतं᳘ माहेन्द्रं ग्र᳘हं गृह्णाति स य᳘देतं᳘ माहेन्द्रं ग्र᳘हं गृह्णा᳘ति पाप्म᳘ना वा᳘ इदमि᳘न्द्रः स᳘ꣳ᳘सृष्टोऽभूत्सं᳘यतो य᳘द्विशा᳘ मरु᳘द्भिर्य᳘था समाने पा᳘त्रे ऽश्नीया᳘द्विजय᳘स्य का᳘माय क्षत्रि᳘यो वै᳘श्येनैव᳘मेतद्य᳘दस्मै समान᳘मेभिर्ग्र᳘हमग्रहीषुस्त᳘मेत᳘द्देवा वि᳘जिते᳘ऽभये ऽनाष्ट्रे य᳘थेषी᳘कां वि᳘मुञ्चां कुर्या᳘देवं᳘ पाप्म᳘नो᳘ व्यवृहन्य᳘न्माहेन्द्रं ग्र᳘हम᳘गृह्णꣳस्त᳘थो वा᳘ एवमे᳘ष एतद्वि᳘जिते᳘ ऽभयेऽनाष्ट्रे य᳘थेषी᳘कां वि᳘मुञ्चां कुर्या᳘देवं᳘ पाप्म᳘नो वि᳘वृहति य᳘न्माहेन्द्रं ग्र᳘हं गृह्णाती᳘न्द्रो वा᳘ एष᳘ पुरा᳘ वृत्र᳘स्य वधादित्य᳘थ वृत्रं᳘ जघ्निवान्य᳘था महाराजो᳘ विजिग्यान᳘ एव᳘ माहेन्द्र इ᳘ति त᳘स्मान्माहेन्द्रं᳘ गृह्णाति ॥१२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he draws this Māhendra graha. Now as to why he draws the Māhendra graha. For, Indra was then bound up by sin since he got associated with viś (the commoners), namely the Maruts. Because when they drew for both (Indra Marutvat) in the same vessel, it amounted to Ksatriya eating from the same vessel with the commoners for the sake of victory (as an expediency). After the victory was achieved, at a place free from fear and safe, drew out (from Indra) that sin (of sharing the same vessel with the commoners), by drawing the Mahendra graha, just as a reed could be pulled out of the sheeth. In like manner this one by virtue of the Māhendra graha, separates him (Yajamāna) from sin after victory (at a place), free from fear and safe in the manner of pulling out a reed from its sheath. He was only Indra before slaying of Vṛtra. Just as one (a king) vanquishing (his enemies) becomes Mahārāja (emperor), he (Indra) became Mahendra (after killing Vṛtra). So he draws the Māhendra graha.
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

शुक्रपात्रे᳘ण गृह्णात्येष वाव᳘ शुक्रो य᳘ एष त᳘पत्येष᳘ उ वा इ᳘न्द्रस्त᳘स्माच्छुक्रपात्रे᳘ण गृह्णाति ॥१३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He draws it in the Śukra vessel. That one who burns yonder (Sun) is Śukra (bright). He is indeed, Indra. Therefore he draws in the Śukra vessel.
</details>


## १४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ गृह्णाति महां ३ इ᳘न्द्रो नृवदा᳘ चर्षणिप्रा᳘ उत द्विब᳘र्हा अमिनः स᳘होभिः । अस्म᳘द्र्यग्वावृधे वी᳘र्यायोरुः᳘ पृथुः सु᳘कृतः कर्तृ᳘भिर्भूत् । उपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसि माहेन्द्रा᳘य त्वैष᳘ ते यो᳘निर्माहेन्द्रा᳘य त्वे᳘ति सादयति सादयित्वा स्तोत्र᳘मुपा᳘करोति स्तोत्र᳘मुपाकृ᳘त्य संप्रे᳘ष्यत्य᳘भिषोतारोऽभि᳘षुणुतौलूखलानु᳘द्वादयता᳘ग्नीदाशि᳘रं वि᳘नय सौम्य᳘स्य वित्तादि᳘ति ते᳘ यथा संप्रेषितं᳘ कुर्वन्ति स यद᳘त्रैत᳘त्संप्रेष्यत्य᳘त्त्रैतत्क᳘र्म क्रिय᳘त एते वै र᳘सवती स᳘वने शुक्र᳘वती य᳘त्प्रातःसवनं᳘ च मा᳘ध्यन्दिनञ्च स᳘वनम᳘थैतन्नि᳘र्धीतशुक्रं य᳘त्तृतीयसवनं तन्मा᳘ध्यन्दिनादे᳘वैतत्स᳘वनात्तृतीयसवनं नि᳘र्मिमीत एते᳘नो हैतत्स᳘वनꣳ शुक्र᳘वद्र᳘सवद्भवति त᳘स्माद᳘त्रैत᳘त्संप्रे᳘ष्यत्य᳘त्रैतत्क᳘र्म क्रियते ॥१४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He draws (saying "Great is Indra and hero-like, gladdening the people, of double stature and unimpaired in power. For our sake, he waxed strong for heroic deed—great and broad was he, and well-shapen by the makers:––Thou art taken with a support; thee to Mahendra". "This is thy womb; thee to Mahendra"-(so saying) he deposits. After depositing he offers praise. Having offered praise, he gives directions—"Soma pressers, press on and make the mortars resound; Agnīdh, stir the sour milk (āśira); be mindful of soma's (pap)". They do as they are directed. As to why he gives these directions now and get them done now (at the midday savana)—These two savanas, the morning and midday savanas, are indeed, rich in juice, that is rich in pure soma. That which is the third savana is the one where the pure soma is dried up (emptied). That is, the third savana is formed only from the midday savana. By these (preparatory directions) the third savana becomes one with juice, pure soma. That is why here (in the midday savana) these directions are given and these (preparatory acts like pressing) are got done. (Fourth Brāhmaṇa Ends) (Chapter Three Ends)
</details>
