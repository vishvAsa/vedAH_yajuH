## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘र्वे ह वा अ᳘ग्रे सदृ᳘शा एव᳘ देवा᳘ आसुस्ते᳘षाꣳ स᳘र्वेषाꣳ सदृ᳘शानाꣳ सता᳘मि᳘मेऽकामयन्ताग्निरि᳘न्द्रो᳘ योऽसौ त᳘पत्यतिष्ठा᳘वान इव स्यामे᳘ति त᳘ एतान्ग्र᳘हानपश्यꣳस्तान᳘त्यगृह्णत तद्य᳘देतानत्य᳘गृह्णत त᳘स्मादतिग्रा᳘ह्या ना᳘म ते स᳘र्वेषाꣳ सदृ᳘शानां पु᳘ण्यानाꣳ सतां᳘ देवा᳘नामतिष्ठा᳘वान इवाभवन्नतिष्ठा᳘वेव ह वै᳘ भवति य᳘स्यैवं᳘ विदु᳘ष एता᳘न्गृह्ण᳘न्ति᳘ ॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

At the beginning all the gods were alike. When all were alike, these three Agni, Indra and the one who burns (Sun) desired "Let us be above (others)". They saw these Soma grahas. They drew them. So they are called Atigrāhyās (those to be drawn in excess). They (all gods) though equal and meritorious, these three became superior, as it were. He who knows this and draws them becomes superior.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

नो ह वा᳘ इद᳘मग्ना᳘वासा᳘ग्रे व᳘र्चो य᳘दस्मिन्निदं व᳘र्चः᳘ सो ऽकामयतेदं म᳘यि व᳘र्चः स्यादि᳘ति स᳘ एतं ग्र᳘हमपश्यत्तम᳘त्यगृह्णीत त᳘तोऽस्मिन्नेतद्व᳘र्चो ऽभव᳘त् ॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And in the beginning, this (much of) lustre was not in Agni, which, is there now. This lustre he desired for (thinking) 'let me have this lustre' and saw this (Atigrāha) graha. He drew it and then this lustre came into him.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

नो है᳘वैतदि᳘न्द्र ओ᳘ज आस य᳘दस्मिन्नेतदो᳘जः᳘ सोऽकामयतेदं मय्यो᳘जः स्यादि᳘ति स᳘ एतं ग्र᳘हमपश्यत्तम᳘त्यगृह्णीत त᳘तोऽस्मिन्नेतदो᳘जो ऽभव᳘त् ॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And (in the beginning) this (much of) vigour was not there in Indra, which is there now. He desired 'let me have this vigour' and he saw this graha and drew it. Then this vigour came into him.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

नो है᳘वैतत्सू᳘र्ये भ्रा᳘ज आस य᳘दस्मिन्नेतद्भ्रा᳘जो येनैतत्त᳘पति᳘ सोऽकामयतेदं म᳘यि भ्रा᳘जः स्यादि᳘ति स᳘ एतं ग्र᳘हमपश्यत्तम᳘त्यगृह्णीत त᳘तोऽस्मिन्नेतद्भ्रा᳘जोऽभवदेता᳘नि हैव᳘ वी᳘र्याण्यात्म᳘नि धत्ते य᳘स्यैवं᳘ विदु᳘ष एता᳘न्गृह्ण᳘न्ति ॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And (in the beginning) this (much of) splendour was not there in Sun, which is there now and by which he burns. He desired 'let me have this splendour' and he saw this graha and drew it. Then this splendour came into him. One who knows thus and draws these grahas, comes to possess those powers.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तान्वा᳘ आग्रयण᳘मेव᳘ गृहीत्वा᳘थैता᳘न्गृह्णात्यात्मा वा᳘ आग्रयण᳘ आत्म᳘नि वा᳘ इदं᳘ बह्व᳘तिरिक्तं क्लोमहृदयं᳘ त्वद्य᳘त्वक्त᳘स्मादाग्रयण᳘मेव᳘ गृहीत्वा᳘थैता᳘न्गृह्णात्य᳘थो अ᳘प्यु᳘क्थ्यमेव᳘ गृहीत्वा᳘थैता᳘न्गृह्णीयादि᳘त्याहुरयꣳ ह्य᳘निरुक्तः प्राणः उ᳘क्थ्य इ᳘त्यात्मा᳘ ह्ययम᳘निरुक्तः प्राण इ᳘ति त᳘स्मादु᳘क्थ्यमेव᳘ गृहीत्वा᳘थैता᳘न्गृह्णीयादि᳘त्याग्रयणं᳘ त्वेव᳘ गृहीत्वा᳘थैता᳘न्गृह्णाति ॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He draws these (grahas) only after drawing the Āgrayaṇa graha, for, the Āgrayaṇa is the self (body), because it is in the body that these many parts like lung, heart and skin are. So only after drawing Āgrayaṇa, he draws these (grahas). Some say that these should be drawn only after drawing the Ukthya graha, because this Ukthya is the undefined vital air. This body (self) is the undefined vital air. So only after drawing Ukthya, these (grahas) are to be drawn". Nevertheless, let him draw these after drawing Āgrayaṇa.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते᳘ माहेन्द्र᳘स्यैवा᳘नु हो᳘मꣳ हूयन्त एष वा इ᳘न्द्रस्य नि᳘ष्केवल्यो ग्र᳘हो य᳘न्माहेन्द्रो᳘ऽप्यस्यैतन्नि᳘ष्केवल्यमेव᳘ स्तोत्रं नि᳘ष्केवल्यꣳ शस्त्रमि᳘न्द्रो वै य᳘जमानो य᳘जमानस्य वा᳘ एते का᳘माय गृह्यन्ते त᳘स्मान्माहेन्द्र᳘स्यैवा᳘नु हो᳘मꣳ हूयन्ते ॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They (Atigrāhya grahas) are to be offered following the offering of the Mahendra graha. For, that (Mahendra) is Indra's special graha and hence it has special stotra and special śāstra. The Yajamāna is Indra and these (Atigrāhyās) are drawn for (fulfilling) the wish of the Yajamāna. Therefore they are offered after the Mahendra graha.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ गृह्णात्य᳘ग्न आ᳘यूꣳषि पवस आ᳘सुवो᳘र्जमि᳘षं च नः । आरे᳘ बाधस्व दुच्छु᳘नाम् ॥ उपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽस्यग्न᳘ये त्वा व᳘र्चस एष᳘ ते यो᳘निरग्न᳘ये त्वा व᳘र्चस इ᳘ति सादयति ॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He draws (saying) “O Agni, becoming pure, bestow upon us long-life, vigour and health; afflict our enemies. Thou art taken with a support—thee to Agni, for lustre. "This is thy womb; then to Agni for lustre” (so saying) he deposits it.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

उत्ति᳘ष्ठन्नो᳘जसा सह᳘ पीत्वी शि᳘प्रे अवेपयः । सो᳘ममिन्द्र चमू᳘ सुतम् ॥ उपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसी᳘न्द्राय त्वौ᳘जसा एष᳘ ते यो᳘निरि᳘न्द्राय त्वौ᳘जस इ᳘ति सादयति ॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Uprising by thy power amidst thou move thy jaws. O Indra, drinking the juice drawn in the cup—Thou art taken with a support—Thee to Indra, for vigour;—this is thy womb—Thee to Indra for vigour” (so saying) he deposits it.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘दृश्रमस्य केत᳘वो वि᳘ रश्म᳘यो ज᳘नां ३ अ᳘नु भ्रा᳘जन्तो अग्न᳘यो यथा । उपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसि सू᳘र्याय त्वा भ्राज᳘ एष᳘ ते यो᳘निः सू᳘र्याय त्वा भ्राज इ᳘ति सादयति ॥९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

His beacons have appeared, his beams, wide and far over the people, shining splendidly like fires—Thou art taken with a support; thee to Sūrya for splendour—this is thy womb. Thee to Sūrya for splendour" (So saying) he deposits it.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ता᳘न्भक्षयत्य᳘ग्ने वर्चस्वन्नि᳘ति व᳘र्चस्वाꣳस्त्वं᳘ देवेष्व᳘सि व᳘र्चस्वानह᳘ मनु᳘ष्येषु भूयासमिति ॥१०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he (Yajamāna) drinks these (grahas) (saying) "O Agni the lustrous, lustrous art thou among the gods, let me become lustrous among men".
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थैन्द्रमि᳘न्द्रौजस्वन्नित्यो᳘जस्वाꣳस्त्वं᳘ देवेष्व᳘सि । ओ᳘जस्वानहं᳘ मनु᳘ष्येषु भूयासमिति ॥११॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then (he drinks) the one for Indra (saying) "O Indra, the vigorous, those art vigorous among gods; let me become one with vigour among men".
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ सौर्यꣳसू᳘र्य भ्राजस्वन्नि᳘ति भ्रा᳘जस्वाꣳस्त्वं᳘ देवेष्व᳘सि । भ्रा᳘जस्वानहं मनु᳘ष्येषु भूयासमि᳘ति ॥१२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then (he drinks) the one for Sūrya (saying) "O Sūrya, the splendid; thou . art splendid among gods; let me become splendid among men.
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते वा᳘ एते पृ᳘ष्ठ्ये षळहे᳘ गृह्यन्ते तान्पू᳘र्वस्मिꣳस्त्र्यहे᳘ गृह्णीयादाग्नेय᳘मेव᳘ प्रथमे᳘ऽहन्यैन्द्रं᳘ द्वितीये᳘ऽहनि सौर्यं᳘ तृतीये᳘ऽहन्येवं᳘ त्र्यह᳘मन्वहं ता᳘नु ह च᳘रका उ᳘त्तरे त्र्यहे᳘ गृह्णन्ति पू᳘र्वस्मि᳘ꣳ᳘स्त्वेव᳘ त्र्यहे᳘ गृह्णीयाद्य᳘द्यु काम᳘येताप्यु᳘त्तरे त्र्यहे᳘ गृह्णीयाद्विश्वजि᳘त्यु स᳘र्वपृष्ठे स᳘र्व एकाहं᳘ गृह्यन्ते ॥१३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

These (Atigrāhya grahas) are drawn on the first three days of the pṛṣṭhya ṣaḍaha. The one to Agni is drawn in the first day; the one to Indra on the second day and the one to Sūrya on the third day. Thus for three days one each day. But Carakas, however draw them on the last three days. But he should draw them only on the first three days. If he so desires he may draw in the last three days. In Viśvajit- sarvapṛṣṭha all are drawn in one and the same day. (Second Brāhmaṇa Ends).
</details>
