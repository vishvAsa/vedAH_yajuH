## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

इ᳘न्द्रो ह वाव᳘ षोळशी तं नु᳘ सकृदि᳘न्द्रं भूतान्य᳘त्यरिच्यन्ते᳘त्याहुः प्रजा वै᳘ भूता᳘नि ता᳘नि हैनेन सदृग्भा᳘वमिवासुः ॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Ṣodaśī graha is Indra. At one time, so they say, other beings surpassed him (Indra). The creatures are the other beings. They assumed as it were, equal status with him.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स हे᳘न्द्र ईक्षां᳘चक्रे कथं᳘ न्वह᳘मिदꣳ स᳘र्वम᳘तितिष्ठेयमि᳘त्यर्वा᳘गेव म᳘दन्यत्स᳘र्वꣳ स्यामि᳘ति स᳘ एतं ग्र᳘हमपश्यत्त᳘मगृह्णीत त᳘त इदꣳस᳘र्वम᳘त्यतिष्ठदर्वा᳘गे᳘वास्मादन्यत्स᳘र्वमभवन्न᳘ हास्मा अन्यत्सिफगं द्यौः प᳘रिबभूव तथे᳘न्नूनं त᳘दास ॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then Indra be-thought himself “How can I excel all these (creatures claiming equality), so that all others shall be below me”. He saw this Soma graha. He drew it and with that he excelled all other creatures and all others became submissive to him. The heaven did not suffer from the ignominy of (bearing) the buttacks of any other than that of his (Indra) and he prevailed over (no other being than Indra had a sway over the heavens).
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अथा᳘प्येतदृ᳘षिणाभ्य᳘नूक्तं न᳘ ते महित्वम᳘न्वभूद᳘थ द्यौर्य᳘दन्य᳘या स्फि᳘ग्या क्षाम᳘वस्था इ᳘त्येव᳘ꣳ᳘ ह वा᳘ इदꣳ स᳘र्वम᳘तितिष्ठत्यर्वा᳘गे᳘वास्मादन्यत्स᳘र्वं भवति य᳘स्यैवं᳘ विदु᳘ष एतं᳘ गृह्ण᳘न्ति ॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Accordingly it was said by a Ṛṣi. “The heaven did not experience any infamy to your greatness, caused by any affronts by another's buttocks. (O Indra! your greatness is not affected by anybody else claiming equality in status with you)” —Knowing this, he who draws the Ṣodaśī graha, excels all others and all others become submissive to him.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तं वै ह᳘रिवत्या गृह्णा᳘ति ह᳘रिवतीषु स्तुवते ह᳘रिवत्यो᳘ऽनुशस्यन्ते वी᳘र्यं वै ह᳘र एते᳘नेष्ट्वैन्द्रः सप᳘त्नानां स᳘मवृञ्जत त᳘थो वा᳘ एष᳘ एत᳘द्वी᳘र्यꣳ ह᳘र एते᳘नेष्ट्वा᳘ सप᳘त्नानाꣳ सं᳘वृङ्क्ते त᳘स्मादेनꣳ ह᳘रिवत्या गृह्णा᳘ति ह᳘रिवतीषु स्तुवते ह᳘रिवत्यो᳘ऽनुशस्यन्ते ॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He draws it with a Harivat verse (a verse addressed to Indra—Harivat). They (the Udgātṛs) sing the praise of Indra—Harivat and the Hotṛs follow with their recital of verses to Indra—Harivat. Hara means fury. Indra seized upon the fury of his enemies and in like manner does he (Yajamāna) now seize upon the Hara, fury, of his enemies by offering with this (Harivat chant). So he draws it with a verse addressed to Indra—Harivat.; they (Udgātṛs) sing the praise of Indra—Harivat and (the Hotṛs) follow with recitals for Indra—Harivat.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अनुष्टु᳘भा गृह्णाति गायत्रं वै᳘ प्रातःसवनं त्रै᳘ष्टुभं मा᳘ध्यन्दिनꣳ स᳘वनं जा᳘गतं तृतीयसवनमथा᳘तिरिक्तानुष्टुब᳘तिरेचयत्ये᳘वैनं त᳘या ॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He draws it with an Anuṣṭubh verse, for the morning savana belongs to Gāyatrī; the midday savana to Triṣṭubh and the third savana to Jagatī metre. The Anuṣṭubh is over and above those and he exceeds them by her (by using the Anuṣṭubh metre).
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

च᳘तुः स्रक्तिना पा᳘त्रेण गृह्णाति त्र᳘यो वा᳘ इमे᳘ लोकास्ता᳘ꣳस्तिसृ᳘भिः स्रक्ति᳘भिराप्नोत्य᳘थ या᳘ चतुर्थ्य᳘तिरेचयत्ये᳘वैनं त᳘या ॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He draws it in a square vessel, for, these worlds are three (in number) and these three he gains by three corners and by that fourth corner he makes it (Soma) excess.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ प्रातः सवने᳘ गृह्णीत᳘ एत᳘स्मात्कालादु᳘पशेते तत्स᳘र्वाणि स᳘वनान्य᳘तिरिच्यते᳘ऽतिरेचयत्ये᳘वैनं तेना᳘थो अ᳘प्याहुस्तृतीयसवन᳘ ए᳘वैनं गृह्णीयादि᳘त्याग्रयण᳘मेव᳘ गृहीत्वा᳘ऽथैनम᳘थो᳘क्थ्यमि᳘त्युक्थ्या᳘न्यु त᳘र्ह्यभ्य᳘तिरिच्यत इ᳘त्युपाकरिष्य᳘न्नेव᳘ पूतभृ᳘तो गृह्णीयादित्य᳘प्याहुः᳘ सैषा᳘ मीमा᳘ꣳसैव᳘ प्रातः सवने᳘ त्वे᳘वैनं गृह्णाति ॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That graha drawn at the morning savana, it lies aside since then. It out-lasts all the savanas. So he makes it excess (overflow). Some, therefore, say that one should draw it only at the third savana, after drawing the Āgrayaṇa graha, draw this. (Some say) after the drawal of the Ukthyā graha, following Ukthyā, then it will out- last; bringing it near the Pūtabhṛt”. So they say. This is only a speculation. It should be drawn only in the morning savana.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ गृह्णाति युक्ष्वा हि᳘ केशि᳘ना हरी इ᳘ति वा᳘ तिष्ठ वृत्रहन्र᳘थमि᳘ति वोपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसी᳘न्द्राय त्वा षोळशि᳘न एष᳘ ते यो᳘निरि᳘न्द्राय त्वा षोळशि᳘न इ᳘ति सादयति सादयित्वा᳘ स्तोत्र᳘मुपा᳘करोत्युपा᳘वर्तध्वꣳ सोमो᳘ऽत्यरेचीत्य᳘तिरेचयत्ये᳘वैनं ते᳘न ॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He draws it (saying) "The bay steads have been harnessed by prayer". "Mount thy chariot O slayer of Vṛtra-ṣoḍaśin (Indra the sixteen fold)". This is thy womb—To Indra—the ṣoḍaśin". (So saying) he deposits it. After depositing he offers the prayer "Turn back. Soma has been left over". (First Brāhmaṇa Ends)
</details>
