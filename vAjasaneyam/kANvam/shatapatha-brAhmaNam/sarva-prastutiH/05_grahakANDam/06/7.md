## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

एष वाव᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिर्यज्ञो य᳘ एष᳘ ताय᳘त एत᳘स्मादे᳘वेमाः᳘ प्रजाः प्र᳘जाता एत᳘मेवा᳘नुप्र᳘जायन्ते᳘ऽप्येत᳘र्हि त᳘दुपाꣳशुपात्र᳘मेवा᳘न्वजाः प्र᳘जायन्ते तद्वा᳘ एतत्पु᳘नर्यज्ञे प्र᳘युज्यते त᳘स्माद्विमाः᳘ प्रजाः᳘ पुनरभ्याका᳘रं प्र᳘जायन्ते ता वा᳘ एता ने᳘दिष्ठं प्रजा᳘पतेः प्रजा अ᳘नु प्र᳘जायन्ते य᳘दुपाꣳशुपात्रं त᳘स्मात्त्रिः᳘ संवत्सर᳘स्य विजा᳘यन्त उत त्री᳘ꣳ᳘स्त्रीन् जनयन्ति ने᳘दिष्ठ᳘ꣳ᳘ह्येताः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतेः प्रजा अ᳘नु प्रजा᳘यन्ते ॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This Prajāpati, forsooth, is sacrifice which is performed here. From this, these creatures are born and in the same manner they are being produced again and again. From that Upāṁśu graha, the goats are produced and that is again employed in sacrifice. Therefore these creatures are again produced in the same forms. So these creatures (goats) are nearest to Prajāpati. They are again produced. This Upāṁśu vessel belongs to Prajāpati. They are produced thrice a year or they are born in three (triplets). They are nearest to Prajāpati and are produced again and again.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थान्तर्यामपात्र᳘मेवान्व᳘वयः प्र᳘जायन्ते तद्वा᳘ एतत्पु᳘नर्यज्ञे प्र᳘युज्यते त᳘स्माद्विमाः᳘ प्रजाः᳘ पुनरभ्याका᳘रं प्र᳘जायन्ते स य᳘दुपाꣳशुं पू᳘र्वं जुहो᳘त्यन्तर्यामात्त᳘स्माद्य᳘दजाव᳘यः स᳘ꣳ᳘सृष्टा य᳘न्त्यजा᳘ एव पू᳘र्वाः प्र᳘तिपद्यन्ते तꣳ हि पू᳘र्वं जुहो᳘ति य᳘मेता अ᳘नु प्रजा᳘यन्ते ॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

From the Antaryāma graha sheep are produced. That graha is again employed in the sacrifice. So these creatures (sheep) are again produced in the same forms. Since the Upāṁśu graha is offered first before the Antaryāma, when the goats and sheep move together, the goats walk ahead. It is because it (the Upāṁśu) is offered first and these goats are born of it.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ शुक्रपात्र᳘मेवा᳘नु मनु᳘ष्याः प्र᳘जायन्ते तद्वा᳘ एतत्पु᳘नर्यज्ञे प्र᳘युज्यते त᳘स्माद्विमाः᳘ प्रजाः᳘ पुनरभ्याका᳘रं प्र᳘जायन्त एष वाव᳘ शुक्रो य᳘ एष त᳘पत्येष᳘ उ वा इ᳘न्द्रस्त᳘स्मात्पशूना᳘मैन्द्रा᳘ मनुष्यास्त᳘स्मादेषाꣳ स᳘र्वेषाम᳘धिपतयः स᳘र्वेषामीशत ऐन्द्रा हि ॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

From the Śukra-graha, humans are produced. It is again employed in the sacrifice. So these creatures are born again in the same forms. This Śukra is the one who shines (Sun). He is indeed Indra. So among the creatures, human beings belong to Indra. So they are the lords of all (other creatures). Those who belong to Indra are indeed the masters.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थर्तुपात्र᳘मेवान्वे᳘कशफः प्र᳘जायते तद्वा᳘ एतत्पु᳘नर्यज्ञे प्र᳘युज्यते त᳘स्माद्विमाः᳘ प्रजाः᳘ पुनरभ्याका᳘रं प्र᳘जायन्ते तद्वा᳘ इदमि᳘तीवर्तुपात्रं त᳘स्मादे᳘कशफस्ये᳘तीव शिरः ॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

From the Ṛtu graha, the single-hoofed (animals) are produced. This is again employed in the sacrifice. So these creatures (single-hoofed) are again produced in the same forms. Such like is the Ṛtu graha vessel (shaped as a bowl with spouts on both sides) and such like is the head of the one-hoofed.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाग्रयण᳘मुक्थ्यमादित्यपात्रमि᳘त्येतान्य᳘नु गा᳘वः प्र᳘जायन्ते ता᳘नि वा᳘ एता᳘नि पु᳘नर्यज्ञे प्र᳘युज्यन्ते त᳘स्माद्विमाः᳘ प्रजाः᳘ पुनरभ्याका᳘रं प्र᳘जायन्ते ता वा᳘ एता भू᳘यिष्ठानि पा᳘त्राण्य᳘नुप्र᳘जायन्ते यद्गा᳘वस्त᳘स्मादे᳘कैकꣳ संवत्सरे᳘ संवत्सरे जन᳘यन्ति भू᳘यिष्ठाः पशूनां भू᳘यिष्ठानि᳘ ह्येता पा᳘त्राण्य᳘नुप्रजा᳘यन्ते ॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

From the Āgrayaṇa, Ukthya and Āditya vessels, the cows are produced. These animals are again employed in the sacrifice. Hence these creatures are again born in the same forms. These cows are produced from many grahas. So (even though) each one gives birth to one every year, (still) they are numerous, because they are produced from numerous vessels.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ द्रोणकलशे᳘ हारियोजनं᳘ गृह्णाति प्रजा᳘पतिर्वै᳘ द्रोणकलशः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतेरे᳘वैत᳘दिमाः᳘ प्रजाः᳘ सृष्ट्वा ता᳘ अभ्या᳘वर्तते ता᳘ अभि᳘जिघ्रति ता᳘ अवति त᳘स्माद्द्रोणकलशे᳘ हारियोजनं᳘ गृह्णाति ॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then draws the Hāriyojana graha in the Droṇa kalaśa. Droṇa kalaśa, forsooth, is Prajāpati. It indeed, belongs to Prajāpati. After creating these creatures, he turns into these creatures, he caresses them (by smelling on their heads) he fosters them. Therefore Hāriyojana is drawn in the Droṇa kalaśa.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ता᳘नि वा एता᳘नि पा᳘त्राण्य᳘न्विमाः᳘ प्रजाः प्र᳘जायन्ते स य᳘देता᳘नि पा᳘त्राण्य᳘न्विमाः᳘ प्रजाः᳘ प्रजा᳘यन्ते त᳘स्मात्पा᳘त्रः प्रजा᳘पतिरित्या᳘ख्यायते ॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

From these vessels, these creatures are produced. Since these creatures are produced after these vessels, these (graha cups) are called Prajāpati.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ता᳘नि वा᳘ एता᳘नि प᳘ञ्च पा᳘त्राण्य᳘न्विमाः᳘ प्रजाः प्र᳘जायन्ते समानं तद्य᳘दुपाꣳश्वन्तर्याम᳘योः शुक्रपात्र᳘मृतुपात्र᳘माग्रयण᳘मु᳘क्थ्यं पा᳘ङ्क्तो वै᳘ यज्ञः पा᳘ङ्क्ताः प᳘शवः प᳘ञ्चर्त᳘वः संवत्सर᳘स्य त᳘स्मादेता᳘नि प᳘ञ्च पा᳘त्राण्य᳘न्विमाः᳘ प्रजाः प्र᳘जायन्ते ॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now, these are five vessels from which these creatures are produced. Upāṁśu and Antaryāma being same are reckoned as (one vessel). With the Śukra vessel, the Ṛtuvessel, Āgrayaṇa and Ukthya (they become five). Sacrifice is five-fold. The animals are five-fold. There are five-seasons for a year. Therefore these creatures are produced from those five vessels.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दाहुः षड्वा᳘ ऋतवः संवत्सरस्ये᳘ति य᳘दि वै ष᳘ळृत᳘वः संवत्सर᳘स्यादित्यपात्र᳘मे᳘वैषाꣳषष्ठं᳘ भवति ते᳘नैव तं का᳘ममाप्नोति यस्त᳘त्र का᳘मस्तद्वा᳘ एतदे᳘कमेव पा᳘त्रम᳘न्विमाः᳘ प्रजाः प्र᳘जायन्त उपाꣳशुपात्र᳘मेव᳘ प्राणो᳘ ह्युपाꣳशुः᳘ प्राणो हि᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिः प्राण᳘ꣳ᳘ ह्येवा᳘न्विमाः᳘ प्रजाः᳘ प्रजा᳘यन्ते त᳘स्मादुपाꣳशुपात्र᳘मेवा᳘न्विमाः᳘ प्रजाः प्र᳘जायन्ते ॥९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Some say 'There are six seasons for a year'. If six are the seasons for the year, the Āditya graha becomes the sixth one. Only with that (Āditya graha), he achieves what he desires for. But indeed, there is only that one graha after which creatures are produced here; with the Upāṁśu graha. For Upāṁśu is the life-breath and life-breath is Prajāpati. Only from the prāṇa, these creatures are born. So only from the Upāṁśu graha, these creatures are produced. (Seventh Brāhmaṇa Ends) (Chapter Six Ends)
</details>
