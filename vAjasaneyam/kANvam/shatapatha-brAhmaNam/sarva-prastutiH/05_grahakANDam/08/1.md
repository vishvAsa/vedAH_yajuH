## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

प्र᳘जापतिर्हि वा᳘ अꣳशुः स वा᳘ अस्यैष᳘ आ᳘त्मै᳘वात्मा हि᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिः स य᳘दꣳशुं᳘ गृह्न᳘न्त्यात्मा᳘नमे᳘वास्यैत᳘त्सꣳस्कुर्व᳘न्ति त᳘मस्यात्मा᳘नꣳ सꣳस्कृ᳘त्य त᳘स्मिन्नेता᳘न्प्राणा᳘न्दधति य᳘था-यथास्यैते᳘ प्राणा ग्र᳘हाः ॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Aḿśu graha is, forsooth, Prajāpati. That (graha) is the self of this (sacrifice); for, Prajāpati is the self. Therefore when they draw that Aḿśu graha, they fashion that self of his. Thus having fashioned his self, they put in, these vital airs. As these vital airs are (infused) they are (becoming) grahas.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स य᳘स्यैतं᳘ गृह्ण᳘न्ति त᳘स्यार᳘म्भणवत्तरा इव प्र᳘तिष्ठिता इव प्राणा᳘ भवन्त्य᳘थ य᳘स्यैतन्न᳘ गृह्ण᳘न्त्यनारम्भण᳘तरा इवास्या᳘यततरा इव प्राणा᳘ भवन्ति स य᳘स्यैतं᳘ गृह्ण᳘न्ति स᳘ ह कृत्स्न᳘ एव स᳘र्वतनूरमु᳘ष्मिँल्लोके सं᳘भवति त᳘स्माद्वा᳘ अꣳशु᳘र्गृहीत᳘व्यः ॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For whomsoever they draw this Aḿśu graha, his vital airs get established at the commencement itself, as it were. And for whomsoever they do not draw it, his vital airs as it were, are not-well-commenced and not well-spread. For whom he draws, he becomes whole and fully shaped in the yonder world. So the Aḿśu graha should be drawn.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स यस्या᳘हैतं᳘ गृह्णन्त्येष᳘ ए᳘वास्य त᳘र्ह्यात्मा᳘ भवत्य᳘थ य᳘स्यैतं न᳘ गृह्ण᳘न्त्येष᳘ ए᳘वास्य त᳘र्हि सो᳘म आत्मा᳘ भवति सो᳘मो हि᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिः स य᳘देत᳘स्यैतान्ग्र᳘हान्गृह्ण᳘न्ति त᳘दस्यैत᳘स्मिन्नात्म᳘न्येता᳘न्प्राणा᳘न् दधाति य᳘था यथास्यैते᳘ प्राणा ग्र᳘हाः स᳘ उ कृत्स्न᳘ एव स᳘र्वतनूरमु᳘ष्मिँल्लोके सं᳘भवति ॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For whomsoever, they draw this (graha) this becomes the self of his. For whomsoever it is not drawn, for him the Soma itself becomes the self. Soma is Prajāpati. Because they draw these grahas for this sava (rite); these vital airs happen to be installed in the self of this (Yajamāna). As and when these grahas which become the self, are drawn, then he becomes whole and fully fashioned in the other world.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तमौ᳘दुम्बरेण पा᳘त्रेण गृह्णाति प्रजा᳘पतिर्वा᳘ एष᳘ प्राजापत्यो य᳘दुदुम्ब᳘रः प्रजा᳘पतिर्वा᳘ अꣳशुस्त᳘स्मादौ᳘दुम्बरेण गृह्णाति ॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He draws it with a vessel made of Audumbara wood. This (graha) is indeed, Prajāpati and Udumbara belongs to Prajāpati and Prajāpati is Aḿśu graha. So he draws it with an Audumbara vessel.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

च᳘तुःस्रक्तिना गृह्णाति त्र᳘य इमे᳘ लोकाः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिमेवा᳘तिचतुर्थी त᳘स्माच्च᳘तुःस्रक्तिना गृह्णाति ॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He draws it with a square vessel. For, here are the three worlds and Prajāpati is the fourth. So he draws with a square vessel.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘द्वेव च᳘तुःस्रक्तिना गृह्णा᳘ति प्रजा᳘पतिर्वा᳘ एष दि᳘शः स्रक्त᳘यः स᳘र्वास्वे᳘वैत᳘द्दिक्षु᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिं दधाति त᳘स्माद्विमाः᳘ प्रजा᳘ दिशि᳘ दिश्येव प्र᳘जायन्ते ॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Again why he draws with a square vessel is this—Prajāpati is these quarters. These four sides (of the square vessel) install Prajāpati in all these (four) directions (in the four sides of the vessel). That is why these creatures are born in each of the quarters.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘द्वेव च᳘तुःस्रक्तिना गृह्णा᳘ति प्रजा᳘पतिर्वा᳘ एष दि᳘शः स्रक्त᳘यः स᳘र्वाभ्य ए᳘वास्मा ए᳘तद्दि᳘ग्भ्यो ऽन्ना᳘द्यमिन्द्रियं᳘ वी᳘र्यं दधाति त᳘स्माच्च᳘तुःस्रक्तिना गृह्णाति ॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Again why he draws it with a square vessel is this—Prajāpati being these quarters, i.e. the four sides (of the vessel) he provides with food, energy and vigour to all these quarters. Hence he draws with a square vessel.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वै᳘ तूष्णी᳘मे᳘वाꣳशू᳘न्निव᳘पति तूष्णीं ग्रा᳘वाणमा᳘दत्ते तूष्णी᳘मुपसृज᳘ति तूष्णी᳘ꣳ᳘ सकृ᳘दभिषुत्या᳘नवानं जुहोति त᳘देनं प्रजा᳘पतिं करोत्य᳘थ हि᳘रण्यमभि᳘व्यनित्या᳘युर्वा᳘ अमृ᳘तꣳ हि᳘रण्यꣳ स य᳘देत᳘स्य प्राण᳘स्य प्रजा᳘पतेः प्रा᳘णन्वोद᳘नन्वा वि᳘ वा लिश᳘ति क्षिणो᳘ति वा᳘युर्वा᳘ अमृ᳘तꣳ हि᳘रण्यं त᳘दे᳘वास्या᳘युषामृ᳘तेन स᳘न्दधाति त᳘च्छमयति त᳘स्माद्धि᳘रण्यमभि᳘व्यनिति ॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He silently throws down the aḿśus (soma plants); silently he takes up the (pressing) stone; silently he drenches it (with water) and after silently pressing (the stone) once, he offers. Thereby he makes him (Yajamāna) to be Prajāpati. Then he smells a piece of gold (placed on it). Gold is immortal life. When he inhales Prajāpati's life-breath along with his in-breathing, he disarranges (the Prajāpati's V.8.1.13] prāṇa) and hurts him. So he unites him with immortal life and makes him 383 wholesome. Therefore he smells the piece of gold.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दुहोवाच राम औ᳘पतस्विनिरुरुगा इ᳘तरं म᳘ह्यमाचा᳘र्यो ऽब्रवीदि᳘ति होवाच का᳘ममेव प्रा᳘ण्याच्चो᳘दन्याच्चेति यद्वाव᳘ तूष्णी᳘मꣳशू᳘न्निव᳘पति तूष्णीं ग्रा᳘वाणमादत्ते᳘ तूष्णी᳘मुपसृज᳘ति तूष्णी᳘ꣳ᳘ सकृ᳘दभिषु᳘त्य जुहो᳘ति त᳘दे᳘वैनं प्रजा᳘पतिं करोती᳘ति होवाच तूष्णीं क᳘र्मणैवेत्य᳘थ हि᳘रण्यमभि᳘व्यनिति त᳘स्योक्तो ब᳘न्धुः ॥९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now Rāma, son of Upatasvin said "My very learned preceptor told me otherwise. (He has said) let him freely breath out and freely breath in". "That he silently throws down the aṁśus, silently takes up the stone, silently drenches it and after silently pressing it once, he offers it and thereby makes him (the Yajamāna) to be Prajāpati—thus that the entire action to be done silently"—the significance of this has been told.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दु होवाच बुळिल आ᳘श्वतराश्वि᳘नौ त᳘मभि᳘षुणुयादि᳘ति होवाचाभि᳘षुणोति वा᳘ अन्या᳘भ्यो देव᳘ताभ्यः स य᳘देनमभिषुणुयादि᳘त्यन्या᳘भिरेनं देव᳘ताभिरभ्या᳘रोहयेदुष्टुत्यैव᳘ गृह्णीयात्त᳘दे᳘वाभिषुपतभाजनमि᳘ति ॥१०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now Buḷila, the son of Aśvataraśvi—"Let him not press (the stone)". He further said "They do press for other deities and if he does the pressing here too, it would amount to equating him (Soma) with other deities. He should draw it after (only) raising (the pressing stone). That (raising) itself makes it (as if) subjected to pressing.
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दु होवाच या᳘ज्ञवल्क्यो ऽभि᳘षुणुयादे᳘वैतमि᳘ति होवाच न सो᳘म इ᳘न्द्रम᳘सुतो ममाद ना᳘ब्रह्माणो मघ᳘वानꣳ सुता᳘स इ᳘ति वा ऋ᳘षिणाभ्य᳘नूक्तमस्ति या᳘वद्वा᳘ एतं᳘ नाभिषुण्वन्ती᳘ति᳘ नै᳘वास्मिꣳस्ता᳘वद्र᳘सं दधति न ता᳘वद᳘लमा᳘हुतये भवत्य᳘थ य᳘दे᳘वैनमभिषुण्वन्तीत्य᳘थै᳘वास्मिन्र᳘सं दधत्यथा᳘लमा᳘हुतये भवति त᳘स्मादेनमभि᳘षुणुयादेवे᳘ति तं वा᳘ अभिषुण्व᳘न्त्सकृ᳘दभि᳘षुणोति स यद᳘हैनमभिषुणो᳘ति त᳘दाहास्मिन्र᳘सं दधाति त᳘थो अ᳘लमा᳘हुतये भवति य᳘द्वभिषुण्व᳘न्त्सकृ᳘दभिषुणो᳘ति त᳘थो एनमन्या᳘भिर्देव᳘ताभि᳘र्नाभ्या᳘रोहयति त᳘स्मादेनमभि᳘षुणुयादेवे᳘ति ॥११॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now Yājñavalkya spoke. "Let him press" he said, "The unpressed soma delighted not the mighty Indra, nor the outpressed draughts without prayer"— Thus spoke the Ṛṣi (in Ṛg Veda). As long as he is not pressed, it does not hold (give) the juice to that extent and what (it gives) will not be enough for oblation. Only when he is pressed, he holds (gives) that much juice enough for the oblation. Therefore he has to be pressed. While pressing, he presses (only) once. When he thus presses (once) he bears (gives) juice and then it becomes sufficient for oblation. While pressing, he presses (only) once and thereby he does not equate it with other deities. Therefore let him press it.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘स्य द्वा᳘दश प्रथम᳘गर्भाः पष्ठौ᳘ह्यो द᳘क्षिणा द्वा᳘दश वै मा᳘साः संवत्सर᳘स्य संवत्सरो वै᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिः प्रजा᳘पतिर्वा᳘ अꣳशुस्त᳘स्माद्द्वादश प्रथम᳘गर्भाः पष्ठौ᳘ह्यो द᳘क्षिणाः ॥१२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Twelve young cows, pregnant for the first time are the dakṣiṇā (for the priest for this graha). Now there are twelve months in the year and Prajāpati is the year and the aṁśu is Prajāpati. Therefore twelve young cows pregnant for the first time are the dakṣiṇā.
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ता ग᳘र्भैः सह च᳘तुर्विꣳशतिर्भवन्ति च᳘तुर्विꣳशतिर्वा᳘ अर्धमासाः᳘ संवत्सर᳘स्य संवत्सरो वै᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिः प्रजा᳘पतिर्वा᳘ अꣳशुस्त᳘स्माद्ग᳘र्भैः सह च᳘तुर्विꣳशतिर्भवन्ति च᳘तुर्विꣳशतिꣳ ह त्वा᳘ एता᳘ ददौ प्रथम᳘गर्भाः ककुस्त᳘ ऋषभाद्धि᳘रण्यम् ॥१३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They along with their calves in the wombs make twenty-four. There are twenty-four half-months for a year and Prajāpati is the year and the aṁśu is Prajāpati. So it (the dakṣiṇā) becomes twenty-four with the calves in the wombs. Indeed twenty-four of these (cows and calves together) in first pregnancy, did Kaukusta give, besides a bull and gold (as dakṣiṇā).
</details>


## १४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वा᳘ एष᳘ नैव स᳘र्वस्येव ग्रहीत᳘व्य आत्मा᳘ ह्यस्यैष स᳘र्व᳘ꣳ᳘ह्यात्मा नेत्स᳘र्वस्येव स᳘र्वं कर᳘वाणी᳘ति यो᳘न्वेव᳘ ज्ञातस्त᳘स्य ग्रहीतव्यो᳘ऽथ᳘ योऽनूचानो᳘ ऽनूक्तेन हि त᳘स्य प्रा᳘प्तो भ᳘वति ॥१४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This (graha) should not be drawn for everybody, since this is the self of his (sacrifice). Self is everything and everything should not be done for everybody (indiscriminately). For him who is well-known (to the Adhvaryu), it can be drawn. One who is learned (in Vedic lore), by him it is achieved (even) by his study itself.
</details>


## १५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

सह᳘स्रे ग्रहीत᳘व्यः स᳘र्वꣳहि᳘ सह᳘स्रꣳ स᳘र्व᳘ꣳ᳘ह्येष᳘ सर्ववेदसे᳘ ग्रहीत᳘व्यः स᳘र्वꣳ हि᳘ सर्ववेदसꣳ स᳘र्व᳘ꣳ᳘ह्येष᳘ सत्रे᳘ ग्रहीत᳘व्यः स᳘र्वꣳ हि᳘ सत्रꣳ स᳘र्व᳘ꣳ᳘ह्येष᳘ वाजपे᳘ये राजसू᳘येविश्व जि᳘ति स᳘र्वपृष्ठे ॥१५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It should be drawn at (a sacrifice) where thousand (cows are dakṣiṇā). Because thousand (cows for dakṣiṇā) is everything and this Amśu graha is every- thing. In Sarvavedas (it can be drawn where everything is given as dakṣiṇā). Because Sarvavedas is everything and this Amśu graha is everything. In the Sātra Yāga it can be drawn, because Sātra Yāga is everything and the Amśu graha is everything. It can be drawn in Vājapeya, Rājasūya and Sarvaprṣṭha sacrifices. (First Brāhmaṇa Ends)
</details>
