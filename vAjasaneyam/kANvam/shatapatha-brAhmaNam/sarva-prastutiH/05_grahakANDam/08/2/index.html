प्रजा᳓पतिर्ह वा᳓ अंशुः᳓ स᳓ वा᳓ अस्यै᳓ष᳓ आत्मै᳙वा᳙त्मा᳓ हि᳓ प्रजा᳓पतिर्वा᳓गेवा᳓दाभ्यः स᳓ य᳓दंशुं᳓ गृहीत्वा᳓दाभ्यं गृह्णा᳓त्यात्मा᳓नमेवा᳙स्यै᳓त᳓त्सं᳓स᳓कृ᳓त्य त᳓स्मिन्नेतां᳓ वा᳓चं दधाति ॥

Now the Amśu graha is indeed, Prajāpati and it is the body of this (sacrifice) for Prajāpati, indeed is the body. And the Adābhya graha is no other than Vāk (speech). He then puts back the Soma plants (in the heap of Soma plants in the Havirdhāna) (saying) “Enter thou gladly Agni’s favourite seat, O divine Soma; enter thou willingly into Indra’s favourite seat; O divine Soma. Because you are our friend. You O divine Soma enter into the favourite seat of the Viśvedevas”. On the former occasion he captured the (three) sāvanas; he now restores them again and make them fresh.
