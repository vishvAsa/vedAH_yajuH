## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

बृ᳘हस्प᳘तिरकामयत तेजस्वी᳘ ब्रह्मवर्चसी᳘ स्यां प्र᳘ पुरोधां᳘ देवा᳘नामाप्नुयामि᳘ति स᳘ एतꣳस्तो᳘ममपश्यदेत᳘ꣳ᳘ सत्रं ते᳘नायजत स᳘ तेजस्वी᳘ ब्रह्मवर्च᳘स्यभवत्प्र᳘ पुरोधां᳘ देवा᳘नामाप्नोत् ॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Bṛhaspati desired 'let me acquire glory and brahminic lustre and attain the chief priesthood of the gods. He saw this stoma (song of praise). He conducted this sava (rite). He became glorious and possessed of brahminic lustre and attained chief priesthood of the gods.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स यः᳘ काम᳘येत तेजस्वी᳘ ब्रह्मवर्चसी᳘ स्यां प्र᳘ पुरोधा᳘माप्नुयामि᳘ति स᳘ एते᳘न यजेत तेजस्वी᳘ हैव᳘ ब्रह्मवर्चसी᳘ भवति प्र᳘ पुरोधा᳘माप्नोति ॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He who desires 'May I become glorious and possessed of brahminic lustre and attain chief priesthood'; let him perform this (stoma). He attains glory and brahminical lustre and attains chief priesthood.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त्रिवृत्स्तो᳘मो भवति ते᳘जो वै᳘ ब्रह्मवर्चसं᳘ त्रिवृत्स्तो᳘मस्ते᳘ज ए᳘वैत᳘द्ब्रह्मवर्चस᳘मवरुन्धे ते᳘जस्येव᳘ ब्रह्मवर्चसे प्र᳘तितिष्ठति ॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The stoma happens to be three fold. Three fold stoma becomes glory and brahminic lustre. It includes glory and brahminic lustre. He gets established in glory and brahminic lustre.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

रथन्तरं᳘ पृष्ठं᳘ भवति ते᳘जो वै᳘ ब्रह्मवर्चस᳘ꣳ᳘ रथन्तरं ते᳘ज ए᳘वैत᳘द्ब्रह्मवर्चस᳘मवरुन्धे ते᳘जस्येव᳘ ब्रह्मवर्चसे प्र᳘तितिष्ठति ॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Rathantara becomes the pṛṣṭha (supporting Sāman). Rathantara is glory and brahminic lustre. It includes glory and brahminic lustre. He gets established in glory and brahminic lustre.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अष्टा᳘शतꣳस्तोत्रि᳘या भवन्ति शता᳘युर्वा᳘ अयं पु᳘रुषः शत᳘वीर्यः शत᳘तेजाः पु᳘रुषमे᳘वैतत्स᳘ꣳ᳘ स्करोति ॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

There are one hundred and eight Stotriya Sāmans (Sāmans which contain trio of Ṛks in each). This man is of hundred years life-span with hundred fold vigour and hundred-fold glory. It only fashions this man.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अष्टा᳘ उ वा᳘ इमा᳘ स्तोत्रि᳘या भवन्त्यष्टा᳘क्षरा वै᳘ गायत्री ते᳘जो वै᳘ ब्रह्मवर्चसं᳘ गायत्री पु᳘रुषमे᳘वैत᳘त्सꣳस्कृत्या᳘थास्मिन्नेतत्ते᳘जो ब्रह्मवर्चसं᳘ दधाति ॥६॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Or there are eight stotriya Sāmans. For, Gāyatrī has eight syllables. Gāyatrī is glory and brahminic lustre. It fashions the man and puts this glory and brahminic lustre into him.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

बृहत्या सं᳘पद्यते बृहत्या वै᳘ देवाः᳘ स्वर्गं᳘ लोकꣳस᳘माश्नुवत त᳘थो वा᳘ एष᳘ एत᳘द्बृह᳘त्यैव᳘ स्वर्गं᳘ लोकꣳ स᳘माश्नुते ॥७॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It gets connected with Bṛhatī. It is by means of Bṛhatī, the gods attained the heavenly world. That is why it is bṛhat (big) by which one attains the heavenly world.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

आ᳘ज्येनाभि᳘षिञ्चति ते᳘जो वा आ᳘ज्यं ते᳘ज ए᳘वास्मिꣳ स्त᳘द्दधाति तद्वा᳘ आहुस्ते᳘जो वा आ᳘ज्यं ना᳘ज्येनाभि᳘षिञ्चेदीश्वर᳘ एनं प्र᳘दहेत्सो᳘मेनै᳘वाभि᳘षिञ्चेदि᳘त्येष᳘ ह वा᳘ अभि᳘षिक्तो य᳘त्सोमाभिषेकस्तद्वा᳘ आहुर्व᳘ज्रो वै सो᳘मो न सो᳘मेनाभि᳘षिञ्चेन्ने᳘देतं व᳘ज्रेण हि᳘नसानी᳘ति त᳘दु सो᳘मेनै᳘वाभि᳘षिञ्चेद्य᳘थास्मै शिवः᳘ शान्तो भ᳘वति ग्रहोच्छिष्टे᳘नै᳘वाभि᳘षिञ्चेत्स हि᳘ शान्त᳘ उपा᳘हतं का᳘ममाप्नोति य᳘त्सोमाभिषेके᳘ शिव᳘ उ अस्मै शान्तो᳘ भवति ॥८॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They bathe him (anoint him) with ghee. Ghee is brilliance. So it puts brilliance into him. But they say that ghee being brilliance, one should not bathe him with ghee. Because it will burn him. So only with soma juice, he should be bathed. This one so bathed becomes Somābhişikta (bathed in soma). There they say that Soma is thunderbolt and so one should not bathe him with soma lest he should injure him with thunderbolt. No, he should be bathed only with soma juice. In his case it becomes sober and benificial. He should bathe only with (soma) which is remanent in the graha. That they say is sober. Thus it is said he achieves that wish which he could get by Somābhişikta (even though it may be only with remanent of Soma in the graha). It becomes beneficial and sober for him.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

शुक्रा᳘मन्थि᳘नोः सꣳस्रवे᳘णाभि᳘षिञ्चत्येतौ वा अ᳘नु सर्वꣳ स᳘वनꣳ हूयते स᳘र्व᳘ꣳ᳘ह्येतौ ग्र᳘हौ सर्वत्वा᳘यैव᳘ कृत्स्न᳘ताया अ᳘थो प्रज᳘ननमु वा᳘ एतौ ग्र᳘हौ प्र᳘जायते ह प्रज᳘या पशु᳘भिस्त᳘स्माच्छुक्रा᳘मन्थि᳘नोः सꣳस्रवे᳘णाभि᳘षिञ्चति ॥९॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He should be bathed by that (soma) which trickles from the Śukra and Manthi grahas. These (Śukra and Manthi grahas) are offered at every savana. These two grahas are everything. To become everything and to become whole (he should be bathed by those two). These two grahas are creation. He is born along with issues and cattle. So he should be bathed by what trickles from Śukra and Manthi grahas.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

कृष्णाजिने᳘ऽध्यभि᳘षिञ्चत्यृक्साम᳘नोर्वा᳘ एतद्ब्र᳘ह्मण आय᳘तनं य᳘त्कृष्णाजिन᳘मृक्साम᳘नोरे᳘वैनं ब्र᳘ह्मण्यध्यभि᳘षिञ्चति ॥१०॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Seated on a back antelope's skin, he bathes him. This is the abode of the Ṛg Veda and Sāma Veda, which is the black antelope's skin. So he bathes him seated on the Ṛg Veda and Sāma Veda.
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

देव᳘स्वो भवन्त्येता᳘वन्तो वै᳘ देवा᳘नाꣳ स वा य᳘द्देव᳘स्वस्तै᳘रेवैवमेतत्प्र᳘सूतंत᳘ꣳ᳘ सवे᳘नाभि᳘षिञ्चति ॥११॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

These become Devasus (rites of Devas). They, the Devasus are the feast for the gods and hence they are Devasus. He who is born of these very Devasus, is bathed by this sava.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त्र᳘यस्त्रिꣳशद्द᳘क्षिणा भवन्ति त्र᳘यस्त्रिꣳशद्वै स᳘र्वा देव᳘ताः स᳘र्वासां देव᳘तानामाप्तये᳘ ऽश्वश्चतुस्त्रिꣳशो द᳘क्षिणानां भवति प्रजा᳘पतिर्वै᳘ चतुस्त्रिꣳशो᳘ देव᳘तानां प्राजापत्य᳘ उ वा अ᳘श्वस्ते᳘न प्रजा᳘पतिमाप्नोति बार्हस्पत्यो ग्र᳘हो ऽतिग्रा᳘ह्यो भ᳘वति बार्हस्पत्यः᳘ पशु᳘रुपाल᳘म्भ्यो भवति सव᳘स्य स᳘मृद्धये᳘ ऽथो सव᳘मे᳘वैतद्व्या᳘करोति त᳘थास्यैष᳘ सवः स᳘मृद्धो भवति ॥१२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(On this occasion) there are thirty-three (cows) as Dakṣiṇās. All the gods together, come to thirty-three. To achieve (win over) all those gods (thirty three cows are to be gifted). Prajāpati is the thirty-fourth among the gods. The horses does indeed, belong to Prajāpati. By (giving) that he attains (wins over) Prajāpati. The Bṛhaspatya graha is an extra graha drawn. The pāśu for Bṛhaspati happens to be seized as a supplement, for the completion of the sacrifice. Thus all these are explained. This and this sava of his becomes complete (wholesome). (Fifth Brāhmaṇa Ends)
</details>
