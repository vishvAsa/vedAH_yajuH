## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

प्राणो᳘ ह वाव ग्र᳘हः प्राणे᳘न᳘ हीदꣳ स᳘र्वं गृहीतं᳘ प्राणो᳘ वास्य स᳘र्वस्य गृहीतस्त᳘स्माद्ध प्राण᳘ एव ग्र᳘हः ॥१॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Graha, forsooth, is prāṇa (vital air). All these are collected by the prāṇa. It is the prāṇa that has collected all these. Hence prāṇa itself is graha.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘स्य प्राणस्या᳘न्नमेव ग्र᳘हो᳘ऽन्नेन ह्ययं᳘ प्राणो᳘ गृहीतो᳘ऽन्नाद्धि᳘ प्राणो जा᳘यते त᳘स्माद्धा᳘न्नमेव ग्र᳘हः ॥२॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For that prāṇa, food is the source. This prāṇa is collected by food. It is from food that prāṇa emanates. Therefore food itself is graha.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘स्यान्नस्या᳘प एव ग्र᳘होऽद्भि᳘र्हीदम᳘न्नं गृहीत᳘मद्भ्यो ह्य᳘न्नं जा᳘यते त᳘स्माद्धा᳘प एव ग्र᳘हः ॥३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For that food, waters are the source. This food is collected by the waters. It is from waters that food emanates. Hence waters are themselves, graha.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ता᳘सामपा᳘मग्निरेव ग्र᳘होऽग्नि᳘ना᳘ हीमा आ᳘पो गृहीता᳘ अग्नेर्ह्या᳘पो जा᳘यन्ते त᳘स्माद्धा᳘ग्नि᳘रेव ग्र᳘हः ॥४॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For those water, Agni is the source. These waters are collected by Agni. It is from Agni, the water emanates. Therefore Agni itself is graha.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘स्याग्नेः᳘ प्राण᳘ एव ग्र᳘हः प्राणे᳘न᳘ ह्यय᳘मग्नि᳘र्गृहीतः᳘ प्राणा᳘द्ध्यग्निर्जा᳘यते त᳘स्माद्ध प्राण᳘ एव ग्र᳘ह एव᳘मेता᳘ देव᳘ता अ᳘न्योऽन्य᳘स्य गृहीता᳘ एव᳘ꣳ᳘ह वा᳘ एता᳘सां देव᳘तानां गृहीतो᳘ भवत्येवं᳘ वास्यैता᳘ देव᳘ता गृहीता᳘ भवन्त्येता᳘सां देव᳘तानाꣳ सा᳘युज्यꣳ सलोक᳘तां जयति ॥५॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For that Agni, prāṇa itself is the source. This Agni is collected by prāṇa. It is from prāṇa, that the Agni emanates. So prāṇa, itself is graha. Thus these deities are sustainers of one another and it is thus these deities get emanated. In the same manner these deities are got emanated by this one (Yajamāna) and thus he attains oneness (sāyujya) with these deities and attains share in their worlds (salokatā). (First Brāhmaṇa Ends)
</details>
