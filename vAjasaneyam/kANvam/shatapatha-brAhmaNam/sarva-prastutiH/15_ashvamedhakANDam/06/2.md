He should transfer the two fires into himself and stand in prayer of the Sun while chanting Uttara Nārāyaṇa hymns and proceed to the forest without looking back. That place happens to be away from the human beings. If he wishes to live in a village, let him transfer the two fires on to the two Araṇis and after standing in prayer of the Sun while chanting the Uttara Nārāyaṇa, he may stay at his home. Let him continue performing such sacrifices as he can afford. The technique of this sacrifice should not be imparted to everyone, lest it should amount to a free for all. Because the Puruṣamedha is everything and it can be imparted only to a known person like one well-versed in Vedic lore are one very dear to oneself but not to anybody and everybody.
