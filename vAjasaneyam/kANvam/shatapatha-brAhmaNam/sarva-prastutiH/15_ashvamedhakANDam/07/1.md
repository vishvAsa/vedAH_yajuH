## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ब्र᳘ह्म वै᳘ स्वयम्भु तपो᳘ऽतप्यत त᳘दैक्षत न वै त᳘पस्या᳘नन्त्यमस्ति ह᳘न्ताहं᳘ भूते᳘ष्वात्मा᳘नं जुह᳘वानि भूता᳘नि चात्मनी᳘ति तत्स᳘र्वेषु भूते᳘ष्वात्मा᳘नꣳ हुत्वा᳘ भूता᳘नि चात्म᳘नि स᳘र्वेषां भूता᳘नाꣳ श्रै᳘ष्ठ्यꣳ स्वा᳘राज्यमा᳘धिपत्यं प᳘र्यैत्त᳘थै᳘वैतद्यजमानः सर्वमेधे स᳘र्वान् मे᳘द्यान्हुत्वा स᳘र्वेषां भूतानां श्रै᳘ष्ठ्य᳘ꣳ स्वा᳘राज्यमा᳘धिपत्यं प᳘र्येति ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Brahman, the self-born, underwent *Tapas* (austerities). He discovered "There is no eternality in *Tapas*. So I will offer mine own self in the beings and the beings into myself." Then by offering his own self into all beings and all the beings in his self, he attained supremacy, the sovereignty, and overlordship over all creatures. In the same way this Yajamāna, by offering all essence in the *Sarvamedha* sacrifice, attains supremacy, sovereignty and overlordship over all beings.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वा᳘ एष᳘ सर्वमेधो᳘ दशरात्रो᳘ यज्ञक्रतु᳘र्भवति द᳘शाक्षरा विरा᳘ड्विराडु कृत्स्नमन्नं कृत्स्नस्यैवान्नाद्यस्या᳘वरुद्ध्यै त᳘स्मिन्नग्निं परा᳘र्ध्यं चिनोति परमो वा᳘ एष᳘ यज्ञक्रतूनां य᳘त्सर्वमेधः परमेणै᳘वैनं परम᳘तां गमयति ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This *Sarvamedha* is a sacrificial performance extending over ten days. The Virāḍ metre has ten syllables and Virāḍ is all food and so it is for owning all food (he performs it). He builds up the largest altar in that (sacrifice). This *Sarvamedha* is the super-most among the *Yajña-kratus* (sacrificial performances). Thus he attains supremacy by means of the super-most.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘स्याग्निष्टु᳘दग्निष्टोमः᳘ प्रथमम᳘हर्भवत्यग्निर्वा᳘ अग्निष्टु᳘दग्निष्टो᳘मोऽग्नि᳘मुखा उ वै स᳘र्वे देवाः स᳘र्वेषां देवा᳘नामा᳘प्त्यै त᳘स्याग्रेया ग्र᳘हा भवन्त्या᳘ग्ने᳘च्यः पुरोरु᳘चः स᳘र्वमाग्नेय᳘मसदि᳘ति ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For that, the first day is of *Agnistut-Agniṣṭoma* and *Agniṣṭut-Agniṣṭoma* is indeed fire (itself). All gods have fire as their mouth and so, for gaining all the gods (he performs the *Agnistut*); for that the *Soma-Grahās* are for Agni; the *Puroruk* chant are related to Agni, because he thinks, "Let all about it be re- lated to Agni."
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

इन्द्रस्तु᳘दुक्थ्यो द्विती᳘यम᳘हर्भवती᳘न्द्रो वै स᳘र्वे देवाः स᳘र्वेषां देवा᳘नामाप्त्यै त᳘स्येन्द्रा ग्रहा भवन्त्यै᳘न्द्रयः पुरोरु᳘चः स᳘र्वमैन्द्र᳘मसदि᳘ति ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The second day is of *Indrastut-Ukthya*. Indra is indeed all the gods and for the gaining of all the gods (he performs the *Indrastut*). For that the *Soma*- *Grahās* are of Indra, the *Puroruk* chants are also on Indra, so that all about it is connected with Indra.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

सूर्यस्तुदु᳘क्थ्यस्तृती᳘यम᳘हर्भवति सू᳘र्यो वै स᳘र्वे देवाः स᳘र्वेषां देवा᳘नामा᳘प्त्यै तस्य सौर्या ग्र᳘हा भवन्ति सौ᳘र्यः पुरोरु᳘चः स᳘र्वꣳ सौर्य᳘मसदि᳘ति ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The third-day is of *Sūryastut-Ukthya*, because the Sun is indeed all the gods and it is for gaining all the gods. For it, the *Soma-Grahās* are of Sūrya; the *Puroruk* chants are on Sūrya; so that all about it is connected to Sūrya.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

वैश्वदेवश्चतुर्थम᳘हर्भवति वि᳘श्वे वै स᳘र्वे देवाः स᳘र्वेषां देवा᳘नामाप्त्यै त᳘स्य वैश्वदेवा ग्र᳘हा भवन्ति वैश्वदे᳘व्यः पुरोरु᳘चः स᳘र्वं वैश्वदेव᳘मसदि᳘ति ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The fourth day is a *Vaiśvadeva*; for Viśvedevas mean all the gods and for the gaining of all the gods (it is performed). For that the *Soma-Grahās* are of Viśvedevas and the *Puroruk* chants are on Viśvedevas; so that all about it is connected to Viśvedevas.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

आ᳘श्वमेधिकं मध्यमं᳘ पञ्चमम᳘हर्भवति त᳘स्मिन्न᳘श्वं मे᳘ध्यमा᳘लभतेऽश्वमेध᳘स्यैवाप्त्यै ॥ ७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The fifth day is a central Aśvamedhika (Ukthya) sacrifice. At this, a horse fit for sacrifice is immolated, to gain the (fruit of) Aśvamedha.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

पौ᳘रुषमेधिकं मध्यम᳘ꣳ᳘ षष्ठम᳘हर्भवति त᳘स्मिन्मे᳘ध्यान्पुरुषानाल᳘भते पुरुषमेध᳘स्यैवाप्त्यै ॥ ८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The sixth day is a central Puruṣamedha (an Atirātra sacrifice). For that, men fit for sacrifice are tied (to the Yūpa) to obtain (the fruit of Puruṣamedha).
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अप्तोर्यामः᳘ सप्तमम᳘हर्भवति स᳘र्वेषां यज्ञक्रतूनामा᳘प्त्यै त᳘स्मिन्त्स᳘र्वान्मे᳘ध्याना᳘लभते य᳘च्च प्राणि य᳘च्चाप्राणं᳘ वपा वपा᳘वतां जुहोति त्व᳘च उत्क᳘र्तमवपा᳘कानाꣳ संव्रश्चमोषधि वनस्पतीनां प्र᳘किरन्ति शु᳘ष्काणां चार्द्रा᳘णां चा᳘न्नमन्नं जुहोत्यन्नस्यान्नस्याप्त्यै स᳘र्वं जुहोति स᳘र्वस्मै जुहोति सर्व᳘स्याप्त्यै स᳘र्वस्या᳘वरुद्धयै प्रातः सवने᳘ हुता᳘सु वपा᳘स्वेव᳘मेव तृतीयसवने᳘ हुते᳘ष हविः᳘षु ॥ ९ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The seventh day is an Aptoryāma, for the sake of his gaining all kinds of Soma sacrifices and for that all Paśus, fit for sacrifice are immolated, both ani- mate and inanimate. He offers the Vapā of those having Vapā and of those without Vapā, they throw pieces cut out of the skin; of the herbs and trees, they throw after cutting. He offers every kind of food of both dry and wet, in order to gain every kind of food. He offers everything, offers to every one, in order to gain and own everything. Just as the offering of the Vapās is made in the morning savana, in the same way, the oblations are offered in the third savana.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त्रिणव᳘मष्टमम᳘हर्भवति व᳘ज्रो वै त्रिणवो व᳘ज्रेण ख᳘लु वै क्षत्र᳘ꣳ स्पृतं तद्व᳘ज्रेणैव᳘ क्षत्र᳘ꣳ᳘ स्पृणोति ॥ १० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The eighth day is Trinava Stoma day, for the Trinava-Stoma is a thunderbolt and by means of the thunderbolt, indeed, Kṣatra (royal authority) is gained. Thus by means of thunderbolt he gains royal authority.
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त्रयस्त्रिꣳशं᳘ नवमम᳘हर्भवति प्रतिष्ठा वै᳘ त्रयस्त्रिꣳशः प्र᳘तिष्ठित्यै ॥ ११ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The ninth day is a Trayastrimśa-Stoma day, for the sake of gaining a foothold; for the Trayastrimśa-Stoma is a foothold.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

विश्वजित्स᳘र्वपृष्ठोऽतिरात्रो᳘ दशमम᳘हर्भवति सर्वं वै विश्वजित्स᳘र्वपृष्ठोऽतिरात्रः स᳘र्वꣳ सर्वमेधः स᳘र्वस्या᳘प्त्यै सर्व᳘स्था᳘वरुद्ध्यै ॥ १२ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The tenth day is a Viśvajit-Atirātra day with all the Pṛṣṭhas, for the sake of gaining and owning everything. Because, the Viśvajit-Atirātra with all the Pṛṣṭhas is everything and the Sarvamedha is everything.
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अथा᳘तो द᳘क्षिणानां म᳘ध्यं प्रति राष्ट्र᳘स्य य᳘दन्य᳘द्ब्राह्मण᳘स्य वित्तात्स᳘भूमि स᳘पुरुषं प्रा᳘ची दिग्धोतुर्द᳘क्षिणा ब्रह्म᳘णः प्रती᳘च्यध्वर्योरु᳘दीच्युद्गातुः त᳘देव हो᳘तृका अन्वाभक्ताः ॥ १३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now about the Dakṣiṇās - whatever there is on the middle of the king- dom other than the property of the brahmins, but including land and men, of that, those in the eastern region are to the Hotā; those in the southern side go to the Brahmā; the western to the Adhvaryu; and the northern to the Udgātā and the other Ṛtviks share these along with them.
</details>


## १४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते᳘न हैते᳘न वि᳘श्वकर्मा भौवन᳘ ईजे ते᳘नेष्ट्वा᳘त्यतिष्ठत्स᳘र्वाणि भूता᳘नीदꣳ स᳘र्वमभवद᳘तितिष्ठति स᳘र्वाणि भूता᳘नीदꣳ स᳘र्वं भवति य᳘ एवं᳘ विद्वान्त्सर्वमेधे᳘न यजते यो᳘ वैत᳘देवं वे᳘द ॥ १४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Viśvakarma-Bhauvana, once performed this (Sarvamedha) sacrifice and having performed it, he surpassed all beings and became everything here. He who knows thus and performs the Sarvamedha or he who even knows this, surpasses all beings and becomes everything here.
</details>


## १५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘ꣳ᳘ ह क᳘श्य᳘पो याजयाञ्चकार तद᳘पि भू᳘मिः श्लो᳘कं जगौ न᳘ मा म᳘र्त्यः क᳘श्चन दा᳘तुमर्हति वि᳘श्वकर्मन् भौवन मन्द᳘ आसिथ । उ᳘प᳘मङ्क्ष्यति᳘ स्या᳘ सलिल᳘स्य म᳘ध्ये मृ᳘षैष᳘ ते सङ्गरः᳘ कश्य᳘पाये᳘ति ॥ १५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It was Kaśyapa who officiated for him. In that the earth sung the śloka (euology). "No mortal shall give me away; you were foolish, O Viśvakarma- Bhauvana. This I (the earth) will sink into the midst of water and your prom- ise (to give) to Kaśyapa is going to be a false." (First Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>
