<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He discovered "There is no eternality in *Tapas*. So I will offer mine own self in the beings and the beings into myself." Then by offering his own self into all beings and all the beings in his self, he attained supremacy, the sovereignty, and overlordship over all creatures. In the same way this Yajamāna, by offering all essence in the *Sarvamedha* sacrifice, attains supremacy, sovereignty and overlordship over all beings.
</details>
