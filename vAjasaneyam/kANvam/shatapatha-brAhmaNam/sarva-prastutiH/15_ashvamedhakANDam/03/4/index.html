विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (3/20 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)

एकविंशो᳙ऽग्निः᳓ [[भ᳓वत्येकविं᳓श|भवत्येकविंश᳓]] स्तो᳓म [[ए᳓कविं᳓शतिर्यू᳓पा|ए᳓कविंशतिर्यू᳓पा]] य᳓था वा᳓ ऋषभा᳓ वा वृ᳓षाणो वा सं᳓स्फुरे᳓रन्नेव᳓मेते᳓ स्तो᳓माः स᳓मृच्छन्ते ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
C R Swaminathan

The fire-altar is twenty-one-fold (measuring twenty-one man’s length in each of the four sides). There are twenty-one Stomas and Yūpas are also twenty- one. Even like the bulls and the male horses in combat, these twenty-one Stomas run counter to one another.

and that which is its eleventh (stake) is its teat: thereby he milks it.

C R Swaminathan

There may be a twelve-fold altar (measuring twelve mens’ length) and eleven Yūpas. Why the altar can be twelve-fold is because the year has twelve months and thereby he gains the year, the sacrifice. Why the Yūpas can be eleven is because it corresponds to the Virāḍ metre and the Virāḍ is eleven-syllablled. The elev- enth syllable is its teat, by which he milks it.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
C R Swaminathan

He who knows them becomes the supreme among the kings. (Fourth Brāhmaṇa Ends.)

मूलम्

एकवि᳘ꣳ᳘शोऽग्निर्भवत्येकविꣳशः स्तो᳘म ए᳘कविꣳशतिर्यू᳘पा एवा अश्वमेधे᳘ तिस्रः᳘ ककु᳘दस्ता य᳘ एवं वे᳘द ककु᳘द्ध रा᳘ज्ञां भवति ॥ ३॥
