<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When he offers oblation to Brahmahatyā, he pleases the Mṛtyu himself with the offering and thereby rendering himself absolutely worthy (fully immunised to sin), he effects a remedy for the killer of a brahmin.
