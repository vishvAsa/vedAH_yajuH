## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

य᳘त्तिस्रो᳘ऽनुष्टु᳘भो भ᳘वन्ति त᳘स्माद᳘श्वस्रिभिस्ति᳘ष्ठम᳘ꣳस्तिष्ठति यच्चतस्रो गाय᳘त्र्यस्त᳘स्माद᳘श्वः स᳘र्वैः पद्भिः᳘ प्रतिद᳘धत्प᳘लायते परमं वा᳘ एतच्छ᳘न्दो यद᳘नुष्टु᳘प्परमो᳘ऽश्वः पशूनां᳘ परमश्चतुष्टोमः स्तो᳘मानां परमे᳘णैवैनं परम᳘तां गमयति ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Since there are three chants in Anuṣṭhubh metre (for the Bahiṣpavamāna Stotra of the Catuṣṭoma on the last day), the horse, while standing, stands on three legs. Since they are made into four chants in Gāyatrī metre, the horse when on the move, runs on all the four legs. That Anuṣṭhubh is the super- most among the metres and the horse is the super-most among the Paśus. The Catuṣṭoma is the super-most among the Stomas. So, by means of the super- most, he causes it to become super-most.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

श᳘क्वर्यः पृष्ठं᳘ भवन्त्यन्य᳘दन्यच्छ᳘न्दो᳘ऽन्येऽन्ये हि स्तो᳘माः क्रिय᳘न्ते य᳘च्छक्वर्यः पृष्ठं भवन्त्य᳘श्वस्यैव᳘ सर्वत्वाय ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The chants in Śakvarī metre form the Pṛṣṭha (of the second day). They are in different metres, for different Stomas are performed on each day, the Śakvarī chants form the Pṛṣṭha for the completeness of the horse (sacrifice).
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

एकवि᳘ꣳशं᳘ मध्यमम᳘हर्भवत्यसौ वा᳘ आदित्य᳘ एकवि᳘ꣳशः᳘ सोऽश्वमेधः स्वे᳘नैवैन᳘ꣳ स्तो᳘मेन स्वा᳘यां देव᳘तायां प्रतिष्ठापयति ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The middle day is an Ekaviṁśa one; for the Ekaviṁśa is yonder sun and so is Aśvamedha. Thus by means of its own Stoma, he makes it established in its own deity.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

वामदेव्यं᳘ मैत्रावरुणसाम᳘ भवति प्रजा᳘पतिर्वै᳘ वामदेव्यं᳘ प्राजापत्यो᳘ऽश्वः स्वै᳘यै᳘वै (नं) देव᳘तया समर्द्धयति ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Sāman sung for the Maitrāvaruṇa is the Vāmadevya Sāman. The Vāmadevya is indeed Prajāpati and the Aśvamedha belongs to Prajāpati. Thus he enriches it with its own deity.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

पार्थुर᳘श्मं ब्रह्मसाम᳘ भवति रश्मि᳘ना वा अ᳘श्वो यत᳘ ईश्वरो वा अ᳘श्वोऽयतो᳘ऽधृतो᳘ऽप्रतिष्ठितः प᳘रां पराव᳘तं ग᳘न्तोर्य᳘त्पार्थुर᳘श्मं ब्रह्मसाम भवत्य᳘श्वस्यैव धृ᳘त्यै ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Brahma Sāman is the Pārthuraśma Sāman (out of the two varieties of the Pārthuraśma which is to be sung on this occasion by the Brāhmaṇācchamsin). Because the horse is controlled by Raśmi (reins). If the horse is uncontrolled, unrestricted and unsteadied, it is likely to go far away. So adoption of Brahmasāman as Pārthuraśma is to steady the horse.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ङ्कृत्यच्छावाकसाम᳘ भवत्यु᳘त्सन्नयज्ञ᳘ इव वा᳘ एष य᳘दश्वमेधः किं᳘ वा᳘ ह्येत᳘स्य क्रिय᳘ते किं᳘ वा न यत्स᳘ङ्कृत्यच्छावाकसाम भ᳘वत्य᳘श्वस्यैव᳘ सर्वत्वा᳘य स᳘र्वस्तोमोऽतिरात्र᳘ उतमम᳘हर्भवति स᳘र्वं वै स᳘र्वस्तोमोऽतिरात्रः स᳘र्वमश्वमेधः स᳘र्वस्या᳘ध्यै स᳘र्वस्या᳘वरुद्ध्यै ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Saṃkṛti Sāman is the Sāman of the Acchāvāka. For, the Aśvamedha is an unfinished sacrifice (some part of it is always likely to be missed in the performance). Some parts of it are done and some are not done. Making the Saṅkṛti Sāman as the Sāman of the Acchāvāka, he ensures completeness of the horse sacrifice. The Atirātra with all the Stomas (Sarvastoma) happens to be the last day. The Sarvastoma Atirātra means all and Aśvamedha is all. So (the Atirātra is Sarvastoma on the last day) for obtaining everything, for owning everything. (Third Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>
