They say on this - "goats, sheep and the wild beasts - all these are not Paśus." Only the cow and others are known as Paśus. On the last day, he immo- lates the cow and others, because they are all Paśus; the cows etc. In this way he immolates all the Paśus. This is for Viśvedevas; for the sake of completeness of the horse (sacrifice). They are of many forms. That is why Paśus are of many forms.
