## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

एकवि᳘ꣳ᳘शोऽग्निः भ᳘वत्येकवि᳘ꣳश स्तो᳘म ए᳘कवि᳘ꣳशतिर्यू᳘पा य᳘था वा᳘ ऋषभा᳘ वा वृ᳘षाणो वा स᳘ꣳस्फुरे᳘रन्नेव᳘मेते स्तो᳘माः स᳘मृच्छन्ते य᳘देकवि᳘ꣳशास्तान्य᳘त्समर्प᳘येदा᳘र्तिमा᳘र्च्छेद्य᳘जमानो हन्ये᳘तास्य यज्ञो᳘ द्वादश᳘ ए᳘वाग्निः᳘ स्यादे᳘कादश यू᳘पा य᳘द्वाद᳘शो᳘ऽग्निर्भ᳘वति द्वा᳘दश मा᳘साः संवत्सरः᳘ संवत्सर᳘मेव᳘ यज्ञ᳘माप्नोति यदे᳘कादश यूपा विराड्वा᳘ एषा स᳘म्मीय᳘ते य᳘देकादश᳘नी त᳘स्यै य᳘ एकादश स्त᳘न ए᳘वास्यै स᳘ दुह᳘ ए᳘वैनां ते᳘न ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The fire-altar is twenty-one-fold (measuring twenty-one man's length in each of the four sides). There are twenty-one Stomas and Yūpas are also twenty- one. Even like the bulls and the male horses in combat, these twenty-one Stomas run counter to one another. If he were to bring them together, the Yajamāna will have trouble and his sacrifice would be destroyed. There may be a twelve-fold altar (measuring twelve mens' length) and eleven Yūpas. Why the altar can be twelve-fold is because the year has twelve months and thereby he gains the year, the sacrifice. Why the Yūpas can be eleven is because it corresponds to the Virāḍ metre and the Virāḍ is eleven-syllablled. The elev- enth syllable is its teat, by which he milks it.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दाहुर्य᳘द्द्वाद᳘शोऽग्निः स्यादे᳘कादश यू᳘पा य᳘था स्थू᳘रिणा या᳘या᳘त्तादृक्तदि᳘त्येकविꣳश᳘ ए᳘वाग्निर्भ᳘वत्येकविꣳश स्तो᳘म ए᳘कविꣳशतिर्यू᳘पास्तद्य᳘था पृ᳘ष्टिभिर्या या᳘त्तादृक्तत् ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they ask- “If Agni is twelve-fold and the Yūpas are eleven, it would be like cart driven with one bullock yoked.” There are twenty-one-fold altar, twenty-one-fold Stoma and twenty-one Yūpas. That is like a cart driven with side-horses.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

शि᳘रो वा᳘ एत᳘द्यज्ञ᳘स्य य᳘देकविꣳशो᳘ यो वा᳘ अश्वमेधे त्री᳘णि शीर्षा᳘णि वे᳘द शि᳘रो ह रा᳘ज्ञां भवत्येकवि᳘ꣳ᳘शोऽग्निर्भवत्येकविꣳशः स्तो᳘म ए᳘कविꣳशतिर्यू᳘पा एता᳘नि वा᳘ अश्वमेधे त्री᳘णि शीर्षा᳘णि ता᳘नि य᳘ एवं वे᳘द शि᳘रो ह रा᳘ज्ञां भवति यो वा᳘ अश्वमेधे᳘ तिस्रः᳘ ककुदो᳘ वे᳘द ककु᳘द्ध रा᳘ज्ञां भवत्येकवि᳘ꣳ᳘शोऽग्निर्भवत्येकविꣳशः स्तो᳘म ए᳘कविꣳशतिर्यू᳘पा एवा अश्वमेधे᳘ तिस्रः᳘ ककु᳘दस्ता य᳘ एवं वे᳘द ककु᳘द्ध रा᳘ज्ञां भवति ॥ ३॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This twenty-one-fold is the head of the sacrifice. He who knows the three heads in the Aśvamedha, becomes the head among the kings. Twenty-one-fold altar, twenty-one-fold Stomas and twenty-one Yūpas and these are the three heads of Aśvamedha. He who knows them thus, becomes the head of the kings. He who knows the three Kakuds (humps) in the Aśvamedha, becomes the high- est among the kings. Twenty-one-fold altar, twenty-one-fold Stomas and twenty- one Yūpas - the three Kakuds are in Aśvamedha. He who knows them becomes the supreme among the kings. (Fourth Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>
