<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (3/16 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)</summary>

[[संवत्सरे|संवत्सरे᳓]] पर्य᳓वते [[दीक्षा|दीक्षा᳓]] प्राजापत्य᳓मालभ्यो᳓त्सीदन्ती᳓ष्टयः [[पुरो᳓हितस्याग्निषु|पुरो᳓हितस्याग्नि᳓षु]] यजेते᳓त्यु है᳓क आहुः ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When the year has passed (after releasing the horse), Dīkṣā takes place. After the Paśu (he-goat) for Prajāpati is slaughtered, the Iṣṭis (the three Iṣṭis to Savitā offered daily throughout that year) come to close. “Let him (continue) offering them in the fires of the Purohita (family priest)” say some.
</details>
