## ०१

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (1/21 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)</summary>

अ᳓थ प्रात᳓र्गो᳓तमस्य चतुरुत्तर᳓ स्तो᳓मो भवति त᳓स्य चतसृ᳓षु बहिष्पवमान᳓मष्टा᳓स्वा᳓ज्यानि [[द्वा᳓दश᳓सु|द्वादश᳓सु]] मा᳓ध्यन्दिनः प᳓वमानः षोडश᳓सु पृष्ठा᳓नि विंशत्या᳓मा᳓र्भवः प᳓वमानश्च᳓तुर्विंशत्यामग्निष्टोमसाम᳓ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then at the next morning, there is the chanting of the Stoma of Gotama in which each Stoma successively increases by four verses. The Bahiṣ-Pavamāna on four, Ājya Stotra on eight, the Mādhyandina Pavamāna on the twelve, the Pṛṣṭha Stotra on the sixteen, the Ārbhave Pavamāna on the twenty and the Agniṣṭoma Sāman on twenty-four verses.
</details>
