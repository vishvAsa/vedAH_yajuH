विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

अ᳓प वा᳓ एत᳓स्माच्छ्री᳓ राष्ट्रं᳓ क्रामति यो᳙ऽश्वमेधे᳓न य᳓जत ऊर्ध्वा᳓मेनामु᳓च्छ्रा पये᳓ति ।

From him who performs Aśvamedha, that prosperity, royal power goes away. “Raise her upwards” (the Udgātā says referring to the king’s consort). The Aśvamedha is indeed that prosperity, that royal power.

“I have sung the praises of Dadhikrāvan…… etc.” Thus they finally utter verse containing the work ‘Surabhi’ (fragrance). Thus they purify the speech and so the Prāṇas do not depart from them. (Ninth Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
