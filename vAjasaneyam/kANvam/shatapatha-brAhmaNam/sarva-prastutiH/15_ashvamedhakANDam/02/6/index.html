In this connection they say - “Surely he is losing his sacrifice, if the Paśu brought for the sacrifice goes else where than the sacrificial vedī.” Hence (he chants) “You who sings praise! bring that horse back to us here, by that route.” One who sings praise is indeed Vāyu.
