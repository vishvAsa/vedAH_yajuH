विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (2/37 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)

रा᳓जा वा᳓ एष᳓ यज्ञा᳓नां य᳓दश्वमेधो᳓ य᳓जमानो वा᳙ऽश्वमेधो᳓ य᳓जमानो यज्ञो᳓ य᳓द᳓श्वे पशू᳓न्नियुन᳓क्ति यज्ञ᳓ एव᳓ त᳓द्यज्ञ᳓मा᳓रभते᳓ऽश्वं तूपरं᳓ गोमृग᳓मि᳓ति [[तान्मध्यमे᳓|ता᳓न्मध्यमे᳓]] यू᳓प आ᳓लभते [[सेनामुख᳓मेवा᳙स्यैतेन|सेनामुख᳓मेवा᳙स्यैते᳓न]] सं᳓श्यति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
C R Swaminathan

This Aśvamedha is verily the king of sacrifices. Indeed the Aśvamedha is the Yajamāna; for the sacrifice is the sacrificer. When he (The Adhvaryu) binds the Pasus for the horse-sacrifice, he starts the sacrifice with the sacrifice itself. The horse, the hornless he-goat and Gomṛga (A kind of ox) - These they tie at the central Yūpa. Thereby he sharpens (makes ready) the front of his army. Therefore the front of a king’s army becomes terrible.

He thus places the horse in its own quarter. Now why on a bamboo mat - the horse is from the womb of the water and the bamboos are born of waters. thus he endows it with its own womb. (Second Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
