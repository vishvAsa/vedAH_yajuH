## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

प्रजा᳘पतिर्देवे᳘भ्यो यज्ञान्व्या᳘दिशत्स᳘ आत्म᳘न्न᳘श्वमेध᳘मधत्त ते᳘ देवाः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिमब्रुवन्नेष वै᳘ यज्ञो य᳘दश्वमेधो᳘ऽपि नो᳘ऽत्रास्तु भाग इ᳘ति ते᳘भ्य एता᳘मा᳘नकल्पयद्य᳘दन्नहोमा᳘ञ्जुहोति देवा᳘नेव त᳘त्प्रीणाति ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Prajāpati ear-marked the sacrifices to the gods and kept Aśvamedha for himself. Those gods told Prajāpati “Let us have share in this Aśvamedha sacri- fice as well.” He assigned these Anna-homas (Food- oblations) to them. So, when he offers the Anna-homas, he is only pleasing the gods.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

आ᳘ज्येन जुहोति ते᳘जो वा आ᳘ज्यं तेजसै᳘वास्मिंस्तत्ते᳘जो दधात्या᳘ज्येन जुहोत्येतद्वै᳘ देवा᳘नां प्रियं धा᳘म यदा᳘ज्यं प्रिये᳘णै᳘वैनान्धा᳘म्ना स᳘मर्धयति ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He makes offerings with ghee, for ghee is Tejas (brilliance). So, he imparts brilliance by means of Tejas into him (into the Yajamāna and into the horse). He makes offerings with ghee, for ghee is favourite resort of the gods. Thus, he supplies them with their favourite resort.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘क्तुर्भिर्ज्जुहोति देवानां वा एत᳘द्रूपं यास᳘क्तवो देवा᳘नेव त᳘त्प्रीणाति ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He makes offering with saktu (flour made of fried barley). These saktus are a form of the gods. Thereby he pleases the gods themselves.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

धानाभिर्ज्जुहोत्यहोरात्रा᳘णां वा᳘ एत᳘द्रूपं य᳘द्धाना᳘ अहोरात्रा᳘ण्येव त᳘त्प्रीणाति ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He makes offering with grains. These grains are a form of the days and nights. Thus he pleases the days and nights.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

लाजैर्ज्जुहोति न᳘क्षत्राणां वा᳘ एत᳘द्रूपं य᳘ल्लाजा न᳘ न᳘क्षत्राण्येव त᳘त्प्रीणाति प्राणा᳘य स्वा᳘हाऽपाना᳘य स्वाहे᳘ति नामग्रा᳘हं जुहोति नामग्रा᳘हमे᳘वैनांस्त᳘त्प्रीणात्ये᳘ कस्मै स्वाहा द्वाभ्याꣳ स्वा᳘हा शता᳘य स्वाहै᳘कशताय स्वाहे᳘त्यनुपूर्वं᳘ जुहोत्यनुपूर्व᳘मे᳘वैनांस्त᳘त्प्रीणात्ये᳘कोत्तरा जुहोत्येकवृद्वै᳘ स्वर्गो᳘ लोक᳘ एक᳘धै᳘वैनꣳ स्वर्गं᳘ लोकं᳘ गमयति प᳘राचीर्जुहोति प᳘राडिव वै᳘ स्वर्गो᳘ लोकः᳘ स्वर्ग᳘स्य लोक᳘स्याभि᳘जित्यै ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He makes offerings with parched grains. The parched grains are a form of the Nakṣatras (asterism). He thereby pleases the Nakṣatras. “To the Prāṇa- Svāhā; to the Apāna-Svāhā – thus he offers by the names. In this way he pleases those Prāṇa and Apāna by reciting their names. “To number one - Svāhā, to number two -Svāhā ”; “to number hundred -Svāhā, to number hundred and one-Svāhā.” This way he offers in the numerical order. Thus he pleases these gods in their order. He performs offerings successively increasing by one; for the heaven is single - thus he causes him (The Yajamāna) go to the heaven single. He makes the offering straight-away (towards the front), for the heaven is as it were, straight in front. That is for gaining the heavenly world.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ईश्वरो वा᳘ एषः प᳘राङ् प्रद᳘धोर्यः प᳘राचीरा᳘हुती र्जुहोति नै᳘कशतम᳘त्येति यदे᳘कशतमतीयादा᳘युषा य᳘जमानं᳘ व्यर्द्धयेदे᳘कशतं जुहोति शतायुर्वै पु᳘रुष आत्मै᳘कशत आ᳘युष्ये᳘वात्मन्प्र᳘तितिष्ठति᳘ व्युष्ट्यै स्वा᳘हा स्वर्गा᳘य स्वाहे᳘त्युत्तमे आ᳘हुती जुहोति रा᳘त्रिर्वै व्युष्टिर᳘हः स्व᳘र्गोऽहोरात्रे᳘ एव त᳘त्प्रीणाति ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Verily he who offers the oblations straight-away, he is liable to get harmed. If he exceeds hundred and one, he will shorten the life of the Yajamāna. So he offers only hundred oblations; for man has hundred years’ life-span and his own self is the hundred and first. So he gets established in his life-span, in his own self. “To the dawn-Svāhā, to the heaven-Svāhā.” Thus he offers at the end, two oblations. The dawn represents the nights and the heaven repre- sents the day. Thus he gratifies the day and the night.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दाहुर्य᳘दुभे दि᳘वा वा न᳘क्तं वा जुहुयादहोरात्रे᳘ मोहये᳘द्वयुष्ट्यै स्वाहेत्य᳘नुदित आदित्ये᳘ जुहो᳘ति स्वर्गा᳘य स्वाहेत्यु᳘दितेऽहोरात्र᳘योख्य᳘तिमोहाय ॥ ७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They say - "If he were to make both offerings either by the day or by the night, he would mix-up the day and night." So he offers to the dawn, when the sun has not risen, in order to avoid mixing up of the day and night. (First Brāhmaṇa Ends.)
</details>
