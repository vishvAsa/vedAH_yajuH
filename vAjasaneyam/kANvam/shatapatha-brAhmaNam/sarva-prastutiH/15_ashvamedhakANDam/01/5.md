<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they ask – “If both who sing were to be brāhmins; royalty would depart from him; for brāhmaṇa belongs to the priestly class and royalty does not enjoy in priesthood.” If both the singers were to be of the princely class, brahminical lustre would go away from him. Now they say - “If both were to sing in day time, his prosperity will aban- don him; for, the day symbolises the priesthood and when the king chooses he can win the brahmin and thereby he will become a sinner. There are six seasons for the year. Then he gets established in the seasons in the year.
