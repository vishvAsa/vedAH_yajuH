<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (2/43 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)</summary>

त᳓दाहुर्द्वा᳓दशारत्नी रशना᳓ [[कार्या३|कार्या᳙३]] त्र᳓योदशारत्नी३ [[री᳓त्यृ᳓षभो|री᳓त्यृषभो᳓]] वा᳓ एष᳓ ऋतूनां᳓ य᳓त्संवत्सर᳓स्त᳓स्य त्रयोदशो᳓ मा᳓सो विष्ट᳓पमृषभ᳓ एष᳓ यज्ञा᳓नां य᳓दश्वमेधो᳓ य᳓था वा᳓ ऋषभ᳓स्य विष्ट᳓पमेव᳓मेत᳓स्य विष्ट᳓पं त्रयोदश᳓मरत्निं᳓ रशना᳓यामुपा᳓दध्यात्त᳓द्य᳓थ ऋषभ᳓स्य विष्ट᳓पं संस्क्रिय᳓ते तादृ᳓क्त᳓त् ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now they ask- "Should the rope be made twelve cubits (long) or thirteen cubits." The year is the bull among the seasons and the thirteenth month is its hump and this Aśvamedha is the bull (leader) of the sacrifices. Since the bull has a hump (projection) one can take a thirteenth cubit for this rope as hump for this (Aśvamedha). Just as the hump is attached to the (back of the) bull, this is like that.
</details>

</details>
