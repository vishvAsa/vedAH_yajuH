## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘थै᳘तेऽध्यात्म᳘मग्न᳘यो भ᳘वन्ति मु᳘खमेवास्याहवनी᳘य उ᳘त्तरा ना᳘सिका गा᳘र्हपत्यो द᳘क्षिणान्वाहार्यप᳘चन इय᳘मेव स्रुक्ते᳘षाम᳘न्नमेव᳘ दी᳘प्तिः स᳘र्वमा᳘युरेति य᳘ एव᳘मेतद्वे᳘द ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now, how these fires are related to the soul (body) (of the sacrifice) (is thus): Āhavanīya is verily its mouth. The Gārhapatya is the northern (left) nostril; the southern (right) (nostril) is the Anvāhāryapacana (Dakṣiṇāgni). This (earth) itself is the ladle. For them, food itself is (their) brightness (splendour). He who knows it thus obtains full (span of) life. (Seventh Brāhmaṇa Ends)
</details>
