त᳓द्धैत᳓ज्जनको᳓ वै᳓देहो या᳓ज्ञवल्क्यं पप्रच्छ या᳓ज्ञवल्क्य वे᳓त्थाग्निहोत्र᳓मि᳓ति वे᳓द सम्रा᳓ळि᳓ति होवाच प᳓य एवे᳓ति य᳓त्प᳓यो ना᳓भविष्यत्के᳓नाहोष्य इ᳓ति व्रीहियवा᳓भ्यामि᳓ति होवाच य᳓द्व्रीहियवौ᳓ ना᳓भविष्यतां के᳓नाहोष्य इ᳓त्यारण्या᳓भिरो᳓षधिभिरि᳓ति य᳓दारण्या᳓ ओ᳓षधयो ना᳓भविष्यन्के᳓नाहोष्य इ᳓ति वृक्षफलै᳓रि᳓ति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
C R Swaminathan

About that (then) Janaka, King of the Videha, thus asked Yājñavalkya, “Oh Yājñavalkya! Do you know Agnihotra (fire-worship)?" “Oh Paramount Sovereign! I know”, so he (Yājñavalkya) said indeed. “It is verily milk. For, if there had been no milk, with what would you have made the offering?” He (the king) said, “With rice (paddy) and barley (would I have offered)”. (Yājñavalkya asked), “If there were no rice and barley, with what would you have offered?" (The king replied), “With wild plants (herbs)”.
