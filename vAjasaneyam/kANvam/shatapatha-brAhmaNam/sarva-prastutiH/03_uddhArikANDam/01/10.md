When he reaches the yonder world he verily enters Agni. Agni, having produced him there, maintains him. Then the man (Agnihotrin) becomes the son of Agni indeed. For he indeed is born from Agni. Therefore, having established the two fires, one should not remove them. As long as he maintains them and then removes them, so long indeed this (Agni) maintains him in the yonder world and removes him (thinking), “So long indeed he has maintained us”. And he who maintains this (Agni), indeed, young as long as he verily lives —him does this (Agni) reproduce in the yonder world and maintain him young, so long as is the life-span of Agni. Infinite (immortal) is the life-span of Agni, indeed. He (the Agnihotrin) comes to be without old age and becomes immortal. (Tenth Brāhmaṇa Ends)
