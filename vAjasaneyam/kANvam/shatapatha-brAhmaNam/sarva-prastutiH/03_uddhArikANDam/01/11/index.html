[[एष|एष᳓]] वाव᳓ प्रजा᳓पतिर्यज्ञो᳓ य᳓दग्निहोत्रं᳓ [[त᳓स्यैतस्य|त᳓स्यैत᳓स्य]] प्रजा᳓पतेर्यज्ञ᳓स्य य᳓जमान एव᳓ प्राणो᳓ या᳓वद्ध्ये᳙व᳓ य᳓जमानः [[प्राणेन|प्राणे᳓न]] प्रा᳓णिति ता᳓वदेव᳓ जुहो᳓त्य᳓थ यदा᳓ प्राणो᳙ऽपक्रा᳓मत्य᳓थ व्य᳓वच्छिद्यते स᳓ य᳓द᳓न्तरेणेत्वो᳙पविश᳓ति प्राण᳓ एव᳓ त᳓द्भूत्वा᳓ प्र᳓विशत्य᳓थ य᳓द्धुते᳓ पु᳓नरै᳓त्युदान᳓ एव᳓ त᳓द्भूत्वा᳓ पु᳓नरै᳓ति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
C R Swaminathan

Agnihotra indeed is this Prajāpati, the sacrifice. The sacrificer himself is the life- energy (prāṇa) of this Prajāpati, the sacrifice. As long verily as the sacrificer breathes with his life-energy, till then he makes the offerings. And when life departs, then it (the offering/Agnihotra) is cut off. When he sits after reaching in between (the two fires), then becoming vital energy he enters it. And when he returns after the offering has been made, then he returns verily after-having become udāna (the inward breathing).
