## ०१

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (1/54 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)</summary>

स᳓ य᳓त्र ह वा᳓ एष᳓ प्रथमं᳓ संप्र᳓धू᳓प्य प्रज्व᳓लति त᳓द्ध व᳓रुणो भवत्य᳓थ य᳓त्र संप्र᳓ज्वलितो भ᳓वत्य᳓वरेणेव वर्षिमा᳓णं त᳓द्ध [[रुद्रो|रुद्रो᳓]] भवत्य᳓थ य᳓त्र व᳓र्षिष्ठं ज्व᳓लति त᳓द्धे᳓न्द्रो भवत्य᳓थ य᳓त्र नितरा᳓मर्च᳓यो भ᳓वन्ति त᳓द्ध मित्रो᳓ भवत्य᳓थ य᳓त्रा᳓ङ्गारा म᳓न्म᳓लाय᳓न्तीव त᳓द्ध ब्र᳓ह्म भवति ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When this (fire) blazes well, first having produced much smoke, then surely it is Varuṇa. And when it becomes greatly blazing (but) blowing downward, (having been well kindled, it grows less) as it were, then surely it is Rudra. And when it blazes strongest, then verily it is Indra. And when the flames are much, then it is indeed Mitra. And when the live cinders seem to glisten, then it is surely Brahman.
</details>
