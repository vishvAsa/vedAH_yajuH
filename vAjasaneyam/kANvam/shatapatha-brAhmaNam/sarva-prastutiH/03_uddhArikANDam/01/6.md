## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स य᳘थैत᳘न्मह᳘दुक्थ᳘माप्नोत्या᳘हुतिमेतां᳘ जुहोति स᳘ सप्त᳘विꣳशति᳘शतानि सायं᳘प्रातरा᳘हुती᳘र्जुहोति᳘ यैव पू᳘र्वा ता᳘मेतत्सं᳘पश्यामः स्विष्टकृद्भाजनं वो᳘त्तरा मिथुनं᳘ व या᳘ उ वै हो᳘ता न᳘वाशीति श᳘ꣳ᳘सति सप्त ता᳘नि विꣳशति᳘शतानि स᳘ संवत्सरे᳘ संवत्सर᳘ एत᳘न्मह᳘दुक्थ᳘माप्नोति तद्य᳘थोक्थे᳘न शस्ते᳘न लोकं ज᳘येत्ता᳘वन्तꣳह संवत्सरे᳘ संवत्सरे लोकं᳘ जयति ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

As he obtains the great chant (mahaduktha), he makes this offering (Agnihōtra). Seven hundred and twenty (saptaviṃśati śataṃ) morning and evening offering he makes. What verily is the first (of the oblations), that indeed do we notice. The subsequent (offering) is what serves ‘sviṣṭakṛt' and (forms) a pair. Those ‘nine- eighties' which the Hotṛ recites (as śastra), they are seven hundred and twenty. (They are seven-twenty hundreds (Sapta tāni viṃśati śatāni). Every year he obtains this great chant. Therefore, as one would conquer the world by the chant (uktha) that is recited (as śastra), so much world does he conquer indeed in every year. (Sixth Brāhmaṇa Ends)
</details>
