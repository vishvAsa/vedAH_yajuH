## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

घ्न᳘न्ति ह वा᳘ एत᳘द्यज्ञं य᳘देनं तन्व᳘ते यद्रा᳘जानमभिषुण्व᳘न्ति तत्तं᳘ घ्नन्त्य᳘थ य᳘त्पशु᳘ꣳ᳘ संज्ञप᳘यन्ति य᳘द्विशा᳘सति तत्तं᳘ घ्नन्त्युलूखलमुसले᳘न दृषदुपले᳘न हविर्यज्ञं᳘ घ्नन्ति त᳘मेव᳘ꣳ᳘ ह᳘त्वाग्नौ यो᳘नौ रे᳘तोभूत᳘ꣳ᳘ सिञ्चत्यग्निर्वै᳘ यज्ञ᳘स्य यो᳘निः स त᳘तः प्र᳘जायते ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now, when they thus perform this sacrifice (elaborately), they kill it indeed. When they press the King (Soma) (for extraction of juice), then they kill him. And when they kill the sacrificial animal and when they cut it up, they kill it. They kill the Haviryajña (sacrifice with offerings of grain-preparations) with mortar and pestle and the two millstones. Having thus killed it (the sacrifice), he pours it that has become the seed into the womb viz., Agni, for Agni is the source of the sacrifice. It is born from it (Agni).
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ दश ता आ᳘हुतीः सं᳘पादयेद्या᳘भ्यो वषट्क्रिय᳘तेऽयं वाव᳘ यज्ञो᳘ योऽयं प᳘वते स वा᳘ अयमे᳘क इवैव᳘ पवते᳘ सोऽयं पुरुषेऽन्तः प्र᳘विष्टो दशधाविहितः स᳘ एवं᳘ प्राणैः᳘ क्लृप्तै᳘रग्नेर्यो᳘नेर᳘धि जायते द᳘शाक्षरा वै᳘ विरा᳘ट् सैषा᳘ संप᳘देव᳘ यज्ञ᳘ एव ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He should make those ten oblations for which Vaṣaṭ is uttered. Indeed this which blows (purifies) (the wind) is the sacrifice. It blows as if it were verily one. Having entered into man (a person) it is made into ten (parts). Thus with the vital energies thus arranged, it is born from its source, Agni. It is verily Virāṭ of ten syllables. It is verily perfection (prosperity). It is verily the sacrifice.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थो न᳘व स्यु᳘र्न्यूनामे᳘वैत᳘द्विरा᳘जं करोति प्रज᳘ननाय᳘ न्यूना᳘द्धीमाः᳘ प्रजाः᳘ प्रजा᳘यन्ते सैषा᳘ संप᳘देव᳘ यज्ञ᳘ एव ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now, there may be nine (oblations). Thus he makes the Virāṭ defective (less) for the sake of its birth. Indeed these creatures are born from the lesser (lower) (part). This is indeed perfection (prosperity), indeed the sacrifice.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थो एका᳘तिरिक्ता स्यात्प्रजा᳘पतिमेव᳘ साभ्य᳘तिरिच्यते᳘ सैषा᳘ संप᳘देव᳘ यज्ञ᳘ एव ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And, also it may be excessive by one (oblation). It is excessive verily with regard to Prajāpati. This is indeed perfection, verily the sacrifice.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थो द्वे अ᳘तिरिक्ते स्यातां द्वन्द्वं᳘ मिथुनं᳘ प्रज᳘नन᳘ꣳसैषा᳘ संप᳘देव᳘ यज्ञ᳘ एव ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And also there may be two additional (oblations). A pair is a productive couple. This is indeed perfection, verily the sacrifice.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थो तिस्रो᳘ऽतिरिक्ताः स्युर्द्वन्द्वं᳘ मिथुनं᳘ प्रज᳘ननं यज्जायते त᳘त्तृतीय᳘ꣳ᳘ सैषा᳘ संप᳘देव᳘ यज्ञ᳘ एव ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And also there may be three (oblations) in excess. A pair is a productive couple. What is born is the third. This is verily perfection (prosperity), indeed the sacrifice.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थो च᳘तस्रो᳘ऽतिरिक्ताः स्युर्यथै᳘कैवं च᳘तस्रस्त्रय इमे᳘ लोकाः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिमेवा᳘तिचतुर्थं प्रजा᳘पतिमेव᳘ साभ्य᳘तिरिच्यते᳘ सैषा᳘ संप᳘देव᳘ यज्ञ᳘ एव न᳘द्धोनं यद्द्वाभ्यामूनं त᳘दु हा᳘तिरिक्तं य᳘त्पञ्च᳘भिः स हा᳘यज्ञस्त᳘देत᳘देवा᳘धि दशस्वा᳘ विꣳशतेरा᳘ सह᳘स्रादेषा᳘न्वाहुतिसंप᳘त् ॥ ७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And also there may be four (oblations) in excess. As is one so are four. These worlds are three. The fourth passes beyond Prajāpati himself. It verily surpasses Prajāpati. This is verily perfection (prosperity), indeed the sacrifice. What is deficient by two (oblations) is indeed defective. What is excessive, by five (obla- tions) is verily excessive. Verily thus is the perfection of oblations in tens (ten oblations), from twenty upto a thousand.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

आजि᳘ꣳ᳘ ह वा᳘ एते᳘ धावन्ति ये᳘ दर्शपूर्णमासा᳘भ्यां य᳘जन्ते स प᳘ञ्चदश वर्षा᳘णि यजेत पञ्चदशानां वै᳘ वर्षा᳘णां त्री᳘णि च शता᳘नि षष्टि᳘श्च पौर्णमास्यश्चामावा᳘स्याश्च त्री᳘णि च वै᳘ शता᳘नि षष्टि᳘श्च संवत्सर᳘स्य रा᳘त्रयस्तद्रा᳘त्रीराप्नोत्यथा᳘पराणि पञ्च᳘दश व᳘र्षाणि यजेत पञ्चदशानां वै᳘ वर्षा᳘णां त्री᳘णि च शता᳘नि षष्टि᳘श्च पौर्णमा᳘स्यश्चामावा᳘स्याश्च त्री᳘णि चो एव᳘ शता᳘नि षष्टि᳘श्च संवत्सरस्या᳘हानि तद᳘हान्याप्नोति स᳘ यदा᳘ त्रिꣳशतं वर्षा᳘णि य᳘जतेऽथ संवत्सर᳘माप्नोति यदा᳘ ह वै᳘ देवाः᳘ संवत्सरमा᳘प्नुवन्न᳘थामृ᳘ता आसुर्म᳘र्त्या हैव त᳘तः पुरा᳘ बभूवुः स᳘र्वं वै᳘ संवत्स᳘रोऽक्षीयं वै स᳘र्वं ते᳘न हास्याक्षीय᳘ꣳ᳘ सुकृतं᳘ भवति य᳘ एवं᳘ विद्वा᳘ꣳ᳘स्त्रिश᳘तं वर्षा᳘णि य᳘जते प᳘ञ्चदशो एव᳘ वर्षा᳘णि यजते दाक्षायणयज्ञी᳘ सो एषा᳘त्रैव᳘ संद्द्वे हि᳘ पौर्णमा᳘स्यौ य᳘जते द्वे᳘ अमावा᳘स्ये ॥ ८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Those who sacrifice with the new moon and full moon rituals verily run a race. One should perform that sacrifice for fifteen years. Of the fifteen years, there are three hundred and sixty full moons and three hundred and sixty new moons. The nights of the year are verily three hundred and sixty. He thus gains the nights. And then he should perform the sacrifice for another fifteen years. Of the fifteen years there are three hundred and sixty full moons and new moons. Three hundred and sixty verily are the days of a year. Thus he obtains the days. When he sacrifices for thirty years, then he obtains the year. When the gods obtained the year, then they became immortal. Before then they were indeed mortal. The year is ‘all’. ‘All’ is undiminishing (indestructible). Thereby his merit (of good deeds) verily becomes indestructible for him who, knowing thus, sacrifices for thirty years. One who has performed the Dākṣāyaṇa sacrifice, sacrifices for only fifteen years. That perfection (prosperity) is verily in this, for he performs two full moon sacrifices and two new moon sacrifices. (First Brāhmaṇa Ends)
</details>
