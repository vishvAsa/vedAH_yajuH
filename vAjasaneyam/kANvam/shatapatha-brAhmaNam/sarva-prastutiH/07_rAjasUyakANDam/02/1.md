## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वा᳘ उप᳘रिष्टाद्र᳘त्नानाꣳ सौमारौद्रे᳘ण यजते स य᳘त्सौमारौद्रे᳘ण य᳘जते᳘ स्वर्भानुर्हासु᳘रस्सू᳘र्य त᳘मसा विव्याध स त᳘मसा विद्धो न᳘ व्य᳘रोचत त᳘स्य सोमारुद्रौ तत्त᳘मोऽपजघ्नतुस्स᳘ एषो᳘ऽपहतपाप्मा तपति त᳘म उ वा᳘ एतं प्र᳘विशति त᳘मो वैष प्र᳘विशति᳘ योऽयज्ञिया᳘न्यज्ञे᳘न प्रस᳘जत्य᳘यज्ञिया᳘नु वा᳘ एष᳘ एत᳘द्यज्ञे᳘न प्र᳘सजति वि᳘शं त्व᳘च्छूद्रा᳘ꣳ᳘स्त्वत्स य᳘दे᳘वैवैनं त᳘त्र त᳘मः प्रविश᳘ति य᳘द्वैष त᳘त्र त᳘मः प्रविश᳘ति त᳘स्य सोमारुद्रौ तत्तमो᳘ऽपहतस्सो᳘ऽपहतपाप्मा सूयते ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

After that (offering of jewels), he makes an offering to Soma and Rudra. Now, why he makes an offering to Soma and Rudra is this. Once upon a time an Asura (called) Svarbhānu, struck the Sun with darkness. Stricken by darkness, he (the Sun) did not shine. That darkness of his, Soma and Rudra dispelled. Thus rid of evil he burns yonder. Likewise darkness does enter this one (Yajamāna). He enters into darkness when he associates those unworthy of sacrifice, with the sacrifice. He does associate with the sacrifice those unworthy of sacrifice like the Viṣ and Śūdras (like charioteer, huntsman etc. in the jewel offerings) and hence darkness enters him or he enters into darkness. Soma and Rudra dispel that darkness of his. He, rid of evil, becomes consecrated.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वै᳘ श्वेता᳘याश्वेत᳘वत्सायाः प᳘यसि शृतो᳘ भवति कृष्ण᳘मिव वै त᳘मस्त᳘था हा᳘पहतस्त᳘स्य᳘ सैव द᳘क्षिणा श्वेता᳘ श्वेत᳘वत्सा ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That (caru for Soma and Rudra) gets cooked in the milk of a white cow with a white calf. Because darkness is black, as it were. That darkness is dispelled. For this that same white cow with a white calf is dakṣiṇā.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते᳘न हा᳘प्येते᳘न विष्ट्वाव्रा᳘जे स᳘ यजेत यो᳘ऽलं य᳘शसे सन्न य᳘शस्स्याद्यो वा᳘ अनूचानस्सो वा अ᳘लं य᳘शसे सन्न य᳘शो भवति य᳘दि स य᳘शो न भ᳘वति त᳘दस्य सोमारुद्रौ तत्त᳘मो᳘ऽपहतस्स᳘ ह ज्यो᳘तिरेव᳘ श्रिया य᳘शसा भवति ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

With this (caru) one can also offer at the cow-shed where cow-dung is heaped, if he, while being qualified for (deserving) fame is not yet famous; one learned in Vedic lore, while being entitled for fame is not famous. That darkness in him (which prevents his becoming famous), Soma and Rudra dispel. He becomes glorious with prosperity and fame.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वै᳘ श्वेता᳘याः श्वेत᳘वत्सायाः प᳘यसि शृतो᳘ भवति कृष्ण᳘मिव वै त᳘मस्त᳘थाहा᳘पहतस्त᳘स्य सैव द᳘क्षिणा श्वेता᳘ श्वेत᳘वत्सा ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That (caru to be offered at the cow-shed) gets cooked in the milk of a white cow with a white calf. Because darkness is black as it were; that darkness is dispelled. For this that same white cow with a white calf is the dakṣiṇā.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ श्वो᳘भूते᳘ मैत्राबार्हस्पत्ये᳘न यजते ह्व᳘लति वै स᳘ यज्ञपथा᳘द्यो यज्ञिया᳘न्यज्ञे᳘न प्रस᳘जत्य᳘यज्ञिया᳘नु वा एष᳘ एत᳘द्यज्ञे᳘न प्र᳘सजति वि᳘शं त्व᳘च्छूद्रा᳘ꣳ᳘स्त्वन्मित्राबृ᳘हस्प᳘ती उ वै᳘ यज्ञपथो ब्र᳘ह्मा वै᳘ मित्रो᳘ ब्र᳘ह्म यज्ञो ब्र᳘ह्म वै बृ᳘हस्प᳘तिर्ब्र᳘ह्म यज्ञस्तत्पु᳘नर्यज्ञपथम᳘भिपद्यते स᳘ यज्ञपथ᳘मभिप᳘द्य सूयते ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

On the next day, he offers a caru for Mitra and Bṛhaspati. He who associates with the sacrifice those who are unworthy of sacrifice, faulters and swerves from the path of sacrifice. He (the Yajamāna i.e. the king) does herein, associates with the sacrifice those unworthy of sacrifice like the Viṣ and Śūdras. Mitra and Bṛhaspati constitute the path of sacrifice. For, Mitra is Brahman and Brahman is the sacrifice; Bṛhaspati is Brahman and the sacrifice is Brahman. Thereby he comes back to the path of sacrifice. Having returned to the path of sacrifice he becomes consecrated.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘स्यावृद्या᳘ स्वयंप्रशीर्णा प्रा᳘ची वो᳘दीची वा᳘श्वत्थी शा᳘खा त᳘स्या मैत्रं पा᳘त्रꣳ स्याद्वरु᳘ण्यं वा᳘ एतत्पा᳘त्रं य᳘त्परशु᳘वृक्णम᳘थैत᳘न्मैत्रं य᳘त्स्वयंप्रशीर्णा᳘याः ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The procedure thereon is this—The vessel (to hold the caru) for Mitra is to be made of a branch on the eastern or northern side, that has fallen on its own accord from an Aśvattha tree. For, that (vessel) made of a branch is hewn by an axe, belongs to Varuṇa; that (vessel made of a branch) broken off by itself belongs to Mitra.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ द᳘ध्यात᳘च्य दृ᳘तावासि᳘च्य र᳘थे निब᳘ध्य ब्रूयादेते᳘न दे᳘दीयध्वमि᳘ति स यत्त᳘त्र न᳘वनीतमु᳘दियात्तदा᳘ज्यꣳ स्याद्व᳘रुण्यं वा᳘ एतदा᳘ज्यं य᳘न्मथा᳘ मथितम᳘थैत᳘न्मैत्रं य᳘त्स्वयमुदितम् ॥ ७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thereupon having curdled the (milk into) curds and poured it into a leathern container and having tied that leathern container to the chariot, he says "You (horse) run away". Therein that butter which comes out is the ājya (self- produced ghee). That (lather) produced by churning with a churning stick belongs to Varuṇa. This one that comes out on its own belongs to Mitra.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ द्वेधा᳘ तण्डुला᳘न्व्यूहेद्ये᳘ परिभिन्नास्ता᳘न्त्सार्धं तद्ये᳘ऽपरिभिन्नास्ते᳘ मैत्राः᳘ स्युर्न᳘ ह वै᳘ मित्रः क᳘ञ्चन हिनस्ति नो᳘ ह मित्रं क᳘श्चन᳘ हिनस्ति न᳘ हैनं कुशो न क᳘ण्टको वि᳘भिनत्ति स᳘र्वस्य᳘ ह्येव᳘ मित्रो᳘ मित्रं त᳘स्माद्ये᳘ऽपरिभिन्ना᳘स्ते मैत्राः᳘ स्युः ॥ ८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then rice is to be collected in two separate lots. Those which are broken are to be collected (separately) and those that are unbroken would belong to Mitra. For, Mitra neither injures anybody nor anyone injures Mitra. Him not even a grass or a thorn hurts; Mitra is the friend of everyone. So those that are unbroken (of the rice) belong to Mitra.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थास्मिन्मैत्रे पा᳘त्र आ᳘ज्यमानी᳘य त᳘देता᳘न्मैत्रा᳘ꣳ᳘स्तण्डुलाना᳘वपति ते᳘ नैतं᳘ बार्हस्पत्यं᳘ चरु᳘ꣳ᳘ शृतम᳘पिदधाति वरु᳘ण्यो वा᳘ एष᳘ चरु᳘र्योऽग्नि᳘पक्वो᳘ऽथैष᳘ मैत्रो य᳘त्स्वयꣳ शृतस्त᳘द्वे᳘वोष्म᳘णा शृतो᳘ भवति ॥ ९ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And now in that vessel of Mitra, he brings ghee and throws into it these rice grains (unbroken) of Mitra and with that he covers the caru meant for Bṛhaspati, which has been cooked (so that the Mitra one is cooked by the steam of the latter cooked one). If the caru were to be (directly) cooked by fire, it would become that of Varuṇa. This caru for Mitra is self-cooked because it is cooked in steam.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘योस्समवद्य᳘न्नाह मित्राबृ᳘हस्प᳘तिभ्याम᳘नुब्रूही᳘त्याश्रा᳘व्याह मित्राबृ᳘हस्प᳘ती यजे᳘ति व᳘षट्कृते जुहोति त᳘स्य गौ᳘रेव द᳘क्षिणा ॥ १० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Making cutting from both these (carus) he says, "Pronounce the invitatory chant to Mitra and Bṛhaspati". Having called for the Śrausat, he says "Pronounce the offering prayer to Mitra and Bṛhaspati" and offers as the Vaṣaṭkāra is uttered. For this, a cow is the dakṣiṇā.
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते᳘न हा᳘प्येते᳘न विष्ठाव्रा᳘जे स᳘ यजेत᳘ योऽप्रतिगृह्य᳘स्य वा प्रतिगृह्णीया᳘दयाज्यं᳘ वा याजयेत्द्ध᳘लति वै स᳘ योऽप्रतिगृह्य᳘स्य वा प्रतिगृह्णा᳘त्ययाज्यं᳘ वा याजयति मित्राबृ᳘हस्प᳘ती उ वै᳘ यज्ञपथो ब्र᳘ह्म वै᳘ मित्रो ब्र᳘ह्म यज्ञो ब्र᳘ह्म वै बृ᳘हस्प᳘तिर्ब्र᳘ह्म यज्ञस्तत्पु᳘नः प᳘न्थानम᳘भिपद्यते त᳘था न᳘ ह्वलति ॥ ११ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This offering, one can also make at a cow-shed where cow-dung is heaped, by a person who accepts a gift from one not worthy to be a giver or one who officiates in a sacrifice performed by an unworthy Yajamāna. For, he who accepts a gift from one unworthy to be the giver or who officiates in a sacrifice performed by one unworthy to be the Yajamāna, does indeed faulter. Mitra and Bṛhaspati constitute the path of sacrifice. Mitra forsooth, is Brahman and the sacrifice is Brahman. Bṛhaspati is Brahman and the sacrifice is Brahman. (Thereby) he again returns to the path of sacrifice and thus does not faulter. (First Brāhmaṇa Ends)
</details>
