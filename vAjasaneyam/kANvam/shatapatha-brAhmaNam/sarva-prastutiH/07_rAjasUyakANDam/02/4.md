## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तं वै मा᳘ध्यन्दिने स᳘वनेऽभि᳘षिञ्चन्ति स य᳘देनं मा᳘ध्यन्दिने स᳘वनेऽभि᳘षिञ्चत्येष वाव᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिर्यज्ञो य᳘ एष᳘ ताय᳘त एत᳘स्यैवैनमेत᳘त्प्रजा᳘पतेर्यज्ञ᳘स्य मध्यत᳘ आद᳘धाति मध्य᳘तोऽभि᳘षिञ्चन्ति त᳘स्मादेनं मा᳘ध्यन्दिने स᳘वनेऽभि᳘षिञ्चन्ति ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He (the Adhvaryu) bathes (anoints) him at the midday savana. As to why he anoints him in the savana is this. This sacrifice which is performed is verily Prajāpati. From the middle of this Prajāpati, the sacrifice, he takes him and in the middle (of it) he anoints him. Therefore he anoints him in the midday savana.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘गृहीते माहेन्द्रे ग्र᳘ह एष वा इ᳘न्द्रस्य नि᳘ष्केवल्यो ग्र᳘हो य᳘न्माहेन्द्रो᳘ऽप्यस्यैतं नि᳘ष्केवल्यमेव᳘ स्तोत्रं नि᳘ष्केवल्यꣳ श᳘स्त्रं द्वये᳘न वा᳘ एष इ᳘न्द्रो य᳘च्च क्षत्रि᳘यो य᳘च्च य᳘जमानस्य᳘ ए᳘वैनं त᳘दाय᳘तनेऽभि᳘षिञ्चति त᳘स्माद᳘गृहीते माहेन्द्रे ग्र᳘हे ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(He anoints) when the Māhendragraha is not drawn. This Māhendra is indeed the exclusive graha (soma cup) of Indra. The stotra for it is exclusive; the śastra chant is exclusive. On two counts this one (the king) is Indra since he is a Kṣatriya and he is a Yajamāna. He is bathed in his own abode. Therefore when the Māhendragraha is not drawn (he anoints him).
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अथा᳘ग्रेण मैत्रावरुण᳘स्य धि᳘ष्ण्यं त᳘दस्मै शार्दूलचर्मो᳘पस्तृणाति सो᳘मस्य त्वि᳘षिरसी᳘ति य᳘त्र वा अ᳘द इ᳘न्द्रꣳ सो᳘मोऽत्य᳘पवत त᳘तश्शार्दूलस्स᳘मभवत्त᳘स्मादाह सो᳘मस्य त्वि᳘षिरसी᳘ति ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He spread a tiger skin for him in front of the Maitrā-Varuṇa hearth (saying) “Thou art the brilliance of Soma”. When the Soma flowed through Indra, thence he became a tiger. Therefore he says ‘Thou art Soma’s brilliance’.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थास्मै पार्था᳘नि जुहोति स य᳘दस्मै पार्था᳘नि जुहो᳘ति पृ᳘थ्वी ह वै᳘ वै᳘न्यः प्रथमो᳘ मनु᳘ष्याणामभिषिषिचे᳘ सोऽकामयत स᳘र्वमन्ना᳘द्यम᳘वरुन्धीये᳘ति य᳘च्च ग्राम्यं य᳘च्चारण्यमि᳘ति त᳘स्मा एता᳘न्यजुहवुः स स᳘र्वमन्ना᳘द्यम᳘वारुन्ध य᳘च्च ग्राम्यं य᳘च्चारण्यम᳘पि हास्यारण्याः᳘ पश᳘वो हवामे᳘युरसावे᳘हि रा᳘जा त्वा वक्ष्यत इ᳘ति त᳘था स᳘र्वमन्ना᳘द्यम᳘वरुरोध एव᳘ꣳ᳘ ह वाव स᳘र्वमन्ना᳘द्यम᳘वरुन्धे य᳘च्च ग्राम्यं य᳘च्चारण्यं य᳘स्यैवं᳘ विदु᳘ष एता᳘नि जु᳘ह्वति स य᳘देता᳘नि पृ᳘थ्व्यै वैन्याया᳘जुहवुस्त᳘स्मात्पार्था᳘नि नाम ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then offers the Pārtha oblations. Now Pṛthu Vainya was consecrated first among men. He desired that he might appropriate to himself all food; both cultivated and wild. Therefore he offered these (Pārtha oblations). Thereby he appropriated all food; both cultivated and wild. The wild animals too reached his sacrifice (when called thus) “Come hither so and so animal, the king would cook you”. Thus he appropriated all food. In like manner, this one (Yajamāna) appro- priates all food both cultivated and wild. On behalf of him (Yajamāna) who knows this, these (oblations) are offered which were offered in the case of Pṛthu Vainya. Therefore they are called Pārthas.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ता᳘नि वै द्वा᳘दश जुहोति द्वा᳘दश वै मा᳘सास्सं᳘वत्सर᳘स्य त᳘स्माद्द्वा᳘दश जुहोति ष᳘ट् पुर᳘स्तादभिषेक᳘स्य जुहो᳘ति ष᳘ळुप᳘रिष्टादेष वाव᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिर्यज्ञो य᳘ एष᳘ ताय᳘त एत᳘स्यै᳘वैनमेत᳘त्प्रजा᳘पतेर्यज्ञ᳘स्य मध्यत᳘ आद᳘धाति मध्य᳘तोऽभि᳘षिञ्चति त᳘स्मात्ष᳘ट्पुर᳘स्तादभिषेक᳘स्य जुहो᳘ति ष᳘ळुप᳘रिष्टाद्या᳘नि पुर᳘स्तादभिषेक᳘स्य ष᳘ड् जुहो᳘ति बृहस्प᳘तिस्तेषा᳘मुत्तमो᳘ऽथ या᳘न्युप᳘रिष्टादभिषेक᳘स्य ष᳘ड् जुहोती᳘न्द्रस्ते᳘षां प्रथमो ब्र᳘ह्म वै बृ᳘हस्प᳘तिः क्षत्रमि᳘न्द्रो ब्र᳘ह्मणा चै᳘वैत᳘त्क्षत्रे᳘ण चोभा᳘भ्याम᳘नन्तर्हिताभ्यां प᳘रिबृꣳहति त᳘स्य ना᳘र्तिर्न ह्वाला᳘ भवति ब्र᳘ह्मणा च क्षत्रे᳘ण चोभय᳘तः प᳘रिगृहीतस्य ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Those are twelve (Pārtha oblations) which he offers. For, there are twelve months for a year and hence twelve offerings are made. Six of them he offers before consecration and six afterwards. This sacrifice which is performed is verily Prajāpati. From the middle of this Prajāpati sacrifice, he takes him (Yajamāna). In the middle of it he anoints him. Therefore six he offers before the consecration and six after— wards. Of the six offerings he makes before anointing, the last one is (for) Bṛhaspati. Of the six he makes after the anointing, the first one of them is (for) Indra. Bṛhaspati is Brahman and Indra is Kṣatriya. Thus with Brahmanic (lustre) and Kṣatriya (vigour) uninterrupted (covered on both sides) he enriches him. For him (Yajamāna) so covered on either side by Brahman and Kṣatra, there is no suffering, no unsteadiness.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ जुहोत्यग्न᳘ये स्वाहे᳘ति ते᳘जो वा᳘ अग्निस्ते᳘जसै᳘वैनं त᳘दभि᳘षिञ्चति सो᳘माय स्वाहे᳘ति क्षत्रं वै सो᳘मः क्षत्रे᳘णै᳘वैनं तदभि᳘षिञ्चति सवित्रे स्वाहे᳘ति सविता वै᳘ देवा᳘नां प्रसविता᳘ सवितृ᳘प्रसूत ए᳘वैनं त᳘दभि᳘षिञ्चति स᳘रस्वत्यै स्वाहे᳘ति वाग्वै स᳘रस्वती वा᳘चै᳘वैनं त᳘दभि᳘षिञ्चति पूष्णे स्वाहे᳘ति पश᳘वो वै᳘ पूषा᳘ पशु᳘भिरे᳘वैनं त᳘दभि᳘षिञ्चति बृ᳘हस्प᳘तये स्वाहे᳘ति ब्र᳘ह्म वै बृ᳘हस्प᳘तिर्ब्र᳘ह्मणै᳘वैनं त᳘दभि᳘षिञ्चत्येता᳘नि पुर᳘स्तादभिषेक᳘स्य ष᳘ड् जुहोत्य᳘थाभिषिक्ते᳘ जुहोति ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He offers (saying) "To Agni—Svāhā". Agni is glory and with glory he bathes him. "To Soma—Svāhā"—Kṣatra (grandeur) is Soma and with grandeur, he bathes him. "To Savitṛ—Svāhā". Savitṛ is the impeller of gods; impelled by Savitṛ, he thus consecrates him. "To Sarasvati—Svāhā"—Sarasvati is forsooth, speech. He thus bathes him with speech. "To Pūṣan—Svāhā". Pūṣan is the cattle. Thus he bathes him with cattle. "To Bṛhaspati—Svāhā". Bṛhaspati is Brahmanic (lustre). He thus bathes him with Brahmanic lustre. These are the six offerings he makes before the anointing. Then he offers (after he) is anointed.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

इ᳘न्द्राय स्वाहे᳘तीन्द्रियं वै᳘ वी᳘र्यमि᳘न्द्र इन्द्रिये᳘णै᳘वैनं त᳘द्वी᳘र्येणाभि᳘षिञ्चत्य᳘ꣳशाय स्वाहे᳘ति वी᳘र्यं वा अ᳘ꣳ᳘शो वी᳘र्येणै᳘वैनं त᳘दभिषिञ्चति श्लो᳘काय स्वाहे᳘ति वी᳘र्यं वै श्लो᳘को वी᳘र्येणै᳘वैनं त᳘दभि᳘षिञ्चति घो᳘षाय स्वाहे᳘ति वी᳘र्यं वै घो᳘षो वी᳘र्येणै᳘वैनं त᳘दभि᳘षिञ्चति भ᳘गाय स्वाहे᳘ति वी᳘र्यं वै भ᳘गो वी᳘र्येणै᳘वैनं त᳘दभि᳘षिञ्चत्यर्यम्णे स्वाहेत्य᳘स्यै᳘वैनमेतत्स᳘र्वस्यार्यम᳘णं करोति᳘ सोऽस्य स᳘र्वस्यार्यमा᳘ भवति तद्या᳘न्येता᳘न्युप᳘रिष्टादभिषेक᳘स्य ष᳘ड् जुहो᳘त्यादित्यनामानी᳘त्येतान्या᳘ख्याय᳘न्तेऽथैता᳘न्यभिषेचनी᳘यानि पा᳘त्राण्यु᳘पनिहिता᳘नि भवन्ति ॥ ७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"To Indra—Svāhā"—Indra means the vigour of the mighty Indra. He thus anoints him with the vigour of that mighty Indra. "To Aṁśu—Svāhā"—Aṁśu means prowess. He thus anoints him with prowess. "To the Śloka—Svāhā". Śloka means might. He thus anoints him with might. "To the Ghoṣa (noise)—Svāhā". Ghoṣa means strength. He thus anoints him with strength. "To Bhaga—Svāhā"—Bhaga means vigour. He thus anoints him with vigour. "To Aryaman—Svāhā"—he thereby makes this one (Yajamāna) a friend of everything here. He thus becomes the friend of all. These are the six (offerings) after the consecration. These are said to be the names of Ādityas. Then these vessels meant for consecration get deposited (before the hearth of Maitrā-Varuṇa).
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तत्पा᳘लाशं भवति ते᳘न ब्राह्म᳘णोऽभि᳘षिञ्चति ब्र᳘ह्म वै पलाशो ब्र᳘ह्म वै᳘ ब्राह्मणस्त᳘स्मात्पा᳘लाशेन ब्राह्म᳘णोऽभि᳘षिञ्चति ॥ ८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

There is a vessel made of Palāśa wood. With that the Brahmin anoints him. For, Palāśa is Brahman and the Brahmin is Brahman. Therefore with the Palāsa (vessel), the Brahmin consecrates.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

औ᳘दुम्बरेण᳘ स्व ऊर्वै स्वं या᳘वद्व्यस्वं भ᳘वति स हि ता᳘वदशनायति त᳘स्मादौ᳘दुम्बरेण᳘ स्वः ॥ ९ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

One of his own (kinsmen) anoints him with the Udumbara vessel. Udumbara means sustenance. As long as one has sustenance, till then he is not hungry. So with the Udumbara vessel.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

नै᳘य्यग्रोधपादे᳘न मि᳘त्रियः पद्भि᳘र्वै न्यग्रो᳘धः प्र᳘तिष्ठितो मित्रे᳘ण वै᳘ क्षत्रं प्र᳘तिष्ठितं त᳘स्मान्नै᳘य्यग्रोधपादे᳘न मि᳘त्रियः ॥ १० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

A friend (anoints) with that (vessel) made of the bottom (stem) of Nyagrodha tree. The Nyagrodha is supported by its stems. The Kṣatra is well- supported by a friend (by an ally). Therefore a friend (anoints) with that (vessel) made of the bottom (stem) of Nyagrodha tree.
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘श्वत्थेन वै᳘श्यो मारुतो वा᳘ अश्वत्थो य᳘दश्वत्थे ति᳘ष्ठत इ᳘न्द्रो मरु᳘त उपा᳘मन्त्रयत ते᳘न मारु᳘तोऽश्वत्थो वि᳘शो वै᳘ मरुतो᳘ऽन्नमु वै वि᳘शस्त᳘स्मादा᳘श्वत्थेन वै᳘श्यः ॥ ११ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

With that (vessel) made of Aśvattha wood, a Vaiśya (peasant) (anoints him). Aśvattha belongs to the Maruts; because Indra addressed the Maruts while they were seated on an Aśvattha tree. Therefore Aśvattha belongs to the Maruts. Viś (peasantry) is food. Therefore Vaiśya (peasant) with an Aśvattha (vessel).
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ सुषिरे᳘ इव हि᳘रण्ये कृते᳘ भवतस्तौ᳘ पवित्रयोः प्रो᳘ते भ᳘वतो सो᳘ऽन्वेव᳘ पवि᳘त्रयोर्ब᳘न्धुता यै᳘वासौ᳘ दर्शपूर्णमास᳘योर᳘थ य᳘त्सुषिरे᳘ इव हि᳘रण्ये प्रो᳘ते भ᳘वत आ᳘युर्वा᳘ अमृ᳘तꣳ हि᳘रण्यमा᳘युरे᳘वैत᳘दमृ᳘तमेता᳘स्वभिषेचनी᳘यास्वप्सु᳘ दधाति त᳘स्माद्धि᳘रण्ये प्रो᳘ते भ᳘वतः ॥ १२ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

There are two pieces of gold with holes made in them. Two Pavitras (strainers) are inserted into those (holes). The significance of these Pavitras is the same as told in (the context of) Darśapūrṇamāsa (sacrifice). As to why the two pieces of gold with holes are tugged to them (Pavitras)—the immortal gold (stands) for longevity of life. Thereby he infuses immortal life into these waters that are (kept) for anointing. Therefore they (strainers) are inserted into gold pieces.
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स उ᳘त्पुनाति सवितु᳘र्वः प्रसव उ᳘त्पुनाम्य᳘च्छिद्रेण पवि᳘त्रेण सू᳘र्यस्य रश्मि᳘भिरि᳘ति सोऽसा᳘वे᳘वैत᳘स्य य᳘जुषो ब᳘न्धुर᳘निभृष्टमसि वाचो ब᳘न्धुस्तपोजा इत्य᳘नाधृष्टा स्थ र᳘क्षोभिर्नाष्ट्रा᳘भिरि᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह यदाहा᳘निभृष्टमसी᳘ति वाचो ब᳘न्धुरि᳘ति या᳘वद्वै पु᳘रुष आ᳘पो भ᳘वन्ति ता᳘वद्धै᳘ वाचा᳘ वदति त᳘स्मादाह वाचो ब᳘न्धुरि᳘ति तपोजा इ᳘त्यग्रेर्वै᳘ धूमो᳘ जायते धूमा᳘दभ्र᳘मभ्राद्व्यृ᳘ष्टिरग्नि᳘रु वै त᳘पस्त᳘स्मादाह तपोजा इ᳘ति सो᳘मस्य दात्र᳘मसीति यदा वा᳘ अद्भिस्सो᳘मꣳ रा᳘जानमु᳘पसृजन्त्य᳘थ वै᳘ सोऽलमा᳘हुतयेऽभवद᳘लं भक्षा᳘य त᳘स्मादाह सो᳘मस्य दात्र᳘मसी᳘ति स्वा᳘हा राजस्व इ᳘ति त᳘देना एते᳘न स्वाहाकारे᳘ण पुनाति ॥ १३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He purifies (saying) "With the impulse of Savitṛ, I purify you with a flawless purifier with the rays of the Sun". The significance of this is same as before. "Not overcome by anybody, thou art; the friend of speech, born of heat" (he says). He means 'You are not challenged by the terrible Rākṣasas'. When he says "Not overcome by anybody, thou art". "The friend of speech",—as long as there is water in (the body of) man, till then he speaks with speech. Therefore he says "Friend of speech". "Born of heat"---the smoke comes out of fire and from smoke comes the cloud. From the cloud comes rain. Tāpa (heat) means Agni. Therefore he says "Born of heat". (He says) "(Thou art) the giver of Soma"—when they soak king Soma (plant) with waters (and produce somajuice) then only he (Soma) becomes fit for oblation, fit for drinking. Therefore he says (thou art) the giver of Soma. (He says) "Svāhā—spiriters of kings"—for, these waters he purifies by uttering Svāhā".
</details>


## १४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थैते᳘ष्वभिषेचनी᳘येषु पात्रेषु᳘ व्या᳘नयति सधमा᳘दो द्युम्निनीरा᳘प एता इत्य᳘नतिमानिन्य इ᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह यदा᳘ह सधमाद इ᳘ति द्युम्नि᳘नीरि᳘ति वी᳘र्यवत इ᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाहा᳘नाधृष्टा अप᳘स्यो व᳘साना इत्य᳘नाधृष्टा र᳘क्षो᳘भिर्नाष्ट्राभिरि᳘त्ये᳘वैतदाह प᳘स्त्यासु चक्रे व᳘रुणस्सध᳘स्थमि᳘ति क्षत्रं वै व᳘रुणो वि᳘शः प᳘स्त्या विक्षु᳘ चक्रे व᳘रुणः प्रतिष्ठामि᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाहापाꣳ शि᳘शुर्मातृ᳘तमास्वन्तरि᳘त्यापा᳘ꣳ᳘ ह्येष शि᳘शुर्भ᳘वति यो᳘ राजसू᳘येन य᳘जते त᳘स्मादाहापाꣳ शि᳘शुर्मातृ᳘तमास्वन्तरि᳘ति ॥ १४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then distributes them over the consecrating vessels (saying) "These glorious waters are mutually respecting ones". He means that they are not over- bearing when he says "These waters are mutually respecting ones". He means that they are not over-bearing when he says "These waters are mutually respecting ones". (By saying) "Glorious" he only says "Powerful ones". (He says) "Unchallenged, eager (to participate in Rājasūya) and thou which envelop (the earth)". By "Unchallanged" he means 'unchallenged by terrible Rākṣasas'. (He says) "Whom Varuņa caused to remain united in the inside of the houses". Varuņa is Kṣatra and the houses are peasants (Viś) and Varuņa established these waters in the peasantry". This is what he says. "The child of waters, inside the best of mothers". This one who performs Rājasūya happens to be the child of waters and hence he says "The child of waters, inside the best of mothers".
</details>


## १५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थैनमेता᳘नि वा᳘साꣳसि प᳘रिधापयति त᳘त्तार्यमि᳘ति वा᳘सो भवति त᳘स्मिन्त्स᳘र्वाणि यज्ञरूपा᳘णि नि᳘ष्पूतानि भवन्ति त᳘देनं प᳘रिधापयति क्षत्रस्यो᳘ल्बमसी᳘ति क्षत्र᳘स्य वा᳘ एतदु᳘ल्बꣳ स य᳘देव᳘ क्षत्रस्यो᳘ल्बं त᳘त ए᳘वैनमेत᳘ज्जनयति ॥ १५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he makes him wear these garments. There is that one called Tārpya (a garment soaked in ghee). In that all sacrificial figures (like Camasā, Adhiṣavaṇa, etc.) are sewn. He makes him wear this (saying) "Thou art the Ulba (membrane surrounding the embryo) of Kṣatra (martial power)". This garment is indeed, the Ulba of Kṣatra. That is why he causes him to be born out of it.
</details>


## १६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ पा᳘ण्ड्वं क्षत्र᳘स्य जरा᳘य्वसी᳘ति क्षत्र᳘स्य वा᳘ एत᳘ज्जरा᳘यु स य᳘देव᳘ क्षत्र᳘स्य जरा᳘यु त᳘त ए᳘वैनमेत᳘ज्जनयति ॥ १६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then (he makes him wear) a white shawl (saying) "Thou art the Jarāyu (outer foetal membrane) of Kṣatra". This is indeed the Jarāyu of martial power. From that he causes him to be born.
</details>


## १७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ सꣳहृ᳘त्य पुर᳘स्तादुष्णी᳘षम᳘वगूहति क्षत्र᳘स्य ना᳘भिरसी᳘ति क्षत्र᳘स्य वा᳘ एषा ना᳘भिस्स᳘ यैव᳘ क्षत्र᳘स्य ना᳘भिस्त᳘त ए᳘वैनमेत᳘ज्जनयति ॥ १७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he causes to wear a turban with its edges hanging over the chest and tucked together and concealed (at the navel) (saying) "Thou art the navel of Kṣatra". It is indeed the navel of martial power. From that which is the navel of Kṣatra, he causes him to be born.
</details>


## १८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तद्धै᳘के समन्तं प᳘रिवेष्टयन्ति त᳘दु त᳘था न᳘ कुर्यात्पुर᳘स्तादेवा᳘वगूहेत्पुर᳘स्ता᳘द्धीयं ना᳘भिर᳘थाधिवासं᳘ क्षत्र᳘स्य यो᳘निरसी᳘ति क्षत्र᳘स्य वा᳘ एषा यो᳘निस्सा यैव᳘ क्षत्र᳘स्य यो᳘निस्त᳘त ए᳘वैनमेत᳘ज्जनयति स य᳘देनमेता᳘नि वा᳘साꣳसि प᳘रिधाप᳘यति जन᳘यत्ये᳘वैनं त᳘ज्जात᳘मेनमभि᳘षिञ्चामे᳘ति ॥ १८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Some of them wind it around (the navel). That is not to be done so. It should be (with its edges) concealed only in the front, because the navel is in the front side. He then (puts on him) the mantle (adhivāsa) (saying) "Thou art the womb of Kṣatra". This (mantle) is indeed the womb of martial power. From that which is the womb of Kṣatra, he causes him to be born. By making him wear these garments, he causes him to be born (thinking) 'I shall consecrate the one who is thus born'.
</details>


## १९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तद्धै᳘के परिधा᳘प्यैव पु᳘नर्निधाया᳘थ दीक्षितव᳘सनं प᳘रिधापयन्ति त᳘दु त᳘था न᳘ कुर्याद᳘ङ्गानि वा᳘ अस्यैता᳘नि जन्वो᳘ऽङ्गैर्हैनꣳ सज᳘न्वा व्यर्धयति त᳘स्माद᳘त ए᳘वैकतमं᳘ वसीत त᳘था हैनम᳘ङ्गैर्ज᳘न्वा न᳘ व्यर्धयति वरु᳘ण्यमु वै᳘ दीक्षितव᳘सनं त᳘थो व᳘रुण्याद्दीक्षितव᳘सनात्प्र᳘मुच्यते स य᳘त्रावभृथ᳘मभ्यवै᳘ति त᳘देतत्प्र᳘तिवेशं पार्श्व᳘तोऽन्वभ्य᳘वहरन्ति त᳘दु स᳘लोम क्रियतेऽथा᳘त ए᳘वैकतमं᳘ वसानः पु᳘नरुदै᳘ति ता᳘न्यनुब᳘न्ध्यायां वा वशा᳘यां ददात्युदवसानी᳘यायां वे᳘ष्टौ ॥ १९ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Some of them, having made him wear (these garments) take them off and again make him wear the garment of initiation. That is not to be done so. These are his limbs by birth (born with him). They (by doing as above) deprive him of his limbs with which he is born. Therefore he should wear one of those (garments). In doing so he does not separate him from the limbs he has from birth. The initiation garment belongs to Varuṇa. Thus he saves him from the Varuṇic garment of initiation. When he gets down (the water) for final ablution (avabhr̥ta), this (garment of initiation) they carry along by his side. That is disposed off in the prescribed way (as in Soma sacrifice where the garment of initiation is to be thrown in the water at the time of Avabhr̥ta). Then he emerges out (of the ablution) wearing one of those garments. That he gives away (as gift) at the offering by Anbandhya vasā or at the Udavasanīya Iṣṭi.
</details>


## २०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थास्मै ध᳘नुरुज्यं᳘ करोती᳘न्द्रस्य वा᳘र्त्रघ्नमसी᳘ति वा᳘र्त्रघ्नं वा᳘ एतद्यद्ध᳘नुर्द्वये᳘न वा᳘ एष इ᳘न्द्रो य᳘च्च क्षत्रि᳘यो य᳘च्च य᳘जमानस्त᳘स्मादाहे᳘न्द्रस्य वा᳘र्त्रघ्नमसी᳘ति त्व᳘यायं᳘ वृत्रं᳘ वध्यादि᳘ति त्व᳘यायं᳘ द्विष᳘न्तं भ्रा᳘तृव्यꣳ हन्त्वि᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह यदा᳘ह त्व᳘यायं᳘ वृत्रं᳘ वध्यादि᳘ति ॥ २० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then draws up the string on the bow for him (saying) "Thou art Indra's Vr̥tra-killer". For, this bow is indeed the killer of Vr̥tra. He (the king) is Indra on two counts; because he is a Kṣatriya and he is the Yajamāna. Therefore he says "Thou art Indra's Vr̥tra-killer", "Let this one kill Vr̥tra with you". He means "By you, let this one (Yajamāna) kill his hateful enemy", by saying 'Let this one kill Vr̥tra with you".
</details>


## २१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ बाहू वि᳘मार्टि मित्र᳘स्यासि व᳘रुणस्यासी᳘ति बाह्वोर्वै ध᳘नुर्बाहु᳘भ्यां वै᳘ मैत्रावरुणो᳘ राज᳘न्यस्त᳘स्मादाह मित्र᳘स्यासि व᳘रुणस्यासी᳘ति ॥ २१ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he strokes his (king's) arms (saying) "Thou art of Mitra; thou art of Varuṇa". The bow belongs to the arms. It is by his arms, a Kṣatriya belongs to Mitra and Varuṇa. Therefore he says 'Thou art of Mitra, thou art of Varuṇa.'
</details>


## २२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थास्मा इ᳘षूस्तिस्रः प्र᳘यच्छति तिस्रो᳘ ह वा इ᳘षव इदं नु य᳘या सम᳘र्पणेन पराभिन᳘त्ति सै᳘का᳘ सेयं᳘ पृथिवी᳘ सैषा᳘ रुजा नामा᳘थ य᳘या विद्धः᳘ शिश्यानो जी᳘वति वा म्रिय᳘ते वा सा᳘ द्विती᳘या त᳘दिद᳘मन्त᳘रिक्ष᳘ꣳ᳘ सैषा᳘ दृबा नामा᳘थ यया᳘ प्रैव᳘ राध्नो᳘ति सा᳘ तृती᳘या सा᳘सौ द्यौः᳘ सैषा᳘ क्षपा ना᳘मैता हि वै᳘ तिस्र इ᳘षवस्त᳘स्मादस्मा इ᳘षूस्तिस्रः प्र᳘यच्छति स य᳘दस्मा इ᳘षुध᳘नुः प्र᳘यच्छति ध᳘नुर्वै᳘ राज᳘न्यस्य वी᳘र्यं धनुषा᳘ हि᳘ राज᳘न्यो वी᳘र्यं करो᳘ति जात᳘ ए᳘वास्मिन्नेत᳘द्वी᳘र्यं दधाति स᳘वीर्यमेनमि᳘ति वी᳘र्यवन्तमभि᳘षिञ्चामे᳘ति ॥ २२ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he gives him (the king) three arrows. They are three arrows of which that first one, by discharging which, he pierces (the enemy), that one is the earth. That is called Rujā. That (second) one by discharging which (the person hit) falls down, not sure whether alive or dead, that is the second and this one is the air. It is called Drubā. That (third) one by discharging which (the king) does kill (the enemy) that is the third one. That is the yonder sky. It is called Kṣupā. These are the three arrows. Therefore he gives these three to him. As to why he gives him the bow with arrows—the bow is the might of Kṣatriya. It is with the bow, a Kṣatriya shows his might. This might he (Adhvaryu) invests in him as (soon as) he is born (thinking) 'Him invested with might (let me consecrate)'.
</details>


## २३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थैनमभि᳘मृशति पा᳘तैनं प्रा᳘ञ्चं पा᳘तैनं प्रत्य᳘ञ्चं पा᳘तैनं तिर्य᳘ञ्चं दिग्भ्यः᳘ पाते᳘ति स᳘र्वाभ्य ए᳘वास्मा एत᳘द्दिग्भ्यो᳘ऽशरव्यं करोति त᳘स्मै स᳘र्वाभ्यो दिग्भ्यो᳘ऽशरव्यं᳘ भवति ॥ २३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He touches (each of) the arrows (saying) "Protect this one (king) in front; protect him behind; protect him from the sides; protect him from all quarters". Thus he makes him safe from arrows on all the quarters. He becomes unassailable from all quarters.
</details>


## २४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थैनमावि᳘दो वाच्यत्या᳘वि᳘र्मर्या इत्य᳘निरुक्तम᳘निरुक्तो वै᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिस्त᳘देनं प्रजा᳘पतय आ᳘वेदयति᳘ सोऽस्मा एत᳘ꣳ᳘सवम᳘नुमन्यते तेना᳘नुमतस्सूयते ॥ २४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he makes him (the king) chant (seven) mantras (having the word) avid. "In sight ye mortals". This is a mysterious chant for, Prajāpati is mysterious. So he thus commends him to Prajāpati. He (Prajāpati) approves this, his consecration. Approved by him, he is consecrated.
</details>


## २५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

आ᳘वित्तो अग्नि᳘र्गृह᳘पतिरि᳘ति ब्र᳘ह्मा वा᳘ अग्निस्त᳘देनं ब्र᳘ह्मण आ᳘वेदयति᳘ सोऽस्मा एत᳘ꣳ᳘ सवम᳘नुमन्यते तेना᳘नुमतस्सूयते ॥ २५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Present is Agni, the lord of the house". Agni is indeed Brahman. He thus commends him to the priesthood and it approves of his consecration. Approved by him he is consecrated.
</details>


## २६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

आ᳘वित्त इ᳘न्द्रो वृद्ध᳘श्रवा इ᳘ति क्षत्रं वा इ᳘न्द्रस्त᳘देनं क्षत्राया᳘वेदयति᳘ सोऽस्मा एत᳘ꣳ᳘ सवम᳘नुमन्यते तेना᳘नुमतस्सूयते ॥ २६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Present is Indra, possessed of great fame". Indra is indeed Kṣatra. He thus commends him to the martial class and the Kṣatra class approves of his consecra- tion. Approved by it, he is consecrated.
</details>


## २७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

आ᳘वित्तः पूषा᳘ विश्व᳘वेदा᳘ इ᳘ति पश᳘वो वै᳘ पूषा त᳘देनं पशु᳘भ्य आ᳘वेदय᳘ति तेऽस्मा एत᳘ꣳ᳘ सवम᳘नुमन्यन्ते तैर᳘नुमतस्सूयते ॥ २७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Commentary not Available.
</details>


## २८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

आ᳘वित्तौ मित्राव᳘रुणौ धृत᳘व्रतावि᳘ति प्राणोदानौ वै᳘ मित्राव᳘रुणौ त᳘देनं प्राणोदाना᳘भ्यामा᳘वेदयति ता᳘वस्मा एत᳘ꣳ᳘ सवम᳘नुमन्यते ता᳘भ्याम᳘नुमतस्सूयते ॥ २८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Present are Mitra and Varuṇa, the upholders of holy practice”. Mitra and Varuṇa are *prāṇa* (out-breathing) and *udāna* (in-breathing). He commends him to *prāṇa* and *udāna*. They approve of his consecration. Approved by them he is consecrated.
</details>


## २९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

आ᳘वित्ते द्या᳘वापृथिवी᳘ विश्व᳘शम्भू इ᳘ति त᳘देनमाभ्यां द्या᳘वापृथिवी᳘भ्यामा᳘वेदयति य᳘योरिदꣳ स᳘र्वम᳘धि ते᳘ अस्मा एत᳘ꣳ᳘ सवम᳘नुमन्यते ता᳘भ्याम᳘नुमतस्सूयते ॥ २९ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Present are the Heaven and Earth, the all-propitious”. He thus commends him to Heaven and Earth on whom all these rest. They approve of this consecration. Approved by them he is consecrated.
</details>


## ३०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

आ᳘वित्ता᳘दितिरुरु᳘शर्मे᳘तीयं वा अ᳘दितिस्त᳘देनमस्यै᳘ पृथिव्या आ᳘वेदयति᳘ सास्मा एत᳘ꣳ᳘ सवम᳘नुमन्यते तया᳘नुमतस्सूयते स य᳘देनमेता᳘भ्यो देव᳘ताभ्य आवेदयत्येता᳘भिर᳘नुमतस्सूयते त᳘स्मादा᳘विदो ना᳘म त᳘स्माद्वै᳘नमावि᳘दो वाचयति ॥ ३० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“Present is Aditi, of Supreme Happiness”. Aditi is indeed this earth. He thus commends him to this Earth. She approves of his consecration. Approved by her, he is consecrated. Since he commends him to these deities and approved by these deities, he is consecrated. These (*mantras*) are called *Avids*. Therefore he makes him recite the *Avid* chants. (Fourth Brāhmaṇa Ends) (Chapter Two Ends)
</details>
