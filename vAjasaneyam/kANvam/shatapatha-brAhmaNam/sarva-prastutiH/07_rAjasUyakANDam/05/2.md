## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वा᳘ एते᳘नाभिषेचनी᳘येनेष्ट्वा᳘ संवत्सरं के᳘शान्न᳘ वपते स यत्के᳘शान्न व᳘पते र᳘सो वा᳘ अयं᳘ वी᳘र्यं ते᳘ज इद᳘मपाꣳ सं᳘भृतं ये᳘नैनमेत᳘दभिषिञ्च᳘ति त᳘स्याभि᳘षिक्तस्यै᳘कोऽभिषेकः के᳘शान्प्रथमा᳘न्गच्छति स य᳘द्ध के᳘शान्वपेत जिह्मा᳘ꣳ हैताꣳ श्रि᳘यं वि᳘नाशयेद्युदुह्यात्तस्मात्के᳘शान्न᳘ वपते ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Having performed the consecration ceremony, he does not shave his hair for a year. Why he does not shave is because that collected essence of waters with which he was bathed (at the consecration) is vigour and glory. When he is consecrated, the flow (of waters) first reaches the hairs. If he is to shave, it renders that (vigour) fickle and destroys that glory and make (him) bereft of energy. Therefore he does not shave the hair.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वै᳘ संवत्सरं न वपते संवत्सर᳘सम्मितो᳘ हि᳘ व्रतचर्या त᳘स्मात्संवत्सरं न᳘ वपते᳘ऽथ ख᳘लु संवत्सर᳘ एते᳘न व्रतविसर्जनी᳘येन य᳘जते केशवपनी᳘येन स᳘ ए᳘षोऽपयोग इ᳘ति स्तो᳘मः ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He does not shave for a year. The observance of vow is one year duration. Hence he does not shave for a year. After one year, he performs the offering to mark the termination of the vow; namely the discarding of the hairs. That is the day meant for stoma (praising).
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘स्यैकविꣳशं᳘ प्रातस्सवन᳘ꣳ᳘ सप्तदशं मा᳘ध्यन्दिनꣳ स᳘वनं पञ्चदशं᳘ तृतीयसवन᳘ꣳ स᳘होक्थ्यै᳘स्सह᳘ षोळशि᳘ना सह रा᳘त्र्या त्रिवृद्रा᳘थान्तरस्सन्धि᳘रेव᳘मेष᳘ क्लृप्तो᳘ भवति स य᳘देव᳘मेष᳘ क्लृप्तो भ᳘वत्येतं वा᳘ एष᳘ गच्छति य᳘ एष त᳘पति यो᳘ राजसू᳘येन य᳘जते त᳘देतां गतिं᳘ गत्वा᳘ योऽस्यैष᳘ जितः᳘ स्वर्गो᳘ लोक᳘ इमां᳘ प्रतिष्ठां᳘ प्रत्यवै᳘ति या᳘वदस्येह᳘ मानुषमा᳘युस्त᳘स्यैतत् ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

There are twenty-one stomas in the morning savana, seventeen in the midday savana, twelve in the third savana. With three Uktha stotras, one Ṣoḍāśī and twelve Rātriparyāya stotras. At the end of the previous night, there is one Trivṛt to be sung in Rathantara which is called Sandhistotra (sung at the dawn of the day). This should be connected with Trivṛt stoma. Thus they are fixed. With that which is thus ordained, the one who performs Rājasūya goes to that one who burns (Sun). Having gone there and conquered the heavenly world, he comes back to this (earth, i.e. the Rathantara); which is his firm settlement and remains there for the duration of the human life-span.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

एकविꣳशं᳘ प्रातस्सवनं᳘ भ᳘वत्येकविꣳशो वा᳘ एव य᳘ एष त᳘पति द्वा᳘दश वै मा᳘साः प᳘ञ्चर्त᳘वस्त्र᳘य इमे᳘ लोका᳘ असा᳘वादित्य᳘ एकविꣳशस्स᳘ एते᳘नैकविꣳशे᳘न प्रातः सवने᳘नैत᳘स्मादेकविꣳशाद᳘पयुङ्क्ते ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The morning savana has twenty-one stotras. This one who burns yonder (Sun) is twenty-onefold. There are twelve months, five seasons and three worlds and Āditya is the twenty-first. By this morning savana with twenty-one stotras, he gets separated from the twenty-first one (i.e. Āditya).
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ सप्तदशं᳘ प्रत्य᳘वैति सप्तदशा᳘त्पञ्चदशं᳘ पञ्चदशा᳘त्त्रिवृ᳘तमियं वै᳘ त्रिवृ᳘दियं᳘ प्रतिष्ठा त᳘दस्या᳘मेव᳘ प्रतिष्ठा᳘यामन्ततः प्र᳘तितिष्ठति ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He returns to (the midday savana of) seventeen stotras and from seventeen- fold, he reaches fifteen-fold and from fifteen, he moves to the Trivṛt. This (earth) is Trivṛt and it is the firm settlement. Thus he gets established in this.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

रथन्तरं᳘ पृष्ठं᳘ भवतीयं वै᳘ रथन्त᳘रमियं᳘ प्रतिष्ठा त᳘दस्या᳘मेव᳘ प्रतिष्ठा᳘यामन्ततः प्र᳘तितिष्ठति ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Rathantara indeed is Pṛṣṭha (stotra) of this (sacrifice). Rathantara is this (earth) which is well-established. So he ultimately gets established in this firm earth.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ एते᳘नेष्ट्वा नि᳘वर्तयत एव के᳘शान्न᳘ वपते य᳘द्ध व᳘पेतैता᳘ꣳ᳘ ह श्रि᳘यं जिह्मां वि᳘नाशयेद्व्युदु᳘ह्यात्त᳘दात्म᳘न्ये᳘वैतच्छ्रि᳘यं नि᳘युङ्क्त आ᳘त्मन्ये᳘वैतच्छ्रि᳘यं धत्ते त᳘स्मान्नि᳘वर्तयत एव के᳘शान्न᳘ वपते ॥ ७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He only clips his hair after making this offering. He should not shave the hair. He who shaves, makes that vigour fickle and make that glory to fall and get uprooted. By (cutting and not shaving), he retains that glory connected to himself. He carries that prosperity in himself. Therefore he should only clip and not shave the hair.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थोपान᳘द्भ्यामेव᳘ चरत्युपान᳘द्भ्यामे᳘वासन्दी᳘मासन्द्या᳘ वोपान᳘हावुपान᳘द्भ्यां वा या᳘नं या᳘नाद्वोपान᳘हौ सर्व᳘ꣳ᳘ हेष᳘ उप᳘र्युपरि भ᳘वति यो᳘ राजसू᳘येन य᳘जते ॥ ८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He gets down with the shoes (put on); and with shoes (he mounts) the stool and gets down from the stool with shoes on; or he mounts the chariot with shoes on or gets down from the chariot into the shoes. Because he who performs Rājasūya is above everything. (Second Brāhmaṇa Ends)
</details>
