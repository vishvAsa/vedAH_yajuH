<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Having performed the consecration ceremony, he does not shave his hair for a year. Why he does not shave is because that collected essence of waters with which he was bathed (at the consecration) is vigour and glory. When he is consecrated, the flow (of waters) first reaches the hairs. If he is to shave, it renders that (vigour) fickle and destroys that glory and make (him) bereft of energy. Therefore he does not shave the hair.
</details>
