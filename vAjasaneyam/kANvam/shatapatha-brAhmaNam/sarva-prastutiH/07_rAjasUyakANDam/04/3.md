## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वै᳘ प्रयु᳘जाꣳ हवि᳘र्भिर्य᳘जते स य᳘त्प्रयु᳘जाꣳ हवि᳘र्भिर्य᳘जत ऋतू᳘न्है᳘वैत᳘त्सुषुवाणः प्र᳘युङ्क्ते त᳘ एनमृत᳘वो युक्ता᳘ वहन्ति ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He performs the oblations of *prayuja (offering associated with *prayuja chants). The reason why he performs the oblations of *prayuja is that the anointed (king) thereby yokes the seasons. Those seasons thus yoked carry him.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ता᳘नि वै द्वा᳘दश भवन्ति द्वा᳘दश वै मासास्संवत्सर᳘स्य त᳘स्माद्द्वा᳘दश भवन्ति तै᳘र्मासि᳘ मासि य᳘जते मासि᳘ मास्यै᳘वैत᳘दृतू᳘न्युङ्क्ते त᳘ एनमृत᳘वो युक्ता᳘ वहन्ति ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They are twelve (offerings). A year has twelve months. So they are twelve. By them he offers each month. He yokes the seasons monthly. Thus those seasons get yoked.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दु ना᳘द्रियेत शम्यापराव्याध᳘ꣳ᳘ शम्यापराव्याध᳘मेव᳘ षड्भि᳘र्हवि᳘र्भिः प्राग्य᳘जमान ए᳘ति त᳘दृतून्प्रा᳘चो युङ्क्ते त᳘ एनमृत᳘वो युक्ताः प्रा᳘ञ्चं वह᳘न्ति शम्यापराव्याध᳘ꣳ᳘ शम्यापराव्याध᳘मेव᳘ षड्भि᳘र्हवि᳘र्भिः पुनर्य᳘जमान एति त᳘दृतू᳘न्प्रती᳘चो युङ्क्ते त᳘ एनमृत᳘वो युक्ताः पु᳘नरा᳘वहन्ति ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That (above injunction of offering each month) should not be taken note of. Moving eastwards, he offers six of them, each at the distance of the yoke-pin's throw from the other. (Moving from the Āhavanīya, he performs six offerings at six different spots each at a distance measured by throwing the yoke-pin). Thus he yokes the seasons at the eastern side. Thus yoked, the seasons carry him eastwards. Then he moves (in reverse) each time at a distance measured by the throw of the yoke-pin; that yokes the seasons at the western side. Thus yoked, these seasons carry him back.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दु ना᳘द्रियेत समान᳘बर्हीꣳष्येव᳘ पूर्वेद्युः ष᳘ड्ढवी᳘ꣳषि नि᳘र्वपेत्त᳘दृतून्प्रा᳘चो युङ्क्ते त᳘ एनमृत᳘वो युक्ताः प्रा᳘ञ्चं वहन्ति ते᳘षां पूर्वाग्निवाहा᳘नां द्वा᳘वनड्वा᳘हौ द᳘क्षिणा व᳘हन्ति᳘ ह्येन᳘ मृत᳘वो युक्ता᳘ व᳘हत्यनड्वान्युक्त᳘स्समान᳘बर्हीꣳष्येव श्वो᳘भूते ष᳘ड्ढवी᳘ꣳ᳘षि नि᳘र्वपेत्त᳘दृतू᳘न्प्रती᳘चो युङ्क्ते त᳘ एनमृत᳘वो युक्ताः पु᳘नरा᳘वहन्ति ते᳘षां पूर्वाग्निवाहा᳘नां द्वा᳘वनड्वा᳘हौ द᳘क्षिणा व᳘हन्ति᳘ ह्येनमृत᳘वो युक्ता᳘ वहत्यनड्वा᳘न्युक्तः᳘ ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This (procedure) is not to be followed. On the previous day, he should prepare the six oblations on a common *barhis* (on a single fire). That yokes the seasons eastwards. Thus yoked, these seasons carry him eastwards. Two of the oxen drawing the original fire are the *dakṣiṇā* for them. Yoked are the seasons. An ox yoked, draws (the carriage). On the next day, on a common *barhis* (on a single fire), he should prepare six oblations. That yokes the seasons westwards. Thus yoked, these seasons carry him back. For them, two of the oxen drawing the original fire are the *dakṣiṇā*. Yoked are the seasons. An oxen yoked, draws (the carriage).
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते य᳘ ऊर्ध्वाः᳘ शैशिरादा᳘ प्रावृषो ये᳘ ते षण्मा᳘सास्ते᳘षाꣳ रूपं पू᳘र्वाणि ष᳘ड्ढवी᳘ꣳ᳘षि य᳘था ग्रै᳘ष्माये᳘ति प्रा᳘ङ् याया अ᳘थ य᳘ ऊर्ध्वाः᳘ प्रावृष आ᳘शैशिराद्ये᳘ ते षण्मा᳘सास्ते᳘षाꣳ रूपमु᳘त्तराणि ष᳘ड्ढवी᳘ꣳ᳘षि य᳘था वा᳘र्षिकाये᳘ति प्रत्य᳘ङ्यास्त᳘द्ध स्मैत᳘त्पु᳘रैव᳘ कुरुपञ्चाला᳘ आहुर्ऋत᳘वो वा᳘ अस्मा᳘न्युक्ता वहन्ती᳘त्यृतू᳘न्वा वयं युक्तान᳘नुचराम इ᳘त्येते᳘ ह स्म त᳘दाहु राजसूययाजि᳘नो हि ते᳘षाꣳ रा᳘जान आसुः ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The first six oblations represent the six months from Śiśira (winter) onwards upto Prāvṛt (rainy season) proceeding towards east for the summer. The next six oblations represent the six months from Prāvṛt (rainy season) onwards upto śiśira (winter) proceeds towards west for the rainy season. This is what the Kuru-Pañcālas said once, "The seasons yoked, carry us; we follow the seasons yoked". They referred to this (procedure in this sacrifice) because their (Kuru-Pañcāla's) kings were those who performed Rājasūya. (Third Brāhmaṇa Ends)
</details>
