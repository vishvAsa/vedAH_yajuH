स᳓ य᳓देते᳓न य᳓जते य᳓देवै᳙नमदो᳓ दि᳓शः समारोह᳓यति य᳓दृतू᳓न्य᳓त्स्तो᳓मान्य᳓च्छ᳓न्दांसि य᳓त्सा᳓मानि त᳓त एवै᳙नमेते᳓न नि᳓ष्क्रीणाति ।

Now as to why he (priest) makes him offer these (pañca-bila oblations) is this—because he had made him (the Yajamāna) ascend these regions, these seasons, these stomas, these metres and these sāmans, therefrom he now redeems him (Yajamāna) by these offerings. If he were not to perfrom these offerings, then he would become arrogant or die or he may collapse.
