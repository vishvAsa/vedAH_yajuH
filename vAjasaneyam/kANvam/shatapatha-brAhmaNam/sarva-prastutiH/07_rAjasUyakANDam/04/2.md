## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

आग्ने᳘योऽष्टाकपालो भवति तं᳘ पुर᳘स्तादा᳘सादयत्यैन्द्र ए᳘कादशकपालो भवति तं᳘ दक्षिणत आ᳘सादयति सौ᳘म्यो वा चरु᳘र्वैश्वदेव᳘श्चरु᳘र्भवति तं᳘ पश्चादा᳘सादयति मैत्रावरुणी᳘ पय᳘स्या भवति ता᳘मुत्तरत आ᳘सादयति बार्हस्पत्य᳘श्चरु᳘र्भवति तं म᳘ध्य आ᳘सादयति ते᳘षां वा᳘ एते᳘षां पञ्चाना᳘ꣳ᳘ हवि᳘षां प᳘ञ्च बि᳘लानि स य᳘देते᳘षां पञ्चाना᳘ꣳ᳘ हवि᳘षां प᳘ञ्च बि᳘लानि त᳘स्माच्चरुः प᳘ञ्चबिल इत्या᳘ख्यायते ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The purodāśa for Agni is in eight potsherds. He places it on the eastern side. There is a purodāśa in eleven potsherds for Indra or a caru for Soma. He places it on the southern side. There is a caru for Viśvedevas which, he keeps on the Western side. There is a curd preparation for Mitra-Varuṇa which he places on the northern side. There is a caru for Bṛhaspati. He keeps it in the middle. For these five oblations, there are five bilas (holes or conclave depressions to hold the dishes). Therefore (the collection of five offerings) is called pañcabila (five-holed).
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स य᳘देते᳘न य᳘जते य᳘दे᳘वैनमदो दि᳘शः समारोह᳘यति य᳘दृतून्यत्स्तो᳘मान्यच्छ᳘न्दाꣳसि यत्सा᳘मानि त᳘त ए᳘वैनमेते᳘न नि᳘ष्क्रीणाति य᳘द्धैते᳘न न य᳘जेत दृ᳘ष्येद्वा᳘ह म्रिये᳘त वा प्र᳘ वा पतेत्त᳘स्मादेते᳘न यजते᳘ ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now as to why he (priest) makes him offer these (pañca-bila oblations) is this—because he had made him (the Yajamāna) ascend these regions, these seasons, these stomas, these metres and these sāmans, therefrom he now redeems him (Yajamāna) by these offerings. If he were not to perfrom these offerings, then he would become arrogant or die or he may collapse. Therefore he should make these offerings.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

सोऽने᳘नाग्नेये᳘नाष्टा᳘कपालेन पुरोळा᳘शेन प्र᳘चरति य᳘दे᳘वैनं प्रा᳘चीं दि᳘शꣳ समारोह᳘यति य᳘दृतून्यत्स्तो᳘मान्यच्छ᳘न्दाꣳसि यत्सा᳘मानि त᳘त ए᳘वैनमेते᳘न नि᳘ष्क्रीणाति स᳘ हु᳘त्वैवा᳘हुतिꣳ सꣳस्रवं᳘ बार्हस्पत्येऽवनयति ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then proceeds with this purodāśa, in eight potsherds for Agni. With this, it was he who made him ascend the eastern region; those seasons, those stomas, those metres and those sāmans (corresponding to it). From those, he redeems him (Yajamāna). After making the offering, he pours the residue in the caru for Bṛhaspati.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाने᳘नैन्द्रेणै᳘कादशकपालेन पुरोळा᳘शेन प्र᳘चरति सौम्ये᳘न वा चरु᳘णा स᳘ यतरे᳘णैत᳘योश्च᳘रति य᳘दे᳘वैनं द᳘क्षिणां दि᳘शꣳ समारोह᳘यति य᳘दृतून्यत्स्तो᳘मान्यच्छ᳘न्दाꣳसि यत्सा᳘मानि त᳘त ए᳘वैनमेते᳘न नि᳘ष्क्रीणाति स᳘ हु᳘त्वैवा᳘हुतिꣳ सꣳस्रवं᳘ बार्हस्पत्ये᳘ऽवनयति ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then proceeds with the Purodāśa in eleven potsherds for Indra or with a caru for Soma. By either of these had he made him ascend the southern region, those seasons, those stomas, those metres and those Sāmans (corresponding to it). From those he redeems him (Yajamāna). After making the offering he pours the residue in the caru for Bṛhaspati.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाने᳘न वैश्वदेवे᳘न चरु᳘णा प्र᳘चरति य᳘दे᳘वैनं प्रती᳘चीं दि᳘शꣳ समारोह᳘यति य᳘दृतून्यत्स्तो᳘मान्यच्छ᳘न्दाꣳसि यत्सा᳘मानि त᳘त ए᳘वैनमेते᳘न नि᳘ष्क्रीणाति स᳘ हु᳘त्वैवा᳘हुतिꣳ सꣳस्रवं᳘ बार्हस्पत्ये᳘ऽवनयति ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then proceeds with the caru for Viśvedevas. By that had he made him ascend the western region; those seasons, those stomas, those metres and those sāmans (corresponding to it). From those, he redeems him (Yajamāna). After making the offering, he pours the residue in the caru for Bṛhaspati.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थान᳘या मैत्रावरुण्या᳘ पय᳘स्यया प्र᳘चरति य᳘दे᳘वैनमु᳘दीचीं दि᳘शꣳ समारोह᳘यति य᳘दृतून्यत्स्तो᳘मान्यच्छ᳘न्दाꣳसि यत्सा᳘मानि त᳘त ए᳘वैनमेते᳘न नि᳘ष्क्रीणाति स᳘ हु᳘त्वैवा᳘हुतिꣳ सꣳस्रावं᳘ बार्हस्पत्ये᳘ऽवनयति ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then proceeds with the purodāśa in eleven potsherds for Indra or with a caru for Soma. By either of these had he made him ascend the southern region, those seasons, those stomas, those metres and those sāmans (corresponding to it). From those he redeemds him (the Yajamāna). After making the offering, he pours the residue in the caru for Bṛhaspati.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाने᳘न बार्हस्पत्ये᳘न चरु᳘णा प्र᳘चरति य᳘दे᳘वैनमूर्ध्वां दि᳘शꣳ समारोह᳘यति य᳘दृतून्यत्स्तो᳘मान्यच्छ᳘न्दाꣳसि यत्सा᳘मानि त᳘त ए᳘वैनमेते᳘न नि᳘ष्क्रीणाति स य᳘द्धुत्वा᳘ हुत्वा सꣳस्रावं᳘ बार्हस्पत्ये᳘ऽवनयति स᳘र्वाभ्य ए᳘वास्मा एत᳘द्दि᳘ग्भ्योऽन्ना᳘द्यं बलि᳘ꣳ हारयति तꣳ स᳘र्वाभ्यो दि᳘ग्भ्योऽन्ना᳘द्यं बलि᳘रुपसमै᳘ति ॥ ७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then proceeds with the caru for Bṛhaspati. By that he had made him ascend the upper region. Each time after making the offerings, he pours the residue into the caru for Bṛhaspati. Thereby he causes food to be bestowed upon him (Yajamāna) and hence food offerings reach him (the king).
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तस्या᳘स्याग्नेय᳘स्याष्टा᳘कपालस्य हि᳘रण्यं द᳘क्षिणाग्नेयो᳘ ह्येष᳘ यज्ञो᳘ऽग्नेर्हि रे᳘तो हि᳘रण्यं त᳘स्मादस्य हि᳘रण्यं द᳘क्षिणा त᳘दग्नी᳘धे ददात्यग्नि᳘र्ह्येष᳘ निदा᳘नेन य᳘दग्नीत्त᳘स्मात्त᳘दग्नी᳘धे ददाति ॥ ८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For this (purodāśa offering) in eight potsherds for Agni, gold is dakṣiṇā. For this sacrifice belongs to Agni and gold is Agni's seed. Therefore gold is dakṣiṇā for this and it has to be given to Agnīdh, for he (Agnīdh) is virtually Agni himself. So it is to be given to Agnīdh.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘था᳘स्यैन्द्रस्यै᳘कादशकपालस्यर्षभो द᳘क्षिणा त᳘द्धि पशु᳘ष्वैन्द्र᳘ꣳ᳘ रूपं य᳘दृषभ᳘स्य य᳘द्यु सौम्य᳘श्चरुः᳘ स्याद्वभ्रुगौर्द᳘क्षिणा स्यात्तद्धि᳘ पशु᳘षु सौम्य᳘ꣳ᳘ रूपं य᳘द्बभ्रुगौस्स᳘ यतर᳘ एत᳘योर्भ᳘वति तं᳘ ब्रह्म᳘णे ददाति ब्रह्मा हि᳘ दक्षिणतो᳘ यज्ञ᳘स्याभिगोप्ता ॥ ९ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For this (purodāśa offering) in eleven potsherds for Indra, a bull is dakṣiṇā. For it (bull) is the Indra's form among animals. If it is the caru of Soma, then a brown ox is the dakṣiṇā. Because it (brown ox) is the Soma's form among animals. Whichever of these happens to be (the dakṣiṇā), it is to be given to Brahmā. Because Brahmā is the protector of the sacrifice from the south.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थास्य वैश्वदेव᳘स्य चरोः पृ᳘षन्गौर्द᳘क्षिणा भूमा वा᳘ एत᳘द्रूपा᳘णां यत्पृ᳘षतो गोर्भू᳘मो वै वि᳘श्वेदेवास्त᳘स्मादस्य पृ᳘षन्गौर्द᳘क्षिणा तꣳ होत्रे᳘ ददाति भू᳘मो हि हो᳘ता ॥ १० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For this (offering) of caru for Viśvedevas, a spotted cow is dakṣiṇā. That which is a spotted cow, has abundance of colours. Viśvedevas form a multitude. Therefore for this, a spotted cow is dakṣiṇā. That is to be given to Hotṛ, because Hotṛ means abundance.
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थास्या᳘ मैत्रावरुण्याः᳘ पय᳘स्याया वशा द᳘क्षिणा सा हि᳘ मैत्रावरुणी य᳘द्वशा य᳘दि वशां न᳘ विन्देद᳘पि या᳘प्रवीता सा᳘ स्याद᳘प्रवीतो हि᳘ वशा भ᳘वति ता᳘मध्वर्य᳘वे ददाति प्राणोदानौ वै᳘ मित्राव᳘रुणौ प्राणोदाना᳘ उ वा᳘ अध्व᳘र्यु त᳘स्मात्ता᳘मद्ध्वर्य᳘वे ददाति ॥ ११ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For (the offering of) the curd preparation for Mitrā-Varuṇa, a sterile cow is dakṣiṇa. She, sterile cow is sacred to Mitrā-Varuṇa. If one does not get a sterile cow, it can be a cow which has not yet conceived. For a sterile cow is also not impregnated. She should be given to Adhvaryu. Mitrā and Varuṇa are the prāṇa (out-breathing) and udāna (in-breathing). The (two) Adhvaryus are prāṇa and udāna. Therefore she should be given to Adhvaryu.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थास्य᳘ बार्हस्पत्य᳘स्य चरो᳘श्शितिपृष्ठो गौर्द᳘क्षिणैषा᳘ ह्यूर्ध्वा दिग्बृ᳘हस्प᳘तेस्त᳘देष᳘ उप᳘रिष्टादर्यम्णः प᳘न्थास्त᳘स्मादस्य शितिपृष्ठो गौर्द᳘क्षिणा तं᳘ ब्रह्म᳘णे ददाति बृ᳘हस्प᳘तिर्वै᳘ देवा᳘नां ब्र᳘ह्मैव᳘मु वा᳘ एष᳘ एत᳘स्य ब्रह्मा त᳘स्मात्तं᳘ ब्रह्म᳘णे ददाति ॥ १२ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For (the offering of) the caru for Bṛhaspati, a white-backed bullock is dakṣiṇā. This upper region belongs to Bṛhaspati and above that is the path of Aryaman (Sun). Therefore, white-backed bullock is dakṣiṇā for this. That is to be given to Brahmā. Bṛhaspati is the Brahmā for the gods. In the same way this one is Brahmā for this (sacrificer). Therefore it should be given to Brahmā.
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते᳘न हा᳘प्येते᳘न विष्ठाव्रा᳘जोऽन्ना᳘द्यकामो य᳘जेत स᳘र्वाभ्य ए᳘वास्मा एत᳘द्दि᳘ग्भ्योऽन्ना᳘द्यं बलि᳘ꣳ᳘ हारयति तꣳ स᳘र्वाभ्यो दि᳘ग्भ्योऽन्ना᳘द्यं बलि᳘रुपसमै᳘ति स स᳘र्वासां दिशा᳘मन्नादो᳘ भवति ॥ १३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

With these (oblations), one desirous of food can offer (in a non-sacrificial context) at a cow-shed heaped with cow-dung. By those, food offerings from all regions are caused to reach him. From all regions food offerings converge on him. He verily becomes the eater of food from all quarters. (Second Brāhmaṇa Ends)
</details>
