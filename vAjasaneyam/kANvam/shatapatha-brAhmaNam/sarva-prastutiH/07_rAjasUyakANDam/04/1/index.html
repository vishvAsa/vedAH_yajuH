He chased it along with these deities—with Savitr, the impeller; with Sarasvatī, the speech; with Tvaṣṭr, the forms (of being) with Pūṣan, the cattle; with Indra himself (Yajamāna); with Bṛhaspati, the Brahman; with Varuņa, the might; with Agni, the glow; with Soma, the king; with Viṣņu, the tenth deity.
