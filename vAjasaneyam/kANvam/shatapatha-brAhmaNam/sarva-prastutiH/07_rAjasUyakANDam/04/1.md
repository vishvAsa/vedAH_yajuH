## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

व᳘रुणाद्धाभिषिषिचानाद्भर्गो᳘ऽपचक्राम वी᳘र्यं वै भ᳘र्ग एष᳘ एव᳘ यज्ञो वि᳘ष्णुः श᳘श्वद्ध य᳘दे᳘वैतत्ते᳘जो वी᳘र्यꣳ र᳘स ए᳘षोऽपाꣳ सं᳘भृत᳘स्त᳘द्धै᳘वास्य श᳘श्वद्भ᳘र्गं नि᳘र्जघान ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now when Varuņa was being consecrated, his lustre departed from him. Lustre being vigour, it is the same as Viṣņu, the sacrifice. The essence of the collected waters (used for consecration) perhaps washed away that glory, that vigour. That perhaps drove away that lustre.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘मु वै व᳘रुणो᳘ऽन्ववैच्छत्त᳘मेता᳘भिर्देव᳘ताभिरनु स᳘मसर्पत्सवित्रा᳘ प्रसवित्रा स᳘रस्वत्या वाचा त्व᳘ष्ट्रा रूपैः᳘ पूष्णा᳘ पशु᳘भिरि᳘न्द्रेणास्मै । बृ᳘हस्प᳘तिना ब्र᳘ह्मणा व᳘रुणेनौ᳘जसाग्नि᳘ना ते᳘जसा सो᳘मेन रा᳘ज्ञा । वि᳘ष्णुना देव᳘तया दश᳘म्यैनम᳘न्वविन्दत्त᳘मनुविद्य त᳘मात्म᳘न्यकुरुत त᳘मात्म᳘न्यधत्त त᳘थो वा᳘ एनमेष᳘ एत᳘द्विष्णु᳘नैव᳘ देव᳘तया दशम्या᳘नुविन्दति त᳘मनुवि᳘द्य त᳘मात्म᳘नि कुरुते त᳘मात्म᳘नि धत्ते ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Varuņa went in search of it. He chased it along with these deities—with Savitr, the impeller; with Sarasvatī, the speech; with Tvaṣṭr, the forms (of being) with Pūṣan, the cattle; with Indra himself (Yajamāna); with Bṛhaspati, the Brahman; with Varuņa, the might; with Agni, the glow; with Soma, the king; with Viṣņu, the tenth deity. He found it (lustre) and having found it, he put it unto himself, he owned it in himself. Therefore he finds it out only with Viṣņu, the tenth deity. Having found it, he puts it unto himself, he owns it to himself.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वा᳘ एष᳘ दशमे᳘ऽहनि प्र᳘सुतो भवति दशम्या᳘ ह्येनं देव᳘तयान्व᳘विन्दत्त᳘स्माद्दशपे᳘यो द᳘श दशै᳘कैकं चमस᳘मनुप्र᳘सृप्ता भवन्ति त᳘स्मा᳘न्न्वेव᳘ दशपे᳘यो द᳘श सोमपा᳘न्पितामहा᳘ननुसंख्या᳘य प्र᳘सर्पति स हा᳘स्य सोमपीथ᳘मश्नुते यो द᳘श सोमपा᳘न्पितामहा᳘ननुसं᳘चष्टे ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This one (the king) becomes consecrated on the tenth day. Because he gains it by the tenth deity, it is called daśapeya. Each time, ten men steal forth each graha. Thence too it is called daśapeya. He steals forth enumerating ten of his forefathers who had drunk soma. It is thus that he obtains soma-draught for himself, because he recalls ten forefathers who had drunk soma.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दु त᳘द्ब्राह्मणस्त्री᳘नेव चतु᳘रः प᳘ञ्च षड्व्या᳘वे᳘वैता᳘ एव᳘ देव᳘ता अनुसंख्या᳘य प्र᳘सर्पदेता᳘भिर्वा᳘ एनं देव᳘ताभिर्व᳘रुणोऽनुस᳘मसर्पदेता᳘भिरेनमाप्नोत्ता᳘भि रे᳘वास्यैष᳘ एत᳘त्सोमपीथ᳘मश्नुते त᳘स्मादेता᳘ एव᳘ देव᳘ता अनुसंख्या᳘य प्र᳘सर्पेत् ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

If he is a Brahmin, he might (recall) only three, four, five or six (of his forefathers and in the case of a Kṣatriya much less number). So he should only enumerate these deities while he steals forth. Only with these deities, Varuņa chased and found it. This one (king) also obtains the status of a soma pīthī (drinker of soma draught) only by (enumerating) these deities. Therefore he sould steal forth enumerating the names of these deities.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स यत्रा᳘दोभिषेचनी᳘यस्योदवसानी᳘ये᳘ष्ट्या य᳘जते त᳘देत᳘ꣳ᳘ सावित्रं द्वा᳘दशकपालं वाष्टा᳘कपालं वा पुरोळा᳘शं नि᳘र्वपति सविता वै᳘ देवा᳘नां प्रसविता᳘ सवितृ᳘प्रसूतो वा᳘ एनं तद्व᳘रुणोऽनुस᳘मसर्पत्त᳘थो वा᳘ एनमेष᳘ एत᳘त्सवितृ᳘प्रसूत ए᳘वानुस᳘ꣳ᳘ सर्पति तदे᳘कमेव᳘ पुण्ड᳘रीकं प्र᳘यच्छति सा द᳘क्षिणा ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now when he offers the completing oblation related to the consecration, he prepares the purodāśa for Savitr in twelve potsherds or in eighteen potsherds. Savitr is the impeller of gods. Impelled by Savitr, Varuņa chased it. Therefore this one (Yajamāna) steals forth behind it only on being impelled by Savitr. He offers only one (golden) lotus and that is the dakṣiņā.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ श्वो᳘भूते᳘ सारस्वतं᳘ चरुं नि᳘र्वपति वाग्वै स᳘रस्वती वाचा वा᳘ एनं तद्व᳘रुणोऽनुस᳘मसर्पत्त᳘थो वा᳘ एनमेष᳘ एतद्वा᳘चै᳘वानुस᳘ꣳ᳘ सर्पति तदे᳘कमेव᳘ पुण्ड᳘रीकं प्र᳘यच्छति ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

On the next day, he prepares a caru for Sarasvatī. Sarasvatī is indeed speech. It is with speech that Varuņa chased it. Therefore this one (king) steals forth with speech. He gives only one (golden) lotus.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ श्वो᳘भूते᳘ त्वाष्ट्रं द᳘शकपालं पुरोळा᳘शं नि᳘र्वपति त्व᳘ष्ट्रा वा᳘ एनं त᳘द्रूपैर्व᳘रुणोऽनु स᳘मसर्पत्त᳘थो वा᳘ एनमेष᳘ एतत्त्व᳘ष्ट्रैव᳘ रूपै᳘रनुस᳘ꣳ᳘सर्पति तदे᳘कमेव᳘ पुण्ड᳘रीकं प्र᳘यच्छति ॥ ७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

On the next day, he prepares a purodāśa for Tvaṣṭr in ten potsherds. With Tvaṣṭr, the forms (of the beings), Varuṇa chased it. Therefore this one (Yajamāna) steals forth behind it, with those forms of Tvaṣṭr. He gives only one (golden) lotus.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ श्वो᳘भूते᳘ पौष्णं᳘ चरुं नि᳘र्वपति पश᳘वो वै᳘ पूषा᳘ पशु᳘भिर्वा᳘ एनं तद्व᳘रुणोऽनुस᳘मसर्पत्त᳘थो वा᳘ एनमेष᳘ एत᳘त्पशु᳘भिरे᳘वानुस᳘ꣳ᳘सर्पति तदे᳘कमेव᳘ पुण्ड᳘रीकं प्र᳘यच्छति ॥ ८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

On the next day, he prepares a caru for Pūṣan. Pūṣan is the cattle. It is with the cattle that Varuṇa chased it. Therefore this one (Yajamāna) steals forth behind it with the cattle. He gives only one (golden) lotus.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ श्वो᳘भूत᳘ ऐन्द्रमे᳘कादशकपालं पुरोळा᳘शं नि᳘र्वपतीन्द्रियं वै᳘ वी᳘र्यमि᳘न्द्र इन्द्रिये᳘ण वा᳘ एनं त᳘द्वीर्येण व᳘रुणोऽनुस᳘मसर्पत्त᳘थो वा᳘ एनमेष᳘ एत᳘दिन्द्रिये᳘णैव᳘ वी᳘र्येणानुस᳘ꣳ᳘सर्पति तदे᳘कमे᳘व पुण्ड᳘रीकं प्र᳘यच्छति ॥ ९ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

On the next day, he prepares a purodāśa for Indra in eleven potsherds. Indra is energy, the vigour. It is with energy, that is vigour, Varuṇa chased it. Therefore this one (Yajamāna) steals forth behind him with energy, that is vigour. He gives only one (golden) lotus.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ श्वो᳘भूते᳘ बार्हस्पत्यं᳘ चरुं नि᳘र्वपति ब्र᳘ह्म वै बृ᳘हस्प᳘तिर्ब्र᳘ह्मणा वा᳘ एनं तद्व᳘रुणोऽनु स᳘मसर्पत्त᳘थो वा᳘ एनमेष᳘ एतद्ब्र᳘ह्मणै᳘वानुस᳘ꣳ᳘सर्पति तदे᳘कमेव᳘ पुण्ड᳘रीकं प्र᳘यच्छति ॥ १० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

On the next day he prepares a caru for Bṛhaspati. Bṛhaspati is Brahman. It is with the Brahman that Varuṇa chased it. Therefore this one (Yajamāna) steals forth behind it with Brahman. He gives only one (golden) lotus.
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ श्वो᳘भूते᳘ वारुणं᳘ यवम᳘यं चरुं नि᳘र्वपति स ये᳘न हौ᳘जसेद᳘मिमाः᳘ प्रजा व᳘रुणो गृह्णा᳘ति ते᳘नै᳘वैनं तदो᳘जसा व᳘रुणोऽनुस᳘मसर्पत्त᳘थो वा᳘ एनमेष᳘ एतत्ते᳘नैवौ᳘जसानुस᳘ꣳ᳘सर्पति तदे᳘कमेव पुण्ड᳘रीकं प्र᳘यच्छति ॥ ११ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

On the next day, he prepares a caru made of barley for Varuṇa. That glory with which Varuṇa seized these creatures, with that glory, Varuṇa chased it. Therefore this one (Yajamāna) steals forth behind it with that glory. He gives only one (golden) lotus.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ता᳘सु सप्तस्वि᳘ष्टिषु सप्त᳘ पुण्ड᳘रीकाणि प्र᳘यच्छत्यु᳘प प᳘ञ्च प्र᳘यच्छति ता᳘मस्मै द्वा᳘दशपुण्डरीकाꣳ स्र᳘जं कृत्वा᳘पिनह्यति सा᳘ दीक्षा तद्या᳘नि बि᳘सानि ता᳘न्यस्याः᳘ पृथिव्या᳘ रूपं व᳘धका अन्त᳘रिक्षस्य पुण्ड᳘रीकाणि न᳘क्षत्राणां त᳘द्दिवो रूपं यन्न᳘क्षत्राणामेभि᳘रेवैनं त᳘ल्लोकै᳘र्दीक्षयति स᳘र्वं वा᳘ इमे᳘ लोकास्स᳘र्वेणै᳘वैनं त᳘द्दीक्षयत्य᳘थ यद्द्वा᳘दश पुण्डरीका भ᳘वन्ति द्वा᳘दश वै मा᳘सास्संवत्सर᳘स्य स᳘र्वमु वै᳘ संवत्सरस्स᳘र्वेणै᳘वैनं त᳘द्दीक्षयति ॥ १२ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

In those seven oblations, he gives seven (golden) lotuses. In addition, he gives five (lotuses). Then he makes a garland of twelve lotuses and makes him (king) wear it. What fibres there are, they are a forṁ of this earth. What seed-stalks there are, they are a form of the aerial region. The lotus flowers are the stars and they are a form of the sky which is of the stars. He initiates him with these (three) regions. These regions encompass all. So he initiates him with all. Now, as to why there are twelve lotuses—a year has twelve months. The year is all and he is initiated with all.
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘त्रा᳘दोऽभिषेचनी᳘यं क्रीण᳘न्ति त᳘त्क्रीत्वै᳘वैनं द्वे᳘धोपन᳘ह्यति द्वे᳘धोपन᳘ह्य प᳘रिवहन्ति प᳘र्युह्यान्यतरम्महा᳘त्रासाद᳘यत्यन्यतरं᳘ पुरो᳘हितस्य वा गृहे᳘षु ब्रह्म᳘णो वा हृत्वा नि᳘दधति स यं तं᳘ पुरो᳘हितस्य वा गृहे᳘षु ब्रह्म᳘णो वा हृत्वा᳘ निद᳘धति तम᳘त्राहृ᳘त्यासन्द्या᳘मासा᳘द्य प्रपा᳘द्यातिथ्ये᳘न च᳘रन्त्यातिथ्ये᳘न चरि᳘त्वोपस᳘द्भिः ॥ १३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

When he buys (the soma) for consecration, he divides it into two. Having divided it into two, he drives him around (on the cart). Having taken round, he places one half here (on the throne-seat) and the other half, he takes and keeps it in the house of the priest or the Brahman. That he brings here and places it on the throne-seat. Having placed, he proceeds with offering honour due to the guest. After proceeding with honouring the guest, he performs the upasads.
</details>


## १४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तद्धै᳘के हवी᳘ꣳ᳘ष्ये᳘वैता᳘नि नि᳘र्वपन्त्याग्नेय᳘मष्टा᳘कपालं पु᳘रोळा᳘शꣳ सौम्यं᳘ चरुं᳘ वैष्णवं त्रि᳘कपालं वा चरुं᳘ वा त᳘दु त᳘था न᳘ कुर्याद्ध्व᳘लति वै स यो᳘ यज्ञपथादेत्ये᳘त्यु वा᳘ एष᳘ यज्ञपथाद्य᳘ एवं᳘ करोति त᳘स्मादा᳘ज्यहवि᳘ष एव᳘ स्युः ॥ १४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Some prepare only these offerings—The purodāśa in eight potsherds for Agni; the caru for Soma; and (purodāsa) in three potsherds or a caru for Viṣṇu. That should not be done so. Because he who swerves from the path of the sacrifice, faulters. He who does this way is swerving from the path of the sacrifice. So let there be ghee oblations only.
</details>


## १५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वा᳘ अग्निं᳘ यजति ते᳘जो वा᳘ अग्निस्ते᳘जसा वा᳘ एनं तद्व᳘रुणोऽनुस᳘मसर्पत्त᳘थो वा᳘ एनमेष᳘ एतत्ते᳘जसै᳘वानुस᳘ꣳ᳘सर्पति ॥ १५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now he offers to Agni. Agni is glow. It is with glow that Varuṇa chased it. Therefore this one (Yajamāna) steals forth behind it with glow.
</details>


## १६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ सो᳘मं यजति क्षत्रं वै सो᳘मः क्षत्रे᳘ण वा᳘ एनं तद्व᳘रुणोऽनुस᳘मसर्पत्त᳘थो वा᳘ एनमेष᳘ एत᳘त्क्षत्रे᳘णै᳘वानुस᳘ꣳ᳘सर्पति ॥ १६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he offers to Soma. Soma is Kṣatra (valour). It is with valour that Varuṇa chased it. Therefore this one (Yajamāna) steals forth behind it with valour.
</details>


## १७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ वि᳘ष्णुं यजति वि᳘ष्णुना वा᳘ एनं त᳘द्देव᳘तया दशम्या व᳘रुणो᳘ऽन्वविन्दत्त᳘मनुवि᳘द्य त᳘मात्म᳘न्यकुरुत त᳘मात्म᳘न्यधत्त त᳘थो वा᳘ एनमेष᳘ एतद्वि᳘ष्णुनैव᳘ देव᳘तया दशम्या᳘नुविन्दति त᳘मनुवि᳘द्य त᳘मात्म᳘नि कुरुते त᳘मात्म᳘नि धत्ते ॥ १७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he offers to Viṣṇu. It is with Viṣṇu, that tenth deity, Varuṇa found it. Having found it, he put it into himself; he owned it to himself. Therefore this one (Yajamāna) with Viṣṇu, the tenth deity, finds it out and having found it, puts it unto himself, owns it to himself.
</details>


## १८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वा᳘ एष᳘ सप्तदशो᳘ भवति सप्तदशो हि᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिः प्रजा᳘पति᳘र्ह्येष त᳘स्मादेष᳘ सप्तदशो᳘ भवति ॥ १८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus it becomes seventeenfold (stoma) for, Prajāpati is seventeenfold. This sacrifice is indeed Prajāpati. Hence it becomes seventeenfold.
</details>


## १९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तद्द्वा᳘दश प्रथम᳘गर्भाः पष्ठौ᳘ह्यस्ता᳘ ब्रह्मणो द᳘क्षिणा द्वा᳘दश वै मा᳘सास्संवत्सर᳘स्य संवत्सरो᳘ यज्ञः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिस्त᳘स्माद्द्वा᳘दश प्रथम᳘गर्भाः पष्ठौ᳘ह्यो द᳘क्षिणाः ॥ १९ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Twelve cows in their first pregnancy are the dakṣiṇā for the Brahmin. A year has twelve months. The year is sacrifice i.e. Prajāpati. Therefore twelve cows in their first pregnancy are the dakṣiṇā.
</details>


## २०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ता ग᳘र्भैः सह च᳘तुर्विꣳशतिर्भवन्ति च᳘तुर्विꣳशतिर्वा᳘ अर्धमासा᳘स्संवत्सर᳘स्य संवत्सरो᳘ यज्ञः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिस्त᳘स्माद्ग᳘र्भैस्सह च᳘तुर्विꣳशतिर्भवन्ति ॥ २० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They (the cows) with their twelve embryo calves become twenty-four. There are twenty-four half months for a year. The year is sacrifice i.e. Prajāpati. Therefore with embryo they become twenty-four.
</details>


## २१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

हिरण्म᳘यीꣳ स्र᳘जमुद्गात्रे᳘ रुक्मꣳ होत्रे᳘ हिरण्म᳘यान्याकाशा᳘नध्वर्यू᳘भ्याम᳘श्वं प्रस्तोत्रे᳘ वशां᳘ मैत्रावरुणा᳘यर्षभं᳘ ब्राह्मणाच्छꣳसि᳘ने वा᳘ससी नेष्टृ᳘पोतृ᳘भ्यामन्य᳘तोयुक्तं यवाचित᳘मच्छावाका᳘यानड्वा᳘हमग्नी᳘धे ता वा᳘ एता द्वा᳘दश वा त्र᳘योदश वा द᳘क्षिणा ददाति द्वा᳘दश वा वै त्र᳘योदश वा संवत्सर᳘स्य मा᳘सास्संवत्सरो᳘ यज्ञः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिस्त᳘देतं᳘ प्रत्य᳘क्षं यज्ञं᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिमात्वा तं᳘ परिगृ᳘ह्य त᳘मात्म᳘नि कुरुते त᳘मात्म᳘नि धत्ते ॥ २१ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He gives the golden garland (as dakṣiṇā) to the Udgātṛ; a gold plate to the Hotṛ; two golden mirrors to the two Adhvaryu; a horse to Prastotṛ; a sterile cow to Maitra-Varuṇa; a bull to the Brahmaṇācchaṁsin; garment to Neṣṭṛ and Potā, each; to the Acchāvāka, a cart laden with barley and yoked (by an ox) on one side and an ox to Agnīdhra. Thus he gives twelve or thirteen (items as) dakṣiṇā. A year has twelve VII.4.2.5 ] or thirteen months and the sacrifice, Prajāpati is year. Thus he directly obtains the 119 sacrifice, i.e. Prajāpati, having acquired him, he puts him into himself and owns him to himself. (First Brāhmaṇa Ends)
</details>
