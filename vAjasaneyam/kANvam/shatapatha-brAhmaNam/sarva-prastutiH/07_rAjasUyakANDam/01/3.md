<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (3/19 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)</summary>

स᳓ वा᳓ एत᳓माग्नावैष्णव᳓मे᳓कादशकपालं पुरोळा᳓शं नि᳓र्वपत्यैन्द्रावैष्णवं᳓ चरुं᳓ वैष्णवं᳓ त्रि᳓कपालं वा [[चरुं|चरुं᳓]] वै᳓ते᳓न [[श्वो᳓भूते᳓|श्वो᳓भूते]] त्रिषयुक्ते᳓न [[य᳓जते|यजते]] ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He prepares this puroḍāśa in eight potsherds for Agni and Viṣṇu, a caru for Indra and Viṣṇu and a puroḍāśa in three potsherds or a caru for Viṣṇu. With this he performs on the following day that Triṣamyukta offering. He offers the (Triṣamyukta) because the gods came to men by means of this. In like manner this one (Yajamāna, here, the king) now thereby comes closer to men (thinking) 'Let me consecrate after becoming accompanied by men'.
</details>

Why he makes this (third Triṣamyukta) is because the gods obtained glory by this. Now, why this (puroḍāśa) is of Agni and Soma—Agni is the giver and these creatures are surrounding the giver. Soma is glory. So whosoever is the giver and whomsoever as giver, these creatures surround, him he propitiates by this and he thus propitated gives him glory; he makes him glorious. He thus consecrates himself after becoming glorious. Now, as to why the caru is for Agni and Soma—the Yajamāna is Indra and Soma is glory. So that very glory which Agni, the giver bestows on him, he gets united with that and owns it unto himself.
