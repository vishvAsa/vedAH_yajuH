Agni told him (Prajāpati) — “Let me come near to you”. The latter asked “With what (would you come near)?”. Agni said, “With cattle”. Prajāpati agreed and said “do so”. What he (Agni) meant was ‘with cattle brick or Paśvistakā’. Because the Paśvistakā is the same as Dūrveṣṭaka (a stalk of Dūrvā grass). The Dūrvā brick is placed close to the naturally perforated (clay) brick, so that it is not separated from the plants, from the cattle. Of course, Agni leaves no gap. (That is how) he (Agni) came near.
