</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Holding it (the lump of clay) in his hand, he addresses the animals. This is because, the gods while collecting it (Agni) first of all imparted vigour into them and in the like manner does he (the Yajamāna) now, while collecting him (Agni), first impart vigour into the cattle.
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

In as much as the horse, among those that go (towards the hole), goes first, the Kṣatriya who goes ahead is followed by the other three castes. In as much as, while returning (towards the Āhavanīya), among those that return, the he-goat first, the Brāhmaṇa who goes in advance is followed by the other three castes. And since the ass does not lead either while going or while returning, never do Brāhmaṇa and Kṣatriya follow the Vaiśya and the Śūdra.
</details>
