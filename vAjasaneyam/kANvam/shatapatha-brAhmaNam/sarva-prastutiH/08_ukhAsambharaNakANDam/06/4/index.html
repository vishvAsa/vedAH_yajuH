अ᳓थ [[विष्णुक्रमा᳓न्क्रान्त्वा|विष्णुक्रमा᳓न्क्रान्त्वा᳓]] वात्सप्रे᳓णोपस्था᳓या᳓स्तमित आदित्ये᳓ भ᳓स्मैव᳓ [[प्रथम᳓मुपत्ये᳓द्वा᳓|प्रथम᳓मु᳓पत्ये᳓द्वा᳓]] एनमेते᳓ना᳓न्नेन [[प्री᳓णाति|प्रीणाति]] ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
C R Swaminathan

Having then stridden the Viṣṇukramas and stood in attendance (before the fire) with Vatsapra chantings, when the Sun has set, he first removes the ashes. For, just then (a while ago), he has gratified him (Agni) with samidhs. The rejected part of that consumed food, which has settled down, is the ash. This (Agni) is separated from that (ash) and now that he is rid of the dirt, he infuses speech into him.
