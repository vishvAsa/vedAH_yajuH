<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This earth has indeed become the foundation (bhū); so it is called Bhūmi. It was spread out (prath) and hence became Pṛthivī. The earth considered itself complete (perfect) and sang (gā) and in as much as it sang, it is Gāyatrī. They also say that it was Agni on the back of her (earth) which considered itself complete (perfect) and sang and hence became Gāyatra. That is why, indeed, if one feels complete (perfect) he sings merily or rejoices by songs. (First Brāhmaṇa Ends)
