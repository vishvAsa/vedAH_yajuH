One ought to build him (the fire-altar) in one year and in (that) one year he should recite (the Veda before the preceptor). Some say it should be for two years. The impregnation into Uṣas was done for one year and the boy was born in one year thereafter. So the building up (of the fire-altar) should be for two years and recitation also for two years. (No) Let him build up in one year and for (that) one year let him recite. Because, the same seed which is implanted is brought forth. It then lies changing and growing; hence let him build for one year. When built up, he gives a name to that Agni by which he wards off evil from him. He calls him by a wonderful name saying, “Thou art brilliant”, for Agni is all brilliance. (Third Brāhmaṇa Ends) (Chapter One Ends)
