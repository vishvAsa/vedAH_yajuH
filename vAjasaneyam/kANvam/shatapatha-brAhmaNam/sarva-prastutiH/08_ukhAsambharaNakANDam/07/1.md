## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

रुक्मं᳘ प्रतिमु᳘च्य बिभर्त्ति सत्य᳘ꣳ᳘ हैतद्य᳘द्रुक्मः᳘ सत्यं वा᳘ एतं य᳘न्तुमर्हति सत्ये᳘नैतं᳘ देवा᳘ अबिभरुः सत्ये᳘नै᳘वैन᳘मेत᳘द्बिभर्त्ति ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The Yajamāna, wearing a golden ornament (around his neck) carries the ukhā. This rukma or gold is reality (never perishing even in extreme heat) and because it is reality, it deserves to carry this (ukhā). Because gods carried it by means of reality (truth) and so by means of reality he carries it now.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तद्यत्त᳘त्सत्यम᳘सौ स᳘ आदित्यः स᳘ हिरण्म᳘यो भवति ज्यो᳘तिर्वै हि᳘रण्यं ज्यो᳘तिरे᳘षोऽमृ᳘तꣳहि᳘रण्यममृ᳘तमेष᳘ परिमण्डलो᳘ भवति परिमण्डलो᳘ ह्येष ए᳘कविꣳशति निर्बाध᳘ एकविꣳशो ह्येष᳘ बहि᳘ष्ठां निर्बाधं᳘ बिभर्त्ति रश्म᳘यो वा᳘ एत᳘स्य निर्बाधा᳘ बाह्यत उ वा᳘ एत᳘स्य रश्मयः ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The gold which is reality, is yonder Sun and he (Sun) is golden. For, his light is gold and this light is immortality and gold is immortality (indestructible). This (gold) forms a circle, for, he (the Sun) is round. It (the gold chain) has twenty-one knobs and he (the Sun) is the twenty-first. He wears it (in such a way) so that the knobs (project) outwards. The rays are the knobs of the Sun and the rays indeed, are (projecting) outwards.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

य᳘द्वेव᳘ रुक्मं᳘ प्रतिमु᳘च्य बिभ᳘र्त्त्यसौ वा᳘ आ᳘दित्य᳘ एष᳘ रुक्मो᳘ नो हैत᳘मग्निं᳘ मनु᳘ष्यो मनु᳘ष्यरूपे᳘ण य᳘न्तुमर्हत्येते᳘नैव᳘ रूपे᳘णैत᳘द्रूपं बिभर्त्ति ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And again why he wears a gold piece for carrying it (the ukhā) is because that gold is yonder Sun and man in his human form is unable to bear that fire. It is only in this divine form (solar form got by wearing gold) that he carries it (Agni) of divine form.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

य᳘द्वेव᳘ रुक्मं प्रतिमु᳘च्य बिभ᳘र्त्ति रे᳘तो वा᳘ इद᳘ꣳ᳘ सिक्त᳘मय᳘मग्निस्ते᳘जो वी᳘र्यꣳ रु᳘क्मोऽस्मिꣳस्तद्रे᳘तसि ते᳘जो वी᳘र्यं दधाति ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And again why he wears gold for carrying it (the ukhā) is because the fire is the semen deposited here and this (the gold) is brilliance and vitality. Thereby he imparts brilliance and vitality into that semen.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

य᳘द्वेव᳘ रुक्मं᳘ प्रतिमु᳘च्य बिभ᳘र्त्त्येतद्वै᳘ देवा᳘ अबिभयुर्यद्वै᳘न इम᳘मिह र᳘क्षाꣳसि नाष्ट्रा न᳘ हन्युरि᳘ति त᳘स्मा ए᳘तमन्तिका᳘द्गोप्ता᳘रमकुर्वन्नमु᳘मेवादित्य᳘मसौ वा᳘ आदित्य᳘ एष᳘ रुक्मस्त᳘थै᳘वास्मा अय᳘मेत᳘मन्तिका᳘द्गोप्तारं करोति ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And again why he wears gold and carries it (ukhā)? Once the gods became apprehensive lest the terrible Rakṣasas may not destroy here that fire of theirs. They made, by his side, yonder Sun to be his (Agni's) protector. Now, indeed, yonder Sun is what is gold here. In like manner, he (Yajamāna) makes that (gold necklace) by his side as protector.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

कृष्णाजिने नि᳘ष्यूतो भवति यज्ञो वै᳘` कृष्णाजिनं᳘ यज्ञो वा᳘ एतं य᳘न्तुमर्हति यज्ञे᳘नैतं᳘ देवा᳘ अबिभरुर्य᳘ज्ञे᳘नैवैनमेत᳘द्बिभर्त्ति लो᳘मतश्छ᳘न्दाꣳसि वै लो᳘मानि छ᳘न्दाꣳसि वा᳘ ह ते य᳘न्तुम᳘र्हन्ति छ᳘न्दोभिरे᳘तं देवा᳘ अबिभरुश्छ᳘न्दोभिरे᳘वैनमेत᳘द्बिभर्त्ति ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It gets packed in a black antelope's skin, for, the black antelope's skin is the sacrifice and sacrifice, indeed deserves to hold it. Because the gods carried it by means of sacrifice. So he now holds it with the sacrifice. (He carries him) with the hairs (inside), for the hairs are the metres and the metres deserve to hold him. Because the gods carried it by means of the meter. So he holds it by means of the hairs.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अभि᳘ शुक्ला᳘णि च कृष्णा᳘नि च लो᳘मानि नि᳘ष्पूतो भवति ऋक्साम᳘योर्हैते᳘ रूपे᳘ ऋक्सामे वा᳘ एतं य᳘न्तुमर्हतः ऋक्सामा᳘भ्यामेतं᳘ देवा᳘ अबिभरुर्ऋक्सामा᳘भ्यामे᳘वैनमेत᳘द्बिभर्त्ति शाणो᳘ रुक्मपाश᳘स्त्रिवृत्त᳘स्योक्तो बन्धुः ॥ ७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He gets packed by the white and black hairs (of the skin), for, these two are the forms of Ṛks and Sāmans and the Ṛks and Sāmans deserve to hold him. Because the gods carried him by means of Ṛks and Sāmans. So he carries them by means of Ṛks and Sāmans. The hemp-made string of the gold piece is threefold and its significance has been told.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘मुपरि नाभि᳘ भर्त्यसौ वा᳘ आदित्य᳘ एष᳘ रुक्म᳘ उपरि वा᳘भ्यु वा᳘ एषः ॥ ८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He wears it (the gold piece) above the navel. This gold piece is yonder Sun. He (the Sun) is above the navel (Sun in the form of Jāṭhara Agni is situated above the navel).
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

य᳘द्वे᳘वोपरिनाभि अ᳘वाग्वै ना᳘भेरे᳘तः प्र᳘जातिस्ते᳘जो वी᳘र्यं रुक्मो ने᳘न्मे रे᳘तः प्र᳘जातिं ते᳘जो वी᳘र्यं रुक्मः᳘ प्रद᳘हादि᳘ति ॥ ९ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And again, why above the navel is because below the navel is the semen (the source of semen); the procreating region. In order that the gold may impart bri- lliance and vitality to it (the procreating region) (it is now above the navel).
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

य᳘द्वे᳘वोपरिना᳘भ्येतद्वै᳘ पशोर्मे᳘ध्यतरं य᳘दुपरिनाभि पु᳘रीषसꣳ हिततरंयदवाङ्ना᳘भेस्तद्य᳘देव᳘ पशोर्मे᳘ध्यतरं ते᳘नैनमेत᳘द्बिभर्ति ॥ १० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And again, why above the navel is because the region above the navel is the purer part in an animal. The region below is in greater proximity to the foul- smelling part. So this (gold) is worn on that part which is purer in an animal.
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

य᳘दे᳘वोपरिनाभि यद्वै᳘ प्राण᳘स्यामृ᳘तमूर्ध्वं तन्ना᳘भेरूर्ध्वैः᳘ प्राणैरु᳘च्चरत्य᳘थ यन्म᳘र्त्यं प᳘राक्तन्ना᳘भिम᳘त्येति तद्य᳘देव᳘ प्राण᳘स्यामृ᳘तं त᳘देनमेत᳘दभिसंपादयति ते᳘नैनमेत᳘द्बिभर्ति ॥ ११ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And again why above the navel is because that which is the immortal part of the vital air is upward-moving. It (the immortal part) is moving up along the vital airs above the navel. That which is the mortal part of the vital air is downward and it moves below the navel. He thus makes him (the sacrificer) obtain the immortal part of the vital air (by wearing the gold above navel) and then carries it (the fire).
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थैनमासन्द्या᳘ बिभर्तीयं वा᳘ आस᳘न्द्यस्या᳘ꣳ᳘ हीदꣳ स᳘र्वमा᳘सन्नमियं वा᳘ एतं य᳘न्तुमर्हत्येनयैतं देवा अबिभरुरन᳘यै᳘वैनमेत᳘द्बिभर्ति ॥ १२ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then carries him (fire) on a seat—the seat is doubtless, this earth. For, on her (earth) every thing here is settled (āsanna) and she (the earth) indeed deserves to sustain him (Agni) because gods carried him by means of her (earth) and therefore he carries him on her (the earth i.e. seat).
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

औ᳘दुम्बरी भवत्यूर्ग्वै र᳘स उदुम्ब᳘र ऊ᳘र्जे᳘वैनमेतद्र᳘सेन बिभर्त्य᳘थो स᳘र्व एते व᳘नस्प᳘तयो य᳘दुदुम्ब᳘रः स᳘र्वे वा᳘ एतं व᳘नस्प᳘तयो य᳘न्तु मर्हन्ति स᳘र्वैरेतं व᳘नस्प᳘तिभिर्द्देवा᳘ अबिभरुः स᳘र्वैरे᳘वैनमेतद्व᳘नस्प᳘तीभिर्बिभर्ति ॥ १३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It (the seat) is made of Udumbara wood, for, the Udumbara tree is vitality and life-sap and by means of vitality and life-sap, he thus carries him (Agni). Moreover, that Udumbara is (representing) all the trees here (on earth) and thus all the trees are engaged in sustaining that (fire). The gods carried him by means of all the trees. So he (Yajamāna) carries him by means of all the trees.
</details>


## १४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

प्रादेशमा᳘त्र्यूर्ध्वा᳘ भवति प्रादेशमात्रो वै ग᳘र्भो वि᳘ष्णुर्यो᳘निरेषा ग᳘र्भसंमितां तद्यो᳘निं करोत्यरत्निमात्री᳘ तिर᳘श्ची बाहूर्वा᳘ अरत्नि᳘र्बाहु᳘ नो वै᳘ वी᳘र्यं क्रियते वी᳘र्यसंमितैव त᳘द्भवति वी᳘र्यं वा᳘ एतं य᳘न्तुमर्हति वी᳘र्येणैतं᳘ देवा᳘ अबि᳘भरुर्वीर्येणै᳘वैनमेत᳘द्बिभर्ति ॥ १४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

It (the seat) is a span-high, for Viṣṇu, as an embryo was span-size. This (seat) is a womb and the womb is made proportionate to the size of the embryo. It (the seat) is a cubit-broad, for a cubit is the length of the fore-arm and strength is exercised by the fore-arm. So it is proportionate to the strength. Strength, it is, that deserves to carry him (Agni), because it is by means of strength that gods carried him. So this one (Yajamāna) carries him by means of strength.
</details>


## १५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

च᳘तुः स्रक्तयः पा᳘दा भ᳘वन्ति च᳘तुः स्रक्तीन्यनू᳘च्यानि च᳘तस्रो वै दि᳘शो दि᳘शो वा᳘ एतं य᳘न्तुमर्हन्ति दिग्भि᳘रेतं᳘ देवा᳘ अबिभरुर्दिग्भि᳘रे᳘वैनमेत᳘द्बिभर्ति मौञ्जी᳘भी र᳘ज्जुभि᳘र्व्यूता भवति त्रिवृ᳘द्धिस्त᳘स्योक्तो ब᳘न्धुर्मृदा᳘ दिग्धा तस्यो ए᳘वोक्तो᳘ऽथ अ᳘नतिदाहाय ॥ १५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The feet (of the seat) make it four-cornered and the four corners are the entrances; for there are four directions. The directions, indeed, deserve to carry him (fire) because the gods, carried him by means of the directions (quarters). So he carries him by means of the quarters. It (the seat) is wound round by cords made of reed grass. The cords are threefold (three-threaded) and the significance of this has been told. It is smeared over with clay and this has also been explained. It is also to prevent over-heating.
</details>


## १६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थैनꣳशि᳘क्येन बिभर्त्तीमे वै᳘ लोका᳘ ए᳘षोऽग्निर्द्दि᳘शः शि᳘क्यं दिग्भि᳘र्हीमे᳘ लोकाः᳘ शक्नुव᳘न्ति स्थातुं य᳘च्छक्नुव᳘न्ति त᳘स्माच्छि᳘क्यं दिग्भि᳘रे᳘वैनमेतद्बिभर्त्ति ष᳘ळुद्यामं भवति ष᳘ढ्ढि दि᳘शो मौञ्जं᳘ त्रिवृत्त᳘स्योक्तो बन्धुर्मृदा᳘ दिग्धं तस्यो ए᳘वोक्तो᳘ऽथो अ᳘नतिदाहाय तस्या᳘प एव᳘ प्रति᳘ष्ठाऽप्सु हीमे᳘ लोका प्र᳘तिष्ठिता आदित्य᳘ आस᳘ञ्जनमा᳘दित्ये᳘ हीमे᳘ लोका᳘ दिग्भिरा᳘सक्ताः स यो᳘ हैत᳘देवं वे᳘देतै᳘नैव᳘ रूपे᳘णैत᳘द्रूपं᳘ बिभर्त्ति ॥ १६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now he carries him (the fire on the stool) by means of a sling (loop made of coir). Agni is these worlds and the quarters are the sling. It is by means of these quarters that these worlds are enabled to remain steady. So they are enabled (to stand steady or *śaknuvanti*) it is called *śikya* (sling). He thus carries him by the regions. So it (the sling) has six cords; for, there are also six regions. (Each) cord is threefold (made of winding three strings) and its significance has been told. It (the sling) is smeared with clay and this has also been explained. But it is also to prevent getting burnt (due to over-heating). For that sling, waters are the support (here the word *pratiṣṭhā* or support or foundation refers to the base of the sling which is matlike bottom formation made of cords). Because it is in the waters that these worlds are established. The Sun is the top (beam from which the sling is suspended) and these worlds are tied to (linked to) the Sun by means of these quarters—whoever thus knows this, is carrying this form (of Agni) by this form (of a sling made of waters, quarters and Sun).
</details>


## १७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

य᳘द्वे᳘वैनꣳशि᳘क्येन बिभ᳘र्त्ति संवत्सर᳘ एषोऽग्नि᳘र्ऋत᳘वः शिक्यमृतु᳘भिर्हि᳘ संवत्सरः᳘ शक्नो᳘ति स्था᳘तुं य᳘च्छक्नो᳘ति त᳘स्माच्छि᳘क्यमृतु᳘भिरेवैनमेत᳘द्बिभर्त्ति षळुद्यामं भवति ष᳘ळृत᳘वस्त᳘स्याहोरात्रे᳘ एव᳘ प्रति᳘ष्ठाहोरात्रयो᳘र्ह्यय᳘ꣳ᳘ संवत्सरः प्र᳘तिष्ठितश्चन्द्र᳘मा आस᳘ञ्जनं चन्द्र᳘मसि᳘ ह्यय᳘ꣳ᳘ संवत्सर᳘ ऋतु᳘भिरा᳘सक्तः स यो᳘ हैत᳘देवं वेदेतैनै रूपे᳘णैत᳘द्रूपं बिभर्त्ति त᳘स्य ह वा᳘ एष᳘ संवत्सर᳘भृतो᳘ भवति य᳘ एवं वेद᳘ संवत्सरो᳘पासितो हैव त᳘स्य भवति य᳘ एवं न वेदे᳘त्यधिदेवतम् ॥ १७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And again why carries him by means of a sling is because this fire is the year. The seasons are the sling. It is by means of the seasons that the year is enabled (*śaknoti*) to stand and hence it (the seasons) is called *śikya* (sling). So he carries it by means of the seasons. There are six cords corresponding to six seasons. For the year, the day and the night are the support (the matlike bottom of the sling); because the year is established on days and nights. The moon is the top (beam) wherefrom the sling (the year) is suspended. This year is suspended (or linked to the moon) by these seasons. He who knows thus, carries this form of Agni (*ukhyāgni*) by this form (of the year i.e. sling). For him (who knows thus), the *ukhyāgni* becomes one that is carried for a whole year (even if it is carried for a duration less than a year). But whoever does not know thus, for him, the *ukhyāgni* becomes one that is (only) worshipped for a year (and not the one that is carried for a year). Thus it applies to the deities.
</details>


## १८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाध्यात्ममा᳘त्मै᳘वाग्निः᳘ प्राणाः᳘ शि᳘क्यं प्राणै᳘र्ह्यय᳘मात्मा᳘ शक्नो᳘ति स्थातुं यच्छक्नो᳘ति त᳘स्माच्छि᳘क्यं प्राणै᳘रे᳘वैनमेत᳘द्बिभर्त्ति ष᳘ळुद्यामं भवति षढ्ढि᳘ प्रा᳘णास्त᳘स्य म᳘न एव᳘ प्रतिष्ठा म᳘नसि᳘ ह्यय᳘मात्मा प्र᳘तिष्ठितोऽन्नमास᳘ञ्जनमन्ने᳘ ह्यय᳘मात्मा᳘ प्राणैरा᳘सक्तः स यो᳘ हैत᳘देवं वे᳘देतै᳘नैव᳘ रूपे᳘णैत᳘द्रूपं᳘ बिभर्त्ति ॥ १८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now (let us deal with) as applied to self (body). Agni, doubtless is the self. The vital airs are the sling. It is indeed by means of the vital airs that the body is enabled (*śaknoti*) to sustain and hence it is *śikya* (sling). He thus carries him (*ukhyāgni*) by means of vital airs. It is having six cords for, there are six vital airs. Mind, doubtless is the support (matlike bottom of the sling); because this self is established in the mind. Food is the top (beam) from which the sling i.e. the vital airs are suspended). This self is linked to the food by these vital airs. He who knows thus, is carrying this form of *ukhyāgni* by means of this form (of a sling made of *prāṇas*, mind and food).
</details>


## १९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थैनमुखा᳘या बिभर्त्तीमे वै लोका᳘ उ᳘खेमे वा᳘ एतं᳘ लोका य᳘न्तुमर्हन्त्येभि᳘रेतं᳘ लोकै᳘र्देवा᳘ अबिभरु᳘रेभिरे᳘वैनमेत᳘ल्लोकै᳘र्बिर्त्ति ॥ १९ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now he carried him (fire) by means of the *ukhā* The *ukhā* is these worlds and these worlds, indeed, deserve to carry him. The gods carried him by means of these worlds. So he carries him only by means of these worlds.
</details>


## २०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

सा य᳘दुखा ना᳘मैतद्वै᳘ देवा᳘ एते᳘न क᳘र्मणैत᳘यावृ᳘तेमाँ᳘ल्लोकानु᳘दखनन्य᳘दुद᳘खनꣳस्तस्मादु᳘त्खोत्खा᳘ ह वै ता᳘मुखेत्या᳘चक्षते परो᳘क्षं परो᳘क्षकामा हि᳘ देवाः ॥ २० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And now why it is called *ukhā* — because at that time, the gods dug out (produced) these worlds by doing this act and by this technique. Since they dug out, it became (*utkha*), and therefore it is called *ukhā* in a mystic style. For, gods like to be concealed (in mystic appellations).
</details>


## २१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तद्वा᳘ उखे᳘ति द्वे अक्ष᳘रे द्विपाद्य᳘जमानो य᳘जमानोऽग्निर्या᳘वानग्निर्या᳘वत्यस्य मा᳘त्रा ता᳘वतै᳘वैनमेत᳘द्बिभर्ति सो एव᳘ कुम्भी सा᳘ स्थाली तत्ष᳘ट् ष᳘ळृतवः संवत्स᳘रः संवत्सरोऽग्निर्या᳘वानग्निर्या᳘वत्यस्य मा᳘त्रा ता᳘वत्त᳘द्भवति ॥ २१ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now (the word) *ukhā* has two syllables; the Yajamāna is two-footed and the Yajamāna is Agni; as great as Agni is, as great as is his size, by so much, he thus carries him. That same *ukhā* is a vessel (*kumbhī*), it is a cauldron (*sthālī*). This makes six syllables (two each of the three). Six seasons (make) a year and the year is Agni; as great as Agni is, as great as is his size, so great does this become.
</details>


## २२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थैनमि᳘ण्ड्वाभ्यां प᳘रिगृह्णात्य᳘सौ वा᳘ आदित्य᳘ ए᳘षोऽग्नि᳘रहो᳘रात्रे᳘ इण्ड्वेऽमुं त᳘दादित्य᳘महोरात्राभ्यां प᳘रिगृह्णा᳘ति त᳘स्मादे᳘षोऽहोरात्रा᳘भ्यां प᳘रिगृहीतः ॥ २२ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He then holds him (the *ukhyāgni*) with two *Indvas* (pads made of grass to hold a hot plate). For, he (Agni), is yonder Sun and the two pads are the day and the night. So he holds that Sun by means of the day and the night. So he (Agni) becomes one held by day and night.
</details>


## २३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

यद्वे᳘वैनमि᳘ण्ड्वाभ्यां प᳘रिगृह्णा᳘त्यसौ वा᳘ आदित्य᳘ ए᳘षोऽग्नि᳘रिमा᳘ उ लोका᳘वि᳘ण्ड्वेऽमुं त᳘दादित्य᳘माभ्यां᳘ लोका᳘भ्यां प᳘रिगृह्णाति त᳘स्मादेष᳘ आभ्यां᳘ लोका᳘भ्यां प᳘रिगृहीतः परिमण्डले᳘ भवतः परिमण्डलौ हीमौ᳘ लोकौ᳘ मौञ्जे᳘ त्रिवृ᳘ती त᳘स्योक्तो ब᳘न्धुर्मृ᳘दा दिग्धे त᳘स्योए᳘वोक्तोऽथोऽन᳘तिदाहाय ॥ २३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

And again why he holds him with a pair of pads is because this Agni is yonder Sun and these worlds are the pair of pads, he thereby holds that Sun with these two worlds. So he becomes held by these two worlds. They form a circle because these two worlds are circular (halves). (These two pads) are made of triple stringed reed grass and the significance of this has been told. These two are plastered with clay and that too has been explained. It is also to prevent them from being burnt (due to over-heat).
</details>


## २४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अथा᳘तः संप᳘देवा᳘सन्दी᳘ चोखा᳘ च शि᳘क्यं च रुक्मपाश᳘श्चाग्नि᳘श्च रुक्मश्च तत्ष᳘ट् ष᳘ळृतवः संवत्स᳘रः संवत्स᳘रोऽग्निर्या᳘वानग्निर्या᳘वत्यस्य मा᳘त्रा ता᳘वत्त᳘द्भवती᳘ण्ड्वे त᳘दष्टा᳘वष्टा᳘क्षरा गायत्री᳘ गाय᳘त्रोऽग्निर्या᳘वानग्निर्या᳘वत्यस्य मा᳘त्रा ता᳘वत्त᳘द्भवत्य᳘थ स᳘र्वसं᳘यच्चत्वा᳘रः पा᳘दाश्चत्वा᳘र्यनू᳘च्यानि शि᳘क्यं च रुक्मपाश᳘श्च य᳘दु कि᳘ञ्च र᳘ज्जव्यꣳशि᳘क्यं त᳘दनू᳘खाग्नी᳘ रुक्मस्तत्त्र᳘योदश त्रयोदशमासाः संवत्सरः᳘ संवत्स᳘रोऽग्निर्यावानग्निर्या᳘वत्यस्य मा᳘त्रा तावत्त᳘द्भवति ॥ २४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now then (let us make) the count. The seat, the *ukhā*, the sling, the string with gold piece, the fire, the gold—(all these) make for six. There are six seasons for the year and Agni is the year; as great as Agni is, as great as is his size, so great does that become. With the pair of holding pads, it becomes eight and Gāyatrī has eight syllables. Agni is Gāyatra; as great as Agni is, as great as is his size, so great does this become. Now the total counting — four feet (of the seat); four planks (of the seat); the sling (*sikya*); the binding cords; whatever other accessories made of strings; and after the sling (comes) the *ukhā* which together with the fire and the gold piece make thirteen (items). Thirteen months (make) a year and the year is Agni; as great as Agni is, as great as is his size, by so much it becomes. (First Brāhmaṇa Ends).
</details>
