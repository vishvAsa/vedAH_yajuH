Now then (let us make) the count. The seat, the *ukhā*, the sling, the string with gold piece, the fire, the gold—(all these) make for six. There are six seasons for the year and Agni is the year; as great as Agni is, as great as is his size, so great does that become. With the pair of holding pads, it becomes eight and Gāyatrī has eight syllables. Agni is Gāyatra; as great as Agni is, as great as is his size, so great does this become. Thirteen months (make) a year and the year is Agni; as great as Agni is, as great as is his size, by so much it becomes. (First Brāhmaṇa Ends).
