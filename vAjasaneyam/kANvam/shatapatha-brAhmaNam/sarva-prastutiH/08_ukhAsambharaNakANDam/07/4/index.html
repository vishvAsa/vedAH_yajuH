विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (2/25 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)

अ᳓थ वात्सप्रे᳓णो᳓पतिष्ठते एत᳓द्वै᳓ प्रजा᳓पतिर्विष्णुक्रमैः᳓ प्रजाः᳓ सृष्ट्वा᳓ [[ताभ्यो|ता᳓भ्यो]] वात्सप्रे᳓णायु᳓ष्यमकरोत्त᳓थैवै᳙त᳓द्य᳓जमानो विष्णुक्रमैः᳓ प्रजाः᳓ सृष्ट्वा᳓ [[ताभ्यो|ता᳓भ्यो]] वात्सप्रे᳓णायु᳓ष्यं करोति ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
C R Swaminathan

He then stands by him worshipping with the Vātsapra chants (ascribed to Vātsaprī Bhalandana), because, Prajāpati by means of the Viṣṇu strides produced living beings and endowed them with longevity of life by means of the Vātsapra chants. In the like manner, the Yajamāna having produced the creatures by means of Viṣṇu strides, endows them with longevity of life by Vātsapra.
