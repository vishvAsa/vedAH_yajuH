## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वै᳘ य᳘दैते᳘न बार्हस्पत्ये᳘न चरु᳘णा प्रच᳘रति त᳘दिष्टा᳘ देव᳘ता भवत्य᳘निष्टः स्विष्टकृद᳘थास्मा एतद᳘न्नꣳ सं᳘भरत्येत᳘त्सर्वौषधꣳ स यद᳘स्मा एतद᳘न्नꣳ संभ᳘रत्य᳘न्नं वा᳘ एष उ᳘ज्जयति यो᳘ वाजपे᳘येन य᳘जतेऽन्नपे᳘यꣳ ह वै ना᳘मैतद्य᳘द्वाजपे᳘यमि᳘त्याहुः स य᳘दे᳘वैतद᳘न्नमुज्ज᳘यति त᳘दे᳘वास्मा एतत्सं᳘भरति ते᳘नैनमभि᳘षिञ्चति ते᳘न स᳘मर्धयति त᳘दस्मिन्दधाति त᳘स्मादस्मा एतद᳘न्नꣳ सं᳘भरति ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He proceeds with this caru belonging to Bṛhaspati. The offering is made to its deity. Even when its Sviṣṭakṛt offering remains yet to be given, he (the Adhvaryu) collects this food. This is the medicine for everything. Thus he collects that food for him. He who performs Vājapeya, wins food. What they call Vājapeya is really Annapeya. Thus, whatever food he (the Yajamāna) has thereby gained, that he (Adhvaryu) now brings to him.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

औ᳘दुंबरे चमसे᳘ऽन्नं वा ऊ᳘र्गुदुंबरो᳘ऽन्नमु वा᳘ एतत्सं᳘भरति त᳘स्मादौ᳘दुंबरे चमसे᳘ ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

In a camasa made of Udumbarawood, he brings the food. For, Udumbara tree is sustenance, that is food. He indeed collects food. Therefore (he collects) in the camasa made of Udumbara wood.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

सोऽप᳘ एवा᳘ग्रे सं᳘भरत्य᳘थ पयो᳘ऽथ यथोपस्मारम᳘न्नान्यन्नानि ता᳘नि सप्त᳘दश सं᳘भरेदि᳘त्याहुः सप्तदशो हि᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिरि᳘ति त᳘दु या᳘वन्त्ये᳘वोपस्म᳘रेद्या᳘वन्ति वा विन्देत्ता᳘वन्ति सं᳘भरेत् ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He first collects water, then milk and then other foods that occur to his mind. Some say that seventeen (items of food) are to be collected, because Prajāpati is seventeenfold. He need collect (only) those (types of food) he remembers or that he could get.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘स्यास्या᳘न्नस्य सं᳘भृतस्यै᳘कमन्नमु᳘द्धरेत्तदुद्ब्रवीत तन्नाश्रीयाद्यावज्जी᳘वेत्ता᳘वद᳘पि वै᳘ प्रजा᳘पतेः स᳘र्वम᳘न्नम᳘नवरुद्धम᳘थ कस्त᳘स्मै मनु᳘ष्यो यत्स᳘र्वम᳘न्नमवरुन्धीत त᳘था ह ना᳘न्तं गच्छति त᳘था ज्यो᳘ग्जीवति त᳘दिह᳘ प्रजा᳘यै प᳘रिशिनष्टि ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

From these types of food so collected, one (type of) food he should select and announce it (by name). That one type of food, he should not eat throughout his life. By doing so, all the food of Prajāpati is not appropriated, (his abstention from that particular food amounts to his not appropriating for himself all the food of Prajāpati). Which man can (dare to) appropriate all his (Prajāpati's) food? Thereby he does not go to the end (exhaust all food) and hence he lives long. That food (which he avoids throughout his life) is here left over for his progeny (subjects).
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘स्यास्या᳘न्नस्य सं᳘भृतस्यैता आ᳘हुतीर्जुहोति वाजप्रस᳘व्या अ᳘न्नं वा᳘ एष उ᳘ज्जयति यो᳘ वाजपे᳘येन य᳘जते स या᳘भ्यो देव᳘ताभ्य एता आ᳘हुतीर्जु᳘होति ता᳘ अस्मै देव᳘ताः प्रीता᳘ एतद᳘न्नं प्र᳘सुवन्ति ता᳘भिः प्र᳘सूत उ᳘ज्जयति त᳘स्माद्वा᳘ एता आ᳘हुतीर्जुहोति ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Of the food he so collects, these offerings called *Vājaprasavya* are offered. He who performs *Vājapeya* indeed, wins food. To which deities he offers these (*Vājaprasavya*) offerings, those deities are pleased with him and grant this food unto him. Thus granted by them, he wins (the food).
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ जुहोति वा᳘जस्येमं प्रसवः᳘ सुषुवे᳘ऽग्र इ᳘त्येताः᳘ सप्ता᳘हुतीर्जुहोत्य᳘थ य᳘दिदम᳘न्नं प᳘रिशिष्टं भ᳘वति ते᳘नैनमभि᳘षिञ्चत्य᳘न्नं वा᳘ एष उज्जयति यो᳘ वाजपे᳘येन य᳘जते स य᳘दे᳘वैतद᳘न्नमुज्ज᳘यति ते᳘नै᳘वैनमेत᳘दभि᳘षिञ्चति ते᳘न स᳘मर्धयति त᳘दस्मिन्दधाति त᳘स्मादेनमभि᳘षिञ्चति ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He offers (saying) "In the beginning, the producer of food granted"—thus he offers seven offerings. Then what food is left over, (which remains of the food after the seven offerings) with that he bathes him. He, who performs *Vājapeya* does win food. That food which he thus wins, with that he is bathed. He enriches him with that. He puts it (food) into him. So he bathes him (with that).
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

सोऽभि᳘षिञ्चति देव᳘स्य त्वा सवितुः᳘ प्रस᳘वेऽश्वि᳘नोर्बाहु᳘भ्यां पूष्णो ह᳘स्ताभ्याम् । स᳘रस्वत्यै वाचो᳘ यन्तु᳘र्ये तु᳘र्यं दधामी᳘ति वाग्वै स᳘रस्वती त᳘देनं वाच᳘ एव तु᳘र्ये तु᳘र्यं दधामी᳘ति त᳘दु है᳘क आहु᳘र्वि᳘श्वेषां त्वा देवा᳘नां तु᳘र्ये तु᳘र्यं दधामी᳘ति स᳘र्वं वै वि᳘श्वे देवा इ᳘ति त᳘देनमस्य स᳘र्वस्य तु᳘र्ये तु᳘र्यं दधाती᳘ति त᳘दु त᳘था न ब्रूयात्स᳘रस्वत्या इ᳘त्येव᳘ ब्रूयाद्वाग्वै स᳘रस्वती वाग्वै᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिः स᳘र्वं प्रजा᳘पतिस्त᳘देनमस्य स᳘र्वस्य तु᳘र्ये तु᳘र्यं दधाति त᳘स्मात्स᳘रस्वत्या इ᳘त्येव᳘ ब्रूयात् ॥ ७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He bathes (saying) "At the impulse of the divine *savitṛ* (I bathe) thee, by the arms of the Aśvins, by the hands of Pūşan. Unto Sarasvati I deposit the fourth aspect of speech (i.e. *vaikharī*) in the form of the *stotra* hymns containing comprehensible letters. Sarasvatī is forsooth, *vāK*. So he puts the fourth (aspect of speech) in the *stotra* of Sarasvati whose name is made of four letters.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

बृ᳘हस्प᳘तेष्ट्वा सा᳘म्राज्येनाभि᳘षिञ्चामी᳘ति ब्रूयाद्य᳘दि ब्राह्मणो य᳘जेत ब्र᳘ह्म हि बृ᳘हस्प᳘तिर्ब्र᳘ह्म हि᳘ ब्राह्मण इ᳘न्द्रस्य त्वा सा᳘म्राज्येनाभि᳘षिञ्चामी᳘ति ब्रूयाद्य᳘दि क्षत्रि᳘यो य᳘जेत क्षत्रꣳ ही᳘न्द्रः क्षत्र᳘मु हि क्षत्रियः ॥ ८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"O Bṛhaspati ! I bathe you with the empire". So it should be said, if it is a Brāhmaṇa who is the Yajamāna. For, Bṛhaspati is Brahmā and Brahmā is Brāhmaņa. "O Indra! I bathe you, with the empire". So it is said if it is a Kṣatriya who is the Yajamāna. For, Indra is Kṣatra and Kṣatra is Kṣatriya.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ सम्रा᳘ळय᳘मभूदि᳘ति त्रिरु᳘द्वदति नि᳘वेदितमे᳘वैनमेतत्स᳘न्तं देवे᳘भ्यो भू᳘यो नि᳘वेदयत्य᳘यं युष्मा᳘कं इ᳘तीमं᳘ गोपायतेमं᳘ वित्ते᳘ति देवा᳘न्ह्येष᳘ उपाव᳘र्तते यो᳘ वाजपे᳘येन य᳘जते स᳘ देव᳘नामेवै᳘को भवति ॥ ९ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He thrice announces "This one (Yajamāna) had become an emperor". Him thus already indicated (associated) as (emperor) he again announces to the gods. "This one (Yajamāna) is yours; you may protect him, recognise him". (He says so) for, he is joining the gods. He who performs *Vājapeya* does become one among the gods.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थैनमेता उ᳘ज्जितीर्वाच᳘यति वा जुहो᳘ति वा समान᳘ एव ब᳘न्धुर्य᳘दि च वाच᳘यति य᳘दि च जुहो᳘ति य᳘दे᳘वैता᳘भिरु᳘ज्जितिभिरेता᳘ देव᳘ता उद᳘जयꣳस्त᳘दे᳘वैता᳘भिर्य᳘जमान उ᳘ज्जयति ॥ १० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thus he either announces or offers (oblations) to (celebrate) his victory. The significance is the same, whether he announces or offers. These gods won by those announcements of victory. In the same way, the Yajamāna wins by these (announcements of victory).
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अग्निरे᳘काक्षरेण प्राणमु᳘दजयत्तमु᳘ज्जेषं प्राजा᳘पतिः सप्त᳘दशाक्षरेण सप्तदशꣳ स्तो᳘त्रमुदजयत्तमु᳘ज्जेषमि᳘ति स य᳘दे᳘वैता᳘भिरु᳘ज्जितिभिरेता᳘ देव᳘ता उदजयꣳस्त᳘दे᳘वैता᳘भिर्य᳘जमान उ᳘ज्जयति ॥ ११ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Agni won the life-breath with one syllable. Prajāpati won the seventeen- fold stotra by means of seventeen syllables and (said), "I have won him". Just as these deities won by these (announcements of) victories, this Yajamāna wins with these (announcements of victory).
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाहाग्न᳘ये स्विष्टकृते᳘ऽनुब्रूही᳘त्यग्नि᳘ꣳ᳘ स्विष्टकृ᳘तं यजे᳘ति व᳘षट्कृते जुहोति स य᳘दुभे आ᳘हुती अ᳘न्तरेणैतत्क᳘र्म क्रिय᳘त एष वाव᳘ प्राजा᳘पतिर्यज्ञो य᳘ एष᳘ ताय᳘त एत᳘स्यै᳘वैत᳘त्प्राजा᳘पतेर्यज्ञ᳘स्य मध्यत आ᳘दधाति मध्य᳘तोऽभिविञ्चत्यथे᳘ळामु᳘पहूय मार्जयन्ते मार्जयित्वा᳘ माहेन्द्रं ग्र᳘हं गृह्णाति माहेन्द्रं ग्र᳘हं गृहीत्वा᳘ स्तोत्रा᳘य प्र᳘मीवति स᳘ स्तोत्र᳘मुपा᳘वहरति सो᳘ऽन्ते स्तोत्र᳘स्य भ᳘वत्य᳘न्ते शस्त्र᳘स्यात्मा वै᳘ स्तोत्रं᳘ प्रजा᳘ शस्त्रं त᳘योरे᳘वैत᳘दुभयोर᳘न्ते भवति ॥ १२ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he says "Recite (the invitatory chant for Agni the Svistakṛt). Pro- nounce the offering prayer for Agni, the Svistakṛt". He offers when the Vaṣaṭ is uttered. Now why this rite is performed between the two oblations is this—this sacrifice which is being performed is of course, the Prajāpati. He thereby wins Prajāpati in the very middle of this sacrifice that belongs to Prajāpati. He annoints him in the middle. He then puts Iḍā (in the Iḍāpātra) and he having sprinkled water, draws Māhendragraha. After having drawn the Māhendragraha, he calls for the stotra (to be chanted). He (Yajamāna) gets down (from the seat) when the stotra is started (chanting). He is in attendance at the chant of the stotra and in the end he is in attendance at the recitation of the śastra. Stotra is the self and śastra is the subjects. He is in attendance at both these.
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तद्वै᳘के स्तोत्र᳘मुपाकृत्या᳘थैतत्क᳘र्म कुर्वन्ति त᳘दु त᳘था न᳘ कुर्यादात्मा वै᳘ स्तोत्रं᳘ प्रजा᳘ शस्त्रं ता᳘भ्याꣳ हैनं त᳘ उभा᳘भ्यां प्र᳘नाशयन्ति प्र᳘धमन्ति स पा᳘पीयान्निष्ट्वा भवति त᳘स्मात्पु᳘रस्तादेवैतत्क᳘र्म कृत्वा᳘न्त एव᳘ स्तोत्र᳘स्य स्याद᳘न्ते शस्त्र᳘स्यात्मा वै᳘ स्तोत्रं᳘ प्रजा᳘ शस्त्रं त᳘योरे᳘वैत᳘दुभ᳘योर᳘न्ते भवति स श्रे᳘यानिष्ट्वा᳘ भवति ॥ १३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Some chant the stotra and then perform this rite. It should not be done so. For stotra is self and śastra is the subjects. Thereby, with those two he ruins the sacrifice, he goes astray and becomes a sinner by offering. So this (Vājaprasavīya offering) rite should be done before and only at the end, the stotra should be chanted and at the end of it, śastra to be recited. Stotra is self and śastra is subjects and they both should be at the end of this rite. He becomes prosperous by (so) offering. (Third Brāhmaṇa Ends) (Chapter Two Ends) VĀJAPEYA KĀṆḌA ENDS RĀJASŪYA KĀŅDA
</details>
