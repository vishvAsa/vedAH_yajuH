## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तं वै मा᳘ध्यन्दिने स᳘वनेऽभि᳘षिञ्चन्ति मा᳘ध्यन्दिने स᳘वन आजिं᳘ धावन्त्येष वाव᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिर्यज्ञो य᳘ एष᳘ ताय᳘त एत᳘मे᳘वैत᳘त्प्रजा᳘पतिं यज्ञं᳘ मध्यत उ᳘ज्जयति ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

At the midday savana, he consecrates (the Yajamāna) by pouring water and at the midday savana, they run a race. This sacrifice which is being performed is Prajāpati. Him, the Prajapati who is sacrifice, he wins in the middle (of the sacrifice).
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘गृहीते माहेन्द्रे ग्र᳘ह एष वा इ᳘न्द्रस्य नि᳘ष्केवल्यो ग्र᳘हो य᳘न्माहेन्द्रो᳘ऽप्यस्यैतन्नि᳘ष्केवल्यमेव᳘ स्तोत्रं नि᳘ष्केवल्यꣳ शस्त्रमि᳘न्द्रो वै य᳘जमानः स्व᳘ ए᳘वैनं त᳘दाय᳘तन उ᳘ज्जयति त᳘स्माद᳘गृहीते माहेन्द्रे ग्रहे ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(What is being stated below, he does) when the Mahendragrahais not drawn. This (Mahendra) is the exclusive graha for Indra. Its stotra is (also) exclusive (to Indra) and its śastra is (also) exclusive. Yajamāna is indeed, the sovereign (Indra). So he wins him (Indra) in his own abode (Yajamāna). So it is (to be done) before the Mahendragraha is drawn.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थास्मै र᳘थमुपा᳘वहरन्ती᳘न्द्रस्य व᳘ज्रोऽसी᳘ति व᳘ज्रो वा᳘ एष यद्र᳘थ इन्द्रो वै य᳘जमानो य᳘जमानस्याशी᳘त्ये᳘वैतदाह य᳘दाहे᳘न्द्रस्य व᳘ज्रोऽसी᳘ति वा᳘जसा इत्य᳘न्नं वै वाजो᳘ऽञ्जसा इ᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह त्व᳘यायं वा᳘जꣳसेदि᳘ति त्व᳘यायम᳘न्नमुज्जयत्वि᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह यदा᳘ह त्व᳘यायं वा᳘जꣳ सेदिति ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He brings down the chariot for him (Indra, saying) "Thou art the thunder- bolt of Indra". This chariot is indeed, the thunderbolt and the Yajamāna is Indra, when he says 'Thou art the thunderbolt of Indra' he only means 'You belong to Yajamāna'. (He says) "Winner of wealth (vāja)" for, vāja means food and he only says 'winner of food'. (He says) "Let this one (Yajamāna) win wealth by you". 'By you let him win food' is what he means by saying "Let this one win wealth by you".
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थैनं धूर्गृही᳘तमन्तर्वे᳘द्यभ्य᳘ववर्तयति वा᳘जस्य नु᳘ प्रसवे᳘ मात᳘रं महीमित्य᳘न्नं वै वाजो᳘ऽन्नस्य नु᳘ प्रसवे᳘ मात᳘रं मात᳘रं महीमि᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाहादितिं ना᳘म व᳘चसा करामह इ᳘तीयं वा अ᳘दितिस्त᳘स्मादाहा᳘दितिं ना᳘म व᳘चसा करामह इ᳘ति य᳘स्यामिदं वि᳘श्वं भु᳘वनमाविवेशे᳘त्यस्या᳘ꣳ हीदꣳ स᳘र्वं भु᳘वनमा᳘विष्टं त᳘स्यां नो देवः᳘ सविता ध᳘र्मसाविषगि᳘ति त᳘स्यां देव᳘स्सविता यज᳘मानः सा᳘म्राज्याय सुवतामि᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह य᳘जमानो हि ध᳘र्मो यदा᳘ह त᳘स्यां नो देवः᳘ सविता ध᳘र्मसाविषगिति ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then that chariot which is yoked, he turns it into the vedi (saying) "In the winning of wealth, the great mother". Vāja is food and thereby he says 'in the winning of food the great mother'. (He says) "Aditi by name, we praise with speech". Now, Aditi is this earth. So he says "Aditi by name, we praise with speech". "In whom all this universe has entered" for, this entire universe has entered into her (earth). "Thereon, may the divine Savitṛ acclaim this our ritual". (It means) 'On her, let the divine Savitṛ enable the Yajamāna to (win the) empire', for, the Yajamāna is the ritual. So he says "Thereon may the divine Savitṛ acclaim this our ritual".
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थैतान᳘श्वान्त्स्नपयन्ति स य᳘त्र वै᳘वैनानेत᳘त्स्नप᳘यन्ति त᳘द्धैनानेते᳘न य᳘जुषा᳘भ्युक्षति स्न᳘पिता᳘न्वेदा᳘नीतान्त्स य᳘देनानद्भि᳘रभ्यु᳘क्षत्यद्भ्यो᳘ ह वा अग्रे᳘ऽश्वः सं᳘बभूव᳘ सोऽद्भ्य᳘स्सं᳘भवन्नकृत्स्नस्सं᳘बभूवा᳘कृत्स्नो हि वै᳘ संबभू᳘व त᳘स्मान्नैव᳘ कदा᳘चन स᳘र्वैः पद्भिः प्र᳘तितिष्ठति स य᳘देवास्य त᳘त्राप्स्व᳘हीयत ते᳘नैवैनमेतत्स᳘मर्धयति कृत्स्नं᳘ करोति त᳘स्मादद्भि᳘रभ्यु᳘क्षति ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They then bathe the horses (with water). When they bathe them, they sprinkle them with water uttering this Yajus verse. He sprinkles them with water when they are bathed or when they are being brought up after being watered. As to why they sprinkle them with waters is because at the beginning, horse originated from waters. When it (horse) originated from waters, it emerged incomplete. It was indeed, incomplete when originated and hence it never stands on all its legs. What part of it was left behind in the waters, by that he is now filling it; completing it. So he sprinkles with waters.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

सो᳘ऽभ्युक्षति दे᳘वीरापो अपांनपाद्यो व ऊर्मिः प्र᳘तुर्तिः । ककु᳘न्मान्वाजसास्ते᳘नायं वा᳘जꣳ सेदि᳘ति वा᳘प्वन्त᳘रमृ᳘तमप्सु᳘ भेषज᳘मपा᳘मुत प्र᳘शस्तिभिः अश्वा भ᳘वत वाजि᳘न इ᳘ति वा ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He sprinkles with waters (saying) "O divine waters! what rushing, high- peaked, wealth-winning wave you have, therewith may this one win wealth". Or (by saying) "Within the waters is nectar; in the waters is medicine, at the praises of the waters, may ye wax strong, ye horses!"
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ प्रतिहृ᳘त्य युनक्ति स᳘ दक्षिणायुग्यमेवा᳘ग्रे युनक्त्य᳘थ सव्यायुग्य᳘ꣳ᳘ सव्यायुग्यं᳘ वा अ᳘ग्रे मानुषे᳘ युनक्त्यथै᳘वं देवत्रा ॥ ७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He having turned them, yokes them (to the chariot). He yokes the right one (horse) first and then the left one. According to human (practice) it is the left one that is first yoked and in the divine (practice) it is thus (the right one first).
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ युनक्ति वा᳘तो वा वो म᳘नो वे᳘ति वा᳘ताद्धै ना᳘शीयोऽस्ति म᳘नसो ना᳘शीयोऽस्ति त᳘स्मादाह वा᳘तो वा वो म᳘नो वे᳘ति गन्धर्वा᳘स्सप्त᳘विꣳशतिः ते अग्रे᳘ऽश्वमयुञ्जन्नि᳘त्येते वाग्रे᳘ऽश्वमयुञ्जन्य᳘द्गन्धर्वा᳘स्सप्त᳘विꣳशतिः तद्येऽग्रे᳘ऽश्वम᳘युञ्जꣳस्ते त्वा युञ्जन्त्वि᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह ते᳘ अस्मिञ्जवमा᳘दधुरि᳘त्येते वाग्रे᳘ऽश्वे जवमा᳘दधुर्य᳘द्गन्धर्वा᳘स्सप्त᳘विꣳशतिः । तद्येऽग्रे᳘ऽश्वे जवमा᳘दधुस्ते त्व᳘यि जवमा᳘दधत्वि᳘त्ये᳘वै᳘त᳘दाह ॥ ८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He yokes (saying) "Either the wind, or thought"—for there is nothing swifter than the wind and nothing swifter than the thought. So he says "Either the wind or thought". "The twenty-seven Gandharvas, they yoked the horse at first". These Gandharvas who are twenty-seven happened to yoke the horse at first. "Those who first yoked the horse, let them yoke thee"—that is what he says. They laid speed unto him at first—for, it is the twenty-seven Gandharvas, who at first laid speed into the horse. So 'those who first laid speed in the horse, let them put speed into you'— is what he says.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ सव्यायुग्यं᳘ युनक्ति वा᳘तरꣳहा भव वाजिन्युज्य᳘मान इ᳘ति वा᳘तजवसमे᳘वैनमेतन्म᳘नोजवसं करोती᳘न्द्रस्येव द᳘क्षिणः श्रि᳘यैधी᳘ति य᳘थायमि᳘न्द्रस्य द᳘क्षिणः श्रि᳘यैवं त्वं य᳘जमानस्य श्रि᳘यैधी᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह युञ्जन्तु त्वा मरु᳘तो विश्व᳘वेदस इ᳘ति युञ्ज᳘न्तु त्वा देवा इ᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह यदा᳘ह युञ्जन्तु त्वा मरु᳘तो विश्व᳘वेदस इत्या᳘ ते त्व᳘ष्टा पत्सु᳘ जवं᳘ दधात्वि᳘ति त्वाष्ट्रा हि᳘ पश᳘वः स य᳘स्यैव᳘ पश᳘वो यः᳘ पशूनामी᳘ष्टे स᳘ ते त्व᳘ष्टा पत्सु᳘ जवमा᳘दधात्वि᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाहा᳘थ दक्षिणाप्रष्टिं युनक्ति न सव्याप्रष्टिꣳ सव्याप्रष्टि वै᳘ मानुषे᳘ युनक्त्य᳘थैवं᳘ देवत्रा ॥ ९ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he yokes the left one (saying) "Become possessed of the wind's speed, O horse!, when thou art yoked"—thereby he renders him as swift as the wind; as swift as the thought. "Be thou possessed of the beauty of Indra's right (steed)". Just as Indra's right (steed) is prosperous, so you be for this Yajamāna to prosper—is what he says. "Let the Maruts that know everything, yoke thee; let Tvaṣṭā impart speed unto your legs"—for, the animals belong to Tvaṣṭā. He whose are the animals, he is the master of those animals. "Let that Tvaṣṭā impart speed unto the legs" is what he says. He then yokes the right side (horse). In human practice, the left one is yoked first, but in divine practice, it is this way (the right one first).
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

सयुनक्ति जवो य᳘स्ते वाजिन्नि᳘हितो गु᳘हा यः᳘ श्येने प᳘रीतो अ᳘चरच्च वा᳘त इ᳘ति य᳘स्ते जवो᳘ऽप्युपनि᳘हितो यः᳘ श्येने प᳘रीतो यो वा᳘त इ᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह ते᳘न नो वाजिन्ब᳘लवान्बलेनेति ते᳘न नो᳘ वीर्येणे᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह यदा᳘ह ते᳘न नो वाजिन्ब᳘लवान्ब᳘लेने᳘ति वाजजि᳘च्चैधि स᳘मने च पारयिष्णुरित्य᳘न्नं वै वा᳘जोऽन्नजि᳘च्च न ए᳘ध्यस्मि᳘ꣳ᳘श्च नः स᳘मने देवस᳘मन इमं᳘ यज्ञं᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिमु᳘ज्जये᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह यदा᳘ह वाजजि᳘च्चैधि स᳘मने च पारयिष्णुरि᳘ति ॥ १० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He yokes it (saying) "What speed has been secretly kept in you, O horse! what (speed) has been bestowed in the eagle and what the moving wind has". He thereby says "What speed is concealed in you (O horse!). What speed is bestowed in the eagle and into the wind". (He says) "With that strength, you the mighty". He only says "With that prowess, for us". When he says "With that strength, you the mighty". (He says) "Become winner of food (for us) and cause us to cross over the battle of the sacrifice". Vāja means food and (he says) "Become winner of food for us and enable us to cross over this divine battle (i.e. sacrifice)". Thereby he says 'Win thou this sacrifice, namely Prajāpati, for us' when he says "Become winner of food and enable us to cross over this battle of sacrifice".
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘ एत᳘ एव त्र᳘यो युक्ता᳘ स्युर्मानुष᳘ꣳ᳘ ह कुर्याद्य᳘च्चतुर्थ᳘मुपयुञ्ज्याद्व्यृद्धं वै त᳘द्यज्ञ᳘स्य य᳘न्मानुषं त्रिर्वै᳘ देवत्रा᳘ त्रिवृ᳘द्यज्ञस्त्र᳘य इमे᳘ लोकास्त᳘स्मादेत᳘ एव त्र᳘यो युक्ता᳘ स्युः प्र᳘तिहृताधा᳘न एवाभिप्रष्टियुगश्चतुर्थ उ᳘पतिष्ठेत स य᳘त्र दद्यात्तत्तं᳘ चतुर्थ᳘मुपयु᳘ज्य दद्यात् ॥ ११ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now only those three (horses) are yoked. If the fourth is yoked, it would be doing it in the human way. What is a human practice is taboo in the divine (context). The sacrifice is threefold and there are three worlds. Therefore only these three are yoked. The fourth unyoked, stands by the side of the yoke (when the third is yoked). When they (the horses) are given (when the chariot with four horses is given to the Adhvaryu) this one (the fourth) is also to be given after using it (after yoking to the chariot).
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थैत᳘ꣳ सप्त᳘दशशरावं᳘ नैवारं᳘ चरुमा᳘हरन्ति बा᳘र्हस्पत्यम᳘न्नं वा᳘ एष उ᳘ज्जयति यो᳘ वाजपे᳘येन य᳘जत एतद्वै᳘ प्रत्य᳘क्षम᳘न्नं य᳘च्चरु᳘रोदनो᳘ ह्येष᳘ ओदनो हि᳘ प्रत्य᳘क्षम᳘न्नं त᳘स्माच्चरु᳘र्भवति सप्त᳘दशशरावो भ᳘वति सप्तदशो हि᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिर्नैवारो᳘ भवत्येते वै ब्र᳘ह्मणा पच्यन्ते य᳘न्नीवा᳘रास्त᳘स्मान्नैवारो᳘ भवति बार्हस्पत्यो᳘ भवति बृ᳘हस्प᳘तिर्ह्येतम᳘ग्रे यज्ञ᳘मुद᳘जयत्त᳘स्माद्बार्हस्पत्यो᳘ भवति ॥ १२ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then they bring this caru (rice-pap) for Bṛhaspati, made of wild rice measuring seventeen śarāvas (shallow cups for measuring grains). He (the Yajamāna) is indeed, winning food when he performs Vājapeya. Caru is the visible food. This is odana (cooked rice) and cooked rice is the ready food. From that, caru is made. It measures seventeen śarāvas (of wild rice) for, Prajāpati is seventeenfold. They are wild rice, for, wild rice is ripened by Brahman, and hence wild rice. This (caru) belongs to Bṛhaspati because it was Bṛhaspati who first won this sacrifice. So it (caru) belongs to Bṛhaspati.
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तम᳘श्वान᳘वघ्रापयति वा᳘जिनो वाजजित इ᳘ति वाजि᳘नो᳘ ह्येते᳘ वाजजित इ᳘त्यन्नं वै वा᳘जोऽन्नजित इ᳘त्ये᳘वैत᳘दाह वा᳘जꣳ सरिष्य᳘न्त इ᳘ति सरिष्य᳘न्तो हि भ᳘वन्ति बृ᳘हस्प᳘तेर्भागम᳘वजिघ्रते᳘ति बार्हस्पत्यो᳘ बार्हस्पत्यो᳘ ह्येष᳘ चरुर्भ᳘वति स यद᳘श्वानवघ्राप᳘यति वी᳘र्यमे᳘वैष्वेत᳘द्दधात्य᳘थो इदम᳘न्नमिमं᳘ यज्ञं᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिमु᳘ज्जयानी᳘ति त᳘थाहैतद᳘न्नमेतं᳘ यज्ञं᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिमु᳘ज्जयति ॥ १३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He makes the horses smell it (caru). (He says) "Ye horses, the winners of vāja"—vāja means food. So he says, 'winners of food'. (He says 'Ye who advance proceed forward, to the food'. For they do indeed charge forward (as in a battle to win-food). (He says) "Smell Bṛhaspati's portion"—this caru which he causes the horses to smell, belongs to Bṛhaspati. So he only puts vigour into them. (He says) "This food, this sacrifice, namely Prajāpati—let me win". That is how he wins this food, this sacrifice, namely Prajāpati. (Fourth Brāhmaņa Ends) (Chapter One Ends)
</details>
