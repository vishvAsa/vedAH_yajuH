## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वा᳘ अꣳशुं᳘ गृह्णाति स य᳘दꣳशुं᳘ गृह्णा᳘ति सर्वत्वा᳘यैव᳘ कृत्स्न᳘तायै त᳘स्मादꣳशुं᳘ गृह्णात्य᳘थैतान्प्र᳘ज्ञाताना᳘ग्निष्टोमिकान्ग्र᳘हान्गृह्णा᳘त्याग्रयणात् ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He draws the Amśugraha. He draws the Amśugraha for completeness sake, for making it whole. Therefore he draws the Amśugraha. Then he draws the grahas (that are) of Agniṣṭoma, which are well-known upto the Āgrayaṇagraha.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ पृ᳘ष्ठ्यान्गृह्णाति स य᳘देव᳘ देवाः पृ᳘ष्ठ्यैरुद᳘जयꣳस्त᳘देवैतैर्य᳘जमान उ᳘ज्जयति ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he draws the Pṛṣṭhyas (the three Atigrāhyagrahas prescribed for the Pṛṣṭha-stotras). Whatever the gods (Agni, Indra and Sūrya) won by them, that the Yajamāna wins thereby.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अथ षोळशि᳘नं गृह्णाति स य᳘देवे᳘न्द्रः षोळशि᳘नोद᳘जयत्त᳘दे᳘वैते᳘न य᳘जमान उ᳘ज्जयति ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he draws the Ṣoḍaśīgraha. Whatever Indra won by means of the Ṣoḍaśī, the Yajamāna wins thereby.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थैता᳘न्वाजपेयग्रहा᳘न्गृह्णाति ध्रुवस᳘दं त्वा नृष᳘दं मनस्स᳘दम् । उपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसी᳘न्द्राय त्वा जु᳘ष्टं गृह्णाम्येष᳘ ते यो᳘निरि᳘न्द्राय त्वा जु᳘ष्टतममि᳘ति सादयत्ययं वै᳘ लोको᳘ ध्रुवो᳘ ध्रुवस᳘दिम᳘मे᳘वैते᳘न लोकमु᳘ज्जयति ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he draws the grahas (special) for the Vājapeya (saying) "Thee the firm- seated, the man-seated, the mind-seated! Thou art taken with a support, I take thee, agreeable to Indra. This is thy womb, thee most agreeable to Indra", he deposits it. This world (earth) is firm. By this firmly seated, he wins the world.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थाप्सुष᳘दं त्वा घृतस᳘दं व्योमस᳘दम् । उपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसी᳘न्द्राय त्वा जु᳘ष्टं गृह्णाम्ये᳘ष ते यो᳘निरि᳘न्द्राय त्वा जु᳘ष्टतममि᳘ति सादयत्ययं वै᳘ लोको᳘ व्योमेद᳘मन्त᳘रिक्षमेत᳘मे᳘वैते᳘न लोकमु᳘ज्जयति ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(He says) "Thee the water-seated, the ghee-seated, the ether-seated! Thou art taken with a support; I take thee, agreeable to Indra. This is thy womb; thee most agreeable to Indra", and deposits it. That ether is this aerial region. By this he wins that (aerial) world.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ पृथिविस᳘दं त्वान्तरिक्षस᳘दं दिविस᳘दं देवस᳘दं नाकस᳘दम् । उपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसी᳘न्द्राय त्वा जु᳘ष्टं गृह्णाम्ये᳘ष ते यो᳘निरि᳘न्द्राय त्वा जु᳘ष्टतममि᳘ति सादयत्यसौ वै᳘ लोको᳘ देवस᳘न्नाकस᳘दसा᳘वेव᳘ देवलोक᳘ एत᳘मे᳘वैते᳘न लोकमु᳘ज्जयति ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Thee, the earth-seated, the air-seated, the sky-seated, the god-seated, the heaven-seated; I take with a support; thee most agreeable to Indra" (so saying) he deposits it. The yonder world is the god-seated, the heaven-seated. That is indeed, the world of gods. By this he wins that very world.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थापाꣳ र᳘समु᳘द्वयसꣳ सूर्ये स᳘न्तꣳसमा᳘हितम् । अपाꣳ र᳘सस्य यो र᳘सस्तं᳘ वो गृह्णाम्युत्तमम् । उपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसी᳘न्द्राय त्वा जु᳘ष्टं गृह्णाम्येष᳘ ते यो᳘निरि᳘न्द्राय त्वा जु᳘ष्टतममि᳘ति सादयत्ययं वा᳘ अपाꣳर᳘स उ᳘द्वया᳘ योऽयं प᳘वते स वा᳘ एष सू᳘र्ये समा᳘हितः स सू᳘र्यात्पवत एत᳘मे᳘वैते᳘न र᳘समु᳘ज्जयति ॥ ७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"The invigorating essence of the waters, being contained in the Sun; the essence which is the essence of waters; thee agreeable to Indra, I take with a support! This is thy womb; thee most agreeable to Indra" (so saying) he deposits it. For, the essence of the waters is the one who blows (or purifies) yonder (the vāyu) and he is contained in the Sun, he flows from the Sun; and that same essence, he thereby wins.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ ग्र᳘हा ऊर्जाहुतयो व्य᳘न्तो वि᳘प्राय मतिम् । ते᳘षां वि᳘शिप्रियाणां वोऽहमि᳘षमू᳘र्जꣳ स᳘मग्रभम् । उपयाम᳘गृहीतोऽसी᳘न्द्राय त्वा जु᳘ष्टं गृह्णाम्येष᳘ ते यो᳘निरि᳘न्द्राय त्वा जुष्टतममि᳘ति सादयत्यूर्ग्वै र᳘स ऊ᳘र्जमे᳘वैते᳘न र᳘समु᳘ज्जयति ॥ ८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

"Ye grahas, of energising libations, inspiring the sage with thought! I have gathered the sap and energy of thine, the handleless. I take thee with a support; thee agreeable to Indra"—(so saying) he deposits it. Ūrja or energy is the essence. With this, he wins the energy which is the essence.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तान्वा᳘ एतान्प᳘ञ्च वाजपेयग्रहा᳘न्गृह्णाति प्रजा᳘पतिं वा᳘ एष उ᳘ज्जयति यो᳘ वाजपे᳘येन य᳘जते पञ्च वा᳘ ऋत᳘वः संवत्स᳘रस्य संवत्सरो वै᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिस्त᳘त्प्रजा᳘पतिमु᳘ज्जयति ॥ ९ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

These are thus the five Vājapeyagrahas, he draws. He who performs Vājapeya wins Prajāpati. There are five seasons for the year and the year is Prajāpati. So he wins Prajāpati.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ ख᳘लु सप्त᳘दश सोमग्रहा᳘न्गृह्णा᳘ति सप्त᳘दश सुराग्रहा᳘नुभे वा᳘ एते᳘ प्रजा᳘पतेर᳘न्धसी यत्सो᳘मश्च सु᳘रा च त᳘तः श्री᳘स्सत्यं ज्यो᳘तिः सोमो᳘ऽनृतं पाप्मा त᳘मः सु᳘रा त ए᳘वास्यैते᳘नोभे अ᳘न्धसी उ᳘ज्जयति ॥ १० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then he draws the seventeen grahas of soma and the seventeen grahas of surā. Both these, soma and surā are food for Prajāpati. Now, prosperity, truth and splendour are soma; untruth, sin and darkness are surā. Those two foods of his (Prajāpati's) he (the Yajamāna) wins thereby.
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ सप्त᳘दश सोमग्रहा᳘न्गृह्णाति सप्तदशो वै᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिः स या᳘वानेव प्रजा᳘पतिर्या᳘वत्यस्य मा᳘त्रा ता᳘वत्तै᳘वास्यैतच्छ्रि᳘यꣳ सत्यं ज्यो᳘तिरु᳘ज्जयति सप्त᳘दश सुराग्रहा᳘न्त्सप्तदशो वै प्रजा᳘पतिस्स या᳘वानेव᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिर्या᳘वत्यस्य मा᳘त्रा ता᳘वत्तै᳘वास्यैत᳘दनृतं पाप्मा᳘नं त᳘म उ᳘ज्जयति ॥ ११ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He draws the seventeen Somagrahas, for, Prajāpati is seventeenfold. As great as Prajāpati is, as great as is his measure, with that much he thus wins his prosperity, truth and splendour. He draws the seventeen Surāgrahas, for, Prajāpati is seventeenfold. As great as Prajāpati is, as great as is his measure, with that much he thus wins his untruth, sin and darkness.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘ उभ᳘ये च᳘तुस्त्रिꣳशद्भवन्ति त्र᳘यस्त्रिꣳशद्धै स᳘र्वे देवाः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिश्च᳘तुस्त्रिꣳशस्तत्स᳘र्वाश्च देव᳘ता उ᳘ज्जयति प्रजा᳘पतिं च ॥ १२ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They both together make thirty-four. All the gods (together) come to thirty-three and Prajāpati is the thirty-fourth. So he wins all the gods and also Prajāpati.
</details>


## १३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ य᳘त्रादः सो᳘मं क्रीण᳘न्ति तद᳘प्येतां᳘ परिस्रु᳘तं क्रीणाति केशवात्पु᳘रुषान्न वा᳘ एष स्त्री न पुमान्यकेशवो य᳘दह पु᳘माꣳस्ते᳘न न स्त्री य᳘द्वस्य के᳘शास्ते᳘न न पु᳘मान् ॥ १३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

From where they buy soma, this Parisrut (surā—an intoxicating herbal extract) also they buy from that Keśava (a man with long hairs). That Keśava is neither male nor female. Since he is a man he is not a female; since he has long hairs he is not a male.
</details>


## १४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

सी᳘सेन क्रीणाति न वा᳘ एतद्धि᳘रण्यं ना᳘यो यत्सी᳘सं त᳘स्मात्सी᳘सेन क्रीणाति ॥ १४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He buys it with lead. This lead is neither gold nor iron. So he buys with lead.
</details>


## १५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थ द्वौ ख᳘रौ कुर्वन्ति पश्चादक्ष᳘मन्यं ने᳘त्सोमग्रहा᳘ꣳ᳘श्च सुराग्रहा᳘ꣳ᳘श्च सह᳘ साद᳘यामेत्य᳘थ य᳘त्रादः᳘ प्रातः पू᳘र्वया द्वा᳘रापः᳘ प्रपाद᳘यन्ति त᳘ज्जघ᳘नेनाहृत्या᳘ परया द्वा᳘रैतां᳘ परिस्रु᳘तं प्र᳘पादयन्ति स पुर᳘स्तादा᳘सीनोऽध्वर्यु᳘र्गृह्णा᳘ति पश्चात्प्रा᳘ङ् प्रतिप्रस्थाता स᳘ सोमग्रह᳘मेवा᳘ग्रे गृह्णात्य᳘थ सुराग्रह᳘मेवं᳘ वित्या संगृहीतः स᳘होत्तमौ गृह्णीतः स᳘ उप᳘र्युपर्य᳘क्षमध्वर्यु᳘र्धार᳘यत्य᳘धोऽधः प्रतिप्रस्थाता स ना᳘हैव᳘ प्रत्यङ्क्षमध्वर्यु᳘रतिह᳘रेन्न प्रा᳘ङ् प्रतिप्रस्थाता ॥ १५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They prepare two earthen mounds (earthen platforms on which grahasare kept in the Havirdhāna); one at the back of the axle (of the soma cart and the other in front of it) (thinking) "lest we mix up the Somagrahas and the Surāgrahas". There on the previous day they pour water by the front door. Bringing from behind, they pour this Parisrut by the other door (in the back side). The Adhvaryu, sitting in front, facing eastwards (draws the Surāgrahas). First he (the Adhvaryu) draws a Somagraha, then (the Pratiprasthātā), a Surāgraha. Thus they both draw alterna- tively each. The last one each draws simultaneously. Adhvaryu holds each Somagraha above the axle and the Pratiprasthātā holds down the axle each Surāgraha. The Adhvaryu should not hold it beyond the axle westwards and the Pratiprasthātā should not hold it beyond the axle eastwards.
</details>


## १६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स᳘ जपति संपृ᳘च स्थ सं᳘ मा भद्रे᳘ण पृङ्क्ते᳘ति ने᳘त्पापेने᳘ति ब्र᳘वामेत्य᳘थ पु᳘नर्वि᳘हरतो विपृ᳘च स्थ वि᳘ मा पापे᳘न पृङ्क्ते᳘ति स य᳘थैषी᳘कां वि᳘मुञ्जां कुर्या᳘दिव᳘मे᳘वैतद्य᳘जमानꣳ स᳘र्वस्मात्पाप्म᳘नो वि᳘वृहतो᳘ऽथ हिरण्म᳘येन पा᳘त्रेण मधुग्रहं᳘ गृह्णात्य᳘थो᳘क्थ्यम᳘थ ध्रुवꣳ स य᳘त्राद उत्तम᳘ꣳ᳘ सप्तदश᳘ꣳ᳘ स्तोत्रं त᳘देता᳘न्त्सोमग्रहा᳘नृत्वि᳘जां चमसे᳘षु व्यानी᳘य भक्षयन्त्य᳘थ मा᳘ध्यन्दिने स᳘वने मधुग्रह᳘स्य चोच्य᳘ते सुराग्रहा᳘णां च ॥ १६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

He (each of them) chants "United Ye are; unite with happiness". (Think- ing) "Lest we say (unite us) with evil". They withdraw them again (saying) 'Disunited Ye are; disunite us with evil'. Even as one might tear a reed (out of a reed grass) in the same manner they separate that Yajamāna from all evil. Then with a golden vessel, they draw the Madhugraha. Then he draws the Ukthyagraha; then the Dhruvagraha. When the last chant of seventeen stotras (prescribed for the third savana) has been said, he pours those Somagrahas into the cups of the officiating priests and drink them. In the midday savana, this (procedure) is said about the Madhugraha and also of the Surāgrahas. (Second Brāhmaṇa Ends)
</details>
