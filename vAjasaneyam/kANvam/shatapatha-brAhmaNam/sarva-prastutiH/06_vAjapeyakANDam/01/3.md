## ०१

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स वा᳘ आग्नेय᳘मे᳘वाग्निष्टोमाया᳘लभतेऽग्निष्टोम᳘मेव तेनो᳘ज्जयत्यैन्द्रा᳘ग्नमुक्थ्ये᳘भ्य आ᳘लभत ऐन्द्राग्ना᳘नि ह्युक्थ्या᳘न्युक्थ्या᳘नेव तेनो᳘ज्जयत्यैन्द्र᳘ꣳ᳘ षोळशि᳘न आ᳘लभत इ᳘न्द्रो हि᳘ षोळशी᳘ षोळशि᳘नमेव तेनो᳘ज्जयति ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For the Agniṣṭoma, he slays (a paśu) for Agni, for, the Agniṣṭoma itself, he thereby wins. For the Ukthyas, he slays (a paśu) for Indra and Agni, for the Ukthyas belong to Indra and Agni and thereby he wins the Ukthyas. For the Ṣodaśī, he slays (a paśu) for Indra, for Ṣodaśī is Indra and thereby he wins the Ṣoḍaśī.
</details>


## ०२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थैत᳘ꣳ सारस्वत᳘मेत᳘स्मा उत्तमा᳘य सप्तदशाय स्तोत्राया᳘लभते त᳘देत᳘दनतिरात्रे सति रा᳘त्रे रू᳘पं क्रियते स᳘र्वं वा᳘ एष उ᳘ज्जयति यो᳘ वाजपे᳘येन य᳘जतेऽहोरात्रे᳘ उ वै᳘ संवत्स᳘र एते हि᳘ परिप्ल᳘वमाने संवत्सरं᳘ कुरुतः स᳘र्वमु वै᳘ संवत्सरस्तत्सर्वमु᳘ज्जयति स यदाहा᳘नतिरात्रो भ᳘वति तेना᳘हरु᳘ज्जयति यद्व᳘नतिरात्रे सति रा᳘त्रे रू᳘पं क्रिय᳘ते ते᳘नो रा᳘त्रिमु᳘ज्जयत्ये᳘व᳘मुभे᳘ अहोरात्रे उ᳘ज्जयति ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

For this final seventeenfold stotra (Vājapeya sāman), he slays (a paśu) for Sarasvatī. By this, even though it is not an overnight performance. It is yet made like the night (performance). He who performs Vājapeya wins everything. The year is made of days and nights. They by alternating each other make the year. The year is everything and so he wins everything. The fact that it is not an overnight performance, he wins the day thereby. The fact that even though it is not over-night performance, it is made a night performance, he wins the night, thereby, thus he wins both day and night.
</details>


## ०३

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थैतां᳘ वशां पृ᳘श्नि मरु᳘द्भ्य उज्जेषे᳘भ्य आ᳘लभत इयं वै पृ᳘श्निर्य᳘दस्यां᳘ मूलि᳘ चामूलं᳘ चोभ᳘यं ते᳘नेयं पृ᳘श्निरस्या᳘मु वा᳘ इदम᳘न्नमस्या᳘ꣳहीद᳘मन्ना᳘द्यमन्ना᳘द्यमे᳘वैतयो᳘ज्जयति स य᳘न्मरु᳘द्भ्यो वि᳘शो वै᳘ मरु᳘तो देवविशम᳘न्नमु वै वि᳘शस्त᳘स्मान्मरु᳘द्भ्य उज्जेषे᳘भ्य उ᳘ज्जितये स यद्य᳘ह म᳘रुद्भ्य उ᳘ज्जेषे᳘भ्यो याज्यानुवा᳘क्ये विन्देद᳘पि मारु᳘त्यावेव᳘ स्यातां दुर्वे᳘दो वै᳘ वशा पृ᳘श्निर्य᳘दि पृ᳘श्नि स विन्देदपि᳘ यैव का᳘ च वशा स्यात् ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Thereupon he slays a spotted sterile paśu for the victorious Maruts. This (spotted paśu) is this earth where both the rooted and the rootless are established. There she is called Pṛśni (spotted paśu). This food is (contained) in (the earth). Indeed, this food and drink are in this (earth). By this he wins food and drink for the Maruts, the Viś. Maruts are the peasants and peasants are the food for the nobles (elites). (So he says) “For Maruts the victorious” for the sake of victory. If he could get at two invitatory and offering prayers (Yājya and Anuvākya) for the victorious Maruts, let him adopt them. If he could not get at two invitatory and offering prayers for the victorious Maruts, let him do with (any two) prayers meant for Maruts. It is difficult to get a spotted sterile paśu, and so if he can’t get a spotted one, any sterile paśu will do.
</details>


## ०४

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘स्या आवृद्य᳘त्र हो᳘ता माहेन्द्रं ग्र᳘हमनुश᳘ꣳ᳘सति त᳘दस्या वप᳘या प्र᳘चरेयुरेष वा इ᳘न्द्रस्य नि᳘ष्केवल्यो ग्र᳘हो य᳘न्माहेन्द्रो᳘ऽप्यस्यैतन्नि᳘ष्केवल्यमेव᳘ स्तोत्रं नि᳘ष्केवल्यꣳ शस्त्रं᳘ क्षत्र᳘मु वा इ᳘न्द्रो वि᳘शो मरु᳘तो विशो वै᳘ क्षत्रि᳘यो ब᳘लीयान्भवति क्षत्र᳘ ए᳘वैतद्ब᳘लं दधाति ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

This is the procedure in the case of that (sterile paśu)—when the Hotṛ recites after the Māhendragraha libation, then he should proceed with the vapā of this (sterile paśu). Because, this Māhendragraha is the exclusive graha for Indra. The prayer (stotra) is also exclusive and the śāstra thereto is also exclusive. Indra is Kṣatra (nobility) and the Maruts are the peasantry. With the peasantry, Kṣatriya becomes more powerful. Thereby (i.e. by following the Māhendragraha) this strength comes to be placed in the Kṣatra.
</details>


## ०५

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अथ द्वे᳘धावदा᳘नानि कृत्वा᳘ श्रपयति स य᳘दर्ध᳘स्यावदा᳘नं त᳘स्योपस्ती᳘र्य द्वि᳘र्द्विरवदा᳘याभिघा᳘र्य प्र᳘त्यनक्त्यवदा᳘नान्य᳘थोपभृ᳘त्युपस्ती᳘र्य सकृ᳘त्सकृदवदा᳘य द्वि᳘रभि᳘घारयति स य᳘दर्धा᳘नाꣳ सतां द्वि᳘रवद्य᳘ति ते᳘नैषा᳘ कृत्स्ना᳘ भवति ते᳘न प्र᳘चरन्ति ते᳘न देवविशमु᳘ज्जयत्य᳘थ य᳘दिदम᳘र्धं त᳘न्मानुष्यै᳘ विश उ᳘पहरन्ति ते᳘न मनुष्यविशमु᳘ज्जयति ॥ ५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then having made the cuttings (of vapā) into two (portions), they cook. The cut piece of one, he smears with ghee and (again) cuts each twice, sprinkles with ghee and anoints the cuttings. Then having smeared ghee at the bottom of the upabhṛt, having cut each once, sprinkles ghee twice. When he cuts two times each half, it becomes whole and by proceeding with that, he wins the divine race (deva viśa—the common folk among the gods). Then they present this (other) half to the human kind (Manuṣyaviśa — to the common folk among men). Thereby they win the commoners among men.
</details>


## ०६

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दु त᳘था न᳘ कुर्याद्ध्व᳘लति वै स यो᳘ यज्ञपथादेत्ये᳘त्यु वा᳘ एष᳘ यज्ञपथाद्य᳘ एवं᳘ करो᳘ति त᳘स्माद्य᳘त्रैवै᳘तेषां पशूनां᳘ वपा᳘भिः प्रच᳘रेयुस्त᳘दे᳘वास्या वप᳘या प्र᳘चरेयुर्य᳘त्रैवे᳘तेषां पशूना᳘ꣳ᳘ हवि᳘र्भिः प्र᳘चरेयुस्त᳘देवास्या हवि᳘षा प्र᳘चरेयुर्न᳘ मानुष्यै᳘ विश उ᳘पहरेत् ॥ ६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

That should not be done so. He (who does so) sways away from the path of the sacrifice. He who does thus, indeed departs from the (right) path of the sacrifice. So just in the same way as they proceed with the vapās of the other paśus, they should follow the same way in the case of the vapā of this paśu. In the same way as they proceed with the libations of the other paśus, in the same way they should proceed with the libation of this (paśu). So they should not present to the common folk among men.
</details>


## ०७

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थैता᳘न्त्सप्त᳘दश प्राजापत्या᳘न्पशूना᳘लभते ते स᳘र्वे तूपरा᳘ भवन्ति स᳘र्वे श्यामास्स᳘र्वे मुष्करा᳘ एतद्वै᳘ प्रत्य᳘क्षं प्रजा᳘पतिमुज्जयति य᳘देता᳘नाल᳘भते᳘ऽन्नं वै᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिर्य᳘दि वा अ᳘न्नं प्रजा᳘पतिरेतद्वै᳘ प्रत्य᳘क्षम᳘न्नं यत्प᳘शुर्य᳘दि वै सो᳘मः प्रजा᳘पतिरेष वै᳘ प्रत्य᳘क्षꣳ सो᳘मो य᳘त्पशुस्त᳘त्प्रत्य᳘क्षं प्रजा᳘पतिमु᳘ज्जयति ॥ ७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Then they seize there seventeen paśus belonging to Prajāpati. All of them are hornless, all of them having a black spot in the fore-head; all having large testicles. This is what directly wins Prajāpati, when this (kind of paśu) is slaughtered. Prajāpati is forsooth, food. If food is Prajāpati, this (type of) paśu is the visible food. If soma is Prajāpati, this (type of) paśu is the visible soma. So directly he wins Prajāpati.
</details>


## ०८

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते वै᳘ सप्त᳘दश भवन्ति सप्तदशो हि᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिः स᳘र्वे तूपरा᳘ भवन्ति पु᳘रुषो वा᳘ अयं ने᳘दिष्ठं प्रजा᳘पतेः᳘ सोऽयं᳘ तूप᳘रोऽविषाण᳘स्तूपरो वा᳘ अविषाणः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिस्त᳘स्मात्स᳘र्वे तूपराः स᳘र्वे श्यामा द्वे वै᳘ श्याम᳘स्य रूपे᳘ शुक्लं᳘ चैव᳘ कृष्णं᳘ च द्वन्द्व᳘मु वै᳘ मिथुनं᳘ प्रज᳘ननं प्रज᳘ननमु वै᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिस्त᳘स्मात्स᳘र्वे श्यामाः स᳘र्वे मुष्कराः᳘ प्रज᳘ननः हि᳘ मुष्करः᳘ प्रज᳘ननꣳ हि᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिस्त᳘स्मात्स᳘र्वे मुष्करास्तान्य᳘द्येव᳘ꣳ᳘ समृद्धानेता᳘न्पशू᳘न्विन्दे᳘देत᳘ एव᳘ स्युर्य᳘द्येव᳘ꣳ᳘ समृद्धान्न᳘ विन्देद᳘पि य᳘ एव के᳘ च पश᳘वस्स्युः स᳘र्वꣳ हि᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिः ॥ ८ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

They (paśus) happen to be seventeen, for, Prajāpati is seventeenfold. They are all hornless. This man is the nearest to Prajāpati and he is Tūpara, i.e. without horns. Prajāpati is also Tūpara, without horns. So all (the seventeen paśus) are hornless. All of them are śyāmā (dark grey in colour). Śyāmā consists of two colours; the white and the black, the two together forming a productive pair. Prajāpati is, indeed, productive. So all of them are śyāmā. All of them have large testicles. Testicles are the reproductive parts. Prajāpati is productive. So all of them have large testicles. Let him perform it with all these paśus complete in all these (stipulated) respects. If he does not get such complete ones (as stipulated) whatever (type of) paśu he might obtain, let him have them. For, Prajāpati is everything.
</details>


## ०९

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थैते᳘षामे᳘वोत्तमं᳘ वाच आ᳘लभते य᳘दि वै᳘ प्रजा᳘पतेः प᳘रम᳘स्ति वा᳘गेव तत्ते᳘नो वा᳘चमु᳘ज्जयति त᳘दु त᳘था न᳘ कुर्यात्स᳘र्वं वा᳘ इदं᳘ प्रजा᳘पतिर्य᳘दिमे᳘ लोका᳘ ए᳘षो वा᳘ इयं᳘ लोके᳘षु वा᳘ग्वदति ते᳘नो एव वा᳘चमु᳘ज्जयति त᳘स्मात्प्राजापत्या᳘ एव᳘ स्युः ॥ ९ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The last one of these (seventeen paśus) is seized for the vāk (speech). For, if there is anything beyond Prajāpati it is only speech. So thereby he wins that speech. Let him not do so. Prajāpati is all these that form these worlds. This speech is what is spoken in these worlds. So by that itself (by winning Prajāpati) he wins the speech. So all of them should be for Prajāpati.
</details>


## १०

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ते᳘षामावृद्य᳘त्र मैत्रावरुणो᳘ वामदेव्य᳘मनुश᳘ꣳ᳘सति त᳘देषां वपा᳘भिः प्रचरेयुः प्रजा᳘पतिर्वै᳘ वामदेव्यं᳘ प्राजापत्या᳘ उ वा᳘ एते त᳘स्मादेषां त᳘त्र वपा᳘भिः प्र᳘चरेयुः ॥ १० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

The procedure with these (paśus) is as follows—The Maitrā-Varuṇa recites the śastra after the Vāmadevya sāman is sung. Thence they should proceed with the vapās of these paśus. For Vāmadevya sāman is (of) Prajāpati. These (paśus) belong to Prajāpati. So in that context (just after the Vāmadevya sāman), they should proceed with the vapās of those (paśus).
</details>


## ११

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अ᳘थेष्ट्वा᳘ अनुयाजाः स्युरव्यू᳘ळ्हे स्रु᳘चाव᳘थैषाꣳ हवि᳘र्भिः प्र᳘चरेयुरेतद्वै᳘ प्रत्य᳘क्षं प्रजा᳘पतिमु᳘ज्जयति य᳘देतैः᳘ प्रच᳘रन्त्य᳘न्तो वा᳘ एष᳘ यज्ञ᳘स्यान्तत᳘ ए᳘वैत᳘त्प्रजा᳘पतिमु᳘ज्जयत्य᳘थ यद्धा᳘तः पुरा᳘ प्रच᳘रेयुर्य᳘था यम᳘ध्वानमीयात्तं᳘ गत्वा᳘ क्व च त᳘तस्स्यात्त᳘स्माद᳘त्रैव प्र᳘चरेयुः ॥ ११ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

Now, when the anuyājās (after offerings) are made, and when the pair of sruks have not been separated, then they should proceed with their libations. Their proceeding with them (libations of the seventeen paśus) is the direct winning of Prajāpati. This is the end of the sacrifice. Only at the end of the sacrifice, he wins Prajāpati. If they happen to proceed with these libations earlier, it would be just as if a man had already gone the way (and reached his destination) and still intends to proceed—and where would he be after that? So only here (at the end) they should proceed with these libations.
</details>


## १२

<details open><summary>मूलम्</summary>

त᳘दु त᳘था न᳘ कुर्याद्ध्व᳘लति वै स यो᳘ यज्ञपथादेत्ये᳘त्यु वा᳘ एष᳘ यज्ञपथाद्य᳘ एवं᳘ करो᳘ति त᳘स्माद्य᳘त्रैवे᳘तरेषां पशूनां᳘ वपा᳘भिः प्रच᳘रेयुस्त᳘देवैषां वपा᳘भिः प्र᳘चरेयुर्य᳘त्रैवे᳘तेषां पशूना᳘ꣳ᳘ हवि᳘र्भिः प्रच᳘रेयुस्त᳘दे᳘वैषाꣳ हवि᳘र्भिः प्र᳘चरेयु᳘रेकानुवाक्यै᳘का या᳘ज्यैकदेव᳘त्या हि᳘ प्रजा᳘पतय इ᳘त्युपा᳘ꣳ᳘शूक्त्वा छा᳘गानाꣳ हवि᳘षाम᳘नुब्रूही᳘त्युच्चैः᳘ प्रजा᳘पतय इ᳘त्युपा᳘ꣳ᳘शूक्त्वा छा᳘गानाꣳ हवि᳘षां प्रस्थितं प्रेष्ये᳘त्युच्चैः ॥ १२ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

But let him not do it in this way, for, he who departs from the path of the sacrifice, stumbles and he who does it in this way, definitely departs from the path of the sacrifice. Hence, whenever they proceed with the vapās of the other paśus, at that same time, let them proceed with the vapās of these (paśus). Whenever the libations of other paśus take place, then only they should proceed with the libations of these paśus. There is only one anuvākya (invitatory chant) and one offering prayer (yājya). “These (offerings) belong to only one deity namely Prajāpati”. So saying in a low-tone, he should loudly say “Recite the invitatory chant for the offerings of the bucks” and again in a low voice “To Prajāpati” and again in a loud voice “Urge the offerings of the bucks that are standing ready”. (Third Brāhmaṇa Ends)
</details>
