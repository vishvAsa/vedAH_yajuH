+++
title = "३७ विज्ञानं ब्रह्म?"
+++

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स᳓ त᳓पस्तप्त्वा᳓ । (36) विज्ञा᳓नं ब्र᳓ह्मे᳓ति व्य᳙जानात् ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>त्रिभुवनः</summary>

Having performed penance:— He understood knowledge as Brahman.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स तप॑स्त॒प्त्वा । (36) वि॒ज्ञानं॒ ब्रह्मेति॒ व्य॑जानात् ।
</details>
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

विज्ञा᳓नाद्ध्ये᳙व खल्विमा᳓नि भू᳓तानि जा᳓यन्ते ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>त्रिभुवनः</summary>

For from knowledge, verily, these beings are born;
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

वि॒ज्ञाना॒द्ध्ये॑व खल्वि॒मानि॒ भूता॑नि॒ जाय॑न्ते ।
</details>
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

विज्ञा᳓नेन जा᳓तानि जी᳓वन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>त्रिभुवनः</summary>

by knowledge, when born, they live;
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

वि॒ज्ञाने॑न॒ जाता॑नि॒ जीव॑न्ति ।
</details>
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

विज्ञा᳓नं प्र᳓यन्त्यभि᳓सव्ँ᳓ विशन्ती᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>त्रिभुवनः</summary>

to knowledge they go and merge (thereinto).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

वि॒ज्ञानं॒ प्रय॑न्त्य॒भिसव्ँ वि॑श॒न्तीति॑ ।
</details>
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓द्विज्ञा᳓य । पु᳓नरेव᳓ व᳓रुणं पि᳓तरमु᳓पससार ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>त्रिभुवनः</summary>

Having understood that, he went again to (his) father Varuṇa (and said):
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तद्वि॒ज्ञाय॑ । पुन॑रे॒व वरु॑णं॒ पित॑र॒मुप॑ससार ।
</details>
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अ᳓धीहि भगवो ब्र᳓ह्मे᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>त्रिभुवनः</summary>

"Teach (me) Brahman, Sir."
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अधी॑हि भगवो॒ ब्रह्मेति॑ ।
</details>
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

तँ᳓ होवाच । त᳓पसा ब्र᳓ह्म वि᳓जिज्ञासस्व ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>त्रिभुवनः</summary>

To him he said: "By penance desire to know Brahman;
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तँ हो॑वाच । तप॑सा॒ ब्रह्म॒ विजि॑ज्ञासस्व ।
</details>
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓पो ब्र᳓ह्मे᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>त्रिभुवनः</summary>

penance is Brahman."
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तपो॒ ब्रह्मेति॑ ।
</details>
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स᳓ त᳓पोऽतप्यत ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>त्रिभुवनः</summary>

He performed penance.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स तपो॑ऽतप्यत ।
</details>
</details>
