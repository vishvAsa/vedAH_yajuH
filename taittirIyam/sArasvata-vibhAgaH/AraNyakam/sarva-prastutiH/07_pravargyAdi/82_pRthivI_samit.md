<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

पृथिवी᳓ समि᳓त् ।  
</details>

<details><summary>त्रिभुवनः</summary>

1\. The earth is the fuel;
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

पृ॒थि॒वी स॒मित् ।  
</details>

<details><summary>भट्टभास्कर-टीका</summary>

पृथिव्येव इयं समित् ।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ता᳓मग्नि᳓स्स᳓मिन्धे ।
</details>

<details><summary>त्रिभुवनः</summary>

the fire burned that,
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ताम॒ग्निस्समि॑न्धे ।
</details>

<details><summary>भट्टभास्कर-टीका</summary>

तामग्निस्समिन्धे दीपयति ।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

सा᳓ऽग्निँ᳓ स᳓मिन्धे ।
</details>

<details><summary>त्रिभुवनः</summary>

that (earth) burned the fire;
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

साऽग्निँ समि॑न्धे ।
</details>

<details><summary>भट्टभास्कर-टीका</summary>

साऽग्निं समिन्धे समेधयति ।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ता᳓महँ᳓ स᳓मिन्धे ।
</details>

<details><summary>त्रिभुवनः</summary>

I burned that (earth),
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ताम॒हँ समि॑न्धे ।
</details>

<details><summary>भट्टभास्कर-टीका</summary>

तामहं समिन्धे समेधयामि ।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

सा᳓ मा स᳓मिद्धा ।  
आ᳓युषा ते᳓जसा ।  
व᳓र्चसा श्रिया᳓ ।  
य᳓शसा ब्रह्मवर्चसे᳓न ।  
अन्ना᳓द्येन स᳓मिन्ताँ स्वा᳓हा ।
</details>

<details><summary>त्रिभुवनः</summary>

may that, being burnt, burn me (make me shine) with life, brilliance, splendour, prosperity, fame, brilliance of sacred knowledge[P206_6], food etc., Hail![P205_r1_3]

[P206_6]: Brilliance of sacred knowledge: divine intuition.
[P205_r1_3]: *(Mantras 1–3)* Mantras for offering the Udumbara wood.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

सा मा॒ समि॑द्धा ।  
आयु॑षा॒ तेज॑सा ।  
वर्च॑सा श्रि॒या ।  
यश॑सा ब्रह्मवर्च॒सेन॑ ।  
अ॒न्नाद्ये॑न॒ समि॑न्ताँ॒ स्वाहा॑ ।
</details>

<details><summary>भट्टभास्कर-टीका</summary>

सा मा समिद्धा समिद्धा सा समिद् आयुरादिभिर् मां समिन्तां सन्दीपयतु ॥
</details>
