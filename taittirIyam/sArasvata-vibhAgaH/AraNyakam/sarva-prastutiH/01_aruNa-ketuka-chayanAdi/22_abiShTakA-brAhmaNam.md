<details><summary>त्रिभुवनः - विनियोगः</summary>

Brāhmaṇas (praises etc.) relating to the placing of bricks. 1–9. Waters as the abodes of the things.
</details>

<details><summary>त्रिभुवनः</summary>

1\. He, who knows the flower of the waters, becomes the possessor of the flower, progeny and cattle.

[P109_1]: The moon is considered Amṛtamaya (possessor of nectar), and therefore, the flower of the waters.
