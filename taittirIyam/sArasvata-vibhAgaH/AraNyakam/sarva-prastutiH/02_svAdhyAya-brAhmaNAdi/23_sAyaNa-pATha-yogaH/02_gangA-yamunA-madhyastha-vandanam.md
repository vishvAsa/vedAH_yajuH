नमो गङ्गायमुनयोर्मध्ये ये वसन्ति  
ते मे प्रसन्नात्मानश्चिरं जीवितं वर्धयन्ति
</details>

<details><summary>त्रिभुवनः</summary>

3\. Salutation to those, who live between the Ganga and the Yamuna; may they, pleasant-minded ones increase our life;
</details>
