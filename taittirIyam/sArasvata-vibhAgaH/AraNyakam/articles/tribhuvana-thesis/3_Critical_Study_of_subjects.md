1 The Philosophical & Religious Subjects:-

This Aranyaka contains two Upanisads, the Taittiriya and the Narayana; besides these there are many philosophical ideals scattered in the whole Aranyaka. Let us deal with them in various headings:-

Atman, Brahman, Puruss, Aka ara, Paratma, Narayana-

These words have been used for the Supreme Universal Soul. It has been said that Atman was first one, non-second and that is in all beings. "Through fear of him the wind blows, through fear the sun rises, through fear of him Indra and Death move." (T.A. 8.8.1). Atman is across the water, in the world, at the back of the heaven, greater than the great'. In that supreme and immortal one are all beings. He has pervaded the sky, the heaven, the earth. By him the sun shines.

That has been said as to be our brother, father, disposer; he is knowledge, he is know able. Whence words turn back together with the mind without reaching, he who knows that bliss of Braham an fears not from any side.' Atman (Self) is less than an atom and greater than the great; that has been put in the cave (heart) of all beings. Seven breaths are born of him; seven rays, fuels and seven tongues (seven flames of the fire), these seven words in which the beings move all these sevenfold ones are established by that soul which is put in the cave. All the seas and the mountains are born of him. Various-formed rivers flow, from that came forth all the plants and the elixirs connected with which the inner soul lives. That Atman

This is the modern Ganga. In the Rg.Veda this word comes time. This word comes again in the satapatha Brahmana only stone (13.5.4.11) and in the Taittiriya Aranyaka (2.20).

The Yamuna:-

This river flows with the Ganga. This word comes in the Rg.Vede, in the Atharva Veds, in the Aitareys Brahmans, in the Satapatha Brahmana and in the Taittiriya Aranyaka.

The Sarasvati:-

This river is mentioned in the Ṛgveda and also in the later Vedic literature. But this vanishes in the desert afterwards. In the RgVeda when the word Sarasvati occurs, the modern scholars think that to be a river of Punjab. But in this Aranyaka this word has been used for that Sarasvati which vanishes in the desert. It is certain that this river vanished in the desert in the time of the Brahmanas. This was the famous river of Kurukgetra.

The Sutud ri:-

This is the shatalaj river of the modern time. This river has changed its ways many times.

The Parusn1:-

This name comes in the Nadi-Stuti (praise of the rivers). This is the modern Rabi flowing in Punjab.

The Asikni-

This is a river which was called Candrabhage afterwards and which is now called Chenab of Punjab. The word Asikni means a river having the black water.

The Merudvrdha:-

This name also comes in the 'Wadi-stuti (praise of the rivers) and also in the Nirukts. This word means a river which is increased by the wind. This is according to some in Punjab as the

The Susoma:

This is the river of Punjab. But there are differences of opinions about its real place. This is perhaps the Indus river.

The Apaya

This is a river about which there is difference of opinions. Ludwig thinks it as the other name of the Ganges. Zimmer thinks it as the Indramati river of Thaneshwar of the modern time. Pischel thinks it to be a river of Kuruksetra flowing only in the rainy seasons.

In the text there occur many words, which prove that the development of the Grammar was obtaining honour in the literary sphere. In the text the following words occur:- sike, Verne, Svara, Mátra, Bala, sama, Sastan, Samhita, Upanisad, Nirukta and Kalpa. These words show that the grammar was in developed form.
