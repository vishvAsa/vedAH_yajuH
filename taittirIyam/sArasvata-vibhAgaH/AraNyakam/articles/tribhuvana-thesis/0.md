## Preface

Aranyakas deserve high place in Indien Philosophy and Vedic studies. The Taittiriya Aranyaka is an important one; but it has not been studied critically in the modern world.

Aranyakas of the

Rgveda Aitareya and sankhayana or Kausitaki have been translated by the famous writer Prof. A.B. Keith; Brhadaranyaka of the white Yajurveda has been also translated by J. Eggeling, the learned translator of the Satapatha Brahmana; but the Taittiriya Aranyaka has not even been translated. This is a very lengthy Aranyaka having many difficult and obscure passages as well as many beautiful passages which threw abundant light on the philosophy, history, society and culture of ancient India. So many words of this Arenyske have not come in any dictionary and Bloomfield has not noted many passages of this Aranyaka in his vedio Concordance. astonished why this excellent Aranyaka has been neglected in this modern age. This is why I took it for my Research. I believe that the critical study and the translation of this Aranyaka will be a great contribution to our knowledge of Indology.

In a short time of about two years it was difficult to finish it; but my respected Prof. Dr. Surya Kanta Shastri gave me inspiration, respected Pt. Rama Chandra Meleviya helped me, not only with the books but also by the great kindness and love; in this way they enabled me to perform this great work. I am afraid whether the mere thanks giving can show my great honour to these kind scholars. I am very much grateful to them and I dedicate this work to them.

[[P003]]

I thank my typist, shri B.K. Mukherjee, who was helpful to me in many ways. My brothers have also helped and impelled me to be successful; but how can I thank them? I have also to thank my other Professors who encouraged me and suggested me.

In a work of this kind which has been completed in less than two years, it is possible that there may remain some faults or it is so, I beg apology of my Readers kindly to excuse missings. me.

Dated: 31st March, 1958\.

Tribhuwan Lal

Banaras Hindu University.
