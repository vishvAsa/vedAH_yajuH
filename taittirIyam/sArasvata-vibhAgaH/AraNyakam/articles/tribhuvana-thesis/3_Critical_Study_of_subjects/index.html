The Critical Study of the subjects discussed

1 The Philosophical & Religious Subjects:-

This Aranyaka contains two Upanisads, the Taittiriya and the Narayana; besides these there are many philosophical ideals scattered in the whole Aranyaka. Let us deal with them in various headings:-

Atman, Brahman, Puruss, Aka ara, Paratma, Narayana-

These words have been used for the Supreme Universal Soul. It has been said that Atman was first one, non-second and that is in all beings. “Through fear of him the wind blows, through fear the sun rises, through fear of him Indra and Death move.” (T.A. 8.8.1). Atman is across the water, in the world, at the back of the heaven, greater than the great’. In that supreme and immortal one are all beings. He has pervaded the sky, the heaven, the earth. By him the sun shines.

That has been said as to be our brother, father, disposer; he is knowledge, he is know able. Whence words turn back together with the mind without reaching, he who knows that bliss of Braham an fears not from any side.’ Atman (Self) is less than an atom and greater than the great; that has been put in the cave (heart) of all beings. Seven breaths are born of him; seven rays, fuels and seven tongues (seven flames of the fire), these seven words in which the beings move all these sevenfold ones are established by that soul which is put in the cave. All the seas and the mountains are born of him. Various-formed rivers flow, from that came forth all the plants and the elixirs connected with which the inner soul lives. That Atman

Like any river that flows from an undecaying source, is the time or year; as the other little rivers meet that river and that becoming great does not vanish; similarly variously rising little and great divisions of time are dependent upon year (Samvatsara); all of them approach into the wide year. That year filled with all those divisions of time being does not vanish. The year, mounted on by the little and great divisions of time, is observed by the perception; but the stronger One (Adhisattva), that is Eternal Time is not observed so. Here, in reality, neither there is a being, nor sun; only Samvatsara (Eternal Time), indeed, resides.
