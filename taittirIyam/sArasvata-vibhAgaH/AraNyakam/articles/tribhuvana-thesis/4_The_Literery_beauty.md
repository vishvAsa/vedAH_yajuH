There is some flow and impressing power in the description and the depiction of the matters in this Aranyaka. Many stories have been invented to give impression of any abstract idea. For example we may take the mythology of the Pravargya sacrifice. gods began a great sacrificial sessions Visnu obtained fame in that session. Visnu wished that very much, not to distribute among the gods; he escaped away with that, the gods followed him to check the fame; of him, being followed, the bow was born from the left and the arrows were born from the right; therefore, arrow and bow have pious birth, for they are born of the sacrifice; many gods could not defeat Visnu, who was alone; therefore, many men, having no arrow and bow do not defeat one who has arrow and bow. He laughed, 'many gods could not defeat me alone'; from him, laughing in pride, the brilliance ran away; the gods hided that in the plants; they became the Syamaks plants having the names Smayakes caused by the laughter. He stood holding firmly his bow on the earth. Those white ants spoke to the gods, "we pray for the boon, we will perform this for you; whenever we dig, may we wet that by the waters; therefore, whenever the white ants dig, they wet that by the waters; for that is chosed by them; they ate even the rope of his (Vienu's) bow; his bow going forcefully above out and put his head above; that moved following the heaven and the earth.

It is enough to show the bad result of the pride.

The description of the nature is also attractive. The six seasons have been described in metaphorical language.
