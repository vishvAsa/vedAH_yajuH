Now we have to discuss the Kandanukrams, the Kalpa Sutras and the Taittiriya Pratisakhya. The Kandanukrama is of the Atreyi Sakha of the Black Yajurveda. The text of the Aranyaka is found discussed in that work along with the Samhita and the Brahmana. There are five Kalpasutras connected with the Taittiriya sakha of the Veda.

These are:-

1. The Baudhayana Srautasutra

2. The Apastamba srautasutra

3. The Hiranyakesi or Satyasadha Srautasutra

4. The Bharadvaja srautasutra

5. The Manava Srautasutra

Among these the Baudhayana and the Apastamba schools have the complete texts and they are more concordant with the texts of the Taittiriya Sakha than the other Sutras. The Baudhayana Srautasutra is the oldest one. The Sutras have dealt with the text of the Aranyaka. The Sutras do not make any difference between the Samhita, Brahmana or the Aranyaka. They only mention Mantra and Brahmana, may they be of the Samhita, of the Brahmana or of the Aranyaka.

The Pada Paths of the Samhita is only found. There is no Pada Patha for the Brahmans or the Aranyakas. The Pratisakhya also does not deal with these texts.

There are many passages in this Aranyaka which appear at the first time and which have been variously quoted in the later works.
