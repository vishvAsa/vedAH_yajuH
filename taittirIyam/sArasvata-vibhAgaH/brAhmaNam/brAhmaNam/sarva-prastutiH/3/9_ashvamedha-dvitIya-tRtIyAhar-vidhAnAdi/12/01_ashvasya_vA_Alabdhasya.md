<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

1. When the horse was slaughtered, the life-sap [^egg_871] went out of it;

[^egg_871]: 341:4 Or, sacrificial essence.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

From the horse that was slaughtered, its Medhas (life-sap) came out.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

When the horse was seized, its sacrificial essence went out.
</details>
