<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

श᳓रीरँ हृ᳓दये ।  
हृ᳓दयम्म᳓यि ।  
अह᳓ममृ᳓ते ।  
अमृ᳓तम्ब्र᳓ह्मणि ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

The earth rests in my body; the body in the heart; the heart in me; I in the immortal; the immortal in Brahman.
</details>

</details>
