<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He hath overcome every foe, every enemy;  
That Agni saith, that saith Soma too;  
Brhaspati, Savitr, say this of me,  
Pusan hath placed me in the world of good action.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

I have overcome all battles, malicious forces; that Agni said, and that Soma said;  
Bṛhaspati, Savitṛ, Indra said that; Pūṣan placed us in the world of the well-doer.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

He has stepped upon all hostile armies; so Agni has said, and so Soma has said;  
Bṛhaspati, Savitṛ, Indra have said that; may Pūṣan place me in the world of the well-done!
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - तैत्तिरीयम्</summary>

अ॒भ्य॑स्था॒द्विश्वा॒ᳶ पृत॑ना॒ अरा॑ती॒स्तद॒ग्निरा॑ह॒ तदु॒ सोम॑ आह ।  
बृह॒स्पति॑स्सवि॒ता तन्म॑ आह पु॒षा मा॑धात्सुकृ॒तस्य॑ लो॒के ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

अभ्य᳙स्थाँ वि᳓श्वाः पृ᳓तना अ᳓रातीस् त᳓द् अग्नि᳓र् आह त᳓द् उ सो᳓म आह  
बृ᳓हस्प᳓तिः सविते᳓न्द्रस् त᳓द् आह पूषा᳓ ना आ᳓धात् सुकृत᳓स्य लोके᳓
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काठकम्</summary>

अभ्यस्थाद्विश्वाः पृतना अरातीस्तदग्निराह तदु सोम आह।  
बृहस्पतिस्सवितेन्द्रस्तदाह पूषा माधात्सुकृतस्य लोके
</details>
