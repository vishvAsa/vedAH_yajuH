<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Homage [1] to the ruddy one, the ruler, to the lord of woods homage!
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Homage to the red builder,  
homage to the lord of trees!
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - तैत्तिरीयम्</summary>

नमो॒ रोहि॑ताय स्थ॒पत॑ये वृ॒क्षाणा॒म्पत॑ये॒ नमः॑ ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

न᳓मो रो᳓हिताय स्थप᳓तये  
वृक्षा᳓णां प᳓तये न᳓मस्
</details>

<details><summary>पद-पाठः</summary>

नमः॑ । रोहि॑ताय । स्थ॒पत॑ये । वृ॒क्षाणा॑म् । पत॑ये । नमः॑ ।
</details>

तस्मै रुद्राय नमोऽस्तु।
