<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The gods, destroyers of the sacrifice, stealers of the sacrifice,  
That sit in the sky,  
From them may Surya guard me;  
May we go to those that do good deeds.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

The gods that slay the sacrifice, who sit in the sky:  
from them may Sūrya protect us; may we go to the well-doers!
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

Those gods, slayers of the sacrifice, thieves of the sacrifice, who sit in heaven —  
may Sūrya protect me from them; may we go to the righteous!
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - तैत्तिरीयम्</summary>

ये दे॒वा य॑ज्ञ॒हनो॑ यज्ञ॒मुषो॑ दि॒व्यध्यास॑ते ।   
सूर्यो॑ मा॒ तेभ्यो॑ रक्षतु॒ गच्छे॑म सु॒कृतो॑ व॒यम् ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

ये᳓ देवा᳓ यज्ञह᳓नो दिव्य् अ᳓ध्य् आ᳓सते  
सू᳓र्यो नस् ते᳓भ्यो रक्षतु ग᳓छेम सुकृ᳓तो वय᳓म्
</details>

<details><summary>पद-पाठः</summary>

</details>
