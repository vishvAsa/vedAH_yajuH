<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The gods, destroyers of the sacrifice, stealers of the sacrifice,  
That sit in the atmosphere,  
From them may Vayu guard me;  
May we go to those that do good deeds.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

The gods that slay the sacrifice, who sit in the intermediate space:  
from them may Vāyu protect us; may we go to the well-doers!
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

Those gods, slayers of the sacrifice, thieves of the sacrifice, who sit in the intermediate space —  
may Vāyu protect me from them; may we go to the righteous!
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - तैत्तिरीयम्</summary>

ये दे॒वा य॑ज्ञ॒हनो॑ यज्ञ॒मुषो॒ऽन्तरि॒क्षेऽध्यास॑ते ।    
वा॒युर्मा॒ तेभ्यो॑ रक्षतु॒ गच्छे॑म सु॒कृतो॑ व॒यम् ।
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

ये᳓ देवा᳓ यज्ञह᳓नो अन्त᳓रिक्षे अ᳓ध्य् आ᳓सते  
वायु᳓र् नस् ते᳓भ्यो रक्षतु ग᳓छेम सुकृ᳓तो वय᳓म्
</details>
