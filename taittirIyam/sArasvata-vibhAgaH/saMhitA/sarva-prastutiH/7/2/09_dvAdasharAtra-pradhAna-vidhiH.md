## फलम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

प्र॒जाप॑तिर् अकामयत॒ - प्र जा॑ये॒येति॑ ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Prajapati desired, 'May I be propagated.'
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स ए॒तन् द्वा॑दशरा॒त्रम् अ॑पश्य॒त् । तमाह॑रत् ।  
तेना॑यजत ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He saw this (rite) of twelve nights; he grasped it, and sacrificed with it.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ततो॒ वै स प्राजा॑यत ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Then indeed was he propagated.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

यᳵ का॒मये॑त॒ - प्र जा॑ये॒येति॑, स द्वा॑दशरा॒त्रेण॑ यजेत । प्रैव जा॑यते ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He who desires, 'May I be propagated', should sacrifice with (the rite) of twelve nights; verily he is propagated.
</details>



## अतिरात्रारम्भः

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ब्र॒ह्म॒वा॒दिनो॑ वदन्त्य् - "अग्निष्टो॒म-प्रा॑यणा+++(=आरम्भणा)+++ य॒ज्ञा +++(द्विरात्रादयः)+++, अथ॒ कस्मा॑द् अतिरा॒त्रᳶ पूर्व॒ᳶ प्र यु॑ज्यत॒" इति॑ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The theologians say, 'The sacrifices have Agnistomas at the beginning; why then is Atiratra first employed?'
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

चक्षु॑षी॒ वा ए॒ते य॒ज्ञस्य॑ यद् अ॑तिरा॒त्रौ +++(द्वादशाहस्यादिमान्तिमौ)+++, क॒नीनि॑के+++(=चक्षुस्स्थं कृष्णमण्डलं)+++ अग्निष्टो॒मौ ।
यद् अ॑ग्निष्टो॒मम् पूर्व॑म् प्रयुञ्जी॒रन् ब॑हि॒र्धा क॒नीनि॑के दध्युः ।
तस्मा॑द् अतिरा॒त्रᳶ पूर्व॒ᳶ प्र यु॑ज्यते ।
चक्षु॑षी ए॒व य॒ज्ञे धि॒त्वा+++(=स्थापयित्वा)+++ म॑ध्य॒तᳵ क॒नीनि॑के॒ प्रति॑ दधति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The two Atiratras are the eyes of the sacrifice, the two Agnistomas the pupils; if [1] they were to employ the Agnistoma first, they would put the pupils outside; therefore the Atiratra is employed first; verily having inserted the eyes of the sacrifice they put the pupils in them.
</details>



## गायत्रीपक्षिरूपम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

यो वै गा॑य॒त्रीञ् ज्योतिᳶ॑+++(ष्टोम)+++पक्षाव्ँ॒ वेद॒, ज्योति॑षा भा॒सा सु॑व॒र्गल्ँ लो॒कम् ए॑ति ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He, who knows the Gayatri to have sides of light, goes to the world of heaven with light and radiance.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

याव् अ॑ग्निष्टो॒मौ +++(दशरात्रस्याद्यन्तौ)+++, तौ प॒क्षौ । येऽन्त॑रे॒ ऽष्टाव् उ॒क्थ्याः॑ स आ॒त्मा ।  
एषा वै गा॑य॒त्री, ज्योतिᳶ॑पक्षा ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The sides are the Agnistomas, the eight Ukthyas in between are the body; verily the Gayatri has sides of light.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य ए॒वव्ँ वेद॒, ज्योति॑षा भा॒सा सु॑व॒र्गल्ँ लो॒कम् ए॑ति (35)।  
+++(अतिरात्रनेत्रत्वम् पूर्वम् उक्तम्।)+++
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He who knows thus goes to the world of heaven with light and radiance [2].
</details>



## प्रजापतिरूपम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>प्र॒जाप॑तिर् वा ए॒ष द्वा॑दश॒धा विहि॑तो यद् द्वा॑दशरा॒त्रः ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The twelve nights' (rite) is Prajapati in twelve divisions.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

याव् अ॑तिरा॒त्रौ तौ प॒क्षौ ।
ये ऽन्त॑रे॒ ऽष्टावु॒क्थ्याः॑, स आ॒त्मा ।  
+++(सकनीनिके अस्य चक्षुषी उक्ते ॥)+++
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The two sides are the Atiratras, the eight Ukthyas within are the body; Prajapati in such guise touches truth with the Sattra, truth is the breaths; verily he touches the breaths.
</details>
