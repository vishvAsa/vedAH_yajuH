## फलम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

प्रजा᳓पतिर् अकामयत - प्र᳓ जायेये᳓ति ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Prajapati desired, 'May I be propagated.'
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स᳓ एत᳓न् द्वादशरात्र᳓म् अपश्यत् । त᳓मा᳓हरत् ।  
ते᳓नायजत ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He saw this (rite) of twelve nights; he grasped it, and sacrificed with it.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓तो वै᳓ स᳓ प्रा᳓जायत ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Then indeed was he propagated.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

यᳵ᳓ काम᳓येत - प्र᳓ जायेये᳓ति, स᳓ द्वादशरात्रे᳓ण यजेत । प्रै᳓व᳓ जायते ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He who desires, 'May I be propagated', should sacrifice with (the rite) of twelve nights; verily he is propagated.
</details>

## अतिरात्रारम्भः

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ब्रह्मवादि᳓नो वदन्त्य् - "अग्निष्टोम᳓-प्रायणा+++(=आरम्भणा)+++ यज्ञा᳓ +++(द्विरात्रादयः)+++, अ᳓थ क᳓स्माद् अतिरात्रᳶ᳓ पू᳓र्वᳶ प्र᳓ युज्यत" इ᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The theologians say, 'The sacrifices have Agnistomas at the beginning; why then is Atiratra first employed?'
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

च᳓क्षुषी वा᳓ एते᳓ यज्ञ᳓स्य य᳓द् अतिरात्रौ᳓ +++(द्वादशाहस्यादिमान्तिमौ)+++, कनी᳓निके+++(=चक्षुस्स्थं कृष्णमण्डलं)+++ अग्निष्टोमौ᳓ ।
य᳓द् अग्निष्टोम᳓म् पू᳓र्वम् प्रयुञ्जीर᳓न् बहिर्धा᳓ कनी᳓निके दध्युः ।
त᳓स्माद् अतिरात्रᳶ᳓ पू᳓र्वᳶ प्र᳓ युज्यते ।
च᳓क्षुषी एव᳓ यज्ञे᳓ धित्वा᳓+++(=स्थापयित्वा)+++ मध्यतᳵ᳓ कनी᳓निके प्र᳓ति दधति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The two Atiratras are the eyes of the sacrifice, the two Agnistomas the pupils; if [1] they were to employ the Agnistoma first, they would put the pupils outside; therefore the Atiratra is employed first; verily having inserted the eyes of the sacrifice they put the pupils in them.
</details>

## गायत्रीपक्षिरूपम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

यो᳓ वै᳓ गायत्री᳓ञ् ज्यो᳓तिᳶ+++(ष्टोम)+++पक्षाव्ँ वे᳓द, ज्यो᳓तिषा भासा᳓ सुवर्गल्ँ᳓ लोक᳓म् एति ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He, who knows the Gayatri to have sides of light, goes to the world of heaven with light and radiance.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

या᳓व् अग्निष्टोमौ᳓ +++(दशरात्रस्याद्यन्तौ)+++, तौ᳓ पक्षौ᳓ । ये᳓ऽन्तरे ऽष्टा᳓व् उक्थ्याः᳙ स आत्मा᳓ ।  
ए᳓षा᳓ वै᳓ गायत्री᳓, ज्यो᳓तिᳶपक्षा ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The sides are the Agnistomas, the eight Ukthyas in between are the body; verily the Gayatri has sides of light.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓ एवव्ँ᳓ वे᳓द, ज्यो᳓तिषा भासा᳓ सुवर्गल्ँ᳓ लोक᳓म् एति (35)।  
+++(अतिरात्रनेत्रत्वम् पूर्वम् उक्तम्।)+++
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He who knows thus goes to the world of heaven with light and radiance [2].
</details>

## प्रजापतिरूपम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>प्रजा᳓पतिर् वा एष᳓ द्वादशधा᳓ वि᳓हितो᳓ य᳓द् द्वादशरात्रः᳓ ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The twelve nights' (rite) is Prajapati in twelve divisions.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

या᳓व् अतिरात्रौ᳓ तौ᳓ पक्षौ᳓ ।
ये᳓ ऽन्तरे ऽष्टा᳓वुक्थ्याः᳙, स आत्मा᳓ ।  
+++(सकनीनिके अस्य चक्षुषी उक्ते ॥)+++
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The two sides are the Atiratras, the eight Ukthyas within are the body; Prajapati in such guise touches truth with the Sattra, truth is the breaths; verily he touches the breaths.
</details>

Those who perform the Sattra perform it with the breath of all people, and therefore they ask, 'Are these performers of a Sattra?'
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Dear to people and noble is he who knows thus.
</details>
