## फलम् सत्राणाम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

न वा ए॒षो॑ऽन्यतो॑वैश्वानरस् सुव॒र्गाय॑ लो॒काय॒ प्राभ॑वत् ।
ऊ॒र्ध्वो ह॒ वा ए॒ष +++(स्वर्ग)+++ आत॑त आसीत् ।
ते दे॒वा ए॒तव्ँ वै॑श्वान॒रम् पर्यौ॑हन्थ्+++(=पर्यवहन् [द्वादशाहेन])+++ ,
सुव॒र्गस्य॑ लो॒कस्य॒ प्रभू॑त्यै ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

This (rite) with a Vaiśvanara on one side (only) was not able to win the world of heaven; it was stretched upwards and the gods piled round this Vaiśvanara, to overcome the world of heaven.
</details>

## फलम् अहीनयागस्य

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ऋ॒तवो॒ +++(ऋत्विग्भूता)+++ वा ए॒तेन॑ प्र॒जाप॑तिम् अयाजयन् ।
+++(ऋत्विक्षु)+++ तेष्व् आ॑र्ध्नो॒द् अधि॒+++(कं)+++ तत्+++(=ततः)+++ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The seasons made Prajapati sacrifice with it, and in them because of it he prospered.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>ऋ॒ध्नोति॑ ह॒ वा ऋ॒त्विक्षु॒ य ए॒वव्ँ वि॒द्वान् द्वा॑दशा॒हेन॒ यज॑ते।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He prospers among the priests who knowing thus sacrifices with (the rite) of twelve days.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ते॑ ऽस्मिन्न् ऐच्छन्त +++(दक्षिणाम्)+++।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

They were desirous of obtaining (their desires) from him.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स रस॒म् अह॑ वस॒न्ताय॒ प्राय॑च्छत् ।
यव॑ङ् ग्री॒ष्मायौष॑धीर् व॒र्षाभ्यो॑, व्री॒हीञ् छ॒रदे॑, माषति॒लौ हे॑मन्तशिशि॒राभ्या॑म्।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He gave to the spring the sap [1], to the hot season barley, to the rains plants, to autumn rice, beans and sesamum to winter and the cool season.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>तेनेन्द्र॑म् प्र॒जाप॑तिर् अयाजयत् ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Prajapati made Indra sacrifice with it.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ततो॒ वा इन्द्र॒ इन्द्रो॑ ऽभवत् ।
तस्मा॑द् आहुर् - "आ॒नुजाव॒रस्य॑ य॒ज्ञ" इति॑ ।
स ह्य् ए॑तेनाग्रे ऽय॑जत +++(इन्द्रः)+++।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Then indeed did Indra become Indra; therefore they say, 'It is the sacrifice of the inferior', for he by it first sacrificed.
</details>



## दक्षिणा सत्त्रे

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ए॒ष ह॒ वै कु॒णप॑म्+++(=मांसम्)+++ अत्ति॒,
यस् स॒त्त्रे प्र॑तिगृ॒ह्णाति॑ +++(यतस् सत्त्रे सर्वे यजमानाः)+++ - पु॒रु॒ष॒कु॒ण॒पम् अ॑श्वकुण॒पम् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He eats a corpse who accepts a present at a Sattra; a human corpse or the corpse of a horse.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(दक्षिणा-)+++गौर् वा अन्न॑म् ।
येन॒ पात्रे॒णान्न॒म् बिभ्र॑ति, यत् तन् न नि॒र्णेनि॑जति, ततोऽधि॒ मल॑ञ् जायते । +++(एवम् पात्रतुल्यो प्रतिगृहीता।)+++[38]
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Food is the cow; in that he does not purify the vessel in which they carry food, filth is produced from it [2].
</details>



## अहीन-याग-गुणः

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

एक॑ ए॒व य॑जेत +++(अहीनयागेषु / असत्त्रयागेषु)+++।  
एको॒ हि प्र॒जाप॑ति॒र् आर्ध्नो॑त् ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

One should sacrifice by oneself, for Prajapati prospered by himself.
</details>



## यागसामान्ये दीक्षा

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

द्वाद॑श॒ रात्री॑र् दीक्षि॒तस् स्यात् ।  
द्वाद॑श॒ मासाः॑ सव्ँवत्स॒रः ।  
सव्ँ॑वत्स॒रᳶ प्र॒जाप॑तिः,
प्र॒जाप॑ति॒र् वाव+ए॒षः +++(याजकः)+++।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

One should be consecrated for twelve nights; the year consists of twelve months, Prajapati is the year, he is Prajapati,
</details>



## यागसामान्ये उपसद्

<details><summary>विश्वास-टिप्पनी</summary>

दीक्षायाः परं, सोमसवात् प्राग् भवति तत् कर्म।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ए॒ष ह॒ त्वै जा॑यते, यस् तप॒सोऽधि॒ जाय॑ते ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

he is born indeed who is born from fervour.
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

चतु॒र्धा वा ए॒तास् ति॒स्रस् ति॑स्रो॒ रात्र॑यो॒ यद् द्वाद॑शोप॒सदः॑  ।  
याᳶ प्र॑थ॒मा य॒ज्ञन् ताभि॒स् सम्भ॑रति ।  
या द्वि॒तीया॑ य॒ज्ञन्ताभि॒रा र॑भते [39]  ।  
यास् तृ॒तीया॒ᳶ पात्रा॑णि॒ ताभि॒र् निर्णे॑निक्ते ।  
याश् च॑तु॒र्थीर् अपि॒ ताभि॑र् आ॒त्मान॑म् अन्तर॒तश् शु॑न्धते ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The twelve Upasads are these four sets of three nights; with the first three he prepares the sacrifice, with the second three he grasps the sacrifice [3], with the third three he cleanses the vessels, and with the fourth three he purifies himself within.
</details>



## सत्त्र-दक्षिणा-निषेधः

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(तत्र दक्षिणा न भवति।)+++  
यो वा अ॑स्य प॒शुम् अत्ति॑ माँ॒सँ सो॑ऽत्ति ।
यᳶ पु॑रो॒डाश॑म् म॒स्तिष्कँ॒ सः +++(अत्ति)+++ ।
यᳶ प॑रिवा॒पम्+++(=लाजान्)+++ पुरी॑षँ॒ सः +++(अत्ति)+++ ।
य आज्य॑म् म॒ज्जानँ॒ सः +++(अत्ति)+++ ।
यस् सोमँ॒ स्वेदँ॒ सः +++(अत्ति)+++ ।
अपि॑ ह॒ वा अ॑स्य शीर्ष॒ण्या॑ नि॒ष्पद॒ᳶ +++(यूकादयः)+++ प्रति॑ गृह्णाति॒ , यो द्वा॑दशा॒हे प्र॑तिगृ॒ह्णाति॑ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He who eats his victim, eats his flesh, who eats his sacrificial cake, eats his brains, who eats his fried grain, eats his excrement, who eats his ghee, eats his marrow, who eats his Soma, eats his sweat, and also he eats the excrements from his head, who accepts (a present) at the twelve-day (rite).
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

तस्मा॑द् द्वादशा॒हेन॒ न याज्य॑म् +++(ऋत्विक्त्वेन)+++, पा॒प्मनो॒ व्यावृ॑त्त्यै ॥ [40]
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Therefore one should not cause one to sacrifice with the twelve-day (rite), to avert evil.
</details>
