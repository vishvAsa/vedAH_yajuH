## फलम् सत्राणाम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

न᳓ वा᳓ एषो᳙ऽन्य᳓तोवैश्वानरस् सुवर्गा᳓य लोका᳓य प्रा᳓भवत् ।
ऊर्ध्वो᳓ ह वा᳓ एष᳓ +++(स्वर्ग)+++ आ᳓तत आसीत् ।
ते᳓ देवा᳓ एतव्ँ᳓ वैश्वानर᳓म् प᳓र्यौहन्थ्+++(=पर्यवहन् [द्वादशाहेन])+++ ,
सुवर्ग᳓स्य लोक᳓स्य प्र᳓भूत्यै ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

This (rite) with a Vaiśvanara on one side (only) was not able to win the world of heaven; it was stretched upwards and the gods piled round this Vaiśvanara, to overcome the world of heaven.
</details>

## फलम् अहीनयागस्य

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ऋत᳓वो +++(ऋत्विग्भूता)+++ वा᳓ एते᳓न प्रजा᳓पतिम् अयाजयन् ।
+++(ऋत्विक्षु)+++ ते᳓ष्व् आर्ध्नोद् अ᳓धि+++(कं)+++ त᳓त्+++(=ततः)+++ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The seasons made Prajapati sacrifice with it, and in them because of it he prospered.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>ऋध्नो᳓ति ह वा᳓ ऋत्वि᳓क्षु य᳓ एवव्ँ᳓ विद्वा᳓न् द्वादशाहे᳓न य᳓जते।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He prospers among the priests who knowing thus sacrifices with (the rite) of twelve days.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ते᳓ ऽस्मिन्न् ऐच्छन्त +++(दक्षिणाम्)+++।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

They were desirous of obtaining (their desires) from him.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स᳓ र᳓सम् अ᳓ह वसन्ता᳓य प्रा᳓यच्छत् ।
य᳓वङ् ग्रीष्मा᳓यौ᳓षधीर् वर्षा᳓भ्यो, व्रीही᳓ञ् छर᳓दे, माषतिलौ᳓ हेमन्तशिशिरा᳓भ्याम्।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He gave to the spring the sap [1], to the hot season barley, to the rains plants, to autumn rice, beans and sesamum to winter and the cool season.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>ते᳓ने᳓न्द्रम् प्रजा᳓पतिर् अयाजयत् ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Prajapati made Indra sacrifice with it.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓तो वा᳓ इ᳓न्द्र इ᳓न्द्रो ऽभवत् ।
त᳓स्माद् आहुर् - "आनु᳓जा᳓वर᳓स्य यज्ञ᳓" इ᳓ति ।
स᳓ ह्य् एते᳓ना᳓ग्रे᳓ ऽयजत +++(इन्द्रः)+++।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Then indeed did Indra become Indra; therefore they say, 'It is the sacrifice of the inferior', for he by it first sacrificed.
</details>

## दक्षिणा सत्त्रे

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

एष᳓ ह वै᳓ कुण᳓पम्+++(=मांसम्)+++ अत्ति,
य᳓स् सत्त्रे᳓ प्रतिगृह्णा᳓ति +++(यतस् सत्त्रे सर्वे यजमानाः)+++ - पुरुषकुणप᳓म् अश्वकुणप᳓म् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He eats a corpse who accepts a present at a Sattra; a human corpse or the corpse of a horse.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(दक्षिणा-)+++गौ᳓र् वा᳓ अ᳓न्नम् ।
ये᳓न पा᳓त्रेणा᳓न्नम् बि᳓भ्रति, यत् तन् न निर्णे᳓निजति, ततोऽधि म᳓लञ् जायते । +++(एवम् पात्रतुल्यो प्रतिगृहीता।)+++[38]
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Food is the cow; in that he does not purify the vessel in which they carry food, filth is produced from it [2].
</details>

## अहीन-याग-गुणः

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ए᳓क एव᳓ यजेत +++(अहीनयागेषु / असत्त्रयागेषु)+++।  
ए᳓को हि᳓ प्रजा᳓पतिर् आ᳓र्ध्नोत् ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

One should sacrifice by oneself, for Prajapati prospered by himself.
</details>

## यागसामान्ये दीक्षा

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

द्वा᳓दश रा᳓त्रीर् दीक्षित᳓स् स्या᳓त् ।  
द्वा᳓दश मा᳓साः सव्ँवत्सरः᳓ ।  
सव्ँ᳓वत्सरᳶ᳓ प्रजा᳓पतिः,
प्रजा᳓पतिर् वा᳓व᳓+एषः᳓ +++(याजकः)+++।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

One should be consecrated for twelve nights; the year consists of twelve months, Prajapati is the year, he is Prajapati,
</details>

## यागसामान्ये उपसद्

<details><summary>विश्वास-टिप्पनी</summary>

दीक्षायाः परं, सोमसवात् प्राग् भवति तत् कर्म।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

एष᳓ ह त्वै᳓ जायते, यस् तपसो᳓ऽधि जा᳓यते ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

he is born indeed who is born from fervour.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

च᳓तुर्धा᳓ वा᳓ एता᳓स् तिस्र᳓स् तिस्रो रा᳓त्रयो य᳓द् द्वा᳓दशोपस᳓दः  ।  
याᳶ᳓ प्रथमा᳓ यज्ञ᳓न् ता᳓भिस् स᳓म्भरति ।  
या᳓ द्विती᳓या यज्ञ᳓न्ता᳓भिरा᳓ रभते [39]  ।  
या᳓स् तृती᳓याᳶ पा᳓त्राणि ता᳓भिर् नि᳓र्णेनिक्ते ।  
या᳓श् चतुर्थी᳓र् अ᳓पि ता᳓भिर् आत्मा᳓नम् अन्तरत᳓श् शुन्धते ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The twelve Upasads are these four sets of three nights; with the first three he prepares the sacrifice, with the second three he grasps the sacrifice [3], with the third three he cleanses the vessels, and with the fourth three he purifies himself within.
</details>

## सत्त्र-दक्षिणा-निषेधः

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(तत्र दक्षिणा न भवति।)+++  
यो᳓ वा᳓ अस्य पशु᳓म् अ᳓त्ति माँसँ᳓ सोऽत्ति ।
यᳶ᳓ पुरोडा᳓शम् मस्ति᳓ष्कँ सः᳓ +++(अत्ति)+++ ।
यᳶ᳓ परिवाप᳓म्+++(=लाजान्)+++ पु᳓रीषँ सः᳓ +++(अत्ति)+++ ।
य᳓ आ᳓ज्यम् मज्जा᳓नँ सः᳓ +++(अत्ति)+++ ।
य᳓स् सो᳓मँ स्वे᳓दँ सः᳓ +++(अत्ति)+++ ।
अ᳓पि ह वा᳓ अस्य शीर्षण्या᳙ निष्प᳓दᳶ +++(यूकादयः)+++ प्र᳓ति गृह्णाति , यो᳓ द्वादशाहे᳓ प्रतिगृह्णा᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He who eats his victim, eats his flesh, who eats his sacrificial cake, eats his brains, who eats his fried grain, eats his excrement, who eats his ghee, eats his marrow, who eats his Soma, eats his sweat, and also he eats the excrements from his head, who accepts (a present) at the twelve-day (rite).
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓स्माद् द्वादशाहे᳓न न᳓ या᳓ज्यम् +++(ऋत्विक्त्वेन)+++, पाप्म᳓नो व्या᳓वृत्त्यै ॥ [40]
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Therefore one should not cause one to sacrifice with the twelve-day (rite), to avert evil.
</details>
