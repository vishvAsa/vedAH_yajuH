</details>

## फलम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>प्र॒जाप॑तिर् अकामयत - प्र जा॑ये॒येति॑ ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Prajapati desired, 'May I be propagated.'
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स ए॒तन् दश॑होतारम्+++(='चित्तिस्स्रुक्' इत्यादिकं)+++ अपश्यत् ।  
तम् अ॑जुहो॒त् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He saw this Daśahotr, and offered it.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

तेन॑ दशरा॒त्रम् अ॑सृजत ।
तेन॑ दशरा॒त्रेण॒ प्राजा॑यत ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

By this he created (the rite) of ten nights, and by this (rite) often nights he was propagated.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

द॒श॒रा॒त्राय॑ दीक्षि॒ष्यमा॑णो॒ दश॑होतारञ् जुहुयात्।  
दश॑होत्रै॒व द॑शरा॒त्रँ सृ॑जते ।  
तेन॑ दशरा॒त्रेण॒ प्र जा॑यते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

If one is about to consecrate oneself for (the rite) of ten nights one should offer the Daśahotr; verily he creates by the Daśahotr (the rite) of ten nights, and by (the rite) of ten nights he is propagated.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(दशत्वान्वयाद्)+++ वैरा॒जो वा ए॒ष य॒ज्ञो यद् द॑शरा॒त्रः ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The sacrifice of ten nights is connected with the Viraj.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>य ए॒वव्ँ वि॒द्वान् द॑शरा॒त्रेण॒ यज॑ते, वि॒राज॑म् ए॒व ग॑च्छति ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He, who knowing thus sacrifices with (the rite) of ten nights, attains the Viraj.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

प्रा॒जा॒प॒त्यो वा ए॒ष य॒ज्ञो , यद् द॑शरा॒त्रः [13] ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The sacrifice of ten nights is connected with Prajapati [1].
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य ए॒वव्ँ वि॒द्वान् द॑शरा॒त्रेण॒ यज॑ते॒ , प्रैव जा॑यते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He, who knowing thus sacrifices with (the rite) of ten nights, is propagated.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

इन्द्रो॒ वै स॒दृङ् दे॒वता॑भिर् आसीत् । स न व्या॒वृत॑म् अगच्छत् ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Indra was on a level with the gods, he was not separated from them.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स प्र॒जाप॑ति॒म् उपा॑धावत् । तस्मा॑ ए॒तन् द॑शरा॒त्रम् प्राय॑च्छ॒त् ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He ran up to Prajapati; he gave him this (rite) of ten nights.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

तमाह॑रत् । तेना॑यजत ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He grasped it, and sacrificed with it.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ततो॒ वै सो॑ऽन्याभि॑र् दे॒वता॑भिर् व्या॒वृत॑म् अगच्छत् ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Then indeed he became separated from the other gods.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य ए॒वव्ँ वि॒द्वान् द॑शरा॒त्रेण॒ यज॑ते , व्या॒वृत॑म् ए॒व पा॒प्मना॒ भ्रातृ॑व्येण गच्छति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He, who knowing thus sacrifices with (the rite) of ten nights, attains separation from the evil enemy.
</details>

## विधानम्

<details><summary>विश्वास-टिप्पनी</summary>

त्रिवृदग्नीष्टोमः, १५, त्रिवृदग्नीष्टोमः। १७, २१, १७। २७, ३३, २७। विश्वजित्।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त्रि॒क॒कुद् वा ए॒ष य॒ज्ञो यद् द॑शरात्रः।
+++(त्रिवृद्-अग्निष्टोमाव् अभितः)+++ क॒कुत्+++(→उन्नतः)+++ प॑ञ्चद॒शः।  
+++(सप्तदशाग्निष्टोमाव् अभितः)+++ क॒कुद् ए॑कविँ॒शः ।  
+++(त्रिणवाग्निष्टोमाव् अभितः)+++ क॒कुत् त्र॑यस्त्रिँ॒शः ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The sacrifice of ten nights has three eminences [2]; the Pañcadaśa (Stoma) is one eminence; the Ekavinśa one eminence, the Trayastrinśa one eminence.
</details>

## फलान्तरम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य ए॒वव्ँ वि॒द्वान् द॑शरा॒त्रेण॒ यज॑ते त्रिक॒कुद् ए॒व स॑मा॒नाना॑म् भवति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He, who knowing thus sacrifices with (the rite) of ten nights, becomes thrice eminent among his peers.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

यज॑मानᳶ पञ्चद॒शः ,  
यज॑मान एकविँ॒शः ,  
यज॑मानस् त्रयस्त्रिँ॒शः ,  
पुर॒ इत॑राः ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The sacrificer is the Pañcadaśa; the sacrificer the Ekavimśa, the sacrificer the Trayastrinśa, and the others are the citadels.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अ॒भि॒च॒र्य॒मा॑णो दशरा॒त्रेण॑ यजेत ।  
+++(दशरात्रसम्बन्धीन्यहानि देवपुराख्यानि ऊढ्वा)+++ दे॒व॒पु॒रा ए॒व पर्यू॑हते +++(आत्मनो रक्षार्थम्)+++ । +++('उपसर्गाद् अस्यत्यूह्योः' इति आत्मनेपदम् ।)+++
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

If a man have witchcraft practised against him, he should sacrifice with (the rite) of ten nights; verily he surrounds himself with the divine citadels;
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

तस्य॒ न कुत॑श् च॒नोपा॑व्या॒धो+++(→अभिचारादिबाधा)+++ भ॑वति ।
नैन॑म् अभि॒चर॑न् स्तृणुते+++(=छादयति)+++ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

no harm whatever can befall him, the practiser of witchcraft overcomes him not.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>दे॒वा॒सु॒रास् सय्ँय॑त्ता आसन् ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The gods and the Asuras were in conflict.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ते दे॒वा ए॒ता दे॑वपु॒रा+++(=दशरात्रसम्बन्धीन्यहानि)+++ अ॑पश्य॒न् यद् द॑शरा॒त्रः+++(→तदवयवभूताः)+++ [15]।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The gods [3] saw in (the rite) of ten nights the divine citadels.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>ताᳶ पर्यौ॑हन्त ।  
तेषा॒न् न कुत॑श् च॒नोपा॑व्या॒धो॑ ऽभवत् ।
ततो॑ दे॒वा अभ॑व॒न् परासु॑राः ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

They surrounded themselves with them; no harm whatever befell them; then the gods prospered, the Asuras were defeated.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

यो भ्रातृ॑व्यवा॒न्त् स्यात् स द॑शरा॒त्रेण॑ यजेत ।  
दे॒व॒पु॒रा ए॒व पर्यू॑हते ।  
तस्य॒ न कुत॑श् च॒नोपा॑व्या॒धो भ॑वति ।

</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He who has enemies should sacrifice with (the rite) of ten nights; verily he surrounds himself with the divine citadels; no harm whatever befalls him; he prospers and his enemy is defeated.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(अभितस् स्थितः)+++ स्तोम॒स् +++(ककुत्स्थानीय)+++स्तोम॒स्य उप॑स्तिर्+++(=हीनः)+++ भवति ।  
भ्रातृ॑व्यम् ए॒व उप॑स्तिङ्+++(=हीनं)+++ कुरुते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Stoma serves Stoma; verily he makes his enemy his servant.
</details>

## सामविन्यासः

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

जा॒मि+++(तां)+++ वै ए॒तत् कु॑र्वन्ति॒ , यज् ज्यायाँ॑सँ॒ स्तोम॑म् उ॒पेत्य॒ कनी॑याँसम् उप॒यन्ति ।  
यद् अ॑ग्निष्टोम-सा॒मानि अ॒वस्ता॑च् च प॒रस्ता॑च् च॒ भव॒न्त्य् अजा॑मित्वाय ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

In that they perform the lesser Stoma after per forming the greater there is uniformity [4]; to break the uniformity the Agnistoma Samans are before and after (the greater Stoma).
</details>

### प्रतिदिनविन्यासः

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(प्रथमदिने)+++ त्रि॒वृद् अ॑ग्निष्टो॒मो॑ ऽग्नि॒ष्टुद्+++(=अग्निष्टोमविशेषः)+++ आ॒ग्ने॒यीषु॑ +++(ऋक्षु)+++ भवति । तेज॑ ए॒वाव॑ रुन्धे ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is an Agnistoma, with the Trivrt (Stoma), in praise of Agni, with verses addressed to Agni; verily he wins brilliance.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(द्वितीयदिने ककुदि)+++ प॒ञ्च॒द॒श उ॒क्थ्य॑+++(=उक्थसाधनशस्त्रम्?)+++ ऐ॒न्द्रीषु॑  +++(ऋक्षु)+++। इन्द्रि॒यम् ए॒वाव॑ रुन्धे ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is an Ukthya, with the Pañcadaśa (Stoma), and verses addressed to Indra; verily he wins power.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(तृतीयदिने)+++ त्रि॒वृद् अ॑ग्निष्टो॒मो वै॑श्वदे॒वीषु॑  +++(ऋक्षु)+++। पुष्टि॑म् ए॒वाव॑ रुन्धे ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is an Agnistoma, with the Trivrt (Stoma), and verses addressed to the All-gods; verily he wins prosperity.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स॒प्त॒द॒शो॑ ऽग्निष्टो॒मᳶ प्रा॑जाप॒त्यासु॑ तीव्रसो॒मः+++(=उक्थ्यविशेषो यत्र शतम् आशिरे दुहन्ति)+++ । अ॒न्नाद्य॒स्य अव॑रुद्ध्यै । अथो॒ प्रैव तेन॑ जायते [17] ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is an Agni stoma, with the Saptadaśa (Stoma), and verses addressed to Prajapati, in which the Soma offering is bitter, to win food; verily also by it he is propagated [5].
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(पञ्चम-दिने ककुदि)+++ ए॒क॒विँ॒श उ॒क्थ्य॑स् सौ॒रीषु॒ +++(ऋक्षु)+++ प्रति॑ष्ठित्यै । अथो॒ रुच॑म् ए॒वाऽऽत्मन् ध॑त्ते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is an Ukthya with the Ekavinśa (Stoma), and verses addressed to the sun, for support; verily he places radiance in himself.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स॒प्त॒द॒शो॑ऽग्निष्टो॒मᳶ प्रा॑जाप॒त्यासू॑पह॒व्यः॑+++(=उपहवे साधुः)+++ । उपह॒वमे॒व+++(=पूजां)+++ ग॑च्छति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is an Agnistoma, with the Saptadaśa (Stoma), and verses addressed to Prajapati, (called) the added oblation; verily he is invited by all.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त्रि॒ण॒वाव् अ॑ग्निष्टो॒माव् +++(त्रयस्त्रिंशस्य)+++ अ॒भित॑  ऐ॒न्द्रीषु॒ , विजि॑त्यै।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There are two Agnistomas, with the Trinava (Stoma) on either side (of the Ukthya), with verses addressed to Indra, for conquest.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(अष्टमदिने ककुत्स्थाने)+++ त्र॒य॒स्त्रिँ॒श उ॒क्थ्यो॑ वैश्वदे॒वीषु॒ प्रति॑ष्ठित्यै।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is an Ukthya, with the Trayastrinśa (Stoma) with verses addressed to the All-gods, for rest.
</details>
