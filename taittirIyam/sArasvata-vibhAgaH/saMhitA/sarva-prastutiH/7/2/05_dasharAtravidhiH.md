<details><summary>विश्वास-टिप्पनी</summary>

अथ चत्वारो दशरात्राः - त्रिककुत् प्रथमः । देवपुरा द्वितीयः । छन्दोमवान् तृतीयः । कौसुरुबिन्दश्चतुर्थः ।
</details>

## फलम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>प्रजा᳓पति᳓र् अ᳓का᳓म᳓य᳓त᳓ - प्र᳓ जायेये᳓ति ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Prajapati desired, 'May I be propagated.'
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स᳓ एत᳓न् द᳓शहोतारम्+++(='चित्तिस्स्रुक्' इत्यादिकं)+++ अपश्यत् ।  
त᳓म् अजुहोत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He saw this Daśahotr, and offered it.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ते᳓न दशरात्र᳓म् असृजत ।
ते᳓न दशरात्रे᳓ण प्रा᳓जायत ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

By this he created (the rite) of ten nights, and by this (rite) often nights he was propagated.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

दशरात्रा᳓य दीक्षिष्य᳓माणो द᳓शहोतारञ् जुहुयात्।  
द᳓शहोत्रैव᳓ दशरात्रँ᳓ सृजते ।  
ते᳓न दशरात्रे᳓ण प्र᳓ जायते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

If one is about to consecrate oneself for (the rite) of ten nights one should offer the Daśahotr; verily he creates by the Daśahotr (the rite) of ten nights, and by (the rite) of ten nights he is propagated.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(दशत्वान्वयाद्)+++ वै᳓राजो᳓ वा᳓ एष᳓ यज्ञो᳓ य᳓द् दशरात्रः᳓ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The sacrifice of ten nights is connected with the Viraj.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>य᳓ एवव्ँ᳓ विद्वा᳓न् दशरात्रे᳓ण य᳓जते, विरा᳓जम् एव᳓ गच्छति ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He, who knowing thus sacrifices with (the rite) of ten nights, attains the Viraj.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

प्राजापत्यो᳓ वा᳓ एष᳓ यज्ञो᳓ , य᳓द् दशरात्रः᳓ [13] ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The sacrifice of ten nights is connected with Prajapati [1].
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓ एवव्ँ᳓ विद्वा᳓न् दशरात्रे᳓ण य᳓जते , प्रै᳓व᳓ जायते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He, who knowing thus sacrifices with (the rite) of ten nights, is propagated.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

इ᳓न्द्रो वै᳓ सदृ᳓ङ् देव᳓ताभिर् आसीत् । स᳓ न᳓ व्यावृ᳓तम् अगच्छत् ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Indra was on a level with the gods, he was not separated from them.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स᳓ प्रजा᳓पतिम् उ᳓पाधावत् । त᳓स्मा एत᳓न् दशरात्र᳓म् प्रा᳓यच्छत् ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He ran up to Prajapati; he gave him this (rite) of ten nights.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓मा᳓हरत् । ते᳓नायजत ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He grasped it, and sacrificed with it.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓तो वै᳓ सोऽन्या᳓भिर् देव᳓ताभिर् व्यावृ᳓तम् अगच्छत् ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Then indeed he became separated from the other gods.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓ एवव्ँ᳓ विद्वा᳓न् दशरात्रे᳓ण य᳓जते , व्यावृ᳓तम् एव᳓ पाप्म᳓ना भ्रा᳓तृव्येण गच्छति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He, who knowing thus sacrifices with (the rite) of ten nights, attains separation from the evil enemy.
</details>

## विधानम्

<details><summary>विश्वास-टिप्पनी</summary>

त्रिवृदग्नीष्टोमः, १५, त्रिवृदग्नीष्टोमः। १७, २१, १७। २७, ३३, २७। विश्वजित्।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त्रिककु᳓द् वा᳓ एष᳓ यज्ञो᳓ य᳓द् दशरात्रः।
+++(त्रिवृद्-अग्निष्टोमाव् अभितः)+++ ककु᳓त्+++(→उन्नतः)+++ पञ्चदशः᳓।  
+++(सप्तदशाग्निष्टोमाव् अभितः)+++ ककु᳓द् एकविँशः᳓ ।  
+++(त्रिणवाग्निष्टोमाव् अभितः)+++ ककु᳓त् त्रयस्त्रिँशः᳓ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The sacrifice of ten nights has three eminences [2]; the Pañcadaśa (Stoma) is one eminence; the Ekavinśa one eminence, the Trayastrinśa one eminence.
</details>

## फलान्तरम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓ एवव्ँ᳓ विद्वा᳓न् दशरात्रे᳓ण य᳓जते त्रिककु᳓द् एव᳓ समाना᳓नाम् भवति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He, who knowing thus sacrifices with (the rite) of ten nights, becomes thrice eminent among his peers.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓जमानᳶ पञ्चदशः᳓ ,  
य᳓जमान एकविँशः᳓ ,  
य᳓जमानस् त्रयस्त्रिँशः᳓ ,  
पु᳓र इ᳓तराः ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The sacrificer is the Pañcadaśa; the sacrificer the Ekavimśa, the sacrificer the Trayastrinśa, and the others are the citadels.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अभिचर्यमा᳙णो दशरात्रे᳓ण यजेत ।  
+++(दशरात्रसम्बन्धीन्यहानि देवपुराख्यानि ऊढ्वा)+++ देवपुरा᳓ एव᳓ प᳓र्यूहते +++(आत्मनो रक्षार्थम्)+++ । +++('उपसर्गाद् अस्यत्यूह्योः' इति आत्मनेपदम् ।)+++
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

If a man have witchcraft practised against him, he should sacrifice with (the rite) of ten nights; verily he surrounds himself with the divine citadels;
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓स्य न᳓ कु᳓तश् चनो᳓पाव्याधो᳓+++(→अभिचारादिबाधा)+++ भवति ।
नै᳓नम् अभिच᳓रन् स्तृणुते+++(=छादयति)+++ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

no harm whatever can befall him, the practiser of witchcraft overcomes him not.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>देवासुरा᳓स् सय्ँ᳓यत्ता आसन् ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The gods and the Asuras were in conflict.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ते᳓ देवा᳓ एता᳓ देवपुरा᳓+++(=दशरात्रसम्बन्धीन्यहानि)+++ अपश्यन् य᳓द् दशरात्रः᳓+++(→तदवयवभूताः)+++ [15]।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The gods [3] saw in (the rite) of ten nights the divine citadels.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>ताᳶ᳓ प᳓र्यौहन्त ।  
ते᳓षान् न᳓ कु᳓तश् चनो᳓पाव्याधो᳙ ऽभवत् ।
त᳓तो देवा᳓ अ᳓भवन् प᳓रा᳓सुराः ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

They surrounded themselves with them; no harm whatever befell them; then the gods prospered, the Asuras were defeated.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

यो᳓ भ्रा᳓तृव्यवान्त् स्या᳓त् स᳓ दशरात्रे᳓ण यजेत ।  
देवपुरा᳓ एव᳓ प᳓र्यूहते ।  
त᳓स्य न᳓ कु᳓तश् चनो᳓पाव्याधो᳓ भवति ।
भ᳓वत्य् आत्म᳓ना। प᳓रा+अस्य भ्रा᳓तृव्यो +++(परा)+++भवति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He who has enemies should sacrifice with (the rite) of ten nights; verily he surrounds himself with the divine citadels; no harm whatever befalls him; he prospers and his enemy is defeated.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(अभितस् स्थितः)+++ स्तो᳓मस् +++(ककुत्स्थानीय)+++स्तो᳓मस्य᳓ उ᳓पस्तिर्+++(=हीनः)+++ भवति ।  
भ्रा᳓तृव्यम् एव᳓ उ᳓पस्तिङ्+++(=हीनं)+++ कुरुते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Stoma serves Stoma; verily he makes his enemy his servant.
</details>

## सामविन्यासः

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

जामि᳓+++(तां)+++ वै᳓ एत᳓त् कुर्वन्ति , य᳓ज् ज्या᳓याँसँ स्तो᳓मम् उपे᳓त्य क᳓नीयाँसम् उपय᳓न्ति᳓ ।  
य᳓द् अग्निष्टोम-सामा᳓नि᳓ अव᳓स्ताच् च पर᳓स्ताच् च भ᳓वन्त्य् अ᳓जामित्वाय ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

In that they perform the lesser Stoma after per forming the greater there is uniformity [4]; to break the uniformity the Agnistoma Samans are before and after (the greater Stoma).
</details>

### प्रतिदिनविन्यासः

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(प्रथमदिने)+++ त्रिवृ᳓द् अग्निष्टोमो᳙ ऽग्निष्टु᳓द्+++(=अग्निष्टोमविशेषः)+++ आग्नेयी᳓षु +++(ऋक्षु)+++ भवति । ते᳓ज एवा᳓व रुन्धे ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is an Agnistoma, with the Trivrt (Stoma), in praise of Agni, with verses addressed to Agni; verily he wins brilliance.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(द्वितीयदिने ककुदि)+++ पञ्चदश᳓ उक्थ्य᳙+++(=उक्थसाधनशस्त्रम्?)+++ ऐन्द्री᳓षु  +++(ऋक्षु)+++। इ᳓न्द्रिय᳓म् एवा᳓व रुन्धे ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is an Ukthya, with the Pañcadaśa (Stoma), and verses addressed to Indra; verily he wins power.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(तृतीयदिने)+++ त्रिवृ᳓द् अग्निष्टोमो᳓ वैश्वदेवी᳓षु  +++(ऋक्षु)+++। पु᳓ष्टिम् एवा᳓व रुन्धे ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is an Agnistoma, with the Trivrt (Stoma), and verses addressed to the All-gods; verily he wins prosperity.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

सप्तदशो᳙ ऽग्निष्टोमᳶ᳓ प्राजापत्या᳓सु तीव्रसोमः᳓+++(=उक्थ्यविशेषो यत्र शतम् आशिरे दुहन्ति)+++ । अन्ना᳓द्यस्य᳓ अ᳓वरुद्ध्यै । अ᳓थो प्रै᳓व᳓ ते᳓न जायते [17] ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is an Agni stoma, with the Saptadaśa (Stoma), and verses addressed to Prajapati, in which the Soma offering is bitter, to win food; verily also by it he is propagated [5].
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(पञ्चम-दिने ककुदि)+++ एकविँश᳓ उक्थ्य᳙स् सौरी᳓षु +++(ऋक्षु)+++ प्र᳓तिष्ठित्यै । अ᳓थो रु᳓चम् एवा᳓ऽऽत्म᳓न् धत्ते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is an Ukthya with the Ekavinśa (Stoma), and verses addressed to the sun, for support; verily he places radiance in himself.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

सप्तदशो᳙ऽग्निष्टोमᳶ᳓ प्राजापत्या᳓सूपहव्यः᳙+++(=उपहवे साधुः)+++ । उ᳓प᳓हव᳓मेव᳓+++(=पूजां)+++ गच्छति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is an Agnistoma, with the Saptadaśa (Stoma), and verses addressed to Prajapati, (called) the added oblation; verily he is invited by all.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त्रिणवा᳓व् अग्निष्टोमा᳓व् +++(त्रयस्त्रिंशस्य)+++ अभि᳓त  ऐन्द्री᳓षु , वि᳓जित्यै।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There are two Agnistomas, with the Trinava (Stoma) on either side (of the Ukthya), with verses addressed to Indra, for conquest.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(अष्टमदिने ककुत्स्थाने)+++ त्रयस्त्रिँश᳓ उक्थ्यो᳙ वैश्वदेवी᳓षु प्र᳓तिष्ठित्यै।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is an Ukthya, with the Trayastrinśa (Stoma) with verses addressed to the All-gods, for rest.
</details>
