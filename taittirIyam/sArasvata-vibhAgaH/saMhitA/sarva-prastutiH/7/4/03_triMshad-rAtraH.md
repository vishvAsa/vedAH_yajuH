## फलम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ऋक्षा᳓+++(=तृणादिरहिता स्थली)+++ वा᳓ इय᳓म् +++(पृथिवी)+++ अलोम᳓कासीत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

This (earth) was bare and hairless.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

सा᳓कामयत᳓ - ओ᳓षधीभिर् व᳓नस्प᳓तिभिᳶ प्र᳓ जायेये᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

It desired, 'Let me be propagated with plants and trees.'
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

सै᳓ता᳓स् त्रिँश᳓तँ रा᳓त्रीर् अपश्यत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

It saw these thirty nights.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓तो वा᳓ इय᳓म् ओ᳓षधीभिर् व᳓नस्प᳓तिभिᳶ प्रा᳓जायत ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Then indeed it was propagated with plants and trees.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ये᳓ प्रजा᳓कामाᳶ पशु᳓कामास् स्यु᳓स् त᳓ एता᳓ आसीरन् ।  
प्रै᳓व᳓ जायन्ते प्रज᳓या पशु᳓भिः ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Those who desire offspring and cattle should perform (the rite of) these (nights); they are propagated with offspring and cattle.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

इयव्ँ᳓ वा᳓ अक्षुध्यत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

This (earth) was hungry.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

सै᳓ताव्ँ᳓ विरा᳓जम्+++(=अन्नम्)+++ अपश्यत् ।
ता᳓म् आत्म᳓न् धित्वा᳓+++(=स्थापयित्वा)+++ ऽन्ना᳓द्यम्+++(=अन्नादनसामर्थ्यं)+++ अ᳓वारुन्द्ध् औ᳓षधीः [9]।  
व᳓नस्प᳓तीन् प्रजा᳓म् पशू᳓न्
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

It saw this Viraj, and placing it within itself it won food, plants [1], trees, offspring, cattle.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ते᳓नावर्धत ।
सा᳓ जेमा᳓नम्+++(=जयं)+++ महिमा᳓नम् अगच्छत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Thereby it grew, it attained victoriousness and greatness.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓ एवव्ँ᳓ विद्वाँ᳓स एता᳓ आ᳓सते , विरा᳓जम् एवा᳓त्म᳓न् धित्वा᳓ ऽन्ना᳓द्यम् अ᳓व रुन्धते ।  
व᳓र्धन्ते प्रज᳓या पशु᳓भिः ।  
जेमा᳓नम्+++(=जयं)+++ महिमा᳓नङ् गच्छन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Those, who knowing thus perform (the rite of) these (nights), placing the Viraj in themselves, win food, grow great with offspring and cattle, and attain victoriousness and might.
</details>

## विधानम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ज्यो᳓तिर् अतिरात्रो᳓ भवति, सुवर्ग᳓स्य लोक᳓स्य᳓ अ᳓नुख्यात्यै ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is a Jyotistoma Atiratra, for the revealing of the world of heaven.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

पृ᳓ष्ठ्यष् षडहो᳓ भवति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is a Prsthya Sadaha.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ष᳓ड्वा᳓ ऋत᳓वष् । ष᳓ट्पृष्ठा᳓नि ।  
पृष्ठै᳓रेव᳓ + ऋ᳓तू᳓न् अन्वा᳓रोहन्ति + ऋर्तु᳓भिस् सव्ँवत्सर᳓म् ।  
ते᳓ सव्ँवत्सर᳓ एव᳓ प्र᳓ति तिष्ठन्ति ।[10]
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The seasons are six, the Prsthas are six; verily by the Prsthas they reach the seasons, with the seasons the year; verily; the year [2] they find support.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त्रयस्त्रिँशा᳓त् त्रयस्त्रिँश᳓म् उ᳓प यन्ति, यज्ञ᳓स्य स᳓न्तत्यै ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

From the Trayastrinśas they go to the Trayastrinśa, for the continuity of the sacrifice.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अ᳓थो प्रजा᳓पतिर् वै᳓ त्रयस्त्रिँशᳶ᳓ +++(सर्वदेवतामयत्वात्)+++।  
प्रजा᳓पतिम् एवा᳓ ऽऽरभन्ते , प्र᳓तिष्ठित्यै ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Now the Trayastrinśa is Prajapati; verily they seize hold of Prajapati, for support.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त्रिणवो᳓ भवति वि᳓जित्यै ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is a Trinava, for victory.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

एकविँशो᳓ भवति प्र᳓तिष्ठित्यै । अ᳓थो रु᳓चम् एवा᳓त्म᳓न् दधते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is an Ekavinśa, for support; verily they place radiance in themselves.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त्रिवृ᳓द् अग्निष्टु᳓द्+++(=सर्वाग्नेयः)+++ भवति ।
पाप्मा᳓नम् एव᳓ ते᳓न नि᳓र्दहन्ते᳓।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is a praise of Agni with the Trivrt; verily they bum away evil with it.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अ᳓थो ते᳓जो वै᳓ त्रिवृ᳓त्। ते᳓ज एवा᳓त्म᳓न् दधते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Now the Trivrt is brilliance; verily they place brilliance in themselves.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

पञ्चदश᳓ इन्द्रस्तोमो᳓ भवति ।  
इन्द्रिय᳓मेव᳓ अ᳓व रुन्धते ।[11]  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is a praise of Indra with the Pañcadaśa; verily they win power (indriyá) [3].
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

सप्तदशो᳓ भवत्य् अन्ना᳓द्यस्या᳓वरुद्ध्यै +++(सप्तदशस्य प्राजापत्यत्वात्)+++।  
अ᳓थो प्रै᳓व᳓ ते᳓न जायन्ते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is a Saptadaśa, for the winning of food; verily by it they are propagated.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

एकविँशो᳓ भवति प्र᳓तिष्ठित्यै ।  
अ᳓थो रु᳓चम् एवा᳓त्म᳓न् दधते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is an Ekavinśa, for support; verily they place radiance in themselves.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

चतुर्विँशो᳓ भवति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is a Caturvinśa.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

च᳓तुर्विँशतिर् अर्धमासा᳓स् सव्ँवत्सरः᳓ ।  
सव्ँवत्सर᳓स् सुवर्गो᳓ लोकः᳓ ।  
सव्ँवत्सर᳓ एव᳓ सुवर्गे᳓ लोके᳓ प्र᳓ति तिष्ठन्ति᳓ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Twenty-four half-months make up the year, the year is the world of heaven; verily they find support in the year, the world of heaven.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अ᳓थो एष᳓ वै᳓ विषूवा᳓न् +++(=यज्ञस्य मध्यदिनम्)+++।
विषूव᳓न्तो+++(=मध्यस्था)+++ भवन्ति , य᳓ एवव्ँ᳓ विद्वाँ᳓स एता᳓ आ᳓सते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Now there is the chief (day), and those who knowing thus perform (the rite of) these (nights) become the chief.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

चतुर्विँशा᳓त् पृष्ठा᳓न्य् उ᳓प यन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

From the Caturvinśa they go to the Prsthas.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(चतुर्विंशतिर् अर्धमासा इत्यादि कृत्वा)+++ सव्ँवत्सर एव᳓ प्रतिष्ठा᳓य[12] देव᳓ता अभ्या᳓रोहन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Verily having found support in the year [4] they attain the gods.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त्रयस्त्रिँशा᳓त् त्रयस्त्रिँश᳓म् उ᳓प यन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

From the Trayastrinśa they go to the Trayastrinśa.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त्र᳓यस्त्रिँशद् वै᳓ देव᳓ताः ।
देव᳓तास्व् एव᳓ प्र᳓ति तिष्ठन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The gods are three and thirty; verily in the gods they find support.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त्रिणवो᳓ भवति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is a Trinava.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

इमे᳓ वै᳓ लोका᳓स् त्रिणवः᳓ ।  
एष्वे᳙व लोके᳓षु प्र᳓ति तिष्ठन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The Trinava is these worlds; verily in these worlds they find support.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

द्वा᳓व् एकविँशौ᳓ भवतᳶ, प्र᳓तिष्ठित्यै ।  
अ᳓थो रु᳓चम् एवा᳓त्म᳓न् दधते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There are two Ekavinśas, for support; verily they place radiance in themselves.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(एवं)+++ बह᳓वष् षोडशि᳓नो+++(=एकविंशो)+++ भवन्ति ।  
त᳓स्माद् बह᳓वᳶ प्रजा᳓सु वृ᳓षाणः ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There are many Sodaśins, and therefore there are many males among offspring.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓द् एते᳓ स्तो᳓मा व्य᳓तिषक्ता+++(=व्यत्यासेन परस्परानुषक्ताः)+++ भ᳓वन्ति ।
त᳓स्माद् इय᳓म् ओ᳓षधीभिर् व᳓नस्प᳓तिभि᳓र् व्य᳓तिषक्ता [13]।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

In that these Stomas are intermingled, therefore this (earth) is covered with mingled plants and trees [5].
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

व्य᳓तिषज्यन्ते प्रज᳓या पशु᳓भिर् , य᳓ एवव्ँ᳓ विद्वाँ᳓स एता᳓ आ᳓सते᳓ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Those who knowing thus perform (the rite of) these (nights) have both offspring and cattle.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अ᳓कॢप्ता+++(=असंपन्ना)+++ वा᳓ एते᳓ सुवर्गल्ँ᳓ लोकय्ँ᳓ यन्ति ।
+++(यतः)+++ उच्चावचा᳓न् हि᳓ स्तो᳓मान् उपय᳓न्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

These go imperfect to the world of heaven, for they resort to various Stomas;
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓द् एत᳓ ऊर्ध्वाᳵ᳓ कॢप्ता᳓स् +++(अव्यतिषक्ताः)+++ स्तो᳓मा +++(चतुष्टोमात्मकाः)+++ भ᳓वन्ति,  
कॢप्ता᳓ एव᳓ सुवर्गल्ँ᳓ लोकय्ँ᳓ यन्ति ।
उभ᳓योर् एभ्यो लोक᳓योः कल्पते ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

in that the Stomas are arranged in order, they go in order to the world of heaven, and there is order in both these worlds.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त्रिँश᳓द् एता᳓स्, त्रिँश᳓द् अक्षरा विरा᳓ड् ।
अ᳓न्नव्ँ विरा᳓ट् ।  
विरा᳓जा᳓ + एवा᳓न्ना᳓द्यम् अ᳓व रुन्धते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

These (nights) are thirty in number, the Viraj has thirty syllables; verily by the Viraj they win food.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अतिरात्रा᳓व् अभि᳓तो भवतो ,
ऽन्ना᳓द्यस्य प᳓रिगृहीत्यै ॥ [14]
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There are Atiratras on either hand, to secure food.
</details>
