## फलम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ऋ॒क्षा+++(=तृणादिरहिता स्थली)+++ वा इ॒यम् +++(पृथिवी)+++ अ॑लो॒मका॑सीत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

This (earth) was bare and hairless.
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

साका॑मय॒त - ओष॑धीभि॒र् वन॒स्पति॑भि॒ᳶ प्र जा॑ये॒येति॑ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

It desired, 'Let me be propagated with plants and trees.'
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

सैतास् त्रिँ॒शतँ॒ रात्री॑र् अपश्यत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

It saw these thirty nights.
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ततो॒ वा इ॒यम् ओष॑धीभि॒र् वन॒स्पति॑भि॒ᳶ प्राजा॑यत ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Then indeed it was propagated with plants and trees.
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ये प्र॒जाका॑माᳶ प॒शुका॑मा॒स् स्युस् त ए॒ता आ॑सीरन् ।  
प्रैव जा॑यन्ते प्र॒जया॑ प॒शुभिः॑ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Those who desire offspring and cattle should perform (the rite of) these (nights); they are propagated with offspring and cattle.
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

इ॒यव्ँ वा अ॑क्षुध्यत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

This (earth) was hungry.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

सैताव्ँ वि॒राज॑म्+++(=अन्नम्)+++ अपश्यत् ।
ताम् आ॒त्मन् धि॒त्वा+++(=स्थापयित्वा)+++ ऽन्नाद्य॒म्+++(=अन्नादनसामर्थ्यं)+++ अवा॑रु॒न्द्ध् औष॑धीः [9]।  
वन॒स्पती॑न् प्र॒जाम् प॒शून्
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

It saw this Viraj, and placing it within itself it won food, plants [1], trees, offspring, cattle.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

तेना॑वर्धत ।
सा जे॒मान॑म्+++(=जयं)+++ महि॒मान॑म् अगच्छत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Thereby it grew, it attained victoriousness and greatness.
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य ए॒वव्ँ वि॒द्वाँस॑ ए॒ता आस॑ते , वि॒राज॑म् ए॒वात्मन् धि॒त्वा ऽन्नाद्य॒म् अव॑ रुन्धते ।  
वर्ध॑न्ते प्र॒जया॑ प॒शुभिः॑ ।  
जे॒मान॑म्+++(=जयं)+++ महि॒मान॑ङ् गच्छन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Those, who knowing thus perform (the rite of) these (nights), placing the Viraj in themselves, win food, grow great with offspring and cattle, and attain victoriousness and might.
</details>



## विधानम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ज्योति॑र् अतिरा॒त्रो भ॑वति, सुव॒र्गस्य॑ लो॒कस्य अनु॑ख्यात्यै ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is a Jyotistoma Atiratra, for the revealing of the world of heaven.
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

पृष्ठ्य॑ष् षड॒हो भ॑वति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is a Prsthya Sadaha.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

षड्वा ऋ॒तव॒ष् । षट्पृ॒ष्ठानि॑ ।  
पृ॒ष्ठैरे॒व + ऋतून् अ॒न्वारो॑हन्ति + ऋ॒र्तुभि॑स् सव्ँवत्स॒रम् ।  
ते सव्ँ॑वत्स॒र ए॒व प्रति॑ तिष्ठन्ति ।[10]
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The seasons are six, the Prsthas are six; verily by the Prsthas they reach the seasons, with the seasons the year; verily; the year [2] they find support.
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त्र॒य॒स्त्रिँ॒शात् त्र॑यस्त्रिँ॒शम् उप॑ यन्ति, य॒ज्ञस्य॒ सन्त॑त्यै ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

From the Trayastrinśas they go to the Trayastrinśa, for the continuity of the sacrifice.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अथो॑ प्र॒जाप॑ति॒र् वै त्र॑यस्त्रिँ॒शᳶ +++(सर्वदेवतामयत्वात्)+++।  
प्र॒जाप॑तिम् ए॒वा ऽऽर॑भन्ते॒ , प्रति॑ष्ठित्यै ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Now the Trayastrinśa is Prajapati; verily they seize hold of Prajapati, for support.
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त्रि॒ण॒वो भ॑वति॒ विजि॑त्यै ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is a Trinava, for victory.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ए॒क॒विँ॒शो भ॑वति॒ प्रति॑ष्ठित्यै । अथो॒ रुच॑म् ए॒वात्मन् द॑धते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is an Ekavinśa, for support; verily they place radiance in themselves.
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त्रि॒वृद् अ॑ग्नि॒ष्टुद्+++(=सर्वाग्नेयः)+++ भ॑वति ।
पा॒प्मान॑म् ए॒व तेन॒ निर्द॑ह॒न्ते।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is a praise of Agni with the Trivrt; verily they bum away evil with it.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अथो॒ तेजो॒ वै त्रि॒वृत्। तेज॑ ए॒वात्मन् द॑धते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Now the Trivrt is brilliance; verily they place brilliance in themselves.
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

प॒ञ्च॒द॒श इ॑न्द्रस्तो॒मो भ॑वति ।  
इ॒न्द्रि॒यमे॒व अव॑ रुन्धते ।[11]  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is a praise of Indra with the Pañcadaśa; verily they win power (indriyá) [3].
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स॒प्त॒द॒शो भ॑वत्य् अ॒न्नाद्य॒स्याव॑रुद्ध्यै +++(सप्तदशस्य प्राजापत्यत्वात्)+++।  
अथो॒ प्रैव तेन॑ जायन्ते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is a Saptadaśa, for the winning of food; verily by it they are propagated.
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ए॒क॒विँ॒शो भ॑वति॒ प्रति॑ष्ठित्यै ।  
अथो॒ रुच॑म् ए॒वात्मन् द॑धते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is an Ekavinśa, for support; verily they place radiance in themselves.
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

च॒तु॒र्विँ॒शो भ॑वति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is a Caturvinśa.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

चतु॑र्विँशतिर् अर्धमा॒सास् सव्ँ॑वत्स॒रः ।  
सव्ँ॒व॒त्स॒रस् सु॑व॒र्गो लो॒कः ।  
सव्ँ॒व॒त्स॒र ए॒व सु॑व॒र्गे लो॒के प्रति॑ तिष्ठ॒न्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Twenty-four half-months make up the year, the year is the world of heaven; verily they find support in the year, the world of heaven.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अथो॑ ए॒ष वै वि॑षू॒वान् +++(=यज्ञस्य मध्यदिनम्)+++।
वि॒षू॒वन्तो॑+++(=मध्यस्था)+++ भवन्ति॒ , य ए॒वव्ँ वि॒द्वाँस॑ ए॒ता आस॑ते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Now there is the chief (day), and those who knowing thus perform (the rite of) these (nights) become the chief.
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

च॒तु॒र्विँ॒शात् पृ॒ष्ठान्य् उप॑ यन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

From the Caturvinśa they go to the Prsthas.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(चतुर्विंशतिर् अर्धमासा इत्यादि कृत्वा)+++ सव्ँ॒व॒त्स॒र॒ ए॒व प्र॑ति॒ष्ठाय॑[12] दे॒वता॑ अ॒भ्यारो॑हन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Verily having found support in the year [4] they attain the gods.
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त्र॒य॒स्त्रिँ॒शात् त्र॑यस्त्रिँ॒शम् उप॑ यन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

From the Trayastrinśa they go to the Trayastrinśa.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त्रय॑स्त्रिँश॒द् वै दे॒वताः॑ ।
दे॒वता॑स्व् ए॒व प्रति॑ तिष्ठन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The gods are three and thirty; verily in the gods they find support.
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त्रि॒ण॒वो भ॑वति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is a Trinava.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

इ॒मे वै लो॒कास् त्रि॑ण॒वः ।  
ए॒ष्वे॑व लो॒केषु॒ प्रति॑ तिष्ठन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The Trinava is these worlds; verily in these worlds they find support.
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

द्वाव् ए॑कविँ॒शौ भ॑वत॒ᳶ, प्रति॑ष्ठित्यै ।  
अथो॒ रुच॑म् ए॒वात्मन् द॑धते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There are two Ekavinśas, for support; verily they place radiance in themselves.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(एवं)+++ ब॒हव॑ष् षोड॒शिनो॑+++(=एकविंशो)+++ भवन्ति ।  
तस्मा॑द् ब॒हवᳶ॑ प्र॒जासु॒ वृषा॑णः ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There are many Sodaśins, and therefore there are many males among offspring.
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

यद् ए॒ते स्तोमा॒ व्यति॑षक्ता॒+++(=व्यत्यासेन परस्परानुषक्ताः)+++ भव॑न्ति ।
तस्मा॑द् इ॒यम् ओष॑धीभि॒र् वन॒स्पति॑भिर् व्यति॑षक्ता [13]।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

In that these Stomas are intermingled, therefore this (earth) is covered with mingled plants and trees [5].
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

व्यति॑षज्यन्ते प्र॒जया॑ प॒शुभि॒र् , य ए॒वव्ँ वि॒द्वाँस॑ ए॒ता आस॒ते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Those who knowing thus perform (the rite of) these (nights) have both offspring and cattle.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अकॢ॑प्ता॒+++(=असंपन्ना)+++ वा ए॒ते सु॑व॒र्गल्ँ लो॒कय्ँ य॑न्ति ।
+++(यतः)+++ उ॒च्चा॒व॒चान् हि स्तोमा॑न् उप॒यन्ति॑ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

These go imperfect to the world of heaven, for they resort to various Stomas;
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

यद् ए॒त ऊ॒र्ध्वाᳵ कॢ॒प्तास् +++(अव्यतिषक्ताः)+++ स्तोमा॒ +++(चतुष्टोमात्मकाः)+++ भव॑न्ति,  
कॢ॒प्ता ए॒व सु॑व॒र्गल्ँ लो॒कय्ँ य॑न्ति ।
उ॒भयो॑र् एभ्यो लो॒कयोः॑ कल्पते ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

in that the Stomas are arranged in order, they go in order to the world of heaven, and there is order in both these worlds.
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त्रिँ॒शद् ए॒तास्, त्रिँ॒शद् अ॑क्षरा वि॒राड् ।
अन्नव्ँ॑ वि॒राट् ।  
वि॒राजा + ए॒वान्नाद्य॒म् अव॑ रुन्धते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

These (nights) are thirty in number, the Viraj has thirty syllables; verily by the Viraj they win food.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अ॒ति॒रा॒त्राव् अ॒भितो॑ भवतो॒ ,
ऽन्नाद्य॑स्य॒ परि॑गृहीत्यै ॥ [14]
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There are Atiratras on either hand, to secure food.
</details>
