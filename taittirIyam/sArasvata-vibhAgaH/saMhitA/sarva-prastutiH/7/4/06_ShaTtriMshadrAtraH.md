## फलम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

आदित्या᳓ अकामयन्त -  सुवर्गल्ँ᳓ लोक᳓म् इयामे᳓ति᳓ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The Adityas desired, 'Let us go to the world of heaven.'
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ते᳓ सुवर्गल्ँ᳓ लोक᳓न् न᳓ प्रा᳓जानन्, न᳓ सुवर्गल्ँ᳓ लोक᳓मायन् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

They discerned not the world of heaven, they went not to the world of heaven.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓ एतँ᳓ षट्त्रिँशद् रात्र᳓म् अपश्यन् । त᳓मा᳓हरन् । ते᳓नायजन्त ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

They saw this (rite) of thirty-six nights; they grasped it, and sacrificed with it.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓तो वै᳓ ते᳓ सुवर्गल्ँ᳓ लोक᳓म् प्रा᳓जानन्त्, सुवर्गल्ँ᳓ लोक᳓मायन् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Then indeed did they discern the world of heaven, and went to the world of heaven.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓ एवव्ँ᳓ विद्वाँ᳓सष् षट्त्रिँशद्रात्र᳓म् आ᳓सते सुवर्ग᳓म् एव᳓ लोक᳓म् प्र᳓ जानन्ति ,  सुवर्गल्ँ᳓ लोकय्ँ᳓ यन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Those, who knowing thus perform (the rite) of thirty six nights, discern the world of heaven, and go to the world of heaven.
</details>

## विधानम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ज्यो᳓तिर् अतिरात्रो᳓ भवति ।[22]  
ज्यो᳓तिर् एव᳓ पुर᳓स्ताद् दधते सुवर्ग᳓स्य लोक᳓स्या᳓नुख्यात्यै ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The Atiratra is light [1]; verily they put light before them, to reveal the world of heaven.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>ष᳓डहा᳓ भवन्ति । ष᳓ड्वा᳓ ऋत᳓वः ।  
ऋतु᳓ष्वेव᳓ प्र᳓ति तिष्ठन्ति ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There are Sadahas; six are the seasons; verily in the seasons they find support.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(ष᳓डहा᳓)+++ चत्वा᳓रो भवन्ति ।  
च᳓तस्रो दि᳓शः । दिक्ष्वे᳙व᳓ प्र᳓ति तिष्ठन्ति᳓ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There are four (Sadahas); four are the quarters; verily in the quarters they find support.
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There can be no Sattra where there is no Chandoma; in that there axe Chandomas, there is the Sattra.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अस॑त्त्रव्ँ॒ वा ए॒तद् यद॑छन्दो॒मम् ।
यच्छ॑न्दो॒मा भव॑न्ति॒ तेन॑ स॒त्त्रम् ।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

देव᳓ता एव᳓ पृष्ठै᳓र् अ᳓व रुन्धते पशू᳓ञ् छन्दोमैः᳓ ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The gods they win by the Prsthas, the cattle by the Chandomas.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

दे॒वता॑ ए॒व पृ॒ष्ठैरव॑ रुन्धते प॒शूञ्छ॑न्दो॒मैः ।  
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ओ᳓जो वा᳓ वीर्य᳙म् पृष्ठा᳓नि, पश᳓वश् छन्दोमा᳓ ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The Prsthas are force and strength, the Chandomas cattle;
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ओजो॒ वै वी॒र्य॑म्पृ॒ष्ठानि॑ प॒शव॑श्छन्दो॒माः ।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

verily in force [2] and strength they find support.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ओज॑स्ये॒व वी॒र्ये॑ प॒शुषु॒ प्रति॑ तिष्ठन्ति ।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

षट्त्रिँशद्रात्रो᳓ भवति ।  
ष᳓ट्त्रिँशद्-अक्षरा बृहती᳓ ।  
बा᳓र्हताᳶ पश᳓वः ।  
बृहत्यै᳓व᳓ पशू᳓न् अ᳓व रुन्धते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

(The rite has) thirty-six nights; the Brhati has thirty-six syllables, cattle are connected with the Brhati; verily by the Brhati they win cattle.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

बृहती᳓ छ᳓न्दसाँ स्वा᳓राज्यम् आश्नुत ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The Brhati obtained the sovereignty over the metres.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अश्नुव᳓ते स्वा᳓राज्यय्ँ य एवव्ँ᳓ विद्वाँ᳓सष् षट्त्रिँशद् रात्र᳓मा᳓सते।  
सु᳓वर्ग᳓मेव᳓ लोकय्ँ᳓ यन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

They obtain sovereignty who knowing thus perform (the rite) of thirty-six nights; verily they go to the world of heaven.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अतिरात्रा᳓व् अभि᳓तो भवतस् सुवर्ग᳓स्य लोक᳓स्य प᳓रिगृहीत्यै ॥ [24]  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There are Atiratras on either side, for the securing of the world of heaven.
</details>
