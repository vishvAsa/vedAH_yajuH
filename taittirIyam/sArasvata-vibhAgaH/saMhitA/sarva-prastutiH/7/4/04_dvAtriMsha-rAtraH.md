## परिचयः

## फलम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

प्रजा᳓पतिस् सुवर्गल्ँ᳓ लोक᳓म् ऐत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Prajapati went to the world of heaven.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓न् देवा᳓ ये᳓न येन छ᳓न्दसा᳓ ऽनुप्रा᳓युञ्जत, ते᳓न ना᳓प्नुवन् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

But with whatever metre the gods yoked him, they achieved not him.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓ एता᳓ द्वा᳓त्रिँशतँ रा᳓त्रीर् अपश्यन् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

They saw (the rite of) these thirty-two nights.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः - ऋक्</summary>

द्वा᳓त्रिँशद्-अक्षरा ऽनुष्टु᳓क् ।  
आ᳓नुष्टुभᳶ प्रजा᳓पतिः ।  
स्वे᳓नैव᳓ छ᳓न्दसा प्रजा᳓पतिम् आप्त्वा᳓  
ऽभ्यारु᳓ह्य सुवर्गल्ँ᳓ लोक᳓म् आयन् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The Anustubh has thirty-two syllables, Prajapati is connected with the Anustubh; verily having gained Prajapati by his own metre they mounted on him and went to the world of heaven.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓ एवव्ँ᳓ विद्वाँ᳓स एता᳓ आ᳓सते -
द्वा᳓त्रिँशद् एताः᳓, द्वा᳓त्रिँशद् अक्षरा ऽनुष्टु᳓क्, आ᳓नुष्टुभᳶ प्रजा᳓पतिः,
स्वेनैव᳓ छ᳓न्दसा प्रजा᳓पतिम् आप्त्वा᳓ श्रि᳓यङ् गच्छन्ति [15]।
श्री᳓र् हि᳓ मनुष्य᳙स्य सुवर्गो᳓ लोकः᳓ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Those who knowing thus perform (the rite of) these thirty-two (nights)--the Anustubh has thirty-two syllables, Prajapati is connected with the Anustubh-gaining Prajapati by his own metre, go to prosperity [1], for the world of heaven for man is prosperity.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

द्वा᳓त्रिँशद् एताः᳓ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

These (nights) are thirty-two,
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

द्वा᳓त्रिँशद् अक्षरा ऽनुष्टु᳓क् ।
वा᳓ग् अनुष्टु᳓प्।  
स᳓र्वाम् एव᳓ वा᳓चम् आप्नुवन्ति ।  
स᳓र्वे वाचो᳓ वदिता᳓रो भवन्ति ।
स᳓र्वे हि᳓ श्रि᳓यङ् ग᳓च्छन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

the Anustubh has thirty-two syllables, the Anustubh is speech; verily they gain all speech; all become speakers of speech, for all attain prosperity.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ज्यो᳓तिर् गौ᳓र् आ᳓युर् इ᳓ति त्र्यहा᳓ भवन्ति।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There are Tryahas consisting of Jyotis, Go, and Ayus.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

इयव्ँ᳓ वा᳓व᳓ ज्यो᳓तिर्, अन्त᳓रिक्षङ् गौ᳓र्, असा᳓व् आ᳓युः।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The Jyotis is this (earth), the Go the atmosphere, the Ayus yonder (sky); verily they mount upon these worlds.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अभिपूर्व᳓न् +++(नव)+++ त्र्यहा᳓ भवन्ति ।  
अभिपूर्व᳓मेव᳓ सुवर्गल्ँ᳓ लोक᳓म् अभ्या᳓रोहन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The three days occur in order; verily in order do they mount upon the world of heaven.
</details>

बृहद्रथन्त॒राभ्याय्ँ॑यन्ती॒यव्ँ वाव र॑थन्त॒रम॒सौ बृ॒हदा॒भ्यामे॒व [2]

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(दिवसेषु एकान्तराभ्यां)+++ बृ᳓ह᳓द्र᳓थ᳓न्तरा᳓भ्याय्ँ यन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

They proceed with the Brhat and the Rathantara (Samans).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

बृ॒ह॒द्र॒थ॒न्त॒राभ्याय्ँ॑ यन्ति ।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

इयव्ँ᳓ वा᳓व᳓ रथन्तर᳓म्, असौ᳓ बृह᳓त् +++(चास्ति)+++।
आभ्या᳓मेव᳓ यन्ति᳓ +++(प्रथमदिनयोः)+++ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The Rathantara is this (earth), the Brhat yonder (sky); verily with them do they proceed;
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

इ॒यव्ँ वाव र॑थन्त॒रम् अ॒सौ बृ॒हत् ।
आ॒भ्यामे॒व य॒न्ति [2]।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अ᳓थो अन᳓योरेव᳓ प्र᳓ति तिष्ठन्ति ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

verily also in them they find support.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अथो॑ अ॒नयो॑रे॒व प्रति॑ तिष्ठन्ति ।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

एते᳓ वै᳓ यज्ञ᳓स्याञ्जसा᳓यनी स्रुती᳓+++(=मार्गौ)+++ ।  
ता᳓भ्याम् एव᳓ सुवर्गल्ँ᳓ लोकय्ँ᳓ यन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

These indeed are the quick paths of the sacrifice; verily by them do they proceed to the world of heaven.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ए॒ते वै य॒ज्ञस्या॑ञ्ज॒साय॑नी स्रु॒ती ।
ताभ्या॒मे॒व सु॑व॒र्गल्ँ लो॒कय्ँ य॑न्ति ।
</details>
</div>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

प᳓राञ्चो वा᳓ एते᳓ सुवर्गल्ँ᳓ लोक᳓म् अभ्या᳓रोह᳓न्ति᳓, ये᳓ प᳓राचस् त्र्यहा᳓न् उपय᳓न्ति ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Those who perform the three days in succession mount upon the world of heaven turning away (from earth).
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

प्रत्य᳓ङ् त्र्यहो᳓ भवति प्रत्य᳓वरूढ्या , अ᳓थो प्र᳓तिष्ठित्यै ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is a three day period reversed, for returning (from heaven), and also for support.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

उभ᳓योर् लोक᳓योर् ऋद्ध्वा᳓ + उ᳓त्तिष्ठन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Having prospered in both worlds they cease (the rite).
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

द्वा᳓त्रिँशद् एताः᳓ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

These (nights) are thirty-two.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ता᳓साय्ँ या᳓स् त्रिँश᳓त् - त्रिँश᳓द् अक्षरा विरा᳓ड्, अ᳓न्नव्ँ विरा᳓ट्।  
विरा᳓जा᳓ + एवा᳓न्ना᳓द्यम् अ᳓वरुन्धते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

As for thirty of them, the Viraj has thirty syllables, the Viraj is food; verily by the Viraj they win food.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ये᳓ द्वे᳓ - अहोरात्रे᳓ एव᳓ ते᳓। उभा᳓भ्याँ रूपा᳓भ्याँ सुवर्गल्ँ᳓ लोकय्ँ᳓ यन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

As for the (remaining) two, they are days and nights, and by both forms they go to the world of heaven.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अतिरात्रा᳓व् अभि᳓तो भवतᳶ प᳓रिगृहीत्यै ॥ [17]
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There are Atiratras on either side, for security.
</details>
