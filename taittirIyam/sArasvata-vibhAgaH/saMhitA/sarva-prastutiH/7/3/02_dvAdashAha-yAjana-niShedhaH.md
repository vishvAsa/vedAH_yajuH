<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ब्रह्मवादि᳓नो वदन्ति -  
कि᳓न् द्वादशाह᳓स्य प्रथमे᳓ना᳓ह्नर्त्वि᳓जाय्ँ य᳓जमानो वृङ्क्त इ᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The theologians say, 'By the first day of the twelve-day rite what is it that the sacrificer takes from the priests?'
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ते᳓ज इन्द्रिय᳓म् इ᳓ति  ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

'Brilliance and power' is (the answer).
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

'What by the second?'
</details>
