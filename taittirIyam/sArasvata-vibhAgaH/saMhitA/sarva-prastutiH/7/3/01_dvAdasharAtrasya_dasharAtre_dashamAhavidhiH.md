## जवः पापनाशश्च

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>प्र॒जवव्ँ॒ वा ए॒तेन॑ यन्ति॒, यद् द॑श॒मम् अहः॑।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

They go swiftly in that it is the tenth day.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(यतः - )+++ पापाव॒हीयव्ँ॒+++(=पापावहतिर्)+++ वा ए॒तेन॑ भवन्ति॒ , यद् द॑श॒मम् अहः॑ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

In that it is the tenth day they loosen their sins.
</details>



## अविवाक्यता

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

यो वै प्र॒जवय्ँ॑ य॒ताम् +++(मध्ये)+++ अप॑थेन प्रति॒पद्य॑ते॒ ,  
यस् स्था॒णुँ हन्ति॒+++(→गच्छति)+++ ,
यो भ्रेष॒न्+++(=निपतन्)+++ न्य्+एति॒ स ही॑यते । 
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He who among men going swiftly falls in with a trackless way, he who strikes a post, and he who stumbles, are left out.
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(एवम् अस्मिन्नपि कर्मणि - )+++ यो वै द॑श॒मे ऽह॑न्न् अविवा॒क्य +++(स्मृतिविनाशादिना)+++ उ॑पह॒न्यते॒ , स ही॑यते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

So he, who on this tenth day, the Avivakya, comes to grief, is left out.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

तस्मै॒ य उप॑हताय॒ व्याह॒+++(=विरुद्धमाह)+++, तम् ए॒वान्वा॒रभ्य॒ +++(हीयमानः)+++ सम॑श्नुते +++(सिद्धिम्)+++। अथ॒ यो व्याह॒ स ही॑यते [1]।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

If one points out (an error) to one who comes to grief, he seizes hold of him and comes out successfully, and then the one who pointed out (his error) is left out [1].
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>तस्मा॑द् दश॒मेऽह॑न्न् अविवा॒क्य उप॑हताय॒ न व्युच्यम्॑ ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Therefore on the tenth day, the Avivakya, one should not point out (errors) to one who comes to grief.
</details>



### दोषवारणम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(अविवाक्यतायाः खलु यज्ञफले दोषो भवेद् इति शङ्कायाम्,)+++  
अथो॒ खल्वा॑हुर् -
य॒ज्ञस्य॒ वै समृ॑द्धेन दे॒वास् सु॑व॒र्गल्ँ लो॒कम् आ॑यन् ,
य॒ज्ञस्य॒ व्यृ॑द्धे॒नासु॑रा॒न् परा॑भावय॒न्न् इति॑ ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Or rather they say, 'By that which was successful in the sacrifice the gods went to the world of heaven, and overcame the Asuras by what was unsuccessful.'
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>यत् खलु॒ वै य॒ज्ञस्य॒ समृ॑द्ध॒न् , तद् यज॑मानस्य॒, यद् व्यृ॑द्ध॒न् तद् भ्रातृ॑व्यस्य ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

What is successful in the sacrifice belongs to the sacrificer, what is unsuccessful to his enemy.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स यो वै द॑श॒मे ऽह॑न्न् अविवा॒क्य +++(स्मृतिभ्रंशादिना)+++ उ॑पह॒न्यते॑, स ए॒वाति॑ रेचयति +++(=शेषयति [यज्ञांशम्])+++ ।  
+++(तेन भ्रातृव्यः पुष्ट्टस् स्यात् - अतो वक्तव्यम् एव।)+++
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Now he, who comes to grief on the tenth day, the Avivakya, produces overmuch.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ते ये बाह्या॑ दृशी॒कवः॒+++(=प्रेक्षकाः)+++ स्युः, ते वि ब्रू॑युः।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Those who are outside as spectators [2] should point out (his error).
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

यदि॒ तत्र॒ न वि॒न्देयु॑र् +++(स्खलितज्ञातॄन्)+++, अन्तस् सद॒साद् +++(ऋत्विग्भिर्)+++ व्युच्य॑म् ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

If there be none there, then (the error) should be pointed out from within the Sadas.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

यदि॒ तत्र॒ न वि॒न्देयु॑र्, गृ॒हप॑तिना॒+++(=मुख्ययजमानेन)+++ व्युच्य॑म् ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

If there be none there, then it should be pointed out by the Grhapati.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>तद् व्युच्य॑मे॒व ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

At any rate it should be pointed out.
</details>



## सर्पराज्ञि-मन्त्र-वचनम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अथ॒ वा ए॒तत् स॑र्परा॒ज्ञिया॑+++(ज्ञ्याः)+++ +++('भूमिर्भूम्ना' इत्यादिभिः)+++ ऋ॒ग्भिस् स्तु॑वन्ति +++(तस्मिन्नहनि समाप्तिकाले)+++ ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

They sing the verses of the serpent queen on that day.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

इ॒यव्ँ वै +++(पृथिवी)+++ सर्प॑तो॒ राज्ञी॑ ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The queen of what creeps is this (earth).
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

यद् वा अ॒स्याङ् किञ्चार्च॑न्ति॒ ,  
यद् +++(तामेव)+++ आ॑नृ॒चुस्+++(=अर्चयन्ति)+++,  
तेने॒यँ स॑र्परा॒ज्ञी ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Whatever on this (earth) they praise, whatever they have praised, through that is this (earth) the serpent queen.
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ते यद् ए॒व किञ्च॑ वा॒चा ऽऽनृ॒चुर्+++(=अर्चयन्ति)+++  
यद् अ॒तो ऽध्य् अ॑र्चि॒तारः॑,  
तद् उ॒भय॑म् आ॒प्त्वा ऽव॒रुध्योत्ति॑ष्ठा॒मेति॑
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Now whatever they have praised with speech and what they will praise thereafter, (it is done thinking), 'Having won both and obtained them, let us stop.'
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>ताभि॒र् मन॑सा स्तुवते ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

They sing these (verses) with the mind.
</details>



<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

न वा इ॒माम् अ॑श्वर॒थो नाश्व॑तरीर॒थस् स॒द्यᳶ पर्या॑प्तुम् अर्हति ।  
मनो॒ वा इ॒माँ स॒द्यᳶ पर्या॑प्तुम् अर्हति॒ ,  
मन॒ᳶ परि॑भवितुम् +++(तस्मान् मनसा स्तोतव्यमिति)+++ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Neither a horse chariot nor a mule chariot can in one moment encompass this (earth), but mind can in one moment encompass it, mind can overcome it.
</details>
