## फलम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

प्र॒जाप॑तिर् अकामयत - "अन्ना॒दस् स्या॒म्" इति॑ ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Prajapati desired, 'May I be an eater of food.'
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स ए॒तँ स॑प्तदशरा॒त्रम् अ॑पश्यत् ।  
तम् आह॑रत् । तेना॑यजत ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He saw this (rite of) seventeen nights; he grasped it, and sacrificed with it.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ततो॒ वै सो॑ऽन्ना॒दो॑ऽभवत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Then indeed he became an eater of food.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य ए॒वव्ँ वि॒द्वाँस॑स् सप्तदशरा॒त्रम् आस॑ते, ऽन्ना॒दा ए॒व भ॑वन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Those, who knowing thus perform (the rite) of seventeen nights, become eaters of food.
</details>

## विधानम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

पञ्चा॒हो भ॑वति ।  
+++(हेमन्त-शिशिरयोर् एकत्वात्)+++ पञ्च॒ वा ऋ॒तव॑स् सव्ँवत्स॒रः ।
ऋ॒तुष्व् ए॒व सव्ँ॑वत्स॒रे प्रति॑ तिष्ठ॒न्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There is a period of five days; the seasons are five in the year; verily in the seasons and the year they find support.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अथो॒ पञ्चा॑क्षरा प॒ङ्क्तिः ।
+++(पङ्क्तिप्रभवत्वाद्वा धानादिपञ्चकसाधनत्वाद्वा)+++ पाङ्क्तो॑ य॒ज्ञः । य॒ज्ञम् ए॒वाव॑ रुन्धते ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Again the Pankti is of five elements, the sacrifice is fivefold; verily they win the sacrifice.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

+++(छन्दोमवतः प्रशंसार्थं वक्ति - )+++ अस॑त्त्रव्ँ॒ वा ए॒तद् यद् अ॑छन्दो॒मम् ।

</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There can be no Sattra where there is no Chandoma; in that there axe Chandomas, there is the Sattra.
</details>

</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>दे॒वता॑ ए॒व पृ॒ष्ठैर् अव॑ रुन्धते प॒शूञ् छ॑न्दो॒मैः ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The gods they win by the Prsthas, the cattle by the Chandomas.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The Prsthas are force and strength, the Chandomas cattle;
</details>

ओजो॒ वै वी॒र्य॑म्पृ॒ष्ठानि॑ प॒शव॑श्छन्दो॒माः ।
