</details>

## फलम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

प्रजा᳓पतिस् सुवर्गल्ँ᳓ लोक᳓म् ऐत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Prajapati went to the world of heaven.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓न् देवा᳓ अ᳓न्वायन् ।  
ता᳓न् आदित्या᳓श् च पश᳓वश् चा᳓न्वायन् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The gods followed him, and the Adityas and the cattle followed them.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>ते᳓ देवा᳓ अब्रुवन्-
या᳓न् पशू᳓न् उपा᳓जीविष्म त᳓ इमे᳙ ऽन्वाग्मन्न् इ᳓ति ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The gods said, 'The cattle on which we have lived have followed us.'
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ते᳓भ्य एत᳓ञ् चतुर्दशरात्र᳓म् प्र᳓त्यौहन्+++(=प्रत्यवहन्)+++ ।  
+++(पृष्ठानन्तरं भूयोपि तत्रैव त्रिरात्रे प्रत्यवरोहणं प्रत्यूहः ।)+++
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

They arranged this (rite) of fourteen nights away for them.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓ आदित्याᳶ᳓ पृष्ठै᳓स् सुवर्गल्ँ᳓ लोक᳓म् आ᳓रोहन् , त्र्यहा᳓भ्याम् अस्मिल्ँ᳓ लोके᳓ पशू᳓न् प्र᳓त्यौहन् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The Adityas mounted the world of heaven with the Prsthas; they arranged the cattle in this world with the two Tryahas.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

पृष्ठै᳓र् आदित्या᳓ अमु᳓ष्मिल्ँ लोक᳓ आ᳓र्ध्नुवन् , त्र्यहा᳓भ्याम् अस्मि᳓न् लोके᳓ पश᳓वः । [11] ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

By means of the Prsthas the Adityas prospered in yonder world, the cattle in this world by the Tryahas [1].
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य᳓ एवव्ँ᳓ विद्वाँ᳓सश् चतुर्दश-रात्र᳓म् आ᳓सत ,
उभ᳓योर् एव᳓ लोक᳓योर् ऋध्नुवन्त्य्- अस्मिँ᳓श् चामु᳓ष्मिँश्च ।  
पृष्ठै᳓र् एवा᳓मु᳓ष्मिल्ँ लोक᳓ ऋध्नुव᳓न्ति ,
त्र्यहा᳓भ्याम् अस्मिल्ँ᳓  लोके᳓ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Those who, knowing thus, perform (the rite of) fourteen nights, prosper in both worlds, in this and in yonder; by the Prsthas they prosper in yonder world and by the Tryahas in this world.
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The three days are the Jyotis, Go, and Ayus.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

इयव्ँ᳓ वा᳓व᳓ ज्यो᳓तिर्, अन्त᳓रिक्षङ् गौ᳓र्, असा᳓व् आ᳓युः ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The Jyotis is this (earth), the Go the atmosphere, the Ayus yonder (sky); verily they mount upon these worlds.
</details>

The Jyotis is this (earth), the Go the atmosphere, the Ayus yonder (sky); verily they mount upon these worlds.

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

If the Prsthas were on one side, there would be a lack of balance; the Prsthas are in the middle, for balance [2].

</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The Prsthas are force and strength; verily they place force and strength in the middle.
</details>

They proceed with the Brhat and the Rathantara (Samans).

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The Rathantara is this (earth), the Brhat yonder (sky); verily with them do they proceed;

इ॒यव्ँ वाव र॑थन्त॒रम् अ॒सौ बृ॒हत् ।
आ॒भ्यामे॒व य॒न्ति [2]।

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

verily also in them they find support.
</details>

ता᳓भ्याम् एव᳓ सुवर्गल्ँ᳓ लोकय्ँ᳓ यन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

These indeed are the quick paths of the sacrifice; verily by them do they proceed to the world of heaven.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

Having prospered in either world they cease (the rite).
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The (nights) are fourteen.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ता᳓साय्ँ या᳓ द᳓श द᳓शाक्षरा विरा᳓ड्। अ᳓न्नव्ँ विरा᳓ड्। विरा᳓जैवा᳓न्ना᳓द्यम् अ᳓व रुन्धते ।

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

</details>

</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

As for four, the quarters are four; verily they find support in the quarters.
</details>
