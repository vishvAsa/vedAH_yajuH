## परिचयः

<details><summary>विश्वास-टिप्पनी</summary>

पञ्चदशरात्रयोर् एकतरश् छन्दोमवान् - यथा ऽतिरात्रो, (ज्योतिर्, गौर्, आयुस्), द्वादशाहस्य दशाहान्य् (ज्योतिष्टोमः, ८ उक्थ्याः, ज्योत्ष्टोमः), अतिरात्र इति ।  
एतदिदानीं विदधाति।
</details>

## फलम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

इन्द्रो॒ वै स॒दृङ् दे॒वता॑भिर् आसीत् ।  
स न व्या॒वृत॑म् अगच्छत् ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Indra was on a level with the gods, he was not distinguished from them.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स प्र॒जाप॑ति॒म् उपा॑धावत् ।  
तस्मा॑ ए॒तम् प॑ञ्चदशरा॒त्रम् प्राय॑च्छत् ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He ran up to Prajapati; he gave him this (rite) of fifteen nights.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

तमाह॑रत् । तेना॑यजत ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He grasped it, and sacrificed with it.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ततो॒ वै सो॑ ऽन्याभि॑र् दे॒वता॑भिर् व्या॒वृत॑म् अगच्छत् ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Then indeed he became distinguished from the other gods.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

य ए॒वव्ँ वि॒द्वाँसᳶ॑ पञ्चदशरा॒त्रम् आस॑ते,  
व्या॒वृत॑म् ए॒व पा॒प्मना॒ भ्रातृ॑व्येण गच्छन्ति ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Those who knowing thus perform (the rite) of fifteen nights attain distinction from the evil enemy.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ज्योति॒र् गौर् आयु॒र् इति॑ त्र्य॒हो भ॑वति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The three days are the Jyotis, Go, and Ayus.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

इ॒यव्ँ वाव ज्योति॑र्, अ॒न्तरि॑क्ष॒ङ् गौर्, अ॒साव् आयुः॑ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The Jyotis is this (earth), the Go the atmosphere, the Ayus yonder (sky); verily they mount upon these worlds.
</details>

ए॒ष्वे॑व लो॒केषु॒ प्रति॑ तिष्ठ॒न्ति ।

The Jyotis is this (earth), the Go the atmosphere [1], the Ayus yonder (sky); verily they find support in these worlds.
</details>

+++(छन्दोमवतः प्रशंसार्थं वक्ति - )+++ अस॑त्त्रव्ँ॒ वा ए॒तद् यद् अ॑छन्दो॒मम् ।
यच् छ॑न्दो॒मा भव॑न्ति॒ तेन॑ स॒त्त्रम् ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There can be no Sattra where there is no Chandoma; in that there axe Chandomas, there is the Sattra.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अस॑त्त्रव्ँ॒ वा ए॒तद् यद॑छन्दो॒मम् ।
यच्छ॑न्दो॒मा भव॑न्ति॒ तेन॑ स॒त्त्रम् ।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>दे॒वता॑ ए॒व पृ॒ष्ठैर् अव॑ रुन्धते प॒शूञ् छ॑न्दो॒मैः ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The gods they win by the Prsthas, the cattle by the Chandomas.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

दे॒वता॑ ए॒व पृ॒ष्ठैरव॑ रुन्धते प॒शूञ्छ॑न्दो॒मैः ।  
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>ओजो॒ वा वी॒र्य॑म् पृ॒ष्ठानि॑, प॒शव॑श् छन्दो॒मा ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

The Prsthas are force and strength, the Chandomas cattle;
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

ओजो॒ वै वी॒र्य॑म्पृ॒ष्ठानि॑ प॒शव॑श्छन्दो॒माः ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

verily in force [2] and strength they find support.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

पञ्चदशरा॒त्रो भ॑वति, पञ्चद॒शो वज्रः॑।  
वज्र॑मे॒व भ्रातृ॑व्येभ्य॒ᳶ प्र ह॑रन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

(The rite) is of fifteen nights; the bolt is fifteenfold; verily they hurl the bolt at their enemies.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अ॒ति॒रा॒त्राव॒भितो॑ भवतः ।
इन्द्रि॒यस्य॒ परि॑गृहीत्यै ॥ [15]  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

There are Atiratras on either side, for securing power.
</details>
