## फलम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

इन्द्रो॒ वै शि॑थि॒ल इ॒वाप्र॑तिष्ठित आसीत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Indra was as it were loose and unfixed.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

सोऽसु॑रेभ्योऽबिभेत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He was afraid of the Asuras.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स प्र॒जाप॑ति॒म् उपा॑धावत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He ran up to Prajapati;
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

तस्मा॑ ए॒तम् प॑ञ्चदशरा॒त्रव्ँ वज्र॒म् प्राय॑च्छत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

he gave him this (rite) of fifteen nights as a bolt.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

तेनासु॑रान् परा॒भाव्य॑ वि॒जित्य॒ श्रिय॑म् अगच्छत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

With it he overcame and conquered the Asuras and attained prosperity.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अ॒ग्नि॒ष्टुता॑+++(=सर्वाग्नेयस् त्रिवृद्-एकाहविशेषः)+++ पा॒प्मान॒न् निर॑दहत ।
प॒ञ्च॒द॒श॒रा॒त्रेणौजो॑ बल॑म् इन्द्रि॒यव्ँ वी॒र्य॑म् आ॒त्मन्न् अ॑धत्त ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

By the Agnistut he burned away the evil, by (the rite) of fifteen nights he placed force, might, power, and strength in himself.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>य ए॒वव्ँ वि॒द्वाँसᳶ॑ पञ्चदशरा॒त्रम् आस॑ते,
भ्रातृ॑व्यान् ए॒व प॑रा॒भाव्य॑ वि॒जित्य॒ श्रिय॑ङ् गच्छन्ति ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Those, who knowing thus perform (the rite) of fifteen nights overcome and conquer their enemies and attain prosperity.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>अग्नि॒ष्टुता॑ पा॒प्मान॒न् निर्द॑हन्ते [16] ।
प॒ञ्च॒द॒श॒रा॒त्रेणौजो॒, बल॑म् इन्द्रि॒यव्ँ वी॒र्य॑म् आ॒त्मन् द॑धते ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

By the Agnistut they burn away the evil [1], by (the rite) of fifteen nights they place force, might, power, and strength in themselves.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ए॒ता ए॒व प॑श॒व्याः॑ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

These (nights) are full of cattle.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

पञ्च॑दश॒ वा अ॑र्धमा॒सस्य॒ रात्र॑यः ।
अ॒र्ध॒मा॒स॒शस् सव्ँ॑वत्स॒र आ॑प्यते ।  
सव्ँ॒व॒त्स॒र॒म् प॒शवोऽनु॒ प्र जा॑यन्ते ।
तस्मा॑त् पश॒व्याः।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Fifteen indeed are the nights of the half-month, the year is made up of half-months, cattle are born throughout the year; therefore (these nights) are full of cattle.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ए॒ता ए॒व सु॑व॒र्ग्याः॑ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

These (nights) are heavenly.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

पञ्च॑दश॒ वा अ॑र्धमा॒सस्य॒ रात्र॑यः । अ॒र्ध॒मा॒स॒शस् सव्ँ॑वत्स॒र आ॑प्यते ।  
सव्ँ॒व॒त्स॒रस् सु॑व॒र्गो लो॒कः । तस्मा॑त् सुव॒र्ग्याः॑ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Fifteen indeed are the nights of the half-month, the year is made up of half months, the world of heaven is the year; verily (these nights) are heavenly.
</details>

These (nights) are fifteen.
