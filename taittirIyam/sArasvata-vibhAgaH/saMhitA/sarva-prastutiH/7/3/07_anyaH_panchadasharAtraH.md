## फलम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

इ᳓न्द्रो वै᳓ शिथिल᳓ इवा᳓प्रतिष्ठित आसीत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Indra was as it were loose and unfixed.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

सो᳓ऽसुरेभ्योऽबिभेत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He was afraid of the Asuras.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स᳓ प्रजा᳓पतिम् उ᳓पाधावत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

He ran up to Prajapati;
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

त᳓स्मा एत᳓म् पञ्चदशरात्रव्ँ᳓ व᳓ज्रम् प्रा᳓यच्छत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

he gave him this (rite) of fifteen nights as a bolt.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

ते᳓ना᳓सुरान् पराभा᳓व्य विजि᳓त्य श्रि᳓यम् अगच्छत् ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

With it he overcame and conquered the Asuras and attained prosperity.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अग्निष्टु᳓ता+++(=सर्वाग्नेयस् त्रिवृद्-एकाहविशेषः)+++ पाप्मा᳓नन् नि᳓रदहत ।
पञ्चदशरात्रे᳓णौ᳓जो ब᳓लम् इन्द्रियव्ँ᳓ वीर्य᳙म् आत्म᳓न्न् अधत्त ।  
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

By the Agnistut he burned away the evil, by (the rite) of fifteen nights he placed force, might, power, and strength in himself.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>य᳓ एवव्ँ᳓ विद्वाँ᳓सᳶ पञ्चदशरात्र᳓म् आ᳓सते᳓,
भ्रा᳓तृव्यान् एव᳓ पराभा᳓व्य विजि᳓त्य श्रि᳓यङ् गच्छन्ति ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Those, who knowing thus perform (the rite) of fifteen nights overcome and conquer their enemies and attain prosperity.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>अ᳓ग्निष्टु᳓ता पाप्मा᳓नन् नि᳓र्दहन्ते [16] ।
पञ्चदशरात्रे᳓णौ᳓जो, ब᳓लम् इन्द्रियव्ँ᳓ वीर्य᳙म् आत्म᳓न् दधते ।</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

By the Agnistut they burn away the evil [1], by (the rite) of fifteen nights they place force, might, power, and strength in themselves.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

एता᳓ एव᳓ पशव्याः᳙ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

These (nights) are full of cattle.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

प᳓ञ्चदश वा᳓ अर्धमास᳓स्य रा᳓त्रयः ।
अर्धमासश᳓स् सव्ँवत्सर᳓ आप्यते ।  
सव्ँवत्सरम् पश᳓वो᳓ऽनु प्र᳓ जायन्ते ।
त᳓स्मात् पशव्याः᳓।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Fifteen indeed are the nights of the half-month, the year is made up of half-months, cattle are born throughout the year; therefore (these nights) are full of cattle.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

एता᳓ एव᳓ सुवर्ग्याः᳙ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

These (nights) are heavenly.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

प᳓ञ्चदश वा᳓ अर्धमास᳓स्य रा᳓त्रयः । अर्धमासश᳓स् सव्ँवत्सर᳓ आप्यते ।  
सव्ँवत्सर᳓स् सुवर्गो᳓ लोकः᳓ । त᳓स्मात् सुवर्ग्याः᳙ ।
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

Fifteen indeed are the nights of the half-month, the year is made up of half months, the world of heaven is the year; verily (these nights) are heavenly.
</details>

These indeed are the quick paths of the sacrifice; verily by them do they proceed to the world of heaven.

</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

These (nights) are fifteen.
