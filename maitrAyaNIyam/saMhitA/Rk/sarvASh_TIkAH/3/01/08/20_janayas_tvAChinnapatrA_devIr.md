<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

'May the divine Janīs of unbroken wings, possessing all gods, cook you, the cauldron, like an Aṅgiras on the seat of the earth.'
</details>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

8. 'May the divine women, with unclipped wings, dear to all the gods, bake thee, Aṅgiras-like, O fire-pan, in the lap of the earth!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“O Ukhā! may the divine Jānis (Goddesses of that name), with unclipped wings, dear to all the gods, bake thee in the bosom of the earth, just as Aṅgiras (did)”.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

ज᳓नयस् त्वा᳓छिन्नपत्रा देवी᳓र् विश्व᳓देव्यवतीः पृथिव्याः᳓ सध᳓स्थे अङ्गिरस्व᳓त् पचन्तूखा इ᳓ति॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

ज᳘नयस्त्वा᳘ च्छिन्नपत्रा देवीः᳘॥  
(र्व्वि) व्विश्व᳘देव्यावतीः पृथिव्याः᳘ सध᳘स्थे ऽअङ्गिरस्व᳘त्पचन्तूख ऽइ᳘ति
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - Weber</summary>

ज᳘नयस्त्वा᳘छिन्नपत्रा देवीः᳟॥  
विश्व᳘देव्यावतीः पृथिव्याः᳘ सध᳘स्थे अङ्गिरस्व᳘त्पचन्तूख इ᳘ति
</details>
