<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

May Prajāpati generate offspring for us; may Dhātṛ establish [it], being well-disposed!  
May the Year, in accord with the seasons, may the lord of nourishment establish nourishment in me!

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

May Prajāpati produce offspring for us; may the Creator, well-disposed, grant [them];  
may the Year, adapted with the seasons, the lord of nourishment, place nourishment in me!

May Prajāpati generate offspring for us; may the Creator, benevolent, ordain [it];  
and the Year, in harmony with the seasons; may the lord of nourishment bestow nourishment upon me.
</details>
