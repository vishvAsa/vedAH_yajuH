<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Who became king of the breathing and the blinking, the lord of all the moving world,  
who rules over this two-footed and four-footed: to which god shall we do homage with an oblation?
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

यः᳓ प्राणतो᳓ निमिषत᳓श् च रा᳓जा प᳓तिर् वि᳓श्वस्य ज᳓गतो बभू᳓व  
ई᳓शे यो᳓ अस्य᳓ द्विप᳓दश् च᳓तुष्पदः क᳓स्मै देवा᳓य हवि᳓षा विधेम
</details>
