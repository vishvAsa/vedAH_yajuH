<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Who supported these two, heaven and earth, propped apart, the two trembling worlds,  
upon whom stretched out the sun goes forth: to which god shall we do homage with an oblation?
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

Who supported these two, heaven and earth, propped up though trembling;  
upon whom the sun, extended, travels: to which god shall we make offering with the oblation?
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

य᳓ इमे᳓ द्या᳓वापृथिवी᳓ तस्तभाने᳓ अ᳓धारयद् रो᳓दसी रे᳓जमाने  
य᳓स्मिन्न् अ᳓धि वि᳓ततः सू᳓रा ए᳓ति क᳓स्मै देवा᳓य हवि᳓षा विधेम
</details>
