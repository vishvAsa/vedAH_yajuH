<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

By whom the sky was made mighty and the earth firm, by whom the sun was propped up, by whom the vault of heaven,  
who measured out the wider intermediate space: to which god shall we do homage with an oblation?
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

By whom the mighty heaven and the earth were made firm, by whom the sun was propped up, by whom the firmament;  
who measured out the wide midspace: to which god shall we make offering with the oblation?
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

ये᳓न द्यौ᳓र् उग्रा᳓ पृथिवी᳓ च दृढा᳓ ये᳓न स्वः᳙ स्तभितँ᳓ ये᳓न ना᳓कः  
यो᳓ अन्त᳓रिक्षँ विममे᳓ व᳓रीयः क᳓स्मै देवा᳓य हवि᳓षा विधेम
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काठकम्</summary>

यो᳓ अन्त᳓रिक्षं विममे᳓ व᳓रीयः क᳓स्मै देवा᳓य हवि᳓षा विधेम
</details>
