<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Whose these mountains are all by his greatness, whose the ocean along with the Rasā they call,  
whose the directions, the five divine quarters: to which god shall we do homage with an oblation?
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

Whose are all these mountains by his greatness; whose, they say, is the ocean together with the Rasā;  
whose regions are the five divine directions: to which god shall we make offering with the oblation?
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

य᳓स्येमे᳓ वि᳓श्वे गिर᳓यो महित्वा᳓ समुद्रँ᳓ य᳓स्य रस᳓या सहा᳓हुः᳓  
दि᳓शो य᳓स्य प्रदि᳓शः प᳓ञ्च देवीः᳓ क᳓स्मै देवा᳓य हवि᳓षा विधेम
</details>
