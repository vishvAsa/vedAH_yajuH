<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

We have approached with great reverence the youngest [god], who has shone, kindled in his own house,  
of wondrous light between the two broad worlds, well-sacrificed, facing every direction.  
This [Agni] here [...].
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

We have approached with great reverence the youngest [god], who has shone brightly, kindled in his own home,  
shining brightly between the broad heaven and earth, well-offered, turned toward all sides.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

अ᳓गन्म महा᳓ न᳓मसा य᳓विष्ठँ यो᳓ दीदा᳓य स᳓मिद्धः स्वे᳓ दुरोणे᳓  
चित्र᳓भानुँ रो᳓दसी अन्त᳓र् उर्वी᳓ स्वा᳙हुतँ विश्व᳓तः प्रत्य᳓ञ्चम्  
अय᳓म् इह᳓
</details>

</details>
