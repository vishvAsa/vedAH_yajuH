<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

This indeed is she who shone forth first; she moves having entered into the waters.  
The young bride has brought forth, newly giving birth; three greatnesses attend upon her.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

This indeed is she who shone forth first; entering into these she moves within.  
The bride, the newly-calved mother, has lowed; three greatnesses attend upon her.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

वधू᳓र् मिमाय नवग᳓ज् ज᳓नित्री त्र᳓य एनां महिमा᳓नः सचन्ते
</details>

<details><summary>Schroeder - टिप्पणी</summary>

- **[591_1]** So Ch; in MS 2, 13, 10 haben wir साप्स्वन्तश्चरति ; AV 8, 9, 11 व्यौछदास्वितरासु चरति; TS 4, 3, 11, 1 व्योच्छदन्तरस्यां चरति; keine dieser Lesungen liegt die von Ch so nahe, daß die Correctur evident wäre; vielleicht ist सा तास्व° zu lesen ? oder doch nach MS zu corrigiren? (Thus Ch; in MS 2, 13, 10 we have साप्स्वन्तश्चरति; AV 8, 9, 11 reads व्यौछदास्वितरासु चरति; TS 4, 3, 11, 1 reads व्योच्छदन्तरस्यां चरति; none of these readings is so close to that of Ch that the emendation would be obvious; perhaps सा तास्व° is to be read? or should it after all be emended after MS?)
