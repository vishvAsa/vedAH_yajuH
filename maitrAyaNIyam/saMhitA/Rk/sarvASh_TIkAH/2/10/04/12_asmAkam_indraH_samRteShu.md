<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Indra be ours when the banners clash; let those arrows of ours conquer!  
Let our heroes be superior, and do you, O gods, protect us in contests!
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

May Indra be ours when the banners clash; may our arrows conquer;  
may our heroes be superior, and may the gods protect us in battles.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>
