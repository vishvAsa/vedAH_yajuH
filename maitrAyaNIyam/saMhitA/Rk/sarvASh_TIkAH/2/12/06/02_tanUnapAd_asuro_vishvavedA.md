<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Tanūnapāt, the Asura who knows all, the god, onto the god-leading  
paths for the gods, let him anoint [them] with honey, with ghee; pleased, he reaches the sacrifice with honey.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

Tanūnapāt, the asura, all-knowing, the god among gods,  
anoints the paths with honey, with ghee; rejoicing, he reaches the sacrifice with honey.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

त᳓नून᳓पाद् अ᳓सुरो विश्व᳓वेदा देवो᳓ देवे᳓भ्यो देवया᳓नान्  
पथो᳓ अनक्तु म᳓ध्वा घृते᳓न । म᳓ध्वा यज्ञं᳓ नक्षति प्रीणानः᳓
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काठकम्</summary>

तनूनपादसुरो विश्ववेदा देवो देवेषु देवः।  
पथो अनक्ति मध्वा घृतेन मध्वा यज्ञं नक्षति प्रीणानः
</details>
