<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Homage to those who string [the bow] and fit [the arrow] among you, homage!  
Homage to those who draw [the bow] and discharge [it] among you, homage!  
Homage to those who shoot and pierce among you, homage!
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

Homage to those who string [the bow] and to those who fit [the arrow], homage to you.  
Homage to those who draw [the bow] and to those who shoot, homage to you.  
Homage to those who loose [the arrow] and to those who pierce, homage to you.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

न᳓म आतन्वाने᳓भ्यः प्रतिद᳓धानेभ्यश् च वो न᳓मस्  
न᳓म आय᳓छद्भ्यो विसृज᳓द्भ्यश् च वो न᳓मस्  
न᳓मो᳓ ऽस्यद्भ्यो वि᳓ध्यद्भ्यश् च वो न᳓मस्
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काठकम्</summary>

न᳓म आय᳓च्छद्भ्यो᳓ऽस्यद्भ्यश्च वो न᳓मो  
न᳓मो विसृज᳓द्भ्यो वि᳓ध्यद्भ्यश्च वो न᳓मो
</details>
