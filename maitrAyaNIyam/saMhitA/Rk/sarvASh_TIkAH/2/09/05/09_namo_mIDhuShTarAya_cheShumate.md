<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Homage to the most bounteous one and to the arrow-bearer!  
Homage to the short one and to the dwarf!  
Homage to the great one and to the taller one!  
Homage to the elder and to the prosperous one!
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

न᳓मो मीढु᳓ष्टराय चे᳓षुमते च  
न᳓मो ह्रस्वा᳓य च वामना᳓य च  
न᳓मो बृहते᳓ च व᳓र्षीयसे च  
न᳓मो वृद्धा᳓य च सुवृ᳓ध्वने च
</details>
