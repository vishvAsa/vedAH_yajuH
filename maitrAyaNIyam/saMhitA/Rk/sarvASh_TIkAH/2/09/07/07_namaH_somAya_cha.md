<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Homage to Soma and to Rudra!  
Homage to the copper-colored one and to the ruddy one!  
Homage to the one who brings welfare to cattle and to the Lord of Cattle!
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

न᳓मः सो᳓माय च रुद्रा᳓य च  
न᳓मस् ताम्रा᳓य चारुणा᳓य च  
न᳓मः शंग᳓वे च पशुप᳓तये च
</details>
