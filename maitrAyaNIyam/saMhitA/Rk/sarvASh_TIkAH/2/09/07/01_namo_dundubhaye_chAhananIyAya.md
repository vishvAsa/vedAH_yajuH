<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Homage to the drum and to the drumstick!  
Homage to the bold and to the seizing one!  
Homage to the one with the quiver and to the one with the arrow-case!  
Homage to the one of sharp arrows and to the weaponed one!
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

Homage to the one of the drum and to the one of the drumstick.  
Homage to the bold one and to the prudent one.  
Homage to the quiver-bearer and to the arrow-holder.  
Homage to the one of sharp arrows and to the weapon-bearer.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

न᳓मो दुन्दुभ᳓ये चाहननी᳓याय च  
न᳓मो धृष्ण᳓वे च प्रमृशा᳓य च  
न᳓मो निषङ्गि᳓णे चेषुधिम᳓ते च  
न᳓मस् तिग्मे᳓षवे चायुधि᳓ने च
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काठकम्</summary>

न᳓मस्तीक्ष्णे᳓षवे चायुधि᳓ने च
</details>
