<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

'Golden-coloured at the dawning of the dawn, with iron pillars at the rising of the sun,  
do you mount the chariot-seat, O Varuṇa and Mitra, and thence behold Aditi and Diti!'
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

Golden-hued at the dawn of the morn, iron-pillared at the rising of the sun,  
mount, Varuṇa and Mitra, your throne-car; thence do you look upon Aditi and Diti.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

हि᳓रण्यवर्णम् उष᳓सो व्यु᳙ष्टा अ᳓यःस्थूणम् उ᳓दितौ सू᳓र्यस्य  
आ᳓रोहथो वरुण मित्र ग᳓र्तं त᳓तश् चक्राथे अ᳓दितिं दि᳓तिं च
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काठकम्</summary>

हिरण्यवर्णमुषसो व्युष्टा अयस्स्थूणमुदितौ सूर्यस्य।  
आरोहथो वरुण मित्र गर्तं तत्र चक्राथे अदितिं दितिं च
</details>
