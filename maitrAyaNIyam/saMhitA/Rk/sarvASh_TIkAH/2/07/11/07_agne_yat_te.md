<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

O Agni, what brilliance is yours in heaven, on earth, what on the mountains, in the plants, in the waters,  
with which you have stretched out the wide atmosphere, that shining beam of yours is billowy, watching over men.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

O Agni, what brilliance is yours in heaven, what on earth, what in the mountains, in the plants, in the waters,  
by which you have overspread the broad atmosphere, fierce is that beam of yours, billowy, watching over men.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

अ᳓ग्ने य᳓त् ते दिवि᳓ व᳓र्चः पृथिव्याँ᳓ य᳓त् प᳓र्वतेष्व् ओ᳓षधीष्व् अप्सु᳓  
ये᳓नान्त᳓रिक्षम् उर्व् आ᳙तत᳓न्थ त्वेषः᳓ स᳓ भानु᳓र् अर्णवो᳓ नृचक्षः
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काठकम्</summary>

अग्ने यत्ते दिवि वर्चः पृथिव्यां यत्पर्वतेष्वोषधीष्वप्सु।  
येनान्तरिक्षमुर्वाततन्थ त्वेषस्स भानुरर्णवो नृचक्षाः
</details>
