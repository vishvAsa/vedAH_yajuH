<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Take heed of this speech of mine, O youngest, most generous, offered, O powerful one!  
One finds fault, one praises; I praise your praise-worthy body, O Agni.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

Be attentive to this word of mine, o youngest one, most generous, brought forth, o independent one!  
One finds fault, another praises; I praise your praiseworthy body, o Agni.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

नि᳓न्दति त्वो अ᳓नु त्वो ववन्द वन्दा᳓रुं ते तन्वँ᳙ वन्दे अग्ने
</details>
