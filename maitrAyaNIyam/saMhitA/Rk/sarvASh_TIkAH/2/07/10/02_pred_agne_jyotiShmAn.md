<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Go forward, O Agni, full of light, with your auspicious flames!
</details>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

9. [Whilst driving thither, he mutters, Vāj. S. XII, 32] 'Go forth, O Agni, brilliant thou with propitious flames!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

So (it amounts to) his proceeding towards his own direction.
</details>
