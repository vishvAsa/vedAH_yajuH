<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

the one of good resolve in the place of meeting, in the seats;  
Vaiśvānara, of all-encompassing range, through the sacred formulation, in the station of praise, the purifying one brought hither.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

They kindle with fuel Agni as he is kindled, the wonderful, in the common seat, in the seats;  
Vaiśvānara, all-embracing through the holy power, brought here, purifying, in the dwelling of the praise-song.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

समिध्य᳓मानँ समि᳓धा स᳓मिन्धते अग्निँ᳓ सध᳓स्थे स᳓दनेषु सुक्र᳓तुम्  
वैश्वानरं᳓ ब्र᳓ह्मणा विश्व᳓व्यचसँ स्तो᳓मस्य धा᳓मन् प᳓वमानम् आ᳓भृतम्
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काठकम्</summary>

समिध्यमानँ समिधा समिन्धतेऽग्निँ सधस्थे सदनेष्वद्भुतम्।  
वैश्वानरं ब्रह्मणा विश्वव्यचसँ स्तोमस्य धामन्पवमानमाभृतम्
</details>
