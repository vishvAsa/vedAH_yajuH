<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Vaiśvānara, of all-encompassing range, through the sacred formulation, deposited in the station of praise, [Agni] suitable for the loose earth.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

We seek the insight, the immortality of the gods, Agni, unmoved in common seat, in seats;  
Vaiśvānara, all-embracing through the holy power, the dweller in the loose soil, I set down in the dwelling of the praise-song.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

क्र᳓तुं देवा᳓नां महिमा᳓नम् ईमहे अग्निँ᳓ सध᳓स्थे स᳓दनेष्व् अ᳓द्भुतम्  
वैश्वानरं᳓ ब्र᳓ह्मणा विश्व᳓व्यचसँ स्तो᳓मस्य धा᳓मन् नि᳓दधे पुरीष्य᳙म्
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काठकम्</summary>

क्रतुं देवानाममृतत्वमीमहेऽग्निँ सधस्थे सदनेष्वच्युतम्।  
वैश्वानरं ब्रह्मणा विश्वव्यचसँ स्तोमस्य धामन्निदधे पुरीष्यम्
</details>
