<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Aditi yielding ghee for the folk—O Agni, do not harm her in the highest firmament!
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

This thousandfold fountain of a hundred streams, swelling in the midst of the flood,  
milking ghee for men, Aditi: o Agni, do not harm [her] in the highest firmament!
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

इमँ᳓ साहस्रँ᳓ शत᳓धारम् उ᳓त्सँ व्यच्य᳓मानँ सलिल᳓स्य म᳓धे  
घृतं᳓ दु᳓हानाम् अ᳓दितिं ज᳓नाया᳓ग्ने मा᳓ हिँसीः परमे᳓ व्यो᳙मन्
</details>

</details>
