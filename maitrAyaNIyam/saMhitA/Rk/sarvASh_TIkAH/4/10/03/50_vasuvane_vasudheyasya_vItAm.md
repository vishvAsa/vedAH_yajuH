enjoy [it] for the winning of wealth, of the foundation of wealth.  
May the three divine goddesses, the three goddesses,  
enjoy [it] for the winning of wealth, of the foundation of wealth.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

May the three divine ones, the three goddesses,  
for the winning of wealth, partake of the treasure to be given;
</details>
