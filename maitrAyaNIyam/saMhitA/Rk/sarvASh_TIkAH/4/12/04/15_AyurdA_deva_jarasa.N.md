<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Granting long life, O god, choosing old age, ghee-faced, ghee-backed, O Agni,  
drinking the sweet, lovely nectar of the cow, [lead] this [son] of mine to old age like a father a son.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

O God, life-giver, choosing old age, clad in ghee, ghee-backed, O Agni,  
drinking ghee, the sweet bovine ambrosia, lead this one to old age like a father his son!
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

आयुर्दा᳓ देव जर᳓सँ वृणानो᳓ घृत᳓प्रतीको घृत᳓पृष्ठो अग्ने  
घृतं᳓ पि᳓बन्न् अमृ᳓तं चा᳓रु ग᳓व्यं पिते᳓व पुत्रं᳓ जर᳓से म एम᳓म्
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काठकम्</summary>

आयुर्दा᳓ देव जर᳓सं वृणानो᳓ घृतं᳓ व᳓सानो घृत᳓पृष्ठो अग्ने।  
घृतं᳓ पि᳓बन्नमृ᳓तं चा᳓रु ऽव्यं पिते᳓व पुत्रं᳓ जर᳓से नयेम᳓म्
</details>
