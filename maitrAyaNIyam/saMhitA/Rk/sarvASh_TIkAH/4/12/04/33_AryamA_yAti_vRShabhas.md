<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Aryaman comes, the bull, overcoming the swift, the giver, he has established wealth, guarding the body;  
the thousand-eyed, splitter of cow-pens, with the thunderbolt in hand—may the god bestow wealth upon us.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

May Aryaman come, the mighty bull, the restrainer, who guards the body, granting wealth to the worshipper;  
may the thousand-eyed, pen-splitting, thunderbolt-armed god establish wealth in us.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

आ᳓र्यमा᳓ याति वृषभ᳓स् तुराषा᳓ड् दा᳓ता व᳓सूनि विदधे᳓ तनूपाः᳓  
सहस्राक्षो᳓ गोत्रभि᳓द् व᳓ज्रबाहुर् अस्मा᳓सु देवो᳓ द्र᳓विणं दधातु
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काठकम्</summary>

आ᳓र्यमा᳓ यातु वृषभ᳓स्तु᳓विष्मान्यन्ता᳓ व᳓सूनि विधते᳓ तनूपाः᳓।  
सहस्राक्षो᳓ गोत्रभि᳓द्व᳓ज्रबाहुरस्मा᳓सु देवो᳓ द्र᳓विणं दधातु
</details>
