<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

May Agni be the first to favor us together with the Vasus; may Soma himself guard [us] with the Rudras;  
may Indra with the Maruts act in due season; may Varuṇa with the Ādityas grant us protection.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

May Agni with the Vasus protect us first; may Soma with the Rudras guard us himself;  
may Indra with the Maruts act in season; may Varuṇa with the Ādityas grant us shelter.
</details>
