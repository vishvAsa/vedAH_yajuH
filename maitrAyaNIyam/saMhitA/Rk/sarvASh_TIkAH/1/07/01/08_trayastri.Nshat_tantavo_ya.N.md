<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

The thirty-three threads which stretch him out and maintain this sacrifice with nectar,  
with them we cover up whatever gap is here; svāhā! May the sacrifice reach the gods!
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

Thirty-three threads do they stretch out, they who partake of this sacrifice with svadhā;  
what rent there is of theirs, we repair it; svāhā! May this sacrifice go to the gods!
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

त्र᳓यस्त्रिँशत् त᳓न्तवो यँ᳓ वितन्व᳓त इमं᳓ च यज्ञँ᳓ सुध᳓या द᳓दन्ते  
ते᳓भिश् छिद्र᳓म् अ᳓पिदध्मो य᳓द् अ᳓त्र स्वा᳓हा यज्ञो᳓ अ᳓प्येतु देवा᳓न्
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काठकम्</summary>

त्रयस्त्रिँशत्तन्तवो यान्वितन्वत इमं यज्ञँ स्वधया ये ददन्ते।  
तेषां छिद्रं प्रतिदध्मो यदत्र स्वाहायं यज्ञो अप्येतु देवान्
</details>
