<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Those nights of yours, O Savitṛ, leading to the gods, which have embraced the thousandfold sacrifice,  
together with all houses and with offspring now first ascending to heaven, do you cross the realms of space!
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

Those nights of yours, O Savitṛ, that lead to the gods, which have embraced the thousandfold sacrifice —  
together with all houses and with offspring now at first, mounting heaven, cross through the dark realms!
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

या᳓स् ते रा᳓त्रयः सवितर् देवया᳓नीः सहस्रयज्ञ᳓म् अभि᳓ संबभूवुः᳓  
गृहै᳓श् च स᳓र्वैः प्रज᳓या न्व् अ᳓ग्रे स्वो᳙ रु᳓हाणास् तरता र᳓जाँसि
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काठकम्</summary>

यास्ते रात्रयस्सवितर्देवयानीस्सहस्रयज्ञमभिसम्बभूवुः।  
गृहैश्च सर्वैः प्रजया न्वग्ने स्वो रुहाणास्तरता रजाँसि
</details>
