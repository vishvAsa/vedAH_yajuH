<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Truly, seeking to milk blessings, accompanied by Indra, we call;  
may we milk offspring and food!
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

Desiring milking, we invoke the blessings possessing Indra;  
may we milk offspring and nourishment!
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

आ᳓म् आशि᳓षो दो᳓हकामा इ᳓न्द्रवन्तो हवामहे  
धुक्षीम᳓हि प्रजा᳓म् इ᳓षम्
