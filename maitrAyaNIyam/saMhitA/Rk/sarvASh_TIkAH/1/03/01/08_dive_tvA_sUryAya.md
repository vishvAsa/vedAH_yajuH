<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

For heaven, you!  
For Sūrya, you!  
Extend upward this sacrificial rite in heaven among the gods, O Hotrās!
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

for heaven, you;  
for Sūrya, you!  
Raise this sacrifice aloft to heaven, among the gods; hold fast the priestly services!
</details>
