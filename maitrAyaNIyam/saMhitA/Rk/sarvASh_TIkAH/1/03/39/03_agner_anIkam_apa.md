<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

The face of Agni has entered the waters; Apāṁ Napāt guards against the asura-powers.  
In house after house worship the kindling stick, O Agni; may your tongue leap up towards the ghee!
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash (काठकम्)</summary>

The face of Agni entered the waters, the Son of Waters protecting the divine power.  
In house after house you will worship the kindling stick, O Agni; toward you the tongue of ghee shall rise up.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

अग्ने᳓र् अ᳓नीकम् अप᳓ आ᳓विवेशापां᳓ न᳓पात् प्रतिर᳓क्षद् असुर्या᳙न्  
द᳓मेदमे समि᳓धँ य᳓क्ष्य् अग्ने प्र᳓ति ते जिह्वा᳓ घृत᳓म् उ᳓च्चरण्यत्
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काठकम्</summary>

अग्ने᳓र᳓नीकमप᳓ आ᳓विवेशापां᳓ न᳓पात्प्रतिर᳓क्षन्नसुर्य᳙म्।  
द᳓मे दमे समि᳓धं य᳓क्ष्यग्ने प्र᳓ति ते जिह्वा᳓ घृत᳓मु᳓च्चरण्यत्
</details>
