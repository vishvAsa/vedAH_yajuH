शार्गः᳓ सृजयः᳓ शयाण्डकस् ते᳓ मैत्राः᳓  
श्वावि᳓द् भौमी᳓

</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

The egret belongs to Sūrya;  
the śārga, the sṛjaya, the śayāṇḍaka: those belong to Mitra;  
the porcupine belongs to the earth;  
for Sarasvatī, the starling that speaks human speech;
</details>
