यः᳓ प्राणतो᳓ निमिषत᳓श् च रा᳓जा प᳓तिर् वि᳓श्वस्य ज᳓गतो बभू᳓व
ई᳓शे यो᳓ अस्य᳓ द्विप᳓दश् च᳓तुष्पदः क᳓स्मै देवा᳓य हवि᳓षा विधेम ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Who became king of what breathes and blinks, the lord of the whole world;
who rules over this two-footed and four-footed [world]—to what god should we offer worship with oblation?
