आ᳓ वायो भूष शुचिपा उ᳓प नः सह᳓स्रं ते नियु᳓तो विश्ववार
उ᳓पो ते अ᳓न्धो म᳓द्यम् अयामि य᳓स्य देव दधिषे᳓ पूर्वपे᳓यम्
उपयाम᳓गृहीतो ऽसि
वाय᳓वे त्वा ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Approach us, O Vāyu, drinking the pure [Soma]; a thousand are your teams, O all-desirable one!
To you is offered the intoxicating food, of which, O god, you hold the first drink.
You are taken with a support.
For Vāyu, you!
