<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

'For let the divine ones wash away all stain,' he says;
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(He chants), "Indeed they, the divine, carry away all impurity (sin etc.) ".
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

</details>
