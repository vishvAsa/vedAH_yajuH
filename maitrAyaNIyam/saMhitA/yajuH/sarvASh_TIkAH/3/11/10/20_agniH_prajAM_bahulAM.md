<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Let Agni make my offspring abundant; place food, milk, semen in us!
</details>

<details><summary>Eggeling</summary>

--'May Agni raise for me abundant offspring,

and bestow ye upon us food, milk, and seed!'
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

“May Agni make my progeny mul- tiply and bestow food, milk and potency in us.” To those who cause him to perform to sacrifice, he says thus “Bestow everything to me.” Then they clean themselves with gold, for gold is immortality and thereby they get established in immortality.
</details>
