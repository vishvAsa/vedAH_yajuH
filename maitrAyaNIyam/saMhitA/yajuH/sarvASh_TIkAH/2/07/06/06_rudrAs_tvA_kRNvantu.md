<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

May the Rudras fashion you with the traiṣṭubha metre, like Aṅgiras, O cooking-pot!
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - मैत्रायणीयम्</summary>

रुद्रा᳓स् त्वा कृण्वन्तु त्रै᳓ष्टुभेन छ᳓न्दसाङ्गिरस्व᳓द् उखे
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - श्रीधरादि</summary>

</details>
