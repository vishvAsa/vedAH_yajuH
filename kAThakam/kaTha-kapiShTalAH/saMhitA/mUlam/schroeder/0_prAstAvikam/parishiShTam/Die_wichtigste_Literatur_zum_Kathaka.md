## English Translation

*The Most Important Literature on the Kāṭhaka*

1. Weber, *On the Manuscript Chambers 40* (*Ueber die Handschrift Chambers 40*): Catalogue of the Sanskrit Manuscripts of the Royal Library, Berlin 1853, p. 37/38.
2. Weber, *Some Remarks on the Kāṭhakam* (*Einiges über das Kāṭhakam*): *Indische Studien* III, pp. 451–479, 1855.
3. v. Schroeder, *On the Maitrāyaṇī-Saṃhitā, its Age, its Relation to the Related Śākhās, and its Linguistic and Historical Significance*: *ZDMG* 33, pp. 177–207, 1879.
4. v. Schroeder, *The Kāṭhakam and the Maitrāyaṇī-Saṃhitā*: *Monatsbericht der k. Akademie der Wissenschaften zu Berlin*, 24 July 1879, pp. 675–704.
5. v. Schroeder, *The Accentuation of the Vienna Kāṭhaka Manuscript*: *ZDMG* 45, pp. 432–438, 1891 (OCR: *184*).
6. v. Schroeder, *The Kāṭhaka Manuscript of Dayārām Jotsī in Śrīnagar and its Accents*: *ZDMG* 46, pp. 427–431, 1892.
7. v. Schroeder, *The Kāṭhaka, its Manuscripts, its Accentuation, and its Relation to the Indian Lexicographers and Grammarians*: *ZDMG* 49, pp. 145–171, 1895 (OCR: *185*).
8. v. Schroeder, *Note (On Ms. G)*: *Anzeiger der kais. Akademie der Wissenschaften in Wien*, phil.-hist. Class, 10 June 1896, No. XV.
9. v. Schroeder, *Two Newly Acquired Manuscripts of the Imperial and Royal Court Library in Vienna with Fragments of the Kāṭhaka*: *Sitzungsberichte der kais. Akademie der Wissenschaften in Wien*, phil.-hist. Class, Vol. 133, pp. 1–38, 1896.
10. v. Schroeder, *A Newly Discovered Ṛcaka of the Kaṭha School*: *ZDMG* 51, pp. 666–668, 1897.
11. v. Schroeder, *A Fragment of the Kapiṣṭhala-Saṃhitā*: *WZKM* 12, pp. 362–363, 1898.

13. v. Schroeder, *The Tübingen Kaṭha Manuscripts and their Relation to the Taittirīya-Āraṇyaka, with an Addendum by Bühler*: *Sitzungsberichte der kais. Akademie der Wissenschaften in Wien*, phil.-hist. Class, Vol. 137, pp. 1–126, 1898.
14. v. Schroeder, *ārmya, adj. "derived from a site of ruins"* (*"aus einer Trümmerstätte stammend"*): *WZKM* 14, pp. 275–276, 1900.
15. Caland, *Review of Kāṭhakam I* (*Anzeige von Kāṭhakam I*): *Göttingische Gelehrte Anzeigen* 1900, p. 702.
16. Pischel, *Review of Kāṭhakam I* (*Anzeige von Kāṭhakam I*): *DLZ* 1901, pp. 1173–1175.
