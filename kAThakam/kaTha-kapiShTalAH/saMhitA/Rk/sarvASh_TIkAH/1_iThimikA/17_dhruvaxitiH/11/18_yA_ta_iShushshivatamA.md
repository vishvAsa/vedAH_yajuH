<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

That arrow of yours which is most auspicious, auspicious has your bow become;  
auspicious is that shaft of yours, with that be gracious to us for living.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

या᳓ त इ᳓षुश्शिव᳓तमा शिवं᳓ बभू᳓व ते ध᳓नुः।  
शिवा᳓ शरव्या᳙ या᳓ त᳓व त᳓या नो मृड जीव᳓से
</details>
