<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Varuṇa of firm vows has sat down in the homesteads,  
endowed with good insight, for universal sovereignty.
</details>

<details><summary>C R Swaminathan</summary>

(He says while mounting) “Varuṇa, the up- holder of law has taken his seat in his abode, for the regal authority, that wise one.”
</details>
