<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

O Anumati, and make blessing for us!  
Bestow food upon our offspring; prolong our lives!
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - काठकम्</summary>

अ᳓न्वि᳓दनुमते त्वं᳓ मन्यासै शं᳓ च नस्कृधि।  
इ᳓षं तोका᳓य नो द᳓धः प्र᳓ ण आ᳓यूँषि तारिषः
</details>
