<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

The divine Agni has gone around, slayer of the Rakṣases, driver-away of disease,  
warding off all enmities, burning up the Rakṣases forever.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

प᳓रि प्रा᳓गाद्देवो᳓ अग्नी᳓ रक्षोहा᳓मीवचा᳓तनः।  
से᳓धन्वि᳓श्वा अ᳓प द्वि᳓षो द᳓हन्र᳓क्षाँसि विश्व᳓हा
</details>
