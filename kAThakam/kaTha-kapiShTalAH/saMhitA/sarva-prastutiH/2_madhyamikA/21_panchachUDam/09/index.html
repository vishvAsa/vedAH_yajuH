विद्वा᳓नि᳓ति देवलोक᳓मेवो᳓पा᳓वर्तते वि᳓श्वा आ᳓शा दी᳓द्यद्वि᳓भाही᳓ति त᳓स्मादग्नि᳓स्स᳓र्वा दि᳓शो वि᳓भाति पृथिव्या᳓ अह᳓मु᳓दन्त᳓रिक्षमा᳓रुहमन्त᳓रिक्षाद्दि᳓वमा᳓रुहमि᳓त्येषा᳓मेवै᳓ते᳓ लोका᳓नाँ सङ्क्रमा᳓स्स्वर्य᳙न्तो᳓ ना᳓पेक्षन्त इ᳓ति स्वर्ग᳓स्य लोक᳓स्य स᳓मष्ट्या अ᳓ग्ने प्रे᳓हि ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

‘Shining, illumine all regions,’ [he says];
therefore Agni shines upon all directions.
‘From the earth I have mounted into the intermediate space, from the intermediate space I have mounted into heaven,’ [he says];
these indeed are the steps to these worlds.
‘Going to heaven, they looked not back,’ [he says];
[it is] for the attainment of the heavenly world.
‘O Agni, go forward first of those who long for the gods,’ [he says];
he places sight both in the gods and in men.

प्रथमो᳓ देवा᳓यता᳓मि᳓ति देवे᳓षु चैव᳓ मनुष्ये᳙षु च च᳓क्षुर्दधाती᳓यक्षमाना᳓ भृ᳓गुभिस्सजो᳓षा इ᳓ति स्वर्ग᳓मेवै᳓नं लोकं᳓ गमयति पञ्च᳓भिर᳓धिक्रामति पा᳓ङ्क्तो ऽग्नि᳓र्या᳓वानेवा᳓ग्नि᳓स्त᳓स्मै नमस्कृ᳓त्या᳓धिक्रामति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

‘Desiring to sacrifice, in harmony with the Bhṛgus,’ [he says];
he leads him to the heavenly world.
With five [verses] he steps upon [it];
fivefold is Agni;
as great as Agni is, having paid homage to him, he steps upon [him].
With ‘Night and dawn,’ [and] ‘O Agni, thousand-eyed,’ two linked verses, he offers;
he joins together day and night;
therefore day and night are joined.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

He offers with sour milk in front, with ghee from above;
vigour indeed is sour milk, fiery energy is ghee;
vigour and fiery energy, united, he places in him at the end;
moreover, he causes both of these to be given to him.
