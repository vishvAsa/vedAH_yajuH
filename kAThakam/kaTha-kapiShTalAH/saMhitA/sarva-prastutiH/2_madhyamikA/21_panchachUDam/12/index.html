विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

देवा᳓ वै᳓ वाजप्रसव्य᳙ आजि᳓मयुस्त᳓दग्नि᳓रु᳓दजयद्य᳓द्वाजप्रसव्य᳙ं जुहो᳓त्यन्ना᳓द्यस्यो᳓ज्जित्या अ᳓न्नस्यान्नस्य जुहोति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

The gods ran a race for the propagation of strength (vājaprasavya);
Agni won it.
When he offers the vājaprasavya [oblations],
[it is] for the winning of proper food.
He offers of each kind of food;
he obtains all food.

सर्वाष् टीकाः

Now, these are the kingdom-holders (rāṣṭrabhṛt);
he who is piled is the royal power among the fires;
these verily uphold his kingdom;
that is the kingdom-holders’ nature as kingdom-holders;
through them he hands over the kingdom to him.
These are six;
six are the seasons;
by means of the seasons he hands over the kingdom to him;
moreover, he is established in the seasons.
