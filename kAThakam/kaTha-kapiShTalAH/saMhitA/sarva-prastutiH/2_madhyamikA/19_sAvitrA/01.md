<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

With verses to Savitṛ he takes the spade,  
for impulsion.  
This [earth] is the gāyatrī, the intermediate space the triṣṭubh, the sky the jagatī, the directions the anuṣṭubh;  
with the meters he turns it toward himself from these worlds.  
It is sharpened on both sides.  
With a plowshare sharpened on only one side, indeed, this much food is produced.  
Food is Arka; Arka is Agni;  
for the securing of food, of Arka.  
He should make it a cubit long.  
The sacrifice is measured by man;  
[he makes it] measured by the sacrificial man.  
He should make it a fathom long;  
so great is the vigor in man;  
[he makes it] measured by vigor.  
He should make it of unmeasured [length],  
for the securing of what is unmeasured.  
He should make it from a tree that bears fruit;  
this is the most vigorous of trees,  
for the sake of [possessing] vigor.  
He should make it spotted, of bamboo, hollow.  
Agni departed from the gods;  
he entered bamboo.
</details>
