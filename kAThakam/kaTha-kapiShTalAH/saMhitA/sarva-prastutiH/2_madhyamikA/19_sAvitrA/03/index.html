अ᳓न्वग्नि᳓रुष᳓साम᳓ग्रमख्यदि᳓त्यनु᳓क्शात्या आग᳓त्य वाज्य᳓ध्वानँ स᳓र्वा मृ᳓धो वि᳓धूनुत इ᳓ति मृ᳓ध एवै᳓त᳓या᳓पहत᳓ आक्र᳓म्य वा᳓जिन्पृथिवी᳓मग्नि᳓मिच्छ᳓ रुचा᳓ त्व᳓मि᳓त्यै᳓च्छद्वा᳓ एतं᳓ पू᳓र्वया प्रजा᳓पतिरविन्ददु᳓त्तरयेच्छत्येव᳓ पू᳓र्वया विन्दत्यु᳓त्तरया द्यौ᳓स्ते पृष्ठं᳓ पृथिवी᳓ सध᳓स्थमि᳓ति वज्री᳓ वा᳓ अ᳓श्वः प्राजापत्यो᳓ लो᳓मभिरुभया᳓दतः पशू᳓न᳓ति दद्भि᳓रन्य᳓तोदतो व᳓ज्रेणैव᳓ भ्रा᳓तृव्यम᳓वगृह्णाति य᳓त्र वै᳓ यज्ञ᳓स्या᳓नुरूपं क्रिय᳓ते त᳓त्पश᳓वो᳓ऽनुरूपा जायन्ते ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

“Agni has looked along the forefront of the dawns,” [he says],
for discovery.
“The prize-winner having reached the path shakes off all combatants,” [he says];
with this [verse] he drives away combatants.
“Having stepped upon the earth, O prize-winner, seek Agni with thy light,” [he says];
Prajāpati sought him with the previous [verse] and found him with the following one;
he seeks with the previous one and finds with the following one.
“The sky is thy back, the earth thy dwelling-place,” [he says].
The horse belonging to Prajāpati is indeed armed with the thunderbolt;
with its hair [it surpasses] animals with teeth on both jaws, with its teeth those with teeth on one side;
with the thunderbolt indeed he holds down the rival.
Where the counterpart of the sacrifice is performed, there counterpart animals are born.
