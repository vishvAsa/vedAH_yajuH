विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

अ᳓थैत᳓द्वात्सप्र᳓मेते᳓न वै᳓ वत्सप्री᳓र्भालन्दनो᳓ ऽग्नेः᳓ प्रियं᳓ धा᳓मा᳓वारुन्द्धाग्ने᳓रेवै᳓ते᳓न प्रियं᳓ धा᳓मा᳓वरुन्द्ध एते᳓न वै᳓ स᳓ ऋ᳓षीणामधिवाद᳓म᳓पाजयत्पाप्मा᳓नमेवै᳓ते᳓नाधिवाद᳓म᳓पजयत्येते᳓न वै᳓ सो᳓ ऽभि᳓शस्तीरजयदभि᳓शस्तीरेव᳓ जयति य᳓ एवं᳓ विद्वा᳓नेते᳓नोपति᳓ष्ठते य᳓त्प्रक्रमा᳓न्प्रक्रा᳓मति या᳓मं ते᳓न दाधा᳓र ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Now this is the Vātsapra [hymn].
By this, indeed, Vatsaprī Bhālandana obtained Agni’s dear domain.
By this he obtains Agni’s dear domain.
By this, indeed, he conquered the superior speech of the ṛṣis;
by this he conquers the evil superior speech.
By this, indeed, he conquered curses.
He who, knowing thus, worships with this, conquers curses.
In that he strides the strides,
thereby he sustains setting out on a journey.

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

य᳓दुपति᳓ष्ठते क्षे᳓मं ते᳓न पूर्वेद्युः᳓ प्र᳓क्रामत्यपरेद्यु᳓रु᳓पतिष्ठते त᳓स्माद्या᳓मे ऽन्या᳓सां प्रजा᳓नां म᳓नः क्षे᳓मे ऽन्या᳓सां त᳓स्माद्यायावरः᳓ क्षे᳓मस्ये᳓शे त᳓स्माद्यायावरः᳓ क्षेम्य᳓मध्य᳓वस्यत्ये᳓कादशं भवत्ये᳓कादशाक्षरा त्रिष्टु᳓ब्वीर्यं᳙ त्रिष्टु᳓ब्वीर्य᳙मेवा᳓वरुन्द्धे ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

In that he worships,
thereby [he sustains] peaceful dwelling.
On the previous day he strides;
on the following day he worships.
Therefore, the mind of some creatures is on journeying, that of others on peaceful dwelling.
Therefore, the wanderer rules over peaceful dwelling;
therefore, the wanderer settles upon what belongs to peaceful dwelling.
It is eleven [verses];
the triṣṭubh has eleven syllables;
the triṣṭubh is vigor;
he obtains vigor.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

The waters are Agni’s womb;
he makes him go to his own womb.
With three [verses];
Agni is threefold;
as great as Agni is, to that extent he makes him go to a firm standing.
He indeed casts Agni away who scatters the ash into the waters;
“having united with the mothers, shining, sit down again,” [he says];
he places light in him.
He indeed is deprived of strength and cattle who scatters the ash into the waters;
“again with strength, together with wealth,” [he says];
again he obtains strength and cattle.
“Be mindful of this speech of mine, O youngest [god],” [he says];
