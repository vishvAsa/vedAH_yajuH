विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

देवं᳓ बर्हि᳓स्सुदेवं᳓ देवै᳓स्स्यात्सुवी᳓रं वीरै᳓र्व᳓स्तोर्वृज्येताक्तोः᳓ प्र᳓भ्रियेत ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

May the divine sacrificial grass be well-divine through the gods,
having good heroes through heroes.
May it be cut at daybreak;
may it be brought forth at night.
May we rejoice with wealth, having sacrificial grass, excelling others.

For the win of wealth, may the two partake of the gift of wealth.

For the win of wealth, may the two partake of the gift of wealth.

The two divine Hotṛs, the Potṛs, the Neṣṭṛs,
slayers of evil speech, bringing wealth.

The divine Lord of the Forest, favourer of rain, clothed in ghee:
with his tip he has touched heaven,
with his middle he has filled the intermediate space,
with his lower part he has made firm the earth.

Sacrificer, recite the yājyā.
The divine sacrificial grass of the streams,
the foundation of the fallen, unfallen, sustaining at will, much-desired, glorious:
with this sacrificial grass may we overcome other sacrificial grasses.
For the win of wealth, may it partake of the gift of wealth.
Sacrificer, recite the yājyā.
The divine Agni Sviṣṭakṛt, rich in wealth, pleasant, the sage,
of true sacrifice, the Hotṛ, sacrificing better than any Hotṛ:
O Agni, the gods whom you have sacrificed to, whom you have pleased, who rejoiced in your Hotṛ-office, them you have brought together; speed this god-going Hotrā, this sacrifice, in heaven among the gods.
