सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

With two [verses] to Savitṛ he digs,
for impulsion.
He finds smoke with the previous [verse], light with the following one;
with two;
the sacrificer has two feet,
for firm standing.
“Thou art the back of the waters, the womb of Agni,” [saying this] he takes the lotus leaf;
for this is the back of the waters, the womb of Agni.
With the lotus leaf he collects [the earth];
with his own womb he collects him,
for appeasement,
so that he will not burn through.
With a black antelope skin he collects [it];
the black antelope skin is the sacrifice;
with the sacrifice he collects the sacrifice,
so that it will not spill;
for the sacrifice does not spill in the sacrifice.
This indeed is the form of the brahman, namely the black antelope skin;
with the brahman and with the ṛc and sāman he collects him.
“Ye are shelter and ye are armor,” [saying this] he spreads them;
the lotus leaf is this [earth], the black antelope skin that [sky];
these two indeed he thus spreads out.
“Both unrent and thick,” [he says];
for both of these are unrent and thick.
“Capacious,” [he says];
therefore these two are capacious.
“Bear the purīṣya Agni,” [he says];
for he is purīṣya.
