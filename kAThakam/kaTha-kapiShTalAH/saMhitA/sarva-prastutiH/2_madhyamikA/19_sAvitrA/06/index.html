मित्र᳓स्सँसृ᳓ज्य पृथिवी᳓मि᳓ति वरुणमेनि᳓र्वा᳓ एषा᳓ मित्रे᳓णैव᳓ वरुणमेनि᳓मु᳓पैति रुद्र᳓स्सँसृ᳓ज्य पृथिवीँ᳓ सँ᳓सृष्टां व᳓सु᳓भी रुद्रै᳓रि᳓त्येता᳓भिर्वा᳓ एतां᳓ देव᳓ताभिः प्रजा᳓पतिस्स᳓मसृजत्ता᳓भिरेवै᳓नाँ सँ᳓सृजति प्रा᳓न्यैर्यच्छत्य᳓न्वन्यै᳓र्मन्त्रयते मिथुन᳓मेव᳓ करोत्य᳓थो मध्यत᳓ एव᳓ यज्ञ᳓स्याशि᳓षम᳓वरुन्द्धे मख᳓स्य शि᳓रो ऽसी᳓ति यज्ञो᳓ वै᳓ मखो᳓ यज्ञ᳓स्यैव᳓ शि᳓रः करोति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

“Mitra having united the earth,” [he says];
this [earth/pot] is indeed Varuṇa’s wrath;
with Mitra indeed he approaches Varuṇa’s wrath.
“The Rudras having united the earth; united by the Vasus, by the Rudras,” [he says];
with these deities Prajāpati indeed united it;
with them he unites it.
With some [materials] he holds out, with others he recites formulas;
he forms a pair;
and he secures the blessing of the sacrifice from the center.
“Thou art the head of Makha,” [he says];
Makha is indeed the sacrifice;
he makes the head of the sacrifice.
