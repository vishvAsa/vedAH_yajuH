सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

The saptadaśa is indeed food;
food propagates after the seventeen-month year.
Food comes to be on the south;
therefore a man eats food with his right hand.
Because he lays down [a brick] containing the saptadaśa on the south,
therefore birds fly following along with the right wing.
[He lays down a brick] containing the ekaviṃśa on the west,
for firm standing;
for birds rest upon [their tail] behind.
[He lays down a brick] containing the pañcadaśa on the north.

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

त᳓स्मादुत्तरा᳓द᳓भिप्रयायी जयत्यग्ने᳓र्भागो᳓ ऽसि दीक्षा᳓या आ᳓धिपत्यमि᳓ति पुर᳓स्ताद्द्वौ᳓ त्रिवृता᳓ अभिपूर्व᳓मेव᳓ यज्ञमुखे᳓ वि᳓यातयति पञ्चदश᳓वतीर्दक्षिणत᳓स्सप्तदश᳓वतीमुत्तरा᳓त्पक्ष᳓योस्सयत्वा᳓या᳓न्नं वै᳓ सप्तदशो᳓ य᳓त्सप्तदश᳓वतीमुभय᳓त उपद᳓धात्य᳓न्नमेवो᳓भय᳓तो दधाति त᳓स्मादुभा᳓भ्यां ह᳓स्ताभ्यां परिगृ᳓ह्य पु᳓रुषो᳓ ऽन्नमत्त्येकविँश᳓वती भवति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

The pañcadaśa is indeed vigour;
he places vigour on the north;
therefore he who advances from the north conquers.
“Agni’s share art thou, the overlordship of the consecration,” [he says];
in front are the two trivṛt [formulas];
he arranges [them] in regular succession in the face of the sacrifice.
[He lays down a brick] containing the pañcadaśa on the south, [and a brick] containing the saptadaśa on the north,
for the unification of the two wings.
The saptadaśa is indeed food;
because he lays down [bricks] containing the saptadaśa on both sides,
he places food on both sides;
therefore a man eats food, grasping it with both hands.
