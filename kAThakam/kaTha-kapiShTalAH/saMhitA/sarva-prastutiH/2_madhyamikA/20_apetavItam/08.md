<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Thereby all are laid down.  
The Adhvaryu incurs no misfortune, falls into no failure.  
So many are the cattle: two-footed and four-footed.  
He inflicts pain upon them at that time.  
"I direct that wild [animal] after you," [he says];  
he releases pain from the domestic animals onto the wild animals.  
Therefore, though born in equal measure with domestic animals, these are the smallest;  
for they are stricken with pain.
