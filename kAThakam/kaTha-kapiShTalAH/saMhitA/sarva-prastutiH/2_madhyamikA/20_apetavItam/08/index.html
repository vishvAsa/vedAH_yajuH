There are twenty-one [beans].
Man consists of twenty-one parts.
He buys as much as his self is.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

He sings the Yama-verses;
he excludes it from Yama’s world.
He sings three [verses].
Three are these worlds;
he excludes it from these worlds.
Therefore nothing should be given to the singer,
for it is the verse that excludes it.
From this [head], power departs—the breaths in the head, vigor, sight, hearing, speech.
Deprived of power, this [head] is unfit for sacrifice.
They call it a “dead man’s head”.
Gold is fit for sacrifice.
When he inserts [gold] with flakes of gold,
he makes it fit for sacrifice, suitable for worship.
Having covered it in the pot, he inserts [the flakes].
Having brought it to a firm foundation, he equips it with the breaths.
