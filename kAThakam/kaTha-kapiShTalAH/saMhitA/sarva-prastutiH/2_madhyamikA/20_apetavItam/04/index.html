सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

from its direction he should bring [them], saying: “Food, vigour, I take from here.”
Food and vigour indeed he takes from it;
it becomes hungry.
He scatters the high altar.
For on the high altar the fire-altar is built.
Moreover, he does not omit any sacrificial joint.
The high altar is domestic animals;
he obtains domestic animals.
With a six-verse hymn, “Agni, your fame, food…”, he scatters [it].
Six indeed are the seasons, the year;
the year is Agni Vaiśvānara.
Through form itself he obtains Vaiśvānara.
The first [verse] has eight syllables.
Domestic animals have cloven hooves;
he obtains domestic animals.
Deficient and redundant [meters are] pairs,
for procreation.
This meter of Prajāpati is indeed named “Ocean”.
From the ocean domestic animals are born,
