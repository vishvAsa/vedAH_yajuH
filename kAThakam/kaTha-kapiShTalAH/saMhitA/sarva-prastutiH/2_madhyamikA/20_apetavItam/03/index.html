सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

a span to the tail.
The sacrifice is measured by man;

Therefore birds have stronger wings.

There are six seasons.
[It is] with the seasons indeed.
He completes three furrows each.
For Agni is threefold.
They amount to twelve.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Twelve months make a year.
He conforms to the measure of the year itself.
Moreover, having plowed with the seasons, he establishes himself in the year.
This [earth] indeed feared the excessive burning of Agni.
She made this double: plowed and unplowed.
In that there is plowed and unplowed,
[it is] for non-excessive burning of her.
Moreover, with the double he raises up her virility.
All these are domestic animals: two-footed and four-footed.
He deposits them now in the fire.

ते᳓नैव᳓ त᳓द᳓वरुन्धे चतुर्दश᳓भिर्वपति सप्त᳓ वै᳓ ग्राम्या᳓ ओ᳓षधयस्सप्ता᳓रण्या᳓स्ता᳓ एवो᳓भ᳓यीर᳓वरुन्द्धे᳓ ऽन्नमर्को᳓ ऽर्को᳓ ऽग्नि᳓रर्क᳓ एवै᳓त᳓दर्क᳓श्चीयते कृष्टे᳓ वपति कृष्टे᳓ ह्या᳓शिष्ठमो᳓षधयः प्रतिति᳓ष्ठन्त्यनुसीतं᳓ प्र᳓जात्यै द्वादश᳓सु सी᳓तासु वपति द्वा᳓दश मा᳓सास्संवत्सर᳓स्संवत्सरे᳓णैवा᳓स्मा᳓ अ᳓न्नं पचति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

by that itself he obtains it.
He sows with fourteen [seeds].
Seven indeed are the cultivated plants, seven wild;
both of these he obtains.
Food is Arka, Arka is Agni;
in this way Arka is built upon Arka.
He sows in the plowed [ground];
for plants settle most quickly in the plowed [ground].
Along the furrow, for procreation.
He sows in twelve furrows.
Twelve months make a year;
by the year itself he ripens food for him.
