<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

'Of good renown' indeed is his name.  
He does not become deaf who knows thus.  
When he drives [the calves] away with a parṇa branch,  
he drives them away with the sacred lore itself.  
Prajāpati created cattle.  
This god [Rudra] set his mind against them.  
He appeased him with a śamī [branch].  
That is the śamī-nature of the śamī.  
When he drives [them] away with a śamī branch,  
[it is] for appeasement.  
The energy of the waters and plants indeed is what darbha grass is.  
When he drives [them] away with bunches of darbha grass,  
he drives them away with the energy of the waters and the plants.
