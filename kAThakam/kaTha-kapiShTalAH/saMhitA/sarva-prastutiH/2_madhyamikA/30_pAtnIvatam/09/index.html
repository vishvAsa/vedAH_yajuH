विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

प्रजा᳓पतेर्वै᳓ पश᳓वो जाता᳓श्च जा᳓यमानाश्च रुद्र᳓स्य जाता᳓ व्यृ᳙द्धेन वा᳓ एष᳓ पशु᳓ना चरति य᳓ एत᳓योस्स᳓न्तं पशुं᳓ त᳓म᳓निर्याच्यैत᳓योराल᳓भते य᳓योरेव᳓ पशु᳓स्त᳓योरेवै᳓नं निर्या᳓च्य ते᳓नानुमते᳓न प्रसू᳓तेनर्ध्नो᳓त्येव᳓ जीव᳓न्पशु᳓स्स्वर्गं᳓ लोकं᳓ ग᳓मयितव्य इ᳓त्याहु᳓र्न᳓ मृत᳓स्स्वर्गं᳓ लोकं᳓ ग᳓न्तुमर्हती᳓ति प्रजान᳓न्तः प्र᳓तिगृह्णन्ति पू᳓र्व इ᳓ति जी᳓वन्तमेवै᳓नँ स्वर्गं᳓ लोकं᳓ गमयति ये᳓ च वै᳓ ग्राम्याः᳓ पश᳓वो ये᳓ चारण्या᳓स्त एत᳓मालभ्य᳓मानमनुध्या᳓यन्ति स᳓ ईश्वरो᳓ य᳓जमानोऽपशुः᳓ प᳓राभवितोर्ये᳓ बध्य᳓मानम᳓नुबध्य᳓माना य᳓ आरण्याः᳓ पश᳓वो विश्व᳓रूपा इ᳓ति पशूना᳓मेवै᳓षा᳓ स᳓म्प्रमुक्तिः प᳓ञ्चैता᳓नि जुहोति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Prajāpati’s indeed are the animals unborn and being born, and Rudra’s are those born.
Deprived of the animal indeed he wanders, who, when it belongs to these two, seizes the animal without begging it from these two.
Begging it from those two alone to whom the animal belongs, with it approved [and] impelled by them,
he indeed prospers.
They say: ‘Living, the animal is to be caused to go to the heavenly world; dead, it is not fit to go to the heavenly world.’
With ‘Knowing, the ancestors receive [the breath]’,
he causes it to go to the heavenly world while still living.
Both whichever animals are domestic and whichever are wild, they reflect upon this one being seized.
That sacrificer would be liable to perish without cattle.
With ‘Those who, being bound along, looked after him while he was being bound’, [and] ‘Whichever wild animals are of all forms’,
this is indeed the release of the cattle.
He offers these five [oblations].

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

With ‘O Jātavedas, go to the gods with the omentum’,
he causes it to go to the heavenly world.
Whatever indeed was unpurified of the sacrifice of the ṛṣis and the gods, that they purified with these [verses].
Whatever of his sacrifice is unpurified, that he purifies with these.
By these they warded off that which was to be warded off.
That is the apāvyatva of the Apāvyas.
He wards off that which is to be warded off by these.
