The sacrifice trickles away when not concluded;  
after it trickling away, the sacrificer trickles away with offspring and with cattle.  
It is indeed to be concluded,  
for the firm standing of the sacrifice.  
If he should conclude [it], as many portions as are of the animal, of so many [portions] of ghee he should cut off;  
for this ghee belonging to Prajāpati has not lost its potency;  
Prajāpati of the gods has not lost his potency.  
Then he both concludes it and does not conclude it;
