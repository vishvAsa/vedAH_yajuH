<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

‘You are swift,’ [he says].  
Speech indeed is the cow for purchasing Soma;  
for by speech one hastens this way and that.  
Looking at the cow for purchasing Soma, he offers an oblation.  
Speech indeed is the cow for purchasing Soma;  
looking at her, he grasps her.  
‘Held by the mind,’ [he says];  
for by the mind speech is held.  
‘Pleasing to Viṣṇu,’ [he says].  
Viṣṇu indeed is the sacrifice;  
he makes her pleasing to the sacrifice.
</details>
