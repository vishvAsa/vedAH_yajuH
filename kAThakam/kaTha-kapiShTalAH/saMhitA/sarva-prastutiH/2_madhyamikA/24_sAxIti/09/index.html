देवा᳓ वा᳓ अन्यो᳓ऽन्य᳓स्य श्रै᳓ष्ठ्याय ना᳓तिष्ठन्त ते चतुर्धा᳓ व्यु᳓दक्रामन्नग्नि᳓र्व᳓सुभिस्सो᳓मो रुद्रै᳓रि᳓न्द्रो मरु᳓द्भिर्व᳓रुण आदित्यै᳓स्ते देवाः᳓ पा᳓पीयाँसोऽभवन्वी᳓याँ᳓सो᳓ऽसुरास्तेऽविदुः पा᳓पीयाँसो वै᳓ त्ये᳓ भवन्ति व᳓सीयाँसो᳓ऽसुरा इ᳓ति ते᳓ प्रिया᳓स्तन्व᳙स्स᳓मवाद्यन्त तेऽब्रुवन्नेषां᳓ नस्त᳓न्न᳓ पार᳓याद्यो᳓ नोऽन्यो᳓ऽन्य᳓स्मै द्रु᳓ह्यादि᳓ति त᳓स्मात्स᳓तानूनप्त्रिणे न᳓ द्रो᳓ग्धवै᳓ य᳓द्द्रु᳓ह्यति प्रिया᳓यै त᳓न्वै᳓ द्रुह्यति य᳓त्तन्व᳙स्सम᳓वाद्यन्त त᳓त्तानूनप्त्र᳓स्य तानूनप्त्र᳓त्वं᳓ त᳓तो देवा᳓ अभवन्परा᳓सुरा अभवन्य᳓ एवं᳓ विद्वाँ᳓स्तानूनप्त्र᳓मुपै᳓ति भवत्यात्म᳓ना परा᳓स्य᳓ भ्रा᳓तृव्यो भवत्यसौ᳓ वा᳓ आदित्य᳓ इ᳓न्द्रस्त᳓स्य ते श्रै᳓ष्ठ्यायातिष्ठन्त तिष्ठन्तेऽस्य᳓ समाना᳓श्श्रै᳓ष्ठ्याय य᳓ एवं᳓ वे᳓दैत᳓स्मिन्वै᳓ ते ता᳓स्तन्व᳙स्स᳓न्न्य᳓दधत त᳓स्मादेष᳓ ते᳓जिष्ठं तपति त᳓स्मात्सूर्यदेवत्या᳓स्सो᳓मा भू᳓यिष्ठा हूयन्त आ᳓ज्येन वै᳓ व᳓ज्रेण देवा᳓ वृत्र᳓मघ्नन्स्रु᳓ग्भ्यां बाहु᳓भ्याँ सो᳓मो वृत्रो᳓ घ्नन्ति वा᳓ एत᳓त्सो᳓मं य᳓द᳓स्या᳓ज्येनान्तिकं᳓ जुह्वत्यँशु᳓रँशुस्ते दे᳓व सो᳓माप्यायतामिति य᳓देवा᳓स्य घ्नन्ति य᳓न्नितम᳓यन्ति तदा᳓प्याययन्ति यन्ति वा᳓ एते᳙ऽस्मा᳓ल्लोका᳓द्ये᳓ सो᳓ममाप्याय᳓यन्त्ये᳓ष्टा रा᳓यः प्रेषे भ᳓गाये᳓त्यस्मि᳓न्नेव᳓ लोके᳓ प्र᳓तितिष्ठन्ति पित᳓रं च वा मात᳓रं च हिनस्ति यो᳓ गँ᳓र्भ ह᳓न्त्यन᳓योर्ग᳓र्भस्सो᳓मो रा᳓जा न᳓मो दिवे᳓ न᳓मः ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

The gods indeed did not yield to one another’s superiority.
They departed in four directions: Agni with the Vasus, Soma with the Rudras, Indra with the Maruts, Varuṇa with the Ādityas.
Those gods became worse off; the Asuras became better off.
They knew: ‘Those [gods] are becoming worse off, the Asuras better off.’
They deposited together their dear bodies.
They said: ‘Let him not bring this to completion of ours who shall act maliciously against another of us.’
Therefore, one should not act maliciously against a participant in the Tānūnaptra [covenant].
If he acts maliciously, he acts maliciously against his own dear body.
Because they deposited together their bodies, that is the nature of the Tānūnaptra covenant.
Then the gods flourished, the Asuras were defeated.
He who, knowing thus, undertakes the Tānūnaptra covenant, flourishes himself, and his rival is defeated.
Yonder sun is indeed Indra.
They yielded to his superiority.
His peers yield to the superiority of him who knows thus.
In him indeed they deposited those bodies.
Therefore he burns most intensely.
Therefore Somas are mostly offered with Sūrya as their deity.
With clarified butter as a thunderbolt indeed, with the ladles as their two arms, the gods struck Vṛtra.
Soma is Vṛtra.
They strike Soma indeed when they sacrifice near him with clarified butter.
‘May shoot after shoot of yours swell, O divine Soma,’ [he says].
What they strike, what they crush of him, that they swell up.
Those who swell up Soma indeed go away from this world.
‘Sought are riches for impulse, for fortune,’ [he says].
They establish themselves in this very world.
He injures both the father and the mother who strikes an embryo.
King Soma is the embryo of these two.
