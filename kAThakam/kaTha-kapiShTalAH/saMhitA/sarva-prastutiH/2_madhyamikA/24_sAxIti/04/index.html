विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (1/22 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)

द᳓क्षिणेन पदा᳓ द᳓क्षिणानि पदा᳓न्यनुनि᳓क्रामति त᳓स्माद्दक्षिणो᳓ऽर्ध आत्म᳓नो वीर्या᳓वत्तरष्ष᳓ट् पदा᳓न्यनुनि᳓क्रामति [[ष᳓डृत᳓व|ष᳓डृतव]] ऋतु᳓ष्वेव᳓ प्र᳓तितिष्ठति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

With the right foot he steps along the right footprints.
Therefore the right half of the body is more full of virility.
He steps along six footprints.
Six are the seasons.
In the seasons he firmly establishes himself.

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

ष᳓ट् पृष्ठा᳓नि ता᳓न्येवा᳓वरुन्द्धे सप्तमं᳓ पद᳓मभि᳓गृह्णाति सप्त᳓ ग्राम्याः᳓ पश᳓वस्ता᳓नेवा᳓वरुन्द्वे सप्तधे᳓यं᳓ वा᳓ग्वदति ता᳓मेवा᳓प्नोति व᳓स्व्यसि रुद्रा᳓सी᳓ति देवना᳓मानि वा᳓ एषै᳓ता᳓नि प्र᳓पद्यत इय᳓मसीय᳓मसी᳓त्येवै᳓त᳓दाह य᳓द्यद्भ᳓वत्य᳓थो वाच᳓ एवै᳓त᳓त्सम्य᳓गुद्यते बृ᳓हस्प᳓तिस्त्वा सुम्ने᳓ रम्णातु रुद्रो᳓ व᳓सुभिराच᳓क इ᳓ति ब्र᳓ह्म वै᳓ बृ᳓हस्प᳓ती रुद्रो᳓ देवा᳓नामो᳓जिष्ठो या᳓ एव᳓ देवा᳓नामो᳓जिष्ठौ ता᳓भ्यामेनामन्ततो᳓ यच्छति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Six are the Pṛṣṭha [stotras];
those indeed he obtains.
He grasps the seventh footprint.
Seven are the domestic animals;
those indeed he obtains.
Sevenfold this speech speaks;
that indeed he attains.
‘You are Vasvī, you are Rudrā,’ [he says].
To these names of the gods he indeed resorts.
‘You are this one, you are this one,’ so indeed he says this,
whatever it is; and furthermore, it is correctly uttered for speech.
‘May Bṛhaspati delight you in favour; may Rudra with the Vasus look upon you,’ [he says].
Bṛhaspati is the bráhman; Rudra is the mightiest of the gods.
To the two who are the mightiest of the gods he at the end entrusts her.

Gemini 3.8 Flash

‘On the head of the earth I sprinkle you, at the place for worship of the gods,’ [he says].
For this is the head of the earth: the place for worship of the gods.
‘In the footprint of Iḍā, full of ghee, svāhā!’ [he says].
Iḍā indeed is she; her footprint is this, full of ghee.
With the svāhā-call he grasps her.
‘Here I cut off the neck of the Rakṣas,’ [he says].
The rival indeed is the Rakṣas.
He cuts off the neck of his rival.
With the wooden sword he outlines the footprint.
The wooden sword is a thunderbolt;
the footprint is cattle.
With the thunderbolt he encloses cattle for him.
As far as the ghee runs, so far he should outline around.
Ghee indeed is cattle.
He obtains cattle.
He throws the [earth of the] footprint together into the pot.
Cattle indeed belong to Aditi;
the earth is Aditi;
the pot is gathered from the earth.
He places them in their own womb.
‘Delight in us; in us be your riches,’ [he says].
If he were to say, ‘In you be riches,’ he would hand over the cattle to another; the Adhvaryu would become devoid of cattle.
‘In me be riches,’ [he says].
In himself indeed he grasps the cattle.
