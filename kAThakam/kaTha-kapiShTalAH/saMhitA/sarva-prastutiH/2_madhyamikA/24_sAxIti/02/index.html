सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Cattle indeed are the third pressing.
Because he purchases with a cow,
by that alone he obtains the third pressing.
He measures [Soma] on a red hide.
Therefore cattle are mostly of red form.
He should not consent to its being picked apart.
He should not be an onlooker of it while being picked apart.
