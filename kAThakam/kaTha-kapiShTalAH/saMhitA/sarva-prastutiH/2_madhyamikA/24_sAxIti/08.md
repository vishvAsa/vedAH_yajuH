<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)</summary>

पितृदेवत्यँ᳙ हवि᳓स्स्याद्वि᳓च्छिन्नो यज्ञो᳓ऽसन्ततो᳓ऽनवरुद्धो य᳓दुभा᳓ अ᳓विमुक्तौ वा᳓र्त्रघ्नँ हवि᳓र᳓स्स्या᳓त्स᳓न्ततो यज्ञो᳓ऽविच्छिन्नो᳓ऽवरुद्वो य᳓द्वि᳓मुक्तोऽन्यो᳓ भ᳓वत्य᳓विमुक्तोऽन्यो᳓ वा᳓र्त्रघ्नँ हवि᳓र्भवत्य᳓पितृदेवत्यँ स᳓न्ततो यज्ञो᳓ऽविच्छिन्नो᳓ऽवरुद्धः प᳓त्नीम᳓न्वारम्भयित्वा᳓ नि᳓र्वपति प᳓त्नी वै᳓ पा᳓रीणह्यस्ये᳓शे᳓ऽनुमतमेव᳓ प्र᳓सूतं देवे᳓भ्यो हवि᳓र्नि᳓र्वपति प᳓त्न्या एवै᳓ष᳓ यज्ञ᳓स्यान्वारम्भो᳓ऽथो मिथुन᳓मेव᳓ यज्ञमुखे᳓ दधाति प्रज᳓ननाय ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

the sacrifice would be interrupted, discontinuous, not obtained.  
If both were unyoked, the oblation would belong to Vṛtra's slayer; the sacrifice would be continuous, uninterrupted, obtained.  
Since one is unyoked and the other unyoked,  
the oblation belongs to Vṛtra's slayer, does not have the ancestors as its deity; the sacrifice is continuous, uninterrupted, obtained.  
Having made the wife touch [him] from behind, he pours it out.  
The wife indeed rules over the household goods.  
Permitted, impelled [by her], he pours out the oblation for the gods.  
This is indeed the wife’s grasping of the sacrifice from behind.  
Moreover, he places a pair at the opening of the sacrifice,  
for procreation.
</details>
