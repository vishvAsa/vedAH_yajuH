सुप्रतिष्ठा᳓नम᳓धरमु᳓त्तरस्मै बृहदु᳓क्षे न᳓म इ᳓ति बृह᳓द्धि᳓ देवा᳓नां न᳓मः पितॄणा᳓मुक्थ᳓भागा᳓ वै᳓ पित᳓रो᳓ऽस्तो᳓मभा᳓जो य᳓त्रैवै᳓भ्य᳓ उक्थँ᳓ शँ᳓सति त᳓देभ्यो᳓ ग्र᳓हं गृह्णाति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

and the lower a good resting place for the upper.
“Homage to the Bṛhadukṣa,” [he says];
for Bṛhat is of the gods, homage of the fathers.
The fathers indeed have recitation as their share, not chant as their share;
wherever he recites the recitation for them, there he draws the cup for them.

मूलम्
