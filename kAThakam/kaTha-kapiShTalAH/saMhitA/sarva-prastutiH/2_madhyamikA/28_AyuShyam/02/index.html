संवत्सर᳓स्य क्लृ᳓प्त्यै᳓ स्वर्ग᳓स्य लोक᳓स्य प्र᳓ज्ञात्या उभौ᳓ सह᳓ प्रथमं᳓ गृ᳓ह्ल्लाते उभौ᳓ ह्ये᳙तौ᳓ सह᳓ संवत्सरं᳓ क᳓ल्पयितुमुपप्रे᳓त᳓ उभौ᳓ सहो᳓त्तमँ᳓ संवत्सर᳓मेव᳓ कल्पयित्वो᳓भौ᳓ सह᳓ विमुच्ये᳓ते ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

is for the ordering of the year,
for the discernment of the heavenly world.
Both draw together first;
for both of these proceed together to order the year.
Both [draw] together last;
having ordered the year, both are unyoked together.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

he gratifies the All-Gods at the morning pressing.
Gratified, pleased, they release the two later pressings for him.
He draws the Vaiśvadeva [cup] with the Śukra-vessel.
That sun is the Śukra, these creatures belong to the All-Gods;
therefore it faces all creatures towards the west;
for they think: “Towards me, towards me!”
