After the Dhruva, indeed, human beings are propagated; after the other cups, the other creatures.
He sets down the Dhruva on [an un-earthed place] where earth is not strewn; the other cups on where earth is strewn;

Firm indeed is he who is settled; un-firm is he who is expelled.
He expels him who is settled; he causes him who is expelled to become settled; quickly they change places.
If he were to do so, he would dislodge the life of creatures.
He should pass grass over and above with this mantra;
thereby he causes the expelled to become settled, [and] does not dislodge the life of creatures.
The altar is indeed the realm of the gods;
as great as is the altar, so great is the earth;
by means of the realm of the gods he drives him away from the realm of men.
He should draw [it] full for him of whom he should desire: ‘May he go to the full span of life.’
He draws full life for him;
he goes to the full span of life.
He should draw a little for him of whom he should desire: ‘May he go to a short span of life.’
He makes his life short; quickly he perishes.
He should draw above the half for him of whom he should desire: ‘May he go to higher life.’
He brings him to higher life.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Awaiting this very Soma-drink, which is the Dhruva;
for this pushes them away.
Sitting facing west, he pours [it] out;
along the current indeed he lets them flow.
He should not urinate halfway through;
he should sit through the Agniṣṭoma.
The Dhruva is life;
he would cause life to flow away.
As great indeed is the Agniṣṭoma, so great is the sacrifice.
When he sits through the Agniṣṭoma,
he goes within his breath;
he goes to the full span of life.
The Sadas is the belly of this sacrifice;
thither they run, slipping out.
The sacrifice is Prajāpati;
when he sits through the Agniṣṭoma,
he does not empty Prajāpati’s belly; he does not become hungry.
