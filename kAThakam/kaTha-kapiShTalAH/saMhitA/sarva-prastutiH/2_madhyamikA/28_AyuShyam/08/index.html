Gemini 3.8 Flash

for continuity.
He mixes [it] from above;
therefore a woman is prone to not be bald.
He offers this boiled Soma; the others are unboiled.
Therefore a woman is devoid of vigor; a man is not devoid of vigor.
The Hotṛ does not sacrifice for this [cup],
for he is behind;
he would divert the breaths from behind.
The Āgnīdhra sacrifices,
for he is in front.
In front he does not perform the after-sacrifices for the breaths.
If he were to perform the after-sacrifices, he would separate the breaths with Agni; he would perish.
He consumes this laid-low Soma;
therefore the Āgnīdhra is prone to be a eunuch.
The Pātnīvata [cup] indeed is procreation;
therefore he is prone to have much livestock.
Along with the vessel, he glides toward the Neṣṭṛ;
he brings the wives to the Soma-drink.
He should not sit in the lap;
