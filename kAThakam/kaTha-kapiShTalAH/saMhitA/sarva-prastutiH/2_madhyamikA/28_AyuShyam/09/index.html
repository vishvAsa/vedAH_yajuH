विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

इ᳓न्द्रो वै᳓ वृत्र᳓महँस्त᳓स्य मूर्धा᳓नमु᳓दरूज᳓त्स᳓ द्रोणकलशो᳓ऽभवत्त᳓तो य᳓स्सो᳓मस्स᳓म᳓स्रवत्स᳓ हारियोजनो᳓ऽभवदि᳓न्द्रो वै᳓ वृत्रँ᳓ हत्वा᳓ त᳓स्य क्लोम्नो᳓ हृ᳓दयात्सो᳓मँ स᳓मसिञ्चत्स᳓ हारियोजनो᳓ऽभवत्सो᳓ऽमन्यत य᳓दिम᳓मशृत᳓म᳓द्वितीयँ होष्या᳓मि त᳓द᳓न्व᳓सुरा आ᳓भविष्यन्ति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Indra indeed slew Vṛtra.
He broke open his head;
that became the droṇakalaśa trough.
The Soma that flowed together from that, that became the Hāriyojana [cup].
Indra indeed, having slain Vṛtra, poured together the Soma from his lung and heart;
that became the Hāriyojana [cup].
He thought: “If I offer this unboiled and without a second, the Asuras will arise after [me];

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

He should suck out as it were,
for the securing of whatever Soma-drink is there.
He scatters [them] on the high altar;
the high altar is livestock; the Hāriyojana grains are livestock;
he establishes livestock in livestock.
