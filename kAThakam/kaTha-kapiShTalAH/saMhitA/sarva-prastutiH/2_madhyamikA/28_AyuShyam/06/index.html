विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

आदित्या᳓ वा᳓ अ᳓सुरान्हत्वा᳓ वैरदेया᳓दी᳓षमाणास्ते देवा᳓त्प्रा᳓विशन्द्विदेवत्या᳙न्वा᳓व᳓ त᳓त्प्रा᳓विशन्नेते᳓ हि᳓ देवा᳓नाँ सह᳓ भू᳓यिष्ठास्त᳓स्मादादित्यो᳓ द्विदेवत्ये᳙भ्यो गृह्य᳓त उ᳓च्छिष्टाद्वा᳓ आदित्या᳓ जाता᳓स्त᳓स्मादेष᳓ उ᳓च्छिष्टस्सो᳓मो गृह्यत उच्छे᳓षणभागा᳓ वा᳓ आदित्या᳓स्त᳓स्मादेष᳓ उ᳓च्छिष्टस्सो᳓मो गृह्यते ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

The Ādityas, having slain the Asuras and fleeing from blood-money, entered the gods.
They entered the [gods] with two deities.
For these are the most united among the gods;
therefore the Āditya [cup] is drawn from the [cups] with two deities.
The Ādityas indeed were born from the remnant [of the oblation];
therefore this remnant Soma is drawn.
The Ādityas indeed have the remnant as their share;
therefore this remnant Soma is drawn.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Agni indeed is Rudra; the Āditya [cup] is livestock,
for the protection of livestock.
The gods indeed excluded Rudra from the sacrifice.
They assigned together: “This is your share in the mother”; bringing it thus far to the end of the Āditya [cup], in connection with this they shut [him] out from the sacrifice.
When he draws the Āditya [cup] behind an enclosure,
