चातुर्मास्या᳓न्येष᳓ वा᳓व᳓ त᳓र्हि यज्ञ᳓ आसीत्ते देवा᳓ एतँ᳓ सौम्य᳓मध्वर᳓मपश्यँस्त᳓माहरँस्तेऽदीक्षयँस्तेऽग्निहोत्रं᳓ व्रत᳓मकुर्वताग्निहोत्रं᳓ वा᳓व᳓ व्रतं᳓ त᳓स्माद्या᳓ग्निहोत्र᳓स्य स्कन्न᳓स्य प्रा᳓यश्चित्तस्मा व्रत᳓स्य स्व᳓न्नस्य प्रा᳓यश्चित्तिस्त᳓स्माद्द्वि᳓व्रतो दीक्षित᳓स्स्या᳓द्द्वि᳓र्ह्य᳙ग्निहोत्रं᳓ जु᳓ह्वति ते᳓षां य᳓त्पौर्णमास᳓मा᳓सीत्त᳓मग्नीषोमी᳓यं पशु᳓मकुर्वत यः᳓ पूर्वेद्यु᳓रालभ्य᳓ते य᳓द्दा᳓र्शं त᳓माग्नेयं᳓ यो᳓ऽनुसवन᳓मालभ्य᳓ते वैश्वदेवं᳓ प्रातस्सवन᳓मकुर्वत वरुणप्रघासा᳓न्मा᳓ध्यन्दिनँ स᳓वनँ साकमेधाँ᳓स्तृतीयसवनं᳓ तृतीयसवने᳓ पितृयज्ञ᳓म᳓वाकल्पयँस्तृतीयसवने᳓ त्र्य᳙म्बकाँस्ते᳓सुरा अ᳓पक्रा᳓मन्तोऽब्रुवन्न᳓ वा᳓ इमे᳓ ध्व᳓र्तवा᳓ अभूवन्नि᳓ति त᳓दध्वर᳓स्याध्वरत्वं᳓ त᳓तो देवा᳓ अभवन्परा᳓सुरा अभवन्य᳓ एवं᳓ विद्वा᳓न्दी᳓क्षते य᳓मेवं᳓ विद्वा᳓न्दक्ष᳓यति भवत्यात्म᳓ना परा᳓स्य᳓ भ्रा᳓तृव्यो भवति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

This indeed was the sacrifice at that time.  
Those gods saw this Soma sacrifice.  
They brought it.  
They consecrated [themselves].  
They made the Agnihotra the vow-food.  
The Agnihotra indeed is the vow-food.  
Therefore, the atonement that belongs to the spilled Agnihotra is the atonement for the spilled vow-food.  
Therefore the consecrated one should have two vow-meals.  
For twice they offer the Agnihotra.  
What was their full-moon sacrifice, that they made the animal for Agni and Soma, which is seized on the preceding day.  
What was the new-moon sacrifice, that they made the animal for Agni, which is seized at each pressing.  
They made the Vaiśvadeva the morning pressing,  
the Varuṇapraghāsas the midday pressing,  
the Sākamedhas the third pressing.  
In the third pressing they arranged the sacrifice to the Fathers;  
in the third pressing the Tryambakas.  
Those Asuras, stepping away, said:  
"These indeed have become impossible to injure!"  
That is the non-injuriousness of the sacrifice.  
Thence the gods prospered, the Asuras were defeated.  
He who, knowing thus, is consecrated, and whom [a priest] knowing thus consecrates, prospers himself, and his rival is defeated.
</details>
