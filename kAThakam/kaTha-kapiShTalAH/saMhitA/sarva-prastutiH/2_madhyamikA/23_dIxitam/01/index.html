विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (1/96 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)

हवि᳓र्वै᳓ दीक्षितो᳓ न᳓ [[हवि᳓र᳓नभिघृतं|हवि᳓रनभिघृतं]] मे᳓धमश्नुते य᳓दभ्यन᳓क्त्यभ्ये᳙᳓वै᳓नं धारयति मेध्यत्वा᳓य घृतं᳓ देवा᳓नां नि᳓ष्पक्वं मनुष्या᳙णामा᳓युतं गन्धर्वा᳓णाँ स्वयंविलीन᳓मादित्य᳓स्य नै᳓त᳓द्देवत्रा᳓ न᳓ मनुष्यत्रा᳓ य᳓न्न᳓वनीतं नै᳓ष᳓ देवत्रा᳓ न᳓ मनुष्यत्रा᳓ यो᳓ दीक्षितः᳓ प्र᳓च्युतोऽस्मा᳓ल्लोका᳓द᳓प्राप्तोऽमुं᳓ य᳓न्न᳓वनीतेनाभ्यङ्क्ते᳓ स᳓मृद्ध्यै वा᳓सः प᳓रिधत्तेऽग्ने᳓स्तू᳓षः पितॄणां᳓ नीवि᳓रो᳓षधीनां प्रघातो᳓ वायो᳓र्वातपा᳓ वि᳓श्वेषां देवा᳓नामो᳓तवश्च त᳓न्तवश्च न᳓क्षत्राणामतीरोका᳓स्सर्वदेवत्यं᳙ वा᳓सस्सर्वदेवत्यो᳙ दीक्षित᳓स्स᳓र्वाभिरेवै᳓नं देव᳓ताभिस्स᳓मर्धयत्या᳓ङ्क्त इ᳓न्द्रं वै᳓ वृत्रं᳓ जघ्निँवा᳓सं र᳓क्षाँस्यसचन्त ता᳓न्यञ्जनगिरि᳓णान्त᳓रधत्त र᳓क्षाँस्येतँ᳓ सचन्ते ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

The consecrated one is an oblation.
An oblation not smeared with ghee does not attain sacrificial fitness.
When he anoints him,
he sprinkles ghee upon him
for sacrificial fitness.
Ghee belongs to the gods; clarified butter [belongs] to men; half-churned butter to the gandharvas; butter melted by itself to the sun.
Fresh butter is neither among the gods nor among men.
The consecrated one is neither among the gods nor among men,
having fallen from this world [and] not having reached yonder [world].
When he anoints himself with fresh butter,
[it is] for proper fulfillment.
He puts on a garment.
The hem belongs to Agni; the waistband to the ancestors; the fringe of the border to the plants; the wind-protector to Vāyu; the warps and the woofs to the All-gods; the eyelets to the nakṣatras.
The garment belongs to all the gods; the consecrated one belongs to all the gods.
He equips him with all the deities.
He applies collyrium.
When Indra had slain Vṛtra, the demons pursued him;
he blocked them off by means of the collyrium-mountain.
The demons pursue him

It has a tuft;
that which has a tuft belongs to Mitra; that which is withered belongs to Varuṇa; the consecrated one belongs to Mitra;
[it is] for proper fulfillment.
Deficient, as it were, defective, as it were, is the eye;
when he applies collyrium,
he fills that very [defect];
[it is] for defectlessness.
He purifies [him].
Indra slew Vṛtra;
he slew him right upon the waters.
Those of them that were fit for sacrifice, pure,
they stepped forth;
they became the plants.
The brilliance that was theirs, that is the darbha grass;
therefore it is a purifier;
therefore with darbha blades they purify the waters.
When he purifies [him] with bunches of darbha grass,
he purifies him with the brilliance of the waters and the plants.
He purifies [him] with two [stalks].
Two are truth and untruth;
with truth alone he purifies his untruth.
Two are the in-breath and the out-breath.
He purifies [him] with three [stalks];
for the gods are thrice-truthful;
moreover, three are the breaths: the in-breath, the circulating breath, and the out-breath;
these he places in the sacrificer.

Gemini 3.8 Flash

He purifies [him] with five [stalks].
Man is fivefold;
as great as his self is, so much of him he purifies.
He purifies [him] with seven [stalks].
Seven are the metres;
with the metres he purifies him.
He purifies [him] with nine [stalks].
Nine are the breaths;
with the breaths he purifies him.
He purifies [him] with twenty-one [stalks].
Man is twenty-one-fold: ten fingers on the hands, ten toes on the feet, the self is the twenty-first;
as great as his self is, so much of him he purifies.
“May the lord of thought purify you,” [he says];
the lord of thought is holy power (brahman);
by holy power he purifies him.
“May the lord of speech purify you,” [he says];
for he is the purifier.
“May the divine Savitṛ purify you,” [he says];
impelled by Savitṛ, he purifies him with the deities.
“With an unbroken purifier,” [he says];
without a flaw he purifies him.
“With the rays of the sun,” [he says];
with the very body of the sun he purifies him.
“With the purifier belonging to thee, the lord of the purifier,” [he says];
the lord of the purifier is the sacrifice;
for the sacrifice he purifies this.
“May I be able to achieve that for which I purify [thee],” [he says];
“May I be able to achieve the sacrifice,” is what he thereby says.
He purifies him from front to back.
From front to back is the breath of man;
when the breath goes crosswise, then he perishes.
