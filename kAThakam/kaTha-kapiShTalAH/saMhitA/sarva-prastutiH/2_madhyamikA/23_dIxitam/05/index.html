स्वा᳓हा यज्ञं᳓ म᳓नस इ᳓ति म᳓नसा वै᳓ यज्ञ᳓ आ᳓लभ्यते वा᳓तयो᳓निर्यज्ञो᳓ दिवि᳓ यज्ञो᳓ऽन्त᳓रिक्षे पृथिव्या᳓म᳓त्रा᳓त्र वै᳓ यज्ञो᳓ य᳓त्र य᳓त्रैव᳓ यज्ञ᳓स्त᳓त एनं म᳓नसाल᳓भते तं᳓ य᳓त्वास्ते यज्ञ᳓मेव᳓ त᳓द्य᳓त्वास्ते य᳓द्विसृजे᳓द्यज्ञं᳓ वि᳓सृजेद्य᳓दि विसृजे᳓त्पु᳓नर्दीक्षयेद्य᳓त्पु᳓नर्दीक्ष᳓येदा᳓हुतीरभि᳓रेचयेद्गायत्रीं᳓ वैष्णवी᳓म᳓नुब्रूयाद्वा᳓ग्वै᳓ गायत्री᳓ यज्ञो᳓ वि᳓ष्णुर्वाचै᳓व᳓ यज्ञँ᳓ स᳓न्तनोति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

“Svāhā! [I offer] the sacrifice by mind,” [he says].
By mind indeed the sacrifice is seized.
The sacrifice has the wind as its womb.
The sacrifice is in heaven, in the intermediate space, on earth;
here and there indeed is the sacrifice.
Wherever the sacrifice is, thence he seizes it with the mind.
He sits restraining it.
He sits restraining the sacrifice indeed.
If he were to release [speech], he would release the sacrifice.
If he should release [it], he should consecrate [himself] again.
If he were to consecrate [himself] again, he would cause the oblations to be redundant.
He should recite a Gāyatrī [verse] addressed to Viṣṇu.
Speech indeed is the Gāyatrī;
Viṣṇu is the sacrifice.
By speech indeed he connects the sacrifice.
