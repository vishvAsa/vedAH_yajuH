<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Agni is all the deities.  
The sacrifice is the chariot.  
Together with the deities and the sacrifice he crosses.  
Until reaching the opposite bank he should sit crushing a lump of earth.  
Thereby he does not depart from it.  
"Dwell together, this is thy seasonable womb," [he says],  
as the yajus-formula runs.  
"Here we have reached the place for worship of the gods on earth,"—with this he settles on the place for worship of the gods.  
"Crossing over together with the Ṛc and the Sāman, with the Yajuses," [he says].  
With the Ṛc and the Sāman, and with the deities, he crosses over to the place for worship of the gods.
