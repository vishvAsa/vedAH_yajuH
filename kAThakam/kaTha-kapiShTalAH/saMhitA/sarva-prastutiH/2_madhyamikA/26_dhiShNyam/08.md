<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

He recites [a verse] addressed to Savitṛ;  
impelled by Savitṛ, he begets it.  
He recites [a verse] having one deity;  
in a single way he begets it.  
If he were to recite [a verse] having two deities, he would generate for him a rival as a second.  
He recites [a verse] addressed to Heaven and Earth,  
for the comprehension of born offspring.  
He should not recite [a verse] containing [the word] "smoke";  
for when Indra slew Vṛtra, wherever he had failed, smoke arose from there;  
he who is born from failure—  
he becomes destructive to his failure.  
If [the fire] being churned should not be born, he should recite [a verse] slaying the Rakṣases,  
for the warding off of the Rakṣases.  
He should recite without breathing out;  
for the Rakṣases seek to destroy the sacrifice along the breath;  
he makes an obstacle for the Rakṣases.  
He should recite a gāyatrī for a brāhmaṇa;  
for the brāhmaṇa belongs to the gāyatrī;  
a triṣṭubh for a rājanya;  
for the rājanya belongs to the triṣṭubh;  
a jagatī for a vaiśya;  
for the vaiśya belongs to the jagatī;  
according to the respective meters.
</details>
