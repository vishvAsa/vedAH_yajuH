Gemini 3.8 Flash

he would exclude a joint of the sacrifice.
If the Soma has been purchased, he should offer in the fire of the consecrated one.
For then the sacrifice has been released;
the oblations go forth.

Gemini 3.8 Flash

he approaches it with its own deity.

For those whom he does not favor,
