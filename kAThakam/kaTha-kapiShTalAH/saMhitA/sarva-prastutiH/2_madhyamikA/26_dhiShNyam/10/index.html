The gods and the Asuras did equally at the sacrifice;
whatever the gods did, that the Asuras did.
Then the gods saw this Upāṃśu [cup];
they saw the sacrifice as concluding in this [cup];
in it alone they brought the sacrifice to conclusion.

thereby they obtained the gāyatrī meter.
Eleven times [they pressed];
thereby [they obtained] the triṣṭubh.
Twelve times [they pressed];
thereby [they obtained] the jagatī.
Three times they drew [it];
thereby they obtained the Soma-pressings.
Those gods, having enclosed as much as the sacrifice was, stepped up to offer.
Those Asuras saw: “The gods are offering.”
Having raised the vajra, they rushed at them.
The gods shut them off from these worlds with the Antaryāma [cup].
Then the gods flourished, the Asuras perished.
He for whom they offer the Upāṃśu knowing thus, and who knowing thus offers, shuts off his rival from these worlds; he himself flourishes, his rival perishes.

प्राण᳓प᳓वित्रो वा᳓ उपाँशुः᳓ प्राणै᳓रेवै᳓नं पावयति ष᳓डँपशून्परिगृह्ल्लाति त᳓स्मात्ष᳓ट् प्राणा᳓ द्वन्द्वं᳓ पावयति त᳓स्मात्प्राणा᳓ द्वन्द्व᳓मुपाँशुस᳓वनादु᳓पाँश्रुं᳓ नि᳓गृह्ल्लाति प्राणो᳓ वा᳓ उपाँशुस᳓वनः प्राण᳓ उपाँशुः᳓ प्राणा᳓देव᳓ प्राणं᳓ नि᳓गृह्ल्लात्युत्तर उपाँशुस᳓वनो भवत्य᳓धरे ऽँश᳓वः प्राणो᳓ वा᳓ उ᳓पाँश्रु᳓स᳓वनः प्रजा᳓ अँश᳓वः प्रजा᳓स्वेवं᳓ प्राणं᳓ दधाति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

He holds down the Upāṃśu [shoot] with the Upāṃśusavana stone;
the Upāṃśusavana stone is breath;
the Upāṃśu is breath;
from breath alone he holds down breath.
The Upāṃśusavana stone is above, the shoots below;
the Upāṃśusavana stone is breath, the shoots are offspring;
in offspring alone he places breath;
