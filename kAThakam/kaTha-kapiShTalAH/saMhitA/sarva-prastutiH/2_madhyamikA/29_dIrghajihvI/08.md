<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

He should make it sloping down to the south,  
[which is] the form of the place for divine worship,  
for the warding off of the rakṣases.  
Therefore the ford of the waters is shallow on the south.  
The three that are central, those he should make equal.  
To Agni indeed cattle attend,  
for the attendance of cattle.  
The central post is the self;  
his two rivals are the other two.  
Those he should make shorter;  
he thereby makes his rival inferior.  
The two beyond are equal to the central post,  
for those two are his own.  
Up to the furthest side he should measure thus,  
for the differentiation of the worse and the better;  
and he also makes his rival inferior.
