य᳓था मत्य᳙म᳓न्ववा᳓स्यतये᳓वं᳓ त᳓द्य᳓द्वशा᳓नूबन्ध्या᳙ यज्ञ᳓स्य शा᳓न्त्या अनुबध्य᳓ते य᳓त्प्रथम᳓म᳓स्रवत्त᳓द्बृ᳓हस्प᳓तिरुपा᳓गृह्णाद्य᳓द्द्विती᳓यं त᳓न्मित्रा᳓व᳓रुणौ य᳓त्तृती᳓यं त᳓द्वि᳓श्वे देवा᳓ य᳓दि तिस्रो᳓ऽनूबन्ध्या᳙स्स्यु᳓र्मैत्रावरुणीं᳓ प्रथमां᳓ कुर्याद᳓थ वैश्वदेवी᳓म᳓थ बार्हस्पत्यां᳓ यज्ञ᳓स्य सर्वत्वा᳓य सरसत्वा᳓या᳓त्रा᳓त्र हि᳓ यज्ञ᳓स्य र᳓सो᳓ऽयातयामा वा᳓ एत᳓स्य देव᳓ताश्च ब्र᳓ह्म च य᳓स्सो᳓मेन य᳓जते य᳓देते᳓ वैश्वदेवी᳓ च बार्हस्पत्या᳓ च भ᳓वतो देव᳓तानां चैव᳓ ब्र᳓ह्मणश्चा᳓यातयामत्वाय वैश्वदेवी᳓ म᳓ध्ये भ᳓वति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Just as one breaks up clods with a harrow, so is that.
When there is an anūbandhyā barren cow,
it is tied on for the pacification of the sacrifice.
What flowed out first, Bṛhaspati caught underneath.
What [flowed] second, Mitra and Varuṇa [caught].
What [flowed] third, the All-gods [caught].
If there should be three anūbandhyā [cows], he should make the first for Mitra and Varuṇa, then one for the All-gods, then one for Bṛhaspati,
for the wholeness of the sacrifice, for its possessing sap.
For here and there is the sap of the sacrifice.
Having lost their potency indeed are his deities and the sacred word, of him who sacrifices with Soma.
When there are these two, one for the All-gods and one for Bṛhaspati,
[it is] for the deities and the sacred word not to have lost their potency.
The one for the All-gods is in the middle.
