विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

अ᳓थैते᳓ऽतिग्रा᳓ह्या य᳓देव᳓ प᳓रमो᳓जो वीर्य᳙म᳓नवरुद्धं त᳓स्या᳓वरुद्ध्यै य᳓दाग्नेय᳓स्ते᳓जो वा᳓ अग्नि᳓स्ते᳓ज एव᳓ ते᳓न पुर᳓स्ताद्धत्ते᳓ य᳓दैन्द्र᳓ इन्द्रियं᳓ वा᳓ इ᳓न्द्र इन्द्रिय᳓मेव᳓ ते᳓न मध्यतो᳓ धत्ते᳓ य᳓त्सौर्यो᳓ ब्रह्मवर्चस᳓म᳓सा आदित्य᳓ उप᳓रिष्टादेव᳓ ते᳓न ब्रह्मवर्चसं᳓ धत्ते᳓ ते᳓जो वा᳓ अग्नि᳓रिन्द्रिय᳓मि᳓न्द्रो ब्रह्मवर्चस᳓मसा आदित्य᳓स्ते᳓जसा चैव᳓ ब्रह्मवर्चसे᳓न चेन्द्रिय᳓मुभय᳓त आत्म᳓न्प᳓रिगृह्णात्युपस्त᳓म्भनं वा᳓ एत᳓द्यज्ञ᳓स्य य᳓द᳓तिग्रा᳓ह्याश्चक्रि᳓यौ पृष्ठा᳓नि य᳓देते᳓ न᳓ गृह्ये᳓रन्नुप᳓रिष्टाद्ग᳓रीयाँसि पृष्ठा᳓नि प्रा᳓ञ्चं यज्ञँ᳓ स᳓म्मृ᳓ण्यु᳓र्य᳓देते᳓ गृह्य᳓न्ते पुर᳓स्तादेव᳓ यज्ञ᳓मुपस्त᳓भ्य चक्रिया उ᳓पास्यति सयत्वा᳓य प्रातस्सवने᳓ गृह्णीयादेत᳓द्वै᳓ स᳓वनानां वीर्या᳙वत्त᳓मं वीर्यादेवै᳓नान᳓धिगृह्णाति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Now these are the atigrāhya cups.
What highest ojas and virility is un-secured, it is for the securing thereof.
When there is one for Agni,
fiery energy is Agni;
fiery energy he thus places in front.
When there is one for Indra,
indriya-power is Indra;
indriya-power he thus places in the middle.
When there is one for Sūrya,
spiritual lustre is yonder sun;
spiritual lustre he thus places above.
Fiery energy is Agni, indriya-power is Indra, spiritual lustre is yonder sun;
with fiery energy and spiritual lustre he encloses indriya-power on both sides within himself.
A prop of the sacrifice are the atigrāhya cups.
The pṛṣṭha [stotras] are the two wheels.
If these should not be drawn, the pṛṣṭhas, being heavier on top, would crush the sacrifice facing forward.
When these are drawn,
having propped up the sacrifice in front, he attaches the two wheels,
for moving along.
He should draw [them] at the morning pressing.
This is the most virile of the pressings.
From virility he takes them.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

The gods were all alike;
they did not attain distinction.
Then they saw these cups: Agni the one for Agni, Indra the one for Indra, Sūrya the one for Sūrya.
Then they attained distinction, preeminence among the gods.
For whom these are drawn, he attains distinction, preeminence among his equals.
Prajāpati assigned the shares to the gods.
He thought, “I have excluded myself from the sacrifice.”
The three dear forms that he had, he deposited them away.
These very cups he then deposited away.
In whichever sacrifice he should desire to succeed, in that he should draw these cups.
Whole, with its virility, with its womb, with its body, he brings it together for success.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

These [cups] were formerly in Vṛtra.
Having slain Vṛtra, Indra found this one for Agni.
He gave it to Agni: “Hold this for me.”
He found the one for Sūrya.
He gave it to Sūrya: “Hold this for me.”
Having found the one for Indra, he asked the other two back.
They did not give [them] back to him.
They entered into these worlds: Agni into this [world], Indra into the intermediate space, Sūrya into yonder [world].
By these cups they prospered in these worlds.
By these they attained sovereignty over these worlds.
In all these worlds he prospers, sovereignty over these worlds he attains, for whom these are drawn.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

What is the fiery energy of the Virāj, that is the one for Agni;
what is [the fiery energy] of the Śakvarī, that is the one for Indra;
what is [the fiery energy] of the Revatī, that is the one for Sūrya.
Where these sāmans are [sung], there he should draw them,
for the sāmans to possess fiery energy.
Now these are offerings and consumptions.
By these Bambā and Viśvavayas knew these worlds, both hither and thither.
For whom these are drawn, for him these worlds shine together, both hither and thither.
By their virility Agni shines upward from here;
downward shines the sun;
across blows the wind.
Well known from here in this manner is yonder world.
From there hither, however, it is hard to know.
He indeed knows this world from there hither, for whom these are drawn.
