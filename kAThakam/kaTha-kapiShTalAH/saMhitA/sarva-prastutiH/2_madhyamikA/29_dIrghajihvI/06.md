<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)</summary>

देवा᳓ वा᳓ अ᳓सुरान्यज्ञ᳓मभिजि᳓त्य ते प्र᳓बाहुग्य्रहान्गृह्णाना आ᳓यन्स᳓ प्रजा᳓पतिरमन्यत यः᳓ प्रथमो᳓ ग्रहीष्य᳓ते स᳓ एवे᳓दं᳓ भविष्यती᳓ति स᳓ एत᳓मँशु᳓मपश्यत्तं᳓ प᳓राङ् प्राण᳓न्न᳓ गृह्णीत ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

The gods and the asuras—having conquered the sacrifice, they came drawing the cups in a continuous row.  
Prajāpati thought: "He who shall be drawn first, he indeed shall become this [all]."  
He saw this Aṃśu [cup].  
He drew it breathing out, turned away.
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)</summary>

स᳓ प᳓राङ् श्रियो᳓ऽन्तमगच्छद्बु᳓भूषन्नेतं᳓ गृह्णीत प᳓राङेव᳓ प्रा᳓ण्यान्ना᳓पान्यात्प᳓राङेव᳓ श्रियो᳓ऽन्तं गच्छति य᳓दपा᳓नित्या᳓र्तिमार्छ᳓ति प्र᳓ वा मीयते सर्वज्यानिं᳓ वा जीयते य᳓दि काम᳓येत सदृ᳓ङ्य᳓जमानस्स्यादि᳓ति न᳓ प्रा᳓ण्यान्ना᳓पान्यात्सदृ᳓ङ्ङेव᳓ भवति चतु᳓रस्स्रक्ति᳓ पा᳓त्रं भवति च᳓तस्रो दि᳓शस्स᳓र्वास्वेव᳓ दिक्ष्वृ᳙ध्नोति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Turned away, he went to the end of prosperity.  
Desiring to prosper, he should draw this.  
He should only breathe out, turned away; he should not breathe in.  
Turned away, he goes to the end of prosperity.  
When he breathes in, he falls into distress, or he perishes, or he suffers complete ruin.  
If he should desire, "May the sacrificer remain the same," he should neither breathe out nor breathe in.  
He remains just the same.  
The vessel is four-cornered.  
Four are the quarters.  
In all the quarters he prospers.
</details>
