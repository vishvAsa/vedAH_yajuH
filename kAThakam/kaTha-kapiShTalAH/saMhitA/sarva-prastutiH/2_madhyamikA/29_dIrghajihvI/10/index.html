आग्नेयँ᳓ सारस्वतीँ᳓ सौम्यं᳓ बार्हस्पत्यं᳓ ता᳓न्सहा᳓लभेत ब्रह्मवर्चस᳓कामः प्रजा᳓कामः पशु᳓कामः पुरोधा᳓कामो य᳓दाग्नेय᳓ आग्नेयो᳓ हि᳓ ब्राह्मणो᳓ य᳓त्सारस्वती᳓ वा᳓ग्वै᳓ स᳓रस्वती वा᳓चँ हि᳓ व᳓दति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

The one for Agni, the one for Sarasvatī, the one for Soma, the one for Bṛhaspati: one should seize these together if desiring spiritual lustre, desiring offspring, desiring cattle, desiring the office of purohita, desiring the office of purohita.
In that there is one for Agni,
for the brāhmaṇa belongs to Agni.
In that there is one for Sarasvatī,
for Sarasvatī is speech,
for he speaks speech.

ब्रह्मवर्चसी᳓ भवत्य᳓थ पौष्णः᳓ पश᳓वो वै᳓ पूषा᳓ पशू᳓नेव᳓ ब्र᳓ह्मन्ननुनि᳓युनक्त्य᳓थैन्द्राग्नो᳓ऽथैन्द्रः᳓ क्षत्रं᳓ वा᳓ इन्द्राग्नी᳓ क्षत्र᳓मि᳓न्द्रः क्षत्र᳓मेव᳓ सम्पा᳓द्य प्र᳓तिष्ठापयत्य᳓थो क्षत्रे᳓णैव᳓ पशू᳓न्ब्र᳓ह्मण उ᳓पो᳓हत्याग्नेयो᳓ म᳓ध्ये भवत्यैन्द्रा᳓ अभि᳓त आग्नेयो᳓ वै᳓ ब्राह्मण᳓ ऐन्द्रो᳓ राजन्यो᳙ ब्र᳓ह्मणैव᳓ क्षत्रं᳓ मध्यतो᳓ व्य᳓वसर्पति प्र᳓ पुरोधा᳓माप्नोति य᳓ एवं᳓ वे᳓देन्द्राग्नी᳓ पशूनां᳓ भूयिष्ठभा᳓जौ करो᳓त्यग्ने᳓राग्नेय᳓ इ᳓न्द्रस्यैन्द्र᳓ ऐन्द्राग्न᳓स्सह᳓ त᳓स्माद्ब्राह्मण᳓श्च राजन्य᳙श्च प्रजा᳓नां भूयिष्ठभाजा᳓ अ᳓थ वैश्वदेवो᳓ऽथ मारुतो᳓ वि᳓ड्वै᳓ वि᳓श्वे देवा᳓ विरमरु᳓तो वि᳓शमेव᳓ सम्पा᳓द्य तां᳓ क्षत्रा᳓यानुनि᳓युनक्ति ब्रह्ममुख᳓मेव᳓ क्षत्रं᳓ कृत्वा᳓ त᳓स्मै वि᳓शमनुनि᳓युनक्ति प्रसवा᳓यैव᳓ सावित्री᳓ निर्वरुणत्वा᳓य वारुण᳓स्सम्य᳓गेव᳓ ब्र᳓ह्म स᳓मू᳓हति सम्य᳓क्क्षत्रँ᳓ समी᳓चीं वि᳓शं य᳓त्रैव᳓ क्लृ᳓प्तैकादशि᳓न्यालभ्य᳓ते कल्पते त᳓त्र प्रजा᳓भ्यो ब्राह्मण᳓ एव᳓ ब्राह्मणो᳓ भवति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Then the one for Pūṣan:
Pūṣan is cattle;
he yokes cattle after the brāhmaṇa.
Then the one for Indra and Agni,
then the one for Indra:
Indra and Agni are royal power, Indra is royal power;
having brought royal power to completion, he establishes it;
and with royal power he pushes cattle toward the brāhmaṇa.
The one for Agni is in the middle, those for Indra on both sides.
The brāhmaṇa belongs to Agni, the rājanya to Indra;
with the brāhmaṇa, royal power glides apart in the middle.
He attains the office of purohita who knows thus.
He makes Indra and Agni the greatest share-holders of the cattle.
Agni’s is that for Agni, Indra’s is that for Indra, that for Indra and Agni is for both together;
therefore the brāhmaṇa and the rājanya are the greatest share-holders among creatures.
Then the one for the Viśve Devas,
then the one for the Maruts:
the Viśve Devas are the commonalty, the Maruts are the commonalty;
having brought the commonalty to completion, he yokes it after royal power;
having made royal power head the brāhmaṇa, he yokes the commonalty after it.
The one for Savitṛ is for impulsion;
the one for Varuṇa is for freedom from Varuṇa.
Properly he unites the brāhmaṇa, properly royal power, properly the commonalty.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Where the properly arranged set of eleven is seized, there it is in order for creatures:
the brāhmaṇa becomes a brāhmaṇa, the rājanya a rājanya, the vaiśya a vaiśya.
Following this disordered [arrangement] of the set of eleven, indeed, it is not in order for creatures.
This indeed is the set of eleven;
this the Kāpeyas know;
this he should seize as the final one of the Atirātra.
This other is sacrificial-session-like, which creatures follow falling into trouble.
