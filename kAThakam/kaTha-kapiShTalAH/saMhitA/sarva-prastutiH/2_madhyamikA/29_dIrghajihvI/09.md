out should take this set of eleven in whatever sacrifice it may be.

Agni is all the deities.  
In Agni indeed these deities are seized.  
The one for Agni is the self.  
Sarasvatī is speech.  
In that there is one for Sarasvatī,  
he yokes speech as a pair in the self.  
Then the one for Soma:  
Soma is the placer of seed;  
he places seed in the pair itself.  
Then the one for Pūṣan:  
Pūṣan is cattle;  
he generates indeed.
</details>
