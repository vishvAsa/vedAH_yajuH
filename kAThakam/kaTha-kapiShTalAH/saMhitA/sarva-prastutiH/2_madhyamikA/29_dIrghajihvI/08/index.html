सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

with fiery energy and spiritual lustre he encloses indriya-power on both sides within himself.
From the south the rakṣases sought to strike the sacrifice of the gods.
For those deities that are more endowed with ojas, they are seized on the south,
for the warding off of the rakṣases.
“What is done at the set of eleven [posts],” ask the theological disputants, “whereby the commonalty settles in subordination to the ruling power?”
For those deities that are more endowed with ojas, they are seized on the south;
for those that are weaker, on the north.
Therefore the commonalty settles in subordination to the ruling power.

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

ताज᳓क्प्र᳓धन्वति य᳓दि ना᳓भिच᳓रेदारण्यं᳓ पशुं᳓ नि᳓र्दिशेत्त᳓स्य ना᳓श्नीयादुपशयं᳓ वा᳓ अ᳓न्वारण्याः᳓ पश᳓व उपतिष्ठन्त एकादशि᳓नीं ग्राम्या᳓ य᳓देकादशि᳓न्या वे᳓दिर्मीय᳓ते त᳓स्माद्ग्राम्याः᳓ पश᳓व आवि᳓रिव शान्ता᳓ इव य᳓दुपशयो᳓ गु᳓हेव श᳓ये त᳓स्मादारण्याः᳓ पश᳓वो गु᳓हेव निला᳓यमिव प्रला᳓यमिव च᳓रन्ति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

He quickly perishes.
If he should not practice sorcery, he should assign a wild beast;
he should not eat of it.
Along with the upaśaya the wild beasts attend;
along with the set of eleven, the domestic ones.
Because the vedi is measured with the set of eleven,
therefore domestic animals are as it were visible, as it were peaceful.
Because the upaśaya lies as if in a hiding place,
therefore wild beasts wander as if in a hiding place, as if in concealment, as if vanished.
