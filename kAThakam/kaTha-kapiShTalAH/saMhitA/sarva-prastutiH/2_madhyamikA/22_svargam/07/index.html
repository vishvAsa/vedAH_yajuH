सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

By the fire, indeed, the gods went to the heavenly world.

Those gods saw these naturally perforated [bricks] (vitṛṇṇī).
With them they struck away the demons.
When he places two in front

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

द्वे᳓ पश्चा᳓च्च᳓क्षुरेवो᳓भय᳓तो दधाति र᳓क्षसाम᳓पहत्यै स्वय᳓मा᳓तृ᳓ष्णा भवन्ति प्राणा᳓नामु᳓त्सृष्ट्यै स्वर्ग᳓स्य लोक᳓स्या᳓नु᳓क्शात्यै᳓ च᳓तस्रस्त᳓स्माच्चत्वा᳓रि च᳓क्षुषो रूपा᳓णि द्वे᳓ शुक्ले᳓ द्वे᳓ कृष्णे᳓ स्वर्गा᳓य वै᳓ लोका᳓याग्नि᳓श्चीय᳓ते य᳓देकादशि᳓नीं कुर्या᳓द्व᳓ज्रेण पुर᳓स्ताद᳓वगृह्णीयाद᳓स्वर्ग्यस्स्या᳓द्य᳓द᳓नेकादशि᳓नीम᳓न᳓वरुद्वा᳓ अस्य᳓ पश᳓वस्स्युरेकयूप᳓ ए᳓कादश पशू᳓ना᳓लभेत ते᳓नैव᳓ स्वर्ग्य᳙स्ते᳓न पशू᳓न᳓वरु᳓न्द्वे᳓ ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

and two behind,
he places sight indeed on both sides
for the striking away of the demons.
They are naturally perforated [bricks] (svayamātṛṇṇā)
for the emission of the vital breaths,
for the sighting of the heavenly world.
They are four;
therefore the eye has four forms: two white, two black.
For the heavenly world, indeed, the fire is piled.
If he were to make an eleven-[stake animal sacrifice] (ekādaśinī), he would obstruct it in front with a thunderbolt; it would not belong to the heavenly world.
If he were not to make an eleven-[stake animal sacrifice], his cattle would not be secured.
He should sacrifice eleven cattle at one sacrificial stake;
thereby it belongs to the heavenly world,
and thereby he secures cattle.
