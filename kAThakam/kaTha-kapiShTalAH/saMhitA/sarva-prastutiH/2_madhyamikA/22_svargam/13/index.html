सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

He offers an eleven-potsherd cake to Agni and Viṣṇu.
Agni is all the deities;
Viṣṇu is the sacrifice.
Having taken hold of both the deities and the sacrifice, he enters upon the consecration.
Agni is a purifier (pavitra), Viṣṇu is the sacrifice;
purified by the purifier, he takes hold of the sacrifice.
Because it is on eight potsherds,
thereby it belongs to Agni;
because it is on three potsherds,
thereby it belongs to Viṣṇu;
thereby he does not depart from either.
He offers an eleven-potsherd cake to Agni and Viṣṇu.
Agni is the lowest of the gods, Viṣṇu the highest;
just as one binds the two ends with a tie, even so, having grasped them from both sides, he places the deities in the middle.
