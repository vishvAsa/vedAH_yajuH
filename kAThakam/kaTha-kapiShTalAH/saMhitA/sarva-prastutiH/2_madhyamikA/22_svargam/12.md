<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)</summary>

य᳓द्युख्ये᳓ भ्रिय᳓माणेऽयं᳓ देवः᳓ प्रजा᳓ अभिम᳓न्येत या᳓ आग्नेयी᳓र्भि᳓षग्वतीस्ता᳓भिस्तिसृ᳓भिस्तिस्र᳓स्समि᳓ध आदध्या᳓देष᳓ वा᳓वै᳓त᳓त्प्रजा᳓ अभि᳓मन्यते य᳓द्भि᳓षग्वतीभिराद᳓धाति भेषज᳓मेव᳓ करोति शा᳓न्तिरेव᳓ सा᳓ य᳓द्युख्ये᳓ भ्रिय᳓माणे काम᳓येत वर्षेदि᳓ति या᳓स्सौरी᳓ रश्मिव᳓तीस्ता᳓भिस्तिसृ᳓भिस्तिस्र᳓स्समि᳓ध आदध्या᳓देष᳓ वा᳓वै᳓त᳓त्प्रजा᳓श्श्रु᳓चार्पयति य᳓र्हि पर्ज᳓न्यो न᳓ व᳓र्षति य᳓द्रश्मिव᳓तीभिराद᳓धाति वृ᳓ष्टिमेव᳓ नि᳓नयति ताजकप्र᳓वर्षति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

If, while [the fire] is being borne in the pan, this god should afflict the creatures, he should put on three kindling-sticks with three verses to Agni containing [the word] "physician" (bhiṣajvant).  
He, indeed, afflicts the creatures in this way.  
When he puts [them] on with [verses] containing "physician",  
he makes medicine;  
that is indeed an appeasement.  
If, while [the fire] is being borne in the pan, he should desire: "May it rain", he should put on three kindling-sticks with three verses to Sūrya containing [the word] "ray" (raśmivant).  
He, indeed, afflicts the creatures with heat in this way when Parjanya does not rain.  
When he puts [them] on with [verses] containing "ray",  
he brings down rain;  
it rains quickly.
</details>
