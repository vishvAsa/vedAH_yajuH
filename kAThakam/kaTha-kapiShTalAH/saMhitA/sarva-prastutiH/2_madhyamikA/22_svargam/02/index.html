सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

if one should not prosper by the piled fire, he should pile the re-piling;
he does indeed prosper.
The first layer is the self;
the second, offspring;
the third, grandson.
Piling the third fire, he should place these [bricks] in the middle layer,
for continuity.
This fire is called Napātka (‘having a grandson’);
he eats food who, knowing thus, places these.
He places eight of these;
eight syllables has the gāyatrī;
he establishes himself in the gāyatrī.
