<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)</summary>

य᳓दि म᳓न्येत पू᳓र्वो मा᳓तिक्रान्तो भ्रा᳓तृव्य इ᳓ति बहिष्पवमान᳓स्य पुर᳓स्तादष्टौ᳓ लोकम्पृण᳓यो᳓पदध्यान्मा᳓ध्यन्दिनस्य प᳓वमानस्य पुर᳓स्तादे᳓कादशा᳓र्भवस्य प᳓वमानस्य पुर᳓स्ताद्द्वा᳓दशायं᳓ वा᳓व᳓ यः᳓ प᳓वते स᳓ प᳓वमानो वायु᳓रस्ते᳓जो य᳓ एवै᳓नं पू᳓र्वो᳓ऽतिक्रान्तो भ्रा᳓तृव्यस्तं᳓ ते᳓जसा प्रणुद᳓ते य᳓दि म᳓न्येत ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

If he should think: "My rival has gone ahead before [me]", he should place eight [bricks] with the space-filler (lokaṃpṛṇā) in front of the Bahiṣpavamāna, eleven in front of the Mādhyandina Pavamāna, twelve in front of the Ārbhava Pavamāna.  
This one, indeed, who purifies himself is the Pavamāna;  
the wind is Agni's fiery energy.  
Whichever rival has gone ahead before him, him he drives forward with fiery energy.
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

If he should think: "My rival is behind me", he should place [them] behind these Pavamānas.  
This one, indeed, who purifies himself is the Pavamāna;  
the wind is Agni's fiery energy.

</details>
