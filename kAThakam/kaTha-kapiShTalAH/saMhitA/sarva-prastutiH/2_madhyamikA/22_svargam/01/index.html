Gemini 3.8 Flash

These two fires heat him, the Agniṣṭoma and the Vaiśvānara.
With two [verses] containing [the word] ‘water’ (apasvatī) he touches [it],
for pacification;
with two,
[for] the sacrificer has two feet,
for establishment.
‘May the seasons, O Agni, strengthen you through the years,’
with the years he strengthens the seasons; with the seasons, the year.
‘Shine together with divine splendor,’
therefore Agni shines through all directions.
‘The two Aśvins pushed death away from him’—he should perform these for one who is diseased;
he repels death from him.
‘Rising out from darkness…’
Darkness is evil;
he strikes away evil by this [verse].
‘Seeing the higher light…’
that sun yonder is the highest light;
he goes to union with that sun yonder.
