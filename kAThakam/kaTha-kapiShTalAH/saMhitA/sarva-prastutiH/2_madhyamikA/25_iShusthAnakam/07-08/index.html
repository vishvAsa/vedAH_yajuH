विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

चत्वा᳓रो वै᳓ देवा᳓नाँ हो᳓तार आसन्भूपति᳓र्भु᳓वनपतिर्भूता᳓नां प᳓तिर्भूत᳓स्ते᳓षां त्र᳓यो होत्रे᳓ण प्रा᳓मीयन्ता᳓थो य᳓द्भूत᳓ उद᳓शिष्यत स᳓ प्र᳓म᳓यादबिभेत्स᳓ न्य᳙लयत स᳓ समुद्रं᳓ प्रा᳓विशत् ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

The gods indeed had four Hotṛs: Bhūpati, Bhuvanapati, Bhūtānām Pati, and Bhūta.
Three of them perished through the office of Hotṛ.
Now, because Bhūta was left over, he feared death.
He hid himself.
He entered the sea.

Running up from there, he entered a reed.

If at the third pressing, he should accompany it with the formula: ‘The sacrifice has gone to the fathers, to the foreign people; of that sacrifice, sacrificed, enjoyed, may the wealth come here.’

They untie the knots.
Intending to tie the knots of the gods, they untie the knots of men.
