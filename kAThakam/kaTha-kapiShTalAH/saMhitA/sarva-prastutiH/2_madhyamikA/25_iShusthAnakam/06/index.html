सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

The gods and the Asuras indeed were in conflict.
Whatever sacrificial brilliance of this [earth] existed, that, having assumed the form of a lioness as it were, the form of a female buffalo as it were, stood between them.
They knew: ‘To whichever [party] she turns, they will become this [all].’
The gods addressed her.

for protection.
In that he besprinkles [it], in that way the oblation reaches her before Agni.
Then she said that: ‘May the oblation reach me before Agni, then through me may you attain all desires.’
Then at that time the Asuras were their rivals.
They desired: ‘May we conquer the rivals.’
‘You are a lioness, conqueror of rivals, svāhā,’ [he says];
thereby they conquered the rivals.
They desired: ‘May we find cattle.’
‘You are a lioness, winner of wealth and prosperity, svāhā,’ [he says];
thereby they found cattle.
They desired: ‘May we find offspring.’
‘You are a lioness, winner of good offspring, svāhā,’ [he says];
thereby they found offspring.
They desired: ‘May we become the Ādityas.’
‘You are a lioness, winner of the Ādityas, svāhā,’ [he says];
thereby they became the Ādityas.
Therefore these creatures are Ādityas.
They desired: ‘May we be worthy of sacrifice.’
‘You are a lioness; bring the gods for the sacrificer who longs for the gods, svāhā,’ [he says];
thereby they became worthy of sacrifice.
They desired: ‘May we go to the heavenly world.’
‘To the beings, for you,’ [he says].
