विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

शि᳓रो वा᳓ एत᳓द्यज्ञ᳓स्य य᳓द्धविर्धा᳓नं प्राणा᳓ उपरवा᳓ द᳓क्षिणस्य हविर्धा᳓नस्याध᳓स्तात्परिलिख᳓त्येत᳓द्वा᳓ एत᳓योः प᳓र्वं मुखत᳓ एव᳓ यज्ञ᳓स्य प्रा᳓णा᳓न्प्र᳓तिदधाति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

The head of the sacrifice indeed is the oblation-cart shed, the vital breaths are the sound-holes.
He draws a line around beneath the southern oblation-cart.
This indeed is the earlier of the two.
He places the vital breaths right at the mouth of the sacrifice.

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

त᳓स्मान्मुखतः᳓ प्राणा᳓श्चतु᳓रः प᳓रिलिखति चत्वा᳓रो हि᳓ प्राणाः᳓ प्रादेशं᳓ प्रादेशं वि᳓परिलिख᳓त्येता᳓वदेता᳓वद्धी᳓मे᳙ऽभि᳓ प्राणा᳓ अ᳓सम्भिन्द᳓न्तः ख᳓नन्ति प्राणा᳓नाम᳓सम्भेदाय य᳓त्सम्भिन्द्या᳓त्प्रा᳓णा᳓न्स᳓म्भिन्द्यात्प्र᳓मीयेत ता᳓न्पर᳓ एकधा᳓ स᳓न्तृन्दन्ति त᳓स्मात्प्राणाः᳓ पर᳓ एकधा᳓ स᳓न्तृण्णा देव᳓स्य त्वा सवितुः᳓ प्रसव᳓ इ᳓त्य᳓भ्रिमा᳓दत्ते सवितृ᳓प्रसूत एवै᳓नान्देव᳓ताभिरा᳓दत्त इद᳓महं᳓ रक्ष᳓सो ग्रीवा᳓ अ᳓धिकृन्तामी᳓ति भ्रा᳓तृव्यो वै᳓ र᳓क्षो भ्रा᳓तृव्यस्यैव᳓ ग्रीवा᳓ अ᳓धिकृन्ततीद᳓महं᳓ यो᳓ नस्स᳓मा᳓नो यो᳓ऽसमानोऽरातीय᳓ति त᳓स्य ग्रीवा᳓ अपिकृन्ता᳓मी᳓ति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Therefore the vital breaths are at the mouth.
He draws a line around four [of them];
for the vital breaths are four.
He draws lines around apart from one another at a span’s distance each.
For by so much these vital breaths correspond to one another. Not breaking [into each other], they dig,
so that the vital breaths do not break into one another.
If he were to break them into one another, he would break the vital breaths into one another; [the sacrificer] would perish.
Beyond, they pierce them together into one.
Therefore the vital breaths beyond are pierced together into one.
‘At the impulse of the god Savitṛ [I take you],’ [saying this] he takes the spade.
Impelled by Savitṛ, he takes it with the deities.
‘Here I cut off the neck of the Rakṣas,’ [he says].
The rival indeed is the Rakṣas.
He cuts off the neck of the rival.
‘Here I cut off the neck of him who, being our equal or not our equal, plots evil against us,’ [he says].
