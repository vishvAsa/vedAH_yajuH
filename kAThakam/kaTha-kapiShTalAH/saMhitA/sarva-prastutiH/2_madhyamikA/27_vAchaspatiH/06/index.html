य᳓दि काम᳓येत य᳓जमानाद᳓यजमानं भ्रा᳓तृव्यं पू᳓र्वं कुर्या᳓मि᳓ति प्रतिप्रस्थाता᳓ पू᳓र्वो गृहीत्वा᳓ स᳓ पू᳓र्वो नि᳓रूहे᳓द्य᳓जमानो वै᳓ श्रे᳓यान्पा᳓पीयानस्मा᳓द्भ्रा᳓तृव्यो᳓ ऽयजमानो भ्रा᳓तृव्यमेवा᳓स्मा᳓त्पू᳓र्वं करोत्यध्वर्यो᳓र्लोके᳓ ति᳓ष्ठञ्जुहोत्यध्वर्यु᳓र्वै᳓ श्रे᳓यान्पा᳓पीयान्प्रतिप्रस्थाता᳓ भ्रा᳓तृव्यमेवा᳓स्य᳓ लोक᳓मभ्यारोह᳓य᳓त्यलोक᳓मेनं करोति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

If he should desire: “May I make the rival who does not sacrifice superior to the sacrificer!"—the Pratiprasthātṛ, having taken [it] first, should push [it] out first.
The sacrificer indeed is the better; the rival who does not sacrifice is worse than he;
he makes the rival superior to him.
Standing in the place of the Adhvaryu, he offers.
The Adhvaryu indeed is the better; the Pratiprasthātṛ is the worse.
His rival mounts upon his place;
he deprives him of his place.

Gemini 3.8 Flash

he would become propertyless [otherwise].
