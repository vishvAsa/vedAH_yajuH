सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Therefore a brahmin should sit near the Bahiṣpavamāna,
for that is a purifier.
Therefore, whom he hates, him he should exclude from the Bahiṣpavamāna;
he excludes him from the purifier itself.
Turning back to the gods, the healing body that was theirs,
that they deposited in three parts: a third in fire, a third in a brahmin, a third in the waters.
Whom he wishes, [being] diseased, “May he live!”, having spoken to a brahmin in the presence of fire, he should make declaration to the waters.
So much indeed is the remedy;
as much as the remedy is, that he performs for him.
