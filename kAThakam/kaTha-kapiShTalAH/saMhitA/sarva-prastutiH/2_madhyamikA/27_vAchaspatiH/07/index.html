आ᳓युस्स᳓न्धत्तं प्राणँ᳓ स᳓न्धत्तमि᳓ति प्रा᳓णा᳓नेव᳓ स᳓न्धत्तस्त᳓स्मात्प्राणा᳓ द्वन्द्वँ᳓ सञ्जग्माना᳓ दि᳓वा पृथिव्ये᳓तीमा᳓नेव᳓ लोका᳓न्स᳓मर्धयतो निर᳓स्ता श᳓ण्डाम᳓र्कौ सह᳓ ते᳓न यं᳓ द्विष्म᳓ इ᳓त्येता᳓वान्वै᳓ यज्ञो᳓ या᳓वच्छुक्रा᳓म᳓न्थिना एता᳓वती पृथिवी᳓ या᳓वत्युत्तरवेदि᳓र᳓नृतँ श᳓ण्डामर्का᳓ अ᳓नृतं भ्रा᳓तृव्यो᳓ ऽनृतमेव᳓ भ्रा᳓तृव्यमेता᳓वता यज्ञा᳓च्च पृथिव्या᳓श्च नि᳓र्भ᳓जतश्शुक्र᳓स्याधिष्ठा᳓नम᳓सि मन्थि᳓नो ऽधिष्ठा᳓नमसी᳓ति स᳓मिद्ध एव᳓ जुहुतो ऽग्नि᳓र्वा᳓ आ᳓हुत्या अ᳓तिद्रवादबिभेद्य᳓च्छल्कौ᳓ प्रा᳓स्यत ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

[With the words:] “Unite the lifetime, unite the breath!”
they indeed unite the vital breaths;
therefore the vital breaths are in pairs.
[With the words:] “Meeting together with sky and earth”
they enrich these worlds.
[With the words:] “Cast out are Śaṇḍa and Marka together with him whom we hate!”
As great as are the Śukra and Manthin [cups], so great indeed is the sacrifice.
As great as is the high altar, so great is the earth.
Untruth are Śaṇḍa and Marka;
untruth is the rival.
Untruth indeed, the rival, they exclude by this much from both the sacrifice and the earth.
[With the words:] “You are the seat of Śukra; you are the seat of Manthin,”
they offer indeed when [the fire is] kindled.
Agni feared the overflowing of the oblation;
in that they cast two chips into [the fire],
