विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

इ᳓डैव᳓ पू᳓र्वो᳓पहूया यज्ञ᳓स्य य᳓थापू᳓र्वत्वाय प्राणा᳓ वै᳓ द्विदेवत्याः᳙ पश᳓व इ᳓डा य᳓देवं᳓ कुर्या᳓त्पशु᳓भिः प्रा᳓णा᳓नन्तरिया᳓त्प्र᳓मीयेत द्विदेवत्या᳙ एव᳓ पू᳓र्वे भ᳓क्षयितव्या अ᳓न्तरे वै᳓ प्राणाः᳓ प᳓रे पश᳓वः प्रा᳓णा᳓नेवा᳓त्म᳓न्धित्वा᳓ पशू᳓नु᳓पहूते ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

The Iḍā alone should be invoked first,
for the proper sequence of the sacrifice.
The vital breaths indeed are the [cups] with two deities; cattle are the Iḍā.
If he were to do so [otherwise], he would separate the vital breaths by cattle; [the sacrificer] would perish.
The [cups] with two deities alone should be consumed first.
The vital breaths indeed are inside; cattle are outside.
Having placed the vital breaths in himself, he invokes cattle.
