आदित्ये᳓भ्यो धार᳓यद्वद्भ्यश्चरुं᳓ नि᳓र्वपेद᳓परुद्धो वापरुरुत्स्य᳓मानो वादित्या᳓ वै᳓ त्राता᳓र आदित्या᳓ अपरोद्धा᳓रस्ता᳓नेव᳓ भागधे᳓येनो᳓पधावति त᳓ एनं दाध्रत्यादित्ये᳓भ्यो भु᳓वद्वद्भ्यश्चरुं᳓ नि᳓र्वपेद्बु᳓भूषन्न᳓दितिर्वै᳓ प्रजा᳓कामौदन᳓मपचत्त᳓स्यो᳓च्छिष्टमाश्नात्सा᳓ ग᳓र्भमधत्त त᳓त आदित्या᳓ अजायन्त ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

One who is expelled or about to be expelled should offer a caru to the sustaining Ādityas.
The Ādityas are indeed protectors;
the Ādityas are expellers.
He resorts to them with their share;
they sustain him.
One who desires to thrive should offer a caru to the Ādityas possessed of being.
Aditi, desiring offspring, cooked a rice mash.
She ate of its remainder.
She conceived an embryo.
Thence the Ādityas were born.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

She thought: ‘Better [sons] would have been born to me if I had eaten beforehand.’
She cooked another [portion].
She ate of it on both sides: beforehand and afterwards.
She conceived an embryo.
It spoke while still an embryo within.
The Ādityas thought: ‘If this one is born, he alone will become this [all].’
They struck him out;
