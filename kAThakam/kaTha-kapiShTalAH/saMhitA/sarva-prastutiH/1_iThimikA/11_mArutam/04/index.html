सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Bṛhaspati is the deity of him who is born after;
he takes hold of him;
he leads him round to the foremost place.

the brahman is the father of the Brahmin;
the father has power over the son, whether forwards or backwards, not vice versa;
the father, the brahman itself, leads him round to the foremost place.
After-ripening, indeed, are these rice grains;
he who is born after—them he now leads round to the foremost place.
In that he prepares the offering,
