आग्नेय᳓मष्टा᳓कपालं नि᳓र्वपेत्सावित्र᳓मष्टा᳓कपालं वायव्यां᳙ य᳓वागुं᳓ भौम᳓मे᳓ककपालं य᳓स्य हि᳓रण्यम᳓पहृतं वो᳓पहृतं वा स्या᳓द्यो᳓ वा काम᳓येत ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

One whose gold has been carried off or hidden, or who desires, ‘May I find gold,’ should offer a cake on eight potsherds to Agni, a cake on eight potsherds to Savitṛ, barley gruel to Vāyu, and a cake on one potsherd to the Earth.
Agni, indeed, in the beginning found gold.

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

हि᳓रण्यं विन्देये᳓त्यग्नि᳓र्वा᳓ अ᳓न्ने हि᳓रण्यमविन्दत्त᳓स्मिन्सविता᳓ चामन्त्रयता᳓न्ने᳓ऽतो वा᳓युनानु᳓पगतमस्त्यस्या᳓मेत᳓द्विन्दन्त्यस्यां᳓ पुनन्ति य᳓दाग्नेयो᳓ वि᳓त्त्या एव᳓ स᳓ जात᳓वेदा इ᳓ति हि᳓ प्रसवा᳓यैव᳓ सावित्रो᳓ऽभिनीत्यै वायव्या᳓स्या᳓मेत᳓द्विन्द᳓न्त्यस्यां᳓ पुनन्त्येता᳓ वै᳓ देव᳓ता हि᳓रण्यस्येशते ता᳓ एव᳓ भागधे᳓येनो᳓पधावति ता᳓ अस्मै᳓ हि᳓रण्यं ददति यदा᳓ हि᳓रण्यं विन्दे᳓ता᳓थैता᳓मेव᳓ नि᳓र्वपेत् ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

In regard to it, Savitṛ and [Vāyu] took counsel.
There is nothing that is not reached by Vāyu from here.
In this [Earth] they find it.
In this [Earth] they purify [it].
In that there is [a cake] to Agni, [it is] for finding [it];
for [they say], ‘He is Jātavedas.’
[The cake] to Savitṛ is for impulsion.
[The gruel] to Vāyu is for bringing [it] here.
In this [Earth] they find it.
In this [Earth] they purify [it].
These deities, indeed, rule over gold.
He runs toward those very [deities] with their share;
they give him gold.
When he finds gold, then he should offer this very [sacrifice].
Those very deities who give him gold he makes share-possessing,
for the non-injury of himself.

Gemini 3.8 Flash

One should offer a cake on eight potsherds to Agni, and a cake on eleven potsherds to Indra.
Curd, honey, ghee, parched grains, and husked rice grains: that should be mixed together.
One who desires cattle should sacrifice with that.
[The cake] to Agni is for prosperity.
In that there is [a cake] to Indra,
Indra is the giver of cattle.
He runs toward him with his share;
he gives him cattle.
In that it is mixed,
cattle, indeed, are mixed.
He wins cattle.
It should be made for Indra;
Indra is the giver of cattle.
If he should make two offerings together to Indra, it would be a tautology.
It should be made for Prajāpati.
Prajāpati is the womb of cattle;
he generates them from their own womb.
Prajāpati is the giver;
he runs toward him with his share;
he gives him cattle.
The Revatī [verses are] the offering and invitatory verses of the mixed [offering], the Śakvarī [verses] of the other.
Cattle, indeed, are Revatī; cattle are Śakvarī.
He wins cattle.
These mixed ones are five; the other two are [two].
They are seven.
The Śakvarī has seven feet;
cattle belong to the Śakvarī.
He wins cattle.
It also becomes a Paṅkti;
man is fivefold.
Becoming complete, he approaches cattle.
