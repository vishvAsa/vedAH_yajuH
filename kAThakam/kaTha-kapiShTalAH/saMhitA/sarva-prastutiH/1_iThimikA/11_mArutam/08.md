</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

One who is going to sacrifice with this [rite] should offer on the previous day an eleven-pot-sherd [cake] to Agni and Viṣṇu, a caru to Sarasvatī, and a caru to Bṛhaspati.  
Agni indeed is all the deities; Viṣṇu is the sacrifice; Sarasvatī is speech; Bṛhaspati is the brahman.  
These very deities he takes hold of on the previous day.  
The vital breaths indeed are the deities;  
having taken hold of the vital breaths, he fasts.  
A sick man should offer tomorrow an eight-pot-sherd [cake] to Agni,
</details>

</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

a caru to Soma, a caru to Aditi, a caru made of barley to Varuṇa, and a twelve-pot-sherd [cake] to Agni Vaiśvānara.  
To Agni indeed go the flesh-parts of a deceased man;  
to Soma, the sap;  
to the earth, the body.  
This [earth] is Aditi;  
for in her he lies.  
These deities indeed have power over a man;  
from them he rouses him upward.
</details>

'With the purifying stoma, you,' [he says].  
Only so much exists as cups, stomas, and metres;  
with as much as exists, with so much he treats him.  
'I raise up, I release, let him lift up,' [he says].  
Just as one raises up, releases, and lifts up one condemned to death, even so does he now raise him up, release him, and lift him up.  
'The long-lived gods,' [saying this] he hands it to him.  
These gods indeed are long-lived;  
through them he bestows life on him.  
He drinks it.  
He drinks life itself.  
Whatever of that ghee he would, as it were, suck out from the gold—  
gold is brilliance; ghee is life;  
from brilliance he places life in himself.  
'Make him, O Agni, for life, for lustre,' [saying this] he ties on the gold.  
Agni indeed is all deities;  
gold is life;  
with all deities he binds it on for him.  
'Sharpen, O Varuṇa, sharp might,' [he says];  
he whets him.  
'Bestow protection on him, O Aditi, like a mother,' [he says].  
This [earth] is Aditi;  
he establishes him upon her.
