He mixes [them] on the black antelope skin;
he prepares an oblation.
He mixes [them] with honey.
Honey, indeed, is this sap of the waters and plants.
It rains from the waters and plants;
when it rains, he brings down rain from the waters and plants.
‘Gentle, barren cows,’ [he says].
These indeed are the names of the waters.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

When yonder sun turns around downward with his rays, then it rains.
These deities indeed rule the rain;
he approaches them with their own share;
pleased, they bring down rain for him.
Even if they were not going to rain, it rains indeed.
These are six oblations;
six indeed are the seasons;
from the seasons he sets rain moving forth.
In that there is a seventh,
by that he moves rain from the year.
