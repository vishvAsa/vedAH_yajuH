<details><summary>सर्वा अवतारिकाः</summary>

<details><summary>काठकब्राह्मण-विनियोगः</summary>

देवस्य त्वा सवितुः प्रसवेऽश्विनोर्बाहुभ्यां पूष्णो हस्ताभ्यामादद इत्याददीत प्रहर्षिणो मदिरस्य मदे मृषासो अस्त्वथ त्वा होष्यामीति मृषैवैनं करोति सर्वदेवत्यो वै सोमस्सर्वा (का॰सं॰ २७.१)
</details>

<details><summary>मानवश्रौतसूत्र-विनियोगः</summary>

देवस्य त्वा सवितुः प्रसव इत्यनुवाकशेषेण खनति (२१.१.२३)
</details>

<details><summary>वाराहश्रौतसूत्र-विनियोगः</summary>

देवस्य त्वेत्यभिषेकमादाय त्रया देवा इति यजमानमीक्षमाणो जुहोति (११.७.३३)
</details>
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)</summary>

देव᳓स्य त्वा सवितुः᳓ प्रसवे᳓ ऽश्वि᳓नोर्बाहु᳓भ्यां पूष्णो᳓ ह᳓स्ताभ्यामा᳓दद्ऋत᳓स्य त्वा देवहविः पा᳓शेन ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

At the impulse of god Savitṛ, with the arms of the Aśvins, with the hands of Pūṣan, I take you.  
With the bond of truth, divine oblation, I fasten you, welcome to such-and-such.  
Be bold!
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

From the waters, from the plants [I take] you.  
May your mother consent to you, may your father, may your brother from the same womb,  
may your companion from the same herd.  
Bring hither the gods for the sacrificer who seeks the gods!  
You are the drinker of the waters.  
An oblation sweet to the gods, full of ghee.  
May your breath unite with the wind,  
your limbs with the adorable ones,  
the lord of sacrifice with [his] prayer!
</details>
