विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

य᳓त्प᳓ञ्चकपालो यज्ञ᳓मेवा᳓ल᳓भते त᳓दाहुरष्टा᳓कपाल एव᳓ कार्यो᳙ न᳓ प᳓ञ्चकपाल इ᳓त्यष्टा᳓क्षरा गायत्री᳓ गायत्रो᳓ऽग्नि᳓र्गायत्रच्छन्दा᳓ आग्नेय᳓मेत᳓त्क्रियते य᳓त्पुनराधे᳓यं त᳓स्मादष्टा᳓कपालस्त᳓त्तन्न᳓ सू᳓र्क्ष्यं प᳓ञ्चकपाल एव᳓ कार्यो᳙ऽग्नि᳓र्वा᳓ उत्सी᳓दन्संवत्सर᳓म᳓नूत्सी᳓दति प᳓ञ्च वा᳓ ऋत᳓वस्संवत्सरः᳓ प᳓ञ्चथाद्वा᳓ अ᳓ध्यृतो᳓ष्ष᳓ष्ट ऋतु᳓र्बभूव स᳓मान᳓मेत᳓द्य᳓त्प᳓ञ्चथश्चर्तु᳓ष्ष᳓ष्टश्च य᳓त्प᳓ञ्चकपालस्संवत्सरा᳓देवै᳓नम᳓ध्यवरुन्द्धे᳓ प᳓ञ्च वा᳓ ऋत᳓वः प्रज᳓ननमृत᳓वो य᳓त्प᳓ञ्चकपालः प्रज᳓ननाय प्रज᳓ननं वा᳓ ऋत᳓वोऽग्निः᳓ प्रजनयिता᳓ य᳓त्प᳓ञ्चकपालः प्रज᳓ननादेवै᳓नं प्रजनयिता᳓ प्रजन᳓यत्यादित्या᳓ वा᳓ इत᳓स्स᳓र्वेणैव᳓ सहा᳓मुं᳓ लोक᳓मा᳓यँस्तेऽमुं᳓ लोकं᳓ गत्वा᳓ व्य᳙तृष्यँस्तेऽविदुरमु᳓तः प्रदाना᳓द्वा᳓ इहा᳓जगामे᳓ति त᳓ एतं᳓ पु᳓नरा᳓दधत ते᳓नार्ध्नुवन्नादित्या᳓ वा᳓ अस्मिं᳓ल्लोक ऋद्धा᳓ आदित्या᳓ अमु᳓ष्मिन्पश᳓वोऽस्मि᳓न्नृत᳓वोऽमु᳓ष्मिन्नुभ᳓योरेव᳓ लोक᳓योर्ऋध्नोति य᳓ एवं᳓ विद्वा᳓नेत᳓माधत्ते᳓ के᳓तस्सुके᳓तस्स᳓केतस्ते न आदित्या᳓ जुषाणा᳓ अस्य᳓ हवि᳓षो व्य᳓न्तु स्वा᳓हा ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

In that [the cake] is on five potsherds,
he takes hold of the sacrifice itself.
Regarding this they say:
“It should be prepared on eight potsherds only, not on five potsherds.
The gāyatrī has eight syllables, Agni belongs to the gāyatrī, [he has] the gāyatrī meter.
This is made belonging to Agni, which is the re-establishment.
Therefore [it should be] on eight potsherds.”
That is not to be heeded.
It should be prepared on five potsherds only.
Agni, indeed, when going up, goes up following the year.
Five, indeed, are the seasons, [namely] the year.
From the fifth season the sixth season arose.
It is the same, whether the fifth season or the sixth.
In that it is on five potsherds,
he wins him from the year itself.
Five, indeed, are the seasons; the seasons are procreation.
In that it is on five potsherds,
[it is] for procreation.
The seasons, indeed, are procreation; Agni is the procreator.
In that it is on five potsherds,
from procreation itself the procreator procreates him.
The Ādityas, indeed, went from here to yonder world with everything.
Having gone to yonder world, they became thirsty.
They recognized: “From offering from yonder have we come here.”
They re-established this [fire].
Thereby they prospered.
The Ādityas are prosperous in this world; the Ādityas [are prosperous] in yonder [world].
Domestic animals are in this [world]; the seasons are in yonder [world].
In both worlds he prospers, who knowing thus establishes this [fire].
“Keta, Suketa, Saketa, may you Ādityas, taking pleasure in this oblation, partake [of it], svāhā!
