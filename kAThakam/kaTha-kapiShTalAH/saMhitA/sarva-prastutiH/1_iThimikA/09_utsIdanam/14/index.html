सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

One who does not prosper with offspring or domestic animals should put on a garment of coarse wool, drink warm [water], and lie on the ground for twelve nights.

For this [man] is without a mate, therefore he does not prosper.

At the new moon he should touch the oblations that have been set down with the Pañcahotṛ.
By the Pañcahotṛ the gods went to heaven.
The new moon leads to heaven;
this is indeed the taking possession of the heavenly world.
