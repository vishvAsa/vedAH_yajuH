विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

प्रजा᳓स्सृष्ट्वा᳓ सो᳙ऽकामयत यज्ञ᳓ सृजेये᳓ति स᳓ एत᳓ सप्र᳓होतारमपश्यत्तं᳓ म᳓नसानूद्द्रु᳓त्याजुहोत् ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Having created creatures, he desired: “May I create the sacrifice!”
He saw this Saptahotṛ;
having pursued [it] with the mind, he offered [it];
by it he created the sacrifice.
The sacrifice, once created, went away from him.

Gemini 3.8 Flash

There is a libation-cup
for the firm holding of the created sacrifice.
Having created the sacrifice, he desired:
