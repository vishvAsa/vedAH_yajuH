विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

ना᳓नाग्नेयं᳓ क्रियत इ᳓त्याहुः᳓ क᳓स्मादा᳓ज्यभागौ यजन्ती᳓ति च᳓क्षुषी वा᳓ एते᳓ यज्ञ᳓स्य य᳓दा᳓ज्यभागौ य᳓दा᳓ज्यभागा अन्तरिया᳓च्च᳓क्षुषी यज्ञ᳓स्यान्तरिया᳓द᳓ग्न आ᳓यूँषि प᳓वस इ᳓त्येताँ᳓ सो᳓मस्या᳓ज्यभागस्य लोके᳓ कुर्याद्य᳓दाग्नेयं᳓ ते᳓नाग्नेयी᳓ य᳓त्पावमानं᳓ ते᳓न सौमी᳓ ना᳓नाग्नेयं᳓ क्रिय᳓ते ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

They say: “It is made not belonging to Agni alone. Why do they sacrifice the two butter-portions?”
The two butter-portions, indeed, are the eyes of the sacrifice.
If he were to omit the two butter-portions, he would omit the eyes of the sacrifice.
In the place of the butter-portion for Soma, he should use this [verse]: “O Agni, you purify our life-spans.”
In that it is addressed to Agni, it is connected with Agni by that.
In that it belongs to the purifying [Soma], it is connected with Soma by that.

न᳓ सो᳓ममन्तरे᳓त्यग्नि᳓र्मूर्धा᳓ दिवः᳓ ककु᳓दि᳓त्येताँ᳓ सो᳓मस्या᳓ज्यभागस्य लोके᳓ कुर्यात्प्रजा᳓कामस्य वा पशु᳓कामस्य वा य᳓न्मिथुना᳓ ते᳓न प्रजननवती य᳓द्रे᳓तस्वती ते᳓न सौमी᳓ ना᳓नाग्नेयं᳓ क्रिय᳓ते ।

Gemini 3.8 Flash

It is not made belonging to Agni alone, nor does he omit Soma.
He should use this [verse]: “Agni is the head, the summit of heaven,” in the place of the butter-portion for Soma, for one desiring offspring or desiring domestic animals.
In that [the verse contains] a pair, it has the nature of procreation by that.

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

वीरहा᳓ वा᳓ एष᳓ देवा᳓नां यो᳓ ऽग्नि᳓मुत्साद᳓यते शत᳓दायो वीरो᳓ य᳓देता᳓श्शता᳓क्षरा अक्षरपङ्क्तयो᳓ वीर᳓मेवै᳓त᳓द्देवा᳓नामवद᳓यते ऽन्य᳓स्यै वै᳓ प्रमाया आधे᳓यो ऽन्य᳓स्यै ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

It is not made belonging to Agni alone, nor does he omit Soma.
A slayer of a hero of the gods is he who lets the fire go out.
A hero has a hundred portions of wealth.
In that these are lines of a hundred syllables, he makes amends for the hero of the gods by this.
For one measure of life is the fire to be established; for another is the re-establishment.
