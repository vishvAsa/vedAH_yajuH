विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (1/23 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)

चि᳓त्तिस्स्रु᳓क् चि᳓त्तमा᳓ज्यं वा᳓ग्वे᳓दिरा᳓धीतं बर्हिः᳓ के᳓तो [[अग्नि᳓र्विज्ञातमग्नि᳓द्वाच᳓स्प᳓तिर्हो᳓ता|अग्नि᳓र्वि᳓ज्ञातमग्नि᳓द्वाच᳓स्प᳓तिर्हो᳓ता]] म᳓न उपवक्ता᳓ प्राणो᳓ हवि᳓स्मा᳓मध्व᳓र्युरि᳓न्द्रं गच्छ स्वा᳓हा ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Thought is the offering spoon;
intention is the clarified butter;
speech is the altar;
meditation is the barhis;
intention is the fire;
understanding is the Agnīdh;
the Lord of Speech is the Hotṛ;
the mind is the Upavaktṛ;
the breath is the oblation;
the Sāman is the Adhvaryu.
Go to Indra, svāhā!

पृथिवी᳓ हो᳓ता [[द्यौ᳓रध्व᳓र्युस्त्व᳓ष्टाग्नि᳓न्मित्र|द्यौ᳓रध्व᳓र्युस्त्व᳓ष्टाग्नि᳓न्मित्र᳓]] उपवक्ता᳓ वा᳓चस्पते वाचो᳓ वीर्ये᳙ण सं᳓भृततमेना᳓यक्षसे यज᳓पतये वा᳓र्यमा᳓ स्व᳙स्करो वाच᳓स्प᳓तिस्सो᳓मं पिबतु जज᳓नदि᳓न्द्रमिन्द्रिया᳓य स्वाहा ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

The earth is the Hotṛ;
the sky is the Adhvaryu;
Tvaṣṭṛ is the Agnīdh;
Mitra is the Upavaktṛ.
O Lord of Speech, with the strength of speech, most fully collected, you will sacrifice to this;
bring choice blessing to the lord of the sacrifice, svāhā!
May the Lord of Speech drink Soma;
he has generated Indra for indriyá power, svāhā!

सो᳓मस्सो᳓मस्य [[पुरोगाश्शुक्रश्शुक्र᳓स्य|पुरोगाश्शुक्र᳓श्शुक्र᳓स्य]] पुरोगा᳓श्श्राता᳓स्त इन्द्र सो᳓मा वा᳓तापे हवनश्रु᳓तस्स्वहाग्नि᳓र्हो᳓ताश्वि᳓नाध्वर्यू᳓ रुद्रो᳙ऽग्नि᳓द्बृ᳓हस्प᳓तिरुपवक्ता᳓ वा᳓चस्पते हृ᳓द्वि᳓धे नामन्वाच᳓स्प᳓तिस्सो᳓मपादा᳓स्मा᳓सु नृम्णं᳓ धात्स्वा᳓हा ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Soma is the forerunner of Soma;
the bright one is the forerunner of the bright one;
cooked for you, O Indra, are the Soma drops that listen to the invocation at the wind-drinking, svāhā!
Agni is the Hotṛ;
the two Aśvins are the two Adhvaryus;
Rudra is the Agnīdh;
Bṛhaspati is the Upavaktṛ.
O Lord of Speech, in the name of the knowledge of the heart,
the Lord of Speech has drunk the Soma;
may he place manly strength in us, svāhā!

Gemini 3.8 Flash

Soma is the forerunner of Soma;
the bright one is the forerunner of the bright one;
cooked for you, O Indra, are the Soma drops that listen to the invocation at the wind-drinking, svāhā!
