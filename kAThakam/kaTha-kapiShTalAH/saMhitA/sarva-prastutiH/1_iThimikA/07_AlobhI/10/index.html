अ᳓हर्वावासीन्न᳓ रा᳓त्री सा᳓ यमी᳓ भ्रा᳓तरं मृतं᳓ ना᳓मृष्यत तां᳓ य᳓द᳓पृच्छन्यमि क᳓र्हि ते भ्रा᳓तामृते᳓त्य᳓द्ये᳓त्ये᳓वा᳓ब्रवीत्ते देवा᳓ अब्रुवन्नन्त᳓र्दधामेदं᳓ रा᳓त्रीं करवामे᳓ति ते᳓ रा᳓त्रीमकुर्वँस्ते रा᳓त्र्यां भू᳓तायां पशू᳓न्ना᳓पश्यन्सा᳓वेन्न वै᳓ पश्यन्ती᳓ति सा᳓ न᳓ व्यौ᳙च्छदरे᳓क्ल᳓स्य᳓त्पशु᳓षु ता᳓न्देवा᳓ इच्छ᳓न्तः प᳓ल्या᳓यन्त ताँ᳓श्छ᳓न्दोभिर᳓न्वपश्यँस्त᳓स्माच्छ᳓न्दोभिर्न᳓क्तमग्नि᳓रुपस्थे᳓यः पशू᳓ना᳓मनु᳓क्शात्यै ना᳓स्मा᳓त्पश᳓वस्तिरो᳓भवन्ति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

There was only day, not night.
Yamī could not endure her dead brother.
When they asked her: ‘O Yamī, when did your brother die?’—
she said just: ‘Today.’
The gods said: ‘Let us veil this; let us make night.’
They made night.
When night had come to be, they could not see the cattle.
She [the night] knew:
‘They do not see.’
She did not dawn.
She desired wealth in the cattle.
The gods, searching for them, walked around;
they saw them by means of the meters.
Therefore, by night Agni is to be approached with the meters
for the comfort of the cattle.
Cattle do not vanish from him who knows thus.
She knew:
‘They have perceived.’
She dawned.
The gods said: ‘Our wealth has stayed home.’

Gemini 3.8 Flash

That is the New Moon night [Amāvasyā].
Wealth stays at home for him;
he finds the wealth of another, another does not find his wealth, who knowing thus attends upon the fire.
The gods drove away the Rakṣases from the day.
They entered the night.
The gods did not dare to scatter it.
They said to Indra:
‘You are indeed the mightiest among us; go through this [night].’
