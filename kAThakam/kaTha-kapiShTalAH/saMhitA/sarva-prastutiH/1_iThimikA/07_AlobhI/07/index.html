सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

With ‘You are waters’—
for these are waters.
With ‘You are greatness’—
for these are greatness.
With ‘You are vigor’—
for these are vigor.
With ‘You are the increase of wealth’—
for these are the increase of wealth.
With ‘O wealthy ones, take pleasure’—
the cattle indeed are the wealthy ones;
the cattle are that which is between the two fires;
he has caused them to take pleasure in their own pen.
With ‘In this womb’—
for this is their womb.
With ‘In this pen’—
for this is their pen.

ना᳓पक्रामन्ति य᳓ एवं᳓ विद्वा᳓नग्नि᳓मुपति᳓ष्ठत एति वा᳓ एषो᳓ऽस्मा᳓ल्लोका᳓द्यो᳓ऽग्नि᳓मुपति᳓ष्ठते᳓ऽम्भस्स्थे᳓ति पश᳓वो वा᳓ अ᳓म्भः पश᳓वोऽन्त᳓रिक्षं पशु᳓ष्वेव᳓ प्र᳓तितिष्ठति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

With ‘This is your kinsman; do not depart from here’—
the cattle approach nearer and nearer to him;
they do not depart, for him who knowing thus attends Agni.
He who attends Agni indeed goes away from this world.
With ‘You are waters’—
cattle indeed are waters, cattle are the atmosphere;
he establishes himself firmly in cattle.
