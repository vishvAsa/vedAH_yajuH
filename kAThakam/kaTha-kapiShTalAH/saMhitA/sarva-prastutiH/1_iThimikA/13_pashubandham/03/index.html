Gemini 3.8 Flash

Indra indeed is the nobility; the Maruts are the clan.

It is one with speckled thighs;

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

With that [animal] free from debt, ransomed, fit for sacrifice, and released, he indeed prospers.
One who would hope for kingship should seize a brown bull, with a prominent chest, dedicated to Soma.
Soma indeed is king of the gods;
Soma is the deity of him who hopes for kingship.
He runs to his own deity with his share;
he leads him to kingship.
In that it has a prominent chest,
that is the form of virility.
He should seize a red bull, with a prominent chest, dedicated to Indra the thunderbolt-wielder, for a nobleman who is either practicing sorcery or wishing to thrive.
Noble indeed is the nobleman by deity;
the thunderbolt is his virility.
He places the thunderbolt in him;
