विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

आ᳓ग्निवारुणी᳓म᳓नड्वाहीमा᳓लभेतानाज्ञातय᳓क्ष्मगृहीतो ऽनाज्ञातय᳓क्ष्मो वा᳓ एतां᳓ वित्तो᳓ या᳓ स्त्री᳓ स᳓त्यड्वाही᳓ त᳓स्मादेनामनाज्ञातय᳓क्ष्मगृहीत आल᳓भेताग्नि᳓स्स᳓र्वा देव᳓ता य᳓देवा᳓स्य देव᳓ताभिर्नि᳓षितं त᳓दग्नि᳓ना मुञ्चति य᳓द्व᳓रुणगृहीतं त᳓द्व᳓रुणेन य᳓द्वहि᳓नी ते᳓नाग्नेयी᳓ य᳓त्स्त्री᳓ सती᳓ दान्ता᳓ ते᳓न वारुणी᳓ त᳓स्मादा᳓ग्निवारुणी᳓ भ᳓वति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

One seized by an unknown pulmonary disease should seize a draught-cow dedicated to Agni and Varuṇa.
An unknown pulmonary disease, verily, has seized her who, being female, is a draught-cow.
Therefore, one seized by an unknown pulmonary disease should seize her.
Agni is all deities.
Whatever of him has been bound by the deities, that he releases through Agni;
whatever is seized by Varuṇa, that [he releases] through Varuṇa.
In that she is a draught-animal,
thereby she belongs to Agni.
In that she is tamed, being female,
thereby she belongs to Varuṇa.
Therefore, she belongs to Agni and Varuṇa.

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

त्वाष्ट्र᳓मँ᳓सेपा᳓दमा᳓लभेत पशु᳓कामो मिथुनो᳓ वा᳓ एष᳓ योँ ऽसेपा᳓त्त्व᳓ष्टा मिथुन᳓स्य प्रजनयिता᳓ त᳓मेव᳓ भागधे᳓येनोपधा᳓वति सो᳓ ऽस्मा᳓ एत᳓स्मान्मिथुना᳓त्पशू᳓न्प्र᳓जनयति वारुणँ᳓ श्यामशितिकण्ठ᳓मा᳓लभेत यं᳓ व्ये᳓मानं य᳓क्ष्मी गृह्णीया᳓त् ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

One desiring cattle should seize [an animal] with a foot [like] a shoulder dedicated to Tvaṣṭṛ.
He who has a foot [like] a shoulder, verily, is a pair.
Tvaṣṭṛ is the generator of the pair.
He approaches him indeed with his share.
He generates cattle for him from this pair.
One whom consumption seizes while he is weaving should seize [an animal] dark and white-throated dedicated to Varuṇa.
