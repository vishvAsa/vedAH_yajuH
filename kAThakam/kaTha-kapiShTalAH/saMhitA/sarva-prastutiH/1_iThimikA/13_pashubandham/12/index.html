ता᳓मग्नि᳓रग्रसत तां᳓ प्रजा᳓पतिरेते᳓न पुरोडा᳓शेन नि᳓रक्रीणाद्य᳓दाग्नेयः᳓ पुरोडा᳓शो भ᳓वति नि᳓ष्क्रीत्यै य᳓द्वायु᳓र्व्य᳓वात्त᳓स्माद्वायव्या᳙ य᳓द् द्या᳓वापृथिवी᳓ ग᳓र्भम᳓दधातां त᳓स्माद् द्यावापृथिव्ये᳙मे᳓ वै᳓ सहा᳓स्तां᳓ ते य᳓था वेणू᳓ सन्धाव्ये᳓ते ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Agni swallowed her.
Prajāpati bought her back with this cake.
In that there is a cake for Agni,
[it is] for buying back.
Because Vāyu blew between them, therefore [she is] sacred to Vāyu.
Because Heaven and Earth conceived an embryo, therefore [she is] sacred to Heaven and Earth.
These two were together indeed.
As two bamboos rub against each other, so they rubbed together.

Gemini 3.8 Flash

Through Agni he eats food;
through Soma he obtains [it].
The Āruṇis know this.
“Come hither, O Vāyu, drinker of the pure, unto us” [saying this],
from him who produced her, he obtains her.
“For intention thee!” [saying this],
for intention indeed is she seized.
“For desire thee!” [saying this],
for desire indeed is she seized.
“For success thee!” [saying this],

“Kikkiṭā to thee! Thy voice to Sarasvatī, svāhā!” [saying this],
