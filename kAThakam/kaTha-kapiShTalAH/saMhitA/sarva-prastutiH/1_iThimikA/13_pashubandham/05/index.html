सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

One who is excluded, or who has been excluded for a long time, should seize a milch cow with calves of various ages dedicated to Heaven and Earth.
Verily, he is excluded from Heaven and Earth who is excluded, or excluded for a long time.
In that it is dedicated to Heaven and Earth,
he gives him a share in Heaven and Earth.
Verily, he is like one [belonging to] various ages who is excluded, or excluded for a long time;
various-aged is his deity.
In that it is [a cow] with calves of various ages,
his own beast through that is of this [deity] alone.
He should seize a calf dedicated to Vāyu on the morrow.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

‘May a well-proportioned son be born to me,’ should seize a gelded [beast] dedicated to Tvaṣṭṛ.
Tvaṣṭṛ, verily, is the fashioner of forms;
he runs to him with his own share;
he fashions forms for him.
One who has a rival should seize [a beast] with a black blaze dedicated to the Aśvins.
The gods, verily, drove the Asuras away from the day;
what was their own, what was their property, what was to be known, with that they entered into the night.
The gods saw this [beast] with a black blaze dedicated to the Aśvins;
they seized it;
by it they followed them into the night.
The Aśvins, verily, are Heaven and Earth;
having hemmed them in from below with Heaven and Earth, and from beyond with the sun, they drove them from the night.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

he indeed prospers.
In battle, one who desires, ‘May this army not conquer,’ should seize a wide-horned bull dedicated to Indra.
Indra, verily, is the leader of battle;
Indra is the bringer of victory;
he runs to him with his own share;
he leads these away.
In that it is wide-horned,
that is the form of dispersing.
At the junction of two tribes, one who desires, ‘May I fall upon both tribes,’ should seize a wide-horned bull for Indra the Destroyer.
He falls upon both tribes;
they go about slaying one another.
In that it is wide-horned,
he causes the two to go to complete destruction.
