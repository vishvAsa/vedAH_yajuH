य᳓ल्लला᳓मः पुर᳓स्तान्मन्यो᳓स्समृड्व्यै᳓ देवा᳓श्च वा᳓ अ᳓सुराश्च सं᳓यत्ता आसँ᳓स्ते न᳓ व्य᳓जयन्त ते ऽब्रुवन्ब्र᳓ह्मणा नो मेनी᳓ वि᳓जयेतामि᳓ति त᳓ ऋ᳓ृषभौ सम᳓वासृजञ्छ्वेत्रेयो᳓ ऽरुण᳓स्तूपरो᳓ देवा᳓नामा᳓सीच्छ्येने᳓य᳓श्श्ये᳓तो᳓ ऽयश्शृ᳓ङ्गो᳓ ऽसुराणां तौ᳓ सम᳓हताम् ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

In that it has a blaze,
[it is] for the prosperity of spirit at the front.
The gods and the Asuras, verily, were engaged in battle.
They did not conquer [each other].
They said: ‘Let our two missiles conquer through the sacred power.’
They released two bulls together.
The reddish hornless one belonging to Śvitrā belonged to the gods; the white one with iron horns belonging to Śyenī belonged to the Asuras.
The two fought together.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

The one belonging to Śvitrā broke the other right in the middle.
The speech that was defeated fell downward;
the one that conquered [rose] upward.
Therefore, he whose speech is downward is unfit for the priesthood,
for that color belongs to the Asuras.
Therefore, he whose speech is upward is fit for the priesthood,
for he is among the gods, as it were.
Practising sorcery, he should seize a reddish, hornless [animal] dedicated to Bṛhaspati.
Bṛhaspati, verily, is sacred power;
he employs against him with sacred power itself.
By this, verily, the gods laid low the Asuras.
In that it is hornless,
[it is] for laying low.
Indra, verily, slew Vṛtra.
Slain, he encircled him with seven coils.
From his head went up the Vaidehīs;
they went eastward.
Therefore, they [are] in front. He thought of the last bull, of the Videha breed, that was rising after them: ‘Now I should seize this; through it may I be freed from you here.’
He seized it;
he was not freed through it.
He seized a he-goat dedicated to Agni [and] a bull dedicated to Indra.
Having burned away those coils of evil by Agni, he then bestowed Indra’s power and manly vigour in himself through that [offering] dedicated to Indra.
Thereupon, verily, he prospered.
One who desires to prosper, who considers himself to be seized by evil, as it were, should seize a he-goat dedicated to Agni [and] a bull dedicated to Indra.
