सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

The metres, verily, envied the sacrifice.

The blood, verily, which flowed first,
that Bṛhaspati received;
she became the reddish one;
therefore she belongs to Bṛhaspati.
That which flowed second, Mitra and Varuṇa received;
