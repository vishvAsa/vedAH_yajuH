Through Agni he eats food;  
through Soma he obtains [it].  
The Āruṇis know this.

"For intention thee!" [saying this],

"Kikkiṭā to thee! Thy hearing to Heaven and Earth, svāhā!" [saying this],

"Kikkiṭā to thee! Thy voice to Sarasvatī, svāhā!" [saying this],
