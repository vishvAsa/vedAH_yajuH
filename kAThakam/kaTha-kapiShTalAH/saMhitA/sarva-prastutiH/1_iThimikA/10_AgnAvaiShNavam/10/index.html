Gemini 3.8 Flash

Each succeeding one is greater;
for each succeeding one of these worlds is greater:
to Indra the king first, then to Indra the self-ruler, then to Indra the overlord.
Indra indeed became all these, not otherwise;
all these indeed becomes he who, knowing thus, sacrifices with this [offering].
There are three preparatory verses; they are the offering verses.
Having recited the first of them, he sacrifices with the middle one;
having recited the middle one, he sacrifices with the highest;
having recited the highest, he sacrifices again with the first,
for the continuity of these worlds,
for recognition.
All he makes preparatory verses, all offering verses.
