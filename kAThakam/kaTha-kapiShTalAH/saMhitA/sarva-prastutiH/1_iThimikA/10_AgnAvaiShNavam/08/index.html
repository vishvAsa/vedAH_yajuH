Gemini 3.8 Flash

offer a cake on eleven potsherds to Indra Manasvat Tviṣīmat.
Mind is fortune and brilliance.
He places mind, fortune, and brilliance in him.
He should not utter it for a year prior to the mind.
By a year what is unobtained is obtained.
Having obtained the year, he secures [it].
