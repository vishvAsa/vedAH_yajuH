<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

This is Indra's healing body, which is Aṃhomuc.  
Indra is the releaser from distress.  
He approaches him with his share;  
he releases him from distress.  
One who through himself or through his house should enter into straits should offer a cake on eleven potsherds to Indra Aṃhomuc.  
He is seized by distress who through himself or through his house enters into straits.  
Indra is the releaser from distress.  
He approaches him with his share;  
he releases him from distress.  
One who desires, "May the great sacrifice come to me," should offer a cake on eleven potsherds to Indra Arkavat Aśvamedhavat.  
This [earth] is the arka, yonder is the arka, yonder is the Aśvamedha; the sacrifice belongs to Indra.  
His sacrifice goes to the end, who, desiring a sacrifice, does not have the sacrifice come to him.  
These are Indra's final bodies, which are Arkavatī.  
Those which are Indra's final bodies, through them he lays hold of the sacrifice.  
The sacrifice comes to him.  
One who desires kinsmen should offer a cake on eleven potsherds to Indra Anvṛju.  
Sensory power is primacy.  
By sensory power he makes his kinsmen straightforward.  
One who has a rival should offer a cake on eleven potsherds to Indra Prababhra.  
The thunderbolt is the reddish-brown one.  
With the reddish-brown one Indra hurled the thunderbolt at the Asuras.  
He hurls the thunderbolt at his rival;  
he lays him low.  
In battle, one should offer a cake on eight potsherds to Agni and a cake on eleven potsherds to Indra Vimṛdh.  
The one to Agni is for prosperity.  
When Indra had slain Vṛtra, hostilities attached themselves to him.  
He saw this Vaimṛdha [offering];  
by it he drove away those hostilities.  
He seeks to slay Vṛtra who wishes to win a battle.  
That which is Indra's expelling body, he approaches that with its share;  
by it he drives away hostilities;  
he slays Vṛtra;  
he wins the battle.  
One whom fear should approach from all sides should offer a cake on eight potsherds to Agni and a cake on eleven potsherds to Indra Vimṛdh.  
The one to Agni is for prosperity.  
When Indra had slain Vṛtra, hostilities shook him violently all around.  
He saw this Vaimṛdha [offering];  
by it he drove away those hostilities.  
Hostilities shake him violently all around, whom fear approaches from all sides.  
That which is Indra's expelling body, he approaches that with its share;  
by it he drives away hostilities from himself.
</details>
