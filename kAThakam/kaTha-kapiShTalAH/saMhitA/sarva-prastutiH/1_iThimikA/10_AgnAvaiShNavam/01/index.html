सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

One who is practicing sorcery or one against whom sorcery is being practiced should offer a cake on eleven potsherds to Agni and Viṣṇu, a mess of cooked rice to Sarasvatī, and a mess of cooked rice to Bṛhaspati.
Agni is all the deities, Viṣṇu is the sacrifice, Sarasvatī is speech, Bṛhaspati is the sacred word.
By Agni with the deities he counteracts his deities; by Viṣṇu with the sacrifice, his sacrifice; by Sarasvatī with speech, his speech; by Bṛhaspati with the sacred word, his sacred word.
