अग्न᳓ये य᳓विष्ठायाष्टा᳓कपालं नि᳓र्वपेदभिच᳓रत्वाभिच᳓र्यमाणो वा देवा᳓श्च वा᳓ अ᳓सुराश्च व्यभ्य᳙चरन्त ते᳓ऽसुरा देवे᳓भ्यो वि᳓सृष्टीर्व्य᳙सृजँस्ते᳓ देवा᳓ [[अविदुर्सृवि᳓ष्टीर्वै᳓|अ᳓विदुर्सृविष्टीर्वै]] नो᳓ऽसुरा व्य᳙स्राक्षुरि᳓ति ते᳙ऽग्न᳓ये य᳓विष्ठायाष्टा᳓कपालं नि᳓रवपँस्ते᳓न वै᳓ ते᳓ ता᳓ वि᳓सृष्टीरयावयन्तात्म᳓नो᳓ऽधि वि᳓सृष्टित᳓स्मै वि᳓सृजन्ति य᳓मभिच᳓रन्ति य᳓दग्न᳓ये य᳓विष्ठाय नि᳓र्वपति ते᳓नैव᳓ तां᳓ वि᳓सृष्टिं यावयत ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

One should offer a cake on eight potsherds to Agni Yaviṣṭha when practicing sorcery or when being practiced upon with sorcery.
The gods and the Asuras were contending with each other by sorcery.
Those Asuras cast discharges against the gods.
Those gods knew: “The Asuras have indeed cast discharges against us.”
They offered a cake on eight potsherds to Agni Yaviṣṭha.
By that, indeed, they warded off those discharges from themselves.
A discharge they cast against him whom they practice sorcery upon.
When he offers to Agni Yaviṣṭha,
by that indeed he wards off that

Gemini 3.8 Flash

In that [he offers] to Pratīkavat,
whatever rival is behind him, him he drives back with that.
He pushes forward the superior rival; he steps beyond the equal;
a worse [rival] does not reach him who, knowing thus, sacrifices with this [offering].
The gods and the Asuras were engaged in conflict.
The Gāyatrī stood between them,
having encompassed power, virility, and proper food; or rather, the year stood between them.
Twenty-four are the syllables of the Gāyatrī;
twenty-four are the half-months of the year.
They knew: “Whichever of the two she will turn to, they will become this [all].”
They called to her.
“O Dābhī!” the Asuras called;
“O Viśvakarman!” the gods [called].
She turned to neither of the two.
Those gods saw this Yajus-formula:
“You are power; you are force; you are strength; you are radiance; you are the domain of the gods; you are name; you are the universe; you are full lifespan; you are all; you are complete lifespan; you are overcoming!”
She turned to the gods.
Thereupon the gods prospered, the Asuras were defeated.
One who has a rival or one who is competing should offer [a cake] on eight potsherds to Agni.
Having placed it, he should touch it with these Yajus-formulas.
The year is power and virility.
He appropriates the power and virility of his rival;
he, deprived of virility, is defeated.
