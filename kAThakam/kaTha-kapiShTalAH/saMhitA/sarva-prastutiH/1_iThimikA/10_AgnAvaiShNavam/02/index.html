देवा᳓ वै᳓ सत्र᳓मासत य᳓शस्कामा अग्नि᳓स्सो᳓म इ᳓न्द्रस्तेऽब्रुवन्य᳓न्नो य᳓श [[ऋृच्छा᳓त्तन्नस्सहे᳓ति|ऋ᳓ृच्छात्तन्नस्सहेति]] ते᳓षाँ सो᳓मं य᳓श आर्छत् ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

The gods sat at a sacrificial session desiring fame: Agni, Soma, Indra.
They said: “Whatever fame comes to us, that shall be common to us.”
Fame came to Soma among them.

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

त᳓स्मात्स᳓ यशस्वी᳓ स᳓ गिरि᳓मगच्छत्त᳓स्मात्स᳓ गिरौ᳓ त᳓मग्नि᳓रन्वा᳓गच्छत्ता᳓ अग्नीषो᳓मौ स᳓मभवताम᳓र्थेन्द्रो᳓ऽधृतश्शिथिल᳓ इवामन्यत स्रोऽन्वा᳓गच्छत्स्रो᳙ऽग्नौ᳓ चैव᳓ सो᳓मे चानाथत त᳓मेत᳓ये᳓ष्ट्यायाजयतामग्नीषोमीयेणै᳓कादश-कपालेन ते᳓जो वा᳓ अग्नि᳓रिन्द्रिय᳓ सो᳓मस्ते᳓ज एवा᳓स्मिन्नग्नि᳓राग्नेये᳓ना᳓दधातीन्द्रिय᳓ सो᳓मस्सौम्ये᳓न त᳓तो वै सो᳙ ऽभवदेते᳓न यजेत ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Therefore he is famous.
He went to the mountain.
Therefore he is on the mountain.
Agni followed after him.
Those two became Agni and Soma.
Then Indra, unsupported, felt as if slack.
He went after [them].
He took refuge with both Agni and Soma.
They two sacrificed for him with this rite, with a cake on eleven potsherds to Agni and Soma.
Fiery energy indeed is Agni, sensory power is Soma.
Agni placed fiery energy in him through what pertains to Agni, Soma [placed] sensory power through what pertains to Soma.
Then indeed he became [prosperous].

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

In that it is on eight potsherds,
by that it pertains to Agni.
In that it is made of millet,
by that it pertains to Soma.
By that he does not fall away from either.
The invitatory and offering verses belong to Agni and Soma; the two accompanying offering verses belong to Soma and Agni.
Fiery energy indeed is Agni, sensory power is Soma.
Having seized spiritual luster from both sides by fiery energy and sensory power, he places it in himself.
