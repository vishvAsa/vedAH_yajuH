अग्न᳓ये वैश्वानरा᳓य द्वा᳓दशकपालं नि᳓र्वपेद्ग्रा᳓मकामो भू᳓तिकामो ब्रह्मवर्चस᳓कामस्त᳓स्य ह᳓रितँ हि᳓रण्यं म᳓ध्ये कुर्याद्रजत᳓मुप᳓रिष्टात्संवत्सरो᳓ वा᳓ अग्नि᳓र्वैश्वानरो᳓ वीर्यँ᳙ संवत्सर᳓ आत्मा᳓ ह᳓रितँ हि᳓रण्यं वीर्य᳙स्यैवै᳓नं मध्यतो᳓ दधाति भू᳓त्यै भवत्येव᳓ य᳓द्रजतं᳓ त᳓त्स᳓ह अस्म᳓ना᳓पो᳓हति श᳓मलगृहीतो वा᳓ एष᳓ यो᳓ऽलं ब्रह्मवर्चसा᳓य सन्न᳓ ब्रह्मवर्चसी᳓ भ᳓वति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

One who desires a village, one who desires prosperity, one who desires spiritual luster should offer a cake on twelve potsherds to Agni Vaiśvānara.
In the middle of it he should put yellow gold, [and] silver on top.
Agni Vaiśvānara is the year, virility is the year, the self is yellow gold.
He places him right in the middle of virility,
for prosperity;
he does prosper.
That which is silver, that he removes together with the ashes.
Seized by defilement indeed is he who, being fit for spiritual luster, is not possessed of spiritual luster.
