<details><summary>काठकब्राह्मण-विनियोगः</summary>

देवस्य त्वा सवितुः प्रसवेऽश्विनोर्बाहुभ्यां पूष्णो हस्ताभ्यामादद इत्याददीत प्रहर्षिणो मदिरस्य मदे मृषासो अस्त्वथ त्वा होष्यामीति मृषैवैनं करोति सर्वदेवत्यो वै सोमस्सर्वा (का॰सं॰ २७.१)
</details>

<details><summary>मानवश्रौतसूत्र-विनियोगः</summary>

</details>

</details>
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Here I cut off the neck of him who, whether our equal or unequal, acts as our enemy.  
You are Taptāyanī;  
you are Vittāyanī.  
May she protect me in distress;  
may she protect [me] in trembling.  
O Agni, O Aṅgiras, who are on this earth, come with the name of Āyu!  
With that name of yours which is unassailed, unassailable, with that I place you.  
O Agni, O Aṅgiras, who are on the second, who are on the third earth, come with the name of Āyu!
