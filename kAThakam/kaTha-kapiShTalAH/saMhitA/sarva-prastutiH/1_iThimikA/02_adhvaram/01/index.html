विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

आ᳓र्द्रदानवस्स्थ᳓ जीव᳓दानवस्स्थो᳓न्दतीरिह᳓ मा᳓वताप उन्दन्तु जीव᳓से दीर्घायुत्वा᳓य व᳓चसे ओ᳓षधे त्रायस्वैनँ स्व᳓धिते मैनँ हिँसीर्दे᳓वायुरिमा᳓त्प्र᳓वपे स्वस्त्यु᳓त्तराँ अशी᳓य ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

You are givers of moisture;
you are givers of life.
Moistening [waters], help me here!
May the waters moisten [my head] for life,
for long life, for splendor!
O plant, protect him!
O axe, do not harm him!
Seeking the gods, I shave these [hairs];
may I attain subsequent well-being.

May the waters of the desert be auspicious to us, may those of the marshy lands be auspicious to us;
may the waters of the ocean be auspicious to us, and may those of the wells be auspicious to us!
