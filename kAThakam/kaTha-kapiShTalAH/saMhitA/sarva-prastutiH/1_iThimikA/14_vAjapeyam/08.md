<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

They bring a bowl of honey for the Brahman priest;  
he buys off from the Brahman priest with that.  
The king's son eats the kakubh cake.  
Vigor, indeed, is the kakubh cake, vigor is the king's son;  
he places vigor in vigor.  
'Wife, let us two ascend to heaven,' [he says].  
About to ascend to heaven, he converses with his wife.  
This is the wife's taking hold of the sacrifice from behind.  
Moreover, he establishes a pair at the head of the sacrifice,  
for procreation.  
'May life-span succeed through the sacrifice,' [he says].  
So great, indeed, is man;  
becoming whole, he goes to the heavenly world.  
The headpiece of the sacrificial post is of wheat.  
Wheat belongs to Prajāpati, equal to half of the plants.  
Together with food he goes to the heavenly world.  
'To the prize, svāhā!
</details>

with food they pelt him.

With the verses of the impulse of the prize, he offers of every food,  
for the securing of food;  
with seven [verses].  
Seven, indeed, are the meters;  
with the meters he secures food.  
The meters are speech;  
with speech he secures food.
