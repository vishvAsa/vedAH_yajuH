<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (1/62 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)</summary>

[[रथन्तँ᳓रं|र᳓थन्तँ᳓रं]] सा᳓म भवत्या᳓शीय उ᳓ज्जित्या इयं᳓ वै᳓ रथन्तर᳓मस्या᳓सेवा᳓ध्य—



भि᳓षिच्यते वि᳓योनिर्वै᳓ वाजपे᳓यो᳓ ऽनिरुक्तस्स᳓न्प्रा᳓जापत्यो᳓ ऽनिरुक्तसामा ते᳓न वि᳓योनिर्य᳓द᳓निरुक्ताः प्रातस्सवा᳓स्ते᳓न स᳓योनिर᳓निरुक्ताः प्रातस्सवा᳓ अ᳓निरुक्तः प्रजा᳓पतिः प्रजा᳓पतिमेवा᳓प्नोति वा᳓जवतीभिर्मध्यं᳓दिने स्तुवन्त्य᳓न्नंवै᳓ वा᳓जो ऽन्ना᳓द्यस्या᳓वरुद्ध्यै चित्र᳓वतीभिरा᳓र्भवे प᳓वमाने स्वर्ग᳓स्य लोक᳓स्य स᳓मष्ट्या आष्ट्रादँष्ट्र᳓मुत्तम᳓मुक्था᳓नां भवत्यु᳓त्तरे एव᳓ स्तोत्रे᳓ अभिसं᳓तनोति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

The sāman is the Rathantara,  
for the winning of blessings.  
This [earth], indeed, is the Rathantara;  
upon it he is anointed.  
The Vājapeya, indeed, is out of womb [in origin], being enunciated, belonging to Prajāpati,  
having an enunciated sāman;  
thereby it is out of womb.  
In that the morning pressings are unenunciated,  
thereby it has a womb.  
The morning pressings are unenunciated;  
inenunciated is Prajāpati;  
he obtains Prajāpati.  
They praise with verses containing the word 'prize' (vājavatī) at midday.  
Food, indeed, is the prize,  
for the securing of food.  
[They praise] with verses containing the word 'bright' (citravatī) at the Ārbhava-pavamāna,  
for the attainment of the heavenly world.  
The Āṣṭrādam̐ṣṭra is the last of the Ukthas;  
he connects it with the subsequent two chants.
</details>
