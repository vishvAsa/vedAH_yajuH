What they took away first, that became a black sheep;
what [they took away] second, that was a reddish-brown [sheep];
what [they took away] third, that was a white [sheep];
what they took away from the bone, that became a barren sheep.
Now at that time this [earth] was bald and hairless.
The gods seized that [sheep] for Aditi, for desire;
by it they made hair grow on her;
then this [earth] received hair.
He should seize this very [sheep] thus for Aditi, for desire;
whatever desire he desires, that he wins.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

That it is for [Vāyu] Niyutvat,
he makes a support,
for non-wiping-out.
For desires, he should seize a white he-goat for Vāyu.
He who desires, desires subtly;
Vāyu blows subtly along the gods;
he runs up to him with his own share;
he blows along his desires.
