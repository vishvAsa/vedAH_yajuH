सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

The gods ran a race when the plants had ripened.
Indra knew: “Agni will surely win.”
He said: “Whichever of the two of us wins, that [gain] shall belong to both of us.”
Agni won first;
Indra won after that.
Therefore an offering to Agni-Indra should be made.
For Agni won first,
and Indra won after that.
The Āgrāyaṇa [offering] is indeed for conquering.
It is on twelve potsherds,

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

for this arises from the year.
At that time, there was only a single cluster of rice, one of barley, one of beans, one of sesame.
The Viśve Devas said: “Let there be a share for us; we will expand this.”
They made this share for them.
Then indeed this expanded into abundance.
It belongs to the Viśve Devas;
he expands [it] indeed with this.
It is in milk,

सर्वाष् टीकाः

for he is seed, unprocreated,
for procreation.
Indeed, it is for everything that he is anointed, that [offering] which is the Āgrāyaṇa.
It takes place at the Rājasūya;
he secures food itself;
that becomes sweetened and sacrificed for him.
He who has established the sacred fires is indeed brought near to the gods;
they eat his food.
If, without sacrificing with the Āgrāyaṇa, he should eat of the new [grain], he would eat the share allotted to the gods; he would incur affliction.
Therefore one must not eat of the new [grain] without having sacrificed with the Āgrāyaṇa.
By this, indeed, the gods reached the forefront;
that is the Āgrāyaṇa-nature of the Āgrāyaṇa.
He who, knowing thus, sacrifices with the Āgrāyaṇa reaches the forefront among his equals.
