सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

The gods and the asuras were in conflict.
Those asuras had more mind;
the gods were as it were without mind.
The gods saw this [means of] seizing.
With it they seized the minds of the asuras;
then they, deprived of mind, were defeated.
He seizes the mind of his rival;
he, deprived of mind, is defeated.
One who desires cattle should sacrifice with this.
With this the gods seized the cattle of the asuras;
then they, deprived of cattle, were defeated.
He seizes the cattle of his rival;
he, deprived of cattle, is defeated.
One who desires relatives should sacrifice with this.
This is a seizing of the mind; relatives are as it were minded.
As many relatives as there are, of them he seizes the minds;
seized in mind, they do not depart from him.
