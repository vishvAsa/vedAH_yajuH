सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Viśvarūpa, son of Tvaṣṭṛ, had three heads; he was the nephew of the Asuras.
With one head he drank soma;
with one he consumed food;
with one he drank liquor.
Indra thought:
“Surely this one will become this [all]!”
He grappled with him;
he brought about a joint death with him.
