विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

अ᳓पनुत्ता श᳓ण्डाम᳓र्कौ सह᳓ ते᳓न यं᳓ द्विष्म᳓स्तुथो᳙ ऽसि ज᳓नधाया देवा᳓स्त्वा शुक्रपाः᳓ प्र᳓णयन्तु तुथो᳙ ऽसि ज᳓नधाया देवा᳓स्त्वा मन्थिपाः᳓ प्र᳓णयन्त्वा᳓युस्सं᳓धत्तं प्राणँ सं᳓धत्तं च᳓क्षुस्सं᳓धत्तँ श्रो᳓त्रँ सं᳓धत्तं वा᳓चँ सं᳓धत्तम᳓नाधृष्टासि जि᳓न्वेथाँ स्वपत्या᳓ म᳓ध्वा वी᳙तँ सुवी᳓राः प्रजाः᳓ प्रजन᳓न्प᳓रीहि सुप्रजाः᳓ प्रजाः᳓ प्रजन᳓यन्प᳓रीहि ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Driven away are Śaṇḍa and Marka together with the one whom we hate!
You are Tutha, nourishing the people!
May the gods who drink the bright [cup] lead you forward!
You are Tutha, nourishing the people!
May the gods who drink the stirred [cup] lead you forward!
Unite life!
Unite breath!
Unite sight!
Unite hearing!
Unite speech!
You are unassailable;
enliven [us] with good offspring; enjoy with sweetness!
Go around, generating offspring rich in heroes!
Go around, generating offspring rich in good progeny!

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

इ᳓न्द्रेण मन्यु᳓ना युजा᳓ सासह्या᳓म पृतन्यतः᳓।
घ्न᳓न्तो वृत्रा᳓ण्यप्रति᳓ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः ...{Loading}...
Whitney
Translation
  1. With Indra, with fury may we overcome them that play the foe, smiting
    Vritras irresistibly.
Notes

The verse is found also in TS. iii. 5. 3² and MS. i. 3. 12. TS. reads
sayújas for manyúnā, and sāsahyā́ma for abhí syāma; syāma for
vayám, áva bādhe for abhi syāma, and ghnatā́ for ghnántas. Most
of the saṁhitā-mss. give ṣyāma (our W.O., and two fifths of SPP’s
authorities, sy-), and both printed texts read it; but the Prāt. (ii.
107) expressly requires syāma, and that accordingly should be the
accepted text. ⌊In c, rather, ‘smiting adversaries’?⌋

Griffith

With Indra’s and with Manyu’s aid may we subdue our enemies, resistlessly destroying foes.

Gemini 3.8 Flash

With Indra as ally, with wrath, may we overcome those who fight [us],
smiting the obstacles resistlessly!

Gemini 3.8 Flash

Uniting with heaven, with earth;
bright with bright radiance;
stirred with stirred radiance;
bright are you, with bright radiance;
stirred are you, with stirred radiance!
