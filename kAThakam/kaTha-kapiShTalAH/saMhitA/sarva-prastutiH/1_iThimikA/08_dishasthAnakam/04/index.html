अ᳓ङ्गिरसां त्वा देवा᳓नां व्रते᳓ना᳓दध᳓ इ᳓ति ब्राह्मणे᳓नाधे᳓यो ये᳓ वै᳓ देवा᳓नाम᳓ङ्गिरसस्ते ब्राह्मण᳓स्य प्र᳓त्येनसोऽग्नि᳓र्वायु᳓र्वा᳓ग्बृ᳓हस्प᳓तिस्त᳓तो ब᳓न्धुरस्य᳓ यज्ञ᳓स्त᳓त आ᳓यनो य᳓तो ब᳓न्धुरेवा᳓स्य᳓ यज्ञो᳓ य᳓त आ᳓यनस्त᳓त एनं प्रतीच्छ᳓ती᳓न्द्रस्य त्वा मरु᳓त्वतो व्रते᳓ना᳓दध᳓ इ᳓ति राजन्ये᳙नाधे᳓यो ये᳓ वै᳓ देवा᳓नां रा᳓जानस्ते राजन्य᳙स्य प्र᳓त्येनस इ᳓न्द्रो व᳓रुणो धाता᳓ त्व᳓ष्टा त᳓तो ब᳓न्धुरस्य᳓ यज्ञ᳓स्त᳓त आ᳓यनो य᳓तो ब᳓न्धुरेवा᳓स्य᳓ यज्ञो᳓ य᳓त आ᳓यनस्त᳓त एनं प्रतीच्छ᳓ति म᳓नोस्त्वा ग्राम᳓ण्यो व्रते᳓ना᳓दध᳓ इ᳓ति वै᳓श्येनाधे᳓यो मरु᳓तो वै᳓ देवा᳓नां वि᳓शस्ते वै᳓श्यस्य प्र᳓त्येनसस्त᳓तो ब᳓न्धुरस्य᳓ यज्ञ᳓स्त᳓त आ᳓यनो य᳓तो ब᳓न्धुरेवा᳓स्य᳓ यज्ञो᳓ य᳓त आ᳓यनस्त᳓त एनं प्रतीच्छ᳓ती᳓डा वै᳓ म᳓ना आसीत्सा᳓सुरानग्नि᳓माद᳓धानानगच्छत्त आहवनी᳓यम᳓ग्न आ᳓दधता᳓थ गा᳓र्हपत्यम᳓थौ᳓दनपचनं ता᳓नब्रवीदाप्त्वा᳓ श्रि᳓यं प्रत्य᳓वारुक्षन्पु᳓ण्या भविष्यन्ति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

“By the vow of the Aṅgiras deities I establish you”—[thus the fire] is to be established by a Brahmin.
Those among the gods who are Aṅgirases are the corresponding deities of the Brahmin: Agni, Vāyu, Vāc, Bṛhaspati.
From there his sacrifice has its kinship, from there its origin.
From where his sacrifice has its kinship, from where its origin,
from there he receives it.
“By the vow of Indra attended by the Maruts I establish you”—[thus] it is to be established by a Rājanya.
Those among the gods who are kings are the corresponding deities of the Rājanya: Indra, Varuṇa, Dhātṛ, Tvaṣṭṛ.
From there his sacrifice has its kinship, from there its origin.
From where his sacrifice has its kinship, from where its origin,
from there he receives it.
“By the vow of Manu the village headman I establish you”—[thus] it is to be established by a Vaiśya.
The Maruts are indeed the commoners of the gods; they are the corresponding deities of the Vaiśya.
From there his sacrifice has its kinship, from there its origin.
From where his sacrifice has its kinship, from where its origin,
from there he receives it.
Iḍā indeed belonged to Manu.
She came upon the Asuras establishing their fire.
They established the Āhavanīya first,
then the Gārhapatya,
then the Odanapacana.
She said to them: “Having obtained prosperity, they stepped down; they will be auspicious, but they will perish.”
