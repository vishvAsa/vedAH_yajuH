अग्नि᳓र्वा᳓ इमं᳓ लोकं᳓ नो᳓पाकामयत य᳓दस्मि᳓न्नामं᳓ माँसं᳓ प᳓चन्ति य᳓त्पु᳓रुषं द᳓हन्ति य᳓त्स्ते᳓यं प᳓चन्ति त᳓दभी᳓मं᳓ लोकं᳓ नो᳓पाकामयत स᳓ य᳓दिमं᳓ लोक᳓मुपा᳓वर्तत या᳓ अस्य᳓ यज्ञि᳓यास्तन्व᳙ आसँ᳓स्ता᳓भिरु᳓दक्रामत्ता᳓ एताः᳓ प᳓वमाना पावका᳓ शु᳓चिस्त᳓स्य या᳓ प᳓वमाना तनू᳓रा᳓सीत्पशूँ᳓स्त᳓या प्रा᳓विशत् ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Agni indeed did not desire this world.
Because they cook raw meat in it, because they burn a man, because they cook stolen goods, on account of that he did not desire this world.
When he turned toward this world, he departed with those bodies of his that were sacrificial.
These are they: the purifying (pavamāna), the cleansing (pāvaka), the bright (śuci).
The purifying body that he had, with that he entered cattle;

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

या᳓ पावका᳓पस्त᳓या या᳓ शु᳓चिरमु᳓मादित्यं᳓ त᳓या प्राणो᳓ वै᳓ प᳓वमानः प्राणे᳓न पश᳓वो यता᳓ य᳓दग्न᳓ये प᳓वमानाय पशू᳓नेवा᳓स्मै᳓ ते᳓न य᳓च्छत्येषा᳓ वा᳓ अस्य᳓ सा᳓ तनू᳓र्य᳓या पशू᳓न्प्रा᳓विशद्य᳓दिदं᳓ घृते᳓ हुते᳓ प्र᳓तीवार्चि᳓रुज्ज्व᳓लत्येषा᳓ वा᳓ अस्य᳓ सा᳓ तनू᳓र्य᳓या᳓पः प्रा᳓विशद्य᳓दिद᳓मप्सु᳓ प᳓रीव द᳓दृशे ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

the cleansing, with that the waters;
the bright, with that yonder sun.
The out-breath indeed is the purifying [fire];
by the out-breath cattle are held fast.
In that [there is an offering] to Agni Pavamāna,
he thereby grants cattle to him.
This indeed is that body of his with which he entered cattle, in that this flame flares up as if confronting [one] when ghee is offered.
This indeed is that body of his with which he entered the waters, in that this is seen as if surrounded in the waters,
