अष्टासु᳓ प्रक्रमे᳓षु ब्राह्मणे᳓नाधे᳓योऽष्टा᳓क्षरा गायत्री᳓ गायत्रच्छन्दा᳓ ब्राह्मण᳓स्स्व᳓स्यैवै᳓नं छ᳓न्दसः प्रात्येनस्य᳙ आ᳓धत्ते ।

Gemini 3.8 Flash

Just as a father invigorates his son, even so his own meter invigorates him.
“May I obtain the unlimited,” thus indeed he establishes the fire;
it is to be established without limit,
for the attainment of the unlimited.
As far as he might think with his eye, at that distance he should establish it;
the eye is truth;
he establishes him in truth.
