सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

he reaches the dear domain of cattle.
This earth was trembling;
she was afraid,
thinking: “Agni will burn me overmuch.”
Agni was afraid,
thinking: “She will extinguish my heat.”
For she was moist, as it were.
The gods made her firm with gravel; they placed brilliance in Agni.
When there is gravel,
he makes this [earth] firm;
he places brilliance in Agni.
This [earth] was touched by Vṛtra;
but the ants know where the living, sacrificial part of her is.
When there is a mound of an anthill,
he establishes him in the living, sacrificial part of her.
