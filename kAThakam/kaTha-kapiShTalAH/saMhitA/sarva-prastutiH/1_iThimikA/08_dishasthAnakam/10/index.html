The sacrifice indeed did not incline toward the gods.
They toiled [saying]:
“The sacrifice does not incline toward us.”
To Agni and Soma first the sacrifice inclined among them.
They knew:
“To Agni and Soma first the sacrifice inclined for us;
let us seek the inclination of the sacrifice from them two.”
They said:
“To you two first the sacrifice inclined for us;
let the sacrifice incline toward us upon you two.”
The two said:
“Let us choose a boon:
let them sacrifice to us two first as the two portions of clarified butter.”
Therefore they sacrifice to Agni and Soma first as the two portions of clarified butter,
for it was chosen as a boon by them two.
In that he pours out [an offering] to Agni and Soma,
[it is] for the inclination of the sacrifice.
For from there first the sacrifice inclined toward the gods.
If he were to pour out for another deity, he would cut off the sacrifice from its deity.
Agni and Soma are a pair;
Soma is the placer of seed, Agni the progenitor.
Agni indeed is all this;
the waters, plants, and trees—that very thing is Soma.
These two indeed of the gods are nearest to us,
for these two we see.
