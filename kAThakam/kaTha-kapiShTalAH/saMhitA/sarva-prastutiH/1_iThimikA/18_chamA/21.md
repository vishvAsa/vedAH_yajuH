<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

May the Hotṛ sacrifice to Bṛhaspati from the goat offering: he tasted today the fat drawn out from the middle, before hatreds, before human grasp; may he eat now of the pasture-pasturing, grass-prominent, happily flowing, hundred-Rudrian, Agniṣvātta, fat-clad [portions] cut off from the flank, from the haunch, from the liver, from the rump, from limb after limb; may Bṛhaspati do so; may he enjoy the oblation.
</details>

Hotṛ, sacrifice.

Hotṛ, sacrifice.  
May the Hotṛ sacrifice to Sarasvatī from the sheep offering: may she taste, may she eat; may Sarasvatī do so; may she enjoy the oblation.

May the Hotṛ sacrifice to Indra from the ram offering: may he taste, may he eat; may Indra do so; may he enjoy the oblation.
