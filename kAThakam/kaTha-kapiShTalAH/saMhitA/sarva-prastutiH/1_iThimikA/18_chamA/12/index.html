सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Both the ṛc and the sāman,
and the chant and the yajus,
and the Bṛhat and the Rathantara.
To the heavenly light, O gods, have we gone!
The offspring of Prajāpati have we become!
Immortal have we become!
Veṭ! Svāhā!
