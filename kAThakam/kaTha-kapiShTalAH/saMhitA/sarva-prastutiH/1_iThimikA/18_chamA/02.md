च᳓क्षुषः पिता᳓ म᳓नसा हि᳓ धी᳓रो घृत᳓मेने अजनन्न᳓म्नमाने।  
य᳓दि᳓द् द्या᳓वापृथिवी᳓ [[अ᳓प्रथेतामा᳓दि᳓द᳓न्ता|अ᳓प्रथेतामा᳓दिद᳓न्ता]] अददृहन्त पू᳓र्वे।  
कि᳓ स्विद्व᳓नं क᳓ उ स᳓ वृक्ष᳓ आसीद्य᳓तो द्या᳓वापृथिवी᳓ निष्टतक्षुः᳓।  
म᳓नीषिणो म᳓नसा पृच्छ᳓ते᳓दु त᳓द्य᳓दध्य᳓तिष्ठद्भु᳓वनानि धार᳓यन् ॥

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Father of the eye, wise in mind, he begot the two [worlds] bowing [to each other] as ghee;  
when indeed heaven and earth spread out, then the ancient boundaries were made firm.  
What indeed was the forest, what was that tree, from which they carved out heaven and earth?  
Inquire into that with your mind, O thoughtful ones, whereon he stood, sustaining the worlds.
