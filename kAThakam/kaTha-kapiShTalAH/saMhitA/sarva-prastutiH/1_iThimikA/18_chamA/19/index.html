Gemini 3.8 Flash

The juhū is indeed the head of the sacrifice;
he takes hold of the head of the sacrifice itself.

that is the juhū-nature of the juhū.
Agni is indeed the Virāj;
he thus calls Agni, the Virāj.
The meters departed from the gods, desiring a portion at the sacrifice, saying: ‘We will not carry the offering for you who have no shares.’

Because he offers with four ladlefuls,
