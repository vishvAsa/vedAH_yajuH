सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Cattle did not lick them.
For them the gods sought an expiation.
Prajāpati said: ‘I choose a wish: I will sweeten them for you.’
He chose this kindling stick.
Therefore it is one,
for Prajāpati is one. He brought them near with fire;
fire sweetened them.
When this [kindling stick] is laid on, the plants taste sweet to the Agnihotra-offerer.
His food becomes sweetened who knows thus.
Bahu makes it two,
[saying:] ‘Let me offer both oblations when it is kindled.’

य᳓ एवं᳓ वे᳓द बहु᳓र्द्वी᳓करो᳓त्युभे᳓ आ᳓हुती स᳓मिद्धे जुहवानी᳓त्ये᳓कैव᳓ कार्या᳙ सकृ᳓द्ध्ये᳙व᳓ स᳓र्वस्मै यज्ञा᳓य समिध्य᳓त आ᳓युर्मे यच्छ व᳓र्चो मे यच्छ प्रजां᳓ मे यच्छे᳓ति पुर᳓ उ᳓पसादयेत्सृ᳓ष्टिर्वा᳓ एत᳓द्य᳓दग्निहोत्रं᳓ यन्त्रो᳓पसाद᳓येत्प᳓राचीः प्रजा᳓ इयुर्य᳓दुपसाद᳓यति त᳓स्मादिदं᳓ प्रजा᳓ यता᳓स्त᳓स्मादु᳓पतिष्ठन्तेऽग्नौ᳓ ज्यो᳓तिर्ज्यो᳓तिर᳓ग्ना इ᳓ति साय᳓मग्निहोत्रं᳓ जुहुयाद्गर्भि᳓ण्या वाचा᳓ ग᳓र्भं दधाति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Only one is to be made,
for once only is it kindled for the whole sacrifice.
With ‘Grant me life, grant me luster, grant me offspring,’ he should set [it] down in front.
Creation, truly, is this which is the Agnihotra.
If he should not set it down, offspring would go away.
Because he sets it down,
therefore creatures are restrained here, therefore they wait upon [it].
With ‘Light in Agni, light in Agni,’ he should offer the Agnihotra in the evening.
