अग्निहोत्रे᳓ वै᳓ जा᳓यम्प᳓ती व्यभिचरेते᳓ य᳓त्प्राची᳓नमुद्वास᳓येत्प᳓तिः पू᳓र्वः प्र᳓मीयेत ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

In the Agnihotra, truly, husband and wife act deceitfully [towards each other].
If he should take [the vessel] off toward the east, the husband would die first.

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

य᳓त्प्रतीचीन᳓मुद्वास᳓येज्जाया᳓ पू᳓र्वा प्र᳓मीयेत सम्प्रत्यु᳙दीची᳓नमु᳓द्वासयेत्स᳓मेवं᳓ जी᳓र्यतः पूर्ण᳓म᳓ग्न उ᳓न्नयेद᳓थ स᳓म्मितम᳓थ स᳓म्मितम᳓थ स᳓म्मिततममुत्तम᳓मनुज्येष्ठ᳓मेवा᳓स्य᳓ पुत्रा᳓ ऋध्नुवन्ति स᳓म्मितम᳓ग्न उ᳓न्नयेद᳓थ भू᳓यो᳓ ऽथ भू᳓यो᳓ ऽथ पूर्ण᳓मुत्तमं᳓ भू᳓यो भू᳓य एवा᳓न्ना᳓द्यमभ्यु᳓त्क्रामति क᳓निष्ठस्त्व᳙स्य᳓ पुत्रा᳓णाम᳓र्धुको भवति स᳓र्वान्स᳓मावदु᳓न्नयेद्यः᳓ काम᳓येत स᳓र्वे से᳓ पुत्रा᳓स्स᳓मावदृध्नुयुरि᳓ति स᳓र्वेषां वै᳓ पिता᳓ पुत्रा᳓णामृ᳓द्धिं कामयते स᳓र्व एव᳓ स᳓मावदृध्नुवन्ति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

If he should take it off toward the west, the wife would die first.
He should take it off directly toward the north.
Thus, they grow old together. He should draw out full at first,
then measured, then measured, then most measured the last.
In order of seniority his sons prosper.
He should draw out measured at first,
then more, then more, then full, more as the last.
More and more food does he step up to.
His youngest son, however, becomes prosperous.
He who desires, ‘May all my sons prosper equally,’ should draw out all equally.
A father desires prosperity for all his sons;
all indeed prosper equally.
None surpasses the other.

He indeed broods after this: when, having placed it over [the fire] behind, he offers in front.

From this Gārhapatya, truly, yonder first Agni was created.
