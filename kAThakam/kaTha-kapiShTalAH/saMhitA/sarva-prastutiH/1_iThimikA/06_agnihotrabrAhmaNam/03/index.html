सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

As it was being heated, he placed it in the cow.
That is this milk.
Therefore, milk freshly milked heats a vessel that is not of tin or iron.
When one offers the Agnihotra with milk,
it is that Sun he offers.
For he indeed is the Agnihotra.
Rice grains are the milk of the ox.
Yonder Sun is the ox.
When one offers the Agnihotra with barley gruel,
it is that Sun he offers.
For he indeed is the Agnihotra.
One [vessel] is turned on a potter’s wheel; another is not turned on a potter’s wheel.
What is turned on a wheel belongs to the Asuras; what is not turned on a wheel is a vessel for the gods.
