<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Only one is to be made,  
for once only is it kindled for the whole sacrifice.  
With 'Grant me life, grant me luster, grant me offspring,' he should set [it] down in front.  
Creation, truly, is this which is the Agnihotra.  
If he should not set it down, offspring would go away.  
Because he sets it down,  
therefore creatures are restrained here, therefore they wait upon [it].  
With 'Light in Agni, light in Agni,' he should offer the Agnihotra in the evening.
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Agni is the one who causes to set;  
Sūrya is the generator.  
Just as the sun has set, he should offer the Agnihotra in the evening,  
about the rising of the sun in the morning.  
Agni indeed, having caused Sūrya to set, places the embryo for the night.  
With pregnant, paired speech he brings it forth in the morning.  
This [sun] being born, creatures are born after him.  
He is propagated with offspring, with cattle, for whom the Agnihotra is offered thus.  
This, truly, is the post of the Agnihotra.  
If he should offer passing over that which is the first oblation,  
he avoids him.
