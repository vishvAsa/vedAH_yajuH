<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

To me the waters set forth what is full of sweetness; for me the sun brought that light;  
the All-gods born of ascetic heat attend upon me; the divine Savitṛ established wide space for me.  
I propped up the earth and heaven; I generated the seven seasons together;  
I encompassed all speech, I who won Indra and Agni as two comrades.  
I encompassed heaven and earth; I [encompassed] all plants, the seven streams;  
to me the divine clans bowed down; I became the formidable one of a hundred offerings.  
Born for wealth, grown for overpowering force, the foremost supporter of dominion among dominions, the nourisher,  
great by greatness in the firm support, awake in dominion and in the realm!  
Nine times may king Indra, may Savitṛ sprinkle you;  
may Mitra, Vāyu, Bṛhaspati, and Dhātṛ bestow dominion upon you!  
May Aditi stimulate you, O king, for great dominion, for great primacy, for great kingship, for great rulership over the people, for great overlordship of the whole world!  
Agni, the years [… you].  
At the impulse of the divine Savitṛ, with the arms of the Aśvins, with the hands of Pūṣan, with the voice of Sarasvatī, with the harness of the driver, this [man] N.N., descendant of N.N., son of N.N., I sprinkle with the imperial sovereignty of Agni, I sprinkle with the imperial sovereignty of Indra!  
May my splendor, O Agni, prevail in conflicting invocations! Kindling you, may we nourish ourselves!  
May the four quarters bow down to me; with you as overseer, may we conquer the armies!  
May all the gods be with me in the conflicting invocation: the Maruts accompanied by Indra, Viṣṇu, and Agni!  
May the intermediate space be a wide realm for me; for me the wind purifies itself in this desire!
</details>

##### Translation
5. On me let the gods bestow (*ā-yaj*) property; with me be blessing  
(*āśís*), with me divine invocation; may the divine invokers (*hótar*)  
win that for us; may we be unharmed with our self (*tanū́*), rich in  
heroes.

##### Notes
RV. has for **c** *dāivyā́ hótāro vanuṣanta pū́rve;* and TS. the same,  
except *hótārā* and *vaniṣanta*. One or two of our mss. (Bp.H.) read  
*saniṣam* in **c**. Ppp. begins **a** with *mahyam*, and has *mama* for  
*mayi* both times in **b**.
</details>
