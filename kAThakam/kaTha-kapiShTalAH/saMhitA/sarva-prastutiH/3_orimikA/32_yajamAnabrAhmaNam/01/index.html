Indra-power is indeed Indra.
He places Indra-power in himself.

Gemini 3.8 Flash

“Through sacrifice to the gods, to Mahendra, may I attain victory and abundance,” he says;
he attains victory and abundance.
“Through sacrifice to the gods, to Indra Vimṛdh, may I be without rivals,” [he says].
He becomes without rivals.
“Through sacrifice to the gods, to Indra possessed of Indra-power, may I be possessed of cattle,” [he says].
Cattle are indeed Indra-power.
He places cattle in himself.
“Through sacrifice to the gods, to Sarasvatī, may I foster speech and food,” he says.
Sarasvatī is speech.
By speech itself he secures food.
“Through sacrifice to the gods, to Pūṣan, may I be possessed of prosperity, possessed of cattle,” he says.
Pūṣan is prosperity.
He secures prosperity.
“Through sacrifice to the gods, to Aditi, may I attain a firm foundation,” [he says].
Aditi is this [earth].
Upon her he firmly establishes himself.
