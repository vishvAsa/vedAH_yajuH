विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

इष्टो᳓ यज्ञो᳓ भृ᳓गुभिर्द्रविणोदा᳓ य᳓ति᳓भिरि᳓ति यज्ञ᳓स्य वा᳓ एष᳓ दो᳓हो यज्ञ᳓मेवै᳓त᳓द्दुहे᳓ त᳓स्य यज्ञ᳓स्येष्ट᳓स्य स्विष्ट᳓स्य द्र᳓विणमा᳓गच्छत्त्वि᳓त्याशि᳓षमेवा᳓शास्ते᳓ ऽङ्गिरसो मे ऽस्य᳓ यज्ञ᳓स्य प्रातरनुवाकै᳓रहौषुरि᳓त्याशि᳓षामेवै᳓ष᳓ दो᳓हो व᳓सुर्यज्ञो᳓ व᳓सुमान्यज्ञ᳓ इ᳓ति व᳓सुमन्तमेव᳓ यज्ञं᳓ कुरुते ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

“Sacrificed is the sacrifice by the Bhṛgus, giving wealth through the Yatis,” [he says].
This is indeed the milking of the sacrifice.
He milks the sacrifice itself.
“May the wealth of that sacrificed, well-sacrificed sacrifice come to me,” [he says].
He prays for a blessing.
“The Aṅgirases have sacrificed for me in this sacrifice with morning-recitations,” [he says].
This is indeed the milking of blessings.
“A Vasu is the sacrifice, possessing Vasus is the sacrifice,” [he says].
He makes the sacrifice possess Vasus.
