देव᳓तानां वा᳓ एत᳓दाय᳓तनं य᳓दाहवनी᳓यो य᳓दन्तरा᳓ग्नी᳓ त᳓त्पशूनां᳓ मनुष्या᳙णां गा᳓र्हपत्यो ऽन्वाहार्यप᳓चनः पितॄणां᳓ पू᳓र्वं चाग्नि᳓म᳓परं च परिस्त᳓रि᳓त᳓वा आह ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

The Āhavanīya is indeed the dwelling of the deities;
what is between the fires is that of the cattle;
the Gārhapatya is that of men;
the Anvāhāryapacana is that of the ancestors.
He says to strew both the eastern and the western fire.

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

मनुष्या᳙णामि᳓न्न्वे᳓ न᳓वावसानं प्रियं᳓ न᳓वावसानमेवा᳓क्रन्मेध्यत्वा᳓य स᳓र्वा ह वा᳓ अस्य᳓ यक्ष्य᳓माणस्य देव᳓ता यज्ञ᳓मा᳓गच्छन्ति य᳓ एवं᳓ वे᳓दाग्ने᳓स्तनू᳓र᳓सि वाचो᳓ विस᳓र्जनमि᳓ति पु᳓रोडाशीयाना᳓वपति देव᳓तानां वा᳓ एष᳓ ग्र᳓हो देव᳓ता एवै᳓त᳓दग्रहीद᳓य ह स्माहारुण᳓ औ᳓पवेशिर᳓हुतासु वा᳓ अह᳓मा᳓हुतिषु देव᳓ता हव्यं᳓ गमयामि सँ᳓स्थितेन यज्ञे᳓न सँस्थां᳓ ग᳓च्छानी᳓ति य᳓ एवं᳓ वे᳓दा᳓हुतास्ववास्या᳓हुतिषु देव᳓ता हव्यं᳓ ग᳓च्छति सँ᳓स्थितेन यज्ञे᳓न सँस्थां᳓ गच्छत्यपः᳓ प्रणे᳓ष्यत्वा᳓चं यच्छेदौलूखले᳓न वै᳓ दृष᳓दा देवा᳓ यज्ञमुखा᳓द्र᳓क्षाँस्य᳓पाघ्नन्म᳓नसा वै᳓ प्रजा᳓पतिर्यज्ञ᳓मतनुतौलूखल᳓स्यो᳓द्व᳓दितोरध्वर्यु᳓श्च य᳓जमानश्च वा᳓चं यच्छेतां प्रजा᳓पतिरेव᳓ भूत्वा᳓ म᳓नसा यज्ञं᳓ त᳓त्त्वा ते᳓न स᳓र्वाणि सह᳓ यज्ञायुधा᳓नि प्रहृ᳓त्यानि᳓ मानुषं᳓ त᳓त्क्रियते ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

A new settlement is indeed dear to men;
they have made a new settlement
for sacrificial fitness.
All the deities indeed come to the sacrifice of him about to sacrifice who knows thus.
With: “Thou art the body of Agni, the release of speech,” he pours in the [grains] for the sacrificial cake.
This is indeed the taking of the deities;
he has thus taken the deities.
Now Aruṇa Aupaveśi used to say:
“When the oblations are not yet offered, I cause the oblation to reach the deity,
so that by a completed sacrifice I may reach completion.”
For him who knows thus, when the oblations are not yet offered, the oblation goes to the deity;
by a completed sacrifice he reaches completion.
When about to lead forth the waters, he should restrain his speech.
By means of the mortar and the lower crushing-stone, the gods smote away the rakṣases from the face of the sacrifice.
With the mind Prajāpati extended the sacrifice.
While the mortar and [pestle] sound, both the Adhvaryu and the sacrificer should restrain their speech.
Having become Prajāpati himself, having extended the sacrifice with the mind, thereby all the sacrificial utensils are to be thrown forward together; that is done in the human way.

Gemini 3.8 Flash

Not one by one;
that belongs to the ancestors.
Two by two they are to be thrown forward.
He fasts in the manner of the yājyā and the anuvākyā.
He thereby wins both kinds of cattle, both the domestic and the wild.
In that he does not eat of what is domestic,

In that he eats of what is wild,
thereby [he wins] wild ones.
Moreover, what is wild is vigor;
he wins vigor itself.
He should not eat of kidney beans;
kidney beans are unfit for sacrifice.
