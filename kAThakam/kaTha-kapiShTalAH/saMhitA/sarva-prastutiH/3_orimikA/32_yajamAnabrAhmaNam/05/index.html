पूर्ण᳓मसि पूर्णं᳓ मे भूया इ᳓ति ये᳓भ्य एव᳓ का᳓मेभ्य ऊन᳓स्ता᳓नापूर᳓यते ऽग्नि᳓र्वै᳓ भू᳓याँसं य᳓जमानं प्र᳓दहति स᳓न्ततँ स्रावयत्यपो᳓ ऽतिमुच्य᳓माना अ᳓नु य᳓जमानो᳓ ऽतिमुच्यते᳓ ऽथो अद्भि᳓रेवै᳓त᳓दग्ने᳓रात्मा᳓नमन्त᳓र्धत्ते᳓ ऽप्रदाहाय स᳓र्वं वा᳓ एत᳓स्य यज्ञो᳓ मृष्ट᳓श्शुचा᳓भि᳓षीदति य᳓द्गृहा᳓ य᳓त्पश᳓वः प्रा᳓च्या दिशा᳓ देवा᳓ ऋत्वि᳓जो मार्ज᳓यन्तामि᳓त्येता᳓ वै᳓ यज्ञ᳓स्य मृ᳓ष्टय एता᳓श्शा᳓न्तयो मृष्टो᳓ ऽस्य᳓ यज्ञो᳓ भवति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

“Thou art full; may there be fullness for me!”, [he says].
From whatever desires he is deficient, those he fills up.
Agni indeed burns the sacrificer who is excessive.
He causes [water] to flow continuously.
Following the waters being released, the sacrificer is released.
Moreover, by means of the waters he thus shields himself from Agni,
so as not to be burned.
Truly the sacrifice of this one, wiping itself, settles down upon everything with sorrow—what is his homes, what is his cattle.
“May the divine officiating priests wipe [themselves] with the eastern quarter!”, [he says].
These are indeed the wipings of the sacrifice, these are its pacifications.
Wiped is his sacrifice;

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

pacified, Paśupati does not harm his cattle.
“Earth did Viṣṇu stride out through the gāyatrī metre”, [he says].
With Viṣṇu as their head, the gods through the metres drove out the Asuras from these worlds.
With Viṣṇu as his head, the sacrificer thus through the metres drives out his rival from these worlds.
“We have gone to heaven!”, [he says].
To heaven

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

He departs from this world who strides the strides,
for by means of the metres he ascends away [from it].
He is liable to perish.
With: “O Agni, lord of the house!”, he attends upon the Gārhapatya [fire].
In this very world he firmly establishes himself.
