सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Prajāpati desired: “May I become [great].”

Thereby he became [great].
Therefore they say: “It is the sacrifice of one who desires to become [great].”
The months, uninitiated, caused him, initiated, to sacrifice.
Therefore uninitiated [priests] cause an initiated one to sacrifice.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Austerity is the glue of the sacrifice.
Just as there is glue of a chariot, so is austerity of the sacrifice.
He who is devoid of austerity, his sacrifice is unjoined.
He should be full of austerity;
he just joins the sacrifice together.
The half-months indeed were [there].
They desired: “May we become months.”
They undertook the Twelve-day rite.
Having made a thirteenth as Brahman, looking at him they concluded [the rite].
Therefore he, without an abode, lives off the others.
Therefore there should not be a thirteenth Brahman of the Twelve-day rite.
Twelve should be initiated, having the year as their abode.
Twelve months are the year;
having the year as their abode, they prosper.
Thirteen should be initiated, having the year as their abode.
Thirteen months are the year;
having the year as their abode, they prosper.
Fifteen should be initiated, having the half-months as their abode.
Fifteen are the nights of a half-month;
having the half-months as their abode, they prosper.
Seventeen should be initiated, having Prajāpati as their abode.
Prajāpati is seventeenfold;
having Prajāpati as their abode, they prosper.
Twenty-one should be initiated, if desiring radiance.
Yonder sun is the twenty-first;
he is the bestower of radiance;
he grants radiance to them.
Twenty-four should be initiated, if desiring the preeminence in sacred learning.
The Gāyatrī has twenty-four syllables;
the Gāyatrī is brilliance, preeminence in sacred learning;
they secure just brilliance, preeminence in sacred learning.
Twenty-seven should be initiated, having the triṇava [stoma] as their abode.
These worlds are threefold-nine;
having the triṇava [stoma] as their abode, they prosper.
Thirty should be initiated, having the month as their abode.
Thirty are the nights of the month;
having the month as their abode, they prosper.
Thirty-three should be initiated, having the deities as their abode.
Thirty-three are the deities;
having the deities as their abode, they prosper.
Forty-four should be initiated, if desiring vigor, desiring virile power.
The Triṣṭubh has forty-four syllables;
the Triṣṭubh is vigor, virile power;
they secure just vigor, virile power.
Forty-eight should be initiated, if desiring cattle.
The Jagatī has forty-eight syllables;
cattle belong to the Jagatī;
they secure just cattle.
An unmeasured number should be initiated,
for the securing of the unmeasured.
Unmeasured is Prajāpati;
they obtain Prajāpati himself.
The seasons indeed were [there], and the months were their followers.
Those months desired: “May we reach the seasons.”
They undertook the Twelve-day rite.
Having made a thirteenth as Brahman, having suppressed the seasons by means of the cool season, they rose up toward spring, their sap.
He who, knowing thus, is initiated in the cool season,
