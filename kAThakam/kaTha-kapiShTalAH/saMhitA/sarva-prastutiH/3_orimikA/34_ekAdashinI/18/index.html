सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

He who knows the stretches and counter-placements of the stomabhāgas may be the Brahman priest of a concurrent Soma sacrifice. When the Bahiṣpavamāna is being chanted with the Prastāva, he should say: “Praise for food! Praise for strength! Praise at the impulse of the divine Savitṛ, impelled by Bṛhaspati!”
These, indeed, are the stretches and counter-placements of the stomabhāgas.
He who knows thus may be the Brahman priest of a concurrent Soma sacrifice.
In whichever direction the two Soma sacrifices are concluded, towards that direction he should look closely.
“Here do I pierce that one, of such lineage, son of such a mother, with grief,” so he should say of one of the priests.
With grief alone he pierces them.
He who becomes a Brahman priest without knowing the stretches and counter-placements of the stomabhāgas is deprived of his power on all sides.
