<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)</summary>

ज्यो᳓तिष्टोमं प्रथम᳓मु᳓पयन्त्यस्मिँ᳓स्ते᳓न लोके᳓ प्र᳓तितिष्ठन्ति गो᳓ष्टोमं द्विती᳓यमु᳓पयन्त्यन्त᳓रिक्षे ते᳓न प्र᳓तितिष्ठन्त्या᳓युष्टोममुत्तम᳓मु᳓पयन्त्यस्मिँ᳓स्ते᳓न लोके᳓ प्र᳓तितिष्ठन्त्ययं᳓ वै᳓ लोको᳓ ज्यो᳓तिरन्त᳓रिक्षं गौर᳓सा᳓ एवा᳓युर्य᳓देता᳓न्स्तो᳓मानुपय᳓न्त्येष्वे᳙व᳓ त᳓ल्लोके᳓षु सत्त्रि᳓णः प्रतिति᳓ष्ठन्तो यन्ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

They undertake the Jyotiṣṭoma first;  
by it they become established in this world.  
They undertake the Goṣṭoma second;  
by it they become established in the intermediate space.  
They undertake the Āyuṣṭoma last;  
by it they become established in that world.  
This world is indeed light, the intermediate space is the cow, and yonder [world] is life.  
In that they undertake these stomas,  
the sacrificial session performers go establishing themselves in these worlds.
</details>

The Jyotiṣṭoma attains to the virāj: the Go is redundant by one, the Āyus is deficient by one.
