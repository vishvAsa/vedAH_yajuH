विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

स᳓र्वान्वा᳓ एष्रो ऽग्नौ᳓ का᳓मान्प्र᳓वेशयते यो᳙ ऽग्नि᳓मन्वाधा᳓य व्रत᳓मुपै᳓ति स᳓ य᳓द᳓निष्ट्वा प्रयाया᳓द᳓कामप्रीता एनं का᳓मा ना᳓नु प्र᳓यायुरतेजा᳓ अवी᳓र्यस्स्यात् ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

He indeed introduces all desires into the fire who, having added fuel to the fire, approaches the observance.
If he should depart without sacrificing, desires unsatisfied would not follow after him; he would be without fiery brilliance, without vigor.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Vāyu indeed is the giver of milk;
he approaches him with his share;
he bestows milk upon him.
Then he should turn the calves away for the later oblation;
that is the expiation in that case.
