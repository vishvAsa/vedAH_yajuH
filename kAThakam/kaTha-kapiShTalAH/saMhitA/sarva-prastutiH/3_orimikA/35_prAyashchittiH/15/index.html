विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (1/18 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)

अग्नि᳓ना त᳓पो ऽन्वा᳓भवत्सू᳓र्येण ते᳓जो वायु᳓ना प्राणा᳓ [[इन्द्रेण|इ᳓न्द्रेण]] देवा᳓ य᳓मेन पित᳓रस्स᳓रस्वत्या मनुष्या᳙स्सप्तदशे᳓न स्तो᳓मा वामदेव्ये᳓न सा᳓मानि ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Through Agni, heat followed in being;
through the sun, fiery brilliance;
through Vāyu, the life-breaths;
through Indra, the gods;
through Yama, the fathers;
through Sarasvatī, humans;
through the seventeenfold [stoma], the stomas;
through the Vāmadevya, the Sāmans;

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (3/69 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)

गायत्र्या᳓ छ᳓न्दाँसि मण्या᳓ रूपा᳓णि हि᳓रण्येन व᳓र्चाँसि तिर᳓श्चिराजिना सर्पा᳓ अजगरे᳓णाप्सव्या᳙श्श्येने᳓न पतत्रि᳓णो व्याघ्रे᳓णरण्याः᳓ पश᳓वस्सुपर्णे᳓न व᳓याँस्यश्वत्थे᳓न व᳓नस्प᳓तय ओदने᳓ना᳓न्नानि य᳓वेनौ᳓षधयो ऽश्ववृषे᳓णै᳓कशफाः पश᳓वो वृषभे᳓ण गा᳓वो [[वृष्णि᳓ना᳓वयो|वृष्णि᳓नावयो]] बस्ते᳓नाजा᳓श्चन्द्र᳓मसा न᳓क्षत्राणि ब्राह्मणे᳓न वा᳓चो वाचा᳓ देव᳓तास्स्त्रिया᳓ विरा᳓ड् ग्रा᳓व्णा [[प᳓र्वतास्समुद्रे᳓णा᳓पो|प᳓र्वतास्समुद्रे᳓णा᳓पो᳓]] [[ऽड्नि᳓स्सत्यँ᳓|ऽड्निस्सत्यँ]] [[सत्ये᳓नर्तमृते᳓न|सत्ये᳓नर्त᳓मृते᳓न]] त᳓पस्त᳓पसा परमेष्ठी᳓ परमेष्ठि᳓ना मृत्यु᳓र्मृत्यु᳓ना प्रजाः᳓ प्रजा᳓भिस्स᳓गरस्तनू᳓भिः प्रजा᳓पतिस्तै᳓र्देवै᳓रन्वाभू᳓तिर᳓नु च भूयासम᳓ति च भूयासम् ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

through the Gāyatrī, the meters;
through the jewel, forms;
through gold, splendors;
through the cross-striped snake, the serpents;
through the python, the water-creatures;
through the falcon, the winged ones;
through the tiger, the wild beasts;
through the fair-winged bird, the birds;
through the aśvattha tree, the trees of the forest;
through cooked rice, foods;
through barley, plants;
through the stallion, the one-hoofed animals;
through the bull, cattle;
through the ram, sheep;
through the he-goat, goats;
through the moon, the lunar mansions;
through the brāhmaṇa, speeches;
through speech, the deities;
through the woman, the Virāj;
through the pressing-stone, the mountains;
through the ocean, the waters;
through the waters, truth;
through truth, cosmic order;
through cosmic order, heat;
through heat, Parameṣṭhin;
through Parameṣṭhin, Death;
through Death, creatures;
through creatures, the ocean;
through forms, Prajāpati.
With those gods may I be accompanied, may I become conformable, may I become superior,
