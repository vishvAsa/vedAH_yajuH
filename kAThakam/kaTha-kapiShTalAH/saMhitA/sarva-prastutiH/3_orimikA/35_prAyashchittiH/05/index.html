विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः - ऋक् (1/12 व्यत्यस्त-स्वराः)

यज्ञ᳓स्य हि᳓ स्थ᳓ ऋृत्वि᳓जा इ᳓न्द्राग्नी क᳓ल्पना युव᳓म्।
[[हुता᳓हुत᳓स्य|हु᳓ताहुतस्य]] तृम्पतम᳓हुतस्य हुत᳓स्य च ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

For you two, O Indra and Agni, are the priests of the sacrifice, you are the ordinance;
be satisfied with what is offered and what is unoffered, and with what is unoffered and what is offered!

Gemini 3.8 Flash

Seven are the priests, seven their seats; ten are the fingers; the two Aśvins have five provisions;
prāṇa, vyāna, apāna, the mind, the intention, Agni—svāhā! May the gods enjoy this oblation!
