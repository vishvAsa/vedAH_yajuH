To the lord of day, svāhā!
You are the cup of Indra, ungrasped, to be grasped.
You are the stronghold of the gods; that one I enter, that one I resort to, together with household, together with offspring, together with cattle, together with priests, together with the members of the assembly, together with those worthy of Soma, together with those worthy of the dakṣiṇā, together with the sacrifice, together with the lord of the sacrifice!

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Indra and Agni are my enclosing-sticks,
the wind is made the divine stronghold;
to that Indra-cup I resort, with cattle, with horses, with men, together with whatever is mine!
