through the Cāturmāsyas he emitted offspring.
He who, knowing thus, sacrifices with the Cāturmāsyas repels his rival; he is propagated with offspring, with cattle.
Having fashioned the Vaiśvadeva sacrificial rite out of the Agniṣṭoma, Prajāpati emitted offspring.
Having fashioned the Varuṇapraghāsa sacrificial rite out of the Ukthya, he caused the offspring to be seized by Varuṇa.
Having fashioned the Sākamedha sacrificial rite out of the Atirātra, Indra slew Vṛtra.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

By means of the Vaiśvadeva indeed he repelled the Asuras; by means of the Vaiśvadeva he emitted offspring.
Some offspring were created, some were uncreated.
Then Prajāpati desired: “May I emit offspring.”
The sacrifice indeed is the year; the sacrifice is Prajāpati.
He fashioned from his own self these two milks: that of the udder and that of the body.
Then he measured out these oblations as a share for these deities;
with them he emitted offspring.
From the seasons indeed those offspring were propagated.
These five oblations are the seasons,
for the seasons are five;
from them indeed one is propagated.
Agni indeed caused the sowing;
Soma placed the semen.
Agni and Soma indeed are a pair.
Savitṛ impelled.
Savitṛ indeed is the year;
twelve months are the year;
therefore [the cake] is on twelve potsherds.
Also, it is on twelve potsherds just for the sake of belonging to all the gods (vaiśvadeva).
He sacrifices silently,
for the year is unexpressed.
Sarasvatī indeed placed speech in those that were created;
they created towards Pūṣan as a firm foundation.
Speech indeed is Sarasvatī;
cattle are Pūṣan.
Speech and cattle are a pair.
From the middle indeed are offspring emitted;
at the end, from the pair, they are propagated in all directions.
That is this: from Prajāpati indeed, from the middle, offspring are emitted;
then there, at the end, from the pair, on both sides, they are propagated in all directions.
These oblations indeed destroy Vṛtra.
With Agni as the vanguard Indra slew Vṛtra, with king Soma, impelled by Savitṛ; and with Sarasvatī at the Caitra [full moon], Pūṣan followed with heroic powers.
