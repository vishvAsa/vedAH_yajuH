Griffith

Bring the Gods hither, Agni, born for him who strews the sacred grass. Thou art our herald, meet for praise.

Gemini 3.8 Flash

O Agni, bring the gods here, being born for the one whose sacred grass is strewn;
you are our invoker to be praised.

“Agni, the all-beholding, gives, verily, to the man (who worships him, a son) possessing abundant food; Agni, when propitiated proceeds (to bestow) that wealth which is of its own nature precious; do you, Agni, bring food to your adorers.”
