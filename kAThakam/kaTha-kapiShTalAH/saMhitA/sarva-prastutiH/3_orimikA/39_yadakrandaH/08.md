<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)</summary>

आ᳓युषः प्राणँ᳓ स᳓न्तनु प्राणा᳓द्व्यानँ᳓ स᳓न्तनु व्या᳓नाद᳓पानँ᳓ स᳓न्तन्वपाना᳓च्च᳓क्षुस्स᳓न्तनु च᳓क्षुषश्श्रो᳓त्रँ स᳓न्तनु श्रो᳓त्राद्वा᳓चँ स᳓न्तनु वाच᳓ आत्मा᳓नँ स᳓न्तन्वात्म᳓नः पृथिवीँ᳓ स᳓न्तनु पृथिव्या᳓ अन्त᳓रिक्षँ स᳓न्तन्वन्त᳓रिक्षाद्दि᳓वँ स᳓न्तनु दिव᳓स्स्व᳙स्स᳓न्तनु पृथिवी᳓ वशामावस्या᳓ ग᳓र्भो व᳓नस्प᳓तयो जरा᳓य्वग्नि᳓र्वत्सो᳓ऽग्निहोत्रं᳓ पीयू᳓षोऽन्त᳓रिक्षं वशा᳓ धाता᳓ ग᳓र्भो रुद्रा᳓ जरा᳓यु वायु᳓र्वत्सो᳓ ध᳓र्मः पीयू᳓षो द्यौ᳓र्वशा᳓ स्तनयित्नु᳓र्ग᳓र्भो न᳓क्षत्राणि जरा᳓यु सू᳓र्यो वत्सो᳓ वृ᳓ष्टिः पीयू᳓ष ऋ᳓ग्वशा बृहद्रथन्तरे᳓ ग᳓र्भः प्रै᳓ष᳓निवि᳓दो जरा᳓यु यज्ञो᳓ वत्सो᳓ द᳓क्षिणा पीयू᳓षो वि᳓ड्वशा᳓ राजन्यो᳙ ग᳓र्भः पश᳓वो जरा᳓यु रा᳓जा वत्सो᳓ बलिः᳓ पीयू᳓षः ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Extend out in breath from life;  
extend out through-breath from out-breath;  
extend out in-breath from through-breath;  
extend out sight from in-breath;  
extend out hearing from sight;  
extend out speech from hearing;  
extend out the self from speech;  
extend out the earth from the self;  
extend out the intermediate space from the earth;  
extend out heaven from the intermediate space;  
extend out the sun from heaven.  
The earth is the barren cow, the new moon the embryo, the plants the afterbirth, Agni the calf, the Agnihotra the colostrum.  
The intermediate space is the barren cow, Dhātṛ the embryo, the Rudras the afterbirth, Vāyu the calf, the Gharma the colostrum.  
Heaven is the barren cow, thunder the embryo, the constellations the afterbirth, Sūrya the calf, rain the colostrum.  
The Ṛgveda is the barren cow, the Bṛhat and Rathantara the embryo, the summons and Nivids the afterbirth, the sacrifice the calf, the sacrificial fee the colostrum.  
The commoners are the barren cow, the warrior is the embryo, the cattle are the afterbirth, the king is the calf, tribute is the colostrum.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>
