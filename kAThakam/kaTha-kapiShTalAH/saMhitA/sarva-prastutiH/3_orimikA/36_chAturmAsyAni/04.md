<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)</summary>

वाजि᳓नो यजति पश᳓वो वै᳓ वाजि᳓नस्ता᳓नेव᳓ त᳓द्यजति न᳓ वा᳓ एष᳓ सुयज्ञ᳓ इव सँ᳓स्थिते हि᳓ यज्ञे᳓ प्र᳓हृतेषु परिधि᳓षु जुहो᳓ति ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

He sacrifices to the Vājins;  
the Vājins indeed are cattle;  
them he sacrifices then.  
This is not quite like a well-performed sacrifice;  
for when the sacrifice has concluded and the enclosing-sticks have been thrown into the fire, he offers.
</details>

</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)</summary>

य᳓द᳓नवानँ हो᳓ता य᳓जति ते᳓न यज्ञः᳓ क्रियते᳓ऽनुयजत्यग्नि᳓स्स᳓मिष्टिरग्निः᳓ प्रतिष्ठि᳓तिस्स᳓मिष्ट्या एव᳓ प्र᳓तिष्ठित्यै पश᳓वो वै᳓ वाजि᳓नो य᳓त्सँस्थाप᳓येत्ता᳓न्सँ᳓स्थापयेद᳓सँस्थिता ह्ये᳙ते᳓ सददि᳓ प्रजा᳓यन्ते ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Because the Hotṛ sacrifices without drawing breath,  
thereby the sacrifice is performed.  
He offers an after-sacrifice;  
Agni is the finished work, Agni is the firm foundation;  
[it is] for the firm foundation of the finished work.  
The Vājins indeed are cattle;  
if he were to conclude, he would conclude them;  
for un-concluded, they are continually propagated.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

</details>
