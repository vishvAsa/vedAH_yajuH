आमपेषा᳓ भवन्ति स᳓र्वस्याँ᳓हसो᳓ऽवेष्ट्यै य᳓द्भृज्ज्ये᳓युर्वि᳓त्त्वकमँ᳓हो᳓ऽवेष्टँ स्यात्पा᳓त्रेभ्यो वै᳓ ताः᳓ प्रजा᳓ व᳓रुणो ऽगृह्णाद्य᳓त्पा᳓त्राणि पा᳓त्रेभ्य एवै᳓ना व᳓रुणान्मुञ्जति प्रतिपुरुषं᳓ भवन्ति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

They are made of raw flour,
for the expiation of all distress.
If they were roasted, the expiation of distress would be limited.
From dishes, indeed, Varuṇa seized those creatures.
Because there are dishes,
he frees them from Varuṇa precisely from the dishes.
They are one per person;
