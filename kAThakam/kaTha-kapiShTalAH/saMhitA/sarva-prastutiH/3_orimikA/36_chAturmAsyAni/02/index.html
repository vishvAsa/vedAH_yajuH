Gemini 3.8 Flash

therefore it is of manifold form: ghee belongs to Agni, curd to Indra, [and] the clotted butter to Indra and Agni according to deity.
Prāṇa and apāna, indeed, are Indra and Agni.
Prāṇa and apāna are a pair.
Offspring have their origin in a pair;
from that pair itself he is propagated.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

With each oblation, indeed, they held off twelve nights each;
as many nights as belong to the year, so many are they in number.
They held off the year from the rival.
With the Vaiśvadeva they held off four months;
with the Varuṇapraghāsas the next four;
with the Sākamedhas the next four.
Those they held off from the rival.
Because they held off four months at a time, that is the Cāturmāsya nature of the Cāturmāsyas.
