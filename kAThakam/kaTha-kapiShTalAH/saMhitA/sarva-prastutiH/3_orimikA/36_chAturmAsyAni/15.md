<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

The sap of the waters that is in their flowing, that I take for this N.N., son of N.N., for brilliance, for luster.  
The sap of the waters that is in their wave, that I take for this N.N., son of N.N., for might, for dominion.  
The sap of the waters that is in their middle, that I take for this N.N., son of N.N., for offspring, for prosperity.  
The sacrificial body of the waters that there is, that I take for this N.N., son of N.N., for life, for longevity.
