विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

सो᳓मम᳓ग्रे यजति सो᳓मो वै᳓ पितॄणां᳓ देव᳓ता पितृदेवत्यो᳙ हि᳓ सो᳓मो य᳓त्सो᳓मं पितृम᳓न्तं य᳓जति सोमपाँ᳓स्त᳓त्पितॄ᳓न्यजति य᳓द्बर्हिष᳓दो य᳓ज्वनस्त᳓द्य᳓दग्निष्वात्ता᳓न्गृहमेधि᳓नस्त᳓द्य᳓दग्निं᳓ कव्यवा᳓हनं द्वे᳓ वा᳓ अग्ने᳓स्तन्वौ᳓ हव्यवा᳓हन्या᳓ देवे᳓भ्यो हव्यं᳓ व᳓हति कव्य᳓वा᳓हन्या पितृ᳓भ्योऽग्नि᳓स्स᳓मिष्टिरग्निः᳓ प्रतिष्ठि᳓तिस्स᳓मिष्ट्या एव᳓ प्र᳓तिष्ठित्यै प᳓ञ्च प᳓ञ्च कृ᳓त्वो᳓ऽवद्यति प᳓ञ्च ह्यृ᳙त᳓वस्स्वधा᳓ न᳓म इ᳓ति व᳓षट्करोति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

He sacrifices to Soma first;
Soma indeed is the deity of the fathers,
for Soma has the fathers as his deity.
In that he sacrifices to Soma accompanied by the fathers,
he thereby sacrifices to the Soma-drinking fathers.
In that [he sacrifices to those] seated on the sacrificial grass, [he sacrifices] to those who sacrificed;
in that [he sacrifices to the] Agniṣvāttas, [he sacrifices] to the householders;
in that [he sacrifices to] Agni, the bearer of the Kavyas—
two indeed are Agni’s bodies:
with the one that bears the Havyas he bears the oblation to the gods, with the one that bears the Kavyas to the fathers.
Agni is the Samiṣṭi, Agni is the firm foundation;
[it is] for the Samiṣṭi, for the firm foundation.
He cuts portions five times each;
for five are the seasons.
He utters the vaṣaṭ-call as ‘Svadhā, namaḥ’;

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)

स्वधा᳓ हि᳓ पितॄणां᳓ नमस्कारो᳓ देवा᳓नां द्वे᳓ वै᳓ देवा᳓नां याज्यानुवाक्ये᳙ प्रा᳓न्यया य᳓च्छति गमयत्यन्य᳓या᳓था᳓त्र तिस्रः᳓ कार्या᳙ व्या᳓वृत्त्यै द्वे᳓ अनुवाक्ये᳙ ए᳓का याज्या᳙ प᳓रे वै᳓ देवे᳓भ्यः पित᳓रो यै᳓षा᳓ तृती᳓याभ्ये᳙वै᳓त᳓या प्रय᳓च्छति ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

for svadhā is for the fathers, namaskāra for the gods.
Two indeed are the yājyā and the anuvākyā of the gods;
with the one he holds forth, with the other he causes [it] to reach [them].
Now here three are to be made,
for separation:
two anuvākyās, one yājyā.
Farther indeed than the gods are the fathers;
that which is the third, with it indeed he reaches out.
