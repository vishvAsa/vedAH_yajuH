Gemini 3.8 Flash

With, ‘Splendor art thou; place splendor in me,’

Ambī indeed is a woman whose name is good fortune (bhagā);
therefore [they are called] Tryambakas.
He indeed is without a firm foundation whose oblation has no firm foundation; the Tryambakas have no firm foundation:
no fore-sacrifices are offered, nor after-sacrifices;
he does not recite the kindling-verses.
