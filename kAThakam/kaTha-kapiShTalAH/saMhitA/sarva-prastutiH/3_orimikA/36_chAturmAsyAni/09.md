<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)</summary>

ते᳓ श्वो᳓ वृत्रँ᳓ ह᳓निष्यन्त उ᳓पावसँस्तेऽब्रुवन्क᳓स्य वाहेदँ᳓ श्वो᳓ भविता᳓ क᳓स्य वा पचते᳓ति त᳓ एत᳓मोदन᳓मपचन्त ते पशू᳓नचिकयुर्यतरा᳓न्वा᳓ इमे᳓ श्वः᳓ कमितार᳓स्ते जेता᳓र इ᳓ति ते᳓भ्य एते᳓न प्रा᳓तिष्ठँस्ता᳓नेते᳓नायच्छन्पशूना᳓मेवै᳓षा᳓ य᳓तिस्ते श्वोविजयि᳓नोऽवसँस्तेऽब्रुवन्क᳓स्य वाहेदँ᳓ श्वो᳓ भविता᳓ क᳓स्य वा पचते᳓ति त᳓ एत᳓मोदन᳓मपचन्त तेऽब्रुवन्माहुत᳓मशिष्मे᳓ति ते᳓ मरु᳓द्भो᳓ गृहमेधे᳓भ्योऽजुहवुः पश᳓वो वै᳓ मरु᳓तो गृहमेधा᳓स्ते᳓भ्य एव᳓ त᳓दजुहवुस्ते सं᳓यत्ता आसँस्ते᳓ऽसुरा देवे᳓भ्यः क्षु᳓धं प्रा᳓हिण्वँस्तां᳓ देवाः᳓ प्र᳓तिश्रुत्यैत᳓मोदन᳓मपचन्त सा᳓ देवे᳓षु लोक᳓म᳓वित्त्वा पु᳓नर᳓सुरान्प्रा᳓विशत्सै᳓नान्नि᳓रदहद᳓पि प्र᳓तिवेश ओदनं᳓ पचेत ।

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

About to slay Vṛtra tomorrow, they fasted nearby.  
They said: “To whom indeed will this [world] belong tomorrow? For whom will there be cooking?”  
They cooked this boiled rice.  
They looked out for the cattle: “Whichever of these two [sides] they shall favor tomorrow, they shall be the conquerors.”  
With this [rice] they went forth towards them;  
with this they held them;  
this is the path of cattle.  
They dwelt [there], victorious on the morrow.  
They said: “To whom indeed will this [world] belong tomorrow? For whom will there be cooking?”  
They cooked this boiled rice.  
They said: “Let us not eat what has not been offered.”  
They offered to the Maruts, the Gṛhamedhas;  
the Maruts, the Gṛhamedhas, are cattle;  
to them indeed they offered then.  
They were arrayed for battle.  
The Asuras sent hunger against the gods.  
The gods, having heard it coming, cooked this boiled rice.  
Not having found a place among the gods, it entered the Asuras again;  
it burned them up.  
Even in the vicinity, one should cook boiled rice;

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

The gods deposited this portion for Indra:  
“Having his portion set down, may he choose us tomorrow.”  
The roar is the vaṣaṭ-call.  
Moreover, they then called away the bull of the Asuras:  
“May he propagate us.”  
He calls upon the bull;  
he thereby invokes Indra;  
and he also wrenches away the prosperity of his rival.
