<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)</summary>

ता᳓ वैश्वदेवे᳓न सृष्टा᳓स्त᳓स्मिँस्तरुणिम᳓न्व᳓रुणोऽगृह्णाद᳓थो आहुर᳓ति वै᳓ ता᳓स्त᳓मचरँस्ता᳓ अतिच᳓रन्तीर्व᳓रुणेनाग्राहयदि᳓ति त᳓स्मात्पिता᳓ ना᳓तिचरितवै᳓ वैश्वदेवे᳓न वै᳓ प्रजा᳓पतिः प्रजा᳓ असृजत ।

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Those, created by the Vaiśvadeva, Varuṇa seized in that tender state.  
Moreover they say: “They transgressed against him; because they transgressed, he caused Varuṇa to seize them.”  
Therefore a father is not to be transgressed against.  
Prajāpati, indeed, created creatures by the Vaiśvadeva.
</details>
