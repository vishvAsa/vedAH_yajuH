<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

With 'Let it be heard!', with that [he yokes] the Upabhṛt.  
With 'Sacrifice!', with that [he yokes] the Dhruvā.  
With 'We who sacrifice', with that [he yokes] the butter-container.  
With the vaṣaṭ-call [he yokes] the dipping spoon [sruva].  
Yonder [sky] is the Juhū,  
the atmosphere is the Upabhṛt,  
this [earth] is the Dhruvā,  
the quarters are the butter-container,  
yonder sun is the dipping spoon.  
Yonder [sky] gives to this [earth], this [earth gives] to yonder.  
Therefore, with what is in the Juhū, with that he swells the Dhruvā;  
with what is in the Dhruvā, with that [he swells] the Juhū.  
For yonder [sky] gives to this [earth], and this [earth] to yonder.  
Continuously without interruption [gifts are] given to him
</details>
