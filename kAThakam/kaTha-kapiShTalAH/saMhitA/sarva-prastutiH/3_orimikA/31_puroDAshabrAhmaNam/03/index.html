सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

The waters are the dear abode of the gods.
Having led forward the dear abode of the gods, he proceeds.
The waters are smiters of demons,
for the smiting away of the demons.
The waters are the vajra.
He hurls the vajra at the rival.
The waters are faith.
Verily, the gods put faith, men put faith in him, for whom he who knows thus, [or] who, knowing thus, leads forward the waters.
With ‘For the work, [I wash] you two’, he washes his hands;
for this work is being performed.
With ‘Thou art of the tree; burned away is the demon’,
he smites away the demons from the mouth of the sacrifice.
With ‘Go thou through the wide mid-space’,
[it is] for attainment.
