भु᳓वनमसि वि᳓प्रथस्वे᳓ति प्रथ᳓यत्येवा᳓ग्नि᳓र्य᳓ष्टेदं᳓ न᳓म इ᳓ति नमस्कृ᳓त्य हि᳓ श्रे᳓याँसमुपच᳓रन्ति जुह्वे᳓ह्य᳙ग्नि᳓स्त्वाह्व᳓यति देवयज्या᳓या इ᳓त्यग्नि᳓स्स᳓र्वादेव᳓तास्स᳓र्वा एव᳓ देव᳓ता अ᳓वरुन्द्ध ।

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

With ‘Thou art the world, spread wide’,
he spreads [it] out.
With ‘Agni is the sacrificer; here is homage’,
for after paying homage they approach a superior.
With ‘Come, Juhū, Agni calls thee to the divine worship’,
Agni is all the deities;
he secures all the deities.

Gemini 3.8 Flash

// With ‘To the earth thee, to the intermediate space thee, to heaven thee’,
he causes it to reach these worlds.
With ‘Licking the anointed [food], let the birds eat’,
he makes it savory.
With ‘Having become a spotted barren cow of the Maruts, dappled, go to heaven’,
the prastara bunch is indeed the sacrificer;
having become an oblation, he causes him to reach the heavenly world.
With ‘Thence impel rain for us’,
he secures rain.
With ‘Unimpaired is the out-breath’,
the adhvaryu thereby arranges the breaths at the head.
With ‘A guardian of the eye art thou, protect my eye’,
[it is] for the protection of the eye.
With ‘Which enclosing stick thou didst put around, O god Agni, when kindled by the Paṇis’,
Agni is all the deities;
he secures all the deities.
