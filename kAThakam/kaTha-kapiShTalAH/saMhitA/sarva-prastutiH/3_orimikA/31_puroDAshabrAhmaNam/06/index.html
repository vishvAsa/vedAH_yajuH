burnt up the enemy’,

When he rolls a burning coal beneath,
thereby he places light in this world.
When he rolls [one] over it above,
thereby he places light in the intermediate space.

He puts down three [potsherds] joined together.
Three are the breaths: prāṇa, vyāna, apāna;
these he places in the sacrificer.
Three are these worlds;
in these worlds he thrives.
