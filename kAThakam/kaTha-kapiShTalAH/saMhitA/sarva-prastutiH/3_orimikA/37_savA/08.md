<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)</summary>

पञ्चदश᳓स्स्तो᳓म ओ᳓जो वै᳓ वीर्यं᳙ पञ्चदश᳓ ओ᳓जसैव᳓ वीर्ये᳙णाभि᳓षिच्यते बृहत्सामौ᳓जो वै᳓ वीर्यं᳙ बृह᳓दो᳓जसैव᳓ वीर्ये᳙णाभि᳓षिच्यत उक्थ्य᳙स्स्तो᳓म ओ᳓जो वै᳓ वीर्य᳙मुक्था᳓न्यो᳓जसैव᳓ वीर्ये᳙णाभि᳓षिच्यत ऐन्द्रँ᳓ स्तोत्रं᳓ च शस्त्रं᳓ च त्रै᳓ष्टुभाः प᳓वमाना ओ᳓जो वै᳓ वीर्यं᳙ त्रिष्टु᳓बो᳓जसैव᳓ वीर्ये᳙णाभि᳓षिच्यत इ᳓न्द्रं वै᳓ स᳓र्वा मृ᳓धस्स᳓र्वा नाष्ट्रा᳓स्स᳓र्वाणि र᳓क्षाँस्यजिघाँसँस्त᳓मेते᳓न प्रजा᳓पतिरयाजयत्स᳓ स᳓र्वा मृ᳓धस्स᳓र्वा नाष्ट्रा᳓स्स᳓र्वाणि र᳓क्षाँस्यतरदि᳓न्द्रो य᳓जमानो य᳓देते᳓न य᳓जते स᳓र्वा एव᳓ मृ᳓धस्स᳓र्वा नाष्ट्रा᳓- स्स᳓र्वाणि र᳓क्षाँसि तरति स᳓ एष᳓ इन्द्रसवः᳓ ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

The stoma is fifteenfold;  
the fifteenfold is vigor and virility;  
with vigor and virility he is consecrated.  
The Bṛhat is the sāman;  
the Bṛhat is vigor and virility;  
with vigor and virility he is consecrated.  
The Ukthya is the stoma;  
the Ukthas are vigor and virility;  
with vigor and virility he is consecrated.  
The stotra and the śastra belong to Indra, and the Pavamānas are in the Triṣṭubh meter;  
the Triṣṭubh is vigor and virility;  
with vigor and virility he is consecrated.  
All adversaries, all destructions, all demons wished to slay Indra;  
Prajāpati caused him to sacrifice with this;  
he overcame all adversaries, all destructions, all demons.  
Indra is the sacrificer;  
in that he sacrifices with this,  
he overcomes all adversaries, all destructions, all demons.  
That is this Indrasava.
</details>
