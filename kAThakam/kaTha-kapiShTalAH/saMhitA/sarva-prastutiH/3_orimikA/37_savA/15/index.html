Gemini 3.8 Flash

To the night, svāhā!
Satisfied are you;
satisfied am I.
Satisfied is my out-breath; satisfied is my diffused breath; satisfied is my in-breath;
satisfied is my sight; satisfied is my hearing; satisfied is my speech; satisfied is my self.
Sky is my father; Earth is my mother;
Sun is my sight; Wind is my breath.
Unconquered by name am I; as such, I entrust this self of mine to Sky and Earth; upon them two I lean, upon them two in heroic stride.
Carry me across unto protection!
Do not harm me!
Protect [me] from the hater; protect from injury;
protect from ungodly defamation!
You are a stone, body-protecting;
as such, protect my body here.

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

In you is my vulnerable spot, in Bṛhaspati my breath; as such, protect me from death.
Upward speech, upward insight, upward faculty, upward offspring, upward cattle—may he reach this who threatens me from this direction.
May Indra and Agni protect me from this direction;
may the fathers protect me from this direction;
may Savitṛ protect me from this direction;
may Mitra and Varuṇa protect me from this direction;
may Bṛhaspati protect me from this direction.
Life-making, mistress of life, O one of independent power!
Our herdsmen are you, protectors;
may no one dig us out; may we not perish!
