<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

The ṛṣis did not see Indra directly.  
Vasiṣṭha alone saw him directly.  
He feared: 'He will tell the other ṛṣis.'  
He said: 'I will tell you a Brāhmaṇa, so that offspring with you as Purohita will be born, but do not speak of me to the other ṛṣis.'  
To him he told these Stomabhāgas.  
Thence offspring with Vasiṣṭha as Purohita were born.  
The stoma indeed is their share;  
that is the Stomabhāganess of the Stomabhāgas.  
'You are a ray; for dwelling [I take] you; quicken the dwelling!' [he says].  
The dwelling indeed is the gods.
