<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Griffith</summary>

To Agni Anîkavân is sacrificed a red-marked ox;
</details>

<details><summary>Keith</summary>

To Agni of the front the red-limbed ox;
</details>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

For Agni with the frontline, a red-streaked draft-ox;
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>पदपाठः - तैत्तिरीयः</summary>

</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)</summary>

अधो᳓रामौ सावित्रौ᳓ पौष्णो᳓ रजत᳓नाभी वैश्वदेवौ᳓ पि‍श᳓ङ्गौ तूपरौ᳓ मारुतः᳓ कल्मा᳓ष आग्नेयः᳓ कृष्णो᳓ऽज᳓स्सारस्वती᳓ मेषी᳓ वारुणः᳓ कृष्ण᳓ ए᳓कशितिपात्पे᳓त्वः ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

two black-bellied ones for Savitṛ;  
two with silver navels for Pūṣan;  
two tawny, hornless ones for the All-gods;  
the Maruts' is a piebald one;  
Agni's is a black he-goat;  
for Sarasvatī, a ewe;  
Varuṇa's is a black ram with one white foot.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

</details>
