<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः (यन्त्र-स्वरः)</summary>

मेष᳓स्त्वा पचतै᳓रवतु लो᳓हितग्रीवश्छागैश्शल्मलिर्ऋद्या पर्णो᳓ ब्र᳓ह्मणा प्लक्षो᳓ मे᳓धेन न्यग्रो᳓धश्चमसै᳓रुदुम्ब᳓र ऊर्जा᳓ ।
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

Let the ram protect you with cooked offerings,  
the red-necked [Agni] with he-goats,  
the śalmali with prosperity,  
the parṇa with the sacred formulation,  
the plakṣa with sacrifice,  
the banyan with cups,  
the udumbara with vigor,
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः - ऋक् (यन्त्र-स्वरः)</summary>

प्रियं᳓ त्वा प्रिया᳓णां व᳓र्षिष्ठमा᳓प्यानां गणा᳓नां त्वा गण᳓पतिँ हवामहे व᳓सो म᳓म  
प्रिया᳓णां त्वा प्रिय᳓पतिँ हवामहे व᳓सो म᳓म  
निधीनां᳓ त्वा निधि᳓पतिँ हवामहे व᳓सो मम ॥
</details>

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

you, the dear one of the dear,  
the highest of friends.  
We call upon you, the host-leader of hosts, O my treasure;  
we call upon you, the dear-lord of dear ones, O my treasure;  
we call upon you, the treasure-lord of treasures, O my treasure.
</details>

निधीनां त्वा निधिपतिँ हवामहे वसो मम
</details>
