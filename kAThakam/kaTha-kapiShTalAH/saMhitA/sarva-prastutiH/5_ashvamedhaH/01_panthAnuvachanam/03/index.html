सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

Pervading through your mother, dominating through your father.
You are a horse;
you are a courser;
you are delight;
you are a racer;
you are a man;
you are a steed;
you are a runner;
you are swift;
you are a stallion;
you are manly-minded;
Yayur is your name.
Follow the flight of the Ādityas!
To Agni, svāhā!
