सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

To the sun, svāhā!
You are truth.
You are the truth of truth.
You are reality.
You are the reality of reality.
You are the path of truth, the shadow of the gods, the name of immortality;
that is reality, that you are Prajāpati.

अपो᳓ वृणानः᳓ पवते क᳓वीयन्पशू᳓न्न᳓ गोपा᳓ इ᳓र्यः प᳓रिज्मा ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

When, as over a steed, the clans of heaven vie with the sun for beauty over him,
choosing the waters, he purifies himself acting as a sage, like an active herdsman wandering around the cattle.
