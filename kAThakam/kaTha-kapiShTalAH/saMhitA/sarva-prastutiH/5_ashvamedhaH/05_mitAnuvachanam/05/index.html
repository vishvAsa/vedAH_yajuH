सर्वाष् टीकाः
Gemini 3.8 Flash

the ocean is your womb;
the sun is your eye;
the wind is your breath;
the moon is your ear;
the quarters are your feet;
the intermediate quarters are your animals;
the lunar mansions are your form;
the months and the half-months are your joints;
the seasons are your limbs;
the year is your majesty.
