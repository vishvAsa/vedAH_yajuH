<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

It bowed to Agni; it bowed to the earth.  
As Agni bowed together with the earth, so may auspicious compliances bow to me!  
It bowed to Vāyu; it bowed to the intermediate space.  
As Vāyu bowed together with the intermediate space, so may auspicious compliances bow to me!  
It bowed to Sūrya; it bowed to heaven.  
As Sūrya bowed together with heaven, so may auspicious compliances bow to me!  
It bowed to Varuṇa; it bowed to the waters.  
As Varuṇa bowed together with the waters, so may auspicious compliances bow to me!  
It bowed to the Sāman; it bowed to the Ṛc.  
As the Sāman bowed together with the Ṛc, so may auspicious compliances bow to me!  
It bowed to the priesthood; it bowed to the nobility.  
As the priesthood bowed together with the nobility, so may auspicious compliances bow to me!  
It bowed to the king; it bowed to the people.  
As the king bowed together with the people, so may auspicious compliances bow to me!  
It bowed to the chariot; it bowed to the horses.  
As the chariot bowed together with the horses, so may auspicious compliances bow to me!
</details>
