<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः</summary>

<details><summary>Gemini 3.8 Flash</summary>

to the Aśvins, the deliverers from sin, roasted grains;  
to the Maruts, the deliverers from fault, [a cake] on seven potsherds;  
to the All-Gods, the deliverers from fault, [a cake] on twelve potsherds;  
to Anumati a mess of boiled rice;  
to Agni Vaiśvānara [a cake] on twelve potsherds;  
to Heaven and Earth, the deliverers from distress, [a cake] on two potsherds.
</details>
